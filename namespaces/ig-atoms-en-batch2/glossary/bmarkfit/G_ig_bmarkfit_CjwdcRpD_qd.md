# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjwdcRpD_qd — skin in the game

## Term

Skin in the game is the reason the reel gives for why free information does not move people: unless people pay, they don't pay attention, and the success you have not achieved yet is because you have not put any skin in the game. The phrase appears once, beside unless people pay, they don't pay attention, 750 podcast episodes, 1400 clients, and invest in yourself. All the information needed is on Google for free, and the speaker has over 750 podcast episodes to listen to — yet his business has 1,400 clients while giving everything away for free. The question it ends on turns the rule back on the listener: how are you trying to get clients to invest in you if you are not even willing to invest in yourself?

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The reason you haven't achieved success yet is because you haven't put any skin in the game." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CjwdcRpD_qd.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that free information is worthless: the reel says all the information is on Google for free and he gives everything away, and its point is that paying creates attention, not that free content teaches nothing.
- Read as proof that his 1400 clients paid for attention alone: the business has 1400 clients while giving everything away for free, and the source never says attention is the only thing they bought.
