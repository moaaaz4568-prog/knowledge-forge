# G_ig_bmarkfit_Cj6KqK7jf3h — proximity is power

## Term

Proximity is power is the third of three lessons the speaker says he learned from $100,000 of coaching with Bedros Keuilian: surround yourself with individuals that can take you to the level you want to get. The phrase appears once, beside protect your time, brotherhood is everything, Bedros Keuilian, and pick your brain. The first two lessons frame it — stop letting individuals pick your brain and focus on yourself, and create a culture people want to buy into for life, not just for two months. Proximity here is about who is around you, and the power is the level they can take you to.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Third, proximity is power. You need to surround yourself with individuals that can take you to the level that you want to get." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_Cj6KqK7jf3h.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that proximity replaces skill: the lesson pairs surrounding yourself with people who can lift your level, and the source never says their presence alone does the work.
- Read as a promise that expensive coaching buys the level: the $100,000 bought coaching with Bedros Keuilian, and the reel reports three lessons learned, not a guaranteed outcome.
