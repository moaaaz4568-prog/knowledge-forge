# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkMBeG4jEwV — going all in on you

## Term

Going all in on you is the closing move of the reel's Bali story: the speaker is making $6 million a year right now, and it all started in Bali, where he first had to get $10 hotel rooms because he could not afford anything else. The phrase appears once, beside $6 million a year, $10 hotel rooms, nine to five, and life of your dreams. The scare is named — stepping out of your nine to five and starting to work on your own business — and the reward is declared worth it. The condition is the same as the opening claim: it does not matter where you are in life right now, you can achieve massive success.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you wanna live the life of your dreams, it's time you stop working for somebody else and start going all in on you." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CkMBeG4jEwV.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that Bali made the money: the success started in Bali, and the source never says the place caused it.
- Read as a promise that quitting a job guarantees success: the reel says the reward is worth it, and it never measures the risk or the time it takes.
