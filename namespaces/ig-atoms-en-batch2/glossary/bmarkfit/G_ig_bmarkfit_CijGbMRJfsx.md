# G_ig_bmarkfit_CijGbMRJfsx — never give up on yourself

## Term

Never give up on yourself is the reel's closing charge, drawn from the speaker's own turnaround: nine years ago he suffered with a drug addiction and was fired from the bar he worked at, and now he runs a successful online business, has his own podcast, owns his own gym, and is married and happiest he has ever been. The phrase appears once, beside drug addiction, fired, successful online business, and chase your dreams. The firing itself is retold in detail — a call from the manager, caught on camera filling up bottles and doing drugs behind the bar. The charge is the moral of that reversal: never quit on yourself, chase your dreams.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that persistence alone builds a business: the reel pairs never giving up with the turn from addiction and firing to business, podcast, and gym, not with waiting alone.
- Read as a claim that the past is erased: the speaker retells the firing and the addiction as the starting point, and the source never says those years stopped mattering.
