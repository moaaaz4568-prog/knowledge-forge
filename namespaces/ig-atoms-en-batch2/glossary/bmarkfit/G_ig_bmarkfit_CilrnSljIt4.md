# G_ig_bmarkfit_CilrnSljIt4 — built my offer

## Term

Built my offer is step one of the reel's four-step path from a tiny two-bedroom apartment to a 6,500 square foot mansion: decide what you will coach and create the offer itself. The phrase appears once, beside $400 a month subscription, business coach, got super loud, and delivered the results. The offer in question was a $400 a month subscription coaching individuals on how to start their online business, as a business coach for online fitness coaches. The later steps only work because the offer exists first — getting loud about it, selling it hard once confident, and delivering the promised results.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Step number one is I built my offer. I decided I was gonna be a business coach for online fitness coaches, and I created a $400 a month subscription where I was coaching individuals on how to start their online business." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CilrnSljIt4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that an offer alone makes money: the offer here is step one of four, followed by getting loud, selling hard, and delivering results.
- Read as a promise that the same four steps give the same mansion: the reel tells one person's path from a two-bedroom apartment to a 6,500 square foot mansion, not a measured guarantee for anyone repeating the steps.
