# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjB_wC-JzUq — your fucking fault

## Term

Your fucking fault is the reel's blunt rule: every single thing in your life is your fault, including the things you think you have no control over. The phrase appears twice, beside she left you, broke your heart, solve all the problems, and my fault. The heartbreak example makes the logic concrete — she left and broke your heart, and that is your fault too. The payoff the reel claims is power: because everything that happens is my fault, I can also solve all the problems in my life. Fault here is not punishment, it is the lever for solving.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as literal blame for things outside your control: the reel says even things he thinks he has no control over count as his fault, and its point is that fault brings the power to solve the problems.
- Read as a claim that heartbreak is deserved: the example of being left and heartbroken is used to show that owning it means being able to solve it, not that the pain was fair.
