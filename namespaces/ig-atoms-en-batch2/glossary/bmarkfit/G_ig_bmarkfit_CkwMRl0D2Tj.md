# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkwMRl0D2Tj — patiently urgent

## Term

Patiently urgent is number three of the three things the speaker says turned his life around from struggling to make $3,000 a month to making $600,000 a month. The phrase appears once, beside $600,000 a month, honest self-assessment, hired mentors, and multimillionaire. The definition is given in the same breath: every single day taking urgent action towards the things he wants, while also being patient enough to not rush the process. The other two frame it — a commitment to go all in regardless of risk and fear, and an honest self-assessment of strengths and weaknesses with mentors hired for the weaknesses.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Number three is I was patiently urgent, meaning every single day I took urgent action towards the things that I want, but I'm also patient enough to not rush the process." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CkwMRl0D2Tj.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that three things make a multimillionaire: the three things helped him become a multimillionaire, and the source never says they are enough for anyone else.
- Read as a claim that patience and urgency never conflict: patiently urgent means urgent action every day without rushing the process, and the source never says where the line between them sits.
