# G_ig_bmarkfit_Cl9bM_pjmt0 — retention is everything

## Term

Retention is everything is step five of the reel's five-step path to a million by 30, and the reason the speaker gives for his business growing every single month: when clients come in, they actually stay. The phrase appears once, beside sell before you're ready, work for free, build a community, and fulfilling the promise. The earlier steps feed it — selling before ready, becoming the trusted resource, working for free to gain testimonials, and building a community so clients do not leave as soon as they hit their goals. Retention here is the payoff of fulfilling the promise offered, not a tactic on its own. The reel closes by returning to the start: at 23 he wrote down he would be a millionaire by 30, and he did it.

## Live Example

> "Five is retention is everything. The reason our business grows every single month is when our clients come in, they actually stay." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_Cl9bM_pjmt0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that retention alone grows a business: retention is step five of five, and the source never says it works without the selling, the free work, and the community before it.
- Read as a promise that writing down big goals achieves them: the reel says writing down big goals puts you one step closer, and it never says the writing does the achieving.
