# G_ig_bmarkfit_CiWOc_lJ2PB — university is a scam

## Term

University is a scam is the reel's verdict on higher education: people go in expecting a future good payout and come out with debt and no job that pays. The phrase appears twice, beside university degrees, $8,000, hundreds of thousands of dollars in debt, and 19 years old. The contrast case is a 19-year-old online coach who made $8,000 in his first four weeks of coaching, while degree holders struggle to find work. The reel's alternative is posting consistently online, where it says making between five and $10,000 a month within 30 days is possible.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Now the reason I think university is a scam is I think so many people go to university with this idea of a future good payout in mind." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CiWOc_lJ2PB.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that all degrees are worthless: the reel argues from job outcomes and debt, not from what anyone learns, and it never discusses fields where a degree is required.
- Read as a promise that posting online earns $10,000 a month: the reel says it is possible within 30 days of posting consistently, not that it happens to everyone who posts.
