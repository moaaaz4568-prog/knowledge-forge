# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkBuYjWD7Pm — ideal profile

## Term

Ideal profile is step one of the reel's four steps to get clients for an online fitness business: set up your ideal profile to speak to your ideal client. The phrase appears once, beside ideal client, lose 10 pounds without giving up wine, 10 moms per day, and 40% close rate. The example is a coach who helps moms lose 10 pounds without giving up wine, and the profile must speak to them in the words they use. The later steps build on it — content aimed specifically at them, reaching out to 10 moms per day to build relationships, and aiming to book one call per day, five calls per week, selling two clients a week at a 40% close rate.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that the four steps fill a business: the steps end at two clients a week at a 40% close rate, and the source presents that as the arithmetic of the plan, not a guarantee.
- Read as a claim that the example client is the only client: the moms who lose 10 pounds without giving up wine are the example for speaking to the ideal client, and the source never says other clients do not exist.
