# G_ig_bmarkfit_CiJWRSwD4fu — turn the fucking world off

## Term

Turn the fucking world off names the six-month shutdown the reel prescribes: stop listening to other people's approval and go all in on your coaching business. The turn-off wording appears twice, once as "turning off the fucking world" and once as "turn the fucking world off", beside six months, coaching business, other people's approval, and your own success. The contrast is the point — caring more about approval than about your own success is what keeps the world on. The reel frames the shutdown as the price of going all in, not as a permanent retreat.

## Synonyms

- turning off the fucking world (the same phrasal verb in its continuous form)

## Live Example

> "You're not willing to turn the fucking world off for six months and go all in on your coaching business because you care more about other people's approval than you do your own success." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CiJWRSwD4fu.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to abandon all contact for good: the shutdown here lasts six months and serves the coaching business, and the source never extends it beyond that stretch.
- Read as a claim that approval-seeking is the only obstacle: the reel names approval as what holds the coach back, but it does not measure what else might hold a business back.
