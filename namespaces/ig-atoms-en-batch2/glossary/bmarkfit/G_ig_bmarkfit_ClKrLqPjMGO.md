# G_ig_bmarkfit_ClKrLqPjMGO — lose momentum

## Term

Lose momentum is the reel's warning about what happens when you stop: you lose momentum, and then when you lose momentum, you have to grind to get it back. The phrase appears three times, beside grind to get it back, it sucks ass, it should be painful, and not to lose momentum again. The reel does not soften it — it sucks ass, the speaker hates it too, and it should suck right now. The pain is the point: the suck is gonna remind you not to lose momentum again.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that momentum never returns: losing it means grinding to get it back, and the source never says the grind fails.
- Read as proof that pain alone prevents the next loss: the suck is said to remind you not to lose momentum again, and the source never says the reminder always works.
