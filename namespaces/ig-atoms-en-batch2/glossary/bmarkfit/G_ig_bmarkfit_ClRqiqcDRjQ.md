# G_ig_bmarkfit_ClRqiqcDRjQ — make progress or make changes

## Term

Make progress or make changes is the weekly rule of the reel's pitch script: with check-ins on a weekly basis, every single week the coach and client are either gonna make progress or make changes to the program. The phrase appears twice, beside 16-week program, 999 for 16 weeks, 299 a month, and the check-in process. The pitch walks the program in order — the 16-week workout program changing every four weeks, the nutrition program changing the meal plan every week so the client never gets bored, then the check-ins. The close is two options to enroll: 999 for 16 weeks to save money, or 299 a month for easier cash flow. The reel's verdict on the whole script is that it is a lot fucking easier than the listener has been making it out to be in his head.
