# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkryQdcD3oB — bad business coach

## Term

Bad business coach is the figure the reel warns about: have you been burned by a bad business coach that promised you the world but delivered you shit? The phrase appears once, beside promised you the world but delivered you shit, phonies in the industry, $20,000 for basic information, and Do your research before you hire a coach. The phonies come in two types — people that have never done it before and think they can teach, or people that have done it before and charge you $20,000 for basic information. The reel's only protection is the closing command: do your research before you hire a coach.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that every coach is a phony: the phonies are a lot in the industry, and the source never says all coaches are.
- Read as proof that $20,000 buys only basic information: the second type charges $20,000 for basic information, and the source never shows what that information actually is.
