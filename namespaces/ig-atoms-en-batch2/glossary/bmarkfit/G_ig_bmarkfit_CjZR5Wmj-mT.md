# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjZR5Wmj-mT — block out the opinions

## Term

Block out the opinions is the reel's closing instruction after a story of being told it would fail: everybody said it was a bad idea and they were not going to be successful, so the speaker followed his heart instead of taking the opinion of everybody else. The phrase appears once, beside followed my heart, one of the best gyms in Western Canada, focus on your vision, and believe in yourself. The result offered is time — two years later they are still here, one of the best gyms in Western Canada. The instruction is to focus on the vision and believe in yourself when everybody else doubts you.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Block out the opinions of other people. Focus on your vision and believe in yourself when everybody else doubts you." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CjZR5Wmj-mT.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to ignore all feedback: the reel is about not taking the opinion of everybody else when it contradicts your vision, and it never says feedback itself is worthless.
- Read as proof that doubters are always wrong: the gym is still here two years later as one of the best in Western Canada, and the source presents that as one outcome, not as a rule that every doubted plan succeeds.
