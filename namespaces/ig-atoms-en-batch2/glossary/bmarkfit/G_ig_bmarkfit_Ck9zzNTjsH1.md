# G_ig_bmarkfit_Ck9zzNTjsH1 — getting clients

## Term

Getting clients names the skill the reel says is different from training clients: a lot of entitled personal trainers know how to train clients and then bitch and moan because other people are getting all the clients. The phrase appears once, beside entitled personal trainers, exercise science degree, sell and market yourself, and studying sales and marketing. The test is blunt — no one gives a fuck that you have an exercise science degree if you do not know how to sell and market yourself. The alternative offered is to get your head out of your ass and start studying sales and marketing, and maybe do a little bit better.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "There's a big difference between training clients and getting clients." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_Ck9zzNTjsH1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that training skill is worthless: the reel says no one cares about the exercise science degree if you cannot sell and market yourself, and it never says the training itself does not matter.
- Read as proof that new online trainers steal clients: the trainers taking all the clients are the contrast case for studying sales and marketing, and the source never says they took anyone's clients unfairly.
