# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjY6sDLD1dr — closed mouths don't get fed

## Term

Closed mouths don't get fed is the first of three lessons from hosting seven time Mr. Olympia Phil Heath at the speaker's house: he should have just asked Phil before giving him the event, and Phil said yes. The phrase appears once, beside Phil Heath, seven time Mr. Olympia, staying the champ, and get hate. The other two lessons frame the same arc — becoming the champ is one thing and staying the champ is another, and you need to be willing to get hate in order to be successful. The asking lesson is the most practical of the three: the yes was waiting behind the question he did not ask.
