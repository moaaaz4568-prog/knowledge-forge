# G_ig_bmarkfit_ClMgPbxDdHR — coming across as needy

## Term

Coming across as needy is the first of the reel's four reasons you are not booking any sales calls: when you start chasing people in the DMs, they can feel that you need them more than they need you. The phrase appears once, beside chasing people in the DMs, actionable content, low quality, low engagement content, and focused on you and not them. The other reasons are all about value — not delivering enough actionable content, low quality and low engagement content, and creating to serve yourself instead of the audience. The fix the reel offers is the closing question: ask yourself how you can start delivering more value, and you will start booking more sales calls.

> "First things first is you're coming across as needy." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_ClMgPbxDdHR.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that following up is needy: the reel faults chasing people in the DMs so they feel you need them more, and it never says any follow-up counts as chasing.
- Read as proof that value alone books calls: delivering more value is the fix offered, and the source never says value without the other three reasons is enough.
