# G_ig_bmarkfit_CitantDDN5P — walk the fucking walk

## Term

Walk the fucking walk is the reel's command to fitness coaches: follow your own advice before coaching anybody, or you blend in with everybody else. The phrase appears once, beside keep it real, lose 20 pounds, following your own advice, and stand out as a leader. The coaches it calls out talk about how to lose 20 pounds while looking like they have not touched the gym in a year. The test is visible consistency — becoming an authority online by doing what you tell others to do, so you stand out as a leader in the marketplace.
