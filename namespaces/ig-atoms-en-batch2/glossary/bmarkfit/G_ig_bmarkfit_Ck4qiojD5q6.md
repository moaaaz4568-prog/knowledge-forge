# G_ig_bmarkfit_Ck4qiojD5q6 — obsession with delivery

## Term

Obsession with delivery is what the speaker calls his reason for comping every mastermind student's next month when Caleb said he was dropping out because he could not afford it after COVID hit. The phrase appears once, beside comp all of their next months, Caleb, I don't give a fuck about the money, and work with you for free. He cared so much about getting clients' results that he would go above and beyond and do absolutely anything to make sure they succeeded — and when a client loses a job and cannot pay the next monthly installment, his answer is that he has got it. The commitment is to work with the client for free for the next 30 days, and if they get back on their feet, start again.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that free months always bring clients back: the comping is for mastermind students hit by COVID who cannot afford it, and the source says if they get back on their feet they can start again — if not, that is cool too.
- Read as proof that money never matters: the speaker says he does not give a fuck about the money in this case, and the source never says the business runs without it.
