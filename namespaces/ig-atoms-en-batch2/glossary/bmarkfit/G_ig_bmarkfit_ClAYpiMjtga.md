# G_ig_bmarkfit_ClAYpiMjtga — the definition of insanity

## Term

The definition of insanity is the Einstein line the reel opens with: doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results. The phrase appears once, beside doing the same thing, expecting different results, analyze your actions, and what needs to change. The reel turns the quote into a challenge — analyze your actions and ask yourself what needs to change — rather than leaving it as a saying. The whole reel is 33 words, and the insanity line is its entire argument: repetition with the same expectation is the problem named.
