# G_ig_bmarkfit_ClC9XI-jqEs — the voice of unlimited power

## Term

The voice of unlimited power is how the speaker describes seeing himself in the mirror: a powerful individual, somebody that is gonna be bigger than Gary Vaynerchuk, somebody that has unlimited power. The phrase appears once, beside you're not worthy, you don't deserve it, you're not good enough, and the voice of weakness. The same moment holds the opposite voice creeping in the back of his mind — you are not worthy, you do not deserve it, you are not good enough, you are not talented enough, you are not gonna make it. The reel says both strength and weakness exist at the same time inside our minds, and the goal is to make the voice of unlimited power stronger than the voice of weakness.
