# G_ig_bmarkfit_ClCNHpDDfsr — keep your fucking promises

## Term

Keep your fucking promises is the reel's opening command, and the test is small: when you say you are gonna do something and you do not do it, you break trust with yourself. The phrase appears once, beside break trust with yourself, snooze when the alarm clock goes off, follow through, and not that serious about your own goals. The snooze is the example given — even the small things like snoozing when the alarm clock goes off count as breaking a promise. Every single time you do not follow through on something you said you would do, the reel says, you are telling yourself you are just not that serious about your own goals.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Keep your fucking promises. When you say you're gonna do something and you don't do it, you break trust with yourself." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_ClCNHpDDfsr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that snoozing once ruins self-trust: the snooze is the small example of breaking trust with yourself, and the source never says one snooze ends it.
- Read as proof that keeping promises alone builds seriousness: every broken promise is said to tell yourself you are not serious about your goals, and the source never says kept promises are the whole of it.
