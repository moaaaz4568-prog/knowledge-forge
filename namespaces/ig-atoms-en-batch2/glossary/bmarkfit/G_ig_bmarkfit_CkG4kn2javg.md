# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkG4kn2javg — trust the process

## Term

Trust the process is the patience the reel demands after the image is set and the work begins: put the image of the thing you want in your head, affirm it as if you are already in that reality, work your ass off with the belief that it is on the way, and be patient enough to trust the process even when the results are not there. The phrase appears once, beside affirm it, work your ass off, be patient, and you will receive when you are ready. The receiving comes at the end of the sequence — you will receive when you are ready — and the trust is what carries the gap between the work and the results.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "And you need to be patient enough to trust the process even when the results aren't there." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CkG4kn2javg.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that belief alone delivers: the sequence pairs affirming the image with working your ass off and being patient, and the source never says belief without work receives anything.
- Read as a claim that readiness can be scheduled: the receiving comes when you are ready, and the source gives no date or test for when that is.
