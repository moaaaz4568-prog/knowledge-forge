# G_ig_bmarkfit_Ck6fStrjTNX — get comfortable with discomfort

## Term

Get comfortable with discomfort is number five of the six things the speaker did to go from being a broke bartender to a successful business owner. The phrase appears once, beside exercise, own who you are, meditate, and define who you want to become. The other five frame it — exercise, owning who you are, meditating, taking care of your appearance, and defining who you want to become. Discomfort here is not a mood to endure but a condition to get used to, listed alongside the body, the mind, and the identity as one of the six moves.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complete formula for success: the six things are what he did to go from broke bartender to successful business owner, and the source never says they are sufficient on their own.
- Read as a claim that discomfort alone builds a business: getting comfortable with discomfort is only number five of six, alongside exercise, meditation, appearance, identity, and definition.
