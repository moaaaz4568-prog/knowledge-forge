# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkETb7tjWgF — work for them for free

## Term

Work for them for free is the plan the reel offers a high school student who wants to make money: do not focus on making money, try to find experience instead, by working for a local entrepreneur or influencer doing super well in the area. The phrase appears once, beside high school, local entrepreneur, one or two years, and way more money. The reasoning is arithmetic — the money a high school level of knowledge can earn would not be very much — while one or two years of free work would eventually lead to way more money than the student could ever make as a high school student. Experience is presented as the investment that pays later.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But if I could find a local entrepreneur or influencer or somebody that was doing super well in my area and I could work for them for free for the next one or two years, I know that eventually that experience would lead me to way more money than I'd ever be able to make as a high school student." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CkETb7tjWgF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to work for free indefinitely: the free work here is for the next one or two years to gain experience, and the source frames it as the path to way more money, not as permanent unpaid work.
- Read as a claim that high schoolers cannot earn: the reel says the money from high-school-level knowledge would not be very much, and it never says a student cannot earn anything.
