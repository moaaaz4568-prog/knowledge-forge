# G_ig_bmarkfit_CiL7oCWDufJ — jump out of the airplane

## Term

Jump out of the airplane names the reel's way of starting an online coaching business: sign a client first and learn how to deliver on the way down, instead of waiting until the program, meal plans, training plan, website, funnels, and ads are all in order. The phrase appears twice, once as "Jump out of the airplane" and once as "Jumping out of the airplane", beside learn how to fly on the way down, go sign a client, and Take the 250. The example is a new client paying 250: once the money is in, the coach becomes responsible for delivering the product and designs the best meal plan the client has ever seen. None of the setup matters, the reel argues, if you do not have clients.

## Synonyms

- jumping out of the airplane (the same phrase in its continuous form)

## Live Example

> "Jump out of the airplane and learn how to fly on the way down." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CiL7oCWDufJ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a licence to take money without a plan: the reel pairs signing the client with immediate responsibility for delivering the product, not with keeping the money and improvising.
- Read as a claim that preparation is worthless: the setup the reel dismisses is the waiting-before-starting kind, and the source still expects the meal plan and training plan to be designed once the client is signed.
