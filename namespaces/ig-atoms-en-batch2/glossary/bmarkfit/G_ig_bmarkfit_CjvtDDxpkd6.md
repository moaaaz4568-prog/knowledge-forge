# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjvtDDxpkd6 — cutting myself off

## Term

Cutting myself off names how the speaker says he got sober and clean: by cutting himself off from toxic people, places, and environments, then working on his fitness goals and his business goals. The phrase appears once, beside toxic people, places, and environments, fitness goals, business goals, and made my dreams become a reality. Nine years later the result is named in three pieces — living in this mansion, driving that car, and dreams become a reality. The charge that closes it is the same one the reel returns to: never give up on yourself, no matter what your situation is, and chase your dreams.

## When the Meaning Breaks
