# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkzgxY3DI1B — negative mindset around money

## Term

Negative mindset around money is the attitude the reel laughs at in the comments: people who do not have money take it off the benchmark that is success because it makes them feel better about their current situation. The phrase appears once, beside the benchmark that is success, take care of my family, take care of my community, and fix your own financial situation. Having more money is presented as the tool for taking care of family, friends, and community, not as the goal itself. The reel closes the argument with a command: make a lot of money and do good things with it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Seeing comments like this makes me laugh because so many people have such a negative mindset around money." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CkzgxY3DI1B.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that money itself changes the world: the reel says you are never gonna change the world if you cannot fix your own financial situation, and it never says money alone does the changing.
- Read as mockery of poor people: the laugh is aimed at the comments and the mindset around money, and the source never says the commenters themselves are worthless.
