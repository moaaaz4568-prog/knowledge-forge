# G_ig_bmarkfit_Cjt5TQLjAcU — going all in on you

## Term

Going all in on you is the invitation the reel closes with, after the Bali trip that started it: three weeks in Bali with $500, seven years ago, was the trip where he started going all in on entrepreneurship and on his coaching business. The phrase appears once, beside three weeks, $500, $5,000, and 1,400 people. The trip produced $5,000 in only three weeks, and the business now impacts 1,400 people on a day-to-day basis, with a podcast over a million downloads. The invitation is to start working on your goals, your dreams, and your passions — and the promise attached is that you will shock yourself with what you can accomplish.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that three weeks in Bali builds a business: the trip seven years ago started the all-in turn, and the $5,000 came from the work that followed, not from the location.
- Read as a claim that going all in shocks everyone the same way: the shock promised here is what you can accomplish, and the source measures it only through his own numbers.
