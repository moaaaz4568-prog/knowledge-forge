# G_ig_bmarkfit_CiTp7DEjx8z — sell before you're ready

## Term

Sell before you're ready is step one of the reel's five-step path to a million by 30: start selling before you feel qualified, because there is always a reason you cannot get started. The phrase appears twice, beside not certified, not qualified, you don't have enough followers, and trusted resource. The fear behind the delay is overcomplication, the reel argues — everybody has massive fears of getting started because they overcomplicate things. The speaker's own case is the proof offered: at 23 he wrote down that he would be a millionaire by 30, and he did it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to sell something you cannot deliver: the reel pairs selling early with the later steps — working for free, building community, fulfilling the promise — not with taking money and disappearing.
- Read as a claim that readiness never matters: the fear list here is about certification, followers, and qualification holding back the start, and the source never says skill itself is unnecessary.
