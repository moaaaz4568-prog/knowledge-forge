# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjEly3SJ9Dh — greatest product of all time

## Term

Greatest product of all time is the belief the reel demands about your own product: you must believe it, and you must be willing to scream from the rooftops about its value. The phrase appears once, beside scream from the rooftops, value of your product, resources are scarce, and change their life. The language itself is not enough, the reel adds — the scarcity of resources has to come from the program actually being that good and actually going to change their life. Belief and real quality are presented as a pair, not as alternatives.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You must believe that your product is the greatest product of all time." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CjEly3SJ9Dh.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that the product is objectively the greatest: the reel demands belief in the product as a selling stance, not a measurement against all products.
- Read as a scarcity trick: the scarce resources here are explained by the program actually being that good and changing lives, not by manufactured shortage.
