# G_ig_bmarkfit_Ck2FjGtD7FJ — one month for free

## Term

One month for free is the offer the reel teaches coaches with zero clients: offer one month for free in the online coaching program, because with zero clients you should be thinking about creating opportunity, not about money. The phrase appears once, beside zero clients, first five, post every single day, and collect social proof. The plan is to post every single day about the free month until five people fill it, then provide the best experience possible to earn their business. The three payoffs named are just enough results to prove it works, social proof to post and attract more clients, and referrals they are more likely to give because you helped them for free.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that free work always converts: the free month is meant to earn proof, social proof, and referrals, and the source never says all five free clients will pay.
- Read as advice to work for free forever: the free month here is for coaches with zero clients to create opportunity, and the reel frames it as the start of selling, not as the business model.
