# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkPVXBVDDYL — planting seeds

## Term

Planting seeds is the reel's image for the daily habit that keeps a business from growing: you are planting seeds and walking into each day with a negative mind. The phrase appears once, beside bullshit stories, negative mind, your business grows faster, and get out of your head. The contrast is the opening claim — when you love what you do, when you get out of your head and stop telling yourself bullshit stories, your business grows faster. The struggling majority, the reel says, do the opposite: they plant seeds of negativity and wonder why nothing grows.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But what most of you guys do, and the reason that you're struggling, is you're planting seeds and you're walking into each day with a negative mind." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CkPVXBVDDYL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that loving the work guarantees growth: the reel says a business grows faster when you love what you do, and it never measures how fast or for whom.
- Read as proof that a negative mind is the only cause of struggle: the negative mind is the reason the reel gives for struggling, and the source never rules out other causes.
