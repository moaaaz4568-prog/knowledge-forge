# G_ig_bmarkfit_Cktm6emDoHR — last rep

## Term

Last rep is the rep the reel says counts: it is the rep that gets your body shaking, the rep that gets your mind questioning, and the rep that makes you want to give up. The phrase appears twice, beside gets your body shaking, makes you want to give up, Your content isn't hitting, and one more extra rep. The gym logic is the model — we understand that the last rep decides whether or not you make progress — but in business most people are not putting in that last rep: the content is not hitting, the sales are not enough, the growth is not there. When it gets hard, when it actually counts, when you need to push for one more rep, most people give up, and the very few who put in one more extra rep are the ones who get the result.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that one more rep guarantees progress: the last rep decides whether or not you make progress, and the source never says the progress always comes.
- Read as a claim that business works like the gym: the gym logic is the model for the business rep, and the source never shows the business rep paying off the same way.
