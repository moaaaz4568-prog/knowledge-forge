# G_ig_bmarkfit_Cjn-D-tjriC — growing pains

## Term

Growing pains names what a successful online entrepreneur is going to experience: the same process as a caterpillar turning into a butterfly, which the speaker does not imagine is a painless experience. The phrase appears twice, beside caterpillar turning into a butterfly, shedding the old version of yourself, losing friends, and cutting people off. The process is shedding the old version of yourself to emerge as the new version, and the pains might mean losing friends, losing family members, and cutting people off you have known forever. The truth the reel opens with is that a lot of people should not be entrepreneurs — the pains are the filter.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you want to be a successful online entrepreneur, you're going to experience growing pains." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_Cjn-D-tjriC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that all entrepreneurs must lose friends: the growing pains might mean losing friends and family and cutting people off, and the source presents that as a possible cost, not a requirement.
- Read as a biological claim about caterpillars: the caterpillar turning into a butterfly is the reel's image for shedding the old version of yourself, not a lesson in entomology.
