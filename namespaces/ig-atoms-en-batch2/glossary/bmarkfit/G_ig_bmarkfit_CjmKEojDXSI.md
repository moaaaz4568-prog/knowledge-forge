# G_ig_bmarkfit_CjmKEojDXSI — make a bigger impact

## Term

Make a bigger impact is the reel's long-lasting way to generate more income: stop overfocusing on the income and make a bigger impact on the client instead. The phrase appears once, beside $54,000 in a month, 54 grand, overfocus on the income, and great results. The story behind it is a surprise — in July 2018 the business hit $54,000 in a month without noticing, because the only focus was delivering the client experience and making sure clients got great results. A year later the accounting showed 54 grand in a month, and the lesson drawn is that impact comes first and income follows it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that income follows automatically from impact: the reel says the long-lasting way to generate more income is a bigger impact, and it never promises the income arrives on a schedule.
- Read as proof that he ignored money: the $54,000 month was discovered a year later in accounting, and the focus was the client experience — not that money was never counted.
