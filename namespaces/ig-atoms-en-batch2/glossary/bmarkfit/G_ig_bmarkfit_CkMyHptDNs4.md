# G_ig_bmarkfit_CkMyHptDNs4 — phone script

## Term

Phone script is one of the only three things the reel says you need to reach $10,000 a month: content, a good phone script, and a good messenger script. The phrase appears once, beside You don't need a website, paid ads, messenger script, and $10,000 a month. The reel opens by striking the usual tools off the list — no website, no funnel, no paid ads — and leaves only the content and the two scripts. Those things alone, it claims, will get you to $10,000 a month.
