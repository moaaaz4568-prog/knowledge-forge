# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSeo1oEkVjN — Website Embeds

## Term

Website Embeds here is the Sharing and resharing switch the speaker tells artists to turn on: it appears once, beside Sharing and resharing, green checkmark, and Media Quality. It is the first of six settings to increase followers faster for 2026, followed by Account Status checkmarks, Upload at highest quality, More Options before posting, and Suggest your profile to art lovers. The switch is a distribution permission, not a content change. Its purpose in the clip is to let the work be embedded elsewhere.
