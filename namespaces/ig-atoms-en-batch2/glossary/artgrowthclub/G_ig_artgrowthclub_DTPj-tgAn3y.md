# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DTPj-tgAn3y — hook

## Term

A hook here is the opening of a video — the first five seconds, which the speaker calls the most important: it appears once, beside first five seconds, most important, and captions. It is the first of five simple ways the CEO of Instagram shared to increase reach faster for 2026, followed by captions always on, translation, music always, and trials. The hook is an opening, not a topic. Its job in the clip is to hold viewers from the first seconds.
