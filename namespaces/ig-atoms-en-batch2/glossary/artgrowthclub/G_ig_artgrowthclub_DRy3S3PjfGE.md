# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DRy3S3PjfGE — Category

## Term

Category here is the Instagram Edit Profile setting the speaker tells artists to set to Artist: it appears once, beside Edit Profile, Artist, and Account Status. The first of three settings, it is paired with turning off Display on Profile so the profile targets the art community, and the other two settings check green checkmarks in Account Status and switch the account type to Professional under Creator tools and controls. The setting is a classification switch, not a description of the art. Its purpose in the clip is to make the profile legible to Instagram as an artist profile.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "First, go to Edit Profile, click on Category and select Artist." — sources/S_ig_artgrowthclub_DRy3S3PjfGE.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee of reach: the clip claims the settings help art posts attract art lovers and rank higher, but shows no before-and-after evidence, so the entry records a recommended switch, not a measured result.
- Read as content advice: Category changes how Instagram classifies the profile, not what the artist posts, so it cannot substitute for the art itself.
