# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSuFqwvisdC — average watch time

## Term

Average watch time here is the metric the speaker says to rely on instead of views: it appears once, beside views, don't matter, and 15 to 30-second reels. It is the second of five key changes to the 2026 algorithm — hashtags dead in favor of keywords, views set aside for watch time, short reels replaced by 15 to 30-second reels, B-rolls dropped for voiceover, saves dead in favor of the share button. The metric is a measure of how long people stay, not how many arrive. The entry records a replacement metric, not a measurement of this account.
