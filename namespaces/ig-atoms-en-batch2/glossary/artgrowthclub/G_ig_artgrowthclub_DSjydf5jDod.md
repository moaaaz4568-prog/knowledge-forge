# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSjydf5jDod — trial Reel

## Term

A trial Reel here is the duplicate posted with the trial option so only non-followers see it: it appears once, beside duplicate, drafts, and non-followers. The speaker tells artists not to post without turning on three settings, starting with the duplicate-and-trial move — post the first video as the main Reel and the second with the trial option on — so the main Reel earns views while the trial Reel reaches non-followers and gains extra followers. The trial is a distribution switch, not a different video. Its job in the clip is to force Instagram to show the account to non-followers.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "This will make the main Reel get you views, but the trial Reel will only be shown to non-followers, which will help you get extra followers." — sources/S_ig_artgrowthclub_DSjydf5jDod.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that trials always work: the clip presents the trial move as one of three settings without any follower numbers, so the entry records a recommended first step, not a proven gain.
- Read as one of three complete settings: the clip cuts off after the first setting ('Number two, go to...'), so the other two settings cannot be recovered from this source.
