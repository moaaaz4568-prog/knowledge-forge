# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSHduNRCiQ4 — alt text

## Term

Alt text here is the Instagram advanced-settings field the speaker tells artists to fill with keywords: it appears 2 times, beside advanced settings, keyword-rich, and captions. The first of three hacks, it is presented as the way to describe a post so it attracts art lovers and potential customers; the second hack applies the same keyword-rich idea to captions. The field is a discoverability tool, not a description for its own sake. Its job in the clip is to make the post findable by the right audience.
