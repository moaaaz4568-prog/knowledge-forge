# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DRbsMg7jOYr — profile audit

## Term

A profile audit here is the personalized report ChatGPT returns for an artist's Instagram profile: it appears once, beside ChatGPT, social proof, and call to action. The speaker's prompt asks a social media expert persona to analyze the profile picture and suggest improvements, formatted as a bio in three lines — who the artist helps and the outcome, social proof for credibility, and a call to action to view or purchase art. The audit is a deliverable the speaker promises the prompt will produce, offered in exchange for reading the caption for the full prompt. It is a bio rewrite plan, not a follower count.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a proven ChatGPT result: the source shows the prompt, not any audit ChatGPT actually returned, so the entry records an instruction, not an outcome.
- Read as the speaker's own audit: the three-line bio format comes from the pasted prompt text, not from the speaker demonstrating an audit on a real profile.
