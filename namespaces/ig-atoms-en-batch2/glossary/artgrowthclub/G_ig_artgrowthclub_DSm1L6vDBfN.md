# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSm1L6vDBfN — posting sizes

## Term

Posting sizes here are the old-versus-new formats the speaker maps to goals for 2026: it appears once, beside Reels, Carousels, and Stories. The speaker assigns each format a job — Reels for followers and for educational content and storytelling, Carousels for likes and for art posts and sales content, Stories for art sales, testimonials, and results — then gives a weekly frequency for each: Reels four times, Carousels twice, Stories five times. The sizes are a posting plan, not a design rule. The speaker closes by saying to choose a frequency that can be kept without hurting quality.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as measured performance data: the format-to-goal mapping is the speaker's prescription with no analytics shown, so the entry records a plan, not results.
- Read as a rule about image dimensions: 'sizes' here means post formats and their weekly frequency, not pixel dimensions, so the entry records a schedule, not a design spec.
