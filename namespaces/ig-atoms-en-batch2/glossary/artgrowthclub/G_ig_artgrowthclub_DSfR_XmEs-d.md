# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSfR_XmEs-d — Limit Interactions

## Term

Limit Interactions here is the Settings and Privacy search term the speaker tells artists to find so more people can follow them: it appears once, beside Follow and Invite friends, and bots. The speaker says Instagram is not letting some people follow because it thinks they are bots, and gives two settings — turning off Follow and Invite friends, then finding Limit Interactions — to let people follow and increase views faster. The setting is a gate on who can interact, not a growth tactic. Its job in the clip is to remove a block the speaker believes Instagram imposed.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof Instagram blocks followers as bots: the bot claim is the speaker's explanation with no message from Instagram shown, so the entry records an inference, not a verified cause.
- Read as a growth method: the clip's point is removing a follow block, not creating content, so the entry records an unblocking step, not a way to earn followers.
