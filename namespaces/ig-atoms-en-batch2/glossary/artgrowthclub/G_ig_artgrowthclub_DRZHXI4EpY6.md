# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DRZHXI4EpY6 — transition hook

## Term

A transition hook here is the reusable clip the speaker says a website collects: it appears once, beside website, download, and gold mine. The speaker claims the site holds every single transition hook out there, free to download and drop into videos, and calls it a gold mine for instantly getting more views on an art account.
