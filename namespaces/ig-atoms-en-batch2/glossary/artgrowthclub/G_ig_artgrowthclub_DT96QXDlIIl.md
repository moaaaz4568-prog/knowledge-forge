# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DT96QXDlIIl — Limit Interactions

## Term

Limit Interactions here is the Settings and Privacy search term the speaker tells artists to find so followers are not blocked: it appears once, beside Who will be limited, don't follow you, and bots. The speaker says Instagram is not letting some people follow because it thinks they are bots, and gives two settings — Limit Interactions, turning off accounts that don't follow you, and Follow and Invite friends — to let people follow and comment and increase engagement faster. The setting is a gate on who can interact, not a growth tactic. Its job in the clip is to remove a block the speaker believes Instagram imposed.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Go to Settings and Privacy and search for Limit Interactions. Then all you have to do is click on Who will be limited and make sure to turn off accounts that don't follow you." — sources/S_ig_artgrowthclub_DT96QXDlIIl.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complete procedure: the clip walks through two settings in order, but the second is cut off at 'Follow. And invite friends.', so the exact taps cannot be fully recovered from this source.
- Read as a guarantee of engagement: the clip claims the settings let non-followers comment and increase engagement, but shows no engagement numbers, so the entry records a tactic, not a result.
