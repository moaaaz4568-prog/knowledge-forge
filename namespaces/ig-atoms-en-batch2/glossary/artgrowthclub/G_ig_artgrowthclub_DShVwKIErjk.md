# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DShVwKIErjk — Monetization Status

## Term

Monetization Status here is the Creator Tools and Controls switch the speaker says must show a green checkmark for artists to earn: it appears once, beside green checkmark, Creator Marketplace, and collab request. It is the first of two settings in a clip about a new Instagram feature putting money in artists' pockets in 2026; the second is Partner Discovery Settings in Creator Marketplace, where artists pick content topics and send brands a collab request. The status is an eligibility gate, not a payment. Its job in the clip is to unlock the marketplace step that follows.
