# G_ig_artgrowthclub_DSzPPYaCfs1 — Website Embeds

## Term

Website Embeds here is the Sharing and Resharing switch the speaker tells artists to turn on before anything else: it appears once, beside Sharing and Resharing, Media Quality, and Account Status. It is the first of five settings to increase followers faster for 2026, followed by Upload at Highest Quality with the other two options disabled, Account Status green checkmarks, and the speaker's desktop step. The switch is a distribution permission, not a content change. Its purpose in the clip is to let the work be embedded elsewhere.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "First, go to Settings and Activity, scroll down, click on Sharing and Resharing, and turn on Website Embeds." — sources/S_ig_artgrowthclub_DSzPPYaCfs1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a proven follower gain: the clip lists the switch among five settings with no before-and-after numbers, so the entry records a recommended toggle, not a measured result.
- Read as a complete procedure: the clip cuts off mid-sentence at the desktop step ('Next, go to your desktop.'), so the full list cannot be recovered from this source.
