# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGlbsDnxZeu — scraper

## Term

In this source, scraper is the new tool Jarvis just got for cold outreach to businesses: given a business type and area, it pulls the business from Google Maps, parses the data, and enriches it with Perplexity. The word appears 2 times, beside Jarvis, Google Maps, and Perplexity, with the plural scrapers once for scrapers like this one. His concrete case is gyms in Chicago: the scraper gets the name, address, and phone number from Maps, the data parser turns the ugly metadata into something useful for Google Sheets, and Perplexity adds a summary and the email. The loop runs once per company, skips duplicates, and updates Sheets automatically, so outreach starts from what the company does rather than a generic pitch.

## Synonyms

- scrapers (plural form, in "scrapers like this")

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a LinkedIn scraper: this scraper targets businesses through Google Maps, while the LinkedIn scraper in the speaker's other videos targets people by role and city.
- Read as Jarvis sending the outreach: the scraper only gathers and enriches the business data; the customized email is a separate step the speaker writes from that information.
