# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJfg01etPnw — Airtable

## Term

In this source, Airtable is where the speaker controls his ad-making AI agent: inside Airtable you decide whether to edit or combine photos or generate something from scratch, designate the action, the images, and the prompt, and the agent intelligently knows whether to edit one image into another. The name appears twice, beside AI agent, ads, and image generator, and the demo takes a current product photo and an example ad and combines them into one edited image. His concrete case is that two-image edit, where the agent produces different iterations of the ad to look through.

- Read as the image generator: Airtable is the control surface where choices are made — the image work happens through the ChatGPT image generator workflow — so a generated image with no Airtable step would not be this agent's output.
