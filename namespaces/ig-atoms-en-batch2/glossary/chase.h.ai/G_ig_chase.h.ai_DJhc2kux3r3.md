# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJhc2kux3r3 — cold outreach

## Term

In this source, cold outreach is the customized output of the speaker's LinkedIn-scraping AI workflow: it starts with a form submission — a query like tech founders in Chicago, 50 of them — finds their LinkedIn URLs through a Google custom search engine, scrapes the profiles with Apify, does company research, and combines personal information, company information, and his unique offer into one message AI generates. The phrase appears once, beside Apify, LinkedIn, and Google sheet, because the video is a walkthrough of the whole chain rather than a repeat of the name. His concrete case is the finished message, stored in Google Sheets and sent via Gmail, at up to 1,000 profiles a day.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you're looking for an AI workflow that scrapes LinkedIn and produces customized cold outreach, then I got one for you." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DJhc2kux3r3.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as bulk spam: every message is customized from the person's own profile and company, so the same generic pitch sent to all 1,000 would not be this workflow's output.
- Read as a finished list: the workflow ends with a generated message per person, so scraped profiles with no message step would not be the cold outreach he shows.
