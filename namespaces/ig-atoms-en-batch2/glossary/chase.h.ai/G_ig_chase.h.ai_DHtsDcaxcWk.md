# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DHtsDcaxcWk — MCP

## Term

In this source, MCP is the Model Context Protocol from Anthropic, the makers of Claude, and the speaker explains it as a standard for connecting programs — Anthropic's USB-C analogy. The term appears 10 times, beside list tools, execute tools, and modules, and the visual proof is the speaker's email agent: a huge mass of modules, one for every Gmail action, collapses into two MCP modules, list tools and execute tools. His concrete case is the collapse itself: instead of eight Gmail modules for deleting, writing, and drafting, the list tools module lists them all and the execute tools module runs the chosen one. OpenAI announced the same day that it will adopt MCP, and the speaker's summary is that lots of tools become little tools under one shared standard.
