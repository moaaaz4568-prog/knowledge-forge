# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJ5AnYyRXn9 — AI Ultra

## Term

In this source, AI Ultra is Google's $250-a-month package the speaker reviews: the big sell is the Gemini 2.5 Pro DeepThink model, plus higher limits for NotebookLM, YouTube Premium, and Wisk, Google's version of Sora. The phrase appears twice, beside DeepThink, Gemini 2.5, and NotebookLM, and his verdict is that the package is interesting — DeepThink beats o3 and o4 in coding, multimodal, and mathematics — but Google is probably still losing money on it, like OpenAI does with its own package. His concrete case is the benchmark comparison, where DeepThink smashes the others in mathematics.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as just a model: AI Ultra is the whole $250 package with limits and subscriptions, so the DeepThink model alone would not be the package.
- Read as a money-maker: he says Google is probably still losing money on it, so calling it a profitable bundle would flip his read.
