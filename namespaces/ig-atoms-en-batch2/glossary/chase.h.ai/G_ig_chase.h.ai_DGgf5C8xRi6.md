# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGgf5C8xRi6 — prompt

## Term

In this source, prompt is the single input that builds a website: the speaker gives Bolt, Lovable, and v0 the same prompt — build me a beautiful website for my travel blog — and compares what each makes. The word appears 5 times, beside website, code, and detail, and the speaker's lesson is that a short prompt gets standard results while more detail gets a much more unique website. His concrete case is the travel blog test: Lovable looks cleanest with a pretty picture and articles, Bolt is similar with a tagline and newsletter, and v0 is least fleshed out with no pictures. All three have robust free trials, and the speaker recommends them for anyone with no coding background who wants to build on their own.
