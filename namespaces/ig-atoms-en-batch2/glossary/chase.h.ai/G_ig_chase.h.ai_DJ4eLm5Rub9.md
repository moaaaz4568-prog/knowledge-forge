# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJ4eLm5Rub9 — AI agent

## Term

In this source, an AI agent is the speaker's own system that does his content, controls his entire Gmail suite and calendar, answers leads, and scrapes LinkedIn and Google Maps to generate leads — all with virtually no code. The phrase appears 7 times, beside brains, ElevenLabs, and leads, and the agent has one brains of the operation he reaches by voice through ElevenLabs or by text. His concrete case is the lead reply, where the agent acts like a member of his team and answers people who reach out.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single tool: the agent is a whole setup with many tools connected to one brains, so one Gmail automation on its own would not be this agent.
- Read as a coder's build: he built it with virtually no code, so a build that needs programming would not be the thing he is showing.
