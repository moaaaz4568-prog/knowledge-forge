# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJXUlBgxU6d — RAG agent

## Term

In this source, a RAG agent is the speaker's number one selling AI agent — and the surprise of the video: not his personal Friday stack with its content, executive assistant, and lead generation sub-agents, but a very simple RAG agent hooked up with Pinecone and Google Drive. The phrase appears once, beside RAG, Friday, and vector database, because the video is a sales observation, not a build guide — buyers want agents that act as a member of the team, and the RAG base lets him add tools like an email classifier or content creation on top. His concrete case is that simple base, which he calls the baseline everything else comes from.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as his personal stack: Friday is the sophisticated personal system that does not sell — the seller is the simple RAG agent — so confusing the two would flip the video's point.
- Read as a complex build: the selling version is deliberately simple, a database plus an agent, so a complicated stack would not be the thing clients actually buy.
