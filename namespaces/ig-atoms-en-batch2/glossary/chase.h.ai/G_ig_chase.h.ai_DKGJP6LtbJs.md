# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DKGJP6LtbJs — carousels

## Term

In this source, carousels are what the speaker's AI workflow automatically posts to TikTok and LinkedIn from a simple form: topic, number of slides, visual style, and a general storyline, and the workflow generates the images, the description, and the captions, then posts through Upload Posts. The word appears twice, beside Upload Posts, TikTok, and LinkedIn, and the trick is self-referencing images: image two is edited from image one, image three from image two, so the carousel tells a story and keeps its characters. His concrete case is the main character robot, who looks pretty similar throughout — a little more blue at the end — because each slide is built from the last.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as random images: each slide is edited from the previous one, so a set of unrelated images would not be the carousel he builds.
- Read as manual posting: the workflow sends the finished carousel to TikTok or Instagram based on slide count, so a carousel posted by hand would not be this automation's output.
