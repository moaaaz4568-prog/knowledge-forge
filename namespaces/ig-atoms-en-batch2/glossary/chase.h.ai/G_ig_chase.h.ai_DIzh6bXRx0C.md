# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIzh6bXRx0C — RAG agents

## Term

In this source, RAG agents are the number one AI agent clients ask the speaker to build: an AI system hooked to internal documentation — documents from Google Drive into a vector database in the simple version, with more complicated hookups available. The phrase appears twice, beside RAG, email agents, and leads, and the video ranks three sellers: RAG agents first, then lead generation, then email agents. His concrete case is the simple build, where Drive documents land in a vector database and the system answers from them.

## Synonyms

- RAG agent (the singular form the speaker uses)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any AI agent: a RAG agent is specifically hooked to internal documentation, so an agent with no documents attached is one of his other sellers, not this one.
- Read as the simplest seller: RAG agents top the list because clients ask for them most, not because they are easiest — the email agents are the simplest ones.
