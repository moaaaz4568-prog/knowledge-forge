# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DK7t_P8REkr — social media

## Term

In this source, social media is the first of two ways the speaker says a new AI agency differentiates itself: go out, talk about your solutions, and let the content become a live portfolio people can judge, because in a saturating market a faceless email from a nameless person will not sell. The phrase appears 5 times, beside AI agency, live portfolio, and body of work, and the cycle he describes is content from real work bringing real clients, which brings more real work to show. His concrete case is the friends-and-people-you-know start, where the first jobs pay at cost and build the body of work.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as guru posturing: the content has to be backed by real work, so posting without having done the work would not be the differentiation he teaches.
- Read as a replacement for skill: he says you do not need to be a complete expert first — you learn as you help others — so waiting until you have done ten billion tutorials would miss his point.
