# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGrC4Yjxw6G — automation

## Term

In this source, automation is the simple email watcher the speaker builds in a few minutes to save hours every week: it monitors the inbox for specific emails, sends each one to ChatGPT, and files the reply back as a draft. The word appears 3 times, beside email, draft, and ChatGPT, with the plural automations once for automations like this one. His concrete case is the guardrail: the draft goes back into the inbox as a draft so nothing sends without the speaker reviewing it. He says the same simple pattern scales really well, and the draft always waits in the speaker's own voice.
