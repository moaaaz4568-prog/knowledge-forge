# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJwlEr0x4nH — AI email assistant

## Term

In this source, an AI email assistant is what the speaker's workflow becomes: it automatically classifies, tags, and drafts up responses based on your rules and in your voice to whatever emails you want. The phrase appears once, beside text classifier, potential leads, and LLM chain, because the video is the build itself — pin the email data, add a text classifier with a potential leads category, add an LLM chain for the reply, then draft the email. His concrete case is the potential leads category, where the classifier sends matching mail down the leads branch and the draft comes back as a reply with RE plus the original subject.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So this workflow becomes your AI email assistant and automatically classifies, tags, and drafts up responses based on your rules and in your voice to whatever emails you want, all automatically." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DJwlEr0x4nH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as auto-send: the assistant drafts replies for review, so mail that leaves the inbox with no draft step would not be this workflow's doing.
- Read as a fixed sorter: the categories and rules are yours to define, so an assistant that classifies by someone else's rules would not be the one he builds.
