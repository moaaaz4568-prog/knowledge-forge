# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGRRUiURPPj — DeepSeek

## Term

In this source, DeepSeek is the open-source model Perplexity retuned into R1 1776, a version with none of the Chinese restrictions on the original. The name appears 4 times, beside open source, Perplexity, and Hugging Face, and the speaker stresses that the retune kept the same level of performance while giving more factual answers on sensitive topics. His concrete case is the release itself: the weights are public, Perplexity published its stats and a blog about the training, and the model is available on Hugging Face and through the Sonar API. He frames it as another win for the open-source community, since the open weights let anyone change the guidelines and keep essentially the same model.
