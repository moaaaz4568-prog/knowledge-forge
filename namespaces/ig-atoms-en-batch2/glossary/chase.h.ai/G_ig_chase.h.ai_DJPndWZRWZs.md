# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJPndWZRWZs — sentiment analysis

## Term

In this source, sentiment analysis is one of the two jobs of the stock agent the speaker builds without code: the agent goes out on the web and scrapes data about how people feel about a stock, alongside technical analysis of its charts. The phrase appears 3 times, beside technical analysis, AI agent, and chart-image, and part one only sets up the pieces — a chat model as the brains, simple memory, a web scraping tool for sentiment, and a stock tool with chart-image for charts. His concrete case is the chart-image API on the free basic plan, giving up to 50 charts a day for the technical side while the sentiment tool is still to come.
