# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIrxTt7RoN8 — Gmail trigger

## Term

In this source, a Gmail trigger is the always-on start of the speaker's beginner AI workflow: it watches the inbox for new mail, and once a message arrives the workflow sends it through a basic LLM chain with a prompt that drafts a reply in the sender's voice — his demo voice is Shakespeare — then creates the draft in Gmail. The phrase appears once, beside automation, Gmail, and basic LLM chain, because the video is a step-by-step build and the trigger is only the first step. His concrete case is the test email he sent himself, which comes back in Shakespearean English and lands as a draft in his inbox.

> "Okay, so the way this automation is gonna work is we're gonna have a Gmail trigger that's always looking for new emails inside our inbox." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DIrxTt7RoN8.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole workflow: the trigger only watches for mail and starts the flow — the LLM chain and the draft step do the actual writing, so a trigger with no chain drafts nothing.
- Read as auto-send: the workflow creates a draft for review, so mail that goes out by itself would not be this automation's doing.
