# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIw8HdIR18P — RAG

## Term

In this source, RAG is the system that turns an AI agent from a glorified ChatGPT that sends emails into an actual assistant and teammate: a special database holding internal documents, so the agent works with the business's own stuff — Supabase in his build, Pinecone in his simpler version. The term appears 5 times, beside RAG tool, Supabase, and Pinecone, and the speaker calls it imperative: without it the agent has nothing of yours to work with. His concrete case is the RAG agent he walks through step by step, built on Supabase with its tools connected.
