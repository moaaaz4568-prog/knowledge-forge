# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGr3qMjg_mq — N8N

## Term

In this source, N8N is the platform the speaker says is posted everywhere because it lets non-developers make agents fast without code. The name appears 6 times, beside agent, tools, and Jarvis, and the demo builds an agent in about 60 to 90 seconds: add the AI agent step, connect ChatGPT as the brain, add memory in two clicks, then add tools. His concrete case is the finished agent: email through Gmail with the AI figuring out recipient, subject, and message, internet through Google search, and Notion access the same way. He closes with Jarvis, his personal assistant agent with a database on Telegram, as proof the same platform scales from a 90-second demo to a sophisticated assistant.
