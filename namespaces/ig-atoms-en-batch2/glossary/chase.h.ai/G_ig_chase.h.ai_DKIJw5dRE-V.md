# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DKIJw5dRE-V — carousel

## Term

In this source, a carousel is the finished post the speaker's n8n workflow creates and publishes from one form: a robot learning to create AI agents, five slides, Studio Ghibli style, with the storyline figured out on its own. The word appears 5 times, beside upload post, storyline, and TikTok, and the chain is the same self-referencing trick — image two from image one, image three from image two — so the characters stay the same from slide one to slide five. His concrete case is the robot carousel itself, posted on IG with its images, captions, and storyline all generated by the workflow.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single image: a carousel is five linked slides built from one another, so one standalone picture would not be this post.
- Read as a template fill: the workflow invents the storyline itself from the topic, so a carousel with a pre-written story pasted in would not be the automation he demos.
