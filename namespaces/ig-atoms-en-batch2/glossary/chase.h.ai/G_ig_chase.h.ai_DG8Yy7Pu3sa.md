# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DG8Yy7Pu3sa — RAG

## Term

In this source, RAG is the special database the speaker builds into a client agent so its replies use the client's own voice and knowledge instead of fluff. The term appears 5 times, beside database, meeting notes, and memos, and the speaker explains it plainly: a place to put internal documentation, meeting notes, and memos for the agent to check. His concrete case is a consultant client: form submissions come in, the agent checks the database, and it answers the way the client would for that particular problem. The speaker got the RAG template from Cole Medin on YouTube and added his own cleanup, and he calls RAG-plus-action the setup where an agent gives the most value.
