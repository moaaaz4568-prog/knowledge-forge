# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DK-JMY3R2is — Apify

## Term

In this source, Apify is the speaker's number one place to find web scrapers that actually work: a web scraper marketplace with a million different scrapers — TikTok comments, Instagram comments, LinkedIn, Apollo — with easy-to-use APIs to drop into automations. The name appears 7 times, beside Apollo, scraper, and lead generation, and the pitch is the data it returns: tell Apollo you want founders in New York City in tech and it comes back with a million people with emails, LinkedIn, organization, revenue, and headcount. His concrete case is the Apollo scraper for lead generation, which powers the LinkedIn automation he shows next to it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a scraper itself: Apify is the marketplace where scrapers live, so a single scraper built by hand would not be Apify.
- Read as a data vendor: the value is the scrapers and their APIs inside your own automations, so a bought list with no scraping step would not be what he recommends.
