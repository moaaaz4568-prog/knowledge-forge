# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIpf37QNZTi — faceless videos

## Term

In this source, faceless videos are the single-click product of the speaker's AI workflow: Flux makes the image for a cent and a half through Pi API, Kling turns it into video, Eleven Labs adds background music, and Creatomate stitches image, video, captions, and music into one uploadable video. The phrase appears once, beside Flux, Kling, and Creatomate, because the video is a costed walkthrough of the four parts, not a repeat of the name — Kling 1.5 costs 26 cents per five-second video, Kling 2.0 nearly $2, and Creatomate $50 a month for its API. His concrete case is the four-part chain itself, which he says looks complicated but is not out of reach for a non-technical builder.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as free: every part has a price — image, video, music, and the Creatomate stitch — so a faceless video made this way is cheap, not costless.
- Read as fully hands-off: the workflow still needs the four parts generated and combined, so a video with no image, video, captions, or music step would not be this workflow's output.
