# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIZMOSjRaog — custom GPT

## Term

In this source, a custom GPT is the speaker's own tool that turns a plain description of an AI agent or workflow into a file he uploads to N8N for free, giving him the base of the agent — trigger, RSS feeds, loop, aggregation, and the system message already done. The phrase appears twice, beside N8N, AI agent, and JSON, and the pitch is that a few prompts plus a couple of tweaks replace building the workflow by hand. His concrete case is the RSS workflow it generated, which pulls feeds, loops them, aggregates them into the LLM, and sends the result to Gmail.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a finished agent: the GPT hands over a base that still needs account connections and tweaks, so the file it produces is a start, not a working agent.
- Read as a paid tool: the upload to N8N is free and the link sits in his bio, so charging for access or requiring his community would misstate how he offers it.
