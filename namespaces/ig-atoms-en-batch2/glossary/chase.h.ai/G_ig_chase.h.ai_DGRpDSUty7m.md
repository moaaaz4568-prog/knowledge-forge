# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGRpDSUty7m — author

## Term

In this source, author is the second column of the quote table the speaker builds with ChatGPT for bulk content: each quote gets its author, and Canva's bulk create maps the two onto a template. The word appears 3 times, beside quote, table, and bulk create, with the plural authors once for the column itself. His concrete case is ten quotes with authors, pasted as a table into Canva, which then creates ten pieces of content with one click. The speaker scales the same table to a hundred quotes, and the author field stays attached to its quote all the way from ChatGPT to the finished post.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the person who wrote the quote: the author here is a field in a table ChatGPT generates, and the speaker pairs it with a quote only so Canva's bulk create can place both on a template.
- Read as a guarantee of real attribution: the table is AI-generated text, so an author name attached to a quote in this workflow is a label for the design, not proof the person said it.
