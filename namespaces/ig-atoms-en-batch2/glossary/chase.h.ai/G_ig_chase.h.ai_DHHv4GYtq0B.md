# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DHHv4GYtq0B — template

## Term

In this source, template is the new RAG-based agent skeleton the speaker sells to clients: it takes PDFs and text documents, and even very large Excel and CSV files, because it uses some SQL functionality. The word appears 2 times, beside RAG, clients, and webhook, and the speaker's pitch is an internal ChatGPT — an agent that knows what the business is working on, its problems, and what it is trying to solve. His concrete case is the web front end: two prompts to Bolt — one for a visually stunning chat interface, one with the N8N webhook URL — and he can talk to the agent right through it, asking for action items from the last meeting. The whole thing is built without code, and the speaker says anyone can leverage AI this way no matter where they are starting from.
