# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DHyy5zby_ur — Gemini 2.5 Pro

## Term

In this source, Gemini 2.5 Pro is the model the speaker calls the best AI model on the market right now, whooping Claude, DeepSeek, and ChatGPT on multiple benchmarks while nobody talks about it. The name appears once in full, with the short form 2.5 Pro twice more, beside LiveBench, reasoning, and N8N, and the speaker's evidence is LiveBench: a global average of 82, well ahead of everyone, with only the o1 Pro model close on reasoning. His concrete case is the price gap: o1 Pro sits behind a $200 paywall, while 2.5 Pro can be tried for free by Googling it. He then builds a simple agent on it in N8N — Gemini as chat model, Gmail, SERP API, and Slack — that finds an AI article and posts it to Slack.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a paid model: 2.5 Pro is free to try, and the speaker contrasts it with o1 Pro behind a $200 paywall, not with a free tier of the same model.
- Read as the agent itself: Gemini 2.5 Pro is only the chat model inside the N8N agent; the agent is the N8N build with Gmail, search, and Slack around it.
