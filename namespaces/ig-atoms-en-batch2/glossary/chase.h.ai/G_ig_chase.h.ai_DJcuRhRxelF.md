# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJcuRhRxelF — AI automation

## Term

In this source, an AI automation is the linear half of the speaker's comparison: the data always flows the same way from left to right — what is pulled and pushed changes, but the path never does — while an AI agent is dynamic, intelligently choosing which tool fits the prompt, query, or instructions, with tools that talk to one another in a loop. The phrase appears 5 times, beside AI agent, dynamic, and linear, and the test he gives is the flow itself: dynamic or linear, not how complex it is or how much AI is involved. His concrete case is the two builds side by side, one agent and one automation, no less complicated than the other.

## Synonyms

- AI automations (the plural form the speaker uses)
