# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIClebux-R5 — meeting prep

## Term

In this source, meeting prep is the workflow the speaker calls one of his favorite tools alongside Friday: it reads his calendar every day, filters out people he already knows, and briefs him on the rest. The phrase appears 2 times, beside workflow, RAG, and Google Gemini, and the brief is assembled from the last few emails, a LinkedIn background search, and a write-up on the company. His concrete case is the morning document: Google Gemini turns all of it into a meeting prep document listing the meetings, the times, and most importantly the attendees, what they do, and what they have been talking about. The workflow is a behemoth, but the speaker says it is super useful in his line of work, and no coding is needed to build it.
