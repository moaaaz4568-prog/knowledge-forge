# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGpDrw0NBnB — platform

## Term

In this source, platform is the no-code builder a beginner must pick first — Make or N8N — before worrying about anything else in AI agents. The word appears once as platform, with the plural platforms twice for the million out there, beside N8N, Make, and code. His concrete case is the advice itself: pick a path, walk down it for a couple of months, learn how module one connects to module two, and start small with very basic automations. The speaker prefers N8N but says it truthfully does not matter which one you pick, and the second step is learning what an AI agent actually is — Anthropic's article defines it as following a dynamic path that calls different tools at different times.
