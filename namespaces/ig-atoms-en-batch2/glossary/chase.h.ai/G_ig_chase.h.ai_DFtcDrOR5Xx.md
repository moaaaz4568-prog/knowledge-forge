# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DFtcDrOR5Xx — Deep Research

## Term

In this source, Deep Research is OpenAI's new AI agent for researching, which the speaker says can produce PhD-level research papers by combining its o3 reasoning model with web search. The name appears 5 times, beside Hugging Face, Gaia test, and free, and the speaker's point is the price gap: OpenAI's version sits behind a $200-a-month paywall while Hugging Face rebuilt it open-source and free within 24 hours. His concrete case is the Gaia test, where OpenAI's Deep Research scores about 67%, Hugging Face about 54%, and plain 4o only 4%. He tells viewers to try the free version themselves, warning they may have to wait in queues.
