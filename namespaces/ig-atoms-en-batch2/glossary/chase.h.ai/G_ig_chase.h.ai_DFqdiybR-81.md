# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DFqdiybR-81 — o3

## Term

In this source, o3 is OpenAI's model line the speaker recommends first for coding: o3 high is the best option for coding and scores best across the models there, while staying cheap on API calls. The token appears 7 times, beside coding, reasoning, and 4o, and the speaker contrasts it with o1 for reasoning and math and with 4o for multimodal files. His concrete case is price: o1's output costs about $60 per million tokens while the o3 series costs about $4. He also notes o3 cannot take many files, so a specific file o1 or o3 cannot handle falls to 4o.
