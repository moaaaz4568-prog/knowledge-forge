# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DJCUSeLR-Ve — custom GPT

## Term

In this source, a custom GPT is the speaker's own builder trained on a bunch of his own AI agents: describe what you want to build, go back and forth, and it hands you a JSON file with all the code to upload to N8N. The phrase appears 3 times, beside AI agent, JSON file, and newsletter automation, and the demo build is a custom newsletter automation that pulls RSS feeds, sends them to an LLM, synthesizes the information, and emails it every day. His concrete case is that newsletter automation, which comes out as an 80% solution that still needs OpenAI and Gmail connected.
