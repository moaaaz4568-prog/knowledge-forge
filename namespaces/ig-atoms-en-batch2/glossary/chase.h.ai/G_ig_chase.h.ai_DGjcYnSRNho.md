# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGjcYnSRNho — leads

## Term

In this source, leads are the new businesses the speaker's Perplexity and Google Maps scraper tool finds for cold outreach: it grabs a business's information from Maps, sends it to Perplexity to enrich, and files it in Google Sheets. The word appears once as leads, with the singular lead once more for a single enriched business, beside Google Maps, Perplexity, and email. His concrete case is the handoff: the enriched lead goes to his AI personal assistant, which drafts personalized emails in his voice to each business. He built the whole flow in N8N with almost no code, and the point is reaching out with real information about the company rather than a generic pitch.

## Synonyms

- lead (singular form, in "to enrich the lead")

## Live Example

> "Let me show you my brand new Perplexity and Google Maps scraper tool that I use with my AI agent to generate me new leads." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DGjcYnSRNho.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a bought list: the leads are scraped fresh per search — Maps data enriched by Perplexity — not a purchased list, and each one carries its own background.
- Read as the agent sending the emails itself: the scraper only produces the enriched lead in Sheets; the personal assistant drafts the emails, and the speaker still reviews them in his voice.
