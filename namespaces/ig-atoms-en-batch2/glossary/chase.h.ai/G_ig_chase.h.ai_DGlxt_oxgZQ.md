# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGlxt_oxgZQ — Deep Research

## Term

In this source, Deep Research is ChatGPT's research mode, now released to all Plus users instead of sitting behind a $200 paywall. The name appears 3 times, beside Plus, pinnacle, and time, and the speaker calls it the pinnacle of AI-driven research capabilities, with results that are really, really good. His concrete case is the cost in time: a request is limited to 10 a month and takes five to 20 minutes, and the time it takes is directly related to its performance. He says to use it when you really need to drill down on a specific topic and are not pressed for time, to become a quote-unquote expert in that space.
