# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DIUTA-KxWYH — roadmap

## Term

In this source, a roadmap is the beginner's path the speaker would follow if he were starting over in the AI space: get familiar with ChatGPT projects and the system prompt, build a rhythm of drafting real emails in Gmail, then add tools on the front and back end to automate the process, and finally give agents multiple tools so they choose their own path per task. The word appears 3 times, beside ChatGPT projects, system prompt, and AI agents, and every step is framed as what he would do first, not as a rule for everyone. His concrete case is the first jump into a system prompt — custom instructions so the AI always knows how to respond to the task.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fixed curriculum: this is the path he would give himself, so following it in a different order or skipping the Gmail rhythm still counts as using it, not breaking it.
- Read as agent-first advice: agents come only after the ChatGPT-projects step and the automation step, so jumping straight to multi-tool agents skips the roadmap's own order.
