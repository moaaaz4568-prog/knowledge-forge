# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGTScZRRHFb — tool

## Term

In this source, tool is the speaker's unit for the five no-code AI app builders he ranks: Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, Make, and n8n. The word appears 4 times, beside beginner, prompt, and code, with the plural tools twice for the set as a whole. His concrete case is Lovable for a beginner with no real experience: open the website, put in a prompt, and it starts building a web app or website, even a backend with Supabase at a click. The list runs from friendliest to most powerful, ending with n8n, his favorite for real, live AI agents that call tools and use LLMs.
