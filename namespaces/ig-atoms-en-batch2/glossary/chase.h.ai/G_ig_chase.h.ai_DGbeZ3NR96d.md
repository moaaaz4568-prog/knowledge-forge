# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGbeZ3NR96d — N8N

## Term

In this source, N8N is the speaker's recommendation over Make for anyone getting into the AI space: more robust, more AI-agent-centric, with more power and flexibility as you learn it. The name appears 7 times, beside Make, agents, and modular, and the comparison is explicit — Make wins on AI automation and an intuitive, beautiful UI with a free tier, while N8N wins on creating AI agents without code. His concrete case is the LinkedIn scraper: a standalone automation in N8N that also sits integrated inside his greater AI personal assistant. The price of the extra power is a steeper learning curve, though he insists a non-technical person can still overcome it.
