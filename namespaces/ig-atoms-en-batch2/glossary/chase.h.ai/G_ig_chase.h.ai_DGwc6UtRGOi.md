# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGwc6UtRGOi — Meeting Prep Monster

## Term

In this source, Meeting Prep Monster is the name the speaker gives his newest agent tool, which runs every 24 hours and hands him a master brief sheet for the day's meetings. The name appears once, beside meetings, bio, and LinkedIn, and the sheet details the meetings, gives a quick bio on each person, summarizes the last few emails with them, and adds their LinkedIn URL and a company background check. His concrete case is the external meeting problem: with many outside people, he does not always remember what they last talked about or who they work for. The tool also filters out internal people so the brief never covers colleagues he already knows, and the speaker still has a few finishing touches to put on the final document.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a calendar: the Monster does not just list meetings; it briefs the speaker with bios, email summaries, LinkedIn, and company background for each one.
- Read as a brief for everyone: internal people are filtered out on purpose, so the sheet covers only external meetings.
