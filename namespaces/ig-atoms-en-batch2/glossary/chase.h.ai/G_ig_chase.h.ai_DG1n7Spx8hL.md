# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DG1n7Spx8hL — meetings

## Term

In this source, meetings are what the speaker's AI agent prepares for: it reads the Google Calendar each day, filters out internal meetings, and briefs the speaker on the external ones. The word appears 4 times, beside external, bio, and LinkedIn, with the singular meeting once in "meeting prep monster". His concrete case is a single external meeting: the agent gives a brief bio of each attendee, their LinkedIn, their company background, and a summary of recent emails between them. The whole flow runs with almost no code, mostly in N8N, and the agent hands back the meetings up top with the bios underneath.

## Synonyms

- meeting (singular form, in "meeting prep monster")

## Live Example

> "So what it actually does is it goes into your Google Calendar each day and it looks at all your meetings." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DG1n7Spx8hL.md
> "It filters out all the meetings that already have like internal people in your company because you don't care about them, and it goes and looks at the attendees that are external." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DG1n7Spx8hL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a calendar that lists meetings: the agent does not just list them, it filters out internal people, looks up each external attendee, and summarizes recent emails with them.
- Read as a tool for every meeting: the prep is built for external meetings with people outside the company, and internal meetings are filtered out because the speaker says you do not care about them.
