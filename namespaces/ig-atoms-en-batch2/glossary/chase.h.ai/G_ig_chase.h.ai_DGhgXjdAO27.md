# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGhgXjdAO27 — Perplexity

## Term

In this source, Perplexity is the enrichment half of the speaker's new business scraper: Google Maps pulls the business data, and Perplexity finds the email and a quick background on the company. The name appears 4 times, beside Google Maps, email, and background, and the speaker likes it because outreach then starts from real information about the company, not just a name and an email. His concrete case is gyms in South Loop Chicago: Maps returns the name, website, hours, and phone number, then Perplexity adds the email and background. The other half of the video is his updated LinkedIn scraper, which now cross-references profiles so he stops getting duplicates.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Perplexity finding the businesses: Google Maps finds the businesses and their data; Perplexity only enriches a business Maps already returned.
- Read as a people finder: this scraper is business-centric for leads that are companies, while the LinkedIn scraper covers people by role, industry, and city.
