## Term

In this source, PRD is the product requirements document — the set of instructions the speaker writes before any coding, spelling out what to build and its specific features. The abbreviation appears 2 times, beside step two, instructions, and AI coder, and the full form appears once right after the abbreviation. His concrete case is the handoff itself: the PRD is given to the AI coder of choice, which then builds the app step by step from it.
