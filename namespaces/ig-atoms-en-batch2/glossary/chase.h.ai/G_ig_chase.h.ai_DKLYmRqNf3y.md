# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DKLYmRqNf3y — RAG agents

## Term

In this source, RAG agents are the best-selling AI agents the speaker names first among the three types that make him the most money: simple ones, where an AI agent is hooked to a database — usually a vector database — holding the company's personal proprietary information, so the build acts like a member of the team. The phrase appears once, beside email agents, AI agency, and vector database, because the video ranks the sellers rather than building them — RAG agents first, then email agents that live in the inbox, then the third type. His concrete case is the simple RAG build, which sells precisely because it is not a giant monstrosity.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So the best-selling AI agents are RAG agents, and they either look like this or they look like this, but these ones sell the most, the simple ones." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DKLYmRqNf3y.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as complex systems: the sellers are the simple RAG builds, so a giant monstrosity would not be the thing that sells most.
- Read as a database alone: the agent has to act like a team member on the company's information, so a database nobody queries would not be a RAG agent.
