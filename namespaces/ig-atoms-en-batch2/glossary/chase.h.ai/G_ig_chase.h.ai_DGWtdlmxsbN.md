# G_ig_chase.h.ai_DGWtdlmxsbN — LinkedIn scraper

## Term

In this source, LinkedIn scraper is the new tool the speaker added to Jarvis, his no-code personal assistant agent, to pull profiles for cold outreach. The phrase appears 3 times, beside profiles, free, and Google Sheets, twice in the singular and once as the plural LinkedIn scrapers. His concrete case is a search for a hundred software engineers in Chicago in tech: the scraper pulls the profiles, filters them to URL, title, and name, and uploads them to Google Sheets automatically. He built it in N8N in about 15 minutes and can scrape up to a thousand profiles a day for free through Google's custom search API.

## Synonyms

- LinkedIn scrapers (plural form)

## Live Example

> "Let me show you the brand new LinkedIn scraper tool that I added to my AI agent that acts as my personal assistant, known as Jarvis." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DGWtdlmxsbN.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a paid LinkedIn tool: the scraper is free through Google's custom search API, and the speaker's point is not paying for LinkedIn scrapers that cost a ton of money.
- Read as scraping everything: the tool returns only the URL, title, and name after filtering, not the full profile data.
