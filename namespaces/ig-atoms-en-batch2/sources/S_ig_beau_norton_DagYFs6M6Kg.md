There's one thing about online marketing that I think a lot of people don't understand, and it's something called the familiarity bias. A lot of people think that every piece of content they create has to offer significant value, or it has to get a lot of views, or be designed to go viral. And they think that if a piece of content doesn't perform well on social media, that it was kind of a waste of time or that it was a flop. But what you have to see is that a lot of your results from marketing are going to come from people seeing your face on social media over and over and over again for many months. Even if those people don't even watch your content, even if they just see like the first two seconds of your video and then they scroll right by, that still has value for your brand because of the familiarity bias. When people see your face or hear your voice over and over again for many months, they eventually become familiar with you, and that familiarity leads to a higher level of trust with you, and that trust makes the likelihood of you making a sale much, much higher.
