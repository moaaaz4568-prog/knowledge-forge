This is how I decide what content to post for my business. I don't start with the content. I start with a strategy. So first, I need to define who I'm trying to reach, who they are, what they want, what they struggle with, what they are trying to achieve. Then I look at the offer I'm trying to sell and I try to map where they might be in the customer journey. So do they need to discover my business? Do I need to build trust? Or are they just ready to buy? That gives me all the context I need to know what to talk about and how to talk about it. That way I can attract the right audience and achieve whatever business goals I have.
