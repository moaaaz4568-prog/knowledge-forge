The hardest part of trying to change your life when you first begin your self improvement journey are those days where you want to do nothing but stay in bed. Three years ago when I first began self improvement, I would plan my goals, stick to it for a couple of days, see some progress, and then bam, back to my old habits by day four. Everybody tells you oh the key to success is consistency, but how do we actually achieve that? And the key is to master those days where you can't even be bothered to wake up. So this is the method that I use and it's called the minimum method. When we first set goals since we're so motivated, we often try to go all in. So when these bad days inevitably come, you feel so overwhelmed that you can't even be bothered to start. So the trick is to hit a bare minimum limit that you must hit no matter what. I'm currently studying for exams and ideally I want to study six hours a day, but my goal is to get two hours a day no matter what. This way I can stay so much more consistent instead of forcing myself to study six hours a day and eventually burning out.
