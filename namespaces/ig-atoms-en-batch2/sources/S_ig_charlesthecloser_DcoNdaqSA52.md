The most important decision you will ever make in your life, and I can say this with one hundred percent certainty, is not your career. It's where you choose to live. Six years ago, I was sat in my car, absolutely freezing outside a Starbucks in Lancashire. I just wasted an hour meeting with a complete idiot who just wanted to pick my brain. That is when I began travelling around the world looking for a new place to call home. So here are the three things that actually matter when you decide where you want to live. The first is people. Free thinkers, big thinkers, people already operating a level above where you are at. It's cliche, but you do become who you associate with. Higher standards and new ideas are infectious. The second thing is weather. It sounds soft until you've lived on both sides of it. The grey mornings in Lancashire versus January in Bali are not the same species of day. Your mood, your energy, your willingness to actually get up and work, all of it shifts with the sun. The third is what moment in time is that place at? Is it rising or is it dying? When I arrived in Bali three or four years ago now, it looked nothing like it did when I arrived. My friends here now are entrepreneurs doing anything from five hundred k to three million dollars a month. Get in a place while it's climbing, not after it's already peaked. None of this is available to you on a salary tied to an income in one country. Remote high ticket sales is what actually bought me the ticket to choose where I spend my time. I've helped over one hundred and sixty other salespeople make this same transition, who I've trained one on one and they're living in Thailand, Colombia or wherever they choose because they realise the same thing.
