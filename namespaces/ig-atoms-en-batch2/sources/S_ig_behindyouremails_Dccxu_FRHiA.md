If you're running a subscription store and your contribution margin is lower than a CAC, and on top of that you're not prioritizing your repeat and returning customers, you're heading for a big downfall. You're already unprofitable at this stage. To recover that money, you need about 70% of those customers to come back and buy again, then stay for a few more months so you can actually squeeze out enough revenue to be profitable. So how do you do that? First, you need to make sure your customers stay with you through the first month and their first rebuild. You do this by sending emails on why they should stick with your product for at least 90 days and stay consistent, because that's when they'll actually see results. And don't be shy about sending gifts on every month of a successful subscription either. That's how you win them and keep them around longer. A lot of brands don't send subscription notifications. You might lose a few customers by sending them, but it's the right way to avoid getting banned from Shopify. So use your emails to remind your customers where they are in the journey with your store.
