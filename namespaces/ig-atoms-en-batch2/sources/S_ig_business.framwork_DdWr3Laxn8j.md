It's really easy to think your marketing is complete when it's actually missing entire parts. Maybe you're making content, you're running ads, you have a good offer, you're making sales. Great. But what happens after the sale? Do you have a good onboarding process? Are you doing anything to keep those customers? Are you asking for referrals? Do you have any upsells or cross-sells?
