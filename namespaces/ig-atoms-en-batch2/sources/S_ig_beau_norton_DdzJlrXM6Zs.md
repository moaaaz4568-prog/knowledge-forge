If you sell coaching or courses or any type of information, there's one critical thing that you need to do if you really want your offers to sell. And an easy way to understand this is to just look at the physical products that you buy every day. This drink here, your computer, your microphone, all the physical items in your world. And then ask yourself, how difficult was it for you to make the decision to buy that thing? In most cases, it probably wasn't very difficult. When I saw this microphone and I learned that it was a quality microphone, I immediately wanted it. I didn't have to think that much about it. But if you sell information, that is not the case. Your customers are not looking at your online course or your coaching program and immediately they want it. It's a different type of sale. The person has to clearly understand your offer in their mind, and they have to be able to see it in their mind, just like they would see a physical product that they want to buy. What you're really selling is something physical, something tangible, something that somebody can experience in their real life. Maybe that experience is a feeling inside of their body, or maybe it's an external transformation, like their life is gonna look different. They're gonna be able to live in a new place maybe. They're gonna be able to have different interactions in their relationships. They're gonna step on the scale or look in the mirror and like what they see. So the better that you can get at turning your intangible information into a clear picture in somebody's minds of what they're actually going to receive, the easier it will be to sell your offer.
