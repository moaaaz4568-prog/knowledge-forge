Q4 has started and you have probably planned everything already. But here are four things which will make it better. Number one, make your pop-up match the season. Halloween themed in October, PFCM themed in November, Christmas, then New Year. It takes 20 minutes and it converts better than the same generic pop-up you have had since March. Number two, don't just theme your campaigns, theme your flows too. Everyone updates their campaign design for the holidays and completely forgets that their welcome flow and abandoned cart are still sitting there in plain branding. Those are running all through December too. Number three, protect your subscribers. This one costs brands real money every single year. If your Black Friday discount is bigger than what your subscribers pay every month, they will cancel and rebuy at the same sale price. Think about that. Your most loyal customers are being punished for being loyal. Give them their own offer, something better, so they feel rewarded instead of stupid. Number four, send one plain text email. In November, every inbox is wall-to-wall designed sale graphics. Everyone's banner look the same. Send one short email from the founder, no design, no images, just text. It stands out more than any graphic you could possibly make because it's the only thing in there that looks like a person sent it. Follow and comment Q4 for more tips.
