Despite what you might have learned as you first got certified as a personal trainer, you do not have to do formal assessments the first time that you meet with a client. This might be a little bit different than what you've been doing and what you learn inside of your certifications, but the truth of the matter is is that most new personal trainers are just not that great at actually doing assessments. Until you have a high, high level of comfort and in the moment recall and mastery of anatomy and physiology, it's not gonna serve you. Not to mention the fact that if it's not delivered right, formalized assessment can be kind of awkward and actually demotivating to a lot of clients. Only do those assessments if it's gonna increase buy-in, if they know, hey, this person knows what they're doing, and I feel really comfortable and safe working with them as a coach and trainer. If you don't feel like it's gonna move that person towards action and help them say yes to training with you, then don't do it. End up trying to fit too much inside of this first hour when someone first comes in, but if you get them to take action and start training with you, you can be patient.
