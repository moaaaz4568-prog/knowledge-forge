If you're trying to sell something online and it's not really selling that well, I bet you the problem is not the product or service. You might think that you need to create a better offer, you need to have something more unique, you need to promise more or pack more value into whatever you're selling, and you might try to launch many different things because you think that someday you're gonna stumble upon the right thing to sell, right? And I'm only saying this because I've been there, but what I've realized is that it's not what you're selling, it's the energy that you bring when you're talking about what you're selling. It's the mission behind what you're selling. It's the why. Because when you talk about your product, people feel that. People can feel if you're excited to talk about what you're selling or if you're a little bit hesitant. They can feel if you believe in your product or not. So instead of creating something that you think is going to sell, create something that you would be proud to create. Create something that you strongly believe in, something that you're excited about, because that energy is gonna come through and people will buy because of that, not necessarily because the product is perfect, because it's the best offer, but because they feel your energy and they resonate with that.
