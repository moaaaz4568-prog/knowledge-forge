Brands like Himss use a post-purchase education sequence to educate customers, reduce buyer's remorse, and increase repeat purchases. Steal it for your brand. It starts with five emails, each timed to where the customer is in their journey. Email one is the confirmation email. This sets expectations: when the order arrives, what the first experience should look like, and anything they should prepare for. Email two is about reducing buyer's remorse before it starts. This is where brands usually send a founder note, brand story, or social proof to reinforce why the customer made the right decision. Email three is all about onboarding: a short tutorial, walkthrough, or step-by-step guide showing exactly how to use the product correctly. After delivery, the next message focuses on maximizing value: tips, best practices, common mistakes to avoid, or things most customers don't know. This helps the product become part of their routine. And the final email is a check-in: ask how it's going, request a review, and recommend the next logical purchase. It naturally opens the door for order number two. Himss runs this because a customer who quits before seeing results rarely buys again, but a customer who understands what's happening is much more likely to stay.
