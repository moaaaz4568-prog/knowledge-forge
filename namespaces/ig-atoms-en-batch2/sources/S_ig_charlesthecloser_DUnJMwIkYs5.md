The reason that you sound shit on sales calls is because you treat it like a sales call, following some script and saying things like, "How I'd like to conduct this call is ask you some questions, see if we're a good fit and go through the next steps from there." Mate, imagine if you run a first date and you spoke like that. They'd run across the room like you were Jeffrey Dahmer. Start treating your sales calls like actual conversations. That's it. That's the secret sauce. But somewhere along the way you started chasing the sale, looking for the perfect line, the perfect framework, and so focused on sounding a certain way that you forgot to just be a human. At the end of the day, people aren't buying a script, they're buying into you and whether they trust you to get the result they came on the call for. And in most cases, they can smell if you're desperate for a sale. So trust me, you don't need a better script or to watch another objection handling video. You need to remember how to have a fucking conversation.
