There are three questions I think we should ask ourselves before we publish any piece of content on the internet. Is what I'm sharing common knowledge already? If so, how can I put a unique spin on it to make it more interesting? If my past self saw this, would I find it interesting or would I just keep on scrolling? Will this make someone feel better or worse about themselves or the world? And how can I inject an element of positivity into what I'm creating?
