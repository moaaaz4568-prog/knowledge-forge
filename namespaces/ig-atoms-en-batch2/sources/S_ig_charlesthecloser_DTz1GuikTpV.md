Here are five generic phrases that you should avoid in sales. Hi John, nice to meet you. How's it going? Where is it that you're calling from? Instead, say, John, pleasure to meet you. What was it that actually prompted you to book a call? Don't say, what's your biggest challenge right now? Instead, say, share with me if you don't mind some of the things you're looking to accomplish in the near future. Definitely don't say, and when you've achieved all that, how would that feel? Instead, say, by achieving all this, what version of you are we unlocking here? Using scripts and these generic phrases will only get you so far in remote high ticket sales. But truly listening and altering out you think is ultimately what makes a top tier closer.
