Coaching survival mode is a classic symptom that I see business owners being stuck from ten to twenty k for a very long time, and it really comes down to Maslow's hierarchy of needs. So at the bottom is your psychological needs: food, shelter, sleep, clothing—the very basic stuff—and acquiring this causes you to create short-term decisions because you absolutely need this stuff to live. And being in survival mode here is you thinking that you are still needing these psychological needs. Getting to thirty k a month, getting to that next level isn't a psychological need because if you think like that, I must land this client, you start creating short-term decisions that truly prevent you to scaling to the next level.
