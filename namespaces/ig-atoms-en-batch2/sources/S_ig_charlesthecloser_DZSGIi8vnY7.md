When a prospect says, I need to think about it, it's the same as when you speak to your girlfriend and they say, I'm fine. You know something is wrong. You know you're in trouble. You know there's something very specific going on. She just doesn't want to tell you what it is yet. And if you accept it and walk away, then you're going to pay for it later. Your prospect on a sales call is doing the exact same thing. When they say, I need to think about it, they are never ever just going to go away and sit there and think. It's a smokescreen or a bullshit filter for something that was in the conversation that got left unresolved. Maybe it's the money, maybe it's fear. More often than anything else, they've got a feeling of uncertainty or hesitation. And I need to think about it is just a polite way of them saying that. Most salespeople try to handle the I need to think about it, or they schedule a follow-up and say, okay, no worries, take your time. And then they wonder why the prospect never even shows up to the follow-up call. Two questions pierce that bullshit filter every single time. The first is, do you feel like what we've gone through today gives you a real shot of achieving whatever they said they want to achieve? And the second is, what's the real reason between putting more time and distance between achieving the outcome you came on the call looking for? That question, that tonality, you will start to figure out what is really going on behind the smokescreen. Drop me a follow if you want more tips like this.
