Look at your data. If people who buy product one come back for product three, package them together so it becomes one easy immediate decision. And use discounts to turn the bundle into an absolute no-brainer. Ten to twenty percent is considered the sweet spot. Small enough that it doesn't affect your margins and high enough that it is worth it for your customer. Brands like The Ordinary do this perfectly by naming their bundles after the exact problem they solve. For example, the regimen for uneven skin tone. It's a nice way to double or triple your AOV instead of selling a single product.
