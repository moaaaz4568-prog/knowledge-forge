One of the best things I did for my business was putting all of my marketing in one place. Because now I can actually see the whole thing, and that makes it so much easier for me to spot what's missing. If sales are low, for example, I can look at it and figure out where the problem might be. Maybe it's the offer. Maybe we're getting the wrong people. Maybe our content is not working. Or maybe the sales process needs some work. I can look at the entire operation, find what's not working, and make the right change.
