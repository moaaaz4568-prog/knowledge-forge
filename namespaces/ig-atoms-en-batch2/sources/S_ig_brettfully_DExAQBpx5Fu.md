Ninety-nine percent of coaches and info sellers are getting this wrong about their audience's pains and problems. You might have already written down your ideal customer's pains, and it was probably like they want more money, they don't have time, they don't have energy, they want six-pack abs, and they want more freedom. All of these pains and problems that they're experiencing is just smoke screens. Everybody is experiencing these things and they all want more money and they all want more freedom. This isn't the true pains and problems that your audience is having, and it's not helping you out with your offer, marketing, and content. The best marketers, such as myself, turn the perspective of pain points into obstacles. This allows you to get around the smoke screen and actually discover what are the true obstacles that they are facing in order to get to their dream state. So what you need to do is write down your audience's obstacles and not their pains.
