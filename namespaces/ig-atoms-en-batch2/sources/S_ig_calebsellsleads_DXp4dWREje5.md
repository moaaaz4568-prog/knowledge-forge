If I was gonna start my agency again today, I would not run the retainer model. Because in the retainer model, you're trading time for money. At the end of the day, you're setting up the ad accounts into their businesses, you're managing the ads for their businesses under their brand. You're just their marketing guy at the end of the day. But with this model called the pay per lead brand model, you actually build your own pay per lead brand under a specific niche. So for example, cleaning, I'd be called Cleaning Quote Connector. I'd advertise under that brand and generate heaps of leads. And through there, I would then sell those leads that I generate under my own brand, my asset, to cleaning companies who are looking for more work. So I would sell them at, let's say, $90 each, fixed price per lead. If I even just generated 10 leads a day, that's $900 a day in revenue that I'd be making, compared to being paid $1, 000 a month for generating that person hundreds of leads. So you can see the scalability to it. If you end up selling like two, three, 400 leads a month to these large cleaning companies or these large companies in your specific niche, you're gonna be making 10 times more money than charging a monthly retainer and then hoping that they pay that invoice.
