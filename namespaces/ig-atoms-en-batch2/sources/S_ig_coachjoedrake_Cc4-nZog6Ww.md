The one trait that I see all great coaches and personal trainers share is the enjoyment and the love for problem solving. Really, problem solving is what great training boils down to. Problem solving the right exercises for your client based upon how they move, working to identify the habits and the things they need to change, and then problem solving with that person, how do we make this happen in your life? Because although when you're first getting certified and you're learning about training, everything is presented in this research laboratory driven optimal mindset. But the truth is that your clients and people, they live in the real world with real problems and real challenges, and they need a coach who not only knows the inner workings of the human body, but who's willing to help them problem solve how to make all of this work in their life so they see the changes they're looking for.
