So there's a famous story about Ralph Waldo Emerson, and if you've read How to Win Friends and Influence People, you're gonna know this one. If you haven't read it, you should. But the way the story goes is that Ralph and his son were trying to get a calf into the barn, and no matter how hard he pushed and no matter how hard his son pulled, the calf just wouldn't budge. The harder they pushed, the harder they pulled, the more that he resisted. And all of a sudden, out of nowhere comes the Irish maid who takes her finger, sticks it in the calf's mouth, and just walks the calf into the barn. And so it's just a really simple story that sticks to help understand how to get people to make change, how to take action, and a lot of it has to do with understanding what are they looking for. And that's hard for new coaches, especially personal trainers, to do when you feel like you know so much and you want this for this person so bad. You want them to feel good, you want them to be in shape, maybe even sometimes it seems like more than they do. But the key to getting them there is understanding what does this person really want? How can I give them what they want while I might be giving them what they need? And the best coaches figure out how to do both.
