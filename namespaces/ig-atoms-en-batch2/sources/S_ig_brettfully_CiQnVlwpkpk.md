My absolute favorite quote for business is actually taken from an artist, Picasso. Good artists borrow and great artists steal. What Picasso is talking about is that there's no such thing as an original idea. Everyone steals ideas and turns it into their own. And in fact, if you look at great artists, you'll notice that they steal a lot of things. Especially in the rap industry, a lot of sounds are stolen. And the best way to create something unique is take all these pre-existing ideas and combine them into one. A quick example is Jeff Bezos. When he created Amazon, he essentially took the idea of a bookstore, internet, and eBay and combined it. This led him to create a unique idea of an online bookstore, and then eventually selling other products on Amazon like eBay already does. You can use this for business to come up with creative solutions, or just in general come up with a great idea. Look at your competitors and then look at some outside sources and write down what you like from each one of them. Take all those likes and then combine it, and that's how you come up with your own unique idea for business.
