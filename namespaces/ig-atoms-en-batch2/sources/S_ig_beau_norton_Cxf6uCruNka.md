What if taking massive action towards your goals could feel just as easy as sitting on the couch watching Netflix? Impossible, you might say. But have you ever wondered how some people can seem to work so hard? Like they work 12 hour days, they hustle, they work on their business, and it seems easy for them. And maybe it's not so easy for you. Maybe you struggle to stay motivated and to work on your business or your goals. So what is the difference between you and that other person who finds it so easy? What I've realized is that it's not the task itself, it's not the work itself, it's our degree of resistance to the work that makes it feel either easy or difficult. So if we were able to find ways to just release some of that resistance, the things that were once difficult could start to feel easy. But the thing is, you can't learn how to release the resistance until you face the things that you're resisting head on. So you have to do the things that are uncomfortable and do them long enough until they start to feel easy. As Steven Pressfield in the book The War of Art says, resistance is a liar.
