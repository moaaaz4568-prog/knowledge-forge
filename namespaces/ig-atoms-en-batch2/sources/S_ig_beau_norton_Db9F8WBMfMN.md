If you post short form content but you're not getting a lot of sales from it, that doesn't mean you're doing anything wrong in your short form content. You might just be missing another piece of the puzzle. You see, short form content is great for getting attention, getting people to know about your brand and what you do, but it's horrible for generating sales and conversions. Because think about it, the average person when they see a piece of content from you, they maybe watch like 30 seconds of it, but then they're also scrolling on social media and they're watching like 100 pieces of content from 100 different creators all in the same hour. By the time they're done with that scrolling session, they probably don't even remember who you are. So short form content is actually not designed to get sales, it's just designed to keep you top of mind and just make people aware that you exist. And that's why I think every business needs to have a long form content strategy in addition to their short form strategy. You should use your short form content to drive people to your long form content, because that's where the sales are gonna happen. That's where people are gonna get to know you better and get to trust you enough to where they feel comfortable making a purchase.
