One of the easiest ways to spot a bad marketing decision is to put it next to the rest of your strategy. And that's actually one of the reasons I built this. Everything is on one page, so whenever I want to change something, I can see what else that decision affects. Maybe I want to target a different customer. Okay, does my offer still make sense for them? Does my content attract them? Does the way I position the business speak to them? Does my sales process actually work for them? Suddenly, one small decision becomes a bunch of questions I probably wouldn't have asked if it wasn't for this. That's what I like about having everything organized in a single place. It makes these kind of decisions a lot harder to make.
