Here are five beliefs that I installed into my subconscious after I realized that sales scripts are complete bullshit and prospects just buy you. The first is investment over time. It's always better to invest money rather than just waste loads of time trying to figure something out on your own. Time is the one resource you can never ever get back, whilst money is replaceable. The second is free time is about priority, not availability. It's never about how much time you have, it's about how much time you are willing to make. We all have the exact same 24 hours, but priorities reveal themselves through action, not words. So if a prospect's telling you they've not had time, what they're actually telling you is they've not made it a priority yet. The third is that you have complete control and ownership of where you are in life. Zero victim mentality. No blaming circumstances. Where you are is a direct result of the decisions you've made. You need to actually live this belief for it to land on a call. If you're still blaming external circumstances for where you are in life, it's very difficult for you to challenge a prospect authentically. The fourth is resourcefulness versus resources. It's never about how much money you have. It's about how resourceful you are to find it. Money is an infinite resource. It can be found, it can be borrowed, it can be restructured. So when a prospect is telling you they don't have the money, what they are really telling you is that it's not matter to them enough for them to become resourceful. And fifth is action over analysis. Research and due diligence are often just used to delay action. Taking a step is the only way to either find out and grow or succeed. Analysis paralysis is just fear dressed up as wisdom. Every day spent researching, the only way to know if something works is to do it.
