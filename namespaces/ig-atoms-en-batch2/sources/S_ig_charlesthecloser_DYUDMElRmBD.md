The month that I finally hit twenty k, I was genuinely depressed because I'd been building towards that figure in my mind for so long, and I thought that when I reached that point, something would change. And there I was in my office at the business that I owned at that time, still in an office. I'd still commuted for an hour and a half that morning. Looked outside the window, still pissing with rain. Nothing changed, and that's when I'd realised that I'd been optimising for the wrong thing this whole time. I realised money was never the thing I was actually looking for. I was looking for freedom, to choose where I was, to choose what my day looked like, to choose who I actually wanted to spend time with and who I spoke to. None of that shifted because of a number. It only shifted when I used a completely different model, a model where the income and the life that you actually want work together. So if you're chasing a number right now, it could be worthwhile having an honest conversation with yourself about what actually changes when you reach that.
