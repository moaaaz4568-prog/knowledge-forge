Here are the four ways to increase your AOV using emails and no one is talking about this. Number one, the pre-shipment add-on email. Between your order confirmation and fulfillment, there is a window where customers are at their peak buying temperature. If you send an email after 30 to 60 minutes of order confirmation, saying that do you want to add anything else? We will pack it together and there is no extra shipping in it. This converts unusually well, but the blocker here is operation. Your 3PL or warehouse has to support order merging. That's exactly why a lot of brands skip this email. Number two, stop wasting your order confirmation. It's your highest open rate email by wide margin, 60 to 70 percent. And most brands hand it to Shopify's default receipt template. If you just put a subscribe and save or consumable refill or a complementary product above the fold, this one change moves more revenue than any campaign calendar. Number three, dollar thresholds beat percentage discounts. 15% off scales down with the order and gives your cheapest customer the same deal. $25 off $125 or $60 off $250, people opt here. Percent is off is an AOV depressant that most brands run permanently. Number four, gift with purchase over discount. A GWP at a spend costs you cogs instead of margin, and it gives people a reason to add rather than a reason to subtract. This works especially well if the gift is of trial size.
