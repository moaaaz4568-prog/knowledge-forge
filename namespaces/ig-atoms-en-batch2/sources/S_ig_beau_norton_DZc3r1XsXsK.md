If you want your product or service to sell like crazy, you need to understand offer positioning. And this is based on a psychological principle called juxtaposition or contrast. Meaning the human mind doesn't really understand things in isolation. It understands things in contrast. So it has to be able to compare a concept or an idea to something else in order for it to understand what it really is. For example, you only understand what hot is because you know what cold feels like. You only understand what happiness is because you've also experienced the opposite of happiness, right? That's how the mind works. So when you're selling something, you have to be able to compare and contrast what you're offering to something else that the person already understands. An easy way to think about this is like the old way versus the new way. Let's say you help people be better parents or something. And the old way is you put your kids in time out in order to get them to behave. And now your new way, in comparison to that, is some sort of different solution, a way to get your child to behave that doesn't require you to put them in time out. So when you compare your new way to the old way, it makes the benefits of your way more obvious in somebody's mind.
