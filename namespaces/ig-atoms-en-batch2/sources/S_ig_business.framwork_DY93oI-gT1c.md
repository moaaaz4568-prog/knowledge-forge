Look at this marketing framework. It's designed to make your customers add one more item to their cart. Steal it for your brand. When someone is in their cart, you need to show them exactly how far away they are from unlocking a gift using a progress bar. Tell them you are only twelve dollars away from unlocking your gift. When they cross that line, let them choose their gift from a list of two to three options. Giving them the power of choice massively increases the perceived value of the reward. But keep it under three options to prevent decision fatigue. You need to set your threshold ideally around ten dollars to twenty dollars above your current average order value. And make sure the cost for that gift is only ten percent to fifteen percent so it stays viable for you.
