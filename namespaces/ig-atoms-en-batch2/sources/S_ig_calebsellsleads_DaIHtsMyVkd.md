Most people have never heard of pay per lead, so here's the simple version. Businesses in industries like solar, mortgage, roofing and home services, they spend thousands every single month trying to get new customer conversations. So instead of managing their ads for a marketing monthly retainer, you actually build your own lead source. You run your own ads, you send people to your page, and then you collect that inquiry, you qualify it and then sell that lead to a business at a fixed price. So you're not saying pay me $3,000 a month and I'll try to get the results. What you're saying is I can sell you 50 qualified leads at $80 each. They then pay for the thing that they actually want, and you are paid for the output instead of your time.
