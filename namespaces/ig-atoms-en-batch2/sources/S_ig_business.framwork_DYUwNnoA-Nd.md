Every product has a natural usage cycle. Protein powder might last 30 days, coffee beans two weeks, a vitamin supply 90 days. So instead of blasting everyone with the same reminder, they estimate when the product is likely running low. Then they send the reorder reminder five to seven days before that moment, not after the customer runs out, before. Because once a customer runs out, they enter an open decision window where they can easily buy from a competitor. The reminder itself is simple. First, mention the product they bought, so they instantly know what the email is about. Then, remind them they're probably about to run out of it. And finally, include one simple call to action to reorder. In AG1's case, they map the average usage cycle of their products and send a reminder about a week before the customer is expected to run out. That way, the reminder arrives exactly when the need already exists, and that's why it works.
