A lot of people struggle to be motivated, whether it's to work out or start that business that will make them a bunch of money, or just anything productive in their life. But there's this common misconception about motivation. Most people think that you already have to have the feeling in you that makes you want to get up and do something. Basically, motivation already has to be in you. But it's actually quite the opposite. You only become motivated after starting the thing you wanted. Starting something is a small win that gives your brain dopamine and tells you to keep on going. Like working out. Before you go and lift weights, you most likely have the feeling of not wanting to. But as you force yourself to do it, after 10 minutes, you are now in the zone to keep on going. Action leads to motivation.
