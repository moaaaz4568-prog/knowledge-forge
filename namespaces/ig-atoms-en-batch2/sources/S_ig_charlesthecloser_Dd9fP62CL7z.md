The hardest part about high ticket sales is wasting time on offers that pay you peanuts. Most closers waste that time because they're asking the wrong question. They're asking, "Where do I find the best role?" So what they end up doing is saying yes to the first offer that says yes to them, and it takes them one, two, three months to figure out that the offer is going nowhere. If you do that a few times, you've wasted a full year. I know I did. I probably wasted six months of my high ticket sales career on offers that didn't progress. So I want you to ask yourself this question instead. What do I need to see on day one for an offer to know that this is actually worth my time? Here's how to do it in two steps. The first is the offer economics. Take what the offer brings in in new cash every single month. Look how many closers there is. Look at what your commission is and work out how much you can actually make. If an offer is only making 50K a month, then they definitely can't be paying you 10, 20K a month. Step two is conviction. Number one, can you become a genuine expert or appear to be an expert in this industry? And as you're going through the testimonials and the results, do you actually believe in what you're selling? You need to be so sold and have such high levels of conviction about the thing that you're selling that it transfers to your prospects. Because if you're not sold on it, your prospects will sniff that out in a heartbeat. Inside my community, Remote Wealth, all my clients have an offer vetting checklist so they know how to identify top 1% offers. So before you say yes to another online sales opportunity, run it through those two filters first before you get too excited.
