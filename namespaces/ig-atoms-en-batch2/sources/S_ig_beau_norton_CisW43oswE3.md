You're trying to grow a service-based business of some kind, but you don't feel like an expert. Focus on this. You want to focus on a minimum viable solution. Start as small as you need to with version 1.0 of your service. This is the minimum thing that you feel confident that you can deliver on. People often think that they have to like take someone from A all the way to Z, like they have to get this huge transformation or they have to provide this huge service.
