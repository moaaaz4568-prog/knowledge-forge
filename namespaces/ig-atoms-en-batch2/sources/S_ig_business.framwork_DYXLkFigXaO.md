Sometimes a customer wants a product but finds out it's out of stock. So they add it to a wishlist or click on the "notify me" button for when it comes back. Those are all high-intent signals. The customer already wants the product, they just couldn't complete the purchase. So when the item comes back in stock, the brand sends an alert immediately.
