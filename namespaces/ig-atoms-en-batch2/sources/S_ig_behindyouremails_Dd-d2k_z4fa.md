A lot of people still get this wrong about copywriting. It's not about fancy words. It's not about clever lines or big vocabulary. It's about saying the right thing to the right person. And the only way that works is by being specific about who you're talking to. Not women 25 to 45. That's not a person. Nobody reads an email and thinks that's me. I'm a woman aged 25 to 45. Be specific. A 34 year old mom who's been waking up at 3 am for 8 months and has already tried melatonin and given up. Now you know exactly what to say. You know what she has already tried. You know what she's sick of hearing. Write to her and thousands of people who are not her will still feel like you wrote it for them. Try to write for everyone and you'll write something nobody feels anything about. That's the whole job. Not better words, clear picture of who's reading.
