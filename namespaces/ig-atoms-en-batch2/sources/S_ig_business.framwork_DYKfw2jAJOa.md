Order confirmation emails get opened by around sixty-five to seventy-five percent of customers. Most promo emails usually fifteen to twenty-five percent. But most brands waste that attention on a plain receipt. Brands like BeardBrand use it differently. They first start with the confirmation details: order number, delivery estimate, support contact—everything you would expect from a confirmation email. Then comes a reassurance layer with a review count, a star rating, or a quick piece of social proof—something that reinforces the purchase before introducing anything new. Only after that comes the cross-sell: one or two complementary products. For example, if someone buys beard oil, they might get recommended beard wash, along with one sentence explaining why the two work well together, and one clear link. BeardBrand does this because right after purchase is when buying resistance is lowest. The customer already decided the price was worth it, and the brand already earned enough trust to buy from. So instead of wasting the highest attention email, they use it to plant the seed for purchase number two.
