If you're trying to help clients fix their rounded back when they go into hinging and deadlifting, stop wasting time with the dowel cue. Though it may work occasionally, for a lot of people, it's not gonna help the issue and problem. But the truth is that a lot of people struggle with maintaining tension throughout their lumbar spine and really specifically using their lats as a main stabilizer of that lumbar spine. Which if you know anything about the body, it's a lot harder to create tension through your lats with one arm up overhead. A much more efficient coaching cue is using an external cue or force like the NT loop or another band that they have to drive into, which naturally is going to engage the lats. And I'm telling you, that alone will fix most people's rounded back going into a hinge. Keep in mind as you start to add it with exercises that have load like dumbbell RDLs, keep that band light. It's just enough resistance for them to engage the lats and keep that weight pulled back tight into their body.
