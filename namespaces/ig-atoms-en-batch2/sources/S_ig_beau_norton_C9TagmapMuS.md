If you want to grow a coaching or a membership-based business, this is probably the least stressful way to do it. What I recommend to most of my clients is using something called the splinter scaling method. Everything starts with your core offer. This is your coaching program or your monthly membership. This is where you deliver value to your clients on a regular basis. So you might have like a weekly resource or something you create for your clients and customers and you deliver that to them on a monthly or weekly basis. So over time you're creating this vault of resources. Now the cool thing about this is you're getting paid to create these resources. People are paying you on a monthly or weekly basis to get access to these things. So you're getting paid to create them, but at the same time you can splinter off those little pieces of content and you can bundle them into other products. So I call these satellite products. So you could bundle these resources into template bundles, online courses, workshops, other programs that you can sell separately, maybe for a lower cost, and then those products are also designed to send people back into your core offer. So the way to think about it is you're really only creating one thing, one product, and then all the other things that you need to create in your business, your content and other products, come from the one thing.
