So you guys remember payphones? Yeah, they look like this today. Nobody ever uses them. But there was a multi-million dollar scam going on through these payphones. So say back in the day, you wanted to make a call, but you had no money. Well, if you would press zero, it would initiate a call to an operator that would ask you, "Which collect call company would you like to use?" AT&T was the most common answer to this question, but the second most common was variations of "I don't care," "I don't know," "whatever," or "it doesn't matter." And one company made millions off of these lazy callers. KTNT was a communication company, and they created subsidiary companies with those exact names. So when you told the operator, "I don't care," the operator would connect to the real company called "I don't care," which was really just KTNT. And this scheme gained KTNT millions of dollars in sales until payphones died out.
