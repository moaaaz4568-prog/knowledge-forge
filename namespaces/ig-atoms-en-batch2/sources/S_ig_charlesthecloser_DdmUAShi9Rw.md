There are five levels in sales. What level are you? Level one is the waffler. He thinks sales is all about making friends, having a good chat, and then shoving a product down your prospect's throat. He's basically just a brochure with a heartbeat. Level two is the reader. He's got a perfect question, a perfect script, perfect objection handle for every single scenario. The problem is the prospect can smell it a mile off as he sounds like a robot. Level three is the most dangerous one. This is the questioner. This is the guy who's had a little bit of training. He likes his YouTube videos, he watches sales podcasts. He's always trying to hunt out the perfect sales question, but he's never had real coaching. He never does any self-reflection. He never looks at his own sales calls because he doesn't need to. He feels as though he's got the theory locked in. Level four is the analyst. He stops listening for what a prospect says and starts hearing what they actually mean. This is a different skill entirely. Most guys never even realize this stage exists. But level five is the congruent closer. He doesn't need a script, he doesn't need a perfect question, he doesn't even need the sale. He walks into the call already whole, with strong principles and beliefs that make him unshakable. Most guys get into sales because they're good at talking. Good at talking gets you to level one, but just talking alone is never gonna get you to level five. Getting to mastery isn't gonna be a level that you just jump to. It's on the other side of working through the first four, watching your own calls back, getting coached, doing the reps and the volume and the training that actually counts. Most guys these days want to skip all the way to getting the result, but they never do the work that makes it inevitable.
