You want to create more engaging social media content that actually turns strangers into fans and consider using this framework. I call it "I used to believe." Many people are stuck creating how-to educational content and they're wondering why they don't get that much engagement on their videos. One of the reasons is because educational how-to content doesn't change people's beliefs. It doesn't give people an insight or an aha moment. When you use this framework though, it does. So try this. Start your piece of content with "I used to believe blank" and then insert a common belief that people have that you have discovered to be false or limiting in some way. Then tell a personal story about how believing that made you struggle and mention that even though that belief is common, it's not necessarily the best way. Then talk about how you discovered there's a better way to do things or a new belief that makes the old belief outdated. If you do this right, it's going to give people insights and realizations and change their perspective for the better. And when you do that, they are going to have a hard time forgetting you.
