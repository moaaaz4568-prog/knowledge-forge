When working with clients and talking to other business owners, this is probably one of the biggest mistakes that I see people making in their marketing. These are the five levels of awareness in marketing, and this was popularized by Eugene Schwartz in his book Breakthrough Advertising. It's a simple concept, but it's really important to understand because I see a lot of people making the mistake of talking to their audience as if they were already solution aware. But in reality, the vast majority of your audience, the people you can serve, are down here. They are totally unaware or they're problem aware, but they don't understand the solution yet. And so as a coach or service provider, you probably want to talk about your solution and the unique things you do and the unique ways that you can help people. But when you talk about those things, most people can't hear you because they're not at that level of awareness yet. This is also really important to understand if you want your content to spread on social media and reach more people. Because again, the largest percentage of your audience is down here. So if you want to reach the most amount of people with your message, you have to speak to people down here. And you have to educate them or share your message in a way that gradually moves them from one level of awareness to another. You can do that by talking about the problems or the symptoms and challenges that people are facing and hint at the fact that there is a solution. So you kind of hook people in by talking about the problem, then you gradually bring them to the point where they're aware of the solution. Then once they're aware of your unique solution, you can present your product to them. If you do this in the wrong order, you're going to be speaking to a bunch of people who are not ready to buy and they're not even ready to listen to what you have to offer.
