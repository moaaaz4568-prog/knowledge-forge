The key towards coaching clients into real long term, I'm not talking about short term, but long term lasting change, is to better involve them in the process. And for example, when it comes to a meal plan, rather than giving them a cut and dry vanilla meal plan, it means involving them in creating that plan for eating. In a given training session, it means as a coach being willing to give away your power a little bit and allow them to make some choices. As simple as, do you want to back squat or goblet squat today, or is there anything you want to work on a little bit more? Because in the grand scheme, it's gonna have a very small impact on their long term programming, but it's gonna have a bigger impact on their mindset towards training and going from a place of I have to do this thing to I get to do this thing, and that can be powerful.
