I just saw this newsletter about Absolut Vodka and their absolute perfect marketing campaign. So Absolut Vodka was only selling about 10,000 bottles per year and only had 2.5% of the market. That was until an advertising agency came up with the longest running marketing campaign to date, and the name of it was the Absolut Bottle. This campaign really focused in on Absolut Vodka's unique selling point, which was their bottle and all their physical and verbal puns that can be spun up from it. From their ads, the bottle became an icon that could be recognized from anywhere. Like I said, all their ads were based off of puns from their name. Absolute Squeeze for the new Mandarin Vodka, Absolute Appeal for their citrus flavor. This worked because if you understood the pun, the next thing to do is share it with your friends. So it's great for word of mouth marketing. But then they took it to an entire new level where they focused on actually becoming in touch with their audience. Unlike some brands, when they get bigger, they lose focus on who their audience is and there becomes this disconnect. But Absolut Vodka focused on geographic ads, like Absolut LA, Absolut Chicago, and Absolut Texas. This focused experience for their audience allowed them to build trust. If we think of this in videos, if you try to personalize your videos to who you're trying to speak with and connect to, and then your audience that you're speaking to actually feels like they're understood rather than just being sold to.
