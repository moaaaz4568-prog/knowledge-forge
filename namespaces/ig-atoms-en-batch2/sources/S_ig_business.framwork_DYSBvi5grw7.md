A few days after someone buys, they send a product recommendation in the form of an educational email. Something about the item the customer already bought. How to use it. What result is it designed to deliver? Then they introduce a complementary product. One specific product with one clear reason it pairs well with it. If they bought a cleanser, they get recommended a serum. For coffee beans, a grinder. And for protein powder, a shaker bottle. The goal is simple: turn a one-product purchase into a two-product routine. Because once two products start being used together, they stop feeling like separate purchases. They become part of the same habit. And that's what makes this work. A customer who buys one product can leave anytime. But a customer who builds a routine using the brand is much harder to lose.
