You want to make way more money in your coaching or service-based business? Try this. You only need three things to be successful. I find most people way overcomplicate this. I did for many years. But business can actually be very, very simple. You only need three things to be successful. You need a public communication channel, something like TikTok or some social media platform that you post on consistently. And until your business is doing very well, you don't need more than one of these. The second thing you need is a private communication channel. This could be like an email list or a Facebook group or some sort of online community. And you drive people from your public communication channel to your private communication channel so that they can get to know you better and so they can get to understand what kind of value you have to offer them. And then all you need is one offer, one service, one product to sell those people. Use this very simple model for one year and there's no way you won't have success. Simple scales, complex fails.
