"Coaching survival mode is a classic symptom that I see business owners being stuck from ten to twenty k for a very long time, and it really comes down to Maslow's hierarchy of needs. So at the bottom is your psychological needs: food, shelter, sleep, clothing—the very basic stuff—and acquiring this causes you to create short-term decisions because you absolutely need this stuff to live." — sources/S_ig_brettfully_DKuilStJAkH.md
