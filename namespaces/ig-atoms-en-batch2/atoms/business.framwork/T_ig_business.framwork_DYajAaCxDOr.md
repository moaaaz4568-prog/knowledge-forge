# T_DYajAaCxDOr_01

"Sending the same product launch email to your entire list is a bad idea. Do this instead. Split your launch campaign into two tracks. Track one: past buyers. Track two: your general list. Segment past buyers by purchase history. This lets you position the new product as relevant to what they already bought. If someone bought your cleanser, show them your new toner." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DYajAaCxDOr.md

# T_DYajAaCxDOr_02

"If someone bought protein powder, show them your new flavor drop. Then give these customers early access 24 to 48 hours before launch. Send one email announcing early access, plus one follow up in case they missed it. Then, after the early access window ends, send your standard launch email to everyone else. This includes your general list plus past buyers who didn't purchase during early access." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DYajAaCxDOr.md

# T_DYajAaCxDOr_03

"Past buyers are more likely to buy again when the offer feels relevant, and early access adds exclusivity and urgency. So instead of every launch being just an announcement, it becomes a special event they cannot miss." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DYajAaCxDOr.md
