# T_DbFBFMRxuX3_01

"10 out of 10 habits to become disgustingly good at creating content, but it will just make the post button so much easier to press. Have a vision. Before you do anything, know what you want your brand to feel like. Create a mood board so every piece of content looks and feels the same. Define your strategy. Pick content pillars and creative formats before you start creating anything." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DbFBFMRxuX3.md

# T_DbFBFMRxuX3_02

"Great content has to be intentional for people to stick with it on the long term. Store everything. Every idea, story, hook, CTA, or any kind of useful resources go straight into an asset library so you never have to start from a blank page. Plan in batches. Map out your entire week or month in one sitting. Planning once is much easier than deciding what to post every single day." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DbFBFMRxuX3.md

# T_DbFBFMRxuX3_03

"Study your winners. Keep your top performers in one place. If something worked once, there's probably a lesson you can repeat. Create an inspiration library. Save content that made you stop scrolling, study it, and try to adapt it to your own voice. Build systems. The easier it will be for you to create, the more consistent you'll be." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DbFBFMRxuX3.md
