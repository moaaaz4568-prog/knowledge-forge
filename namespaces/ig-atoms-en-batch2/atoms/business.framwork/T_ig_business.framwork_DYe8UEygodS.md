# T_DYe8UEygodS_01

"When an existing customer returns to the site, views a product and leaves without buying, this is a high intent signal. Send a follow up within one to four hours. Reference the exact product they viewed and try to reduce any buying friction. Use something like reviews, sizing help, return policy or shipping information. Basically answer the reason they may have hesitated." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DYe8UEygodS.md

# T_DYe8UEygodS_02
