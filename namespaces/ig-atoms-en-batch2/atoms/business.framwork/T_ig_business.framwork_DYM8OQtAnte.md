# T_DYM8OQtAnte_01

"One to three weeks after a customer places their first order, the brand sends a second purchase offer—a discount, free shipping, a bonus sample, or loyalty points. The goal is to give the customer a reason to buy again. Acknowledge what they bought, suggest the next logical purchase, and make the offer clear. Then add a deadline, usually seven to fourteen days." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DYM8OQtAnte.md

# T_DYM8OQtAnte_02

"It's long enough for them to act and short enough to create urgency. Graza does this by following up roughly two weeks after a first purchase with a discount on the second bottle. So why does this work? It's because of timing. The customer has already experienced value, and the buying resistance is lower because they now trust the brand." — sources/S_ig_business.framwork_DYM8OQtAnte.md
