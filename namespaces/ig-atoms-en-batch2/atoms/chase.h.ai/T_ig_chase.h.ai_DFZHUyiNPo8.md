# T_DFZHUyiNPo8_01

"So tons of people have been asking questions about how DeepSeek was actually trained, and the answer that keeps coming back is reinforcement learning. But what is reinforcement learning, and how did DeepSeek employ that to get the R1 model? Well, let's take a look. So what they did is they gave their model 144, 000 different coding and math questions." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DFZHUyiNPo8.md

# T_DFZHUyiNPo8_02

"They then graded their answers to these questions, and they gave the model 64 attempts for each question. For each answer the DeepSeek model gave to that question, they compared its specific answer to the average answer to that question." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DFZHUyiNPo8.md

# T_DFZHUyiNPo8_03

"Now, if that specific answer that DeepSeek gave was better than average, they then bumped up the probability that in the future DeepSeek would use that same line of reasoning. Now, if the answer was worse than average, the opposite occurred. They reduced the probability that those specific tokens would be used." — sources/S_ig_chase.h.ai_DFZHUyiNPo8.md

# T_DFZHUyiNPo8_04
