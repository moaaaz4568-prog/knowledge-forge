# T_DZfWlYpMH5C_01

"One of the biggest mistakes all marketers make is they don't give their product a quantifiable end result. So when you're talking about outcomes and benefits, try to quantify things so that it's easier for people to understand what the product or service is going to do for them." — sources/S_ig_beau_norton_DZfWlYpMH5C.md

# T_DZfWlYpMH5C_02

"For example, it's better to say you can lose up to one pound of body fat every 14 days with our system than it is to just say you'll lose body fat quickly with our system. Or instead of saying these guided meditations will help you feel more peaceful and calm, you could say these guided meditations will double your calm and half your stress within seven days or your money back." — sources/S_ig_beau_norton_DZfWlYpMH5C.md
