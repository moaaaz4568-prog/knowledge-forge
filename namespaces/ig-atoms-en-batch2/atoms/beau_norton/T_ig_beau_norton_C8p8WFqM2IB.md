# T_C8p8WFqM2IB_02

"But in reality, the vast majority of your audience, the people you can serve, are down here. They are totally unaware or they're problem aware, but they don't understand the solution yet. And so as a coach or service provider, you probably want to talk about your solution and the unique things you do and the unique ways that you can help people." — sources/S_ig_beau_norton_C8p8WFqM2IB.md

# T_C8p8WFqM2IB_03

"So if you want to reach the most amount of people with your message, you have to speak to people down here. And you have to educate them or share your message in a way that gradually moves them from one level of awareness to another. You can do that by talking about the problems or the symptoms and challenges that people are facing and hint at the fact that there is a solution." — sources/S_ig_beau_norton_C8p8WFqM2IB.md
