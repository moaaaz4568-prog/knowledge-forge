# T_CnIOQS-IGPX_01

"If you have a sales script in front of you and you sound like you're reading a script, you're gonna lose the sale. The biggest mistake that you can make on sales calls is going from question to question sounding like a robot. What's your number one fitness goal? I wanna lose weight. Great, how long have you been struggling with that? For a while. Why is it important to you?" — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CnIOQS-IGPX.md

# T_CnIOQS-IGPX_02

"Because I wanna lose weight. If that's the case, put the script down and practice a concept I call mirroring. So when you're on the call and you ask them a question, what's your number one fitness goal? And they say that they wanna lose weight, you say, okay, cool, so you wanna lose weight, that's important to you, right?" — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CnIOQS-IGPX.md

# T_CnIOQS-IGPX_03

"This simple concept of mirroring what they say and repeating it back to them will show them that you're paying attention." — sources/S_ig_bmarkfit_CnIOQS-IGPX.md
