# T_DU5MTLSAvLD_01

"Tech sales versus high ticket sales. Here is what no one is going to tell you. Tech sales, you're still in a corporate environment, you're still chasing quota, you're still answering to a manager every day. Whilst high ticket sales, you've got complete autonomy. As long as you're closing deals, no one cares where you are. With tech sales, it's a super high barrier to entry." — sources/S_ig_charlesthecloser_DU5MTLSAvLD.md

When high ticket sales, you can start earning 10, 15k a month after three or four months of training. There's no ladder to climb, just skill and execution. In tech sales, layoffs are constant." — sources/S_ig_charlesthecloser_DU5MTLSAvLD.md

# T_DU5MTLSAvLD_03

In high ticket sales, if your offer's not working, you switch to a new one within 48 hours. You're not tied to one company or one product. It's your skill that carries with you. And in tech sales, there's limited skill development." — sources/S_ig_charlesthecloser_DU5MTLSAvLD.md
