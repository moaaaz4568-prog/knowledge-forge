# T_DZSGIi8vnY7_02

"Your prospect on a sales call is doing the exact same thing. When they say, I need to think about it, they are never ever just going to go away and sit there and think. It's a smokescreen or a bullshit filter for something that was in the conversation that got left unresolved. Maybe it's the money, maybe it's fear. More often than anything else, they've got a feeling of uncertainty or hesitation." — sources/S_ig_charlesthecloser_DZSGIi8vnY7.md
