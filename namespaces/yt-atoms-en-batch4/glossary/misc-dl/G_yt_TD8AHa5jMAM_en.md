## Term

Here personal branding is the five-step blueprint for ambitious men to scale their business past $100,000 a year: today we're going to talk about how ambitious men can scale their business with personal branding to over $100,000 a year. The phrase appears 7 times, beside "business", "consider", "number", and "content", in a video that starts from the big issue. In the speaker's use the big issue is the misunderstanding: most business owners think personal branding is for influencers — the TikTok dances and all that stuff — and they say "I'm not an influencer," but you need content more than the influencers, and content marketing is more of a priority for you than the influencers. His concrete first step is to stop being the best kept secret: a lot of business owners built something from the ground up and don't want to be seen, while the reason most are stuck at 60K a year is that they think influencer marketing is what they need, when the real work is a piece of content that makes your ideal avatar understand a problem they don't realize they have.
