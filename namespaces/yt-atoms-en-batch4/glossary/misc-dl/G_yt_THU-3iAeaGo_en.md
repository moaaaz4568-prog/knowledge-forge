# G_yt_THU-3iAeaGo_en — faith

## Term

Here faith is what the ministry turns from storybook into real: these artifacts help make that real for people so they no longer had a storybook Faith they had real faith, and that's one of the things we're trying to do through our ministry. The word appears 35 times, beside "storybook", "real", "live", and "can't", in a long Good Business episode with Dr. Price, the founder of World of the Bible. In his use faith begins as imagination: when he finished his time there and came back to the States he realized he'd been changed — he thought he had more of a storybook faith, he believed what the Bible said but didn't know why, and besides that it was his own imagination, he thought Jesus wore pressed linen and walked on carpet grass. His concrete work is the ground: World of the Bible gives real answers that are grounded in Scripture and done by experts, through the ministry he started and the digs National Geographic featured, so that the Bible isn't just a fairy tale or a myth but something real that has been brought to life.
