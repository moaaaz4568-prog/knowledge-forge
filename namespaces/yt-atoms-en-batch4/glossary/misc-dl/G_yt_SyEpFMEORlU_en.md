# G_yt_SyEpFMEORlU_en — influencers

## Term

Here influencers are the people with attention whom the speaker connects to brands with money: he uses influencers who have attention and brands who have money, connects the two together, and takes his percentage. The word appears 30 times, beside "who", "brands", "thousands", and "find", and its singular "influencer" appears 15 times for one creator. In his use influencers are the supply side of a boring business no one talks about: thousands of influencers in every country want to get paid and sponsored for their content, while brands are throwing money at influencer marketing but are lost about how to invest, pay, and negotiate. His concrete math is the funnel: find 20 influencers, get 6 accepted, and that is 4 to 12k in the same period — and the brand confirms the ROAS before doing three more videos in the summer season.

## Synonyms

- influencer (the singular form, used for one creator)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a get-rich scheme: here the business is matching attention to money for a percentage — the entry records the funnel and the brand's ROAS check, not a promise that any 20 influencers pay out.
- Read as the brands' side: the influencers here are the ones who want to be paid and sponsored, so the entry records their position too — lost about pay and negotiation — not just the brands' spending.
