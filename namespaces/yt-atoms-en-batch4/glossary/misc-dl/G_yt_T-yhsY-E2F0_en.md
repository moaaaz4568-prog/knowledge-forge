# G_yt_T-yhsY-E2F0_en — platform

## Term

Here a platform is what the most valuable companies build first: they built the platform and scaled it to the point where they could attract billions of dollars, made it accessible to a lot of people, and only then thought about monetizing the users. The word appears 13 times, beside "build", "built", "building", and "attracted", and its plural "platforms" appears too. In the speaker's use the platform is the base that comes before the offer: the trend is not about creating a new social network or a new platform, but about becoming an itemized version of these platforms, and the first wave is to build a platform on top of which a lot of people can gather. His concrete warning is the order: monetizing in the first week or month for a hundred grand is the Brokey move, while focusing on building value and attracting the users first is what lets you monetize the attention itself.

## Synonyms

- platforms (the plural form, used for more than one platform)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a website: here the platform is the accessible base that attracts billions of users before any monetization — the entry records the sequence, not the technology.
- Read as anti-monetization: the speaker's point is about timing, not refusal — monetize only once you have attracted the users, so the entry records the order, not a ban on making money.
