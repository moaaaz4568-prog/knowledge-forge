# G_yt_TjuarXRQzSQ_en — double click

## Term

In this video, to double click is the Illustrator gesture that opens the options for a tool: the phrase appears 5 times in the source. The speaker's tip is that you can double click the live corners to bring up a dialog box with new options for changing the style of a corner, and that you can double click a swatch to activate the global swatch option, with gradient, color, and the ellipse tool nearby in the workflow. His concrete demonstration builds a circle with the ellipse tool, adds a gradient fill from the color palettes, and shows how good use of color can make or break a design. In use, double click is the shortcut into a tool's settings rather than a way of selecting something.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a selection shortcut: double clicking here opens a tool's options dialog, so using it to select or edit directly misses the settings the speaker is demonstrating.
- Read as a universal Illustrator habit: the tip is tied to live corners and global swatches, so double clicking elsewhere in the program does not open the same options.
