# G_yt_TGNAp8QkC9I_en — revenue streams

## Term

Here revenue streams are the question the podcast opens with: what's like all of your revenue streams? — and the guest's answer is a list that starts with the gym. The phrase appears 3 times, beside "different", in a podcast with Scott Wilson, the owner of a personal training studio and several other businesses. In his use the streams are the ranking: number one is hands down the gym, his main bread and butter, what he's done for 15 years at Results by Design and 20 plus years since school — and then real estate, life insurance, and the car business, all kind of little different revenue streams that happened around COVID, when the government came in and shut the gym down and he had to figure out other ways to earn a living and take care of his family. His concrete rule for the team is the levels: there are different levels of players on your team, A players and B players, and B players aren't bad — they come in and eat broccoli every day — while the owner finds the same flow state inside the business and tries to replicate it with the employees.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as income: here the revenue streams are the ranking — the gym first, the rest after — so the entry records the order, not the total.
- Read as diversification: the speaker's streams came from necessity when the gym shut down, so the entry records the necessity, not the portfolio.
