# G_yt_SuIs_2qrK-k_en — doing enough

## Term

Here doing enough is the feeling the speaker opens with: you probably feel like you are not doing enough, and that feeling is a big common theme, not just in ecom but in the online money space in general. The phrase appears 26 times, beside "feel", "probably", "every single day", and "result", in the argument that the feeling comes from not having X result — and that is a very big problem. In his use the feeling is a psychological problem, not a fact about the work: people beat themselves up every day because they do not have the result they are trying to get, even when they are actually working and doing enough. His concrete correction is that ecom drop shipping is all skills and luck, and that time has not caught up yet for people who are already there — so the feeling of not doing enough is the thing stopping them from seeing it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a workload measure: here doing enough names a feeling, not a count of hours or tasks — the entry records the speaker's claim that the feeling is psychological, not a timesheet.
- Read as proof you are behind: the speaker says a lot of people are already there but time has not caught up yet, so the feeling of not doing enough is the error, not the evidence.
