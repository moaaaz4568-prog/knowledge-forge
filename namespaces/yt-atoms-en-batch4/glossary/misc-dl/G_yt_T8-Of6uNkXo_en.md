# G_yt_T8-Of6uNkXo_en — money task

## Term

Here a money task is the one task AI picks for the speaker every single day: she uses AI to pick her one money task every single day so that she can't hide in the busy work anymore. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "every", "single", and "busy", in a video about the tasks that move the needle on your business. In her use the money task is the task that requires being visible: it requires asking for something, risking a no, and having the nerve — which is why, left to herself, she would pick the comfortable thing every single time and stay hidden in the busy work. Her concrete system is the list itself: every task gets a status, a rough time, and a type — a money task or busy work, creating content, infrastructure, research, or a quick win — and letting AI pick it has generated real revenue and moved things forward fast, because the safe things, like clearing the inbox or making a Canva image, are important but are not the things that move the needle.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the hardest task: here the money task is the one that moves the needle — being visible, asking, risking a no — so the entry records the effect, not the effort.
- Read as anti-busy-work: the speaker's busy work is still important — the inbox, the Canva image, the content calendar — so the entry records the choice between them, not a ban on the safe things.
