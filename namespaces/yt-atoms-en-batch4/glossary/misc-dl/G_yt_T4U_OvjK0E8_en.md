# G_yt_T4U_OvjK0E8_en — second guess

## Term

Here to second guess is the habit the video is about: the speaker second guesses himself so much, constantly second guessing every single thing he is doing, no matter how prepared he feels or how many videos he makes. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "myself", "spend", and "constantly", and its -ing form "second guessing" runs through the video too. In his use second guessing is the private cost of performing: a dozen ideas sit ready to be made into videos, and he has gone through each one alone and talked himself out of it, while the high performers he admires show only their best and leave him feeling like he is the only one who struggles. His concrete relief is the discovery that the people he looked up to for years, once he met them, feel this way too — very rarely do the people you admire internally feel the way you think they do.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as humility: here second guessing is the constant self-doubt that stalls a dozen ready ideas — the entry records the stall, not modesty.
- Read as a personal flaw: the speaker's point is that the people you admire feel it too, so the entry records the shared feeling, not a diagnosis.
