# G_yt_TXynmHJHo7I_en — LinkedIn profile

## Term

In this tutorial, a LinkedIn profile is the personal page whose sections carry the professional side of a person, and the phrase appears 6 times in the source. The speaker calls it the most important thing on LinkedIn, divides it into Portfolio, Cover, Name, Title, Location, and Followers and Connections, and says the Experience section must hold all your experience because the profile focuses on the professional aspect. He also did a full photoshoot to get professional photos for his own LinkedIn account, and says a strong LinkedIn profile will bring job opportunities almost daily. In use, the profile is the thing a job seeker builds section by section — adding experience, projects, and posts to the Activity section — so that the 77% of jobs filled through LinkedIn can find them.

## Live Example

> "Now let's move on to the most important thing on LinkedIn: your personal profile. Your LinkedIn profile is divided into sections, each section talking about a specific aspect of you." — sources/S_yt_TXynmHJHo7I_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the LinkedIn account itself: the profile is the public page with its sections, while the account also holds Jobs, Messages, Notifications, Groups, Newsletter, and Events, so confusing the two mixes the page with the whole site.
- Read as a CV pasted online: the speaker treats the profile as a living page — sections, photos, posts, and activity — that brings opportunities almost daily, not a static document filed once and forgotten.
