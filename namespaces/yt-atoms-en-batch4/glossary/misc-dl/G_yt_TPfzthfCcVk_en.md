# G_yt_TPfzthfCcVk_en — thinking objectively

## Term

Here thinking objectively is the habit that changes the whole trajectory of your business: how thinking objectively is going to essentially change the entire trajectory of your business and make you a lot more money than it actually is right now. The phrase appears 3 times, beside "how" and "why", in a video by Hassan, the head of sales at clientacquisition.io. In his use thinking objectively is the opposite of the story you tell yourself: you probably think you already think objectively when you make your decisions — you don't, you actually don't — because when it comes to running a business a lot of decisions have to be made on a day-to-day basis, and it's very difficult to stay objective. His concrete fix is the system: if you think objectively you can create systems that will rid you of making emotional decisions, and then you'll lead your lead towards success at a much more rapid rate — and the outreach beside it is the test, because everyone tells him they know they need to do their outreach, they know it's imperative, and if they don't do outreach nothing will happen.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as logic: here thinking objectively is a system that rids you of emotional decisions — the entry records the system, not the logic.
- Read as honesty: the speaker's you-don't-think-objectively is the starting point, so the entry records the starting point, not the claim.
