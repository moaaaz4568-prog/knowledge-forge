# G_yt_Sul177V1Pg8_en — shadow

## Term

Here a shadow is the layer the tutorial separates from its object so it can be controlled on its own: the speaker separates the sphere from its shadow, makes the shadow multiplay, screen, or overlay, and reduces its opacity a little to place it behind. The word appears 8 times, beside "layer", "control", "contact", and "making", and its plural "shadows" appears too. In his use the shadow is built on its own layer in black and white: he brightens the white and darkens the black with Ctrl+M so that when he multiplays it, it starts to work for him. The concrete move is the highlight — he goes to the shadow layer and highlights it with the orange color he has below, so the shadow carries the scene's color instead of sitting as a flat dark shape.

## Synonyms

- shadows (the plural form, used for more than one shadow in the design)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a drop shadow effect: here the shadow is a separate layer the designer builds and blends by hand — the entry records the manual control, not a one-click effect.
- Read as decoration: the shadow carries the orange of the scene and the placement of the object, so removing it would flatten the brand advertisement the tutorial is building.
