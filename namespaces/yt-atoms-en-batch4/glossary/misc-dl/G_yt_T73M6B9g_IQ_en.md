# G_yt_T73M6B9g_IQ_en — prize

## Term

Here the prize is the role you take in a sales appointment: in every sales appointment there is a prize and there is somebody who wants the prize, and the speaker's second key is to reverse the roles so you become the prize. The word appears 7 times, beside "you're", "wants", "usually", and "become", in a video that gives three keys for creating an application selling system. In his use the prize is the walking, talking good deal: usually the prospect is the prize, or their money is the prize, and you're the one who wants it — usually they're the prey and you're the hunter, which leads to a whole lot of trouble at your end. His concrete frame is the first key that sets it up: in your marketing you teach them what to want, and your job is to get inside their head and set the buying criteria, so the prospect arrives already wanting what you have.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as arrogance: here the prize is a reversed role — you become the good deal the prospect wants — so the entry records the reversal, not the ego.
- Read as a trick: the speaker's prize only works with the first key — teaching them what to want and setting the buying criteria — so the entry records the setup, not the posture alone.
