# G_yt_Sa-wuVWwySM_en — responsibility

## Term

Here responsibility is the attitude Stuckey credits with saving two cancelled client relationships: after his team lost the clients, he says he took responsibility for the fact that the business is his problem, then reached out, apologized, and thanked the clients for complaining. The word appears 7 times, clustered around the three things he says he did — reach out and apologize, thank them for complaining, and work on the system so it does not happen again — with neighbors like "took", "my problem", and "three things". In his use it is not a vague value but a sequence: he first gets over himself, then owns the failure as his, then contacts the clients himself instead of defending the team. The episode closes by naming responsibility, with an open heart, as the attitude that makes almost every situation savable.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a personality trait: here responsibility is a set of actions after a specific failure — reaching out, apologizing, thanking the complainers — not a general claim that Stuckey never makes mistakes or that his team is blameless.
- Read as an apology alone: the apology is only the second of three things he lists; without the system fix he promises, the word would not carry the meaning it has in this episode.
