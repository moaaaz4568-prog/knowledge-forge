# G_yt_T_hLBCuNiy0_en — time-saving tip

## Term

In this Illustrator video, a time-saving tip is one of the top tips for working faster in Illustrator, and the phrase appears 2 times in the source. The speaker's list covers the tools panel: some tools hide behind a click you have to hold down, but clicking the tear off tab opens the specific tools in a separate window so they stay incredibly accessible. Another advance on the same tip is to memorize the keyboard shortcuts — he changes the pen to its variations with the keyboard alone — and the list also covers improving a selection, removing unwanted areas, and navigating without the slide bars. In use, the tip is a small change to how the tools are reached, not a new tool: the time saved is the seconds of hunting through panels on every move.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a new Illustrator feature: the tips here are rearrangements of the existing tools — a tear-off tab, a memorized shortcut, a cleaner selection — so expecting a new tool misses that the saving comes from reaching the old ones faster.
- Read as a shortcut for beginners only: the speaker's list assumes real Illustrator work — grouped shapes, selections, the pen tool — so the tips save time inside a busy workflow, not in place of learning the tools.
