# G_yt_TcP4lOv7jS0_en — super car

## Term

In this car video, a super car is the Porsche GT3 the speaker bought after years of work, and the phrase appears 5 times in the source. He generated almost over $8 million by building a thing and releasing it into his company before he was able to get a super car, and the lesson he draws is to work on yourself before you start buying stuff like this — if you make your first million and buy this kind of car, you are stupid. The car itself is a driver's car, not a super car meant only to get attention and noise: in normal drive it will not make any sound, but in track mode it does. In use, the super car is a sign of what it means to chase your dream and go after something you do not know if you will achieve, which is what the reveal is meant to show.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a purchase to show off: the speaker's lesson is the opposite — work on yourself first, and buying this kind of car with your first million is stupid — so the car here is a reward after the work, not the work itself.
- Read as a car for attention: he calls it a driver's car, quiet in normal drive and loud only in track mode, so judging it by the noise it makes mistakes what the car is for.
