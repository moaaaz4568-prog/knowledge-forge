# G_yt_Svn9DvP7XwM_en — PMs

## Term

Here PMs are the project managers whose impact on an agency's bottom line the episode argues is bigger than owners realize: they are often thought to just deliver client projects on time, on scope, and on budget, but thinking that small means missing what they can do. The abbreviation appears 42 times, beside "impact", "job", "develop", and "understand", and the full form "project managers" appears 9 times for the same role. In the guests' use the PM's job is wider than delivery: they keep the team on its feet, create the plans, manage the risks and the budget, and — with AI and tooling — get a path upward instead of a path back into the cave of admin work. The episode's promise is practical: how to increase a PM's impact on profitability, how to assess PM talent in an AI-driven world, and how to level them up from delivering work to growing the agency.
