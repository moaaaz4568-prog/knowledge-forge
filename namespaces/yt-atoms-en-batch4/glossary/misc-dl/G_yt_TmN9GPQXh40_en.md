# G_yt_TmN9GPQXh40_en — sales appointment

## Term

In this video, the sales appointment is where the speaker's diagnostic selling belongs, and mixing it up with marketing is the mistake: the phrase appears 4 times in the source. She was taught that in a sales appointment or on the phone with the sales prospect, you sell coaching by asking diagnostic questions, probing around to find a problem, while marketing should declare the problem instead of asking. The concrete warning is that when you market the way you sell, the sales appointment fills with objections, excuses, and stalls, with people saying let me think about it. In use, the sales appointment is the asking room, and marketing is the declaring room, and crossing them breaks both.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a marketing script: the speaker's rule is to ask in the appointment and declare in marketing, so opening a sales appointment with declared answers turns it into a pitch that earns objections.
- Read as a license to interrogate: diagnostic questions here probe for a real problem to find, so a long list of questions with no listening turns the appointment into a stall.
