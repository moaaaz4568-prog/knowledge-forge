# G_yt_TZhljgIbp4U_en — digital course

## Term

In this video, a digital course is the educational product the speaker builds and sells from his own experience, and the phrase appears 3 times in the source. He asks whether investing your time to create a digital course is still the smart idea people described during the Corona days — the solution to get income while you sleep — and answers from two years of creating courses, having sold four of them and consulted with a huge number of Arab trainers. His method puts marketing before the product: spread awareness of the problem first, then present the solution in the form of a digital course, with the pre-order as a fourth step that works as an advertising campaign. In use, the course is a product you only launch when people are already waiting for it — he warns not to launch a product if no one is waiting for it — rather than a website you build and fill and hope sells.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as passive income while you sleep: the speaker questions exactly that Corona-days promise and answers with two years of work, four courses sold, and marketing before the product, so the income here is earned by building and selling, not by sleeping.
- Read as a website full of courses: the speaker asks whether building a website and putting courses on it is still smart, and his answer is that the course comes after the audience and the marketing, not before them.
