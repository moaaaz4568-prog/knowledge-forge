# G_yt_SrVYYIxuwdI_en — AI clone

## Term

Here an AI clone is a custom GPT built from a creator's own course: the video shows how to create your own AI clone, with prompts included, so that buyers get speed, engagement, customization, and the ability to ask follow-up questions instead of pure information. The phrase appears 4 times, beside "create", "prompts", "why", and "Tony Robbins", in a video that says the big guys are all creating their AI clones, and gives the viewer the prompts to build one without needing to be an AI expert. In the speaker's use the clone is worth more than the course it comes from: if a course takes 60 minutes and the clone takes five, the same material is 12 times as valuable, because the buyer gets exactly what they need. The plan he leaves the viewer with is straight: prompts to build the clone, a hot-to-cold selling strategy, and a membership built from the clones.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a copy of the creator: here the clone is a GPT that answers from the creator's course material — it delivers the same knowledge faster, not a replica of the person, and the entry records the product, not the personality.
- Read as a course replacement: the speaker sells the clone as more valuable than the course because it is faster and customized, but the clone is built from the course, so the entry records it as a new format for the same material, not a different subject.
