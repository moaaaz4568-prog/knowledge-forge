# G_yt_TLGm-IiXeFQ_en — outreach

## Term

Here outreach is how Dan's agency builds relationships before anyone asks for help: you really can start to be building these relationships proactively through Outreach, and that's what we're typically helping clients do. The word appears 14 times, beside "cold", "warm", "focus", and "inbound", in an Agency Bytes episode about an agency that went from doing outreach for clients to a training and implementation model. In the speaker's use outreach is the proactive half of growth: most agencies that have been around for a few years have tons of experience and can really help prospects, especially if they're either specialized or getting in the process of getting specialized — and with the idea that you're going after a market that is infinite, you can start building these relationships proactively. His concrete proof is the work upfront: it's worth it because that's what 99% of people aren't willing to do, and then that's why Outreach doesn't work for them — while it's work upfront, it's not work the clients are doing over and over, and once the system is built you can plug in and get up to a handful of meetings a week pretty fast.
