# G_yt_TIeZQoc3lQ4_en — eyedropper tool

## Term

Here the eyedropper tool is the Illustrator shortcut for matching text, not just color: this next Adobe Illustrator tip for saving time relates to the eyedropper tool, which you can locate by pressing I on your keyboard. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "which" and "it's", in a video of four tips to save time in your graphic design workflow. In the speaker's use the eyedropper does more than change the color of an object: by changing one line of text with different fonts he can then quickly change other text layers with the eyedropper tool — but it's not only going to match the font, it would also match the font size, the kerning values, and so forth. His concrete companions are the other tips: take the artboard tool, hold down the alt option key, and duplicate the board to save each design as an individual JPEG or PNG, then drag each design into the asset export panel — and with upwards of 20 designs this would save you a lot of time, so the tips get stamped into your memory.
