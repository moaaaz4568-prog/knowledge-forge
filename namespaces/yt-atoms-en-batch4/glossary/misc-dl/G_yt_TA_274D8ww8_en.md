## Term

Here psychological is how the speaker describes the design techniques that make a design work on the viewer's mind: psychology is fundamental in graphic design, and the video uses several psychological design techniques to vastly improve a design. The word appears 12 times, beside "use", "design", "techniques", and "point", in a video that reworks a fashion design and a ramen design with the same tools. In his use a psychological technique is a lever on the audience: people who are potential targets for a fashion design can be influenced by the psychological technique of fomo — fear of missing out — so text that states fashion trends and a new era suggests subconsciously that choosing not to buy means missing out. His concrete moves are the eye's journey: a cleaner, more minimal layout so the viewer interacts with ease, a red graphic in the middle as a focal point that lures the audience in, and one part of the text made bold and bright pink so it stands out and makes the audience intrigued enough to fomo into becoming part of the community.
