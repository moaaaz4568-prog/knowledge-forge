# G_yt_Tm25IxJQPWM_en — hierarchy

## Term

In this video, hierarchy is what makes a graphic design readable, the structure that tells the eye what matters first: the word appears 13 times in the source. The speaker says he is always talking about hierarchy on his channel and how important it is to add into graphic designs, because it adds structure and alludes to the important parts of the design, with typography, layouts, and color scheme as the places it shows up. His concrete complaint is a design whose first flaw is the lack of hierarchy in the typography itself, where nothing guides the reader to the top line of the business. In use, hierarchy is the deliberate ordering of a design's elements, not decoration added at the end.
