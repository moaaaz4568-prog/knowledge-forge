# G_yt_Tj35JuOj55U_en — blend

## Term

In this video, to blend is what the speaker and his partner do with life, family, and business instead of slicing them into separate boxes: the word appears 8 times in the source. He rejects the idea that life is a perfectly sliced up little box with family here and business there, saying they blend them together as much as they can, with the kids present while they work and work happening in the evenings while nomading around the world. His concrete rule is that if you are going to blend, blend on purpose, while still keeping times when they are 100% on and times when they are 100% off. In use, blend names a deliberate mixing of work and family, not a failure to separate them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as having no boundaries: the speaker insists on times when they are 100% on and 100% off, so blending without deliberate off-time turns a chosen mix into constant work.
- Read as a family excuse: the mistake he names is blaming family for a stalled business, so using blend to justify not growing the business inverts his point.
