# G_yt_SwwicVgCbtE_en — tier

## Term

Here a tier is one level of the speaker's legendary tier list, the ranking he uses to sort content advice from worst to best: at the very bottom sits the who Let Him Cook tier for the worst advice you will ever receive, and at the top sits the goated tier, where the advice is basically undefeated. The word appears 28 times, beside "list", "goated", "cook", and "advice", and its plural "tiers" appears too. In his use a tier is a verdict on one piece of advice, not a score: posting five times per day or more goes into the bottom of the barrel tier, because the algorithm feeds your followers first and you end up spamming the same audience until they unfollow. The nuance he keeps repeating is that a tier is a starting point — copying an outlier, for example, can only get you so far, and a heartfelt post or a basic 7-second video needs the context the tier list alone does not give.

## Synonyms

- tiers (the plural form, used for more than one level of the list)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a score: here a tier is a judgment on one piece of advice in one list — the entry records his ranking, not a measured result for every creator.
- Read as a rule to copy the top tier: the speaker says the advice in the goated tier is undefeated, but he also warns that copying an outlier only gets you so far, so the tier does not replace the skill of making the video.
