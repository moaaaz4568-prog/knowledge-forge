# G_yt_Ta5hywdHPWU_en — leading

## Term

In this before-and-after typography video, leading is the spacing between each line of body text, and the word appears 3 times in the source. The first design's main issue is that the leading on the body text is too tightly packed together, and the speaker says leading is very important to get right so the viewer can read the text clearly and the design as a whole does not look cramped or messy. He also warns the other way: having leading that is too wide apart can also be an issue. In use, leading is judged by the eye — the viewer feels something is uncomfortable before naming it — and the fix is a spacing change, not a font change, which is why the video asks what you would change in terms of the typography.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the font size: leading is the space between the lines, not the size of the letters, so enlarging the text without opening the leading leaves the same cramped block.
- Read as a fixed number: the speaker's test is whether the viewer can read clearly and the design looks uncramped, so a leading value copied from another design can still be too tight or too wide here.
