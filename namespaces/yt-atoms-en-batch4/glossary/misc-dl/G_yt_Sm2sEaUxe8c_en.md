# G_yt_Sm2sEaUxe8c_en — geek out

## Term

Here a geek out is the live session of the speaker's new community, Strategy Geeks: every couple of weeks the members jump on Zoom together, and each of the 15 people on a call shares something that is working for them, a tip, or a discovery about online business tools, systems, strategies, or AI. The phrase appears 4 times, beside "where", "outs", "session", and "official", and the speaker defines it in the same breath — a geek out is where we all jump on Zoom together. In her use the session is the point of the community: members learn from each other what they are doing in their businesses, in specific use cases, instead of watching generic YouTube advice. The classroom already holds material to geek out on ahead of the official geek out session, so the term covers both the live call and the shared habit of digging into the tools together.
