# G_yt_TadB4KziN38_en — brand identity

## Term

In this agency vlog, a brand identity is the priced project the new agency signs for a client, and the phrase appears 5 times in the source. In the first month the agency has a £2,000 brand identity at a discounted rate for duude brand volatile and has just signed a £4,500 brand identity for Save Our Species Alliance, with a third client signed as well. The work around the identities includes presenting two directions to Jude, making four versions of variant 3 for SAA's merchandise designs, and building brand guidelines on Framer for Non-Stop. In use, the brand identity is the job the agency sells — the thing a client pays for — while the guidelines, the onboarding, and the Notion template are how the agency delivers and runs it.
