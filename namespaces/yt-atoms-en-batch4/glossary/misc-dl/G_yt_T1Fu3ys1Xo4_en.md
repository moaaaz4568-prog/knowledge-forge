# G_yt_T1Fu3ys1Xo4_en — sell by chat

## Term

Here Sell By Chat is the script the workshop teaches: how to turn somebody who is a lead, a follower, somebody you can engage in a conversation, into either booking a call or selling them something. The phrase appears 5 times, beside "script", "scripts", "training", and "phase", in a workshop that was previously only available inside a twenty-thousand-dollar-a-year mastermind. In the speaker's use the script is not a gimmick: the speaker has gone through many Sell By Chat scripts, and the effective ones had in common that they all kind of died when they depended on some kind of gimmick and people caught that. The concrete rules are the frame around it: always have a cap and a deadline, because then you can use those to have true follow-ups, and do outbound in a high status way so it will not be a pain.
