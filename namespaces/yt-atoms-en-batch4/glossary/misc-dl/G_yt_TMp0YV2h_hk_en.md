# G_yt_TMp0YV2h_hk_en — wheelhouse

## Term

Here the wheelhouse is the two things Kirsten is actually good at: my wheelhouse is strategy and project management — so I set everything up so that I outsource the execution part. The word appears 1 time, beside "strategy", in an Agency Bytes episode with Kirsten Russ, the founder of Principal Edge AI and Mountains to Sea Media. In her use the wheelhouse is the reason for the outsource model: one, she doesn't have to carry a big payroll and still has expertise, and two, she doesn't think the marketing space is so noisy that anybody can be really good at more than one or two things. Her concrete setup is the structure around it: it's the infrastructure and the structure and the things that you have in place, because a lot of the tools do the same things with just a different name — and the newsletter she cites says the marketing industry is no longer hiring junior people to do work that wasn't really valuable stuff anyway.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a skill: here the wheelhouse is why the execution gets outsourced — the entry records the outsourcing, not the talent.
- Read as a niche: the speaker's wheelhouse is two things only, so the entry records the limit, not the brand.
