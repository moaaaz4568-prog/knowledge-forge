# G_yt_T16dNeRfCJ0_en — energy

## Term

Here energy is the resource the speaker protects from pendulums: everything that pulls you away from your goals is noise trying to steal your energy, and the net effect of giving it your attention is feeling your energy drained, weaker, and more tired. The word appears 10 times, beside "steal", "drained", "cup", and "keep", in a video that calls the distractions pendulums and links the same idea to stoicism. In his use energy sits in a little cup: the more energy you keep within your cup, the more you can expend toward getting closer to your goals, so the work is to keep the cup full by refusing the noise — news, media, social noise. His concrete frame is the identity shift behind it: he went from a drug addict partying every day to an entrepreneur scaling an agency from $0 to over 10k a month, and the shift is what lets him treat attention as energy to guard.
