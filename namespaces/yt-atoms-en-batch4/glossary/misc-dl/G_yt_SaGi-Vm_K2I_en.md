# G_yt_SaGi-Vm_K2I_en — domains

## Term

Here domains are the sender domains Matt Lucero generates in bulk for cold email outreach: the video shows an AI prompt that produces tens, hundreds, or even thousands of them at a time, so he can send from many lookalike domains instead of coming up with each idea and checking it by hand. The word appears 10 times, with neighbors like "thousands", "lookalike", and "lists", and its singular "domain" appears 4 times for a single sender address. In his use a domain is not a website address in general but a disposable sending identity: he owns thousands of them, sets them up, and uses them as lookalike domains for outreach. The prompt's job is to name 50 lookalike domains for a given brand, for example google.com, so the outreach can run from addresses that resemble the real one.

## Synonyms

- domain (the singular form, used when he talks about one sender address at a time)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as website domains in general: here domains are sending identities for cold outreach, not sites he publishes on — the point is volume and resemblance, not hosting content.
- Read as a promise that generated domains work: the prompt only produces candidate names; he still has to check whether each one exists and set it up before it can send anything.
