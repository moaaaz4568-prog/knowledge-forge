# G_yt_T9unGwxFDc8_en — webinar

## Term

Here a webinar is the funnel Shelby Sap's coaching business runs on: a masterclass funnel, which is basically a slightly nicer name for a webinar funnel, where people secure their spot, sign up, and turn up. The word appears 23 times, beside "funnel", "call", "signup", and "page", and its plural "webinars" appears too. In the speaker's use the webinar is the conversion step after the content: the content strategy attracts people into her ecosystem and educates them to the point of desire, and when people buy the mid ticket offer on the webinar they are redirected to book a group Q&A call with Shelby. The concrete breakdown is the page itself: built on ClickFunnels, a webinar signup page with a countdown timer, a CTA in the top right and in the hero section, a section saying who it is for and who it is not for, and a meet the speaker section — because when people affirm this identity, they are more likely to sign up and turn up.
