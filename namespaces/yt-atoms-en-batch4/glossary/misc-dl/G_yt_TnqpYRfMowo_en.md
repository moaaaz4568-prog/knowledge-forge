# G_yt_TnqpYRfMowo_en — belief systems

## Term

In this video, belief systems are the inherited stories that decide what a person thinks they can become: the phrase appears 8 times in the source, with the singular belief system appearing once. The speaker lists them beside self-perception and habits, saying belief systems are an amalgamation of all your experiences, and that a teacher calling you a bad student makes you confirm that identity because you have been told it. His concrete case is the fat person who keeps confirming the identity, and the counter-move is a conscious decision to change when you do not like who you are. In use, belief systems are the quiet scripts that run underneath habits, and changing them starts with deciding they are not your fault.
