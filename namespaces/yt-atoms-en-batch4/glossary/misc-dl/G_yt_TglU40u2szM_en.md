# G_yt_TglU40u2szM_en — visibility problem

## Term

In this video, a visibility problem is what a business owner has when more marketing will not save the business, and the phrase appears 1 time in the source. The speaker tells the owner thinking "I just need better marketing" that it is not a marketing problem but a visibility problem: "You're invisible", and that is why the business is not scaling past six figures. His own proof is a brand built to over 2.1 million followers across social media and a seven-figure business behind it, using social views and content marketing instead of more spending on CRO, email campaigns, Facebook ads, and Google ads. In use, the visibility problem is solved by being seen — genuine content that lets the target avatar find the page, consume the content, and want to purchase — rather than by buying more marketing.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a marketing problem: the speaker's whole point is that the owner does not have a marketing problem but a visibility problem, so buying more ads, CRO, or email campaigns spends money on the wrong diagnosis.
- Read as a follower count: the speaker's proof is a brand with 2.1 million followers and a seven-figure business, but the visibility here is the target avatar finding the page and wanting to purchase, not the number on the profile.
