# G_yt_Syt98tTKswY_en — swatches

## Term

Here swatches are the Illustrator color swatches the tutorial teaches in a real-life workflow: the speaker designs a retro poster and shows why Illustrator swatches are so useful and so powerful, saving a whole lot of time. The word appears 12 times, beside "why", "illustrator", "use", and "window", in a video that starts by downloading a color scheme of three or four colors and making swatches from it inside Adobe Illustrator. In his use the swatch is what makes the workflow fast: without the swatches he would have to copy the hex color codes or do some other long work, while with them he can add a new stroke in the appearance panel and apply the colors in one move. The concrete setup is the swatches window in the top right fly-down menu, opened before the design starts, with the circle set to a fill first and then flipped to a stroke with shift and X.
