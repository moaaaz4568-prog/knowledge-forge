# G_yt_SaXTst1nloE_en — editor's game

## Term

Here the editor's game is the way FedEx Fearless says most video editors work: charging money per video, chasing views, and competing on freelance platforms, a model he calls a doomed-to-fail business model. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "playing" and "what", in the sentence where he tells editors he knows what game they are playing. In his use the game is the trap itself: editors in it have no incentive to do better work, because they are paid per video rather than for results, and clients judge each video instead of the outcome. The way out he offers is to package the work differently — to stop selling videos and start acting as a director, a media director or an acquisition director who brings clients to the company.

## Live Example

> "If you're an editor right now and you're playing the editor's game, I know what the editor's game is." — sources/S_yt_SaXTst1nloE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a game editors enjoy: here the game is a losing business model, not entertainment — the speaker's point is that playing it leaves editors underpaid per project with no incentive to improve.
- Read as advice to charge more per video: the fix he offers is not a higher per-video rate but a different role — packaging the work so clients stop asking what they get from each video.
