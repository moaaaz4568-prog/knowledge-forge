# G_yt_SeyQ2ZpAxEI_en — gradient

## Term

Here a gradient is the color blend the Satori Graphics tutorial uses to build a sunset sky over a hometown image: the speaker adds a subtle gradient for a sunset vibe, then changes its direction with the gradient tool instead of typing degrees into the gradient window. The word appears 12 times, beside "subtle", "tool", "direction", and "window", and its plural "gradients" appears as well. In his use the gradient is a practical choice, not a theory: the color scheme is up to the viewer, but the gradient gives the sky its sunset feel, and the tool saves time because the design's shape makes typing degrees awkward. The tutorial also warns to leave excess gradient at the bottom, because the extra area makes sense once the shape is cut.

## Synonyms

- gradients (the plural form, used for more than one blend in the design)

## Live Example

> "adding a subtle gradient is always a good idea but you can go with whatever you want and whatever your creativity dictates" — sources/S_yt_SeyQ2ZpAxEI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any color fill: here the gradient is a directional blend that sets the sunset mood — a flat color in the same place would not do the work the tutorial assigns to it.
- Read as a fixed recipe: the speaker says the color scheme is entirely up to you, so the gradient's colors are a choice, while the technique — tool over typed degrees — is the part he recommends.
