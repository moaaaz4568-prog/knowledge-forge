# G_yt_TXV013ZY03Y_en — voice agents

## Term

In this case study, voice agents are the AI phone agents the agency sells to businesses alongside chat bots, and the phrase appears 2 times in the source. Two sellers had been making around $3,000 a month selling voice agents and chat bots while getting only one sales call per week at a close rate of around 10%, which the speaker presents as too little volume to grow on. The same agents reappear as the AI voice agents that give client businesses setters, placed next to content because many of these businesses cannot generate high quality content themselves. In use, a voice agent answers and books for a business such as Kylie's Med Spa rather than replacing the offer, the playbook, or the sales asset that the agency builds around it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole business: the agents are one product inside a larger launch — the clear offer, the lead playbook, and the video sales letter around them — so selling the agent alone does not reproduce the $3,000-a-month result.
- Read as human setters on the phone: these are AI agents that plug into a business to take calls and book, so expecting a person at the other end mistakes what the client is buying.
