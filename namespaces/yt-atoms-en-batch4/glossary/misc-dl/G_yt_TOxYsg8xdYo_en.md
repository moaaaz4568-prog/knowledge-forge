# G_yt_TOxYsg8xdYo_en — Law of Contraction

## Term

Here the Law of Contraction is the second law of the branding book the video leans on: the Law of Contraction, which states that the more a brand contracts — meaning imitates its activities, fields, products, and services — the stronger the brand becomes. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "book", "which", "states", and "follow", in a video about Nike's expansion and the universal laws of branding. In the speaker's use the law is the fix: Nike tried to defy the universal laws of branding by capitalizing on existing trends — Nikeland in the metaverse, cryptocurrency and blockchain projects — and because of this expansion, somewhat diverted from their core purpose, they paid the price of around $30 billion in two years. His concrete rule for the viewer is the same: if you want to expand your project of any kind, in any sector, what you need to do is, just like Nike, you need to follow this Law of Contraction — otherwise there's no contraction of the brand, and the market favors the strongest brand, not the oldest or most established one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as shrinking: here the Law of Contraction makes the brand stronger — the entry records the strength, not the size.
- Read as a rule: the speaker's Nike paid $30 billion for expanding, so the entry records the price, not the law.
