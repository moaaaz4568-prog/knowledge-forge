# G_yt_TN0S_4qaAhc_en — revenue team

## Term

Here the revenue team is marketing and sales together, seen as one team apart from the rest of the business: Every founder actually looks at like the revenue team, marketing and sales completely separate from the other business. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "marketing", in a long Good Business episode about founders whose growth has stalled. In the speakers' use the revenue team is the place where the problem shows: marketing blames sales, sales blames marketing, and they don't have any way to communicate whether or not they're being successful — so you just have a problem like ping pong that you're going back and forth on, and nothing really ever gets fully resolved. Their concrete history is the old deal: back when you could code a website in Notepad and upload it via FTP and charge 300 bucks for it, and later the broccoli-haired dude in Boca Raton serving the info market industry who wants 50% of your revenue for putting up a funnel — and the gift beside it is what the founders pass on to their older daughter and their brand new baby.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a department: here the revenue team is marketing and sales seen together — the entry records the togetherness, not the org chart.
- Read as a metric: the speaker's revenue team is where the blame goes back and forth, so the entry records the back-and-forth, not the number.
