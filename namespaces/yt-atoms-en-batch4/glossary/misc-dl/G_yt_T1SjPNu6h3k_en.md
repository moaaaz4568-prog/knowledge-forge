# G_yt_T1SjPNu6h3k_en — icon

## Term

Here an icon is the single mark you choose for a set, not a decoration you grab at random: the speaker is working on t-shirt designs for a potential brutalist style graphic design t-shirt brand, and the icons have to cohere across the set. The word appears 5 times, beside "dodgy", "packs", "resource", and "cool", and its plural "icons" appears too. In his use the icon is found through a resource — Noun Project, the most extensive and well-created icon resource he found on the web, with millions of high-quality icons in SVG and PNG formats — and the key is not to grab the first cool looking icon you see. His concrete rule is cohesion: when you are creating a set or a system like the t-shirt design, you pay attention to stroke weight, corner radius, shape, and psychology, because those subtle visual cues make the whole thing feel intentional.
