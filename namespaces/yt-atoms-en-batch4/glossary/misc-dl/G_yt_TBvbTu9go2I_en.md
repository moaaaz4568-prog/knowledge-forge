# G_yt_TBvbTu9go2I_en — community

## Term

Here a community is what the speaker says makes his program valuable: it's not necessarily the acquisition, it's not the appointment setters, it's not the offers — it's really about who you actually get to meet and the relationships you'll get to create from it. The word appears 13 times, beside "it's", "makes", "valuable", and "who", in a long community call where new people introduce themselves and share what they do. In his use the community is the combined knowledge in the room: he calls himself the least important person in this community, because the combined knowledge and intelligence and processes that everyone has, if you combine them, is what really makes it valuable. His concrete test is the stuck member: however far along your business is, someone else has dealt with the same thing you're currently dealing with, so if you just stay stuck in your own little world you miss the one thing the community is for — and the warning beside it is high growth, which creates issues because the only person winning is you, not your customers.
