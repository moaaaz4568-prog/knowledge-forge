# G_yt_TTPIpNyF76k_en — weekly rhythm

## Term

Here the weekly rhythm is the once-a-week meeting that looks six weeks out: a second piece of this is we're going to start up two rhythms, our weekly and a daily and weekly rhythm — the day of the weekly rhythm is a once a week meeting with you, Anna V. The phrase appears 3 times, beside "daily", "day", "weekly", and "team", in a video about managing a VA. In the speaker's use the weekly rhythm is the look-ahead: by each week forcing you to look six weeks out, you know what's coming up and you can kind of adjust — and you want to start by collecting the wins from last week, because you're praising progress, not perfection, and it's easy to get momentum when you look at what's been working. His concrete pair is the daily rhythm: a daily huddle, a quick check-in, a stand-up meeting — if you've got one VA it's probably five minutes, it might be 10 — and thirdly the VA needs tasks, daily tasks, weekly tasks, tasks and monthly, routine tasks, not just odd jobs.
