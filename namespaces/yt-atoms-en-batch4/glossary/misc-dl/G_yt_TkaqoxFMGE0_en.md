# G_yt_TkaqoxFMGE0_en — moments

## Term

In this video, moments are the uneven bursts in which a creator's growth actually arrives: the word appears 14 times in the source. The speaker says growth comes in step changes, with moments of things going right followed by long periods of not much happening or even going backwards, and the singular moment appears 11 times for the same idea. His concrete case is a student going through vulnerable moments of second-guessing everything, which he reads as a bridge, an opportunity to pass over something where most people will not. In use, moments are the testing points where patience and daily posting either compound or collapse.

## Synonyms

- moment (the singular form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "You experience these moments of things going right for you, you experience a period of growth and that's followed by usually a long period of not much happening and sometimes even going backwards." — sources/S_yt_TkaqoxFMGE0_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of steady growth: the speaker's whole point is that growth arrives in step changes with long flat periods, so expecting a straight line up makes the flat stretches feel like failure.
- Read as a reason to quit in a flat stretch: the vulnerable moments he describes are the bridge most people will not cross, so stopping there forfeits the compounding that shows up months later.
