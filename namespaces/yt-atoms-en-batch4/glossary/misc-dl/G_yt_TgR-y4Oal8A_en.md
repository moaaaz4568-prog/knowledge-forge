# G_yt_TgR-y4Oal8A_en — Astra 6

## Term

In this video, Astra 6 is ChatGPT's new model, and the phrase appears 7 times in the source. The speaker tests it for one thing only: is it really suitable for content creation, the work he does every day for his business and his clients' businesses — and the results really impressed him. By the end of the video the viewer has a ready-made system using Astra 6 to create content from scratch to a full video script, starting with no ideas and not even knowing what to talk about, down to seven ready-to-record scripts. The system needs a pro level account, because Astra 6 uses up a lot of credits, and when Astra conducts research it analyzes a person's comments and followers to understand exactly what type of target audience is engaging with that content. In use, Astra 6 is the research and scripting engine inside the system, not the whole system: the prompts, the knowledge base, and the Notion setup around it are what turn the model into videos.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a model that writes the videos by itself: here Astra 6 is one part of a system — prompts, a knowledge base, Notion, and a pro account — so opening the model without the system does not reproduce the seven ready-to-record scripts.
- Read as a free tool: the speaker is explicit that a plus account is not enough and Astra 6 burns a lot of credits, so the system assumes a pro account and the cost that comes with it.
