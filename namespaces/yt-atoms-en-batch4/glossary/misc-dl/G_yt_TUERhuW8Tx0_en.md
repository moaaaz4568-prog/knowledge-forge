# G_yt_TUERhuW8Tx0_en — retainers

## Term

Here retainers are how the big brands pay — and how they don't: some of the biggest brands in the world have huge retainers, but they also try before they buy for short of time. The word appears 5 times, beside "huge", "dod", "far", and "how", in an episode with a UK performance agency crowned agency of the year. In the speaker's use the retainer is the opposite of the performance model: large brands never do a performance model, they're just retainer — and the question is how you structure it, retainer or performance model, against the standard seven or eight bigger DOD of retainers. His concrete warning is the promise: under promising and over delivering brings it back to the client, because if you're selling something you know even slightly is going to be off from what you can deliver, you can't make it — and the same logic runs his hiring, where the quality of the people you hire is the quality of the service you provide.
