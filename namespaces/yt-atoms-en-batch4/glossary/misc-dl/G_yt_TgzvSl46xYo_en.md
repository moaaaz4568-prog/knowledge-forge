# G_yt_TgzvSl46xYo_en — first dollar

## Term

In this video, the first dollar is the first dollar a creator earns online, and the speaker treats it as a turning point rather than an amount: the phrase appears 4 times in the source. He says the first dollar he earned online changed a lot for him personally, and that the first check from YouTube showed him what life options were possible, with monetization, watch hours, and the AdSense threshold framing the stakes. His concrete point is that when creators start earning their first dollar online, it is not about the amount but what it opens up for them, and losing that income makes their dreams crumble. In use, the first dollar marks the moment a creator sees YouTube as a livelihood, not a hobby.
