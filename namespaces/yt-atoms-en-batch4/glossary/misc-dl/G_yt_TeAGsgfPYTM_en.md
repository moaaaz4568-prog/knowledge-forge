# G_yt_TeAGsgfPYTM_en — specialization

## Term

In this agency conversation, specialization is the guest's table stakes for agencies in 2025, and the word appears 15 times in the source. The host asks how business development has changed and what the table stakes are now — things that might have only been nice to haves a couple of years ago — and the guest's answer is that specialization is important and necessary, even though larger independent agencies take a firm position that they refuse to specialize. His evidence is the market: Stanford graduates in Silicon Valley start the next billion-dollar business based on specialization, thinking about TAM and annualized revenue per user, while a small TAM that fits into a high school gymnasium means a fundamentally different game. In use, specialization is the focus that keeps an agency from going in a million different directions — an internet connection and a service are a blessing and a curse without it — and the risks of non-specialization over time are greater than the risks of choosing.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as picking any niche: here specialization is the table stakes that shape the whole business — TAM, pricing, and the sales game — so a niche chosen only for the name does not make the agency specialized.
- Read as turning down work: the guest's point is focus, not refusal — the risk is the lack of focus of non-specialization over time — so an agency that specializes still takes the work that fits the focus.
