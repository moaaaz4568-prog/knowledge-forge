# G_yt_TiPRw-cCt5w_en — Facebook advertising

## Term

In this video, Facebook advertising is the service the agency sells to contractors, and the speaker teaches how to sell it by getting the prospect to admit the problem first: the phrase appears 5 times in the source. He gives the example of a contractor who worked with a Facebook advertising agency that took about two leads a month and turned one into a sale, and the shorter form Facebook ads appears 3 times for the same service. His concrete move is to show the prospect why Facebook advertising did not work for them before, so that when he explains why it will work now, the objections disappear. In use, Facebook advertising is not pitched as a product but as the solution to a problem the prospect has already admitted.

## Synonyms

- Facebook ads (the shorter form the speaker uses for the same service in this source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a product pitch: the speaker's method is to get the contractor to admit the problem first, so leading with Facebook advertising as a product invites the same objections the old agency already earned.
- Read as a promise of leads: the contractor's old agency took two leads a month and converted one, so the service here is sold on solving the attraction problem, not on a guaranteed lead count.
