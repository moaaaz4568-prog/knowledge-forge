# G_yt_TZKM9J70KHg_en — inflation

## Term

In this economics video, inflation is the one number the speaker says is inaccurate, and the word appears 65 times in the source. Surface level nominal GDP looks great and real GDP looks decent only because the inflation calculation is wrong: the government uses owners equivalent rent and survey data versus realtime data, so inflation numbers are structurally understated. The speaker says the understatement may be cynical — keeping Social Security down and tax brackets indexed to inflation — and links the same era of zero interest rates and negative real rates to the YOLO mentality. In use, inflation here is not a general price rise but a mismeasured official number that makes the economy look healthier than it is, which is why the speaker calls GDP growth a poor measure of happiness.
