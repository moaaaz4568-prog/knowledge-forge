# G_yt_SbBvwMRnEOs_en — entrepreneurship

## Term

Here entrepreneurship is the life the speaker's student imagined when he started: freedom, buying whatever he wants, making money, working whenever he wants — a picture the speaker says falsifies the reality of what it actually is. The word appears 4 times, beside "reality", "talking", and "getting", in a rant about what entrepreneurship really is, prompted by a student two years into his business who felt like a failure for missing his revenue milestones. In his use the word names the gap between the story people share and the lived grind: the student compares himself to others, feels behind, and hears that most people in the space are not making the money they claim. The speaker's correction is that the decision to become an entrepreneur is not a transition into freedom but into years of work where milestones take longer than planned.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of freedom: here entrepreneurship is the grind the speaker defends against the fantasy — buying whatever you want and working whenever you want is the false picture, not the definition.
- Read as a verdict on the student: the speaker uses the word to reframe one student's two-year mark, not to say every entrepreneur fails — the entry records his rant to that student, not a rule about all businesses.
