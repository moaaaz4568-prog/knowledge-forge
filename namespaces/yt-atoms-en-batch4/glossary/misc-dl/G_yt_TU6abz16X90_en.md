# G_yt_TU6abz16X90_en — franchise

## Term

Here the franchise is the business the speaker bought into and then copied: Yes, they are all the same franchise — and you know how the franchise works, you know, people can meet Midas, there are a thousand branches across the country. The word appears 46 times, beside "agreement", "what", "how", and "midas", in a long episode about a family business that became a Midas franchise in the 70s. In his use the franchise is a job you buy: People who join franchises are basically buying a job — those who earn a ridiculous amount of money fly in private jets that nobody ever hears about — and a great franchise is one you should be able to copy and paste. His concrete math is the stores: making about $5 million between five or six stores, each franchisee independently owning and running the store, and when you become a franchisor you transfer the capital requirements to the franchisees — because people not having enough capital is a very important factor when starting a business.
