# G_yt_TN4FqNjgZW0_en — dynamic duo rule

## Term

Here the dynamic duo rule is the first of two skills the video teaches: a tried and a tested method, a rule where it can actually be applied in many different ways on your graphic designs — and it is called the dynamic duo rule. The phrase appears 6 times, beside "called", "applied", "doesn't", and "missed", in a video that promises the way you design stuff will never be the same ever again. In the speaker's use the dynamic duo rule is about having two contrasting elements that work incredibly well together: in combination they help push the goal or the message of your design, and if you've ever designed a logo you might have totally missed it when working on your design. His concrete example is the grayscale trick: simply by introducing grayscale elements, visual hierarchy can be enhanced in a huge huge way — by muting one part of the composition you can draw more attention to the colored elements — and the second skill beside it is narrative-driven hierarchy, where storytelling and hierarchy together guide the viewer's journey through the design.
