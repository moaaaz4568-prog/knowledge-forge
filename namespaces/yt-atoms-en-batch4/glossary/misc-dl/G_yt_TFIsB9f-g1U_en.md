# G_yt_TFIsB9f-g1U_en — properties panel

## Term

Here the properties panel is the Illustrator panel the video calls one of the most important golden nuggets in it: when you have a shape or an object selected, the panel options actually change, so you can transform the object with scale, rotation, and more, right there. The phrase appears 4 times, beside "changes", "single", "that's", and "pretty", in a video about becoming faster, more efficient, and more organized in Illustrator. In the speaker's use the properties panel is the single window that gathers the tools: when text is selected it changes again, and you have access to so many options, functions, and tools built into Illustrator within this single properties panel — including the artboard options and the name of the artboard. His concrete companions are the other optimizations: select the objects and start the global edits, so resizing or rotating applies the same process throughout the entire design, and utilize the layers panel, where the layers that make up a painting on the wall stay separate while you work.
