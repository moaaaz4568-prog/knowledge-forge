# G_yt_T5Eor95qhXw_en — solution

## Term

Here a solution is the level of the market pyramid the speaker is describing: at the top you have people who are solution aware — they know exactly what the problem is, they know exactly how to solve it, and they know the different tactics in place that they need. The word appears 10 times, beside "aware", "who", and "what", in a video that walks the pyramid down from the top. In his use the solution is what the prospect understands at different depths: lower down are people who understand the problem, and people who understand their own solution but don't understand the outcome they want to achieve, rather than the solution itself. His concrete lesson is the cologne seller at the mall: the guy's pitch is "you have to try this cologne, it's the best cologne," and the speaker just walks past, because the pitch talks about the product instead of putting himself in the prospect's shoes and asking what the prospect actually wants at the end of the day.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the product: here the solution is the prospect's level of understanding — aware, problem-aware, outcome-blind — so the entry records the pyramid, not the pitch.
- Read as a feature list: the speaker's point is that the prospect doesn't care about low CPMs or a retargeting strategy, so the entry records what the prospect wants, not what you sell.
