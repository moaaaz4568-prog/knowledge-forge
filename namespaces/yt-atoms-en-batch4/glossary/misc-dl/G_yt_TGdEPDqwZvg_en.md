# G_yt_TGdEPDqwZvg_en — MBA

## Term

Here the MBA is the certificate the speaker argues about: Look, the MBA is business, and I'm not supposed to talk about business, but the marketing part is far removed — it attracts a certain mentality and way of thinking, a way of thinking that values the certificate more than its worth. The abbreviation appears 4 times, beside "business", "mean", "light", and "won't", in a short video about whether the MBA is worth getting. In the speaker's use the MBA is a course, an academic course — and the problem is in the way of thinking before we decide to go and get it: it's okay to get it if you know it's not necessary, but there's a specific job you want, and that job requires this certificate. His concrete test is the weight of the job: if it's a heavy job, it will remain heavy, with or without the MBA, and if it's light, the MBA won't make it come to you — because ultimately it's about knowing the origin of this MBID, and if you go in not knowing how to do things, you'll come out not knowing how to do things, but knowing how to speak.
