# G_yt_T23tRn8fAJo_en — lifestyle agency

## Term

Here a lifestyle agency is what Marissa Rosen designed Climate Social to be: not a firm with a big team, but her own home office, working with people she has the pleasure to work with, setting her own hours and picking her own clients. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "designed" and "it's", in an interview where the host frames it as the question — are you trying to build a lifestyle agency? — and Marissa confirms it. In her use the lifestyle agency is the structure that lets her bring in partners for the pieces she does not do: if it is building a brand she brings in a brand designer, if it is building a website that is certainly not her doing the coding, and she holds the relationships herself. Her concrete history is the 10 years since she founded Climate Social in 2015, starting from her passion and interest in climate action and sustainable business, and growing it without ever wanting to talk about a tactic, because a tactic always changes.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a small agency: here the lifestyle agency is defined by how Marissa works — her hours, her clients, her home office — not by headcount, so the entry records the design, not the size.
- Read as a climate agency: climate action is her passion and the reason Climate Social exists, but the lifestyle agency is the form — the entry records the form, not the cause.
