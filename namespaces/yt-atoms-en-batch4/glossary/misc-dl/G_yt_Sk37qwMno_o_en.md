# G_yt_Sk37qwMno_o_en — productivity

## Term

Here productivity is the labor productivity the interview keeps coming back to: it has consistently gone down since the 80s worldwide, has even reached negative levels, and is now collapsing in software, one of the few places where it was positive and growing. The word appears 13 times, beside "labor", "marginal", "collapse", and "consistently", in questions about why labor productivity is collapsing and what it means when the market no longer rewards the remaining workers. In the speaker's use the collapse has a mechanism: when the easiest workers are cut, the surviving workers' marginal productivity collapses to the point that they are considered unskilled labor, and the market has no bid for them. His concrete marker is 2022, when productivity reached negative levels for the first two quarters, which he had already discussed on Financial Sense NewsHour.
