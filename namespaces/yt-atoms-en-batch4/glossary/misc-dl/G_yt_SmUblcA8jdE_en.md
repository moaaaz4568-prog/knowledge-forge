# G_yt_SmUblcA8jdE_en — outbound

## Term

Here outbound is the proactive sales motion the whole interview is about: cold emails, dialing, and outbound marketing that book sales appointments, as opposed to the inbound flow where people watch content and then schedule a call. The word appears 26 times, beside "dialing", "dials", "email", and "cold", and the scale is the proof of experience — Markettopia has done over 10 million outbound dials and booked close to 40,000 sales appointments. In the guests' use outbound is a discipline of best practice: making the dials necessary, following up, and doing what it takes to get appointments for customers, with a digital-agency past behind it. The interview's framing is that the channel's viewers are already sending cold emails or dialing, so outbound here names their daily work, not a theory.

## Synonyms

- outbounds (the plural noun form, used for the outbound efforts people want in place)
