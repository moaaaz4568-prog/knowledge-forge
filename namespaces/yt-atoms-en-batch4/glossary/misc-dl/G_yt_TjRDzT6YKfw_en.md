## Term

In this video, contrast is a design tool for drawing attention to specific bits of information: the word appears 19 times in the source. The speaker's second reason for using it is to create contrast on type so readers read and take note of the information he wants them to notice, and he stresses that black and white is not the only color palette that creates high contrast. His concrete guidance covers contrast through type, size, space, and scale, with graphic design examples showing how a big item against a small one pulls the eye. In use, contrast is the deliberate difference that makes one element stand out from the rest of a design.
