# G_yt_TosWR5cTkLY_en — visual impact

## Term

In this video, visual impact is the quality that decides whether a portfolio piece earns its place: the phrase appears 5 times in the source. The reviewer says a rendering can be super cool and still lack the visual impact that is so important, and that a rolltop backpack image lacks visual impact because it is very dark and the area of highest contrast is the maker's overall silhouette rather than the bag. His concrete test is whether the image registers at a glance in a portfolio review, where a hiring manager judges the work in seconds and a good project can still fail if it is photographed badly. In use, visual impact is the immediate read of a piece, not the effort that went into making it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as prettiness: the reviewer keeps projects that show real building skill even when the photo is weak, so a pretty image with no making behind it fails his test in the other direction.
- Read as a photography rule: the fix he suggests is re-photographing, but the lack he names is in the image's read at a glance, so better lighting alone does not save a piece with nothing to show.
