# G_yt_T4BpeUTFWp8_en — portfolio

## Term

Here a portfolio is the designer's website the video teaches you to redesign: if you are redesigning your portfolio or just starting from scratch, you really cannot afford to miss the tips nobody is really talking about. The word appears 12 times, beside "redesigning", "you're", "edition", and "design", and its plural "portfolios" appears too. In the speaker's use the portfolio is not a gallery of finished work but a set of hidden layers: most designers do not use hover tips or footnotes, yet those layers build trust, saying "I don't just make things look good. I make them work," and they reward engaged viewers who hover and read. The concrete rules are the micro CTAs sprinkled throughout the whole website — after a strong case study or a portfolio edition, a quick prompt like "Want a brand that feels this confident? Let's talk." — and the writing, arguably the most underrated part of a portfolio.
