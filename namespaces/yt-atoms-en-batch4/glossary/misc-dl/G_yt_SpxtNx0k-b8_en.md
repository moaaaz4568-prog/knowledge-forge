# G_yt_SpxtNx0k-b8_en — team

## Term

Here a team is what marketing work means to the speaker: a team with harmony, working well together, understanding each other, and supporting each other, so that the creator, the data analyst, and the designer sit together until they achieve something great. The word appears 4 times, beside "harmony", "means", "needs", and "practically", in a contrast with remote freelance work, whose efficiency he calls very weak, both technically and as a team, practically zero. In his use the team is the condition of real work: marketing requires deep collaboration, and doing it remotely leaves each person doing what he calls a "tacky" job for a "tacky" salary. His rule is blunt — marketing is something you cannot do alone, so there needs to be a team, and there needs to be harmony between them.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "For me, work means a team with harmony, working well together, understanding each other, and supporting each other." — sources/S_yt_SpxtNx0k-b8_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any group of workers: here the team is defined by harmony and sitting together until the work is great — a remote group with no collaboration is exactly what the speaker calls not a team.
- Read as an attack on freelancers: the speaker's point is about the work arrangement, not the person — a freelance media buyer is what desperate clients hire, and the entry records his judgment of the arrangement, not of freelancing as a whole.
