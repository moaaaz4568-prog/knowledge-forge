# G_yt_Sr-h0JcEKxY_en — sabatical

## Term

Here sabatical is the method in the title of Alison Caffrey's book, The Sabatical Method: how to leverage rest and grow your business — the spelling is the transcript's own, and the method is about teasing yourself out of the business by taking meaningful time off. The word appears 17 times, beside "method", "plan", "how", and "framework", in an interview about the operations agency she founded to separate founders from their businesses. In her use the method has levels, and the first level is to come in as the assister: tease somebody else up to get the score, hand them the work, and send them over with instructions on how you would do it. She wrote the method because she had trained a fulfillment department to need 70 hours of her time, and pulling herself out was the challenge the method solves.
