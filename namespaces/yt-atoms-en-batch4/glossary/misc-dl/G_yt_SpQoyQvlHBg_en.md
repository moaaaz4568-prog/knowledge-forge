## Term

Here templates are the ready-made documents the second resource in the video offers: the speaker searches for a contract as an example and finds templates that come up in the search results, with a gray label that says free marking the ones to take. The word appears 6 times, beside "use", "invoice", "section", and "edit", and its singular "template" appears too. In his use a template is a starting point for a real document — a contract, an invoice — that the designer downloads free and adapts, rather than a finished design. The video's point is the find itself: the site holds tons of categories at the top, and the invoice templates section alone has a good number of free downloads, so the template is the free resource being recommended.
