# G_yt_TVWJtmyNnFA_en — edutainment

## Term

Here edutainment is the middle between entertainment and learning: in general, and in the middle is the so-called edutainment — so it's kind of like learning through entertainment. The word appears 4 times, beside "so-called", "it's", "channels", and "brand", in a video about building a six-figure YouTube business in 12 months. In the speakers' use edutainment is the monetization problem: this is very important for monetization, because of the problem that entertainment videos solve — the problem we solve with entertainment is boredom and loneliness, while for learning the problem you are solving is very specific. His concrete rule is the transformation: if you know what the value is, that is, the transformation, and you have captured it, you can start a channel — and it's very difficult to switch from an entertainment channel to an educational channel, because your audience came to you out of boredom and suddenly you're trying to help them with something else.
