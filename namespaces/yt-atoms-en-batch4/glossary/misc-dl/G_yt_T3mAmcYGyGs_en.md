# G_yt_T3mAmcYGyGs_en — Black Friday

## Term

Here Black Friday is the sale that proves Who Is Elijah's scale: this year alone the brand made over 3 million in a Black Friday sale with a 400% year on growth, on a $20 million e-commerce empire the host expects to double and triple very soon. The phrase appears 4 times, beside "sale", "night", and "year", in a long interview with the founder and creative director. In the speaker's use Black Friday is also the team's test: the creative and content manager's EGC video for Black Friday did nearly a million dollars in sales, and the team worked till 8:00 at night for it without being asked, because they live and breathe the brand. His concrete frame is the family the brand became: hand-filling bottles, not mass-produced in some factory, with everyone walking up to retail shelves asking who Elijah is.
