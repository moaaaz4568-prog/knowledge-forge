# G_yt_TZvfru0AS-k_en — webinar

## Term

In this video, a webinar is the live online event a coach runs to grow a coaching business, and the word appears 11 times in the source. The speaker's numbers are the point: of a hundred people who register for a live webinar, only 25 or 30 percent show up, yet about twelve percent of the people who register request a sales appointment before the webinar and a similar number after it, regardless of whether they show up or not. He says webinars are either the thing you must have to grow your coaching business or they are dead, and his answer is that the structure and the presentation skills matter — with a great structure and incredible presentation skills, your webinars can be amazing. In use, the webinar is a registration funnel where the show-up rate and the appointment rate are the real measures, not the size of the audience that signed up.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a live talk that sells by itself: here the webinar is a funnel — a hundred register, a quarter show up, about twelve percent book before and another twelve after — so the talk alone without the structure and the follow-up does not reproduce those numbers.
- Read as a numbers game about registrations: the speaker's point is that the best prospects probably don't show up because they are busy and successful already, so counting sign-ups as interest mistakes the audience for the buyers.
