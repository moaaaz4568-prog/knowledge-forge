## Term

Here content is the funnel: If you do not have a way to take advantage of this new world of marketing, which is just understanding that content is the funnel, then you're not going to make it. The word appears 46 times, beside "piece", "time", "create", and "format", in a video about how the way money is made has completely flipped on its head. In the speaker's use content drives the calls: he wishes when he was starting out he could have a way to have content drive — put an ad out there, spend money on ads, a few, you know, 20 bucks a day on ads, and get his first calls — and the proof is Hammad, whose CAC right now is like a $100 or 100 to 200 bucks per client, because every time he spends $100 and $200 to boost some of his content, he ends up with someone giving him a thousand or 2.5K. His concrete contrast is the old way: get someone to book an appointment, take five sales calls, get five people, 10 people to show up and pitch all of them, close one or two new clients and collect 5K from each — and then spend maybe a thousand bucks on ads and call it a great business — while the new figures do infinitely better whether they're selling insurance or selling roofs or selling whatever.
