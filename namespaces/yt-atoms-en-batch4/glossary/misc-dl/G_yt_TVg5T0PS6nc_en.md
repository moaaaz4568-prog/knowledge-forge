# G_yt_TVg5T0PS6nc_en — pre-call asset

## Term

Here the pre-call asset is the Loom video that closes the client before the call: I firmly believe that the main reason many of you fail to close clients is because no one listens to me about creating a pre-call asset, i.e. a video in Loom for perpetual use. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "creating", in a long New York episode about the biggest program the agency has ever launched. In the speaker's use the pre-call asset is the evergreen video: he only has one video like this on Loom, the same evergreen video — he's using it to explain the program, and for anything else that he needs an evergreen video for, and it works for him. His concrete rule is the introduction: focus on results, and if you're going to do something personalized, consider this — he never does anything for clients unless it's a video introduction on Loom — and the proposal beside it is the warning, because his proposal got completely ruined when someone slandered him to his own client.
