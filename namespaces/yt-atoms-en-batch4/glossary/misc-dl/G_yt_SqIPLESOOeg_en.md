# G_yt_SqIPLESOOeg_en — retension

## Term

Here retension is the speaker's own word for re-raising tension after releasing it: when you answer a question, tension releases and the viewer's brain relaxes, and if you give them an off-ramp, most will take it — so when you release tension, you need to retension or your viewers have no reason to keep watching. The word appears 15 times, beside "retention", "understand", "fourth", and "power", in a video that teaches tension as a cycle: you create tension, they lean in; you answer a question, tension releases. In his use retension is a system, not a trick: you cannot just wing it, and the scripting system he gives away exists to build the cycle in — create tension, release it, raise it again. The fourth piece of the system is where retension sits, after the value and the answer have already been given.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as retention: retension is the act that produces retention, not the metric — the entry records the re-raising of tension, while retention is the result measured afterwards.
- Read as withholding answers: the speaker's cycle requires answering the question and releasing tension first, so retension without the release is just teasing, and the entry records the full cycle, not the tease alone.
