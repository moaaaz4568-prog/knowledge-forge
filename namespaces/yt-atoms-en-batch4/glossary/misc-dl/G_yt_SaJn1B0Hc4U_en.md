# G_yt_SaJn1B0Hc4U_en — email copy

## Term

Here email copy is the text of the cold emails the speaker and his team test all week to find what gets a response: they try different copies, watch what works and what does not, and conclude why one version fails while another starts working. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "game" and "getting", while the shorter "copy" appears 10 times for the same text — the copies they test, the copy that was terrible at first, the copy that started working. In his use the copy is judged by one thing only, whether it makes the reader respond: he wants the question positioned so the reader answers, and the aim is to elicit a yes. The week ends with the copy still being built through what he calls a trench stage, with results expected only once the copy is built out.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as polished writing advice: here the copy is tested against replies, not style — a version counts as working only when it gets a response, and the early version is called trash because it got none.
- Read as a finished playbook: the speaker is mid-test, still building the copy through a trench stage, so the entry records what they are trying this week, not a proven template.
