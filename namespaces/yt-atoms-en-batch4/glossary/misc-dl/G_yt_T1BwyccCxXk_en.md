# G_yt_T1BwyccCxXk_en — lead magnet

## Term

Here a lead magnet is the third step of the speaker's four-step formula for generating leads with Facebook: market, message, magnet, mechanism — and the magnet is the lead magnet itself. The phrase appears 5 times, beside "comes", "perfect", "wants", and "page", in a video that walks the four steps in order. In his use the lead magnet is what you create once the message is crystal clear: it wants to be short and highly valuable, the quicker it is to get the result the easier it is to get access to and get through, and the more valuable the content. His concrete mechanism is the funnel after it — a Facebook ad to the lead magnet page, a thank you page with a webinar registration, and on the webinar that sells — or the shorter path, a Facebook ad to the lead magnet to a short case study.
