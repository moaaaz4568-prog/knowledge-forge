# G_yt_TXSLC_2iGoI_en — reflection effect

## Term

For this Photoshop tutorial, a reflection effect is the mirrored copy of objects and text that the speaker builds to showcase work, and the phrase appears 1 time in the source. He says he has been using this reflection effect in Photoshop in his portfolio work for quite some time, and the tutorial walks through duplicating the layer while holding alt, rasterizing the text layer so it can be edited, then flipping it so it reflects at the same scale. The neighboring steps name a soft brush at low hardness and a smooth gradient the speaker asks the viewer to take time over. In use, the effect turns a plain piece of text or an object into a reflected presentation piece rather than leaving the artwork flat on the page.
