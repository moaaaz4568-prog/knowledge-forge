# G_yt_TlUoq7CPjfE_en — target audience

## Term

In this video, the target audience is the specific group a marketing campaign must reach before anything else is decided: the phrase appears 13 times in the source. The speaker ties it to the ideal customer, saying you just need to know that you have a target audience and an ideal customer, and that the purpose of having one is knowing how to identify them through age, location, language, family size, and the other traits in the empathy map. His concrete warning is that marketers promise financial gain, wealth, or success they cannot control, while the real work is moving the audience through the sales funnel and spreading awareness on social media. In use, the target audience is the defined group whose traits shape every later marketing decision.
