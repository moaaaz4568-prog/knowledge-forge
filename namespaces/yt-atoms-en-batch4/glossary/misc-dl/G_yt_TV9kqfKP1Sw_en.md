# G_yt_TV9kqfKP1Sw_en — extended brain

## Term

Here the extended brain is Notion holding everything you know: if notion is my extended brain, then Q&A is like my assistant — but an assistant that knows everything that I know. The phrase appears 4 times, beside "itself", in an early look at Notion Q&A from Cage and Koi. In the speakers' use the extended brain is what you have to build first: in order to reap this benefit you have to first build out your extended brain — if we want to take advantage of AI that's trained on our brain and knows everything that we know, then we got to give it a brain to learn. His concrete picture is the workspace: their Notion is kind of like an amalgamation of all the different content that they consume, with content loosely broken into categories like learning and business, and they've even been exploring philosophy lately — and the warning beside it is the staleness: we're all using the same AI, trained on the same templates, spitting out the same answers, so the content all looks and feels the same.
