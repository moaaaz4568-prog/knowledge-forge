# G_yt_TN2HCYoCltg_en — neon vector buttons

## Term

Here neon vector buttons are the web graphics the video teaches you to make in Illustrator: learn how to make yourself some nifty and some cool neon vector buttons using illustrator. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "cool", "using", "illustrator", and "work", in a tutorial that also plugs TubeBuddy at the end. In the speaker's use the neon vector buttons work best as an RGB document at a screen resolution of 72 PPI — the dimensions didn't actually matter so much — so you generate a document, select a background color, press M for the rectangle tool, cover the canvas with a rectangle, and lock it down in place. His concrete build is the power symbol: take the ellipse tool by pressing L, generate a perfect circle by holding down shift, press command or control R to open the rulers and drag down a guide, then increase the stroke weight of the circular symbol and head into the stroke window to round off the ends — and expand the appearance so the blur effects stay proportional no matter how small or large you make the design.
