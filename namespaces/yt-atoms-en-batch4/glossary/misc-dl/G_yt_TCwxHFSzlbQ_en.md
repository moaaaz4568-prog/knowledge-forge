# G_yt_TCwxHFSzlbQ_en — stage

## Term

Here a stage is one of the stages of success in a freelance career: when you start your freelance career, you're entering what's called the first stage of success, and you're fundamentally very weak in the craft itself. The word appears 17 times, beside "success", "initial", "early", and "you'll", in a video that tells the speaker's journey from his first website designs to $15,000 projects. In his use the first stage is the first two years, and success in it is very simple: number one, seek out practical experience — any volunteer work you find, take it, and any mentorship or training under someone with more experience — and number two, build a good reputation in the community, so people say you're hardworking and have good morals. His concrete story is the price ladder: one of the first website designs he ever created, five years ago when he first entered the world of freelancing and quit his job, learning by copying pixel by pixel, from $500 to $1,000 and then a little more, until today, in November 2025, he is working on slightly larger projects for $15,000.
