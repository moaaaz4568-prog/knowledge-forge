# G_yt_SzW8TUoN01M_en — price buyer

## Term

Here the price buyer is one of the four buyer types the episode teaches: the person who wants to buy at the lowest possible price, the bargain shopper who asks "is there a discount, is that on sale, you could do better, that's not your final price". The phrase appears 6 times, beside "comes", "fastest", and "who", in a video about identifying the four buyer types and speaking to each differently. In the speaker's use the price buyer is handled by referral, not by persuasion: the fastest thing is to refer them to somebody else — Fiverr or 99designs — because they will go to Joe down the street for cheaper anyway, and if it works out they have saved a ton of money. The contrast is the decisive buyer, the opposite of the indecisive one, who knows the problem, knows the value of their time, and wants it done right the first time with the best of everything.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a cheapskate to argue with: here the price buyer is referred out, not converted — the entry records the speaker's rule that arguing price with this type wastes the sale.
- Read as all buyers: the price buyer is one of four types, so the entry records only this type's handling — the decisive buyer who wants the best of everything is spoken to differently.
