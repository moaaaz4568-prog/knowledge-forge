# G_yt_T-Qch6veNhg_en — retainer

## Term

Here a retainer is the consistent retainer arrangement that keeps an agency alive when revenue falls: before covid the agency's revenue was not very heavy on retainers, and then 40% of the revenue deeply faced trouble, which took at least a couple of quarters to overcome. The word appears 2 times, beside "arrangements" and "consistent", and its plural "retainers" appears too. In the speaker's use the retainer is the aha moment of the story: with a large team to support, consistent retainer arrangements are what stop a single bad instance from destroying things massively. His concrete rule is the scale test — if you have a large team and no retainers in play, any such instance can destroy things, so the retainer is the protection, not just the revenue.

## Synonyms

- retainers (the plural form, used for more than one arrangement)
