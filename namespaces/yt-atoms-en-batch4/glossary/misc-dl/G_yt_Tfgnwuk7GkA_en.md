# G_yt_Tfgnwuk7GkA_en — myfonts.com

## Term

In this typography video, myfonts.com is the first of seven must-have inspiration resources for designers with a passion for all things typographic, and the name appears 1 time in the source. The speaker introduces it as a website that lets you upload an image of some text and do its best to work out what the font is, so if you love the look of a font and do not know what it is, the tool has a good step of finding it. The rest of the list runs from calligraphy and typographic pens bought from Amazon or eBay to a Wacom tablet and social media hashtags for photography. In use, myfonts.com is the identification step: screen capture the text, type it in, and let the website throw up its ideas, rather than guessing the font by eye.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a font shop only: here myfonts.com is the identification tool — upload an image of text and it works out the font — so opening it to browse fonts misses the resource the video is recommending.
