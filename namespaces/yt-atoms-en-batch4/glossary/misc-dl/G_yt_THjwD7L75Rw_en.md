# G_yt_THjwD7L75Rw_en — MSP

## Term

Here MSP is the market the guest's whole business lives in: My name is Nate Freriedman. I run a company called TechPro Marketing, which we've been operating in the MSP space, which is managed IT services providers for the last eight years. The abbreviation appears 3 times, beside "space", "which", "called", and "sites", in a Smart Agency Masterclass episode about switching from big deals to a ton of little deals. In his use the MSP space is where the product grew: over the last two years they've launched a product inside that space called MSP sites, where they help MSPs get their technology and website set up, add on some coaching, and generate leads without investing in an agency — and then the MSP lead gen playbook course, with office hours twice a week. His concrete turning point is the deal size: from selling big deals and charging $4,000 a month and hoping clients are around the 2 million mark, to selling a ton of little deals — and the $19,237 website proposal he put out as a lead magnet like 10 years ago.
