# G_yt_TR3oqNc1PUQ_en — time and money

## Term

Here time and money are what the client actually wants: they want leads, they want the phone to ring, they want to solve a problem that they have — and everything is time and money. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "everything", in an episode with Nico Beiji, who has been running the Guerilla Agency in Oregon for almost 11 years. In his use time and money are the filter for the work: when they go into things, he's going to attack the most important things that either get you time or money, so that they're maximizing their time and therefore their money — and they don't promote, push, or do anything that doesn't have some sort of tangible value or data point to it. His concrete proof is the Guerilla-first rule: they do it at Guerilla first, and if they see it, feel it, and it works, then they roll it out with a client — because it's not all theory, it's actually stuff that actually works, and the question that never changes is what's not going to change, so they adapt or die.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a price: here time and money are what the client wants solved — the entry records the solving, not the billing.
- Read as a slogan: the speaker's agency attacks what gets time or money first, so the entry records the attacking, not the phrase.
