# G_yt_SduKH5rf850_en — hire

## Term

Here hire is what a growing business must learn to do: the video opens by asking how to hire people when the business starts to grow, and how to trust them to do the same quality work with the same passion. The word appears 4 times, beside "how", "people", and "train them up", in advice that starts by listing every function the owner does and then hiring for the least essential ones. In Chris's use you do not hire one person to do all seven functions — that is a job of three people — you take the least essential functions, hire for those, and train the hires up. The reason he gives for training them up is that you are not paying them market rate, so the hire is a trade: lower pay for teaching.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as hiring stars: here the hire is for the least essential functions, trained up from below market rate — the opposite of buying ready-made talent, and the entry records that trade as spoken.
- Read as a single hire solving growth: the advice is to split the owner's functions across several people, so one hire for all seven functions is exactly the mistake the video warns against.
