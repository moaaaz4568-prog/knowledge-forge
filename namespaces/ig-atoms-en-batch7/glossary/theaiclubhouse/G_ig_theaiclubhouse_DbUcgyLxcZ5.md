# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DbUcgyLxcZ5 — say no

## Term

Say no is the decision Sam's team made to a big client: it appears 1 time, beside Jacksonville, 50 million, and get rich off of rap. The clip opens with the craziest day ever and the refusal itself: they had a big client out of Jacksonville offering big amounts of money, 50 million, 10 million, 5 million, something like that, and they turned it down. The reason given is blunt and unexplained: he had to get rich off of rap instead. The concrete case is the refusal itself: the money was on the table and the team said no to all of it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pricing rule: the refusal here is a single anecdote about one Jacksonville client, not a general rule about which clients to refuse.
- Read as a claim the money was real: the figures are Sam's own telling of the offer, and the clip gives no contract or proof behind them.
