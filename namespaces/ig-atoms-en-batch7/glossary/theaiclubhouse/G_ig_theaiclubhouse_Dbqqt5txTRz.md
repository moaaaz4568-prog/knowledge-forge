# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_Dbqqt5txTRz — ballpark

## Term

The ballpark is the rough price range Sam gives before a full discovery: it appears 3 times, beside one thousand, one hundred thousand, and accurate range. A client asks for a ballpark of the build cost and the ongoing management, and Sam answers with a range between one thousand and one hundred thousand dollars, which the client loves as a range. The clip then contrasts that with his real practice: he usually waits until after discovery, sometimes even after discovery, because he does not want the headache of over promising. The concrete move is the one week full discovery: read through, give an accurate range, and let the client approve additions or removals from the original proposal.

## When the Meaning Breaks
