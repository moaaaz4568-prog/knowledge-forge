# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DYnFxUyRcGj — AI command centers

## Term

AI command centers are what Sam sees customers buying next: it appears 1 time, beside memory, Claude code, and Obsidian. The centers are built on the client's memory, by linking Claude code with all the skills and the Clio eyes and pairing it with Obsidian as a second brain, for companies that want ultimate power. The clip places them at the end of a ladder: a basic Claude setup first, then Slack AI cowork agents, then a 15 to 20K build of Open Clone Hermes agents, then AI media buying, and finally the command centers. The concrete point is the ordering itself: distribution and the client acquisition pipeline matter more than any product on the ladder, including the centers.
