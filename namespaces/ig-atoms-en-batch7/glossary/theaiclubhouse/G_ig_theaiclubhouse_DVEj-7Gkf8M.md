# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DVEj-7Gkf8M — client acquisition channel

## Term

The client acquisition channel is LinkedIn, named as the main one: it appears 1 time, beside decision makers, lead flow, and ICPs. Sam presents it as free access to decision makers on demand, with the extra advantage that you can see who everybody is before you message them. The channel sits in a list of tools that build a seven-figure AI company, next to Lovable for web apps, Supabase for the backend, and Stripe for payments. The concrete use is the outreach step: after Lead Shark automates the LinkedIn response, the channel delivers warm leads instead of a hired team.
