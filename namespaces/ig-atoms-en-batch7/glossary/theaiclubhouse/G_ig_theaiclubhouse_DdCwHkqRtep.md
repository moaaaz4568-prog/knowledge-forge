# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DdCwHkqRtep — lifetime customer value

## Term

Lifetime customer value is the first of two goals Sam sets for any business: it appears 3 times, beside cost of customer acquisition, churn, and MRR. The real money, he says, is not made getting new clients and churning them out, but selling more to current customers, because they have less of a guard up and already trust the seller's competence. The second goal is lowering the cost of customer acquisition, and the formula for the best businesses ties them together: current MRR plus new monthly MRR minus churn lost, divided by the original MRR. The concrete threshold is the 1 percent line: a business over 1 percent is growing and will do well, while under 0.9 it will eventually die and turn to zero.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a formula Sam invented: he says he believes someone else is talking about the MRR formula, so the entry records his use of it, not his authorship.
- Read as a guarantee at 1 percent: the over-1-percent line is Sam's stated rule for a growing business, not a measured law of business survival.
