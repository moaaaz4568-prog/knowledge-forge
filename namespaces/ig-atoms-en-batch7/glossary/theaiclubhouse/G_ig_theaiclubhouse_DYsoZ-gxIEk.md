# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DYsoZ-gxIEk — AI SaaS

## Term

The AI SaaS is the fifth step that turns an agency into an actual company: it appears 1 time, beside replicate, high value vertical, and new logo. Sam's step is to take a service built for one client in a high value vertical and replicate it for the same type at no cost, with the same software, a new logo, and new revenue. The earlier steps set it up: pick a niche with money like lawyers, financial services, and healthcare, stop selling AI and sell outcomes, partner with the first client, and use LinkedIn instead of cold email. The concrete payoff is the transformation itself: the same software sold again under a new logo is what takes you from an agency to a company that can scale forever.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of free replication: at no cost describes reusing the same software for the same type of client, not a guarantee of zero cost in every case.
- Read as a new product idea: the AI SaaS here is the same service repackaged, not a different product built from scratch.
