# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DXKipjIkbsQ — proven business model

## Term

The proven business model is what marks the mid-market companies as worth targeting: it appears 1 time, beside real budgets, expensive problems, and defensible businesses. The companies Sam describes are too big for the garbage ChatGPT and GoHighLevel builds but cannot fork up 500K for an enterprise contract, and the proven model is the third trait that makes them a sweet spot. His examples are a locksmith company doing around $5 million a year and a financing firm processing around 300 transactions a month. The locksmiths were dying to pay 8K a month so they could save 100K in a year, which is the kind of proven economics the label points at.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee the model works: proven here describes companies already earning, not a promise that any buyer can copy their model.
- Read as a synonym for profitable: the phrase sits beside real budgets and expensive problems, marking a tested business, not just a rich one.
