# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_Ddm9RU7R7kO — niche

## Term

The niche is what rich businesses want, in Sam's status argument: it appears 3 times, beside rich people, 911 Turbo, and custom CRM. The clip says value is not just transformation, and uses rich buyers as the proof: rich people buy a 911 Turbo or a 911 Targa as a status symbol because it is expensive and niche. In the same way, rich businesses want the niche, and their end goal is a custom CRM they can brag about to friends, saying they do it all custom. The concrete boast is the line itself: we do not have a single wasted thing, we do not even pay money anymore on it, we just pay this much per month.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that niche always wins: the clip argues rich buyers like niche status symbols, which is a claim about taste, not proof every niche product sells.
- Read as a pricing claim: the custom CRM is presented as a status purchase, and the clip never gives its price.
