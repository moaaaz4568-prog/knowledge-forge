# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DU_ftxtkZAR — custom build

## Term

The custom build is the work ChatGPT cannot replace: it appears 1 time, beside $15,000, six weeks, competitive advantage, and commoditized. Sam defines it against the $1,500-a-month client whose switching cost is zero, because a wrapper can be replaced in five minutes. A $10 million furniture company needing CRM integration, advanced workflows, and live data projection is his example of work that is not a ChatGPT wrapper anymore. The build takes around six weeks and creates a real competitive advantage while everyone else gets commoditized and crushed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any custom work: the clip reserves the term for integration and workflow builds a client cannot swap out in five minutes, not for a tweaked chatbot.
- Read as a price tag: the $15,000 is the example figure for one furniture-company build, not a fixed rate for every custom build.
