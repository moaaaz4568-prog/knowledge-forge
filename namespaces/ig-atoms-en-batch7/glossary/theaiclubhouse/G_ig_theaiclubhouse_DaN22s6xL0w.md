# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DaN22s6xL0w — developer on demand

## Term

The developer on demand is Cursor, named as what you get when you connect it to your repo: it appears 1 time, beside writes and ships code, repo, and Lead Shark. In the same tools list as the earlier clip, Sam assigns Cursor the job of writing and shipping code for you, so the team never needs a hire. The list around it runs from Lovable for web apps and Supabase for the backend to LinkedIn as the main client acquisition channel. The concrete use is the repo connection itself: connect Cursor to the repo and the coding work is covered without employing a developer.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a hired developer: the developer on demand here is the Cursor tool connected to a repo, not a person employed by the company.
- Read as a promise of no engineers ever: the clip says you never need to hire a team if you do this right, which is a conditional claim about this stack, not a tested fact.
