# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DUZcB1CESBG — venture studio

## Term

The venture studio is the model Sam switched to when he stopped selling agency services: it appears 6 times, four times as venture studios and twice inside venture studio model, beside agencies, competitive advantages, irreplaceable partners, and leverage. The contrast is drawn three ways: agencies sell services while studios build infrastructure, agencies compete on price while studios create competitive advantages, and agencies get replaced by tools while studios become irreplaceable partners. The switch changed his clients to larger companies with real budgets and his pricing to 25K per partnership instead of $500 monthly retainers. The concrete case is his own transition: instead of selling voice agents he now builds custom call center infrastructure, and instead of CRM integrations he designs fully custom dashboards.

## Synonyms

- venture studios (the plural form the source uses)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a bigger agency: the studio's work is building infrastructure and selecting leverage with fewer clients, not selling more services to more clients.
- Read as a guarantee of higher prices: the 25K figure is what Sam charges to partner up, and the clip ties it to a different caliber of client, not to the label alone.
