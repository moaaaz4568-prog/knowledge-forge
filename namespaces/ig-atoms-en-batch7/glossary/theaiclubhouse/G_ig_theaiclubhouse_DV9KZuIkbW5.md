# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DV9KZuIkbW5 — retainer

## Term

The retainer is the monthly fee the AI tools are destroying: it appears 3 times, always as retainers, beside $500 a month, 50K a month, commoditizing, and government deals. The stupid $500 a month retainers are the agency work ChatGPT Agent Builder and Google Gemini are replacing, while the 50K a month retainers are what Sam closes with his infrastructure partners on government deals. The contrast is the whole clip: the same word covers the dying low-end fee and the high-end deal, and the difference is the custom infrastructure behind it. The concrete case is the $500 chatbot retainer, raced to the bottom while studios sell 25K projects instead.

## Synonyms

- retainers (the plural form the source uses)

> "ChatGPT Agent Builder and Google Gemini are coming after everyone, and they're commoditizing every agency, and they're replacing all these stupid $500 a month retainers." — sources/S_ig_theaiclubhouse_DV9KZuIkbW5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof retainers are dead: only the $500 retainers are being replaced here, while 50K a month retainers are presented as alive and closing.
- Read as a price for a tool: the retainer here is a monthly client fee for agency work, not the cost of ChatGPT or Gemini themselves.
