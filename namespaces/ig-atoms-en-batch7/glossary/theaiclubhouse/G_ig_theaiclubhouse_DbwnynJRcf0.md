# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DbwnynJRcf0 — private equity

## Term

Private equity is the buyer Sam says is buying agency systems and clients: it appears 2 times, beside PPLs, buyouts, and $5 million a month. The buyers do not care what tools are used because they are not acquiring a startup with unique code; they are buying the mechanism, the ad buying system, the licensing, and the funnel. What private equity does with PPLs is combine them together, so there is less competition and they can choke out the ad spend of others. The concrete case is the scale: combine three PPL companies spending $5 million a month in ads and they destroy anyone doing anything less than a million.
