# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DWAJfeSETn8 — lead magnet

## Term

The lead magnet is the LinkedIn post that starts the acquisition machine: it appears 2 times, once as lead magnet posts and once as the lead magnet, beside profile optimization, setter, and booking appointments. Sam's sequence is simple profile optimization, then lead magnet posts, then a setter on the account who books appointments when a post goes viral. The magnet is what the setter sends while also going into the post to book, so the post and the setter work as one system. The concrete case is the viral post: it does not just collect likes, it triggers the setter to send the magnet and book the call.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole strategy: the magnet is one step in a sequence with profile optimization and a setter, and the clip never presents it as sufficient alone.
- Read as a freebie for anyone: the magnet is sent when a post goes viral to people already engaging, not blasted to strangers.
