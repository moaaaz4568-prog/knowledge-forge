# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_Ddo6K-6xxzu — 20 grand

## Term

20 grand is the price Sam says to charge for the thing a company actually needs: it appears 1 time, beside 200 bucks a month, slave, and solve it once. The clip stages a dialogue with a seller charging 200 a month for an AI agent to small businesses, calling him essentially a slave: three clients paying 400 a month total, less than his maid makes before lunch. At 200 a month they cancel in March and the seller starts over in April, so it does not scale. The concrete instruction is the fix: pick one industry, find one company in it with real money, build the thing they actually need, and charge 20 grand, then sell that same build to every other company in the industry.

## When the Meaning Breaks
