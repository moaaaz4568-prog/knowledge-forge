# G_ig_theaiclubhouse_DRiDh0TEUfH — standard offer

## Term

The standard offer is the fixed, ready-made package Sam sells to a buyer who arrives knowing nothing: it appears 1 time, beside its variant standardized offer, and beside custom AI offer, niche, and outcome. The clip pairs it against the custom AI offer, which is built around one client's specific project, and says the standard offer is what lets him sell to the newcomer at all. With both offers in place, he says, you cannot miss anybody, because the standard one catches the buyer the custom one cannot serve. The concrete case is the visitor who knows nothing: instead of turning him away, Sam sells him the standard offer.

## Synonyms

- standardized offer (the form the source uses when introducing the pair)

## Live Example

> "So in the case that somebody comes to you and they know nothing, at least you can sell them the standard offer." — sources/S_ig_theaiclubhouse_DRiDh0TEUfH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a discount or cheap tier: the standard offer is a fixed package for a defined niche and outcome, not a cheaper version of the custom build.
- Read as the only offer a beginner needs: the clip insists on having both offers, because the standard one alone cannot serve a buyer with a specific project.
