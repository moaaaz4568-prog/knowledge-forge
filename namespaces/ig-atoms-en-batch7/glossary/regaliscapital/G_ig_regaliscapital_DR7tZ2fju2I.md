# G_ig_regaliscapital_DR7tZ2fju2I — SBA loan

## Term

In this source the SBA loan is the legal 'cheat code' for buying a half-million-dollar-a-year business without spending a million bucks: the SBA finances 90% of the deal, putting up $900, 000, while the buyer puts down only $50, 000. The phrase occurs once, beside 'the cheat code that's perfectly legal', 'SBA will finance 90% of the deal', and 'you only put down $50, 000'. The speaker stresses the deal is not easy, only possible, and points viewers to a detailed guide for the exact steps.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "We're gonna use the cheat code that's perfectly legal called the SBA loan." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DR7tZ2fju2I.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as free money: the loan still has to be repaid from the business, and the speaker says plainly 'this isn't easy to do'.
- Read as a promise anyone can buy any business for $50, 000: the example is one drainage contractor priced around a million bucks, and the split depends on the seller agreeing to carry part of the deal.
