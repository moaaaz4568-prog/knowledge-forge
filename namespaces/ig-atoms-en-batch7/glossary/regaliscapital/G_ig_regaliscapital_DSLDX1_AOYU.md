# G_ig_regaliscapital_DSLDX1_AOYU — chant

## Term

This source has no conceptual term: the twelve words are a sung hook, and 'chant' here is only the entry's type label, not a concept the account defines. The hook opens on 'No need to ask', then repeats the smooth operator line three times, the last time standing alone without 'He's a'. Nothing around the hook names a business, a deal, or a strategy, so the clip works as music, not as a claim the account makes about any person.
