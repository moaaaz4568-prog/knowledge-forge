# G_ig_regaliscapital_DQzfU1EAP9Y — seller note

## Term

In this source a seller note is the slice of the price the seller agrees to hold back: the lender puts up 90% of the deal, the seller holds 5% as a seller note, and the buyer comes in for the remaining 5%. The phrase occurs once, beside 'the lender will put up 90% of the deal', 'you get the seller to hold 5%', and 'you only have to come in for 5%'. The speaker presents it as the last piece of a formula for buying an established business without a ton of cash, alongside hiring an accountant and a lawyer to check the deal.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Last, the lender will put up 90% of the deal. You get the seller to hold 5% as a seller note, and you only have to come in for 5%." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DQzfU1EAP9Y.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a loan from the bank: the note is held by the seller, not the lender, and the bank's share is the separate 90% the lender puts up.
- Read as a 5% discount: the seller still gets the 5%, only later, so the note is deferred payment rather than money taken off the price.
