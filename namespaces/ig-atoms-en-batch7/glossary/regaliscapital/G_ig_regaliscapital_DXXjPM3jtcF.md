# G_ig_regaliscapital_DXXjPM3jtcF — blueprint

## Term

In this source the blueprint is Jim Ratcliffe's simple method: acquire undervalued, boring assets, strip out inefficiencies, and scale them by prioritizing profitability. The word occurs once, beside 'undervalued, boring assets', 'strip out inefficiencies', and 'over 20 acquisitions'. Ratcliffe mortgaged his house, put in $140, 000 of his own savings, and bought an overlooked BP chemicals unit for $37 million, then took Inspec public for $164 million within two years — and kept buying companies with the same playbook every time.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a detailed plan: the source states the blueprint in one sentence, and the detail is the story — the mortgage, the $37 million purchase, the $164 million listing — not a step-by-step guide.
- Read as a promise that mortgaging a house buys a fortune: Ratcliffe's first deal took two years to list and twenty more acquisitions followed, so the blueprint is a repeated method, not a single bet.
