# G_ig_regaliscapital_DSspcjJjnsF — acquisition mentor

## Term

In this source an acquisition mentor is what the free guide acts like: a personal mentor who explains the whole process in plain English without charging thousands of dollars per hour. The phrase occurs once, beside 'plain English', 'thousands of dollars per hour', and 'simple deal structures'. The guide shows how independent buyers use SBA loans, seller financing, and simple deal structures to buy profitable small businesses with as little as 5% down, and how regular people find sellers and negotiate deals owners prefer over big firms.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It's basically like having your own personal acquisition mentor who explains the whole process in plain English without charging you thousands of dollars per hour." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DSspcjJjnsF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a real person offering mentorship: the mentor is a simile for the guide, not a person the account introduces.
- Read as a promise the guide replaces professional advice: the source says the guide explains the process in plain English, not that it substitutes for lawyers, accountants, or lenders.
