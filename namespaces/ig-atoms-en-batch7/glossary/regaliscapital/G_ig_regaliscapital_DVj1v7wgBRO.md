# G_ig_regaliscapital_DVj1v7wgBRO — economic indicator

## Term

In this source an economic indicator is what casino cash flow and tip volume become when high rollers stop tipping: analysts track them because gambling and luxury entertainment are usually the first things people cut when money gets tight, and falling tips often signal that people with disposable income are pulling back. The phrase occurs once, beside 'casino cash flow', 'tip volume', and 'high rollers aren't tipping'. The speaker turns the warning into an opening — buy a recession-resistant business like HVAC with 5% down, acquire more companies at discounted prices during the downturn, and roll them under one brand.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Some analysts actually track casino cash flow and tip volume as an economic indicator because gambling and luxury entertainment are usually the first thing that people cut down on when money gets tight." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DVj1v7wgBRO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof a recession is already here: the source calls the drop one of the earliest warning signs, not a confirmation, and says only that things might get rough.
- Read as advice to watch casinos instead of buying: the indicator is only the setup, and the action the speaker recommends is buying a recession-resistant business with 5% down.
