# G_ig_regaliscapital_DURUXfTDvmB — aggressive monetization

## Term

In this source aggressive monetization is the last step of Bending Spoons' playbook for forgotten consumer apps: after a low all-cash offer, layoffs, and centralized operations, they maximize revenue by turning one-time purchases into subscriptions and raising prices. The phrase occurs once, beside 'one-time purchases into subscriptions', 'raising prices', and 'low all-cash offer'. The machine buys $1 of profit for $3 to $5 and turns it into $13 of enterprise value, and the speaker says the same strategy works without tech risk on boring businesses that are AI-proof and recession-proof.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And finally, they maximize revenue through aggressive monetization by turning one-time purchases into subscriptions and raising prices." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DURUXfTDvmB.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as simply raising prices: monetization here also means converting one-time purchases into subscriptions, so the price rise is only half the step.
- Read as a strategy for new apps: Bending Spoons buys apps with millions of users that nobody uses or remembers, not new products, so the playbook starts from forgotten assets.
