# G_ig_regaliscapital_DS0W789AB2w — cash flowing assets

## Term

In this source cash flowing assets are what the wealthiest people buy: businesses that pay for themselves within the first year, bought and repeated over and over again, explicitly not fancy houses or watches. The phrase occurs once, beside 'repeat that process over and over again', 'pays for itself within the first year', and 'they buy businesses'. The example is a small business making 800 grand in profit, selling for around 3 million, bought for 5% down — $150, 000 upfront — and pocketing around 31 grand a month after loan payments.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "They buy cash flowing assets and repeat that process over and over again." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DS0W789AB2w.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any asset that produces income: the speaker excludes houses and watches, so the phrase here means operating businesses, not property or luxuries.
- Read as a promise of 31 grand a month for any buyer: the figure belongs to the one 800-grand example after loan payments, and the source never says every business performs the same way.
