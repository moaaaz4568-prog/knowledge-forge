# G_ig_regaliscapital_DV9gmb0DrqO — cash machines

## Term

In this source cash machines are what HVAC companies are: businesses with consistent demand that generate around 300 grand a year in profit and can be bought for around a million dollars with just 50 grand down. The phrase occurs once, beside 'generate around 300 grand a year', 'consistent demand', and 'fast track to building real wealth'. The speaker lists five boring businesses — duct cleaning, plumbing, pest control, HVAC, refrigeration — each with its profit and its small cash-down price, and stresses that every one has consistent demand.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Number four, HVAC companies. These are cash machines and everybody wants them." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DV9gmb0DrqO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as machines that print money with no work: the source still prices the HVAC company at a million dollars and 50 grand down, so the cash machine must be bought and run, not merely owned.
- Read as a claim that all five businesses are cash machines: the label is given to HVAC alone in this source, while the others are described by profit and price, not by the same phrase.
