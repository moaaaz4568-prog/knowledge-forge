# G_ig_regaliscapital_DWUe7FYD8aY — multiple

## Term

In this source a multiple is the number that turns seven small plumbing businesses into a $42 million company: each makes a million bucks in profit and sells at a four times multiple, so seven cost four million apiece, then centralizing operations and rolling them under one brand lifts the combined $7 million-a-year business to a six times multiple. The word occurs three times, beside 'centralize operations', 'roll them together under one brand', and '$42 million'. The buyers pay only 5% cash for each one using seller financing and an SBA loan, bringing the total down to just $1.4 million.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because your business is way bigger now, you can trade it at a six times multiple instead of a four times multiple." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DWUe7FYD8aY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guaranteed re-rating from four to six times: the higher multiple follows from the business being way bigger, and the source never says every roll-up earns the same uplift.
- Read as buying seven businesses for $1.4 million total: the $1.4 million is only the cash paid, with the rest financed, so the purchase price is still four million apiece.
