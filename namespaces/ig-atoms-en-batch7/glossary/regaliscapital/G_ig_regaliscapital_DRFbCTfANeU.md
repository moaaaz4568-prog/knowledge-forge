# G_ig_regaliscapital_DRFbCTfANeU — seller finance

## Term

In this source to seller finance is to ask the seller to carry most of the price: after agreeing on the price and negotiating down, the buyer asks the seller to finance three quarters of the deal, takes a bank or SBA loan for the remainder, and puts $50, 000 down on a $625, 000 business. The phrase occurs once, beside 'once you've agreed on the price', 'you negotiate down whatever', and 'a note from the bank'. The example ends with the buyer upgrading income from 60 to 250, 000 a year, which the speaker calls a pretty decent deal against starting a business from scratch.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "and then you say, I need you to seller finance three quarters of the deal." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DRFbCTfANeU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the seller giving a discount: the seller still gets the full $625, 000, only $200, 000 upfront, so the financed share is delayed payment, not a price cut.
- Read as a standard bank mortgage: the bank covers only the leftover after the seller's share, and the buyer puts in $50, 000 of the $200, 000 the bank sees.
