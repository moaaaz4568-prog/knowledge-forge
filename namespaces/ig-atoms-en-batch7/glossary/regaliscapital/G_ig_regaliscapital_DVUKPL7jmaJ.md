# G_ig_regaliscapital_DVUKPL7jmaJ — franchise-style model

## Term

In this source a franchise-style model is the pivot that turned a $30, 000 investment into a $77 million exit: Kevin Ramsier and Jamie bought a radon testing business for $60, 000, systematized everything, reinvested every dollar into vans and crews, then sold off mature territories while keeping royalties and control of lead generation. The phrase occurs once, beside 'sold off mature territories', 'keeping royalties', and 'control of lead generation'. The company was valued over $25 million in 2011 and sold to private equity for just under $80 million a few years later.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Eventually, they pivoted to a franchise-style model, and they sold off mature territories while keeping royalties and control of lead generation." — sources/S_ig_regaliscapital_DVUKPL7jmaJ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as franchising the business in the usual sense: the source describes selling territories while keeping royalties and lead generation, a model the pair built themselves, not a bought franchise brand.
- Read as a $30, 000-to-$77 million overnight flip: the exit took from 2001 to a sale a few years after 2011, with years of systematizing and city-by-city expansion in between.
