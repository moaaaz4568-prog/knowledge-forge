# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGvpiL3oaCy — modeling

## Term

Sean uses "modeling" for the distilled rule of all business: copy only people who have what you want and have caused others to have it too. The word appears twice, beside "distilled by studying all business", "what should you model", and "who should you listen to". Everything on how-to is based on modeling, he says, so the choice of model is the whole decision. His concrete filter is the only people worth modeling and listening to: those who have what you want and have caused other people to have it too.

## Synonyms

- model (the verb form Sean uses for the same act)

## Live Example

> "Modeling. So this is what I have distilled by studying all business. That this is what must happen, because everything on how to is based on modeling and what should you model and who should you listen to." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGvpiL3oaCy.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as copying anyone successful: Sean's filter is twofold, having it yourself and causing others to have it, and either alone fails the test.
- Read as a theory: he presents it as what he distilled by studying all business, a rule for what must happen, not an observation.
