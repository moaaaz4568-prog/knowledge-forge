# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGV5nKxpMdK — lever three

## Term

Sean uses "lever three" for the creation of lots more sales meetings, the step that lets sales grow exponentially. The phrase appears twice, beside "cause yeses at each lever", "exponentially grow sales meetings", and "lever four". The seven levers run from causing yeses at each step to growing meetings at lever three, sales at lever four, and disposable income at lever five. His concrete test is the challenge: you may be great at sales, but are you great at creating sales meetings, because that is what really matters.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales technique: lever three is about creating the meetings themselves, before any selling happens at lever four.
- Read as one lever among equals: Sean ranks it as the step that makes exponential growth possible, while conversion at lever four stays linear.
