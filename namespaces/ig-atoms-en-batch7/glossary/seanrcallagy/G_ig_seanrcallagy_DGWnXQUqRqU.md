# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGWnXQUqRqU — fabric

## Term

Sean uses "fabric" for how the system has become the material his whole life is wrapped in, personal and professional at once. The word appears once, beside "almost five years", "integrated into everything that I do", and "the absolute core". He says it lets him respond to clients and reach places he would never have reached without it, in medical revenue recovery. His concrete picture is a life wrapped around this fabric, from personal relationships to professional ones.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Since I've been part of it for almost five years, it's literally become the fabric that my life is wrapped around." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGWnXQUqRqU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a literal cloth or brand: fabric here is the metaphor for a system integrated into everything, and the proof Sean gives is client responses he could not have made before.
- Read as a claim about a product: the source names no product, only a way of working that became the core of what he does.
