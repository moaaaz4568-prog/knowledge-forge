# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DHGtzs2N8AN — lightning rod

## Term

Sean uses "lightning rod" for Muhammad Ali lighting the torch at the Atlanta Olympics, shaking uncontrollably from Parkinson's, as a figure of division. The phrase appears twice, beside "lighting the torch", "refusing to succumb and be silenced", and "a lightning rod of division". Ali changed his name, refused the religion they wanted for him, and chose his identity. His concrete question is the dare: would you be willing to do this to create the impact and influence this man did on the planet.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a compliment about popularity: a lightning rod of division marks someone who divides opinion by refusing to be silenced, not someone universally loved.
- Read as a story about the Olympics only: the torch is the image, and the point is Ali's refusal to succumb, which Sean offers as a model of willingness.
