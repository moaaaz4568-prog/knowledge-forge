# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGTUNwCMxPX — grateful

## Term

Sean uses "grateful" as the repeated opening of a litany for the people in the room and what they care about. The word appears eight times, beside "lessen suffering", "bring more justice and safety", and "the hearts in this room". He is grateful for the justice system protecting the minority and the weakest, for caring about homelessness, hunger, lack, anger, and war, and for people who believe in things more than themselves. His concrete close turns the litany into a question: what are you grateful for.

## Live Example

> "I'm grateful for the hearts in this room to help people who've been abused, harmed, trafficked, beaten, subjected to domestic violence." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGTUNwCMxPX.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as polite thanks: the gratitude here names specific suffering and specific protection, from abused and trafficked people to the homeless, not a general pleasantry.
- Read as a speech about Sean himself: the litany is about the hearts in the room and a higher power he calls God, and it ends by asking the listener.
