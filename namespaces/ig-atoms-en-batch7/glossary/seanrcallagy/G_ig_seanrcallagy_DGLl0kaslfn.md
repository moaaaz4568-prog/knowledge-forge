# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGLl0kaslfn — dopamine

## Term

Sean uses "dopamine" for the brain chemical we must wire deliberately instead of letting it drip for scrolling, gambling, smoking, food, and alcohol. The word appears five times, beside "endorphins, cortisol", "immediate gratification", and "long-term fulfillment". Alcohol is a massive dopamine hit, he says, and immediate gratification destroys the dopamine system. His concrete alternative is initial pain with sustained effort in zone action, which produces a different chemical effect and sustains dopamine production.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "We must wire our dopamine not to be dripping for scrolling, gambling, smoking, foods that we're eating, alcohol." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGLl0kaslfn.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on pleasure: Sean's point is wiring dopamine toward long-term fulfillment, not removing every hit, and he names effort and zone action as the replacement.
- Read as advice to hurt yourself: he explicitly says not hammers on thumbs, but relevant pain, effort that produces a different chemical effect.
