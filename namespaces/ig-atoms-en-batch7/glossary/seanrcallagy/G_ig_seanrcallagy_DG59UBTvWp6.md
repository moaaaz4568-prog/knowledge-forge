# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DG59UBTvWp6 — cause yes

## Term

Sean uses "cause yes" for getting a willing yes without pressure, and here he teaches it through his three-year-old daughter Selena feeding deer. The phrase appears twice, beside "feed deer", "integrously", and "integrity-based human influence". The deer run when Selena runs at them, and eat out of her hands once she gives them what they want, food, so she can have what she wants. His concrete rule is the contrast: a primary outcome of getting people to say yes makes them run away, while giving first makes the yes come freely.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I was teaching her how to feed deer, how to cause yes with deer, and to cause yes with deer integrously." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DG59UBTvWp6.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a closing trick: Sean calls the deer example integrous, and the yes comes because Selena loves the deer and feeds them, not because she chases them.
- Read as something that works by asking directly: the source shows asking "would you like to come to my house" makes people hear a sale coming and run.
