# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGD3d5xvrjK — residual

## Term

Sean uses "residual" for income and businesses that keep paying after the work is done, against things that begin and end. The word appears eight times, beside "sell residual", "real estate", and "ecosystem partnership". Real estate is beautiful but not residual, he says, unless a relocation partnership makes it more residual. His concrete test is the business list: litigation for Cal U law is not residual except when representing in-house and general counsel makes it so.

## Synonyms

- residually (the adverb form Sean uses for thinking this way)

## Live Example

> "You want to sell residual. You do not want to be in the business of selling things that begin and end." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGD3d5xvrjK.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as passive income with no work: Sean's residual comes from partnerships and in-house representation, structures he builds, not money that appears.
- Read as a verdict against real estate: he tells people not to get out of it, only to add businesses that are residual beside it.
