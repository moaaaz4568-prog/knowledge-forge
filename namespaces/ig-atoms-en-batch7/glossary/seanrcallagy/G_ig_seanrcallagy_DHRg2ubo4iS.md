# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DHRg2ubo4iS — refrain

## Term

This source has no conceptual term: it is a sung refrain, and the label `refrain` here says only what kind of passage this is — a repeated sung chorus — not the name of any idea in it. The line "We are the champions" appears three times, beside "No time for losers", "There is no failure", and "We will rock you". It pictures a closing celebration where failure is declared impossible and the only failure is not keeping on the journey. The entry records the refrain exactly as sung and does not turn it into a coaching concept.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Sean's own words about his audience: the lines are the Queen chorus "We are the champions", sung as a closing celebration, not a statement Sean wrote.
- Read as a claim that the audience cannot fail: the lyric says there is no failure except not keeping on the journey, which is a song's declaration, not a measured fact.
