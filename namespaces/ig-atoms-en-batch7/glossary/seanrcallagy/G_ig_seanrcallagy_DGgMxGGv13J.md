# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGgMxGGv13J — magic

## Term

Sean uses "magic" for the third more people want, after time and money: purpose, fulfillment, your why. The word appears twice, beside "call it purpose", "call it fulfillment", and "it begins with a vision". He pictures it as bringing everyone who works with you to a Caribbean island, giving a million dollars to charity in a year, or taking a private jet on twenty-four hours' notice to ski beautiful mountains. His concrete instruction is to think of one, two, or three really magical things you want and are committed to.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "What is it that you want to create in the form of magic?" — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGgMxGGv13J.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as literal magic tricks: magic here is the category of purpose and fulfillment, and Sean's examples are a vision of giving, travel, and family time.
- Read as a vague dream: he ties the vision to commitment, asking for one to three magical things you are committed to, not a wish list.
