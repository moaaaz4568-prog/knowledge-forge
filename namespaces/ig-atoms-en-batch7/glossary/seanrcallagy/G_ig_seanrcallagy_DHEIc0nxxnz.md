# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DHEIc0nxxnz — ecosystem merge

## Term

Sean uses "ecosystem merge" for co-creating with the people who matter most in your money, time, and magic game. The phrase appears twice, beside "the most important, highest paid position", "quarterback", and "people who can cause yes". The quarterback is the highest paid position in football and the pitcher in baseball because they have the most impact on the game. His concrete rule is the filter: ecosystem merge with people who can cause yes, not simply with the best people in the world.
