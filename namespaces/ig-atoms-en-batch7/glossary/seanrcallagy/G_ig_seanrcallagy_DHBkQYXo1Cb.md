# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DHBkQYXo1Cb — lightning bolts

## Term

Sean uses "lightning bolts" for the rare, disruptive drop of Zeus energy when someone is dismissive, disrespectful, condescending, or false framing. The phrase appears twice, beside "it is rare that I drop lightning bolts", "create a disruption", and "go back into our heart". He drops them only to reopen things, then goes back into the heart, because sustainable influence never comes out of fear. His concrete example is Rick in the role play, who did not want to be out of integrity, and the warning that people will get called out and confronted if they are disrespectful or dismissive.

## Synonyms

- lightning bolt (the singular form Sean uses for the same drop)

## Live Example

> "So as we think about how we show up in communication, it is rare that I drop lightning bolts in talking to people." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DHBkQYXo1Cb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a habit of shocking people: Sean says it is rare, and the bolt exists only to create a disruption before returning to the heart.
- Read as punishment: the stated purpose is reopening a conversation, not tyrannizing or oppressing anyone, which he explicitly refuses.
