# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGbCpowt3z9 — wash brains

## Term

Sean uses "wash brains" for cleaning out the dirty, filthy infections other people put into minds, and he insists it is the opposite of brainwashing. The phrase appears twice, beside "I protect", "I don't brain and wash", and "Malpractice". He gives the example of a corporate husband diseasing his company with "I don't care" talk, and promises to silence him forever. His concrete rule is the distinction: he does not brain and wash, he washes brains because they are full of infections, often put in by very well intended people.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I protect, I wash brains. I don't brain and wash. I wash brains because they are full of dirty, filthy infections that people put in, often very well intended people." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGbCpowt3z9.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as brainwashing: Sean explicitly reverses it, saying he does not brain and wash, and the washing removes infections rather than installing beliefs.
- Read as an attack on the husband: the target is the diseasing talk in the company, and Sean's stated aim is protecting the business from it.
