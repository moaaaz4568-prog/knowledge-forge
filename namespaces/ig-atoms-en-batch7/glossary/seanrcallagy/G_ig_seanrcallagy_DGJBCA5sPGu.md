# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGJBCA5sPGu — Zeus energy

## Term

Sean uses "Zeus energy" for the protective, boundary-setting force a person already has, like locks on a door. The phrase appears three times, beside "protect your family", "it's a boundary", and "leadership, management, recruiting". He teaches it as a weapon learned the way you learn to use locks: if somebody attacks your family, would you attack back. His concrete rule is to bring it forward integrously and optimally across leadership, management, recruiting, marketing, selling, and fundraising.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you lock your doors, that's Zeus energy; it's a boundary." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGJBCA5sPGu.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as aggression to use on people: Sean frames Zeus energy as protection of family and life, brought forward integrously, not as an attack.
- Read as a power only some people have: he says you have it already, and the work is learning to use what is already there.
