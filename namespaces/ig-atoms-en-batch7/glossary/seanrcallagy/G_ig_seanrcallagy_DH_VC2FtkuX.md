# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DH_VC2FtkuX — mission, vision, and purpose

## Term

Sean uses "mission, vision, and purpose" for the true aim a learnable way can actualize, once it is right in front of you. The phrase appears once, beside "actually right in front of you", "learned and mastered by every single one of you", and "win the game of everything". The way can be learned and mastered, he says, and when we are ourselves in all places and always at all times, with hierarchical societal appropriateness, we can win the game of everything. His concrete test is the question itself: what if there is actually a way, right in front of you.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And what if the way that is actually right in front of you can be learned and mastered by every single one of you to actualize your true mission, vision, and purpose in the world?" — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DH_VC2FtkuX.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a motivational slogan: the phrase names the aim the way actualizes, and the source's claim is that the way is learnable and masterable.
- Read as permission to ignore context: Sean adds hierarchical societal appropriateness as a condition on being ourselves in all places at all times.
