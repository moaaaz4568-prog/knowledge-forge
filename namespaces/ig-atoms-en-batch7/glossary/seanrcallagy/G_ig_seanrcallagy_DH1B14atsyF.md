# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DH1B14atsyF — mindset

## Term

Sean uses "mindset" for the motivation and inspiration that, on its own, cannot do a technical job. The word appears twice, beside "greatest mindset in the world", "eighty-nine mile an hour slider", and "technical how-to". The greatest mindset in the world still leaves you in trouble against a major league pitcher, and still leaves an attorney in deep trouble arguing evidence rules before a judge. His concrete rule is the gap: no matter how motivated and inspired you are, there is a technical how-to.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You got the greatest mindset in the world and try to hit an eighty-nine mile an hour slider from major league baseball pitcher." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DH1B14atsyF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a dismissal of mindset: Sean calls it the greatest in the world, and the point is that mindset alone is not the how-to, not that it is worthless.
- Read as a sports lesson: the slider and the courtroom are both examples of the same gap, motivation without technique.
