# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGYfi1Oiy6x — Zeus energy

## Term

Sean uses "Zeus energy" for the direct, boundary-setting force that leaders carry alongside softer energies, and he compares how Malcolm X, Martin Luther King, and Muhammad Ali used it. The phrase appears four times, beside "aspirational and heart energy", "goddess", and "beautiful subtlety". Malcolm X used it more directly and consistently, King led with goddess and aspirational yet carried a lot of it in the I Have a Dream speech, and Ali spoke much less in it than in his fun goddess aspirational side. His concrete measure is memory: Malcolm is remembered so much more and so much less than King because of which energy each led with.

## Live Example

> "Martin Luther King certainly had access to Zeus energy, but he led more with goddess and aspirational, but he dropped his Zeus." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGYfi1Oiy6x.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ranking of the men: Sean calls each one's purpose vital and loving, and the comparison is about which energy they led with, not who was better.
- Read as saying King lacked Zeus energy: he says King certainly had access to it and shows it with beautiful subtlety in the speech.
