# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DG--3QFPP18 — transfer energy

## Term

Sean uses "transfer energy" for the human ability to move feeling and influence from one person to another, which he traces through Washington, Lincoln, Robin Williams, Martin Luther King, Oprah Winfrey, and Muhammad Ali. The phrase appears three times, beside "ability", "challenges cortisol and dopamine wiring", and "the ability to influence people". School, teachers, parents, and bullies taught us to shut up, sit down, and not stand out, shutting this transfer down. His concrete test is the historical figures: every human being whose name we know had the ability to transfer energy, and losing it is what must go away.
