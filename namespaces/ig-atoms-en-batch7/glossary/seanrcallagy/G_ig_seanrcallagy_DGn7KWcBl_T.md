# G_ig_seanrcallagy_DGn7KWcBl_T — relationship capital

## Term

Sean uses "relationship capital" for the asset of people with relationships, the groups whose trust lets sales grow exponentially. The phrase appears once, beside "exponentially grow sales means of level three", "becoming exponentially more masterful", and "bringing on people". He names the groups: the neurosurgical society, the plastic surgical society, trial attorneys across New Jersey, Pennsylvania, and the nation, the national orthopedic society, and the national association of Hispanic realtors. His concrete memory is speaking in front of a thousand realtors a year and a half ago, and these places and possibilities are what the listener is here to learn.

> "And what we do is point this mastery requiring into directions to exponentially grow sales means of level three by becoming exponentially more masterful at bringing on people with relationship capital." — sources/S_ig_seanrcallagy_DGn7KWcBl_T.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of clients to pitch: Sean presents the societies as places to bring on people with relationship capital, not as audiences to sell to on stage.
- Read as a sales training: he says this is not a sales training, but a conversation about exponentially growing money, time, and magic.
