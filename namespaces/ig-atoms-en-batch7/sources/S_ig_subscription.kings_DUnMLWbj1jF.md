Something kind of obvious occurred to me yesterday and I can't stop thinking about it. If you're running ads to a high ticket offer, you're probably converting somewhere between 3 to 5% of your leads, which is normal. That's just how the math works. But that means you're paying for 100 leads and you're only converting 3 to 5 of them into high ticket clients. The other 95 just exist. They stay on your email list and maybe someday they'll buy, but let's be honest, most of them probably won't. And I started wondering, what if those leads could pay me while they figured out when they were ready? So I added a subscription at $97 a week for people who wanted to work with me but weren't ready for the big commitment yet. And those leads that used to give me nothing, now they're paying me every single week and a bunch of them eventually upgrade to high ticket anyway. Same ad spend for high ticket, same leads, just more of them turning into revenue. Sometimes it's not about working harder, it's about getting more out of what you're already doing.
