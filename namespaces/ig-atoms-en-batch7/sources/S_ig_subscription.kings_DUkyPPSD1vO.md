I've been thinking about something a lot lately, and I wanted to share it with you. When I was running my high ticket business, I was spending about 30 to 40 thousand dollars per month on ads to generate leads. And out of every 100 leads, maybe 20 of them would book a call. From those 20, maybe 10 would show up, and I'd probably close like three to five of them. Which meant that 97 people out of 100 gave me nothing. They just sat on my email list or ended up working with a competitor. But when I switched to a subscription model, like something clicked. I stopped paying to generate leads and I started getting paid to generate leads. Like instead of spending $100 to acquire a lead who might ghost me, I spend that same $100 to acquire a subscriber who's paying me $97 a week while I build trust with them. And when I offer them something that's higher ticket, more premium down the road, they already know me, they already trust me. The conversation, it's completely different. And don't get me wrong, I'm not saying high ticket is bad. What I am saying is, what if your subscription was your lead gen? Like what if you got paid to build your own pipeline instead of paying and hoping people that showed up to the sales call? Anyway, something I've been thinking about.
