About five or six years ago, when I was taking boxing more seriously, we had this thing at training where it was called the man in the middle. And so basically what would happen was there'll be like 20 boxers standing around a circle, and I would be in the middle and I'd have to stay in the middle for 10 minutes. And every single minute, someone fresh from outside that circle would come in and their job would be to put the pressure on me. And the exercise was to train your mind to condition yourself to be like, what do you do when you physically can't fight back, when you're so tired, when you're exhausted? Like, where does your mind go to? And what happens when those survival instincts kick in? And at that point in my life, I was running a business and it was quite stressful. I had a few team members on board and, you know, there were frequent fires to put out. But when I was training and doing this man in the middle exercise, I found myself so physically exhausted that I simply didn't have the mental capacity to feel stressed or feel any sort of pressure in business. And I think that's so powerful as a business owner. If you are doing any sort of training where you're pushing yourself physically, I think that helps massively in terms of, you know, reducing stress and reducing this pressure that we do feel inside of business. So if you're a business owner and you're not putting yourself through physical exhaustion through activity, through training, I think that you're doing yourself a massive disservice and you're causing yourself a ton of unnecessary stress in business when you could be taking that out physically through training.
