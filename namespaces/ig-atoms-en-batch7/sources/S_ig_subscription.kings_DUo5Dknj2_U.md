There is invisible revenue sitting in most high ticket businesses that nobody talks about, and it's revenue that you could be making from the leads who didn't buy. Think about it. You're spending money on ads, and for every 100 leads you pay to acquire, maybe five of them buy your high ticket offer. But what about the other 95 leads who didn't buy? They're interested. They raised their hand. The reality is is that they're just not ready yet. You know, maybe they need more time or they need more trust or they just need to see more proof. Under the current high ticket model, they give you nothing, $0. They just sit there. But what if they could pay you while they figure it out? That's what a subscription layer does. It offers a second pathway for your prospects so that you're getting paid from people who don't buy your high ticket offer. Again, these are the same leads that you're already paying for. You're just offering them a second pathway for them to take. So if you're spending money on ads right now and you don't have a subscription layer bolted onto your high ticket ecosystem, I can guarantee that you are leaving hundreds of thousands of dollars on the table, unless you just hate money.
