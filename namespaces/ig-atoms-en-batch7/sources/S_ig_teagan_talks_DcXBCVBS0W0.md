You judge other people by their worst moments, but you judge yourself by your best intentions. It's this psychological bias called the fundamental attribution error, where when somebody else does something that is bad, you assume it's part of their character and who they are, but when you do something that's bad, you assume it's situational. For example, if someone cuts you off in traffic, they're an asshole or a reckless driver. If you cut someone off in traffic, you've had a bad day, you didn't see them, you were late, you were stressed. And the moment you realize that so much of how you perceive other people is based on this internal mental bias, you develop a new sense of self-awareness and ability to resolve conflict.
