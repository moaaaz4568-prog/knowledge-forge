One of the biggest problems that I'm seeing with online fitness coaches on social media is that they spend too much time talking about themselves. When the reality is, is that your prospects don't give a shit about you. What they care about more than anything else is somebody actually understanding them on a deeper level. So if you spent less time talking about yourself and more time actually articulating the pains, the fears, the desires, the frustrations, and the roadblocks that your ideal clients are experiencing, then that's going to immediately position yourself as an authority and you're going to have more clients than you know what to do with.
