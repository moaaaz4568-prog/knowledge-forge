One of the worst pieces of advice that I used to subscribe to is that you just need to work harder in order to grow your business. If you just put in more hours, if you just outwork everybody else, you are going to build your business and scale. And what happened as a result of being subscribed to that mentality is I was working so hard that I was doing everything in my business, thinking that was the way to do it, but I was doing things that a VA could do, things that I could easily delegate. I realized I just created a high-paying job for myself. Instead of having the mindset of "Oh, I just need to work harder," how do I create more leverage in my business? How do I hire people so I can buy back time and work on things that actually matter inside of my business? Because once you master those skill sets, that's when your business really starts to take off.
