We've worked with over 650 agency owners. Many of them have hit $50k, $100k, $200k, $300,000 a month. And the clear difference between the ones that stay stuck at $10,000 or $20,000 a month and the ones actually scale past $100,000 a month is fairly simple. The $10k a month agencies—they get a new client and they think to themselves, 'How do I fit this in within my schedule?' A $100k a month agency gets a new client and they think, 'Okay, how can I create a system for this? Who do I need to hire? Who do I need to delegate in order to get this actually done?' They think of not how much work that they can handle, but how much work can be handed off so that they can work on the things that actually matter in the business and move the business forward, like the sales and the marketing. And when you can adopt this mindset even early on in your business and think of your business that way, that's how you set yourself up to actually scale.
