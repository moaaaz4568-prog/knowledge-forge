Hot take, but personality hires are the future of tech. Literally across the globe, so many insane ideas are getting funded. It almost feels like the more complicated and crazy the idea is, the more funding it gets. But a lot of genius founders are missing one thing: the ability to take their brilliant idea and dumb it down. If you can't explain your product to the average person in 30 seconds, you can't sell it, you can't recruit for it, and you certainly can't raise any money. More than anything, startups need communicators. Maybe I'm a little bit biased because I definitely consider myself to be a personality hire, but this is exactly why I built Elevator. My startup allows students just like me to pitch themselves through a 60-second video to potential employers. You can be loud, you can be charismatic, you can be funny. Most importantly, you can show that your communication skills are better than your competitors.
