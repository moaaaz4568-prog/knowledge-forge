If you had $300,000 sitting around, would you rather buy a business or invest in mutual funds? Most people choose to invest the money and let it grow over time, but here's why buying a business might be the smarter move. With $300,000, you can put a 5% down payment on a $3 million business and finance the rest using an SBA loan and seller financing. And depending on the industry, a $3 million business can generate around $800,000 a year in profit. So even after paying back the loan, you could still be left with over $400,000 a year in cash flow. On the other hand, if that same $300,000 was invested in a well-diversified mutual fund, it would earn around 8% a year, taking close to a decade to just double. And yes, both options have their place, but if you're entrepreneurial, buying a business is a much faster way to accelerate your wealth.
