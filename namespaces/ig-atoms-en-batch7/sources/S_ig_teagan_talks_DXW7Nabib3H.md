If you've ever driven on a San Francisco highway and you've seen those super meta AI billboards, you've probably noticed that none of them actually make that much sense. And these companies are pouring thousands of dollars into making sure their logo gets in front of your face, so there has to be some kind of larger marketing strategy at play. It's basically called high context marketing, insider marketing to put it more simply. These billboards are not trying to be understood by everyone. They're trying to be instantly recognizable to a very specific niche audience. They're betting that of the thousands of people who pass by and are confused, a very small handful will get it immediately. I think this says a lot about the direction of marketing in AI, where it's so overheated, everyone is claiming to build the future, and therefore attention is extremely scarce. But if your slogan is so specific that the exact people you're trying to target feel like you're speaking directly to them, almost like a wink, you win and the billboard did exactly what it was supposed to do.
