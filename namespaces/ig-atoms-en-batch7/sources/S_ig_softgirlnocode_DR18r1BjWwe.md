Ever since I started going to a new gym, I realized there is something missing. An app just for girls who go to the same gym. The goals are simple: make friends, find gym buddies, and avoid awkwardness of approaching in person. I wanted to bring back old school online social vibe where you only saw someone's digital avatar. It made everything feel warm and low pressure, like meeting a familiar character for the first time. So I knew I wanted to create a cozy anime style UI. After 10 rounds of prompting in Replit, I finally had my first version. You choose your gym and you get a comic style floor plan filled with illustrated girly avatars who go there as well. You can scroll left and right through the room or switch to a clean list view. Tap on an avatar to see her details, favorite workouts. If you notice someone who goes around the same time as you, you can send a high five as a simple nudge that says, hey, I will be there too. Oh, and don't forget to set up your own profile. There's also direct messaging and interest-based groups make it easy to stay connected.
