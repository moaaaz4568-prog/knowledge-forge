There's a reason why the most successful people you know, the most accredited founders, are also the most insecure, and it's not because they don't have self-esteem. I was having a conversation with one of my friends the other day about the difference between self-esteem and self-worth, and he mentioned that to him, self-esteem can be measured on a scale from zero to 100. For example, you get a good-looking girlfriend and it can go up from 50 to 53. You get into a good school, it can go up from 53 to 63. But self-worth is something that you cannot develop through achieving certain concrete things. It is an intrinsic belief that your life matters. I think ambition is oftentimes born from insecurity.
