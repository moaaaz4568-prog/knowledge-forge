You don't need more leads for your business. What you need is somewhere for those leads to go. Because right now, if you are a high ticket coach, you probably only have one door for them to walk through, and that door costs somewhere between $3, 000 to $10, 000, maybe even $20, 000. And if they can't financially invest, you know, you might offer them a two-pay or a three-pay. But outside of that, what happens to all of these leads that can't afford your high ticket offer? They just end up rotting on your email list. They go cold and eventually they end up working with a competitor who does have another door for them to walk through. And this is why having a subscription is so powerful, because it allows you to build trust with these prospects. It allows you to help them get some wins on the board. It allows you to sell them on your methodology, and then when they're ready to invest into high ticket, you stay top of mind. Because if you don't have somewhere else for them to go, and they just end up falling on the nurture list, they're only going to end up working with somebody else who does have somewhere for them to go. Something to think about.
