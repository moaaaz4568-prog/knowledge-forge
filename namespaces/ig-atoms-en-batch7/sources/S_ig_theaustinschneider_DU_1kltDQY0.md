One of the most commonly asked questions that we get is should I niche down or should I keep my offer broad? And what people are really asking is if I niche down will increase my likelihood of closing a deal and the answer is simple, no. Unless you're doing $200,000 to $300,000 a month, niching down will not be in your favor. What we want to do is we want to optimize for total addressable market. The problem with niching down is that you are drastically shrinking your total addressable market, therefore decreasing your likelihood of closing a deal because your offer can likely serve multiple different industries.
