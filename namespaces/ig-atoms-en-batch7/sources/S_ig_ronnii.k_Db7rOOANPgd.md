So this is the correct cold outreach sequence that you need to follow if you are doing cold outreach on Instagram or email or any other platform. So basically you don't send a DM directly. You firstly have to define your ICB, which is your ideal client profile, so that you can find leads in bulk based on your ICP. Then once you have find the leads in bulk in very high volume, then you need to qualify and filter them out and you need to remove the best, highest quality leads possible. And after qualifying the leads in bulk, then you can send the DMs finally. The most important thing you need to follow in cold outreach is sending DMs which are perfect fit for your ICP. If you just send the DMs to the perfect fit leads, then probably your reply rates will go higher and you will convert better.
