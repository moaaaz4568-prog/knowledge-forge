I left college to work full time for a tech startup in San Francisco. I am 100% going to regret this at some point. But if I stayed in school, I'd regret that too. This is day 16 of betting on myself, and today I want to talk about why every decision is also a loss. In Kierkegaard's Either/Or, he argues that no matter what life you choose, you will always wonder about the path not taken. And that is terrifying. Because it means there's no right choice, just a choice, and a version of your life you'll never get to live. And you can let that terrify you, or you can take it as permission to stop trying to make the perfect decision. For me, risk is always better than wondering what if. And while regret may find me at some point no matter what, I would much rather regret something I chose than something I was too scared to do.
