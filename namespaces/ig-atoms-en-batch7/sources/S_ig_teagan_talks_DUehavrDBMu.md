As a college student working multiple tech internships, here's the one thing that nobody tells you about how to land a job. When you cold apply to something, you are basically volunteering to be filtered. You're competing against thousands of other resumes based on keywords. Every startup role I've gotten has come from direct outreach. Sure, sometimes I will toss my resume into the pile, but I will never apply to a job without being certain that my name is at the top of someone's inbox. And I won't just reiterate what's on my resume. I'll add actual value to the company. Sometimes I'll audit their landing page, or I'll come up with a social media campaign. Regardless, I'm essentially showing them why they should hire me before they even know who I am.
