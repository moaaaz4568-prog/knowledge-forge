When I was 21 years old, my mother died and I fell into severe depression. And I hired a human behavior coach. And I remember one day he brought me to the front of the room and he said, "Dan, are you depressed?" And I said, "Yeah, I have depression." And he goes, "Oh, interesting. Are you depressed right now? Like right now, in this moment?" And I said, "Oh, no, not right now." And he goes, "Oh, so you were depressed in the past?" And I was a bit confused and he said, "Dan, what happened in the past, whether it was one second, one minute, one hour, one day, one week, one month, one year ago, he said, what happened in the past and who you were is not who you are. And who you are is whoever the fuck you decide to be in this very moment and change can happen in an instant." And he said, "But what happens, Dan, is because your actions are governed by what you believe to be true. If you don't believe that change can happen in an instant, then it won't." And that was like the biggest light bulb moment for me.
