I work at a startup called Bond, where half of the team is based in Europe, and because of that, I've obviously noticed a ton of cultural differences between the way that I work and the way that they work. But I would say the biggest lesson I have learned from them is that being blunt and straightforward is the best way to show that you respect someone's time. I specifically remember there was a user of Bond who was flooding the Slack channel with so much feedback—things that could be improved, things they didn't like, bugs they encountered—and my immediate reaction was to jump to defense of the product. But I will never forget our CTO Floor wrote in the Slack channel, "This guy is awesome. Look at how much he cares. Like this could be our next champion." And the moment I read that, I feel like a switch turned on in my brain because it made me realize how much people in the startup industry value saving their time. And I think in Europe there's this culture of directness as a sign of respect that in America we don't really have yet and should definitely adopt.
