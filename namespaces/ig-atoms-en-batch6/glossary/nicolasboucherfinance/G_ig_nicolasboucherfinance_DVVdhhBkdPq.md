# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVVdhhBkdPq — personal CFO

## Term

Personal CFO here is the Custom GPT Nicolas builds from his own documents so it writes like him and understands his company. The phrase appears three times, beside Custom GPT, digital twin, LinkedIn profile, CV, company website, and examples of his emails, and the build is concrete: add instructions about how you write and who you are, add your CV and company website, add email examples, and the result acts like a digital twin. He also uses it as a coach, because it knows him and his context well enough to brainstorm with. The personal CFO is a trained copy of its owner, not a generic finance chatbot.
