# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DX7vU0LThh_ — three-statement financial model

## Term

Three-statement financial model here is the model Claude builds in an Excel file in five minutes from one written prompt, the whole subject of the video. The phrase appears once, beside its shorter form "three-statement model", Claude, five years, and a software company, and the prompt itself is the content: a product revenue tab with three subscription tiers, a cost product tab, a headcount planning tab with costs, and summarized results with important graphs in each tab. The model is the output of that prompt, not a file he hands out.

## Synonyms

- three-statement model (the shorter form used in this source)
