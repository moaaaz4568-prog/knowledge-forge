# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVoPNPQiRor — prompt optimization technique

## Term

Prompt optimization technique here is asking AI to improve your own prompt before you reuse it, so the rewritten prompt works better than the one you wrote. The phrase appears once, beside intention, improve this prompt, and reuse this prompt, and the move is simple: AI starts with your intention, you ask "Please improve this prompt," and you see how well your prompt is written. His concrete payoff is the reuse: once improved, you can reuse this prompt and ask AI to work on it again and again. The technique treats the prompt itself as the work to improve, not the answer it produces.
