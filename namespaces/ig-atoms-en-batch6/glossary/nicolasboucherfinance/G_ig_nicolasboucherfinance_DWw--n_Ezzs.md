# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DWw--n_Ezzs — Copilot in Excel

## Term

Copilot in Excel here is the Copilot version of the one-prompt Excel cleaning: tabs merged, empty lines and columns removed, in one minute. The phrase appears once, beside cleaning Excel files, consolidate all the tabs, remove all empty lines, and remove all empty columns, and the prompt is the whole instruction: consolidate all the tabs into one, then remove all empty lines and remove all empty columns. His concrete image is the ease: it is as simple as asking somebody in your team to do it. Copilot in Excel is the tool that runs the prompt, not the prompt itself.
