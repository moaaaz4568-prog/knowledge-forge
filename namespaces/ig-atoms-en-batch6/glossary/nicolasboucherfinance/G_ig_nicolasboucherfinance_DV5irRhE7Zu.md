# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DV5irRhE7Zu — CSV files

## Term

CSV files here are the 80 monthly sales files a German tax audit forced Nicolas to re-book line by line, a week of cleaning Excel by hand. The phrase appears twice, beside closing, tax audit, accountant, and consolidate, and the files existed because his sales had been booked as one amount per month. His concrete fix is the story of the video: AI consolidated the 80 files into one in about 15 minutes, then two or three iterations of asking it to change columns and clean the file produced the perfect code, which now runs his whole closing at the click of a button. The files matter as the raw material AI had to merge, not as the work itself.
