# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DW9tW9vyKqm — tier three

## Term

Tier three here is the highest tier of the three-tier model for handling confidential data: critical and confidential information, where you must know both which AI tool and which people should have access. The phrase appears twice, beside tier one, tier two, public data, internal information, and enterprise grade security, and the tiers are the decision: tier one is public data, where you do not care which tool it goes to, and tier two is internal information, where you must know which AI tool you can use. His concrete demand is to decide how you handle confidential data before you upload anything into your AI tool, and to do a proper analysis of whether the tool has enterprise grade security. Tier three is a gate, not a label.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "tier three means use a better tool": the tier decides who may access the information as well as which tool may touch it.
- Read as a classification you do after uploading: the decision comes before anything is uploaded into the AI tool.
