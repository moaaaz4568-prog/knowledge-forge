# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DW0_R6QkRgt — Canvas

## Term

Canvas here is Gemini's secret feature for creating dynamic dashboards in minutes, without knowing how to code. The word appears twice, beside dynamic dashboards, BI team, storytelling, infographic, and financials, and the video's contrast is the cost of the old way: imagine how long it will take a BI team to code something like this, while the dashboards are super pretty, really efficient, and follow a good storytelling. His concrete move is to click the plus in Gemini, activate the Canvas, and ask for an infographic on the financials, then bring it to the meeting or send it by email. Canvas is the place the dashboard is made, not the dashboard itself.
