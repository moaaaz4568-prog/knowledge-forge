# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVa4j4EkUi8 — custom GPT

## Term

Custom GPT here is the own-built GPT that marks an advanced AI user, set against merely knowing how to prompt. The phrase appears once, beside Copilot agents, gems, advanced user, and productivity, and the video's complaint is specific: even people who think they are advanced do not know how to use custom GPTs, Copilot agents, or gems, though he has seen all of that in his course. His concrete advice is to create your own custom GPT, your own Copilot agents, or your own gems, because that saves a lot of hours and increases productivity when using AI. The custom GPT is something you build for yourself, not a prompt you write once.
