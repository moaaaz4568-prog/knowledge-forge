# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DWGTppkEUAU — fact checking technique

## Term

Fact checking technique here is asking AI to give the fact, give the source, and review online whether the source is right — instead of searching yourself whether a tax rate AI gave you is correct. The phrase appears once, beside facts, source, tax rate, and back up all of the work, and the video's problem is specific: often we ask AI for facts, but you might not know where it comes from, and you might not know the source. His concrete move is to ask AI to give the fact, to give the source, to review online if the source is the right one and if it is exactly the same tax rate that it gave you. The technique backs up the work with AI, not with your own searching.
