## Term

Excel AI agent here is the agent inside Excel that does all of the Excel work — models, formulas, graphs — prettier and faster than a human, in five minutes. The phrase appears three times, twice inside the fuller name Excel AI Agent Mode, beside best revolution since ChatGPT, simple models, and correct itself, and the video's warning is the point: the mode does 80-90% of simple models, but always check the work, because at the end you are the human and you are the one owning the work. His concrete safety net is the correction loop: you can always ask the Excel AI Agent Mode to correct itself. The agent does the work, but the ownership stays yours.
