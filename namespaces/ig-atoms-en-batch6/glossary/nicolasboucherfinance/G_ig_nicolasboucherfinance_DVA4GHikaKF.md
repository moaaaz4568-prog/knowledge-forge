# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVA4GHikaKF — structured data

## Term

Structured data here means data laid out in columns with transactions in lines, the shape AI needs before it can analyse anything. The phrase appears once, beside AI ready, raw data, ERP, and systems, and Nicolas contrasts it with tables made for humans, which give AI a bad output. His concrete test is the source of the data: raw data pulled from your ERP or your systems is the most ideal, because it already arrives in that column-and-line shape. Preparing the data is therefore the first step, not an afterthought to the analysis.

> "You need to make your data AI ready. AI likes to have structured data." — sources/S_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVA4GHikaKF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "any spreadsheet works": tables made for humans are exactly what he says will not give a good output.
- Read as formatting for looks: the structure is what lets AI analyse the tables, so a pretty human table that hides the structure still fails.
