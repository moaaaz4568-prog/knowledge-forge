# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DX2OvMbTcbn — Claude Artifacts

## Term

Claude Artifacts here is the Claude tool to use when you need to make a dashboard, the first pair in a routing of tools by job and by provider. The phrase appears once, beside ChatGPT Canvas, Claude Projects, ChatGPT Projects, and Claude in PowerPoint, and the routing is the whole video: Claude plus a dashboard means Claude Artifacts, ChatGPT plus a dashboard means ChatGPT Canvas, a client proposition means Claude Projects or ChatGPT Projects, and a PowerPoint means Claude in PowerPoint or the new 5.5 model in ChatGPT. His concrete rule is the pairing itself: the tool follows the job and the provider you already have. Claude Artifacts is a routing choice, not a better dashboard.
