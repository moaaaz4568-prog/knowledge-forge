# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVOGTkNkTe9 — workshop

## Term

Workshop here is the two-hour, learning-by-doing session that teaches a whole team to use AI, the middle step of his rollout for avoiding useless tools. The word appears three times, once as "two-hour workshop", beside professional license, champions, Slack channel, Teams channel, and hackathon, and the session is run either by an outside expert or by the team's own champions, the ones who are more advanced. His concrete sequence puts the workshop between two other moves: give every employee a professional licence first, then run the workshop, then keep a shared channel for use cases and finish with a hackathon to build a custom GPT, a GEMs, or a Copilot agent. The workshop is where the team learns by doing, not where an expert lectures them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as training only a few people: the licence and the workshop cover the whole team, not a small part of the employees.
- Read as a one-off event: the shared channel and the hackathon carry the learning into daily use cases after the workshop ends.
