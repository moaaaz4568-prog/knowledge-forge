# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DVlVjPmEULd — CSI and FBI framework

## Term

CSI and FBI framework here is Nicolas's own prompting framework, taught to more than ten thousand people, that moves a prompt from general to specific. The phrase appears once, beside context, specific, instruct, format, blueprint, and identity, and the letters are the method: CSI gives the context about who you are and where you work, names the specific problem, and instructs AI what to do, while FBI adds the format — a table, a PowerPoint, code — and the identity, an expert in cost cutting, a teacher, a mentor, a coach. His concrete case is reducing cost: ask for the plan, drill down into one action you like, ask for more details, then ask how to implement it. The framework is a sequence of moves, not a single magic prompt.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a crime-solving joke: the crime words are only the memory hook; the framework itself is context, specific, instruct, format, identity.
- Read as "good input is enough": CSI alone gives quite a good prompt, and the excellent value comes only when FBI is added.
