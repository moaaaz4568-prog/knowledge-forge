# G_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DWOy4WQCQvs — Socratic prompting

## Term

Socratic prompting here is telling AI to ask you questions, so the questions surface what you never thought about and lead somewhere you would never have imagined. The phrase appears once, beside Socrates, root cause, and asking you questions, and the video's logic is the reversal: instead of you questioning AI, you let AI ask you questions, which also lets AI know more about the topic. The goal is to find the root cause, and that works only by asking you questions. Socratic prompting is a questioning direction, not a question format.
