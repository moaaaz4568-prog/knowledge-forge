# G_ig_onlyzita_CzMI5Mkr0wF — Bardeen

## Term

Bardeen is the ChatGPT plugin onlyzita recommends to people who want AI to do all the work for them. The name appears three times in the source, beside "tweets", "Google Sheet", "personalized emails" and "scrape the data". Installed from the ChatGPT plugin store, it automates tasks right inside ChatGPT: saving thousands of tweets into a Google Sheet given a keyword, getting the data of every person who comments on a LinkedIn post, creating personalized emails for hundreds of people, and scraping data off almost any site. His concrete picture of the payoff is laying on the couch, watching Netflix, while AI does the work.
