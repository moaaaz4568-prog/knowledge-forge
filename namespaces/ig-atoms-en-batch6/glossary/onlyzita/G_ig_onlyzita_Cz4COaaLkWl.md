# G_ig_onlyzita_Cz4COaaLkWl — cold email

## Term

A cold email is the outreach message onlyzita puts in step two of scaling Iman Gadzhi's business model in 60 seconds with AI. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "ListKit", "Woodpecker", "potential clients" and "Facebook ads". The sequence is to choose a service to sell, find potential clients with ListKit, and use the ChatGPT prompt on screen to write an effective cold email, then automate sending the messages with Woodpecker. The concrete setting is Iman selling Facebook ads for businesses, but onlyzita stresses any in-demand service on screen can be chosen instead.
