# G_ig_onlyzita_CzHBZFArDfl — Bardeen

## Term

Bardeen is the third ChatGPT plugin onlyzita wishes he knew about earlier, presented as the automation plugin for business owners. The name appears three times in the source, beside "LinkedIn", "Google Sheet", "automations" and "most popular sites". It runs automations on popular sites, and his concrete example is finding everyone who liked a post on LinkedIn and putting all their data into a Google Sheet. The other two plugins in the same video are Diagram Show Me, which makes a flowchart or mind map on any topic, and Video Summary, which summarizes a full-length YouTube video from just the link.
