# G_ig_onlyzita_CyWCh4WO6Hn — Viral Finder

## Term

Viral Finder is the tool onlyzita uses in step one of automating content creation: finding viral videos in a particular space to use as inspiration. The name appears once in the source, beside "Talk Audit", "content ideas", "viral videos" and "likely to go viral". He pairs it with Talk Audit to find videos of people in a space, then scripts the content with proven ChatGPT prompts and Midro, and finishes the videos with Adobe Podcast, Submagic and CapCut. The concrete payoff he claims is over 100 shorts a month for clients with 90% of the creation automated, and millions of views for himself and his clients.
