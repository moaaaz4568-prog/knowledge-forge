# G_ig_onlyzita_Czl-oc_uwqY — controversial script

## Term

A controversial script is the end product of onlyzita's method for creating viral video scripts by copying the top writers in the world with AI. The singular appears once in the source and the plural "controversial scripts" appears once more, beside "Content Rewriter", "Medium", "viewers' emotions" and "top writers".
