# G_ig_onlyzita_CyqrES9Lwa0 — $100 million offer

## Term

The $100 million offer is the offer onlyzita says a ChatGPT prompt can create for a business in a few seconds. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "irresistible offer", "ideal audience", "perceived value" and "feel stupid saying no". The method is to paste the prompt on screen, change everything in bold to the brand, and get an offer that attracts the ideal audience and increases the perceived value so the business gets paid more. The concrete warning behind it is his opening claim that a bad offer is killing the business, set against begging for pennies on the street versus driving a Lamborghini with a Swiss model.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Go to ChatGPT and put this prompt in that you see on the screen right now to create a $100 million offer that will make people feel stupid saying no." — sources/S_ig_onlyzita_CyqrES9Lwa0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a price the business should charge: the $100 million is the name of the offer framework in the prompt, not a price tag on anything the business sells.
- Read as a guarantee of sales: the source only says the offer will attract the ideal audience and increase perceived value, and no sale resulting from it is shown.
