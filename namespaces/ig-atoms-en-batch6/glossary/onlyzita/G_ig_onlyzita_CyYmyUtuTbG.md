# G_ig_onlyzita_CyYmyUtuTbG — lead magnet

## Term

A lead magnet is the free ebook or mini course onlyzita puts at the center of his five-step framework for warm leads. The phrase appears five times in the source, beside "ebook", "mini course", "email and phone number" and "warm leads". The framework is to post content giving value, create the lead magnet, give it to followers for free, and in exchange collect the email and phone number of everybody who wanted it, then email and call every one of them. His concrete proof is a lead magnet he posted the other day that over a thousand people wanted, which he counts as a thousand warm leads he could sell his service to directly.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I posted a lead magnet the other day and over a thousand people wanted it." — sources/S_ig_onlyzita_CyYmyUtuTbG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that a thousand downloads mean a thousand sales: the source only counts them as warm leads he could sell to directly, and no sale is shown.
- Read as a free gift with no catch: the magnet is free, but the exchange is explicit — the follower hands over an email and phone number, and then gets emailed and called.
