# G_ig_onlyzita_CzT6a0brn13 — video summary plugin

## Term

The video summary plugin is the ChatGPT plugin onlyzita uses to turn a viral YouTube video into a video script in less than 30 seconds. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "viral framework", "60 second summary", "YouTube video" and "scripting content". The method is to install the plugin, copy the link of a video going viral in the space, ask ChatGPT to summarize it, and then use the prompt on screen to turn the short summary into a script built on a proven viral framework. His concrete claim is that scriptwriters are officially dead, because a script that gets millions of views can be produced this way in under 30 seconds.
