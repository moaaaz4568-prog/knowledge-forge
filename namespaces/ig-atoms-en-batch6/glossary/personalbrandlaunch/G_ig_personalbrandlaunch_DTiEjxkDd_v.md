# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTiEjxkDd_v — structure

## Term

A structure is a reusable script shape — the 'if you want X use Y' structure, a high-performing video structure, a viral hook and format — that Ava lifts from one video and fits to a client's niche. The word appears 4 times, beside niche, adapted, adjusted, and viral, and nearly every sentence follows the same arc: she noticed it working, adjusted it to a client's niche, and it performed well. Her concrete case is the wedding niche: a viral men-versus-women hook, adjusted to a client's wedding niche, performed really well. The payoff line names the craft: take small components from outlier videos, in your niche or other people's, and adapt them into a masterpiece that is virally proven.

- Read as stealing scripts: the source's move is adapting the base of a structure to a new niche, not copying the script — the words change even when the shape stays.
