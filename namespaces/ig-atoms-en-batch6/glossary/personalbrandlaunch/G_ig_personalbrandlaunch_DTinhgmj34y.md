# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTinhgmj34y — comparison hooks

## Term

Comparison hooks are the first family in Ava's hundred-hooks-in-a-minute tour: specific comparisons — taste, activity, price, product, time, metric — presented through popular templates and one-off hooks. The plural phrase appears once, beside comparisons, templates, tutorial, and transformation, and the video then marches through tutorial, transformation, educational, mistakes and myth-busting, tier list, rating, ranking, and cost breakdown hooks the same way. Her concrete case is the comparison menu itself: six kinds of comparison, each with its own templates, before the tour moves on. The video is a catalog, not a lesson — it names the families and points at templates without teaching any single hook in depth.
