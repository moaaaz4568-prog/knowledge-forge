# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTda8l0jup0 — niche

## Term

A niche is the place a borrowed structure has to be fitted into: Ava notices a high-performing structure, a viral storytelling structure, or a hook format, and adapts or adjusts it to her niche to get the same results. The word appears 8 times, beside structure, hook format, outlier videos, and the verbs adapted and adjusted, and the repetition is the lesson — every example ends the same way, with the structure working in her niche. Her concrete case is the cross-language one: a video structure in a different language, working extremely well, adjusted to her niche and recreated. The rule underneath is to take small components of outlier videos, in your niche or not, and readjust them to fit you rather than copying the whole video.
