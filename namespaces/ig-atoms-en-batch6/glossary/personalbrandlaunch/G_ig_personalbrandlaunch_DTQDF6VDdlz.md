# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTQDF6VDdlz — Competitive Insights

## Term

Competitive Insights is Instagram's feature for stalking competitors the allowed way: Ava's 45-second tutorial walks through opening it from the Professional Dashboard and picking up to 10 competitor accounts. The name appears once, beside Professional Dashboard, competitor accounts, trial reels, and growth, and the payoff is comparing post counts, post types, and analytics against your own page. Her concrete case is step three — selecting the 10 accounts whose insights you want to see — after which the advice is to study their content for common patterns. The feature is new and still rolling out, so Ava adds the fallback for viewers who do not have it yet: sort a competitor's posts from most to least viral on your phone.
