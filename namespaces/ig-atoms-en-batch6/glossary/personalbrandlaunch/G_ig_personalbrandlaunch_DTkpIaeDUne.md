# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTkpIaeDUne — founder story

## Term

A founder story is video number one of the four every creator-founder should make: your story as a founder, how you built your business from zero to where it is now. The phrase appears once, beside pajama business, product story, breakdown, and day in the life, and the example is Ava's own arc — a pajama business started at nineteen with eight hundred dollars, sold last year for fifty-two million. Her concrete case is the arc's shape: start small and specific, then jump to the win, with every idea tried before the big win in between. The other three videos — product or service story, product or service breakdown, and day in the life — all hang off this first one, because the founder is the reason the business exists.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a biography video: the founder story here is a business arc — zero to now, with the big win — not a life story or a personal confession.
- Read as the only video a founder needs: it is number one of four, and the product story, breakdown, and day in the life are presented as equally required.
