# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DUEEpU0lg91 — four-line framework

## Term

A four-line framework is Ava's shape for a bio: four lines, in order, as part of a full profile setup — username, picture, tagline, bio, highlights, pin post. The phrase appears once, beside bio, username, tagline, and highlights, and the bio is one item in a checklist that runs from the username down to the pinned videos. Her concrete case is the tagline beside it: a name, a line, and then an occupation or title, chosen because that is searchable on Instagram. The four lines themselves are never given in this source, so the framework names the container — four lines — not the lines to write.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a bio template: the four lines are never spelled out here, so nothing in this source can be copied into a bio as-is.
- Read as the whole profile guide: the framework is one step among six — username, picture, tagline, bio, highlights, pin post — and the other steps carry their own instructions.
