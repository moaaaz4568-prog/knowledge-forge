# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DU03Daujn1V — top of funnel

## Term

Top of funnel is the first of three content layers: easy-to-understand education with simple structures, which gets views and engagement. The phrase appears once, beside middle-of-funnel, bottom-of-funnel, storytelling, and authority, and each layer has its own payoff — middle gets views and followers, bottom gets followers and leads. Her concrete case is the failure mode: post only one type and you get a following with inconsistent views, but post all three and people know, like, and trust you. The funnel is a mix to rotate, not a ladder to climb once — the personal brand comes from holding all three at once.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales funnel for strangers: the layers describe content types for followers and leads on social media, not a checkout or email sequence.
- Read as 'start at the top and graduate': the source says posting all three types together is what builds the brand — dropping the top layer after 'graduating' would break the mix.
