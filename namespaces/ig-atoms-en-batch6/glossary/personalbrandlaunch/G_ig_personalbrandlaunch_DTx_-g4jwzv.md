# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTx_-g4jwzv — viral outlier

## Term

A viral outlier is a topic or hook borrowed from a video that broke out — and Ava's cadence guide says to use viral outlier topics and hooks for your videos often. The phrase appears once, beside often, from time to time, and every once in a while, and the whole video is a frequency map: educational content often, storytelling from time to time, sales content every once in a while. Her concrete case is the pairing itself: viral outlier material gets the 'often' slot, while doubling down on winning videos gets only 'from time to time'. The outlier is an input to borrow, not a video to clone — the guide also reserves a slot for completely original videos with no inspiration used.
