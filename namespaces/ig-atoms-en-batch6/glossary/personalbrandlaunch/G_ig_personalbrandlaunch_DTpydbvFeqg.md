# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTpydbvFeqg — batch

## Term

To batch is to do one kind of work all at once on a schedule: scripts once per week, content research once per month, film once per month, edit and review once per month. The word appears 3 times, beside scripts, research, film, and edit, and the schedule is the whole video — daily story, weekly scripts, monthly research, with reels daily and carousels weekly. Her concrete case is the monthly block: a bunch of content research all at once per month, instead of researching before every video. Batching is a rhythm here, not a bulk upload — nothing in the source says to post everything at once.
