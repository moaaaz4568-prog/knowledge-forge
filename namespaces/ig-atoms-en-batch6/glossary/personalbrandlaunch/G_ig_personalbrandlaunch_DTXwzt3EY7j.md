# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTXwzt3EY7j — outliers

## Term

An outlier is a video on your own page that did better than average — and Ava's laziest growth move is to repost it in a new format with nearly the same script. The plural outliers appears once, beside reformat, re-edit, re-film, and repost, and the whole trick is step one: scroll your page and find one. Her concrete case is her own test: the first post got one view count, the second post of the exact same script got another, and the same held for all of these videos. The laziness is strategic, not sloppy: the format changes while the proven script stays, because data already says the script works.
