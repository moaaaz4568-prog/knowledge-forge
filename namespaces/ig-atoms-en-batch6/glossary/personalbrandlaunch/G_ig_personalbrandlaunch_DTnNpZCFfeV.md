# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTnNpZCFfeV — pinned post

## Term

A pinned post is a profile's fixed front door: Ava's formula pins the right videos per creator type — a founder story and an authority reel for service businesses, a product creation story and a before and after for product businesses. The phrase pinned post appears once, beside pin, founder story, and authority reel, while the short form pin runs through the whole formula — pin number one, pin number two, pin number three. Her concrete case is the service-based business: pin one is my story or a founder story, pin two is the authority reel showing how you work with clients. The formula changes by creator type, so there is no single set of three videos that fits everyone.

- Read as pinning your most viral videos: the formula pins by role — story, authority, product creation — not by view count, and the most viral video is only pin three for educators.
