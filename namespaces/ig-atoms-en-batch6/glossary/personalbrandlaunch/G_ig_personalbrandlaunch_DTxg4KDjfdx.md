# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTxg4KDjfdx — CPS

## Term

CPS — cuts per second — is Ava's explanation for why one video outdrew another: the high-view videos had three to six shots or cuts inside the hook, the low-view ones had a single shot. The abbreviation appears once, beside cuts, shots, hook, and views, and the spelled-out form cuts per second appears once with it, so the term is defined in the same breath it is introduced. Her concrete case is the A-or-B test: viewers pick which video got more views, and the answer is always the one with more cuts in the hook. The fix is small and mechanical — add multiple cuts or shots within the hook — which is why the video treats CPS as a lever, not a theory.
