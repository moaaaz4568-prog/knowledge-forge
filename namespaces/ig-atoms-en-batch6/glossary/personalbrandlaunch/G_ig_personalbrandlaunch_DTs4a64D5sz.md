# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTs4a64D5sz — double down

## Term

To double down is to repeat a winning hook until it stops working: one creator posted the same hook nine times and went viral every single time. The phrase appears twice, beside hook, repeat, visual, verbal, and written, and the source splits hooks three ways — what you see, what you hear, and what you read in the first three seconds. Her concrete case is the face question: what makes Italian men's faces so attractive, Danish women's, Indian women's — nine nationalities, one hook, repeated. The goal is stated plainly: keep posting until you get one of these videos, then double down on it and print results.
