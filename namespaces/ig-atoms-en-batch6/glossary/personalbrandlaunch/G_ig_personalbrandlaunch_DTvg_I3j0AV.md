# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTvg_I3j0AV — connected reach

## Term

Connected reach is one of Instagram's two algorithms: the one for people who already follow you, fed by likes and retention. The phrase appears twice, beside unconnected reach, followers, retention, and shares, and the rule is symmetrical — do well with followers and the content spreads to more followers; do well with strangers and it spreads to strangers. Her concrete case is the content that wins connected reach: hyper-educational, problem-aware, or storytelling content, because people who know you respond to the personal. The choice between the two algorithms follows the goal, though most people end up catering to both.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as two separate apps: the two reaches are two rankings inside one Instagram — the same video can win one, the other, or both.
- Read as 'likes are everything': likes and retention drive connected reach, but shares and retention drive unconnected reach, so the metric that matters depends on the audience.
