# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DUGpPwPDtrf — batch

## Term

To batch is the dividing line in this do-and-don't list: Ava batches research, outlier hooks, and topics once per month, and batches scripting, filming, and editing once per month, against doing each of them daily. The word appears twice, beside research, outlier hooks, script, and film, and the monthly rhythm is the point — an outlier educational hook that worked six months ago still has a good shot today. Her concrete case is the evergreen contrast: she creates educational and storytelling content people will watch and search today and years from now, while trend content dies with the trend. Batching here buys durability: the work is done in monthly blocks so the videos keep performing after the trend is gone.
