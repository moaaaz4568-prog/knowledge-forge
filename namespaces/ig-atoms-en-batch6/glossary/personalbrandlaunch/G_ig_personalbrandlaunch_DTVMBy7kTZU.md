## Term

A series is method number one for growing like the creators Ava studied: pick a niche/topic, pick a series type, draft it, then script, film, edit, and post episode by episode. The word appears 8 times, beside niche/topic, episodes, outline, and posting schedule, and the named types are the daily series, the progress update series, and the lessons series. Her concrete case is the setup order: the niche/topic comes first, the series type second, and only then the rough draft with its episode count and outline. The series is presented as a proven method — used successfully by a ton of creators — rather than as a single viral video to copy.
