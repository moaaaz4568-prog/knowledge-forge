# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DUBAUHqDcmK — script structures

## Term

Script structures are the engine of trends here: a viral video's shape gets turned into a fill-in-the-blank template, adjusted to a new niche, and it goes viral again. The phrase appears 5 times, beside trends, fill-in-the-blank, viral, and niche, and the video names both kinds — easy ones to spot, like the bad-good-excellent ranking, and complex ones that are hard to spot while watching. Her concrete case is the eggs ranking: fried eggs are bad for weight loss, scrambled are good, boiled are excellent — a fitness video whose structure a smart creator moved into another niche. The warning is attached: never repeat fluffy structures; every one must carry value or education, or the trend is empty.
