# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DUGHeJ6DnMT — stories

## Term

Stories are the contrast in this do-and-don't list: Ava pairs what she does against what she never does — multiple stories back to back, then waiting for them to expire before a one-slide story. The plural appears 6 times, beside face, testimonials, sale, and text, while the singular story appears once for the one-slide format, and the list covers cadence, proof, text, and face in one breath. Her concrete case is the sale-story split: three times a day on one side, three to seven times per week on the other, with doubling down on the best templates, CTAs, and social proofs as the rule. The list is a set of opposed habits, not a single method — each line is a choice between her way and the way she avoids.
