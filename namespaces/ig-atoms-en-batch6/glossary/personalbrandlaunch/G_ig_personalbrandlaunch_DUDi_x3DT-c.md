# G_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DUDi_x3DT-c — paid

## Term

Paid is one side of a rapid-fire sorting game: this one's paid, this one's free, repeated four times until the pattern is unmistakable. The word appears 4 times, beside free, follow, and education, while free appears 5 times as its partner, and the game has no explanation — the viewer is meant to feel the difference by repetition alone. Her concrete case is the break in the pattern: after four matched pairs comes 'Oh, and this one's free,' a fifth free with no paid partner. The video never says what is paid or what is free — the sorting is the content, and the follow is the ask at the end.
