# G_ig_optimizedjake_DKzMu_mv3r6 — find the right partner

## Term

To find the right partner is the foundational piece optimizedjake says he can speak on, after his partnerships fizzled and a Facebook group introduction changed everything. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "Facebook group", "talker", "salesperson" and "equity partner". He met an individual in a Facebook group who turned out to be his long term business partner of current: the talker, the salesperson, with the smarts of sales and reading people, and people liked him — while Jake was not that person. His concrete rule is that it does not mean going equity: find somebody to bring in as a team member who can also fit that role, so if you like systems and can do sales, start working on the back end, hire for the back end, and keep doing the front end sales yourself.
