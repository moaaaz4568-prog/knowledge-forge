# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFo9slPofrv — bootstrapped

## Term

Bootstrapped is how optimizedjake describes everything he did when starting out: no funds raised, no third party, no loan. The word appears once in the source, beside "free prototyping", "portfolio", "own money" and "no loan". He learned marketing and used it to get his first couple of clients for the prototyping business for free, offering free prototyping to anyone who wanted something done. His concrete method was free, free, free: get projects under his belt, build a portfolio, and then say this is what I have done — and the same do-it-for-myself marketing is what he realized he could do for other businesses.

## When the Meaning Breaks
