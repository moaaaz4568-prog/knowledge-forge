That cycle is the pattern optimizedjake says he has been on a couple of times now: fail, a little bit of success, fail, success, fail. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "fail", "success", "mission" and "my story". His mission right now is to help as many people as possible who were in his position or currently are, by sharing his story.
