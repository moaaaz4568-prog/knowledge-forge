# G_ig_optimizedjake_DHqWvRVPVvl — separate your businesses

## Term

To separate your businesses is optimizedjake's advice for keeping the numbers readable when one main business serves several others. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "profit margin", "cookie jar", "hiring too many people" and "scale". Using one business for multiple others makes it very hard to look at numbers, so separating them lets the owner keep healthy and really see what the profit margin and everything looks like. His concrete second reason for shrinking margins is too many hands in the cookie jar: paying for stupid stuff and hiring too many people, in the cycle where margins shrink as you scale — which he says is not for every business, but from his experience with online businesses it is one hundred percent the biggest challenge faced.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as separation fixing the margins: separation only makes the numbers visible, while the shrinking itself comes from the hands in the cookie jar.
- Read as a universal law of scaling: he says it is not for every business, only his experience with online businesses.
