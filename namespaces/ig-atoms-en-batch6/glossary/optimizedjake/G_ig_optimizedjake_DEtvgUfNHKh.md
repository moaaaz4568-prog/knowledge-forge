# G_ig_optimizedjake_DEtvgUfNHKh — grind

## Term

Grind is the hustle culture optimizedjake fell into from the YouTube space and Gary Vee, and he now calls it an unsustainable way to approach business. The word appears three times in the source, beside "hustle", "birthday parties", "family events" and "pillars". The pattern was grind, hustle, grind: not going to birthday parties or family events, and even skipping the gym and some of the pillars of life, putting milestones in life aside for work. His concrete counter is to work nine hours, eight hours, even seven hours a day, but more efficiently — because nobody is really working efficiently fourteen, fifteen or sixteen hours a day.

> "You're not going to birthday parties, you're not going to family events because it's like grind, hustle, grind." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DEtvgUfNHKh.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rejection of hard work: he still calls for sacrifice and for working efficiently, and the rejection is aimed at the grind-all-hours version, not at working hard.
- Read as a claim that long hours prove dedication: the source asks directly whether anyone really works efficiently for fourteen to sixteen hours, and answers that most likely they do not.
