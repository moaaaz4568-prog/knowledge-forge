# G_ig_optimizedjake_DE1d2vehUH4 — unlimited accounts

## Term

Unlimited accounts are the core of the software deal optimizedjake credits for his biggest bootstrapping success with marketing agencies. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "$300 a month", "payment processor", "Stripe" and "unlimited businesses". For $300 a month the software allows unlimited accounts, so unlimited businesses can be added, and in each account the payment processor can be hooked up by signing up for Stripe for free, along with the website, phone numbers, calling, texting and email. His concrete claim is that the software is the big secret behind starting anything he wants, because funnels, website and client communication are all inside it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as unlimited everything for $300: the source only promises unlimited accounts and businesses, and the payment processing itself still runs through Stripe.
- Read as a business that runs itself: the software holds the pieces, but the source still has him doing Facebook ads to get the ball rolling and testing new businesses himself.
