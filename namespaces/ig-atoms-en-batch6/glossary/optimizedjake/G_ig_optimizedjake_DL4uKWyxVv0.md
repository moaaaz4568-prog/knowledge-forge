# G_ig_optimizedjake_DL4uKWyxVv0 — just different

## Term

Just different is how optimizedjake describes business as it grows: it does not get easier, it does not get harder, it is just different. The phrase appears twice in the source, beside "first dollar", "team drama", "second dollar" and "systems". When first starting out, the hard things are making the first dollar, then the second dollar, and building systems and teams — but once there are teams, the hard things are something breaking, or team drama, or somebody falling off the face of the earth and having to replace them. His concrete advice is to get comfortable dealing with those different problems and comfortable facing those challenges, rather than waiting for it to get easier.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I feel like it doesn't get easier, it doesn't get harder. It's just different." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DL4uKWyxVv0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as business getting easy: he explicitly says it does not get easier.
- Read as the problems staying the same: the problems change, from the first dollar to team drama and replacements.
