# G_ig_optimizedjake_DMP5Wbvihw3 — pizza method

## Term

The pizza method is optimizedjake's rule for selling to people who have already tried the thing: it is very hard to sell somebody something they have done in the past. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "pineapple", "risk reversal", "guarantee" and "winning formula". If someone does not like pineapple on pizza and has had it before, the best pineapple pizza down the road will not get them in the door — and if the offer is labeled like everybody else's, it is very hard for them to join. His concrete fix is a spin: how to grow your podcast without paid ads, which floods the door because growing a podcast is proven, plus a guarantee or warranty, some kind of risk reversal, like fifty features in the first six months or your money back — a little different but not super alien, and a good guarantee, which to him is a winning formula.

- Read as the guarantee alone closing: the spin, a little different but not alien, plus the guarantee together make the formula.
