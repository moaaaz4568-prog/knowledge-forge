# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFUXXZPiZS2 — old head

## Term

An old head is what people called optimizedjake because he has been doing this for nine years. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "nine years", "Gary V", "grind" and "freedom". He fell into the Gary Vee grind culture — grind, grind, grind — but he is one hundred percent a proponent of anybody going out there, trying their online stuff, having success and having some kind of freedom in their work. His concrete example is a great friend from high school whose own business never worked out, yet who became the CEO and CMO doing marketing for one of the biggest apps in the app store — so even without reaching the goal, trying lands you in a much better spot.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I remember even back in my day, people would call me an old head because you know I've been doing this for nine years." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DFUXXZPiZS2.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as nine years making him an expert on everything: the source only uses the nine years to explain the nickname, and his advice is to try, not a claim of mastery.
- Read as a promise that trying guarantees success: his friend's business never worked out, and the claim is only that trying lands you in a much better spot than never trying.
