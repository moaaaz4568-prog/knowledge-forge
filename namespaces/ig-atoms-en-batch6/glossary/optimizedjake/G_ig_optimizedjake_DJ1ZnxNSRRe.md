# G_ig_optimizedjake_DJ1ZnxNSRRe — meta

## Term

Meta is the video-games word optimizedjake borrows for the current winning strategy in marketing. The word appears twice in the source, beside "VSLs", "trends", "consumer" and "burn it into the ground". The question is what is the meta currently: what is the strategy, what is everybody doing to get a one up — maybe people running VSLs to a booking, or a specific type of ad, image ads, more text-based ads, video ads. His concrete method is being a consumer of advertising and marketing: see what is working right now, stay in people's drips, check what competitors are doing, because somebody finds something that works, burns it into the ground, and then something else takes its place.
