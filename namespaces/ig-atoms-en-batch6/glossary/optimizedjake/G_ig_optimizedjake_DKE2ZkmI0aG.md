# G_ig_optimizedjake_DKE2ZkmI0aG — give value

## Term

To give value is what optimizedjake says most of the week's emails should do, against a minority that sells. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "marketing emails", "twice a week", "checking in" and "convert". People are used to getting a bunch of emails, so as long as not too many marketing emails go out, the mix is image-heavy emails and just-checking-in emails — twice a week or three times a week sell, and six or seven times a week give value. His concrete constraint is money: for an online business without hundreds of thousands of dollars a month to spend just to acquire an email list, things need to convert, so email marketing has to work as staying in front of people.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never selling by email: selling happens twice or three times a week, and the value emails are the majority, not the whole.
- Read as volume for its own sake: the constraint is conversion, because without a big list budget the emails have to convert.
