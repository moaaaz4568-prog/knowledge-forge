# G_ig_optimizedjake_DE6ngGdNl1s — self confidence

## Term

Self confidence is what optimizedjake says everything comes down to when a big goal meets road bumps and blockages. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "start chipping away", "road bumps", "blockages" and "one thing every day". His method is to start chipping away: do one thing every day toward the big goal, then another thing, then another, and after hitting road bumps see how far you have come and get over them. The concrete payoff is confidence in yourself and your knowledge giving the ability to overcome any kind of situations, losses or failures in the business.

## Synonyms

- confidence (the short form used in the same passage)

## Live Example

> "I think that really comes down to self confidence because if you have confidence in yourself and your knowledge, that's gonna give you the ability to overcome any kind of situations, losses, or failures in your business." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DE6ngGdNl1s.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice that confidence alone replaces skill: the source ties confidence to knowledge — confidence in yourself and your knowledge — and the daily work of chipping away still has to happen.
- Read as a claim that starting young removes the risk: he says the worst thing for him was going back home for the summer, and he explicitly refuses to speak for people with families and kids who take on more risk.
