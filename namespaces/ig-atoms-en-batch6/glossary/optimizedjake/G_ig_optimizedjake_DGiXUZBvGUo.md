# G_ig_optimizedjake_DGiXUZBvGUo — sales teams

## Term

Sales teams are what optimizedjake says actually made his company succeed, more than the funnels and designs he was passionate about. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "funnels", "designs", "processes and systems" and "golf courses". He was always passionate about building funnels and doing designs, but sales and building out sales teams and very good processes and systems is what allowed the company to scale — a world he did not come from, but had to learn and is very good at now. His concrete counter-example is golf courses: he had a passion for golf, but there are only so many courses, they are very cheap and do not have large budgets, and they often have in-house marketing people, so pursuing them would not have made a successful business.

## Live Example

> "But at the end of the day, what helped our company succeed is sales and building out sales teams and very good processes and systems." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DGiXUZBvGUo.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as funnels being worthless: he still loves building funnels, and the claim is only that funnels alone would have made a good living, not the successes he has now.
- Read as passion being irrelevant: the lesson is to disassociate some passions and sacrifice them to reach the goal, not to have no passion at all.
