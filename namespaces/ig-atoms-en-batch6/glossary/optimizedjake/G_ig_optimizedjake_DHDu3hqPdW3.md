# G_ig_optimizedjake_DHDu3hqPdW3 — customer retention

## Term

Customer retention is the outcome optimizedjake ties to getting somebody actually into the product and using it. The phrase appears twice in the source, beside "adaptation", "usability", "adoption" and "good product". For a service business there is no such worry, since the business is paid to perform a task — but for education, courses or software, the biggest retention factor is the customer's adaptation, usability and adoption into the software and system. His concrete rule is threefold: somebody actually using the product, a good product where they see a good end result, and good pricing — and people will not keep paying thousands of dollars just because of a relationship and a shit product.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as retention coming from relationships: he says nobody keeps paying thousands just for a relationship with a shit product.
- Read as a rule for service businesses too: service businesses are explicitly excluded, since they are paid to perform the task rather than to keep users on a product.
