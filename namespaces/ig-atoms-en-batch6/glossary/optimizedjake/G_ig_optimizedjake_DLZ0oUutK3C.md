# G_ig_optimizedjake_DLZ0oUutK3C — cost per acquisition

## Term

Cost per acquisition is the number optimizedjake says falls when the brand is instilled in the customer before the sales call. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "show rates", "close rates", "retargeting" and "written reviews". Once the prospect has already decided this is a good company, they just need to know the price is right, and then it is super easy for the closer to actually sell. His concrete mechanism is retargeting with written reviews and case studies, plus emails and texts, to instill the brand before the call — which increases show rates and close rates, lowers the cost per acquisition, and helps the business scale faster and make more money.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as price being the only objection: the price matters only after the prospect has decided it is a good company, which the retargeting established.
- Read as retargeting being only ads: emails and texts are part of it too.
