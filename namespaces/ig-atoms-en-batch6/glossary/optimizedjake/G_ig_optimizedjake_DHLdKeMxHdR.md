# G_ig_optimizedjake_DHLdKeMxHdR — one on one coaching

## Term

One on one coaching is the passion optimizedjake is moving into as he exits some of his marketing agencies. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "exiting", "marketing agencies", "real estate fund" and "ten businesses every single day". It is not like selling a course: he is talking about literally working one on one with people who are starting and scaling their business. His concrete advice in that role is direct — do not spend two weeks working on a website, call ten businesses every single day and set meetings — and alongside it he is getting really big into real estate, working on a real estate fund that lets individuals in on an equity basis in some of the properties.
