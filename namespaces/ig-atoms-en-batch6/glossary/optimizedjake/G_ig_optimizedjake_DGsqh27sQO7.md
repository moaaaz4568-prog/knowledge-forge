# G_ig_optimizedjake_DGsqh27sQO7 — holding people accountable

## Term

Holding people accountable is what optimizedjake calls the biggest thing for building a strong and motivated team in a marketing agency or small business. The phrase "holding people accountable" appears once in the source, and the form "holding their team members accountable" appears once more, beside "authoritative good leader", "kumbaya", "systematic flow" and "strong and motivated team". There is no real secret and no real science: be an authoritative good leader, which does not mean being an asshole and does not mean being soft, because people pick up on softness and take advantage of it. His concrete dismissal is the weekly meeting icebreakers and kumbaya get-to-know-you sessions, which he calls stupid shit that will not build a team — a team is built when you hold them accountable and they hold their team members accountable, until the team is fully functioning and working in a systematic flow.

## Synonyms

- holding their team members accountable (the form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "But holding people accountable is going to be the biggest thing that you can do to build a strong and motivated team." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DGsqh27sQO7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as being an asshole: he explicitly says an authoritative good leader does not mean being an asshole.
- Read as softness being good leadership: being soft is named as the failure mode, because people pick up on it and take advantage of you.
