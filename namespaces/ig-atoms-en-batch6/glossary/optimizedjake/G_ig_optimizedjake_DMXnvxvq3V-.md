# G_ig_optimizedjake_DMXnvxvq3V- — done with you offer

## Term

A done with you offer is the offer optimizedjake switched to during COVID, when people were eating it up and the business scaled. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "sticking to something", "scaled horizontally", "two thousand dollars" and "winning formula". He learned marketing, worked the first year without making a dime, did a bunch of free work, had a partner that did not work out, tried another business partner with a different offer, made a little money and had some success — and then COVID hit and the offer had to switch again. His concrete deal is the software plus setup: other people charged thousands of dollars a month for marketing services, while they charged two thousand dollars one time instead of two thousand dollars a month, and from that winning formula they scaled horizontally.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the offer working from day one: it came after a year of no money, failed partners and a COVID switch.
- Read as a cheap version of the same service: the price is one-time two thousand dollars versus two thousand a month, and the offer includes the setup, which is the difference.
