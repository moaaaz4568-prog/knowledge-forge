# G_ig_optimizedjake_DHyFLcJP6X- — sick of being in contracts

## Term

Sick of being in contracts is the feedback optimizedjake says his market gave when everybody was charging thousands a month with a six month lock-in. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "six month contract", "pay us one time", "25, 30, 40, 50K" and "Go High Level". This was back in COVID, when people did not know what would happen in the next month or two, especially in real estate, so they did not want to be in a contract — and the answer was to pay one time and get the system forever, with the little back end subscription pausable and restartable. His concrete read of the market now is a split: really cheap, like a hundred dollars a month for a CRM with Go High Level and a school community, or super expensive, with guys selling 25, 30, 40 or 50K done-for-you programs, plus the high ticket sellers in between.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "We came into the game when everybody was charging, you know, thousands a month, lock you up in a six month contract, and the feedback we got was literally sick of being in contracts." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DHyFLcJP6X-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as contracts being dead everywhere: the split he describes still includes the high-ticket program sellers.
- Read as the one-time payment being the whole offer: the small back end subscription continues, and it can be paused and restarted.
