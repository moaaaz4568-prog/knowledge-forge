# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFj0NggR15l — personal brand

## Term

A personal brand is what optimizedjake says everybody wants now, built by documenting every single day. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "documenting", "day to day", "shadow you" and "ask questions". The advice for someone starting out is that they do not need the best content in the world and will find it hard to give business advice — instead show the day to day and show the work being put in. His concrete in-person method is to ask questions: what do you do, how did you get here, what advice do you have for somebody younger, is there anything I can do for you, and can I shadow you for a day for free.
