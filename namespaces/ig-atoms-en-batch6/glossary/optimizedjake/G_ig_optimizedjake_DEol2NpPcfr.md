# G_ig_optimizedjake_DEol2NpPcfr — unemotional

## Term

Unemotional is how optimizedjake says decisions have to be when a business is not working yet. The word appears once in the source, beside "free work", "speed", "switch things up" and "$100,000, million dollar months". He worked two to three years making very little money, and his first year in business he did not make a dime, doing a lot of free work to build the skill set. His concrete method is speed to changing and adapting: when something does not work, do not worry about it, just be very fast, switch things up and keep going and keep trying new things, because eventually something sticks and something works.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as not caring about the business: unemotional here is about decisions — switching fast when something does not work — while he still did years of free work to build the skill set.
- Read as a promise that something will stick: the source only says eventually something sticks and something works if you keep trying new things, and no timeline for that is given.
