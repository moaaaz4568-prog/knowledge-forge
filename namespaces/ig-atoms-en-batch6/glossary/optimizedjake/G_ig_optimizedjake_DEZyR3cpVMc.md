# G_ig_optimizedjake_DEZyR3cpVMc — burn the bridges

## Term

To burn the bridges is the mentality optimizedjake calls by far the biggest thing in his business journey, even while admitting it sounds really bad. The phrase appears twice in the source, beside "calculated risks", "background job", "no other option" and "failures and losses". With no other option, you make decisions you normally would not, and those are the calculated risks needed to succeed — while a background job holding you back makes it easy to wake up, go to a job and get paid. His concrete claim is that anybody can make it happen if they work towards it and work through the failures and the losses.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I'm a big believer, and this is really bad, and it's manifested and come up a lot in my business journey and some of the people I'm in business with, but the burn the bridges mentality." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DEZyR3cpVMc.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as reckless risk-taking: the source frames the risks as calculated, and the point is removing the fallback so the hard decisions get made, not gambling blindly.
- Read as a guarantee that anyone will make it: he says anybody can make it happen if they work towards it and through failures and losses, and the condition — the work — is part of the claim.
