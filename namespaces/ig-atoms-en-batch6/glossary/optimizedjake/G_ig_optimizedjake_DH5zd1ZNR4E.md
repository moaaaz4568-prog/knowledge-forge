# G_ig_optimizedjake_DH5zd1ZNR4E — hamburger stand

## Term

The hamburger stand is optimizedjake's own name for his volume-play business model: cheap, high-volume products instead of steaks. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "volume play", "8,000 individuals", "turn rate" and "real estate". They have signed up 8,000 individuals, which he calls a lot of people for any company, and the product is not a $2,000 a month type of product. His concrete math is the turn rate: in real estate there might be half a million agents with a five, six or seven percent turn rate, so more new agents come into the business than they sign up annually — and by mastering volume and the client acquisition process, the model scales to signing up hundreds of thousands of individuals.

## Live Example

> "We call ourselves like the hamburger stand. We don't sell steaks." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DH5zd1ZNR4E.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as low prices meaning a low-quality product: volume is the model, and the argument is the turn-rate math, not the price level.
- Read as a model for real estate only: the same volume logic is applied to any service or community at $100 or $200 a month, not just the bigger industries named.
