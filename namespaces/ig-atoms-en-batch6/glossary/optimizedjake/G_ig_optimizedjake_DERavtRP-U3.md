# G_ig_optimizedjake_DERavtRP-U3 — octopus

## Term

The octopus is optimizedjake's own name for his home base: one main software with a lot of tentacles. The word appears once in the source, beside "home base", "tentacles", "GHL" and "Synth". The main software is the AI using Synth plugged into GHL, which is the main software given to people, and each tentacle is a different industry with a different product, because speaking to a niche brings a higher conversion and close rate. His concrete list of tentacles is real estate, real estate mortgage, insurance, home services, health and wellness, and online businesses and entrepreneurs — the full spectrum, as he puts it.
