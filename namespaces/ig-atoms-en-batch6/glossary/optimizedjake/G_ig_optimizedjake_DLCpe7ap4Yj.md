# G_ig_optimizedjake_DLCpe7ap4Yj — bulletproof

## Term

Bulletproof is how optimizedjake describes the brand a company needs before it can get the best show rates and close rates. The word appears once in the source, beside "VSL", "video sales letter", "KPIs" and "show rates". One of the biggest underrated things for more sales is having a brand that is literally bulletproof, and then taking people through the steps of that brand — because running ads directly to a calendar gets people on a call who do not know the brand, the company or the owner. His concrete mechanism is the VSL, the video sales letter after booking: the background of the company, how long it has been around, B-roll of client success stories, KPIs, results and team members, until the prospect thinks, oh shit, this is a legit company, they have depth and client results, I am going to work with them.
