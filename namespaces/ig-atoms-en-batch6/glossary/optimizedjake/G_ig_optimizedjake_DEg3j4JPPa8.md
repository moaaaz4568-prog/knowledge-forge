# G_ig_optimizedjake_DEg3j4JPPa8 — prompting

## Term

Prompting is the biggest learning curve optimizedjake names for connecting voice AI with Synth, even though connecting it is by far the easiest thing they have used. The word appears twice in the source, beside "Synth", "sub account", "agent ID" and "ChatGPT prompt". The connection itself is simple — install Synth into the sub account, put in the prompt and the agent ID, with no webhook — but writing the prompt out in depth is the hard part, because the more in depth the prompt goes, the better the AI agent will be. His concrete relief is that with Synth you can prompt it like a ChatGPT prompt, instead of having to write out a script.
