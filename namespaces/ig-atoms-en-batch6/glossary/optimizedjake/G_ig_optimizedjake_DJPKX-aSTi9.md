# G_ig_optimizedjake_DJPKX-aSTi9 — lead gen

## Term

Lead gen is how optimizedjake says he grew his business and got started, after learning advertising. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "advertising", "prototyping", "project list" and "chopstick holder". He would get the lead, call these people up, and run an ad asking if they were interested in doing some prototyping — back in 2016 — and that first interaction with advertising was literally for himself. His concrete reason for taking projects and paying for them out of pocket was to build up a project list, which is a big thing in the engineering world: what projects have you worked on — so he created work like the chopstick holder, met the guy in person a few times, and learned in those first interactions how to talk about business.
