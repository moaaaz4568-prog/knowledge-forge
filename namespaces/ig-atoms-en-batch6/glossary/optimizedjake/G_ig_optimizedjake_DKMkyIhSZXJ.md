# G_ig_optimizedjake_DKMkyIhSZXJ — cold audit

## Term

A cold audit is the opener optimizedjake used when starting out: telling a company how they could be doing better, unasked. The phrase appears twice in the source, beside "proposals", "mailers", "chamber of commerce" and "golf courses". He learned advertising for himself, then for others, started a little by himself, and partnered with a very close friend; the company did social media posting and advertising, and signed its first client, who owned a string of golf courses. His concrete method was traditional business networking: joining an in-person chamber of commerce and a group of golf course owners, going to shake hands, meeting people, sending out proposals and audits — cold auditing people by saying, hey, this is your company, this is about how we think you could be doing better.
