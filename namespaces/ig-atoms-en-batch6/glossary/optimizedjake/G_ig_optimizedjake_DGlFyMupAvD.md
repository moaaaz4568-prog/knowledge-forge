# G_ig_optimizedjake_DGlFyMupAvD — grassroots

## Term

Grassroots is how optimizedjake describes the middle layer of growing a personal brand: people start coming to you. The word appears twice in the source, beside "personal brand", "shorts", "podcast" and "running ads". The layers are a little bit of content — shorts, podcasts, articles, website posted everywhere — and then from there it is grassroots, with people starting to come to you. His concrete third layer is running ads into the personal brand: take the best performing shorts, run them as ads to get people back onto the page, and they start following, which grows the following further until it snowballs from there.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "From there, a lot of it's gonna come organically. So it's how can I kind of grassroots my personal brand to start?" — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DGlFyMupAvD.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as ads being the first step: ads are the third layer, and they only work after the content and the organic arrivals are already happening.
- Read as content about anything: the content produced has to actually relate to whatever is being offered, and he stresses that as part of the recommendation.
