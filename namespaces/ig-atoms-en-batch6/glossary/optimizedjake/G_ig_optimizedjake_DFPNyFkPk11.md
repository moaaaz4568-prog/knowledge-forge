# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFPNyFkPk11 — handcuffed

## Term

Handcuffed is how optimizedjake describes a business owner who pays somebody else to set up the basics. The word appears twice in the source, beside "website", "contracts", "phone number systems" and "GHL". His challenge is to build a business without raising capital or getting a loan: get a High Level subscription, get the website up, do not spend so much time on it, do not pay anybody, and learn the little things — website, phone number, contracts — so you can then teach team members. His concrete cost is that paying somebody to make the website leaves you always handcuffed to them, paying them again whenever something needs doing.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But if you pay somebody to make your website, you're always going to be handcuffed to them, you know, having to pay somebody to do a website or same thing with a contract software." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DFPNyFkPk11.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on hiring anyone: the point is to learn the setup yourself first so you can teach team members, not to do everything alone forever.
- Read as a claim that GHL alone removes the handcuffs: the subscription only helps if you learn to do the setup yourself, and the source calls the platform super easy, not automatic.
