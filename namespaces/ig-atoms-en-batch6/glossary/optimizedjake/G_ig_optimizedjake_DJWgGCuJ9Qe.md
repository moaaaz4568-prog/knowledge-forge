# G_ig_optimizedjake_DJWgGCuJ9Qe — customer experience journey

## Term

The customer experience journey is what optimizedjake says matters most for a customer's success with a product: how easy it is to pick up and use, and how good the product is. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "user experience", "unlock", "friction" and "adopt". It is not so much the communication with support: they had the best support in the world, with the best people having really good conversations, and customers were still complaining because the user experience with the product was bad. His concrete method is to unlock new things inside the platform every week or every month, giving the user something new every time they pay or a week before they pay, so the user adapts the system and the friction of getting in, using and adopting the platform falls away.

- Read as unlocking features being a trick: the unlocks help the user adapt the system, which is the mechanism he names.
