# G_ig_optimizedjake_DLpRaXLBa8t — giving free value

## Term

Giving free value is what optimizedjake says his networking is: giving free value, free advice, helping out, expecting nothing in return. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "networked", "biggest software companies", "podcast" and "refer". He recently booked an insane podcast with one of the biggest software companies, because he had networked a little with the owner and was a big user of the software. His concrete method is to give away all your shit: if they need help, ask them, check in on them, chat it up, do not try to sell them anything — and eventually something comes in that relationship, a partner, an employee, help, or referrals to other people, the same way friends are friends because of the value given.
