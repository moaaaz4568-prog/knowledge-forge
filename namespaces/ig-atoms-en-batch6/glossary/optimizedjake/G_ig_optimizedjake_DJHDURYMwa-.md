# G_ig_optimizedjake_DJHDURYMwa- — being a consumer

## Term

Being a consumer is the way optimizedjake says to stay on top of marketing: literally being a consumer of advertising. The phrase appears three times in the source, beside "million dollar months", "ego", "sales funnels" and "market trends". When they were doing million dollar months, they stepped back from being a consumer: they were not clicking on as many ads, not getting pitched by as many people, not going through as many sales funnels, with a little bit of ego saying they did not need that. His concrete warning is that you are constantly seeing ads, but if you are not going through funnels you are not learning the process — and one of the biggest market trends to master is just being a consumer.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as consuming ads for fun: the purpose is going through funnels and learning the process, not the ads themselves.
- Read as a beginner tip only: the failure he describes happened at million-dollar months, after success, not at the start.
