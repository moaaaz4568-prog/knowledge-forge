# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFmZAT6sVw2 — Inc five thousand award

## Term

The Inc five thousand award is the award optimizedjake says his company just won as the fastest growing company in America. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "six online businesses", "seven figures", "eight figures" and "accountable". He has started six online businesses that have all done over seven figures, two that have done eight figures, and the award is his concrete proof of that success. His concrete offer is to work with people who want somebody to hold them accountable and get them producing and doing revenue producing activities, for no more than a couple hundred dollars a month to get started.
