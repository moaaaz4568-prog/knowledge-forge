# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFMpIMiJjaL — solid routine

## Term

A solid routine is what optimizedjake says carries him through the storms and tough times in business. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "two hours every single day", "fitness", "mental roadblocks" and "second nature". The routine is to get comfortable working on the business and going through the motions every single day, dedicating two hours daily, working on yourself as well as the business — and for him, working on each business an hour and a half every single day. His concrete claim is that the routine takes years and years to develop, but once it is there it becomes second nature and lets him get through any mental roadblock.

## Synonyms

- routine (the short form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "The way to persevere through those storms and get through those tough times is definitely having a solid routine." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DFMpIMiJjaL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a routine that works overnight: the source is explicit that it takes years and years to develop, and only then does it become second nature.
- Read as a routine made only of business work: the routine includes working on yourself — fitness and staying healthy — alongside the business, every single day.
