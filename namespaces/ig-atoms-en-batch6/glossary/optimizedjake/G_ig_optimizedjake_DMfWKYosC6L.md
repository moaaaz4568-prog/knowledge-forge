# G_ig_optimizedjake_DMfWKYosC6L — morning cardio

## Term

Morning cardio is the first block of optimizedjake's day, before the work starts. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "fitness", "boring crap", "in my zone" and "time management". The day is set up as fitness, run, morning cardio, and then getting into work to knock out the boring crap — the little stuff done every single day because it has to be done and nobody else can do it inside the business. His concrete rhythm is to go lift, take a break, and then get back in his zone working on new stuff, because he loves having things on his plate, creating, and getting into new projects and new avenues of business — and that structure, plus time management, is what gets him through.
