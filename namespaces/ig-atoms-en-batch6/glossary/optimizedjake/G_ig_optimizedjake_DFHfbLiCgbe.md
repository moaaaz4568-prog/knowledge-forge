# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFHfbLiCgbe — non-revenue producing activities

## Term

Non-revenue producing activities are the trap optimizedjake says entrepreneurs fall into when starting a business: focusing on them one hundred percent. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "setting meetings", "pitch", "proposals" and "follow-ups". Revenue producing activities are setting meetings, getting in front of individuals, working on the pitch, sending out proposals and doing follow-ups to generate clients and revenue — or running ads in e-com. His concrete warning is that systems, SOPs and automations are great, and they are his specialty, but without a client to do work for, all of it is just a waste of time.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So one of the biggest things entrepreneurs make when starting their entrepreneurial journey and starting a business is one hundred focusing on non-revenue producing activities." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DFHfbLiCgbe.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on systems and automations: the source calls them great and his own specialty, and the point is only that they come after having a client, not instead of one.
- Read as a claim that meetings alone build a business: the meetings, pitch and proposals still need the systems behind them once a client exists, and the source never says to skip them forever.
