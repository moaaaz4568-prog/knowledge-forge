# G_ig_optimizedjake_DKUTJb-SB_b — practitioners

## Term

Practitioners is what optimizedjake calls a lot of small business owners: the ones doing the work, not there to own a small business. The word appears once in the source, beside "franchises", "SOPs", "personalized experience" and "winning formula". His biggest opinion on small businesses staying ahead of the curve and innovative in a rapidly changing market, especially with AI, is very simple — and the threatening small businesses are often franchises, because a business owner comes in with already set marketing, set SOPs and processes. His concrete synthesis is to mesh the two: a lot of people still go to the smaller guy for a more personalized experience, and meshing the franchise systems with that experience is when you really create a winning formula and a winning small business, especially in a local market.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "Unfortunately, a lot of small business owners are practitioners, which means they are the ones doing the work." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DKUTJb-SB_b.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as practitioners being bad owners: the point is role confusion — doing the work versus owning the business.
- Read as franchises always winning: the smaller guy keeps the personalized-experience edge, and the win is meshing both.
