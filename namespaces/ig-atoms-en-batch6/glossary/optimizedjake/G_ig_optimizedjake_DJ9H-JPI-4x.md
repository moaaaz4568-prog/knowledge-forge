# G_ig_optimizedjake_DJ9H-JPI-4x — top of mind

## Term

Top of mind is where optimizedjake keeps a lead with email after they become a lead and after they book. The phrase appears twice in the source, beside "nurturing", "subject", "headline" and "winning ads". After somebody becomes a lead, he emails them and hits them up a bunch of times to stay in front of them and top of mind before they book; after they book, he sends stuff over email plus reminders for the appointment to increase the show rate. His concrete method for the email itself is to take the subject from the headline in the marketing on the ads, and the image from the ads as the first thing they see — headline, image, copy — literally taking the winning ads, putting them into an email, and shooting them out, with no traditional email needed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as building a list being the goal: he says building a list is good, but his best emails convert on a good subject, and the system runs ads to email to booking.
- Read as needing traditional email copy: the headline, image and copy are literally taken from the winning ads.
