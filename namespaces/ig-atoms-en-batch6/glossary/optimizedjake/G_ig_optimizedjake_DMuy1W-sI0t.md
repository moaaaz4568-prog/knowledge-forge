# G_ig_optimizedjake_DMuy1W-sI0t — bridge the gap

## Term

To bridge the gap is the analogy optimizedjake uses for getting a prospect to the product: they are over here, and the business is over here. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "FOMO", "scarcity", "desired outcome" and "walk across". There are two things that get them across the bridge: number one is to push them across, using fear, FOMO and scarcity tactics — or to use the desired outcome, whether they want to get richer, do something easier or save time — so they walk across the bridge themselves and come to you. His concrete preference is the second: they walk across the bridge themselves, rather than being pushed.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I like to use this analogy of a bridge. If you think like they're over here, and then you know we're over here, and how can we bridge the gap and get them to walk across the bridge over to our product, to our service?" — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DMuy1W-sI0t.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as pushing being the recommended way: pushing is listed first, but the preference is that they walk across themselves toward the desired outcome.
- Read as the bridge being a trick: the two options are fear and scarcity versus a genuinely desired outcome, and the source presents both.
