# G_ig_optimizedjake_DJl81wpuhT5 — self onboard

## Term

To self onboard is what optimizedjake says a customer should be able to do by themselves, so support does not have to be the main focus. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "good support", "adopt", "customer experience" and "hundreds of thousands". Having good support is great, but if somebody can self onboard, get in the system and adopt the system into their business, support does not have to be such a main focus — not that it takes the second seat, but it does not have to be the main one. His concrete scale is money: you do not need one-to-one support as if people are paying hundreds of thousands of dollars a month; you can have support that matches what they are paying, because the product is much easier for them to use, and that is all that matters for the customer experience.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as support being unnecessary: support still exists, matched to what the customer pays, and self-onboarding only lowers its priority.
- Read as a claim about enterprise clients: the example is explicitly not people paying hundreds of thousands of dollars a month.
