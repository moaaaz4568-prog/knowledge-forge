# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFE6njgI3wr — Go High Level

## Term

Go High Level is the software optimizedjake calls hands down the best thing for bootstrapping and starting up new businesses. The name appears three times in the source, beside "payment processing", "invoicing", "contracts" and "templates". One account holds payment processing, invoicing, contracts, a phone number for text, phone and email, clients and support, websites, and funnels for sales presentations — a whole business. His concrete method is to template it out with an onboarding calendar, a sales funnel and a website, then duplicate the template when starting something new, spend an hour customizing it, and have a company thrown up within two or three hours.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that Go High Level does everything: the source lists what one account holds, and the templates and the hour of customizing are still his work on top of it.
- Read as the only tool he uses: Go High Level is the best thing for bootstrapping here, and the same source still needs his templates, his funnels and his hour of setup for each new company.
