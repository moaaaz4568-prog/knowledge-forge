# G_ig_optimizedjake_DEmBEiTIccW — transparency

## Term

Transparency is what optimizedjake says the business lost when its support team was outsourced, and losing it was the biggest and hardest failure of his career. The word appears twice in the source, beside "outsourced", "support", "million dollars a month" and "crumbled". Growth was super fast — almost a million dollars a month — but without control over the business there was no transparency: clients complained about support, the team hid things, and the business crumbled back down to about thirty to forty thousand dollars a month. His concrete response was to start bringing support in house, because without transparency no judgment about the business can be made.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And that really spiraled because when you don't have control over what's going on in your business, you don't have transparency." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DEmBEiTIccW.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complaint about slow reporting: the transparency lost here was control itself — not knowing what was going on — and the team hiding things from him.
- Read as a failure of growth: the business grew fast while transparency was already gone, and the crumbling came when the hidden support problems surfaced, not when growth stopped.
