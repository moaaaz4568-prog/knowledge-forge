# G_ig_optimizedjake_DHTLkO9vXKG — profit margins

## Term

Profit margins are the biggest challenge optimizedjake names for scaling any kind of business, in person or online. The plural "profit margins" appears twice in the source, beside "scaling horizontally", "ROAS", "fifty percent" and "ceiling", with the singular form appearing alongside it. As you grow and scale, margins shrink, which demotivates an owner used to a fifty percent margin: scale two, three or four times and the margin falls to twenty or fifteen percent, so the business makes the same or less at a bigger revenue point than a one hundred thousand dollar a month business at fifty percent. His concrete prescription is to scale horizontally: take a good offer into another industry instead of trying to scale one offer into infinity in one agency, where a ceiling is hit.

## Synonyms

- profit margin (the singular form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "The biggest challenge that anybody is going to have as far as scaling any kind of business that could be in person, that could be online, it really could be any of them. The biggest challenge is profit margins." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DHTLkO9vXKG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as revenue growth always meaning more profit: the source shows bigger revenue producing the same or less profit once the margin shrinks.
- Read as vertical scaling being always wrong: the advice is to scale horizontally once the offer is good, not to never scale the agency itself.
