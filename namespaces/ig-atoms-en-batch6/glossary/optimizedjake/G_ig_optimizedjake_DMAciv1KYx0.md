# G_ig_optimizedjake_DMAciv1KYx0 — JV offer

## Term

A JV offer is the end goal of optimizedjake's new business model: a joint-venture offer, not a course. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "million-dollar months", "partnership level", "educate" and "outline". The model has a pretty good outline, nothing fine-tuned, and the work is to educate and help individuals who want to grow a marketing company or an online business. His concrete end state is a solid team, systems and processes coming into individuals' businesses on a partnership level, to scale them from doing ten, fifteen, twenty or a hundred thousand dollars a month to million-dollar months.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "but the end goal is more of a JV offer where, you know, we have a solid team, systems and processes, and we can come into individuals' businesses and help them on a partnership level to scale from, you know, doing 10, 15, 20, $100, 000 a month and get to those million-dollar months." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DMAciv1KYx0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the JV being available now: it is the end goal after people learn, and the model is still an outline, nothing fine-tuned.
- Read as a course: the end goal is partnership-level help inside their businesses, not information alone.
