# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFRykZ3tX9h — voice agents

## Term

Voice agents are the standard optimizedjake says every business will have within three to five years. The phrase "voice agents" appears once in the source, and the fuller form "voice AI agents" appears once more, beside "three to five year mark", "screen", "appointments" and "reschedules". It used to be banks and airlines with a robotic kind of prompt when you called, but soon every business will use voice to screen why somebody is calling in, or for outbound calling for appointments and reschedules. His concrete comparison is web chat: everybody now has some kind of web chat or AI chat on their website, and voice will reach the point where the end user does not even know the difference between the AI and a real person.
