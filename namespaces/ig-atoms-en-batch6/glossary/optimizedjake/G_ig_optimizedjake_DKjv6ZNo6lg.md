# G_ig_optimizedjake_DKjv6ZNo6lg — hiring around your strengths

## Term

Hiring around your strengths is the lesson optimizedjake draws from always having a business partner: find the person who fits where you are lacking. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "golf courses", "closing team", "salesperson" and "three grand a month". The transition to advertising did not work too well with golf courses, because they would pay for social media content but did not want to spend money or understand converting leads — and then they fucked a big deal up, going from five or six hundred bucks per golf course to proposing three grand a month on the gurus' advice, and got shut down. His concrete pattern is that along the way he always had somebody, because he was never the upfront person or the salesperson, so he wanted somebody for that role.

> "So I think that's important. You really need to focus on hiring around your strengths and finding a person that fits into where you are lacking." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DKjv6ZNo6lg.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never learning sales: he learned sales later, and the point here is hiring for the gap while starting.
- Read as the price hike being the only mistake: the golf courses also did not understand converting leads.
