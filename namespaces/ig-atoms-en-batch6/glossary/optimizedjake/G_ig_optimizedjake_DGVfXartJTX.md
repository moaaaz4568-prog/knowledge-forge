# G_ig_optimizedjake_DGVfXartJTX — study the algorithm

## Term

Study the algorithm is what optimizedjake tells a would-be YouTuber to do instead of trusting passion to grow the channel. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "study what works", "attracting views", "framework" and "60 subscribers". He did YouTube eight years ago, reached about 60 subscribers, and calls those videos very cringey — and he will not promise that passion alone grows and blossoms, even over 15 years. His concrete method is to study the algorithm, study what works and study what is attracting views, then take that framework and apply it to your own channel or content, even though you might not like making those videos.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But I will say that if you want to succeed as a YouTuber, study the algorithm, study what works, study what's attracting views." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DGVfXartJTX.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that studying the algorithm guarantees views: the source only says you can see the views by applying the framework, and his own channel stalled at 60 subscribers.
- Read as a claim that passion does not matter: he says success and passion do not intersect too well, not that passion is worthless — the framework still has to be applied to content you make.
