# G_ig_optimizedjake_DM-PlkqyANs — lonely period

## Term

A lonely period is what optimizedjake says Alex Ramosi talks about, and what he went through himself while dedicating everything to the business. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "Alex Ramosi", "self-induced", "four years" and "sacrifice". Part of it was self-induced, and the other part was dedicating one hundred percent of his time and energy to making sure he and the business were successful — about four years where he did not see his friends, just himself and his business partner, with no human contact at all. His concrete feeling was loneliness and missing out, which he struggled with a lot and overcame — and he says it is something everybody working towards something, or who has achieved something, definitely went through, with sacrifice and lonely times.
