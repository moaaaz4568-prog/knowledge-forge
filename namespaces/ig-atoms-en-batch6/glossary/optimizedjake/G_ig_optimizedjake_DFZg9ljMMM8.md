# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFZg9ljMMM8 — marketing trends

## Term

Marketing trends are what optimizedjake lists for 2024, with one trend on top: using AI inside marketing. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "AI", "clip out that content", "replicate yourself" and "captions". The trend is not AI-focused or AI-heavy work, but using AI in creation: recording content and then using AI to clip it out, replicating yourself with AI, and using AI to generate reels and put captions in them. His concrete framing is integration — from a marketing standpoint, integrating AI into everything being done.
