# G_ig_optimizedjake_DFcFv-cs76N — unique offer

## Term

A unique offer is what optimizedjake says solves the biggest problem when it is custom-tailored to a unique industry. The phrase appears once in the source, beside "unique industry", "custom-tailored solution", "voice AI" and "three to five years". His agency has had success selling leads and marketing, but the plan is to take it worldwide with a super developed offer built on voice AI — the same three-to-five-year shift he describes elsewhere, where voice AI becomes the standard in businesses. His concrete direction is a transition: from a forefront deliverable of marketing with voice AI, to voice AI that reduces the number of people in the business.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any offer in any industry: the offer has to be unique and the industry unique, with a custom-tailored solution, and the source never presents a generic offer as the answer.
- Read as voice AI replacing the marketing offer at once: the transition he describes is a plan for the three-to-five-year horizon, not a switch already made.
