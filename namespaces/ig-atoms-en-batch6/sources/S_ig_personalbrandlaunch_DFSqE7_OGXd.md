If I had zero followers and had to get to ten thousand with one video, this is what I would post. It’s called a My Story video and it almost always goes viral. And it follows these six steps.

Number one: the intro. State the problem you are feeling. For example: One year ago, I was trying to lose weight. Number two: the inflection point. State the pain point. For example: I was going to my high school reunion in a year where I would have to see my ex Chad and I wanted him to see what he broke up with. Freaking loser.

Number three: the rising action. State the failed solutions. For example: But the problem was nothing was working. I tried running, different diets, YouTube workouts, but the scale stayed the same. Number four: the climax. State the solution that you finally found aka your product or service. For example: I was fed up so I started to do my own research, created my own workouts and meal plans.

Number five: the falling action. State results from solution. For example: Because of this, I lost twenty pounds, got my dream six-pack and felt good. When I walked into my high school reunion six months later, Chad had visibly drooled, but I didn't even care about that prick or what he thought anymore.

Number six: the resolution. State the next step slash CTA. For example: After this experience, I made it my mission to help women just like me get the same results. I even took my client from this to this in twelve weeks. Interested? Follow for more fitness education. Common story for my My Story scripting template.
