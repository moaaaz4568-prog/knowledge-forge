If I had zero followers and I had to get to ten thousand with one video, this is what I would post. It's called a My Story video and it almost always goes viral. And it follows these six steps.

Number one, the intro: state the problem you're feeling. For example: one year ago, I was trying to lose weight.

Number two, the inflection point: state the pain point. For example: I was going to my high school reunion in a year where I would have to see my ex Chad and I wanted him to see what he broke up with—freaking loser.

Number three, the rising action: state the failed solutions. For example: but the problem was nothing was working. I tried running, different diets, YouTube workouts, but the scale stayed the same.

Number four, the climax: state the solution that you finally found, aka your product or service. For example: I was fed up so I started to do my own research, created my own workouts and meal plans.

Number five, the falling action: state results from the solution. For example: because of this, I lost twenty pounds, got my dream six-pack and felt good.

Number six, the resolution: state the next step/CTA. For example: after this experience, I made it my mission to help women just like me get the same results. Interested? Follow for more fitness education.

Common story for my My Story scripting template.
