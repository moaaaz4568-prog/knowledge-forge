You guys have been asking how I color my videos, so here's my process. I shoot all my footage in S-Log2 on my Sony ZV-E10. I keep my ISO as close to 640 as I can because it's my camera's native ISO. In DaVinci, I convert all my footage back to Rec. 709 using Color Space Transform to take it back to a normal look. Then I make sure my white balance is correct using my global wheel and watch my vectorscope to make sure it's right. Next, I make an S-curve to give it a darker, more contrasty look. Then I add my creative LUT and add a little blue to the highlights and shadows for a more moody look.
