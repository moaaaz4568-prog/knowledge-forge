Stop building AI agents. You're wasting a ton of time. Do this instead. About six months ago when I first started getting into AI, I wasted hundreds of hours because I wanted to get right into building AI agents. I thought it was really cool because I was seeing the biggest creators build them and I really wanted to use them for my own business. But in reality, that actually hurt me more than it helped me. Because when you first get into AI and you start building your first flows, you don't have any knowledge on the basics. And building AI agents right off the bat is like you learning how to swim but you jumping in the deep end of the pool. You're gonna fucking drown. So what you need to do instead is you need to learn to build simple workflows. Learning how to take one simple input and getting one simple output across a few nodes. Once you get a workflow going, then you'll start understanding the complexities behind it. Then once you master building workflows, then you can move to AI agents. So if you're just getting into AI, start simple and work your way up.
