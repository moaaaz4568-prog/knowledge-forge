Here are three simple tips to unlock ChatGPT genius mode, and all you have to do is just add these to the end of your prompt. One is to tell it to act like an expert in that field with a real human example. So for example, if you're writing ad copy, you would say act like Dan Kennedy or act like Russell Brunson. Two is to ask me clarifying questions until you're about 98% confident in your answer. This will stop ChatGPT from just making stuff up. Three, in the actual prompt itself, give a ton of detailed context. For example, if I'm asking for it to write me an ad, instead of saying, give me an ad for a basketball academy, instead I would say, give me an ad for a basketball academy who trains kids ages 10 to 14 who are in the Nashville area, blah blah blah.
