Stop slamming your head against a wall trying to come up with content ideas. Instead, do this simple trick that would take you less than 60 seconds to do to find proven content ideas from accounts similar to yours. First, log into a different Instagram account and follow your main profile. Once you hit follow, Instagram will suggest other accounts to follow. These accounts are grouped in with yours by Instagram's profile because the same type of people that follow those accounts also follow you. This means the posts that performed well on those accounts have a high chance of performing well on yours.
