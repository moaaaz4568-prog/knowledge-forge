Underbuild, don't overbuild. Most people, including myself, love to build these complex systems. In reality, all of these automations, AI agents are all a waste of time. When you start building a system out, you should just be doing the work 100% manually. Then instead of trying to automate giant chunks of it, automate the littlest things. Even the littlest things that give you 20, 30 minutes a week back. Once you have those 20 or 30 minutes, you can start working on the bigger chunks. I have wasted so much time trying to overbuild systems, so don't make the same mistake that I've done.
