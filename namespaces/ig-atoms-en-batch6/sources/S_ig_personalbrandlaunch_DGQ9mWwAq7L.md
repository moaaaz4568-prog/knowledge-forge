If I had zero followers and had to get to 10,000 with one video, this is what I would post. It's called a My Story video and it almost always goes viral. And it follows these six steps. Number one, the intro: state the problem you were feeling. For example: 'One year ago, I was trying to lose weight.' Number two, the inflection point: state the pain point. For example: 'I was going to my high school reunion in a year where I would have to see my ex Chad and I wanted him to see what he broke up with—freaking loser.' Number three, the rising action: state the failed solutions. For example: 'But the problem was nothing was working. I tried running, different diets, YouTube workouts, but the scale stayed the same.' Number four, the climax: state the solution that you finally found, aka your product or service. For example: 'I was fed up, so I started to do my own research, created my own workouts and meal plans.' Number five, the falling action: state results from solution. For example: 'Because of this, I lost 20 pounds, got my dream six-pack, and felt good.' When I walked into my high school reunion six months later, Chad had visibly drooled, but I didn't even care about that prick or what he thought anymore. Number six, the resolution: state the next step slash CTA. For example: 'After this experience, I made it my mission to help women just like me get the same results.
