Instagram hook mistakes that are making you go from this to this. Number one, narrow versus broad hooks. Low-view videos use narrow hooks that only make one person care. High-view videos use broad hooks that make a lot of people care. For example, narrow hook. Three non-business books that I think every entrepreneur should read. Broad hook. I've read 997 books on making money. These five will make you rich. Number two, using three hooks. There are three types of hooks: the verbal hook, what you hear in the first three seconds; the visual hook, what you see in the first three seconds; and the written hook, what you read in the first three seconds. Low-view videos use one hook. High-view videos use three hooks. And number three, research hooks. Low-view videos freestyle their hooks, and high-view videos research and find outlier hooks in their niche that follow the 5X rule, aka they have 5X users than the account's following, and use those hooks in their videos. If you want a list of 1,000 viral hooks that you can use in your own videos, just comment 'hook' and I will send it over.
