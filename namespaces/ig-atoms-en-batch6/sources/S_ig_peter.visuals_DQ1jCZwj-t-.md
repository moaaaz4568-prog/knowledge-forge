Four outdated Instagram myths that are secretly killing your reach right now. Myth one: drafting your post before publishing lowers its reach. It's not true. The ranking system doesn't even know if you originally saved your post as a draft or not. If you want to use drafts, please feel free to do so. Your reach will not suffer. Myth two: using third-party apps to edit will lower your reach. We do not penalize content for being created in other apps. Myth three: posting two to four times a day will restrict your account. You should prioritize quality over quantity, but in general, posting more will help. The more you post, the more people you're going to reach and the more your follower count is going to grow over time. Myth four: Instagram is killing carousels and photos and pushing only videos. We do not downrank photos and carousels. Some people find that photos and carousels do better than reels, but most people don't. And that's because reels tend to be more engaging. Moving video, audio is just more engaging.
