Did you know there are five different types of stories?
Number one: personal stories. Sharing things about your life, feelings, or daily activities so people can get to know you better.
Number two: sales paid offer stories. Showing case studies, results, testimonials, before and afters, etc., and then plugging a link to your paid offer.
Number three: sales stories for lead magnets. Promoting something valuable that's free to get people's email or contact information.
Number four: authority stories. Posting stories showing you know what you're doing by sharing results, experiences, success, or knowledge.
Number five: educational stories. Teaching people something useful that helps them learn something or do better.

You'll probably post a mix of these, but I'm going to recommend predominantly sales stories and maybe personal one here and there. Follow for more social media marketing education.
