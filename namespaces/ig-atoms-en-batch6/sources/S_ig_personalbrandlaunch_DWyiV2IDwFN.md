What should people do after their first viral video? The dumb creators just do nothing and they keep posting like normal, but the smart creators break down the viral video into seven main components: the verbal, written, and visual hook; the structure; the format; the editing; the topic; the value that was used in the video; and the CTA. And with those components, they template-itize the viral video into a repeatable template and adjust the value slightly each time and recreate that over and over and over again until the video stops performing.
