Did you know that there are eight types of carousels? Number one, the tutorial carousel showing a niche step-by-step process system acronym or framework. Number two, the do's versus don'ts or right versus wrong old versus new carousel where you show what to do versus not to do to achieve a niche dream result.

Number three, an educational tips slash hack carousel. This is just a one-off educational niche tip slash hack. Number four, a storytelling carousel where you tell a story and each slide shows a new line and image from that time. Number five, a transformation carousel where you show before and after results and explain step by step how you got there. Number six, a problem solution carousel. This is where you share a niche problem then a solution to that problem over and over again on a carousel.

Number seven, list carousels. Each slide shows a new point on your list and number eight, a types carousel. This is when you show multiple different types of one niche related thing.
