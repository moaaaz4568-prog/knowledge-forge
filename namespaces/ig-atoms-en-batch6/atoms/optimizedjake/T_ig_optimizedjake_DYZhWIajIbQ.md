"If your revenue has been the exact same since last year, you don't have a motivation problem. You simply have a bottleneck. And the best way to remove a bottleneck in any business is by simply looking at KPIs. It's not going and investing in another course or talking to an expert. You don't need to do that." — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DYZhWIajIbQ.md

# T_DYZhWIajIbQ_02

"And the four main KPIs that I love to look at in any business is going to be leads, appointments, closed customers, and then your turn rate. So that means how many leads am I bringing in? How many of those are going to an appointment? How many of the appointments are closing? And then out of the customers that I'm bringing in, how many am I retaining every single month?" — sources/S_ig_optimizedjake_DYZhWIajIbQ.md

# T_DYZhWIajIbQ_03
