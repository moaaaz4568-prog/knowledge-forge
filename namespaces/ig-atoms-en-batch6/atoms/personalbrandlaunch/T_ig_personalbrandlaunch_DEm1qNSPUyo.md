# T_DEm1qNSPUyo_01

"Stop posting until you understand how the algorithm actually works. When you upload a video, it will get shown to a small group of people. There'll be a certain amount of watch time it needs to meet in order to get pushed out to other people. If it doesn't meet that watch time, it will die right there." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DEm1qNSPUyo.md

# T_DEm1qNSPUyo_02

"If it does meet that watch time, it will get pushed out to a bigger group of people, and a new watch time limit will be set. This process repeats over and over again; each new group of people is bigger, and each watch time limit gets harder. Eventually, it dies out. Instagram makes money by getting people to stay longer on their app and see more ads. So watch time is really all they care about." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DEm1qNSPUyo.md
