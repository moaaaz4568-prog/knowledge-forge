# T_DTvg_I3j0AV_01

"There are two types of Instagram algorithms. One is for your followers, connected reach, and one is for people who don't follow you yet, unconnected reach. If a piece of content performs well with people who already follow you, it will get shown to more people who follow you. If it does well with people who don't follow you, it will get shown to more people who don't follow you." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTvg_I3j0AV.md

# T_DTvg_I3j0AV_02

"If it performs well with both, it's going to get shown to both. Now there are different types of content you can post to do well with each algorithm. If you want more connected reach, you're going to want to focus on likes and retention. So post hyper-educational content... or problem-aware content." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTvg_I3j0AV.md

# T_DTvg_I3j0AV_03

"Or storytelling content because it's so much more personal; people who know you will respond better to it. For unconnected reach, you're going to want shares and retention." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTvg_I3j0AV.md

# T_DTvg_I3j0AV_04

"For this, you're going to want to post light educational, unaware content, which means your viewer doesn't need to understand your product, a problem, a solution, in order to understand your content. Depending on what your social media goals are, that'll determine what algorithm you want to cater towards, but most people are always going to be catering towards both." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DTvg_I3j0AV.md
