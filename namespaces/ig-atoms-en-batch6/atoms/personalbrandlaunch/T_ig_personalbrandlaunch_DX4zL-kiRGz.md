# T_DX4zL-kiRGz_01

"There are four levels of awareness. You may be using the wrong one in your videos to get results on social media. So listen up. Level number one is product-aware videos. These are videos about your product or service. They have low reach potential because not many people are going to know what your product or service is. Level number two are solution-aware videos." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DX4zL-kiRGz.md

# T_DX4zL-kiRGz_02

"These videos are about niche solutions to problems people know they have. They have low reach potential because most people don't know what the solutions are to their problem. Level number three is problem-aware videos. These videos are about niche problems people know they have. They have high reach potential because most people know what problems they have." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DX4zL-kiRGz.md

# T_DX4zL-kiRGz_03

"And level number four are unaware videos. These videos are about niche problems people didn't even know were problems, but they can relate to them. They have the highest reach potential because so many people can relate. So make sure the videos you're posting on social media follow level number three and four." — sources/S_ig_personalbrandlaunch_DX4zL-kiRGz.md
