I'm gonna give you three things that I do differently, and the last one is probably the thing that most people miss. Number one, I call every single prospect 30 minutes to an hour before the meeting. But on that call, I'm not saying like, hey John, are you coming to the meeting?" — sources/S_ig_promptedbyamber_Db8sF9-Bhe7.md

Number two, get a micro commitment while they're still on the cold call." — sources/S_ig_promptedbyamber_Db8sF9-Bhe7.md
