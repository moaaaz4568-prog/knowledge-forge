# T_DDII3tORykz_01

"You guys have been asking how I color my videos, so here's my process. I shoot all my footage in S-Log2 on my Sony ZV-E10. I keep my ISO as close to 640 as I can because it's my camera's native ISO. In DaVinci, I convert all my footage back to Rec. 709 using Color Space Transform to take it back to a normal look." — sources/S_ig_pdinbo_DDII3tORykz.md

# T_DDII3tORykz_02
