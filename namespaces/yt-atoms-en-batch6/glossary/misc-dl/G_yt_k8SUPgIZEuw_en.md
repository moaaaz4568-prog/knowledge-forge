# G_yt_k8SUPgIZEuw_en — beginnings

## Term

In this source beginnings are the series' subject: the speaker is exploring the concept of new beginnings — starting in a new field, leaving a job, or quitting to start a project — for people who feel stuck and want a radical change in their life. The word appears 5 times, beside new field, stuck, radical change, and series, with the singular beginning 8 more times, and he opens the episode by refusing to prepare his guest, asking him to tell one specific story instead. His concrete case is his own start: a friend told him to create a website and promised to advertise for him, and that story, he says, was the story of his beginnings in freelancing and content creation.
