# G_yt_lGdCaQtNxl8_en — modern architecture

## Term

In this design video, modern architecture is the style the landing page has to express, not just depict: the designer asks whether the page truly embodies the essence of modern and innovative architecture or is just another two-dimensional illustration kind of design. The phrase appears 3 times, beside Lasting Impression, bold imagery, dark overlay, and color scheme, and the fix is concrete — a main focus on the right, text content on the left, and imagery of modern architecture that falls in line with the color scheme. Its concrete case is the first of three designs in the video, a landing page about modern architecture that the designer strips down to its bare essentials before rebuilding it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that any modern building photo counts: the video wants the design itself to scream innovation, so an image that merely shows a building without the bold imagery and layout does not meet its test.
- Read as a synonym for minimalism: the designer pairs modern architecture with a dark overlay and bold imagery, and a stripped-down page that loses that energy is called out as missing something important.
