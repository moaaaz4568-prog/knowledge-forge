# G_yt_kCiDrMyo3OU_en — relentless focus

## Term

Here relentless focus is the speaker's answer to the secret of rapid agency growth: a relentless focus on building a product that solves a really narrowly defined client's biggest issues, which let the team innovate quickly and pile up compounded learnings. The phrase appears 3 times, beside narrowly defined, client's biggest issues, compounded learnings, and product, and he gives the scale — the agency 10xed its revenue in the past year and hit six figures a month — as the proof of the focus. His concrete case is the industry they picked: cosmetic dentistry, an industry that needed disruption for a long time, where getting to know the gaps took only a bit of time and the growth followed.

## Live Example

> "What is the secret to that rapid growth in the agency? >> I think it's a relentless focus on building a product that solves a really narrowly defined client's biggest issues." — sources/S_yt_kCiDrMyo3OU_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as working harder on everything: relentless focus here is narrowing to one product for one narrowly defined client problem, and the growth is credited to that narrowing.
- Read as a guarantee of ten times revenue: the 10x figure is this agency's result in the past year, and the speaker presents the focus as the secret, not as a promise for any agency.
