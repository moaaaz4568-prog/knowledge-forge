# G_yt_kyFzrCO3wBs_en — exclusive blueprint

## Term

In this source exclusive blueprint is Levi's method for making over one million dollars a year from YouTube with only 3,000 subscribers, which the speaker says viewers will get the chance to steal. The phrase appears twice, beside lead quality, lead flow, and YouTube channel, and the video is a conversation with Levi himself — the background, the company he started 11 or 12 months ago, and the questions a business owner would ask to copy him. His concrete case is the promise at the end: remember the exclusive blueprint mentioned at the beginning, the one that reveals how to make multi-seven figures and even eight figures from YouTube.
