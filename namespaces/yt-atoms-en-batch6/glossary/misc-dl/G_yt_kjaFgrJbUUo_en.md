# G_yt_kjaFgrJbUUo_en — accidental agency owner

## Term

In this source an accidental agency owner is what the interviewer asks Brandon Naw if he is, and he answers 100%: he never set out to own an agency, he stumbled into it through SEO after a real estate practice. The phrase appears once, beside Seattle Organic SEO, SEO, real estate practice, and Amazon, and his story runs from working at Amazon, Expedia, and a company bought by Google, to a friend convincing him he would be good at sales — which he detested. His concrete case is the first foray itself: instead of selling, he asked how people could find him, and that question became SEO, which his agency still specializes in.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a founder with a plan: the accidental agency owner here started a real estate practice and fell into SEO because he hated selling, not because he planned an agency.
- Read as a story about luck: the accident was the entry, and he did not create the agency until about six years later, after years of doing SEO first.
