# G_yt_ll_MEL2fun4_en — facebook group

## Term

In this video, a facebook group is a small client community — the example has thirty people in it — and the whole video is about increasing engagement inside one. The phrase appears 6 times, beside engagement, Monday, and Friday, and the concrete move is to run a rhythm: a Monday post and a Friday post, tag people by number, and give a high-five or an atta boy when someone contributes, so the group keeps talking. Its use here is a room, not an audience: the speaker answers a question about a client's group, and the advice is about what the members do for each other, with a mindset piece at the end of each email about staying with it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a broadcast channel: the group in the video is thirty clients who are supposed to engage with each other, and posting at them without tagging or replies is not the method.
- Read as a growth tactic: the advice is about engagement inside an existing small group, and no claim is made about growing the group's size.
