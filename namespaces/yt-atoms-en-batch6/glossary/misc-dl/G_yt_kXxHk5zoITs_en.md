# G_yt_kXxHk5zoITs_en — decision making

## Term

In this source decision making is the one concept the speaker says explains everything: whether you are at 10K a month or a billion a month, it all comes down to the decisions you have made in the past. The phrase appears 3 times, beside decisions, Bill Gates, sales calls, and appointment setting, and he makes the point personal — the only difference between him and Bill Gates is time, and more importantly the way they make decisions. His concrete case is the money itself: improve the quality of the decisions and the amount of money follows, which is how he says his agency reached $30,000 to $40,000 every single month.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a motivational slogan: decision making here is the speaker's whole premise, tied to past decisions and monthly revenue, not a poster quote.
- Read as a claim that effort does not matter: the speaker's point is that better decisions compound into more money, not that work itself is worthless.
