# G_yt_lvTragmYUFU_en — leads

## Term

In this video, leads are the contacts — names and valid emails — that the speaker extracts by the millions every month, and the video is a demonstration of how. The word appears 16 times, beside Million Verifier, contacts, and emails, and the concrete move is to run prospects through the Get Leads pipeline with Claude Code, then put them through Million Verifier, so that a hundred thousand at once takes about fifteen or twenty minutes and the emails that come out are valid. Its use here is a volume, not a list: the speaker says doing it manually would take a very long time and end up bankrupting him, and the leads are what the whole pipeline exists to produce.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any email addresses: the speaker's leads are contacts run through Million Verifier, and validity is the point — unfiltered addresses are not what the pipeline produces.
- Read as a guarantee of replies: the video shows extraction and verification, and nothing is said about whether the leads answer.
