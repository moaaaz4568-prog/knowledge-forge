# G_yt_k8diRxAcr9U_en — brand script

## Term

Here a brand script is the StoryBrand message a company hands its team: the one-liner and the message, which the current team must memorize or at least learn, because everything else — ads, video, SEO, blogs — is the wrong thing if the message is not down. The phrase appears 8 times, beside one-liner, message, memorize, and StoryBrand, with the plural brand scripts once more, and the speaker puts the cost bluntly: a confused visitor gives you two seconds before going back to Google and trying the next person in line. His concrete case is the survival test — a stranger who does not know what you do or how you can help must grasp it fast, or the sale is already lost.
