## Term

In this video, a digital product is the thing that pays its creator twenty to eighty percent commission: the speaker has helped over five thousand people build digital product businesses, and condenses them into the top four most profitable digital product ideas. The phrase appears 27 times, beside commission, niche, business, and profit, and the concrete cases are the Digital Wealth Academy at five hundred dollars and a thousand-dollar product teaching skiers, which made over a hundred and thirty-three thousand dollars in ten days. Its use here is a business model: a digital product is built once and sold to a passionate niche, and the video ranks the ideas by how much profit they can add per month.
