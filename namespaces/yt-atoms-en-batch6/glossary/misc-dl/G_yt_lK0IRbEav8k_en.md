# G_yt_lK0IRbEav8k_en — retention

## Term

In this Natural Born Leaders podcast, retention is the art of keeping students inside a business once they join: Luke says getting to a hundred thousand students is just about keeping him and keeping retention, because he knew the entire Playbook of how the business would play out before the first customer. The word appears 5 times, beside Playbook, students, customers, and outflow, and the concrete math is that a million a month can leave through drop-off, so the speaker says it is better to put capital into keeping people in than into getting new people in. Its use here is a growth argument: getting a company big on a recurring model is mostly about keeping a student, not about acquiring the next one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as customer service politeness: retention here is a numbers game about outflow versus inflow, and the video treats it as a capital allocation choice, not as being nice to students.
- Read as a synonym for acquisition: the speakers contrast the two directly, and the retention play is about the students already inside, while acquisition is the separate cost of bringing in new ones.
