# G_yt_kkjaw0JXpdI_en — hook archetypes

## Term

In this source hook archetypes are the six patterns the speaker says just work, whatever the niche: they come from testing thousands of hooks across the channel's own videos and those of its clients. The phrase appears once, beside hooks, six hooks, short form content, and thousands of hooks, and he frames them as fundamental principles of content learned from experience, not guesses. His concrete case is the scale itself: hundreds of videos posted, thousands of hooks tested, and one single video reaching upwards of 40 million views, all from short form content.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as six magic openers: the archetypes here are patterns from thousands of tested hooks, and the speaker says a video nobody stops to watch is worth nothing, archetype or not.
- Read as a trick for any niche: the claim is that they work whatever the niche, but the evidence is the channel's own tests, not a trial across every niche.
