# G_yt_kntcUApUBoM_en — chunk

## Term

In this source chunk is the verb for the first step of the memory method: turn what you must memorize into images, then group those images so the brain handles a few units instead of many. The word appears 6 times, beside visualize, images, and memorize, and the grouping is the point — ten individual digits become three units, and twelve items become eight when opposite columns are chunked together. His concrete case is the number nine as a cat, because a cat has nine lives, and the zebra for anything uncommon or rare.

## Synonyms

- chunked

> "number as 10 individual digits we chunk them together so that the brain only has to process three individual units another tip is to make the stories interesting and what do our brains find interesting action humor and sex so for example don't just turn" — sources/S_yt_kntcUApUBoM_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as memorizing by repetition: chunking groups items so the brain processes fewer units, and the repetition is what the grouping replaces.
- Read as a trick for numbers only: he applies the same grouping to drug names, columns of opposites, and whole stories, not just to digits.
