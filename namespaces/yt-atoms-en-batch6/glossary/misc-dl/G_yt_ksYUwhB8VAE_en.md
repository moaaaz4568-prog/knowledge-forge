# G_yt_ksYUwhB8VAE_en — problem solver perspective

## Term

Here problem solver perspective is the switch Serge tells a woman who asked how to deal with hardship in business: stop asking how to endure the painful problem and start seeing it as a specific problem that can be solved, duplicated, and sold. The phrase appears once, beside mechanism, duplicated and sold, and specific problem, and the logic is the whole video — if you solve a problem that many people have, you can build a mechanism around how you solved it and sell it to them. His concrete case is the mirror test: look at yourself and admit you have been avoiding problems, then deal with as many as possible.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "and accepting them and then just switching to a problem solver perspective solving that issue and then building a mechanism around how you solved it finding an audience and going and selling it that's really it and that's why i don't really understand how people" — sources/S_yt_ksYUwhB8VAE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to ignore the pain: he says to accept the problems first, and then switch to the problem solver perspective — the accepting comes before the solving.
- Read as hiring someone else to solve it: hiring is probably a good thing, he says, but you cannot replicate the result because you are not the one actually solving it.
