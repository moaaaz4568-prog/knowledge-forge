# G_yt_klLuB0tAfe0_en — logo type

## Term

In this source the logo type is the lettering half of a logo, and the speaker's third rule is that it must always complement the brand's identity and the message: getting the mark or symbol right is only half the game. The phrase appears 5 times, beside brand's identity, Chanel, Airbnb, and kerning, and he shows it with two cases — Chanel's modern, sleek, uppercase logo type with wide kerning, and Airbnb's rounded, modern, friendly lowercase type. His concrete case is the selling point: showing how the logo type targets a certain group and represents the brand psychologically adds to the designer's conviction when presenting the idea.

## Live Example

> "only half of the game the logo type itself is equally as crucial and should continue the message you're trying to convey to the target markets if you take a look at the chanel logo you can see the logo type is modern it's sleek" — sources/S_yt_klLuB0tAfe0_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the font choice alone: the logo type here must carry the message to the target market, and the speaker judges it by case, kerning, and style, not by the typeface name.
- Read as the less important half: the speaker says the logo type is equally as crucial as the mark, so a correct symbol with a wrong type still fails the rule.
