# G_yt_lj7GfJ1DMsU_en — sales tax

## Term

In this agency podcast, sales tax is the tax agency owners have to charge and remit, and the episode turns on the story of an infamous state sales tax audit from the early days of the agency. The phrase appears 25 times, beside agency, state, and charging, and the concrete move the speakers discuss is to get the charging right up front — if the agency messes up and does not charge it for months or a year, the customer gets hit with it all at once, which they call unfair. Its use here is a liability, not a pricing detail: the speakers treat sales tax as something that can quietly sink a small business owner, and the free trial of the agency playbook is offered in the same breath as the warning.
