# G_yt_kk9kJIg8SXI_en — database of freelancers

## Term

In this source a database of freelancers is the speaker's prescription for hiring: start building a database of at least 12 people who do exactly the job you want, so you are never starting from zero. The phrase appears once, beside freelancers, vet, hiring, and past work, and the episode is about how to vet them — look at their past work and make sure it is similar to the work you want. His concrete case is the style test itself: if they do grunge style, expect grunge style, and if they do not have enough examples in the style you want, move on.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "In this episode, Chris discusses hiring freelancers, how to vet them, the qualities they should have, and what to do when you first start building a database of freelancers." — sources/S_yt_kk9kJIg8SXI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of cheap labor: the database here is 12 people vetted by past work in the exact style wanted, not a pile of names to spam.
- Read as hiring on price: the speaker says to ask freelancers to explain how they work and where it was most successful, so the vetting is about fit, not cost.
