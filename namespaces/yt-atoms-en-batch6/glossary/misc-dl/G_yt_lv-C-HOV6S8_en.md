# G_yt_lv-C-HOV6S8_en — margin

## Term

In this video, margin is the profit kept after costs — the number the speaker says business owners should protect like a guardrail, instead of chasing revenue. The word appears 16 times, beside revenue, number, and business, and the concrete move is to stop chasing the top line number that feels good and start setting goals around margin, so raw momentum turns into stable, durable growth. Its use here is a guardrail, not a report: the speaker says revenue without margin is just running money through the business to keep the lights on, and the wrong number to chase is the one that makes owners feel good.

## Synonyms

- margins (plural form)

## Live Example

> "The problem is revenue without margin is just running money through your business to keep the lights on." — sources/S_yt_lv-C-HOV6S8_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as revenue: the speaker's whole point is that revenue and margin are different numbers, and chasing revenue while ignoring margin is the mistake the video is about.
- Read as a target you hit once: margin is described as a guardrail to protect continuously, and the growth it produces is called stable and durable, not a one-time win.
