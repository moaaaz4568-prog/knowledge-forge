# G_yt_kWZx6-UW3Tc_en — system for automating

## Term

In this source a system for automating is the missing piece the slide names after two failures: strategies that do not consistently work, and a lack of time — and the answer is not another strategy but a system that automates the work. The phrase appears once, beside strategies, lack of time, marketing project, and every month, and the slide ends with the warning that every month brings a new marketing project, so the work never stops arriving. Its concrete case is the gap itself: no system for automating means each new monthly project starts from scratch, and the line 'You need one!' is the whole prescription.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You don’t have strategies that consistently work. Lack of time. You don’t have a system for automating. You need one! Every month, there’s a new marketing project." — sources/S_yt_kWZx6-UW3Tc_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a tool to buy: the system here is a way of working that automates the marketing, not a named piece of software.
- Read as a complaint about time: lack of time is the symptom the slide names, and the system for automating is the fix, not another way to say you are busy.
