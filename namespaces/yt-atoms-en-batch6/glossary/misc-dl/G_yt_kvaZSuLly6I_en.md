# G_yt_kvaZSuLly6I_en — customer funnel

## Term

Here customer funnel is the best funnel the speaker says he ever used to build three businesses past $1 million a year: instead of turning a lead into a client, you turn a lead into a customer of something cheap, and that customer is far more likely to buy the real offer. The phrase appears 6 times, beside typical funnel, volatility, and coaching, and he opens with the pain that made him build it — $159,000 in June, then $46,000 in July, a swing of over $100,000 that made the business emotionally volatile too. His concrete case is the number he puts on it: a customer is 56 times more likely to net you a client than a cold lead, measured on 546 extra leads in one calendar year.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as leads do not matter: the funnel still starts with leads — it changes what happens after, turning a buyer of a cheap thing into a client instead of stopping at the lead.
- Read as a promise of stable revenue: he built it because month-to-month volatility hurt, and the funnel is his answer to it, not a guarantee that revenue stops moving.
