# G_yt_kYwbEropYW4_en — lens flare

## Term

In this source a lens flare is the Photoshop light effect the speaker adds to a bland image to improve it fast, and his whole tip is about putting it on its own layer: a new layer holds the flare, so it can be erased and moved without touching the image. The phrase appears 18 times, beside layers panel, Dodge and burn, Photoshop, and filter render, with the plural lens flares 2 more times, and he warns against the novice way — filter, render, lens flare straight onto the image layer — because it locks the effect in place. His concrete case is the layers panel itself: open it from the window drop down menu or press F7, then add the flare on a new layer and erase parts of it with a soft brush at low opacity.

## Synonyms

- lens flares (plural form in the source)

## Live Example

> "a lens flare you will need your layers panel open which you can access in the window drop down menu or you can press f7 on your keyboard a way that many people add a lens flare is quite a novice way and I did" — sources/S_yt_kYwbEropYW4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a filter you apply once: the lens flare here lives on its own layer, so it can be erased and repositioned, and flattening it onto the image is the mistake.
- Read as decoration only: the speaker uses the flare plus Dodge and burn to change the dramatic lighting and feel of the image, not to add a random shine.
