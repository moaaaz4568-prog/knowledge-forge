# G_yt_kAIIlknJSII_en — build and release model

## Term

Here the build and release model is the speaker's path to a first twenty-five to fifty thousand a month online: build a system once, then implement it into different companies every single month, instead of selling services nobody wants to buy. The phrase appears 3 times, beside scale, systems, AI, and clients, with the shorter build and release 12 times in all, and he says AI changed the math — cheap to acquire leads, appointments, and clients, where late last year no easy system existed. His concrete case is the offer behind the webinar: improve one system by ten percent, add five new clients from it, and a single client becomes worth a hundred and ten thousand dollars over its lifetime.

## Synonyms

- build and release (shorter form the speaker also uses)

## Live Example

> "companies grow but who wants to skip the commoditize route of selling services that no one wants to buy and instead wants to Leverage The build and release model where they essentially build system once and implement it into different companies every single month to scal priz their first 25k 50k" — sources/S_yt_kAIIlknJSII_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as selling services: the model is building a system once and implementing it in many companies, which the speaker contrasts with the commoditized route of selling services nobody wants.
- Read as an AI trick: the speaker says AI made client acquisition cheap, but the model itself is the system and its monthly implementation, not the AI tool alone.
