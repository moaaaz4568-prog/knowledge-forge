# G_yt_lUYaDcD2sLc_en — local meetups

## Term

In this video about landing clients for an AI services business, local meetups are the single best method the speaker has used himself: hosting them got him seven qualified leads in about two hours of effort, and he walks through all seven methods in the video. The phrase appears once, beside LinkedIn outreach, partnering with agencies, and seven ways, and the concrete promise is that the three methods to start with first are revealed at the end, after everything from hosting local meetups to LinkedIn outreach has been covered. Its use here is a ranking: local meetups sit at the top of the seven, because they put the seller in the room with the buyers.

> "them even got me seven qualified leads in about two hours of effort. So I'm going to walk you through all seven, everything from hosting local meetups to LinkedIn outreach to partnering with agencies and more real tactics that are actually working for folks selling AI services right now." — sources/S_yt_lUYaDcD2sLc_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that meetups replace all outreach: the video teaches seven ways, and local meetups are the best of them, not the only one — LinkedIn outreach and agency partnerships are taught alongside.
- Read as a guarantee of seven leads: the seven qualified leads in two hours are the speaker's own result from one meetup, not a promise every meetup delivers the same.
