# G_yt_kew6UGCaPQs_en — typeface combo

## Term

In this source a typeface combo is the speaker's unit for pairing fonts on a design: the first really useful font, or shall he say typeface combo, is Anek matched up with Elron, and each pair gets a job — headings, body text, or a serious look. The phrase appears once, beside font combinations, Anek, Elron, and Roboto Slab, and he walks through the pairs one by one — Roboto Slab with Mulish for serious designs, Oswald with Open Sans for posters, Liberation Serif with Liberation Sans. His concrete case is the Anek and Elron pair itself: standard bold Anek, or Anek Bold Expanded, for headings, with Elron regular or medium as the small body text, which he calls a real game changer on modern designs.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any two fonts together: a typeface combo here is a tested pairing with a job — Anek for headings, Elron for body text — not two fonts picked at random.
- Read as a rule that pairs must be different families: the Liberation pair is a serif and a sans serif of the same typeface, so staying in one family can be the combo.
