## Term

In this agency podcast, visibility is the practice of getting yourself in front of the right opportunities: Sharon says the whole episode is about the appropriate ways to do it, and Caroli Moore is brought on to give a master class in it. The word appears 24 times, beside agency, opportunities, clients, and podcast, and the concrete turn is Caroli's own story — from agency owner to focusing on visibility and how to achieve it in her business today. Its use here is a business discipline, not vanity: visibility means the right people knowing what you do, and the guest keeps hearing that if people just knew what she did more, she would be far more successful.
