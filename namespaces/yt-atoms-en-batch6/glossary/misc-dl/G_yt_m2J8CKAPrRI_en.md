# G_yt_m2J8CKAPrRI_en — business

## Term

In this interview with chronic-fatigue coach Toby Morrison, a business is the coaching practice he built from a huge vision he held since he was 16 into a program serving clients in 75 countries. The word appears 17 times, beside clients, team, 75 countries, and documentary, and the concrete move is to put the money the program makes back into better people and a better team so clients win more. Its use here is a working practice, not an abstract idea: lifetime access once sold 2,000 memberships yet the business was not producing enough income to support the clients better, so the speaker's early mistake was not foreseeing that future from a business standpoint.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a straight line from wanting to getting: the speaker says no business runs straight from wanting to getting, so the early screw-ups and the 2,000-member shortfall are part of the business, not proof it failed.
- Read as the program's size: 75 countries and 2,000 members describe reach, while the speaker measures the business by whether its income supports clients better with a better team.
