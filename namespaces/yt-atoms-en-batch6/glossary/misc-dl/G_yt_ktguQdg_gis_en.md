# G_yt_ktguQdg_gis_en — memetic

## Term

In this long interview memetic is the speaker's word for how people pick up who they become: we copy people, not just ideas, and we copy the ones we look up to, so the person you end up as depends on who you chose to copy. The word appears 10 times, beside copy people, outbound, and 500k a month, and the conversation keeps returning to the same move — look at someone like Hermosi or an artist you admire, and notice what you take from them. His concrete case is his own path: from door-to-door sales and MLMs that never panned out to outbound on LinkedIn, then running a business doing over 500k a month, by copying the people doing it.
