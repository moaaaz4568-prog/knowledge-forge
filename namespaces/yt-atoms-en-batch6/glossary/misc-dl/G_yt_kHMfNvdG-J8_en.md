# G_yt_kHMfNvdG-J8_en — non-manufacturing PMI

## Term

In this source the non-manufacturing PMI is the one economic indicator the speaker says is flashing red: the ISM survey of services companies, historically the most reliable indicator for recession on a coincident and slightly leading basis. The phrase appears once, beside ISM, services, contraction, and expansion, and the rule is a single number — anything below fifty is a sign of contraction, anything above fifty is expansion. His concrete case is the world it measures: as the economy became more service-based and less manufacturing-based, the services survey moved in line with the economy, and its first negative turn in years is the red flag.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a manufacturing index: the non-manufacturing PMI surveys services companies, and the speaker's red flag is its first negative turn in years, not the factory survey.
- Read as a prediction of recession: the speaker calls it the most reliable indicator on a coincident and slightly leading basis, and the fifty line marks contraction, not a dated forecast.
