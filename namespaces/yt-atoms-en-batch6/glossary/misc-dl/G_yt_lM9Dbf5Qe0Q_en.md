# G_yt_lM9Dbf5Qe0Q_en — storyboard

## Term

In this video about making films with AI, a storyboard is the drawn plan for a short film: the speaker helps a filmmaker create a whole storyboard for the movie he is going to shoot, and use it for financing the full-length movie. The word appears 37 times, beside artificial intelligence, concept creation, short film, and financing, and the concrete case is a storyboard built with AI image tools — Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2 are named as the two examples for editing images in storyboards. Its use here is a production step: the storyboard turns an idea into something a financier can see, and traditionally an artist would draw it, while here AI does the drawing.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the finished film: the storyboard is a plan for the short film and a tool for financing the feature, so a set of images with no film behind them is not a storyboard in this video.
- Read as proof that AI replaces the artist: the video still calls them traditionally artist storyboards, and the AI tools are used to create the boards faster, not to remove the filmmaker's decisions.
