# G_yt_m55DxnESELY_en — client work

## Term

In this video for editors, client work is paid editing for a client, the path the speaker took from editing as a hobby to making over $100,000 from it in a year. The phrase appears 19 times, beside editing, hobby, long form, and six step system, and the concrete move is to shoot for client work first and, if that fails, negotiate down to a pay trial or a fee trial while spending most of the time editing. Its use here is a revenue ladder, not a portfolio: the speaker orders the steps, niching down, showcasing work, finding clients, then getting good and getting fast, and warns that editors who try both long form and short form end up good at neither.
