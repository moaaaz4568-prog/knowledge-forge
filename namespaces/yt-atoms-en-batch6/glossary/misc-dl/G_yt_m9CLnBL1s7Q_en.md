# G_yt_m9CLnBL1s7Q_en — normalize

## Term

In this Spain vlog, to normalize is to make an extraordinary life feel ordinary, and the speaker treats that as a condition for achieving it. The word appears 1 time, beside Spain, team, villa, and life, and the concrete move is the one he names from a six-million-dollar villa near Marbella: when you can actually do this it becomes normal, and if you cannot normalize it you probably cannot achieve it. Its use here is a mental step, not showing off: the video is a house tour and a team trip, but the point the speaker draws from it is that the life has to stop feeling insane before it can be lived.

## When the Meaning Breaks
