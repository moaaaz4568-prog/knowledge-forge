# G_yt_kTsZAvOC3E4_en — chart of accounts

## Term

In this source the chart of accounts is the accounting structure the speaker tells agency owners to take charge of: it is the list of accounts behind the agency's books, and few people are doing it even though it is simple. The phrase appears twice, beside accountants, profitability, agency operations, and cash, with the plural charts of accounts 1 more time, and the episode frames it as one of the unglamorous operations metrics that decide whether an agency can grow. His concrete case is the advice itself — if nothing else, take charge of your chart of accounts — delivered after talking it over with a whole bunch of accountants.

## Synonyms

- charts of accounts (plural form in the source)

> "a whole bunch of accountants about this very thing and it's so simple and yet it's so few people are doing it so if you're listening to this I would encourage you to if nothing else just take charge of your chart of accounts the" — sources/S_yt_kTsZAvOC3E4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as bookkeeping software: the chart of accounts here is the structure of the accounts themselves, and the advice is to take charge of it, not to buy a tool.
- Read as a growth tactic: the speaker puts it among operations metrics, so it is about knowing where money goes, not a trick for winning clients.
