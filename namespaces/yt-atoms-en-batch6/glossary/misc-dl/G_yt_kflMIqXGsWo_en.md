# G_yt_kflMIqXGsWo_en — hidden message

## Term

In this source a hidden message is the secret meaning inside a logo design, and the speaker's example is a festival logo designed to appear as two eyes watching something — a message that sits inside the mark without being spelled out. The phrase appears once, beside logo design, two eyes, Meta, and Walmart, and he contrasts it with the previous story, where the design was not well received, while this one was well received online. His concrete case is the logo itself: two eyes watching, obviously relating back to the festival, so the hidden message is the watching itself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a subliminal trick: the hidden message here is a designed pair of watching eyes, and the speaker says the project was well received, not that viewers were manipulated.
- Read as the whole video's topic: the hidden message belongs to one logo story among several — Walmart, Meta, the BBC — so it is one case, not the series.
