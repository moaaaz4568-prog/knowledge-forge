# G_yt_lLnyjgjgYLw_en — talent

## Term

In this agency podcast, talent is the top concern agency owners raise about building and scaling: no matter what the economy does, talent stays the huge question mark that keeps owners perplexed, either about scaling or about being as profitable as they need to be. The word appears 50 times, beside agency, global workforce, cost arbitrage, and culture, and the concrete case is building a dispersed talent base overseas, where the language barrier is easier to solve than the differences in culture and skills. Its use here is a management problem: talent is not just hiring, but putting people in a position to be successful, and the structure around them decides whether they work out.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a hiring numbers game: the video says people fail to work again because the structure is not there for them to succeed, so adding headcount without that structure does not solve the talent problem.
- Read as a synonym for cheap overseas labor: the video discusses cost arbitrage openly, but frames it as one reason to look overseas, while the talent question is about culture, skills, and integration with the team.
