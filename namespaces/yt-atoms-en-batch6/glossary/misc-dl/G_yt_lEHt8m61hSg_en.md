# G_yt_lEHt8m61hSg_en — quest log

## Term

Here quest log is the game mechanic the speaker borrows for studying: in every game you are given quest logs and objectives with specific tasks to complete, and the same list keeps you going when studying feels like a drag. The phrase appears once, beside power-ups, well-defined storyline, and dopamine, and the video works through three ideas that make video games compelling — a storyline, objectives, and power-ups — then applies each one to a study routine. His concrete case is the games themselves: in Super Mario, Mario always rescues Peach from Bowser, and in Zelda, Link always has an epic showdown with Ganon, so the player always knows what the next objective is.
