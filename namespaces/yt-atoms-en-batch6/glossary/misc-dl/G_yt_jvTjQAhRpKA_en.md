# G_yt_jvTjQAhRpKA_en — limiting beliefs

## Term

In this source limiting beliefs are the beliefs people build about themselves that cap what they attempt, and the speaker asks how they form and how to begin identifying them. The phrase appears 6 times, beside identify, build, end goal, and visualize, and he pairs it with a practice: visualizing the end goal and letting it guide day-to-day choices, so that losing focus is caught early. His concrete case is his own habit — when he feels lost or starts to lose focus on the goal he is trying to achieve, he returns to the visualized end goal to re-aim his day.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a diagnosis of laziness: the speaker treats limiting beliefs as ideas people build about themselves, not as a lack of effort, and asks how they form before fixing them.
- Read as positive thinking: visualizing the end goal here is a focusing practice for daily choices, not a promise that picturing success creates it.
