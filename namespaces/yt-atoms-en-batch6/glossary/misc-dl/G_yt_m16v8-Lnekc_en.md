# G_yt_m16v8-Lnekc_en — high agency

## Term

In this podcast, high agency is the quality of people who see what is coming before others can and act on it — the guest says his mind has always worked that way, and the hosts tie it to neurodivergence and ADHD. The phrase appears 10 times, beside agents, start, and neurodivergence, and the concrete move is to stop doing machine-like things, anything with a step-by-step rhythm that turns into a system, and hand those to AI agents instead — OpenClaw running on a Mac mini, meetings that send an Uber automatically — so the person's time goes to what only they can do. Its use here is a way of living, not a productivity hack: the guest says the first goal is a life in complete alignment with the way your mind works, and high agency is the name for that alignment.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for using AI agents: the agents are the tools high agency people use, and the agency itself is the human quality — seeing ahead and acting — that the tools serve.
- Read as a diagnosis: the speakers mention neurodivergence and ADHD as part of the conversation, and high agency is presented as a way of operating, not a medical claim.
