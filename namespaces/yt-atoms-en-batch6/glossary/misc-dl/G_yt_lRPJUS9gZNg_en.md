# G_yt_lRPJUS9gZNg_en — data

## Term

In this podcast, data is the advantage that matters now that AI is out: the speaker says the value is not about who has the team of media buyers and services, but about who gets data right now — data from his own internal company. The word appears 20 times, beside media buyers, AI, ads, and companies, and the concrete comparison is one business against three hundred companies running ads, selling, and fulfilling: data from three hundred businesses is infinitely greater than data from just one. Its use here is a moat argument: a single company's data is not valuable on its own, and the build and release model is pushed because it collects data across many companies at once.
