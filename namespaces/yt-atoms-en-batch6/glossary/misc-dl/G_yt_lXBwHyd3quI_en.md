# G_yt_lXBwHyd3quI_en — deconstruct

## Term

In this video about learning skills fast, to deconstruct is the first step of Tim Ferriss's framework: most skills worth learning are made up of multiple smaller sub-skills, and deconstructing the new skill means breaking it into those parts. The word appears twice, beside skill, interviewing, sub-skills, and YouTuber, and the concrete method is interviewing based — interview people who have mastered the skill you are trying to learn, so they can give an overview of what to practice. Its use here is demonstrated on becoming a better YouTuber: writing, speaking on camera, and editing videos are the sub-skills the breakdown starts with, before the other steps of the framework.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for breaking down in general: deconstruct here is the named first step of a framework, with interviewing masters as its method, not just any analysis of a skill.
- Read as the whole framework: deconstruct is step one, and the video also teaches the other steps — including Stakes, setting consequences so you follow through — so deconstructing alone is not the method.
