# G_yt_m4XzGzS7dmQ_en — project

## Term

In this video about turning a hobby into something real, a project is work with a deliverable and four criteria, set against a hobby that is just a fun thing done for ten minutes. The word appears 13 times, beside hobby, guitar, four criteria, and deliverable, and the concrete move is to check the four criteria before calling something a project: the speaker's own guitar playing, learned from YouTube videos, is a hobby because it has no deliverable, while a project must. Its use here is a defined undertaking, not any pastime: the distinction is the whole point, so messing around with a few chords is explicitly not a project.
