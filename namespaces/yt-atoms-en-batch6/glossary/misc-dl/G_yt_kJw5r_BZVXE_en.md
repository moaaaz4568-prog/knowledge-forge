# G_yt_kJw5r_BZVXE_en — typography

## Term

In this source typography is the lettering-logo craft the speaker teaches in Illustrator: he builds a logo from custom hand lettering or a typeface, then shapes it with the appearance window, a stroke, and anchor points. The word appears 9 times, beside lettering, stroke, appearance window, and anchor points, and he keeps returning to the same move — create outlines of the lettering so each letter can be edited and moved on its own. His concrete case is the top layer of the logo, where a white fill plus a stroke makes the typography sit above a matching textured layer below.
