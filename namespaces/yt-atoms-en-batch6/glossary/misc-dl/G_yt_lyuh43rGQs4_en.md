# G_yt_lyuh43rGQs4_en — middle class

## Term

In this video, the middle class is the group whose jobs sit mostly in government, education, and health care, and the speaker's argument is that this dependence makes them the most exposed to federal layoffs and spending cuts. The phrase appears 29 times, beside government, private sector, and jobs, and the concrete move is to count the exposure: layoffs might affect at most two percent of total middle class employment directly, but the bigger issue is getting rid of government inefficiency, which hits the middle-income range hardest. Its use here is an economic group, not a mood: the speaker calls the Department of Government Efficiency a weapon of class destruction, and says the beneficiaries will be the middle class in the private sector, who will spend less on taxes and health care.
