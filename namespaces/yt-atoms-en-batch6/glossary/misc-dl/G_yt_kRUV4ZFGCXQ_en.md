# G_yt_kRUV4ZFGCXQ_en — intent data

## Term

In this source intent data is the speaker's name for insights into the online behaviors of a target audience, and he splits it at once: first party intent is the data from your own channels, third party intent comes from elsewhere. The phrase appears 3 times, beside first party intent, third party intent, online behaviors, and target audience, and he pauses to define it because the whole system depends on knowing what the audience is already doing. His concrete case is the priority rule that follows it: do the triggers first, because the people behind them are more likely to reply, and then work the larger list.
