# G_yt_lHv8j6IYGys_en — free for commercial use

## Term

In this roundup of resource websites for designers, free for commercial use is the label on a font site's button that tells the designer a download is legally safe to use on any project. The phrase appears twice, beside font, download, browse categories, and creative artwork, and the concrete case is the first site in the video, a font website called font-esque where every download is shown with creative artwork, and the button promises the typeface can be used commercially without a separate license. Its use here is a trust signal: the designer checks the button before using a font, rather than assuming a free download is free to use.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee that every font on the site is free: the button marks individual downloads, and the video shows the designer checking it per font, not assuming the whole site is covered.
- Read as a design judgment: the button says nothing about whether the typeface looks good — the video separates the legal check from the creative artwork shown with each download.
