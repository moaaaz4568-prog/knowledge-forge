# G_yt_lBhpHISZ03Y_en — awareness

## Term

In this source awareness is the level an expert must read before speaking: followers who already follow you have a certain level of awareness, and you address them differently from strangers who do not. The word appears 28 times, beside client network, target audience, and experts, and the question comes from consultations with experts across the Arab world — trainers, consultants, people with courses and books — who also do client work. His concrete case is the architect who has projects for clients and also trains people: should he talk to real estate developers, or to people who want to become architects like him.
