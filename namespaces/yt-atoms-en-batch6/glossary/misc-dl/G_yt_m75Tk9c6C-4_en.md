# G_yt_m75Tk9c6C-4_en — limiting belief

## Term

In this video about money in business, a limiting belief is a belief about money that caps what the speaker can earn, named so it can be listed and faced. The phrase appears 1 time, beside money, revenue, scale, and capping, and the concrete move is the question he puts to the viewer: what is one limiting belief about money that is literally capping your revenue right now, with his own examples being that his industry cannot scale and his business cannot scale. Its use here is a cap to remove, not a feeling to accept: the monthly math around it, 50K a month with a team taking 15 or 20 percent, is the evidence of what the belief costs.

## Synonyms

- limiting beliefs (plural form)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a mindset excuse: the speaker ties the belief to concrete monthly numbers, so it is tested against revenue and team costs, not treated as a mood.
- Read as proof the industry cannot scale: the belief that an industry cannot scale is the speaker's own example of a limiting belief, and he lists it to challenge it, not to endorse it.
