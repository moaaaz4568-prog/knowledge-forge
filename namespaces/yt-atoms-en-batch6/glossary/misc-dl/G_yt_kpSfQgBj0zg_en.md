# G_yt_kpSfQgBj0zg_en — fonts in use

## Term

Here fonts in use is the tab of a typography resource site that lets a designer see which fonts famous brands actually use, in categories such as logo, movies, music, games, and books, each with subcategories. The phrase appears twice, beside commercial use license, famous brands, and typography, and the video tours free resources one by one — a text-size calculator, then this site, then more. His concrete case is the study itself: look at a brand's font, learn why it was chosen, and even download the font directly when the license allows it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a font download site only: the speaker says the main attraction is studying what fonts are used in famous brands and learning why they are used — the download is secondary.
- Read as a claim that every font there is free to use: some fonts on the site do not have a commercial use license, and he flags that before recommending anything.
