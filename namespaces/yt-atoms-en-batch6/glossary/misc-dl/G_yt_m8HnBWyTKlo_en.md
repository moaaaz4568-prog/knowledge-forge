# G_yt_m8HnBWyTKlo_en — retention

## Term

In this agency podcast, retention is the work of keeping a brand's customers coming back, and the specialist's job the speaker recommends to sellers who cannot win on ads. The word appears 4 times, beside email marketing flows, SMS, community building, and brands, and the concrete move is to become the retention specialist: find brands whose retention is bad, whose email flows, SMS, and community building are all weak, and build a program that acquires those brands and increases their retention. Its use here is a service to sell, not a metric to watch: the speaker packages it as a three-part done-for-you offer, and the point is that brands do not need another advertiser when their retention is broken.
