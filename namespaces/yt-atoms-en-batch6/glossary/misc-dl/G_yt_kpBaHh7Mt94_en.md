# G_yt_kpBaHh7Mt94_en — appointment setting strategy

## Term

Here appointment setting strategy is the exact method the speaker says his team uses to scale to six figures: a genuine and personal connection with the audience, built through content, then converted into booked calls. The phrase appears 7 times, beside postbooking process, voice memos, and warm outreach, and he is explicit that the strategy is not his invention — his team spoke to the best in the space and invests tens of thousands of dollars a month to learn it. His concrete case is the database of voice memos — openers and things that happen often in conversation — that setters are trained to send because people immediately think it is actually him.
