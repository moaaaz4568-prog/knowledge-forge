# G_yt_ldnk0Rj9Rvc_en — earned insights

## Term

In this Cape Town vlog about launching Try Cook AI 2.0, earned insights are the understanding a business gains by doing the work — the platform the speaker describes talked about them as the thing that lets you find product market fit. The phrase appears 5 times, beside Try Cook and product market fit, and the concrete move is to build the platform first and let the insights come from using it, instead of guessing what customers want from the outside. Its use here is a payoff, not a feature list: the speaker is excited because the goal is actually to solve something for people, and the earned insights are what the work produces along the way.
