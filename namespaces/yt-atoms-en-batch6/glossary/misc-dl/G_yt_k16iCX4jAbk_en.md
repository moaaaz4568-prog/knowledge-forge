## Term

Here the pen tool is the most important tool in Adobe Illustrator, introduced with the program's debut in 1987, a year before the speaker was born: it creates the anchor points that form the basis of designs, and lines connect to those points to make curves and shapes. The phrase appears 6 times, beside Adobe Illustrator, P, anchor points, and 1987, and the speaker builds the video as a guessing game — facts first, then the reveal that the tool sits on the P key. His concrete case is the tip from the corner of the video: check the linked game that walks you step by step through the pen tool, because even experienced designers found it almost impossible to use when it first appeared.
