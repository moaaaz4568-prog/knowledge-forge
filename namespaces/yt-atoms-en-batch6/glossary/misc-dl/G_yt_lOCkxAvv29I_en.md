# G_yt_lOCkxAvv29I_en — appointment sitters

## Term

In this agency clarity session, appointment sitters are the team members who handle the outreach volume the owner cannot do alone: the speaker says you need at least two of them, because the volume of email outreach cannot be done without a team. The phrase appears once, beside outreach message, channel, team, and volume, and the concrete rule is that the outreach message stays the same and the channel stays the same — only the sitters change, because one person doing it alone is what the speaker calls madness. Its use here is a staffing argument: the process is built first, and the sitters are hired to run it at a volume a single owner cannot reach.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for the owner doing outreach: the video says you need an actual business and a team, so an owner sending every message alone is exactly the madness the sitters are meant to replace.
- Read as a guarantee that two sitters fix everything: the speaker pairs the sitters with a fixed outreach message and channel, so hiring sitters without that process is not what the session teaches.
