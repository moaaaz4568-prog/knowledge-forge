# G_yt_mQfXh-RUncw_en — scalable

## Term

In this video ranking outreach channels, scalable is the quality that decides whether a channel can grow, weighed against whether it is effective. The word appears 21 times, beside Discord, appointment booking rate, niches, and effective, and the concrete move is the ranking itself: a Discord appointment rate of 5 percent is really high but the channel is not going to work for many niches because the group cannot be found, so it is not scalable, while a channel that needs many people on the team to create many profiles fails the same test. Its use here is a growth test, not a compliment: the speaker sorts channels into scalable and not effective and not scalable, and the verdict follows from whether the channel can be repeated across niches.

- Read as a synonym for effective: the speaker ranks channels on both axes, and a channel can be effective in one niche yet not scalable because its group cannot be found.
