# G_yt_mGCWSGoiKss_en — media buyer

## Term

In this video about hiring for ads, a media buyer is the person hired to run a client's advertising, and the hire the speaker says only works after you have done it yourself. The phrase appears 13 times, beside hire, service delivery, Facebook ads, and performance offer, and the concrete move is the warning he opens with: hiring someone without having done it yourself goes badly, and so does doing it with no experience, so the way in is a performance offer that lets you gain experience and close with conviction. Its use here is a role to grow into, not a shortcut: the whole business can crash when service delivery is out of control, and the speaker's restart plan is to learn Facebook ads first and then hire.
