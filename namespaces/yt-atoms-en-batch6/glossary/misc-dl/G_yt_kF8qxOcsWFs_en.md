# G_yt_kF8qxOcsWFs_en — Niche

## Term

In this source Niche is the market a creator picks first, and the speaker's rule is to pick by fit, not by fashion: ask which sector of life — health, wealth, relationships — then which part of it you relate to most and which problem in it you have solved most. The word appears 14 times, beside health, wealth, relationships, and passionate, and he warns that most entrepreneurs pick the wrong niche because it sounds good or because other people are doing it. His concrete case is the passion test: you do not have to be the most passionate about your niche, but you have to care about solving the problem, because that care is what lets you go through the suffering of building the business.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a topic you like: the speaker's rule is to pick the sector and the problem you have solved, and picking by what sounds good is exactly the mistake he names.
- Read as a promise that passion removes suffering: he says caring about the problem lets you go through the suffering of business, not that the suffering disappears.
