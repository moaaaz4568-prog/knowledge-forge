# G_yt_lJkiUF_pAcA_en — deliverability

## Term

In this cold email guide, deliverability is whether the emails actually land in the inbox: the speaker says a lot of things can mess it up in the first place, and matching the inbox type to the lead's inbox type can dramatically increase it. The word appears 6 times, beside inbox, infrastructure, reply rates, and cold email, and the concrete method is to run multiple layers of cold email infrastructure and rotate in a fresh set when the stats go up — proof the deliverability was the problem. Its use here is a measurement discipline: the speaker tests it about once a month, because it is constantly changing, and treats it as the first thing to fix before changing copy or offer.
