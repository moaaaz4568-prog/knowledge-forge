# G_yt_jti-LqwtBGk_en — initial first clients

## Term

In this source initial first clients names the milestone Daniel Diaz is reporting on day 74 of becoming a millionaire: he has three clients for his lead generation agency, and the next step is getting them results, because getting paid follows only once results arrive. The phrase appears twice, beside results, paid, lead generation, and day 74, and he frames the whole stretch as testing back and forth with clients to learn what they actually want. His concrete marker is the sequence itself — first clients, then results, then payment — which he calls the business tradeoff completing.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "months ago I was talking about how we would have our initial first clients we got our initial first clients the next thing I talked about is is actually getting the results and you know when we get them results we get paid that is" — sources/S_yt_jti-LqwtBGk_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as paying clients: the speaker counts three initial first clients and still has to earn results before payment follows, so the milestone is not revenue yet.
- Read as a finished agency: day 74 is a progress report, and the next step is testing with clients, not a claim that the millionaire goal is reached.
