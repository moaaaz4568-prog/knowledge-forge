# G_yt_lW9jp54Qxio_en — overcommoditized

## Term

In this agency podcast, overcommoditized is the risk Jessica names for agencies now that AI has arrived: being overcommoditized is a real risk and a race to the bottom if you are not careful, because more competition than ever is saying the same things. The word appears once, beside race to the bottom, competition, AI, and content, and the concrete warning is that even sharing content similar to somebody else can land differently — but only if what you say is positioned a little differently. Its use here is a positioning problem: the risk is sounding like everyone else, and the defense is a message that lands differently, not a louder version of the same post.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And and I feel like especially now with the rise of AI and more competition than ever before, being overcommoditized um is like a real risk and kind of a race to the bottom if you're not careful." — sources/S_yt_lW9jp54Qxio_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for competitive: overcommoditized here means interchangeable — agencies blending in until the only difference left is price — not merely having rivals.
- Read as a claim that AI caused it: the video says the rise of AI made the risk sharper, but the race to the bottom is described as price-based, and the cause is sameness, not the tool itself.
