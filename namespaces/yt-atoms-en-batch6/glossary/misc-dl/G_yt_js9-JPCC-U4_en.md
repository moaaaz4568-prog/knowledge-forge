# G_yt_js9-JPCC-U4_en — align

## Term

In this source, align names the Illustrator move of lining text up on a guide: the speaker shows pressing Command or Control R for rulers, placing text along a guide, then holding shift, selecting the text, and clicking the guide again so everything snaps to it. The word appears 9 times, beside guide, text, and snap, with the past form aligned once more, and the speaker contrasts a text that only looks aligned — a small gap remains because Illustrator aligns the font box, not the letters — with one fixed by creating outlines first. His concrete case is a t-shirt design where three text layers are aligned at once with shift, then grouped with Command or Control G.

## Synonyms

- aligned (past form in the source)

## Live Example

> "guide here is a very quick way to do that with this tip hold down shift and then select the guy that you want to align to as well as a text you can then really shift and click the guide one more time and" — sources/S_yt_js9-JPCC-U4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as aligning the letters themselves: Illustrator aligns the font box here, so a text that looks aligned can still sit a small gap off the guide until its outlines are created.
- Read as a one-click fix: the speaker's align needs shift-selecting the text, clicking the guide again, and checking the snap — a single click on the align options does not finish it.
