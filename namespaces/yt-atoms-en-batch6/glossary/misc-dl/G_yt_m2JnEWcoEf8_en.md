## Term

In this video about changing your life, a phase is one of the named stages the speaker walks through, from acknowledging where you are to seeing the gap between where you are and where you want to go. The word appears 18 times, beside breakthrough, momentum, moment, and progress, and the concrete move is to name the stage you are in: phase four is the Breakthrough, the moment things start to click into place with a small win, and phase five is momentum.
