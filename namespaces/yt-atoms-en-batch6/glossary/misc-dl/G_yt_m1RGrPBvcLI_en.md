In this video, a sales process is the whole system the speaker has run for a year and a half — appointment setting on the back of it, a slide deck that walks clients through pillars, and a script for explaining what it is and its benefits before moving to the next deck. The phrase appears 8 times, beside system, slide deck, and appointment, and the concrete move is to target people looking to buy or sell a home right there, since real estate is a relationship game, and to run them through the pillars so they understand the system before they are asked anything. Its use here is a system, not a pitch: the speaker says people think he is crazy when he tells them this has been his whole sales process the whole time, and the video is a walkthrough of exactly how he has done it.
