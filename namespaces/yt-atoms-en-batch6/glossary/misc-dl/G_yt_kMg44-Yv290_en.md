# G_yt_kMg44-Yv290_en — 10 grand a month

## Term

In this source 10 grand a month is the first major milestone the speaker sets for coaches: after the first client comes the first ten thousand dollars, then ten thousand a month for three months in a row. The phrase appears 13 times, beside straightest line plan, first client, stuck in the struggle, and three to five k offer, and he says most coaches stall on it because they get confused by all the competing options sold to them. His concrete case is the straightest line plan itself: an offer of three to five thousand dollars that needs only two to four clients, with leads every day and clients every week.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single good month: the milestone here is ten thousand a month for three months in a row, not one lucky month.
- Read as a mindset problem: the speaker says the stall is mostly the wrong strategy and too many options, not a lack of belief.
