# G_yt_lUGemxzxP58_en — mood board

## Term

In this agency-building diary, a mood board is the direction for a brand identity: the speaker shows a deep dive into exactly how she creates mood board directions, on day eight of building a new design agency from the ground up. The phrase appears 11 times, beside brand identity, direction, fonts, and word mark, and the concrete case is a 2,000 brand identity project where the mood boarding starts today, with bold Sun serif condensed fonts picked for their impact and a word mark to match. Its use here is a working step: the mood board is made before the logo, it sets the direction, and the choices are explained row by row — top, middle, and bottom — against the client's needs.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the finished brand identity: the mood board is the direction made before the logo, so a board with no logo or identity behind it is a step, not the finished brand.
- Read as a free-form inspiration collage: the speaker explains each choice against the client's needs, so a board of pretty images with no reasons behind the picks is not a mood board direction here.
