# G_yt_mKSCF1_XOys_en — garbage

## Term

In this video for editors, garbage is the low-quality information, work, and input an editor takes in, and the speaker's rule is that it comes back out in the work. The word appears 9 times, beside editing, editor, brain, and rush, and the concrete move is the mentor's quoted rule, garbage in garbage out: put garbage into your body and performance is garbage, put garbage information into your brain and the way you speak is garbage, so the fix is to consume good editing, document what you take in, and build a reference for what good is. Its use here is an input-output rule, not an insult: the speaker's charge is that most editors are terrible because they consume no good editing, and the reason he can charge so much is a better reference.
