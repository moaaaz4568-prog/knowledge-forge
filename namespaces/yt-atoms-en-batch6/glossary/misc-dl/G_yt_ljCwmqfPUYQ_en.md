# G_yt_ljCwmqfPUYQ_en — leverage

## Term

In this video about building businesses, leverage is the thing that lets a business make a hundred grand or two hundred grand while the owner works ten hours a week or less on it, because systems and other people carry the work. The word appears 11 times, beside business, sales, and month, and the concrete move is to build the business around a project management tool and a sales process that clients can follow on their own, instead of chasing new clients month after month. Its use here is a structure, not a trick: the speaker says the new business was started without leverage and took far more effort, while the current one runs because the leverage was built in from the start.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as working less for the same money by luck: the speaker's leverage is the tool, the process, and the team that were built deliberately, and the hours drop only after that structure exists.
- Read as a synonym for having money: the leverage described is operational — a system clients can use without the owner — and no amount of capital replaces it in the video's argument.
