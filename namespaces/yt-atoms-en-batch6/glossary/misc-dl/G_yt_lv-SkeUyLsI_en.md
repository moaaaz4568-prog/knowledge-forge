# G_yt_lv-SkeUyLsI_en — biotech

## Term

In this episode of Ask a Hedge Fund Manager, biotech is the sector of drug and vaccine stocks the speaker is asked about, and his answer is that he personally does not trade it unless it is a broader index. The word appears 13 times, beside drug, stocks, and valuation, and the concrete move is to value a biotech company by its total addressable market and the probability that its drug makes it through every FDA approval phase — or its local regulator's phases outside the US. Its use here is a risk, not a tip: the speaker says figuring out the actual TAM and the probability of success is so difficult that it needs a scientific medical background, and that difficulty is the reason he stays away.
