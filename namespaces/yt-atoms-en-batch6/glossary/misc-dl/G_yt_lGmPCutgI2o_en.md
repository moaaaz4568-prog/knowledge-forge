# G_yt_lGmPCutgI2o_en — stylescapes

## Term

In this live design episode, stylescapes are the curated visual boards Chris Do and Jose present as the second step of a design process, after gathering ingredients and before telling the story: a stylescape lays out the look of a brand through images the client reacts to, in mild, medium, and spicy versions. The term appears 5 times, beside brief, narrative, ingredients, and demographics, and the concrete case is a client presentation where the first stylescapes are shown, the client gives feedback on each version, and the team listens before refining the direction. Its use here is practical rather than theoretical — a stylescape is a tool for getting the client to respond to a visual direction before any final design is made.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a finished brand design: a stylescape is a direction the client reacts to, and the video treats it as a step before the design is made, not the design itself.
- Read as a mood board with no client input: the video shows the team listening to the client feedback on each version, so a stylescape presented without that reaction step misses how it is used here.
