# G_yt_lABLakZu_Fk_en — software

## Term

Here software is the speaker's own definition and the pivot of the whole video: when you program a computer in a way that allows a human to skip a task, that is software, and that is what scales where coaching cannot. The word appears 19 times, beside skip a task, coaching business, and recurrent revenue, and the argument is that coaches end up deeply sad and frustrated — smiling and putting videos on the beach so people believe they are happy — while the revenue goes up and then plummets back down. His concrete case is the win that follows the definition: with AI that lets people skip tasks, the offer becomes more attractive, because now you have AI that allows people to skip tasks.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as software replacing coaches: the point is the offer — a product that lets a human skip a task — not that coaches disappear, and the video is about what to sell instead of coaching.
- Read as a claim that coaching never works: it works for one or two months of revenue and then people leave, which is why it cannot scale, not why it never sells.
