# G_yt_kKkrf3Ljh4s_en — Smart short clip

## Term

In this source the Smart short clip is a feature of the video editing software the speaker is reviewing, and he calls it perfect for content creators: it takes a long form video and cuts it into short clips, tracking the action so the subject stays in frame. The phrase appears once, beside aspect ratio, long form videos, break dancing, and content creators, and he shows it by uploading a clip of someone break dancing and letting the AI follow the movement. His concrete case is the aspect-ratio choice in the top left, which decides the shape of the short clip before the AI starts tracking.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a manual cut: the Smart short clip is the AI doing the tracking and the cutting, not the editor trimming by hand.
- Read as a tool for long videos only: the speaker uploads a long form video to make short clips from it, so the input is long and the output is short.
