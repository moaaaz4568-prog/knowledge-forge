# G_yt_l71LEIoGBwA_en — leveraging your pricing

## Term

In this source leveraging your pricing is the reason Serge doubled and tripled the price of his program: he understood it was the easiest way to unlock true wealth, and he would not let the market dictate how much value he was delivering. The phrase appears twice, beside blue ocean, price is relevant, and financial freedom, and the decision was a single one — in less than a moment, the program went from around 3k to two or three times that, becoming one of the most expensive programs out there for agencies and high-ticket services. His concrete case is the result: they are still getting as many customers, probably even more, because the higher price created a kind of blue ocean for him.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as raise prices and customers will follow: the point is refusing to let the market dictate the value delivered — the decision is about who sets the price, not about the number.
- Read as a promise of even more customers: he says they are still getting as many customers, probably even more, which is a report on what happened, not a guarantee.
