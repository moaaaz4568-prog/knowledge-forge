# G_yt_mOFJLBV0LbM_en — dip

## Term

In this video for YouTube creators, the dip is the stuck period every creator hits, where views fall and the work feels both technical and emotional. The word appears 24 times, beside creators, YouTube, emotional, and technical, and the concrete move is the one the speaker teaches: stop making videos that assume familiarity, since videos only interesting to people who already know the creator keep a channel in the dip, and use the seven paradigm shifts to get unstuck. Its use here is a phase to get out of, not a failure: nine creators currently in the dip sat down for the video, and the speaker says every YouTuber admired has gone through multiple dips in their journey.

## Synonyms

- dips (plural form)

## Live Example

> "You see, one of the single most common mistakes keeping creators in the dip is making videos that assume familiarity. videos that are only interesting if you're already familiar with the creator, the topic, or the context." — sources/S_yt_mOFJLBV0LbM_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a slump in views only: the speaker says the dip is just as much emotional as technical, so fixing thumbnails without fixing the feeling misses half of it.
- Read as a sign to quit: the speaker's nine creators are all currently in the dip and working through it, and getting unstuck is the video's whole subject.
