# G_yt_mD434d7Si7A_en — rectangle tool

## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial, the rectangle tool is the tool that draws the rectangles the whole design is revolved from. The phrase appears 2 times, beside thinner rectangle, revolve, 360 degrees, and ellipse tool, and the concrete move is the one the speaker walks through: press M for the rectangle tool, draw a thinner rectangle because thinner works best, then revolve it around 360 degrees and repeat the process, deleting every other rectangle. Its use here is a starting tool, not the finished shape: the rectangles are only the raw material, and the design appears after revolving, repeating, and deleting.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the design itself: the rectangle tool only draws the starting rectangles, and the pattern comes from revolving and deleting, not from the tool alone.
- Read as any rectangle: the speaker insists on a thinner rectangle, ideally thinner than the one shown, because the revolved pattern depends on that proportion.
