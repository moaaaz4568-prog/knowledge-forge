# G_yt_lE_Z4RFjQs0_en — your voice

## Term

Here your voice is the thing Taki says a coach must hang on to even though AI makes it tempting to let someone else write everything: the opinions and the energy must come from you, because the audience feels it. The phrase appears 3 times, beside AI slop, content team, and opinions and the energy, and the video opens with a confession — other people used to write his stuff, every email and article, sort of his voice and sort of not. His concrete case is the content team he hired: they took transcripts of workshops he ran inside Black Belt and turned them into articles and emails, and it worked for a bit, because the coverage was never bare — but the voice was not fully his.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never use AI: he says to use robots and people to help brainstorm and help edit — the line is at replacing your voice, not at using the tools.
- Read as write everything yourself: the voice and the opinions must come from you, while the team and the tech help with the rest — he hired a content team himself and kept the voice.
