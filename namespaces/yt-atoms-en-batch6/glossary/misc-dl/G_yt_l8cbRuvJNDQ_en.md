# G_yt_l8cbRuvJNDQ_en — pivot

## Term

Here pivot is what Daniel's lead generation agency is being forced to do on day 110 of becoming a millionaire: the email tests they expected to work did not, so they must try new things until something sticks. The word appears twice, beside pay on results, email campaigns, and lead generation agency, and the week is framed as a step back — normally 10 to 12 hour days of content and running the agency, now a slower trip while the tests get rebuilt. His concrete case is the pressure: pushed against the clock, with two pay-on-results clients, and new ideas for the emails that they kept believing would work.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as quitting the business: pivoting is testing new things until something sticks, and the agency keeps its three clients and its pay-on-results work while it does.
- Read as a strategy he chose freely: he says they are being forced to pivot, pushed against the clock, because the tests they expected to work did not.
