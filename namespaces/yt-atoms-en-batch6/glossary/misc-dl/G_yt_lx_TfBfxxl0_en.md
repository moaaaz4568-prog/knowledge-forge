# G_yt_lx_TfBfxxl0_en — contract value

## Term

In this video, contract value is the dollar figure on signed deals that gurus flex online, and the speaker's warning is that it is not money in the bank. The phrase appears 7 times, beside grand, month, and business, and the concrete move is to compare the flex — three hundred grand a month in contract value — with what a great business actually does, which is profit at thirty percent margins, reinvest capital into paid ads, and pay taxes the speaker says he has now gotten wrong twice. Its use here is a vanity metric, not a result: the speaker says people look at a hundred thousand a month, about one point two million a year, and think it is a lot of money, and the video explains why it is not.
