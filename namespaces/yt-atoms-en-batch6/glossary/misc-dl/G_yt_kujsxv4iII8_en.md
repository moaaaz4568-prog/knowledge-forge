# G_yt_kujsxv4iII8_en — Blue Ocean strategy

## Term

In this interview Blue Ocean strategy is the name the guest gives to going after a specific area where clients are underserved: you do not fight the whole market, you find the pocket the other agencies ignore. The phrase appears once, beside expensive problems, Ecom, and underserved, and the conversation is about growth for e-commerce brands — a space so competitive that the only opening is a named, narrow area. Her concrete case is the client work she describes: brands like Talkspace, whose biggest competitor is BetterHelp, where the marketing manager spent heavily and the edge came from focus, not from outspending everyone.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as avoiding competition entirely: the strategy is finding an underserved pocket inside a competitive space — ecom is sophisticated and competitive, and the opening is a specific area.
- Read as a market with no competitors at all: the agencies in the space are super outdated, which is the gap, not the absence of a market.
