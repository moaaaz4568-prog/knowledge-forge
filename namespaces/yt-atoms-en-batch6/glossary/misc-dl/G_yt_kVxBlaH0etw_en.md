# G_yt_kVxBlaH0etw_en — AI gold rush

## Term

In this source the AI gold rush is the speaker's name for the wave of money-making openings AI has created, and the episode collects the most popular AI guests to break down exactly which opportunities to go after in 2026. The phrase appears once, beside 2026, micro agencies, Lovable, and Claude Code, and he splits the episode in two — this one on the money and business side, the next on the building side. His concrete case is Patrick Dang's model: one simple business model generating $3,000 to $5,000 a month in micro agencies with AI at the heart of it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that AI prints money: the gold rush here is a set of opportunities the guests break down, and the episode is about which ones to go after, not a guarantee.
- Read as a single trick: the episode condenses many guests — Lovable products, iOS apps, micro agencies — so the rush is many openings, not one model.
