# G_yt_l8cKThmYabY_en — right dopamine

## Term

In this one-on-one session right dopamine is the phrase Serge uses for the things worth chasing: being comfortable or confident, inspired, and proud of himself and his team, instead of chasing whatever the success brings. The phrase appears once, beside craft my offer, 300K a week, and framework, and the session is a check-in on what the team is achieving — close to 300K a week, with him behaving and taking action as if that is what he wants. His concrete case is the discipline he states at the core: his success must not dictate how he behaves, and at the core he is still the same Serge who was born in Rwanda and spent 13 years there.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a neuroscience claim: right dopamine is his own phrase for wanting the right things — being comfortable, confident, and inspired — not a lab measurement.
- Read as dopamine being bad: he distinguishes the right dopamine from the wrong chase, and the point is which things you chase, not the chemical itself.
