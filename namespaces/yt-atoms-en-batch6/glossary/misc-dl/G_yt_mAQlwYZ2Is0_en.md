# G_yt_mAQlwYZ2Is0_en — retainer

## Term

In this agency video, a retainer is a monthly payment from a client, and the missing piece that would let the founders work on their business instead of worrying about bills. The word appears 1 time, beside monthly, clients, bills, and pay on results, and the concrete move is the restructure they are planning: drop the client who has not paid an upfront cost, swap in a person who wants a monthly retainer, and get at least one client paying that way. Its use here is a cash-flow fix, not a pricing choice: the three clients they have all pay on results, so nothing is coming in right now, and the retainer is what would make everything chilling.

## Synonyms

- retainers (plural form)

## Live Example

> "If we were doing monthly retainers, we would have been having enough money come in to just work on our business and not have to worry about bills." — sources/S_yt_mAQlwYZ2Is0_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a premium price: the speaker's retainer is valued because it arrives monthly, and the problem it solves is bills, not status.
- Read as a promise of results: the retainer pays for the work itself, and the speaker's current pay-on-results clients are the ones leaving them unpaid right now.
