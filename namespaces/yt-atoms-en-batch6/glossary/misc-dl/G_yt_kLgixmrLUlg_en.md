## Term

In this source hashtags are the Instagram discovery tool the speaker teaches in part four of her series: look at the hashtags of someone you want to compete against, copy paste them, and research each one until your posts can reach the top nine. The word appears 12 times, beside top nine, carousel, analytics, and copy paste, and she ties them to a posting rhythm of two posts a day, with frequency and consistency as the key she tested herself. Her concrete case is her own experiment: posting once every three weeks brought few followers, posting once a day brought more, and posting up to five a day brought the most.
