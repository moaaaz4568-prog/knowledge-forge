# G_yt_lZGePPbYMfA_en — brain

## Term

In this video about viral videos, the brain is the viewer's neurological system that decides whether a video spreads, and the speaker's whole argument is that content techniques work only when they line up with how it responds. The word appears 43 times, beside dopamine, viral, attention, and content, and the concrete move is to build a video around what the brain does — dopamine spiking when the type of content shifts — rather than around the trend being copied. Its use here is a mechanism, not a metaphor: every technique the speaker lists, from format stealing to open loop hooks, the credential shortcut, and editing pace, is justified by the same claim that it aligns with how the brain acts at the neurological level.
