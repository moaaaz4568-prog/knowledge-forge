# G_yt_mRRN8-iDRbc_en — grades

## Term

In this video about school and work, grades are the marks the speaker says do not compare with real experience when hiring. The word appears 10 times, beside GPA, 4.0, real experience, and website, and the concrete move is the hiring test he gives: two applicants for building a website, one with a 4.0 GPA and perfect grades, and the speaker picks experience, because nothing compares to real experience, which teaches responsibility. Its use here is a foil, not a measure: the speaker's own grades were not going to cut it, and learning how to learn is not even about grades, so the video's argument runs against the transcript of marks.
