# G_yt_ktQ0IqCnXoY_en — free button

## Term

In this source free button is the speaker's metaphor for a video that generates sales for free: a button in your business that you press and it brings you 20,000 pounds in sales, so why not press it 16 times a day. The phrase appears once, beside 237 views, 10 million pounds, and real estate, and the whole video is built on one number that sounds impossible — a video with 237 views on TikTok generating 10 to 15 million pounds in sales. His concrete case is the arithmetic: a video with 3,500 views bringing 100 customers at 200 pounds each equals 200,000 pounds, all from a video that cost nothing to post.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you have a free button that brings you 20,000 pounds in sales, when will it bring you 200,000 pounds in sales?" — sources/S_yt_ktQ0IqCnXoY_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a literal button in the business: the free button is his metaphor for a video that brings in sales when it works — there is no button to install.
- Read as a claim that any video sells: only a video done correctly, hitting the nail on the head in a high-tech industry like real estate, brings the millions he describes.
