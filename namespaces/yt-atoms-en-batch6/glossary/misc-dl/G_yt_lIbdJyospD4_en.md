# G_yt_lIbdJyospD4_en — pen tool

## Term

In this Illustrator tips video, the pen tool is the tool for drawing awkward shapes — a logo mark or custom typography — by clicking to place points and pulling out handles to bend the path. The phrase appears 4 times, beside shape, handles, shift, and anchor points, and the concrete tricks are to hold down shift to keep the handles straight and press shift and C to add anchor points while still using the same tool. Its use here is hands-on: the pen tool is one of two pencil hacks the video promises will change how the viewer draws, and the Navigator panel is introduced separately as a way to move around large documents.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a tool for straight lines only: the video uses the pen tool to pull handles and bend paths around a shape, so a shape drawn only with clicks and no handle work misses its point.
- Read as the same thing as the pencil tool: the video calls these pencil hacks but demonstrates the pen tool's handles and anchor points, and the two tools are not used interchangeably here.
