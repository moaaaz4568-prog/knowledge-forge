# G_yt_m73yaCnF-IQ_en — lead magnet

## Term

In this video about turning content into email subscribers, a lead magnet is the free tool, worksheet, or checklist offered at the end of a video to move a viewer from YouTube to email. The phrase appears 2 times, beside call to action, audience, followers, and email, and the concrete move is to give each video two calls to action, one for a magnet and one for a collaboration, so the magnet is the bridge between watching and subscribing. Its use here is a bridge, not the content itself: the speaker says not every piece of content needs a call to action, but when it has one, the magnet is what takes the viewer off YouTube and onto the list.

## Synonyms

- magnet (short form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "You know, one is an audience that builds followers, one is a lead magnet, the same magnet as that one." — sources/S_yt_m73yaCnF-IQ_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any free download: the speaker's magnet is specifically the thing that moves a viewer from YouTube to email, and a freebie with no such bridge is not doing that job.
- Read as a rule that every video must sell: the speaker says not every piece of organic content needs a call to action, so a magnet is optional per video, not mandatory.
