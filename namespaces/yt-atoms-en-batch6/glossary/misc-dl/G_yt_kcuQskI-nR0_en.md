# G_yt_kcuQskI-nR0_en — shape psychology

## Term

In this source shape psychology is the course the speaker teaches: how shapes influence emotions and perceptions on graphic designs, and how mastering it changes the way clients look at and respond to the work. The phrase appears 6 times, beside circles, geometric shapes, organic shapes, and unity, and he starts with circles because they have no beginning and no end — symmetrical, smooth, and enclosed. His concrete case is the Pepsi circular logo, which tries to convey friendliness and community, and car brands that use circles for the secure and safety factor.

> "What if I told you that if you could master shape psychology, it would actually change how clients look and respond to your work?" — sources/S_yt_kcuQskI-nR0_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule that circles always mean the same thing: shape psychology here is about what each shape evokes, and circles are only the first shape before geometric and organic ones.
- Read as decoration: the speaker says shapes change how clients respond, so a shape is chosen for the feeling it creates, not for looking nice.
