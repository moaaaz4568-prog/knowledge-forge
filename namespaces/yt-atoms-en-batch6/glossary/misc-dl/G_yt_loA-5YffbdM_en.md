# G_yt_loA-5YffbdM_en — paid acquisition

## Term

In this video about SaaS, paid acquisition is buying customers through ads, and the speaker treats it as one channel inside a wider system for acquiring clients affordably. The phrase appears 4 times, beside SaaS, offer, and CPM, and the concrete move is to show the math — a thirty-dollar CPM, twenty-one grand spent — and then build the rest of the system around it: more reach organically, content created and distributed, and an offer that retargets. Its use here is a cost, not a growth guarantee: the speaker says the content side matters because after the first month the same old offer has no more value, and paid acquisition without the system behind it stops working.
