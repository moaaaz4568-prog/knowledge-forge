# G_yt_kFnrvHLjKtE_en — selling websites

## Term

Here selling websites is the first business everyone online recommends, and the speaker's attack is that the recommendation is self-serving: it is easy to deliver, so people think it will make a lot of money, but when something is easy it is also worthless, because everybody can do the same thing. The phrase appears 5 times, beside easy, GoHighLevel, affiliate, and worthless, and he names the hidden incentive — sellers push it because they earn forty percent of everything buyers pay GoHighLevel, a hundred or two hundred grand a month in affiliate income. His concrete case is the pitch itself: a YouTuber telling a fifty-, sixty-, or seventy-year-old to go find clients and sell them a website, calling it the greatest opportunity around making money with AI.
