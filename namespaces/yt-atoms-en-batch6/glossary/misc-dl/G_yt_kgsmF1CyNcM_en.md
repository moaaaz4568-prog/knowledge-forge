# G_yt_kgsmF1CyNcM_en — the same thing differently

## Term

In this source the same thing differently is the speaker's point about communication: there are multiple ways of talking about the same thing differently, and the choice is not only what to say but how often to say it and in what format. The phrase appears once, beside the same topic, communicate, what format, and how often, and the slide pairs the two questions together — the same topic, and then how often do you communicate and what format. Its concrete case is the slide itself: one heading that turns a single topic into many messages, so the format and the frequency are part of the message.
