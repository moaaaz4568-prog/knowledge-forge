# G_yt_lHMTwMHTpvI_en — the bridge

## Term

In this video about AI agencies, the bridge is Marcus's own name for his reframe: a business is a bridge, with the customer on one side — missing calls and losing leads — and more money, less chaos, and fewer hours on the other, and the agency's job is to be the bridge that takes them across. The phrase appears 8 times, beside vehicle, customer, reframe, and business owner, and the concrete warning is that beginners obsess over the vehicle — the latest AI tool or update — while the business owner on the other side of the bridge does not care about the vehicle at all. Its use here is a sales reframe: the bridge names the outcome the client wants, not the technology used to deliver it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a product name: the bridge is a metaphor the speaker invented for this video, not a tool or course, and it describes the gap between the customer present pain and the outcome they want.
- Read as permission to ignore the technology: the video still teaches AI automation, but it says the technical side is the vehicle, and selling the vehicle instead of the crossing is what beginners get wrong.
