## Term

In this source attribution is the hard problem between marketing and sales that Sharon puts to agency owners: which side produced the lead, and which side produced the sale, when both pass the buck between them. The word appears 6 times, beside swim lanes, lead generation, and leads versus sales, and the conversation turns on measurement — agencies that cannot attribute cannot tell a client what the marketing actually did. Her concrete case is the tools that changed the game: call rail and others that let an agency pinpoint the sale and the footprint in the journey, instead of guessing from Analytics.
