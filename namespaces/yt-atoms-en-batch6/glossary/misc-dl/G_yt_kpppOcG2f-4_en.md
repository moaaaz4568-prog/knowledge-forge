# G_yt_kpppOcG2f-4_en — anything can work

## Term

Here anything can work is lesson number one from growing a knowledge business to seven figures: the mistake is never intentionally choosing what to do, and instead being molded into whatever everyone else is doing. The phrase appears 5 times, beside follow the leader, student wins, and knowledge business, and he repeats the lesson as a warning — dozens of his students and peers all followed the leader into the same low-effort content, which by definition ends up in oversupply. His concrete case is the decision itself: build a business aligned with what you want to do, because trust him, anything can work once you actually choose it.

## Live Example

> "And so try to build a business that is aligned with what you want to do because trust me, anything can work." — sources/S_yt_kpppOcG2f-4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as every business model being equally good: the lesson is that choosing deliberately beats following the leader, not that all choices pay off the same.
- Read as permission to copy whatever trend is working: following the leader is exactly the mistake he names — everyone dragged in whatever direction the market is going.
