# G_yt_l79sq9xRLMI_en — procrastination

## Term

In this source procrastination is what the speaker says she was bullied for, and she defines it plainly: it is not laziness, it is running away from a certain task that gives you some feelings, and preferring not to feel them, so you go to your comfort zone instead. The word appears 4 times, beside self-compassion, comfort zone, and laziness, and the conversation moves from the definition to the remedy — how to build self-compassion — and to her own background, from graduating straight into an aviation fuel job to social media. Her concrete case is the group she describes: people in Accountability Coaching with almost the same goal, working toward routines and healthy habits together.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Procrastination is when I'm running away from a certain task that gives me some nice feelings, and I prefer not to feel them." — sources/S_yt_l79sq9xRLMI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as laziness: she says explicitly that procrastination is not laziness — it is running away from a task that gives some feelings you prefer not to feel.
- Read as a willpower problem: the remedy she names is building self-compassion, not discipline — the work is on the feeling, not on the effort.
