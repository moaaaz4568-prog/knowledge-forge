# G_yt_lunbjJJzFiI_en — brand

## Term

In this rebrand walkthrough, a brand is the whole feel of a business — its visuals, its tone of voice, and what it proves without saying it out loud — and the video shows one being rebuilt from a lifeless Canva template into something alive. The word appears 16 times, beside design, identity, and strategy, and the concrete move is to start with a typeface like Portland Italic for its forward-leaning confidence, then push deeper with a mood board and a word mark until the brand feels ownable. Its use here is an identity, not a logo: the speaker says strategy drives every design decision, and the brand is judged by whether it feels alive, not by whether the mock-ups look good in a deck.

## When the Meaning Breaks
