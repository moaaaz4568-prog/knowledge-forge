# G_yt_lolRWBQ9mmQ_en — color group

## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial, a color group is a named set of swatches saved in the Swatches panel, and the video shows how to build one from a logo's colors. The phrase appears 5 times, beside swatch, logo, and panel, and the concrete move is to select the logo, create a new color group, give it a name relevant to the logo or the branding project, and add the swatch to the library so it can be shared with fellow designers or clients and exported to the computer. Its use here is an organizer, not a palette: the group is what turns loose swatches into a brand catalog for the materials used in a project, and the tutorial walks through the quick modifications that can be made inside the panel.
