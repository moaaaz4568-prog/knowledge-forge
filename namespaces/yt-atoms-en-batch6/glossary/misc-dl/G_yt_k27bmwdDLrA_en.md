# G_yt_k27bmwdDLrA_en — pivot

## Term

In this source pivot names the agency's turn from commercial work to nonprofits: the speaker says they let go of their commercial work and pivoted into helping nonprofits, because that was where they could do the greatest good and have the most impact. The word appears 4 times, beside nonprofits, commercial work, letting go, and impact, and he describes the move as a ripple — getting better in the nonprofit space elevated their craft, which flowed back into the nonprofits they serve. His concrete case is the rescue-to-new-life arc: thirteen years working with girls through rescue, rehabilitation, training, education, and new life skills, on what he calls an absolutely transformative new path.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rebrand: the pivot here is letting go of commercial work and doing nonprofit work instead, not a new name on the same clients.
- Read as abandoning craft: the speaker says the nonprofit space elevated their craft through a ripple effect, so the pivot raised their standards rather than lowering them.
