# G_yt_mL77K9ioMYE_en — business email

## Term

In this video for designers, a business email is the professional address a client sees first, and the speaker's case is that it should be set up properly from the start. The phrase appears 6 times, beside client, domain, first thing they see, and designer, and the concrete move is the setup he walks through: choose the business email name, claim the domain, and within a few steps the designer is up and running, able to see when a client has read a message and respond accordingly. Its use here is a first impression, not an inbox: the email is often the very first thing a client sees when reaching out or getting a reply, and the speaker ties it to pricing on results, where the work becomes more valuable once clients see it.
