# G_yt_lzsO09jGYAw_en — fast pass

## Term

In this video, a fast pass is the speaker's image for leverage — like a fast pass tour guide against a normal tour guide, it lets people skip the line and get to the dream outcome faster. The phrase appears 5 times, beside tour guide, leverage, and leave, and the concrete move is to build something that makes it really hard for a customer to leave, because you skip time for them, and skipping time is giving them value they will keep paying more for. Its use here is a metaphor, not a ticket: the speaker's leverage is literally a thousand X because every conversation makes the thing better, and the fast pass is the way that leverage feels to the customer.
