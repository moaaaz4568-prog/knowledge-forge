# G_yt_kEFsZa_odlE_en — Own it

## Term

Here Own it is the name of the body of work Christy Hiler and Jean Freeman are doing about women who own agencies: the host calls it exciting work she wants more people to know about, and the conversation treats the advertising industry as a microcosm of women in American society and their place at the table. The phrase appears 5 times, beside women, agencies, facade, and body of work, and the guests explain the motive — women start businesses because corporate America was not created for women by women. Their concrete case is the facade they want to break apart: the polished image of agency success that does not feel achievable, replaced by showing what is real and what is actually possible.
