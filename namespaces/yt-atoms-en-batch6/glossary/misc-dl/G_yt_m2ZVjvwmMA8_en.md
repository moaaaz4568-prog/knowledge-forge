# G_yt_m2ZVjvwmMA8_en — methodology

## Term

In this agency conversation, a methodology is the shop's own way of taking a client through a process, built from running the work rather than from a course someone else taught. The word appears 9 times, beside client, process, agency, and confidence, and the concrete move is to walk the client through steps that make them a better client for the agency if they say yes while instilling confidence that the shop knows what it is doing. Its use here is a lived process, not a course: the speaker contrasts it with people who took a course from someone else and were never really good, and the methodology is what lets a client see the work and even try it on their own.
