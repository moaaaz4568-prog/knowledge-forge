# G_yt_mIXPOnoRx6Y_en — infrastructure

## Term

The word appears 8 times, beside AI, selling, businesses, and trend, and the concrete move is the pitch the speaker teaches: do not take any chat service and try to sell it to businesses, but sell the specific AI infrastructure, because a billionaire on a podcast he cites says AI will become a new species and the trend is the opportunity.
