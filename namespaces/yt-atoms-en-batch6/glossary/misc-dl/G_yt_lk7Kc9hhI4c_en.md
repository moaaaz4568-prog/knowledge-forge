# G_yt_lk7Kc9hhI4c_en — financial trauma

## Term

In this video, financial trauma is the pattern of fear and avoidance around money that the speaker says needs practice to explore — understanding your patterns and thinking more consciously about them. The phrase appears 4 times, beside invest, yourself, and marketing, and the concrete move offered is to invest in yourself and your project first, through writing and journaling to understand a mind that is constantly changing, before spending on marketing or distribution to reach more people. Its use here is a block, not a budget problem: the speaker says there is no need to market a product that is not appealing yet, and the trauma is what keeps the speaker from investing in the craft itself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as being broke: the speaker's financial trauma is a pattern of thinking about money, and a person with money can carry it just as a person without money can.
- Read as a reason to avoid spending entirely: the practical solution offered is to invest in yourself and the project, so the trauma is something to work through by investing well, not by never investing.
