## Term

In this source the hypothesis creation framework is the mechanism the speaker breaks down for people who want to start: he is launching G specialist, a new product for people who are just used to starting out, and the framework is the method he teaches inside it. The phrase appears once, beside mechanism, G specialist, product, and launch, and the video pairs the teaching with his morning routine — a bagel run for coffee in Montreal, where he records his podcast episodes on the way.
