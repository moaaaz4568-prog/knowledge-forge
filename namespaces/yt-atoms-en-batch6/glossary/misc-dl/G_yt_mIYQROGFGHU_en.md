# G_yt_mIYQROGFGHU_en — newsletter

## Term

In this short coaching tip, a newsletter is the email a coach sends to prospects, and the monthly long one the speaker says to replace with short weekly ones. The word appears 4 times, beside coach, prospects, monthly, and articles, and the concrete move is the two tweaks he made for a coach from Melbourne: go shorter and go more frequent, so a long monthly newsletter full of great articles becomes short articles sent weekly, which is easier to write and keeps the coach in more regular contact. Its use here is a follow-up habit, not a publication: the tip ends go short, go frequent, or go home, and the point is staying in touch, not cramming everything into one basket.
