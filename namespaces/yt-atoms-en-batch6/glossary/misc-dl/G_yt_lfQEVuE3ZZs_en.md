# G_yt_lfQEVuE3ZZs_en — personal brand

## Term

In this video, a personal brand is the following a person builds around their own name and expertise — the speaker built his to over six hundred thousand followers on his main YouTube channel in the self-improvement and dating space, plus hundreds of thousands more on Instagram and TikTok. The phrase appears 7 times, beside followers, short form, and long form, and the concrete move is to find the expertise you have alignment with, then make it as short form and long form content, and remake trending topics in your own lane. Its use here is an asset, not a profile: the speaker says he would have been well over a million followers on Instagram if he had done this exact tip, and the brand is what turns attention into a business.
