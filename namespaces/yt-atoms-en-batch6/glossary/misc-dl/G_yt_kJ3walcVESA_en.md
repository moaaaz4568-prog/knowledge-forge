# G_yt_kJ3walcVESA_en — enterprise value

## Term

Here enterprise value is what an agency is worth when it is sold, and the speaker's point is that organic growth raises it twice over: every million dollars of organic growth adds roughly $200,000 to the bottom line and then a multiple on top of that. The phrase appears 7 times, beside valuation, organic growth, multiple, and bottom line, and he works the arithmetic across a 10, 12, or 14x valuation to reach millions of dollars. His concrete case is the PE firm that judges an agency on its 3-year organic growth trend, producer concentration risk, and owner dependency before it ever looks at a single spike year.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And when you multiply that increase across a 10, 12, or 14x valuation, you're talking about millions of dollars in enterprise value." — sources/S_yt_kJ3walcVESA_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the agency's yearly profit: enterprise value here is the sale price produced by growth times a valuation multiple, not the $200,000 added to the bottom line.
- Read as a reward for one spike year: the speaker says a PE firm is not impressed by a single spike, and the value comes from a steady 3-year trend instead.
