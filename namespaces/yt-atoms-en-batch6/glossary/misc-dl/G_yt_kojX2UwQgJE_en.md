# G_yt_kojX2UwQgJE_en — logo type

## Term

In this source logo type is the element judged in the speaker's multiple-choice critique of a design that tries to be a logo: the first choice is that the main logo type is not legible. The phrase appears once, beside legible, the marriage of the two sets of typography, and fatal error, and the whole video is built as a test — look at the design, guess why it fails, then check against the choices. His concrete case is the design itself, which he calls a fatal error for a logo because the type cannot be read at a glance.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a verdict that the logo is ugly: the fault the speaker names is legibility of the type, not taste — the design fails as a logo because its type cannot be read.
- Read as a rule against mixing typefaces: the marriage of the two sets of typography fails in this design because they do not work together, not because pairing type is banned.
