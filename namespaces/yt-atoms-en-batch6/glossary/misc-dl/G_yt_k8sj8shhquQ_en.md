# G_yt_k8sj8shhquQ_en — consume

## Term

In this source consume is the deep, serial watching that turns viewers into buyers: those who swallow content become buyers, and the ones who get the most customers are not the ones who go viral but the ones who make people dive in headfirst and review everything in a row. The word appears 3 times, beside content, buyers, swallow, and metric, with the forms consumed twice and consuming once more, and the speaker names it his main metric — the one he optimizes all his content for across all platforms, specifically long-form content that gets customers rather than viral reach. His concrete case is his own channel: 165 subscribers and five million dollars in revenue from YouTube in twelve months, because the more people consume, the more likely they are to become customers.
