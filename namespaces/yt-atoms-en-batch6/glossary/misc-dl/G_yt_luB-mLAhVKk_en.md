# G_yt_luB-mLAhVKk_en — one-liner

## Term

In this StoryBrand conversation, a one-liner is the short answer to what a business does — a single sentence that names the problem it uniquely solves — and the whole episode is about helping leaders write their own. The phrase appears 36 times, beside problem, business, and marketing, and the concrete move is to take the seven-parts framework and compress it until the answer fits in one line, so that a listener at a conference, a trade show, or a chamber event immediately understands what the business does. Its use here is a tool, not a tagline: the speakers warn that stuffing everything about the business into a little window is the concept people have that fails, and the one-liner works because it says only the problem and the solution.
