# G_yt_lCbihByRb18_en — hard work is overrated

## Term

In this source hard work is overrated is lesson number one from Tom's road to his first $100,000 month in June of 2023, when he was 26: for a lot of his early days as an entrepreneur, hard work was the thing he leaned on, and it was not what made the difference. The phrase appears once, beside self-awareness, doubt myself, and seven figures, and the video is six lessons from the two years since — what would have got him there sooner, and what made the last two years of maintaining it easier. His concrete case is the scale he reports: record months as high as $320,000, and an average around the $100,000 mark since the milestone.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as do not work hard: the lesson is about what else matters on the way up — self-awareness, and how much doubting himself held him back — not about skipping the work.
- Read as a claim he never worked hard: he built an info product business from zero to seven figures a year, and the lesson comes after the work, not instead of it.
