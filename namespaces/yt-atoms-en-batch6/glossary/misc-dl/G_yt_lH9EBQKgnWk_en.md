# G_yt_lH9EBQKgnWk_en — security guards

## Term

In this interview with the founder of a security company, security guards are the service itself: the company supplies guards to businesses, keeps a pool of two hundred on standby, and sells the promise that a guard is always there to protect people, property, and businesses. The phrase appears 43 times, beside uniforms, pool, standby, and sales, and the concrete case is a banquet hall where too many people crowd into an elevator — the kind of situation a guard is deployed to control. Its use here is operational: guards maintain safety and control access, keeping good people inside and bad people outside, and the business is judged on whether a guard is there when a client calls.

## Synonyms

- security guard (singular form in the source)

## Live Example

> "We deployed security guards and provided them with uniforms, but because of that, sales stagnated in 2020 ." — sources/S_yt_lH9EBQKgnWk_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that uniforms sell the service: the video says deploying guards with uniforms made sales stagnate in 2020, so the uniform is not what clients are buying.
- Read as a guarantee that a guard prevents every incident: the company promises standby coverage and access control, not that no incident can ever happen on a guarded site.
