# G_yt_ktoyIL7zhU0_en — Pantone

## Term

Here Pantone is the color system a client can demand a design in, and the tutorial shows how to convert a finished design to it inside Adobe Illustrator. The word appears 13 times, beside color mode, coated or uncoated, and swatch library, and the steps are short: open the color window, check the current mode — his is CMYK — then open the swatches window and find the Pantone color books. His concrete case is the match itself: pick coated or uncoated, hit OK, and Illustrator finds the closest matching color in the library, which is why starting in Pantone is the better idea.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Pantone being a single color: Pantone is the matching system and its swatch books — coated and uncoated — that hold the colors to match against.
- Read as an exact conversion from another color mode: Illustrator finds the closest matching color, and the speaker says it will almost always be slightly different from the original.
