# G_yt_jy0wpWOUQq4_en — marketing problem

## Term

In this source a marketing problem is the speaker's diagnosis for agencies that think they cannot sell: too many agencies believe they have a sales problem, he says, when the real fault is that their marketing never brings in the right people. The phrase appears once, beside sales problem, agencies, and identify, and he ties it to knowing where you are — an agency must identify its actual stage before choosing a fix. His concrete case is the contrast itself: a team that keeps pushing sales while its pipeline stays empty is treating the symptom, not the marketing gap underneath.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But you have to really kind of identify where you're at because there's too many agencies that think they have a sales problem, but they really have a marketing problem." — sources/S_yt_jy0wpWOUQq4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales-skills gap: the speaker says the agencies' fault is marketing, so more sales training would treat the wrong problem.
- Read as proof every agency has one: the claim covers too many agencies that think they have a sales problem, not a survey of all agencies.
