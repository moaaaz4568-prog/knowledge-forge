# G_yt_kI4n2Yl0cE0_en — level of detail

## Term

In this short source the level of detail is the standard the restaurant Watchillo set for itself: the speaker says even Ruth Chris does not have the level of detail that Watchillo has, and the effect had to be that strong because the place opened upside down, serving no beer or vodka. The phrase appears once, beside Ruth Chris, Watchillo, ocean, and Bourbon Trail, and the speaker measures it by the first sight of the ocean — you see it and say, oh wow, that is crazy — which is the reaction the room had to deliver. His concrete case is the audience it had to serve: the folks of Norton Common first, then the family from Pennsylvania in Florida, and the Bourbon Trail visitors who now come for the restaurant itself.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as luxury: the level of detail is Watchillo's standard for the room's effect, and the speaker compares it with Ruth Chris to show the bar, not to rank restaurants.
- Read as decoration: the detail is the first sight of the ocean and the reaction it forces, and the restaurant had to deliver it because it opened without beer or vodka.
