# G_yt_mOd-cL-WZ58_en — intentionality

## Term

In this sales conversation, intentionality is the deliberate purpose behind a call or a product, set against the spray-and-pray model of the last twenty years. The word appears 12 times, beside AI tool, humanity, problem, and sales, and the concrete move is the question the seller asks himself: how can I do more of that with intentionality, building an AI tool that doubles down on intentionality without losing humanity, and refusing to call when he does not believe he can solve a problem. Its use here is a selling discipline, not a mood: the speaker contrasts it with disingenuous personalization, and the test is whether the product solves a real problem for the buyer.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fancy word for trying hard: the speaker's intentionality is tested by refusing calls that cannot solve a problem, and the alternative he names is the spray-and-pray model.
