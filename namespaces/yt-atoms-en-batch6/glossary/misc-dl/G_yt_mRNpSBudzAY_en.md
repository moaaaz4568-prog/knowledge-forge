# G_yt_mRNpSBudzAY_en — conference kit

## Term

In this workshop-room tour, a conference kit is the set of branded pieces that turns a room into a learning environment, from banners to workbooks. The phrase appears 2 times, beside workshop room, posters, workbook, and big idea, and the concrete move is the tour itself: banner posters make the content be the room itself rather than words on a screen, and the workbook is one page to capture the key ideas at the end of every section, with models and worksheets for each section. Its use here is an environment, not swag: the big idea is that all of the stuff fits together and flows together as one, and a piece standing alone is the mistake the kit is built to avoid.
