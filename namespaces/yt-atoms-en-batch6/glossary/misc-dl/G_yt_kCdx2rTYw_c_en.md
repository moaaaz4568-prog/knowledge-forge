# G_yt_kCdx2rTYw_c_en — logo design process

## Term

In this source the logo design process is the work that must come before any tool: a good quality logo design requires the process — researching the brand, developing an overall image of the project, and creating concepts — and only once those bases are covered should the designer begin incorporating the shape builder tool. The phrase appears once, beside researching, brand, concepts, and shape builder, and the speaker calls skipping it the mistake he has seen designers get totally wrong. His concrete case is the first tip itself: do not start with the shape builder at all, not even a few clicks, until the research and the concepts exist.
