# G_yt_kRBcw2W8bWM_en — paid media

## Term

In this source paid media is the ad spend that the speaker's 10% rule of thumb is measured against: if a brand spends $100,000 on paid media, it should spend $10,000 on the content being created for it. The phrase appears once, beside ad budget, content, rule of thumb, and marketing mix, and she says the ratio varies by brand depending on goals and how the marketing mix is put together. Her concrete case is the question that opens it — how to walk a brand client through the right spend — answered with the 10% rule rather than a fixed number.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So, if you're going to spend $100,000 on paid media, you should spend $10,000 on the content that's being created for it." — sources/S_yt_kRBcw2W8bWM_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole budget: paid media here is only the ad spend, and the content spend is a separate 10% on top of it.
- Read as a fixed law: the speaker says the ratio varies by brand, so the 10% is a rule of thumb, not a number that fits every client.
