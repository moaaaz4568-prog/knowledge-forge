# G_yt_l43l6Qaw_PU_en — order taker

## Term

Here order taker is the role Pinaki says he wants clients to stop treating his agency as: instead of coming with orders to execute, they should come with a problem and ask what the solutions are. The phrase appears once, beside jump on the grenade, local wisdom, and freedom, and the interview is the story of a 25-year-old agency — Local Wisdom, a digital communications agency — told by a CEO who also co-founded a product and a podcast. His concrete case is the sentence he wants to hear from a client: here is the problem, what do you think, what are some solutions, rather than the order taker.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never taking direction from a client: the shift is from orders to problems — the client brings the problem, and the agency brings the solutions.
- Read as a claim that Pinaki planned the agency from the start: he calls himself a bit of an accidental agency owner, and the origin story is the opposite of a plan.
