# G_yt_lyETM3iYppU_en — personal brand

## Term

In this video, a personal brand is the business model the speaker calls by far the best in 2024 and 2025 — build the brand first, then sell from it. The phrase appears 9 times, beside traffic, content, and skill, and the concrete move is to take a skill you have, create and sell a first digital product from it, and use traffic from YouTube and Instagram to carry it, breaking the paradigm that there is still a barrier of entry. Its use here is a model, not a profile: the speaker says traffic comes first because without it there are not many options, and the brand is what turns the content into a business instead of a hobby.
