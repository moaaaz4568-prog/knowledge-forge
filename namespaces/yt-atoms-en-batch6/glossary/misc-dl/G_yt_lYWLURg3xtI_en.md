# G_yt_lYWLURg3xtI_en — traffic

## Term

In this video about going from thirty thousand a month to ten thousand a day, traffic is the number one component the speaker had to figure out: the traditional way to grow is outbound prospecting, but that takes a lot of effort and volume, so his process was to get a lot more traffic without paid ads. The word appears 3 times, beside audience, offer, content, and pricing, and the concrete move is to gain access to a large audience that can be served with the offer, instead of sending a thousand DMs or a thousand emails. Its use here is a component, not the whole business: the speaker lists traffic, audience, content, and pricing together, and traffic is the first of them to fix.
