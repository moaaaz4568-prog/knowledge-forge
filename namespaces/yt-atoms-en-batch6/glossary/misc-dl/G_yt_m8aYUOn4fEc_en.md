# G_yt_m8aYUOn4fEc_en — color

## Term

In this package-design video, color is the tool that changes how a design reads: the same generic coffee bag becomes a different product when its colors change. The word appears 15 times, beside color theory, design, coffee, and feminine scene, and the concrete move is to match colors to the target audience, so vibrant colors give a cosmetics design a burst of energy for younger women while red and white give hotel suites the peace and calmness guests want. Its use here is a meaning-maker, not decoration: the speaker's three examples turn on how color theory and color use can totally change graphic designs, and bold bright colors can read tacky or cheap in one case and exactly right in another.
