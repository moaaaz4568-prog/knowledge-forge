# G_yt_mLa7vSr63gc_en — anchor points

## Term

In this Illustrator tips video, anchor points are the control points of a vector shape, and the ones the speaker says to remove when a design has too many. The phrase appears 3 times, beside redundant, simplify, stroke, and design, and the concrete move is the one he demonstrates: outline the stroke, see the two overlapping shapes, and use the simplify option with the preview box checked to remove redundant and useless points. Its use here is a cleanup step, not a drawing step: the tips run through every single point a professional should master, from alignment to grouping, and anchor points matter because too many of them cause problems for the client or a fellow designer.
