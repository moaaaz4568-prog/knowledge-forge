# G_yt_kstGHf469OU_en — theory of constraints

## Term

In this source theory of constraints is lesson number one from a year on Alex and Leila Hormozi's media team at acquisition.com: at any given time there is one thing constraining the business, and anything you work on that is not that constraint is a distraction. The phrase appears once, beside constraint, money models, and the water pipe, and the speaker introduces it as the answer to why the Hormozis achieve so much so quickly — the ability to recognize the constraint and solve it. His concrete case is the water pipe: water runs through it, and the narrowest point decides how much gets through, whatever you do elsewhere.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as work harder on everything: anything that is not the constraint is a distraction, so the theory says to stop working on the rest, not to add more.
- Read as a claim about what Alex and Leila actually do: he says he does not want to speak on their behalf — the constraint is his own reading of why they move so fast.
