# G_yt_l-whimGR7Jw_en — the same physics

## Term

In this short source the same physics is the speaker's claim that marketing for a billion-dollar company and marketing for a micro-business run by one person standing on the street with a car are the same underneath: both need to communicate so messages get stuck in people's heads. The phrase appears once, beside billion-dollar company, share in my head, and prickly pear cart, and the whole 139-word source is the argument — the marketing is the same, but the projections are different. His concrete case is the position the message buys: two positions, a share in his head, so the company becomes his first choice when he wants to buy a holiday.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "billion- dollar company and the marketing you do for a micro-business run by one person standing on the street with a car. They are both the same physics. The marketing is the same, but the projections are different. The investment is different, the structure and location of the investment" — sources/S_yt_l-whimGR7Jw_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spend the same money on both: the investment, the structure, and the location of the investment are different — only the physics is the same.
- Read as a slogan with no content: the claim is specific — messages that get stuck in heads, so the brand becomes the first choice when the customer wants to buy.
