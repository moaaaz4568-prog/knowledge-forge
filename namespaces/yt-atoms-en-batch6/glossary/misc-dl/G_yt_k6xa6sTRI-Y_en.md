# G_yt_k6xa6sTRI-Y_en — hook

## Term

Here a hook is the opening of a video that decides whether the audience stays or scrolls past: it controls 80% of a video's performance, and the speaker says every viral video follows one of a few proven hook formats. The word appears 36 times, beside format, viral, curiosity, and scroll, with the plural hooks 14 more times, and he explains the mechanism — a good hook creates curiosity by making the viewer anticipate new information or an idea they already know something about. His concrete case is his own proof: these exact hooks generated millions of views for himself and his clients, including 1.17 million accounts reached in 30 days for a golf business and 848,000 views in one day for a fitness business.
