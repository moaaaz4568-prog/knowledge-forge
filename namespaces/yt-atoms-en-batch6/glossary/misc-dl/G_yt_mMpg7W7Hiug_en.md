# G_yt_mMpg7W7Hiug_en — ideal customer

## Term

In this content-strategy video, an ideal customer is the specific person the content and the ads are made for, defined by buyer awareness and journey stage. The phrase appears 13 times, beside content, buyer awareness, ads, and Andromeda, and the concrete move is the one the speaker insists on: make content that makes what someone interested in that piece of content wants to see, because ads now need to be specific to the ideal customer's buyer awareness, journey, and stage. Its use here is a targeting definition, not a demographic: the speaker says each business is different, so the offer is fine-tuned for two days before anything is scripted, and content that ignores the ideal customer will not get shown.
