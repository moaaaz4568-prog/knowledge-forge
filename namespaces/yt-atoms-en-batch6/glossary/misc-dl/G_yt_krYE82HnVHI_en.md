# G_yt_krYE82HnVHI_en — car is an investment

## Term

In this source car is an investment is the speaker's claim from the London tour: a car that draws a crowd buys the same attention as advertising, so technically it is an investment for a lot of attention. The phrase appears once, beside short form, stand on business, and get rich, and the tour around it is all cars and attention — every single person in the bus stopping to take pictures or look. His concrete case is the comparison he offers instead of spending on ads: get a quarter-million-dollar car and it will do the same thing, maybe even better.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as financial advice to buy expensive cars: the investment he names is attention, not resale value — the car earns looks, not money back.
- Read as a claim that cars always beat ads: he says it might actually be a greater ROI than a quarter of a million on ads, as a true story from this London tour, not as a rule.
