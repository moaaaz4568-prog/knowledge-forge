# G_yt_m4qlRxGOLJU_en — framework

## Term

In this cold-email walkthrough, a framework is the reusable way of writing outreach that the speaker teaches instead of handing over a template. The word appears 24 times, beside cold email, template, simple, and LinkedIn, and the concrete move is to apply the same framework to every niche and even repurpose it into cold calls, because templates that worked three or four years ago stop working while a framework survives. Its use here is a method, not a fill-in form: the speaker says plainly this is not going to be a template, and the difference is that a framework is something to learn and apply while a template is something to copy.
