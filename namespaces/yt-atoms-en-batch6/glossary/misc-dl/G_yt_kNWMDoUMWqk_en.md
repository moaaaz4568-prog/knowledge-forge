# G_yt_kNWMDoUMWqk_en — LinkedIn lead generation

## Term

In this source LinkedIn lead generation is the service Amanda Rush sells through Rush Digital Marketing: a done for you agency that helps coaches, consultants, and B2B service providers find clients on LinkedIn, which she has run for seven years. The phrase appears once, beside Rush Digital Marketing, coaches, consultants, and B2B service providers, and the interview frames it as her answer to being stuck at around 20k per month before she joined the program. Her concrete case is the timing itself: she joined on August 19th, had her worst month at 15k right after joining, and then had to stop messing with her sales process so much.
