# G_yt_m4u8JInMSHE_en — blend

## Term

In this Illustrator tips tutorial, a blend is the smooth transition the Blend tool builds between two shapes along a stroke or path. The word appears 7 times, beside stroke, path, toolbar, and scissors tool, and the concrete move is to cut a square with the scissors tool, place two circles in line, set a specific distance such as 0.5 in the blend options, and then make the blend. Its use here is a tool operation, not a color choice: the blend is made through the Objects menu after the shapes are grouped and aligned, and the tutorial treats it as one of several lesser-known tips.
