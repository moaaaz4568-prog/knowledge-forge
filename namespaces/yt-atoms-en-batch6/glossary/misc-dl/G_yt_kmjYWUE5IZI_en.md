# G_yt_kmjYWUE5IZI_en — servant and master

## Term

In this source servant and master is the relationship the slide rejects: as business owners, the two sides speak as equals — kings talking to kings — and not as a servant speaking to a master. The phrase appears once, beside business owners, kings, email, and phone, and the slide gives nothing else, so the contrast itself carries the whole point about how owners address each other. Its concrete case is the medium the slide names: by email or phone, the standing does not change, and neither side talks down to the other.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a job title: servant and master here names a way of speaking between owners, not a role anyone is hired into.
- Read as a claim that owners are kings: the kings are business owners speaking as equals, and servant and master is what the slide says they are not.
