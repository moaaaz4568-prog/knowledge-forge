# G_yt_lGzR-gMHqPA_en — portfolio

## Term

In this guide to scaling a video editing hobby into full-time work, a portfolio is the editor's qualification: the speaker says your portfolio is your qualification, and warns that a Google Drive folder used as a portfolio is the equivalent of handing over a ripped-up resume. The word appears 27 times, beside Behance, Card, Google Drive, and qualification, and the concrete rule is to build it on Behance or Card rather than a Drive list, because a client judges the work from the portfolio before anything else. Its use here is bluntly practical — the portfolio is what gets the editor hired, not a qualification or a degree.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any folder of past work: a Google Drive list is named here as exactly what a portfolio is not, so a shared folder with no presentation does not count as one in this guide.
- Read as a guarantee of clients: the video says the portfolio is your qualification, but it never promises that a good portfolio alone brings work — outreach and pricing are separate problems in the same guide.
