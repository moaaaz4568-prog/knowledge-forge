# G_yt_kN0Xi3FxtwA_en — art direction

## Term

In this source art direction is the craft the speaker teaches in his Designer to Agency course: it is how to develop a big idea for an ad from a real insight, and how to choose the right style for the campaign it belongs to. The phrase appears twice, beside big idea, brief, campaign, and advertising kitchen, and he places it in a longer chain that starts with analyzing a brief and ends with a complete campaign. His concrete case is the course itself — the first Arabic course to show everything inside the advertising kitchen, from receiving a brief to presenting a picture to the client.
