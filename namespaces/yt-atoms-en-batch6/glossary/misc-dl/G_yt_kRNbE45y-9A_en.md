# G_yt_kRNbE45y-9A_en — five days to 50k a month

## Term

In this source the five days to 50k a month is the speaker's free five-day training, built at the start of the year with over 400 agency owners, coaches, and consultants learning the infrastructure to scale past 50k per month. The phrase appears once, beside free training, testimonial, 400 agency owners, and sales coaching, and the video itself is a testimonial about it — not about the main program, but about the free training. His concrete case is the customer in the video: he went through the free program, said it was better than most programs he had spent five figures on, and then signed up and got on a call with the team.
