# G_yt_lDj7WjYJPDY_en — total addressable market

## Term

In this source total addressable market is the list the whole cold email strategy is built on: write one email, send it once to everyone in the market, and simply repeat it every 30 to 60 days. The phrase appears 9 times, beside cold email strategy, closed recurring annual revenue, and Directive Consulting, and the proof is the client it was built for — Directive Consulting, a 200-person marketing agency, where the emails generated $999,750 in closed recurring annual revenue and more than 700 meetings in about six months. His concrete case is the sizing: Directive's market is less than one million people, about 700,000, so the team sent about 350,000 emails a month to reach everyone every 30 to 60 days.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spam: the strategy is one email, sent once to everyone in the market and repeated every 30 to 60 days, with everything tied to closed deals instead of positive responses.
- Read as volume alone converting: if you are missing the strategy, the offer, or the research, more volume just means more leads you cannot convert, and it burns out the channel.
