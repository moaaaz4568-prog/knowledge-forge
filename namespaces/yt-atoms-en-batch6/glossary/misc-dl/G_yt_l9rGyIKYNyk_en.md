# G_yt_l9rGyIKYNyk_en — indispensable

## Term

In this short source indispensable is what the 30-day guide promises to make you: more indispensable at work while becoming less dependent on one career path, in an economy where AI may soon match human performance. The word appears once, beside Adaptwise, career path, and Mustafa Suleiman, and the video opens with the fear itself — are you worried AI will take away your career — then quotes Suleiman's startling prediction that in just 12 to 18 months AI could reach human-level performance in most professional tasks. Its concrete case is the old formula the guide replaces: study, get a qualification, find a job, work there and retire, which used to be reassuring and has now changed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a prediction that AI will take every job: Suleiman's prediction is 12 to 18 months to human-level performance, and the video says things may not happen exactly as he said — the guide is a hedge, not a certainty.
- Read as a course advertisement only: Adaptwise is the response to the fear the video opens with, and the guide is 30 days of practical strategies, not the whole argument.
