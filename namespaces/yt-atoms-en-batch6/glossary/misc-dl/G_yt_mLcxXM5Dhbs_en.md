# G_yt_mLcxXM5Dhbs_en — account

## Term

In this LinkedIn outreach tutorial, an account is the prospect's company as it appears in Sales Navigator, targeted by size and filtered by the account filters. The word appears 23 times, beside filters, employee count, Revenue, and LinkedIn, and the concrete move is the one the speaker teaches: target by Revenue where possible, and where the profile filters cannot do it, go into the account filters and size the business by employee count. Its use here is a targeting unit, not a login: the account is the business being prospected, and the tutorial's math, five appointments from one setter and four from another, is counted per account reached.
