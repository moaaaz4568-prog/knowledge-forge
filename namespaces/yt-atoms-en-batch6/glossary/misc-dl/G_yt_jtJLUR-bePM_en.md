# G_yt_jtJLUR-bePM_en — brand name

## Term

In this source a brand name is the name a business is heard by, and the speaker's rule is that naming it wrong can destroy the brand before it starts: for a single person with a personal brand, the brand name is simply his own name, used as-is on social media and as a domain name. The phrase appears 10 times, beside personal brand, Mohammed, social media, and domain name, and the speaker tests it against foreign markets — whether people from other backgrounds can pronounce it — and against an English name on an Arabic website. His concrete case is his own name: he speaks in his own name as an expert and entrepreneur, keeps his natural name, and refuses to invent a separate business name.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a business name to invent: for a personal brand the speaker says the brand name is the person's own name, and inventing a separate company name is the mistake.
- Read as a logo or a slogan: the brand name here is the name people hear first, not the mark or the tagline that sits beside it.
