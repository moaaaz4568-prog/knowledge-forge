# G_yt_lMta17gXVb8_en — funnel

## Term

In this John Benson lesson, a funnel is a simple series of steps that takes someone from one stage to another inside the selling process: his four-part proven funnel takes a total stranger — a YouTube viewer, a lurker, or a stalker — and moves them toward a sale. The word appears 6 times, beside Ice Cold traffic, magic funnel, steps, and strangers, and the concrete claim is that this funnel has made him millions and earned tens of millions of dollars from ice cold traffic, because anyone can make it, a newbie or a pro. Its use here is a definition first and a proof second: Benson defines the funnel before he reveals it, and the definition is what the rest of the video hangs on.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complicated automation: Benson calls his funnel so simple anyone can make it, so a funnel that needs a team of integrators to run is the opposite of what he teaches here.
- Read as a synonym for traffic: the funnel starts with ice cold traffic, but the traffic is only the input — the funnel is the steps that move a stranger toward a sale.
