# G_yt_kWO2nlgvQHs_en — AOV

## Term

In this source AOV is the average order value the speaker watches on his product page, and his rule is simple: the more variants he adds, the more the AOV rises, because more people buy two or three of them instead of just one. The term appears 5 times, beside variants, buying two or three, product, and creatives, and he credits the mix of AI videos and in-person clips for pulling buyers to a product they had never held in hand. His concrete case is the dashboard itself: the store was doing around 20k a week, almost 17,000 a week, with buyers stacking variants, and the AOV kept climbing as he added more.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the price of one product: AOV here is what buyers spend per order, and it rises when they buy two or three variants, not when the product gets more expensive.
- Read as proof the AI videos alone did it: the speaker mixes AI videos with in-person clips, and the AOV rise follows adding variants, not a single creative.
