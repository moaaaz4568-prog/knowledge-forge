# G_yt_kLoO53KE2fU_en — strategy session

## Term

In this source a strategy session is the speaker's name for the sales call itself, and the whole video is about shrinking it: a green result is 80 percent or more of the people on a strategy session signing up and giving money, while orange is 60 to 80 and red is anything less than 60. The phrase appears twice, beside sales call, conversion rate, green, and triage call, and he sorts prospects with a triage call first so only a perfect match ever reaches the strategy session. His concrete case is the missing step he names: a founder who stays the best salesperson for a while, yet wastes an hour with a non-quality prospect when five minutes would have shown the mismatch.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a free consultation: the strategy session here is the sales call where people sign up and give money, not an advice call with no offer.
- Read as a call for everyone: the speaker's standard only counts quality prospects, and a triage call filters out the rest before a strategy session is ever booked.
