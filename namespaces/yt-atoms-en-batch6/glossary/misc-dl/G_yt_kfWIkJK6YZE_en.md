# G_yt_kfWIkJK6YZE_en — inbound

## Term

In this source inbound is the stream of deals, meetings, recruits, and customers that arrive from old YouTube videos, and the speakers' point is that the work keeps paying months later: a video made six months ago still brings thousands of views a day. The word appears 8 times, beside inbound deals, inbound meetings, GMV, and ROI, and they count the return at about a million dollars of total GMV, roughly a 10x ROI off gross margin over the last six months. Their concrete case is the second speed running video, now at 220,000 to 230,000 views and still creeping up on the original, with the Alex Hormozi cold calling video past 100,000 the same way.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a viral spike: the inbound here comes from old videos gaining views six months to a year later, not from a launch-day surge.
- Read as free traffic: the speakers count a million dollars of GMV and a 10x ROI on what they put into the program, so the inbound was bought with work, not found for free.
