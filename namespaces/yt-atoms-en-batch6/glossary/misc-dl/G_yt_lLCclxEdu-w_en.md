# G_yt_lLCclxEdu-w_en — followers

## Term

In this video, followers are the trap the speaker warns against: followers are the biggest scam on the planet, because two million followers across all platforms does not mean two million buyers. The word appears 18 times, beside scam, views, money, and funnel, and the concrete math is a commenter who assumes that converting just a tiny fraction of a million views must make a bunch of money — while the speaker says views are not one-to-one with buyers. Its use here is a correction: every post needs to know its job within a funnel, and optimizing for followers alone is dangerous because followers who are not the right people will never buy.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that followers are worthless: the speaker has two million of them and sells through them, so the warning is against optimizing for followers alone, not against having any.
- Read as a claim that views equal sales: the video says one view does not mean that person wants to buy, so a million views with no funnel job behind the post is not revenue.
