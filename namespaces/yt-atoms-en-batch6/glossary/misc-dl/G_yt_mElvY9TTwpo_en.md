# G_yt_mElvY9TTwpo_en — artificial intelligence

## Term

In this video about image tools, artificial intelligence is the technology that takes the speaker's terrible drawing and turns it into finished images. The phrase appears 4 times, beside drawing, sheep, Van Gogh, and pixels, and the concrete move is the test he runs: draw badly, let the artificial intelligence come up with options, and pick the sheep that looks neat or the one resembling Van Gogh's work. Its use here is a drawing partner, not a filter: the altered image comes out 12,000 pixels wide with insane detail in an eye, and the speaker judges the result by quality, not by how it was made.
