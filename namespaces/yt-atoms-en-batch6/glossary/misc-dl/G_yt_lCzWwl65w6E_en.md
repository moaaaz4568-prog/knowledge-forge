# G_yt_lCzWwl65w6E_en — higher paying clients

## Term

Here higher paying clients are the clients most designers say they cannot get, because their clients want cheap and nasty artwork — and the speaker says they are approaching the problem all wrong, with the incorrect mindset. The phrase appears 6 times, beside quality clients, portfolio, and freelance website, and the video works through the powerful ways to actually get those clients: LinkedIn, where the business owners and potential clients hang out, and a strong portfolio that proves the work. His concrete case is the visibility math: if 10 people see your work that leads to fewer clients than 10,000 people seeing it, so the work is getting seen by the right people.

- Read as just raise your prices: the video is about access — LinkedIn where the business owners hang out, a strong portfolio, and being seen by more people — not about the number on the invoice.
