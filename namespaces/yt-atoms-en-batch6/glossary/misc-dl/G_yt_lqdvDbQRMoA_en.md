# G_yt_lqdvDbQRMoA_en — infrastructure

## Term

In this video, an infrastructure is the complete system behind an offer — content on the front, a funnel, and appointment setting — and the speaker's thesis is that offers sell infrastructures, not commodity services. The word appears 10 times, beside offer, content, and appointment setting, and the concrete move is to stop running a Facebook ads agency or selling Snapchat ads for e-commerce brands, and instead build the whole infrastructure that makes a client an extra twenty grand or a hundred grand a month. Its use here is a product, not a metaphor: the speaker grew a cold DM department to a hundred thousand a month and an appointment setting operation on the back of infrastructures, and the video is about selling that system.

## Synonyms

- infrastructures (plural form)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single service: the infrastructure is the complete system — content, funnel, and setting — and selling one piece of it is the commodity service the speaker says not to sell.
- Read as a guarantee of twenty grand a month: the speaker says the incomplete version does not work like that, and the infrastructure is what has to be built first.
