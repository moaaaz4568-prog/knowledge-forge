# G_yt_lk2VsajMK8c_en — color

## Term

In this video, a color is one of four personality types people are divided into by how they communicate — red, green, blue, and yellow — and the whole video is a guide to identifying your own color and talking to the others. The word appears 28 times, beside red, yellow, green, and blue, and the concrete move is to match the type to its loves: the red type loves power and results, the green type loves peace and stability, the blue type loves logic and order, and the yellow type loves fun conversation and constant communication. Its use here is a type system, not a favorite shade: the speaker walks through the problems each type faces, like the shy types being forced into problems they do not like, and how to communicate with all four.
