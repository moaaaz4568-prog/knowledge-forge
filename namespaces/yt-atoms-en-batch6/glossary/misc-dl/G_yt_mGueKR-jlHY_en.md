# G_yt_mGueKR-jlHY_en — mentor

## Term

In this School of Greatness anniversary interview, a mentor is one of the great teachers Lewis Howes learned from in sports and then in business, and the reason the show exists at all. The word appears 1 time, beside traffic, coaches, conversations, and School of Greatness, and the concrete move is the one from the car ride in traffic: stuck and asking how he grew so quickly from 2008 to 2012, he realized the conversations with his mentors and coaches were the thing, and he started calling friends who were mentors and recording interviews about getting unstuck. Its use here is a source of the show, not a title: the mentors taught him first, and the podcast is those conversations shared, from a bone taken from his hip to a cast for six months and the choice to go deeper or blame the world.
