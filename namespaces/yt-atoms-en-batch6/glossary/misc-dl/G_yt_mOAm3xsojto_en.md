# G_yt_mOAm3xsojto_en — service delivery

## Term

In this agency-owner interview, service delivery is the actual doing of the work for clients, and the part most outreach agencies struggle with. The phrase appears 4 times, beside outreach agencies, LTV, retaining customers, and sales, and the concrete move is the turn the speaker describes: automation changed how the work gets done, and now the point is to get deals each month, five clients with support or ten deals without providing services, while retaining customers longer and providing much better service. Its use here is the bottleneck, not the pitch: the speaker calls it ironic that agencies struggle with delivery while people keep getting burned by those who are not very good at it, and the higher LTV comes from delivery done right.

## Live Example

> "And in terms of service delivery, a lot of outreach agencies struggle with that, which is quite ironic, because a lot of people have been burned by those who aren't very good at service delivery...and now you're at a point where your LTV is much higher, you're retaining customers longer, you're providing much better service, right?" — sources/S_yt_mOAm3xsojto_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for sales: the speaker separates service delivery from outreach and sales, and the agencies he names struggle with delivery even when their outreach works.
- Read as a reason to avoid services: the speaker's goal is ten deals a month without providing services or five clients with support, and delivery is what keeps customers longer either way.
