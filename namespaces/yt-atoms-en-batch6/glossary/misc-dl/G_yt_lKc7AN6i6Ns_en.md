# G_yt_lKc7AN6i6Ns_en — set the scene

## Term

In this email-writing lesson, set the scene is the first of five steps for a webinar invitation email: put people in the place and time where the situation happens, so the reader feels the problem before the offer arrives. The phrase appears twice, beside amp the importance, the reveal, webinar, and lefties, and the concrete case is a webinar called lefties are all right, where the scene is the hardest part of being a lefty — bumping your arm next to right-handed people. Its use here is structural: set the scene opens the email, then the importance is amped, the reveal names the webinar, and the benefits are bulleted.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as mere scene-setting prose: the step has a job in this email — to place the reader inside the problem — so a pretty opening that never names the situation does not count as setting the scene here.
- Read as the whole email: set the scene is only step one of five, and the video teaches it alongside amping the importance, the reveal, and the benefits, not as a standalone trick.
