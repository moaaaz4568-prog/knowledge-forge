# G_yt_kzwd5wR3P2I_en — gatekeeper

## Term

In this source gatekeeper is the person who stood between an agency salesperson and a personal injury attorney back when attorneys shopped for websites and digital marketing on Google. The word appears 5 times, beside bespoke, focus and specialization, and personal injury attorney, and the story is about Scorpion, whose salesperson could not get access because the gatekeeper's job was to make sure he did not. Her concrete case is how attorneys shopped then: searching for personal injury in San Antonio or whatever market they were in, and getting targeted by a lot of agencies the moment they did.
