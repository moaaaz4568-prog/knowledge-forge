# G_yt_mDSFQiWyLr8_en — hard decisions

## Term

In this video about growing a company, hard decisions are the difficult choices that create an easy life, set against easy decisions that create a hard life. The phrase appears 6 times, beside easy decisions, hard life, company, and life, and the concrete move is the one the speaker opens with: say no when it is hard, refuse to optimize for instant comfort, and accept that growing the company inevitably means hard calls. Its use here is a life rule, not business advice alone: the same frame covers how the speaker buys on Amazon and how he runs acquisition, and the test is always whether the choice is easy now and hard later.
