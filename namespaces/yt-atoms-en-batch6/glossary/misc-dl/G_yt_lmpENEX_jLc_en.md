# G_yt_lmpENEX_jLc_en — traffic

## Term

In this video about making money online, traffic is the flow of people who arrive to buy what you sell, and the speaker's sequence is strict: find something to sell first, then get traffic. The word appears 16 times, beside sell, money, and social media, and the concrete move is to do product research on AliExpress, find things to sell, put them on a Shopify website with social accounts, and only then drive traffic to it. Its use here is a step, not the business itself: the speaker says it is not a game of how hard you can push, and making money online is hard when the order is reversed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for sales: the speaker separates finding something to sell from getting traffic, and traffic without something to sell has nowhere to go.
- Read as a guarantee of income: the video's promise is two thousand six hundred dollars a day as a goal, and traffic is the step after the product, not a promise that visitors buy.
