# G_yt_l7vEZISBtmg_en — packaging

## Term

Here packaging is the reason a video gets watched or dies: every person who clicks asks one question — what do I get out of this — and if the video does not answer it fast, they leave, YouTube reads it as a bad signal, and the video dies. The word appears 6 times, beside seven mistakes, what do I get out of this, and clicks on your video, and the video walks through seven mistakes that do not feel like mistakes, after reviewing over 100 channels in the past year. His concrete case is the cost of getting it wrong: 20 hours put into a video that no one watches, because the packaging was not good enough.
