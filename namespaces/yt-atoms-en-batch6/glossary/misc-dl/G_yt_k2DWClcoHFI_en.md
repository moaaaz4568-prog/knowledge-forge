# G_yt_k2DWClcoHFI_en — hierarchy

## Term

In this source hierarchy is the design principle of ranking elements by size and thickness so the eye knows what matters first: the speaker points to italics chosen because the Nike logo backs them, and to a focal point enlarged so the design gains balance in a different way.
