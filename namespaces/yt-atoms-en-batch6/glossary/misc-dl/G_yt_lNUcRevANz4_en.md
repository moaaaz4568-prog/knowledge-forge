# G_yt_lNUcRevANz4_en — preference settings

## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial, the preference settings are the panel that holds the application's options — tolerance, anchor display, stroke and image trace windows — and the video teaches how to save them so a new document starts with the same setup. The phrase appears 3 times, beside command, control K, document setup, and millimeters, and the concrete test is to change the tolerance to 2 and the anchor display size, then save the settings and reopen them to check they were actually used. Its use here is a workflow saver: the settings are reached with command or control K, and the point is to stop reconfiguring Illustrator every time a new document is created.
