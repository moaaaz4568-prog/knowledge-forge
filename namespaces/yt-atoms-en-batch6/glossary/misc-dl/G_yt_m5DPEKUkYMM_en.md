# G_yt_m5DPEKUkYMM_en — invoice

## Term

In this agency-owner story, an invoice is the bill a client asks the speaker to send, and the moment that exposes how little he knew when he started. The word appears 6 times, beside client, Google, dad, and bartending money, and the concrete move is the one the young speaker had to make: asked for an invoice, he had to ask his dad what one was, and years later the firm was chasing millions in Google payments each month to collect from invoices and pay Google. Its use here is a first-business lesson, not paperwork: the invoice marks the gap between bartending money and running an agency, and the speaker laughs because he did it the wrong way for years.
