# G_yt_knjl7H-HJfI_en — specialize

## Term

Here specialize is the first piece of freelancing advice the speaker gives after five years of projects: do not stay a generalist who can do anything right or wrong, but pick one field and become known for it. The word appears 6 times, beside freelancer, generalist, and freelance income, and he builds the advice as a sequence — start freelancing on the side until the freelance income matches the job, then specialize. His concrete case is the designer who specializes in social media design or logo design instead of taking every job that comes.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "generalists, meaning they can do anything, right or wrong. At the beginning of your freelance career, the first thing you should do is specialize in a specific field. For example, if you're a designer, specialize in social media design or logo design. If you're a programmer, specialize" — sources/S_yt_knjl7H-HJfI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as picking one niche forever: the speaker says specialize first, then keep learning, so the field is a starting point and the learning never stops.
- Read as a ban on general work: generalists are the beginners he warns about, not a forbidden way of working — the advice is to stop doing anything for anyone.
