# G_yt_lIhFMlUfJ8Y_en — transformation

## Term

In this guide to growing a YouTube channel, a transformation is the change the viewer gets from watching: the speaker asks what transformation you want your viewer to have, because it targets a person instead of a general ambiguous term like a niche. The word appears 20 times, beside niche, target audience, content, and channel, and the concrete case is the speaker's own channel, where focusing on the transformation the viewer wants made the channel grow much faster than focusing on a topic. Its use here is a planning question: define who is watching and why they are watching, and the transformation is what gives the content meaning and impact.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for niche: the video contrasts the two directly, and a channel defined only by its topic has no transformation until the viewer change is named.
- Read as a promise of fast growth: the speaker says focusing on a transformation grows a channel faster, but the growth still depends on the content actually delivering the change.
