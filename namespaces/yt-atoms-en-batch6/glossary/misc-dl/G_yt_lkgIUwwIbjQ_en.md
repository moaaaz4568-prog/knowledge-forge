## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial, a grain effect is the color blur grain texture added to a design, and the video teaches how to apply it even over complex gradients. The phrase appears 10 times, beside color blur, gradient, and layer, and the concrete move is to label the layers first — calling the bottom one base because it sits under the effect — then hold shift, select the top two layers, and make a mask in the transparency menu. Its use here is a technique, not a filter preset: the speaker shows the whole setup, from applying gradients to dragging the layer to the top of the stack, and the grain effect is what the setup is for.
