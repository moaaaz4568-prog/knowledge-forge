# G_yt_lQ72hjH_DNE_en — agency life

## Term

In this agency podcast, agency life is the lived reality of running an agency — the good and the bad — and the episode is built on the transparency the host loves: the guest, a HubSpot agency chief, was faced with letting go of multiple team members just weeks before recording. The phrase appears 7 times, beside transparency, vulnerability, team, and entrepreneurs, and the concrete story is the decision to let people go, how it was communicated to the team, and the positive things that came out the other side. Its use here is a frame: agency life means the worst moments involve breaking a promise of opportunity to people, and the episode refuses to tell only the good side.
