# G_yt_kdqkGaCftvU_en — mindset problem

## Term

In this source a mindset problem is the host's diagnosis for Clark Dane, who inherited a 79-year-old American manufacturing company and watched it lose 70% of its revenue in year one: the biggest lesson from the episode is that you do not have a business problem, you have a mindset problem. The phrase appears once, beside business problem, low-hanging fruit, boring, and Clark Dane, and the host says Clark is lost in the weeds and low on energy while a gold mine of low-hanging fruit sits in front of him. His concrete case is the scale itself: Clark runs a million-dollar company, and the host says that after this episode he will see how he can run a billion-dollar company.
