# G_yt_ksuyDx8n5eY_en — cold email

## Term

Here cold email is the channel Josh teaches you to stand out in without crazy guarantees or promises: most people send a generic service pitch, get no reply, and never see the value problem. The phrase appears 5 times, beside saturated niche, guarantees, and cold outreach, and the fix he shows is specificity — name the prospect's exact outcome instead of the service you sell. His concrete case is the comparison he puts on screen: 15 plumbing leads in 90 days against 10 homeowners looking for hot water replacement jobs, where the specific one wins.
