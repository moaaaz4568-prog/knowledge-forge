# G_yt_lSiXK_TMeec_en — giveaway

## Term

In this short video, a giveaway is the whole point of the upload: a brand-new tablet is ready and waiting to go to one winner, and the video tells viewers to enter through the link in the description. The word appears once, beside tablet, winner, gleam, and enter, and the concrete mechanics are several different ways to enter, all of which can be used, with the winner decided completely at random by gleam software and announced two weeks from today. Its use here is an announcement, not a review: the tablet is shown scaled up next to the creator's hand, with a cleaning cloth and a glove, and the video's job is to send people to the entry link.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a product review: the video shows the tablet's size and accessories, but its purpose is the giveaway entry, and no performance claim about the tablet is made.
- Read as a contest with a chosen winner: the video says the winner is decided completely at random by gleam software, so no viewer skill or loyalty decides it.
