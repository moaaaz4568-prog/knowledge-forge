# G_yt_k4I-M-_Piwc_en — things that will never change

## Term

Here things that will never change is the Bezos idea the speaker borrows for the AI era: when Amazon was built, the focus was what customers would forever want — fast shipping, things delivered as fast as possible — because no customer will ever wish a delivery took longer. The phrase appears twice, beside customer needs, fast shipping, Bezos, and tactics, and the speaker turns it into a rule for builders: tactics, systems, and workflows change every day, so a company cannot be built on them. His concrete case is his own question to the audience — for people building companies with AI, what is the thing for us that will never change — asked as the starting point for choosing what to build.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of tactics that last: the speaker's point is that tactics change daily, and only customer needs like fast shipping stay fixed, so the phrase names the stable end, not the means.
- Read as Bezos's own words: the speaker reports what Bezos said in an interview and then applies it to AI builders, so the application to online companies is his, not Bezos's.
