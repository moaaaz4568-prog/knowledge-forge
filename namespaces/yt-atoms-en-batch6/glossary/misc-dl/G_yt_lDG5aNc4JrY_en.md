# G_yt_lDG5aNc4JrY_en — proven product archetype

## Term

Here proven product archetype is the logic Smith uses to pick what to sell with AI videos: a product type already shown to work, found by looking at variations on existing products, then tested with AI-generated clips. The phrase appears once, beside wow products, winning product, and Claude MCP, and the video breaks down everything from the product itself to the logic of choice — the product ethos, creative concepts, niche resources, and advertising texts. His concrete case is the product he chose: a small radio-controlled tarantula spider, a fun toy and a raffle item, picked because joke products and funny wow products have already gone viral.
