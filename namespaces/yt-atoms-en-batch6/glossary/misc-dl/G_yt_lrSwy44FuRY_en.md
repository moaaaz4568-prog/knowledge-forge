# G_yt_lrSwy44FuRY_en — run toward the roar

## Term

In this business-values podcast, run toward the roar is the title of a book the speakers discuss, and it names the response they recommend to hard things: go toward them instead of away. The phrase appears 5 times, beside book, values, and passionately faithful, and the concrete move is to start by defining what you are going to be passionately faithful to — the values the business will not compromise — and then act on them when the roar comes. Its use here is a stance, not a slogan: the speaker is a father of three and a grandfather who talks about vulnerability, clients who cannot be made happy, and a coaching certification, and the roar is the hard moment the values have to survive.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as seeking out conflict for its own sake: the roar in the video is the hard thing that arrives — a failing client, a hard conversation — and running toward it means facing it with defined values, not picking fights.
- Read as a business tactic: the phrase comes from a book about how business leaders should respond, and the speakers tie it to values and faithfulness, not to a sales or growth play.
