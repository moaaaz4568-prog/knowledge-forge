# G_yt_jsseHVKseyk_en — agency

## Term

Here agency means the small service business the host and his guest run, not a big firm: an agency owner doing everything himself, selling a system called the agency playbook, and watching cash flow while the work is project based. The word appears 35 times, beside owner, playbook, pipeline, and cash flow, with the plural agencies 6 more times, and the guest traces his own path from listening to the podcast and taking the playbook training to building his business on it. His concrete worry is the next stage — when the business grows past projects, his problems change, so for now he keeps watching cash flow and keeps the pipeline healthy.
