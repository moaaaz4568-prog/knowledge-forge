# G_yt_ksR6zrj8AEg_en — personal profile

## Term

In this source personal profile is the part of Facebook that now carries the reach, and the video teaches turning it into a funnel instead of hiding the business on a business page. The phrase appears 23 times, beside business page, featured content, and funnel, and the argument is a reversal of the old rule: business pages were where business belonged, but pages get less reach while profiles get the carrot of visibility. Her concrete case is the mistake she sees time and time again — a business page for the business and a personal profile for cat photos and kids photos, while the profile is the place people actually check.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an instruction to delete your business page: the page stays — the profile is simply where the reach is now, because business pages do not get as good a reach.
- Read as permission to post business content everywhere on your profile: Facebook used to punish profiles that were too businessy, and the speaker says the old rule was a wrist slap and a potential shutdown.
