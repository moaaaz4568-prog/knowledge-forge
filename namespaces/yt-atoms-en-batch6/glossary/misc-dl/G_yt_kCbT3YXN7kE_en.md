# G_yt_kCbT3YXN7kE_en — solutionist

## Term

Here solutionist is the word Carl puts on his card for what he does: asked what he calls himself, he answers solutionist, and the host presses him on where the name comes from and how it fits the staffing work he does for agencies. The word appears once, beside card, personality, holistic agency, and staffing, with the plural solutionists once more, and Carl ties it to hiring — find the right personality first, because the training can be woven in afterward. His concrete case is the holistic agency itself: owners and senior managers who hire for personality want every person looked at right, and that is the solutionist's job.

- Read as a job title on a listing: solutionist is what Carl calls himself on his card, and the host's question is where the name comes from, not what a job board calls the role.
