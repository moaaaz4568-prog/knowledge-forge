# G_yt_kQhSdg_VUUQ_en — digital product

## Term

In this source a digital product is a packaged piece of the seller's own knowledge — experience and insights organized into something people will pay for — and AI is the tool that makes building one ten times easier and faster. The phrase appears 6 times, beside AI, generic AI garbage, specific concept, and expert, with the plural digital products 2 more times, and he draws the line sharply: generic AI output has no real value, while a product built from a specific concept based on real experience is specific and valuable. His concrete case is his first digital product, simple and nothing fancy, which taught him that people pay for solutions to problems without needing him to be the world's leading expert.
