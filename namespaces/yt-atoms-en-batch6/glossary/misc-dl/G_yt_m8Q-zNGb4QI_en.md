# G_yt_m8Q-zNGb4QI_en — replaceable

## Term

In this short agency video, replaceable is what an agency becomes when its value is based on tasks, now that AI can do those tasks in under 30 seconds. The word appears 2 times, beside tasks, AI, order taker, and agency, and the concrete move is the repositioning the speaker demands: stop being an order taker doing content, design, drafts, ad copy, and whole websites, and become the adviser who diagnoses the real problem, because AI cannot understand nuances or push back. Its use here is a verdict on the business model, not on a person: the speaker says AI will not kill your agency, being replaceable will, and the question is whether the agency still matters for what it actually does.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a warning that AI takes every agency job: the speaker says AI cannot diagnose a real problem or push back, so the danger is only for agencies whose value is task work.
- Read as advice to stop doing the work: becoming the adviser still means doing the diagnosis, and the speaker's point is to change what the agency is valued for, not to stop working.
