# G_yt_k5Tvu-sjmAY_en — fulfillment

## Term

In this source fulfillment means the service delivery behind the flagship program — the done-for-you systems implementation the company actually builds for clients, as opposed to selling coaching. The word appears 4 times, beside service delivery, flagship program, back end, and acquisition, and the speaker stresses that his company is not a coaching business: it has departments, gets its hands dirty, and builds things for clients. His concrete case is the split he draws — struggling clients come for the back end more than the front end now, so fulfillment, not acquisition, is where the revamped program puts its weight.
