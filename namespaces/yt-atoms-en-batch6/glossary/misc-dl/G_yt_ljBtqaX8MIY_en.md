# G_yt_ljBtqaX8MIY_en — brief

## Term

In this design video, a brief is the short statement of what a design project has to do, and the speaker's core advice is to compress it to a single sentence before judging any work. The word appears 6 times, beside design, project, and sentence, and the concrete move is to pick one design project, squeeze its brief into one sentence, and then test every choice against it — whether the headline fights the product photo, or a QR code is hidden in a corner. Its use here is a test, not a document: feedback goes wrong when people share raw links and get contradictory comments, because no one is judging the design against the goal, and the brief is what supplies that goal.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the client's original request: the brief here is the compressed sentence the designer writes for the project, and a long client email is the raw material, not the brief itself.
- Read as a formality before the real work: the speaker makes the brief the judge of the design, so skipping it is what produces the contradictory feedback the video complains about.
