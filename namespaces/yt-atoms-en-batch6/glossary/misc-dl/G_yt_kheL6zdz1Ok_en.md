# G_yt_kheL6zdz1Ok_en — brand identity

## Term

In this source brand identity is the project the speaker sells, and her story is the price of it: in six months she raised the cost of a brand identity project from £300 to £5,500, working with global brands such as Zara. The phrase appears 3 times, beside brand design, client process, client portal, and £5,500, and she says the reliable customer service process behind it gave her the confidence to raise prices and sell convincingly. Her concrete case is the process itself: a complete client process from start to finish — sales, proposals, contracts, invoices, and a client portal where clients book a strategy meeting.
