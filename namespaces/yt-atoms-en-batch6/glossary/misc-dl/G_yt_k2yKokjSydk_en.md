# G_yt_k2yKokjSydk_en — color palette

## Term

Here a color palette is a set of colors that sets a design's tone, and the speaker's warning is about trendy ones: when a palette becomes trendy it gets overused across countless designs in countless industries, and the saturation makes designs using it less impactful and essentially lost. The phrase appears twice, beside trendy, overused, saturation, and timeless, with the plural color palettes once more, and he contrasts the trendy palette with a signature one — Tiffany blue, which sets a tone of elegance, luxury, and sophistication. His concrete case is the cycle itself: a pastel gradient one year, neon brights the next, each looking current for a season and then dating the work.

## Synonyms

- color palettes (plural form in the source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any pretty set of colors: the warning covers trendy palettes that get overused until they saturate, not a palette chosen for a project's own goals.
- Read as a claim that trends are always bad: the speaker's example, Tiffany blue, is a signature palette, so the fault is following trends blindly rather than using color with intent.
