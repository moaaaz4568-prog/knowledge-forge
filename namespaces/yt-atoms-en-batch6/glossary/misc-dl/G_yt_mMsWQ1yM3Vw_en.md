# G_yt_mMsWQ1yM3Vw_en — prompt

## Term

In this video about using AI well, a prompt is the instruction given to the chosen model, and the speaker's system for getting good outputs from it. The word appears 9 times, beside model, master, assign, and outputs, and the concrete move is the order he teaches: pick the model to master, assign it a prompt, and treat it as an assistant, because students who just write anything into it get nothing back. Its use here is an assignment, not a question: the prompt is why the outputs are what they are, and the video's example task is a YouTube calendar for 30 days with content pillars and dates, built by prompting the model as an assistant.
