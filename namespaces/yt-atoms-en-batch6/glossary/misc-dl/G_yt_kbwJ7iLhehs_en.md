# G_yt_kbwJ7iLhehs_en — doomerism

## Term

In this source doomerism is the bleak outlook the speaker examines and then limits: it is the stance that everything is grinding down with no sign of improvement, and he says it makes sense in some places and not in others. The word appears once, beside doomers, Doomer, geographic determination, and United States, and he gives the geography first — in parts of the world where any chance of elevating fairly is taken away by a corrupt and autocratic government, being a Doomer makes sense. His concrete case is the contrast itself: he has lived in the United States for over 90 percent of his life, and there doomerism does not apply, because the same grinding stagnation is not the American story.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "the United States for over 90 percent of my life and being an American doomerism doesn't really apply in the United States or North America really in general and most of the developed world dumerism does not make sense really to live your life as" — sources/S_yt_kbwJ7iLhehs_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that doomers are always wrong: the speaker says their concerns have valid points, and doomerism makes sense where governments take away any fair chance to rise.
- Read as optimism: the speaker says he is no Pollyanna, and the point is where the outlook fits, not that everything is fine.
