# G_yt_ju8feBT5yj0_en — shape builder tool

## Term

Here the shape builder tool is the Illustrator tool for combining shapes into new ones, taught as the core of logo design and icon design: by default it runs in merge mode, marked by a plus icon, which unites selected shapes, while holding Alt or Option switches it to subtract mode, which erases parts of objects. The phrase appears 6 times, beside merge mode, subtract mode, Shift + M, and logo design, and the speaker opens with the shortcut — Shift + M — and the need to highlight objects before pressing it. His concrete case is uniting two shapes with a click in merge mode, then using subtract mode to erase the overlap.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a drawing tool: the shape builder only combines shapes that are already selected, and it cannot create the shapes it merges.
- Read as merge-only: subtract mode is a separate switch with Alt or Option, so holding the tool's default click never erases anything.
