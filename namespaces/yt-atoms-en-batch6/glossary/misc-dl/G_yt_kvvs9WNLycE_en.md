# G_yt_kvvs9WNLycE_en — selling results

## Term

Here selling results is the lesson Michelle says the fastest AI earners follow: do not start by learning the tools and waiting for clients, start by selling the result the client wants and figure out the tools after the deal. The phrase appears once, beside boring systems, boring niches, and vehicles, and her own story is the proof she offers — zero business or technical knowledge in 2024, training to be an investment banker, then a six-figure AI business in 14 months across more than 50 companies. Her concrete case is the client Anthony, who worked a 9-to-5 with a baby on the way and was stuck selling instead of results, then went from zero to 35,000 deals and 89 sales calls, closing nine clients in 90 days.

## Live Example

> "That's how one of our clients , Anthony, who worked 9-5, had a baby on the way, and was stuck selling instead of results, was able to go from zero to 35,000 deals and schedule 89 sales calls, closing nine clients, all in 90 days because he switched to selling results instead of vehicles ." — sources/S_yt_kvvs9WNLycE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as tools do not matter: tools are so easy to create that part of the construction can be figured out after the deal is closed — the result is what gets sold first.
- Read as a claim that cold outreach never works: the fastest earners do not start with cold outreach, advertising, or cold calling, which is a statement about where they start, not that those channels are dead.
