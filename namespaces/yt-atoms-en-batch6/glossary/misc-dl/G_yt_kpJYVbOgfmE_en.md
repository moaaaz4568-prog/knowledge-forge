# G_yt_kpJYVbOgfmE_en — sales rep

## Term

In this source sales rep is the single hire whose calendar the speaker shows to prove a lean team can scale: the rep takes calls all day, and when he is not on calls he sets calls. The phrase appears 7 times, beside calendar, annual recurring revenue, and profit margin, and the point of the tour is the shape of the business — four people besides himself, 75 to 80 percent profit margins, about $1 million in annual recurring revenue. His concrete case is the training math: 30 hours to get the rep to the point where he is a killer who stays a year or two and makes a couple of million dollars, which the speaker calls a $30,000-an-hour activity.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an instruction to hire many reps to scale: he runs about $1 million in annual recurring revenue with one sales rep plus himself, and only just hired a second.
- Read as a job advertisement: the rep is the schedule he walks through on screen — calls all day, then setting calls — not a vacancy he is filling.
