# G_yt_lXUHIEZ-fis_en — podcast guesting

## Term

In this agency podcast, podcast guesting is the marketing channel Jessica Rhodess turned into a business: she launched the world's first podcast booking agency in 2013, long before most people were even thinking about podcast guesting as a marketing channel. The phrase appears 8 times, beside booking agency, interviews, coaches, and strategy, and the concrete proof is a client, an agency owner, who closed a six-figure contract from podcast guesting, and a team that has booked more than fifty thousand podcast interviews. Its use here is a channel argument: when people hear the founder on a podcast, they hear the story and the personality, and that is what turns a guest appearance into business.
