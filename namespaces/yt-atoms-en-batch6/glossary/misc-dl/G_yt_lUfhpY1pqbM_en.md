# G_yt_lUfhpY1pqbM_en — legacy

## Term

In this video about a Texas land project, legacy is what the Walker family wants to leave: when they started the project and really wanted to leave their legacy, it became more about how to give an opportunity for everybody else to have their legacy too. The word appears twice, beside opportunity, Texas, land, and family, and the concrete picture is a community where families have backyard barbecues, kids play football in the front yard, and schools, hospitals, and grocery stores are all right there. Its use here is a motive: the Walkers have been on the land since about 1910, the land gave them a beautiful life, and the project keeps a portion of it for people who want their own little portion of Texas.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for inheritance: the legacy here is the opportunity the project gives other families, not land passed down within the Walker family alone.
- Read as a completed town: the schools, hospitals, and grocery stores are hopes for the project, and the video presents them as plans, not as buildings already standing.
