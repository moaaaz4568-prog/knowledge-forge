# G_yt_mPX63gC7nK4_en — cold email

## Term

In this hiring video, cold email is the system the speaker is building for clients, sending hundreds of emails a day from many domains and users. The phrase appears 4 times, beside system, 625 emails, five domains, and 25 users, and the concrete move is the scale he names: a cold email system that can send up to 625 emails per day, with five domains and 25 users, scaling like crazy, and the hire he needs is someone who can also act as an executive assistant. Its use here is a sending machine, not a message: the video is an announcement about changing the business and hiring, and the cold email system is the operation the new hire will run.
