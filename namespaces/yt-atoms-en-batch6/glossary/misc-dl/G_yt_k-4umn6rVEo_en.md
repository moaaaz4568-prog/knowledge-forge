# G_yt_k-4umn6rVEo_en — Live Paint Bucket

## Term

Here the Live Paint Bucket is the Illustrator tool for painting sections of artwork quickly, taught as a pro workflow for illustrations like the bus in this video: it lives in the toolbar, opens with the K key, and drops color into each enclosed section once the whole design is selected. The phrase appears 6 times, beside toolbar, K, Shift + X, and fill, and the speaker stresses the setup — select the entire design with the selection tool first, then press K and click — because Illustrator groups the shapes before it lets colors drop in. His concrete case is toggling stroke and fill with Shift + X while keeping only the fill option, so each click fills one section of the bus illustration.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fill bucket for any shape: the tool only paints sections of a selected design, and unselected or open shapes get no color from it.
- Read as the same as the shape builder: the Live Paint Bucket fills enclosed sections, while the shape builder merges shapes — the two tools do different jobs in the same toolbar.
