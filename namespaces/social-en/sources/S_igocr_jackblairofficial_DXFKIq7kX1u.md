1. "I'm going to say the thing everyone else in this conversation is probably thinking but not saying."

Roman Roy's superpower was naming the elephant in the room before it could be used against him. This phrase stops the buyer mid-defence and makes them curious about what comes next. 2. "Look, I'll be the first to admit this isn't a perfect fit for everyone. Let me tell you who it is perfect for."

Roman never needed to. This phrase does something most reps are so terrified to do: it admits limitation. And in doing so it makes everything that follows sound like the truth. Buyers trust the rep who tells them what something is not. Then they believe you completely when you tell them what it is.

Roman Roy never did anything the expected way and that is exactly why people paid attention to him. This phrase signals confidence, self-awareness, and respect for the buyer's intelligence in one sentence. You immediately separate yourself from every other rep who sat in that seat. 4. "I've heard every version of that objection. Here's the one thing nobody ever says back to it."

This is Roman at his sharpest. Disarming, direct, and slightly dangerous. This phrase signals that you have been here before, that you know the script, and that you are about to go off it. The buyer leans in because they want to hear the thing nobody says. Then you deliver it clean and the objection dissolves.

5. "Honestly? If I were sitting where you are, I'd probably ask the same thing. Here's how I'd answer it."

Roman won people over by making them feel like he was genuinely on their side, even mid-negotiation. This phrase steps into the buyer's shoes without surrendering your position. You validate their thinking, demonstrate empathy, and answer the objection from a place of shared perspective. They stop feeling sold to. They start feeling understood.

6. "I'm not going to dress this up. Here's exactly what you get, what it costs, and why it's worth it."

Roman Roy at his most effective stripped everything back to the raw truth. No spin. No theatre. Just the facts delivered with complete confidence. This phrase works because buyers are exhausted by complexity. Clarity is the close. The rep who can say it plainly and mean it fully never needs a deck to back them up.

7. "So are we doing this or not? Because I think we both know the answer already."

Only Roman Roy could make the boldest close in the room sound completely casual. This phrase works because it assumes the yes without applying pressure and delivers it with the kind of easy confidence that makes the buyer feel like saying yes was their idea all along. Casual certainty is the most powerful close there is.
