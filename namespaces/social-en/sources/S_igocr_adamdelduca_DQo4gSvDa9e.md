1. Pick your sub-niche

You don’t need to cover all athletes.

Focus on one angle that’s emotionally charged and repeatable.
Examples:
• NFL comeback stories
• NBA players who went broke and rebuilt
• Soccer players from poverty to fame

2. Study existing winners

"The rise and fall of [athlete name]"

• Video length (usually 8–15 minutes)
• Tone
• Titles and thumbnails

3. Build your video format
Faceless documentaries need structure.
1. Cold open (powerful quote or visual)
2. The struggle (poverty, obstacles, rejection)
3. The breakthrough (moment of success)
4. The rewards (money, fame, legacy)
5. The lesson (why viewers care)
Make every story follow this emotional arc

6. Script like a storyteller

Each line should make the viewer feel something.

Example:
“He grew up in a one-room apartment.
His dream wasn’t to be rich, it was to buy his mother a real bed.”

Then tie it to the payoff:
“Ten years later, he signed a $150 million contract.”

Story over facts only…
