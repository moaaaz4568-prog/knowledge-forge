WHAT DOES UGC
ACTUALLY MEAN?
UGC means User Generated Content.
It's when you make photos or videos
for brands, and they post it on their
social media.
You are the creator
The brand is the publisher
You do NOT need to post anything on
your own account.
<!-- image 2 -->
@aashna_kumar
YOU DON'T NEED
FOLLOWERS, FANCY
GEAR, OR EXPERIENCE
To be a UGC creator:
• You don't need followers because
brands post the content, not you
• You don't need an expensive camera
— a phone is enough
• You can get free products and get paid
for creating videos
Brands are actively looking for
who can create simple, natural content.
That's why this is a great time to start.

HOW TO START UGC
(4-WEEK SIMPLE PLAN)
WEEK 1 – LEARN WHAT UGC IS
This week is only about learning.
Focus on:
• What UGC videos look like
• Different UGC style(reviews, demos, unboxing)
• Basic filming tips
No equipment shopping needed.
Your phone is enough.
<!-- image 4 -->
@aashna_kumar
WEEK 2 – BUILD YOUR PORTFOLIO
Now it’s time to practice.
• Use products you already have at home
• skincare, makeup, clothes, gadgets – anything
• Record short videos as samples
You can create a free portfolio using Canva.
Brands want to see what you can create – not paid work.
<!-- image 5 -->
@aashna_kumar
WEEK 3 – DECIDE YOUR PRICES
Now you price your work.
A good beginner range:
$50–$100 per UGC video
Smart tips:
• Offer bundles (example: 4 videos for a lower total price)
• Charge extra if brands want to use your videos in ads
This helps you earn more without extra filming.
<!-- image 6 -->
@aashna_kumar
WEEK 4 – PITCH TO BRANDS
This is where opportunities come in.
• Create a simple pitch message
• Collect brand emails or Instagram handles
• Apply on UGC platforms (free to join)
• Or message brands directly
Consistency matters more than perfection here.
