Never use statements when you're selling. Instead, use questions. Let me give you an example. When someone asks you, "How do I know if this is gonna work for me?" If you use a statement and say something like, "Well, this has worked for hundreds of our clients, we're the best in the industry, and we give the best support," this is a statement. The conversation is now frozen, and you have to do a lot of pushing and convincing and get them across the board. Instead, use a question. Say something like this: "Well, if it has worked for hundreds of our clients and they were able to double their sales, why do you feel it wouldn't work for you though?" Now this puts the ball back on them. They have the responsibility of their decision, and they have to sell themselves on exactly what you sell.
