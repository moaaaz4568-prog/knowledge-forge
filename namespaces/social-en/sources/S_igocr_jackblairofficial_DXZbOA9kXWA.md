1. "Before I show you anything I want to understand exactly what problem you need this to solve."

Tony Stark never built a solution without first understanding the precise nature of the problem.
A demo built around the buyer's specific situation closes at a completely different level than a generic walkthrough.

This phrase delays the demo by sixty seconds and makes it ten times more powerful.
The buyer who answers this question has just handed you the exact angle your demo needs to land on.
Now you are not showing features. You are solving a problem they described in their own words.

Ask before you show. The demo built around their answer is the only one that matters.

2. "Watch this specific part. This is the moment most people realise what this actually means for them."

Stark always directed the room's attention to the precise moment the technology proved itself.
He never left the audience to find the best part on their own.

This phrase builds anticipation for the most powerful section of your demo
and signals that what is coming is worth their full focus.
The buyer who was half present suddenly gives you everything they have
because you told them exactly where to look and when.

Direct the attention. The close almost always lives in the moment you point them toward.

3. "I am going to show you something that is going to make you question why you have been doing this the hard way."

Tony Stark always made the before look exhausting and the after look inevitable.
That contrast is the emotional engine of every great demo ever delivered.

This phrase sets up the contrast before you create it and the buyer starts feeling the weight of the old way before you have shown them the new one.
By the time you demonstrate the solution they are already relieved.

Make the old way feel heavy before you show them the door out of it.

4. "I am going to let you drive this part. I want you to see how it feels rather than just watch me use it."

Stark always understood that the person controlling the technology became its most committed advocate.
The buyer who touches your solution owns it emotionally before they own it contractually.

This phrase converts a passive demo into an active experience and active experiences close at a completely different rate.
The buyer who drives the demo stops evaluating and starts experiencing.
An experienced solution is infinitely harder to walk away from than a presented one.

Put them in the seat. The buyer who experiences it themselves has already started closing.

5. "Here is what this looks like twelve months from now if nothing changes. Here is what it looks like if it does."

Stark never sold the present state of his technology. He sold the trajectory.
The before and after delivered with precision is the most persuasive structure in any demo.

This phrase creates a fork in the road the buyer has to choose between.
They see the cost of inaction and the reward of movement at the same time
and the contrast does more persuasive work than any feature you could demonstrate.

Show them both futures. The buyer who sees the fork almost always chooses the better road.

6. "I am not going to walk you through every feature. I am going to show you the three that will change how you work."

Stark never buried the room in specifications when a demonstration would make the argument faster and better.
This phrase signals editorial intelligence and buyers respond to it with immediate respect.

You are not showing everything. You are showing the right things.
That discipline signals that you understand their world well enough to know what matters inside it.
The buyer who sees exactly what they need sees nothing they need to object to.

Show less. Land more. The focused demo always closes harder than the comprehensive one.

7. "I am going to stop here and ask you something. Based on what you have seen so far what is your honest reaction?"

Stark always wanted real-time data on how the room was receiving what he was showing. He never waited until the end to find out he had lost them in the middle.

This phrase mid-demo is the most underused in any rep's toolkit.
The buyer who answers honestly gives you the objection while you still have the screen in front of you to address it. Now you are not handling an objection after the demo. You are solving it inside it.

Check the room mid-demo. The objection caught early is the only one you can close against in real time.
