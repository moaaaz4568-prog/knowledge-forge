5 Quick Deliverability Guide (That Actually Works)
Even the best copy flops if you land in spam.
Here’s the protocol I live by:
Send from 24+ inboxes
Rotate inboxes daily
Don’t send from main domain (use variants like yourbrandhq.com)
Keep it under 100 words
Use Spintax to randomize structure
Use lowercase greetings
Add 1 typo to feel human
Plain-text only (no images or links in email #1)
Never track opens or clicks
A/B test angles, not subject lines
If your emails are boring, they won’t get flagged — but they won’t get replies either.
Be brave. Be different.
