I DISCOVERED MY PASSION FOR PHOTOGRAPHY AT 15. I SIGNED UP FOR TRAVEL LANDSCAPE WORKSHOPS ALONE BECAUSE NONE OF MY FRIENDS WERE INTERESTED. THE GROUPS WERE ALL ADULTS IN THEIR LATE 30S AND 40S, SO I STOOD OUT IMMEDIATELY. BUT, BELIEVE IT OR NOT, I FOUND MORE COMMON GROUND WITH MY OWN PEERS.

MY WHOLE WORLD FELL APART WHEN MY PARENTS FORCED ME TO STUDY ARCHITECTURE AND MOVE TO GERMANY. THOSE THREE YEARS WERE SOME OF THE HARDEST OF MY LIFE. WHILE I WAS THERE, I TRIED TRANSFERRING TO FILM SCHOOL IN THE USA, ONLY TO BE REJECTED BECAUSE THEY DIDN'T OFFER SCHOLARSHIPS TO NON-CITIZENS. I FELT LIKE I WAS RUNNING IN THE WRONG DIRECTION, COMPLETELY LOST.

IN THE MEANTIME I SHOT WEDDINGS AND WORKED AT A NIGHTCLUB TO SAVE MONEY FOR TRAVEL. FINALLY I CALLED IT QUITS IN 2020 AND DROVE BACK TO BULGARIA. MY FATHER DIDN'T TALK TO ME FOR ABOUT A YEAR.
