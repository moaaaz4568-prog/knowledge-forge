"I want to create a digital product to sell on Instagram. My niche is [X]. My skills and experience are [Y]. My target audience is [Z] and their biggest problem is [problem]. Give me the top 5 most profitable digital product ideas for this audience, ranked by ease of creation and income potential.

• what to name it
• what to include
• what price point to sell it at and why
• which format works best (ebook, template, course, guide, checklist)

3. BIO THAT CONVERTS PROMPT
Write me 5 Instagram bio options that naturally sell my digital product.
My product is [product name].
It helps [target audience] to [result]. My name is [name].
Include:
• Who I help
• What result they get
• A simple CTA to click the link in bio
Each bio must be under 150 characters.
Make it sound human, warm, and real. Not salesy. Not robotic.

4. CAROUSEL CONTENT PROMPT
Create a 9-slide Instagram carousel that sells my digital product without sounding like an ad. My product is [product name] and it helps [audience] achieve [result].
Structure:
Slide 1: a strong hook (can use a trend, stat, or relatable line)
Slides 2-6: valuable teaching (solve one clear problem)
Slide 7: a common myth people believe Slide 8: a short transformation or example
Slide 9: soft CTA to comment [keyword] for my free training
Tone: conversational, simple, real. Add curiosity in each slide.

Tone: warm, honest, big sister energy. Simple words. No hype.
