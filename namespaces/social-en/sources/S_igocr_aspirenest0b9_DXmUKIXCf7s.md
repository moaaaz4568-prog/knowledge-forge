The Content Repurposing System
→ Turn one piece of content into ten formats across every platform

Why Did I Bring This Into the Game?
Because creating content from scratch is a trap. And every day is a trap.
→ It’s exhausting. It’s inefficient. And it’s completely unnecessary.
The best operators don’t create more content.
They extract more value from what already exists.

One LinkedIn post becomes a newsletter. One podcast episode becomes five carousels. One client case study becomes a PDF lead magnet, a cold email, and a LinkedIn story.
This prompt turns Claude into your content repurposing engine. Feed it one piece of content, tell it what formats you need — walk away with a week’s worth of assets.
→ This is how you build a content machine without burning out.

How the Prompt Works
→ Role: Senior content strategist and multi-platform copywriter
→ Goal: Extract maximum reach from a single piece of content
→ Instructions: Adapt the core idea across multiple formats and platforms
→ Output: Ready-to-publish content across LinkedIn, email, PDF, and more

How to Customize This Prompt
Paste your original content in first. Then tell Claude:
- What formats you need (LinkedIn post, email, carousel, PDF, Reddit, etc.)
- Your tone of voice
- Your target audience
- Any platform-specific requirements
