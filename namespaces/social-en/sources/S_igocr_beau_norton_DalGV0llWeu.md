5 quick tips 👇
<!-- image 1 -->
1
Visual Hooks
A “hook” isn’t just about what you SAY in your content, it’s also about what people SEE. Combine spoken/written hooks with visual hooks to boost watch time and get way more views.
<!-- image 2 -->
2
Say what they’re
afraid to say out loud
Be bold. Call out
the things you
dislike in your
industry. Point out
the downsides and
negative side of
things people try to
hype up and portray
as purely positive.
<!-- image 3 -->
3
Open loops
Humans are driven by curiosity. Don’t reveal the big insight immediately. Tell them what you’re going to tell them, then tell them one piece at a time, saving the big insight for the end.
<!-- image 4 -->
4
Give them stuff to look at
People have extremely short attention spans. If the only thing in the scene to look at is your face, people will scroll past, even if you have good stuff to say. Change up the scenes or visuals on screen throughout the video to keep attention for longer.
<!-- image 5 -->
5
Leverage the desire for novelty
The second someone thinks “I know that already,” they’re gone. Share your ideas in unique ways. Use stories, metaphors, or visuals. Give your concepts unique names. Share interesting statistics or examples. Anything other than plain facts.
