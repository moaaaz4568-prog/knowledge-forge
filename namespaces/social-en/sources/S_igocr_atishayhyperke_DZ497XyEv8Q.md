How I find high-paying
clients using AI
you can steal this workflow
@atishayhyperke
<!-- image 1 -->
Step 1: Build the lead list
I pull raw lead lists using Apify and LinkedIn scrapers.
Raw export:
up to 200,000 companies. The bigger and dirtier the list, the better. Filtering happens next.
<!-- image 2 -->
Step 2: Filter with Claude
I feed the raw list to Claude.
I give it my offer, my ICP, and the data on each row. Claude scores each lead and cuts the list down to the ones that actually match.
Output:
roughly 40,000 qualified leads. I only pay to enrich the ones that pass this filter.
<!-- image 3 -->
Step 3: Enrich with Clay
The filtered list goes into Clay.
I pull verified emails, LinkedIn profiles, company size, tech stack, and recent activity. This is the data that makes personalization actually work.
Without this step, every email reads the same.
<!-- image 4 -->
Step 4: Write the campaign with Claude
I give Claude my offer, my ICP, and the enriched data.
It writes email 1, follow-up 1, follow-up 2, and a few angle variations. I review the output and rewrite any line that feels generic.
<!-- image 5 -->
Step 5: Launch in Smartlead
I upload the campaign and the leads into Smartlead.
I set the sending schedule, configure the follow-ups, and hit send. The first replies usually land within 48 hours.
<!-- image 6 -->
Step 6: Monitor and kill
I check the campaigns every day.
The ones that aren't getting replies get killed. The angles that are working get more leads.
Bad campaigns die before they damage sender reputation. Winners get scaled.
