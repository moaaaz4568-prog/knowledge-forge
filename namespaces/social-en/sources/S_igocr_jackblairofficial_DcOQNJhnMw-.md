THE LINE BUYERS WROTE THEMSELVES
1
Through the 1970s, purchasing departments repeated one sentence: nobody ever got fired for buying IBM. IBM never printed it. It grew on the buyer's side of the table.
That is the tell. The strongest sales copy in B2B history is not about the product. It is about the buyer surviving their own decision.
Buyers don't buy outcomes. They buy alibis.

THE RIVAL IS BLAME, NOT THE COMPETITOR
The line worked against faster and cheaper rivals
because a mainframe that failed was survivable if it
said IBM and career ending if it said anything else.
Map every deal for the blame channel. Who explains
this purchase if it goes sideways, to whom, in what
meeting. Sell to the scapegoat.
Every big deal has a second buyer: the scapegoat.

ASK THE QUESTION THEY ARE HIDING
Underneath every stall on a big ticket is a private calculation: what happens to me if this fails. It never gets said out loud, so it never gets handled.
Ask it straight: if this went sideways in a year, what would it mean for you? The relief you hear is the real objection surfacing at last.
The unspoken risk kills more deals than price.

BUILD THE ALIBI INTO THE OFFER
IBM sold references, service armies, and the comfort of the crowd. Reported ad copy of the era promised a good night's sleep, not better computing.
Give your champion the defense file: named references, rollback plans, the exit ramp in writing. Arm the person who has to defend the yes. Sell the defense before they need one.

GIVE THE COMMITTEE ITS ONE SENTENCE
A committee does not approve the best option. It
approves the most explainable one, the choice that
sounds responsible, title retold in one sentence.
Write the sentence for them: we went with the team
that has done this forty times at our size. If your
champion cannot say it in one breath, tighten it.
The most repeatable reason wins the room.

PRICE THE FAILURE, NOT THE PRODUCT

Safe never means cheap. IBM carried premium prices for decades because the alternative being weighed was not a rival system, it was a career.

When they push the math: what does the wrong choice cost in rework, downtime and explanations. Your premium is the insurance line.

Expensive and safe beats cheap and explained.

BE THE INCUMBENT BEFORE YOU ARE ONE
The line aged out only when challengers stopped
fighting the fear and started borrowing safety: pilots,
guarantees, logos, one small safe yes at a time.

saying yes to you is safer than ignoring you. Then
grow inside the door you opened.
You cannot argue away fear. You can shrink it.
