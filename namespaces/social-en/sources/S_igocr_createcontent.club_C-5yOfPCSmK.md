HOW DOES THE ALGORITHM WORK?

You see, the IG algorithm first shows your Reels to your pre-existing audience, your followers.

Based on how much they watch that video and how they interact with it, the Instagram algorithm then decides if it’s worth pushing your content outside of your following.

So, how do you make your audience watch and interact with your videos? By following these 2 steps:

@Createcontent.club
<!-- image 3 -->
STEP 1 - THE PROMISE OF A PAYOFF

The first thing you need to do is create a video that from the very start promises clearly that if your viewers stay until the end, they will get something out of it, and their time invested in your content will be worth it.

This payoff could be as simple as learning something new that they did not know about, or seeing the final result of a process.

@Createcontent.club
<!-- image 4 -->
STEP 2 - APPLICABLE SOLUTIONS
Even if you promise a payoff, if you do not deliver something that people can actually benefit from, this won't work.
To create an applicable solution, always ask yourself this question:
Can they get results in the next 24-48 hours after watching my content?
