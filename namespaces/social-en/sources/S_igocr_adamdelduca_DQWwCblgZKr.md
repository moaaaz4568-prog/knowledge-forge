1. Start With Proven Hits

Go to the biggest channels in your niche.

Sort by “Most Popular.”

Write down their top 20 videos.

That’s your blueprint for what the audience already loves.
<!-- image 2 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

2. Find the Outliers

Every creator has one video that blew up way beyond the rest.

Study that one.

Was it the story? The pacing? The emotion? The visuals?

Something about it hit different—figure out what.
<!-- image 3 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca
3. Steal the Framework
You’re not copying ideas, you’re copying formats.
“The Rise and Fall of Netflix’s DVD Business” becomes “The Rise and Fall of MoviePass.”
“The Secret Strategy Behind Apple’s Marketing” becomes “The Secret Strategy Behind Tesla’s Fanbase.”
<!-- image 4 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

4. Move Across Niches

Borrow what’s working in one world and
apply it to another.

Finance → Fitness.
Tech → Education.
Gaming → Business.

The structure stays the same—only the
context changes.
<!-- image 5 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

5. Study Curiosity

The best ideas don’t just get clicks—
they demand them.

Ask yourself: “Would I pause my scroll
for this?”

If the answer isn’t immediate, it’s not
strong enough.
<!-- image 6 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

6. Think in Extremes

People click on emotion, tension, and stakes.

No one watches “The Story of a Failed App.”

They watch “How One App Lost $1 Billion Overnight.”
<!-- image 7 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

7. Mine Your Comments

Your viewers are constantly giving you
your next ideas.

Every “You should cover…” or “What
about this topic?” is market research in
disguise.
<!-- image 8 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

8. Go Beyond YouTube

Reddit, X (Twitter), and forums show
you what's heating up before it hits
mainstream.

Find those discussions early—then be
the first to visualize them.
<!-- image 9 -->
Adam | YouTube
@Adam_DelDuca

9. Remix Everything

Originality is just recombination.

Take one viral concept and mash it with another.

Example: “The Rise of AI Tools” + “The Decline of Human Creativity” = “Is AI Killing Original Thought?”
