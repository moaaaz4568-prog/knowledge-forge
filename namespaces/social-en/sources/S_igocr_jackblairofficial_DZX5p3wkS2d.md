3. "The number one reason for failure in sales is an empty pipe."

Blount traced almost every slump back to one cause, the rep stopped prospecting when things were good. Keep filling the top even on your best week, because today's silence is last month's comfort.

4. "Prospect every day, the 30-Day Rule."
He taught that the work you do now pays
out about ninety days later, in three thirty-
day windows. Skip a month and you feel it
a quarter later, when it is too late to fix.
Protect the daily reps and future you stays
booked.

5. "Adopt a replacement mindset, the Law of Replacement."

Closing a deal opens a hole in your pipeline. Blount's rule was that every deal you win has to be replaced with new prospecting the same week. Refill the second you score and you never crash after a big month.

6. "Ask why me, then ask how can I learn from this."

After a brutal no, Blount swapped self-pity for a question. The rep who studies the loss closes the next one, the one who sulks repeats it. Turn the rejection into the lesson and rejection stops running you.

7. "You've got to pay for your success in advance."

He framed prospecting as paying upfront for results you cannot see yet. The comfortable rep waits to feel ready, the paid one does the unpleasant work first. Do the hard part now and collect later.
