<!-- image 2 -->
Most coaches
I work with tell me the same thing:
“I’m confident in my client calls but as soon as the camera turns on I freeze...”
<!-- image 3 -->
Being great
at talking doesn't mean
you'll be great at talking
to the camera
here's the
3.R Framework I used
to go from cringe → confident
<!-- image 4 -->
Record
Record in private
No pressure, no audience
Pull up a random script and start talking
You're not going to post this
Just focus on getting comfortable with speaking
wait until everyone left the house if needed
<!-- image 5 -->
Review
Watch your recording and
evaluate 3 key areas:
1 Fluctuations
do you sound dynamic or flat?
2 Tone of Voice
presenter's voice or talking to a friend?
3 Body language
stiff or natural, laid back?
find one small thing to improve
for each and note it down
<!-- image 6 -->
Repeat
now record the same script again
this time
apply your feedback from the first take
repeat this cycle 3-5 times per sessions
you'll see & feel the difference
<!-- image 7 -->
this takes less than
30 minutes per day
it's the same framework I help my 1:1
clients go through when they tell me:
I'm not ready to show
myself to the public
