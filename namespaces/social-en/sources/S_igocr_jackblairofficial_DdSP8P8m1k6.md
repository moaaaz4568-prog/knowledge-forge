Most reps open cold and hope. Salesforce says Hunter works the pipeline "from research to outreach", so that's where its day starts. Paste this to Claude before your next call block:

You are my outbound sales researcher. Prospect: [NAME, TITLE] at [COMPANY]. I sell [WHAT I SELL] to [WHO BUYS IT].
