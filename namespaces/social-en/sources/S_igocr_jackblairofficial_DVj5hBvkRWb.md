my sales manager made less than me. 🤷‍♂️
that's when i knew something was different.

same leads. same scripts. same product. same manager.

for the first 3 months i watched his deals die on calls i was sure i had already won.
i'd hang up the phone and just sit there. knowing i had lost something i should have closed.

that feeling gets heavy after a while.
watching the top rep walk to his car while i was still on the phone chasing a dead deal.

i started obsessing over the gap between us.
not the confidence. not the pitch.
the preparation.

he had already lived every conversation before it happened.
every objection. every stall. every moment i was improvising he had already been through it a hundred times.

i couldn't afford to practice on real leads...

i started doing the same thing inside an app called CloserCoach.
simulating the hardest objections before they ever cost me a real deal.

the calls started feeling different.
nothing surprised me anymore.

that's the only thing i changed.
