7. "CAN WE START THAT PART OVER, AND DO IT RIGHT THIS TIME?"
The finding beneath Diamond's method: people who feel emotionally paid turn rational again and give more, whatever the final number says.
Close the repair with a reset, named as one. Buyers rarely refuse a genuine do-over, and the second run inherits none of the first one's debt.
Nobody signs from inside a grudge.
