1. "Before anything, tell me what the last person promised you."

The families in Hinkley had been told for years their water was fine. Erin Brockovich started with what they'd been told, then let them say what it cost them. A burned buyer can't hear your pitch until the old lie is on the table. Ask for it first.

You can't sell over a scar. Name it first.

2. "I'm not the polished one in the suit. I'm the one who knows your file."
Brockovich had no law degree and no science training. What she had was every detail of every family's situation. Stop borrowing status from your title or your brand. Lead with the specific things you actually know about their world and watch the guard drop.
Buyers trust the person who knows, not the person who impresses.

3. "Here's your situation back to you, down to the details."

She built a case file nobody asked her to build, and it opened doors lawyers couldn't. Before you propose anything, recite the buyer's world back to them accurately. Their numbers, their timeline, their last attempt. Precision is the fastest trust signal there is.

Being known closes faster than being pitched.
