4. "WHAT CHANGES FOR YOU IF THIS GETS AROUND BEFORE IT'S DONE?"

Ask it plainly. Buyers protect deals they've imagined leaking. You're not hiding anything. You're pricing what exposure costs both sides.
Information is the asset. The product is where it sits.
