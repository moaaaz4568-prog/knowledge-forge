Feature-by-feature bakeoffs are blind taste cups. Reps who fight there accept the one arena where story counts for nothing, and story is where the money is. Never let the deal reduce to a spec grid. Anchor the comparison in outcomes, references and the account's own history before the grid appears. Win the frame or the spreadsheet decides.
