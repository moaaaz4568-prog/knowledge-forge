"SORRY TO BOTHER YOU."

Nobody was taught this line. It leaks out because the rep believes the call is an interruption, and the buyer accepts that frame in the first five seconds.

Open with the reason instead. "The reason I'm calling is..." A call with a reason is business. A call with an apology is spam.

They can't value the call more than you do.

"I'LL BE QUICK."
A promise of speed is a confession of weight. It tells the buyer this conversation is safe to half listen to, and they take the deal exactly as seriously as you framed it.
Promise relevance instead. "This is worth two minutes if you own the number this quarter." Let the wrong people leave.
Sprint through it and they learn it wasn't worth walking through.

06 / 10

"I'LL THROW IN A DISCOUNT."

Nobody asked for it. A price you cut uninvited was never a real price, and the buyer just watched you negotiate against yourself.

Hold the number and trade instead. If price ever moves, something moves back: timing, scope, a decision this week.

An unasked discount tells them the price was fake.

08 / 10

"REACH OUT WHENEVER YOU'RE READY."

It sounds respectful. It hands the calendar to the person who cares least, and no buyer ever marks a date to get sold on.

Give space without giving up the wheel. "Take the time. I'll call on the 14th and we'll decide then."

Deals don't die of no. They die of whenever.
