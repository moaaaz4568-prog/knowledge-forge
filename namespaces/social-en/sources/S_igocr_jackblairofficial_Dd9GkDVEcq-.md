2. "What do you think?"

other person fill the silence.

her own position. By the time she speaks,

3. "I'm not sure that's right."

Wintour does not argue, raise her voice, or explain herself at length when she disagrees. She says one calm, quiet sentence.

The softness is the power. A quiet "I'm not sure that's right" lands harder than a loud objection ever could.

Stay calm under disagreement and the room is yours. Get loud and you hand it away.

7. Silence.

Wintour's most powerful tool is not a phrase at all. It is the pause. The unreadable expression behind dark glasses.

She lets silence sit until the other person reveals their real position. She never rushes to fill it.

Be comfortable in silence and you win. Fill every gap and you give the room away.
