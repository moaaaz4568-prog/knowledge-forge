Task Description

Format: Type
Bullet Points
Numbered List
Table
Paragraph Form

When to Use
Lists, key takeaways, action items
Sequenced steps
Prioritizing details
Comparing data, information
Explanations, narratives, detailed analysis

Example Prompt
"Present findings as bullet points"
"Give me 5 recommendations in order of importance"
"Format results in a 3-column table"
"Write in paragraph format, no lists"

Prompt: "Analyze our Q1 sales performance. Give me the key findings."
Prefilled response: "Based on the Q1 sales data, here are the key findings:
- Overall Performance..."
- Top Performing Areas..."

Without step-by-step:
"Analyze our marketing campaign performance and tell me what to do next."

With step-by-step prompting:
"I need you to analyze our marketing campaign performance. Think through this step by step."

Actionable language - Use clear verbs like "analyze", "write", "summarize", "create", "compare", "explain"
Priority focus - If there are multiple parts, indicate what's most important
Scope definition - Specify constraints like length, format, audience, or deadline
Examples:
- Vague: "Tell me about my data"
- Specific: "Analyze the sales data and create a summary report"
- Prompt: "Analyze the CSV and identify the top 3 performing products"
