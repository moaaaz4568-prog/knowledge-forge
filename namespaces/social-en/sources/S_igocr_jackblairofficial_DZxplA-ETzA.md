Levene spoke to the buyer like a partner who'd already decided together.

Levene never let a vague hesitation sit unnamed.

The stall you can't see is the one that kills the deal. Asking directly, with warmth, forces the real reason into the open where it can actually be handled.

5. "I'm not here to sell you. I'm here to make sure you don't miss this."

Levene reframed himself as the buyer's advocate, not the seller.

People resist being sold and trust being helped. Positioning yourself on their side of the decision changes how every word after it lands.

Be the person protecting their interest, not pushing your own.

Levene made decisiveness feel like the smart person's move.

Buyers hesitate because waiting feels safe. Modeling fast, confident decisions as what sharp people do makes sitting still feel like the actual risk.

Finish it while you're together, not after they leave.
