2022 was a massive year:
• $27M+ in sales for our clients
• 36 successful info-product launches
• 6 clients scaled from $100k to $1M+

Here are 10 lessons that I wish I knew in 2022:

1/ Prepare for the storm.
Most people wait for something "bad"
to happen before they do something.
Take advantage and capitalize on the
opportunities you have now before it's
too late.
<!-- image 2 -->
Ahmed Saddique
@AhmedSaddique

2/ Think 3 steps ahead of your current situation.

With growth comes change and with change things break.

Prepare for those moments early so you aren't thrown off target later.
<!-- image 3 -->
Ahmed Saddique
@AhmedSaddique

3/ Take significant ownership of your health.

You can get sucked into this "hustle-culture" mindset and start to dismiss the idea of taking care of yourself.

But our ability to perform as A-players comes from:

- exercising
- sleeping well
- balancing work & life
<!-- image 4 -->
Ahmed Saddique
@AhmedSaddique

4/ Don't underestimate the value of
making small improvements on a daily
basis.

Your results are a byproduct of your
habits.

When you repeat the same errors day
after day you start to compound
negatively into toxic results.
<!-- image 5 -->
Ahmed Saddique
@AhmedSaddique

5/ Momentum can be your greatest
leverage or weakness.
It can propel you to new heights or
keep you locked into previous choices
and habits.

6/ Build meaningful relationships early.
As you grow and achieve a certain level
of success it becomes significantly
harder to find real friends.

Support each other early, and grow
together.

@AhmedSaddique

7/ The journey doesn't get easier, you just learn to cope with hard better.
Looking back at the challenges I was facing at beginning of the year seem nothing close to the challenges I'm facing today.
New levels, new devils.
<!-- image 7 -->
Ahmed Saddique

8/ Don’t second guess investing in
yourself.

Whether it’s education, nutrition,
mental health, fitness, etc.

These things will have the greatest
return in the long term.

9/ Happiness comes from a place of gratitude.
Telling yourself "I'll be happy when this happens" is a road that never ends.
If you can't be happy where you are today, you won't be happy when you get to X.

10/ Find a mentor or coach.
When you're in the trenches trying to figure things out on your own you'll develop blind spots.
Having someone in your corner to give you clarity could be the thing that propels you.
