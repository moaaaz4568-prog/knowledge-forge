Here's the part nobody retracted.

AI takes the predictable work first.
Anything repeatable, rules-based, done at a desk. The follow-up email. The CRM note. The research. The first qualifying question. The report your manager reads on Monday. If a task can be written down as a series of steps, a machine can learn to run it. That describes most of the white-collar world, and a large part of most sales jobs.

There's one thing it still cannot do well.
Make a nervous human trust another human with a decision that scares them. When someone is about to hand over money they can't easily replace, the moment comes down to reading their fear, hearing the objection under the objection, and making them feel safe enough to say yes out loud. A model can answer questions all day. It cannot sit in that silence and hold it.

The one piece still sitting in a human's hands is the last one. That isn't sentiment. It's just the part nobody has managed to automate yet.
