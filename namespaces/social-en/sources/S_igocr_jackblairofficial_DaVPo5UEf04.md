1. Can I grab two minutes, because I want to get this right for you?

Langer's 1978 Harvard study is famous for one finding. Add the word because and a reason to any request and compliance jumps, even when the reason is almost nothing.

People do not just hear what you ask. They hear whether you gave them a reason.

Never make a request naked. Attach a because to every ask on the call.

2. The reason I'm asking is simple.

In her study, because I have to make copies worked nearly as well as a real reason. The word because triggered the yes, not the logic after it.

Buyers are often on autopilot. A stated reason flips them from reflex to attention.

Before any ask, say the reason out loud. The form of it does half the work.

Langer's mindful approach is about opening possibilities instead of defending a position.

Ask what would make it work, and let them draw you the map.

7. No pressure. I just don't want you deciding on autopilot.

The core of Langer's work is that most bad decisions are made mindlessly, not on purpose.
