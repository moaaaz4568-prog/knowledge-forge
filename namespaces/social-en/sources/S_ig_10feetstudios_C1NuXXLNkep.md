If you're running an agency and you're starting to make some real money, you're probably thinking, "Hmm, when should I actually start outsourcing my service delivery?" So there's no specific amount of revenue you need to hit. You need to outsource as soon as you know how to actually deliver a good service. You shouldn't do it any earlier than that because you will not know how to teach the people that you're hiring. You also shouldn't wait any longer once you know that because you will get stuck in your business. There's no reason for you to edit videos or script them out. Your margins will decrease when hiring and outsourcing, but your time to get new clients will hugely increase. So don't think about the couple thousand bucks you might be spending when outsourcing. Think about how much more money you can generate once you can focus on getting more clients.
