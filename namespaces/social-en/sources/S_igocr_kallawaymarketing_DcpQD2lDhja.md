HOW TO MAKE
CONTENT PSYCHOLOGICALLY
IMPOSSIBLE TO IGNORE
The one degree rule
<!-- image 1 -->
EVERYONE ON EARTH ONLY WANTS 4 THINGS:
Money
Time
Health
Access
These are the Four Horsemen of Desire.
<!-- image 2 -->
THE PROBLEM
If you mention any of these four words as outcomes
in your content, people are trained to scroll away.
Content Script
Money, Time, Health, Access
<!-- image 3 -->
THE BS DETECTOR
Script
"Here are the four best types of videos if you want to make a hundred thousand dollars a month."
BS!
Something feels salesy.
That feeling is your BS Detector going off.
<!-- image 4 -->
THE TRICK
The One Degree Rule
Instead of basing your content on one of the Four
Horsemen, position the outcome one degree away.
MONEY
TIME
HEALTH
ACCESS
Views
Productivity
Energy
Community
<!-- image 5 -->
Instead of
“Make Money”
say
“Get 10x More Views”
In most people's heads, getting views is a proxy for money, but it doesn't trip the BS Detector.
<!-- image 6 -->
Instead of
"Save Time"
say
"Be More Productive"
"Build A Better System"
"Speed Run The Growth"
All are proxies for saving time,
but are one degree away so they don't
sound too good to be true.
<!-- image 7 -->
HOW TO APPLY THIS

Figure out which of the Four Horsemen your viewer actually wants.

MONEY
TIME
HEALTH
ACCESS

Then map out what "one degree away" looks like for your topic.

Those proxies should be the outcome you signal in your content.
