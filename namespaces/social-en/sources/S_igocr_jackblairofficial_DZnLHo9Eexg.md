2. "You don't have a problem with the price. You have a problem with the risk."

Mike never argued the objection a buyer led with, because he knew it was almost never the real one.

Price is what people say out loud. Fear is what they actually mean. He answered the fear and the price stopped mattering.

Find the thing under the thing, and you close what looked dead.

3. "Here's exactly what happens if you do nothing."

Mike never just sold the upside. He made the cost of standing still impossible to ignore.

A buyer who is comfortable where they are will never move. Show them the slow bleed of doing nothing and your offer becomes the safe choice, not the risky one.

Make the status quo scarier than the decision.

4. “I’m not the smartest guy in the room. I’m the one who did the work.”

He beat Harvard pedigrees with pure preparation, and he was not afraid to say it to their face.

Buyers do not trust the slickest person in the room. They trust the one who clearly cared enough to be ready for them.

Effort reads as honesty. Out-prepare everyone and let them feel it.

5. “Ask me anything. I’ll give it to you straight.”

Mike disarmed people by refusing to dodge a single hard question.

The moment a buyer senses you will not spin them, the wall drops and they start telling you the truth. Most reps hedge, and lose the room.

Invite the hardest question, then answer it clean.

6. “I’m not going anywhere. So let’s get this right.”

In a room full of people angling for the fast win, Mike sold permanence instead.

A buyer relaxes the second they believe you are not there to grab the deal and vanish. Patience reads as strength, never weakness.

Show them you would rather get it right than get it fast.
