So even if you do find a product that is made in the United States, the other thing that you need to be aware of is that the price is often significantly higher. And that's because of two reasons. One, it's usually more expensive to get the raw materials in the United States. And two, labor is way more expensive. And that is not because all factories in China work like sweatshops. That is simply a rumor. But if you are concerned, there are third party inspection companies that will provide you with a ethical work environment audit. So it's just important when you're making that decision to decide whether or not the extra cost is worth the time saving in shipping. Which brings me to another important point. One of the big industries of products that people source in the US is supplements, and I've worked with US-based supplement companies before.
