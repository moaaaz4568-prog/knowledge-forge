The External Villain Framework:
hey [name]
[external villain “the algorithm / spam filters / the old way of doing X”] is quietly punishing [target audience] who still [common practice]. most don’t notice until [consequence]
we just helped [similar company] fix exactly this would love to show you how :)
psychology: blame shifts off the reader onto a shared enemy. you’re instantly on the same side
<!-- image 4 -->
The Mid-Action Hook Framework:
[name] – ran [their company] through [tool/process] at like 1am and found [specific finding]
not trying to scare you, just wanted to flag it before you found out the hard way
can send what i found if you want it?
It makes them see the issue their competitor is having by doing a specific thing and it triggers an emotion to reply
<!-- image 5 -->
The Dark Moment Framework:
hey [name]
last year i almost shut down [type of business/project] because [specific failure]
the thing that saved it wasn't [expected solution]
it was [unexpected category of fix]. I still feels illegal to say out loud.. but I wanted to check if it's the same case with you?
happy to help!
psychology: vulnerability at the open earns trust before any claim is made and starting at the bottom makes the turn inevitable to read.
<!-- image 6 -->
The Open Loop Framework:
hey [name]
everyone in [industry] does the same
[specific thing]. including you..
[specific results on why it's not working]
you guys
very much interested to know why you
still follow this?
It makes them see why the
traditional playbook is not
working and it makes
them question their own
methods the loop is open it
gets them to reply.
<!-- image 7 -->
The Two Timelines Framework:
hey [name]
two versions of [their role] exist 6 months from now
one still [doing the painful current thing]. the other
[enjoying outcome] because they changed one
thing this quarter
the difference between them is smaller than you'd
think
thinking if this is priority at {company} now?
psychology: loss aversion +
the reader casting themselves
into a timeline. the story is
their future, not your past.
<!-- image 8 -->
The False Start Framework:
[name]. you know what? everyone says the fix for
[problem] is [common advice]
we did exactly that for 6 months
it made things worse
what actually worked was the opposite. happy send
over something for your team to test it for a week
it makes them confused to
why would a stranger help
them in the first place.. the
same things you mention they
have tried and failed so they
will respond.

The Chain Framework:
[name] – [target audience] all want [outcome]
BUT [obstacle nobody accounts for]
THEREFORE the ones winning are doing
[counterintuitive move] instead
took us [timeframe] to see it. you can skip that part

a list. "but...therefore" is a plot.
tension in every line, zero flat
sentences
