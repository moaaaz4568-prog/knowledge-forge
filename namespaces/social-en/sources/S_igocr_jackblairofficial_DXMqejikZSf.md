1. "I am not in a rush on this. I want to make sure we get it right for you."

The hungrier you sound, the more power you hand across the table. Every rep knows this and almost none of them can stop doing it when the pressure is real.

This phrase signals patience you may not feel and positions you as someone who values the outcome over the timelines. The buyer relaxes. The real conversation begins. Jimmy Darmody’s greatest weakness was wanting the result before the room was ready to give it.

Slow down on purpose. The deal moves faster when you do.

2. "Before I go any further, I want to make sure I actually understand what you need."

Ambitious reps pitch before they listen because they are excited about the solution. The buyer feels the excitement and reads it as desperation.

This phrase stops the pitch before it starts and signals that understanding matters more than selling. The buyer opens up. You gather everything you need to close without forcing anything. The close that comes from genuine understanding never feels like a close at all.

3. "I think we are close. I just want to sit with what you have told me for a moment before we go further."

This phrase is almost never used because it requires the confidence to pause mid-deal. That pause signals to a buyer that you are thinking about them, not your quota.

It separates you from every other rep who has ever pitched this buyer at full speed.

The silence after this phrase is not dead air. It is trust being built in real time.

Jimmy Darmody could never hold this pause. It cost him everything.

The rep who can sit in silence always wins the room that fills it.

4. "You seem like you are working something through. Take your time. I am here."

Most reps panic when a buyer goes quiet mid-call and fill the silence with more pitch. That is the exact moment that loses the deal.

This phrase does the opposite. It gives the buyer room to think and signals complete composure. The buyer who was hesitating suddenly feels no pressure to decide before they are ready. And a buyer with no pressure almost always decides faster than one who feels cornered.

Create space. The close walks into it on its own.

5. "I could push you toward a decision today. I would rather push you toward the right one."

This phrase lands like a reset button on any call that has been moving too fast. It signals integrity in the one moment buyers are most suspicious of reps.

Jimmy Darmody always prioritised the move over the relationship. This phrase does the opposite and builds the kind of trust that makes those inevitable. The buyer who hears this stops evaluating your pitch and starts evaluating your character.

6. "What would make you feel completely confident before moving forward?"

This phrase replaces pressure with partnership in a single sentence.
The buyer tells you exactly what they need and you deliver it without guessing.

Most reps assume they know what is holding the buyer back and pitch against the wrong thing. This phrase removes the assumption and puts the answer in the buyer's own words. Now you are not closing against resistance. You are closing against a target they handed you.
