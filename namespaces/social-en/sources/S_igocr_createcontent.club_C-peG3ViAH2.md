MISTAKE 1 - NOT BEING PRESENT

Think about it: at the end of the day, Instagram is a business. Do you think it wants people to only post and not consume content?

Exactly...

→ So make sure not to close the app immediately after posting. Instead, interact with other accounts like a normal user.
