Worthington was still shooting as many as four commercials a day well into his eighties.

Worthington posed with tigers, bears, and an elephant he deadpanned was his dog Spot.

When a rival dealer started using a cute pet dog in his ads, Worthington did not shrink.

He rolled out a gorilla and called it his dog Spot, and made the competition look small instead of matching it. When someone copies your move, the answer is rarely to defend, it is to raise the stakes.

Staying in the game is its own competitive edge.
