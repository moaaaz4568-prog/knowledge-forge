4. "Most people in your position tell me the same thing at this stage. Can I show you what changed their mind?"

This phrase works because it does three things at once. It validates their hesitation, positions you as experienced, and creates a story they want to hear.

The buyer stops feeling like they are being sold to.
They start feeling like they are being shown something others already know.
Nobody wants to be the last one in the room to understand something important.

FOMO is not a tactic. In the right hands it is a scalpel.
