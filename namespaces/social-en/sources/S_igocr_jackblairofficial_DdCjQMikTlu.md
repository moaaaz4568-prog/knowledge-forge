1. "BEFORE WE TALK PRICE, HOW DID YOUR DAUGHTER'S SEASON END?"
A Mackay rep opens with the buyer's daughter's soccer season because it is in the file, dated, from the last visit. The competitor opens with a price sheet. Same envelopes. Guess who is still there at renewal.
Record one human fact every call and open with it the next time. Not small talk. Filed, dated, deliberate memory.
People are loyal to being remembered.

3. "MY PRODUCT IS IDENTICAL TO THEIRS. SO I STOPPED SELLING THE PRODUCT."
Envelopes are the definition of a commodity.
Mackay banned the definition of a commodity. He armed his reps with the file and won on price and instead. The company grew to one hundred million dollars a year selling what anyone could buy cheaper.

In a commodity market, the relationship is the product.

4. "SEND THE ARTICLE, NOT THE BROCHURE."

Touch the account with things about them between deals. Ten no-pitch touches buy you the one call that is all pitch.
The brochure sells you. The article proves you listened.
