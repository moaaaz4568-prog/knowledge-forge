GHOSTED AFTER A GREAT SALES CALL? THESE 5 CLAUDE PROMPTS JUST REPLACED THE FOLLOW UP YOU'VE BEEN FUMBling.
<!-- image 1 -->
Jack Blair

1. The Ghost Breaker

"I had a sales call with [name] at [company]. They said [exact words they used] and seemed genuinely interested. Then went silent for [X days]. Write me 3 follow up messages that re-open the conversation. Each one a different angle. Each one under 4 lines. Make them feel like they're missing something. Not like I'm chasing them."

They'll reply. They always do.
<!-- image 2 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

2. The Value Bomb

"My prospect works in [industry]
and their biggest problem is
[specific pain point they mentioned
on the call]. Find me one hyper-
relevant insight, stat, or news
story from the last 7 days I can
send them that makes me look like
the most informed person in their
inbox. Write the message around it.
Keep it under 5 lines."

Stop following up empty handed.
<!-- image 3 -->

@jackblairofficial

3. The Assumption Close

"My prospect said [last thing they said on the call] before going silent. Write me a follow up that assumes we're moving forward and gives them a simple yes or no decision. No begging. No 'just checking in.' Make it confident, direct, and impossible to ignore without responding."

Confidence closes what chasing never will.

4. The Pattern Interrupt

"I've followed up with [name] twice since our call. No response. Write me one final message that breaks the pattern completely. Make it short, slightly unexpected, and end with a question so easy to answer they'd feel rude ignoring it. One shot. Make it count."

The message they never see coming.

5. The Re-Opener

"My prospect went cold 3 weeks ago
after [specific stage of conversation].
Something has changed since then:
[new thing — oil prices, new law,
industry news, their competitor].
Write me a message that uses this
as a natural reason to reach back
out. Make it feel like I'm doing
them a favor. Not following up."

New reason. New conversation.
Same deal back on the table.
