HOW INSTAGRAM WORKS
Instagram's 1 job: show people
content they want to see.
It does this by studying who engages with
your content and finding more people like
them.
The problem? If the wrong people engage
or nobody does, the algorithm gets
confused and stops pushing your content.
THE FIX ISN'T POSTING MORE. IT'S GETTING
THE RIGHT PEOPLE TO ENGAGE FIRST.
<!-- image 2 -->
THE 3-STEP SYSTEM
Three moves that retrain the algorithm around
your ideal audience
Step 1
Bring the right followers to your page first.
Trial reel invitations
Step 2
Make those people comment, giving the algorithm
data.
Keyword callouts
Step 3
Stack niche CTAs
Repeat this across every post to build momentum.
<!-- image 3 -->
STEP 1
TRIAL REEL INVITATIONS:
HAND-PICK YOUR FUTURE
FOLLOWERS

A trial reel is shown to non-followers. Use
it to directly call out your ideal audience,
before they even follow you.
Make 4-5 short reels (under 30 seconds
each) that say clearly:

• Who you are
• What you post about
• Exactly who it's for
<!-- image 4 -->
COPY THIS SCRIPT FOR YOUR TRIAL REEL

"If you're a [your ideal follower] and your biggest goal right now is [their goal] — you need to come to my page. I post specifically about how to do exactly that."

When the right people follow because of this reel, Instagram learns your audience from the very start. Every new follower trains it further.
<!-- image 5 -->
STEP 2
KEYWORD CALLOUTS: MAKE
ENGAGEMENT MEAN SOMETHING.

The algorithm doesn't just see who watches,
it sees who comments. Comments are the
strongest signal of audience identity. Add a
CTA that gets your ideal audience to
comment a specific word.

Example: "Comment GUIDE and I'll send you
the free resource.

Use a tool like Superprofile to auto-DM the
resource when they comment, so the
engagement happens naturally at scale.
<!-- image 6 -->
STEP 3
EVERY POST TRAINS THE
ALGORITHM A LITTLE MORE

Don't use keyword CTAs just once. Add
them to every post you publish.
Each time someone from your ideal
audience comments, it's another data point
telling Instagram: "show this account to
more people like this."

One comment = one vote for your audience.
100 comments = a clear signal the
algorithm acts on.
