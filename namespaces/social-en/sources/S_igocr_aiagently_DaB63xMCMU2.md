Prompt #2
Once you have your niche you need a channel identity. Paste this into Claude:
"My YouTube niche is [your niche from step 1]. Create a complete channel identity for me including a channel name, a one-sentence description of what the channel is about, the type of person who watches it, and the tone I should use in every video. Prompt #3
Now you need 90 days of content so you never run out of ideas.
"Here is my channel identity: [paste the output from step 2]. Give me 30 YouTube video ideas for this channel. For each idea give me the title, a one-line description of what the video covers, and why someone would click on it.
Make the titles feel like they belong on a channel with 500K subscribers."

Prompt #4

Now you need a script for your first video.

"Here is my channel identity: [paste step 2]. Here is my first video idea: [paste step 2]. Write me your chosen title and description from step 3]. Write me a complete YouTube script for this video. It should open with a hook that grabs attention in the first 5 seconds, have a clear middle that delivers real value, and end with a call to action asking people to subscribe. Keep it between 8 and 12 minutes when read aloud at a normal pace."

Prompt #5
A great video with a bad thumbnail gets zero views.
"My video is titled: [your title]. Here is what the video is about: [one sentence summary]. Give me 5 thumbnail concepts for this video. For each one describe the image, the text overlay, the colors, and why it will make someone stop scrolling.
Make them feel like MrBeast-level thumbnails not beginner thumbnails."

Make everything optimized for someone searching for this topic on YouTube in 2026. Copy and paste this exactly when you upload. Do not skip the pinned comment, it drives clicks."
