1. "Before I close the loop, did this drop in priority?"

Direct. Calm. Non-needy.

It gives them an easy out, which makes them want to stay in.

Most buyers reply just to tell you it hasn't dropped.

Now you're back in the conversation without ever sounding desperate.
<!-- image 2 -->

2. "What changed since we last spoke?"

This assumes movement on their end.

Budget shifted? New decision maker?
Competing offer?

You get real information in one message
instead of three weeks of silence.

Every word in this line is doing work.
<!-- image 3 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

3. "Is this a 'not now' or a 'not happening'?"

Binary choices force replies. Open questions don't.

Not now — you have a timeline.

Not happening — you move on and save your time.

Either way you win. Silence is the only loss.
<!-- image 4 -->
Jack Blair

4. "Where does this sit compared to your other priorities?"

This makes them self-rank your deal.

High, they re-engage.

Low, now you know exactly what you're competing with.

You can't close a deal you don't understand.

5. "Did you end up moving forward a different way?"

Nobody expects this question.

It shows confidence. It triggers guilt in the good way.

Dead leads come back on this one more than any other line I've ever used.
