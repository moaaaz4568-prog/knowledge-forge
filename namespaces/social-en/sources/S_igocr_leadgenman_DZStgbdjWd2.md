1. ChatGPT
The All-Rounder

Best for: Creativity, writing, coding, strategy, workflow automation.

Strengths:
• Works with text, image, audio, and code all in one place.
• Remembers context from project to project.
• Highly customizable via GPTs and automation tools.

Keep in mind: On very specific or obscure topics, it may “guess.”

Use it when you need one smart assistant for everything, from writing to planning to coding.

2. Deepseek R1
The Open-Source Chinese AI

Best for: Coding, technical reasoning, and math-heavy tasks.

Strengths:
• Delivers strong reasoning for STEM and programming problems.
• Runs efficiently, it's powerful but much cheaper than most U.S. models.
• Works in both Chinese and English.

Keep in mind: Limited English documentation and community support. Not designed for storytelling or brand writing.

Use it when you need brains on a budget for numbers, logic, or code.

3.
Claude
The Deep Thinker

Best for: Coding, Long-form research, legal writing, contracts, complex analysis, deep reasoning.

Strengths:
• Keep track of very long conversations and huge documents with strong memory.
• Excellent reasoning, structure, and accuracy for professional writing.
• Trusted for sensitive business or legal content because of strong data-safety rules.

Keep in mind: Prioritises safety over speed. Not ideal for open-ended creativity or wild brainstorming.

Use it when you need your AI to think before it speaks.

4.
Grok
The Trend Whisperer
Best for: Tracking real-time social trends, online sentiments, and pop culture.
Strengths:
• Directly connected to X (formerly Twitter), so it reads trends as they happen.
• Has personality, it adds humor and context from social character.
• Great for marketers, journalists, and creators who wants to sense what’s hot.
Keep in mind: Accuracy can be uneven with the bigger models on, and it’s still catching up with complex reasoning.
Use it when you want a pulse on what the internet is thinking.

5. Gemini
The Corporate Brain
Best for: Business and technical writing, SEO, data analysis, and visual-data tasks.
Strengths:
• Handles native multimodality, allowing it to process text, image, audio, and video.
• Integrates seamlessly with Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides.
• Analyses very large dataset using its massive 1-million-token context window.
Keep in mind: Works best inside Google ecosystem. It can sound formal or robotic in creative writing.
Use it when you live in Google Drive and want AI that fits right in.
