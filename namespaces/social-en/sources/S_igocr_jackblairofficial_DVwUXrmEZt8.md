1. The Ideal Buyer Profiler

"I sell [your product] to [your market]. Based on the highest converting customers in this industry, build me a detailed profile of my ideal prospect — their daily frustrations, what triggers them to buy, what makes them hesitate, and exactly where to find them."

Bought leads give you a name and a number.

This tells you exactly who you're looking for and why they'll say yes.

2. The Cold Outreach Message

"Write me a cold outreach message for a [homeowner/business owner/etc.] who has never heard of me. It should open with their problem, not my product. No pitching in the first message. Goal: get a reply. Keep it under 5 sentences."

Most bought leads get the same generic pitch everyone else sends.
This is why they don't pick up.

3. The Referral Activator

"Write me 3 different messages I can send to past clients asking for referrals. Each one should feel natural, remind them of the result they got, and make it easy to say yes. Tone: warm, not transactional."

Your best leads are already in your phone. Most reps never ask. This prompt makes it effortless.

"I want to connect with [job title] in [industry/location] on LinkedIn. Write me a connection request message and a follow-up DM sequence of 3 messages that builds rapport before pitching anything. Goal: get a 15 minute call."
