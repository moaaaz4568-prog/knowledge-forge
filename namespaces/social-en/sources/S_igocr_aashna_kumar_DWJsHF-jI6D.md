WEAK VS STRONG HOOKS
Weak Hooks:
• “In today’s video…”
• “This reel is about…”
• “I wanted to share this…”
• Feels safe, performs badly
Strong Hooks:
• “You’re making this one mistake…”
• “This is why your content is stuck”
• “No one told me this at the start”
