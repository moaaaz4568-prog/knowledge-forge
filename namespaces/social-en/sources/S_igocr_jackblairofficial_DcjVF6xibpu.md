In the late 1880s John Patterson of NCR turned his best seller's pitch into a 450 word script called the Primer.
Selling stopped being a talent and became a text.
What is written can be taught, tested and improved.
What lives in a rep's head dies with their mood.
If the pitch isn't written, it isn't real.

3. HE BUILT THE FIRST SALES SCHOOL.
In 1894 NCR opened the first corporate sales training school, paying agents’ travel, board and a stipend to attend.
Companies trained machinists, not sellers. Patterson bet that selling was a trade with an apprenticeship like any other, and the bet built an empire.
Selling is a trade. Serve the apprenticeship.
