7. The entry-level seat itself.

Here's the cold one. One company's AI booked 41 meetings in a month for about $1,200. The three humans doing it cost $22,000 and booked 38.

Nobody got fired. They just stopped replacing the ones who left. Postings for that job are down 23% in two years.

Here's the part that should land. Look back at that list. Not one of those was closing.

A machine can tee up the call. It cannot sit on the phone with a nervous human and get them to say yes when their own money is on the line. That part is still you, and it's the part that always paid. The CEO of Salesforce just said it out loud: almost nobody is getting hired right now, except the people who can close.

You already do the one thing that survived. You've just been doing it in a seat that's shrinking.
