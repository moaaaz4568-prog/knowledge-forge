This is how a skateboard company ended up taking their 7x ROI up to 56x ROI by making one simple tweak to their marketing structure. Originally, whenever somebody would go to their website, they would put an item in their cart and if they didn't buy, the only thing they could do was run a basic retargeting ad and say, hey, you left this in your cart. But they didn't define the reason the person left it in the cart. The reason these abandoned carts were happening was because there was different sizes for different weights of people for the skateboard. So they switched their retargeting structure. Instead of sending them back to the website and saying, hey, you left this here, now what they did was they had an ad that ran them into a messenger sequence and asked them what was their height and what was their weight and then showed them the precise skateboard that they needed. And they were able to take their advertising ROI from 7x to up to 56x ROI by simply asking and helping people find the exact product that they needed. This is a simple little hack that you could add to your business in less than an hour from now.
