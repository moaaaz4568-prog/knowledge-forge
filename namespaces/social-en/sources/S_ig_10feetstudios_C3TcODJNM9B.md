I always get this question: If you should make a separate agency and personal account on Instagram. People are basically afraid that their personal content will make their agency account look more unprofessional. And I know where that thought is coming from, okay? But people buy from people. It's really weird trying to be sold to by an agency that does have no face, no personal brand, or no human behind it. And you have to keep in mind that the people looking to potentially buy from you are also just humans, man. Like they also have interests, problems, and if you share some of yours, then they might resonate with that more and buy from you instead of just another random agency. But if you're so afraid of posting personal stuff on your business account, then do this. Have a 70 and 30% split on your account, 70% being the business content and 30% personal content, and only post your personal content in your stories. So if somebody comes onto your account, there will be a clean feed of business content, but they also have the ability to build a personal connection with you through your stories, which obviously are not permanent unless you put them in your highlights.
