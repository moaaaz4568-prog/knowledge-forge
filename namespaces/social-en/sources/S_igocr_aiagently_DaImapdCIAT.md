Prompt #1
The Real-World Practice:
Don't explain [CONCEPT].
Put me in a realistic scenario where I need to apply it.
If I make a mistake, don't reveal the answer.
Instead, ask guiding questions that help me identify my own error.
Let me try at least two times before explaining the correct solution.
Continue giving me new scenarios until I can solve them confidently without help.
<!-- image 2 -->
Prompt #2
The 4-Hour Skill Accelerator:
You are an expert coach with only 4 hours to make me functional in [SKILL]. Skip unnecessary theory and avoid long lists. Show me:
The 20% of concepts that deliver 80% of the results.
What I should ignore as a beginner.
A practical learning path in the right order.
One real-world project or exercise that will build usable skills quickly.
The biggest mistakes beginners make and how to avoid them.
Focus only on practical skills that help me become productive as fast as possible.
<!-- image 3 -->
Prompt #3
Personalized Learning Roadmap:
My goal is [GOAL], not just to learn [SKILL].
I want to achieve [SPECIFIC RESULT] within [DEADLINE]. Create a 7-day learning plan tailored to my goal.
I already know [CURRENT SKILLS].
For each day, include:
One task that takes 45 minutes or less
A clear way to measure success
What I should avoid to save time
If the plan won't realistically help me reach my goal, improve it until it does.
<!-- image 4 -->
Prompt #4
The Feynman Learning Test:
I just finished learning [TOPIC].
I’ll explain it as if I’m teaching a 10-year-old.
Interrupt me whenever I:
Use jargon without explaining it
Skip an important reasoning step
Oversimplify something until it becomes inaccurate
After I’m done, identify the weak points in my explanation and explain what they reveal about the gaps in my understanding.
<!-- image 5 -->
Prompt #5
The Knowledge Gap Finder:
I believe I'm proficient in [SKILL].
Test my knowledge by asking 5 deceptively simple questions that reveal whether I truly understand the subject.
After each answer, explain what it says about my strengths and the gaps in my understanding.
Be direct and honest. If my knowledge is superficial, point it out clearly and tell me what I need to improve.
