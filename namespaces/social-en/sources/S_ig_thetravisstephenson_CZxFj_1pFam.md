You wanna know what the biggest difference is between an influencer and a brand? Well, I'm here to tell you. It's the fact that a brand controls the ability to reach their audience. See, an influencer usually uses their influence via social media, which means that they're predicated on an algorithm that somebody else controls. Maybe you make a post and you reach 8% of people, maybe 10, but you're not reaching everybody. A brand takes control of their audience so they can send emails, SMS, and reach out to their audience whenever they want. That gives them predictability in their business and allows them to profit whenever they want. An influencer typically relies on other people to pay them based on some vanity metric of how many people they can reach. A brand takes that audience, takes that reach, and says, I can sell my own thing, I can create for myself, I've just got to get these people off of the platform. The key to going from being an influencer and making what other people are willing to pay you to becoming a brand, which is where you control your own income, is taking control of your audience and getting them off of the platform.
