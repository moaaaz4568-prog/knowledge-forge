1. Make a plan

Sit down for a few hours, no screens, no distractions, and find out what really makes you happy + where you want to be.

Set a main goal & a couple milestones along the way.

If you fail, start over.

Stop spending money on pointless things.
Start thinking: how can this next purchase bring me further?
Common examples:
– Fitness gear
– A nice journal
– Good chair for your desk
– A good book for your growth
– A good pillow
– Nutrition and supplements

It's you, your body, your mind,
and your dream.

Cut off:
- Friends that promote bad
habits
- Negative beliefs
- Partying
- Meaningless relationships
- Doom scrolling

You can't take these with you
on your path.

Leave them behind.

upgrade.

3. Exercise 1 hour per day
4. Read 10 pages per day
5. Dedicate 1 hour towards
your new skill
If you fail, start over.

get it.

yesterday.

TODAY.
