# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1iGCp_vhYw — trying too hard

## Term

Trying too hard is chasing the sale itself instead of the prospect's problem, and the speaker says it is probably why you suck at sales without knowing it. The phrase appears 1 time, beside tactics, frameworks, and a genuine want to help, and his order on a call is strict: figure out what the prospect's problem actually is first, hear them out, and see if you can genuinely help them. You might not have the solution, and that is completely fine, but the genuine want to help comes across and makes a huge difference.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "When you're on a sales call, the most important thing is to first figure out what the prospect's problem actually is." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1iGCp_vhYw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on sales tactics: he concedes there are a lot of tactics and frameworks in sales, and the point is that trying too hard drowns the genuine want to help, not that tactics are useless.
- Read as helping everyone: the help is conditional — first check whether you can genuinely help, and if you cannot, that is completely fine.
