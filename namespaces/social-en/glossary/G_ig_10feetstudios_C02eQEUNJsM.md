# G_ig_10feetstudios_C02eQEUNJsM — referral

## Term

A referral is a new client sent by an existing one, and the speaker treats a good referral system as the layer on top of a solid client acquisition method that can seriously move growth. The word appears 4 times, beside check-in calls, big wins, and affiliates, and his timing rule is exact: ask at the end of a call where you just celebrated a big win, get the client hyped on future goals, then ask. The reward he suggests is substantial — 500 bucks plus 10% of the profit the referral brings every month — so clients turn into affiliates.

## Synonyms

- referrals

## Live Example

> "if you already have a solid client acquisition method in place, having a good referral system on top of that can have a serious impact on your growth." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C02eQEUNJsM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a replacement for acquisition: he is explicit that referrals sit on top of a consistent client acquisition method, and the system only works once that method is in place.
- Read as asking any time: the ask belongs at the end of a check-in call after a big win, not cold, and the reward is what turns a one-off referral into affiliates who bring clients repeatedly.
