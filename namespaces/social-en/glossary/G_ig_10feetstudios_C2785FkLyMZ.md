# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2785FkLyMZ — wrong problems

## Term

Wrong problems are the ones you work on out of order, and the speaker says an agency usually has problems in only three areas: appointments, sales, and service delivery. The phrase appears 1 time, beside three areas, an empty sales calendar, and a target audience, and his order is exact: appointments first, then sales, then service, because booking appointments fills your calendar with sales calls where you learn what your audience actually needs. The people he sees failing work on service delivery before they are past 10k a month, with no appointments scheduled for the upcoming week.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The reason you're not moving forward, and you're not scaling as fast as you'd like to, is probably because you're focusing on the wrong problems" — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C2785FkLyMZ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as ignoring service delivery: delivery is the third problem, not a fake one, and the fix is to improve it from what sales calls teach you, after appointments and sales.
- Read as a diagnosis for beginners only: the order applies even with clients already in place, and having clients does not excuse an empty upcoming week.
