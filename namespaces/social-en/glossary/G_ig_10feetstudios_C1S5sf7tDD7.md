# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1S5sf7tDD7 — niche down

## Term

To niche down is to pick one specific type of person and solve their specific problem, and the speaker makes it the first of three steps to scale past ten k a month. The phrase appears 1 time, beside tangible results, MRR, and cold outreach, and the reason is understanding: the more specifically you target people, the easier it is to sell them, because you understand their problems and desires and speak directly to that. The other two steps follow the same simplicity: deliver tangible results and charge more, then acquire clients.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "First, niche down and provide a specific solution for a specific type of person. The more specifically you're targeting people, the easier it's gonna be to sell them." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1S5sf7tDD7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as shrinking your market: niching down is presented as the way to sell more easily, not as turning away money, and the specific solution is what makes the sale.
- Read as needing the best product market fit: he says outright that for ten k a month you do not need a crazy offer, the best product market fit, or a huge team.
