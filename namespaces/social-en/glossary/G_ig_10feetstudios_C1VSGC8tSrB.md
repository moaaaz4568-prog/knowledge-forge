# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1VSGC8tSrB — client retention

## Term

Client retention is keeping clients longer than three months, and the speaker says without it you can never build a long-term business. The phrase appears 1 time, beside expectations, onboarding, and check-in calls, and his six steps run from setting expectations straight with four to six month agreements, through solid onboarding, a quick win, daily or weekly reports, and bi-weekly check-in calls. The last step overrides the rest: get them results, because if the service sucks, clients leave regardless of everything else.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Here are the six basic steps for perfect client retention. If you do these right, you're gonna be able to retain clients for longer than one and a half years, just like I did." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1VSGC8tSrB.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a retention trick: five of the six steps are process — expectations, onboarding, quick win, reports, check-ins — and the sixth is simply delivering results.
- Read as keeping clients at any cost: the horizon he promises is longer than one and a half years, and the minimum agreement he advises is four to six months, not a lifetime lock-in.
