# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1F66hlN_PY — moving the needle

## Term

Moving the needle means doing the tasks that actually change the numbers, and the speaker contrasts them with the easy tasks people do just to feel better. The phrase appears 1 time, beside booking calls, a website, and a logo, and his rule for anyone below 10k a month is single-minded: book more calls and get more clients, and the only way is sending out. He proves the point on himself: he scaled to 20k a month without a website and still does not have one.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Just focus on the tasks that are actually moving the needle." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1F66hlN_PY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as working nonstop: the needle moves through booking calls, not through hours, and he explicitly bans stopping to build a website or a logo.
- Read as a ban on easy tasks: procrastinating with easy tasks is the problem, not the tasks themselves, and the fix is focusing, not grinding everything.
