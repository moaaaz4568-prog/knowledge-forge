# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-SsWqKt5Zf — long term

## Term

The long term is the speaker's answer to feeling behind in life: stop measuring yourself against revenue numbers shouted online and play for years, not months. The phrase appears 2 times, beside unsustainable businesses, millionaires in your 20s, and Warren Buffett becoming a millionaire at 30, and he notes that fast builders usually disappear within five years. His own friends were ahead of him when he started and are not anymore, because they did not focus on the long term.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So if you feel like you're behind just because some people are shouting revenue numbers around and are building unsustainable businesses, then don't be." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-SsWqKt5Zf.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that online success is fake: he concedes there are some killers in this game, so the claim is that most displayed revenue is distorted, not that all of it is.
- Read as permission to move slowly: playing the long term game still means building, and his friends lost their lead because they stopped, not because they went fast.
