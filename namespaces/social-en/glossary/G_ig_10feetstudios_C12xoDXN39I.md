# G_ig_10feetstudios_C12xoDXN39I — appointments

## Term

Appointments are the first letter of his ASS framework — appointments, sales, and service — and the rule is to fill an empty sales calendar with them before anything else. The word appears 3 times, beside outreach, ads, and 693 emails, and his own proof is concrete: he sent 693 emails and closed 31 clients, 17 of them high ticket, with no need to overcomplicate it. Only once appointments exist does the focus shift to sales, and only after closing does service begin, because the offer itself is shaped by what people say on sales calls.
