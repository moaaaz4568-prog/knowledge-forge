# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1LRoiDo8m1 — report

## Term

A report is the daily or weekly update sent to clients showing what got done and what numbers were hit, and the speaker calls it a change worth thousands of dollars. The word appears 2 times, beside retention, Slack, and subscribers gained, and his setup is a report channel in Slack where clients get a daily report with the most important metrics. The effect is trust: clients know that shit is getting done, and without the reports he says you are losing money.

> "The way I do it is we have a report channel in Slack and they will get sent a daily report with the most important metrics like abuse generator, subscribers gained and sales mate." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1LRoiDo8m1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a monthly summary: the cadence he prescribes is daily or weekly, and the value is that clients see progress constantly, not at the end of a month.
- Read as busywork for the owner: he says ideally your assistant handles it, and the report exists to prove work is happening, not to fill the owner's day.
