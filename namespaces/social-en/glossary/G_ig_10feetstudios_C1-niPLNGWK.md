# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1-niPLNGWK — funnel

## Term

A funnel is the journey a client's audience travels from seeing content to becoming paying customers, and the speaker says content agencies should build it out for their clients. The word appears 2 times, beside saturated offer, long form videos, and DMs, and the example path runs from organic and paid content to followers, then nurturing through long form videos, outreach via DMs, booked appointments, and selling on the call. Helping in more parts of that journey is what makes the agency's value tangible and the offer easier to sell.

> "And I think about how you can assist your clients in more parts of this funnel, either by consulting them or doing it for them." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1-niPLNGWK.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as content alone: content is usually just a way to get traffic and eyeballs, and the funnel only starts working once the audience is nurtured and booked.
- Read as a generic marketing term: the funnel here is specific to the client's business — coaches and consultants turning followers into customers — not a diagram for its own sake.
