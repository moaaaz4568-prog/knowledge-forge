# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-AkpCsNxM- — sales

## Term

Sales here is the work of the sales call itself, and the speaker's point is that the caller's motive shows: chasing money off the person in front of you reads as extraction, while trying to help reads as care. The word appears 4 times, beside sales calls, objections, and being pushy, and he argues that believing in your product makes pushiness a responsibility rather than a pressure tactic. The test he gives is simple: get on the call wanting to figure out if you can help that person, and only then how much that help would be worth.

## Synonyms

- sales call
- sales calls

## Live Example

> "You need to shift your perspective on sales." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-AkpCsNxM-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on being pushy: he explicitly defends being pushy when you know you can really help, and says the pushiness is felt as the right reasons.
- Read as a script for closing: the shift he teaches is about motive on the call, not a set of lines, and he offers no word-for-word pitch.
