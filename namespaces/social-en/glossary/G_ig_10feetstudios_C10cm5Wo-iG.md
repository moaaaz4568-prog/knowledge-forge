# G_ig_10feetstudios_C10cm5Wo-iG — personal brand

## Term

A personal brand is content posted as a person rather than as a faceless agency account, and the speaker says it beats a business account because people buy from people. The phrase appears 3 times, beside Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos, and ideal clients, and his rule is that even a personal brand should spend at least 70% of its content giving clients value rather than talking about itself. The reason is simple: nobody follows an agency account just to hear about its work, and nobody cares about you until the content helps them.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "it's way better to have a personal brand than a business account, because people buy from people." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C10cm5Wo-iG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as becoming famous: the biggest companies have faces behind them, but the point is trust that converts followers into clients, not celebrity.
- Read as posting about yourself: he caps self-talk at 30% of content at most, and the rest must give ideal clients value so they engage and follow.
