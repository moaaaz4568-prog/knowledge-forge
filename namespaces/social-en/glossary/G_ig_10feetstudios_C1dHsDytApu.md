# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1dHsDytApu — Loom outreach

## Term

Loom outreach is personalized video outreach recorded with Loom, and the speaker preaches it as the way to scale an agency past 10k a month. The phrase appears 1 time, beside a two-minute sales pitch, value first, and trust, and the crucial mistake he corrects is sending looms that are literally just a two-minute sales pitch. A real Loom provides genuinely good value first, gives a reason to watch, builds just a tiny bit of trust, and leaves the competition in the dirt with a little more effort.
