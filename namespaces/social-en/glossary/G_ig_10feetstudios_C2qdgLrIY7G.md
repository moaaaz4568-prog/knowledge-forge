# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2qdgLrIY7G — buy from you

## Term

To buy from you means clients choose you as a person — your story, your traits, your appearance — instead of comparing your features, price, and guarantee against other agencies. The phrase appears 2 times, beside a personal brand, features, and the hard way, and the speaker's point is that without a brand you compete on exhausting comparisons, while with one the competition disappears because nobody else is you. His own examples are blunt: people bought because they liked how direct and open he speaks, and even because he produced music before running an agency.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "the big change that comes from having a personal brand is that people start to buy from you because of you, because of your story, because of you as a person, because of your traits, your appearance, everything." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C2qdgLrIY7G.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as personal branding being required: he scaled to 20k a month without a website or an Instagram page, so the brand is the easier route, not the only route.
- Read as selling personality instead of service: the brand changes why people buy, and the service, price, and guarantee still exist behind it.
