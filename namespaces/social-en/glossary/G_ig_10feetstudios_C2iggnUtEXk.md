# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2iggnUtEXk — move quick

## Term

Move quick means acting on an idea the same day instead of waiting, and the speaker treats it as the one thing the agency space taught him. The phrase appears 1 time, beside ideas, plans, and goals, and his examples are all immediate: have an idea, implement it the same day; get clients, write a script and send hundreds of them in the same week; have a budget for ads, do that right now. The warning is dated: the first month of 2024 is already over and you are still in the same spot you were in last year.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as rushing without a plan: the speed he demands is on implementation — scripts sent, ads tested — and the perfect ad creative is something you test for, not something you wait for.
- Read as motivation only: the video names concrete same-day actions, and doing just enough is explicitly called out as the failure.
