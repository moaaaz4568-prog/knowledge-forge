# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2a-hPDthBr — onboarding call

## Term

An onboarding call is the first call with a new client after they sign, and here it appears as one item in a day-in-the-life vlog, squeezed between a trip to Amsterdam and an evening concert. The phrase appears 1 time, beside a five star hotel, a package shop, and checking in with the team, and the day around it is ordinary work: waking at 5 a.m., gym, recording content, client stuff, and writing more content. The vlog has no business lesson attached to the call itself; it is simply part of a working day that ends at a hotel in Amsterdam.
