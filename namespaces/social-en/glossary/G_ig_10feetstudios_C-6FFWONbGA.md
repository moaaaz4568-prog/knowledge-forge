# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-6FFWONbGA — dropped out

## Term

To drop out here means to quit college deliberately before finishing, and the speaker tells it as his own story of leaving to build an agency online. The phrase appears 4 times, beside hardest decisions, exams, and making hundreds of thousands of dollars online, and he stresses that he dropped out without telling anybody, not his friends, not his family, not his girl. The decision is framed as the hard choice that comes before the payoff: two months of agency work, then around 40,000 a month, and only then telling everybody.

## Synonyms

- dropping out

## Live Example

> "So you know what I did? I dropped out anyway and didn't tell anybody." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-6FFWONbGA.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to quit school on impulse: he studied for all his exams and was prepared to crush them, so the exit was a calculated refusal to stay, not a failure to cope.
- Read as a promise that dropping out makes money: the income came after two months of agency work in lecture-free time, not from the act of leaving itself.
