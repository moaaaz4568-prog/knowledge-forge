# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-0tmu9O4Zq — agency model

## Term

An agency model here is the way an agency packages and sells its service, and the speaker contrasts two of them: the classic agency model most people use and his new secret agency model. The phrase appears 3 times, beside high churn, low margins, and ten bosses, and the classic version is blamed for clients feeling like bosses and fulfillment becoming a pain. The new model is presented as the fix for all of those problems, with the full breakdown offered to anyone who comments the word secret.

## Synonyms

- secret agency model

## Live Example

> "This is the new agency model that will make you more money than you know what to do." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-0tmu9O4Zq.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee of income: the new agency model is pitched as making more money, but the source gives no earnings proof beyond the pitch itself.
- Read as a critique of every agency: the problems named — churn, low margins, painful fulfillment — describe the classic model, not the new one he is selling.
