# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1IXMtzLUds — daily inputs

## Term

Daily inputs are the exact numbers you must hit every single day to reach a goal — how much outreach to send, how many calls to book a week, how many to close at what price. The phrase appears 1 time, beside goals, a million bucks, and team members, and the speaker's method is to put another zero behind a 5 or 10k goal and calculate the inputs that way. A goal without that calculation is not a goal, he says, it is a dream, and no goal is really too hot once the inputs make it possible first.

## Synonyms

- inputs

## Live Example

> "If you break all of this down into daily inputs, every goal becomes achievable." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1IXMtzLUds.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as setting bigger goals for motivation: the bigger number is only the start, and the work is calculating the exact daily inputs behind it.
- Read as a planning template: the inputs are specific to your offer — outreach volume, call bookings, close rate, price point, team — not a generic daily checklist.
