# G_ig_10feetstudios_C25rissLKn5 — ROI

## Term

ROI is the return a client gets on what they pay you, and the speaker makes a very clear one the first of two changes that took him from 250 bucks a month to 5k. The word appears 1 time, beside a funnel, bigger packages, and a two-way split, and the test is simple: if you are making your clients 50k, they will be happy to pay you five. The method is to break down the client's funnel and help not only at the top, by getting attention, but in the middle and at the bottom, by nurturing and converting that attention into cash.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "First, make sure that your service has a very clear ROI. If you're making your clients 50k, they're going to be happy to pay you fine." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C25rissLKn5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of 50k: the number is an example of what clear ROI looks like, not a guarantee attached to the service.
- Read as a pricing formula: the ROI comes from working more of the funnel, and the second change — bigger packages — is what actually raises the price.
