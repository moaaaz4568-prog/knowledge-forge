# G_ig_10feetstudios_C0pvrdoNvQK — quick win

## Term

A quick win is an early result delivered fast enough that the client feels the investment was right, and the speaker treats it as the lever for client retention. The phrase appears 2 times, beside expectations, quick sales, and a quick viral video, and the method is to say results take time while secretly concentrating on the fastest visible payoff. His content-agency example is concrete: if the client sells a course, first publish the piece that gets quick sales for it, or start with the formats that have proven to perform for other clients.

## Live Example

> "When signing on new clients, you need to communicate that results take time and you know, set the expectations straight. But in reality, you will concentrate on getting them a quick win as soon as possible." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C0pvrdoNvQK.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as lying to clients: he tells them results take time and then works for a fast result, which is sequencing, not a false promise about timelines.
- Read as a trick for any service: the quick win depends on the service, and he says plainly that if the version for you is not obvious, you have to get creative.
