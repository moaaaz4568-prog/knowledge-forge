# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1sPMNirku8 — curiosity

## Term

Curiosity is the third step of a perfect outreach message: hold back the price and the service so the prospect has to get on a call to learn them. The word appears 3 times, beside personalization, value and video, and break the pattern, and the mechanism is simple: when prospects ask about price or service in the messages, do not just let them know, because the curiosity is what books the call. A Loom covers all four steps at once when done right, because it feels personalized, delivers value in video, withholds the full service, and breaks the pattern.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If your prospects respond and ask about something like your price or your service, don't just let them know in the messages. Because now you got them curious and the best way to get them on a call is through curiosity." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1sPMNirku8.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as withholding information to trick people: the curiosity follows genuine value already provided, and the call is where the service is explained, not a bait-and-switch.
- Read as a subject-line trick: the step sits inside a four-part message — personalization, value in video, curiosity, pattern-break — and alone it books nothing.
