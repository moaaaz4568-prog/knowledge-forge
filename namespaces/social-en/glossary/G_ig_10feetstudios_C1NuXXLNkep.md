# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1NuXXLNkep — outsourcing

## Term

Outsourcing is handing service delivery to hired people once you know how to deliver a good service yourself, and the speaker says there is no revenue number to hit first. The word appears 3 times, beside margins, hiring, and new clients, and the timing rule is two-sided: not before you can teach the people you hire, and not a moment later, or you get stuck in your business. The trade he names is explicit: margins decrease, but the time freed to get new clients hugely increases.

## Synonyms

- outsource

## Live Example

> "Your margins will decrease when hiring and outsourcing, but your time to get new clients will hugely increase." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1NuXXLNkep.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as hiring to escape work: the trigger is knowing how to deliver and teach it, and outsourcing before that point is the mistake he warns against.
- Read as a pure cost saving: he admits outsourcing costs a couple thousand bucks, and the payoff is the money you generate by focusing on new clients, not a cheaper service.
