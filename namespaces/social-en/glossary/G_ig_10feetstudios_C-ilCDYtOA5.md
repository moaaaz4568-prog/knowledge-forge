# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-ilCDYtOA5 — niche

## Term

A niche is the client group a content agency serves, and the speaker's rule is to pick one you genuinely like and know from experience, then obsess over its clients. The word appears 6 times, beside online business coaches, sports coaches, and content agencies, and his example is a client who hated business coaches, found them scammy, and now loves working with sports coaches because he shares their world. The guardrails he adds are practical: the niche must not be broke, and you must actually solve a real problem for it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Look, the goal is to stick to your niche, obsess over your clients, and ultimately get to the point where you possibly know more about your clients than they know about themselves." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-ilCDYtOA5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as picking the richest market: he ranks genuine interest and past experience above money, and only then checks that the niche is not broke.
- Read as a one-time choice: the goal he states is to stick to your niche and keep learning its clients until you know more about them than they know about themselves.
