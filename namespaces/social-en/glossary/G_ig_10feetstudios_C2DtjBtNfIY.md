# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2DtjBtNfIY — tangible results

## Term

Tangible results are the concrete outcomes you get for a client — attention turned into cash — and the speaker says they are what makes you more than another replaceable creator. The phrase appears 1 time, beside streamlining service delivery, consulting, and qualified prospects, and the move is to implement more consulting alongside the content, so clients see how attention becomes money. The rest of the system follows: run ads, put effort into your brand, get qualified prospects coming to you, and nurture them with content until they are warm and easy to sell.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Just nurture them with a little bit of content and they will be warm and easy to sell because they already know and trust you." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C2DtjBtNfIY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as views alone: attention is only the start, and the tangible part is showing clients how views turn into cash.
- Read as a rebrand: the change is in the service — more consulting, streamlined delivery — not in calling the same work by a new name.
