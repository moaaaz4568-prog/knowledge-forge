# G_ig_10feetstudios_C0ux6SbNro1 — assistant

## Term

An assistant is the first hire that gets an agency owner out of doing all the service delivery, and the speaker insists on hiring the assistant before the editor. The word appears 7 times, beside Upwork, Philippines, and test edits, and the logic is practical: the assistant hires the editor for you, collecting test edits into a Google sheet while you only mark them decline or maybe. The hiring spec he gives is exact: executive virtual assistant, six to ten dollars an hour, Philippines, and a 30 to 60 second video with every application.
