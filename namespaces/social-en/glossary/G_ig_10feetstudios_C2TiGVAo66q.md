# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2TiGVAo66q — profitable

## Term

Profitable is the question a prospect asks when they want to know how long until the investment pays off, and the speaker admits he once had no idea how to answer it. The word appears 1 time, beside ROI, investment, and more videos, and his failed answer was to promise more videos, more attention, and maybe more business, which the prospect accepted as logical and then pressed further. The lesson he draws is to look at what goes in against what comes out: how much money the client actually invests, and how much they get back, including the person they invest into.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So how long does it take for us to be profitable? And at that time, I also had no idea how to answer that question." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C2TiGVAo66q.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of quick returns: he never gives a timeline, and the point is that he could not answer the question, not that profitability takes a fixed number of weeks.
- Read as a pricing objection: the prospect was looking to make an investment, not haggling, and the gap was the speaker's inability to frame the return.
