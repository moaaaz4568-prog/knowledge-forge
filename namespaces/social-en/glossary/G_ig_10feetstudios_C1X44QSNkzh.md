# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1X44QSNkzh — response rate

## Term

Response rate is the share of outreach messages that get a positive reply, and the speaker uses it to price the cost of a random account with no personal brand. The phrase appears 1 time, beside a hundred DMs, a call book rate, and a close rate, and his math is exact: a hundred DMs at three percent gives three interested people and one booked call, while ten percent gives ten replies and three calls, and at a thirty-three percent close rate that is the difference between zero and two k a month. The optimized account builds trust and gives value, which is what lifts the rate.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "you have a positive response rate of about three percent, and mine is at ten because of the optimized account." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1X44QSNkzh.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a copywriting metric: the rate here is driven by the account itself — personal brand, testimonials, client results — not by the wording of one DM.
- Read as a small gap: three percent against ten percent looks close, but through call booking and closing it compounds into zero versus two k a month.
