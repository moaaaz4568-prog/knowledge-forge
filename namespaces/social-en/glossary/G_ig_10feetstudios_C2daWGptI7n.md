# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2daWGptI7n — irresistible

## Term

Irresistible is the opposite of replaceable: an agency clients cannot swap for a cheaper one because it owns more of their money-making funnel. The word appears 1 time, beside replaceable, better retention, and tangible results, and the speaker's diagnosis is that agencies try to differentiate inside one box — better edits, better retention, faster turnaround, maybe cheaper — while only offering the organic-content part of the funnel. The fix is to map the process the client's customers go through from watching content to buying, learn more parts of it, and consult on it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a branding slogan: irresistible here is earned by owning more of the funnel and delivering tangible results, not by a bolder promise.
- Read as abandoning editing: the harder path still includes the content work, and the choice is between learning the funnel or staying a mediocre content agency.
