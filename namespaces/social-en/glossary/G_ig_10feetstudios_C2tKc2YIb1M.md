# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2tKc2YIb1M — leverage

## Term

Leverage is how much client growth you get per unit of effort, and the speaker says cold outreach does not have enough of it past ten k a month. The word appears 2 times, beside linear, running ads, and a proven offer, and the contrast is exact: outreach is very linear — more time in, more clients out — while ads scale, a dollar in and four dollars out, so five k of ad spend can take you to twenty k a month. The catch is that ads need a proven offer and messaging first, so outreach still comes first to learn the service and the pain point.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as abandoning outreach: he still calls very good outreach one of the fastest ways to get clients quickly, and the sequencing is outreach first, ads after.
- Read as a guaranteed four-to-one return: the dollar-in-four-out math assumes you are profitable with ads, and that requires a proven offer and messaging.
