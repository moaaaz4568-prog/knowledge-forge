# G_ig_10feetstudios_C0sQkP0NzKU — procrastination

## Term

Procrastination here is the agency owner's inability to work more than 30 minutes on a task, and the speaker admits he struggled with it himself just a few months ago. The word appears 2 times, beside a vision, comfort, and a 50 or 20k a month goal, and his whole cure is one thing: a vision you want badly enough, felt by closing your eyes and picturing the goal, your mom's face, and providing for your family. The test is the image of all of it gone — dying without ever living those moments, with no second chance.

## Synonyms

- procrastinate

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a time-management technique: he offers no schedule or app, only a vision, and says the problem is either having none or not wanting it badly enough.
- Read as motivation to grind forever: the vision exercise ends with picturing a family provided for and children raised in safety, not with working without stopping.
