# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-YNM-iNRAW — client-obsessed

## Term

Client-obsessed describes the agencies that win by putting the client experience first, even when building the product is painful and slow. The phrase appears 1 time, beside Charlie Morgan, seven and eight-figure entrepreneurs, and Toyotas, and the speaker contrasts it with agency owners who pick the easiest offer to fulfill and stay systemized and affordable. The Bugatti image makes the point concrete: the harder a product is to build, the easier it is to sell, because nobody is competing with you.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The most successful ones had one thing in common, and that was that they were all client-obsessed." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-YNM-iNRAW.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a call to serve everyone: the client-obsessed agencies he praises serve a narrow few deeply, while the Toyotas try to work with virtually everyone.
- Read as a pricing trick: the higher price follows a product that is actually worth it, and he never claims obsession alone justifies charging more.
