# G_ig_10feetstudios_C0xWQKbN6j0 — vehicle

## Term

A vehicle is what content is: not the product itself, but the means that carries a client to the outcome they actually want, usually more revenue or more clients. The word appears 2 times, beside ideation, scripting, and editing, and the sales rule follows directly: sell the outcome, deliver what they need, and never open a sales call by counting videos or scripts. Once the client sees you as the expert who knows exactly how to reach their goal, they stop questioning whether you do 30 videos instead of 15 or charge 3500 instead of 500.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But content is just a vehicle to get to their goal. Don't sell the vehicle, sell the outcome." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C0xWQKbN6j0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as content being worthless: the vehicle still has to be built — ideation, scripting, and editing are what you deliver — it is only the pitch that must not lead with it.
- Read as a pricing excuse: charging 3500 against a 500-buck fiber editor is justified by expertise and the outcome, not by the vehicle label itself.
