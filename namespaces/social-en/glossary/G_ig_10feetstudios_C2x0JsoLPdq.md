# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2x0JsoLPdq — consulting aspect

## Term

A consulting aspect is advising clients on their funnel instead of only doing the work for them, and the speaker presents it as the quickest way to scale an agency to twenty k a month. The phrase appears 1 time, beside a proven offer, a dollar in, and outreach, and the offer behind it is specific: base it on something proven to work with something unique, learn how the client converts attention into cash, and help along that funnel either by doing it or consulting on it. The consulting adds huge value without much extra workload, and the second need is simply that people see the offer — through ads once there is cash flow, or outreach first.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Adding a consulting aspect to your agency can add huge value for your clients without really adding much workload for you and your team." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C2x0JsoLPdq.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as upselling advice: the consulting is part of the offer itself, and it is what makes the offer worth more than just selling content.
- Read as a replacement for delivery: the agency still does the work along the funnel, and consulting is added alongside it, not instead of it.
