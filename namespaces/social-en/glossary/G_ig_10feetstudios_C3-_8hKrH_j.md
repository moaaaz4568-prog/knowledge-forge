# G_ig_10feetstudios_C3-_8hKrH_j — product-obsessed

## Term

Product-obsessed describes agency millionaires who make sure they have the best product and service possible and that clients get amazing results. The phrase appears 1 time, beside agency millionaires, amazing results, and a long-term business, and the contrast is with new owners who think about what service to offer, who to sell to, and how much money to make, without caring about their clients at all. The reframe he offers is to ask how to provide so much value that people are ready to pay 10k, instead of how to make 10k a month, and to change something right now if clients at 5, 10, or 15k are getting no results.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The trait I'm talking about is that they all were super product-obsessed." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C3-_8hKrH_j.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as perfectionism: the obsession is measured by client results, and a shitty service that brings clients no revenue is the failure, not an imperfect one.
- Read as a trait you are born with: the millionaires share it as a practice — best product, amazing results — and the test is whether your clients' revenue exceeds what they spend.
