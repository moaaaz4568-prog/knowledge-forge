# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1lDk0NIDdr — service delivery

## Term

Service delivery is the actual work done for clients, and the speaker's fix for outsourcing it is to teach it once, properly, in a course. The phrase appears 2 times, beside a good team, intensive feedback, and daily calls, and the method is unglamorous: sit down for a whole day, build a super detailed, super in-depth course, and then almost everybody who watches it can deliver the service with very minimal margins for errors or mistakes. Combine that with intensive feedback and daily calls for the first few days, and you are set.
