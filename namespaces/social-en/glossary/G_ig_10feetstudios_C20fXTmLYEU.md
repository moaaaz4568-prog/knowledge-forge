# G_ig_10feetstudios_C20fXTmLYEU — lifeblood

## Term

Lifeblood is what sales and marketing are to a business: stop them and the business dies, however happy the current clients are. The word appears 1 time, beside client acquisition, a financial goal, and hiring, and the mistake he names is stopping acquisition the moment you hit a goal like 10k a month to focus only on keeping clients happy. The fix is to hire somebody who fully focuses on client results, while your job as the business owner stays making the business grow.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "sales and marketing is kind of the lifeblood of your business. So you can't really stop that for long periods of time or anytime, really." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C20fXTmLYEU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as neglecting clients: the clients still get results, but through a hire, and the owner's job is growth, so the two jobs are split rather than one dropped.
- Read as doing sales forever yourself: the lifeblood must keep flowing, but he explicitly says to hire for the delivery side so you can keep the growth side alive.
