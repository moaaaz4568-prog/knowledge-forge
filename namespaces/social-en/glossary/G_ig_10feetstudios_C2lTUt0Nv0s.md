# G_ig_10feetstudios_C2lTUt0Nv0s — brand authority

## Term

Brand authority is being a brand people know and trust, and the speaker names it as the second of two tweaks that took his agency from 5k to 20k a month in two months. The phrase appears 1 time, beside low ticket, oversaturated, and a generic offer, and the first tweak pairs with it: stop charging low ticket with a bad generic offer and build one actually worth at least 3k a month. The authority itself is built by creating and posting content on Instagram and YouTube, which is what makes an oversaturated market stop mattering.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as fame for its own sake: the authority is built to make price competition irrelevant, and it comes from content people know and trust, not from followers alone.
- Read as a quick fix: the offer must first be worth 3k a month, with more value and new things to learn, and the brand work only pays off on top of that.
