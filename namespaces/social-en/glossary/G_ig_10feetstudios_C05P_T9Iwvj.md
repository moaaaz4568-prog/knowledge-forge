# G_ig_10feetstudios_C05P_T9Iwvj — onboarding

## Term

Onboarding is the stretch between signing a client and delivering work, and the speaker calls it the weird thing between client acquisition and client retention that nobody talks about. The word appears 5 times, beside cold feet, Slack, and a strategy call, and his contrast is sharp: the bad version signs the contract, collects the cash, and leaves the client waiting a week or two in silence, while the solid version books a strategy call within two days, adds them to Slack at once, and plays a welcome video. The first update lands within three days, because a client who paid three grand upfront and hears nothing for two weeks starts to doubt the purchase.

## Synonyms

- onboarding process

## Live Example

> "Did you ever have some client say yes on a sales call and you might have even collected some cash, but just after a week or so they got cold feet and wanted to back out? That might have been your shitty onboarding process at fault." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C05P_T9Iwvj.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as paperwork after the sale: his onboarding is a premium feeling sequence — strategy call, Slack, welcome video, first update in three days — not just signing a contract and collecting cash.
- Read as a fix for bad clients: cold feet after a week is blamed on the shitty onboarding process, not on the client changing their mind for no reason.
