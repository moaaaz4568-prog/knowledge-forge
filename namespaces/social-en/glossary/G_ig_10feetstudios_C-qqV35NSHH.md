# G_ig_10feetstudios_C-qqV35NSHH — purpose

## Term

Purpose is the thing you love doing that makes carrying a business feel light instead of like hauling a huge rock uphill, and the speaker admits it is so fucking hard to find. The word appears 1 time, beside making money fast, going through a whole bunch of shit, and reflecting, and his warning runs both ways: do not wait for purpose to come to you, and do not stay in a business you do not love without asking what else would fulfill you more. The path he describes is to do the things you do not like doing until the thing you love doing surfaces.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You need to find and work on something that you actually love doing. You need to find some kind of purpose." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C-qqV35NSHH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that purpose arrives quickly: he says outright you will not find it right away, and the finding comes only after going through work you dislike.
- Read as permission to quit a profitable business at once: a business that makes money but brings no love is fine for now, and the move he prescribes is steady reflection, not an immediate exit.
