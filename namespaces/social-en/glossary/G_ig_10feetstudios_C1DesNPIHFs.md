# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1DesNPIHFs — repurposing

## Term

Repurposing is the service of re-cutting a client's content into new pieces, and the speaker uses it as the dated offer that proves the market moves on. The word appears 1 time, beside original content, basic editing, and a decent retainer, and the timeline he gives is brutal: a year ago you could sell repurposing with very basic editing, now that shit does not fly anymore, and the editing level has to be much higher than just three months ago. The lesson is the title warning: change and adapt and improve, or your agency might crumble down in a couple of months.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "just about a year ago, you could sell repurposing content with very basic editing for a decent retainer. Nowadays, that shit doesn't fly anymore." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1DesNPIHFs.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as repurposing being dead everywhere: the claim is about selling it as a standalone offer at a decent retainer, not about reusing content in general.
- Read as a call to chase every trend: the change he demands is in the acquisition method and the offer, not in abandoning the agency each time the market shifts.
