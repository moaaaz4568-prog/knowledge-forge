# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1pxboDr2ko — KPIs

## Term

KPIs are the numbers that tell you whether your business is on track, and the speaker's rule is to track them or else you decide from emotion. The word appears 2 times, beside facts and data, daily reports, and a VA, and his example is exact: he felt his outreach was not working and nearly changed his whole client acquisition method, but the data showed decent responses and a failure to book calls, so he changed the sequence after the first response instead. The fix for not tracking it yourself is a VA who sends daily reports on whether the KPIs look good.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as tracking for its own sake: the numbers exist to locate the break — responses fine, bookings broken — and the decision that follows is specific, not a dashboard habit.
- Read as a gut-check replacement: feelings are not banned, they are checked, and the outreach felt broken while the data proved the emails were fine.
