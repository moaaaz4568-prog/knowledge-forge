# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1wQjzQNBrU — conversion strategy

## Term

A conversion strategy is the plan that turns the attention content wins into actual clients and money, and the speaker makes it the bridge between editing and charging more. The phrase appears 1 time, beside tangible results, a vehicle, and personalized outreach, and the choice he gives is direct: implement the strategy for your clients, or consult them on doing it themselves. The rest of the video repeats the simple three-step process — niche down, deliver tangible results, acquire clients — and the conversion work is what makes the middle step real.
