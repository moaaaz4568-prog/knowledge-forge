# G_ig_10feetstudios_C177RzzNgfY — fail fast

## Term

Fail fast means to stop overthinking and move quickly — send the outreach message, run the ads, spend the money, take the risk — and learn from the result instead of playing it safe. The phrase appears 1 time, beside overthink, learn quick, and level up, and he applies it directly to agency owners who stay comfortable making five figures a month while losing respect for themselves. The limit he draws is clear: not spending all your cash flow or savings on stupid shit that does not move you forward, but refusing to stay in the same position.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Don't overthink, fail fast, and learn quick." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C177RzzNgfY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as reckless spending: he explicitly excludes blowing cash flow or savings on things that do not move you forward, so the risk is bounded by movement, not by size.
- Read as a slogan for beginners only: the lesson is framed as the biggest one he learned in 2023, aimed at owners already earning five figures who got comfortable.
