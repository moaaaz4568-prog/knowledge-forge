# G_ig_10feetstudios_C1alfhrtrjO — cash flow

## Term

Cash flow is the money coming in that lets you run ads toward a personal brand, and the speaker treats it as the precondition for content-first growth. The phrase appears 2 times, beside five k a month, outreach, and two huge mistakes, and his sequencing is strict: below five k a month, spend most of your time on outreach to get the first clients and cash flow in, because most personal brands you see run ads and that needs a budget. The second mistake is separate: a personal brand that is not actually yours, acting like somebody you are not.

## Live Example

> "So if you're still below five k a month, spend most of your time on outreach to actually get those first initial clients and get some cash flow in." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C1alfhrtrjO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as profit: cash flow here is the incoming money that funds ads and a budget, and he never claims the agency is profitable at five k a month.
- Read as a reason to avoid content entirely: the rule is about where most of your time goes below five k, not a ban on creating content at all.
