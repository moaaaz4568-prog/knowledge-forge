# G_ig_10feetstudios_C31CCiJtA6x — streamlined service delivery

## Term

Streamlined service delivery is running delivery through systems so the agency is not capped at a couple of clients, and the speaker lists it as one of three needs for scaling from five to twenty k a month. The phrase appears 1 time, beside a good offer, attention, and running ads, and the other two needs frame it: a good offer that brings clients more money than they spend, and attention on that offer, arguably best through ads and growing an Instagram following. Streamlining means putting systems in place so you can focus on getting more clients instead of being stuck in your business.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "then you also need attention on that offer and a streamlined service delivery so you're not just capped at like two clients." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C31CCiJtA6x.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as hiring a big team: streamlining is systems that free the owner to get clients, not headcount, and the cap it removes is two clients, not zero.
- Read as a shortcut past the offer: the delivery only matters once the offer brings clients more money than they spend, and a weak offer goes back to the drawing board first.
