They give you this little bit of unexpected joy and in the process make you feel like humans are actually pretty cute. I know some people are super cynical about this stuff and think, oh, this is just more capitalist crap. But to me, there's something so pure and genuine about creativity." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_CsYlkT3IBw-.md

And every time I see one of these barcodes, I'm reminded of what it felt like to be a child and have that genuine curiosity and sense of play imbued in everything I did." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_CsYlkT3IBw-.md
