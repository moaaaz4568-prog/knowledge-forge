# T_DGoCc5CuMph_01

"Here are three words you should never say on your sales calls. Number one: 'To be honest.' When you say that, it implies that you've been lying up until this point. Instead, say: 'Let's think of it this way.' Number two: 'Are you ready to buy?' Never say the word 'buy.' You want them to think of this as an investment rather than a purchase." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DGoCc5CuMph.md
