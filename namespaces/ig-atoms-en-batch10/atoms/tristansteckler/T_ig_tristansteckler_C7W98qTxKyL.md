# T_C7W98qTxKyL_01

"It is not salesy to handle objections. Think of objections as problems that your prospect is dealing with. The first thing we have to do before we can actually handle objections is get certainty from our prospect that what we do is actually going to get them to their goal. If they tell us yes, we now have permission to handle the problems they're struggling with." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_C7W98qTxKyL.md

# T_C7W98qTxKyL_02

"It could be fear, money, or logistics. It's our job to uncover what is preventing our prospect from moving forward. And listen—if you've ever been a student athlete or had a football coach, did they make you do a lot of things you didn't want to do at the time? Did the challenging and pushing you actually help you become a better person? It's the same in sales." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_C7W98qTxKyL.md

# T_C7W98qTxKyL_03

"If the person you were talking to already had the knowledge and skills to solve their problem, they wouldn't be talking to you in the first place. So roll up your sleeves, get in the trenches with your prospect, and help them overcome their problem. Get the sale and follow for more value." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_C7W98qTxKyL.md
