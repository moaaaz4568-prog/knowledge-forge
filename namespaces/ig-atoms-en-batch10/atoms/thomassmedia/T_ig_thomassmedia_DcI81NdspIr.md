"I used to watch two to three editing tutorials every day because I thought that's how you get better at editing. But then I noticed something. I could copy every single technique in those videos, but my edits still weren't improving. Because tutorials don't actually teach you the thing that matters." — sources/S_ig_thomassmedia_DcI81NdspIr.md

# T_DcI81NdspIr_02

"So I stopped, and instead I started actually watching, like really watching, the videos that I thought were really good. Pausing on cuts, asking why that moment held longer than it should have, why that graphic felt so good, and why silence worked better than music. That's the part tutorials never teach you: taste and judgment. Knowing when to break a rule because the moment calls for it." — sources/S_ig_thomassmedia_DcI81NdspIr.md
