# T_Db50fAiMwty_01

"Beginner editors are actually better than advanced editors, not in skill, but in judgment. Say a word out loud twenty times in a row. By the end, it doesn't even sound like a real word anymore. And that's actually a real thing called semantic satiation. Your brain gets overexposed to something so familiar, it stops being able to judge. Editors do this to their own timeline constantly." — sources/S_ig_thomassmedia_Db50fAiMwty.md

"You watch the same three seconds over and over. Beyond your brain stops seeing an animation, it just sees rainbow bars on the screen. So you second guess a transition that was already fine. Meanwhile, the beginner who watches it twice feels good, exports. Not because they're more talented, well of course they're not. It's because they haven't satiated their own judgment yet." — sources/S_ig_thomassmedia_Db50fAiMwty.md
