# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_BQFEUJ8iq — Wealth3 Reach

## Term

"Wealth3 Reach" is the software Travis pitches for finding buyers: it pulls people out of other influencers' audiences and groups, qualifies them, and lets the user connect with them directly. The name appears once, beside "qualified people", "influencers", "DM", and "newsletter", and its job in his pitch is to solve the hardest part of making money online — finding the people, since finding a product to promote is easy. Concretely, the tool can pull everyone commenting on an influencer's post, filter for people with a thousand followers or five posts a week, and then DM them a link to a product, a newsletter, or a community.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee of sales: the software finds and qualifies people; it does not make them buy.
- Read as a method Travis invented: the name "Wealth3 Reach" is the product's name, and the clip is a pitch for that tool, not a method of his own.
