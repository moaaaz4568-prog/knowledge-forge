# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CaskS2apvfb — peak experience

## Term

A "peak experience" here is the brief window of having little kids — roughly ages zero to four or five — which Travis calls one of the peaks of a life. The phrase appears once, beside "little kids", "four years", "zero and four", and "gone", and his warning is about speed: four years feels long while it lasts, then it is gone, and unlike many things in life it cannot be repeated. Concretely, the point is presence — the years with small children pass so fast that missing them means missing the experience itself, not just postponing it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Now, obviously you can have more than one child, but all I'm saying is that period between zero and four, zero and five, there's something about it that's really, it's like a peak experience in life, and it's much of your life." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CaskS2apvfb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to have more children: the point is about the unrepeatable window with the children already here, not about having another child.
- Read as four years being a long time: Travis says the opposite — four years feels long and then is gone before you believe it.
