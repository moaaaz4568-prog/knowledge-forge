# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_Cck-oHJD3c- — failure

## Term

"Failure" is the success, in Travis's phrase: the missed clients and botched calls are what teach, not the wins. The word appears four times, beside "success", "one hundred businesses", "ninety eighth", and "learn", and the example he gives is outreach — reach a hundred businesses, maybe the ninety-eighth responds, and the question is whether the response came from numbers or from what the first failures taught. Concretely, a call that goes bad lights a fire under you that success cannot light, because you do not learn from the things you are doing right.

## Synonyms

- fail (the verb form the source also uses)

## Live Example

> "The failure is the success, which is really hard to understand." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_Cck-oHJD3c-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an excuse for losing clients: the failures count only when they teach something — the outreach that fails without learning is just a lost client.
- Read as a claim that success teaches nothing at all: the point is comparative — failure lights a fire that success alone cannot light.
