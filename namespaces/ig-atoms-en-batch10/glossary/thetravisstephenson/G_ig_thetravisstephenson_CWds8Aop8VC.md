# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CWds8Aop8VC — Fly In Friday

## Term

A Fly In Friday is Travis's pay-to-attend event, and his proof that a list is an asset: he can pre-sell it to the list before spending anything on it. The phrase appears three times, beside email list, pre-order, and $250, with the plural Fly In Fridays used once more. He goes to his email list and offers a pre-order at $250 instead of $1,000 — ten sales is $2,500 to invest in the event itself, and the rest of the sales are profit, with no ads and no money out of pocket.

## Synonyms

- Fly In Fridays

## Live Example

> "I go to my email list right now and I'm like, hey, if you pre-order a Fly In Friday right now, instead of paying 1,000, you'll pay 250." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CWds8Aop8VC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a free event: the Fly In Friday is a paid offer — $250 pre-order against a $1,000 price — and the list is what lets him sell it first.
- Read as proof the event will sell out: the ten sales are his illustration of the math, not sales he reports having made.
