# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CX-BUikjgGB — sponsored message

## Term

A sponsored message is the paid Messenger ad Travis says made him the most money on ROI: forty-nine cents spent, four thousand dollars made. The phrase appears twice, beside webinar, Messenger subscriber, and $997 offer. The structure is to run webinar leads into Messenger, where they become subscribers, then send a sponsored message to people who visited the order page — this deal is ending soon, get it now — and sell a $997 offer four times over.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Then I had an ad called a sponsored message that was running to anybody who'd been to my order page that had also been in Messenger at some point." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CX-BUikjgGB.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a message anyone can send for free: a sponsored message is a paid ad that lands in the inbox like a broadcast, and the forty-nine cents is what he spent to reach those buyers.
- Read as a guaranteed 8,000-to-1 return: the $4,000 from $0.49 is the result he reports for this one webinar structure, not a rate the ad produces every time.
