# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_TLGgjMAZv — email list

## Term

An "email list" here is the owned audience that lets a business market to the same people again without paying for each contact. The phrase appears three times, beside "remarket", "for free", "funnel", and "four pillars", and the budget argument is the core: a starting business needs a budget big enough to market to people multiple times, and a list removes that cost. Concretely, Travis markets once, captures the email, and then sends as many emails as needed for the price of the platform — about $97 a month — while the prospects move through his funnel.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a free channel: the list still costs the email platform, about $97 a month in his example — "free" means no per-message ad cost.
- Read as a one-time build: the list only keeps working if it keeps being fed new people; a list that stops growing stops remarketing.
