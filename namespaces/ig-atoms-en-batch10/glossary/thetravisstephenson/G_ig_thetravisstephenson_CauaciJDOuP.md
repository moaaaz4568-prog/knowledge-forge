# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CauaciJDOuP — DM call to action

## Term

A "DM call to action" tells the viewer to message the account instead of leaving Instagram to sign up on a website, and Travis says that one change fixes a poorly executed giveaway post. The phrase appears once, beside "win $500 in gear", "sign up here", "Chatmatic", and "discount", and the problem is the broken journey: a person scrolling the feed must go somewhere else and fill out a form, which most will not do. Concretely, the DM version builds the signup sequence inside a chat, collects the email without leaving the platform, and then links the person back to the store with a discount — making sales on the ad instead of only giving away $500 of gear.

## Synonyms

- call to action (the plain form the source also uses)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a small wording tweak: changing the call to action changes the whole journey, from a website form to a chat that never leaves Instagram.
- Read as giving away $500 of gear for free: the DM version links entrants back to the store with a discount, so the ad is meant to make sales, not only give things away.
