# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CSrC4N9DfTr — inbox

## Term

An inbox is where Travis says the focus should move: from an algorithm you do not control to an email or SMS list you do. The word appears three times, beside algorithm, followers, and email list. He argues that on an algorithm you only make an impression, while a list means people are far more likely to buy — and he proves the direction by sending his own email list to his profile to grow his social following.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The number one focus for anybody on social media, regardless of whether you have a lot of followers or very minimal followers, your focus should be going from an algorithm to an inbox." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CSrC4N9DfTr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as abandoning social media: Travis still uses the algorithm to grow, but he wants the audience moved into an inbox where he can reach them every time.
- Read as a promise that a list always buys: the claim is comparative — list subscribers are way more likely to buy than algorithm viewers — and the video shows no sales figures.
