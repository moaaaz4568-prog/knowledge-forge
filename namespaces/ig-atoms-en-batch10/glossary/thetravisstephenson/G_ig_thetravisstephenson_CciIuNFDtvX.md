# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CciIuNFDtvX — success

## Term

"Success" here is redefined outright: not making a billion dollars, but waking up in the morning in a good mood. The word appears once, beside "seven billion people", "billion dollars", "good mood", and "wake up", and Travis frames the redefinition as a conversation seven billion people need to change. Concretely, the measure he offers is a morning mood — if you wake up feeling good, that is what success looks like, and the billion dollars is the wrong target.
