# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C9vtPA-p58c — affiliate program

## Term

An affiliate program is what Travis searches for the moment he buys anything, so he can get paid for recommending it. The phrase appears three times, beside 10X Superfoods, Google, competition, and six sales. His example is 10X Superfoods: he signs up for its program, finds no ads and no competition for 10X health supplement searches on Google, points his affiliate link at those searches, and reports six sales in 48 hours.
