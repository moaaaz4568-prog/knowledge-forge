# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CbaqVaepBuR — job interview

## Term

A "job interview" here is the shady meeting Travis walked into at a random house in Orlando, and the whole clip is the story of why he was convinced it was not a real job. The phrase appears once, beside "shady", "Orlando", "black leather couch", and "porn", and the details pile up: two cameras pointed at a black leather couch in the living room, and an interviewer who could never explain what the internet business actually was. Concretely, Travis walked in wearing white pants and a button-up, thought he looked like a cool hip kid, and then worried he was sending the wrong signal — as if he had come to audition.
