# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CH30AYODEIN — air fry

## Term

Air fry is Travis's default way to cook the farmer's market haul he shows: okra, carrots, eggplant, and beets get sliced and air fried, often instead of roasting or pan frying. The phrase appears six times, beside okra, carrots, roast, and farmer's market. He keeps coming back to it — dice them up and air fry them, slice them up and air fry them — as the lazy way to eat well on a $22 budget, and the repetition is the point: the same quick method for vegetable after vegetable.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a recipe with times and temperatures: Travis names no temperature or time; air frying is his habit for the haul, not a tested recipe.
- Read as a claim that healthy eating needs money: the $22 haul is his answer to that, and air frying is just how he cooks it.
