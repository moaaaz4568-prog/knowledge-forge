# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C7MH-EzP51y — silver

## Term

Silver is the material Travis and his wife shape in a Bali silver-making class, in a date-day video far from his usual marketing talk. The word appears seven times, beside mold, ring, Borneo, and Bali. He shows the silver heated and poured into a mold, pounded to harden it, thinned to the size of a ring, then carved and buffed smooth, with the silver for the class coming from Borneo.
