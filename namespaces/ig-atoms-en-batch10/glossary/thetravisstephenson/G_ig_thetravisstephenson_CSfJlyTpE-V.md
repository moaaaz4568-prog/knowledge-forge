# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CSfJlyTpE-V — hyper segmentation

## Term

Hyper segmentation is Travis's name for the future of marketing: send each message only to the people who asked for that exact thing. The phrase appears twice, beside cookbook, SMS, email, and future, with the verb form hyper-segmenting used once more and the hyphenated Hyper-segmentation once more. His example is a cookbook: people DM cookbook, give an SMS number, and get tagged cookbook, so the launch message goes only to them — while an email blasted to 100,000 people gets 8% opens and pushes the sender to worse IP addresses.

## Synonyms

- hyper-segmenting (the verb form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "Hyper segmentation is the future of marketing. Take that to the bank." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CSfJlyTpE-V.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on large lists: Travis argues for segmenting the list by what people asked for, not for deleting the list — the 10,000 who wanted the cookbook still get the email.
- Read as a technical email setting: hyper segmentation here is the practice of tagging people by interest, and the deliverability effect is the reason, not the mechanism.
