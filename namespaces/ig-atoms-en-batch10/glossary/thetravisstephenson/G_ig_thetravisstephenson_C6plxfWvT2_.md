# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C6plxfWvT2_ — covert affiliate method

## Term

The covert affiliate method is Travis's name for his three-step way of making money with free Facebook groups: build the group, grow it, and monetize it. The phrase appears twice, beside three steps, Facebook group, million dollars, and members. He says the method has produced a million dollars from members, and the covert part is that the selling happens inside groups the members joined for free, with products promoted to people already gathered there.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Now the covert affiliate method is basically teaching you how to use affiliate groups, groups of your own that you own that are free, and how to promote affiliate products." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_C6plxfWvT2_.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a secret trick that needs no work: he lays out three steps — building, growing, and monetizing a group — and says growing it is the most complicated piece.
- Read as a guarantee of a million dollars: the million is what he says members have made using it, not a promise for any single group.
