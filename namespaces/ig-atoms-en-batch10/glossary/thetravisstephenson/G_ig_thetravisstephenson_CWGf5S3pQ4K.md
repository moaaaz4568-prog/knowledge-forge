# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CWGf5S3pQ4K — assessment funnels

## Term

Assessment funnels are Travis's most powerful way to market: ask the buyer questions like a doctor would, instead of handing them medicine before knowing what is wrong. The phrase appears twice, beside bots, doctor, and medicine. His image is a doctor's visit done the marketing way — 'here, here's this medicine' with no questions asked, no allergy check — and the funnel version asks first, so the answer it supplies is one the buyer actually wants.
