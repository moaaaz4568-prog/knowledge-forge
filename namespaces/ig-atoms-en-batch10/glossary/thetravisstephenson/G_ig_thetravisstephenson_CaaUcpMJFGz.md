# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CaaUcpMJFGz — Contrarian Copy Sequence

## Term

The "Contrarian Copy Sequence" is a seven-message sales sequence that walks a new lead from opting in to a buying decision by overturning what they believed first. The name appears twice, beside "seven-step", "seven messages", "lead", and "buying decision", and its shape is a four-beat argument: this is what you probably think is true, this is what is actually true, let me prove it, then here is an offer that improves your situation. Concretely, the sequence is offered as a PDF — comment "more sales" and the system sends the download — so the sequence sells itself by demonstrating its own method.

> "We call it the Contrarian Copy Sequence, and it basically runs you through a seven-step formula, seven messages long, to take somebody from opting in and becoming a lead to actually introducing them to a sale or making the sale sort of moment occur." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CaaUcpMJFGz.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to change minds: the sequence works by proving the true claim, not by hiding it — the proof step comes before the offer.
- Read as a seven-message template for any product: the example is built for digital and coaching offers, and the PDF itself has to be written for the seller's own product.
