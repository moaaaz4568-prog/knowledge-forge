# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CbSaXh1jWTV — affiliate programs

## Term

"Affiliate programs" are the arrangements that pay a creator to promote other people's products, and Travis presents them as the bridge between passion and owning a product. The phrase appears once, beside "passionate", "vitamin D", "own product", and "audience", and the path he sketches has three stages: create content around what you love, earn specific influence, then use the list to make money on other people's stuff. Concretely, a health influencer promoting someone's vitamin D who finds they are selling 10,000 capsules can pull those buyers over and start a vitamin D line of their own.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a get-rich-quick path: the path runs through creating content, building specific influence, and only then earning on other people's products.
- Read as the vitamin D line being the starting point: the own product comes last, after the affiliate income pays for it.
