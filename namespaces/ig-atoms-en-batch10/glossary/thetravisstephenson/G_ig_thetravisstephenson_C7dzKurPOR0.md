# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C7dzKurPOR0 — email list

## Term

An email list is the asset Travis builds from a free Facebook group: new members hand over their email addresses when they join, and those addresses become a newsletter business. The phrase appears four times, beside newsletter, Facebook group, wine, and $39,833. He shows a group with 82 people pending, each of whom gave an email address, and says his last email made $39,833, so the list is what turns a group into a business that can be sold for five to ten times its yearly revenue.
