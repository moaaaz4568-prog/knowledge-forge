# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CVTkk06J8w8 — VIP

## Term

VIP is Travis's list of very important people who hear about a release before social media does, so they get insider access instead of competing with everybody. The word appears six times, beside campaign, notify, social media, and everybody list. His example is a shoe release: the VIP list is notified a day before social media, so members are guaranteed to see the shoes before they sell out, while the social audience may see them late or not at all.

## Live Example

> "Social media should not be your VIP list. That's what's crazy to me. Social media is your everybody list." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CVTkk06J8w8.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a paid membership: Travis describes no fee for the VIP list — it is a list of the best people, rewarded with early notice and special discounts on Black Friday or Memorial Day.
- Read as a claim that only 8% see a post: the 8% is his figure for how many people see a social post, and the VIP list exists precisely because that reach cannot be relied on.
