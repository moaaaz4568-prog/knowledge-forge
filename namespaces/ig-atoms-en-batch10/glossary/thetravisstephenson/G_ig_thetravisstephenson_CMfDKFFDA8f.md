# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CMfDKFFDA8f — pillars

## Term

Pillars are the three or four ideas the big names in a space build all their content on, and then sell products from. The word appears four times, beside Gary Vaynerchuk, content, three, and empathy. Travis says he analyzed what Gary Vaynerchuk, Frank Kern, and Grant Cardone put out and found the same pattern, and his own example is Vaynerchuk talking about empathy and then opening a wine company called Empathy — the pillar becomes the product.

## Live Example

> "I started analyzing what their content was based on. And all of them have three or four common pillars." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CMfDKFFDA8f.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of Travis's own three pillars: he says a business needs three pillars people must understand, but he never names his three in this video.
- Read as proof the pillar method caused the sales: Travis calls the Empathy example no coincidence, but he shows the pattern, not a test of it.
