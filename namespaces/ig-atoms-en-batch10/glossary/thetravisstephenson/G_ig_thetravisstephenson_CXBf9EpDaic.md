# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CXBf9EpDaic — SMS

## Term

SMS is Travis's channel for scarcity: it beats email on deliverability because far fewer people are SMSing the customer, so the open rate is much higher. The word appears four times, beside email, deliverability, and scarcity, with the verb form SMSing used once more. He saves it for 24-hour and 48-hour campaigns — a giveaway live for five days, a flash sale happening right now — where the message must be seen today, not whenever the inbox is checked.

## Synonyms

- SMSing (the verb form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "SMS and email are different mostly for its deliverability to the end client, meaning SMS is a much higher open rate because there's not as many people SMSing them." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CXBf9EpDaic.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a replacement for email: Travis keeps both — SMS for scarce, time-bound offers and email for the rest — and the difference he names is deliverability, not content.
- Read as a claim that SMS always gets opened: the higher open rate is his reason for using SMS on scarcity, and the video gives no open-rate number for it.
