# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CW8lv4QJkaj — email marketer

## Term

An email marketer is what almost every affiliate Travis met turned out to be, and the punchline of his origin story: ninety percent of the revenue coming in was from people who have email lists. The phrase appears three times, beside personal assistant, poker, and Ferrari. He tells it as a job interview for a personal assistant — walk the dog, buy the groceries — with an internet marketer who had won a Ferrari in an affiliate contest, and the discovery that every affiliate he talked to was an email marketer is what pulled him into the business.

## Live Example

> "I go, I talk to all these affiliates. Almost every single one of them is like an email marketer, email marketer, email marketer." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CW8lv4QJkaj.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a job title Travis held: the email marketers are the affiliates he met after joining the company, and he presents himself as the newcomer learning from them.
- Read as proof that email lists earn 90% of all revenue: the ninety percent is what he says he saw coming into that one network, not a measured industry figure.
