# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CU8Iv7jpwRc — quality over quantity

## Term

Quality over quantity is Travis's argument for small lists: 2,000 people in the market for a facelift beat 10,000 who might want some plastic surgery. The phrase appears once, beside 2000 people, facelift, and small broadcasts. His method is to send small broadcasts to small groups of people who actually want the thing, using assessment sequences that find what each person needs — and if you cannot reverse engineer the way to the perfect product for someone, he says, you do not know what you are selling or who you are selling to.

## Live Example

> "I'm here to argue quality over quantity. That if you have a list of 2000 people that you know they are in the market for a facelift versus 10,000 people that you think might want some kind of plastic surgery, you're gonna get way more out of the 2000." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CU8Iv7jpwRc.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on large lists: the claim is comparative — the smaller, better-matched list returns more — and the video gives no sales figure for either list.
- Read as a facelift sales pitch: the facelift is his example of a matched market, and the point is the assessment, not the procedure.
