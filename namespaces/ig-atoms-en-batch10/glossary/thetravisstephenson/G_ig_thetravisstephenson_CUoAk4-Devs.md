# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CUoAk4-Devs — VIP

## Term

VIP is Travis's call to action for moving buyers off the algorithm: a viewer DMs VIP and joins a list that gets a 20% discount through a seven-day email sequence. The word appears three times, beside algorithm, dedicated, and seven-day email sequence. He says the algorithm gives only so much and takes you only so far, so a real business owner wants buyers off-platform — and once the VIP sequence is built, the same list can announce the next affiliate offer, app, sponsor, or brand without depending on any algorithm.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a one-time discount code: the 20% off runs through a seven-day sequence that is evergreen, and the list it builds keeps working after the discount expires.
- Read as a claim that followers see nothing: Travis says some of the best people do not see a post ever, because of filters they did not know were pre-installed — a partial blindness, not a total one.
