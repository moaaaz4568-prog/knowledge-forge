# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CeCL7v0JJxr — ownership

## Term

"Ownership" means taking responsibility for every action, and Travis says it makes a massive difference in life, business, and relationships. The word appears three times, beside "full ownership", "decisions", "control", and "where you are", and the hard part of the idea is its scope: even when something negative happens, only you can get yourself out of it. Concretely, the claim is that you are where you are because of the decisions you made — which does not mean you have to stay there, since you are in full control of getting where you need to go.

## Live Example

> "Ownership of every single action that we take is gonna make a massive difference in our life, in our business, in our relationships, in everything that we have." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CeCL7v0JJxr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as blame for everything bad: ownership here means control over getting out, not fault for what happened.
- Read as a promise that ownership fixes everything at once: the claim is that it makes a massive difference, not that it removes the difficulty.
