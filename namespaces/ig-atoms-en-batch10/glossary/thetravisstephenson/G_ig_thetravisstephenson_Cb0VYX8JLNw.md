# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_Cb0VYX8JLNw — entry points

## Term

"Entry points" are the doors that send people into a Messenger or Instagram sequence — a DM keyword, a paid ad, or a button. The phrase appears twice, beside "opt-in", "Messenger", "Instagram", and "lead magnet", and Travis introduces the idea right after quoting his live sequence's 85% opt-in rate: if people are asked for their information inside a chat, 85% of the time they give it. Concretely, the entry points include someone DMing the word "sale", an ad that routes straight into a sequence, and even a call button inside the chat that dials the seller's phone as soon as it is tapped.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the sequence itself: entry points are only the doors into the sequence — the DM keyword, the ad, the call button — not the messages inside it.
- Read as proof that organic and paid are different: Travis says organic is literally the same thing as ads, only without the money amplifying it.
