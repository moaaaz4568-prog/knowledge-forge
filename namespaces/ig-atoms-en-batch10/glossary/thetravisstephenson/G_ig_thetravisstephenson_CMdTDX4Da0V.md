# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CMdTDX4Da0V — hole patching

## Term

Hole patching is Travis's name for the first of his two ways to serve a business: find where it spends money without capturing all the value, and patch the hole. The phrase appears once, beside Google Ads, ranked second, and services, with the verb form patch holes used for the same method. His example is a business paying to be ranked second on Google: clicking its link costs it money, and the hole is that it pays for visits it does not turn into customers.

## Synonyms

- patch holes (the verb form Travis uses for the same method)
