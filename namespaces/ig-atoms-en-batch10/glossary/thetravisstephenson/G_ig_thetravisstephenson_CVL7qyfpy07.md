# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CVL7qyfpy07 — giveaway

## Term

A giveaway here is a paid slot in a loop giveaway — one of 75 people — and Travis says the number one mistake is not monetizing the followers immediately. The word appears five times, beside 75 people, follower, and monetize. The followers from a loop fall off, he says, because they were incentivized to follow in a way that makes them bad followers — so the fix is to host your own giveaway, for a free MacBook or something in your industry, during the same window, and get them indoctrinated into your process right away.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to avoid loop giveaways: Travis says to join the loop and run your own giveaway inside it — the mistake is waiting to monetize, not entering.
- Read as a promise that a MacBook giveaway keeps followers: the MacBook is his example of an on-industry prize, and the video shows no retention figure for the followers it wins.
