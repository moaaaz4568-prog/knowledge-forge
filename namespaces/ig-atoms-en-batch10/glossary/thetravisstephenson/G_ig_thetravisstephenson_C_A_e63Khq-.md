# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_A_e63Khq- — lead generation

## Term

"Lead generation" is finding new people to put on the email list, and Travis calls it the key difference maker between a goal and the money itself. The phrase appears twice, beside "list", "realistic number", "20,000 a month", and "difference maker", and his framing is deliberately sober: 50,000 or 100,000 a month is possible, but only for the best students, while five to twenty thousand a month is the realistic range. Concretely, the same list that is not overplayed and keeps gaining fresh leads is what turns a posted goal into monthly income.

> "That is with you not overplaying your list, and that is with you being, you know, really good with lead generation, which I think that is the lead generation is gonna be the key difference maker to what actually makes you money." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_A_e63Khq-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of $100,000 a month: Travis calls that possible only for the best students, and names five to twenty thousand as the realistic range.
- Read as buying a list: the generation here means attracting new people onto the list, not purchasing names.
