# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CTml5VNjhZd — giveaway

## Term

A giveaway here is a machine for building lists, not just collecting likes: Travis adds a DM step that turns each comment into an email address and an SMS number. The word appears six times — five as giveaway and once as giveaways — beside tag three friends, followers, and email list. The usual version — tag three friends, like the page, win a laptop — gets only likes on a platform the brand does not control, while his version confirms the email by DM, doubles the odds for an SMS number, and ends with followers plus an email list plus an SMS list at zero additional cost.

## Synonyms

- giveaways

## Live Example

> "On your next giveaway, I can put that on steroids." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CTml5VNjhZd.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a free way to get followers: the followers are still the main benefit, but Travis adds the DM step precisely because followers alone sit on a platform he cannot control.
- Read as zero cost in every sense: zero out of pocket is his claim for the added lists, and the prize itself — a laptop, an Xbox, $5,000 — is still given away.
