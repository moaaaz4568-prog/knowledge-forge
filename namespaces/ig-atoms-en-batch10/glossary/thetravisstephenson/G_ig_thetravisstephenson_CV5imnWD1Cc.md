# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CV5imnWD1Cc — Messenger bots

## Term

Messenger bots are the tool Travis says people ruined by overdoing them: agencies built 8,000-step sequences to look impressive, and sold the complexity. The phrase appears twice, beside agencies, overcomplicated, and 10,000 salesmen, with the short form bots used twice more. His account is that business owners saw the value but had no time to learn it, so agency owners learned it and charged others — and 10,000 salesmen ended up convincing everyone that messenger bots are the best thing in the world.

## Synonyms

- bots (the short form Travis uses when the Messenger context is already clear)

## Live Example

> "So we got all this noise of like messenger bots are incredible and look at how crazy this is, but in order to show value, these agencies, the agency owners did what I, the opposite of what I teach." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CV5imnWD1Cc.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that bots do not work: Travis says people are doing them wrong by overanalyzing, not that the bots themselves fail — his own sequences run on them.
- Read as an attack on all agencies: his target is the agencies that overcomplicated sequences to show value, while his own teaching is the simpler sequence he sells.
