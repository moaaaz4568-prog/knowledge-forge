# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C7VILDKyl_e — screen recorder

## Term

A screen recorder is the product Travis sells in this video: Screen Studio, a recorder that edits videos in real time and pays him $28.35 per sale through its affiliate program. The phrase appears twice, beside Screen Studio, digital course, $28, and affiliate program. His pitch is that anyone making a digital course needs a screen recorder, so he messages the followers of a creator who posts about digital courses and offers them his recorder.
