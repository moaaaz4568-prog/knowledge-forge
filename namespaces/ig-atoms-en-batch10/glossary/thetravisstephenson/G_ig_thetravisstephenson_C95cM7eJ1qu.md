# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C95cM7eJ1qu — affiliate marketing

## Term

Affiliate marketing here is earning money by recommending something you liked: if a friend goes to a restaurant you recommended and you get money back, that is all affiliate marketing is. The phrase appears once, beside restaurant, recommended, and friends. Travis builds the whole definition from one picture — telling friends about a restaurant you loved — and treats the recommendation itself as the product.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you could get money back every time one of your friends went to a place that you recommended, that's all affiliate marketing is." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_C95cM7eJ1qu.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a restaurant discount scheme: the restaurant is only his example; the point is getting paid when people act on your recommendation.
- Read as needing a product of your own: the definition rests on recommending a place you already liked, not on owning or selling anything.
