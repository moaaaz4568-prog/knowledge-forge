# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CZxFj_1pFam — brand

## Term

A "brand", for Travis, is a business that owns the line to its audience instead of renting reach from a platform's algorithm. The word appears five times, beside "influencer", "audience", "control", and "algorithm", and the contrast is the whole argument: an influencer's post may reach 8% or 10% of people, while a brand can send emails and SMS whenever it wants. The concrete switch he prescribes is getting people off the platform and onto a list, so income depends on the brand's own list rather than on what other people will pay for its reach.

## Live Example

> "A brand takes control of their audience so they can send emails, SMS, and reach out to their audience whenever they want." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CZxFj_1pFam.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "brand" meaning a logo or a name: here it means owning the line to the audience — email and SMS — not a visual identity.
- Read as an influencer with a big following: an influencer who cannot reach their own followers without the algorithm is the opposite of the brand Travis describes.
