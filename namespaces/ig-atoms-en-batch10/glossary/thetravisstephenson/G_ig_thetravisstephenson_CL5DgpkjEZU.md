# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CL5DgpkjEZU — education process

## Term

An education process is Travis's replacement for a sales process: instead of pushing a product, you teach the buyer until every objection is gone. The phrase appears twice, beside sales process, benefits, obstacles, and customers. His example is essential oils — the only thing stopping a buyer is not knowing the health benefits and the income benefits — and once each 'I don't know how' is answered by showing, the buyer has nothing left to say no with.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "People are so confused in today's world. You don't need a better sales process. You need a better education process." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CL5DgpkjEZU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a course to sell: the education process is the selling method itself — teaching benefits until objections fall — not a product on offer.
- Read as a claim that education always closes: Travis says it creates better customers and a better relationship, but the video shows no sale made this way.
