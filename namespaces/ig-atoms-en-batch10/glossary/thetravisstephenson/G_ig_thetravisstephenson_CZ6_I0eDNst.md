# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CZ6_I0eDNst — return on ad spend

## Term

"Return on ad spend" is the money an ad brings back for each dollar spent on it, and Travis uses Lego to show how matching the product to the buyer lifts it. The phrase appears twice, beside "ROI 3x", "3x lift", "buying decision", and "customized", and the Lego story is the proof: shoppers were asked how old the kid was, then shown only Lego sets for that age, which made the decision easier. The concrete result he reports is a threefold lift — the same ad money returning three times as much because the product fit the person.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "They got a 3x lift in their return on ad spend." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CZ6_I0eDNst.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Lego's official number: the 3x figure is Travis's retelling of a story, not a published Lego result.
- Read as a guarantee for any product: the lift came from matching products to the buyer's kid's age; an unmatched product would not lift the same way.
