# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CZ1hRQ2DS7Q — rich clone strategy

## Term

The "rich clone strategy" is Travis's name for cloning an ad that already works and routing its clicks through an automated chat sequence instead of straight to a website. The phrase appears once, beside "automated chat sequence", "the exact same ad", "email address", and "three to five times as much money", and the payoff is a list: every person who enters the chat hands over an email, so they can be reached again without paying for them twice. Concretely, a buyer who does not purchase on the first visit can be emailed back for free rather than re-bought with another ad.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a new ad: the strategy clones an ad that already runs; it does not create the ad or the offer.
- Read as free money: the three-to-five-times lift depends on the chat collecting emails; an ad with no sequence behind it gains nothing.
