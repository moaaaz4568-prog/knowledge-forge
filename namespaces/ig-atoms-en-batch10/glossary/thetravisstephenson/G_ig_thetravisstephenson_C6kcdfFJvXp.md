# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C6kcdfFJvXp — coffee alternative

## Term

A coffee alternative is the kind of product Travis promotes without owning it: in this video the example is Mud Water, a coffee alternative that pays $50 for every sale he generates. The phrase appears four times, beside Mud Water, $50, Facebook group, and Quitting Caffeine. He finds buyers for it in a Facebook group for people quitting caffeine, then messages them the link, so the alternative is the product at the center of the whole funnel he walks through.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a health claim about coffee alternatives: the video says nothing about taste or health, only that the product pays $50 a sale.
