# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_kyWhSJLiw — Opus Clip

## Term

"Opus Clip" is the tool Travis uses to cut long-form video into short-form clips automatically. The name appears once, beside "short form content", "long form", "76 pieces", and "Opus Pro", and his process has three steps: upload the long video, let the tool make the clips, then schedule them. Concretely, five minutes of processing produced 120 to 150 clips to choose from, and he picked 76 — about two and a half months of posts — then had them distributed automatically across his channels.
