# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C7JK1ZYPu5I — woodworking

## Term

Woodworking is the niche Travis targets in this video, built around the affiliate product Ted's Woodworking, which pays 75% — about $125 — on every sale. The word appears five times, beside Ted's Woodworking, 75%, $125, and Woodworking Tips. He joins a Facebook group called Woodworking Tips with 200,000 people, pulls their profiles into his software, invites them into his own woodworking group, and collects their email addresses so a seven-day email sequence can sell them the product.
