# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CXjGigqDpFX — birthday

## Term

A birthday is the targeting hook Travis uses for restaurant lead gen: on Facebook you can target people whose birthday falls in a certain month. The word appears four times, beside restaurant, sequence, and Messenger. His sequence offers a free item for booking a table of more than four on a birthday, collects the email and mobile number and party size, and follows up automatically — and he charges five hundred to a thousand a month to manage it for the restaurant.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a birthday party business: the birthday is only the targeting signal; the service is the Messenger sequence that books big parties.
- Read as a guaranteed booking fee: the five hundred to a thousand a month is what he says he would charge for managing the sequence, not what the restaurant earns from it.
