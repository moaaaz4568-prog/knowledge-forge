# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C7DMMmISSEd — dog trainer

## Term

A dog trainer here is the affiliate product Travis picks to sell: an ultrasonic dog trainer that pays $51 to $59 on every sale. The phrase appears three times, beside ultrasonic, followers, Southern, and 1.3 million. He finds the buyers by opening the Instagram followers of a Southern dog trainer with 1.3 million followers, imports them into his software, and sends each one a direct message offering the trainer.
