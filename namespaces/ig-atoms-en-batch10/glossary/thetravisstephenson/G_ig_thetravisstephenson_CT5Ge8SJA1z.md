# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CT5Ge8SJA1z — giveaways

## Term

Giveaways here are no longer loss leaders or brand building material for Travis: a giveaway should pay for itself while it runs. The word appears three times, beside brand building, loss leaders, and email addresses, with the singular giveaway twice more. His model is a cycle — give away $2,000 of stuff, make $5,000, do it again and make $5,200, then $5,600 — because each round collects more email addresses and phone numbers to SMS and email about the next one.

## Synonyms

- giveaway

## Live Example

> "Giveaways are no longer loss leaders in and of themselves. They're no longer brand building material." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CT5Ge8SJA1z.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on brand building: Travis says giveaways do not have to be brand building alone; the side effects — followers, likes, engagement — still happen while the giveaway makes money.
- Read as a guaranteed return per giveaway: the $5,000, $5,200, and $5,600 are his illustration of the cycle compounding, not results he reports from a real giveaway.
