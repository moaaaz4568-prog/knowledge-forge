# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CZR1djADZrz — giveaway

## Term

A "giveaway" here is a free prize offered to pull people into a launch, and this whole clip is a customer saying one giveaway idea started everything. The word appears once, beside "the giveaway idea", "launch", "Instagram", and "60 days", and the numbers she gives are the point: over $80,000 in total after the launch, and just over $40,000 in 60 days, all through Instagram. Concretely, the giveaway was the spark — the revenue came from the launch and the coaching that followed it, not from the prize itself.
