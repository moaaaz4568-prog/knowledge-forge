# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CakTeGTDr_W — audience

## Term

An "audience" here is the group of people who watched one of the creator's videos, collected on Facebook so ads can be run back to exactly those viewers. The word appears three times, beside "701 plays", "video viewers", "Facebook ads", and "shoutouts", and Travis's point is that watching is intent: someone who watched a video is probably relatively interested in what is offered. Concretely, a simple Reel with about 701 plays becomes a well-defined audience, and ads to that audience cost far less than cold ads because every viewer has already shown interest.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any group of viewers: the audience here is only the people who watched this creator's own videos, which is why they are already interested.
- Read as free reach: building the audience from Reels is free, but running the Facebook ads back to them still costs ad money.
