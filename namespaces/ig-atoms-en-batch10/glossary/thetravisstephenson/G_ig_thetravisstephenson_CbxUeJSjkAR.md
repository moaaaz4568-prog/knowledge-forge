# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CbxUeJSjkAR — collect phase

## Term

The "collect phase" is the first phase of Travis's marketing: gather subscribers and followers into one place before trying to sell them anything. The phrase appears once, beside "Messenger sequence", "subscribers", "cultivate", and "core offer", and the order matters — collect people, cultivate the relationship, and only then ask them to buy. Concretely, the Messenger sequence carries a core offer inside it, and the marketer's job is to educate the prospect past their instinct to say no by showing what is in it for them.

> "Messenger sequence, right? This is my collect phase." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CbxUeJSjkAR.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as collecting followers for their own sake: the phase exists so the relationship can be cultivated and a core offer made later.
- Read as a sales pitch: the core offer arrives inside the sequence, but only after the prospect has been educated past their instinct to say no.
