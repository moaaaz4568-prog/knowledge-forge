# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CcqNghyjdZI — time back

## Term

"Time back" is what money buys once a small company turns profitable: the hours the founder spent grinding are recovered by hiring. The phrase appears three times, beside "spend your time", "hire somebody", "full time", and "money", and the sequence he describes is a ladder — first you spend your time making money, then you use that money to get your time. Concretely, each hire returns a slice of the founder's day, until a bunch of people are working full time and the founder does not even have to be involved.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Now I don't even have to be involved. I used my money to get my time back." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CcqNghyjdZI.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as buying free time with no work: the time is bought only after the grinding phase makes the company profitable enough to hire.
- Read as a promise to stop working: the founder ends up not having to be involved, but only because full-time people now fill every hole.
