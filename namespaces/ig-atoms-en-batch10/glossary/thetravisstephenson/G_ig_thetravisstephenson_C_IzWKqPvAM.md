# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_IzWKqPvAM — self-qualified

## Term

"Self-qualified" describes a person who answers yes to the group's one entry question and thereby proves they belong there. The word appears once, beside "yes or no", "belong", "group", and "coffee replacement", and the mechanism is a single filter question: a basketball group asks whether the person wants to get better at basketball, and a coffee-alternatives group asks whether they are actively looking to get off coffee. Concretely, a yes admits them and a no keeps them out, so the group fills only with people who already want what it offers.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "When they say yes, they've self-qualified." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_C_IzWKqPvAM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales qualification call: the qualifying here is one yes-or-no question at the door of a group, not a discovery call.
- Read as letting everyone in: a "no" answer keeps the person out of the group, so the filter only works if the no is enforced.
