# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CTzj-a7jVn8 — direct response marketing

## Term

Direct response marketing is Travis's name for marketing that is deliberately geared to generate sales, measured by whether the giveaway made money. The phrase appears once, beside giveaway, brand marketing, and ROI. He says 99.999% of people running Instagram giveaways are not doing it to make money while doing it, and contrasts that with brand marketing — the Callaways and Taylormades giving away a golf bag they already made, for whom the loss is small — while everyone else giving away a $1,200 laptop wants to know if it worked.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Well, it would just be so nice to be like this giveaway made this much money a month. That's called direct response marketing." — sources/S_ig_thetravisstephenson_CTzj-a7jVn8.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rejection of branding: Travis says the same giveaway can still provide goodwill, fun, and long-term branding while being geared to sales.
- Read as a measured result: the 99.999% is his exclamation, and no giveaway in the video is shown with its actual revenue counted.
