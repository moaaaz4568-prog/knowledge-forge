# G_ig_thetravisstephenson_CahnY63DVhM — DM sequence

## Term

A "DM sequence" is an automated chat that replaces sending ad traffic to a website, and Travis uses a necklace ad to show why it sells more. The phrase appears once, beside "necklace", "Derek", "Klaviyo", and "personalized", and the sequence does two things the website cannot: it asks the buyer's son's name, then reuses that name in every later message. Concretely, the chat learns the son is Derek, the follow-up emails say Derek would absolutely love the necklace, and a person then follows up in every existing chat to ask whether they bought it and offer a 10% discount.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a website with a chat widget: the sequence replaces the website entirely, and the sale depends on the chat learning the buyer's details.
- Read as personal service at scale: the personalization comes from the sequence reusing the son's name, and the final follow-up still needs a hired person to work through the chats.
