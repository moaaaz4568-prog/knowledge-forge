# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DGd6MSiIHBN — personality

## Term

Personality appears twice in this source, where it names the ingredient creators skip while polishing everything else, compared to a burger without a patty. The speaker imagines a video with the best editing, every hack and trick like SEO and hashtags, and a tiny half-baked idea plopped on top, then says no one wants that burger no matter how good the bun, the lettuce, or the tomato. The claim is that everything else can be skipped and people will still want the result, but a big idea and some personality are non-negotiable. The sting is that this is exactly where people spend the least amount of time, which is why no one wants to eat what they cook up.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But you need a big idea and you need some personality." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_DGd6MSiIHBN.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a call to ignore editing and SEO: the source says those can be skipped and still leave something people want, not that they are worthless, and the point is priority, not a ban.
- Taken as a claim that personality alone suffices: the speaker pairs it with a big idea, and a strong personality on top of a half-baked idea is the very failure the burger image mocks.
