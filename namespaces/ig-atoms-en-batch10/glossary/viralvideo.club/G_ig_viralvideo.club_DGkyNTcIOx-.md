# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DGkyNTcIOx- — suffering

## Term

Suffering appears twice in this source, where it names the pain of expecting a hard thing to be easy, contrasted with hardship itself. The speaker says the doubts creep in — maybe I am just not cut out for this, maybe I am not good enough — because the expectation is wrong, not because the work is impossible. The remedy offered is a change of perception: accept that it is hard, and the suffering can go away even while the hardship stays. The closing line draws the boundary sharply: the hardship is part of the business, but the suffering does not have to be.

## Synonyms

- suffer (the verb form used in the source)

## Live Example

> "Maybe all it takes for the suffering to go away is for you to finally accept that it's hard." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_DGkyNTcIOx-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a denial that the work is hard: the source insists the task really is fucking hard, and only the extra suffering from false expectations is optional.
- Taken as a pep talk to push through anything: the change the speaker prescribes is accepting difficulty, not ignoring it, so a task that is genuinely beyond the creator is not answered by this passage.
