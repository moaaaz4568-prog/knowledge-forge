# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DFkyONLoitj — value

## Term

Value appears 7 times in this source, where it means the worth a seller genuinely gives away, illustrated by a man offering an ounce of gold for free. Because the giver knows he is giving away so much value, rejection stops being terrifying: he has more to lose if they say yes than if they say no, which gives him impenetrable confidence. The speaker contrasts this with sellers who try to take or extract value, and says the reason so many people hate sales is that they are not confident in the value they provide. The fix the source prescribes is to focus on delivering more and more value to potential customers, so that a no says more about the buyer than about the offer.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "when you truly have something of value, rejection no longer is this terrifying thing." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_DFkyONLoitj.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Mistaken for a pricing trick: the source's value is the real worth handed over, like the ounce of gold, not a discount or a framing device, so charging more for the same thin offer does not create it.
- Mistaken for confidence as a personality trait: the unshakable confidence here comes from knowing the value of what is offered, and the source says it collapses when the seller doubts what they provide.
