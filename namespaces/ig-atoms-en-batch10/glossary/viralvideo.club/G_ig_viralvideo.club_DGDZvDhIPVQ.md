# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DGDZvDhIPVQ — implied payoff

## Term

Implied payoff appears once in this source, where it names the unspoken promise that keeps viewers watching a fashion video: people want to see the different reactions of the public. The video earns that promise by zigging when everyone else is zagging, with a man in a backwards hoodie pretending to be artwork while strangers take photos, which also supplies the pattern interrupt.
