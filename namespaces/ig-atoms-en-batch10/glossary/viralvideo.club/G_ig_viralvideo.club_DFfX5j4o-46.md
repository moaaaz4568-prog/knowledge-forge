# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DFfX5j4o-46 — recency bias

## Term

Recency bias appears once in this source, where the speaker names it as the psychological effect behind ending a video right after the payoff. The effect means people judge an experience mostly by how they felt at the end, so a video that drags on after its peak, like season eight of The Office, kills the experience even if the start was strong. The speaker's rule follows directly from it: always finish your videos on a high, and end right after the biggest emotional peak, because the ending is what the viewer remembers. Pikachu and the lottery-versus-taxes comparison are the source's own illustrations of a strong ending left clean versus one spoiled by trailing on.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that any strong ending guarantees virality: the source only argues the ending shapes how the experience is judged, and the same video posted twice got different views, so the ending is one lever among others.
- Read as advice to cut the video the moment anything good happens: the source's payoff is the biggest emotional peak, not the first pleasant moment, so cutting early would remove the peak the bias needs.
