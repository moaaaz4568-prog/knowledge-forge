# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DITlLoMIMdx — pain point

## Term

Pain point appears twice in this source, where it names the first of three strategies that made the same video get seven point nine times more views the second time it was posted. In the first video the creator targets a pain point that just is not that big, but in the second he targets one most gym goers will care about, which inherently leads to a larger audience and more views. The other two strategies are value, where ignoring the second video's advice could tear a pec, and surprise, where the second video delights instead of delivering a no shit Sherlock moment. The three work together: a bigger pain point brings more people, higher stakes make the advice matter, and surprise makes it worth valuing.
