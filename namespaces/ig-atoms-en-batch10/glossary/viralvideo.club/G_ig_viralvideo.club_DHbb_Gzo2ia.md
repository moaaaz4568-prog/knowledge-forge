# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DHbb_Gzo2ia — creator matrix

## Term

Creator matrix appears once in this source, where it names a grid for choosing the perfect shots for a video, answering the age-old question of what shots to use. The matrix has only two dimensions: shot sizes of wide, medium, and close, and angles of low, eye line, and high. The speaker shows how it decides the shot: to come across as an authority figure the grid tells the creator which combination to use, and to spark fear or vulnerability as an establishing shot, go high and wide. The offer at the end is the grid itself, available by commenting matrix for a DM.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Don't post another video without using the creator matrix." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_DHbb_Gzo2ia.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a claim that two dimensions cover all shot choice: the source's grid answers which size and angle to use, and it says nothing about light, movement, or editing.
- Taken as a rule that high and wide always means fear: the speaker gives that combination for an establishing shot meant to spark fear or vulnerability, not as a universal meaning of the angle.
