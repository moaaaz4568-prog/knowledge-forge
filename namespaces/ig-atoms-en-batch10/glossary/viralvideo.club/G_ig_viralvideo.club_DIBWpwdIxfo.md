# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DIBWpwdIxfo — post daily

## Term

Post daily appears once in this source, where it names the creator's rule in a short diary of persistence: rain or shine, I post daily. The surrounding lines show what the rule survives: a video worked on hard that flopped, formats copied and then studied until they could be made one's own, and hope that this one goes viral. The speaker's stance after a flop is flat rather than dramatic: it is done, it is out, I will get better on the next one. The closing line gives the rule its purpose: I am building something bigger than a trend, so the daily post is a brick, not a lottery ticket.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a promise that daily posting guarantees growth: the source's own example is a hard-worked video that flopped, so the rule is about showing up, not about winning every day.
- Taken as an endorsement of copying formats: the speaker copies formats only to study why they worked and then make them his own, so the copying is research, not the content.
