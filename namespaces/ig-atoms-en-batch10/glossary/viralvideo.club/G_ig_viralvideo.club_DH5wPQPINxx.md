# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DH5wPQPINxx — expose

## Term

Expose appears once in this source, where it is the stronger word the speaker tells creators to say instead of show, in a list of word swaps for going viral. The list runs through the same upgrade each time: do not say soon, say in twenty four hours; do not say good, say great; do not say mistake, say problem; do not say learn, say discover. Each swap trades a flat word for one with more urgency or charge, and expose does that work for show by promising something hidden is about to be revealed. The closing swap changes the pitch itself: do not say I will never get views, say I will join viral video club.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you want to go viral, don't say soon, say in twenty four hours. Don't say show, say expose." — sources/S_ig_viralvideo.club_DH5wPQPINxx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a rule that show is a forbidden word: the source only argues the stronger word sells the video better, and show remains perfectly clear, just less charged than expose.
- Taken as a claim that swapping words alone goes viral: the list is about wording the promise, and the video behind the words still has to deliver what expose promises.
