# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DFsOKP9qth3 — experimentation

## Term

Experimentation appears once in this source, where it names the hidden 99% of the iceberg behind a successful podcast: guests get the credit, but the real work is testing. The speaker describes Steve Bartlett's setup, a magic trackpad under the table that sends what the guest just said through the wall to a team who test it 90 times behind a Facebook ad to find the most interesting parts. Because so many experiments run, the creator fails quickly and reaches the right answer before the competition, which is the whole advantage. The free version the speaker offers is the same logic without ads: start multiple accounts, post variations of every video with a changed hook, song, or call to action, and learn ten times faster than by judging each new video on its own.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a call to run paid ads: the source's free method is posting variations across multiple accounts, and the Facebook ads are only how Bartlett's team does it, not what the speaker tells the viewer to buy.
- Taken as permission to copy whatever works: the experiments are meant to identify which parts of the speaker's own conversations interest people, so testing a stranger's video would answer a different question.
