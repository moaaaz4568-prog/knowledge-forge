# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DHUBTTdow9s — redirect

## Term

Redirect appears twice in this source, where it names the most effective way to grow on social media: take attention that is already there and redirect it to your own work. The speaker's example is an artist who realized the algorithm prefers footage of crime to footage of painters showing their work, and whose phone camera by his front door caught a man on his property near Shaftesbury in Dorset. The hook makes the viewer stop with a sort of morbid curiosity, and the lack of audio, poorly made captions, and strange visuals signal a real person sharing something real. The advice that follows is to go where the attention is and then creatively redirect it, which the speaker says proves you do not need to be young or have a big juicy ass to succeed on socials.

## Synonyms

- redirects (the third-person form used in the source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a license to steal other people's audiences: the source's redirect is creative reuse of attention already gathered, not reposting the crime footage itself, and the artist's own work is what the attention lands on.
- Taken as proof that crime content is the best niche: the artist's video worked because the hook created morbid curiosity, and the speaker's lesson is about where attention sits, not about filming crime.
