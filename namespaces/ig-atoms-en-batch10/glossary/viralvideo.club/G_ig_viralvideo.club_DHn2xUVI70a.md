# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DHn2xUVI70a — Poppy AI

## Term

Poppy AI appears once in this source, where it names the tool the speaker uses to recreate his most viral videos, presented as the easy alternative to juggling the creative process like a clown. The pitch runs in three steps: upload the sexiest content, let Poppy figure out the repeatable viral formats, and let Poppy create the next viral script in seconds, optimized for engagement, retention, and whatever else makes people watch. The speaker's framing is that using tools is not laziness: ideas, formats, hooks, scripting, and pacing are listed as the work, and AI is offered as the way to stop doing it all by hand. The clip closes with a link and the code viral, marking it as a sponsored recommendation rather than a neutral review.
