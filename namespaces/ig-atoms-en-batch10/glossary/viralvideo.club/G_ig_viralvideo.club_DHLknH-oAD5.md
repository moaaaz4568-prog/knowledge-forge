# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DHLknH-oAD5 — stakes

## Term

Stakes appears twice in this source, where it names the pressure that forces the viewer to keep watching, one of only two Pixar rules the speaker keeps for social media. The other rule is having one core opinion: every big creator has one, and without it no one will watch whether the content is biceps, barbecuing, or competitive duck herding. Stakes are set with the hook, and the easiest way to do it is to create them almost immediately, which is why the speaker opened this very video the way he did. The two rules work together: the opinion gives the viewer a direction to follow, and the stakes give them a reason not to leave.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a demand for life-or-death drama in every video: the source's stakes are whatever makes the viewer need the outcome, and a small honest wager can carry them as well as a big one.
- Taken as one of Pixar's 22 rules: the speaker says only two of the 22 really make a difference for social media, so the stakes rule is his selection, not Pixar's list verbatim.
