# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DHDufQ9sMYX — four hours a week

## Term

Four hours a week appears once in this source, where it names the dream sold by a book the speaker says ruined his life: work four hours a week, automate everything, then spend the rest of the time chilling on a beach. The speaker calls the book the holy grail of entrepreneurship and admits it is a great book that could focus the mind on building the life you actually want. The correction follows at once: business is not quite as easy as Tim Ferris made out, people mistake such books for a cheat code for life, and the real secret to success is grinding harder than ever. The verdict is balanced rather than dismissive: the dream is real as a direction, but it never arrives as sunshine and rainbows.
