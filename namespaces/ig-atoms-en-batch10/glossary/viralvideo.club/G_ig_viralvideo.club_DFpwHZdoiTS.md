# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DFpwHZdoiTS — attention

## Term

Attention appears 7 times in this source, where it means the notice a creator attracts, and the speaker attacks the lie that all attention is good attention. A creator with 130,000 followers who made only around three grand in a year proves the point: running down the street bollock naked would win stares, but stares do not become money in a bank account. What matters instead is the right sort of attention from the right sort of people directed toward the right sorts of products or services, which is why making money with content puts the creator in the business game. That game demands the whole puzzle at once — taxes, finances, hiring, product, delivery, customer service, sales, networking, partnerships, and contracts — so attention alone cannot keep a creator alive.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as proof that attention is worthless: the source still treats attention as one necessary piece of the puzzle, and its complaint is only that attention without the rest of the business cannot pay.
- Taken as a promise that the right audience guarantees income: the speaker lists a dozen business skills the creator must also understand, so the right sort of attention is the start of the work, not the whole of it.
