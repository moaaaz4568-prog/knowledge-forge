# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DGLTsNKIR6W — visual pacifier

## Term

Visual pacifier appears once in this source, where it names the sneaky trick the biggest creators use to keep viewers hooked, and the speaker lists five ways to use it. The five are a progress bar so people know how much of their time the video will take, completing a boring task like applying makeup or getting dressed while talking about something unrelated, overstimulating with rapid frame changes so the viewer is always playing catch-up, putting something in the video that implies a reveal, and changing up the format so the viewer never knows what to expect next. The name fits the function: like a pacifier, the visual soothes and occupies the viewer while the real content plays. The speaker frames it bluntly as the reason viewers stay addicted to their phones and videos go mega viral.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a claim that any background activity counts: the source's examples are chosen to occupy the eye while the talking continues, and a random clip with no link to the video would not serve the same function.
- Taken as a compliment to the creators who use it: the speaker calls the trick sneaky and links it to phone addiction, so the term describes a retention device, not a mark of quality.
