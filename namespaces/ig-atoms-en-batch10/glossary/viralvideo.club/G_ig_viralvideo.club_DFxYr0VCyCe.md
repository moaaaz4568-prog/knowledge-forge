# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DFxYr0VCyCe — exposure therapy

## Term

Exposure therapy appears once in this source, where it names the first of two cures for feeling awkward in front of the camera: post more, create more content, and be in front of the camera more often, because the things we do become easier. The second cure is to break out of the comfort zone by deliberately becoming even more uncomfortable, saying the lines extremely loud and then whispering them, singing them, saying them in a funny accent, or doing them while dancing or running. The speaker demonstrates it on a script about sleep and circadian rhythms, going from a stiff, quiet delivery through the full range of loudness to a looser, more present one. Once both extremes are known, the normal delivery sits in a natural place, and the crazy practice makes the ordinary take easy by comparison.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a promise that posting more alone removes awkwardness: the source pairs volume with a second, deliberate discomfort exercise, and the speaker still calls the singing demonstration awkward to perform.
- Taken as a style the viewer should copy on camera: the loud, whispered, and sung versions are practice drills done alone at home, not the delivery the audience is meant to see.
