# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DIi5l2tIhQx — negative underdog

## Term

Negative underdog appears once in this source, where it names the second of two strategies behind a creator who gained over 200,000 followers in 30 days just by speaking his mind. The technique starts with something negative and a time frame, like a year ago the speaker was reading the Bible front to back and praying every night because he was so down bad and could not quit a lustful habit. The middle is the classic I tried but failed segment, begging God for forgiveness and relapsing again and again, and the end wraps it up with mission success. The speaker says this beaten pattern runs through sob stories on TV and underdog sports teams with surprise wins, which is why it works for Ken too.
