# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DGApBHyItMJ — pattern interrupt

## Term

Pattern interrupt appears twice in this source, where it names a trick content creators use to keep viewers watching by breaking the expected flow. It can be anything shocking, confusing, or so unique that no one has seen it before, and sometimes it is simply putting the emotional high point of the video first. The speaker's advice is practical rather than theoretical: give it a try in the next video and judge the difference it makes. The source even turns the term into its own call to action, inviting viewers who want to dive deeper to drop the word interrupt in the comments for details about Viral Video Bootcamp.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a demand to shock the viewer at any cost: the source lists confusion and uniqueness beside simply moving the emotional high point first, so a quiet reordering can count as the interrupt.
- Taken as a guarantee of retention: the speaker says only that the viewer will be amazed at the difference it makes, and the trick is one device among others, not a promise that any interruption holds attention.
