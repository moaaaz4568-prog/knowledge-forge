# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DHvWSwoIh_a — stop ignoring

## Term

Stop ignoring appears once in this source, where it is the stronger phrase the speaker tells creators to say instead of watch this, opening a list of word swaps like the earlier expose list. The swaps continue in the same pattern: do not say show, say reveal; do not say simple, say foolproof; do not say weak, say pathetic; do not say now, say before it's too late. Each replacement trades a neutral instruction for a phrase that pressures the viewer, and stop ignoring does that work for watch this by implying the viewer is already neglecting something. The list closes as before with the pitch: do not say I will never get views, say I will join viral video club.
