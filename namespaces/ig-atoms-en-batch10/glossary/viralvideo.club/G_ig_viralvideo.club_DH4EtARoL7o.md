# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DH4EtARoL7o — algorithm whack-a-mole

## Term

Algorithm whack-a-mole appears once in this source, where it names the losing game of chasing platform rules instead of getting good, after the head of Instagram denied penalizing content made in other apps. The speaker does not believe the denial: non-native edits mysteriously get half the reach, just as TikTok supposedly does not nerf videos edited outside CapCut. The point is that obsessing over algorithms means the creator has already lost, because creators pulling millions of views use Premiere, DaVinci, or TikTok's editor indifferently. The alternative is to post, adapt, keep moving, and focus on the things you can control, which is making killer content rather than swatting each new rumor.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as proof that Instagram does penalize external edits: the source only reports the speaker's suspicion from observed reach, and the quoted denial is left standing beside it.
- Taken as a ban on learning how platforms work: the speaker's target is obsessing over the algorithm at the expense of craft, not a single informed check of what the platform rewards.
