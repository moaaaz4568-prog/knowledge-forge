# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DItQGzTosRl — product pairing

## Term

Product pairing appears once in this source, where it names the second of two strategies behind a finance creator who made over a hundred thousand pounds in just two hours. The first strategy is that his product is always in plain sight, so the clothing is a feature but not the star of the show. Product pairing itself means mastering attention first and then identifying the perfect product fit for the audience already built, because it is so much easier to sell when you build a cult-like audience first. The order is the lesson: attention first, product second, and the pairing is the fit between the two.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as advice to sell to any large audience: the source stresses the perfect product fit for the audience built, so a mismatched product would not sell even to a cult-like following.
- Taken as a claim that the product should hide: the first strategy keeps the product in plain sight as a feature, and the pairing only works because the audience already sees it.
