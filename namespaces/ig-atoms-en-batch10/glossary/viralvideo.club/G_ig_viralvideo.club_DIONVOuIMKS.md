# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DIONVOuIMKS — mastermind

## Term

Mastermind appears once in this source, where it names the group that changed MrBeast's trajectory: after six years to reach twenty eight thousand nine hundred and twenty seven followers, he started a mastermind with four other creators. The group hopped on calls every day and studied virality like scientists, and they learned four times faster than they could alone. The speaker's question is what changed, and the answer is the feedback the group provided: daily calls, shared study, and the speed of learning together. The lesson is that the power of feedback, not a new camera or format, is what turned a slow six years into one year to a million.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a claim that any group chat is a mastermind: the source's group met on calls every day and studied virality like scientists, so the mastermind is a disciplined study group, not a casual chat.
- Taken as proof that feedback alone creates a million followers: the speaker credits the speed of learning, but the six prior years of publishing are still the base the group accelerated.
