# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DIvtXP3IEyH — showing up

## Term

Showing up appears twice in this source, where it names the act that does 90% of the work: post the content, write the book, run the race, do the thing you want to do. The speaker's numbers make the case: out of every thousand people who start a book, only 30 finish it, and of those 30, six become published writers, while finishing a novel alone puts a writer in the most elite crowd. To be in the top 1% of podcasters you only have to upload 21 podcasts, because most people never reach that count. The point is focus on what you have power over: you cannot control whether you make it, but just by showing up and staying in the game, you massively increase your odds.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a claim that showing up guarantees making it: the source says you do not have power over whether you make it, and showing up only massively increases the odds.
- Taken as advice to publish anything regardless of quality: the speaker's examples are finishing a novel and uploading 21 podcasts, both acts of completion, not of posting unfinished work.
