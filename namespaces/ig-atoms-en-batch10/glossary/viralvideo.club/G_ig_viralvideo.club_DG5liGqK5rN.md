# G_ig_viralvideo.club_DG5liGqK5rN — in medias res

## Term

In medias res appears twice in this source, where it names the technique of jumping straight into the action, illustrated by the opening scene of Fight Club with a gun in the guy's mouth. The viewer has no idea what is going on but is hooked and has to keep watching to see how it all unfolds, and only once invested does the story rewind back to the start and let itself unfold. The speaker says the technique is not just for Hollywood: a creator can open with the most shocking, funny, or dramatic moment, make the audience wonder what is happening, and then show how it all began. The missing ingredient it supplies is the taste of the most intense moment up front, before any setup has earned the viewer's patience.

## Live Example

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as advice to start every video with violence: the source's examples include a shocking, funny, or dramatic moment, and the Fight Club gun is an illustration of intensity, not a prescription for guns.
- Taken as a license to skip the story: the technique rewinds to the start once the audience is invested, so the beginning still has to be told, just after the hook instead of before it.
