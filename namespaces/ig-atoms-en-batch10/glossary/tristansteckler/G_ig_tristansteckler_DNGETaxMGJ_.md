# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNGETaxMGJ_ — FOMO

## Term

FOMO is the fear of missing out Tristan refuses to fake with closing deadlines: telling a prospect the doors close now is a lie nobody believes, so it moves nobody. The word appears four times, beside buy, closing, and bought, and it is the video's whole lesson. His real version is social proof: talk about how many other people have already bought, because people who know what you know make the outsider feel he missed out. The turn is the confession underneath: the fast action bonus worked only while leads were warm, and once cold leads asked who he even was, the lack of skills showed and the money stopped.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on all urgency: Tristan still builds urgency, but through the gap and the burning house, not through a fake deadline.
- Read as proof that social proof alone closes: the video ends on skills paying the bill, and FOMO only works once the prospect sees the gap.
