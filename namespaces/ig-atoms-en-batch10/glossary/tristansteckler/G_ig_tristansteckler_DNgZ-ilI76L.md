# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNgZ-ilI76L — nothing like a salesperson

## Term

Nothing like a salesperson is how Tristan says reps must look, act, sound, taste, and smell on a call: nobody arrives pre-sold with a credit card anymore, and every prospect expects a scripted pitch that takes money instead of helping. The phrase appears once, beside pre-sold, credit card, and scripted, and it is the video's whole goal. He learned it from taking three thousand plus calls and reviewing fifteen thousand hours of calls across teams and industries. The frame he refuses is the opener that asks whether the prospect still has questions: the call must not feel like a sales process at all.

## Live Example

> "There's nobody who's watched hundreds of hours of your content that's ready to come on with their credit card and ready to pay you." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DNgZ-ilI76L.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that prospects never arrive ready to buy: Tristan says no one arrives pre-sold anymore, and the rep must earn the difference instead of assuming it.
- Read as a ban on all structure in a call: the goal is to not feel like a salesperson, and the video still teaches a goal for the opening minutes.
