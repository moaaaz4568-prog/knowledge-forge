# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNjKCoUx22u — I don't quit

## Term

I don't quit is the three-word belief Tristan says will change a life: if you do not believe in yourself, no one will. The phrase appears twice, beside life, quit, and money, and it is the video's whole spine. He grew up with financial stress, a sewer line lawsuit lost for twelve years, an inheritance stolen, and food and pornography as the coping that took him to three hundred pounds. The turn came at the decision to lose a hundred and ten pounds in five months, and the same refusal carried the failed first business into high-ticket sales at forty or fifty calls a week.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that willpower alone builds a business: Tristan pairs the refusal to quit with learning to market, sell, and fulfill, and the first business failed without those skills.
- Read as a story that ends at the weight loss: the weight loss is the proof of the belief, and the sales career is where the same belief is applied.
