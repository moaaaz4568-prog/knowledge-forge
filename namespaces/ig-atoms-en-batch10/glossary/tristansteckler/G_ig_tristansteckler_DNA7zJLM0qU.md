# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNA7zJLM0qU — framing

## Term

Framing is what Tristan says sales comes down to: a mechanic who knows the fix is one screw can either shrug and fix it, or frame it as a fast favor squeezed into a full shop, and the framing decides the reaction. The word appears once, beside fix, mechanic, and screw, and it opens the video's lesson. Most reps lack the ability to communicate properly, because they were never taught it, and they stay in their comfort zone without interacting with people. The proof is the bully prospect: if the rep will not stand in the gap and face the confrontation, he gets bulldozed, while confident people know exactly where they are headed the second their mouth opens.
