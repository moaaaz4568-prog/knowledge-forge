# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMaaMd7onIG — art of persuasion

## Term

The art of persuasion is getting the prospect to thank you while taking your beliefs on as their own: praise moves people where pressure cannot, and Tristan demonstrates it with a shaving husband. The phrase appears once, beside praise, reward, and discipline, and it names the video's whole lesson. He contrasts the ignored complaint about stubble with the praised clean shave, then applies the same move to sales: kudos to the client for caring, instead of a one-time offer ultimatum. The second half of the art is discipline: when a belief blocks the outcome, the rep steps in and changes how the prospect thinks, the way a trainer changes what a stuck client eats.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick for manipulating people: Tristan frames it as rewarding the behavior you want, and he warns to use it carefully.
- Read as a ban on ever disciplining a prospect: he says plainly there are two ways on a call, reward or discipline, and discipline is what stops a bad belief from standing.
