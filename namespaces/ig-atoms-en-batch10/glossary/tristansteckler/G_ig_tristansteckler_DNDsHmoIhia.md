# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNDsHmoIhia — high performer question

## Term

A high performer question is the one question Tristan tells interviewers to ask when the employer says do you have any questions: in your eyes, what does the high performer look like. The phrase appears twice, beside interview, job, and shoe-in, and it is the video's whole method. It works twice over: the employer talks about himself and his opinions, which everybody likes doing, and he hands over the exact recipe of attributes and skills he is looking for. The follow-ups then measure the candidate against it: how close am I to that high performer, and what would I need to accomplish in a year to prove the hire right.

## Synonyms

- high performer (the shorter form the source uses when the question is shortened)

## Live Example

> "You're going to interview a lot of people who can do the job, but in your eyes, what does the high performer look like?" — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DNDsHmoIhia.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to flatter the interviewer: the question works because it gets the employer to state the hiring criteria, not because it charms him.
- Read as a guarantee of the job: Tristan says the question makes you a shoe-in only if you can then show how you fulfill every attribute.
