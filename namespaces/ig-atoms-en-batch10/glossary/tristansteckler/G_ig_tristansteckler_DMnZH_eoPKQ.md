# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMnZH_eoPKQ — five questions

## Term

Five questions are the certainty check Tristan runs before any pitch: intent to change, a problem that needs solving, burning desire, significance behind the change, and personal ownership. The phrase appears once, beside vision, confidence, and fire, and it follows his two closing questions. The close itself never asks whether the prospect has questions, because that hands over the steering wheel, and instead asks whether the move aligns with their vision and what would need to be true to say yes with full confidence. With all five answers in place he can move into the pitch knowing they will buy, because the hesitation has already been converted into clarity.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a script to run before listening: the five questions come after the two closing questions, and only once the prospect has already said the move aligns with their vision.
- Read as a guarantee that the prospect will buy: Tristan says the five answers give him confidence to pitch, not a promise the sale is closed.
