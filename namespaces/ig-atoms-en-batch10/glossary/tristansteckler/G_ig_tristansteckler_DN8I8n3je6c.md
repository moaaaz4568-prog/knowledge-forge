# G_ig_tristansteckler_DN8I8n3je6c — pushy

## Term

Pushy is the word a rep uses to describe his own selling: he does not want to be pushy, because pushy makes prospects feel uncomfortable. The word appears twice, beside uncomfortable, comfortable, and millionaire, and it is the video's whole exchange. Tristan refuses the Andy Elliott answer: the point is not to celebrate the discomfort, it is that what the rep is doing is uncomfortable, and if it were comfortable everybody would be a millionaire. The warning is about expectations: tell a prospect the program will be easy and he will do badly in it, because anyone who has made a lot of money knows it is very, very hard.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on ever making a prospect uncomfortable: Tristan says the work itself is uncomfortable, and the issue is the expectation, not the feeling.
- Read as an endorsement of the Andy Elliott line: he names it only to reject it, and says glad-it-makes-them-uncomfortable is not the point.
