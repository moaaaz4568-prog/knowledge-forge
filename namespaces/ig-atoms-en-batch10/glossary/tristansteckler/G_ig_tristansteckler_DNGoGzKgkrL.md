# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNGoGzKgkrL — status

## Term

Status is what a rep lowers every time he makes himself openly available to anyone at any time: things that have value are scarce, and open availability tells the prospect the rep does not value himself. The word appears three times, beside value, scarce, and prospect, and it is the video's whole charge. The first leak is the reschedule offer when the prospect looks away at a glare or a kid or a phone call, and the fix is two questions: is now a good time, and do you need to step away. The second leak is talking about testimonials and personal money before the prospect has burning desire, which is a doctor prescribing without knowing what is wrong.

## Live Example

> "Things that have value... are scarce. They are not abundant." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DNGoGzKgkrL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule to never reschedule a distracted prospect: the fix is to ask whether now is a good time, and the prospect either steps away or gives full attention.
- Read as a ban on testimonials: Tristan only bans leading with them before the prospect's burning desire is established.
