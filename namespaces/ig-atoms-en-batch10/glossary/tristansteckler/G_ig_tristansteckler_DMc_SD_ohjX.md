# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMc_SD_ohjX — sell them the exit

## Term

To sell them the exit is Tristan's first step for the summertime stall: when the prospect wants a couple months, he offers the delay himself so the tension drops. The phrase appears once, beside couple months, tension, and summer, and it opens a four-step sequence. Step two then asks how long they have already been looking to change, and step three asks whether starting six months ago would have left them in a better position. Step four closes on the head start: get going today so the past does not repeat six months down the road.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Sell them the exit so we could drop the tension." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DMc_SD_ohjX.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as actually letting the prospect walk away for the summer: selling the exit is a tension drop, and the very next step pulls them back into the conversation.
- Read as a script for the summer only: Tristan says the same stall shows up at New Year and Christmas, and the four steps are the general answer to delay.
