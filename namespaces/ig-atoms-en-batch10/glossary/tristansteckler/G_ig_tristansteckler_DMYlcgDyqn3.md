# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMYlcgDyqn3 — close rate

## Term

A close rate is the number Tristan uses to test a rep who claims to be good: the caller says he closes at twenty-five percent, and Tristan asks whether that is pretty good to him. The phrase appears once, beside fifty or sixty percent, double, and good to great, and it anchors the whole exchange. The caller wants to know what Tristan does and how he can help, but he is also trying to take the frame, so Tristan answers by questioning the rate instead of pitching. The move is to lift the hood and understand the situation first, because the gap between twenty-five percent and fifty or sixty percent is the sale.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a verdict that twenty-five percent is a bad close rate: Tristan never states a good number, he only asks the caller whether his own rate satisfies him.
- Read as a pitch for a one-size-fits-all solution: he says plainly the work depends on the person and what they are actually struggling with.
