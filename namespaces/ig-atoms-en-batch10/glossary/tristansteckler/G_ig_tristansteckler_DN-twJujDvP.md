# G_ig_tristansteckler_DN-twJujDvP — imperfect action

## Term

Imperfect action is how John Franco built a ninety-day coaching business from nothing: he pulled the phone out and ripped content over and over, without the nice camera, the nice mic, or the perfect script. The phrase appears once, beside PlayStation, porn recovery, and cameras, and it is the video's whole moral. Richard thought he had scammed a kid out of a hundred dollars, then watched him sell his PlayStation and two cameras to get started, post four or five times a day, and make nine thousand in his first month. The story ends at a hundred thousand a month because Franco dumped every dollar back into the business instead of showing off.
