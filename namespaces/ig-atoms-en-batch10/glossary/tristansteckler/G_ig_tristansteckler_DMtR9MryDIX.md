# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMtR9MryDIX — think about it

## Term

To think about it is the stall John repeats while Tristan keeps asking what is coming up for him: a couple of days to think, the same question again and again, and no answer underneath. The phrase appears three times, beside John, couple of days, and bills, and it is the whole hundred-word exchange. The caller says the offer sounds really good, then stalls, and Tristan answers by naming the pressure honestly: it is the end of the month, he needs to pay his bills, they have been on the call for two hours, and he needs the prospect to join now. The video is the stall meeting the deadline, with no softening in between.

## When the Meaning Breaks
