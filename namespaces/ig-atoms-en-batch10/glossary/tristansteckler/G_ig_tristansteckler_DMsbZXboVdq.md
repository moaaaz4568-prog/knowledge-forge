# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMsbZXboVdq — sleep on a decision

## Term

To sleep on a decision is the twenty-four-hour stall Tristan answers with a pillow joke: he is not selling pillows, and there is nothing to sleep on. The phrase appears once, beside accountability, crossroads, and month, and it is the video's whole objection. The prospect has never trained in a program like this, so Tristan asks whether he believes the program can get him to ten thousand a month, and what accountability is actually worth to him. The fork is the close: keep making decisions the same way and getting similar results, or make a different type of decision and get different results.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on ever thinking a decision over: Tristan only attacks the sleep as a delay tactic, and he asks what information the prospect still needs before deciding.
- Read as a claim that accountability alone is worth ten thousand a month: the video ties accountability to the belief that the rep can get the prospect there, not to a price tag.
