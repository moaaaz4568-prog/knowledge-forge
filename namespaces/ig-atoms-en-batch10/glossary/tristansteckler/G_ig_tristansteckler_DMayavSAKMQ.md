# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMayavSAKMQ — high-ticket sales

## Term

High-ticket sales is the game Tristan says changed his life and the lives of Chris and Tatum across town: fifteen thousand, twenty-three thousand, a hundred and twenty-six thousand, a hundred and fifty thousand. The phrase appears twice, beside skills game, consistency, and ceiling, and it is the video's whole subject. Sales is a skills game, so the more skills you have the more money you make, and the separator is consistency: showing up every single day regardless of how you feel. The test he gives is simple: do what you say you will do, put the left foot after the right foot, and there is no ceiling on what you can achieve.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that high-ticket sales pays a hundred and fifty thousand: the numbers are other people's results, and Tristan ties the outcome to skills and consistency, not to the offer.
- Read as a claim that Tristan alone is the guy: he names Chris and Tatum beside himself, and the lesson is the daily showing up, not the personality.
