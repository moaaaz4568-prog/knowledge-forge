# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNRPGr0yAoL — pause

## Term

A pause is what Tristan uses instead of um: say two plus two, answer four without the um, and you look smart, because the um makes you look like an idiot. The word appears twice, beside um, mistake, and smart, and it is the video's first lesson. The second move after a mistake is to repeat himself, and the larger lesson is the same: how you communicate is everything in sales. Inexperienced reps read the script like the Bible, line for line, without listening to the prospect and while saying things the prospect never said, and every one of those habits lowers status in the prospect's mind.

## Live Example

> "If I say what's two plus two and you go um four, you look dumb." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DNRPGr0yAoL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on ever saying um anywhere: the rule is for the sales call, where the filler costs status, not for ordinary speech.
- Read as a claim that pausing alone makes a rep good: the pause is one move beside listening and not putting words in the prospect's mouth.
