# G_ig_tristansteckler_DN0alo_WrGW — pray about this

## Term

To pray about this is the stall Tristan meets with a shared faith: the prospect needs to pray about seven thousand dollars first, and Tristan answers that he is Christian as well. The phrase appears once, beside seven K, pray, and lead, and it is the whole sixty-six-word exchange. He offers to lead, or to let the prospect lead, and they go live to ask what God told him. The video ends before any answer lands, so the stall is shown, not solved.
