# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNONc1qg12Z — scarcity

## Term

Scarcity is the mindset Tristan catches with a red-and-white exercise: close your eyes, name everything red, then everything white, and you see only what you were looking for. The word appears five times, beside abundance, mindset, and reasons, and it is the video's whole psychology. The prospect keeps looking for a reason not to do it — no time, the parents, the counselor — and the guarantee is that he will find those reasons if he keeps looking. The flip is abundance: the successful person looks for what he has and what opportunities he has, and the guy who thinks that way does whatever needs doing in the moment.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that the red exercise is real psychology: Tristan says plainly this is not psychology, it is a demonstration of where attention goes.
- Read as a dismissal of the prospect's college debt: he names the parents paying for college and the dropout, then asks what the scarcity mindset has cost him.
