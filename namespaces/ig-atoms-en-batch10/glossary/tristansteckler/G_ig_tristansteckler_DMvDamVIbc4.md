# G_ig_tristansteckler_DMvDamVIbc4 — which hard

## Term

Which hard is the choice Tristan puts to a stuck prospect: being rich is hard because of the risk, and being poor is hard too, so the question is which hard do you ultimately want. The phrase appears once, beside rut, mirror, and business, and it is the video's whole argument. He walks through the alternatives first: working for someone else means clocking in and out and hating the mirror, and starting a business means inventory, stress, an LLC, and managing people. None of it is easy, and that is the point: the skills to hit consistent ten-thousand-dollar months are a big step, and the only real choice is which hard life to live.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that money removes the hard parts: Tristan says being rich is hard too, and the choice is between hards, not between hard and easy.
- Read as a dismissal of the prospect's rut: he hears the stuck feeling first and walks through each alternative before asking the question.
