# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNQXemXsRNA — brevity

## Term

Brevity is the term Tristan uses for getting from point A to point B in as few words as possible: it keeps him grounded, confident, and calm when someone imposes a frame on him. The word appears once, beside frame, door, and grounded, and it is the video's whole defense. People impose frames left and right — why are you trying so hard, why are you looking so sad — and every defensive answer walks through the door they opened. The sales version is the same trap: prospects ask about success rates to pull the rep into explain mode, and the rep over-explains because he thinks more information will buy a guarantee.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to answer every frame with silence: brevity is the short grounded answer, and the video shows him naming the frame move out loud.
- Read as a claim that information never helps a sale: Tristan's point is that information does not fix a risk-averse identity, and the work is changing how the prospect sees the world.
