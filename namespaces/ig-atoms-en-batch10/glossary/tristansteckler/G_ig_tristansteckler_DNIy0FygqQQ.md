# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNIy0FygqQQ — relationship selling

## Term

Relationship selling is what a Harvard Business School conclusion says all selling would become in the twenty-first century: all success in business based on relationships, and the sale taking care of itself. The phrase appears once, beside relationship, sale, and trust, and it is the video's whole frame. The industry is full of people who have not achieved what they sell, and prospects arrive burned and expecting a bait and switch, so the rep must look, act, sound, and feel nothing like a salesperson. The rule underneath is congruence: never ask a prospect to do what you would not do yourself, and never talk about thirty thousand a month you have not made.
