# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNS8OkYyuCL — MOUNT NUT

## Term

MOUNT NUT is Tristan's acronym for the five reasons customers do not buy: no money, no time, no need, no urgency, no trust. The phrase appears once, beside money, urgency, and trust, and it is the video's whole framework. It is never about the money, he says, because only five or ten percent simply do not have it, and discounting only validates the customer's fear. The key phrase is urgency: the rep's job is to create the sense of it, and the leverage question is what the prospect has specifically tried over the last three years to make the money.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "There's five reasons customers don't buy: no money, no time, no need, no urgency, no trust." — sources/S_ig_tristansteckler_DNS8OkYyuCL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that money never matters: Tristan concedes five or ten percent truly lack it, and the point is that price is usually a symptom of missing skills.
- Read as a script for discounting: he says discounting sends the worst signal, and the answer is leverage from what the prospect has tried.
