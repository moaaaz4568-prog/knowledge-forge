# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNByz4uyYe2 — four questions

## Term

Four questions are what Leo gives Miguel while he thinks it over: do you think it is going to work, are you going to use it, can you afford it, and are you the main decision maker. The phrase appears once, beside Visa, Amex, and proud, and it is the video's whole close. Miguel needs a day to think, and Leo narrows the stall to the one real objection: Miguel does not know if it is going to work, because he does not have access to it yet. The turn is the belief split: the only two reasons not to do it are not believing in Leo or not believing in yourself, and Miguel says it is himself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a script that fits every think-it-over stall: Leo tailors the four to Miguel, and the fourth question only applies when the prospect is not the decision maker.
- Read as proof that the card close always works: the video ends with Miguel choosing Amex, but the four questions are the diagnosis, not the close itself.
