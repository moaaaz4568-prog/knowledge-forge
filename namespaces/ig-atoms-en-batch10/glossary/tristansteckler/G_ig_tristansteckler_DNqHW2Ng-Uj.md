# G_ig_tristansteckler_DNqHW2Ng-Uj — certainty

## Term

Certainty is what the prospect wants before deciding on the first call: he does not want to rush, and Tristan answers that the decision has been waiting twelve months, not one day. The word appears four times, beside action, devil, and decision, and it runs the whole exchange. The devil on one shoulder always says not to take action, and the angel on the other says he could do it and be successful, so the only place certainty lives is in taking action. The close is the identity question: who does he need to become in this moment to get where he wants to go, and the prospect answers that he needs to take action.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that action removes all doubt: Tristan says certainty comes through action, and the prospect still answers that he wants to be committed, not that he is certain.
- Read as a dismissal of careful decisions: the prospect's twelve months of waiting is the evidence, and Tristan's point is about the delay, not about deciding blind.
