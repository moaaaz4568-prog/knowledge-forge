# G_ig_tristansteckler_DN2_aljWqje — standard of excellence

## Term

A standard of excellence is what Tristan holds the prospect to instead of giving them a back door: the rep's job is to help the prospect overcome himself, not to process credit cards. The phrase appears once, beside comfort, back door, and negligence, and it is the video's spine. The prospect was never going to be fully confident, because no one is ever one hundred percent certain of the right move, and the only certain thing is death. The standard inside the program is accountability at every step, and throwing the towel in on a person you believe you can help is negligence on the rep's part.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a license to ignore a prospect's overwhelm: Tristan names the pressure and the sticker shock first, then asks whether the prospect is mentally and emotionally ready.
- Read as a claim that reps must guarantee results: the standard is about holding the prospect accountable, and Tristan says plainly there are zero guarantees.
