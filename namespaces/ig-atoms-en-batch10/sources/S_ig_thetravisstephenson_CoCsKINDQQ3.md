Thing that I suggest to everyone now, everyone, is to work really, really hard on thinking like a producer instead of a consumer. When you see ads, ask yourself why did that work? Why did I stop my scroll here? Why am I reading this? Don't just read it and go, hey, this is great. Why is that happening? What is it about? Do I actually want this product? Because if I actually want this product, how did it show up in my feed? Is the algorithm that good? Wow, the algorithm is pretty good. I'm learning more about the algorithm. Do I want to buy it? What made me buy it? Was it like a story? Was it the stats? Because what kind of buyer am I? Who am I more likely to appeal to? As soon as you start turning on the producer side of your brain, an entirely new world opens up to you.
