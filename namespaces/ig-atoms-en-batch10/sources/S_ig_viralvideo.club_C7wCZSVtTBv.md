What can poker teach you about going viral? Well, let me ask you this: What makes poker interesting? Is it the cards, the chips, or bluffing in sunglasses? I'd argue that it's none of these things. If you've ever played poker without money, then you'll know that it just turns into everyone going all in with shitty hands because there's nothing to lose. But when our money is on the line and our chips represent real value, well now everyone's hooked and trying to play as smart a game as they possibly can. Fold. Raise. Fold. Because now there's something to lose. And the same is true for viral videos. When you fail to introduce stakes, there's nothing to keep the audience hooked. Because if it doesn't matter to you, then it doesn't matter to them. But as soon as there's something to lose for you, now no one can look away.
