Here's one simple and very underrated question I ask to every buyer I sit across the table from. When he's interested in a specific project I'm listing, a specific studio apartment or villa, I ask him, "Why do you think of buying this? What do you have in mind?" That way I get to understand, is it for investment or does he want to live in it with the family? Does he want to rent it out and make rental income? Because if he tells me a reason that I think doesn't align with his investment strategy, for example, I'm gonna tell him. And when I tell him, I'm gonna offer an alternative. That way he's gonna trust me more because I didn't just focus on selling him what we had on the table. I focused on giving him what I think is best.
