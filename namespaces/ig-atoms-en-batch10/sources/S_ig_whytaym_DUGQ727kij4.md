One of the biggest things salespeople miss out on when cold calling a prospect is familiarity. They always tend to start the call by saying, "Hi, is this X?" Why are you doing that? When you do that, you're just telling the prospect that you don't know them and their guard is up. What you need to do is cold call them with confidence and familiarity. Make them feel like you already know them. Say their name, ask them how are they, match their tone, match their energy. If you do this right, you have a higher chance of booking the call with that prospect.
