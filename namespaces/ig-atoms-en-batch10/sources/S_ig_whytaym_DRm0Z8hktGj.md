Here's one of the biggest mistakes I used to do when I first started sales that I see a lot of salespeople still do. It's where they only focus on selling their company. Why is this a mistake? Because I can call up 10 other companies who offer the same exact thing you do and maybe even better. So clients don't care about what you offer, how you can help them. People only care about how you can help them once they know you care. So start selling yourself first, start making the client like you, start making the client feel like they trust you. Once all of that is done, then you can jump into the technical work and actually talk about your company, talk about the technicalities and close the deal. So stop focusing on your company, no matter how good it is. Start focusing on selling yourself to the client.
