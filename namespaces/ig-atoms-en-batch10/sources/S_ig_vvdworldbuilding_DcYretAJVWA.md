If you're a fan of any of these titles, you're already familiar with this genre. It's called raising cubs. These are stories revolving around a single dynamic: an adult protagonist ends up responsible for a powerful child, and the plot is parenting them throughout the dangerous world. This has been a structural genre in Chinese web fiction and becoming a popular trope in Western media. They're almost always lighthearted, focusing on an unlikely emotional bond between the parental figure and the younger character. And a strong example of this other than Logan or The Mandalorian is the Japanese animated film Wolf Children. The mother needs to raise her half-werewolf children to grow and find their own paths in life. The director Mamoru Hosoda wanted to contribute to this genre as a way for the audience to share an experience that no one else has had before: not just being a caretaker, but caring for something that can be seen as more powerful than yourself.
