The number one thing that you need to think about when you start to run Facebook ads or YouTube ads or ads in general or even just start a business is lifetime value. See, most people start a business and they have no concept of what lifetime value is. They just think, well, I'm gonna try to make some sales and put some things together. And to be honest with you, I never knew about lifetime value until five or six years into my career. But lifetime value is simply put, what is every subscriber worth to me over the lifetime of my relationship with them? So if I get a subscriber today, I need to know in my mind I'm gonna make X off of them. So to give you an example, in the last five years, on average, I make twelve dollars for every single lead that comes into my company. What that allows me to do is that allows me to then go out and spend six, seven, eight, nine, ten dollars to get a lead because I know in the lifetime of that relationship, I'll be able to turn that into twelve. And now we have a profitable business. I can now put money into a machine that runs ads twenty four hours a day, seven days a week, and my money is going to turn into more money via the systems that I create behind the scenes, the opt in pages, the email sequences, the sales pages, the offers. You put all that together in a mechanism that can automate all of this, and now you're utilizing lifetime value. But it's really hard to do that if you don't think about what your lifetime value is before you get a campaign started.
