Voice agents suck, and everyone knows that. I made a video where I explain why getting yourself a voice agent is a bad idea, but there is one thing I want to make clear. The only use case for a voice agent which is not bad is when they're used as a follow-up method. So let's say you run ads, and whenever the prospect fills out the form, instead of a simple SMS message or an email, they get a call instead. Using voice agents this way is much better because your booking rates are higher compared to if you'd send an SMS message or an email, and you don't have to hire anyone to call the prospect within two minutes after filling out the form. But still, those AI receptionists or, God forbid, AI cold callers suck. I'll say voice agent as a follow-up method is the only good use case for using voice agents.
