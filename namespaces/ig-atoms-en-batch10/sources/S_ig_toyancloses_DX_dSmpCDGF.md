The online sales world taught a generation of closers to sell to broke people and then called it high ticket. I don't say that to be harsh, I say it because it's true and it matters. Because if you learned sales in the online coaching space, that most people in this space did, then the buyers you were trained on were almost always people who were just fucking struggling. People that had a pain point sharp enough to make them reach for a credit card that they probably shouldn't have done. People who were desperate enough that urgency worked and scarcity worked. Just a few well-placed emotional triggers and reframes could move them across the line. And look, that is a skill, but it's just not the skill that you need when the buyer has money. Because when the buyer has money, they don't have the same urgency, they don't have the same pain. They don't have that same desperation and they've done this before. They know what pressure tactics feel like. And the moment they sense that you're running a pattern on them, the trust is gone. And without trust, there are no deals being made at the top end of the market. And what wealthy buyers respond to is completely different. They respond to expertise, to calm authority, to someone who clearly understands their world and speaks to them like an equal. A conversation that feels like a consultation and not a sales call. That is a completely different environment. And most people in this space have never been trained for that environment. Know which buyer you've been trained for and then decide if that's actually the room that you want to be in.
