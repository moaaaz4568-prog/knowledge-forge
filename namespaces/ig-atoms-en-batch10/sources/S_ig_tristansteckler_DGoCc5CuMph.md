Here are three words you should never say on your sales calls. Number one: 'To be honest.' When you say that, it implies that you've been lying up until this point. Instead, say: 'Let's think of it this way.' Number two: 'Are you ready to buy?' Never say the word 'buy.' You want them to think of this as an investment rather than a purchase. Instead, say: 'Are you ready to move forward today?' Number three: 'Sorry to bother you.' This makes you look like a straight-up door-to-door salesman and it lowers the perceived value of you and the product that you're selling. Instead, say: 'I believe you'll find this useful.'
