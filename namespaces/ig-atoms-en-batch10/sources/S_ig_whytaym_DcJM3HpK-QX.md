To all sales people out there, please, when you get a lead of someone who wants to list their property with you, or someone who's inquiring on one of your listings, don't just try to message them at first. Try dropping them a call. Instead of sending them some emotionless message, with a call, you can connect more with the person. They can hear your voice, they can get to know more about you. You have more time to ask them more questions, understand exactly if and how you can help. So if you want to increase your chance of forming a deeper connection with your client, with your prospect, try calling them. Stop using the boring methods of just messaging.
