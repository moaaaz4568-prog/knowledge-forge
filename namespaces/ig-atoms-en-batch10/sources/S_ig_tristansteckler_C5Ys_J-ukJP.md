Sales is all about transferring certainty to your buyers. Do you get to the end of your sales calls and you're getting objections? It doesn't mean that the objections are real; it doesn't mean they need to speak to their partner; it doesn't mean that they don't have enough money. It means that they're not certain.

Because imagine if I told you I could guarantee that you make $100,000 in 30 days—no question—and it was just a $10k investment, and you were 100% certain that that would happen... You would do everything in your power to get that $10,000.

So the reason that your prospects are not buying from you is because they're not certain that the outcome that you're promising is actually going to come true. So the number one thing that's going to move the needle in your sales process is transferring certainty in your buyer that they're going to get the outcome.
