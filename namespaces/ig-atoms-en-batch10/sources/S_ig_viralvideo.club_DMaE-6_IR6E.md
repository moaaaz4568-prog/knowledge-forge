It's fucked up how high people's expectations are, how much they demand for so little input. There are over 40 million videos posted to Instagram per day, and a lot of the creators making this content do nothing else. This is their whole fucking world. They want it more than you. They have more skill than you, they have more experience than you, and they post significantly more than you. And yet when you post five times and don't get a result, you're angry with the algorithm and you start doubting yourself. There's nothing to doubt. You wouldn't expect to be extraordinary at basketball or tennis or any other sport after 10 minutes. But it's not the algorithm fucking you over, it's not that you're not good enough. It's that your mindset is all fucked up. You demanding extraordinary results from lackluster inputs is the very reason you're stuck. And the only thing you have to do to fix this is to think about how you become extraordinary in five years or 10 years, instead of asking how you get results today. Stop demanding of the world that you get things that you don't yet deserve, and start demanding of yourself that you become the sort of person for whom success is guaranteed.
