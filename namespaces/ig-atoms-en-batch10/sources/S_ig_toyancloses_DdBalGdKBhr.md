I will die on this hill. It is far easier to pre-handle an objection than it is to handle it at the end. People saying that you shouldn't pre-handle are people that fundamentally don't understand what pre-handling even is. The prospect does not know that the call goes in a certain order, right? A call is not linear. It doesn't have a start and a finish. We, the closer, are the only person that knows that. So the prospect will come into the call with their objection prepared, at least subconsciously, and all the way through the call, they will reveal to you what the objection is going to be based on what they talk about, based on what they try to not talk about, based on the answers that they give you, based on the language that they use. It is far easier for you and for them to get that out of the way and then do the pitch and it is a downstream feeling. It's easy for both of you instead of pushing a boulder uphill.
