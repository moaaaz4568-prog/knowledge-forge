A lot of salespeople get really nervous, especially when they're starting out to cold call company owners. And the biggest piece of advice I can give them is stop thinking that you need them. You don't need them. It's okay. No matter what you do, if you mess it up, if they get angry, then what? What's the worst case scenario? You call the other one. So you want to overcome this nervousness, start thinking from a position that you don't need them. Because when you need nothing, you fear nothing. You're not nervous about anything, and you go on about your day.
