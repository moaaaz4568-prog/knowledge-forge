One simple mistake cost me a lot of deals. When I'm sitting with the client, we're almost done with our conversation and he hits me with, "I'm ready, let's go. What's the next step?" Then I get all excited. I don't show composure. I'm so happy. I don't even know what the next steps are. This makes me lose the deal. This makes me look desperate. Instead, what you need to do is be so calm. Be like, "Sure, we can start the paperwork right away. I need X, Y, and Z papers from you, and then we can go ahead and get started." This way you show composure. You're not desperate. You're very used to this. You're not excited. Excited to work with them, but you're not excited for your commission.
