Yasim, yeah, what's a attention graph? A retention graph. It's how much of your audience you retain over the course of your video as a percentage. If a hundred people watch the first second, how many are watching the second second? Fifty percent, twenty percent. Basically, you want that graph to be as flat as possible. You want them to start watching, a hundred percent of them to start watching, and a hundred percent of them to finish. That's basically impossible to do. That's a perfect video. But if you're getting like fifty, sixty percent of people to watch even the first three seconds without swiping, you're doing pretty good. And if you're able to retain like thirty percent of people throughout the video, you've got a viral video on your hands basically.
