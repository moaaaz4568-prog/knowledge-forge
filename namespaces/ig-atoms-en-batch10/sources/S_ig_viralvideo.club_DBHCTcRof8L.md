The versus format: two characters in a head to head battle. This is especially effective when the characters aren't often pitted against each other. We've never before seen a drone go head to head against an F1 car. It's the same reason why videos of lions fighting bears go so viral. Here's an example of how you can do this in your video. If you're an artist, compete against a four year old and see which of you can sell the most artwork. Let's say you're a chef in a fine dining restaurant. Compete against someone who hosts the occasional dinner party and see whose food people like best. You're basically trying to pit two unlikely but surprisingly well matched competitors, and when you nail that, you get huge views.
