So there's this fascinating study that shows that when you offer people 24 varieties of jam, they buy significantly less than when you offer them six jams. What you see is what you get, take it or leave it. This is called decision fatigue. When we're offered too many choices, we end up making no choice at all. And so when I see creators going, like, subscribe, follow, share, comment, buy my things, come to my gig, and watch my videos on repeat while you sleep, I wish they'd realise that by asking for so much, you receive nothing at all. It's actually much better to figure out what the most important action people can take and then ask for just that. By doing this, we eliminate decision fatigue and make it significantly more likely that people will act in the way that we want them to.
