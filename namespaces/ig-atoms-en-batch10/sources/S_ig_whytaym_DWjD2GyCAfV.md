One of the most common objections you will face in sales as a salesperson is when you're about to sign the prospect, they tell you we're talking to someone else. The wrong approach I see a lot of salespeople do is double down on selling themselves. They're like, oh, we're better, I'm sure our price is better, we have a lot more case studies. But the right approach to take is don't sell it. Tell them, very good, I'm glad you're exploring your options. I would do the same exact thing if I was in your shoes. You take your time, think of what's better, and whatever you think is better in your gut, go forward with them. And if you have any questions for me about them, I'm more than happy to help. That way, he knows I'm not desperate for the sale. He knows I'm not pushy. He knows I want what's best for him. And that's more likely for him to move forward with me.
