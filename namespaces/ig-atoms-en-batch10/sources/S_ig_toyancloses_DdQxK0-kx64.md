When a prospect tells you they're considering an alternative business model or strategy, most closers make the same mistake. You immediately tell them why your program is better, which makes sense because you think that showing them more value will give them a higher chance of closing. But what you don't realize is the more that you defend your solution and attack theirs, the more that they feel like they're being sold to. And the danger of that is they start defending the alternative even if they weren't fully committed to it in the first place. Which unfortunately means you can talk someone into the alternative that you were trying to talk them out of. So instead, what I recommend is validating why their alternative makes sense, then help them uncover the hidden consequences of doing it themselves. So then what happens is they lower their resistance, they start questioning the alternative themselves instead of defending it, and they become far more receptive to your recommendation. So would you rather keep trying to prove that you're right, causing the prospect to defend the alternative and potentially lose the deal, or help them recognize the problems with the alternative themselves so that your solution becomes the only and obvious choice? And the way that you do that is by using a cascading anticipation loop. If you want to see an example of one, just watch this video again and pay attention to when the text changes colour.
