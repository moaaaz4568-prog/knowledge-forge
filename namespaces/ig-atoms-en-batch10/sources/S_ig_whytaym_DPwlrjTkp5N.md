You need to look at cold calling like a game of cards. You have three cards in your deck. Card number one is "Hi" with their name. Card number two is "How are you?" Card number three is maybe some compliment or something about their business. What poor salespeople do is they burn all these cards in the first ten seconds. They're like, "Hi, is this X? How are you?" Two cards out of three gone. What if they respond, "Yes, good." What are you going to do next? Set the meeting? Of course not. What you need to do is say "Hi" with their name. Once they respond to you, you match their tone by saying, "How are you?" Now you make them feel safe. Once you make them feel safe, say something about their business. Keep the conversation. They're comfortable. Their guard is down. And close the meeting.
