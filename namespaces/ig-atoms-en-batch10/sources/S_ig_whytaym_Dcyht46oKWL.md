One of the biggest mistakes a lot of real estate agents do when deciding the price of a property they need to list is that they look at the transactions from an overview perspective. They go and tell their seller what their average transactions are, but that's not very fair to the seller. What they need to do is dive a bit deeper. What is their unit's layout? What view is it? What floor is it? And then go ahead and do research accordingly. What's the last unit with this view, this layout, this floor or this floor range sell for? That way you go and suggest a fair price to the seller and you also have evidence to back it up when you pitch the price to the buyer.
