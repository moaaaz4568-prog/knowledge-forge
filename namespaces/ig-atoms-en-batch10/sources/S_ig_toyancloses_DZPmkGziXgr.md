Some of you watching this have absolutely no business being a closer, never mind trying to handle other people's objections, because you believe those objections yourself. 21 years in sales and I've closed over 110 million worth of deals, and one thing has remained true the entire time. You cannot help a prospect overcome a fear that you have not overcame yourself. Prospect says it's too expensive and deep down you agree. Prospect says I need more time and deep down you think that's reasonable. Prospect says I can't make such a big decision without sleeping on it and you think yeah I'd do the same thing. So what then happens? You stop leading, you start negotiating and you then retreat into scripts, frameworks, word tracks, but you don't actually believe in what you're actually saying. Yeah, you don't believe that the thing you're selling is worth fighting for and you don't believe that they should make the decision in the way that you're asking them to, because you don't make decisions in that way either. Objection handling is not about finding the perfect word tracks, it's about conviction. This is the part that nobody tells you. You cannot communicate certainty that you do not possess. Every objection is simply a mirror. It reflects back what you believe. If a prospect says it's too expensive and you've never invested heavily into your own growth, then that objection will hit you. If they say they need to think about it and you spend your life overthinking decisions, then that objection will hit you. If they say I'm scared it won't work and you're constantly playing it safe, then that objection will hit you too. Now watch what happens when you remove that internal conflict. Suddenly you're not defending the opportunity, you're defending the prospect's future and that changes everything. Because objection handling isn't pushing people into something that they don't want. It is clearing a path through the bullshit that they've built around themselves so that they can finally see the truth. The truth that their current situation is costing them more than the investment. The truth that waiting won't make them more certain. The truth that the fear that they're feeling today will still be there in six fucking months time from now if they don't do anything. So that is why the best closers don't fight objections, they dismantle them. I have received so many messages over the years from prospects thanking me for pushing them. Now they've got the result of the thing that I sold to them. So in summary, you cannot handle an objection that you believe in. You cannot hold a prospect to a standard that you do not hold yourself to. So handle your own objections, then start trying to handle other people's.
