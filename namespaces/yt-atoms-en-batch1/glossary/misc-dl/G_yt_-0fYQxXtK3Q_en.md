# G_yt_-0fYQxXtK3Q_en — referral system

## Term

Steve from Agency Outsite uses "referral system" for the organized way agency owners generate referrals on LinkedIn — asking for connections, introductions, and referrals as a standing process, not a one-off request. The phrase appears 1 time, as his claim that it is vital for agency owners to have a solid referral system because it can be a big part of their BISD process and pipeline building. Its neighbours are "LinkedIn", "referral networking", "BISD process", and "pipeline building": Steve does a ton of referral networking himself, connecting people and looking through their LinkedIn connections for referrals, and the video shows two quick ways to use LinkedIn to find the right connections to ask. The concrete method he teaches is to take the work out of it for the person you ask — send them a filtered search link that shows their first-person connections, so they can pick the people they know well enough to introduce you to.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as simply asking your contacts for referrals: in this source the referral system is a built process — Steve's own referral networking of connecting people and looking through LinkedIn connections — plus two specific LinkedIn ways to find the right people to ask, not a single request to a friend.
- Read as a guarantee of referrals: the source calls the system a big and strong element of the BISD process and pipeline building, and its payoff is growing your network by being intentional about who you meet — the asking still has to happen, and you still have to take the work out of it for the person making the introduction.
