# G_yt_-4GDgIod5-w_en — media banking

## Term

The speaker uses "media banking" for the leveraged work of managing ads for stacked businesses — each one paying 2,000 or more per month — the kind of service you can run almost entirely on your own now. The phrase appears 1 time, in his advice to take it on: if you can stack businesses that pay you 2,000 and more per month for something as leveraged as media banking, managing ads, do it, because today you can completely manage all the campaigns and create the assets yourself. Its neighbours are "stack businesses", "managing ads", "contractor", and "margins": the complexity that used to exist is gone, so you either do it yourself or rely on a contractor, and the margins are what make the stacking work. The concrete contrast is his own past pay: he was selling 500 euros a month, at most about 1,000 euros in a month, while the client was making over 100,200,000 euros a month.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as buying media inventory: in this source media banking is a service — managing ads for stacked client businesses — not buying ad space for yourself, and the leverage is that one person can now run all the campaigns and assets.
- Read as a promise of easy money: the speaker's own numbers cut the other way — he once earned 500 to 1,000 euros a month while a client made over 100 million — so the stacking only pays if the businesses actually pay 2,000-plus a month each.
