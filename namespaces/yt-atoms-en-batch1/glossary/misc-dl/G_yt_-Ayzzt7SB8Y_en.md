# G_yt_-Ayzzt7SB8Y_en — artboard

## Term

The tutor uses "artboard" for the canvas you design each logo on in Adobe Illustrator — the video is a quick tutorial on setting up Illustrator for logo design. The word appears 2 times, once singular and once plural, in the one tool tip: you can take the artboard tool and actually move the artboards around on the screen, adding them where you feel most comfortable when designing a logo. Its neighbours are "toolbar", "Illustrator", "logo designing", and "tool": the video shows how to set up Adobe Illustrator for graphic design projects, and the toolbar at the side can be dragged out and moved somewhere you can access it. The concrete move is the rearranging itself: the artboards go where you feel most comfortable, and the toolbar can be dragged out to somewhere you can access it, so the workspace fits how you design.
