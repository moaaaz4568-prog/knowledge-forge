# G_yt_-G34rwQFWcI_en — medical education

## Term

The narrator uses "medical education" for the health teaching at the heart of Dr Mike's content — mixed with familiar shows so that anyone can understand it, even with no medical background at all. The phrase appears 3 times, across a profile of Dr Mike, the internet doctor with over 10 million YouTube subscribers who still practices medicine two to three days a week: first he was accepted into a 7-year combined medical education program, three years undergraduate and four years med school, and later the channel mixed medical education with content familiar to the masses. Its neighbours are "platform", "medical dramas", "ESL", and "health": the fame was never the important part — what he cared about was having the platform to advocate for health — and the mix was reacting to medical dramas and medical scenes in non-medical shows, after a childhood sleeping in the living room with cockroaches and mice while he took ESL classes. The concrete formula is the mix itself: take something already familiar, like a beloved television show, and mix it with something new, like medical education, and the medical content becomes more palatable, engaging, and sticky.
