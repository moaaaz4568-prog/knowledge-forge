# G_yt_-IYYSObo2BM_en — true fans

## Term

The speaker uses "true fans" for the people who know you, like you, and trust you on a topic — and he defines a personal brand as 1,000 of them. The phrase appears 2 times, in his own story: when he was 21 at university he had about 400 to 500 followers who were basically just his friends from high school, but by making content on productivity and waking up early he developed about 500 true fans. Its neighbours are "personal brand", "KLT", "productivity", and "coaches": the fans had KLT — they knew him, liked him, and trusted him on productivity — and he reminds the audience that people buy coaches before they buy the coaching, so it is not about the product but about the relationship with the audience. The concrete contrast is the 100,000: he can have 100,000 people liking and viewing his content daily, and that is not the case — the personal brand is the 1,000 true fans, not the view count.
