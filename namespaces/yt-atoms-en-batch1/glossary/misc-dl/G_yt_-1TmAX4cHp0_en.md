# G_yt_-1TmAX4cHp0_en — law of attraction

## Term

The vlogger uses "law of attraction" for the principle that your ideas, thoughts, and actions create your reality — what you focus on, you attract more of. The phrase appears 2 times, both in one sentence, as a tool inside the business: he asks what viewers are doing if they are not using the law of attraction in a tool within their business. Its neighbours are "reality trans surfing", "energy as a compass", "100K a month", and "day 29": he has been reading Reality Transurfing since November, a 700-page book, and he treats energy as a compass — wherever energy points, intentions and actions go too. The concrete practice is acting like the person who already makes the dream outcome: if the goal is 50K a month, act like the person who makes 50K a month, do their habits, and picture your future self as the anchor for what to do now.
