# G_yt_-30_VLQUd1s_en — trifold brochure

## Term

The tutor uses "trifold brochure" for the six-panel printed piece the whole tutorial sets up in Adobe Illustrator — three sides on the back and three sides on the front, made from two sheets of paper. The phrase appears 7 times, always about setup for print, not about designing the artwork: the video shows how to set up the trifold brochure in Illustrator and export it for print, and says plainly it is not how to design the actual artwork. Its neighbours are "bleed", "artboard", "millimeters", and "guides": printing projects need a bleed, the red line around the artboard marks the bleed that will be cut away, the 420-millimeter width splits into three 140-millimeter panels, and guides sit 5 millimeters from the edge. The concrete rule is the bleed itself: you must bring your design up to the bleed, keep text and important content away from it, and end with 5-millimeter guides on every edge and fold.
