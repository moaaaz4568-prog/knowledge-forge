# G_yt_-3B7cbOOis4_en — personal brand

## Term

Chris Do uses "personal brand" for who you are — your thoughts, your beliefs, your values — not what you do or what you sell. The phrase appears 8 times, as the talk's central correction: most people talking about personal branding have it all wrong, because they use the word instead of advertising, marketing, sales strategy, or funnel building, and talk about it only to get more business. Its neighbours are "commodity", "story", "corporate brand", and "daywalker": a product without a story is just a commodity, your name is your brand, a personal brand is much more powerful than a corporate brand because a corporation is a fictional structure, and he calls himself a daywalker — half accountant-businessman, half crazy creative — who lives in two worlds. The concrete test is the room's habit: when people are asked to talk about their personal brand, they only list strengths, achievements, studies, and cars, while Chris says any conversation about it that does not delve into all the weird things that make you you seems fake.
