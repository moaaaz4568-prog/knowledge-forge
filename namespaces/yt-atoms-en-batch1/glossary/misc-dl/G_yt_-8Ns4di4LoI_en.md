# G_yt_-8Ns4di4LoI_en — brand storytelling

## Term

David uses "brand storytelling" for the craft of telling a brand's story the way a trained artist tells a story — not a buzzword, but the application of creative storytelling to companies. The phrase appears 6 times, across an interview with David, a creative writer turned strategist and author of Kingmakers, a content marketing story. Its neighbours are "storytelling", "hero's journey", "AI", and "copy": the hero's journey is obvious and extremely well known on the creative side, and its commercial application for brand storytelling is in lock step with how good stories are told, while AI copy is predictive by nature — the speaker knows exactly what a competitor using AI will write. The concrete claim is the range: brand storytelling is industry agnostic, used for nonprofits and land-use nonprofits as well as companies, because the greatest asset an audience has is their time and attention, and it is non-renewable.
