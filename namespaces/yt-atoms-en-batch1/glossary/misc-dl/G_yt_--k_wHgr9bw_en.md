# G_yt_--k_wHgr9bw_en — dream follower

## Term

The speaker uses "dream follower" for the person a creator should actually address with their content: someone numerous enough to grow the audience and close enough to the offer to become a paying client. The phrase appears 3 times, always in contrast with the "ideal client" — the person who buys — because the speaker's pain point is that creators do not know the exact difference between the two and keep talking to the buyer instead of the follower. Its neighbours are "ideal client", "ICP", "lead magnet", and "qualified leads": the form asks for the visitor's ICP and offer, the AI builds a custom lead magnet around them, and the answers reveal whether the visitor is a qualified lead. The concrete output is a description of the dream follower — in the demo, high-earning professionals who want a second source of income and want to start investing — plus 10 topics designed to attract exactly that person.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as another name for the ideal client: in this source the two are deliberately different — the ideal client is who buys, the dream follower is who the content speaks to — and confusing them is the exact pain point the lead magnet is built to solve.
- Read as a vague aspiration about audience growth: the dream follower here is a concrete profile the speaker writes down — pains, beliefs, goals — and then turns into 10 specific topics, not a general wish for more followers.
