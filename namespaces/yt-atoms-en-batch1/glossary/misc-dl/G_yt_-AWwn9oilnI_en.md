## Term

The speaker uses "niche" for the narrow slice of freelance work where you can actually compete — a modern and minimal logo, or a logo aimed at DJs — instead of fighting 163 million registered accounts across all freelancing platforms. The word appears 9 times, as the video's core strategy for making serious money on freelance platforms: you need to be niche and you need to stand out, and the trick is to find a niche within a niche. Its neighbours are "freelance platforms", "stand out", "logo design", and "human element": the example is a logo designer selling to DJs with over 1,000 sales, and the video about yourself and your work adds the human element that makes you more relatable to potential clients. The concrete payoff is the sales count: the DJ-logo seller has over 1K sales, which is what a niche within a niche buys you.
