## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a growth hack: in this video there is explicitly no new way to acquire customers — the growth came from announcing the work and from clients sharing it with friends, and the founder refuses to publicize it outside that loop.
- Read as a launch strategy for everyone: the founder's stance is personal and conditional — he will never have to promote it unless the users promote it — so the acquisition depends on the product being worth sharing, not on the announcement alone.
