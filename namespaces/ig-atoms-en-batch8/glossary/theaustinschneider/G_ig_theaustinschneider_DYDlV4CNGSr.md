# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYDlV4CNGSr — AI bubble

## Term

An AI bubble is what the speaker suspects is approaching and about to pop: people start using AI and all of a sudden present as business people because they have AI. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "lack of authenticity", "real-world experience", and "trust is gonna tank", and the picture he paints is specific — "I'm a freaking entrepreneur. Let's freaking get after it!" in every single post, every piece of content, with spelling errors, weird graphics, and carousels he can see right through. His prediction is that more people will catch on, trust will tank with those people, and that will reflect on the actual business person trying to grow their business. He opens and closes on the same question: am I the only one?

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "Am I the only one that thinks maybe we're approaching an AI bubble and maybe maybe it's going to pop at some point soon?" — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DYDlV4CNGSr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a dated market call: the clip is a suspicion — "maybe" — about people faking entrepreneurship with AI, not a prediction with a date or a measure.
- Read as a ban on using AI in content: his complaint is the lack of authenticity and real-world experience behind the posts, not the use of AI itself.
