# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY8ZEY_ietU — Agency U

## Term

Agency U is the program Michael Garrett of Amplify credits with turning his agency around: at one to two thousand dollars a month, he and his partner spent most of their time trying to find clients to believe in them and in their content. The name appears 3 times, beside "dial in our offer", "authority as an agency", and "close two deals", and the change he reports is specific — coaching from Austin on having authority as an agency and dialing in the offer, then two deals closed for six thousand dollars and one one-thousand-dollar deal in a single week. He closes by recommending it to anyone thinking of doing Agency U, saying it really works.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee of deals: one client's week — two six-thousand-dollar deals and one one-thousand-dollar deal — is his own report, not a measured result for everyone.
- Read as the name of any coaching program: here Agency U names the speaker's specific program, with Austin as the coach, not agency coaching in general.
