# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY4v02siM22 — new agency owners

## Term

New agency owners are the contrast the whole clip is built on: where old agency owners run the business themselves, new agency owners build a business that runs without them. The phrase appears 4 times, beside "a team and system", "close a new client", and "walk away from", and each contrast sharpens the same point — old owners answer every client message themselves and feel anxiety at a new client because of the work it brings, while new owners feel excited because they have a system that handles the rest. The sharpest version of the idea is the last one: old owners built a freelance operation with a logo and an LLC, while new owners build an actual business they can walk away from.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as owners of new agencies only: here "new" marks the way the business is built — a system and team that handles the work — not the age of the agency.
- Read as a promise that systems remove all work: the new owner still closes clients and owns the business; what the system handles is the delivery, not the ownership.
