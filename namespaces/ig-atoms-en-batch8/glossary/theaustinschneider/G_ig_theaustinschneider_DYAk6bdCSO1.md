# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYAk6bdCSO1 — niche on the outcome

## Term

To niche on the outcome is the speaker's answer to needing to niche down to grow past thirty thousand dollars a month: do not niche on the actual person, niche on the outcome you can provide. The phrase appears 2 times, beside "niche on the actual person", "narrowing down your TAM", and "plumbers who drive blue cars", and the argument is a bet: niching on one specific person puts the whole brand on a single niche and narrows the TAM, which is risky, and below thirty thousand a month there is not enough experience to niche that deep. The exception proves the rule: at a hundred or two hundred thousand a month, really good at plumbers, then consider niching and changing the whole brand around plumbers — but only to get past thirty thousand, niche on the outcome, not on the person.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never niching: the speaker allows niching on plumbers at a hundred or two hundred thousand a month once genuinely good at them — the ban is on deep niching before thirty thousand.
- Read as ignoring who the client is: niching on the outcome still serves a client; what changes is that the brand is built around the result, not the person's identity.
