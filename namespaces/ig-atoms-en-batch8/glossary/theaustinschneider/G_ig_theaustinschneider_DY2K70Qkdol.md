# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY2K70Qkdol — team dashboard

## Term

A team dashboard is the speaker's way of seeing his team clearly enough to improve it: "You cannot improve what you don't measure and what you don't track." The phrase appears 2 times in this overrated-and-underrated rundown, beside "cannot improve what you don't measure", "what you don't track", and "allows you to see things", and he rates the dashboard underrated against working 16-hour days and doing custom work for clients, both of which he calls overrated. His concrete offer is the dashboard they use themselves, held up as the instrument that turns team work into something visible and measurable.

## Synonyms

- dashboard (the short form he uses when offering the one they use)

## Live Example

> "Building a team dashboard. Underrated. You cannot improve what you don't measure and what you don't track." — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DY2K70Qkdol.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a reporting chore: here the dashboard is the tool for seeing the team and improving what is measured, not a status report filed for its own sake.
- Read as optional polish: he calls it underrated precisely because without tracking you cannot improve, so skipping it leaves the business unmeasured.
