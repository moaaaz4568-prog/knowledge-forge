# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY2-NxLiXFH — AgencyU

## Term

AgencyU is the program Bronson of CPV Media credits with shifting the structure of his business into one that can actually scale. The word appears 3 times in this short testimonial, beside "hamster wheel", "copy and paste", and "50k a month". Before joining he describes the business as stuck on a hamster wheel, and after about four months he says he can copy and paste everything they created and is set up to hit 50k a month just in social media retainers. He presents it as a heavy investment that unlocks earning potential three or four times over.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It has completely shifted and changed the structure of our business into a business that can actually scale." — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DY2-NxLiXFH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that joining guarantees scale: one client reporting his own business after four months is not a measured result for everyone.
- Read as a name for agency training in general: here AgencyU names the speaker's specific program, not any course or coaching offer.
