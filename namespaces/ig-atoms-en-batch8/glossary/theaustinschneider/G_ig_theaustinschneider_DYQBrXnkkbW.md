# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYQBrXnkkbW — shiny object syndrome

## Term

Shiny object syndrome is the speaker's diagnosis for agencies that get attached to building on AI without foundations: they have no solid offer, no client acquisition process, and no fulfillment process, so the AI is discretionary and frankly a waste of money. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "plug into existing systems", "solid offer", and "waste of money", and the speaker speaks from experience — over ten AI employees built inside his business, fluent in AI — yet his point is that AI will not solve a business's problems, it will only plug into existing systems. The fix is foundations first: productize the offer, build systems around it, and then plug AI in where it can replace the manual work.

> "People get shiny object syndrome, get so attached to building this thing on AI" — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DYQBrXnkkbW.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on building with AI: the syndrome is building on AI before the foundations exist — the speaker himself built over ten AI employees, once the systems were there.
- Read as AI being useless: his claim is narrower — AI without a solid offer and processes is a waste of money, not that AI has no use.
