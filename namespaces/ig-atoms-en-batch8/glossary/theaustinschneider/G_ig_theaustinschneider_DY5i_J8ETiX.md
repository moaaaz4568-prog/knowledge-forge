# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY5i_J8ETiX — offer

## Term

The offer is what everything else is derived from: strategy, creative, and messaging all come out of it, so a weak offer cannot be fixed by better lead generation. The word appears 4 times, beside "new offer", "quality offer", and "more predictability", and the clip contrasts two eras — before, the lead-gen strategy was posting content on TikTok and waiting for people to come through the lead form, which left income on a roller coaster, up and down; after, the move was reaching out to past clients and the past network about the new offer. The closing line states the rule plainly: most people think they want leads, but you cannot get quality leads unless you have a quality offer.
