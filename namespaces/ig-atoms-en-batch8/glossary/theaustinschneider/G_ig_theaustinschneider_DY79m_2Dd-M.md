# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY79m_2Dd-M — future casting

## Term

Future casting is the speaker's name for how agency owners around ten or twenty thousand dollars a month waste their time: imagining the systems they will build, the AI thing they will build, the website they will tweak, instead of getting new clients. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "ten thousand", "twenty thousand dollars a month", and "getting new clients", and the judgment is blunt — all of it is a waste of time at their level, because what they really need to focus on is getting new clients. His cutoff makes the point concrete: below twenty thousand dollars a month, doing anything other than getting clients means you do not even know what your problems are yet.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as planning for the future: here future casting is the wasteful imagining of builds and tweaks at a level where the only real problem is getting clients, not a planning step.
- Read as a ban on building systems: the speaker's cutoff is the revenue level — below twenty thousand a month — not a claim that systems never matter.
