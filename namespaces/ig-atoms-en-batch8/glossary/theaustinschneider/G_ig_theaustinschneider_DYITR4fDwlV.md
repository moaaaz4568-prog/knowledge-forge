# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYITR4fDwlV — pre-call engine

## Term

A pre-call engine is the second fix the speaker prescribes for attracting the wrong clients: a system that qualifies people before they ever book, so no time is wasted talking to them. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "price shoppers", "tire kickers", and "video sales letter", and it follows the diagnosis — leads are plentiful but wrong because positioning and marketing make the speaker look attractive to price shoppers and people who want custom everything. The fix has two steps: audit the positioning and marketing first, then make sure the pre-call engine is in place — a video sales letter, a call confirmation process, and steps to get on the calendar that qualify them before they book.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a booking tool: the engine's job is qualifying — video sales letter, confirmation, steps to the calendar — not merely letting people pick a time.
- Read as the first fix: the speaker says to audit positioning and marketing first; the pre-call engine is second, and it cannot fix positioning that attracts the wrong clients.
