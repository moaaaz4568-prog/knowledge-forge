# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYQMG9Vj8MC — profit margin

## Term

A profit margin is the share of revenue kept as profit, and in this testimonial it is the whole story: the speaker started at thirty percent profit margins and was told by the person who onboarded him that that was kind of low — which is why he reached out for help. The phrase "profit margin" appears 1 time, beside "profit margins", "gross profit", and "Agency U", and they showed him how to cut and get back on track until he was sitting at sixty to sixty-six percent profit, with gross profit around thirty thousand dollars a month and more money back into the business. His advice to anyone on the fence about working with them is to do it — no matter whether you are sitting at a hundred thousand dollars a month or five thousand, Agency U has the solution for you.
