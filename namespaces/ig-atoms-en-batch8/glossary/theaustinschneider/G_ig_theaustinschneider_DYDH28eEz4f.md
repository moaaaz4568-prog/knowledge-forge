# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYDH28eEz4f — leverage AI

## Term

To leverage AI is what separates the agencies that will be miles ahead from the ones not using AI: use it in an effective way, and the opportunity is even bigger than before. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "miles ahead", "more efficient", and "automate a lot of tasks", and the payoff he lists is practical — better results for clients, saved time, automated redundant tasks, more profit, more time to get clients and grow the business. The worry to have is not AI itself but the agencies learning AI and implementing it inside their business.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "the agencies that can leverage AI and actually use AI in an effective way are going to be miles ahead" — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DYDH28eEz4f.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as using any AI tool: leverage here means effective use that produces better client results and saved time, not merely having AI in the stack.
- Read as AI replacing the agency model: the speaker's answer is the opposite — the model is worth it, and the opportunity is bigger precisely because AI can be leveraged.
