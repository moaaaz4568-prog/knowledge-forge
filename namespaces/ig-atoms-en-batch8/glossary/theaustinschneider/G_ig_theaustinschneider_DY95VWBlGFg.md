# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY95VWBlGFg — AI employee

## Term

An AI employee is the hire the speaker prescribes when an agency's work has outgrown its people: five signs that the agency needs one yesterday. The phrase appears 3 times, beside "every single week", "every single call", and "every single morning", and each sign is the same pattern — hours lost to work done by hand that an AI employee could do instead. The signs run from three hours every single week prepping for sales calls, which the AI employee preps in Slack, through six hours a week building client reports and a daily fulfillment audit every single morning, to onboarding that takes two weeks and three follow-ups and clients whose unhappiness is only discovered after they leave. The case is closed by the tools named for the last two signs: an onboarding system the AI employee handles end-to-end and a renewal radar that flags clients weeks before they churn.

> "Here are five signs that your agency needs an AI employee yesterday." — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DY95VWBlGFg.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a future hire to plan for: the word "yesterday" is the point — the five signs mean the agency already needed the AI employee, not someday.
- Read as a replacement for the team: the signs are all tasks the team does by hand — sales-call prep, reports, the fulfillment audit, onboarding, renewal radar — that an AI employee takes over, not the team itself.
