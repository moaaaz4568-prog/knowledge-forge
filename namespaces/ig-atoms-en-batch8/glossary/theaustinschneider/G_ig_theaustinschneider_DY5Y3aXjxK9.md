# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DY5Y3aXjxK9 — SOPs

## Term

SOPs are the written processes a team is supposed to follow, and this clip opens on the complaint every owner recognizes: "I have SOPs, but my team never follows them." The word appears 6 times, beside "team never follows them", "lack of accountability", and "five million dollar agency", and the speaker refuses the easy blame — he does not know yet whether the problem is the SOPs or the people, and names lack of accountability as the first of three likely reasons. His own proof is scale: he scaled in his five million dollar agency and says it would never have been possible without team or without SOPs. The point is that handing someone SOPs without holding them accountable to following them does not work.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof the SOPs are bad: the speaker explicitly leaves open whether the problem is the SOPs or the people, and his first reason is accountability, not document quality.
- Read as a reason to skip writing SOPs: he says his five million dollar agency would never have scaled without them, so the complaint is about enforcement, not about the SOPs existing.
