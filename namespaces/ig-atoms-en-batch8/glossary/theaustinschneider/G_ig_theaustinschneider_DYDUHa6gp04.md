# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYDUHa6gp04 — thirty sixty ninety day plan

## Term

A thirty sixty ninety day plan is the post-hire structure the speaker's hiring lacked before Agents You: no plan existed after hiring somebody, and now it does, as one of the program's modules. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "non-negotiables", "core values", and "interview questions", and the before-and-after is the point — before, a job description went up on Indeed, the seat was filled, and the new hire was handed a project with no system; now, days are spent defining exactly what the role looks like, its responsibilities and non-negotiables, and interview questions that test alignment with core values. Hiring feels significantly less intimidating and way more manageable as a result.
