# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYD24pft9oM — adaptability

## Term

Adaptability is the word the speaker wants tattooed in his listeners' brains: the second of two defenses against agencies that use AI replacing agencies that do not. The word appears 1 time, beside "tattooed in your brain", "need to be adaptable", and "AI tools", and the point is aimed at the team — editors and the client success manager must be adaptable to new tools the company introduces, because things will keep changing. The first defense frames it: use AI to enhance what is already working, making the team more efficient and effective at what they are already good at, not to save the business or the offer.

## Synonyms

- adaptable (the adjective form used for the team, editors, and client success manager)

> "Number two is just like a word that I want to get like tattooed in your brain, which is adaptability." — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DYD24pft9oM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as adapting to AI in general: here adaptability means the team adopting each new tool the company introduces, not a vague openness to change.
- Read as AI saving the business: the speaker is explicit that AI will not save the business or the offer — it only enhances what already works.
