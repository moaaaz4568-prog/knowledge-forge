# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYQZ8ZUNUzy — mindset

## Term

A mindset is the way of thinking you carry, and here it is the one that keeps you stuck: the speaker lives in San Diego, where people always say it is too expensive, or hear it and think you must have to leave because of the cost. The word appears 1 time, beside "too expensive", "putting a label on yourself", and "keeps you stuck", and his response is always the same — why would he move somewhere just because it is more affordable, when staying because it is cheap puts a label on yourself, saying you are only staying because it is cheap. That mindset does not bring money; it keeps you stuck — and it is really just a thought.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice about where to live: the point is the thought behind the move, not the city — San Diego is the example, and the claim is about the mindset, not the rent.
- Read as the cost being irrelevant: the speaker does not say San Diego is cheap — he says moving only because somewhere is more affordable labels you as someone who stays because it is cheap.
