# G_ig_theaustinschneider_DYLXRX6kgTj — stepping stone

## Term

A stepping stone is how this short motivational passage treats failure: every failure is a stepping stone to success. The phrase appears 1 time, beside "hard work", "perseverance", and "sacrifice", and the passage's claim is that success is no accident — it is hard work, perseverance, learning, studying, sacrifice, and most of all love of what you are doing or learning to do. There is no agency content here, no claim about the speaker's work, and no second idea; the entry records the quoted lines as they stand.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Remember, every failure is a stepping stone to success." — sources/S_ig_theaustinschneider_DYLXRX6kgTj.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the speaker's own story: the passage is a quoted motivational passage, not an account of his failures or his path.
- Read as a method for handling failure: the line names failure a stepping stone but gives no step for what to do with one — it is encouragement, not a procedure.
