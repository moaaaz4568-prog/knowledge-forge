# G_ig_theianchristianson_DNRul1nPJ-y — memorable

## Term

'Memorable' is the outcome the three questions are meant to produce, appearing once in 'if you want to be instantly memorable'. The speaker says most people forget ninety percent of what you said but never forget how you made them feel, so the term names the lasting impression the questions leave: the person remembers you, not your words. Its neighbors are 'three questions', 'instantly', and 'open up, light up, and remember you', and the three questions — what they wish people asked about, a weird belief they stand by, something they wish they had started five years ago — are the tools that get there. The clip promises no trick; the memorable is the feeling, and the follow-up question asked with curiosity is what keeps it.
