# G_ig_theianchristianson_DSqDuiXEiGK — neuroscience

## Term

Neuroscience is the one thing the speaker says actually worked after months of dead ends: understanding why his brain and body folded under pressure and giving his nervous system proof that he was confident. The word appears twice, first as the punchline answer to months of no results and then in the question of how neuroscience could build confidence at all. Before it, he lists the advice that failed him — just be yourself, fake it until you make it, just say the right thing — while his conversations still died and opportunities slipped away. The result he reports is concrete: his network exploded, conversation felt effortless, and he was invited into rooms he did not feel he belonged in, which is why he now coaches the same transformation directly.
