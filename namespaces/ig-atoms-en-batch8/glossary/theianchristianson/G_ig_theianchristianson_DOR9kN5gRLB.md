# G_ig_theianchristianson_DOR9kN5gRLB — confidence

## Term

Confidence here is not a feeling but the impression other people form in seconds, built from signals sent outward while the speaker still feels nervous and awkward inside. The word confidence appears once, beside the adjective confident, which appears four times, and the two are tied to posture and pace, eye contact, and a pause before speaking. Standing tall with shoulders back, walking with purpose, and holding eyes long enough to show true presence are the behaviors the speaker says make people decide instantly whether you belong. A simple two-second pause is his concrete example: it makes the speaker sound ten times more in control without changing how nervous he feels.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to fake being calm: the speaker separates the inner feeling from the outward signals, so the point is training small behaviors, not pretending the nerves are gone.
- Read as a claim that posture alone creates confidence: the video pairs posture with pace, eye contact, and pausing, and none of the three is presented as working on its own.
