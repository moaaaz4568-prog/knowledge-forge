# G_ig_theianchristianson_Db6rnShhrGm — attention

## Term

Attention is the resource the speaker says determines influence: the more of it you put on yourself, the less you have for the person right in front of you. The word appears five times, tracking the exact failure — sentences getting longer, voice tightening, and the speaker watching himself while talking, asking how am I being perceived and what should I say next. Influence demands that attention leaves the self, because reading the room, noticing reactions, holding eye contact, and making people laugh all need the mental bandwidth that self-monitoring consumes. The characteristic that decides influence, then, is whether attention can stay on other people when the pressure goes up, which is why the speaker trains removing internal interferences inside Neurocharismatic Engineering rather than adding new tricks.
