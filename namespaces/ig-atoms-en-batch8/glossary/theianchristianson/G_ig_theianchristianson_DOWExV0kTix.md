# G_ig_theianchristianson_DOWExV0kTix — micro signals

## Term

Micro signals are the tiny outward cues — posture, tone, and the way eyes move — that the speaker says other people's brains read within seven seconds, before logic ever kicks in. The phrase appears once, while the bare word signals appears three times, and the claim is that these cues hit the other person's brain before a single word is judged. The speaker's three drills all work through them: speaking 10% slower so the brain relaxes and people lean in, using the other person's name for instant familiarity and authority, and nodding once while they speak. Confidence, in this video, is literally a skill wired in the brain, trained by repeating these signals daily until people start to notice.
