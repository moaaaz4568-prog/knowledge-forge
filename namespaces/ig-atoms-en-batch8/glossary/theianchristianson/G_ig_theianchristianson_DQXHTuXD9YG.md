# G_ig_theianchristianson_DQXHTuXD9YG — emotion

## Term

Emotion is one of the three things the speaker tells viewers to listen for when someone is talking, alongside details and meaning, as the way to keep a conversation alive without hunting for new topics. The word appears twice, first in the list of the three things and then in the example question, what made that exciting or stressful for you. The method is to pick one of the three and follow it — asking after the feeling rather than changing the subject — so the conversation expands instead of restarting. Going deeper this way, rather than switching into the left lane, is what the speaker says makes a person unforgettable to talk to.
