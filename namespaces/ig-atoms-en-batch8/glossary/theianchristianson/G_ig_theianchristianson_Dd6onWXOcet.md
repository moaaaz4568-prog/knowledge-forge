# G_ig_theianchristianson_Dd6onWXOcet — clarity

## Term

Clarity is one of the three qualities the speaker says the room rewards: the idea that arrives with the most clarity, certainty, and emotional force wins, not the best idea. The word appears once, in that sentence, and the frustration it answers is the smart person's — a better idea, more nuance, a better understanding of the problem, all lost while trying to say it perfectly. The rival in the story says it simpler, cleaner, and much stronger, and suddenly everybody's attention moves toward them, not because they were smarter or more correct but because they could articulate themselves in the moment. The failure mode for high-level people is named directly: intelligence that apologizes for itself, over-explaining, softening, qualifying, and adding seven caveats before the point.
