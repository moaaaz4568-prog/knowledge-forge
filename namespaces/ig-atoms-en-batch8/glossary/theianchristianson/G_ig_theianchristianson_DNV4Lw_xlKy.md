# G_ig_theianchristianson_DNV4Lw_xlKy — fear

## Term

'Fear' is the thing the speaker says holds people back from trying, appearing once in 'How long are you going to let fear hold you back from trying and doing what you really want to do?' The clip lists what fear costs — wasting life, doom scrolling, not talking to people, not asking that girl out, not taking that chance — and says the scariest thing is not failure but waking up in ten years realizing you never tried anything. Its neighbors are 'hold you back', 'trying', and 'chasing your dreams', and the remedy is the reminder itself: spend every waking second building the life you dream of or genuinely being in the present. The speaker says he does not want to call anyone out; the fear is the obstacle, and the trying is the answer.
