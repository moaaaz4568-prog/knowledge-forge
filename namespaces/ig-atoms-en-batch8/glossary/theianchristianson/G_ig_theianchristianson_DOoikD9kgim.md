# G_ig_theianchristianson_DOoikD9kgim — presence

## Term

Presence is the quality the speaker says actually makes a person memorable: not being the funniest or the smartest, but how you look someone in the eye, listen, and make them feel seen. The word appears once, in the sentence that being memorable is about presence, and it is set against performing and against leaving no mark on a room. People remember how you made them feel rather than what you said, so presence here is attention paid outward — listening for real — instead of a performance staged for applause. The payoff the speaker names is practical: opportunities, friendships, and relationships all come through people, and staying forgettable means missing them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as charisma or charm: presence here is specifically sustained, outward attention, not wit or a lively manner, and the speaker separates it from being funny or smart.
- Read as a technique for impressing a room: the video's point is the opposite — presence works because it drops the performance, so using it to impress would break it.
