# G_ig_theianchristianson_DaJJ4y9ysy0 — oxytocin

## Term

Oxytocin is the first of the three chemicals the speaker says create charisma, the one that creates connection: it is released when other people feel understood, accepted, and emotionally safe. The word appears twice, first in the list of oxytocin, dopamine, and serotonin and then in the sentence that names it the bonding chemical. The other two complete the picture — dopamine creates excitement when people anticipate something rewarding, and serotonin creates respect, tied to competence and perceived value — and the speaker warns that attention and attraction are not the same thing. Charisma, in this video, is what happens when all three work together, and the process he developed to engineer those feelings in others is neurocharismatic engineering.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a biology lesson: the chemicals are used as a teaching frame for charisma, and the video gives no study or dosage behind any of the three claims.
- Read as a claim that charisma is chemical manipulation: the speaker's process is about how the speaker behaves — curiosity, humor, storytelling, conviction — not about altering anyone's chemistry directly.
