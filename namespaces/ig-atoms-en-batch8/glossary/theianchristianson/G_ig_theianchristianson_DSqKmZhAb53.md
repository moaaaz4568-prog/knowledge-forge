# G_ig_theianchristianson_DSqKmZhAb53 — evidence

## Term

Evidence is the mechanism the speaker's coaching program runs on: confidence comes from evidence, not affirmations, so the work is repeatable behaviors that give the brain proof of who the client says he is. The word appears once, in that sentence, and it shapes the whole program — a private Notion page with daily actions, live calls twice a week, and direct feedback on posture, tone, and volume. The promise attached is extreme: show up, do the work, stay active, and if nothing changes he will refund the program, add $1,000 on top, and fly out to coach in person for free. Only the hungry and coachable are accepted — he says he rejects 97% of applicants — because the method depends on the client actually doing the work that creates the evidence.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a demand for credentials: evidence here means lived proof from repeated action, not diplomas or a résumé, and the program is built to generate it daily.
- Read as a guarantee of results: the refund promise is conditional on showing up and doing the work, so the evidence claim covers effort, not a fixed outcome.
