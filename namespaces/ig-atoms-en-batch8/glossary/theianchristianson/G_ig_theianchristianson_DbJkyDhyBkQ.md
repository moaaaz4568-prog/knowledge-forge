# G_ig_theianchristianson_DbJkyDhyBkQ — serotonin

## Term

Serotonin is the third of the three chemicals the speaker says create charisma, the one that creates respect: it is very highly connected to status, competence, and perceived value. The word appears twice, first in the list of oxytocin, dopamine, and serotonin and then in the sentence that names its role, set against dopamine's excitement and oxytocin's connection. The triggers the speaker gives are practical — curiosity about people, being funny, and genuine storytelling for dopamine; making others feel accepted, safe, and understood for oxytocin — while serotonin is tied to how competence shows up in the speaker himself. Charisma is what happens when all three work together, and the speaker offers a full video guide on using them at once to anyone who comments the word guide.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that status can be faked with a chemical trick: serotonin here is a teaching label for respect tied to competence and perceived value, not a substance to manipulate.
- Read as the whole of charisma: the video is explicit that all three chemicals must work together, so serotonin alone explains only the respect leg of the trio.
