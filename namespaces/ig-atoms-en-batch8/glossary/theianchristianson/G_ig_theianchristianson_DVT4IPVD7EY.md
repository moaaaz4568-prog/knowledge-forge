# G_ig_theianchristianson_DVT4IPVD7EY — predictability

## Term

Predictability is what the speaker says confidence reads as: safe, steady, and worth moving toward, while uncertainty reads as risk and people avoid risk. The word appears twice, in the sentence that confidence reads as predictability and predictability feels safe, and it is the hinge of the whole video's argument about why doors open for the most confident person in the room. The advantage the less experienced winners had was not a skill but a signal — certainty sent outward — and the playbook that follows is physical: slow down, take up space, and do something useful the moment you arrive. Over-explaining is the failure mode named at the end: every hedge, justification, and unprompted credential signals that the speaker does not trust his own presence.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to be boring or rigid: predictability here is steadiness that feels safe to others, not sameness, and the video pairs it with useful action, not passivity.
- Read as a claim that confident people never feel uncertain: the video is about what others read from signals, not about the speaker's inner state, and its drills are all outward behaviors.
