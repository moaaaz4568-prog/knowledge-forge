# G_ig_theianchristianson_DU80a8kkhtT — attention

## Term

Attention is the thing the speaker says must be earned: credentials, age, and even being correct do not make people listen to you. The word appears once, in the sentence that you have to earn their attention, and the test is the voice in the speaker's own head — I'm intelligent, I have good ideas, I'm capable, why isn't anybody listening to me. The answer he gives is devastatingly simple: you haven't earned the right to be listened to yet, and earning it means proving you are somebody worth listening to and making people feel something when you speak. His own shift was to stop networking for what he could get and start networking for who he could connect with, asking what he could learn and how he could make the other person's evening better.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You have to earn their attention. You have to prove that you're somebody worth listening to." — sources/S_ig_theianchristianson_DU80a8kkhtT.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that talent goes unrecognized unfairly: the speaker's point is that being right is not the same as being heard, and the remedy he names is earning attention rather than resenting its absence.
- Read as transactional networking advice: the shift he describes moves away from extracting value and toward making rooms better by being in them.
