# G_ig_theianchristianson_DUSGMSZEi0e — prerequisite

## Term

A prerequisite is what the speaker says confidence is not: waiting for it to arrive first means never taking the move, never initiating the conversation, never walking into the group. The word appears once, in the sentence that you are treating confidence like a prerequisite, like it is necessary to take action, while the video insists it only ever comes afterwards. The image is a person standing before a massive opportunity that could change everything, still waiting for an internal green light that will never come, until the chance disappears. The fix is to start moving — steps toward the person, mouth open to speak, eye contact made — so the brain must focus on the action and has no time to overthink, letting the body lead and the mind follow.

## Live Example

> "And so you're treating confidence like a prerequisite, like it's necessary to take action, but it only ever comes afterwards." — sources/S_ig_theianchristianson_DUSGMSZEi0e.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a dismissal of preparation: the video attacks waiting for a feeling of readiness, not preparing, and its fix is concrete movement rather than blind leaping.
- Read as a promise that action always creates confidence: the claim is that confidence follows action, not that every single action succeeds, and the mechanism named is attention consumed by doing.
