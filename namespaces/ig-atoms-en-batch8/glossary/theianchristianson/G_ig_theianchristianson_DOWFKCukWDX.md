# G_ig_theianchristianson_DOWFKCukWDX — certainty

## Term

Certainty is the signal the speaker says opportunities actually follow: not the longest list of achievements, but the way a person carries himself, speaks, and owns his space. The word appears once, in the sentence that opportunities flow to the one who signals certainty, set against skills, credentials, and experience. The speaker's own story is the illustration — stacking up skills while still feeling invisible in rooms, watching a man with half his experience leave with the deal. The practical version is concrete: walk in with presence, shoulders back and a calm pace, speak early so the voice is heard, and hold eye contact like you already belong there.
