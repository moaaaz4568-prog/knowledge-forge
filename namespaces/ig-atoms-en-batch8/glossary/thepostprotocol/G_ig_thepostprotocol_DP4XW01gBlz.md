# G_ig_thepostprotocol_DP4XW01gBlz — visual hook

## Term

A visual hook is the opening device this video names and demonstrates: a repeated go, go, go over an image the viewer must watch to resolve, working because the brain waits to see what happens next. The phrase appears once, right after the urgent repetition, and the speaker calls it the smartest way to grab attention and deliver a message. The second half of the video is the offer — a website with thousands of such hooks, downloadable for free with a single click, with the link sent to anyone who comments. The visual hook here is therefore both the example on screen and the product being pointed at: watch the tension resolve, then go and get more of them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that any repeated motion holds attention: the hook works in the example because the brain waits for an outcome, and the video shows one instance rather than a rule for all motion.
- Read as a review of the website: the site is named only as the place to download thousands of hooks, with no detail about what it contains or whether it is any good.
