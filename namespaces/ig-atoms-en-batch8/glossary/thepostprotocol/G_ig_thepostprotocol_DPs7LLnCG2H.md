# G_ig_thepostprotocol_DPs7LLnCG2H — trending clips

## Term

Trending clips are the raw material of the viral trick this video describes: Judy from the Pancake Cafe takes them, cuts them at the perfect moment, and blends them into her own content as a hook. The phrase appears once, in the sentence explaining her method, and the result claimed is that almost every video on her profile pulls tens of millions of views. The strategy is presented as repeatable — the same pattern, copied, with a website offered to anyone who comments clips for getting clips like these. Trending clips here are the borrowed opening: not Judy's own footage, but a cut of something already moving, spliced into a promo that would otherwise be boring.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that Judy invented the trick: the video says she uses trending clips as her hook, and the pattern is offered for copying, not credited as her invention.
- Read as a guarantee of tens of millions of views: the view counts describe her profile's results as the speaker claims them, not a result promised to anyone who copies the pattern.
