# G_ig_thepostprotocol_DOv00-piP74 — contrast hook

## Term

A contrast hook is the first of three hook types the speaker demonstrates: a small croissant sets an expectation and the huge bowl of raspberries and whipped cream instantly breaks it, surprising the brain. The phrase appears once, attached to that croissant example, and the mechanism named is expectation broken by scale. The other two types follow the same pattern of naming the hook and its mechanism — authenticity plus instant gratification, promising fast results while feeling raw and relatable, and relatability plus value comparison, where saying actual routine builds trust and the Starbucks comparison makes the value crystal clear. All three are offered as named examples, with 35 plus proven viral hooks available to anyone who comments.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a recipe for any video: the contrast hook works here because a small thing sets up a huge thing, and the video gives no rule for when contrast fails to surprise.
- Read as a claim that naming a hook type proves it works: the speaker labels each example and states its mechanism, but the video itself is the demonstration, not a test of the labels.
