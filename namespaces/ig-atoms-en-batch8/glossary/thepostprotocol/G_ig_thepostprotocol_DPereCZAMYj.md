# G_ig_thepostprotocol_DPereCZAMYj — background songs

## Term

Background songs are the resource this video offers: songs going viral time and time again, to be used in the viewer's next video. The phrase appears once, in the opening sentence, and the promise is that the full collection removes the worry of finding a viral track ever again. The video gives no sample of any song and names no title; the entire content is the offer itself, delivered in thirty-seven words, with the collection sent to anyone who comments album. Background songs here are therefore a promised library rather than a demonstrated one: named as viral, repeated over time, and handed over behind the comment.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a playlist: no song is named or played in the video, so the entry cannot list anything the collection contains.
- Read as a guarantee that the songs will go viral: going viral time and time again describes the songs' history as the speaker claims it, not a result promised to the viewer's video.
