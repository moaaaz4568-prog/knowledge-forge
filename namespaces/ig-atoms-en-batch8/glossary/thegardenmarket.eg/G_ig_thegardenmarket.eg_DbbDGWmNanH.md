# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DbbDGWmNanH — Verano

## Term

The source is in Spanish, and 'Verano' — summer — is the season the song closes on: 'Verano eterno', eternal summer, appearing once as the last phrase. The lines before it set the scene — 'Que fluya, sol y mar', let it flow, sun and sea, 'Vida en color', life in color, 'Ríe, respira, siente el calor' — so the term names the endless summer the whole verse is floating toward. Its neighbors are 'eterno', 'sol y mar', and 'cuerpo en paz', and the eternal is the point: not a summer that ends, but one that stays. The song names no place and no person; the summer is the mood, and the peace of the body is its sign.
