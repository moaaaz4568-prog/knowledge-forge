# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DNBhymntc1g — attention

## Term

The source mixes Hebrew lines with English lines, and 'attention' is the demand the English lines keep making — 'I need attention' — appearing twice. The singer calls herself a movie whose eyes 'will never leave me', says she is 'too precious', and admits she should leave but finds it hard to go, so the term names the regard she wants from the person addressed: to be watched, not ignored. Its neighbors are 'like a movie', 'too precious', and 'so annoying', and the Hebrew refrain 'חתפוני, אני חתפוני' frames the same pull in another tongue. The song never explains the relationship; attention is the currency the singer asks for, twice, in the middle of the push and pull.
