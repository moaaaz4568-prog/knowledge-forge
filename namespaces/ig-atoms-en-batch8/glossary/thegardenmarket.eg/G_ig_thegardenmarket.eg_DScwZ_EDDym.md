# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DScwZ_EDDym — snow

## Term

'Snow' is what the singer asks to fall, appearing twice in the closing plea 'let it snow, let it snow'. The verse opens with frightful weather outside and a delightful fire inside, and 'since we've no place to go', the snow is welcome rather than feared — the reason to stay by the fire. Its neighbors are 'weather', 'fire', and 'no place to go', and the doubling is the song's shrug: let it come, we are staying. The clip names no town and no storm; the snow is the excuse, and the fire is the answer.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a weather report: the snow is the song's reason to stay by the fire, and it records no storm, no place, and no snowfall.
- Read as a promise it will snow: the singer asks the snow to fall, and the song is a wish, not a forecast.
