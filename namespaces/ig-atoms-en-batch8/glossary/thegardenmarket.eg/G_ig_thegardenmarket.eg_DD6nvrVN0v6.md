## Term

'Garden Market' is the place and the brand the whole interview keeps naming — six times across the 225 words — as the speaker explains why she is still there. She calls it an Egyptian brand for curly and wavy hair, an authentic market she has loved since it started, and a place where people come to try brands rather than only buy, so the name carries the market, the brands inside it, and the habit of visiting. Its neighbors are 'authentic market', 'curly and wavy hair', and 'Sarah', the superwoman she praises, and the talk of tents, logos, and trying things all hangs off the name. The speaker's point is not a single product: it is that the Garden Market is worth returning to because every visit shows her something new.
