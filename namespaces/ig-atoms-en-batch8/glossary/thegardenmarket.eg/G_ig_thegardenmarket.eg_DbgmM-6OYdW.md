# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DbgmM-6OYdW — cachito

## Term

The source is in Spanish, and 'cachito' — a little piece — is the pet name the singer gives the beloved, appearing four times: three times as 'Cachito, cachito, cachito mío', and once more as 'mi cachito'. The line calls the beloved 'pedazo de cielo que Dios me dio', a piece of heaven God gave me, so the term names the loved one as a small, heaven-sent piece, not a thing. Its neighbors are 'mío', 'pedazo de cielo', and 'mi amor', and the singer even answers the question of why — 'porque eres de mi vida', because you are of my life. The song names no person beyond the pet name; the cachito is the beloved, and the blessing is the reason.

- Read as a claim about a real person: the beloved is named only by the pet name, and the song gives no name, no face, and no story.
