# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DUlPWi7DSJB — love

## Term

'Love' is the subject of the opening line, appearing once in 'Love looks pretty on you, looks pretty on me'. The sentence says love suits them both — it looks pretty on the listener and on the singer alike — and the next line adds 'If heaven's for lovers', so the term names the shared feeling that dresses them both. Its neighbors are 'looks pretty', 'on you', and 'heaven', and the pretty is the point: love here is not a struggle but something becoming. The clip gives no story and no names; love is the look, and heaven is where it belongs.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a description of two people: the line says love looks pretty on them both, and the song names neither person.
- Read as a claim about heaven: 'If heaven's for lovers' is the song's condition, and it proves nothing about heaven or about who goes there.
