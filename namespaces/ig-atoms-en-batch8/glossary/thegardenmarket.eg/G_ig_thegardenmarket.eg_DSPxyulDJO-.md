# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DSPxyulDJO- — العجينة

## Term

The source is in Arabic (Egyptian dialect) mixed with English, and 'العجينة' — the dough — is what the speaker says they are patient with: 'إحنا بنصبر على العجينة بتاعتنا مش بنستعجل عليها'. The word appears once, and the claim around it is that the dough may ferment 'up to three days', which is good for the pizza's taste and for digestion, so the term names the slow-fermented base the whole pizza talk rests on. Its neighbors are 'بنصبر', 'نخمرها', and 'up to three days', and the patience is the point: the dough is not rushed, and the wait is what makes the pizza. The speaker then praises the margherita and pepperoni, but the dough is the craft the passage opens with.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pizza recipe: the passage says the dough ferments up to three days, and it gives no quantities, no flour, and no steps.
- Read as a health claim: the speaker says the slow ferment is good for taste and digestion, and the source offers no evidence beyond the speaker's word.
