# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DKt0kbkt9qh — kickstarts

## Term

'Kickstarts' is the verb for love starting again, appearing twice in the repeated line 'When the love kickstarts again, starts again'. The line says the love restarts the way an engine catches — suddenly, after being still — and the doubling of 'again' and 'starts again' keeps the restart itself in view. Its neighbors are 'love', 'starts again', and 'when', and the verb does the work: love is not described, it is re-ignited. The clip gives no story of who left or returned; the kickstart is the whole event, named twice because the song repeats it.
