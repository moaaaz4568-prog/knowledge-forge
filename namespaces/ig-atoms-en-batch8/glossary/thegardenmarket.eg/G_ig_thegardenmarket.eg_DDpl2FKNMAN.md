# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DDpl2FKNMAN — happiest season

## Term

'Happiest season' is the verdict the song gives on this time of year, appearing once in the closing line 'It's the happiest season of all'. The verse opens by calling it 'the most wonderful time of the year' and fills the scene with kids 'jingle belling' and everyone telling you to 'be of good cheer', so the phrase caps a picture of noise, children, and good wishes. Its neighbors are 'most wonderful time' and 'good cheer', and the superlative is the point: not merely a good season, the happiest one. The song names no date and no place; the season itself, with its ringing and its cheer, is the whole subject.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ranking of seasons: the superlative is the song's cheer, and the source measures nothing against any other season.
- Read as proof the season is happy for everyone: the song describes kids ringing and people wishing good cheer, and it speaks for the singers, not for a whole town.
