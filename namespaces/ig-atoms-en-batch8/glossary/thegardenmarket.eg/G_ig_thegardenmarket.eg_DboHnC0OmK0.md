# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DboHnC0OmK0 — vacation

## Term

'Vacation' is the season the song opens with, appearing twice — 'Vacation in the summer sun' at the start, and 'We're on vacation, having lots of fun' at the close. The lines between put away the books, leave school behind, and play it cool in the warm weather, so the term names the break itself: school out, books away, summer in. Its neighbors are 'summer sun', 'out of school', and 'having lots of fun', and the fun is the point: the vacation is not a trip anywhere, it is the time off. The song names no destination; the vacation is the state, and the warm weather is its weather.
