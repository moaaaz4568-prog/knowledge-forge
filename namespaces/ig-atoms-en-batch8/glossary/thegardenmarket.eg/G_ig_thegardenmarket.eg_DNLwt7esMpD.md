# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DNLwt7esMpD — love

## Term

'Love' is what the singer asks to be shown, appearing twice in 'Show me love' at the opening and again near the close. The request refuses money — 'don't need no money', 'don't need nobody' — and asks only for a body and a sweetness 'like honey', so the term names the thing to be demonstrated rather than declared. Its neighbors are 'show me', 'honey', and 'don't need no money', and the repetition turns the ask into the chorus: show it, do not say it. The song names no lover and no story; love is the demand, and the honey is how sweet the showing should feel.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a love letter: the song asks love to be shown and refuses money, and it names no lover and no relationship to check.
- Read as proof the singer needs nothing else: the 'don't need no money' is the song's refusal, and the source never says what the singer actually lives on.
