# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DKfOyXdtZnQ — hustle

## Term

'Hustle' is the grind the verse says never ends — 'The hustle's never complete' — appearing once near the close. The lines around it count time and money: 'Making good use of time', 'Steady bags to collect', and a brisk skate, so the term names the ongoing work of collecting and moving, not a single job. Its neighbors are 'bags', 'time', and 'never complete', and the 'never' is the point: the hustle is unfinished by definition, always another bag to collect. The verse names no trade and no boss; the hustle is the condition the singer lives in, counted out in 'one two and a' time.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a business plan: the verse counts time and bags to collect, but it names no trade, no income, and no goal.
- Read as proof the hustle pays: the line says the hustle is never complete, and the song claims no earnings at all.
