# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DDJ-OEsNR4Y — sleigh

## Term

In this sung verse 'sleigh' is the vehicle of the winter ride, appearing once inside 'one horse open sleigh'. The line sets the scene with 'dashing through the snow' and 'o'er the fields we go, laughing all the way', so the sleigh names the open, horse-drawn ride the singers are enjoying rather than any vehicle in general. Its neighbors are 'snow', 'fields', and 'bells on bobtail ring', and the verse ends on the fun of the ride rather than where it goes. The sleigh is small in the text — one mention — but it is the thing that makes the dashing, the laughing, and the bright spirits possible.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a travel report: the verse is a sung Christmas scene, and the sleigh ride is the song's picture, not a ride anyone took.
- Read as proof of snow on the day: the song sets its scene in snow for the fun of it, and it records no weather, no place, and no date.
