# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DSVqhXejonQ — artist

## Term

'Artist' is what the rapper insists he really is — 'don't call me a rapper, I'm really an artist' — appearing once as the correction the whole verse builds toward. The lines before it list the grind — 'Get up and make it work', 'Grinding, came out the mud like Rocky', 'putting in the work' — so the term names the identity earned by the work, set against the label 'rapper' as something too small. Its neighbors are 'rapper', 'painting a picture', and 'write lyrics', and the painting image is the proof: lyrics as pictures, not just bars. The verse names no album and no audience; the artist is the claim, and the grind is the evidence.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a biography: the verse lists grinding and coming out of the mud, but it names no album, no show, and no audience.
- Read as proof rappers are not artists: the line is one performer's claim about himself, and it settles nothing about anyone else who raps.
