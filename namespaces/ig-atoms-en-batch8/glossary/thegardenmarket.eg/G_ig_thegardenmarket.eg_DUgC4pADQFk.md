# G_ig_thegardenmarket.eg_DUgC4pADQFk — hurting

## Term

'Hurting' is the wound the singer names, appearing once in 'Like if you only knew how hurting me'. The lines around it hold back what the singer wants to say — 'there's things I wanna say to you, but I'll just lay you here' and 'things I wanna talk about, but better not to give' — so the term names the pain that stays unspoken, the reason for the silence. Its neighbors are 'if you only knew', 'the first to ever did', and 'better not to give', and the conditional is the point: the hurting is real, but the telling is withheld. The song never says who did the hurting; the word is the ache, and the unsaid things are its company.
