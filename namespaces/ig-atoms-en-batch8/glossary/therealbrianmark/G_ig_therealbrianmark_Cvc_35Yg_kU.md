# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cvc_35Yg_kU — four minute mile

## Term

In this source, the "four minute mile" is Brian Mark's proof that everything is impossible until somebody does it: it was impossible until Roger Bannister did it, when everybody literally thought you would die if you pushed your body to break it, and after he broke it over three hundred and fifty people did it in the next two years. The phrase appears 3 times, and the lesson he draws is to look up to people you admire and say to yourself that if he can do it, you can do it. His concrete numbers are the point — one breaker, then more than 350 followers in two years — so the impossible was a belief, not a limit.
