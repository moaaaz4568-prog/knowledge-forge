# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cu5EyIdgx11 — resilience

## Term

Resilience is the capacity Brian says a business needs because two out of three strategies will fail: plans fail, many of them fail, and the only way through is to keep making plans, keep taking action, and never quit until one of them works. "resilience" appears once in the source, beside "two out of three", "keep making plans" and "never ever ever quit". It is not presented as a trait you either have or lack but as the practice of continuing after failures, so resilience here is the refusal to stop iterating rather than toughness in a single attempt.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading resilience as enduring a bad plan: Brian's version is to keep making new plans and taking action, so clinging to a failing strategy would be the opposite of the resilience he describes.
- Treating the two-out-of-three failure rate as a prediction: the numbers explain why quitting early fails, not a guarantee that the third plan will work.
