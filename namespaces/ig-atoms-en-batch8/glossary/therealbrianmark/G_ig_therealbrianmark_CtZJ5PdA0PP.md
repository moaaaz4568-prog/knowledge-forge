# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtZJ5PdA0PP — menu videos

## Term

Menu videos are the first of three video types Brian tells fitness creators to post instead of workout clips: break down what to eat at fast food restaurants without getting fat, and viewers will watch because the information is immediately usable. "menu videos" appears once in the source, beside "form correction videos", "delicious food recipes" and "nobody gives a". The three types share one logic — teach something the viewer wants to save — while workout videos and pictures get ignored, so menu videos are a reach device built on practical food choices, not on showing the creator's training.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating menu videos as nutrition coaching: the videos break down fast food choices for views, and the creator's program is sold separately, so the video's job is reach, not a diet plan.
