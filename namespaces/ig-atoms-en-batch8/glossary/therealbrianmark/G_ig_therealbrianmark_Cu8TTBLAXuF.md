# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cu8TTBLAXuF — $50 deposit

## Term

The $50 deposit is the commitment Brian takes when a prospect says they will start next week: he first gets them to name what stood out most about the program, so they sell themselves, then asks for $50 to secure the spot and show they are serious, collecting the cash while still on the phone. "$50 deposit" appears once in the source, beside "I'll start next week", "secure your spot" and "collect the cash". The rule behind it is blunt — when the prospect hangs up, the money leaves with them — so the deposit exists because a promised start next week without payment almost never becomes a paying start.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating the deposit as a discount or a down payment on a price: the $50 secures the spot and proves seriousness, and the program's price is separate, so the deposit is a commitment signal, not a partial payment.
- Letting the prospect off the call with a promise: Brian's rule is that money left uncollected on the call is money lost, so a yes without the $50 in hand is not a sale.
