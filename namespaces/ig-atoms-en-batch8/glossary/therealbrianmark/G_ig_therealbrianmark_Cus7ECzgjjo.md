# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cus7ECzgjjo — redemption story

## Term

In this source, a "redemption story" is how Brian Mark reframes rock bottom: being at the lowest point, struggling, with no light at the end of the tunnel, is not the end but the beginning of the story. The phrase appears 2 times, next to "rock bottom", "struggling", and "no light at the end of the tunnel", and the whole video is a single address to someone in that position, asking them to think of the present moment as the start rather than the defeat. The story has no steps or tactics attached to it — the turn is purely in how the low point is named.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When a "redemption story" is read as a past victory to retell: here it is a frame for the present struggle itself, with the redemption still ahead of the person at rock bottom.
- When rock bottom is read as proof the story is over: the video insists the lowest point is the beginning of the story, not its ending.
