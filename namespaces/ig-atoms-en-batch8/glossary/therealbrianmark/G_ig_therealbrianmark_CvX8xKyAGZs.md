# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CvX8xKyAGZs — the solution

## Term

In this source, "the solution" is the person in the mirror: it may or may not be your fault that you are broke, but you are the only person who can solve the problem, so stop blaming inflation, the economy, or anything other than the man or woman in the mirror. The phrase appears once, in the closing line "You may or may not be the problem, but you are definitely the solution", and the video splits fault from responsibility — the cause is left open while the fix is assigned to you alone. His concrete list of forbidden excuses is inflation and the economy, named as things to stop blaming.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "the solution" is read as a program or a purchase: here it is the person in the mirror, and the video names no product as the fix.
- When it is read as an admission that you caused the problem: the video explicitly leaves fault open — "it may or may not be your fault" — while assigning the solving to you either way.
