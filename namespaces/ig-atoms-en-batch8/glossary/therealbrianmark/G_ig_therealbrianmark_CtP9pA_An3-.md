# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtP9pA_An3- — hating yourself

## Term

Hating yourself is the twenty percent Brian deliberately keeps alongside eighty percent of self-love: he chooses to be disappointed and disgusted with daily actions that do not lead toward success, and to look in the mirror honestly when he has gained five to ten pounds, so the hate fuels the work the love alone would not start. "hating yourself" appears once in the source, beside "loving yourself", "disappointed with myself" and "do the fucking work". The split is a discipline tool — love yourself, and hate the days you skip the required work — so the hatred is aimed at actions, not at the person, and it is rationed to a fifth of the time.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading hating yourself as self-hatred as a lifestyle: Brian caps it at twenty percent and pairs it with eighty percent of self-love, so a constant hatred would break the ratio the advice depends on.
- Treating it as motivation by shame alone: the hate is tied to specific actions — gaining weight, skipping the work — and ends in doing the work, so shame without the work that follows is not what the term means here.
