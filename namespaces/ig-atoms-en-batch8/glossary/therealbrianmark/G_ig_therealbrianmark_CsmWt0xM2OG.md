# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsmWt0xM2OG — now question

## Term

The now question is the repeated probe Brian uses to anchor a sales call in urgency: he asks why it is important to make a change right now once while uncovering problems and again while uncovering goals, and he flags the repetition as deliberate. "now question" appears once in the source, beside "biggest struggle", "negatively impacting you" and "what would that look like for you". It structures the call's 80/20 split — the first ten minutes on problems, the next ten on goals — so the question is asked twice because the answer changes once the prospect has pictured the outcome.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading the now question as a single scripted line: Brian asks it twice on purpose, once against the problem and once against the goal, so quoting one instance misses that the repetition is the technique.
- Hearing it as pressure to buy today: the question asks why change matters now in the prospect's own life, and the product is only presented after problems and goals are defined, so the urgency comes from the prospect's answer, not from the coach.
