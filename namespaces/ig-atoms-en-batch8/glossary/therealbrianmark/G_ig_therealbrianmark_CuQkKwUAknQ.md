# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CuQkKwUAknQ — red flags

## Term

In this source, "red flags" are the warning signs Brian Mark lists in potential customers — the phrases that mark someone as a headache client to walk away from rather than win over. The phrase appears once, introducing three cases: a prospect who promises referrals in exchange for a good job, one who asks "Are you the cheapest fitness trainer?", and one who wants to lose weight fast, and each is answered with a blunt dismissal. His concrete move is to quote the slow timeline — "the program's gonna take two years" — and then move along, so the flag is a filter for who not to coach.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "red flags" is read as flaws in the coach's own offer: here the flags belong to the prospect's words and attitudes, not to the program being sold.
- When the list is read as negotiation tactics for keeping bad clients: the video's answer to each flag is to refuse the client and move along, not to persuade them.
