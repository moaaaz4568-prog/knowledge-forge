# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtPbS0dgd1k — extra hours

## Term

Extra hours are the hours Brian finds by auditing a prospect's week: sleep, work, and family time are the only three necessities, and subtracting their total from 168 leaves 58 hours the prospect did not know he had. "extra hours" appears once in the source, beside "168", "49 hours", "40 hours a week" and "five of those hours". The count turns the "I don't have time" objection into a choice — five of those 58 hours for a dream body each week — so the hours are presented as already owned and unspent rather than as time to be found.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading extra hours as free time with nothing in it: Brian counts only sleep, work, and family as necessary and calls everything else optional, so the 58 hours are the remainder after those three, not hours with no claims on them.
- Treating the 58 as a fixed number: it comes from one prospect's own answers — 49 hours of sleep, 40 of work, 20 to 25 with family — so another prospect's answers would give a different total.
