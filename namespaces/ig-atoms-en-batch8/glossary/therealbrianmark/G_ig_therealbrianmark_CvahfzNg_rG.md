# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CvahfzNg_rG — Engage, Qualify, Confirm, and Convert

## Term

In this source, "Engage, Qualify, Confirm, and Convert" is the four-step process Brian Mark says made him over $600,000 on Instagram Stories: engage with a poll, qualify in the DMs, confirm, and convert on a call. The phrase appears once, and the steps he actually demonstrates are Engage — post a simple story poll such as "What do you struggle with more, diet or exercise?" — Qualify — DM the voter from their poll answer and ask the three questions about their main struggle, number one goal, and whether they want help — and Convert — go for the phone call, not the sale, in the DMs, offering a 15-minute call with a game plan. The Confirm step is named in the process but never described in the video, so the working sequence shown is poll, DM, call.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the process is read as a DM sales script: here the DMs only qualify and book the call, and the conversion happens on the phone, not in the messages.
- When "Confirm" is read as a described step: the video names four steps but only demonstrates three — Engage, Qualify, and Convert — and Confirm gets no explanation of its own.
