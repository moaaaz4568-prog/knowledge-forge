# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CvHwj3jAr96 — old self

## Term

In this source, the "old self" is Brian Mark's answer to finding a niche as a new online fitness coach: speak to your old self, because nobody understands your own problems like you do. The phrase appears once, and the method is to think about your own pain points, problems, and struggles from when you started your fitness journey, then create content that solves those problems. The people struggling now like you used to are the ones the content attracts, so the niche is found in your own past rather than in a market survey.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The best way to find your niche as a new online fitness coach is to speak to your old self." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_CvHwj3jAr96.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the "old self" is read as nostalgia or a before-photo: here it is a source of problems to solve — pain points and struggles — that become content for people in the same place now.
- When the niche is read as a demographic to target: the video finds the niche by looking backward at your own journey, not by picking a market segment first.
