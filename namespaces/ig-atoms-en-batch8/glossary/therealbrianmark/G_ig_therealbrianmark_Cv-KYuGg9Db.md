# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cv-KYuGg9Db — environment

## Term

In this source, "environment" is the reason Brian Mark gives for cutting toxic people out, including your mom, your dad, your sister, or your brother: our environment shapes our perception of reality, so who you spend your time with decides what you believe is possible. The word appears once, as the justification for the cut rather than as a background detail, and the video's whole instruction is to remove anyone toxic who holds you back from your success. His concrete rule is that closeness of relation does not exempt anyone from it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Our environment shapes our perception of reality, so be careful who you spend your time with." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_Cv-KYuGg9Db.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "environment" is read as a room or a city: here it means the people you spend your time with, including family, and their effect on what you take for reality.
- When cutting people out is read as blaming them: the video's claim is about your perception being shaped by company, not about assigning fault to the people you remove.
