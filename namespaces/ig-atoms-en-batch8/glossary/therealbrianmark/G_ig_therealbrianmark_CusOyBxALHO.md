# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CusOyBxALHO — conviction

## Term

In this source, "conviction" is what Brian Mark calls the single greatest gift a coach can give clients: believing in them, especially when they doubt themselves, with a certainty so strong that they eventually start to believe in themselves too. The word appears once, paired with "certainty" and set against the clients' own doubt, and the video's whole advice is the transfer of belief from coach to client rather than any program detail. His concrete picture is a coach whose certainty in the client outlasts the client's self-doubt until the client adopts it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Make sure your conviction and your certainty in them is so strong that eventually they start to believe in themselves too." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_CusOyBxALHO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "conviction" is read as the coach's confidence in their own program: here it is belief in the client, not in the method, and its job is to survive the client's doubt.
- When the gift is read as encouragement in general: the video specifies belief directed at the client when they doubt themselves, not praise for what they have already done.
