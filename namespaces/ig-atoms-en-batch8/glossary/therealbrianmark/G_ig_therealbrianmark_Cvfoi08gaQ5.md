# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cvfoi08gaQ5 — consultation

## Term

In this source, a "consultation" is what a coach should sell in the DMs instead of the fitness program: pitching training plans, nutrition plans, and accountability in the messages makes the prospect freak out and say they are not interested, and the sale is lost. The word appears 2 times, in "sell the consultation, not the fitness program" and "book the consultation", and the consultation itself is a quick 15-minute call to talk about the prospect's number one struggle and number one goal and give them a game plan. The program is sold on that call, not in the messages, so the consultation is the bridge between the DM conversation and the sale.
