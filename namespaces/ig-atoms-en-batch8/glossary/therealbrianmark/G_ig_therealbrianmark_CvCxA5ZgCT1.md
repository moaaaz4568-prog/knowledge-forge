# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CvCxA5ZgCT1 — protect your dreams

## Term

In this source, to "protect your dreams" means to keep your guard up and keep quiet about what you are going to do, because not everybody wants to see you win. The phrase appears once, and Brian Mark's explanation is that your success is a constant reminder of all the times they gave up on themselves, so they will bash you, criticise you, and drag your name through the mud. His concrete instruction is to show them once it is already done instead of telling them beforehand, so protection here is silence until the result exists.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You have to keep your guard up and protect your dreams." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_CvCxA5ZgCT1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "protect your dreams" is read as hiding them out of fear: here the silence is tactical — the bashing comes from people reminded of their own giving up, and the answer is to show the finished result.
- When the critics are read as strangers: the video's warning is about people around you who do not want to see you win, not about anonymous commenters.
