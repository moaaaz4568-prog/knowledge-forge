# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CuuLqX0g7HD — pain language close

## Term

In this source, the "pain language close" is Brian Mark's secret for doubling conversions in 30 days: during the sales call, write down the prospect's own specific words for their pain, then use exactly those words when pitching the program. The phrase appears once, demonstrated with "love handles", "spare tire", and "tone up" — never paraphrased as "lose weight" — and each part of the pitch mirrors one phrase back: the training plan loses the love handles, the nutrition plan burns fat to get rid of the spare tire, and the accountability keeps the prospect on track to tone up. The close lands when the prospect hears their own language and says the program sounds perfect and asks how to pay.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the "pain language close" is read as echoing any words the prospect says: here only the three to five key pain words are recorded and mirrored, not the whole conversation.
- When it is read as a trick for renaming the same offer: the video's point is that the prospect's exact phrases — love handles, spare tire, tone up — make the pitch sound like their own goal, and paraphrasing them as "lose weight" breaks it.
