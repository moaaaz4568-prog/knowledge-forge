# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Ct1dhLZAhpi — pre-framing

## Term

Pre-framing is the opening move Brian teaches for a sales call: as soon as the call starts, the coach states what will be covered — the main struggle, the number one goal and why it matters, and strategies his best clients use — then asks if that sounds good and whether a hell yes or hell no at the end is fair. "pre-framing" appears once in the source, beside "set the tone as the leader", "friend zone" and "hell yes or a hell no". It is what keeps the coach out of the friend zone, where the prospect controls the conversation, so pre-framing is the act of taking leadership of the call before any selling begins.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating pre-framing as a script to read: Brian gives the shape — struggle, goal, strategies, then the fairness check — but the content must be the prospect's own, so a memorized monologue that ignores their answers is not pre-framing.
- Reading it as small talk at the start of a call: the frame sets the whole call's structure, including the closing standard of a hell yes or hell no, so it is the leadership move the rest of the call depends on.
