# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cu7ocIRAgJd — discipline

## Term

Discipline, in this source, is doing shit that you hate like you fucking love it: jumping out of bed at 3:30 in the morning to work on mindset, five a.m. gym sessions with headphones in, early mornings, late nights, and long hours grinding — all with a smile, because every step gets you closer to your vision. "discipline" appears once in the source, beside "three thirty in the morning", "five a.m. gym sessions" and "carving your physique". The definition is deliberately physical — the hours and the gym are the discipline, not a feeling about them — so the term means loving the hated work while you do it, not enduring it grimly.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading discipline as willpower in general: Brian defines it through concrete hours — 3:30 wake-ups, 5 a.m. sessions — so a general claim to be disciplined without those hours would not match the term here.
- Treating the smile as optional: the source insists the work is done with a smile because each step nears the vision, so discipline without that attitude toward the work is a different thing.
