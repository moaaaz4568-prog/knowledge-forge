# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsO-LD1AXMP — messy action

## Term

Messy action is Brian's cure for waiting: instead of holding out for the right plan or the right time while somebody else works harder and eats your lunch, you start today with imperfect action. "messy action" appears once in the source, beside "don't wait to take action", "waiting for the right plan", "habits and rituals" and "get out of your own way". It sits between two other pieces of advice — habits and rituals as the only thing holding you back, and removing vices like smoking, drinking, and sleeping in — so messy action is the first move of a discipline routine, not a substitute for one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Hearing messy action as permission to act carelessly: Brian pairs it with consistency, discipline, and perseverance, so the messiness is about starting before conditions are perfect, not about skipping the work.
- Reading it as a one-time push: the advice is framed as a life change — start today, build the traits, surround yourself with winners — so a single messy attempt that stops there misses the point.
