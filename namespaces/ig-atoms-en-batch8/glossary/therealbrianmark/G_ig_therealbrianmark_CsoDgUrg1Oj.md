# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsoDgUrg1Oj — network expansion

## Term

Network expansion is Brian's name for daily actions that bring new eyeballs to a trainer's social media: treating the platform like a conversation instead of a billboard, following 20 to 40 ideal clients a day, and adding 20 Facebook friends a day from suggested connections. "network expansion" appears once in the source, beside "zero social media followers", "leave a comment on their stuff" and "bring them into your network". The arithmetic is explicit — even five follow-backs can yield one $1,000 program sale — so expansion is measured in new people who see the content, not in follower count alone.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading network expansion as posting more content: Brian's three moves are all outbound — commenting, following, and friending — so a trainer who only posts and leaves the platform is doing the opposite of what the term means here.
- Treating the follower count as the goal: the point of the new eyeballs is booking phone calls to sell the program, so followers who never see a post or book a call do not count as expansion.
