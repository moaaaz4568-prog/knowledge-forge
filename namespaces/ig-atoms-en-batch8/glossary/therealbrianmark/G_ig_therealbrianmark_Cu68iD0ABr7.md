# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cu68iD0ABr7 — start tomorrow

## Term

"Start tomorrow" is the first of four things Brian says poor people do that keep them poor: it is for brokies, and the correction is to start today, because delay is the habit that holds the other three in place. "start tomorrow" appears once in the source, beside "read books and then do nothing", "take advice from broke people" and "hang out with poor people". The list pairs each poor habit with its fix — implement instead of just reading, take advice only from people you would trade places with, study on Friday night instead of partying — so starting tomorrow is the delay habit that the rest of the advice is built to break.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading it as a scheduling tip: Brian frames starting tomorrow as a class marker — "it's for brokies" — so the phrase names a poverty habit, not a calendar choice.
- Treating "start today" as the whole lesson: the list has three more habits, so starting today without implementing, choosing better advisers, and changing Friday nights would leave the pattern intact.
