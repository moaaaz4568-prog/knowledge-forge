# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsevoR0AuaV — pain points

## Term

Pain points are the raw material Brian's three questions are designed to extract: asking why the prospect needs help, why now, and what happens if they wait makes the prospect state their own pains, which the coach then reuses later on the call to close. "pain points" appears once in the source, beside "convince themselves", "justify their purchase" and "create urgency". The questions work because the prospect convinces himself — the coach never argues for the product — so the pain points are the prospect's own words turned into the reason to buy.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating pain points as a list the coach invents: Brian's questions are built to make the prospect volunteer them, so pain points the coach assumes without asking cannot do the closing work.
- Reading the three questions as small talk: each one has a job — surfacing pain, forcing a now-versus-later choice, and pricing delay — so skipping any of them leaves the prospect unconvinced.
