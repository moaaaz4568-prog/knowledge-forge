# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cuz2TwrgNHe — dump, chunk, and sequence

## Term

In this source, "dump, chunk, and sequence" is Brian Mark's three-step process for getting rid of overwhelm in business: dump every thought onto a notepad, chunk those thoughts into different categories, and sequence them from most important to least important. The phrase appears once, and the video presents the result as a clear plan that only needs action, with each verb doing one job — emptying the head, organising, then ordering. His concrete starting move is the notepad dump itself, before any sorting happens.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Here's a three-step process to get rid of overwhelm in your business. Dump, chunk, and sequence." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_Cuz2TwrgNHe.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the process is read as three ways to prioritise: here dumping comes first and is separate from sorting — writing all thoughts out before organising them into categories and ordering them.
- When the clear plan is read as the finished work: the video says the plan only sets up the action, and the action itself still has to be taken.
