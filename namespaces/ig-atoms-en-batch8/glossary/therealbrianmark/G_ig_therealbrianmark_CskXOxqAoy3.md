# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CskXOxqAoy3 — yes ladder

## Term

The yes ladder is the sequence of small agreements Brian builds before pitching: he explains the workout schedule in the app, the nutrition plan that updates weekly, and the weekly check-ins, asking "does that make sense" and "is that clear" after each piece so the prospect says yes three times before any price appears. "yes ladder" appears once in the source, beside "sell the program back to us", "two different options" and "shut up and let them pick". Its purpose is to get the prospect to sell the program back to the coach — naming what stood out most — before the $400-a-month and $999 options are laid out, so the close is a choice between two prices, not a yes-or-no.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating the yes ladder as tricking the prospect into agreement: each yes follows a real explanation of workouts, nutrition, or check-ins, so the ladder only works if the prospect genuinely agrees with each piece.
- Reading it as the pitch itself: the ladder ends before the price, and the actual pitch is the two-option choice that follows, so the ladder prepares the decision rather than making it.
