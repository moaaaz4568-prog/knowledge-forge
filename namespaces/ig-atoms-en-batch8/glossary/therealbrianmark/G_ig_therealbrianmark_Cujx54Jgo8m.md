# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cujx54Jgo8m — action

## Term

In this source, "action" is the dividing line Brian Mark draws between people who stay broke and people who succeed: the unsuccessful delay taking action while waiting for the right time, and the successful take action fast and figure it out along the way. The word appears 2 times, next to "delay taking action", "wake up every single day at 3:30 in the morning", and "Do it fast. Do it now.", and the video pairs the call to act with a blunt picture of the alternative — making 40 to 50K a year working for somebody you do not like. His concrete test is the morning routine itself: getting after it daily at 3:30 while others keep waiting.

## Live Example

> "You're not successful because you delay taking action." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_Cujx54Jgo8m.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "action" is read as any busy work: here it means starting now on the goal itself, not preparing endlessly for the right time.
- When the 3:30 morning is read as the whole message: the wake-up time is only his illustration of acting daily, while the claim is that delay itself is what keeps people unsuccessful.
