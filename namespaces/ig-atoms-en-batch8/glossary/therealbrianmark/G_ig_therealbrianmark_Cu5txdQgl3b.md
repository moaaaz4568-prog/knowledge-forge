# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cu5txdQgl3b — change your mindset

## Term

Changing your mindset is Brian's answer to a business that is not growing: the thoughts that block growth are "I don't have enough money, there aren't enough leads, the market is too saturated", and they put the mind in a negative place where success cannot even be pictured. "change your mindset" appears once in the source, beside "don't see yourself achieving success", "seven billion people" and "20 of them". The scale argument does the work — seven billion people on the planet, and a full-time online coach needs only twenty — so the mindset change is from scarcity about the market to arithmetic about how few clients suffice.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading it as positive thinking: the change is specific — from believing the market is saturated to counting that twenty clients out of seven billion is enough — so vague optimism without the count is not what the term means here.
- Treating the mindset as the whole business plan: Brian presents the mindset as what restores motivation to work on the business, so the work still has to happen after the mind changes.
