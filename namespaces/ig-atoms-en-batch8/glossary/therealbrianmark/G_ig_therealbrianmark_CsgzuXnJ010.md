# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsgzuXnJ010 — 10K month

## Term

The 10K month is the first income milestone Gabriel Latouf credits to the Change Lives Academy: working with Brian and Cole moved him from face-to-face training to fully online, and hitting $10,000 in a month was the initial goal he has now passed. "10K month" appears once in the source, beside "Change Lives Academy", "fully online" and "earning more than ever". Gabriel frames it as a beginning rather than a peak — he expects to go much further because the academy teaches something new every week — so the milestone marks the point where the online business started working, not a final result.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading the 10K month as Brian's own income: the speaker is Gabriel Latouf, a client of the academy, so the milestone belongs to a student's business, not to Brian's.
- Treating it as a guaranteed outcome of joining: Gabriel describes weekly learning and his own move online, so the month follows the work of switching models, not the enrollment alone.
