# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CvM5v0cAMWs — ghosted

## Term

In this source, "ghosted" is the silence Brian Mark weaponises to get clients responding: instead of asking whether John is still interested, the coach scrolls up, reads what the prospect's goals are, and asks whether he is not interested in that goal anymore. The word appears once, in the claim that people would rather awkwardly respond than leave you ghosted, because going silent says they do not care about their own goals. The follow-up works by tying the silence to the prospect's own stated goal, so ignoring the message becomes ignoring themselves.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "This works so well because people would rather awkwardly respond to you than leave you ghosted because that says that they don't care about their own goals." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_CvM5v0cAMWs.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "ghosted" is read as the prospect rejecting the program: here it is simply the unanswered message, and the video treats it as a state to break by invoking the prospect's goals, not as a no.
- When the follow-up is read as another check-in: the message quotes the prospect's own goal back at them, which is what makes silence feel like abandoning the goal rather than ignoring a salesperson.
