# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtJp9HOgnJs — magic word

## Term

The magic word is the prospect's own name: Brian claims it is used in literally every language, that people love hearing it, and that using it three times in the first three minutes of meeting someone draws them to you and keeps you from forgetting it. "magic word" appears once in the source, beside "addicted to you and your vibe", "made me millions of dollars in sales" and "three times within the first three minutes". The word is magical in his telling because it is personal — it is the one word the other person never tires of hearing — so the technique is repetition of a name, not a secret vocabulary.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating the magic word as a hidden sales term: the source names it plainly as the other person's name, so anyone hunting for a special word has missed that the word is the listener's own.
- Reading the three-times rule as flattery: Brian's stated benefit is mutual — the other person is drawn to you and you remember their name — so mechanical repetition without attention does not deliver either half.
