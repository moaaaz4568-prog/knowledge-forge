# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cvhg2vLAH-8 — PPVCO

## Term

In this source, "PPVCO" is Brian Mark's five-part structure for closing deals: Pre-frame, Pain, Vision, Close, and Objections. The acronym appears once, and each part is scripted: the pre-frame sets the tone by going over the prospect's number one struggle and number one goal and offering a game plan, and it ends by asking for a hell yes or a hell no, nothing in the middle. Pain asks what the struggle is and how it has negatively affected them, vision asks what 16 weeks of accomplishing all goals would look and feel like, close presents the training plan as the bridge from deep pain to dream future, and objections covers the four he expects: no money, no time, check with spouse, need to think about it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "PPVCO" is read as five separate scripts: here it is one call structure in order — pre-frame, pain, vision, close, objections — and the close only works after the pain and vision are established.
- When the pre-frame's "hell yes or hell no" is read as pressure: the video presents it as setting the tone at the start of the call, agreed with "Is that fair?", before any selling begins.
