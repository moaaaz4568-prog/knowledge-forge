# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cuw8RJvg4th — consistency

## Term

In this source, "consistency" is the biggest lesson Kyle Johnson, Brian Mark's client, names from working with him: consistency, drive, and not worrying about the hard work, figuring it all out over time. The word appears once, at the end of an interview that tracks Kyle from zero money and zero clients to over 100 low ticket and over 45 high ticket clients doing over $16,000 a month, with a background in biochemistry and biomechanics and over 450 clients served in three years. The interview presents consistency as the habit behind the numbers rather than a single decision, and Kyle credits the whole turnaround to having met Brian Mark.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "consistency" is read as posting every day: here it is the client's answer to what he learned from coaching, tied to building from zero clients to 150, not to a content calendar.
