# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtzjNqmgPjJ — Get People to Buy Your Shit

## Term

"Get People to Buy Your Shit" is the name Brian gives his six-step sales structure: uncover the main struggle, ask how it has impacted their life, build the dream outcome, explain why action must happen now, introduce the product as the bridge from pain to dream outcome, and reveal the price while smashing objections. "Get People to Buy Your Shit" appears once in the source, beside "sales script", "emotional pain" and "bigger wallet". He presents it as the replacement for a script that keeps coaches poor, so the name is a blunt label for a structure that sells by making the prospect's pain and dream outcome do the persuading.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading the name as permission to be crude with prospects: the six steps are all questions and listening — struggle, impact, dream outcome — so the bluntness is in the title, not in how the coach speaks to the buyer.
- Treating it as a script to memorize: Brian calls scripts the thing that keeps coaches poor, so the six steps are a structure to follow in conversation, not lines to recite word for word.
