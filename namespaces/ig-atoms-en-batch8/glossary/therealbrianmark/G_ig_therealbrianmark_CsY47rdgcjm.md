# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsY47rdgcjm — Facebook group engagement

## Term

Facebook group engagement is the visible activity that keeps Brian's coaching clients from falling off track: if the group is quiet when clients open their phones, motivation fades, but if they keep seeing the Fit for Life group, they stay in the program and start looking like Siva. "Facebook group engagement" appears once in the source, beside "follow the crowd", "weekly challenges", "water chug challenge" and "sweaty selfie challenge". His fix is to bribe participation with $100 in cash or supplements, so engagement is something the coach manufactures with challenges and prizes rather than something that grows on its own.

## Synonyms

- Facebook group (the shorter form Brian uses when he tells coaches to bribe clients to engage in it)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading engagement as proof the coaching works: Brian treats it as a retention lever — clients who keep seeing the group stay motivated — not as evidence that anyone is getting fitter.
- Assuming the challenges are the training: the water chug and sweaty selfie challenges exist to make clients open the group, while the actual coaching happens elsewhere.
