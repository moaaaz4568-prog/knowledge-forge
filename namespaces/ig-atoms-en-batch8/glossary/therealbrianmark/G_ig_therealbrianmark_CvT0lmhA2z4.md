# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CvT0lmhA2z4 — scarcity

## Term

In this source, "scarcity" is the psychology Brian Mark uses to make an offer irresistible: three hacks — personal scarcity, shutting down access, and sales scarcity — that make people beg to buy. The word appears 3 times, in "use scarcity in your marketing", "create personal scarcity", and "create sales scarcity", and each hack is demonstrated: personal scarcity makes customers feel the pain of putting off action, shutting down access means enrolment actually closes on a firm deadline so the next opening draws begging buyers, and sales scarcity raises the pre-sale price from $79 until the 10th to $150 until the 21st. The throughline is that the limit must be real — "when you say enrollments closed, you actually have to mean it".

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When "scarcity" is read as simply raising the price: here the price rise is only the third hack, and the first two are about the pain of delay and a genuinely closed enrolment.
- When a closed enrolment is read as a marketing line: the video insists the deadline must be firm and missed enrolments really closed, or the scarcity stops working.
