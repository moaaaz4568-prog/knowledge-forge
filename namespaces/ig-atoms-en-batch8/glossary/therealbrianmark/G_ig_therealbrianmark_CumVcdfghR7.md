# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CumVcdfghR7 — three step close

## Term

In this source, the "three step close" is Brian Mark's script for forcing a prospect to name their own reason for not buying: he tells Mrs. Prospect there are only three reasons she would not buy, then lists them and asks which one it is. The phrase appears once, and the three reasons are that she does not believe she can become healthy and lose weight, that she does not believe he is the person who can help her, and that she has given up on her goals completely and is okay with being unhealthy. By making her pick one, the close turns a vague stall into a single objection the coach can answer, and the video presents it as working "like f***ing magic".

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the "three step close" is read as three separate closes: here it is one script with three named reasons, and the power is in asking the prospect to choose among them.
- When the third reason is read as an insult to deliver: the video frames it as the prospect's own possible admission, offered with "which could be true, but I don't think so", not as an accusation.
