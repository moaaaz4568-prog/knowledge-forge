# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CuFpnSAAFml — the word but

## Term

The word "but" is Brian's sales hack for disarming objections: start with all the downsides — the program demands hard work and will not be easy — then say "but", and everything after it lands harder while everything before it is negated. "the word but" appears three times in the source, beside "downsides", "negates everything" and "amplifies everything". The hack works because prospects are always hunting for the negatives or the catch, so handing them the negatives first and then flipping them with "but" turns their suspicion into the setup for the positives.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading "but" as a filler word: in this source it is a deliberate pivot that negates the downsides before it and amplifies the positives after it, so casual use of "but" in ordinary speech is not the hack.
- Treating the downsides as honesty for its own sake: the negatives are listed so the "but" can cancel them, so a pitch that lists downsides without the pivot would leave the prospect with only the negatives.
