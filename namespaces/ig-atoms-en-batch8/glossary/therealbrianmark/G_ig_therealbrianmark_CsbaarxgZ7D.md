# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CsbaarxgZ7D — gamifying

## Term

Gamifying is how Brian turns his coaching program into a progression that feels like Super Mario: each level gets harder, more challenging, and more rewarding, so students feel good as they level up and stay excited about the work. "Gamifying" appears once in the source, beside "graduation call", "five K club", "ten K a month club" and "levels to advance through". The concrete rungs are a graduation call after the first six weeks, a five K club at $5,000 a month, and a custom hoodie at $10,000 a month, so the game is a ladder of income milestones with a reward at each step.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading gamifying as adding games to training: Brian's levels are business milestones inside the coaching program, not workouts, so the play is in the progression and rewards, not in the exercises.
- Assuming the rewards are the motivation: the source says the feeling of leveling up is what keeps students excited, with the hoodie and clubs marking progress that already happened rather than buying it.
