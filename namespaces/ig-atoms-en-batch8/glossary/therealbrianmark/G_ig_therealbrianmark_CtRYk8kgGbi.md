# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtRYk8kgGbi — coach you for a minute

## Term

"Coach you for a minute" is the permission question Brian uses on a caller who thinks he is doing everything right: asking "would you mind if I coach you for a minute" and getting a yes gives the coach the right to grill the man about eating too much food and doing too much cardio to compensate. "coach you for a minute" appears once in the source, beside "permission to grill them", "stubborn" and "aware that they have a problem". The permission matters because the prospect believes he has no problem, so the coaching can only land after he agrees to be coached — the question converts a stubborn caller into a listener.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Treating the question as a polite opener: without the yes, Brian says the prospect's defenses stay up, so the minute of coaching only exists because permission was granted, not because the coach started talking.
- Reading it as an attack on the prospect: the grilling names specific mistakes — too much food, too much cardio, poor recovery — and ends with an invitation to change, so the point is making the problem visible, not winning an argument.
