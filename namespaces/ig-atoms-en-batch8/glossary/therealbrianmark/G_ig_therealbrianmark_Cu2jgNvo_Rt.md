# G_ig_therealbrianmark_Cu2jgNvo_Rt — leap of faith

## Term

A leap of faith is the $1,000 Brian spent on a mentor when he had only $1,300 in the bank: he forced himself to invest without the money to spare, cut fast food, weed, alcohol, and cigarettes to afford it, and was making $5,000 a month as an online fitness coach a year later. "leap of faith" appears once in the source, beside "$1,300 in my bank", "cut out spending" and "find a mentor". The leap is presented as a deliberate trade — present comfort for a mentor who can grow the business — so the faith is in the investment's return, not in luck.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading the leap as reckless spending: Brian funded it by cutting specific habits and names the one-year result, so the leap is a calculated reallocation, not a gamble with the last of his money.
- Treating the mentor as the whole explanation: the leap also required cutting spending and a year of work, so the $1,000 bought guidance that the buyer still had to use.
