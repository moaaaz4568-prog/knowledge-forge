# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CtaEwOBAoT_ — weekly rituals

## Term

Weekly rituals are the four self-care appointments Brian keeps to protect a millionaire mindset: a 45-minute hypnosis session, acupuncture for a body tightened by five weekly workouts, a 90-minute full-body massage, and cairo, which he calls easy to put off but beneficial every time. "weekly rituals" appears once in the source, beside "millionaire mindset", "self care rituals", "decompress" and "injury free". They are scheduled, repeated, and paid for every week, so the rituals are maintenance — keeping the mind clear and the body uninjured — that makes making money easy, rather than luxuries bought after success.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Reading weekly rituals as a wealth routine that creates money: Brian says the rituals keep his mind clear and keep him injury free, and then it is easy to make money, so the money follows the maintenance rather than coming from the rituals directly.
- Treating the list as a prescription: the four items are his personal appointments — hypnosis, acupuncture, massage, cairo — so copying them exactly would miss that they are chosen for his schedule and his training load.
