# G_ig_therealbrianmark_CviJqdng657 — block out

## Term

In this source, to "block out" is Brian Mark's instruction for handling the opinions of people who do not understand you or your ambitions: ignore them because you know who you are, you know what your goals are, and nothing will get in your way. The phrase appears once, and the video's test is money — "your opinion doesn't pay my fucking rent, so I'm not paying any attention to it" — so an opinion only earns attention if it pays. His concrete stance is indifference grounded in self-knowledge, not an argument with the critics.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You have to block out the opinions of people who don't understand you or your ambitions." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_CviJqdng657.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When to "block out" is read as never listening to anyone: here it is aimed at the opinions of people who do not understand you or your ambitions, and the reason given is that their opinion does not pay the rent.
- When the indifference is read as arrogance: the video grounds it in knowing who you are and what your goals are, so the blocking follows from self-knowledge, not from contempt.
