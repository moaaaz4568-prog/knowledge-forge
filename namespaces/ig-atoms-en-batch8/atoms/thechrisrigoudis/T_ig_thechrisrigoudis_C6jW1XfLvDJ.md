# T_C6jW1XfLvDJ_01

"Your product and your presentation of your product are two different things. Your presentation should never be a product description. If you're doing that, you're boring your prospects to death. Because the reality is they couldn't care less about what you're offering. What they care about is what it does for them." — sources/S_ig_thechrisrigoudis_C6jW1XfLvDJ.md

# T_C6jW1XfLvDJ_02

"That's why I always advise clients to start with deep discovery, find out what they want, what they need, what they dream about, what they desire. And then you connect all those dreams and pain points with what your product does for them, and you put together a compelling pitch that talks only about these points." — sources/S_ig_thechrisrigoudis_C6jW1XfLvDJ.md

# T_C6jW1XfLvDJ_03

"If I'm out in the market to buy a car for my daughter and the salesperson talks about the rims and how fast it goes, I'm probably not going to buy from them. So focus on what they want and present it." — sources/S_ig_thechrisrigoudis_C6jW1XfLvDJ.md
