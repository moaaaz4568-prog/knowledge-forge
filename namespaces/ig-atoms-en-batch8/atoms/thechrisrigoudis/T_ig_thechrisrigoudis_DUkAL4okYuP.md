# T_DUkAL4okYuP_01

"Never pitch before the prospect admits the cost of not changing. That's what amateurs do. If they don't feel the downside of where they are, your offer feels optional, and optional things don't get bought. So here are my two favorite questions to close them. What happens if you don't make a change and your problems stay the same?" — sources/S_ig_thechrisrigoudis_DUkAL4okYuP.md

# T_DUkAL4okYuP_02

"And when they tell you what that is, you ask them to expand it in a broader time horizon. So first you get them to realize the cost of inaction today, and then you expand by making them calculate the consequences of staying the same. People avoid their problems until the cost of staying the same outweighs the cost of change." — sources/S_ig_thechrisrigoudis_DUkAL4okYuP.md
