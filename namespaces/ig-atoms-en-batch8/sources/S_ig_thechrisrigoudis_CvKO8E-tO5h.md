Want to know how to respond when you send a cold email or a DM and the client replies back with "send me your prices"? What you don't want to do is send them your pricing because that's gonna cost you the sale. Instead, I want you to do this: Yeah, our pricing is dynamic and really depends on clients' needs. What might work for our clients might not work for you, and I'm not quite sure if I can help you in the first place. I'd have to know a little bit more around how you're solving X and Y and Z to see if it even makes sense for us to work together. Would you be against having a short call to see if we can even help you? Now, why are we not sending pricing? Because you haven't taken enough time to build the gap in their mind between the cost and the value. So no matter what you pitch them, they don't have a reference point. So you have to get them on a call, build the gap, and then present.
