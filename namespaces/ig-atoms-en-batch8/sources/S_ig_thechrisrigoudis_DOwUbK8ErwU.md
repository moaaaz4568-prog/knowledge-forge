Therapy doesn't work for most people because it focuses on the wrong things. Most people go to therapy because they want to resolve a traumatic event or a childhood issue. My question is, why would you go back and dig up an old wound and open it up to bleed again? If you have a scar, you don't punch a knife in there to see what the scar tissue looks like. You let it be, you move on, you live with the scar, but you don't let it define you. So instead of digging up old wounds, I focus on defining the best version of myself and reverse engineering my journey towards that. And then along the way, when I find people damaged exactly like me, I step up to heal them. And through that work, I heal myself. My goal is to build a new pattern of behavior, and I cannot do that by looking at the past.
