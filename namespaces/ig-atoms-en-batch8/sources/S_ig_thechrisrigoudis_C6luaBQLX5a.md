These are the two biggest mistakes I see everyone making in the end of the sales call. Number one, they don't finish with a clear next step. The prospect gives them "let me think about it," the wave goodbye, and the deal goes into a black hole. There are only three acceptable options: they're gonna say yes and they get started, they're gonna say no and you close their file, or you bamfam, you book a meeting from a meeting. The second biggest mistake is you only give them one option. You say things like "ready to get started?" People wanna have the illusion of control. So if you don't wanna create sales resistance, instead of closing the call with "ready to get started" and getting thrown into a pit of objections, say "here are the next steps, how would you like to proceed?" Outline the process on how it typically works when you onboard a new client and give them the illusion that they have control.
