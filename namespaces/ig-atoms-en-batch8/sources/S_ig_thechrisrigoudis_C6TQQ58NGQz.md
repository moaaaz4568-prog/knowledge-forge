Never pitch something to a curious prospect. Here's what I mean. When people say things like, "I'm just curious," "I just want to find out more," "I'm looking around right now," it's going to be a waste of your time. Curious people don't buy. Committed people buy. So you need to turn that curiosity into commitment to change. Now, how do you do that is by asking the right questions. You have to ask questions like, "So right now fixing this problem, is it a nice to have or a must have?" You can also ask, "Why is it important for you now though?" Or, "How important is this for you to solve this problem so you can get to this result?" And if you get wishy-washy answers and there's zero commitment from the prospect to make a change, just leave the call. It's not worth your time to go through 10, 20 minutes of trying to sell their ears away only for them to tell you, "Oh, I'll think about it and I'll make a decision next quarter." You're just murdering your time.
