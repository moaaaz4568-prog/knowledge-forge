What are three phrases that make you sound weak? Well, the first phrase is "I think." Language reveals identity. If you speak like you're unsure, your prospect assumes you are. "I think this offer could help" sounds like you're guessing. "I believe this offer solves your problem" sounds like you've seen it work and you're backing it. Then it's "to be honest with you." This phrase makes the buyer wonder, were you not honest before this? So it creates a crack in your authority. "To be honest with you, I was surprised with the results." Instead, you say, "The results were strong and that's why we aimed for." The third thing is "is now a bad time?" It frames your interaction as an inconvenience. Instead, say, "I'll be brief, but I have to say this." So open with confidence and relevance, not with an apology.
