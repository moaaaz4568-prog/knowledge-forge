One of the reasons today's marketing fails is because of hyperbole. Hyperbole happens when you have all these companies making insane claims, which in most cases they fail to deliver. So the market becomes so sophisticated, even when you do have a quality service, they just won't believe you and won't buy. And the easy way to fix this is, instead of making huge claims, you just remove a task that your prospect already has in their to-do list. For example, if you offer a fitness program, instead of saying something like, "You're gonna lose 30 pounds in 30 days, " which is a huge claim, you can say, "I'm gonna give you easy fat-blasting workouts for three times a week. " Which is, they wanna work out three times a week, they want easy workouts that don't take up a lot of time, and tick off a box from their to-do list, also seeing the picture that they're gonna blast their fat.
