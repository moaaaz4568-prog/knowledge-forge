The only way to be good in sales is to enjoy pain. It's a very, very painful occupation. You get your confidence beat to hell all day long. Your head is in the guillotine all day long. You're at risk for losing your job all day long. You're always pissing somebody off. You're pissing your boss off. You're pissing a customer off. You're pissing your significant other off because you're not earning any money. There's no way you're going to be able to learn everything you need to know in the two-week period of ramping that somebody gives you before they fire you for not performing. Do you enjoy pain? I find this to be a very low-performing mentality. You don't need to be in pain to succeed in sales. You just need to make a decision. I'm in this to win and I will perform as a winner. Winners don't create stories about pain and they don't need pain to perform. They tell themselves that I will win no matter what. I will not let rejection get to me. I believe in my product. I believe in myself. I believe that I have the keys to fix the solution. Once I know that, it doesn't matter what story the prospect is telling themselves or what managers tell me or what the numbers tell me. If I tie my identity to the numbers, I will always have performance-based love for myself.
