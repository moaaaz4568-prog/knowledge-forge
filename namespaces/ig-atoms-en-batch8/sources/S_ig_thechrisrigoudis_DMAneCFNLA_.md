Chris, how do I get clients for my agency? Cold emails, cold DMs, or cold calls? Don't cold call, don't cold DM, cold email. Busy people don't pick up the phone. 30% will never answer the phone, 50% won't be interested. And when you DM, you're lost in an ocean of unanswered DMs in the general inbox or in the spam inbox. How I grew my agency back then is I acted as a sniper instead of a shotgun approach. I harvested specific emails of CEOs and I put my entire offer inside the cold email.
