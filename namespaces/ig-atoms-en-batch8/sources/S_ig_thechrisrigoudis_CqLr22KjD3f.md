Your ability to influence others relies on your ability to influence yourself. Let me give you an example. If you're a business owner or a sales professional, look at the conversations that you've had recently. Have you ever had any discussion where you said, "Hmm, let me think about that," or before you bought something, you say, "Hey, just want to check with my partner first"? Then don't be surprised if you're getting "think about it" or "partner" objections. This is a concept called congruency between words and action. So if you're feeling that you're giving these objections yourself, with what level of congruency and consistency are you going to help your own prospects overcome their own objections? So a lot of times, it's the law of the mirror. We are the objection that we give. Always remember that.
