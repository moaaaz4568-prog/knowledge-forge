If you want to get more clients consistently, eliminate proposals from your process. Proposals are a tool of consideration, not conversion. So every time someone tells you on a call, "Yeah, this all sounds perfect, just send me a proposal," you're giving them an easy way out if you let them go. Instead, eliminate proposals, or if you have to do proposals, book a second call and go through the proposal with them so you can get a decision, close the deal.
