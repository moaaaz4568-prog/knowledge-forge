Never lower your pricing when the prospect says they don't have the budget. You say something like, "Our pricing starts at twenty thousand," and the buyer says, "We've only budgeted five thousand for this. What can you do for us?" Simply say this: "That's no problem. I can refer you to a competitor who has cheaper products. Now, may I ask, why do you feel hundreds of clients choose us over them though?" Now just think, what will this do in their mind? They will start coming up with reasons why people choose you even if you're more expensive. You don't need to tell them that you're more qualified, you have more experience, you have more support or resources. Let them come up with the reasons why they should go with you. That's how a collaborative sale happens, instead of being overly combative or having to discount to fit their budget. It's not about their budget, it's about what the actual value they're looking for is. Make sure you follow for more tips.
