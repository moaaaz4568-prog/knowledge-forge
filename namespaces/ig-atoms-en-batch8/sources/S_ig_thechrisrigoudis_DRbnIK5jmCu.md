The difference between selling to employees and business owners is that employees buy safety, business owners buy big wins. Employees want to avoid the worst case scenario, business owners want the biggest best case scenario. Employees want to feel safe and insured, so if you sell them something, say this: nothing will blow up, here's the guarantee, here's the structure, and here's the predictable result. Now if you're selling the exact same thing to business owners, say this instead: this opens up the biggest returns for you, and it gives you leverage to go after the biggest opportunities. So remember, employees will buy because of a big safety net, business owners will buy because of a big vision.
