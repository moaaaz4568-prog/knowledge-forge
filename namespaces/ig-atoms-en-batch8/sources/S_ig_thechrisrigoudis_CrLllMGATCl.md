A common mistake people make is they future pace their prospects way too much. They say things like, "You will be able to do this," "You will lose weight," "You will make so much money," "You will be able to grow your business." That's in the future, right? That's ethereal. You're making a promise that they cannot necessarily visualize. Instead, make the reframe to, "Our clients are able to do X," "Clients are achieving Y." This helps them see that this is happening on the inside. These are things that actually get done. Instead of, "When you buy, you're gonna achieve this and this and this," speak from what's happening today, what's getting done on the inside with other cases just like them, instead of you making big fat promises around the future.
