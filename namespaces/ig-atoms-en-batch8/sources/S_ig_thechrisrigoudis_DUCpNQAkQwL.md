Most salespeople are more motivated to hit their commissions rather than trying to solve the problem of the prospect. This makes it really hard to close sales because you're subjecting the prospect to assumptive selling, pressure tactics and fear mongering. I was coaching this one sales guy where if the prospect didn't make a decision on the call, he will pull the offer completely and he would remove the option for them to ever buy. Because he cared more about getting the money rather than helping the prospect make the best decision for themselves. The best closers don't care about their commissions. They care about unselling their prospects and they create an illusion of choice that either a yes or a no is a hundred percent acceptable.
