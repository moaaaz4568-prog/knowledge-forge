Here's the easiest way to handle a fear and a self-belief objection. This is the case when prospects come in, they say, "Yeah, I feel this is great, but I don't believe I can do it." They have challenges with seeing themselves accomplishing, getting to their desired state with your help. What people tend to do in these cases is they try to push the prospect in, "Hey man, we'll support you." This is really common, but nobody's gonna tell them that. So here's what to do instead. The way to unlock that is with a relatable story, something that we call tactical vulnerability. When I coach people, they're afraid to take the leap, especially if they're new to sales. I tell the story of me. Before I got into sales, I was a pharmacist. I was stuck in a steel box inside production, inhaling antibiotics, rotting myself from the inside out. So if I didn't make the leap to get into sales, to get into copywriting, to get into my own business, I was probably slowly killing myself. So that injects a massive level of trust into my prospect and take the leap.
