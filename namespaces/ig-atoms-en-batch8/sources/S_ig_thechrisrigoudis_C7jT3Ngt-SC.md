Three things to never do in your sales. The first is never fight for the sale, fight for the person. Your questions, your tonality, your language must show that your intent there is not to sell but to help. Once you help the prospect paint a clear picture of their vision, and you work in collaboration with them to bring that mission and vision into fruition, they see that you're on their side of the table, not just there to get the sale. Number two, don't attack the person, attack their perspective. Everyone has sabotaging beliefs that are holding them back from getting to the next level. Don't attack them, attack that false idea. Help them reframe that perspective in a polite way and they will move forward. Three, never assume anything. If you're not 100% clear about what the prospect means when they say something, ask clarifying questions. How do you mean by that? Can you give me an example? How's that showing up for you exactly? This will help you go below the surface and help the prospect unpack their perspective because making assumptions will cost you the sale.
