Don't focus on your inputs, focus on your outputs. I was talking with a client the other day and he's been having back-to-back record-breaking months, but came a day he looked at his pipeline and he's like, "I'm out of leads." What I asked him was, "Well, what did you do 90 days ago that shows up today?" You see, too many people, they come closer and closer to the end of the month, they look at their sales and they're like 5k, 10k, 15k, 20k, and like, "How do I make more?" Well, it's not about how do you make more and asking the universe for more. It's what do you put out to the universe? How many emails are you sending? How many DMs? How many conversations are you having with new people so you can get the outcomes that you want? People underestimate the amount of volume needed to get to where they want to go. So go out there, put more stuff out to the universe, and make sure you follow to get the right output.
