Never do this on your discovery calls. I was discussing with an agency owner the other day and he was running a two-call close system. First call, find out about their problems. Second call, propose your packages. The problem was you spend the entire, any 30 minutes of the first call, unloading all of their problems and then they're asked, hey, how can you solve this for me? And you start explaining your service and then you give them a few ideas and then you propose a second call to make the actual proposal and give them the price. This is a big mistake because if your first call takes too long and they unload all of their problems and you make suggestions and you give them a range of your prices, they're not going to want to jump on a second call unless they look at other providers if they can do this cheaper or in a better way. So instead, keep your first call very short, to the point, only making sure that you can help them and maybe they have the budget for something like this. On the second call, jump in, revalidate their problems and then propose the plan and the packages. Short first call, a longer second call, so you can close the deal.
