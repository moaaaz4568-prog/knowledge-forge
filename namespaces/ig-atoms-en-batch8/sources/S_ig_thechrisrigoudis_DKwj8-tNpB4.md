What are three phrases that break sales resistance? When a prospect resists you, they're protecting something. It's either their ego, their identity, or one belief they've clung to for years. So if you push back directly, their defenses go up. If you agree, even just slightly, you disarm them. This is called pacing their ego. It creates safety, creates a container where they're ready to open up. When there's safety, there's space to lead them. You say something like this: "Of course you do." You use them when the buyer expresses fear, doubt, or hesitation. An example would be they say, "I'm not sure this will work for me." "Of course you do. You've probably seen a hundred people overpromise before." So you instantly align with their skepticism instead of fighting it. The second phrase I love is "That makes sense." You use it where they state a limiting belief. "I just don't know if now's the right time." "That makes sense. If you're juggling a lot, timing feels like it's everything to you." That's when you validate their world before shifting it. Third phrase is "You're not wrong." You use it when they bring up a partially true objection. They say, "It's a big investment." "You're not wrong, and it is a big investment, and that's exactly why it's powerful." You acknowledge the truth while pivoting to yours. So each phrase does two things at once. It paces their current belief, which creates rapport and lowers the defense, and positions you as emotionally intelligent, calm, and in total control. I never argue with resistance. I dissolve resistance with empathy that's sharper than logic.
