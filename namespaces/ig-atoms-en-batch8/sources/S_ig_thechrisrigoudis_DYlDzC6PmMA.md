Closing sales becomes much easier when you see the right questions that prevent objections in the first place. If you know a price objection is going to come up, ask your prospect early what is currently costing them to stay the same. If you know that an "I need to think about it" objection is going to come up, ask them early what would need to be true for you to feel confident to make a decision. If you know that they'll have an "I need to talk to my spouse" objection, ask them early on, "Is your spouse aware that we're having this conversation, or are we in a secret mission here?" When you've asked these questions, they have now told you their conditions, and the sale becomes a lot easier to close when the conditions are naked. Preventing objections from happening in the first place is just good communication. And if you want to learn how to overcome all of your prospect's objections, make sure to follow for more sales tips.
