When a prospect tells you they're shopping around, they're doing two things. They're opening the exit door already, so they can just tell you, "Yes, I'm gonna look at other options later," so you don't push them to buy. And number two, they're looking to get the best deal out of you. So they're gonna be asking for discounts because they already told you that they're looking at other options. How to get ahead of it is the moment they say they're shopping around, before you go into grand presentations, ask them what they have already seen. What have they seen in terms of pricing, in terms of options, and what are their ideal criteria around what they're looking to buy. If you ask that question to a prospect and they start tripping on their own words, they're either trying to squeeze the best deal out of you or they're just opening the exit door so you don't push them to buy.
