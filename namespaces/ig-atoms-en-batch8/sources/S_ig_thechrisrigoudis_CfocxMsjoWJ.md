A lot of people offer a yay, but they forget about the boo. Here's what I mean. Yay is the big promise. It's the claim that you make. Looking at your product, looking at your service, you're taking it to the marketplace and you're pushing out a marketing message. How to get clients, how to lose weight. The boo though is the inherent objection that comes with every claim that we make. For example, when looking at a headline that says how to get clients, a potential customer might think, well, I don't have a big marketing budget, or I don't want to run ads, or I don't want to do webinars. And these are all assumptions in your customer's mind when they're reading your claim. How to fix that is you include the boo, as I call it, inside your headline or your offer and you sexy it up. For example, how to get clients without running ads or webinars, how to lose weight while eating pizzas, how to increase your website conversions without hiring an expensive designer. Always remove the boo, get more sales.
