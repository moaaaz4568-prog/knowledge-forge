If you're starting your sales meetings with "How are you today?" "Where are you calling in from?" You're destroying the sale. Best case scenario, you're wasting time. Worst case scenario, you're triggering sales resistance because that's exactly what everyone else trying to sell them something says to them. Instead, say this: "Well, Chris, good to see you here today. We're on a limited time schedule, so why don't we just get started? What were you hoping to get out of the meeting today?" This puts the focus directly on them, gets things up and running, and you're right into the conversation.
