In marketing and sales, you need to avoid certain phrases that will undervalue you and your service. So when you're marketing your service, don't say, now I'm accepting clients. Say, I only have a few spots left in my coaching program. This implies urgency and scarcity and makes people want to take action now. When you're discussing your price, don't say that your program is $1, 000. Instead, say it's $999. This small effect takes the number from a four-figure number to a three-figure number and makes it more digestible. And finally, when you're explaining your different program options, don't say that one is cheaper than the other. Cheap implies that it's equality and that people shouldn't buy it.
