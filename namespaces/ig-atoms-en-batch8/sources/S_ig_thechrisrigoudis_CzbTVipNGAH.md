Do not ask for the sale after you present. This is a big mistake a lot of people make. They present, they vomit out all the features and benefits, and then they ask, "Okay, are you ready to get started?" They have no idea if that prospect is interested or not interested at that stage. So instead, ask them this: "Out of the plan that I presented to you, what resonates most with you?" Now, what are they going to do? They're gonna sell themselves on the features that they like best. And then you can follow up with, "Okay, why do you feel those are important to you now though?" This drives the urgency behind those features and again gets them to defend those features to them. Once you tick those boxes, all you have to ask is, "Okay, so how would you like to proceed from here?" Because now you know exactly what they like, why they like it, and you can take the final step to ask for the order. Make sure you follow for more tips.
