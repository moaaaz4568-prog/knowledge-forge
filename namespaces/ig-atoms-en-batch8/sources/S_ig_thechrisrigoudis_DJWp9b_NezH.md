When I stopped treating sales calls like speeches and pitches and I started treating them like detective work, I began closing deals a lot faster. Talking instead of listening, your ears will make far more money than your mouth ever will. I used to think differently. I'll be the first to admit that. Selling was about having the perfect words to convince people to buy, but I ended up being broke. So I would walk into calls with a pre-planned script, I would talk at them instead of with them, and I would leave exhausted and without sales. So what I want you to do is practice active listening, but don't just listen to what they say. Listen to the emotion behind their words. Focus on understanding rather than just responding. When prospects talk, stay quiet and take notes. Ask follow-up probing questions, and if you sense hesitation, just ask, "What concerns do you still have?" Make it your mission to uncover the real problem, not just the first one that they mention.
