If you want to get more sales, you don't need more motivation, you need more conviction. You can watch hundreds of Tony Robbins tapes to get pumped up, but if you don't know what to say to sell someone, you'll fail every time. Instead, do these three things to increase your conviction in your offers to sell more. Number one, watch testimonials and case studies. Successful results and proving to yourself that your offer actually works increases your conversion and you're able to share client stories with others. Number two, increase your skill set. Learn how to ask the right questions to find problems and help the prospect brainstorm about their situation and what they're really dealing with. Number three, role play.
