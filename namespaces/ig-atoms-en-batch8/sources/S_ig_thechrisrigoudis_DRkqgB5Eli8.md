Even if the sale doesn't close, you should never leave a sales call without this critical piece of information: how the buyer makes decisions. It doesn't matter if you book a follow up call with them or you keep chasing them for months. You're just going to recycle the same process with them unless you figure out exactly how they buy. How I like to do this is I ask them, "What's the number one most critical factor when you're making this decision?" Is it going to be the cost? Is it going to be a different decision maker? Is it going to be a burning pain point they have right now that they're looking to fix? Now that you have that, when you hop on the next call with them or even your follow ups, the whole new sales call will revolve around that specific decision, and now you will collaborate with them to close the sale.
