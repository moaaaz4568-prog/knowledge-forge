I don't think there's anything wrong with being manipulative in sales. Most people think manipulation is evil, but look at it this way: a surgeon manipulates a scalpel to save a life. A guitarist manipulates strings to make music. A coach manipulates training to get results. But why is it wrong if a salesperson manipulates a conversation? Bad manipulation is tricking someone into doing something that hurts them. Good manipulation is guiding someone to a decision that helps them improve their life. If you're not willing to manipulate, you're basically saying you'd rather let people stuck in their problem instead of leading them out of it. Manipulation isn't the enemy. Selfish intent is.
