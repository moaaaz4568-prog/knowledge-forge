One of the mistakes big companies make is they only have one sales mechanism. They forget that people consume content in different ways. They watch movies and YouTube, they listen to podcasts, they read books, blogs, or the newspaper. So why would they buy in the same way? Yet companies force people down a strategy session where it's a freaking inquisition, a battle of wills for the irresistible offer to come after forty minutes, and they hear no's, I'll think about it, and I get back to you in two months. Instead, when you deploy things like chat conversions, webinars, VSLs, and strategy sessions, you remove a lot of friction of the sales process, you get much happier customers, and much more dough on the back end. Try it out.
