Does price anchoring still work in 2026? Yes, but not in the way most people think. Price anchoring is showing someone something expensive, so anything you show after that feels cheaper. Back in the day, that was enough. You show someone a big number, and then anything you show afterwards feels smaller. But today, buyers are smarter. They see the trick coming. So in 2026, the real price anchor isn't the money. Their time wasted, risk taken, or their reputation. So before you go ahead and pitch your price, ask them exactly this: What is it costing you right now not to make a change in your situation? Now they will anchor the cost of inaction with the cost of action. So moving forward with you will feel like a bargain.
