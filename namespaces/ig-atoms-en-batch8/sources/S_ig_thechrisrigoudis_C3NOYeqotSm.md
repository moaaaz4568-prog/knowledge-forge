Don't treat people how you want to be treated. Treat them like they treat you. In every single interaction, you're either reinforcing or discouraging a behavior. So when someone is kind to you, courteous, they take care of you, they pay attention when you talk, you have to reward that behavior. What gets rewarded gets repeated.
