When you think you deserve something, you stop earning it. You expect doors to open just because you knocked. You walk into rooms thinking, "I'm intelligent, I have good ideas, I'm capable. Why isn't anybody listening to me?" And the answer is devastatingly simple: you haven't earned the right to be listened to yet. Credentials don't make people listen to you. Age doesn't make people listen to you. Even being correct doesn't make people listen to you. You have to earn their attention. You have to prove that you're somebody worth listening to. You have to make them feel something when you speak. And once I accepted that, everything shifted. I stopped networking for what I could get and started networking for who I could connect with. What can I learn from this person? How can I make somebody else's experience better tonight? How can I help out? And people felt the difference immediately. Suddenly, I wasn't just another person trying to extract value. I was somebody who made rooms better by being in them.
