Here are four simple rules to make more sales. Number one is turn statements into questions. That makes the conversation much more engaging, so it's not one-sided. Number two is what the client says is the truth, what you say is a lie, at least in their world. So you have to ask the right powerful questions to get them to sell themselves instead of you going into pitching mode. Number three is listen twice as much as you speak. We have two ears and one mouth, God made it that way for a reason. Number four, always outline the clear next step. Even if you don't win the sale right then and there, have a clear next step in terms of book the next meeting, give them some homework to work on, so they are very clear on what happens next, but they also know what you expect from them.
