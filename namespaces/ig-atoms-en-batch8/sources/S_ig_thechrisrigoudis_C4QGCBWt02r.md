I see so many people make this mistake and it's costing them the sale. They're trying to paint seagulls in the prospect's picture. What does that mean? It means they're trying to create problems where they don't exist or try to create a heaven, an ideal picture that the prospect doesn't care about. The result? The prospect is annoyed because they are being pitched something they don't want. Instead, play dumb. Ask them, "Oh, so you want more leads, but why do you want that though?
