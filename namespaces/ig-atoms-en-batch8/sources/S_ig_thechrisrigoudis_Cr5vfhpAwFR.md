If your content, your ads, your emails are getting lost in a sea of nothingness, it's probably because of this reason. It's because you're using copy like, "Are you tired of this?" "Are you struggling with that?" This is something that ChatGDP would write on its own. This is focusing on the very surface level problem. Instead, shift from the problem to talking about a symptom. Instead of struggling to get attention online, you can reframe it to something like waking up to an empty inbox or ending the month without new clients. So this is something that we call symptomatic messaging. And instead of the problem, it talks about the symptom that they're experiencing. So it resonates much, much more, gets their attention, gets them to raise their hand and reach out to you.
