If your prospect is telling you things like, "I just love your product so much," or "This is a great presentation," "You are so good at what you do," then you're about to get rejected. All that upfront enthusiasm and positivity from them, typically it's an effort from them to try to butter you up because they either can't afford what you're selling or they never intend on actually buying and they're wasting your time. So instead of matching their enthusiasm and asking for the sale, which will lead nowhere, have them defend their own behavior. So say, "Oh really? What did you like best about what I presented?" or "Why do you feel it's now the time to make changes?" This will stress test them and get them to prove to themselves that it is the time to get started, and then you can close the sale.
