One of the most dangerous phrases to hear on a sales call is, "We don't even know what you guys do. What do you do exactly?" This triggers the salesperson to go into pitching mode and right away, "Oh, don't worry, I will tell you exactly what we do. We help people like you do this, do this, do this." And now they're forced into a pitch, to give out pricing, and it blocks the sale from happening. So how do you prevent that? First, you should never hop on a meeting with a buyer who has no idea what they do. You have to have a sales process that educates them along the way with pre-call questionnaire, case studies shared before the call, and even a link to your website and asking them to actually review it before they join the meeting. Then, when it shows up during your call, you have to ask questions like that. Before I share exactly what we do, what caused you to hop on this call in the first place? Or you can ask, "Is there anything that you heard or seen from us online that make you take time out of your day to join the call?" This redirects their focus from you to actually their core desire. Why are they there in the first place, taking time out of their day to talk to a stranger? You see, that's a typical trap most people fall into, and now you know how to prevent that.
