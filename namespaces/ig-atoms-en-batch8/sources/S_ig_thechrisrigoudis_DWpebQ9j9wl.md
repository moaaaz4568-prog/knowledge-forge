You need to take off the clothes of your prospect's objections if you want to close the sale. If you want to sell someone an AI-powered retractable comb for nine thousand dollars and they're scared to invest in it, they're not going to tell you they're scared. They're going to tell you, "I don't have the money." First, ask them something like this: "Putting the money aside, do you feel that this will help you get a quality comb over?" Now we're reframing their thinking from a cost-based thinking to an outcome-based thinking. So once they say yes, I've eliminated the value objection. And to take another layer of clothes off the objection, I would ask, "So is it more about not having the money or that you haven't invested an amount like that for your bald spot before?" And now the objection is naked. So to close more sales, make your objections naked.
