If you're on a sales call with a prospect and they ask you, "Do you have a payment plan?" Don't give them a payment plan. Instead, ask them, "Do you need a payment plan?" In many cases, people are just programmed to say stuff, and asking for a payment plan is part of those things. So don't feel forced to give a payment plan if they don't need one. Just ask them, "Do you need a payment plan?" And say, "Most of my clients do the full pay, so they don't pay extra with a payment plan." Would that help you if we did the full pay, so you don't have to invest extra? And you close the sale.
