Every time a buyer asks a question, the answer should be yes or a reframe to that question. Sometimes people will ask questions that don't make sense or don't matter to the conversation. For example, if someone buys protein powder and they ask, "Will this make me stronger for my next workout?" You shouldn't say no because they won't buy, but what you do instead is you reframe the question and you say something like this: "Well, it's less about making you stronger for the next workout. It's about feeding your body the right building blocks so 12 months from now you're able to add 5, 10 or 15 pounds in your muscle. Would you want something like that?" So instead of rejecting the client with an uninformed response, you reframe their question to their benefit. I call this technique question reframing and it's super powerful because number one, it helps people make the right decision for themselves, and number two, it doesn't allow hidden objections the buyer has to get in the way of you making the sale and helping them out.
