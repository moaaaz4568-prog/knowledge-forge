Here are three of the worst words to never say on sales calls. Number one, price. It makes your product sound like a purchase. Instead of price, say investment. Number two, contract. Never say it. It scares people. Instead, say agreement. Number three, sorry. When your prospect tells you a struggle they're going through, don't say sorry and give them pity. They'll see you as a friend and not a coach. Instead, say I appreciate you for sharing that.
