So you get on a call with someone and they ask for something you don't offer. For example, prospect says, "Hey, I saw that you offer coaching, but does not include one on one at all." Most people would say, "We don't, but we do this and this and this instead," and now you're needy and you're begging and pleading for their approval and the sale. Instead, do this. "No, we don't include any one on one. Is that a deal breaker for you?" This will reveal the prospect's true intention and get them to think if that future is a deal breaker for them.
