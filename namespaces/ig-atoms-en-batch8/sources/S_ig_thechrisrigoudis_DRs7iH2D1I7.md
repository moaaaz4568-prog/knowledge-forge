Confidence is not the most important trait in a salesperson. Curiosity is the most important trait. If you walk into a store and ask for a microwave, and the salesperson goes, "It's the newest and best microwave in the market. It will fucking nuke your food and warm it up in seconds," or you can have a salesperson that goes like, "Oh, what do your meals usually look like? What do you want to warm up in the microwave?" And then you're like, "Oh, sometimes I warm up my chicken and rice, and sometimes my soup." And the salesperson says, "I think we have something that will work for you. Do you want to take a look at it?" Who will you buy from? Someone who's very cocky and cares more about their product, or someone who's more curious and cares more about your situation, what you're looking for? People want to feel seen, heard, and then directed in the direction that they're looking for. Not get pushed around by someone who wants to make the decision for them. So the best scenario is to both be curious and confident, but if I had to pick, I would pick curiosity any day of the week.
