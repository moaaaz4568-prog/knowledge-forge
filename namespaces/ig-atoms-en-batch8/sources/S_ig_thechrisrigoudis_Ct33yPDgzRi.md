Has this ever happened to you? When you call with a prospect, you spend an hour with them explaining everything, and at the end, you hear them say, "This is amazing, but unfortunately, I cannot afford this." Or you hear, "Let me think about it," and you keep chasing them only to hear two weeks later, "We cannot really afford this," and you wasted all this time, and now you're sitting in a void of sadness. Here's how to fix this. The mistake most people make is asking questions like, "What's your budget?" You get into something like this, and this automatically trains the prospect to look at what they have available to buy your thing. Instead, say this. As you might understand, Mr. Prospect, it does take some type of funding to put into a solution so you can get X and Y in the result and fix X and Y and Z problem. So what type of funding have you set aside to put into a solution so you can get the result that you want? This gets them to think about the result that they want versus the budget that they had in place and makes them become much more resourceful. They can buy your thing.
