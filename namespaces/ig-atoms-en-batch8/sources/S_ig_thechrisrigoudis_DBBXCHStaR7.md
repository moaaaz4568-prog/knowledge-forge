One of the biggest mistakes salespeople make is using claim statements and not proof statements. Here's what I mean. When we sell something, we have the natural tendency to make promises and claims. We will make you this much money. You will lose so much weight. You will love this car or new property. And these are claim statements. Prospects are very hard to trust you and believe these statements, and also they trigger massive walls of resistance. Instead, use proof statements. Say something like, "Yeah, most of our clients see ROI in a couple of weeks," or "Our clients are able to achieve these kinds of results in this time." Now, what does this do? You're not making a direct promise to them, but you're showing them what's possible with your methodology and your product.
