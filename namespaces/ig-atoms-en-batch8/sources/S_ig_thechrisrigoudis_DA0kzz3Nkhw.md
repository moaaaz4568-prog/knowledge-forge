Here's a mindset hack to take your objection handling to the next level. It has nothing to do with the words you use or your tonality. It starts within you, and that means do not believe the objection they give you. In most cases, your prospect will never give you the true objection upfront. So if you buy into that objection, it diminishes your ability to overcome it. Instead, act as if it's a fake objection. If it's a money objection, don't make a big deal out of it. It's not your problem to solve. Instead, put it back on them. Say something like this: "I can understand that money can be a challenge, especially if you're not getting help so far. How do you feel you can overcome that where you can come up with the money to get help? " Let them brainstorm and sit with the problem. Collaborate with them instead of trying to be the savior. If you get a partner objection, chances are they're just abdicating responsibility. If you're selling a B2B solution, their spouse has no say really on how they're going to grow the business. They're just pushing the decision to someone else to get rid of you. So put the truth on the table, help them overcome their own objection, and make sure you follow up from there.
