Here's the reason I'm a really great closer. It's because I ask for the sale. Most people, they don't ever ask for the sale. They ask the prospect, "So what do you think about that?" That is not a closing statement. You need to be assertive and you need to take the order. And eight times out of ten, if you do that, you will close the sale. If you do that, you will not close the sale. At the end of the sales call, it doesn't matter if you're assertive or not assertive. The sale isn't made to that point. It's made actually during the call and showing them that you are a credible expert, you're asking the right questions, and you inject certainty along the way. Being assertive at the end can only sabotage the sale with things like assuming the sale or which credit card would you like to put this on? That's not assertiveness. It's assuming the sale and it's actually triggering resistance. So give them the illusion of control. Say something like, "Well, what's the best next step for us?" With confidence and you will close more sales.
