After closing nine hundred thousand in sales last month, I realized why most people can't close more sales. It's not their sales scripts, it's not their lead quality or their objection handling skills. It's them trying to perfect their process. I see way too many people trying to have the perfect sales call when in reality is exactly what's holding them back. You're trying to ask the perfect next question in your script and you forget to listen to what the prospect is saying. And in reality, your prospect has all the answers they need to hear so they can buy. So when your prospect is talking, instead of you thinking about what's the perfect next question should be, actively listen to what the prospect is saying, note down if there are any red flags, sabotaging beliefs, or inconsistencies that come with their answer, and probe into what they're saying. If they throw a hidden objection within what they're saying and you simply ask the next question and you're punching them down through your process, what's going to happen at the end?
