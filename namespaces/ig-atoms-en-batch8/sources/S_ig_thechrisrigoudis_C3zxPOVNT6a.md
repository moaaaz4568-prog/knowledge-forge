If you can't find pain, you won't make the sale. But you should never create pain. The goal is to help them relieve their pain. I call this creating a mini PTSD. So through your expert questions, you have to get specific enough so they can relive the moment when they had the pain. If you're selling fitness, you have to get them to describe that their jeans no longer fit. If you sell a business-related offer, you have to get them to say that last week's calendar was empty. When you help them visualize again the problem that they have, that's when you can help them out by creating the urgency to get them to buy.
