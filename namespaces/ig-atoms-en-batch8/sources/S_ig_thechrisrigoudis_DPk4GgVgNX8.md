If a buyer tells you, "I want to think about it," don't let them think about it. Because when they say "think about it," they're thinking about your product. You have to reframe them so they can start thinking about their problem. So say something like this: "That's not an issue at all. How long have you been thinking about fixing your challenges in the first place?" They're gonna say six months or something. Now ask them, "And in these six months, what do you feel you've missed out on in terms of money, time, or resources?" They're gonna say an amount. And now you ask them this: "Well, can you help me understand how much longer do you want to think about fixing the problem and losing all these resources that you mentioned?" Now what you've done is you've reframed the product thinking to problem thinking, and they will make a decision to move with you.
