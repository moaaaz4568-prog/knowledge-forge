If you want to have flow state in every sales call you have, follow these four commitments. Number one, commit to yourself that you're detaching from the outcome. You don't care if that prospect will buy or not buy, you're just there to help. Number two is commit yourself to the process. Don't get too excited and jump forward, or don't get pushed from the prospect to present early. Follow the sales process and their script that puts you in the best possible position to win the sale. Number three is commit to yourself that you will not present the offer unless you understand there is a clear problem to solve and the prospect is ready to make a change. You're not a tour guide, you have no obligation to present your services unless you see there is a clear fit between the problem and your product. Don't feel pressured into presenting. Number four is have some fun with it. Don't be a complete robot, it will show in your tonality and your presence during the call. You can crack jokes to eliminate resistance and you can be light-hearted at times. If you keep too serious, your prospect will be stiff with you as well and that can create objections.
