I'm gonna talk a little bit about the parasocial relationship. So the parasocial relationship refers to the relationship between an audience member and people on video. People have the same neurons fire when they're watching somebody on video as if they're talking to a friend in person. Which means that the more that you are on video for longer periods of time, the more likely that your cold audience will view you as somebody that is a friend.
