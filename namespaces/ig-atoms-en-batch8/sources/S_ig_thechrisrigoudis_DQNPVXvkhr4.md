When salespeople try to build a rapport in the beginning, they ask shit like, "How's the weather?" "Do you have one or five cats?" Or "I like the painting on the wall." And the buyer instantly sees that it's fake. You don't care about how my room looks like, or where I'm from, or how's the weather over there. You're showing me that you only care about creating a sense of fake rapport so you can sell me later. What should you do instead is focus on the person and actually care about them. Ask them about why they're there. Why did they take time out of their day to talk to a salesperson? And through your probing and asking questions, you will show them that you actually are the person for them.
