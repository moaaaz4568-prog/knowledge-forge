Sending proposals is a sin. You've probably heard me talk about it in the past. You should never send proposals. They're a tool of consideration, not conversion. But if you have to put together a proposal, send it over. Instead of just sending it over, after you have your first call and you know their exact needs, book a second meeting, put together the proposal and walk them through the proposal together. So you can ask questions, handle any objections, and also ask for the sale at the end. Otherwise, you're just sending a message in a bottle and hoping they buy.
