# G_yt_8gBQqH_0nU4_en — AI needs assessment

## Term

An AI needs assessment is the first of nine AI-based business models the speaker reviews and ranks from S to F: you are paid to perform diagnostics for a local entrepreneur or any business owner, telling them where they should apply AI. It requires the least experience on the whole list, because the assessment itself tells the client where AI fits — anyone can sell it, and it is the speaker's favorite type of evaluation. The phrase appears 2 times in the source, beside diagnostics, local, evaluation, and models. His concrete case is the pricing logic: if the client's average deal is 5,000 dollars, a lead from your system can be charged at 500 dollars.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as doing AI work: the assessment is diagnostics and advice, not building the AI — you are paid to find where AI applies.
- Read as needing AI expertise: the speaker says it needs the least experience on the list, since the assessment itself tells the client where to apply it.
