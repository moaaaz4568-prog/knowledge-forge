# G_yt_9ABC_8BN9X4_en — permanent memory

## Term

A permanent memory is the solution he spent the past few days building: a permanent memory that lives in your laptop and that your Claude can access and update, so that at any time you use it, it knows who you are, your preferences, and how to write content — and it has virtually your same exact knowledge. Claude is by far the most powerful tool to create content at scale, but the problem with it, like most AIs, is that when you get their help to make your content, they sound nothing like you — and the reason why is that they lack context. They are fundamentally dumb, not in the sense that they don't have knowledge, but they lack context. The phrase appears 2 times in the source, beside claude, content, exactly, and context. His concrete setup is a folder for Claude with a memory and a scan folder: the first thing you do is just dump everything in here — but this will not be the context that Claude uses, because it will be very unorganized and very messy. How the process will actually look is for Claude to start from here, do a scan of all of this information, and ask you some context, because your context will be different from his — the programs you use, the documents you have at your disposal. He created a Claude skill that guides you step-by-step in exactly what you need to do, because this is a somewhat technical process.
