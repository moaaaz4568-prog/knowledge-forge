# G_yt_98Is31GMbzo_en — focus

## Term

Focus is what this short video is about: how to keep it when your brain won't. Set your deadlines — your brain will only work when it knows that it has to. Kill the micro distractions: those quick 10 seconds checking your phone cost you more than you think. Record yourself while you're working, and look at your screen, because it's a lot harder to get distracted when it feels like Big Brother's watching over your shoulder. Plan tomorrow today: instead of deciding on vibes what you're going to work on, plan what you're going to do today the night before. The word appears 3 times in the source, beside going, work, working, and phone. Stay in the pocket: sometimes it's going to feel like you need a break, but if you just kept on working for 20 minutes, you'll find your focus comes back. Take breaks that prioritize deliberate deep focus — that's when your eyes aren't locked onto anything. Sleep well: your ability to focus tanks when you're sleep deprived, and sometimes it's worth calling it a day and just working on it in the morning. Go outside: if you treat your body like you're a subhuman, you're going to perform like a subhuman.
