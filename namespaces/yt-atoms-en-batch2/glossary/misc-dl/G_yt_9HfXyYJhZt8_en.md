# G_yt_9HfXyYJhZt8_en — cold email

## Term

A cold email is what this video is about, and the speaker has sent over a million of them so far in 2026, booking around 400 sales calls for himself and his clients just doing outbound. He narrowed everything he knows in 2026 down to three principles that most campaigns are completely missing — and if you don't nail these before you send another cold email, you're leaving interested responses and meetings booked on the table that you could have had otherwise. The phrase appears 11 times in the source, beside emails, email, send, and volume. Section one is: Volume is king. This one's going to be a little controversial, but most people get volume completely wrong whenever they're running cold email: most people think that the way you generate a ton of leads with cold email is you get these small, super-perfect lists, and as a result they are sending these hyper-targeted, hyper-accurate emails — which he has nothing wrong with — but they miss the bigger picture, that it's better to send tens of thousands of emails or hundreds of thousands of emails with 80 to 95% accuracy than send 100 emails that are perfectly accurate. People are hyper-obsessed with making their targeting perfect, and as a result they think there's no way they can reach out to 10,000, 50,000, or 100,000 people a month — so they're only reaching out to a handful of people, and a vast majority of them just aren't going to be interested. Versus if you send 100,000 emails, you send a million emails, there's just a way higher chance you're going to get meetings on calendar because you're reaching so many people. And even then, if you just do high volume and you have a really good offer, that's going to make up for 90% of the puzzle — though if you frame your offer in a way that sounds like something they don't want, people aren't going to be interested.
