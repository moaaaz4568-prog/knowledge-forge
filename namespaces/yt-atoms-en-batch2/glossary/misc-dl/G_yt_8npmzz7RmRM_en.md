## Term

Outreach is the service Alex Bney's company runs for insurance businesses, mainly retail brokers: a system for finding clients, with a clear framework for how to structure it and the tools behind it. He joined the OSS program because that framework gave him confidence he had selected the right tools and systems, and a year in he has too many clients to do the outreach himself anymore.
