# G_yt_9-rSKi3HO9E_en — team

## Term

A team is the most important thing to understand as a business owner, in this video: hiring and building a team. Over the last few months his company has grown like crazy, and he realized it's all because he has now hired people who are pretty smart and people he can trust to be productive. In the online space everyone is pushed to build lean businesses, but the only thing that comes with being lean is a lack of impact — he has yet to see anyone with a team of two people change the world. The word appears 68 times in the source, beside business, build, hiring, and company. His concrete advice is to draw the whole picture: the organization chart, and build that organization — that organization will create the amount of money you're making. If you want to be financially free, if you want to be wealthy, build a team.
