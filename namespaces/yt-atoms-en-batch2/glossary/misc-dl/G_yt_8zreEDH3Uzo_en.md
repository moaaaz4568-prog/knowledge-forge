# G_yt_8zreEDH3Uzo_en — personalized videos

## Term

Personalized videos are the product from Tavis's company in this video: imagine if Loom could make one video, one VSL, personalized to one person — "hey search, I just found a contact, I noticed you're already making a ton of money but I believe you could reduce a lot of money in taxes" — and you could send that to every single person making a ton of money, changing the name and the variables in the speech for each one. For real estate, as soon as the lead opts in, instead of a stupid follow-up asking when they're available, you send them a personalized video. The phrase appears 3 times in the source, beside video, money, send, and client. His concrete plan is to offer the tool to every client who joins the program — on average 40 new clients a month.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as mass video spam: it is one video personalized to one person, not a blast — the name and variables change per person.
- Read as just Loom: Loom is the analogy — this changes the speech itself for each recipient, not just the recording.
