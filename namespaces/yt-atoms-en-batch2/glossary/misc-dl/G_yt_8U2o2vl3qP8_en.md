# G_yt_8U2o2vl3qP8_en — communication

## Term

Communication is the speaker's first lesson from twenty years in graphic design: success is not purely about how good you are at designing, because clients judge the designer as much as the design. Two designers can produce work of a similar level, but the one who communicates clearly and confidently is often perceived as the better designer, and clients are effectively buying certainty. The word appears 7 times in the source, beside confidence, clarity, professionalism, and clients. His concrete case is the less skilled but far more confident designer who walks in like they are presenting the cure for sadness and suddenly charges triple the rates.

## Live Example

> "But what they do notice is clarity, confidence, professionalism, communication, and whether working with you feels reassuring." — sources/S_yt_8U2o2vl3qP8_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as being chatty: communication here is clarity and confidence that reassure a client, not the volume of talk — clients are asking whether this person understands what they need and can be trusted.
- Read as a substitute for skill: the speaker says the work matters and you cannot just make a glowing lion icon and expect retirement — communication changes how good work is perceived, it does not replace it.
