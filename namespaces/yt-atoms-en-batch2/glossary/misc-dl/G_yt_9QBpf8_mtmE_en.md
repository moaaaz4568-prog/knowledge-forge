# G_yt_9QBpf8_mtmE_en — time under tension

## Term

Time under tension is the first key important thing behind this growth: how he went from barely making 5k per month to making over $20,000 per month in eight weeks. The phrase appears 3 times in the source, beside because, month, selling, and over. In November and December there was a lack of focus: he tried selling multiple services to multiple groups — coaches as clients, agency owners — with multiple offers being sold and delivered at the same time, and that was not good at all. Then he did not get any shiny object syndrome: he focused on one thing, one group of people. From January 1st to the 31st, they made just over 10.5k, almost doubling December's revenue, and in the following month, February — 30 days, until March 2nd — he achieved 20k. Essentially, he went from 1.7k to 5k to 11k to 20k in four months. The four things behind it are: time under tension, his mindset, his beliefs, and his goals; third, leveraging assets — he started leveraging a lot of assets; and fourth, offer a new market. His concrete content is case studies from the current campaigns being managed, testimonials from clients, his point of view on the market, and the process taken to deliver results for clients — if you have a good novel solution, a new solution the market has not yet seen. And his concrete sales lesson is about the specialist prospecting on Facebook or Instagram: she will never sell his services as good as him, so he puts his sales messages and videos in, and asks the specialist not to try to sell people through messages, but literally just ask them for permission to share the value video he had made — and the value video did all the selling. At the end of the day, if you have a bad offer, you'll not have this kind of feedback loop: a month to making 7.4k in seven days, to making 8k in less than 48 hours.
