# G_yt_902eLImkBoE_en — team

## Term

The team is how Matt forms a company, in this conversation with a former Tony Robbins coach who worked with Donald Miller in StoryBrand and now forms the company at Cass Investments: you are the one who forms a company through people who you hire, because it shapes culture and affects very many things. Eighty percent of success in business and in life is psychology, 20% is mechanics. The word appears 26 times in the source, beside business, hiring, coaching, and robbins. His concrete warning is that most of the usual business owners live and die for confidence score — this is their Achilles heel — and the fear that keeps him up at night is accepting the wrong team member: any new member is like adding an ingredient to a cake recipe, and you wouldn't throw garlic in a chocolate cake.
