# G_yt_8RqT_6M9Am4_en — growth operator

## Term

A growth operator is the speaker's name for someone who runs a creator's or coach's business growth done-for-you: building funnels, sales assets, and backend systems, then taking a share of the revenue. His clients joined the incubator already growth operating, but without the right vehicle — no product-market fit to sell at volume — so the model was getting tricky. The phrase appears 4 times in the source, beside agency, Creator, launch, and sales, with the plural growth operators twice more and the activity growth operating 3 more times. His concrete case is Kenny and Simon's clients: 28k in revenue plus 25% revenue share for every client acquired.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an employee who runs ads: a growth operator owns the whole growth machine — funnels, launches, sales calls, backend — and gets paid from revenue, not a salary for making ads.
- Read as a model that scales by itself: the speaker's own clients found growth operating tricky without the right vehicle and product-market fit, so clients and revenue do not follow automatically.
