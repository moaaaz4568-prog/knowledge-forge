# G_yt_8YdFcp7nNFk_en — trends

## Term

Trends are the graphic design fashions of the year, and the video's real subject is how to judge them: a large portion of designers think graphic design trends are at least semi-important, and the speaker lists the important trends of 2024 and how to use them on designs. The test is the audience and the mission of the brief — once you know those, you can determine whether trends are viable or even useful in a project. The word appears 14 times in the source, beside design, audience, brief, and 2024, with the singular trend 8 more times. His concrete case is sustainability: if the target audience cares about saving the planet, use it whether or not you personally believe in it, with eco-friendly fonts that reduce ink use.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as follow every trend: the speaker says to include a trend only when necessary and only if it fits the audience and the brief — an elderly audience gets a different answer than a young one.
- Read as a prediction list: the value is the viability test, not the list itself — the trends matter only through the brief they serve.
