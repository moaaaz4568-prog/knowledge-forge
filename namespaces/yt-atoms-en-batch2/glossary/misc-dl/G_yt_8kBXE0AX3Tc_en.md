# G_yt_8kBXE0AX3Tc_en — proof of abundance

## Term

The proof of abundance is Randy Schwz's case, quoted from a Peter Diamandis email, that the world is getting better despite the constant negative news: humanity evolved with an amygdala tuned to danger, so the news makes us believe everything is going to hell in a hand basket, while the data says otherwise. His concrete proof is life expectancy: mid-30s in 1920, high 50s in 1970, and 72.6 years in 2020. The phrase appears 3 times in the source, beside abundance, news, negative, and life expectancy. The point is a mindset of realistic optimism — not just being told to be optimistic, but seeing that a lot of great things are going on.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as naive optimism: it is realistic optimism, grounded in data like life expectancy, not a claim that everything is great.
- Read as ignoring the news: the point is that the news overweights danger because of the amygdala, not that bad things do not exist.
