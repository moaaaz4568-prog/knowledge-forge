# G_yt_8sBnT9RkxAw_en — successful students

## Term

Successful students are the subject of this video: the four key characteristics the speaker has consistently and repeatedly seen in his most successful students, so viewers can model them in their own content and business. Number one is they move faster than everyone else — they set the deadline first, before anything even exists, then work toward it regardless of what's required, taking 10, 20, 100 times more action than most people. They also knew they were going to be successful, just not how, and they keep pushing through failure — you will fail dozens and dozens of times — and they have a purpose bigger than the money. The phrase appears 11 times in the source, beside students, move, faster, and deadline. His concrete proof is the tally: in the last 12 months alone the students made over 3.5 million USD in verifiable wins, and menstrual cycle coaches are doing 30 to 40,000 dollar launches consistently.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as talent or luck: the characteristics are behaviors — moving faster, setting deadlines first, believing, and a purpose bigger than money.
- Read as success meaning money: the fourth characteristic is a purpose bigger than the money — if your purpose is purely to make money, people won't buy into your mission because there is no mission.
