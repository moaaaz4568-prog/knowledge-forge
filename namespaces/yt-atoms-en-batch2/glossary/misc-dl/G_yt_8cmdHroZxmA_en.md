# G_yt_8cmdHroZxmA_en — avatar

## Term

An avatar is the ideal client you work with, and in Taki's training it is the thing holding the business back: if your DMs are full but your Stripe account is empty, it is your avatar that is the problem. Within every horizontal niche there are four core categories — bleeding people in real pain, slipping people whose good times ended, and levels above them — and the same skills that help the bleeding can help the slipping, who pay more. The word appears 9 times in the source, beside clients, niche, upgrade, and levels. His concrete rule is to upgrade the avatar at each level, so he works with people he loves and stays ahead of his clients.
