# G_yt_966ZUy9HsOo_en — pattern

## Term

A pattern, in this video, is what you make in Adobe Illustrator from an actual calligraphy design, to demonstrate some really handy and useful things about pattern making in Illustrator — something you may have completely missed in terms of Illustrator as a program. Today's video is a double whammy and a collab with an awesome calligraphy-style artist, Angelo, and you can learn more about him and his design process later in the video. The word appears 15 times in the source, beside design, calligraphy, going, and illustrator. The concrete steps are: come up to Object, Pattern, and Make to get things started — Illustrator is going to give you a preview of what the pattern is going to look like, and you can zoom in or out by holding down the Alt/Option key and then scrolling your mouse. The pattern will come in handy, and then you just click Done at the very top; there are quite a few cool things you can do with a pattern in Illustrator, within the shape parameter.
