# G_yt_8mOBDZH6iHg_en — symbol

## Term

A symbol is the reusable pattern in this Illustrator tutorial: you make a pattern for your vector sphere design, convert it into a symbol, and drag and drop it into the window to use it. Your symbol doesn't need a background — his uses a white rectangle — and it sits in a kind of rectangle so the revolve effect can turn the semicircle into the sphere. The word appears 7 times in the source, beside design, pattern, circle, and rectangle. His concrete steps are to press L for the ellipse tool, hold shift while clicking and dragging to make a circle, make two cuts at the very top and bottom, and remove one half while keeping the other for later.
