# G_yt_99xyy1nUpug_en — financial plan

## Term

A financial plan, in this video, is a system to put your financial life on autopilot in less than 10 minutes — and it doesn't require discipline, budget, and you don't have to make a lot of money to get started. But unless your financial plan is automatic, it will fail. The phrase appears 3 times in the source, beside money, years, financial, and home. The speaker believes the next 10 years will be the greatest opportunity to build wealth in our lifetimes: seven out of 10 people right now are living paycheck to paycheck, more than 50% of Americans don't have savings, and most people don't know where their money goes — when asked how much money it would take to totally change your life, they say $10,000. His concrete numbers are: there are 654,000 people in Fidelity 401k plans that are now millionaires; homeowners in America are worth 40 times more than renters; and if you don't get in the game of home ownership, and you rent in your 20s and in your 30s, you're going to turn around in your 40s and having not built any net worth. He spent the last 33 years of his life helping millions of people with ordinary incomes become financially free, including 9 years as a financial advisor at Morgan Stanley.
