# G_yt_8osUr4gEnqw_en — brand identity

## Term

Brand identity is the whole process Jack Watson shares in this three-part series, from start to finish, for his client Openmouth — a student burger and cocktail bar set to shake up Manchester's dining scene. It runs from the client inquiry to building the brand guidelines from scratch: a face-to-face call to build trust, then a questionnaire covering brand vision, target audience, brand personality, design preferences, and deliverables, then the logo, color, and typography. The phrase appears 4 times in the source, beside process, design, client, and logo. His concrete case is how the client found him: a video every single day on Instagram and TikTok for eight months, drawing around 2 to 4 million views per month.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as just a logo: brand identity is the whole process — questionnaire, guidelines, color, typography — and the logo is only the face of the brand.
- Read as a one-off project: it is a three-part series from inquiry to guidelines, not a single deliverable.
