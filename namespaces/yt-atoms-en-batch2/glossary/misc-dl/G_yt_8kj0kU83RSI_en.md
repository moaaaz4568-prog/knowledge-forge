# G_yt_8kj0kU83RSI_en — lifestyle

## Term

Lifestyle is what Doin Buck, CEO of GetYouWired, a full-service digital marketing agency in business for over two decades, designed his agency around: he and his partners built the agency around their lifestyle — living in the backwoods of North Georgia, outside every day, working in a big open room with the river right over there — rather than having the agency control them. The biggest stress for agencies is money, and as they grow their lifestyle keeps getting more, so he watches the trap of buying a big house or waiting until success arrives to take care of himself. The word appears 3 times in the source, beside agency, guys, built, and money. His concrete start was desperation: his wife said don't come home unless you find a job today, and that job brought him into the agency space.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as work-life balance tips: the agency itself is designed around the lifestyle — where they live and how they work — not tips bolted on after.
- Read as lifestyle meaning spending more as you earn more: the trap he names is "when I make this much money, then I will take care of myself" — the things he values most really don't cost any money.
