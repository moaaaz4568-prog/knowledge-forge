# G_yt_9F_xKevZQxs_en — World Economic Forum

## Term

The World Economic Forum is what Nick talks about in this video: why should we care, if we should care at all. What's going on right now, as he's recording this, is January 16th to 20th, 2023, and the World Economic Forum gets a lot of news because a lot of the world's business elites meet in Davos every January to discuss what's going on in the world and their ideas on how to fix it. The phrase appears 11 times in the source, beside world, economic, forum, and about. This year's theme is about having to deal with a fragmented world and "cooperation in a fragmented world," and if you look at the World Economic Forum blog, it just talks about a lot of their usual things. His concrete test is a barometer check: does the stock market or financial markets of any kind react — if the stock market may not always react, but if there's a niche corner of the market, even if it's a single company. And his sharper point is that it's more just a mouthpiece for the business and political elites to say things they want to get out there, things that are socially unpopular or politically unpopular to say — basically the professional mouthpiece for people who do not want their names to be credited to certain ideas. His opinion really goes to the idea that the World Economic Forum really is not that significant.
