# G_yt_8YQshEF48qM_en — team

## Term

A team is the company itself, in the speaker's blunt rule: if you do not have a team, you do not have a company. Founders feel alone and think controlling more will make them succeed more, but the episode argues the opposite — the team has to reflect the founder to the client the same way the founder wants to be reflected. The word appears 18 times in the source, beside company, client, founders, and peace. His concrete failure is ownership: he gave one person 12 accounts when they had 6 months of experience and no training, and he takes ownership of that instead of blaming the hire.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as headcount: a team here is the people who carry the business to the client, not a number of hires — the speaker's test is reflection, not size.
- Read as hire more people: the point is ownership and reflection, and his own story is a warning — 12 accounts handed to an untrained hire is a leadership failure, not a staffing gap.
