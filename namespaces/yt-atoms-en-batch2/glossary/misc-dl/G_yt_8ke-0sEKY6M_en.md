# G_yt_8ke-0sEKY6M_en — hero

## Term

The hero is the audience in the coach's personal-brand framework: a personal brand is you as the guide, and the people, your audience, are the hero — you are helping them achieve a transformation. The woman being coached has it backwards: her page is all about her, posing through her weight-loss journey, so her bio tells the audience nothing about what they get. The word appears 7 times in the source, beside guide, audience, personal brand, and transformation. Her concrete fix is to post content like "I'm doing this today because of..." so the audience sees how they could lose weight as well.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as you being the hero of your own brand: in this framework the audience is the hero and you are the guide — her page fails because she made herself the hero.
- Read as just posting your journey: a self blog documenting your fitness journey is a different thing from a personal brand built to help the audience transform.
