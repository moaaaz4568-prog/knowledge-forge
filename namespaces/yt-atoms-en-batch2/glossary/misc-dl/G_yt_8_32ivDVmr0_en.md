# G_yt_8_32ivDVmr0_en — bleed

## Term

A bleed is a dedicated area around the edge of a design that will be cut away and discarded after printing: the design must bleed over this area so a professional, clean cutting edge is realized on the final piece. The video exists because Photoshop has no bleed settings for print jobs, so the speaker shows how to make custom bleed settings with guides and document size. The word appears 11 times in the source, beside Photoshop, print, document, and edge, with the plural bleeds 3 more times. His concrete numbers are a five-millimeter bleed: add 10 millimeters of height and 10 millimeters of width, five millimeters all around.

## Synonyms

- bleeds (plural form of the term, used in the source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a mistake: a bleed is intentional and essential for any printing project — the edge area is sacrificed on purpose so the cut lands clean.
- Read as a Photoshop feature: the whole point is that Photoshop lacks it, so you build it yourself with guides, document size, and bleed marks the printer can read.
