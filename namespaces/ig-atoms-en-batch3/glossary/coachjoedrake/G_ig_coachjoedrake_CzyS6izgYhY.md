# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CzyS6izgYhY — spatial programming

## Term

Spatial programming here is Joe's name for thinking about how a session flows through the space of the gym, and he says most coaches are blind to it. The phrase appears once, beside having some thought and some strategy, flow throughout a space, and primary zone, as a concept he claims as his own. His test is practical: in a superset or triplex, are the two or three exercises in different corners with wasted time walking back and forth, or in one primary zone where the work gets done. A session planned this way feels more thoughtful to the client, because the thought is visible in the flow.

## Live Example

> "Most coaches are blind to a concept that I refer to as spatial programming, and that's actually having some thought and some strategy into how you flow throughout a space." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CzyS6izgYhY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a floor-plan rule: spatial programming is about the flow of a session through space, not about where the gym puts its machines.
- Read as a claim that only big gyms need it: the primary-zone test applies to any superset, whether the equipment is a leg press or a pair of dumbbells.
