# G_ig_coachjoedrake_Cz_F1Kzgl8F — recurring package

## Term

Recurring package here is the third way of charging Joe says most trainers are not using: a package that keeps billing as sessions happen, instead of short-term non-commitment deals or a monthly EFT. The phrase appears once, beside win-win for everyone, awkward money conversation, and chasing down paychecks, as the option he recommends. For the trainer it removes the awkward money talk and the chasing of unpaid sessions, and for the client it means paying only for sessions actually done, even when travel or sickness interrupts. The package keeps both sides honest, he says, because the trainer must keep earning trust to keep getting paid.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of steady income: the recurring package only bills for sessions that happen, so a trainer who does not keep clients coming in does not get paid.
- Read as a ban on contracts: Joe contrasts the package with a forced twelve-month contract, not with every kind of agreement.
