# G_passive_income — passive income

## Term

Passive income is the promise that draws new coaches toward online training — money without trading time — and Joe says the promise is false. The phrase is used twice, beside online training, in person first, and nothing passive about it, and he calls jumping straight online a major mistake for newly certified coaches. The coaches earning well online, he says, all started in person, learning programming, pairing, coaching, cueing, and progression, and they interact heavily with clients in a different manner. The opportunity is real, he says, but the work is not passive, and a coach who skips in-person training probably will not make it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It sounds attractive because people like the idea of not trading their time for money and having what they think is gonna be passive income." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CrAsBLLAvom.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that online coaching cannot pay: Joe says there is a ton of opportunity in the online space, and his warning is about skipping in-person experience, not about the income itself.
- Read as advice to stay in person forever: the source makes in-person training the prerequisite, not the destination, and online work follows once the coaching is good.
