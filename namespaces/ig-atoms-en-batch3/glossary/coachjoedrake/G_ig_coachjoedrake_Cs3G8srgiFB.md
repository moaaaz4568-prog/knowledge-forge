# G_ig_coachjoedrake_Cs3G8srgiFB — isometric

## Term

Isometric is the training modality this exercise clip works through with the glute bridge: holding a position rather than repping through it. The word occurs twice — "it's helping to improve that isometric control and stability" and "it's gonna be a yielding isometric" — with the clip's own taxonomy around it: yielding ("I'm yielding to gravity" while holding the bridge) versus overcoming (driving the opposite hand in "as hard as possible"), which the bridge can combine "on opposite sides". His stated payoffs of a 20-to-30-second hold: motor learning — "mind-muscle connection, like body to glutes... I guarantee you you're gonna know exactly where your glutes are" — plus the control and stability itself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any hold being one kind: the source splits isometrics into yielding and overcoming and shows one position combining both; the categories are the content, not a single generic hold.
- Read as a loading prescription: the clip gives one duration anchor (20 to 30 seconds for the glute bridge hold) in service of awareness and control; no sets, progression, or program appear here.
