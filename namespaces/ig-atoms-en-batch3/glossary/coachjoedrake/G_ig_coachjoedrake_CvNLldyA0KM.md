# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CvNLldyA0KM — weird old dude

## Term

Weird old dude here is the joke label Joe puts on himself in a one-line gym caption: the odd older man making up in the gym. The phrase appears once, beside realize, you have become, and making up in the gym, as a self-mocking punchline with no setup around it. It plays on the gap between how a lifter sees himself and how he looks to everyone else on the floor. The line works as a meme caption, not as a training idea or a claim about age.
