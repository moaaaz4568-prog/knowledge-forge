# G_mobility — mobility

## Term

Mobility here means strength through range of motion, not stretching alone, and Joe defines it that way in the first sentence. The word appears once, beside adductors, inner thighs, and RDL, while he coaches Louise through a wide-stance dumbbell movement on a bench. The demonstration pairs a stretch feeling with strengthening: hips pushed back, dumbbells drawing her down, low back position held, and feet pushing through the ground to come back up. He says the deep, wide position should transfer to a better squat, which is why the work counts as mobility rather than as a warm-up stretch.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a stretching routine: Joe defines mobility as strength through range, and the drill loads the adductors with dumbbells rather than holding a passive stretch.
- Read as a claim that any stretch builds strength: the source ties the strength to this specific loaded pattern — hips back, low back held, feet driving — not to stretching in general.
