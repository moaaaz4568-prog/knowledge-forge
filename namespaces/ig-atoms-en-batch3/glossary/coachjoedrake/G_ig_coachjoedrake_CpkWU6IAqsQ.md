# G_confident — confident

## Term

Confident describes how Joe tells trainers to act when a trial session ends: pull out the calendar, name the next session, and behave as if the client is already training with them. The word appears twice, beside next Tuesday, already training with you, and looking for help, and both uses sit inside a single acted-out scene. He contrasts it with the hesitant ask — so what do you think, maybe we'll train together, maybe we'll go on a date again — and calls the confident version the behaviour of someone who believes in the client more than the client believes in themselves. The point of the confidence is action: pushing people to take the step they came for rather than leaving the decision open.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Very confident. In your mind, guys, they're already training with you. All right? Because they're already there with you." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CpkWU6IAqsQ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as arrogance toward the client: Joe's confidence is presented as belief in the client and a clear next step, not as pressure or self-promotion.
- Read as a script for booking anyone: the scene assumes the person is already looking for help, so the confidence follows their interest rather than manufacturing it.
