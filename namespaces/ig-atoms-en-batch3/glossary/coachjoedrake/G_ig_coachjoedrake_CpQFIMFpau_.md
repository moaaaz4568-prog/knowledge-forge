# G_certification — certification

## Term

Certification here names the credential new trainers treat as the hard part, and Joe's point is that it is the easiest step in the journey rather than the most important piece. The word is used once, beside 10, 15, 20 years, certified by, and easiest step, in advice addressed to people watching his videos while the learning feels overwhelming. He says veteran trainers agree no matter who certified them, and he separates the credential from the work that follows: using the knowledge with clients and building a life and career in training. Everything after the certification, he says, is more challenging than the certification itself.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Talk to any trainer who's been doing this for 10, 15, 20 years, and no matter who they're certified by, they're gonna tell you that the certification was not the most important piece." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CpQFIMFpau_.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that certification is worthless: Joe calls it the easiest step, not a useless one, and the source still treats it as the step that starts the journey.
- Read as a promise that the hard part ends with the exam: the source says the opposite, that working with clients and making a career of it is where the greater challenge begins.
