# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CpQFIMFpau_ — certification

## Term

Certification is the target of this career clip: the credential new trainers fixate on, reframed as the easiest step rather than the decisive one. The noun occurs once ("the certification was not the most important piece") with certified once more ("no matter who they're certified by"), and the words around it are trainers, important, and easiest step. The argument: veterans of 10, 15, 20 years all say the same thing, and "everything that comes after this is more challenging" — using the stuff with clients, building a life and career in training other people.

## Synonyms

- certified

## Live Example

> "Talk to any trainer who's been doing this for 10, 15, 20 years, and no matter who they're certified by, they're gonna tell you that the certification was not the most important piece. And truthfully, it may also be the easiest step in the journey." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CpQFIMFpau_.md
> "But as you guys go on, if you want to go on and use this stuff, you want to go on and work with clients, you want to go on and make a life and a career in training other people, this will be the easiest step. Everything that comes after this is more challenging." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CpQFIMFpau_.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as certification being worthless: the speaker's claim is comparative — it may be the easiest step and not the most important piece — and the clip still assumes you are going through the process while watching these videos.
- Read as anti-credential advice for established trainers: the addressed reader is a certification-track beginner weighing how much the credential matters; practicing coaches choosing continuing education are outside this text.
