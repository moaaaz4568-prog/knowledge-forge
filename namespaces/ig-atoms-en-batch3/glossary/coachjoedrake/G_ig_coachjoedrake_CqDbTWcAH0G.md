# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CqDbTWcAH0G — full body

## Term

Full body is the session split this programming clip recommends for newer clients: train everything each day, so each muscle group and movement lands twice a week. The phrase occurs twice — "the nice thing about the full body" and "that full body twice a week probably gonna be your most beneficial to start" — with twice a week, recover, and newer client as companions. The argument runs through recovery math: a hard single lower-body day leaves six to seven days of recovery when two or three sufficed, while full-body days spread lower damage per area across two exposures — and the split "gives you somewhere to go" as the trainer later divides into lower and upper.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as full-body being inherently superior: the recommendation is scoped — for the newer client starting out, where recovery math and a simple progression path favor it; the clip does not rank splits for advanced trainees.
- Read as twice-weekly frequency for its own sake: the frequency is a consequence of the full-body structure; the clip's stated reasons are lower per-area damage and built-in room to progress, not frequency as a goal.
