# G_full_body — full body

## Term

Full body names the training split where clients work the whole body in each session, and Joe recommends it twice a week for newer clients because the recovery fits. The phrase appears twice, beside twice a week, recover, and lower body, and his argument is about dose: one crushing lower-body day needs six or seven days to recover from, while the client probably needed only two or three. Spreading the work means less damage in any one area per session and a second hit within the week. He adds that starting there leaves somewhere to go, since the next step up is a lower-and-upper split.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So the nice thing about the full body is that not only am I gonna do a little bit less damage just that one area, those one muscle groups, those one movements, so they can recover, but then also they hit twice a week." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CqDbTWcAH0G.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as training everything every day: the source prescribes the full body twice a week, with recovery between hits, not daily whole-body work.
- Read as a claim that full body is best for everyone: Joe limits the recommendation to the newer client starting out, and the source says nothing about advanced lifters.
