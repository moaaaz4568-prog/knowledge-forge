# G_integrated_activation — integrated activation

## Term

Integrated activation names the warm-up stage where clients do less intense versions of the exercises they will do in the session, such as body-weight squats before back squatting. The phrase appears once, beside isolated, prime the body, and post activation potentiation, and Joe introduces it as the contrast to isolated work on one joint. The warm-up as a whole, he says, raises intensity and range of motion, improves tissue extensibility and mobility, and adds temperature and neurological priming. The integrated stage is where that priming turns into rehearsal of the real movements at a lower intensity.

## Synonyms

- integrated (the shortened form, used when he contrasts it with isolated work)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole warm-up: integrated activation is one stage inside the warm-up, after the isolated work, and the source also lists temperature, mobility, and neurological priming as separate aims.
- Read as doing the workout itself early: the integrated versions are deliberately less intense rehearsals of the session's exercises, not the working sets.
