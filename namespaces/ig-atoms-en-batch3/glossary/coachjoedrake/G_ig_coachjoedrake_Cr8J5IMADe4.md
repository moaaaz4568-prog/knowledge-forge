# G_spatial_programming — spatial programming

## Term

Spatial programming is Joe's name for planning sessions around how a trainer moves through a small space, and he says cables suit it because one spot can serve many exercises. The phrase appears once, beside cables, force vectors, and small space, and he introduces it as a concept he has talked about before. The practical test is transition time: with free weights the resistance comes only from gravity, up and down, while cables let the trainer change where the resistance comes from and reach a better range of motion. A busy gym is the reason it matters, since a trainer there must plan around minimal equipment and minimal walking between stations.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And part of that's because a concept I've talked about in the past called spatial programming and just your ability to accomplish a lot in a small space, which if you're a trainer who works in a busy gym, you know exactly what I'm talking about." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_Cr8J5IMADe4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that cables are always better than free weights: Joe prefers cables for changing force vectors in tight spaces, while the source still treats gravity-based weights as the normal case.
- Read as a program template: spatial programming is a planning idea about flow through space, and the source gives no set list of exercises for it.
