# G_ig_coachjoedrake_Cr8J5IMADe4 — spatial programming

## Term

Spatial programming is the coaching concept this clip names and credits for its equipment choice: "a concept I've talked about in the past called spatial programming and just your ability to accomplish a lot in a small space." The phrase occurs once, tied directly to cables ("you can find me using cables in almost every single training session") and the constraints of a busy gym — minimal equipment or minimal transition. The second thread is the physics advantage: cables and bands "change where that resistance is coming from" (force vectors) versus free weights working "just with gravity... up and down", opening better range of motion and muscle targeting. Here spatial programming means planning the session around the space, not just the exercises.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an exercise-selection method: the concept governs where and how a session flows in the gym (small space, minimal transitions); the exercises themselves are chosen by other criteria.
- Read as exclusive advocacy for cables: the speaker's "by no means married to or in love with just one piece of equipment" frames the cable preference as situational — busy-gym efficiency plus force-vector freedom — not a universal rule.
