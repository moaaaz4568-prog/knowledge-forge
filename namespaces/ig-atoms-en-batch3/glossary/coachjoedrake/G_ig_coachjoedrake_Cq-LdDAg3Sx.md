# G_ig_coachjoedrake_Cq-LdDAg3Sx — dough

## Term

Dough is the money meaning this rap verse builds its baking metaphor toward: the nursery-rhyme opening ("Patty cake, patty cake, I'm the baker's man") is flipped into hustle boasting, and by the verse's end dough is cash — "So much dough I can't swear I won't change." The word occurs once, with much, swear, and change in its line, and the same wordplay chains through cakes ("I bake them cakes as fast as I can") and bread ("you can tell by how my bread stack up"). In this source dough is the plainest link of the baked-goods-equals-money conceit, framed by boasts about wealth, cockiness, and "them twenties is spinning like windmills".
