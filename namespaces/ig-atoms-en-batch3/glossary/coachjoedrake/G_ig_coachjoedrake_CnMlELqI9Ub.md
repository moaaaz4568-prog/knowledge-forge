# G_flexibility_training — flexibility training

## Term

Flexibility training here names the stretch-and-mobility work a trainer adds inside sessions, and Joe presents it as the area trainers feel least confident about and least sure how to implement. The phrase is used once, beside least confident, too slow, and too much time, the three doubts clients and trainers raise about it. His case for it is practical rather than anatomical: it lets clients train harder in the gym and adds visible value inside the session. He closes by telling trainers to understand it and start using it, because clients love changing their bodies most when they feel good doing it.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a stretching routine on its own: Joe treats flexibility training as session work that serves harder gym training, not as a separate stretch class.
- Read as a warning that clients will reject it: the source says trainers fear clients will find it too slow, while Joe promises the work goes a long way once it is used.
