# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CozZN82AO-r — non-negotiable

## Term

Non-negotiable is the commitment device of this clip: the status the speaker assigns to his summer training plan so that doubt never reopens it. It occurs once, as the final word — "No, this is non-negotiable" — capping the story of the "contract with myself" he signed when he set the plan "at the beginning of the summer". The surrounding lines carry his method: "The deal was already made", and when self-talk starts hedging ("man, I gotta, I think I need to maybe if we, nope"), the answer is to refuse renegotiation. Here the word names a decision already settled by an earlier act of will, not a personality trait.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as inflexibility in all things: the scope is one training plan locked at the start of summer; the clip does not extend the no-renegotiation stance beyond that commitment.
- Read as external accountability: the contract is with himself and the enforcement is internal self-talk, so no coach, app, or partner is implied in this method.
