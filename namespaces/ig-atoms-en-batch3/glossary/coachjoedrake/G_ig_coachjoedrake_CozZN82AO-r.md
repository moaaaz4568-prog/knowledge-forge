# G_non_negotiable — non-negotiable

## Term

Non-negotiable describes the training plan Joe treats as a contract signed with himself at the start of the summer, already decided and closed to argument. The word appears once, at the very end, beside negotiating with myself, the deal was already made, and signed that contract with myself. His test for it is the inner voice that starts bargaining mid-process — man, I gotta, I think I need to maybe — answered with a flat nope. The deal was made when he set out, so the plan is done rather than debated each day.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You know, throughout that process, you'll start talking to yourself like, man, I gotta, I think I need to maybe if we, nope. No, this is non-negotiable." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CozZN82AO-r.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as stubbornness about any plan: the non-negotiable part is the commitment he made to himself at the start, not a refusal to ever adjust training.
- Read as a contract with someone else: Joe says he signed that contract with himself, so the other party in the deal is his own earlier decision.
