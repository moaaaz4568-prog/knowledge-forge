# G_ideal_training_schedule — ideal training schedule

## Term

An ideal training schedule is the question Joe asks near the end of a first meeting — what time of day will you work out — and he treats it as both a filter and a small sales tool. The phrase appears once, beside goals, barriers, and expectations of a coach, after questions about why now, what is holding you back, and what the client needs from a coach. The answers shape the first session itself: a client who wants to feel comfortable and safe gets a first day built around safety, while a bored veteran gets exercises they have never done. The schedule question also tests whether the person fits anywhere in the trainer's week at all.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a question about the client's favourite time: Joe asks it to check real availability and to close the first session, not to record a preference.
- Read as a generic intake form: the source pairs the schedule with the client's barriers and expectations, so the same first session is built differently for a nervous beginner and a bored veteran.
