# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CuwYHTxgSV7 — coaching

## Term

Coaching here means getting in the pool with the client, not telling them what to do from outside it, and Joe builds the whole video on that contrast. The word appears four times, beside telling somebody what to do, swim lessons, and get in the damn water, as the difference between explaining a movement and doing it alongside someone. His example is four-year-olds in swim lessons: the coach explains five or six or seven times from the side with no result, then gets in the water and the kids get it at once. A trainer who stays outside the pool takes the easy road, while getting in — showing the exercise, having the client do it, giving feedback — is the harder work that actually lands.

## Live Example

> "Telling somebody what to do is not the same thing as coaching, and there's literally nowhere where it becomes more apparent to me than watching four-year-olds go through swim lessons." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CuwYHTxgSV7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on explaining anything: Joe still explains the movement first, and his point is that explaining alone is not coaching, not that words have no place.
- Read as a swimming lesson: the pool is Joe's metaphor for doing the work with clients, not a claim that trainers must teach swimming.
