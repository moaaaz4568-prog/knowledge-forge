# G_engaged_coach — engaged coach

## Term

An engaged coach is how Joe describes himself when clients watched him work: moving around the lifter, adjusting, peeking from behind and from the side, never standing still with arms crossed. The phrase appears once, beside ninety degree angles, adjusting people, and counting reps, and he says people picked up clients from watching that style. The contrast is the motionless trainer with arms crossed, looking the other way or at a watch, only counting reps. He credits the engagement — and the fact that back then nobody checked a phone — with winning him clients who observed the difference.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I was coaching at ninety degree angles. I was coaching from behind, from the side. I was peeking this way, peeking that way, adjusting people. You know, I was a pretty engaged coach." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CuHHJPTAn5s.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that movement alone wins clients: the source pairs the moving, adjusting style with watching the lifter from several angles, so the engagement is attention rather than motion.
- Read as a rule never to cross your arms: the crossed arms stand for the motionless, non-engaged manner in the source, and the point is the coaching attention, not the arm position itself.
