# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CufGGY6gQik — income goals

## Term

Income goals here are the yearly money targets Joe tells personal trainers to break down the way they break down a client's fitness goal: into weekly and daily habits. The phrase appears three times, beside tracking, break it down, and forecast your income, with the singular income goal once more for the big overarching figure. His hack is to treat the money like a client's program — track what comes in each week, split the big goal into weekly amounts, and sit down on Sunday to forecast the sessions ahead. A trainer who does that every week, he promises, will make more money and actually hit the goals set at the start of the year.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of a specific income: income goals here name the targets Joe tells trainers to track and split weekly, not a guaranteed amount anyone will earn.
- Read as advice for clients: the breakdown into weekly habits is aimed at trainers managing their own money, not at clients managing their workouts.
