# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CrIrsA5AG_L — semi-private and small group training

## Term

Semi-private and small group training is the step this business clip positions between one-on-one work and a studio or team: the proving ground that forces systems into existence. The full phrase occurs 3 times ("should be your prerequisite to knowing you can take it to the next step", "the best prerequisite", "the semi-private and small group training model"), with prerequisite, two to six people, and processes as companions. The logic: one-on-one success can happen "by accident, by personality", but working two to six people forces organized programming and procedures — and the same organization is what growing trainers and a physical location will demand. Bonus effects: clients enjoy training with each other and other coaches can plug in.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a paywall tier to add for revenue: the clip frames it as a readiness test — the prerequisite that reveals whether you can run the systems a studio or team needs — not as a pricing or income strategy.
- Read as group fitness classes: the model is two to six people sharing organized programming in a training space, distinct from choreographed large-group classes; nothing here describes class-based instruction.
