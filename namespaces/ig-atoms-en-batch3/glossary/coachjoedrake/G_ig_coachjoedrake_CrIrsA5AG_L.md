# G_semi_private_and_small_group_training — semi-private and small group training

## Term

Semi-private and small group training is the step Joe makes a prerequisite for opening a studio or growing a team, because it forces a trainer to organise. The phrase appears three times, beside prerequisite, processes and systems, and two to six people, and the argument is about scale: one-on-one work can succeed by accident and personality, but training groups cannot. Working with two to six people demands thought about pairing exercises, flow inside the physical location, and delivering a better product, which is exactly what running a location or grooming other trainers requires. The payoff he promises is practical too: clients enjoy training together, buy in, and make it easier to plug in other coaches.

## Live Example

> "You have aspirations of opening your own studio or really growing your team as a one-on-one personal trainer, then semi-private and small group training should be your prerequisite to knowing you can take it to the next step." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CrIrsA5AG_L.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a format for advanced clients only: the source presents it as the test of whether a trainer can organise, not as a level reserved for experienced clients.
- Read as a shortcut to owning a studio: Joe calls it the prerequisite that proves the trainer is ready, and the source says the need for systems increases drastically at the next step rather than disappearing.
