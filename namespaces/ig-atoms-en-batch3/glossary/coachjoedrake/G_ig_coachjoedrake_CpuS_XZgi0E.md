# G_genuine_rapport — genuine rapport

## Term

Genuine rapport names the trust that grows when a trainer is actually interested in the person in front of them, and Joe calls being interested the number one strategy for building it. The phrase is used twice, beside genuinely being interested, fake, and gamify, and he warns that people pick up on it when interest is faked or strategised. New trainers, he says, arrive at first sessions loaded with things to accomplish and knowledge to showcase, but those matter less than taking an interest in the person. The payoff he promises is practical: with real interest, building rapport becomes that much easier.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The number one strategy for building genuine rapport with people, clients, or anyone else is actually being interested." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CpuS_XZgi0E.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a set of rapport hacks: Joe names strategies, hacks, and tactics only to dismiss them, and the source says faked interest is detected.
- Read as advice to hide your knowledge: the source says showcasing knowledge matters less than interest at the first session, not that knowledge should be hidden.
