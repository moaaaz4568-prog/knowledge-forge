# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CpuS_XZgi0E — rapport

## Term

Rapport is this clip's whole subject: how real connection with clients is built. It occurs 4 times, always in the phrase building rapport (or "building great genuine rapport"), and its companions are interested, genuine, strategies, and hacks. The thesis is stated twice: the number one strategy is "actually being interested" — genuinely finding the person interesting — while tactics, hacks, and gamified relationship-building get detected ("people will pick up on the fact that you're being fake"). For a new trainer the temptation is showcasing knowledge in initial sessions; the clip says taking interest matters more.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as rapport through technique: the clip argues the opposite — strategies, hacks, and gamification read as fake, and only genuine interest builds it; rapport here is a byproduct, not a deliverable of tactics.
- Read as only mattering for new trainers: the failure mode described (showcasing knowledge in initial sessions) is the new trainer's, but the number-one strategy itself is addressed to anyone building client relationships.
