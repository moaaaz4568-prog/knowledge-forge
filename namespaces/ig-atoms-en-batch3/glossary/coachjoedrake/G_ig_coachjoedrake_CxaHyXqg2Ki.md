# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CxaHyXqg2Ki — experience

## Term

Experience here is what Joe tells newly certified trainers to chase instead of collecting more certifications, and he names it three times. The word appears three times, beside getting started, putting it into action, and getting reps in, as the antidote to studying without doing. His advice is concrete: start at a big box gym for the first six to twelve months, where the marketing is done for you and the people keep coming, and treat it like practice doing free throws — the more you do it, the better you get. A trainer is not going to be good right away, and that is fine, because the reps are what build the skill.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Don't do the other stuff yet. This is the most important step, right? You need to start to get experience, putting it into action." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CxaHyXqg2Ki.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on certifications: Joe tells trainers not to stack more certificates before acting, not that the first certification was worthless.
- Read as a promise that a big box gym guarantees skill: the gym gives reps and access to people, and the experience still has to be put in.
