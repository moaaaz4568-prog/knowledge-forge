# G_first_impression — first impression

## Term

A first impression is the short window a personal trainer gets to create a positive interaction, and Joe's first rule for it is not to look like you just rolled out of bed. The phrase is used once, beside always on stage, shopping your training, and positive first experience, in a list of six things not to do with clients. He says clients paying good money will notice a messy appearance even when they give a long-term trainer the benefit of the doubt, and in a busy box gym there are always people watching. The moment someone decides to buy training, he says, the trainer who comes to mind is the one they already noticed.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "As a personal trainer, you've got a very short window of creating a positive interaction, a positive first experience or first impression." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CqlhWshAP6I.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that looks matter more than coaching: Joe says a trainer can deliver on training and experience while still losing the first impression, so appearance is the window, not the work itself.
- Read as advice for the first session only: the source says the trainer is always on stage in a bigger gym, so the impression is formed before any session begins.
