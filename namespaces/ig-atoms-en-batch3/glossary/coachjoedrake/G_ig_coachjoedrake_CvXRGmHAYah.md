# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CvXRGmHAYah — I love it

## Term

I love it here is the repeated answer in a tiny staged exchange: Joe asks what the viewer thinks about this, and the answer comes back three times, growing each time. The phrase appears three times, beside what do you think about this, what about this, and how about this, as the whole content of the source. Each reply raises the enthusiasm — I love it, I love it more, I love it with an exclamation — and nothing else is said. The line works as a joke about instant approval, not as a verdict on any exercise or method.
