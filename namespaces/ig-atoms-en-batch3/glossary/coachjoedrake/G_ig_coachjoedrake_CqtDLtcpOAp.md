# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CqtDLtcpOAp — niche

## Term

Niche is the identity question this career clip defers: the specific population and brand a trainer will serve. It occurs 3 times — "I need to create a niche, you know, in order to create my personal brand", then "helps you realize and fine tune and find your niche or your niche might even find you" — with personal brand, create, and find as companions. The advice is sequencing: put the first year or two into "getting really good" and delivering results, because that experience reveals the brand you want to be, the people you want to serve, and the clients you want — "your niche might even find you". His warning: "you don't wanna build a brand upon a poor service."

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as niche-choosing as step one: the clip inverts that — the experience of coaching well in the early years is what reveals or attracts the niche, and rushing it builds a brand on a poor service.
- Read as anti-niche: he never says skip the niche; the end state still includes a defined niche and personal brand — only their discovery is deferred behind competence.
