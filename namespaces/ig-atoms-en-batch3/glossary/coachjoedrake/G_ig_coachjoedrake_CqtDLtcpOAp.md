# G_niche — niche

## Term

A niche is the particular group of people and kind of work a trainer ends up serving, and Joe says it is found through early experience rather than chosen up front. The word appears three times, beside personal brand, first year or two, and deliver results, and he warns against rushing to create one before the coaching is good. The experience of the first year or two, he says, shows trainers the brand they want to be and the clients they want to work with, and the niche might even find them in that time. His order of operations is fixed: get really good first, because a brand built on poor service is the failure he wants trainers to avoid.

## Live Example

> "But really for most people, talk to most trainers like myself who've been doing it for a long time and they'll tell you that that experience early on helps you realize and fine tune and find your niche or your niche might even find you in that period of time as well, right?" — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CqtDLtcpOAp.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a marketing label to pick before coaching: Joe says the niche is discovered by delivering results in the first year or two, and choosing one early is the rush he warns against.
- Read as a claim that niches do not matter: the source says the niche matters, but only after the service is great, so the timing is the point rather than the idea itself.
