# G_ig_coachjoedrake_CsOtHIsAoyz — mind muscle connection

## Term

Mind muscle connection is the awareness this coaching clip builds with isometric holds: getting the client to feel the working muscle itself. The phrase occurs once in that unhyphenated spelling ("we really want to create that stronger mind muscle connection"), embedded in the motor-learning frame around it — "this whole motor learning process" so the exercise "becomes a little bit more autonomic and automatic". The method: for a client feeling a lunge or Bulgarian split squat in the back hip and quad instead of the glute, hold the bottom position — "Make them hold it for a little while" — so the coach can adjust torso angle and ask "where do you feel this?" until "oh my gosh, now I feel my glutes." He ties it to "light bulb moments" and teaching moments in the prep-and-prime context, with a different intention: creating awareness.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as mind-muscle connection being trained only by cues: the source's tool is positional — isometric holds that slow everything down for adjustment and feedback — not verbal cueing alone.
- Read as a hypertrophy-science claim: the phrase here indexes client awareness and motor learning (autonomic, automatic movement), not the research debate about hypertrophy outcomes.
