# G_light_bulb_moments — light bulb moments

## Term

Light bulb moments are the sudden realisations Joe tries to create when a client finally feels the right muscle working, and he makes them with slow isometric holds. The phrase appears once, beside teaching moments, Bulgarian split squats, and mind muscle connection, and the example is a client who feels lunges in the back hip and quad instead of the glute. Holding the bottom position lets the coach adjust the torso angle and ask where the client feels it, until the answer changes to the glutes. The hold slows everything down, builds the mind-muscle connection, and makes the movement more automatic afterwards, which is why Joe says it can make a coach seem like a magician.

> "Is when you're trying to create teaching moments or even what I call like light bulb moments." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_CsOtHIsAoyz.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to impress clients: the magician line describes the effect of the client's realisation, and the method is a slow hold with an adjusted position, not a show.
- Read as part of the warm-up: Joe ties the moments back to prep and prime but says the intention is different — awareness of a muscle, not warming up.
