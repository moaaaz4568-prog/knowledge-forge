# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DeCCzz_pMcO — Meta enhancement

## Term

Meta enhancement here is a setting Conner says to turn off before you ever press publish on a campaign. The phrase appears once, beside traffic or engagement campaign, headline and ad level, in a list of campaign rules that opens with launching every campaign on a Monday or a Tuesday. The surrounding rules are to never run a traffic or engagement campaign, keep every headline under forty-five characters, and never run more than one creative per ad set. The enhancement is something Meta adds to the campaign, and Conner wants it off before launch.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Turn off every Meta enhancement before you ever press publish." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DeCCzz_pMcO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complaint about Meta: the rule is to turn the enhancement off before publishing, and the source never says what the enhancement does.
- Read as a one-time toggle: the rule sits among ongoing habits like checking ads every Monday at ad level, not a single setup step.
