# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dc6IDx2ps4r — objection

## Term

Objection here is the single real reason a prospect will not move forward on a sales call, hidden behind vague excuses like "I need to think about it" or "send this to me in an email". The word appears three times, beside isolate, overcome and sales call, and Conner's rule is that there is only ever one: uncertainty, a price that seems too high, or wanting a second opinion. The job is to find that one specific objection, isolate it and overcome it, and doing that handles every objection thrown at you. Handle the discovery right and the pitch right, he says, and you will be fine.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as every excuse the prospect gives: the excuses are not the objection; the objection is the one underlying reason, and Conner says there is only ever one.
- Read as an argument to win: overcoming the objection here means isolating the real concern and addressing it, not talking the prospect down.
