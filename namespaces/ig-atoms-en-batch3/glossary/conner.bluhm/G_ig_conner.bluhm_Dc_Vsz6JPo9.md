# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dc_Vsz6JPo9 — show rate

## Term

Show rate here is the share of booked leads who actually show up to the sales call, and Conner says calling immediately plus a real human conversation doubles it overnight. The phrase appears eight times, beside setting call, genuine human conversation and salesy, and the coffee-shop test is the picture: a friend's show rate is unbelievably good because they are sitting down with a friend for a conversation they are looking forward to. A sales call fails the same test because nobody is excited to sit with a salesperson who will pitch and hard sell them. His rule for a bad show rate is to review the setting calls, because salesy calls or slow calling almost guarantee a bad one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a booking metric: show rate here counts who turns up, not who books, and Conner treats the setting call as the cause.
- Read as a script fix: the fix is a genuine conversation on the setting call, not better lines; salesy setting calls are named as the cause.
