# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DcoAG2OJotk — setting call

## Term

Setting call here is the first call with a lead, and Conner says its job is not only to triage and qualify but to make a connection so the person feels incentivized to show up. The phrase appears twice, beside triage, qualification and show up, in a video about doubling the lead-to-book call ratio and close rate overnight. The failure mode is asking the most basic B2B salesy questions of all time, which turns the person off and makes them want to show up less. Call within two minutes and have a genuine human conversation, he says, and the change will be insane.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Your job of the setting call is not to purely triage them and get an understanding of their qualification." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DcoAG2OJotk.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the sales call itself: the setting call here comes before the sales call, and its job is the connection that gets the prospect to the sales call.
- Read as skipping qualification: triage and understanding qualification are still part of the setting call; the connection is added to them, not a replacement.
