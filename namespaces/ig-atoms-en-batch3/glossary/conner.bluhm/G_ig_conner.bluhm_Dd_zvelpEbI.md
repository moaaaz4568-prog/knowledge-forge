# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dd_zvelpEbI — skin in the game angle

## Term

Skin in the game angle here is the third of three S-tier ad angles, and the one Conner says to test because it works unbelievably well. The phrase appears once, beside ad spend, first two clients and money back, and the move is to put your own money on the line first instead of promising 10 clients in 90 days or your money back, which nobody believes. Something like, "I'll cover your ad spend until you sign your first two clients," means they do not have to take your word for anything because your money is on the line as well. The secret is that you can add money to their upfront payment to pay for their ad spend.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a money-back guarantee: the skin in the game angle puts your money up first, before the client pays, which is the opposite of promising a refund later.
- Read as free work: covering ad spend until two clients sign is paid for by adding money to the upfront payment, not by working for free.
