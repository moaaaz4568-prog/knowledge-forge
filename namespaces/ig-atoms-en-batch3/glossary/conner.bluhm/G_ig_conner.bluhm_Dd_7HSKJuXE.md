# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dd_7HSKJuXE — love bomb

The phrase appears once, beside fastest win, doubt and first week, and the mechanism is to get them the fastest win you possibly can before they have time to doubt you. The love bombing is early affection with a fast result, not a compliment.
