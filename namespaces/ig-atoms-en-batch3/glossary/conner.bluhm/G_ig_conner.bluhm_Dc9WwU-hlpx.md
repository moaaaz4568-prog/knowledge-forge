# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dc9WwU-hlpx — team

## Term

Team here is the single most important part of the entire business, and Conner says owners forget how crucial it is to their success. The word appears five times, beside respect, fade away and lack of effort, and the warning is that taking the team for granted makes them like you less and less until they dissolve into a lack of effort rather than quitting loudly. They do not leave with a hard-hitting I quit message; they fade away. His closing line is the moral: your team is everything and don't take it for granted.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as staff you manage: team here is the people the business depends on, and Conner frames them as the most important part, not a cost to minimize.
- Read as a plea to pay more: the respect Conner asks for is attention and regard, and he never mentions pay or perks in this source.
