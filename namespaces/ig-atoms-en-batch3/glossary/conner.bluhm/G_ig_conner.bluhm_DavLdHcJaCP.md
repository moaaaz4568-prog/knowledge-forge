# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DavLdHcJaCP — conviction

## Term

Conviction here is what Conner says his achievements before twenty give him: a high level of certainty that the next twelve months will bring his first million dollar month. The word appears once, beside extremely high level, before turning 20, and a matter of when not if, as the bridge between a list of past wins and a list of future ones. The past list runs from selling his company to private equity to a marathon with no training and Norwegian at B1; the future list runs from benching 245 pounds to climbing Mount Kilimanjaro and helping twenty followers hit their first hundred-thousand-dollar month. His first point is that wanting something means genuinely believing it is possible, and knowing it is a matter of when, not if.

## Live Example

> "gives me an extremely high level of conviction that within the next 12 months, I will be able to do my first million dollar month" — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DavLdHcJaCP.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that belief alone achieves goals: conviction here is Conner's stated reason for confidence, and the source lists achievements without showing how belief produced them.
- Read as bragging: Conner says he lists the wins to make two points about belief and following along, not to boast.
