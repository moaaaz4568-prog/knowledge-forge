# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DbS7NfAJ5aE — risk

## Term

Risk here is the thing Conner says failing agency owners treat like a plague: any decision with even a remote chance of a negative outcome. The word appears once, in the first of four signs of failure, beside cold outreach, ads and insane, and the adjective risky appears once more for owners who think anyone running ads is insane. Avoiding risk is what pushes owners to free cold outreach and to selling to everyone, which Conner says loses to a narrow niche every time. The point is not that risk is good in itself, but that refusing it caps the business at the safest, cheapest moves.

## Live Example

> "The number one thing is that you treat risk like a plague and completely avoid making any decision that has even a remote chance of a negative outcome." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DbS7NfAJ5aE.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as financial risk only: risk here covers any decision with a chance of a negative outcome, including running ads and charging high prices, not just losing money.
- Read as Conner praising recklessness: he faults owners for avoiding every risky decision, not for weighing a risky one badly; the failure is the refusal to decide at all.
