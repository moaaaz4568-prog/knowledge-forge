# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DcYso3TJMk1 — negative energy

## Term

Negative energy here is what a prospect feels when they sense you need their money, and Conner says it makes the call extremely difficult. The phrase appears once, beside desperately, extremely difficult and pay you money, and the belief behind it is that $20,000 or $40,000 from the prospect would do literally nothing for your life. The point is about the seller's state, not the prospect's objection: neediness is felt before any pitch is made. You must genuinely believe you do not need the money, or the energy on the call works against you.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because when people feel that you need the money or you're desperately trying to get the money from them, there is just going to be a negative energy on that call, which is going to lead to it being extremely, extremely difficult to getting them to pay you money." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DcYso3TJMk1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the prospect's mood: the negative energy here comes from the seller's neediness, not from anything the prospect brings to the call.
- Read as acting detached: Conner asks for a genuine belief that the money would change nothing, not a performance of not caring.
