# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DdHRMPRzftp — expectation setting course

## Term

Expectation setting course here is the onboarding step Conner never skips before launching ads: three to five videos clients go through the moment they are onboarded, then a 20 to 30 question quiz passed with 100% certainty. The phrase appears once, beside expectation setting quiz, onboarding and interpretation, and the reason is Naval Ravikant's line that happiness is results minus expectations. Without brutally clear expectations, the client interprets the promise generously, expects ten or twenty clients, and asks for a refund when reality falls short. Set them brutally clearly and a client never asks for a refund or disputes.

## Live Example

> "Before I ever launch someone's ads, the first thing that I always do is make sure they have gone through our expectation setting course during the onboarding." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DdHRMPRzftp.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as training the client to use the product: the course sets expectations about the program, what is required of each side, and what success looks like, not how to use a tool.
- Read as a legal shield: the course prevents refunds by aligning expectations, and Conner frames it as the way to happy clients, not as paperwork.
