# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DdB8x6ApBft — team trips

## Term

Team trips here is Conner's incentive for hitting targets: promise the team a big all-expenses-paid trip when they hit 100K, a quarter million or a million, and output changes night and day. The phrase appears twice, beside team culture, incentivized and Costa Rica, with the singular team trip once more for the trip they are excited to return to. Last year at OpsGirl they blasted trips to Costa Rica, Greece, Norway and Bali, because people want to travel and do awesome things beyond money. The memory of a trip in a sick villa with the whole company is what makes people push harder to earn the next one.

## Synonyms

- team trip (singular, once)

## Live Example

> "Last year when I was running OpsGirl at like six-figure months and before I had sold a company to private equity, we would just consistently blast team trips." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DdB8x6ApBft.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a holiday perk: team trips here are an earned incentive tied to specific targets, not a holiday the company gives regardless.
- Read as a substitute for pay: Conner pairs trips with bonuses and clear incentive structures, and the trips work because people want to travel, not because they replace money.
