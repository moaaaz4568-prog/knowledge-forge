# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DdEw3Lup8Fv — funnel

## Term

Funnel here is the single page that takes a visitor to one outcome, and Conner says the dumbest thing builders do is make it unbelievably complicated, a borderline website. The word appears seven times, beside tabs, VSL and above the fold, and the failure is tabs to click around, more than one button, more than one hero element: options confuse and distract people with terrible attention spans. It is better to be clear than clever. A good funnel has a headline, a VSL and a simple button, or a headline and a form, almost everything above the fold, and absolutely zero room for interpretation about what to do.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a website: a funnel here is the opposite of a website with tabs and pages; it is one page with one thing to do.
- Read as minimalism for its own sake: the simplicity serves conversions, and Conner still requires a headline, a VSL or form, and a button.
