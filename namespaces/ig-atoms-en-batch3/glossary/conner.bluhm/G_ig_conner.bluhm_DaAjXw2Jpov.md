# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DaAjXw2Jpov — back end

## Term

Back end here is the part of the agency Conner says he neglected while chasing new clients, and now spends almost all his time building. The phrase appears twice, beside don't spend as much time on my back end, building the best back end possible, and clients keep paying me, as the contrast to his old grind of a hundred cold DMs and twenty cold calls a day. In the old way he had no idea where the next client would come from and was one bad month from giving up; in the new way ads bring high ticket clients weekly, referrals add one or two more per client, and the back end is what keeps them paying. The term works as the retention machine behind the acquisition, not as any single tool.

## Live Example

> "I always know exactly where my next client is coming from, and I spend almost all of my time building the best back end possible so clients keep paying me and refer even more people so that the business can grow even faster." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DaAjXw2Jpov.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that outreach is useless: Conner's back end matters because ads already bring clients, and the video contrasts two stages of one business, not two kinds of people.
- Read as a product: the back end here is the whole client-keeping operation — service, referrals, and follow-through — not one software or system.
