# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dc06brgJOdu — speed to lead

## Term

Speed to lead here is how fast you call a new lead after they come through the form, and Conner says a bad lead to book call ratio almost certainly means it is bad. The phrase appears once, beside pickup rate, half life and five minutes: a pickup rate has a half life of five minutes, and the intent of people who pick up gets worse the longer you wait. Call the second they come through and they are impressed at the speed; call two hours later and they ask to be taken off the list. His fix is to call every single lead within 60 seconds of them coming through the form.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a dialer setting: speed to lead here is a behavior, calling within 60 seconds, not a tool or automation feature.
- Read as a guarantee of bookings: fast calling only preserves the intent the lead already had; Conner never claims it creates interest that was not there.
