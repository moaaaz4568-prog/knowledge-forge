# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Db0SDXgJaZy — golden niche

## Term

Golden niche here is the exact market Conner and his client Prince found on day one of a sixty-day challenge: stem cell clinics. The phrase appears once, beside found the exact golden niche, decided on, and after some back and forth, as the first of three things that had to be right. The other two were an Alex Ramozi-style offer speaking to the market's internal desire, and sales and setting that turned booked calls into closed clients. With the niche, the offer, and the sales work right, Prince went from zero to a forty-thousand-dollar month in sixty days, and Conner credits the three together rather than ads alone.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that any niche pays: the golden niche is the specific market Prince chose after back and forth, not a guarantee that picking a niche prints money.
- Read as a story about ads alone: Conner says the result came from getting the niche, the offer, and the sales and setting right together.
