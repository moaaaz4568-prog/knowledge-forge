# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DdZnFuEJqTM — eight core desires

## Term

Eight core desires here is the wiring Cashvertising says every person has, and every ad must hit one of them. The phrase appears once, beside staying alive, social approval and learned wants, opening forty advertising rules from Drew Eric Whitman's book in ninety seconds. The desires run from staying alive and living longer, the strongest, through food and drink, fear, pain and danger, sex, comfortable living, winning, protecting loved ones, to social approval. Those hardwired desires hit harder than learned wants like saving money or being efficient, which is why the ad must strike one of them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complete list of motives: the eight desires are the book's hardwired set, and Conner presents them as stronger than learned wants, not as every reason anyone buys.
- Read as a guarantee: hitting a core desire is what an ad must do, and the other thirty-nine rules still decide whether it works.
