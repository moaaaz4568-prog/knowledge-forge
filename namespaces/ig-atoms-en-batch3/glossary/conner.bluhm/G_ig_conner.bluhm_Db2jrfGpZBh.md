# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Db2jrfGpZBh — risk

## Term

Risk here is the thing Conner says you cannot afford not to take, and he frames his whole story around going all in. The word appears three times, beside take a risk, go all in, and make it happen or die trying, with risks once more in the line about not taking risks. He took out a loan to run ads and spent almost every penny before signing a client, then signed one after another until the agency did six figures a month within ninety days. His point is blunt: you only have one life to live the life you want, so refusing the risk is itself the risk.

## Synonyms

- risks (the plural form, used once in this source)

## Live Example

> "So go take that fucking risk and make it happen." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Db2jrfGpZBh.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to take any loan: the loan here is Conner's own story about going all in on ads, not a recommendation that every agency borrow to start.
- Read as a guarantee: going all in took Conner from zero to a hundred and twenty-four thousand a month in ninety days, but the source proves nothing about the next person who tries it.
