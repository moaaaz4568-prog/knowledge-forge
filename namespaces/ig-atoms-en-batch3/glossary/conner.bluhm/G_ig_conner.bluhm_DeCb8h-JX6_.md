# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DeCb8h-JX6_ — foolish and stupid

## Term

Foolish and stupid here is what Epictetus says to be content with being thought of, and Conner applies it to marketing: the fear of being seen as foolish and stupid is the very thing holding you back from having any success at all. The phrase appears twice, beside Epictetus, face in it and scared, as the third of three philosophies that will make you disgustingly good at marketing. Most agency owners are too scared to run an ad or a video with their face in it because of what people they know will think of them. Being content with looking foolish is presented as the price of getting past that fear.

> "But the fear of being seen as foolish and stupid is the very thing holding you back from having any success at all." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DeCb8h-JX6_.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to look foolish: the philosophy is to accept being thought foolish, not to try to look it; the fear is the obstacle, not the goal.
- Read as a general life lesson: Epictetus is quoted for marketing specifically, and the fear Conner names is about running ads and videos with your face in them.
