# G_ig_conner.bluhm_Dc3fGmXJRH1 — expectation setting quiz

## Term

Expectation setting quiz here is the gate Conner puts between onboarding and any work: a twenty to thirty question quiz clients must get one hundred percent on before ads, email or content work starts. The phrase appears twice, beside expectation setting course, disputes and refunds, and the course is the three to five module version of the same material. Doing that one thing, he says, solved $120,000 of disputes last year and should cut disputes and refunds by about ninety five percent. The remaining disputes are personal reasons, not expectations, which is why the quiz targets the realm of expectations alone.

## Live Example

> "And then don't launch their ads, don't start their email campaign, don't start working with their content or whatever you are working on until after they've completed that expectation setting quiz." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dc3fGmXJRH1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a test of competence: the quiz checks that the client understood what was promised, not whether they can do the work.
- Read as a cure for every dispute: Conner says it drops disputes by about ninety five percent, and the rest are personal reasons outside expectations.
