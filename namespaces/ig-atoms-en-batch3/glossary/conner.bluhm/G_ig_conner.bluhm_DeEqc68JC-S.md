# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DeEqc68JC-S — decision makers

## Term

Decision makers here is who must all be on the sales call: get all decision makers on the call. The phrase appears once, beside Pain first, dream second, thirty percent and record every call, in a list of short closing rules. The surrounding rules are to never close from your bedroom, never read a script, listen to your last close call before every call, and never talk more than thirty percent of the call. Pain comes first and the dream second, the prospect says their pain themselves, and the price is said and then followed by silence.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Pain first, dream second. Get all decision makers on the call. Don't explain their pain to them." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DeEqc68JC-S.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a suggestion: getting all decision makers on the call is a rule in a list of commands, not an optional tip.
- Read as a scheduling detail: the point is that the decision is made on the call, and the other rules about pain, silence and recording serve the same close.
