# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DdgpeHDp1SS — lead magnet

## Term

Lead magnet here is the free offer you package as a high-value report: a good lead magnet solves one problem fast. The phrase appears once, beside free offer, email address and headline, in forty sales laws from Sell Like Crazy by Saver Soubi. The surrounding laws are about giving away your best information for free, teaching what to do and selling the how, and trading something valuable for every email address you collect. The magnet works because it solves one problem quickly enough to earn the address.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "A good lead magnet solves one problem fast." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DdgpeHDp1SS.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any freebie: a lead magnet here solves one specific problem fast, not any free content; the free content should be good enough to charge for.
- Read as the sale itself: the magnet earns the email address, and the sale comes later from nurturing and teaching, not from the magnet.
