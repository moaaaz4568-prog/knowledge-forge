# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DbDaXYZJisa — client retention

## Term

Client retention here is the outcome Conner says to sell instead of fulfillment systems, and it closes a list of five sell-the-result lines. The phrase appears once, beside don't sell fulfillment systems, sell a million views, and sell a six pack, as the last item in a parallel series. Each line swaps a deliverable for its result: content help for a million views, Facebook ads for five clients, fitness coaching for a six pack, relationship coaching for a happy marriage. The term works as the payoff of fulfillment done well, not as a system the video describes.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fulfillment system: client retention here is the result to sell, and the source does not describe any system that produces it.
- Read as a claim that deliverables do not matter: Conner's lines reframe the pitch around outcomes, not a verdict that the work itself is worthless.
