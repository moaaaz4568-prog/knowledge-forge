# G_ig_conner.bluhm_DbQTVqQpqGL — front end

## Term

Front end here is the acquisition side of an agency: ads, calls booked, cold DMs, personal brand and the sales process, everything owners push harder when they try to grow. The phrase appears twice, beside more ad spend, more calls booked and a more dialed sales process, and Conner calls optimizing for it the single most common mistake agency owners make. His counter-model is Steve Jobs scaling Apple by focusing on the product, so the front end is the sales machinery that should follow a product people love, not lead it. The sting in the close is that you do sales because you sucked at marketing, and marketing because you sucked at product.

## Live Example

> "Look, everybody tries to optimize for a bigger and better front end: more ad spend, more calls booked, more cold DMs, a bigger personal brand, a more dialed sales process." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_DbQTVqQpqGL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole business: the front end here is only the sales and acquisition side, and Conner argues the product side matters more for scaling.
- Read as a design term: front end here has nothing to do with web design or interfaces; it means the client-getting machinery of the agency.
