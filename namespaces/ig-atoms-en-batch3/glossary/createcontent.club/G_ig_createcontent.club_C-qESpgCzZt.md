# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-qESpgCzZt — viral hooks

## Term

Viral hooks are the proven opening lines the account collects from videos that already earned millions of views, then hands out as a reusable set. The phrase appears once, in the opening warning to stop inventing hooks from scratch, next to videos, collection and a ChatGPT prompt that returns 100 hooks for any topic. The source's shorter form hooks appears on its own when the prompt's output is described, so the two forms name the same set of lines. A creator using the collection would type in their video's topic and take back 100 hooks that have already been proven to work.

## Synonyms

- hooks (the shorter form the source uses for the prompt's output)

## Live Example

> "Stop trying to come up with viral hooks for your videos." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C-qESpgCzZt.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the hooks are read as written by the account: the collection gathers hooks from videos that already went viral, so the account is curating proven lines rather than inventing them.
- When the 100 hooks are read as guaranteed winners: the clip calls them proven to work because their source videos earned millions of views, not because every use of them will go viral.
