# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-yVb6Bih7O — push-ups

## Term

Push-ups are the measuring stick this clip uses for unrealistic expectations about going viral: nobody does ten push-ups and expects to get jacked, so nobody should post ten videos and expect millions of views. The word appears once, in the push-ups comparison, next to jacked, seven hundred videos and no one watched. The clip's point is volume over time — the account itself posted about seven hundred videos, hundreds of which nobody watched, before the recent ones took off. A creator applying the comparison would judge their tenth video the way they would judge their tenth push-up: as the start of the work, not the moment it pays off.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the push-ups are read as fitness advice: the exercise is only an analogy for posting volume, so treating the clip as a workout tip misses its point about going viral.
- When seven hundred videos is read as the required number: the account cites its own seven hundred posted videos as its history, not as a quota every creator must hit.
