# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-lImNfiXP4 — cheat code

## Term

A cheat code, in this clip's usage, is a shortcut that lets a creator turn any viral video into a ready-to-use script without writing from scratch. The phrase appears once, in the opening line that tells viewers to take notes, next to viral videos, the Glass extension and ChatGPT. The shortcut runs through the free Chrome extension Glass, which transcribes a YouTube video in seconds and hands the transcript to ChatGPT for a script, so the creator never runs out of content ideas. A creator using it would open a viral video in their niche, click the ChatGPT icon and take the generated script as the starting point for their own version.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the cheat code is read as copying the viral video: the shortcut generates a new script from the transcript, and the clip presents it as a starting point, not as permission to re-upload someone else's video.
- When Glass is read as the whole method: the extension only transcribes, and the script still comes from the ChatGPT step the clip describes after it.
