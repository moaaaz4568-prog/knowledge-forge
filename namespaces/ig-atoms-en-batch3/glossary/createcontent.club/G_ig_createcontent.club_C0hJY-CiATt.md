# G_ig_createcontent.club_C0hJY-CiATt — sound design

## Term

Sound design is the art of using sound effects and music to add depth and an epic feel to a video, the element this clip says creators are missing out on. The phrase appears once, in I'm talking about sound design, next to sad, exciting and happy, while the -ing form sound designing appears once in the invitation to start sound designing. The clip demonstrates the art live, switching the same video from sad to exciting and happy with sound alone. A creator starting with it would comment sound for the free website, then layer effects and music over their footage until the mood of the scene changes.

## Synonyms

- sound designing (the -ing form the source uses for starting the practice)

## Live Example

> "I'm talking about sound design. This is the art of using sound effects and music to add depth and an epic feel to your content." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C0hJY-CiATt.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When sound design is read as background music only: the clip's art covers sound effects and music together, so a music track alone is not the full practice described.
- When the free website is read as the lesson: the website only supplies the effects and music, while the design itself is the creator's choice of what each sound does to the mood.
