# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-S7NgHCAQM — Chrome extension

## Term

Chrome extension here is the free tool at the center of an AI script-writing workflow: download it, find a viral video in your niche on YouTube, click a button, and it copies the entire transcript to ChatGPT, summarizes it, and gives the five most important topics. The phrase appears twice, beside viral video, ChatGPT and five ready-to-use scripts, and the next step is a long prompt asking ChatGPT to write a short-form Instagram script about each of the five bullet points, starting with one of 30 hooks. The result is five ready-to-use scripts in seconds every single time, still to be adapted to your style.
