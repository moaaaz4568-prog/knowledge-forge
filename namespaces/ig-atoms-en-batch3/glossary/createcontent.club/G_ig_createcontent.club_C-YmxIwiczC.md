# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-YmxIwiczC — deep link

## Term

Deep link here is the fix for promoting a YouTube video from Instagram stories: a link that brings people outside the Instagram platform instead of into the Instagram browser. The phrase appears once, beside Instagram browser, link sticker and YouTube app, and the problem is that Instagram does not want users to leave, so a plain link sticker opens the video in the Instagram browser where viewers are not logged in. They cannot follow, comment or like the video there. With a deep link, the YouTube app opens automatically and they can interact with the content.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "So what's the solution? You need to use something called a deep link which allows you to bring people outside of the Instagram platform when they click on your link." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C-YmxIwiczC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any link: a deep link here specifically opens the YouTube app outside Instagram, not the Instagram browser like a link sticker does.
- Read as a paid tool: the video points to a free website for making the link, and the link itself costs nothing to use.
