# G_ig_createcontent.club_C1KF_ZILyV7 — Enhance Speech

## Term

Enhance Speech is the Adobe AI feature this clip tells creators to use to turn poor audio into clear, professional sound without an expensive microphone. The name appears once, in the instruction to look for Enhance Speech on Adobe's website, next to poor audio quality, clearer sound and top 10 favorite AIs. The clip proves the point with a live test, playing the same voice first with poor audio quality and then with the enhanced, clearer sound. A creator using it would record with whatever microphone they have, run the recording through Enhance Speech and publish the clearer version.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When Enhance Speech is read as a microphone: the feature processes a recording after it is made, so buying it as hardware misses that it is software on Adobe's website.
- When the clearer sound is read as the clip's own recording quality: the before-and-after test is the demonstration, and the clip never claims its own audio was recorded with expensive gear.
