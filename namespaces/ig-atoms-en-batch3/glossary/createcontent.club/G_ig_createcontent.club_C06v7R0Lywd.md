# G_ig_createcontent.club_C06v7R0Lywd — hook

## Term

A hook is a strong and intriguing opening line that grabs the viewer's interest and makes them want to hear more, the first thing a video needs before anything else. The word appears 3 times, defined once as that opening line and named again in the perfect hook and in the comment instruction to write hook, next to attention, right off the bat and secrets. The clip warns against opening with an introduction — hey guys, I'm Ryan and welcome back — because new viewers care about the opening line, not about who the creator is. A creator using a hook here would drop the intriguing line first and save the introduction for viewers who already stayed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the hook is read as the whole video: the hook is only the opening line that catches attention, so treating it as the video's content confuses the entrance with the lesson.
- When introducing yourself is read as a hook: the clip says new viewers are not interested in who the creator is yet, so an introduction first is exactly the opening the clip warns against.
