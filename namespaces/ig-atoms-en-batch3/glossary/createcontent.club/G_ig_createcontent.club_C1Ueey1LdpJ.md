# G_ig_createcontent.club_C1Ueey1LdpJ — call to action

## Term

A call to action is the clear instruction this clip says a video must give its viewers, because people scrolling on autopilot will not interact on their own. The phrase appears once, in this is why you need a call to action, next to autopilot, clear instruction and keep on scrolling. The clip places it as step three of its four-step formula, after the first three seconds and watch time, and before the warning not to reinvent the wheel. A creator using one would end the video by telling viewers exactly what to do next, instead of hoping they interact without being asked.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Most people when scrolling on social media are pretty much on autopilot. This means that if you don't give them clear instruction, they will simply keep on scrolling after watching your video. This is why you need a call to action." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C1Ueey1LdpJ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When the call to action is read as begging for engagement: the clip presents it as a clear instruction viewers need because they scroll on autopilot, not as a plea for likes.
- When the four steps are read as optional tips: the clip calls them the only four steps needed to repeat its viral results, so skipping the call to action breaks the formula it teaches.
