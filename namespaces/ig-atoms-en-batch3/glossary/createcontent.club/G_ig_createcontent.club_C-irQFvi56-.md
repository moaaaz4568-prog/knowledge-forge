# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-irQFvi56- — loss aversion

## Term

Loss aversion is the psychology principle this clip teaches creators to build their videos around: people pay more attention when they feel they are going to miss out on something than when they are told what they will gain. The term appears once, named directly as the principle behind the secret marketing strategy, next to hook, the video's heart and millions of views. The creator demonstrates it on the clip itself, opening with the threat of never going viral instead of promising virality, so the viewer feels the loss before any value is offered. A creator applying it would frame the hook and the heart of the video around what the viewer loses by scrolling away, not around what they gain by staying.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When loss aversion is read as scaring viewers: the clip uses the fear of missing out to hold attention, not threats about the viewer's life, so treating it as intimidation misses the marketing use.
- When the principle is read as a promise of virality: loss aversion is a way to frame the hook, and the clip never claims the framing alone guarantees millions of views.
