# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-dAsCICkVb — watch time

## Term

Watch time here is the factor the account calls viral, above shares, which are very important, and above likes, hashtags and saves, which are not. The phrase appears once, beside shares, likes and hashtags, in a rapid verdict list on what is actually important to go viral. Comments help but are not crucial, saves are not really important, and a professional camera does not matter. The main goal, turning viewers into leads and clients, is what all of it serves, and the video points to one-on-one coaching to do it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Shares? Very important. Having a professional camera? No, it doesn't really matter. Likes? Not important. Watch time? Viral." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C-dAsCICkVb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the only factor: watch time is called viral, but shares are also very important, and the list ranks several factors, not one.
- Read as a how-to: the video names what matters and never explains how to raise watch time, only that it is what goes viral.
