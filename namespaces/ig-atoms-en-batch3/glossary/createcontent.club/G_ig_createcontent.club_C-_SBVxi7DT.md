# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-_SBVxi7DT — three-line structure

## Term

Three-line structure here is the bio formula the account follows after millions of views and more than 200,000 followers: who you help, what results you got, and a call to action. The phrase appears once, beside username, one million views and seven viral formats, as the first step of starting again from scratch, before searching for ten creators in the niche. The method is to take seven viral formats and seven viral topics, combine them, write scripts, and post one video a day to see what performs best. The bio is the setup; the testing of formats and topics is the growth.
