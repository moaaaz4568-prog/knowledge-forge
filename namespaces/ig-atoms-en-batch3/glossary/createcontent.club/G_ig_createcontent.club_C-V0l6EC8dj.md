# G_ig_createcontent.club_C-V0l6EC8dj — comment glitch strategy

## Term

Comment glitch strategy here is the method the account says got them 40,000 comments on one single video: steal it to get thousands of comments under your posts. The phrase appears once, beside Manychat, 40,000 comments and public replies, and the strategy runs on Manychat, the software behind every "comment the word down below" prompt. The setup is to choose a simple keyword, include misspellings of it, set the rules and public replies, and use questions instead of statements in the replies so people answer back. The public replies alone already double the comments, and questions push them higher still.
