# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DRx9n_Ulfj9 — done for you

## Term

Done for you is the highest-touch version of Daniel's product ladder: one buyer wants to do it yourself, the next wants done with you, and this one wants the work done for them. The phrase is used once, beside do it yourself, done with you, and hundreds of iterations, and his warning opens the clip — selling the same product to multiple different audiences is where people screw up. His concrete payoff is the catalogue that follows: sell each version until you have tons of products, instead of forcing one product onto every audience.

## Live Example

> "Like this person wants to do it yourself, this person wants done with you, this person wants done for you, and that there's all different hundreds of iterations that you could sell each of those until you just have tons of products." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DRx9n_Ulfj9.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the only version worth selling: Daniel presents three versions side by side, and done for you is the full-service end of the ladder, not the whole ladder.
- Read as done with you under another name: the clip separates the two carefully — done with you keeps the buyer involved, while done for you hands the work over entirely.
