# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DVHjxm8jZeC — sifting mechanism

## Term

Sifting mechanism is Daniel's name for the hard points where people quit: outreach that does not immediately work is the filter that shakes people out. The phrase is used once, beside give up, hard, and roadblock, and his setup is the hissy fit — messages sent, no instant result, and the cry that this doesn't work and this is a scam. His math for why it must be hard is the system itself: finding complete strangers on the internet and getting them to pay you thousands of dollars is an insane feat, and if it were easy everyone would do it and it would no longer work.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And when you hit these points where it's hard and something doesn't immediately work, that's where people give up and that's the sifting mechanism to get people out and shake people out." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DVHjxm8jZeC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that quitting proves you never wanted it: the sifting is Daniel's description of who stays, and the clip never says the quitters lacked talent.
- Read as a promise that surviving the filter means money: he says getting really good at it pays because the competition got sifted out, and the clip never prices the payoff.
