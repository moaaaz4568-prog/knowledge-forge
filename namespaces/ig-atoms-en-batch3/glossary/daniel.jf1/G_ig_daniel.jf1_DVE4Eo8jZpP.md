## Term

Guarantee is the word at the center of Daniel's warning about young course sellers: a younger dude who never ran an agency tells prospects, does anything in life have a guarantee, as if that answers a client asking for one. The word appears three times, beside client asks, younger dude, and treating the prospect like an idiot, and his charge is the framing — you got a prospect on a sales call with zero case study and zero authority, and you tell this person they are an idiot for wanting a guarantee. His closing line is the rule: stop treating them like an idiot.
