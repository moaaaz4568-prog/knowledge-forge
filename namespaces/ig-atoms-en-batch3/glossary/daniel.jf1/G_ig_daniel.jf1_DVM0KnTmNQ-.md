# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DVM0KnTmNQ- — 90% of your clients

## Term

90% of your clients is Daniel's opening figure for the agency business: ninety percent of your clients will suck, and that is okay. The phrase is used once, beside will suck, student, and fraud, and his scene is a student on a call, complaining that his clients cannot close the deals he sends and feeling like a fraud when they don't. His rule from it is the majority's nature — 90% plus in any industry or niche will have a hard time closing, every client you ever sell for will be terrible at sales, and 10% of clients make 90% of the money and get 90% of the results.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an excuse to blame clients: Daniel says the failure rate is normal and the seller should try his best, and the clip never says the seller bears no responsibility.
- Read as a measured statistic about all agencies: the 90% figures are Daniel's own claims in the clip, and the source gives no data behind them.
