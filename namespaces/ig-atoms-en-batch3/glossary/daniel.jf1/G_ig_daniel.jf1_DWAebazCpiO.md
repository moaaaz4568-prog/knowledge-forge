# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DWAebazCpiO — multiply one into five

## Term

Daniel's casino button is the image for multiplying one into five: present any human with the chance to put $1 in and get $5 out, and they will click the button infinitely. The phrase is used once, beside put $1 in, get $5 out, and confident, and his problem statement is the client who wants a 3k, 4k, 5k a month client of their own — somebody with their own business and their own money, asked for $5,000 of it per month. His rule is the trust test: they will just never sign clients ever unless they know how to make the client more money, so the issue to work on is the client's confidence in your personal ability to multiply one into five.

## Live Example

> "The problem, if you can't sign people, is they're not confident in your personal ability to multiply one into five." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DWAebazCpiO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of five-to-one returns: the casino button is Daniel's image for the belief a client must hold, and the clip never says the seller can deliver it.
- Read as a claim that clients only care about money: the $5,000 retainer is the example, and the clip never says trust is the only thing a client weighs.
