# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DXkoSuMEXo- — mindset of scarcity

## Term

Mindset of scarcity is Daniel's definition of poor: not a lack of money, but I can't have this, I can't do this, I don't have the capability to learn, I don't have the balls to take any kind of risk. The phrase is used once, beside poor, broke, and temporarily without resources, and his contrast is the broke side — broke is temporarily without resources, and broke people become rich because they have the courage to do stuff they've never tried and the intelligence to figure things out themselves. His closing image is the uncertainty test: operating in any form of uncertainty, they actually do stuff.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I love broke people and I hate poor people. Poor is a mindset of scarcity." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DXkoSuMEXo-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that poor people choose poverty: Daniel's poor is a mindset in his telling, and the clip never says circumstances play no part.
- Read as praise for being broke: broke is temporarily without resources, and the clip's broke people become rich by acting, not by staying broke.
