# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DXQIurgiesX — fifty thousand dollar client

## Term

Fifty thousand dollar client is the meme Daniel quotes to show how rich clients behave: the five hundred dollar client demands results and entrusts you with livelihoods, while the fifty thousand dollar client just says, money sent, thanks. The phrase appears twice, beside five hundred dollar client, money sent, and easier to close, and his twist is the phone — it is more difficult to get the rich client on the phone, but easier to close them once they are there. His rule for the poor side is the booking habit: poor people will book every single call everywhere for no reason at all, and it is very weird.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fact about all rich clients: the meme is Daniel's illustration, and the clip never claims every fifty thousand dollar client says money sent.
- Read as contempt for poor clients: the booking habit is his observation about who takes calls, and the clip never says poor clients never pay.
