# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DTrLstXDqSV — selling a day trading course

## Term

Selling a day trading course is the model Daniel grades twice at once: S tier if you have zero moral compass, C tier because no one you sell it to is gonna get results. The phrase appears twice, beside zero moral compass, pure mathematics, and legitimate model, and his math is the reason — by pure mathematics, legitimately no one gets results from it. His second test is the investor's view: offered equity and a profit share in someone's day trading course, he would say yes, because it's a cash flow and a legitimate thing that makes sense as a business.

## Synonyms

- day trading course (the shorter form Daniel uses when someone offers to invest in his course)

## Live Example

> "Selling a day trading course. If you have zero moral compass and understand that legitimately no one you sell it to is gonna get results just by pure mathematics, if you had zero moral compass, selling a day trading course would be S tier." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DTrLstXDqSV.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an endorsement of the course: the S tier grade is explicitly conditioned on having zero moral compass, and the C tier grade is the one Daniel actually assigns.
- Read as a claim that the course business is a scam: Daniel calls it a legitimate model and a legitimate thing as a business — the immorality he names is selling it knowing buyers won't get results.
