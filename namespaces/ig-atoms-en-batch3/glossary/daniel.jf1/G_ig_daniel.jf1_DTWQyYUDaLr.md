# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DTWQyYUDaLr — free lead magnet

## Term

Free lead magnet is the trap Daniel warns against: people who come in from a free lead magnet never buy anything, because they are non-buyers, and there is nothing you can do to make them buy. The phrase appears three times, twice in the singular and once as free lead magnets, beside Pyros, LTV, and non-buyers, and his proof is the tracking — he spends $10,000 a day on ads and uses Pyros to see whether free-magnet leads ever buy anything at all. His fix is a single dollar: make the lead magnet $1, and it magically works out with LTV, because the people who buy stuff continue to buy stuff, and you only need them to pay any amount of money at all.

## Synonyms

- free lead magnets (the plural form Daniel uses for the people who get them and never buy)

## Live Example

> "People who get free lead magnets never buy anything because they're non-buyers." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DTWQyYUDaLr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on all lead magnets: Daniel's ban is on promoting a free lead magnet to cold traffic, and he allows it to warm traffic on a YouTube video because it doesn't cost money.
- Read as a claim that $1 makes buyers: the dollar is his filter — people who pay anything at all are buyers — not a price that creates buying behavior by itself.
