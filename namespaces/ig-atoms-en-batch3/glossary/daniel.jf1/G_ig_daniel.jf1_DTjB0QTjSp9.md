# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DTjB0QTjSp9 — figure it the fuck out ability

## Term

Figure it the fuck out ability is the skill Daniel names for working things out yourself: everybody who has it ends up being successful. The phrase appears twice, beside skill, figuring stuff out yourself, and precise handholding, and his contrast is the person who requires precise handholding for everything — that person, he says, is gonna be a failure for the rest of their life. The skill is framed as something to develop, not a talent you are born with, and the test is whether you can move without step-by-step guidance.
