# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DRvcLIVjVPz — undeniably confident

## Term

Undeniably confident is the selling posture Daniel demands: certainty so total that the buyer feels it in the shake of the pitch. The phrase is used once, beside ability and dude, it works, after a clip spent on listening — three demands to listen, two warnings that stubbornness and not listening are why results never come. His concrete test is the claim itself: tell the person you are correct, that you have done it a hundred times, and let the confidence carry the sale.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Like be undeniably confident in your ability and you're just like shaking this person like, dude, it works." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DRvcLIVjVPz.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as arrogance for its own sake: Daniel ties the confidence to results already delivered and to listening first, not to bluffing without a record.
- Read as a script to repeat word for word: the clip gives an attitude toward the buyer — that you are correct — not a fixed line, and the listening warnings come before the confidence line.
