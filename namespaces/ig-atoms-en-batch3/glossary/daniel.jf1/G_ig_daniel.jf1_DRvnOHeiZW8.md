# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DRvnOHeiZW8 — closing the deals

## Term

Closing the deals is the skill Daniel says most clients simply lack, and the phrase appears twice, beside vast majority, hard time, and 90% plus. His setup is a student on a call, complaining that his clients cannot close and feeling like a fraud when the deals do not land. The rule he draws from it is blunt: 90% of clients will suck, every client you ever sell for will be terrible at sales, and only 10% make 90% of the money and get 90% of the results — so a closer should expect the failure rate, not take it personally.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The vast majority of clients, 90% plus in any industry or niche, no matter what they're selling, are going to have a hard time closing the deals." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DRvnOHeiZW8.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that good closers fix bad clients: Daniel says the clients stay bad at closing, and the seller's job is to expect it rather than to cure it.
- Read as an excuse to stop trying: he says you obviously try your best, and that sometimes it is the client's fault — the point is where the fault sits, not permission to give up.
