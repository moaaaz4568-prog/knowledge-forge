# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DT_kYOjFMcg — day trading

## Term

Day trading is the most commonly taught business opportunity Daniel grades D tier, and the promise he quotes is making money while doing f***ing nothing with your life. The phrase appears five times, beside 99. 99999% failure rate, hedge funds, and D tier, and his math is the same in both tellings: it is wildly and substantially more probable that you lose money than make it, and you are not going to make money with it. His image of the mismatch is little Jimmy in his basement against billion dollar hedge funds with hundreds of millions in advanced tech, and his sting is the same — the rich-from-trading voices make their money selling the course or the coaching on it.

## Synonyms

- day trade (the verb form Daniel uses in his closing warning, Don't day trade)

## Live Example

> "That's the epitome of what day trading promises. What you should know is that there's easily probably a 99. 99999% failure rate." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DT_kYOjFMcg.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that the failure rate is exact: 99. 99999% is Daniel's own figure in this telling, and the clip presents it as his estimate, not a measured statistic.
- Read as a business like any other: he says flatly that it is not a business, and the money in it flows to course sellers, not to the trading itself.
