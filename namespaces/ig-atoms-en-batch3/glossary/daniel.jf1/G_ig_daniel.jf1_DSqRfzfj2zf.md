# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DSqRfzfj2zf — affiliate marketing

## Term

Affiliate marketing is the model Daniel splits into two types: selling courses, and CPA, cost per acquisition, where you buy leads and sell them on. The phrase appears three times, beside CPA, health insurance, and arbitrage, and his CPA example is exact — spend your own ad money, generate leads for a health insurance offer for $50, sell the leads for $110 to the company, and take an arbitrage on these. The only downside he names is that you don't own the offer, which is why he puts it in A tier, with a warning to sift through the bullshit in that world to find good offers.

## Synonyms

- affiliate (the short form Daniel uses when he counts the two types of affiliate)

## Live Example

> "There's like affiliate marketing where you're selling courses, and there's also a kind of affiliate marketing where it's like CPA, cost per acquisition." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DSqRfzfj2zf.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as owning the product: Daniel says the one downside is that you don't own the offer, and the arbitrage comes from moving leads, not from controlling the product.
- Read as free money from ads: the clip says you use your own money for your own ad spend, so the $50-to-$110 spread only exists after you pay to generate the lead.
