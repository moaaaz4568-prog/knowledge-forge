# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DV16IGIk544 — minimum amount

## Term

Minimum amount is Daniel's work rule: work the minimum necessary to achieve the maximum success you can tolerate, not all the time. The phrase is used once, beside completely wrong, necessary, and maximum amount, and his target is the idea that you should just work all the time, which he calls completely wrong. His concrete picture is the alternative — start your own business, make 20, 30, 40, 50 grand a month profit, and work 20 hours a week — offered as something you can just do.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I think you should work the minimum amount that is necessary to achieve the maximum amount of success you can tolerate." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DV16IGIk544.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that 20 hours a week buys 50 grand a month: the figures are Daniel's example of the minimum-amount life, and the clip never shows the path from one to the other.
- Read as an attack on hard work itself: he respects the hermose hustle and says he is just not obsessed with money, so the rule is about how much is enough, not about avoiding work.
