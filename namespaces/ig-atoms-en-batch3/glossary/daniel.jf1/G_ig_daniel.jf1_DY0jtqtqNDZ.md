# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DY0jtqtqNDZ — a buyer is a buyer

## Term

A buyer is a buyer is Daniel's Publix rule: a buyer will buy from somebody else if you don't sell them more, so sell more. The phrase appears twice, beside Gerald Steiners, Publix, and sell more, and his scene is the grocery run — the same Publix every day in Tampa slash St. Pete, ninety eight percent of his groceries there, water, meat, and fruit, and the waters called Gerald Steiners he has to buy from Amazon. His math is the point: if Publix carried the Gerald Steiners, they'd make about two dollars profit per bottle and an additional six hundred dollars a year from him alone, just from him buying them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that buyers never leave: the rule is that they buy from somebody else if you don't sell more, and the clip never says loyalty doesn't exist.
- Read as a pricing lesson: the two dollars and six hundred dollars are Daniel's Publix math, and the clip never says every product has the same margin.
