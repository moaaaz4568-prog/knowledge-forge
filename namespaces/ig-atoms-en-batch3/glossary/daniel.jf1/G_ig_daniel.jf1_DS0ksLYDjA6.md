# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DS0ksLYDjA6 — dropshipping

## Term

Dropshipping is the model Daniel ranks between C and D tier: real money exists in it, but the field is so filled with complete garbage nonsense that he cannot place it higher. The word appears five times, beside complete nonsense, stepping stone, and legitimate e-commerce brand, and the split he draws is between selling garbage Chinese products and sourcing from a legitimate manufacturer with a product of high quality. His concrete case for beginners is the capital test — it suits people who do not have a ton of capital to invest — while the taint of the garbage products is what drags the tier down.

## Synonyms

- dropship (the verb form Daniel uses when he says there are good products you can dropship)

## Live Example

> "I feel like dropshipping is kind of like a stepping stone into running an actual legitimate e-commerce brand." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DS0ksLYDjA6.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a verdict that dropshipping never works: Daniel says people do make money with it and calls it a stepping stone, and the low tier is about the field's reputation, not a claim of zero winners.
- Read as an endorsement of any cheap product: the clip separates the two kinds sharply, and only the legitimate-manufacturer kind with a high-quality product escapes the garbage verdict.
