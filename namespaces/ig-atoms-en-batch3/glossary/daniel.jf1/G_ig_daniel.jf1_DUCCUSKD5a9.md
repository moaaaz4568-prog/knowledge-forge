# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DUCCUSKD5a9 — VSL

## Term

VSL is Daniel's best trick for building a video sales letter: listen to the sales calls first, then let the buyers' own questions write it. The term appears three times, beside best VSL, sales conversation, and descending order, and his method is exact — ten hours at 1.5x speed, a notepad, every question or objection written down, a tally each time one repeats, three times, four times, eight times. His structure follows the tally: the VSL is a headline of what the offer is, then the questions answered in descending order of the frequency with which they were asked, because in the VSL he is already having the sales conversation with the prospect.

## Synonyms

- video sales letter (the full form Daniel gives for VSL when he introduces it)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a script template to copy: the VSL's order comes from tallied questions on real sales calls, and the clip never gives a fixed script — the content is the buyers' own questions.
- Read as a replacement for sales calls: Daniel builds the VSL from listening to sales calls, and the letter works because it holds the conversation the calls already had.
