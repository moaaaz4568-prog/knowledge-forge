# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DUEZRn9jx0v — availability variance

## Term

Availability variance is Daniel's name for keeping every possible call time open: a beginner below 20k or 30k a month should hold the calendar open for 14 hours a day instead of a three-hour block. The phrase is used once, beside highest, calendar, and probability, and the payoff he names is the show-up rate — more times to pick from means a higher chance the prospect actually shows up. His concrete case is the beginner's mistake itself: limiting calls to one narrow window and then wondering why nobody books.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "keep your calendar open for like 14 hours a day. If you have the highest availability variance possible with all sorts of times that somebody can pick, it increases the probability that they will actually show up to the call" — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DUEZRn9jx0v.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a scheduling tip for busy closers: the clip aims it at beginners under 20k or 30k a month, and the 14-hour day is the prescription, not a general calendar rule.
- Read as a claim that more hours always means more sales: Daniel ties the open calendar to the probability of showing up, and the clip never says the extra hours themselves close deals.
