# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DSoThAlDfI0 — single family house

## Term

Single family house is the investment Daniel calls stupid: put 80 grand down, charge rent 300 above the mortgage, and one broken AC wipes out two years of profit. The phrase is used once, beside stupid investment, rent, and mortgage, and the bet underneath is the whole problem — the entire success is predicated upon the value going up so you can refinance with more debt, while the house makes no actual cash flow. His stress test is the downside people forget to risk mitigate: what happens if it goes down, the AC goes out again and again, and you are losing money for four, five, six years on a house you just bought.

## Live Example

> "Buying like a single family house is like just a stupid investment." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DSoThAlDfI0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that houses never gain value: Daniel says the value does not always go up, and the failure he describes is losing money for years while waiting on appreciation.
- Read as advice against all property: the clip attacks one structure — a house bought for appreciation with thin rent over the mortgage — not property bought for cash flow.
