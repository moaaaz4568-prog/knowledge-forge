# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DWaR3UJAUJ- — performance deal

## Term

Performance deal is the easier sale Daniel contrasts with the retainer: dudes want a retainer so bad and it completely screws them, while a performance deal lets you sign a bunch of clients and get a bunch of case studies. The phrase is used once, beside retainer, case studies, and guarantee, and his sequence is the ladder — sell the performance deal, sign clients, collect case studies, and then you are able to go to a retainer, and with enough case studies you don't need a guarantee anymore. His warning closes the loop: you don't have to work on performance space anymore when you have enough case studies, but again, you don't have case studies.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that retainers are always bad: Daniel says the retainer screws beginners without case studies, and the clip never says a retainer is wrong once you have them.
- Read as a promise that performance deals close themselves: the deal is easier to sell, and the clip never says the work itself is easy.
