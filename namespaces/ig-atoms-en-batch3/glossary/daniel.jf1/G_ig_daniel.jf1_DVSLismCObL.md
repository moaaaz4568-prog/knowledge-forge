# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DVSLismCObL — coaching and consulting

## Term

Coaching and consulting is the most efficient use of money Daniel names for anyone below $100,000 per month: pay someone who already did it to tell you how. The phrase is used once, beside efficient, masterminds, and quadruple, and his own proof is the two masterminds he is actively in — one $60,000 a year, the other $45,000 — run by guys who make more than quadruple what he makes. His rule scales down cleanly: making 5k a month and wanting 30k, just pay someone who already went past 30k a month to tell you how, because it is hilarious that you can just do that.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The most efficient use of money when you're below $100, 000 per month is coaching and consulting." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DVSLismCObL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that coaching always pays: Daniel's own masterminds are his evidence, and the clip never prices what the coaching returns.
- Read as advice to buy any course: his rule is to pay someone who already did the specific thing, and the clip never says every coach qualifies.
