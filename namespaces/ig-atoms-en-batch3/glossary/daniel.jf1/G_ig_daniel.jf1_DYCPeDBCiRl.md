# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DYCPeDBCiRl — parasocial relationship

## Term

Parasocial relationship is the thing Daniel says you are trying to build: the only way someone will purchase something that is ten thousand eight hundred dollars is if they know, like, and trust you. The phrase appears twice, beside indebted, extreme amount of value, and they love you, and his method is the debt — give such an extreme amount of value that the viewer feels indebted to you. His scene is the stranger's greeting: someone walks up, says oh my God, I've seen your videos, I like them so much, and you have no idea who they are, because they love you and you don't know they exist.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Is to give you such an extreme amount of value that you feel indebted to me. Build a parasocial relationship." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DYCPeDBCiRl.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a clinical diagnosis: parasocial relationship is Daniel's working label for the fan's feeling, not a diagnostic category.
- Read as a trick to manufacture love: the value is given to build trust, and the clip never says the feeling itself is faked.
