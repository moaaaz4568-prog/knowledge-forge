# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DWSjatBD2NG — direct ROI

## Term

Direct ROI is the first of Daniel's two kinds of return: the client pays because they believe hiring you makes them more money in return, directly or indirectly. The phrase appears twice, once on its own and once inside indirect ROI, beside quantifiable return, cold email campaign, and 20 hours a month, and his direct case is exact — running ads or a cold email campaign that delivers a quantifiable return, so the client can ask, if I pay $5,000, can I make $10,000 or $20,000 back? His indirect case is the time saved: a ton of work for the client that saves an extra 20 hours a month, hours the client can use to make more money.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Whether that's a direct ROI, so say you're running ads for the clients or running a cold email campaign and you can deliver a quantifiable return, or an indirect ROI, where you're doing a ton of work for the client, which will save him an extra 20 hours a month, and you can use those hours to make more money." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DWSjatBD2NG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that clients only pay for direct returns: Daniel lists the indirect ROI beside the direct one, and the clip never ranks them.
- Read as a formula for pricing: the $5,000 and $10,000 figures are the client's own test, and the clip never sets the seller's price.
