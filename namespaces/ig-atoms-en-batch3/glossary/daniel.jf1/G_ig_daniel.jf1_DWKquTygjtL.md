# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DWKquTygjtL — secret club

## Term

Secret club is the belief Daniel says blocked his early growth: that having a successful business or being wealthy or having freedom was like a club you had to be invited to. The phrase is used once, beside invited, walk into, and ordained, and his correction is the walk-in — you do not have to be invited, you can just walk into the club, make friends, and gradually be allowed inside. His assumption to operate under is ordination: act as if you are ordained to have this, gifted the power, knowledge, and capability to have precisely what you are looking for, because you are not shut out and the door is not locked.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "That having a successful business or being wealthy or having something that would allow you freedom was kind of like a secret club you had to be invited to the club." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DWKquTygjtL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that wealth has no gatekeepers: the club is Daniel's image for the belief, and the clip never says every room opens to anyone.
- Read as magic thinking: ordained to have this is his operating assumption, and the clip pairs it with walking in and making friends, not with wishing alone.
