# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DUG-EbUjXhX — doesn't scale

## Term

Doesn't scale is the objection Daniel hears from people who make no money: they refuse a working client-getting move because it will not scale. The phrase appears twice, beside short form content agency, extract a clip, and made that shit up, and his answer is the move itself — pick a hundred people you would love to work with, extract a clip from their long-form YouTube, and send it to them. His concrete proof is his own video from a long time back, where he gave exactly those steps and got the same objection in the comments.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Here's how to get clients for a short form content agency. Go pick a hundred people who you'd love to work with who probably make long form on YouTube. Go extract a clip from it and like send them the clip, and that's it." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DUG-EbUjXhX.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to ignore scale forever: Daniel attacks the objection from people with no money, and the clip never says a real business should avoid scaling.
- Read as a claim that the clip-sending method works for everyone: his example is a short form content agency finding clients, and the clip never promises the same steps fit another service.
