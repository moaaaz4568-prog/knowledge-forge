# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DSlH79Cj297 — lower prices

## Term

Lower prices is the misconception Daniel calls the biggest in business: the belief that cheaper products bring easier clients. The phrase is used once, beside biggest misconception and more clients, and the fantasy he sketches is a seller who imagines himself as a venture capital funded business that can afford to acquire customers at any cost and beat everybody else on price. His correction is personal — you are not a VC backed company, you are some dude who wants to make 50k per month in profit and not have a boss — and the instruction is to act like it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You think lower prices means more clients. This is the biggest misconception in business." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DSlH79Cj297.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on ever discounting: Daniel attacks the belief that lower prices mean more clients, not a single price cut, and the clip never sets a correct price.
- Read as advice to charge the most: the target figure is 50k a month in profit without a boss, and the clip says nothing about what the right price actually is.
