# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DTbTfjkAuT4 — white label

## Term

White label is the model Daniel grades D tier: take an AI voice agent or Go High Level, sell it to a business, rent it to a business, and collect monthly fees. The phrase appears five times, three times as white label and twice as white labeling, beside Go High Level, AI voice agent, and pyramid scheme, and his test is the payer — no one is paying you 2K or 3K a month to white label your AI tool. His explanation for the money that does exist is the ladder itself: the people who make a lot white labeling Go High Level sell a business opportunity course teaching others to do it, who sign up under them and pay another layer of affiliate commission.

## Synonyms

- white labeling (the -ing form Daniel uses for the activity of selling Go High Level under your own name)

## Live Example

> "What you need to understand about that is no human on the planet makes a lot of money just white labeling Go High Level." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DTbTfjkAuT4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a verdict on all AI tools: Daniel's target is selling AI tools by white label, and the clip never says building AI tools for your own use fails.
- Read as a claim that Go High Level itself is worthless: the clip attacks white labeling it as a business model, and the pyramid charge is about the course-selling ladder, not the software.
