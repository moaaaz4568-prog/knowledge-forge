# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DTvzBDrjL06 — offer times traffic equals sales

## Term

Offer times traffic equals sales is the one equation Daniel says you need to understand: what you sell, times the people made aware of it, equals sales. The phrase is used once, beside equation, get traffic, and make people aware, and his gloss is the whole method — get traffic to the offer, make people aware of your existence, and by doing that you will by nature result in sales. His image is a seed in soil with sunlight and water growing a plant, and the law behind it is the same: make enough people aware of the existence of your offer and some percentage will trade their dollars for it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a literal multiplication: the equation is Daniel's frame for the business, and the clip never assigns numbers to offer or traffic — the percentage who buy is left open.
- Read as a claim that any offer sells: the equation needs an offer people will trade dollars for, and the clip's simplicity is about the mechanism, not about the offer's quality.
