# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DSx_2zGjQ1j — selling a course

## Term

Selling a course is the business Daniel defends against the claim that all course sellers are a scam. The phrase appears twice, beside dispelled, demand, and S tier, and his defense is the premise he rejects: that selling a course is like putting a gun to a buyer's head, when in fact there is demand and people buy because they want to buy. His condition for the S tier verdict is the legitimate course business — not selling nonsense, actually having delivery, and caring about getting people results.

## Synonyms

- sell a course (the verb form Daniel uses when he asks why you sell a course)

## Live Example

> "People buy courses because they want to buy courses. People buy coaching because they want to be coached." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DSx_2zGjQ1j.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a defense of every course seller: Daniel's S tier is conditional on a legitimate course business with real delivery and care for results, not a blanket approval.
- Read as proof that demand makes anything ethical: demand is his answer to the scam charge, and the clip still requires delivery and results, not just buyers.
