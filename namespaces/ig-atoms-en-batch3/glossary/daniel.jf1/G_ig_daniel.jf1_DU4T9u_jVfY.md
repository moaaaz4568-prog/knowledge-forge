# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DU4T9u_jVfY — cold traffic offer

## Term

Cold traffic offer is Daniel's door-opener: the thing you use to get cold traffic in the door, before you ever sell them what they actually need. The phrase is used once, beside sell them what they want, Ozempic, and know, like and trust, and his rule is the want-need order — try to sell them something they need instead of something they want, and you simply will not get any responses or calls booked, literally ever. His picture is the gym against Ozempic: everybody needs to go to the gym and eat healthy food, but they want Ozempic, which is why broccoli farmers aren't billionaires and the creators of Ozempic are.

## Live Example

> "You get cold traffic in the door with a cold traffic offer, and then later on sell them what they actually need." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DU4T9u_jVfY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to mislead buyers: the cold traffic offer is the wanted thing up front, and the needed thing is sold after they are already a customer who knows, likes and trusts you — the clip never says to hide the need.
- Read as a claim that needs never sell: needs sell after the door is open and trust is built, and the clip's rule is about order, not about needs being unsellable.
