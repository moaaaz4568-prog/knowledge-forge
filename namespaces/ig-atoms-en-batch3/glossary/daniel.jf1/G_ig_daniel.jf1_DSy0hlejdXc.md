# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DSy0hlejdXc — ask for permission

## Term

Ask for permission is the life Daniel refuses: a normal corporate job where being caged in means asking if it's okay to go to the bathroom and putting in a request for time off. The phrase is used once, beside caged in, request for time off, and permission, and his bargain is about efficiency — if he can do a job way more efficiently than somebody else, he wants to get paid way more, and if he finishes before a deadline, he wants to go enjoy his life without asking. The line he draws is not about getting as rich as humanly possible; it is about never needing permission from another human to do what he wants to do.

## Synonyms

- permission (the bare noun Daniel uses when he says he never wants to need it from another human)

## Live Example

> "I want to never in my life need to feel like I need permission from another human to do what I want to do." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DSy0hlejdXc.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a refusal to work at all: Daniel wants pay for efficient work and freedom after the job is done, not freedom from work itself.
- Read as a claim that permission is always wrong: the clip attacks needing permission to live your life, and it never says workplaces should have no rules at all.
