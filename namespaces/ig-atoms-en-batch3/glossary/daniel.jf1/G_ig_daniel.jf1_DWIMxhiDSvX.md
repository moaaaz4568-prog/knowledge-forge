# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DWIMxhiDSvX — single family house

## Term

Buying a single family house is the deal Daniel walks through to show why it fails: 80 grand down in some house, rent 300 above the mortgage, and then an AC goes out and you just lost two years of profit. The phrase is used once, beside banking on, predicated, and risk mitigate, and his point is the missing cash flow — the whole success is predicated upon the value going up, and the plan is to refinance it with more debt, while the house itself makes nothing. His stress test is the downside nobody prices: it doesn't always go up, people forget to risk mitigate, and the AC going out again and again leaves you losing money for four, five, six years on a house you just bought.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that rent never covers a mortgage: the 300-above-mortgage rent is Daniel's example, and the failure he names is the repair bill eating the margin.
- Read as a ban on buying houses: his target is buying for appreciation with no cash flow, and the clip never says a cash-flowing house fails the same way.
