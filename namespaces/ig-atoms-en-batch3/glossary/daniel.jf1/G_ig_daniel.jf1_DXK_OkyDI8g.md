# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DXK_OkyDI8g — economics

## Term

Economics is the filter Daniel says to choose clients by first, before figuring out how to serve them. The word appears twice, beside choose clients, expensive high profit products, and target, and his rule is blunt — stop working with clients who have the economics, no matter how much you like their product or think you can take the challenge or how nice they seem. His target is the client who sells expensive, high profit products or services, because business is about making money and you can't make money helping clients who can't make money.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You need to choose clients based on economics first, then figure out how to serve them." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DXK_OkyDI8g.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that only rich clients matter: Daniel's rule is about the client's economics, and the clip never says small clients have nothing to teach.
- Read as advice to drop every current client: stop working with clients who have the economics is his instruction for choosing, and the clip never says to fire anyone mid-contract.
