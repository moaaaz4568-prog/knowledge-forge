# G_ig_daniel.jf1_DXVLhIyDdZa — quiet quitting

## Term

Quiet quitting is the statistic Daniel opens with: 59% of the workforce is quiet quitting, spending their life on work they do not enjoy. The phrase is used once, beside 59%, 40 hours a week, and time freedom, and his contrast is the easy alternative — go sell a service to a business and have time freedom, location freedom, no boss, and do whatever you want while actually enjoying your life. His how is the tutorial path: watch YouTube tutorial videos on selling a service to businesses, learn to fulfill it with AI, and make your own company.
