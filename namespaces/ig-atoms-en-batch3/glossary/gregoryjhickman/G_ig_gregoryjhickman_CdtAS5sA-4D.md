# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CdtAS5sA-4D — Sequence is success

## Term

Sequence is success is his rule that the order of the work matters as much as the work itself. It appears once in this 143-word source, beside do not solve the right problems in the wrong order, punching above your weight, and the fundamentals. The clip's example is a business at 50k a month mimicking the methods of someone doing 1.5 million a month, when it has not mastered the fundamentals at its own level. You have to stop trying to do stuff above your weight, get the basics in place, and only then move to the next level of the roadmap.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Sequence is success. Do not solve the right problems in the wrong order because you'll stay stuck." — sources/S_ig_gregoryjhickman_CdtAS5sA-4D.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as moving slowly: sequence is about doing the right things at the right level, and the clip also says to start the next level once the basics are done.
- Read as copying successful people: mimicking someone at 1.5 million a month while you are at 50k is exactly the mistake the rule forbids.
