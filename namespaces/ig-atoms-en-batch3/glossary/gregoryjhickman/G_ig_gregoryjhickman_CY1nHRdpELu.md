# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CY1nHRdpELu — diagnostic conversation

## Term

A diagnostic conversation is the information-gathering talk that lets the speaker pitch his own vehicle as the way to where the client wants to be. It appears once in this 101-word source, beside where they want to be, my vehicle, and win-win. The more he knows about where they want to be, the better he can make the pitch for them to get there through his vehicle — and if they can see that, it is a win-win. The offer itself becomes more appealing because it is framed as their path, chosen over whatever path they had imagined.
