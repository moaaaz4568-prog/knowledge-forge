# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_C_s1VTUtTwR — one hundred dollar workshop

## Term

A one hundred dollar workshop is the low-friction front end that sells the bigger programs behind it. The phrase appears once in this 206-word source, beside thirty two people, five thousand, and fifteen thousand. The numbers are the story: the first workshop sold thirty two people at one hundred bucks, and seven enrolled in the five thousand program, generating thirty five thousand; the second sold twenty seven, with five buying the five thousand program and one paying fifteen thousand in full. The same workshop was created once, delivered twice with a few tweaks, and produced very similar results.
