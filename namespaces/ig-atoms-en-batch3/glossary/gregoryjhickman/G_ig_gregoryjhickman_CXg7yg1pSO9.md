# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CXg7yg1pSO9 — flatline

## Term

To flatline is to stall in the hamster wheel: adding clients, churning a couple, and going nowhere before starting to dip. It appears twice in this 139-word source, beside hamster wheel, churn, and dip. The clip ties the stall to the business being entirely based on the founder — jumping into MRR too soon or repeating the mistakes that keep him stuck — and warns that the flatline lasts only so long before the dip begins. The choice it offers is stark: dip, or commit to scale by doing the things growth actually requires.

> "And the reality is that when you're in this hamster wheel and you're struggling, whether it's trying to add more clients, but then you churn out a couple, or the business is you, it's based on you, you're only going to flatline for so long before you start to dip." — sources/S_ig_gregoryjhickman_CXg7yg1pSO9.md

- Read as a plateau to wait out: flatlining here is the warning stage before the dip, caused by adding clients and churning them in turn.
