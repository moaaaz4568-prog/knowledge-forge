# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CV0U2NkAJ8q — scope creep

## Term

Scope creep is the unpaid extra work a founder keeps adding for early clients because he is afraid of losing the next one. It appears once in this 146-word source, beside custom projects, selling into retainers, and early clients. The clip ties it to the founder's own fear: he lets the scope grow because he does not know where the next client is coming from, so he starts doing more and more things. That habit is exactly what the clip says has to die if he truly wants to scale.
