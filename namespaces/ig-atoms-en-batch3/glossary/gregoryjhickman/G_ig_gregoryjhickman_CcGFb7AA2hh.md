# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CcGFb7AA2hh — self-inflicted

## Term

Self-inflicted is his label for the problems a business creates for itself by acting too soon. It appears once in this 110-word source, beside solving problems that don't even exist yet, operational drag, and change direction at the drop of a dime. The clip's point is that solving problems that do not exist yet creates friction, drag, and a loss of trust from the team, and that changing direction constantly becomes immature once the business matures and a team is helping. Most of these problems come from not giving something enough time to play out and see whether there was a problem at all.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an excuse to never act: the clip targets acting before letting something play out long enough, not acting itself.
- Read as a team problem: the self-inflicted problems are created by the founder's own early moves, and they cost the team its trust.
