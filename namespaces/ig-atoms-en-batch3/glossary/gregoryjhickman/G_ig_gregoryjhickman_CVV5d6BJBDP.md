# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CVV5d6BJBDP — accidental agency

## Term

The accidental agency is his label for a business that became an agency without ever deciding to: a couple of clients, then more help, then suddenly a team. It appears once in this 158-word source, beside early-stage service providers, ideal client, and the promised land. The clip uses it to name the trap underneath — falling in love with your solution, the technical skill you have, instead of the problem your ideal client has. His own correction was to fall in love with the ideal client and find the happy middle between what he was good at and what they needed.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "And I see that happen a lot with early-stage service providers, small agency owners, you know, as I call them, the accidental agency." — sources/S_ig_gregoryjhickman_CVV5d6BJBDP.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an insult: the label describes how the agency formed — a few clients, then a team — not the quality of the work.
- Read as a business model: it names the trap of falling in love with your solution instead of your client's problem.
