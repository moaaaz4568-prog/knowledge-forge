# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_C_N1i2hKjT5 — total addressable market

## Term

Total addressable market is the third test for whether a service can scale into a product: enough people with the problem to grow a business around. It appears once in this 170-word source, beside teachable, valuable, and repeatable. The three criteria are teachable to others — the team and possibly clients — valuable enough that people will spend money on it, and repeatable for a large enough audience. The clip speaks to creative service professionals who say they cannot productize without becoming a commodity, and sends them to Built to Sell, the first book to break down productization for a creative service company.
