# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_C_8OrR5NRfd — ongoing continuity

## Term

Ongoing continuity is the phase after the upfront term ends, when the client keeps rolling month to month — and its name sets the expectation of staying. It appears once in this 136-word source, beside expectations, roll into, and cancel at any time. The lesson is that names create expectations: a name that sounds like a new sale makes clients leave even though they did not really want to. The fix is to sell the continuity upfront, be clear about how it works, and check in along the way to see where the client is at.
