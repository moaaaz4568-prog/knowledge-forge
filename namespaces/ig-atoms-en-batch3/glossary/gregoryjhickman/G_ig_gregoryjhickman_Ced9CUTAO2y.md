# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Ced9CUTAO2y — continuity agreement

## Term

A continuity agreement is an ongoing service where clients keep paying for your advisory and your thinking. It appears once in this 117-word source, beside one core service, fifty to sixty k a month, and one hundred thousand dollar a month mark. The clip's math is that you can get to seven figures selling just one thing: a ten thousand dollar service with ten clients a month. The recommendation is one core service that gets you to at least fifty to sixty k a month, plus a continuity agreement that helps you cross the one hundred thousand dollar a month mark.
