# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CViVUCega6u — ideal client

## Term

An ideal client is a specific kind of buyer with a specific problem you can serve again and again — and without one, growth stalls. It appears once in this 172-word source, beside too broad of an audience, seven figures, and serve no one. The clip's mistake number one is serving agency owners, dentists, chiropractors and solar companies at once: taking anyone with any budget means there is no ideal client at all. That breadth is fine at the start, he says, but it slowly becomes the reason the business cannot grow, because it touches every part of the business.
