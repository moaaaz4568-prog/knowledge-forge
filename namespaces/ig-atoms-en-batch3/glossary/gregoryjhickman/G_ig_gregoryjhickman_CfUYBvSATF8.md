# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CfUYBvSATF8 — in alignment

## Term

In alignment is the state where what the business demands of you and what you demand of the business match. It appears once in this 120-word source, beside what actually has to be true, demands of you as the owner, and designed it based off of someone else's goal. The clip asks what has to be true for the business to be worth it, and what the business needs to give you, because the two sides are often not in alignment. If you designed the business from someone else's goal or vision, it will demand things of you that you might not want to do, and that is where the resistance comes from.
