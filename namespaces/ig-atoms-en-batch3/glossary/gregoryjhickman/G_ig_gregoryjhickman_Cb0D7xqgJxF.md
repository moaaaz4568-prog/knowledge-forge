# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Cb0D7xqgJxF — pre-call homework

## Term

Pre-call homework is a small course or piece of work a client completes before the kickoff call so they arrive prepared and in momentum. It appears once in this 167-word source, beside mini course, what success looks like, and freestyled the conversation. The clip's problem is the gap between the client paying and the work starting, when clients show up unprepared and he shows up with no plan, so the homework uses content to get the client onto the same page. The payoff is a meaningful, productive onboarding call that moves straight into execution.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as busywork for the client: the homework introduces how you work and what success looks like so the kickoff call can be productive.
- Read as a replacement for the onboarding call: it prepares the client for the call, which is where the meaningful conversation still happens.
