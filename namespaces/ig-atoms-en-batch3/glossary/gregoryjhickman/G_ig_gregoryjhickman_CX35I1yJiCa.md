# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CX35I1yJiCa — modern day media company

## Term

A modern day media company is what a service provider becomes by adding media and software to their skill, instead of only selling done-for-you work. It appears once in this 156-word source, beside media meets software, attention, and authority. The clip's logic splits the two additions: media is what gives attention, and software is possible once a problem is isolated well enough to hold real expertise. Serving those problems through software and service then builds authority, and the exposure from media brings back insights about the people served.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as becoming a publisher: the company still sells services; media is added to win attention and software to serve problems at scale.
- Read as a rebrand: the shift is structural — media plus software plus service builds authority, not a new logo.
