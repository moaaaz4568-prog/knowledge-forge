# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CY7iqxEpfwL — profitable acquisition service

## Term

A profitable acquisition service is a low-priced offer that acquires clients at a profit instead of costing money to find them. It appears once in this 112-word source, beside webinar, five hundred bucks, and acquire clients. The clip's example is an agency that stops merely acquiring leads and starts acquiring clients: it creates a webinar with the client, sells it for one or two hundred bucks, sells thousands of them, and a small percentage upgrade into higher-level work. That is what makes the service profitable — and powerful once it is tied to revenue.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as selling webinars: the service is acquiring clients cheaply — a webinar sold for a few hundred that brings thousands of buyers — not the webinar itself.
- Read as a lead-gen trick: the differentiator is understanding why someone wants it, which is what lets a small percentage upgrade to higher-level work.
