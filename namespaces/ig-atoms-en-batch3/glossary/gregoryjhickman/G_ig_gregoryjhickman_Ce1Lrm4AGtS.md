# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Ce1Lrm4AGtS — invite complexity

## Term

Invite complexity is what business owners do when they say yes to work outside what they are best at. It appears once in this 99-word source, beside of course we can help with that, a little bit outside the normal type of client, and keep it as simple as possible. The clip's examples are agreeing to help with something you do not normally deliver and taking on a client outside the type you have had success with. These little variables creep in and make the business more complex than it has to be, so the rule is to keep it as simple as possible.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a warning against growth: the complexity comes from saying yes outside your best work, not from taking on more of the right clients.
- Read as a one-time mistake: the variables creep in little by little, and each yes adds complexity the business does not need.
