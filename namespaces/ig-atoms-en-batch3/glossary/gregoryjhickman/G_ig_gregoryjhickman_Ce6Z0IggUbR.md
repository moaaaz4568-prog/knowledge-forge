# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Ce6Z0IggUbR — three tiers

## Term

Three tiers is his name for Elon Musk's master plan structure, which he applies to services: low volume, medium volume, and high volume. It appears once in this 181-word source, beside master plan, low volume car, and Model Three. Musk's plan was to create an expensive low volume car, use that money for a medium volume car, then use that money for an affordable high volume car. Services follow the same ladder: custom low volume work, then done with you productized consulting at medium volume, then DIY courses and books at high volume, giving three revenue streams.
