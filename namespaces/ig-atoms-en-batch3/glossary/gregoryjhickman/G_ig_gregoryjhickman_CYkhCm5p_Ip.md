# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CYkhCm5p_Ip — guarantee

## Term

A guarantee is the offer itself here: instead of selling posts, the copywriter sells a promised number of clients. The word appears twice in this 143-word source, once inside guaranteeing and once as guarantee, beside copywriter, 10 grand a month, and two clients. The reframe runs like this: four Facebook posts a week plus emails to those posts each month, guaranteeing at least two clients — and if he gets only one client, he will fire himself. That is how the copywriter stops being someone who writes four organic posts a month and becomes worth 10 grand a month.

## Synonyms

- guaranteeing (the verb form, used once in the source)

## Live Example

> "I'm guaranteeing you, if I'm doing X many posts in your Facebook group and the emails to said posts each and every month, that you'll at least get two clients." — sources/S_ig_gregoryjhickman_CYkhCm5p_Ip.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a boast: the guarantee is the offer — four posts a week reframed as at least two clients a month, with the copywriter offering to fire himself if he lands only one.
- Read as competing on price: the reframe lets him charge 10 grand a month for work others sell as four organic posts, because the buyer is paying for clients, not posts.
