# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Cbc3bxhAZSw — sell it raw and unpolished

## Term

Sell it raw and unpolished is his advice to sell the signature service before it is perfected, so delivery can still bend. It appears once in this 177-word source, beside fall in love with their problem, written in stone, and wiggle room inside of your delivery. The clip's point is that no decision inside the process is final: you can deliver the service 10 times, refine it, and take it to a different market if you hate the people you are serving now. Selling raw leaves room to pivot when a client needs something completely different, and to watch whether others need that too.
