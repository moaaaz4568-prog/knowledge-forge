# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Cazw9zzg8La — diagnostic piece

## Term

The diagnostic piece is the part of the sales process where you learn the prospect's problems, opportunities, needs, and goals before pitching anything. It appears once in this 169-word source, beside signature service, beautiful icons, and connect the gap. The clip warns against leading with a designed signature service, which is only a tool inside sales and fulfillment, and says to have as many conversations as possible focused on the diagnosis first. Only when those findings tie to the signature service do you connect the gap and show how the solution works.
