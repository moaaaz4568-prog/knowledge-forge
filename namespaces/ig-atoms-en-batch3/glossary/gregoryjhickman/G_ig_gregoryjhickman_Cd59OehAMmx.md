# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Cd59OehAMmx — industry math

## Term

Industry math is the collective baseline you borrow from the industry when you do not know your own numbers yet. It appears once in this 74-word source, beside how many customers do I need a month, hit this said revenue goal, and your own baseline. The clip's rule is to define how many customers a month you need to hit a revenue goal, and if you do not know your own baseline, talk to enough people and use the industry math as a target.
