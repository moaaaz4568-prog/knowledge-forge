# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CdgGA4TAZat — why frame

## Term

The why frame is the first of four frames in his format model, and the one that moves prospects from problem focused to solution focused. It appears 2 times in this 135-word source, beside the what frame, the how frame, and the now frame. The four frames run in order: why takes people from problem to solution, what takes them from feeling stuck to feeling ready and open minded, how shows an old way and a new way, and now takes them from no plan to a clear action plan. The model matters in both marketing content and delivery content, and the why frame is majorly important in both.
