# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CWlZ9NDACpK — cross sell

## Term

A cross sell is an additional offering sold to a client you already serve, so each client brings in more revenue. It appears once in this 188-word source, beside 90 day program, 12 month program, and affiliate products. The clip's model starts from a core service dialed in: sell once on the 90-day front end, and a percentage continue into the 12-month experience, while cross sells and affiliate products tied to the service add further revenue. The software parallel is HubSpot adding services and features at different pricing tiers to expand what the same customer pays.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as selling more of the same: a cross sell here adds a different offering — a program, a product, an affiliate tie — to the same client.
- Read as a one-time upsell: the example is structural, a 90-day front end feeding a 12-month back end, not a single pitch.
