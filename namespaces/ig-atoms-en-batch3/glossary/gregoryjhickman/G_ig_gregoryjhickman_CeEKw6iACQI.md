# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CeEKw6iACQI — flex up

## Term

Flex up is his test for whether call volume is strong enough to hire: stretch your own schedule and take more calls. It appears once in this 151-word source, beside 10 sales calls on the calendar a week, 10 already booked into the next week, and go into sales mode. The clip's hiring signal is consistently getting at least 10 sales calls a week, with at least 10 already booked into the next week. To test whether that was sustainable, he flexed up his own schedule and opened space to take 20 sales calls a week before bringing someone on.
