# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CVdK4dyAZ66 — perfect work week

## Term

The perfect work week is an exercise for redesigning the calendar so more of it goes to growing the business and less to fulfillment. It appears once in this 179-word source, beside calendar, bank account, and reorganize and restructure. The clip opens with a test of priorities — show me your calendar and your bank account — then gives the exercise as step one: decrease time in fulfillment, a routine he still does at least four times a year. The promised payoff is concrete: five to 15 hours of the week back to work on the business in a normal 40 to 50 hour week.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fixed schedule: the exercise is a recurring redesign of the calendar, done at least four times a year, not a one-time template.
- Read as time management advice: its purpose here is freeing five to fifteen hours a week to grow the business, not just to feel organized.
