# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_C_WQkhcJyN6 — inside the bottle

## Term

Inside the bottle is the image for losing the outside perspective a client hired you for, by getting too embedded in their business. It appears once in this 228-word source, beside retainers, MRR, expertise, and employee. The clip's warning is that most retainers and MRR services pull the provider closer into the client's business, stripping the expertise that got them hired — you were paid because you know something the client does not. The closer you get to being inside their business, the closer you come to being treated like an employee, until you cannot see the label from inside the bottle.
