# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CaaB3EpA-r5 — Motrin migraine

## Term

Motrin migraine is his example of a specialized product that charges more than the generic version for what are apparently the same ingredients. It appears once in this 175-word source, beside ibuprofen, regular Motrin, and 30% more expensive. The clip's rule is that as your audience decreases and the solution gets more specific and catered to one person, your price can go up, because the person who comes in saying they have a migraine will likely go to the specialized solution. The price difference is earned by specialization, not by a different formula.

- Read as a claim about the medicine: the clip says the ingredients are apparently the same and the price gap comes from specialization, not from a different drug.
