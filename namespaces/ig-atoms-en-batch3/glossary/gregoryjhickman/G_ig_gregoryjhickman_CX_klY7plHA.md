# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CX_klY7plHA — rock star

## Term

A rock star is the founder's word for a potential employee whose future he can see before they can — someone with a bigger vision than they yet believe. It appears once in this 184-word source, beside vision, full-time, and ultimate goals. The clip's leadership problem is that the person may not want the same thing: he may need them full-time while they never want to be full-time, or they may want only part of the vision. The decision then is practical and personal: whether he is comfortable using them in another fashion, and whether the opportunity to work with him aligns with their ultimate goals.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I might have this vision for this potential employee because they're a rock star and they just don't see it yet, and/or they don't want the same thing, but they want a part of that vision." — sources/S_ig_gregoryjhickman_CX_klY7plHA.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as praise for current performance: rock star here is the founder's vision of what the person could become, which the person may not see or want yet.
- Read as a hiring filter: the clip uses it to decide how to use someone — full-time or another fashion — not to rank resumes.
