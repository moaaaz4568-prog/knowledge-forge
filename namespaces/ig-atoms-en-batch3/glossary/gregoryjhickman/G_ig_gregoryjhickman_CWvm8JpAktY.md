# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CWvm8JpAktY — done with you

## Term

Done with you is a delivery model where the client implements the work themselves with your plan, tools and help, instead of you doing it for them. It appears once in this 176-word source, beside plan, tools, and problem to solution. The clip breaks the model into three core things: a plan of steps inside the process that repeatedly gets clients results, the tools the client needs in place to implement, and the help to get through it. The test question is concrete: what plan would you give them, what tools will it need, and how will you help them through the process.
