# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CXL8nIUATEe — capacity

## Term

Capacity is the founder's own ceiling as an entrepreneur — the skills, adaptability and help they can bring — and the business never exceeds it. It appears 4 times in this 148-word source, beside adapt, change, acquire new skills, and get help. The clip repeats the line from a former mentor almost daily: your business will never exceed your own capacity, so building capacity means adapting, changing, acquiring new skills, getting help, committing and taking action. The stakes are communal: the team, community, family and friends will not rise above what the founder's capacity currently is.
