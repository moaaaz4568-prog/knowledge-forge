# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CVLP8XqAEv5 — deliverables

## Term

Deliverables are the working systems a program drops into a client's business, not files handed over. The plural appears once in this 184-word source, beside sales automation, systems, and lead acquisition, with the singular deliverable appearing once more. The clip's program installs systems that operate seven figure businesses from lead acquisition and sales process, and stresses that having the system in place is not the value — the value starts when leads run through it and the client adopts it. Training and a framework then show how to fill the pipeline and package the work so it can be charged for.
