# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CV6SLwqJhqT — fixed ceiling

## Term

A fixed ceiling is the hard cap on how many clients one person can serve when every job runs through them. It appears once in this 137-word source, beside assemble a team, sales, marketing, fulfillment, and a 40 hour work week. The clip makes the ceiling a matter of the founder's own hours: without a team, everything is tied to him inside the business, which also caps what he can make and makes vacations harder. The point is that keeping control by doing it all is what sets the limit.
