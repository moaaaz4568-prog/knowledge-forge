# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CfZOvNTgSUt — twenty to thirty years

## Term

Twenty to thirty years is the horizon he asks you to test a business against: could you live with it and run it that long. It appears 2 times in this 125-word source, beside what does it need to do for me, can you do this business, and what you want your role to be. The clip's test is to ask what the business needs to do for you at a minimum, so you can say you are good if it hits those minimums and ride it for twenty to thirty years. That clarity then decides your role and which other people you need to hire for the things that are not your role.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a retirement plan: twenty to thirty years is the test for whether the business is worth building, not a promise to stay that long.
- Read as doing everything yourself: the test ends by defining your role and hiring other people for the things that are not your role.
