Selling your byproduct is turning the reusable method inside a done-for-you engagement into tools for the done-with-you backend. It appears once in this 155-word source, beside done for you delivery, templatized, codified, and work hard once. The clip's setup is to take one to two clients a month in done-for-you delivery as a means to an end: the steps used to get them results can be templatized, codified, broken down and repurposed.
