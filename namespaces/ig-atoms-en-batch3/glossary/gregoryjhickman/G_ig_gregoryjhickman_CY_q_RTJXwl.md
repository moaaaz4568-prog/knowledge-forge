# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CY_q_RTJXwl — acquisition of media outlets

## Term

Acquisition of media outlets means buying existing audiences and the businesses behind them, then monetizing them many ways. The phrase appears once in this 203-word source, beside sponsorship, affiliate deals, and pool of people. Owning the outlet expands reach and gives a pool of people to market to through sponsorship, affiliate deals, software and services. The clip's proof is small-scale: a friend bought a very small Instagram handle for about five grand, with 18,000 followers and heavy organic traffic, and grew it to almost 100,000 followers.
