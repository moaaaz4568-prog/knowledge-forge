# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_Cc8JvTKAV_2 — bolting on revenue streams

## Term

Bolting on revenue streams is adding new ways to earn on top of the existing business, and one of three forces shaping its future. It appears once in this 114-word source, beside continuation towards specialization, recurring revenue component, and saturated market. The clip lists the three as specialization, bolting on revenue streams, and adding a recurring revenue component, because acquiring customers profitably is easy only in a vertical with no competition. In a saturated market full of other brand strategists and lead gen agencies, the recurring stream is what lets you sustain through the long term.
