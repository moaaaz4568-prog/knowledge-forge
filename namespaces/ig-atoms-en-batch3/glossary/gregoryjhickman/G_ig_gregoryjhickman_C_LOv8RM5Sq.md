# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_C_LOv8RM5Sq — instruction manual

## Term

An instruction manual is step two of productizing a service: the written process for creating a chosen outcome. It appears once in this 151-word source, beside identify the outcome, name it, and sell it to ten people. The five steps run: identify the service or outcome to solve — in Alex's case, the logo — then develop the manual, then name it, then put it on a document with the name, price, features and benefits plus a visual of the process, then go sell it to ten people. The proof the speaker wants is in the comments: how the conversations felt, because clients who do this feel confident, feel like the expert, and get paying clients.
