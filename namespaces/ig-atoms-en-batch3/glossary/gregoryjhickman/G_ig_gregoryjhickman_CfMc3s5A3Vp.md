# G_ig_gregoryjhickman_CfMc3s5A3Vp — lead bolt-on

## Term

A lead bolt-on is the first of three ways he names to use an online course to raise profit margin and streamline delivery. It appears once in this 174-word source, beside side bolt-on, on-ramp bolt-on, and front-end course. A lead bolt-on is a front-end course, heavily based on content, that all clients must go through before reaching any back-end services that might be more custom. The side bolt-on splits into a done for you option and a done with you option, and the on-ramp bolt-on is a two-day implementation where a mini course comes first.
