# T_Dci_igcpTBD_01

"Last year I sold my company to private equity, and the takeaways I had from that were three main things. The first thing is that people do not give a fuck about your revenue. It does not matter when you're selling your business. The thing that actually matters is your EBITDA, so how much you are profiting." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dci_igcpTBD.md

# T_Dci_igcpTBD_02

"Now, the thing is, is that a very tempting trap that people get into when they're starting their business, they're scaling their business, is they try to so heavily optimize for their revenue, right? They'll try to do 100, 000, 200, 000, half a million dollars a month, and they'll feel so fucking awesome about it." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dci_igcpTBD.md

# T_Dci_igcpTBD_03

"because that is genuinely the only thing that matters, regardless if you're just trying to make a living, travel, and have a cool, interesting life, or if you actually ever want to sell your company, you need to focus on the profit, not on the revenue, because revenue is purely vanity and profit is what actually matters." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dci_igcpTBD.md

# T_Dci_igcpTBD_04

"The second thing is that nobody's gonna fucking buy your business if the entire business will fall apart if you step out of it. Founder dependence is one of the main reasons on why people end up not being able to sell their business." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dci_igcpTBD.md

# T_Dci_igcpTBD_05

"Because if someone is gonna pay a million dollars to buy your company, and the second you leave, the company drops all of its profit, then it's not worth fucking anything. You need to build your business so that you can step out of it without having problems." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dci_igcpTBD.md

# T_Dci_igcpTBD_06

"And the final thing that people are looking at is the longevity of the business. If you started a business and it got to a million dollars a month within the first two months of starting it, but it's only been running for three, four months, the chance that someone's gonna buy that business for any decent multiple whatsoever is next to zero, because there's no longevity." — sources/S_ig_conner.bluhm_Dci_igcpTBD.md
