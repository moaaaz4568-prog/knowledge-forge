# T_Dc9xnkPkdgM_01

"Every offer you sell will and will not work on specific channels. So you can't just randomly pick a marketing channel and it will work for your specific offer you're selling. It needs to fit like a puzzle piece. The problem a lot of people have is that they're trying to force a marketing channel to work for their business when it needs to actually be the other way around." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_Dc9xnkPkdgM.md

"You need to make your business fit the marketing channel that you have the resources for. Every marketing channel costs either time or money or both, and you need to assess how much money are you prepared to spend, how much time are you prepared to spend, and then you need to morph your business to fit to the marketing channel that your resources are pointing you towards." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_Dc9xnkPkdgM.md
