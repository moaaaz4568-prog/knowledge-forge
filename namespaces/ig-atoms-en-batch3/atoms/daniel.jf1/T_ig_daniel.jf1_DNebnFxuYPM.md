# T_DNebnFxuYPM_01

"Here's the harsh truth about online business. You've got two different kinds of complexity. It's either complex to sell your offer or it's complex to fulfill your offer, and you have to choose one. If an offer is easy to sell and easy to fulfill, everybody starts selling it. Then you have competition and it stops being easy to sell." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DNebnFxuYPM.md

# T_DNebnFxuYPM_02

"A lot of people get shiny object syndrome when they see someone else running what they think is an easy business. Oh, this guy is able to sell so much more than me. I'm gonna sell that thing too. Except you don't realize that guy also works eight times more hours than you on fulfillment, has much smaller margins, and is constantly dealing with his clients. And the inverse is also true." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DNebnFxuYPM.md

# T_DNebnFxuYPM_03

"You see everybody else not working as much and dealing with clients as much, so you want to switch over to their offer without realizing that these people have to spend eight times more effort on marketing and sales, and you are not closing your calls at 30 to 40% like you're used to. The moral of the story is that everything is hard, so pick your hard." — sources/S_ig_daniel.jf1_DNebnFxuYPM.md
