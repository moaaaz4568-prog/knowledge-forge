# T_C1W4rrJtarS_01

"Three steps to make your product seem cheaper using the decoy effect. One, create another product and price it a little cheaper than the one you actually want to sell. Two, write down the major and minor benefits for each of the products. Three, make the second product unappealing by eliminating its major value." — sources/S_ig_explained.marketing_C1W4rrJtarS.md

# T_C1W4rrJtarS_02

"The second product becomes a decoy, which causes people to perceive the first to have a cheap price for the value it offers. Follow to not miss the next strategy." — sources/S_ig_explained.marketing_C1W4rrJtarS.md
