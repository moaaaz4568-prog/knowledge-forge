"Shoulder blade motion, all right. The shoulder blades themselves are capable of a lot of motion. Oftentimes during exercises, we may or may not want them to be moving, but it's important that we know what they can do. First one, as I squeeze my shoulder blades back together, right, those shoulder blades come back around the rib cage and squeeze together, that's retraction." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_Cxfp5-cga59.md

# T_Cxfp5-cga59_02

"So as they push apart, that's called protraction. So retraction, squeezing those shoulder blades back together. Protraction, shoulder blades separate and go apart. We have elevation, right, where I'm bringing that shoulder blade up, shoulders closer to my ears, and then also depression. And then the last one is actually what we call upward and downward rotation." — sources/S_ig_coachjoedrake_Cxfp5-cga59.md
