"Jack Butcher from Visualize Value did this great analogy. He drew three boxes, and the first one was service. Service is work without process. Productized service is work with a process, and then a product is process without work. If you're a productized service, at a minimum, it is delivering your work with a process that is repeatable." — sources/S_ig_gregoryjhickman_Clgrw-Tsk9g.md
