# T_C6UE_Ccii4F_01

"You should never post your Reels to your stories. When you do that, Instagram pushes your stories to way less people. And even if someone sees them, they will likely scroll past because you're not adding any value or context to your reel. Instead, do this: create an Instagram story that actually gives value to your audience." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C6UE_Ccii4F.md
