# T_DQJN0iVDGsC_01

"If there's anything that made us go from these many followers to these many followers, it's this. If you don't give enough context in your videos, people will always scroll past. Think about it—if they don't know what the video is about, what it will teach them, and who it's for, why would they stay and watch it? How do you give context? By using these three types of hooks. One: The spoken hook." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DQJN0iVDGsC.md

# T_DQJN0iVDGsC_02

"This is the one we all know about, and it's what you say as soon as the video starts. Comment '100' to get a collection of viral hooks template. Two: The text hook. This is even more important than your spoken hook because people take less time to read than to hear. Take your spoken hook and try to summarize it in as little words as possible. Try to aim for six words but never ever go over eight." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DQJN0iVDGsC.md

"Three: The visual hook. This is made by everything that appears on screen as soon as the video starts—from the camera movements to the images and videos you add on top.
