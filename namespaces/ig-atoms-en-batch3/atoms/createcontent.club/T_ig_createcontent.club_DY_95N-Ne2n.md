# T_DY_95N-Ne2n_01

"The key to surviving social media is not by following trends, it's not by giving the most value possible. The key to surviving social media is learning how to actually talk to the people you're trying to help. Rule number one, stop writing for other experts." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DY_95N-Ne2n.md

# T_DY_95N-Ne2n_02

"You need to realize that your audience is not sitting at your level, so they don't care about the buzzwords that make other experts in your niche nod their head. If you want people to actually watch, save your stuff, work with you, you need to realize who you're speaking to. You're talking to the person who's stuck where you used to be, the version of you from five years ago." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DY_95N-Ne2n.md

# T_DY_95N-Ne2n_03

"You need to get really good at making complex stuff simple to them and don't do this only when you're explaining a big concept. Do this with every single video. Hey, here's the problem you're stuck on, here's what's actually going on, and here's the one thing you can do today, boom, boom, boom. You're showing people you understand them and making yourself the obvious choice without confusing them." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DY_95N-Ne2n.md
