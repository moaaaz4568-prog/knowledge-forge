"How to add text behind you in 25 seconds? The first thing you need to do is open DaVinci Resolve, import your video and place it in your timeline. # T_C6ebxb8CgH3_02

"Here, make sure you selected the clip on top and then right-click on the node graph, select Add Alpha Output and connect the blue square to it. Now go to the Magic Mask tool, set it to Better and roughly paint your subject. Click on the tracking button... and give it a few seconds. You now have your subject split from the background. Add single lines of text between the two clips and you're done." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_C6ebxb8CgH3.md
