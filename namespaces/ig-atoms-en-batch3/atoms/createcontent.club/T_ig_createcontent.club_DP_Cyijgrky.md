# T_DP_Cyijgrky_01

"One question we get all the time is how do you always have so much content to post? So today I want to show you how we structure our week day by day to be able to post fourteen videos per week while only working four hours a week on the content. On Monday we do content research, finding the seven most viral videos in our niche and saving the best hooks and ideas." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DP_Cyijgrky.md

# T_DP_Cyijgrky_02

"Tuesday and Wednesday are script writing days where we transform those viral ideas into our own content with strong hooks and value. Thursday is filming day. We record everything in one session, changing locations and energy levels to make videos feel different. On Friday we prepare materials for editing and schedule our content for the entire week." — sources/S_ig_createcontent.club_DP_Cyijgrky.md
