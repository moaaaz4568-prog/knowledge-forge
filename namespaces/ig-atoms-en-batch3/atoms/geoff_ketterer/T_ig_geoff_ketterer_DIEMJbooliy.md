# T_DIEMJbooliy_01

"This is one of the best pieces of advice anybody ever gave me regarding handling objections at the end of your call. So I'll explain where the objection comes from in the first place because that's important to understand, and then I'll tell you how to handle it. So the first thing to understand is that your prospects don't give you objections because they don't want to do it." — sources/S_ig_geoff_ketterer_DIEMJbooliy.md

# T_DIEMJbooliy_02

"They give you objections because they're afraid it won't work. So at the end of the call when they give you an objection, they're not saying that they've changed their mind and they no longer want to do it. What they're saying is they either don't trust themselves or they don't trust you to be able to get the result." — sources/S_ig_geoff_ketterer_DIEMJbooliy.md

# T_DIEMJbooliy_03

"Which means most objections are literally just a smokescreen for some form of doubt. So the next time somebody says, I've got to talk to my partner or let me think about it or give me some time, just ask them this question. If your results with this program were 100% guaranteed, would you spend the money? Almost 100% of the time they'll tell you yes." — sources/S_ig_geoff_ketterer_DIEMJbooliy.md

# T_DIEMJbooliy_04

"Reason is because psychologically what they want somebody else to do is validate their guarantee. They're trying to abdicate their responsibility in the situation. A bit like buying a treadmill. Somebody could ask, if I buy this treadmill, am I guaranteed to lose weight? Well, if you leave it propped up against the garage wall, no." — sources/S_ig_geoff_ketterer_DIEMJbooliy.md

# T_DIEMJbooliy_05

"If you unpack it, get on it, plug it in, use it every single day, the chances are excellent that you'll start to lose weight. Unless you're eating maccies for breakfast all the time. So to handle the objection effectively, you've got to make your prospect realize that success in anything is not about abdicating the responsibility for it or waiting for somebody else to guarantee it for you." — sources/S_ig_geoff_ketterer_DIEMJbooliy.md

# T_DIEMJbooliy_06

"It's about stepping up to the plate, doing what you've got to do, and being the guarantee yourself." — sources/S_ig_geoff_ketterer_DIEMJbooliy.md
