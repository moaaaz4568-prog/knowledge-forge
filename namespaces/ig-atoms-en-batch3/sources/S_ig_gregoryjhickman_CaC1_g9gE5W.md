Here's a quick hiring hack if you guys aren't doing this. In the initial job description, whether you're saying, hey, go here to apply or send an email here, inside of the request of here's what to do, make sure you have them do something that's kind of random to see if they can pay attention to details. For example, I'm unfortunately a New York Giants fan. They're horrible now, but a couple years ago when we were hiring, in the description I said, before you kind of submit your letter, who wears number 13 and how many touchdowns did they score in this year? And there are some people that literally would respond like, I don't like football. That wasn't the question. Delete. So like little things like that in the top of funnel, so to speak, of the process, because you'll take 100 candidates down to 30 real quick.
