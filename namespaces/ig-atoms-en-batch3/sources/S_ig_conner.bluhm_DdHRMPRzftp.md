Before I ever launch someone's ads, the first thing that I always do is make sure they have gone through our expectation setting course during the onboarding. Now, if you don't know what this is, I preach this a lot in my content, is have your clients go through three to five expectation setting videos right when they're onboarded with you, and then make sure that they pass a 20 to 30 question expectation setting quiz with 100% certainty before you launch their ads. Now, the reason this is, is there's this quote from Naval Ravikant that goes, happiness is results minus expectations. Now, the thing that kind of shocks me that people don't realize is that ruthlessly applies to onboarding and working with clients. If you start working with a client and you don't set the expectations brutally clearly, then it's simply left to interpretation. And the thing with interpretation is that it will always be more generous than what happens in reality. So if someone's expecting in their eyes to sign, you know, 10, 20 clients, if they don't get 10, 20 clients, they're gonna be pissed and they're gonna ask for a refund. But if you set the expectations around exactly what the program's gonna look like, what is required of them, what is required of you, how long success will take, what success looks like, and what happens if success doesn't happen within that timeframe, then you're never gonna have a client ask for a refund or dispute because you simply set the expectations brutally clearly. And as I said earlier, happiness is results minus expectations. So if you want happy clients, simply set brutally clear expectations and you will never have a problem with a client being pissed ever again.
