We all go through the phase of wanting to do something new. We talk about it, we read about it, we even pay money to learn more about it. But most people will quit as soon as friction is introduced. Remember that talking about the thing isn't doing the thing. Planning to do the thing isn't doing the thing. Telling people you're going to do the thing isn't doing the thing. The only thing that is doing the thing is doing the thing.
