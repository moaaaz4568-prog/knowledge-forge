"You can create viral content by just documenting your everyday life if you understand this one principle. It's the same principle that some creators are using to hijack attention on social media and grow massive followings while simply documenting their journey. We actually had one of our students ask about this, and I'm sure you can relate to him. He came to us and said, 'I would love to make this kind of content to show my journey, but my day-to-day life is so boring.' Now, the issue is..."

"He was thinking about documenting in a literal sense, but all creators who do it while also getting millions of views aren't just telling you what their day looks like. They take the superficial level of what happens and they associate a deeper meaning by sharing concepts or emotions associated to what they are doing. Instead of telling people what you do, you should tell them what you feel and what you learn from the experiences that you go through. More importantly, you need to share the struggles.
