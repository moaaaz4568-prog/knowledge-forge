You should never put links in your Instagram bio or stories before knowing this. When you use traditional links on Instagram and click on them, you will see that you will get directed to the Instagram browser. This is because Instagram doesn't want users to leave their platform. The problem with that though is that sometimes it can play to your disadvantage. Let's say you want to promote your new YouTube video on your IG stories. If you simply use a link sticker and paste your YouTube video link, people who click on it will have to watch you through the Instagram browser. They will not be able to follow you, leave a comment, or even like the video as they will be not logged in. So what's the solution? You need to use something called a deep link which allows you to bring people outside of the Instagram platform when they click on your link. All you have to do is go to this free website called Link and paste your URL. Let's say for example your YouTube video. Now when a person clicks on this link, the YouTube app will automatically open and they will be able to interact with your account.
