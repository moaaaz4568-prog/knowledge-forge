When we think of our growth potential, there are really two types of owners. There are the people that think in terms of what's probable and those that think in terms of what's possible. The probability thinkers, they base their view of their future financial selves on their past history and current capabilities. They say things to themselves like, "Based on who I've been and who I am, this is probably what I can financially accomplish." Whereas if you're a possibility thinker, you rarely start to utter the words, "I can't do it." Their potential is described in terms of what's conceivable, what's imaginable, and what's possible. They say things like, "I have dreams for a reason. Based on who I can become, this is what I can financially accomplish."
