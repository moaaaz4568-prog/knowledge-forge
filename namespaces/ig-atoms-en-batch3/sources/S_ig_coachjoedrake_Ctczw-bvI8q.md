The longer that I've been training, the more that I personally believe that systems precede creativity. And even for myself, I think about it as a trainer along the way and working with other coaches who claim to be innovative or creative, and the difference between just being randomly innovative and creative and being systematically. And the reason I say that is I think especially as a new coach and trainer, there's so many things that you're managing in your brain as far as the session itself, all the X's and O's of the actual training session, that it's hard to have the brain power if you're there in the moment just making things up like so many coaches do. It's hard to have the mental capacity to then also be thinking about how do I really deliver on this individual experience for the person that I have, how I can best serve them as a coach, and then even on the creative side, how I can make it fun and engaging for them and the personality style. It's a lot easier when you're working off of a consistent system. You just have more mental energy, brain power, and time to actually be creative when you're working off systems.
