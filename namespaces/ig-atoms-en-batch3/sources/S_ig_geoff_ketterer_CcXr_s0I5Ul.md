If you work in sales, then these are two of the most powerful persuasive questions you can ask anybody at the end of your pitch. I'm going to tell you what they are and then I'm going to explain to you why they're so powerful from a psychological perspective. So first question when you get to the end of your pitch is this: Mr Prospect, hypothetically speaking, if this does everything that I say it will, do you feel like it's going to be worth the price? The reason this is such a powerful question is because when your prospect says yes, what they're saying essentially on a psychological basis is I'm agreeing to pay for it. Second question is, and do you believe that this will work for you? The reason that's such a powerful question again is from a psychological perspective. Everybody will buy something if they truly believe it will be worth for them. If you sell a half million pound business consulting, but the business owner truly believes that it will help him make an extra two or three million pounds, he's going to be willing to pay the half million because it's a simple value exchange. Where people come unstuck at the end of their pitch is sometimes everything can feel right, everything can sound right, but if the prospect does not believe with 100% certainty.
