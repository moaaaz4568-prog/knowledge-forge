How did this super simple 10-second video get 1.4 million views? This is a strategy that you can apply to your content as well to turn any boring idea into a viral video. It's called open loop, and it consists in introducing an element that creates curiosity by making people want to see how the video will end. In this example, it's simply done by progressively removing pieces under the drink, creating an expectation that it's going to spill at the end, and keeping your attention because you're curious to see what will happen. You can do the same in your videos in three simple ways. Number one, you can add some kind of visual element just as they did in this video. Number two, you can use graphic overlays such as loading bars or timers. Or number three, you can create a gap of information in which the viewer has to stay until the end to get the information you anticipated in your hook.
