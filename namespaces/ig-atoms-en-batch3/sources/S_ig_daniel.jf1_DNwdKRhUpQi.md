The less authority you have, the stronger your guarantee needs to be. If you're just starting out with few case studies, you might need a performance-based model or a strong money-back guarantee. Why? Because you're risky. Because you have no authority, they're assessing the probability that once they pay you money, the probability that they lose money is high because your authority is low. When your authority is low, you have to compensate for this by de-risking for the client. As your authority increases, you can shift to less risky offer structures. But always remember, the guarantee is there to compensate for a lack of case studies. If you have 50 amazing testimonies and case studies, you don't need a strong guarantee. Less authority equals stronger guarantee required.
