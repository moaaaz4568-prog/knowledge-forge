There are four parts: strategy, content, distribution, and feedback loop. First, strategy. Post three to five times per week and build a simple content funnel. I prefer roughly 40% top of funnel, 40% middle of funnel, and 20% bottom. At the top, you will talk about things your buyer already thinks about. In the middle, you will teach them how you think and about the problems you solve. At the bottom, you will give them proof you can actually solve these problems. Your best source of topics is usually your buyers. Go through sales calls and customer calls and pull out repeated problems, questions, objections, and buying criteria. These all become posts. Part two is making the content. You're gonna use the CPIO framework. Decide what you're trying to convey, how you're gonna package it, what information actually needs to be there, and the most compelling order to present it in. This will become better over time. Then focus on the hook. Create a curiosity gap. Give the reader enough information to care while leaving one important thing unresolved. If you're teaching a process, lead with the outcome before you teach the steps and then give away the actual steps. Tactical content should be useful enough that the reader does not need to ask you what you left out. Then match the media to the idea. Personal or team story, personal photo. Dense process, infographic. Long list, carousel. Industry commentary, screenshot the thing you're commenting on. Part three is distribution. Connect with 100 people from your ICP every week with a blank request. Then build a custom feed of the people your ICP already follow. Twice per day, leave three to five thoughtful comments on their posts. Treat each comment like a mini post. Then track which buyers view your profile, like your posts, or comment. Warm outbound to them. Finally, the feedback loop. Every month, look at which topics, hooks, formats, and media types reach the right people. Make more versions of those winners and stop doing what consistently loses. That is the system. Follow me for more, and if you want us to just do this for you, book a call from my bio.
