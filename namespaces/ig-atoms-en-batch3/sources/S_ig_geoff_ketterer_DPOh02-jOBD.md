I made a commitment to myself when I was 24 to write a tweet every day, and I would screenshot it, then post it on Instagram. Now this was maybe the single biggest hack in my life that I've never really talked about because of all of the downstream consequences that occurred. Downstream consequence number one, got to a million followers on Instagram. Downstream consequence number two is it taught me how to communicate ideas in a concise, high impact way and kind of what people respond to. The power of consistency is such an important law for us all to observe. Showing up only when you feel like it is a surefire way to signal to the algorithm and your audience that you don't really care. Plus the second order consequence that Stephen talks about is actually building the skill of writing. We all know that repetition is the mother of all learning. So it doesn't matter if you get 10 views or 10, 000 views, you're developing the skill which will pay you back over the long term.
