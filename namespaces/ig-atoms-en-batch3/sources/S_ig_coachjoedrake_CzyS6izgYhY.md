Most coaches are blind to a concept that I refer to as spatial programming, and that's actually having some thought and some strategy into how you flow throughout a space. Right, when you're putting together a superset or a triplex, are all two or three of those exercises in different corners of the gym where you've got to waste a lot of time going back and forth between them, or do you have a primary zone where you're accomplishing most of that set? Even if you're using a big machine like a leg press or a lat pull, are you accomplishing your other two to three movements right there in that same space? Are you bringing things over you can use? Because you will create a much better flow and experience for your training session. And I think it also comes across to clients that there's a lot more thought going into what you're doing rather than just pairing together exercises in the gym.
