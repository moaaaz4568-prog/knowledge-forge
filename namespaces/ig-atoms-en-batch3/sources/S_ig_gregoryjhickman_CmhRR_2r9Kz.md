What is client concentration? It means too much of your overall revenue is being generated by only a few clients. Ideally, no client would make up more than 25% of your monthly revenue. Why? For starters, risk. We have clients that if they lost one client, their entire business would be gone overnight. So if all of your income and your revenue is tied to one particular client or most of it, they leave for whatever reason, you literally have no business. For me, I'd rather have 20 clients paying 5 to 10K a month versus five clients with two of those five paying me 50K a month. It's a lot easier to replace 5% of your revenue than 50 plus percent of your revenue.
