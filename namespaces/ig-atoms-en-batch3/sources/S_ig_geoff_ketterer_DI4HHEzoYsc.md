One of the most successful tips anybody ever gave me was never to use the phrase "sign the contract." The reason you shouldn't use that phrase is because all of us have a negative bias in our brain towards the words both "sign" and "contract," and the reason for it is everybody throughout our entire lives has always said things to us like, "Don't sign the contract," "Don't sign your life away," "Make sure you read every word before you sign anything." So now those words and phrases have created a negative association in our brain, and I have seen those words kill deals that were just about to close. So what should you say to be able to not kill the deal and get it over the line? The phrase I've used to be able to sell $100, 000 contracts and cash collect millions in my career is simply this: "Thanks for that, John. The last step is just for you to approve the agreement."
