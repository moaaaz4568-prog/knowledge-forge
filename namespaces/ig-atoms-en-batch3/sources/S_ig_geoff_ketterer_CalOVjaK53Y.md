So I had a client ask me the other day, why do we use neutral language in our sales calls? Words like possibly, maybe, perhaps. And the answer is because you want your client to open up to you. Let me explain. Sometimes the perception is that on a sales call you need to come across strong and bold, but think about it from your client's perspective. If you do that, what are the chances they're going to feel comfortable enough around you to actually answer your questions honestly? Almost zero. As opposed to using phrases like, is this possibly what you're looking for? Do you feel like this might be on the cards for you? You're phrasing those questions in such a way is that they feel like they're neutral. They don't feel pressured to give you a false answer, so they tend to give you a correct answer. That's why you use neutral language on a sales call.
