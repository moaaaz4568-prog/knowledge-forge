If you pay enough attention to Apple's product pricing, you'll realize it's a powerful masterclass. Before launching their new iPhone, iPad and MacBook, they first launched the Vision Pro at a high price of $3,499. This created an anchor for the products they later launched to be seen as cheaper while still premium. This is called price anchoring, and it is a powerful tool to create future demand.
