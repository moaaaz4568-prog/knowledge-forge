I learned this crazy psychological hack that I use to close more business, and you can use it too. It's based in reverse psychology, and it pretty much gets your prospect to focus on all of the positives and all of the things that will work instead of all the things that won't. And it works like this: when you temp check your prospect, and by temp check I mean you ask them, "Okay John, so on a scale of one to ten, where do you see yourself? One being not interested, ten being really interested?" And John replies back with, "Well, I think I'm about a seven." What we're taught to do in traditional sales is to say, "Well, why not a ten? What do I have to do to get you up to a ten?" The problem with this approach is that all it does is get your prospect to focus on what's missing, what they don't like, what might not work. So you're forcing them to focus on the negative. And what would be way more beneficial is to get them to focus on the positive. And to do this, all we have to do is instead of asking, "What's missing? Why aren't you a ten?" Just ask them why they're not a lower number. Okay John, so you're at a seven, pretty high. Can I ask why didn't you choose a lower number? And typically what they're going to reply with is, "I like this, I like X, Y, and Z, and I really like the way you guys do that." And with just one tiny reverse psychology trick, you've got them to focus on all of the positives instead of all of the negatives.
