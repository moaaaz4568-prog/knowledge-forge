One of the worst mistakes you can make when trying to get clients is having vague and unclear headlines on your page. You ever see these headlines where it's like, "Unlock your business's potential," "Optimize your marketing strategy." Like, what does that even mean? What are you even saying to me right now? Here's what you need to do instead. This needs to be a stop sign, needs to be abundantly clear what is happening. And you do that by making it about the result. You should say something like, "We'll set up a webinar funnel for your business and get you 100 new customers in 30 days." Not, "We make webinars," or "We make really good webinars," or "Marketing optimized webinars." What is it? What do you even saying? Or spark some curiosity. You can say something like, "We will send 3,000 people to your store in the next month." People are gonna be like, "Whoa, how do you do that?" Now what are they gonna do? Book a call with you. Writing good headlines is single-handedly the one thing that will make a substantial difference in how many calls you book, how many customers you sign. It's all about the headlines.
