How to get your first digital agency client or clients. Many of you are probably overthinking all of these things and you're probably trying to figure out how do I magically land on the thing that I want to do for the exact person that I want to serve. Real talk, most of us aren't going to nail that in the first try. It's just not realistic. So if you're just getting started, my advice to you is to pick a niche, interview them to find out their pains, problems, wants, aspirations, and put together a package that combines skills you have, things they need, and things that they want. Make an offer for a 90 day trial. From the 90 day trial, you optimize, add, remove things that you liked and/or did not like, and then readjust the pricing and take that back to the market. That's how I got my first few clients.
