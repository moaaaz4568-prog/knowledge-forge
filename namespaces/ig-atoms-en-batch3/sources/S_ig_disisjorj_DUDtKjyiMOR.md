I've made every video mistake you can imagine over the past ten years, from bad lighting to terrible audio to inconsistent backdrops. But the one that cost me the most clients was not having a signature setup. I thought the problem was my quality, so I got a new camera. I thought it was my scripting, so I bought courses on copywriting or even editing, watched a bunch of videos just to figure out how to make them look crispy and good. But none of them actually moved the needle forward when it came to getting more clients. The real problem was that I was making it so difficult for my prospects to recognize me, let alone remember me. What we do instead is that we want to pick one spot in your space and just film there for the next ninety days.
