Most creators sit around and just wait for brands to slide into their DM. That's a very slow route. The fast route is going out and pitching brands directly. Here's how. Make a list of 20 brands that align with your audience and that you genuinely use. Find their marketing or partnership emails. Those are usually available on their websites. Send a short, confident pitch. Who you are, who your audience is, and how partnering with you benefits them, and the link to your media kit. That's it. Just keep it under five sentences. Some will say no, some will not reply, but some will say yes. And just a single yes changes everything.
