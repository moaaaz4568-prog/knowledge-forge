There is a clear difference between content creation and personal branding. Content creation is the hook, the format, the edit. It gets you the views. Personal branding is what people believe about you after they close the app. You can post daily for an year, hit views every single time, but still be forgettable. Because you copied every viral topic, used everyone else's formats, but never showed a real opinion. That gets you content, but not a real brand. Content gets people to watch you. Branding gets people to remember you.
