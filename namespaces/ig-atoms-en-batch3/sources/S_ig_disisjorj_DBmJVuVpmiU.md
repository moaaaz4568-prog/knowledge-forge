If you ever try to stay consistent with posting content online, you would know the struggle of jumping from ideation to scripting to filming to editing to posting, only to wake up and do the same thing all over again. What I have all of my clients go through though is what I call the weakest link exercise. Out of all these steps, what is the one that A takes most of your time, but B you're not that well versed in? Most people go for editing, but we soon find out that it's not the case. What actually is the weakest link more often than not is scripting. Because if I were to deliver 10 new scripts suited for what you do and who you help, in your Notion document ready for you to just sit down and film, you would actually start enjoying editing your videos. Once you identify the leak in your pipeline, start focusing on systems, techniques, and formulas to improve it, since you can only be as strong as your weakest link.
