Three things to do immediately after posting. Number one: engage with previous posts. The first thing you can do is engage with all the people who commented on your previous reels. Try to provide the most value possible and take the time to respond to the comments properly. Doing this reminds people about you and your content, and they will likely visit your profile and see that you just posted new content. Number two: check your DMs. Right after checking your comments, go into your DMs and make sure to reply to all the messages your audience sent you there. By replying to them and helping them out, they will be likely to go on your profile and interact with your new video. Number three: post stories. Now, we're not talking about reposting your reel on your stories as this generally gets little to no engagement. Instead, post genuine and valuable stories that once again remind people of your content. The more interesting and valuable the story, the more likes it will get. And the last bonus tip: interact with other accounts in your feed. This is another great way to put yourself out there and drive people towards your profile.
