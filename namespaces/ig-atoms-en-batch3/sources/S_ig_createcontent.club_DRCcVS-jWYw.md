Top of funnel content, middle of funnel content, bottom of funnel content. Top of funnel is to get more views, to get more people to know that you exist. This is trending topics, catchy hooks, content that sparks curiosity or controversy. Middle of funnel is to build more trust and convert that attention you got into loyal fans. Think of story sequences, in-depth value videos, long-form videos even. Bottom of funnel is to sell yourself on your products. Think of client testimonials and behind-the-scenes content. You need all three to be able to build a personal brand that not only positions you as an expert and an authority in your industry but that also converts that attention into cash for your business. And if you want to learn how to build your own content funnel, make sure to follow us.
