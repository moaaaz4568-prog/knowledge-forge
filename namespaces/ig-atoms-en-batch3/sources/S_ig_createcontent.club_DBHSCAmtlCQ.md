No one cares about you. I'm sorry to be the one telling you this, but that's the harsh truth when you're just starting out with your personal brand. I know you would like to talk about you and your content, just like your favorite content creators, personal brands, and entrepreneurs. But the truth is, when you're just starting out and no one knows you, that simply doesn't work. The way you actually make it work is by focusing all of your attention and all of your content on the actual value that you can provide to a complete stranger. What's your unique expertise? Or skill? What are you doing better than the average person? Create educational content around that, provide value first, and only then will you get people interested and invested in who you are and the person behind the skill.
