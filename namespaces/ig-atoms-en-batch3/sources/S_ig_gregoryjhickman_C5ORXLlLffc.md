Profit killer number one is scope creep and scope seep. They are different. So scope creep is what many of you are familiar with, which is where you just didn't have clear enough deliverables. And when the client comes and says, hey, can you also do insert all of these other things, you end up saying yes because there were no clear deliverables in the first place. And so because there were no clear deliverables, they feel okay and or even inquisitive to see if you can help them with more stuff. And you graciously say yes because you don't want to lose the client. Now scope seep on the other hand is when you proactively suggest work outside of the deliverables because there weren't clear deliverables. And most people do that because again, they're afraid that there's nothing that the client is going to ask them to do. So they invite the opportunity for the client to come and say, yeah, why not? Let's do that too. And so you are voluntarily letting the scope expand beyond what the project should have been.
