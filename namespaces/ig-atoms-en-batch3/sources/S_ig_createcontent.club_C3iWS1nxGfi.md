How to stop procrastinating. Too many times we fall into the trap of overestimating how many things we can do in a single day, creating such high standards for ourselves that they become impossible to reach. But guess what happens when we are too hard on ourselves and start to assign infinite tasks for the day? We fail over and over again. This results in a constant lack of motivation, which starts to make us doubt what we are capable of. Instead, try doing this next time. Focus on just one important task every single day and be proud for how well you completed it. Remember, it's not about how many tasks you mark as done on your infinite to-do list. This way, you will start to appreciate each day more and you'll actually become more productive as you now have full control over that one main task you need to get done for the day. You see, it's one thing to be confident and rely on your abilities, but it's another to just take on too much.
