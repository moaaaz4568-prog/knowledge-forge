B2B tech companies host competing conferences in the same week for the same reason countries have nuclear bombs. If the other guy has one, you probably want one too. Salesforce just held Dreamforce September 15th to 17th in SF. Hubspot, their biggest competitor, just held Unbound in Boston September 16th to 18th. Apollo is holding Apollo Next on September 30th. Eight days later, Clay is hosting Sculpt. These are direct competitors spending millions of dollars to attract much of an overlapping audience within the same week. I think the logic is that a flagship conference gives the company a few days where the entire market is paying attention. You might launch products, bring customers, generate press, and get thousands of people talking about where your category is headed. And giving your biggest competitor that advantage uncontested feels like a flop. So when Salesforce builds Dreamforce, Hubspot has to build Unbound. When Apollo builds Next, Clay has to build Sculpt. I call it mutually assured distribution. Once one company turns a conference into a category-defining event, their competitors should probably follow.
