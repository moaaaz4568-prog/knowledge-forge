Founders need to stop pretending that AI can replace proper sales coaching. It can't. AI can tell you what was said. It can flag missed questions. It can tell you if the objection handle was wrong, and it can tell you if your overall script has a weak structure, which is useful, but the problem is sales calls are not won on words alone. They're won in the moments that a transcript can't describe. The hesitation before the price, or a drop in confidence when the prospect pushes back, or a forced tone behind a great question, which is the difference between sounding certain or sounding like your sales rep memorized the script. And this is why. I'm sure you're familiar with the quote, words are only 7% of communication. Well, that's true, but Mehrabian didn't mean that words only matter 7% of the time in any conversation. But his research did show that when words and nonverbal communication clash, people tend to believe the nonverbal communication. So if your AI call reviewer is saying that the reps said the correct words, but the prospect still didn't buy, then it's probably not the words that were the problem. So sure, use AI to save time, but you should still use a human if you actually want to understand what happened. Because if you've not got a person listening to your sales calls, then you're not coaching sales. You're just grading a transcript.
