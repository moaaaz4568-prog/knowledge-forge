The number of owners that I deal with and have met that forego their own pay and salary to pay their team and the help, which is totally noble, I get it, is actually going to lead to you burning out and probably giving up, or at least not hitting your goals and probably starting to have some sort of hate for your business. If you read the book Buy Back Your Time by Dan Martel, he says one of the biggest mistakes people make, like yourself, is that you hire for capacity. Meaning, if I hire another designer or another developer or another marketer, they can handle another five to ten clients. Yet you are still overworked, working nights and weekends. So what Dan mentions is you actually need to buy back your time, offload all of the administrative stuff, all the stuff that doesn't make you money or get your clients results first, and then spend that newfound time on serving more clients. We've seen our clients by rehiring and going after buying back their time, double and even triple their business because they're capable of taking on a couple more clients once they have the capacity freed up.
