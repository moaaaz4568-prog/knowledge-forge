How Alex Hormozi got millions of followers on all social media platforms. Let's be honest, if you truly want to bring valuable content that will grow your personal brand, you must be on every social media platform. While we as most creators suggest that you start with only a couple of platforms, your ultimate goal has to be conquering them all. The best way to do that is by following what Alex calls the principle of the content unit. Every piece of content can be reduced to a few sentences that give a problem and a solution. That's why the best way to test out what kind of content you should make is to start from the version of it that requires the least amount of time. What Alex does is posting every idea for content that he has in the form of a tweet. From there, he takes the most successful tweets and creates short form videos, and the most successful of these become long form YouTube videos. You can do the same on Instagram, for example, with Instagram stories by making Q&A to see what people want to learn from you and start your content creation process from there.
