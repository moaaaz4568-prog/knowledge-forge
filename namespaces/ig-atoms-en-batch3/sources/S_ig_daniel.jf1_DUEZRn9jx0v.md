Huge problem I see with beginners when they're just starting out, they'll only want to take calls in like a three-hour time block. They won't book a sales call anytime after that. And one of the best pieces of advice you could possibly implement as a beginner when you're just getting started out and you're below like 20k a month or 30k a month, keep your calendar open for like 14 hours a day. If you have the highest availability variance possible with all sorts of times that somebody can pick, it increases the probability that they will actually show up to the call.
