If you're not getting enough response from your marketing contents, it's because you're not applying the most important principle in content marketing: brand resonance. Here's how it works. It is like solving a picture puzzle. In order to move a square to its correct position, there are specific squares you will have to move first and in a specific order. If you're marketing with an Instagram reel and you go straight to introducing your product, it's like trying to get square one to square nine without moving any other squares. It just won't work. No one hops on Instagram to get sold to. If you want to sell a musical product, you'll have to know the kind of contents musicians like to consume and make yours appear similar so that your audience can relate to it. Base it on the set standard for an Instagram reel: educational, motivational or entertaining. After moving these squares, you can now sell.
