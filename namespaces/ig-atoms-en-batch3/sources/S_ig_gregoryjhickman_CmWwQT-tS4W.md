Selling more is usually the most difficult way to increase profits. That's because your percentages stay the same and the expenses generated to support the sales increasing faster actually result in smaller percentages and a smaller profit margin. Which we see time and time again. As you get more clients, you need more help and revenue goes up, expenses usually go up, but the overhead, the management, the headache, the complexities that grow with that shrink that margin.
