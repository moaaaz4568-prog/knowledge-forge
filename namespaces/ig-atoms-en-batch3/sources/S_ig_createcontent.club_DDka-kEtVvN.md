Every time someone tells me that they want to create original content, I find it kind of funny, to be honest. Like, on Instagram alone—so I'm not even talking about other social media platforms—there are a hundred million posts uploaded per day. You really think you will be able to between all that to come up with something that has never been thought before? And besides that, if your goal is to grow your business with content, the whole point should be to take something that's already working and adding your... expertise onto that. That becomes your unique twist, the specific take you have on a given topic. Even if you look at artists, the most creative people in the world—they take something and build on top of it. Mind-blowing, right? But I'm saying this just to bust once and for all the myth of original content.
