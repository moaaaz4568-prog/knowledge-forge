You feel depressed because you're failing without even realizing it. That's what I realized when I first read 12 Rules for Life by Jordan Peterson. I couldn't understand this feeling that I had until I read his rule number 10, which states: "Be precise in your speech." What that means is that you should define exactly the terms in which you are winning or failing in each realm of life. If you do not define terms for winning or failing, you don't have a set goal or a set guide, and as a consequence, you're constantly failing. Not defining terms feels good in the short term because it makes you avoid realizing that you are indeed failing. But in the long term, it causes pain because you will be constantly failing by not even knowing what you're aiming at to begin with. So that explains why.
