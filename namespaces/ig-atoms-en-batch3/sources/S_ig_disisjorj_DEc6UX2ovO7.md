If you ever pulled out your phone or even a camera like this to take a video, you probably came across these lines. We call those the grid lines, and they're there to help you create better content if you use them correctly. A common mistake I see when it comes to beginner content creators is neglecting their focus point, where your viewer's eye naturally goes to when watching a vertical video. Turn on your grid lines and make sure your eyes are aligned either perfectly with that top line or somewhat around that area. I hope this helps.
