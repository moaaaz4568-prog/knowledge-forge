One of the dumbest things that I'll see people do when they're building out their funnel is they make it unbelievably complicated and they make it a borderline website. I cannot say this enough. If your funnel has tabs on it where they can click around from tab to tab to tab, you have a fucking terrible funnel. Because people are easily distracted. If they have options, if they have multiple things they can do and they don't understand a clear single thing that they do on that page, one button to press, one form to go through, one call to book, then they will get confused, they will get distracted, and that will lead them to not proceeding with the outcome that you want them to. So right now I want you to look at your funnel, and if your funnel has any distractions, any more than one single button on the page, and any more than one hero element, you have a bad funnel that is distracting. It is unlikely to actually lead to conversions. It is better to be clear than clever. Never forget that people have terrible attention spans and they get distracted extremely easily. Your funnel should have a headline, a VSL, and a simple button, or a headline and a form, and that is it. Almost everything should be contained above the fold, and there should be absolutely zero room for interpretation on what they need to do.
