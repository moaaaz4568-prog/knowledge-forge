If you find it hard to talk to the camera, do this. It's a super simple exercise that takes literally five to ten minutes a day, but that will change forever not only how you talk to the camera, but your communication skills too. Every day, turn on your phone camera or even just your laptop webcam and record a five-minute video in which you discuss any topic of your choice. Once you're done, watch back the video and take notes of everything that you notice that you could have done better, and... keep on repeating this. This allows you to practice your speaking and analyze it to improve it with little to no effort. I promise you that this will be the best ten minutes you ever invested into something because always remember that you're only as good as your communication level. You could be the best expert in the world on something, but if you can't communicate it effectively to others, it means nothing.
