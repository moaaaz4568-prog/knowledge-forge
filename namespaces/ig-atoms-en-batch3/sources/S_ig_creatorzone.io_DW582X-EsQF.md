Everyone thinks that they have a growth problem, but most of the time it is actually an offer problem. Think about it: if ten thousand people saw your page and no one bought, getting fifty thousand won't fix it. Traffic would never fix the confusion. Before you worry about any reach or any views, focus on who you help, why are you the one to help them, and what problem you solve, and what is the actual problem that you are solving. Because a sharp offer with a smaller audience would always beat a vague solution for a big amount of audience.
