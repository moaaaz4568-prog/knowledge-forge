If the sales culture that you have inside of your organisation is only ever to do with KPIs and targets and tracking, then there's a great chance that you're actually killing the very performance metric that you're trying to improve. And on paper, everything can look right. High numbers, clear standards, good accountability. But if every day starts to feel like some kind of commando hell week, then people will stop enjoying the job, regardless of how much they make. And when the fun disappears, so does the energy. And if the energy disappears, then so does confidence. And if somebody's confidence drops, sales usually follow. But the worst part is that most managers treat fun like a reward. That doesn't mean you have to ignore KPIs or make it okay for missing targets. It means building a team that people actually want to be a part of. Because a miserable fucking sales team with perfect KPIs is still a miserable sales team. So 100%, hold the standards, track the numbers, but for God's sake, make room for fun.
