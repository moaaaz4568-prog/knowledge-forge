# T_Dbdik33Dv2O_01

"Five words to never say in cold emails. Don't say buy, say explore. Buy makes it sound salesy, explore makes it sound like you're trying to learn more. Never say cheap, say simple. Cheap makes it sound low value, simple makes it sound low effort for high return. Never say I, say you. No one cares about you in cold emails, make it about them. Never say maybe, say typically." — sources/S_ig_jimmyfung__Dbdik33Dv2O.md
