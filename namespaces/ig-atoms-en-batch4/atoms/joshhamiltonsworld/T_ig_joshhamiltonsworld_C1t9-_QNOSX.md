# T_C1t9-_QNOSX_01

"Underrated skill: making the first move, otherwise known as initiative. It's rare to find somebody who has the ability to use their judgment to make sound decisions and do things effectively without being told what to do. But it's difficult to take the lead on situations or actions you've never done before. The people who have highly developed initiative will face an unknown situation head on." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_C1t9-_QNOSX.md

# T_C1t9-_QNOSX_02

"They'll own it and find someone who has done the thing before them to learn from them, unlocking the ability to do it themselves. But here is the secret: initiative gets easier the more things you do. The more you do, the more you know, the easier it becomes to own things because you can predict every next step required to succeed. These are wickedly valuable people." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_C1t9-_QNOSX.md
