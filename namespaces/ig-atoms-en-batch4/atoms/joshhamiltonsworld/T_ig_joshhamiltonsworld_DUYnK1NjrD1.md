"The traditional agency retainer model is broken for businesses that want more than just maintenance, and here is why. From an agency owner, agencies have to make all their margin on the retainer. They charge you more out of the gate. That should already tell you something. The retainer model does work short term, but then it quietly breaks every time. Why?" — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_DUYnK1NjrD1.md

# T_DUYnK1NjrD1_02

"Because the incentive becomes do as little work as possible to protect the agency margins. Set it, forget. Assume clients are going to churn, focus on signing new clients when these clients that you just acquired fall off in four months. Results come early, then they plateau, and then they fall off a cliff, and everybody acts surprised." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_DUYnK1NjrD1.md

# T_DUYnK1NjrD1_03

"Real growth fires more time, money, resources, people, especially once you go from spending fifty K a month to one hundred grand a month, two hundred and fifty grand a month, and beyond. There is some fixes out there, and we've tried all of them. The one model that we've seen actually scales and that we like right now is performance bonus." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_DUYnK1NjrD1.md

# T_DUYnK1NjrD1_04

"We go through a ninety day activation phase, we move into a performance based relationship, and set those growth targets together. Once those targets are hit, we earn a percentage of net new revenue above baseline minus ad spend and COGS. We get either our base fee or the performance bonus, not both, and the baseline resets every quarter. So you keep playing at higher and higher level." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_DUYnK1NjrD1.md

# T_DUYnK1NjrD1_05

"That to me is alignment, and it's the same way that you would incentivize an internal team, which we do help people transition into. If your agency only wins when you pay them their retainer, not when you grow, then you don't have a partner, you have a vendor." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_DUYnK1NjrD1.md
