# T_CyBOseLu0OG_01

"I've made a lot of mistakes in marketing, but this is the biggest: not talking to the market before I did the marketing. I've developed entire landing pages, funnels, ads, sales scripts, and outreach message systems before speaking to a single person in the market and relying entirely on my assumptions about what they want. Don't do that. No one will care." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_CyBOseLu0OG.md

# T_CyBOseLu0OG_02

"Here's what I do now instead and make way more money. First, I understand who I'm going to serve. Who is my customer? Second, if relevant, I round up past and current clients that match that profile. If starting fresh, I develop a new list of 20 to 30 people who match my target audience. Think peers, partners, your phone contacts, your followers." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_CyBOseLu0OG.md

# T_CyBOseLu0OG_03

"Then, I set up 20 to 30 minute Zoom calls with each. I can incentivize them with discounted or free or early access if needed. The gist of it is I spend 30 minutes with 30 people just asking questions about their struggles and listening intently to the way that they describe them. And at the very end, remember this isn't a sales conversation." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_CyBOseLu0OG.md

# T_CyBOseLu0OG_04

"I explain my suggested idea for a solution and I gauge their responses. So the point here is just speak to at least 30 human beings face to face before you go developing your marketing materials. Help before you go developing a product. You'll either be validated in your assumptions or you can learn what they really want and save yourself a lot of pain, money, and time." — sources/S_ig_joshhamiltonsworld_CyBOseLu0OG.md
