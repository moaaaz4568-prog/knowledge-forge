# T_DX_0405OqtS_04

"What that spike means is that less people dropped off at that moment than we expected based on how the video is trending. So what you want to do is find in the video where that spike happened and extract out what you said and the visuals you showed. You can then use this in a future video as the hook. This is called retention hunting. Nobody talks about this, but it's actually very powerful." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_DX_0405OqtS.md
