# T_DX6rfVqOXAJ_01

"If you want to get viewers that actually buy your stuff, you gotta change how you're approaching your hooks. My friend Bryer calls this a super hook. I call it a trust anchor. To get someone to view, you just need to make it sound interesting. But to get someone to view and buy, you need them to trust that you know what you're talking about." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_DX6rfVqOXAJ.md

# T_DX6rfVqOXAJ_02

"And to get them to trust, you need proof that what you're saying is actually true. So the second line in your hook needs to signal proof. If you do this, you will 10x lead conversion. Now there's four ways to signal proof in the second line of a hook. The first way is called direct results. And this is the easiest. It's when you have legit quantifiable results of the outcome the buyer wants." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_DX6rfVqOXAJ.md

# T_DX6rfVqOXAJ_03

"Now if you don't have direct results, the next best trust anchor is called effort maxing. In this case, instead of using proof, you just justify how much effort went into the solution you came up with. It could be hours spent, reps taken, interviews conducted, some metric to measure effort." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_DX6rfVqOXAJ.md

# T_DX6rfVqOXAJ_04

"Whatever the highest level of perceived work is, share the lengths you were willing to go to come up with this solution. Now if you don't have either of those, the third best trust anchor is called credibility hijacking. This is when you justify your claim by borrowing proof from someone else who caused it." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_DX6rfVqOXAJ.md

# T_DX6rfVqOXAJ_05

"For example, you could say, 'This is Hormozi's $100 million method,' or, 'This is a study that the FDA just published.' It's still quantifiable, but it's not first-person data, it's third-person. And these are obviously less powerful than having your own proof, but it's way better than nothing. Now the fourth and final trust anchor is association. And this is the weakest type." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_DX6rfVqOXAJ.md

# T_DX6rfVqOXAJ_06
