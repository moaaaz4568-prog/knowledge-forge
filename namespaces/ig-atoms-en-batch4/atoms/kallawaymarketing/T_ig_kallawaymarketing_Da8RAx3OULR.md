# T_Da8RAx3OULR_02

# T_Da8RAx3OULR_03

"And the villain can typically show up in one of three ways: The old way of doing things, a false or outdated belief, or a shared negative emotion that comes from the old way of doing things or the false belief. Now what you want to do is position your hero take as the opposite against the villain. This creates bigger contrast, which drives more curiosity and audience buy-in." — sources/S_ig_kallawaymarketing_Da8RAx3OULR.md
