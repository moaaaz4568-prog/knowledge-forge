# T_DTjnMisE-XA_01

"You can join another fifty masterminds and you can buy a bunch of new courses. If you're actually trying to scale, you need way more implementation-based stuff rather than information-based stuff. A lot of these things are hyper valuable. The information is golden juice. You're just like, oh my god, I want to sit and go through this course because it's like amazing." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DTjnMisE-XA.md

# T_DTjnMisE-XA_02

"But that's a trap because it's so comfortable. It's such a fun and good thing to just gather that information and you keep buying them, you keep spending your money on them. And most cases, the constraint is just that you need to execute on those things that you need to do to grow or get more of your own time back." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DTjnMisE-XA.md

# T_DTjnMisE-XA_03

"As an example, if you're spending all your time in new masterminds and shit, what if you just spent that money on more employees? What if you just spent that money on actual execution-based stuff like hiring an agency to do key task for you? That's the sort of stuff you need to shift into as you start getting past like 30, 40, 50K a month." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DTjnMisE-XA.md

# T_DTjnMisE-XA_04

"Maybe you're in one mastermind, maybe you buy a course or a bit of consulting for specific problems that you have. But in general, there needs to be a huge bias towards executional purchases. Like I need someone to go and do this. Like I need boots on the ground rather than another mastermind guy to come in and tell you what to do." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DTjnMisE-XA.md

# T_DTjnMisE-XA_05

"Because the other thing is that eventually too many people telling you what to do becomes just like conflicting interest. You need to kind of just stick to one methodology, scale, then execute. As simple as it sounds, this is a huge mistake people make around that revenue level." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DTjnMisE-XA.md
