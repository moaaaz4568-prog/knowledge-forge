"The number one reason why I don't like running one ad per ad set for most people is very, very simple and nobody talks about it at all. Some of your ads will be very profitable, yes, but you'll have a lot of very, very unprofitable ads that will blow up your budget and completely destroy your ROAS and you won't even know why." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DcVQJHPzyul.md

# T_DcVQJHPzyul_02

"The reason why is because your cost of testing under this model is very, very high. Unless your winning ads can hold scale well enough to pay for that and more to create profit, you're essentially just not going to have any profit under this one ad per ad set model. It's just not going to work, especially if you're going to start trying to test at volume." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DcVQJHPzyul.md
