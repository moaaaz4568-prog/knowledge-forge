# T_DE0645yMMrC_01

"Making an irresistible offer around a recurring service is simply not possible. So if you're selling an agency service, a software service, or some sort of monthly consulting, what you need to know is that because you are charging someone on a recurring basis, there is inherently more risk and it's just harder to convince someone that it's an irresistible thing." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DE0645yMMrC.md

# T_DE0645yMMrC_02

"So if you're trying to sell something that's recurring, like for example a software, and you're gonna try and sell that to a complete stranger, you're always gonna struggle because they're just gonna think it's risky, which means your ads aren't gonna work as well and your sales cycle is gonna be slower, even if it's like a hundred bucks a month." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DE0645yMMrC.md

# T_DE0645yMMrC_03

"What you wanna do is package up your recurring service into a one-time setup offer so you can introduce yourself to the client with a no-brainer offer, put a guarantee on it, and then upsell to your recurring offer." — sources/S_ig_leoxmoore_DE0645yMMrC.md

# T_DE0645yMMrC_04
