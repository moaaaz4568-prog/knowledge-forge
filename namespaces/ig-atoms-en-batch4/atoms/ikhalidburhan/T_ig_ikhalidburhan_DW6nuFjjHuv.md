# T_DW6nuFjjHuv_01

"Fī shay gharīb jadā shūf fatar ākhiran mṣ ṣūnā muḥtawā wa ḥaqīqat al-munfajjān. Wūllī hūwa taʿdīlak l-bāyu alf marra, mā rāḥ y-fīdik walā ʿazzis sumʿatak fī ʿālam tak-shakhṣiya walā yaḥaggig lak ma biʿāt walā ay shayʾ. Nafs ashyaʾ li-surūt al-provāil, li-takhasṣuṣ li-bit-ḥutt jam bismak li-hāyliṭ, allī huwa kull hādi umūr umūr thānawiyya w laysat asāsiyya." — sources/S_ig_ikhalidburhan_DW6nuFjjHuv.md
