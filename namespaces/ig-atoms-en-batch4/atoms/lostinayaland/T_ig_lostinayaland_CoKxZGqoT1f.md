# T_CoKxZGqoT1f_01

"Highlining in its essence is a form of movement meditation, which really brings you into the present moment and connects you with the line, the nature that you're surrounded in, and the people that bring everybody together." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_CoKxZGqoT1f.md

# T_CoKxZGqoT1f_02

"Prepping all the gear, and then once you're actually in the flow of backpacks on and leaving to arrive to the destination, which will be the top of the mountain or the rigging spot, that momentum almost just will push you through anything, I think. I wouldn't say highlining is a lonely sport. Realistically, the slacklining that you're doing is only a small part of the whole highline experience." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_CoKxZGqoT1f.md

# T_CoKxZGqoT1f_03

"Like one of the biggest parts for highlining for me is connecting with the people around you that are also motivated to achieve goals in this area. When I first got on the line, I could appreciate the beauty, but I think now it's a lot more of appreciation for the moment rather than overwhelming emotions that almost you can't control." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_CoKxZGqoT1f.md

# T_CoKxZGqoT1f_04

"There's nothing like seeing other people going through what you've been through, and it bonds you to another level. You're all in it together. Nobody's giving up, nobody's bringing each other down. Like it's all about the vision, which is that line at the top of that peak that you really, really want to walk on and maybe do some tricks on or whatever." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_CoKxZGqoT1f.md

# T_CoKxZGqoT1f_05
