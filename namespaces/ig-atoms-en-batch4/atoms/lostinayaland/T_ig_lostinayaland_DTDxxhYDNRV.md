"Most creatives think that negotiation is some sort of wizard skill, like there are some special words that you can say on a call in order to magically get paid more. That's bullshit. Negotiation doesn't start when you ask for more money. It starts way before that. First, you actually have to care." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_DTDxxhYDNRV.md

# T_DTDxxhYDNRV_02

"No client out there is going to invest more in you if your work is staying at the same level and there is no visible effort from your side in order to take it to the next level. Second thing is honesty from the start. If you want your client to be clear with you about their goals, you also have to be honest with them about yours." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_DTDxxhYDNRV.md

# T_DTDxxhYDNRV_03

"Are you gonna treat this project as a one-off thing, or do you think there is, you know, the potential of this being a long-term partnership? Basically, you gotta tell the client, are you doing this for the money? Like, is this gonna feel transactional, or do you see room to grow together? And last but not least, feedbacks and debriefs." — sources/S_ig_lostinayaland_DTDxxhYDNRV.md

# T_DTDxxhYDNRV_04
