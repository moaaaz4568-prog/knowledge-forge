This is how most people cut out silences in their content. They remove the pause and then put the clips next to each other. But this still results in a slight silence between the cuts. People's attention spans are way too short for that. So what you want to do is use a J cut. This is where you drag the audio back over the end of the previous clip. Now your next sentence will start as the last one is finishing, removing the silence completely.
