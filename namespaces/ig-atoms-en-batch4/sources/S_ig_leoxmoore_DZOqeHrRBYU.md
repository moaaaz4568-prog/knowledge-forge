So should you run CBO or ABO to scale your ads? And for context, we took this client right here from 100K a month to 200K a month in four months just because we got this right. First, you need to understand that ad set budget optimization means you are forcing spend onto individual ad sets and campaign budget optimization or CBO means that you just let Meta allocate the spend across multiple ad sets at the campaign level, meaning it will just choose where to put the spend. So what ends up happening is Meta will consolidate the spend into only two to three of your ads, meaning most of what your budget is going into is gonna be very limited towards what Meta thinks is the highest probability to work. Which sounds like a good thing except for the fact that it opens you up to a good amount of risk. And the reason why is because if your spend is only going to two to three ads, eventually those ads are gonna fatigue and then your entire budget is essentially gonna have to then be ripped into new untested ads, which is the exact reason why some people opt for the ABO structure, which gives you way more control over the budget distribution and allows you to force spend onto specific ad sets. But the question is which one is gonna work for you in your situation? And unfortunately the answer, much like everything in life, is that it depends. Because for example, let's say you're running a lead gen funnel for a high ticket offer, you're going to have lower total conversion volume.
