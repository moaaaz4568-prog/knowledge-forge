Something one of my mentors told me once that really stuck with me for a long time is the concept of luck engineering. You need to give yourself more opportunities to become lucky. If I'm trying to close a client, will I have a better chance at doing that by sending 100 emails or a thousand emails? If I'm looking to grow my personal brand, would I get more lucky posting twice a month or twice a week? So if you have a goal and you're trying to achieve something, just think about luck engineering. Quality inputs plus consistency, that's when luck happens.
