Landing pitches as a filmmaker happens more easily when you understand that businesses don't hire filmmakers, people do. Even though you're solving a business's problem, you need to write your email for one person, and that's the brand representative who is reading it. I used to send out emails on emails and waste my time creating decks that probably didn't even get opened. And then I was like, wait a minute, let me learn more about the person who is actually reading my email instead of only the brand itself. So I started with researching their social media, getting a feel of who they are, seeing if we have any mutual connections. And from there, I'd craft a message that just felt more personal. Suddenly, it wasn't only about pitching, it was about finding common ground and offering something fresh to them.
