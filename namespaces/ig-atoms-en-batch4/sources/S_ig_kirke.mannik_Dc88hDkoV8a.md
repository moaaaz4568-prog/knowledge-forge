Is roofing a good industry to target with cold email? Well, after running a cold email agency for three years, the answer is yes, but not for the reason you think. Local service businesses open cold email at around 38 to 52%. For SaaS businesses, that sits around 22%. And no, not because roofers love cold email, but because their inbox isn't full. A SaaS business owner gets hundreds of cold emails a week. A roofer gets a handful. That means if you send a cold email to a roofing company owner, you are competing with five other people instead of 200. You would almost never see that when targeting SaaS businesses. But before you write that email, remember this: speak their language, not yours. Roofers don't think in leads or traffic. They think calls or jobs. So say jobs. 10 more calls a week is good. Improve your lead flow is not. And yes, that applies to almost every local service business.
