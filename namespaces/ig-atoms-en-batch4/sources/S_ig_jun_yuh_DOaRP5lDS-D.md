I recently saw a quote that goes, "The only way to escape competition is through authenticity because no one can outdo you at being you," and I couldn't agree more. It's actually also the quickest way that you can grow your first 10, 000 followers on social media. Most people come onto social media and copy what everyone else is doing. So a fitness creator may film the exact same workout clips or say the exact same nutrition advice. But the problem is, is then you're competing with the tens of thousands of other creators that are thinking and doing the exact same thing. So to be uncommon, right, is to build a unique positioning angle. So for example, instead of you saying something along the lines of, "Here are my exercises that I do," you can say, "Here are seven exercises that I do in a small living space." Instead of saying, "Here is my meal prep," you can say, "This is how I meal prep as someone that has hated cooking their entire life." Instead of you saying, "This is how I optimally train," you can say, "Here are five workouts that I do every single week as a busy mom."
