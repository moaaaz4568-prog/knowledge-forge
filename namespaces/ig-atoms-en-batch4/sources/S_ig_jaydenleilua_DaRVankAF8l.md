If you actually want to get results with cold outbound, you need something called a front end offer. Not only a front end offer, but a good front end offer. Not a free guide on a topic, not a recorded webinar, not a checklist PDF, not generic free consultation. If you actually want to know if your offer is good, you have to ask yourself, does delivering this cost you real time or real resources? And if the answer to that question is yes, then you have a good front end offer. Let's say you build websites for example. I made you a list of what your website can improve on. Can I send it over? This is terrible. They do not care. Instead you want to say, I built you a custom website with XYZ. Can I send it over? And if you are actually good at what you do, then this will 99% of the time convert.
