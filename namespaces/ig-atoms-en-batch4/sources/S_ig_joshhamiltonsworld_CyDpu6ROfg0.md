Imagine you're trying to persuade two friends to join your gym. One has literally never worked out before. The other has been training at a different gym for five years. You are not going to give them both the same pitch. It's the same with landing pages. Your words should reflect your customer's mindset. If you've got a website or a landing page with warm traffic, with people looking to solve a known problem, you should have solution-led copywriting. Another website that has cold traffic, unaware that their problem actually exists, your copywriting needs to be problem-led, all about the problem that they have. Make them problem-aware. So remember, just position yourself in alignment with where your audience is in order to be able to bring them where you want them to go.
