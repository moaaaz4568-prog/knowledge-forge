Take a look at Red Bull's Instagram page. Do you see any product images? No. How about Coca Cola? Hardly. And McDonald's? Same here. The reason why these big brands don't have any product images on their pages is because they sell the feeling, not the product. Red Bull sells the feeling of adrenaline and energy. Coca Cola sells joy and happiness. And so does McDonald's. This marketing tactic is called emotional selling, where brands create an emotional connection with their customer and use the same feeling in their advertisements.
