One of the biggest mistakes creatives make is chasing external validation. It can feel so disappointing when you put so much effort into a video, you send it off all excited, only to get some run-of-the-mill feedback, zero praise and no excitement whatsoever. But you have to realize that this is exactly what you signed up for. No one owes you praise, and you're expected to deliver at a certain level. That extra care that you put in your work isn't for your client. It's for you. It shows your consistency, your professionalism, and how your work performs over time.
