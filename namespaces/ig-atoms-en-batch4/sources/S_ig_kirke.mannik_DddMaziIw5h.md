Before you send your next cold email, watch this. Your subject line is not the only thing your leads read before deciding to open your email. Open Gmail and look at any unread email in your inbox. You see the sender name, the subject line, and a bit of grey text next to it. That grey text is called preview text, and it gets drawn right from the first line of your full email. So if your email starts with Hey John or I hope you're doing well, this is what the leads see as your preview text. You get two lines of space on the screen where they decide whether to open your email or not, and you decided to waste it on I hope you're doing well. They learn nothing about why you wrote, so of course they will decide not to open it. Do this instead. Write your first line of the email as if that were your second subject line. Only then write the rest of your email. This is the biggest life hack in cold email and most people don't even know about it. So go open your last campaign in Instantly or SmartLead and look at the first line of your email. If it says anything like I hope you're doing well, you have just thrown away half the space in your inbox. Rewrite that one line before your next batch goes out and go get some leads.
