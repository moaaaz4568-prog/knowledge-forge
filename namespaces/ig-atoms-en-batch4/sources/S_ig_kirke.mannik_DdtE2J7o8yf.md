Here is exactly what to do to book calls in the DMs. Most people mess this up by asking a 30-minute call right away. Message one: Ask something they can answer in one line and make it about their situation, not yours. Something like, "Are you handling this in-house right now, or is somebody else doing it?" That's easy to answer, and whatever they say tells you exactly what they need. Message two: Help them before there's any reason for you to do so. Give them the thing you would normally save for the call, the answer, the specific fix your competitors charge money for. I know that feels like giving away the work, but this makes them trust you. Message three: The proof. Not a case study. Drop a story about your client's situation that you had wins with in the conversation. Something like, "Oh, I know exactly what you mean. I just had a client who we had the same situation with, and this is how we solved it." It's the same information as a case study, but they won't feel sold to. Do those three things and they will ask for the call. You never have to pitch it. Winning somebody's trust in the DMs is 90% of the battle won. Good luck.
