Here's my exact framework for creating video ads to get people to want to buy your stuff. Most people's video ads suck, but mine seem to work. People click on them and end up booking calls. Step one, choose your audience. You need a specific segment because you're only gonna talk to one type of person. Step two, select three core problems for that audience. These are your angles. Step four, write three body copy scripts for each angle. State the benefits of solving the problem, then state three ways you help to solve the main problem from your hook for that audience.
