Tons of advice for how to approach business if you already have an audience by people who already have an audience. But what if you're like me and you don't have one yet? Well, here's my game plan to scale my core business with no audience while building an audience in the long game. It's called the seven step business progression hypothesis. Step number one, build a product that solves a problem and keeps its promise. Step number two, use paid traffics to grow the initial client base. Step three, which is critical, build our core business to a high level of success to have the proof with which to answer the question, why should I listen to you? Step number four, use point number three to build an engaged audience through content that documents our process of achieving success. Leverage that audience into future opportunities in media and product while expanding our reach. Step number five, employ financial leverage to buy back our time by appointing world class talent into key leadership roles. Step number six, use that financial and time success in our core business vehicle to start multiplying it through acquiring other businesses, solving more problems, and making strategic investments. Step number seven, understand that points one through six will take three to five years to do well enough for it to matter. Bonus step number eight, repeat steps one through seven for each product or business line in the future portfolio.
