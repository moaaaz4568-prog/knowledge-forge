If you're starting to make money online, please do not miss out on these five legal steps that will save you thousands of dollars later. Number one, form an LLC. This will protect you legally and it is super simple to do. Just search up on Google your state plus LLC application and it usually costs you about $100. Number two, get your EIN from the IRS. It is completely free and it's like a social security number for your business. Number three, take that EIN number to your bank and open up a business bank account. This is so that your personal transactions and your business transactions are kept and organized completely separately. This is so important for tax purposes later. Step four, every time you get paid, set aside 30% for taxes later. I would advise you to just put it in a completely separate savings account so that you do not touch it. Number five, pay your estimated taxes quarterly. This will save you from getting a huge lump sum at the end of the year that you may not be able to pay if you weren't expecting it.
