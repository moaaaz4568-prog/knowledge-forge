Before you put a link in your LinkedIn post, watch this. LinkedIn wants people to stay on LinkedIn, so a post that sends them anywhere else gets shown to fewer people. So do this instead. Step one, take the link out and make the post useful on its own. So instead of, "I wrote a guide on cold email subject lines, link below," put three of the subject lines in the post itself, and say the other 17 are in the guide. Step two, where the link was, ask people to comment a word. For example, "comment subject and I'll send you all 20." Step three, send the link in a DM to everyone who commented. Every comment shows LinkedIn that people like your post, so it gets shown to more people. And every DM is a conversation with someone who asked for your help, so this is how a post turns into leads. Keep the link out of the post and let people ask you for it.
