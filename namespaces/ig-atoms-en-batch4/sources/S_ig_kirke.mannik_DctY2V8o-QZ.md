If you're struggling with converting leads to booked calls in the DMs, watch this. Opening conversations and closing them are two completely different skills. The biggest mistake people make is asking for the call way too early in the chat. Asking for a stranger to commit to 30 minutes is a huge ask. When someone answers your first message, they answer it out of curiosity. This is not interest yet. Remember, they don't know you, they don't trust you yet. So if you ask for 30 minutes of their time right after the opening, of course they're gonna ghost you. So here's what to do instead. Make the ask smaller. Before you pitch for a call, ask one question about their situation you know they would enjoy answering. Something specific about how they do the thing you would help them with. Their answer does two jobs. First, it tells you if they're a fit. And second, it gives you the exact words to use next. And then you're not asking for a call yet. Ask if you can give away more free value. That builds up trust and makes them ask for the call, not you. And in reality, some people are still gonna say no. That's just the game. You just need to send more, analyze, and then try again. And pro tip, sprinkle in some case studies mid-conversation. Not in a pushy way, but more like, oh by the way, I just had this client who was struggling with the same thing, and this is how we solved it for them. Maybe you should check it out. And if they do check it out and it works, trust me, they're gonna come back to you because they want more value and they trust you. And that's when you can pitch your call.
