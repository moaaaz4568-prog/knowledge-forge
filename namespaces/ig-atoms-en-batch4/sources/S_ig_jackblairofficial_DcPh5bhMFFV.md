When a buyer says your price is too high, he is not asking for a discount. He is asking why it costs this much. Most reps hear that question and reach for ten percent off. Here is what that ten percent actually buys you. The client who got it tells two more, and now every deal you close starts below your number. Within a year you are not the premium option anymore. You are the negotiable one. And the buyers who would have paid full price never called, because the discount told them you did not believe your own price. So answer the question instead. Walk him through what the number buys, piece by piece, and then let the silence sit. If he still walks, he was never a premium buyer. But cut the price once, and you have told every buyer in your market exactly what your word is worth.
