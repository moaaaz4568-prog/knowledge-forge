The reason your content is flopping is because you have an origin ality problem. Now I'm gonna tell you exactly how to solve this, but if you don't fix it soon, your personal brand will not grow. Here's how viewer psychology actually works: people are scrolling the feed looking for new things they haven't heard before. When a niche is relatively untapped, you can say just about anything and it will feel new when they watch it. But as your niche gets more saturated, all the low-hanging fruit ideas are no longer new—they get regurgitated over and over and lose their novelty. And this matters because viewers will not trust, follow, or buy from you if your content doesn't feel new to them. So if you want your content to start improving, you need to increase your originality density. This is the ratio of videos you make that are new and original ideas. The first way is to actually come up with new stuff that no one has ever heard before—literally being original. But to be fair, this is pretty hard to do consistently unless you're just an idea machine. The second way, which is much easier, is to find an original angle or a new frame on an old idea. I call this originality wrapping. You want to wrap a known concept in a way that feels new and original. Now there are actually a bunch of different tactical options for how you can execute this originality wrapping. If you comment "original," I'll send you a doc breaking down all the different ways that I do it so you can start infusing more originality into your content.
