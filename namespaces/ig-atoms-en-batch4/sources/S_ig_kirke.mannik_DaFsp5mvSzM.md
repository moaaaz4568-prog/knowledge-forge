Some people say that your phone background does not matter and they just keep it black or default Apple and I could not agree less. An average person checks their phone 205 times a day. You check nothing else more in your day than your phone. Why not use that as a reminder to keep you on your path? I've had the weirdest phone backgrounds with people who have no idea that I've been checking their face 200 times a day. Why? Because every time I have a conversation that is very important to me, that changes the way I see life, I want to remember that. So I take a picture of the scene that we're in currently. So it might be the skyline, it might be the dinner that we're having, and later I add that as my phone background. Every time I check it, it reminds me of the new perspective that I gained. So imagine looking at the most important conversation of your life 200 times a day. Do you think you're gonna be on track? Probably. So you can either have a blank phone background that benefits you in no way and has no meaning at all, or you can have something that directs you towards your dream 200 times a day. This is why I disagree with those people.
