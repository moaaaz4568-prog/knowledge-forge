We used to compete with agencies until I decided that it was a better strategy to ally with them instead. We were running into three main problems. Number one is offering too many services where operations became overly complex and the focus on strategy with the clients suffered. Number two is charging retainers that had good margins, but it didn't reflect the impact we were having on the businesses in the long run. The third thing is the only way that we could grow the business is by adding more customers to the model and increasing infrastructure costs. So my partner Chase and I decided to make a pivot. We would partner with the absolute best execution agencies in the world at their specialized skill. Our role is to come into an already growing business, diagnose current constraints, identify opportunities for growth, strategize a prioritized plan of attack, and then orchestrate the execution of that plan by bringing in the best agencies and freelancers for the job and managing them. Chase and I cut out the execution part of our role and went all in on what we are best at, strategy and orchestration. We charge companies a one time audit of their operation and then only make a percentage of the revenue growth that we produce by running their growth departments through our agency partners. In other words, it's solely our responsibility to make sure that these companies grow due to our plan and our ability to orchestrate and direct these agencies. We've also made sure that the agencies are coming in at cost and the way that they make money is by us sharing some of the performance bonus that we get every single month of that revenue growth. What this means is we can hit our profit growth goals with just 30 clients instead of 300. The business owners still get the same thing, but in a more targeted and tactically agnostic way. We're not tied to one channel, one service, one tactic or one way of doing things, as you are in an agency model. We deliver this and nothing else, right? So our job is to use the best possible tool for the job at hand. I share this for three reasons. Number one is if you're feeling stuck, stagnant or discouraged in your current model, sometimes the next pivot is just outside of the box that you've been living in. Second reason for sharing is if you're an agency who's world class at what you do, I'd love to talk to you about bringing you into our platinum partner group and deploy you into our clients' operations.
