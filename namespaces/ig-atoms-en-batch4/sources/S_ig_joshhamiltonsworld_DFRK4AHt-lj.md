Selling anything with words boils down to eight simple copywriting steps. If it sounds basic, it is, but people overcomplicate this stuff. Here's the blueprint: tell them the problem, make the problem hurt, tell them there's a solution, show them that solution, tell them what's included, tell them how much it costs, remove the risk, and then ask them to buy. Skip these steps and you're just talking. Nail them and you're cooking.
