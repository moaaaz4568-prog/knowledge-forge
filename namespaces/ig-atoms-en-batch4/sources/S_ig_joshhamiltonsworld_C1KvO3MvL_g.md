Twelve things I'd tell myself to do differently in my next business if I had to start over. Spoiler alert, I am starting over. Don't sleep on number ten. First, start with a simple model where the risk is just whether we execute or not. Don't have high idea risk. Two, start with services, then go into technology, not the other way around.
