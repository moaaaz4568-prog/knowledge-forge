What is a marketing barometer? It is purely bridging the gap between a lead asking you "Who are you?" to when they ask you "Where can I pay? Where can I sign the SPA?" Here is how it works: Picture a scale from zero to a hundred. That is your marketing barometer. And the goal of branding is as follows: You fill up the barometer as close to a hundred as you can before you even interact with the client. So agents that rely on any other acquisition channel than personal branding, they interact with the lead where ninety percent of that barometer is empty. So they have to fill it up with calls, texts, voice notes, property tours, whatever else they can do. It's just way too much time and energy. But on the other hand, content that includes everything that your avatar or the buyer needs to know before purchasing a property shortens the sales cycle and fills up the barometer to ninety percent. That' why leads that come through brand have a way shorter sales cycle than any other acquisition channel. To stop doing endless viewings and hundreds of follow-ups or whatever they do, and start building a system that sells for you.
