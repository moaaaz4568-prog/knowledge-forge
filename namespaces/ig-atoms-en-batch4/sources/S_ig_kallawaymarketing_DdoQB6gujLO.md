"The reason your videos are flopping is because you're using way too much borrowed authority. I've worked on dozens of high-profile personal brands and this is a huge problem I see over and over.

There are three types of authority you can build in your videos: demonstrated authority, owned authority, and borrowed authority. These signal trust in this order: demonstrated is the best and borrowed is the worst.

Demonstrated authority is when you share proof of results you've gotten for others. Owned authority is where you share proof of results you've gotten for yourself. And borrowed authority is when you justify your claim with someone else's proof.

For example, imagine my hook was something like, 'This is the only storytelling framework you need to get million-view videos.' In my next line, I want to add proof that validates what I'm about to say.

Demonstrated authority would be using something like, 'I taught this method to five people and they all got million-view videos in the first two weeks.' Owned authority would be something like, 'I personally used this and it got me two million-view videos in the first two weeks.'

But borrowed authority would be something like, 'Hormozi used this and it got him million-view videos.' In this era, borrowed authority is no longer good enough, because everybody is borrowing from the same few people. And rather than trust a derivative curator, I'd rather just go to the source they're borrowing from in the first place.

Now, as a beginner when you don't have results, it's okay to use borrowed authority, but you want to sprint to building your own results so you can ascend to owned or demonstrated."
