# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtnWGgzI27j — getting rich

## Term

Getting rich appears once in this 47-word lyric, in the opening about just going to keep getting rich, you know. The phrase sits beside Cross my heart, hope to die, and to my lover, I'd never lie, so it names the wealth the singer expects to keep gaining. The oath that follows swears loyalty: never a lie to the lover, every truth attempted, and in the end it is him and I. A concrete instance is the oath after the boast: cross my heart, hope to die, never a lie.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The world, we just gon' keep getting rich, you know." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CtnWGgzI27j.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of wealth to the listener: the singer says we will keep getting rich, which includes the singer, not just the listener.
- Read as the main subject of the song: the wealth line opens the verse, but the oath of loyalty that follows carries the verse.
