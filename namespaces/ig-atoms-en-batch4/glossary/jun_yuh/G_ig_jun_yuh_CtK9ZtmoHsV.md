# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtK9ZtmoHsV — dusk till dawn

## Term

Dusk till dawn appears twice in this 18-word promise, in the repeated line about being with you from dusk till dawn. The phrase sits beside You'll never be alone and I'll be with you, so it names the full span of the night the singer will stay through. The promise is doubled by repetition, and the missing spaces after the periods in the transcript keep the lines running together. A concrete instance is the promise itself: never alone, with you from dusk till dawn.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "You'll never be alone.I'll be with you from dusk till dawn." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CtK9ZtmoHsV.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single night: from dusk till dawn is the span of the night, repeated as a promise to stay through it.
- Read as a claim about daylight: the promise covers the night only, from dusk to dawn, and says nothing about the day.
