# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtxtMwLKkj6 — love

## Term

Love appears once in this 35-word lyric, in the opening So much love that the whole thing feel like a lie. The word sits beside I don't need nobody repeated six times and the whole thing, so it names the overwhelming feeling that makes the whole thing feel unreal. The singer then insists six times that they need nobody, as if that much love left no room for anyone else. A concrete instance is the contrast: so much love it feels like a lie, then six refusals of needing anyone.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a happy love song: the love here is so big the whole thing feels like a lie, which is awe, not simple happiness.
- Read as the singer needing someone: the singer repeats six times that they need nobody, so the line denies need rather than confessing it.
