# G_ig_jun_yuh_CrgaDOTIprP — lost love

## Term

Lost love appears once in this 15-word lyric, in the claim that it is sweeter when it is finally found. The phrase sits beside past lives, hold me down, and finally found, so it names an old love that returns after being lost. Past lives could not hold the singer down, and the recovered love tastes sweeter for having been lost. A concrete instance is the payoff line itself: lost, then found, and sweeter for it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "lost love is sweeter when it's finally found." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CrgaDOTIprP.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a love that stayed lost: the line says the lost love is found again, and sweeter for it.
- Read as a claim about all lost loves: the sweetness belongs to this one recovered love, not to every love that was ever lost.
