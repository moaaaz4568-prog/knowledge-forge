# G_ig_jun_yuh_CrLqCdqK8Ig — lover

## Term

Lover appears once in this 13-word lyric, in the wish about finding a lover. The word sits beside star, feeling lonely, and find, so it names the companion a lonely star wants, not a casual date. The star image frames the singer as distant and shining yet alone. A concrete instance is the wish itself: a star, lonely, asking for a lover to find.
