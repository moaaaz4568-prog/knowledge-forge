# G_ig_jun_yuh_CrEAzicopSF — tell them apart

## Term

Tell them apart appears twice in this 23-word lyric, opening and closing the confession that the singer cannot distinguish two people. The phrase sits beside I try not to and every time I talk, so it names the failure to separate two someones in conversation. Trying not to makes no difference: the singer still cannot tell them apart. A concrete instance is the admission that every time the singer talks, the confusion returns.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I can't tell them apart. Even though I try not to." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CrEAzicopSF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a vision problem: the singer cannot distinguish two people in conversation, not two objects by sight.
- Read as a claim about one person: them is plural through the whole line, so the confusion is between two someones, not one.
