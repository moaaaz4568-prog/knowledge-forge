# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtNfVxWKfB1 — nightclub

## Term

Nightclub appears once in this 22-word lyric, in the scene in the back of the nightclub sippin' champagne. The word sits beside broke bitch, high heels six inch, and I don't trust any of these, so it names the place where the singer poses broke but dressed up. The glamour and the distrust sit together: champagne in hand, trust for no one. A concrete instance is the scene itself: high heels, back of the nightclub, champagne, and no trust.
