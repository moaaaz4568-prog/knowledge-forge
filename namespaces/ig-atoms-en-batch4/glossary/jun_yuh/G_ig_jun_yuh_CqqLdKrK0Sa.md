# G_ig_jun_yuh_CqqLdKrK0Sa — high value individual

## Term

High value individual appears four times in this 248-word talk, where the speaker rejects the idea that high value means keeping options open and treating a partner as disposable. The phrase is defined by conduct beside significant other, self healing, and self discovery journey: protecting, cherishing, and continuously developing the relationship. The speaker ties it to being physically, mentally, and spiritually capable of a prosperous relationship and to leading and providing for a family, with provisions meaning emotional and spiritual availability as well as financial support. A concrete instance is the closing test: how you treat your partner is a deep reflection of you as a person.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "A high value individual recognizes a relationship is the most intimate human connection that they'll ever develop, and it is their responsibility to protect it, to cherish it, and do everything they can possibly do to continuously develop it." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CqqLdKrK0Sa.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a wealth or status label: the talk defines high value by protecting and developing a relationship, not by income, options, or worldly success.
- Read as permission to keep options open: the speaker says the opposite, that a high value individual does not entertain others while in a relationship.
