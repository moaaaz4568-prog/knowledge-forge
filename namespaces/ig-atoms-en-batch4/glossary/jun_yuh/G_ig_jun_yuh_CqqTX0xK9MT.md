# G_ig_jun_yuh_CqqTX0xK9MT — one day

## Term

One day appears twice in this 20-word lyric, first in the line about someone loving the singer one day and then in the wait to get that loving one day. The phrase marks a hoped-for future beside love me, I will wait, and your loving, so it names the day the waiting ends rather than any calendar date. The speaker repeats the wait twice in one line, which makes the waiting itself the action of the lyric. A concrete instance is the second line: the speaker will wait, and wait, to get that loving one day.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I will wait, I will wait to get your loving one day." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CqqTX0xK9MT.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a date on a calendar: one day here is a hoped-for future, not a scheduled day the singer can name.
- Read as a promise the other person made: the line only says some say you will love me, so the love is rumored, not promised.
