# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtlDh5rIRvP — our future

## Term

Our future appears once in this 11-word line, in the same claim as its twin verse: This is our future. The phrase sits beside Remember, not just a story, and this, so it names the shared future the speaker insists is real. The line corrects anyone who would file the story away as fiction. A concrete instance is that correction: remember, not just a story, but our future.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "this is not just a story. This is our future." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CtlDh5rIRvP.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as science fiction: the line insists the story is a real shared future, not a genre label.
- Read as a solo future: our makes the future shared, so reading it as one person's fate misses the point.
