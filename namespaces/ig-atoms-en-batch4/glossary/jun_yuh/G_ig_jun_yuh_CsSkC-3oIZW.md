# G_ig_jun_yuh_CsSkC-3oIZW — forget who I am

## Term

Forget who I am appears once in this 32-word lyric, in the warning that every time you try to forget who I am, the singer will be right there to remind you again. The phrase sits beside You know me repeated five times and remind you, so it names the attempt the singer refuses to allow. Knowing the singer is presented as unavoidable: every attempt to forget meets a reminder. A concrete instance is the threat itself: try to forget, and the singer will be right there to remind you.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Every time you try to forget who I am, I'll be right there to remind you again." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CsSkC-3oIZW.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the singer being forgotten already: the line is a warning against trying, and the reminder follows every attempt.
- Read as a plea to be remembered: the tone is a threat to return and remind, not a request for remembrance.
