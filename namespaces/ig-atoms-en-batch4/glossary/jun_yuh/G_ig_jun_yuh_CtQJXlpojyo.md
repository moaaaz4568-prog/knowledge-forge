# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtQJXlpojyo — past lives

## Term

Past lives appears once in this 21-word lyric, in the line Past lives couldn't ever hold me down, lost loves. The phrase sits beside Don't wake me, I'm not dreaming, and lost loves, so it names former lives that failed to hold the singer. The singer refuses to be woken from a dream and refuses to be held by the past. A concrete instance is the refusal itself: don't wake me, and past lives can't hold me down.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a belief in reincarnation: the line uses past lives as an image of what could not hold the singer, not as a doctrine.
- Read as the singer being awake: Don't wake me, I'm not dreaming refuses waking, so the dream state is the point, not sleep.
