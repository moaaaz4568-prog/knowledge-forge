# G_ig_jun_yuh_CsXg337o0rY — mental health

## Term

Mental health appears once in this 40-word reflection, in the claim that no argument is worth damaging your mental health. The phrase sits beside peaceful life, being misunderstood, and level of perception, so it names the wellbeing the speaker protects by refusing to argue. Peace comes from deciding not to spend energy on arguments, because people only understand from their own level. A concrete instance is the rule itself: no argument is worth the damage to your mental health.
