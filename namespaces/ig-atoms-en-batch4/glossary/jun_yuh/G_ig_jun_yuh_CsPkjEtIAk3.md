# G_ig_jun_yuh_CsPkjEtIAk3 — gonna be alright

## Term

Gonna be alright appears twice in this 26-word lullaby, first for mama and then for everything. The phrase sits beside pretty baby, little baby, and don't you cry, so it names the reassurance a mother figure gives a child. The two promises cover both the mother and the whole situation, and the hush in between turns the line into a sung comfort. A concrete instance is the second promise: hush, little baby, don't cry, everything's gonna be alright.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Now hush, little baby, don't you cry, everything's gonna be alright." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_CsPkjEtIAk3.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that nothing bad will happen: the reassurance is sung to a child to stop crying, not a guarantee about the future.
- Read as the mother speaking about herself only: the second promise covers everything, not just mama.
