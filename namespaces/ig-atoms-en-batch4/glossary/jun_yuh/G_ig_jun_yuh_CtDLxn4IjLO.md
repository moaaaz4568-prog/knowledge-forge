# G_ig_jun_yuh_CtDLxn4IjLO — strangest secret

## Term

Strangest secret appears once in this 35-word talk, in the claim that this is the strangest secret. The phrase sits beside we become what we think about, thinking is so vital, and a goal is so important, so it names the rule that we become what we think about most. Thinking matters and goals matter because they decide what we become. A concrete instance is the chain itself: think about it most, and you will become that.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a hidden fact about the world: the secret is a rule about thinking and becoming, not a concealed piece of information.
- Read as positive thinking advice: the line ties thinking to goals and becoming, not to wishing alone.
