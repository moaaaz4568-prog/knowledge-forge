# G_ig_jun_yuh_CuDqM_nI7NY — healthy diet

## Term

Healthy diet appears twice in this 217-word talk, first as the claim that diet covers thoughts for the mind and soul as well as food for the stomach, and again in the promise that with it you grow from within. The phrase sits beside media that you consume, creators that you follow, and aligns with the values, so it names the whole intake, food, media, music, shows, people, judged by whether it uplifts. The speaker challenges the listener to audit that intake honestly and change what they consume first. A concrete instance is the audit itself: how much of what you consume preaches positivity and helps you grow?
