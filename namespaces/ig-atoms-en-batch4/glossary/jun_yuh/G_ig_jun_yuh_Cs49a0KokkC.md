# G_ig_jun_yuh_Cs49a0KokkC — worthless nothing

## Term

Worthless nothing appears once in this 26-word lyric, in the charge that the angry partner treats the singer like a worthless nothing. The phrase sits beside angry, jerk, and so sad, so it names the lowest value the singer is given in the relationship. The sadness follows the insult, and the singer ends unsure whether they want to see the partner at all. A concrete instance is the sequence itself: anger, insult, sadness, doubt.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "When you're angry, you're a jerk, and then you treat me like a worthless nothing." — sources/S_ig_jun_yuh_Cs49a0KokkC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the singer calling themselves worthless: the insult is what the partner does to the singer, not what the singer believes.
- Read as a breakup: the singer only says they don't know if they want to see the partner, which is doubt, not a goodbye.
