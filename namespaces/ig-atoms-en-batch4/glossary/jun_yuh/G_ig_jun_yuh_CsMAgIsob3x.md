# G_ig_jun_yuh_CsMAgIsob3x — beau soldat

## Term

Beau soldat appears once in this 11-word source, in the French opening Mon beau soldat. The phrase sits beside Days seem sometimes as if they will never end, and the source mixes French and English, opening in French and continuing in English. Beau soldat names the person addressed, a beautiful soldier, and the endless-seeming days that follow carry the longing of the line. A concrete instance is the address itself: the soldier is called beautiful, and then the days stretch out without end.
