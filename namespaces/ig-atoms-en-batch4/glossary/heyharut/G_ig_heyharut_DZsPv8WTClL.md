# G_ig_heyharut_DZsPv8WTClL — relevance

## Term

Relevance is the metric Harut says beats reach: make content for the specific people you want to attract, and reach stops mattering on its own. It appears once in this source, beside way more important than reach alone, if it's not relevant, then you're not gonna convert, and am I attracting the right people. The people worth attracting are serious, committed, with money, in a position to invest, and ready to grow toward the outcome you help with. Reach without relevance fills the views with people who will never become clients, and Harut calls those empty metrics and vanity metrics.

## Synonyms

- relevant (variant form used in the source)

## Live Example

> "And nowadays relevance is way more important than reach alone, because you can get all the reach in the world, but if it's not relevant, then you're not gonna convert these people." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DZsPv8WTClL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as targeting a smaller audience for its own sake: relevance here means attracting people who can actually become clients, not shrinking the audience as a goal.
- Read as reach being worthless: the source says reach alone is not enough, not that views never matter — views that are also relevant are what you want.
