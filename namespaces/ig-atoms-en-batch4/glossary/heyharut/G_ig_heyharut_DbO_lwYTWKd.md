# G_ig_heyharut_DbO_lwYTWKd — peace of mind

## Term

Peace of mind is the item Harut puts last and calls most important in a bare list of things worth having: Codecademy, a great mentor, a great virtual assistant, an expensive gym membership, saying no, Google Drive storage, deleting social media until tomorrow, and then peace of mind. The phrase appears once in this source, beside saying no and deleting social media until tomorrow, and the list gives no explanation for any item, so peace of mind stands as the payoff the other items are meant to buy. It is the only item framed as a state rather than a purchase or a habit, and the most importantly in front of it is the whole argument. The source names what matters without teaching how to get it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Codecademy, a great mentor, a great virtual assistant, an expensive gym membership, saying no, Google Drive storage, deleting social media until tomorrow, and most importantly, a peace of mind." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DbO_lwYTWKd.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a product recommendation: peace of mind is listed beside purchases, but the source never prices it or says where to buy it, so it is the goal, not another item to shop for.
- Read as advice to delete social media forever: the list says deleting social media until tomorrow, so the break is temporary and the peace is the point, not the deletion.
