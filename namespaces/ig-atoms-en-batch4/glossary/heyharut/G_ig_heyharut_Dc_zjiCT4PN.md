# G_ig_heyharut_Dc_zjiCT4PN — free

## Term

Free is the pricing move Harut says can change a business's whole trajectory: his team made a twenty-one-hour course free after charging for it, and it brought more clients than the program itself ever did. The word appears fourteen times in this source, beside twenty-one-hour course, low ticket product, and monetize 1%, and the rule is to make the free thing genuinely good, then monetize one percent of the audience with something expensive and hands-on. A five-dollar price can cut conversion a hundred times against free, so the middle is where most people struggle. Free here is the top of a funnel that ends in a premium offer, not charity.
