# G_ig_heyharut_Daq8k2RzwtJ — New Meta Funnel

## Term

New Meta Funnel is the name Harut gives to the strategy Alex Hormozi uses on his second YouTube channel: posting thousands of videos and making even more money than on his main channel with four million subscribers. The phrase appears once in this source, beside over one hundred K a month with our content system, post over twenty times in a single day, and over one hundred try reels in a day. Harut says his own team uses the same strategy, and he promises a free live breakdown of it, with no replay. The funnel here is a volume system — many posts a day across Instagram and YouTube — not a single page or ad.
