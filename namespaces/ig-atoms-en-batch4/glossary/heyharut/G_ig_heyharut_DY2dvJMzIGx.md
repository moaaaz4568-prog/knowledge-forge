# G_ig_heyharut_DY2dvJMzIGx — double down

## Term

Double down is what Harut says to do at the easy stages of scaling: do more of what is already working instead of changing the approach. It appears once in this source, beside thirty thousand dollars to one hundred thousand dollars, different stages, and one hundred thousand dollars in six weeks. The hard stages are the ones that demand a change in what you are doing, like zero to one thousand dollars or ten thousand to thirty thousand. He points to a client who scaled to one hundred thousand dollars in six weeks as the case where doubling down was the move.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because there are different stages that either break or that you just need to double down." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DY2dvJMzIGx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as doing more of everything: doubling down here means more of what is already working at that stage, not more effort on a method that has stopped working.
- Read as a guarantee: the source says the thirty-to-one-hundred-thousand stage is relatively easier, not effortless, and the hard stages still require changing what you do.
