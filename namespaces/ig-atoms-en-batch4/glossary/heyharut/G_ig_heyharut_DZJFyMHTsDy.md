# G_ig_heyharut_DZJFyMHTsDy — millionaire

## Term

Millionaire is the dare shouted at Harut in this source: be a millionaire in the next six months, like the voice keeps insisting. It appears once, beside I see you on the lives all the time, now he's here, and Hi guys, my name is Harut. The clip is a rowdy greeting, not a lesson — the same dare, the same six-month window, and Harut answering with his own name. Nothing here explains how to become a millionaire; the word is the challenge itself, thrown at him between jokes.
