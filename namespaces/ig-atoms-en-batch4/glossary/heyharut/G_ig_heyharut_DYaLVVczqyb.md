# G_ig_heyharut_DYaLVVczqyb — comfort zone

## Term

Comfort zone is what Harut pushes Ivan out of: the familiar work where income varied month to month with no stability and no clean steps. It appears once in this source, beside do the uncomfortable work, achieve my potential, and 40k in the first month. Ivan says Harut believes in him more than he believes in himself, sees potential he cannot see, and tries to pull the greatness out of him. The result he names is a launch that paid off the entire program in the first month, followed by record month after record month.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "He was there to help me achieve my potential, to do the uncomfortable work and push me out of my comfort zone." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DYaLVVczqyb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a motivational slogan: the comfort zone here is Ivan's specific situation — varying income, no system — and leaving it meant doing the uncomfortable work on a real launch.
- Read as Harut doing the work for him: Ivan stresses that Harut pushes and believes, while the results follow only if Ivan does his own work.
