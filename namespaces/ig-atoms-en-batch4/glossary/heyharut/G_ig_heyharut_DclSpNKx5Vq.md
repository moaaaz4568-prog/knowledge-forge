# G_ig_heyharut_DclSpNKx5Vq — sales calls

## Term

Sales calls are the two options the client in this testimonial refuses: she does not like sales calls, and she will not play the volume game, until Harut explains a concept that bridges the two. The phrase appears once in this source, while the singular sales call appears once more, beside volume game, voice memos, and twenty-two people, and the launch right after brought in twenty-two people against a top end of fifteen, without a single sales call. She had planned for years, quit her job in record time, and hit a wall after her first cohort, and Harut's personal voice memos and one-on-ones are what she credits. Sales calls here are the avoided chore the testimonial is built to solve, not the product.

## Live Example

> "The launch that I ran immediately after brought in 22 people when I thought that 15 was the top end of possible, and I did it without a single sales call." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DclSpNKx5Vq.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of twenty-two clients: the launch brought twenty-two people once, so the number is one client's result, not a guarantee.
- Read as a ban on sales calls: the client avoided them for one launch, so the testimonial shows an alternative, not a rule for every offer.
