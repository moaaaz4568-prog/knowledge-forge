# G_ig_heyharut_DY-h1mOTeFw — live workshop

## Term

A live workshop is Harut's monthly format for doubling a business without more traffic: pick one date and time, invite the audience that already follows, and teach one topic live for an hour. It appears once in this source, beside webinar, Q and A, and nurtured an audience live for one hour. He contrasts it with a plain webinar, where people are invited in and then just pitched; here the teaching comes first and the call to action only closes the hour. A client with only a couple hundred people in the room doubled his business with it, which is the proof he offers.

## Synonyms

- webinar (variant form used in the source)

## Live Example

> "It's going live once a month, which is called the live workshop or you can call it a webinar, which was more popular back then." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DY-h1mOTeFw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pitch dressed as teaching: the format only works here because the hour genuinely helps first, and the invitation to work with him comes after the audience has been nurtured.
- Read as a big-audience tactic: Harut says the audience does not have to be huge, and his example client doubled his business with only a couple hundred people in the room.
