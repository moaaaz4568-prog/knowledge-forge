# G_ig_heyharut_DczAExVhLnu — chant

## Term

This source has no conceptual term: the label `chant` is a type label only, because the whole source is a single name repeated with nothing to define. The name Alex Hermozzi is repeated with variations like Hermozzi and Moezi, and no business idea, instruction, or named concept appears anywhere in the eleven words. The chant is quoted as a name fragment, so the entry records its wording and nothing more.
