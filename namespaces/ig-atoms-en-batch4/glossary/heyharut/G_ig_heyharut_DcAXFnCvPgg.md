# G_ig_heyharut_DcAXFnCvPgg — more leads

## Term

More leads is what Harut says to focus on before better conversions: when asked whether to chase more leads or better conversions first, he answers that more leads is better, because it is objective. The phrase appears three times in this source, beside mental masturbation, more demand, and double their business, and his charge is that endlessly improving conversions is mental masturbation while more leads is a better spend of time. He still says conversions matter and sometimes they focus on them, but on average focusing on more demand is the less likely mistake. More leads here is the objective volume to chase first, not a dismissal of conversions.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Should I focus on more leads or better conversions first? More leads is better, I think." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcAXFnCvPgg.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on improving conversions: Harut says conversions are very important sometimes, so the advice is about order, not about ignoring them.
- Read as a promise to double any business: the doubling happened for people they helped without more traffic, so it is their past result, not a guarantee.
