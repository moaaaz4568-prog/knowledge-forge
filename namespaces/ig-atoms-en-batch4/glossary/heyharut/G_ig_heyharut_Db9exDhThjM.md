# G_ig_heyharut_Db9exDhThjM — volume

## Term

Volume is Harut's answer to slow growth: doing far more of what already works instead of hunting for a new idea. The word appears twice in this source, beside over 1000 times, 72 times every three days, and one an hour, and the test is whether the extra posting brings more hooks, more angles, and more lead magnets to try. He reposted one Hormozi clip more than a thousand times and turned the same winning material into simple Threads posts, and the flywheel he describes is that more posting brings more data about what works, which lets him do still more of it. The point is not noise for its own sake: volume is how he compresses the time it takes to learn what actually works.

## Live Example

> "But what I learned is that volume is the answer, not only because you will get more views, but also because it helps you to test more hooks, more angles, more lead magnets, and you will see what's actually working." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Db9exDhThjM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as posting anything constantly: volume here means repeating the material that already worked, not flooding the feed with untested posts.
- Read as a guarantee of views: Harut ties volume to learning faster through more data, so a high count with no working material to repeat proves nothing.
