# G_ig_heyharut_DY5ks3tzF5k — good friction

## Term

Good friction is Harut's exception to the rule against adding steps to a funnel: extra steps are fine when the friction itself helps. It appears once in this source, beside increase conversions, the more steps the less people go to it, and overrated. The usual case is the opposite — more steps mean fewer people reach the end — so a step earns its place only by being good friction. He judges the same way elsewhere in the video, calling reusing a winning topic underrated and testing a new hook every post overrated.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Adding more steps to your funnel to increase conversions. Overrated. It depends. If it's a good friction, it's great." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DY5ks3tzF5k.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to add steps freely: good friction is the rare case where a step helps, while the default in this source is that more steps lose people.
- Read as friction that only slows buyers down: the friction here has to be good for the buyer, not merely a hurdle before the offer.
