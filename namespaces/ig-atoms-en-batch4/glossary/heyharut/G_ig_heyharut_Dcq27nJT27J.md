# G_ig_heyharut_Dcq27nJT27J — ideal avatar

## Term

The ideal avatar is who Hormozi optimises for instead of going viral: content his ideal avatar watches, that brings clients and profit, made by demonstrating expertise with real businesses. The phrase appears three times in this source, beside not trying to go viral, demonstrating expertise, and the right views are cash, and the proof is a second channel fifty times smaller bringing more sales than the main one. He posts coaching sessions and birthday tips his avatar actually wants, and the comments say they prefer these videos. The ideal avatar here is the specific buyer the content is made for, not a mass audience.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "he's not optimizing for views, he's optimizing for actually making content that his ideal avatar watches, that actually brings clients and brings profit for his companies, which is great." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dcq27nJT27J.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that going viral is bad: Harut says not to fear views, so long as they come from the right people in a sustainable way, and the warning is about empty views.
- Read as proof that Hormozi quit growing: the second channel is smaller yet earns more, so the strategy is about who watches, not about stopping growth.
