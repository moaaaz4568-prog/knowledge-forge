# G_ig_heyharut_Dcq8ginv-P_ — lives

## Term

Lives are where Harut spots his regulars: he greets them, says his name, and tells them he sees them on the lives all the time. The word appears once in this source, beside don't say I didn't say, my name is Harut, and all the time, and the clip is the same short greeting as its twin, a hello with no lesson. He warns the viewer not to say he never said so, then introduces himself to people who already know him. Lives here are the live sessions the audience keeps returning to, not a one-off broadcast.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Hi guys, my name is Harut. We see you on the lives all the time." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dcq8ginv-P_.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a new announcement: the clip repeats the same greeting as its twin, so it introduces nothing and announces nothing.
- Read as evidence of a huge live audience: he greets people he already recognises, so the hello shows regulars, not crowd size.
