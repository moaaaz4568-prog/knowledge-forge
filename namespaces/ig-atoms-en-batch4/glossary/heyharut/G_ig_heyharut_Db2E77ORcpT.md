# G_ig_heyharut_Db2E77ORcpT — signals

## Term

Signals are the outside proof that content is building trust: people doing what the video asks, outside the video itself. The word appears once in this source, beside you know what your content is building trust, join our free community, and people start following the things that you ask them to do. Harut's test is simple: say join the free community in a video, and if nobody does, the video was not helpful enough yet. When people do follow the ask, that is influence — and influence only comes when they trust you.

## Live Example

> "You know what your content is building trust when you get signals outside of the content." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Db2E77ORcpT.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as view counts: signals here happen outside the content — people acting on what was asked — not likes or views inside it.
- Read as a metric to buy: the signal only counts when people follow the ask because the video helped them, not because they were pushed or paid.
