# G_ig_heyharut_DcF0XEjRCD7 — one thing

## Term

One thing is the focus Harut says is missing when growth stalls: you are probably doing too many different things and not doing enough volume of the one thing that matters the most. The phrase appears four times in this source, beside too many different things, all over the place, and if I could only do one thing, and the signs he names are stress, forgetting things, being late to meetings, and being late to respond to people. The question to ask is what you would do if you absolutely had to do only one of the things you do every day, and then to start doing that thing and eliminate everything else. The one thing here is the single daily work to protect until it succeeds enough to diversify.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You are probably doing too many different things and you are not doing enough volume of the one thing that matters the most." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcF0XEjRCD7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as doing one thing forever: Harut says to diversify after reaching enough success, so the single focus is a stage, not a life sentence.
- Read as a diagnosis of laziness: the signs are stress and lateness from overload, so the problem is too many things, not too little effort.
