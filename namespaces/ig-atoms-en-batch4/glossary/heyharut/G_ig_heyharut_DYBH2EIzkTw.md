# G_ig_heyharut_DYBH2EIzkTw — compounding

## Term

Compounding is what Harut says he missed when he chased the fastest, easiest way to grow: a business that keeps growing from what was already built instead of hitting a ceiling. The word appears once in this source, beside build something that compounds over time, five million dollars of actual revenue, and the path of the least resistance. The easy path is also the easy path to copy, so growth built that way stalls once others replicate it. His example is a growing YouTube channel: hard to build and slow to start, but far harder for anyone to copy once it works.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as interest on money: compounding here is business growth that builds on itself, not a financial return on an investment.
- Read as a ban on easy tactics: Harut says everything works, including cold outreach — the point is to choose the path others cannot copy, not to avoid easy wins entirely.
