# G_ig_heyharut_Dboz86-zrbv — inconsistent months

## Term

Inconsistent months is the problem Noah Little says he brought to Harut: months of 5K, then 20K, then zero, then 10, with an up and down he did not like. The phrase appears once in this source, beside best record months, enterprise CSM, and customer outcomes, and the turnaround Noah reports is two best record months ever, 50K and then 65K, in the first two months of working together. He praises Harut's customer outcomes and customer service as elite level, with weekly writing, long voice messages, and help through better LinkedIn content, better VSLs, better sales, and raising his prices. Inconsistent months here is the revenue swing the testimonial is built to solve.
