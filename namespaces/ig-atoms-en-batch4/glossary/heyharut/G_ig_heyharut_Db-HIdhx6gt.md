# G_ig_heyharut_Db-HIdhx6gt — tangible

## Term

Tangible is the test Harut puts on an offer: can the buyer see a concrete end result, or is the outcome vague? The word appears three times in this source, beside How do I make my offer outcome more tangible, Most offers that do well have tangible end results, and if you're not making 100k a month yet. His answer to a weak offer is to name how much, when, why, and with what methods — the numbers and the timing that make the result real. The 100k a month target itself is an example: he picked it because it is what most people want to accomplish right now.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a physical product: tangible here means a concrete, measurable outcome the buyer can picture — not something you can touch.
- Read as a pricing rule: the source ties tangibility to the offer's outcome, not to its price — a cheap offer can be vague and an expensive one can be concrete.
