# G_ig_heyharut_DXsrDjJk8aS — high ticket

## Term

High ticket means a premium-priced offer in Harut's Bad/Better/Best ladder, where the Best tier sells high ticket in a way that is not directly tied to his time. It appears twice in this source, beside low ticket, gazillion people, and selling to average people at average prices. The contrast is the point: average offers to average people at average prices sit at the Bad end, while high ticket sits at the Best end. He pairs the same ladder with sticking to one strategy and qualifying people in the DMs before a call.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Best: Sell high ticket, but in a way that is not directly tied to your time." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DXsrDjJk8aS.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a price tag alone: high ticket here is a way of selling — premium pricing untied from his time — not just any expensive product.
- Read as advice to charge more for the same work: the Best tier changes how the offer is delivered, not only its price, and the ladder also demands qualifying buyers in the DMs first.
