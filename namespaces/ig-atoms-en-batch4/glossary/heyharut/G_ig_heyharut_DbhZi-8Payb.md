# G_ig_heyharut_DbhZi-8Payb — hook

## Term

Hook is the absurd opening Harut uses to prove hooks matter: he claims he made 15 million dollars, actually like 14 million, in five days at the age of 23, then admits it is not true but it got your attention. The word appears once in this source, beside retention, proven hooks, and comment the word hooks, with five more uses as hooks, and the lesson is that normal and real hooks grew the Instagram to tens of thousands of extra followers in three months. He sells the point with a playbook of proven hooks based on Hormozi's content, improved by what worked for them, and the hook is the attention device, not the content itself. A hook here is the opening that earns retention, and the false millions are the demonstration.
