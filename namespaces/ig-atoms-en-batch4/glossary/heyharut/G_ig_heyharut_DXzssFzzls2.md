# G_ig_heyharut_DXzssFzzls2 — slow to trust

## Term

Slow to trust is Harut's rule for B2B partnerships: when a person gives you the feeling that something is off, believe that feeling and move slowly. It appears twice in this source, beside nine out of ten times, alternative motives, and hundreds of thousands of dollars. The warning comes from his own record: every time he ignored the feeling he got screwed, and every time he listened it never let him down. He extends the same rule to reputation, which takes years to build and can be damaged fast by trusting one bad person too quickly.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Be slow to trust because otherwise if you get burned, it's going to hurt for a very long time." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DXzssFzzls2.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never trusting anyone: slow to trust means taking it very slowly with a person who feels off, not refusing every partnership or introduction in B2B.
- Read as a claim that people never change: Harut says people do change and improve, but assuming they won't is what saves the time, money, and stress.
