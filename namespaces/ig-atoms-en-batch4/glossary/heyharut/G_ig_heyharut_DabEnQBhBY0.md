# G_ig_heyharut_DabEnQBhBY0 — next opponent

## Term

Next opponent is the twist Harut ends this short clip with: you move to the next level, and the next opponent waiting for you is yourself. The phrase appears once in this source, beside Congratulations, you're moving to the next level, and a voice saying I never expected to reach this level. The clip is a celebration that turns into a challenge — reaching the level was the easy part, and beating yourself is what comes next. It is a one-line idea, not a method, and the whole source is barely longer than the line itself.
