# G_ig_heyharut_DbjKgF2zhOf — Heart Funnel

## Term

Heart Funnel is Harut's name for the Hormozy Highlight Funnel, a content strategy for 2026 and 2027 in which every video is posted from the heart and ends with a call to action to a lead magnet. The name appears once in this source, beside Hormozy Highlight Funnel, twenty times a day, and forty times a day, with one use of the full name Hormozy Highlight Funnel, and the model is Hormozi's second channel, which posted one thousand and three hundred videos in a few months. The channel is 40 to 50 times smaller than the main one yet makes more sales, because every video ends by sending viewers to the lead magnet. The Heart Funnel is volume with a heart and a lead magnet, not a new platform.

- Hormozy Highlight Funnel

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a funnel software: the Heart Funnel is a posting pattern of heartfelt videos ending in a lead-magnet call to action, not a tool.
- Read as a claim that small channels earn more: the second channel earns more sales because of its lead magnet and volume, so size alone is not the mechanism.
