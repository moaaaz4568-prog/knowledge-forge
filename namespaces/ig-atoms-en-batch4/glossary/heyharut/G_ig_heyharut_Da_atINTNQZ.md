# G_ig_heyharut_Da_atINTNQZ — clients maxing

## Term

Clients maxing is Harut's play on looks maxing: stop chasing a better face and start chasing better clients instead. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside ugly and rich or beautiful and broke, a year ago I was getting clients at 99 dollars a month, and churn was about 10 percent. The proof he gives is his own pricing: at 99 dollars a month the community churned at ten percent, and in every niche he coached, charging more brought better people who expected less. His rule is to raise the price, stick through the fear of rejection, and over-deliver for the clients who stay.

> "If you choose the first one, then stop looks maxing and please start clients maxing." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Da_atINTNQZ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as getting as many clients as possible: clients maxing here means better clients through higher prices, not a bigger headcount.
- Read as looks advice: the looks are only the setup of the joke — the point is to stop optimizing appearance and start optimizing who you serve.
