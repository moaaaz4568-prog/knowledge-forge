# G_ig_heyharut_DcU9iCFx1YX — morning routine

## Term

The morning routine is a joke schedule Harut performs as if it were real: waking at 1:58 AM to a call from the Stripe CEO reporting two to three billion dollars, coffee with Hormozi in Las Vegas, teaching Elon Musk, then chasing chickens in Armenia for protein. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside Stripe CEO, Las Vegas, and very sarcastic entrepreneur, and the gag peaks with nine businesses built, scaled, and sold before 8 AM, all untaxed thanks to powerful connections. By 9 AM he is scrolling Instagram and realises he forgot to post, which is the only honest beat in the routine. The morning routine here is satire of guru schedules, not a plan anyone could follow.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a real schedule: the routine includes billion-dollar calls from a CEO and flights to Las Vegas, so none of its steps are instructions to copy.
- Read as proof the method works: the routine ends with him forgetting to post, so the joke undercuts the success story it pretends to tell.
