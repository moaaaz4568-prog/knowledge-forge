# G_ig_heyharut_DYILTqgzjrn — shiny object

## Term

A shiny object is the distraction that makes people sabotage their own progress: something new appears, they chase it, and they stop compounding on what already works. It appears once in this source, beside changing too many things too often, exponential growth from compounding, and one simple strategy. Harut's diagnosis is that the problem is not effort — people work hard — but working on the wrong things and changing the offer, messaging, and content before they have time to compound. The fix is one simple strategy, worked hard at and stuck to, until it compounds into one hundred k a month.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any new idea: a shiny object here is a distraction that pulls someone off a working strategy, not every new tool or tactic they notice.
- Read as a lack of effort: the source says the opposite — people work hard, but the hard work goes to too many things at once instead of one strategy.
