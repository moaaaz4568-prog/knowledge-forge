# G_ig_heyharut_DcSdIdiRdpH — impact

## Term

Impact is the real reason Harut says he and Hormozi give away so much free business content: once you reach their level it stops being about money or fame and becomes about sharing abundance and helping others. The word appears once in this source, beside abundance, give away, and self-actualization, and the clip names Hormozi giving away millions of books and helping people make billions. He contrasts that with people chasing money and status, and puts himself beside Elon, Bezos, and Jensen as people who think differently at that level. Impact here is the motive for free content, not a metric.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It's about the impact, you know, like when you have so much abundance, you really want to share and help others." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcSdIdiRdpH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that free content always pays: Harut says giving away books helped people make billions, but the clip never promises the same return to anyone else.
- Read as a claim that money no longer matters to him: he says it stopped being about money at his level, which is a statement about his position, not about money itself.
