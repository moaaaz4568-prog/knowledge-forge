# G_ig_heyharut_Da_15SfxS0X — high-ticket coaching program

## Term

High-ticket coaching program is the punchline of Harut's joke life story: he claims he sold his doctor one when he was one year old. The phrase appears once in this source, beside at seven I acquired a school, at 21 I bought Mars, and now it's 23, I help entrepreneurs. The whole story is impossible on purpose — a school at seven, a millionaire at six or seven, Mars sold over poor Wi-Fi — and it ends with a real claim: now, at 23, he helps entrepreneurs build businesses, communities, and personal brands. The joke lands because the last line is true and the rest is not.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "At one year old, I sold my doctor a high-ticket coaching program." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Da_15SfxS0X.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a real sale: a one-year-old cannot sell a coaching program — the whole story is a joke, and only the final line about helping entrepreneurs at 23 is meant as true.
- Read as the price of his program: the phrase here is a punchline in a tall tale, not a price list or an offer you can buy.
