# G_ig_heyharut_Dd0AlVkTkSw — aligned

## Term

Aligned is how Harut describes a business built around the people you genuinely like: the work gets easier to care about, easier to pay attention to, and everything compounds. The word appears twice in this source, with one more use as aligns, beside sacrifice revenue, everyone wins, and triple or quadruple, and the speaker says the team sacrificed short-term revenue to attract the people they want to work with. Mentors aligned on the client's success make the growth feel inevitable, and the client tripled or quadrupled the business in the first month. Aligned here is the fit between the business and its people, not a value statement.

## Synonyms

- aligns

## Live Example

> "When your business is aligned, it's so much easier to grow." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dd0AlVkTkSw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that liking clients is enough: the team also sacrificed revenue and changed avatars to get aligned, so the fit was built, not found.
- Read as a promise to triple any business: the tripling is this client's first month, so the result is one case, not a guarantee.
