# G_ig_heyharut_Dc6ujIezPmd — binary mindset

## Term

Binary mindset is Harut's name for the thinking that makes people quit: they believe they must follow the plan perfectly, and the moment they miss a day of posting they decide they already failed and stop. The phrase appears once in this source, beside perfect plan, a little bit better, and one step faster, and the correction is that the competition is not perfect either, so winning means being a little bit better than them. Posting three videos when the plan said seven beats zero, and sending 30 outreaches after 20 beats quitting, because executing 15% while others quit at 10% is enough to win. A binary mindset here is the all-or-nothing rule that turns a missed day into a surrender.

## Live Example

> "And then when they start executing the plan towards achieving their goal and it's not going perfectly, they quit because they have a binary mindset." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dc6ujIezPmd.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to be lazy: Harut's point is to keep executing imperfectly, so the alternative to perfect is still doing the work, not stopping.
- Read as a claim that talent does not matter: the source says the only separator is not stopping, and it lists brains, personality, status, and connections as things that do not separate winners.
