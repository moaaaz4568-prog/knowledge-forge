# G_ig_heyharut_DbMrn-0RXqD — excellent

## Term

Excellent is the top rung of the bad, good, excellent ladder Harut runs through this whole clip: each pair of choices is ranked, and excellent is what the best version earns. The word appears eight times in this source, beside dream clients, better decisions, and more free time, and the pattern never changes: more followers is bad, more clients is good, more followers and more dream clients is excellent. The ladder covers profit, systems, AI help, and even liking the video, so excellent marks the version where the gain comes with something extra, like free time or leaders who create more leaders. It is a ranking game, not a definition of quality.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a scoring system for business: the ladder is a reel format that ranks whatever Harut lists, so its verdicts are entertainment, not benchmarks.
- Read as a claim that followers are worthless: getting more followers is only bad on its own rung, and the excellent rung still includes more followers when dream clients come with them.
