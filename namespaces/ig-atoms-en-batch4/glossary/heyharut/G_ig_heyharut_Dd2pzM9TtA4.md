# G_ig_heyharut_Dd2pzM9TtA4 — in person

## Term

In person is the move Harut makes for the first time in this clip: putting fifteen entrepreneurs in a room together after ten years of online-only business, because entrepreneurs keep getting lonelier behind screens. The phrase appears once in this source, beside fifteen entrepreneurs, lonelier and lonelier, and meet with each other, and the three reasons are a better team and culture, more fun that makes hard work easier, and content that performs better. He recommends it even to introverts, for a week or two, as one of the most productive things for a business. In person here is the gathering itself, not a content format.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It's all, most of it is in person, him coaching real people in real life, and that content does better than anything else." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dd2pzM9TtA4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that online business failed: Harut ran online businesses for ten years, so the room is an addition, not a replacement.
- Read as advice only for extroverts: he says he is an introvert himself and recommends it anyway, so the invitation is aimed at people like him.
