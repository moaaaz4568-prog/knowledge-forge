# G_ig_heyharut_DZGNoJpzsHj — A players

## Term

A players are the people Harut says to delegate to: hires who can do the job even better than he can. The phrase appears once in this source, beside stop doing everything yourself, one platform, one funnel, and one ideal client. It is one stop in a sixty-second summary of his twenty-one-hour course, where each stop replaces a bad habit with a better one. The surrounding stops are finding the number one constraint, posting for ideal clients instead of random viewers, collecting proof, and sticking to one offer for at least a year.
