# G_ig_heyharut_DcmHjg3z_h9 — highest leverage

## Term

The highest leverage is what Harut works on uninterrupted for the first four to six hours of his day, asking what one thing would make all his other problems go away. The phrase appears once in this source, beside work uninterrupted, draw lines in the sand, and four hours, and the habit comes from Hormozi: a timer, one hour with five-minute breaks, and the number one priority every single day. He says he won a business competition by twenty-one times growth in ninety days on this habit, and now makes over seven figures a year working far less. The highest leverage here is the single priority protected by a timer, not a productivity system.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "it's that I work uninterrupted for the first four to six hours of my day on the things that I think have the highest leverage in the business." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcmHjg3z_h9.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that four hours a day is enough: the habit is uninterrupted work on the top priority, and Harut still credits the timer and the focus, not the short day.
- Read as a guarantee of twenty-one times growth: the competition win is his own result, so the habit is the method, not the promise.
