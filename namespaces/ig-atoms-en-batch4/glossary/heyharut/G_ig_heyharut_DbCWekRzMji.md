# G_ig_heyharut_DbCWekRzMji — daily routine

## Term

The daily routine here is a joke routine Harut performs as if it were his real schedule: waking at 2am to write 500 viral hooks in his sleep, recording five podcasts with himself by 3am, and clipping them into 900 reels before breakfast. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside viral hooks, personal brand, and secret routine, and the gag peaks when his reel gets only 305 views and two likes and he quits being a creator for four minutes. The routine is framed as the secret behind scaling to over 100k month of profit, then undercut by the admission that none of it is real and the promise that copying him makes you a trillionaire. It is satire of guru routines, not a schedule anyone could follow.
