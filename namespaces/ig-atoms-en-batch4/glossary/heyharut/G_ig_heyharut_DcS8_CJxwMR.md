# G_ig_heyharut_DcS8_CJxwMR — Matilda effect

## Term

The Matilda effect is when women do the work but men take the credit, and Harut's example is Dr. Rosalind Franklin taking the first picture of DNA while someone else got famous and won the Nobel Prize. The phrase appears once in this source, beside women do the work, first picture of DNA, and Nobel Prize, and the clip ends with a plug for his course in the bio. The effect names a pattern of stolen credit in science, told here in two sentences before the sales line. The Matilda effect here is a borrowed concept Harut repeats, not one he develops.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Harut's own research: he retells a known concept in two sentences, so the clip is a summary, not a discovery.
- Read as a claim about all science: the example is one physicist and one prize, so the effect describes a pattern, not every case.
