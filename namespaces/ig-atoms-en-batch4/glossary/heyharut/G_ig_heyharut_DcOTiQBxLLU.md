# G_ig_heyharut_DcOTiQBxLLU — face reveal

## Term

A face reveal is the prize Harut offers if the account hits a million followers within about twenty-four hours, with a voice reveal thrown in if it also gets a million likes. The phrase appears once in this source, beside million followers, twenty four hours, and hit follow, and the whole clip is a dare to the audience to make the numbers happen. He has been stuck around one hundred thousand followers for a week and wants the million soon, so the reveal is the trade for the follow. A face reveal here is the hidden identity offered as a reward, not a planned launch.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "if we hit million followers within I don't know twenty four hours or something, I'll do a face reveal." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcOTiQBxLLU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a done deal: the reveal happens only if a million followers arrive in about a day, so the clip is a dare, not a scheduled event.
- Read as proof the account is faceless by choice: the voice reveal is offered alongside the face reveal, so both identity markers are being traded for growth.
