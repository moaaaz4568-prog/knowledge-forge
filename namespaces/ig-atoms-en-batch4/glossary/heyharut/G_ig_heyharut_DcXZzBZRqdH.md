# G_ig_heyharut_DcXZzBZRqdH — ideal client

## Term

The ideal client is who Hormozi's new funnel attracts instead of random viewers: ten views from the ideal client are worth ten thousand views from random people, because the second channel brings more sales than the main one. The phrase appears once in this source, beside Hormozi Highlight, 100 times less subscribers, and comment Hormozi, and the lesson is that views matter only by who they come from. Harut says he learned it on a private one-on-one call with Hormozi and offers the recording to commenters. The ideal client here is the filter that makes a small channel outsell a big one, not a demographic.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to chase views: the lesson is that ten views from the ideal client beat ten thousand random ones, so views are judged by who they come from.
- Read as a promise that a second channel prints money: the sales come from attracting serious buyers with specific clips, so copying the format without the filter earns nothing.
