# G_ig_heyharut_DZQ-rrjzmvL — VSL

## Term

VSL is the video Harut wants every business to make before a sales call: a short video sales letter that shows unqualified people they are not a good fit before they ever book. It appears five times in this source, beside pre-handle the objections, people aren't a good fit, and sales calls recordings. Harut built his from the reasons people buy and don't buy, read out of his recorded calls and fed to AI, then placed it where people see it after they book. The point is fewer pointless conversations and more nurtured buyers arriving at the call.

## Synonyms

- video sales letter (variant form used in the source)

## Live Example

> "And what we have done is we looked at all of these reasons where people aren't a good fit, and we made a quick video which is called VSL, I'm sure you know video sales letter, that people see after they book a call with you." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DZQ-rrjzmvL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales page: a VSL here is a video people watch before the call, not a long written page they read.
- Read as a video for everyone: this VSL exists to disqualify — it shows the wrong people they are not a good fit before they book.
