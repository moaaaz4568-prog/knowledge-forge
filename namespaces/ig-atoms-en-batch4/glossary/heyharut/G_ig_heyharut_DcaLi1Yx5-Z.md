# G_ig_heyharut_DcaLi1Yx5-Z — feedback

## Term

Feedback is what Harut says to get from someone who knows what they are doing instead of blaming the algorithm: hire help, learn, and treat content like anything else that takes time to master. The word appears twice in this source, beside hire someone, the algorithm is against me, and one video away, and the clip opens with the broken-car and broken-toilet analogy, where a mechanic and a plumber are the obvious fix. The complainer lists fake gurus, high competition, and no views, then threatens to quit, while the answer is to get support and keep posting volume. Feedback here is the shortcut past excuses, not a critique.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Invest into yourself, go into communities, join free groups, get feedback, learn, and then do a lot of volume and try to get better and think about this long term." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcaLi1Yx5-Z.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to blame the algorithm: Harut's answer is to hire someone and get feedback, so the algorithm is the excuse, not the cause.
- Read as a promise that one video changes everything: he says you are one video away, but only after learning, getting help, and posting volume, so the win is not luck.
