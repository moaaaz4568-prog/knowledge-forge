# G_ig_heyharut_DcgIeisxytG — a little bit more

## Term

A little bit more is the moving target Harut names in this one-liner: a little bit more followers, a little bit more money, a little bit more clients, and then I'll be happy. The phrase appears three times in this source, beside followers, money, and clients, and the whole clip is a single sentence with no resolution. The happiness is always one increment away, since each more only moves the line. A little bit more here is the hedonic treadmill in one sentence, not a goal.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "No, I need a little bit more followers, a little bit more money, a little bit more clients, and I'll be happy." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcgIeisxytG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a real goal: the target moves with every more, so the happiness it promises never arrives.
- Read as advice to want less: Harut only states the pattern, so the clip names the treadmill without prescribing how to step off it.
