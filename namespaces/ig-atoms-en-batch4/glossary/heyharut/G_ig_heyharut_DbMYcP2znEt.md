# G_ig_heyharut_DbMYcP2znEt — Claude Code

## Term

Claude Code is the tool Harut jokes has killed YouTube, Instagram, sales calls, and personal branding all at once: it writes his tweets, makes his YouTube scripts, replies to his client messages, and does all of his content. The name appears eight times in this source, beside automate everything, brush your teeth, and marry your wife for you, and the joke escalates until the tool can supposedly cut his hair too. He offers an exact prompt that he admits he is not using himself and that does not exist, and the punchline is to like the video if you believe everything you see on Instagram, like him. Claude Code here is a prop for mocking automation hype, not a tool review.
