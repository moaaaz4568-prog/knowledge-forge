# G_ig_heyharut_DcLKY_lxaiA — entrepreneurship

## Term

Entrepreneurship is what the father in this clip tries to imprint into his baby son Harut's DNA, looking at the child he could hold in one hand and talking to him before he could talk back. The word appears once in this source, beside wee lad, imprint, and DNA, and the whole anecdote is a memory of Harut as a just-born child whose feet barely reached his father's elbow. He would look at the baby and try to plant the drive to build businesses in him from the very start, as if the instinct could be passed down like a trait. Entrepreneurship here is a family inheritance the father wants to seed early, not a skill Harut learned later.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And I would look at him and we would talk. We wouldn't talk; he couldn't really talk, but I would basically just look at him and just try and imprint, you know, into his DNA entrepreneurship." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcLKY_lxaiA.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a parenting method: the father is talking to a baby who cannot talk back, so imprinting is a wish, not a technique anyone can copy.
- Read as a claim that entrepreneurship is inherited: the DNA line is a metaphor for early influence, not biology, and the source never says the trait is genetic.
