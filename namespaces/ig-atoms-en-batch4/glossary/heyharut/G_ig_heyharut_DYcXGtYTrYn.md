# G_ig_heyharut_DYcXGtYTrYn — tiny niche

## Term

Tiny niche is how Matt describes his art business: pictures made with molten silver, impressions in sand, a market so small he calls it really, really tiny. It appears once in this source, beside a good friend of mine Alexa, moving the needle, and the staircase. The point of the testimonial is that even this tiny a niche scales with Harut's help: he sold a piece for six thousand seven hundred pounds, nearly ten thousand dollars, within six months. He credits calls with Harut, the strategies, and the roadmap they give him for each next level.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "So you could say I've got a really, really tiny niche." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DYcXGtYTrYn.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a reason a niche cannot scale: Matt's whole point is that a tiny niche did scale — from pieces at one or two hundred to a sale near ten thousand dollars.
- Read as a claim about the art itself: tiny niche describes the size of his market, not the quality of the molten-silver work.
