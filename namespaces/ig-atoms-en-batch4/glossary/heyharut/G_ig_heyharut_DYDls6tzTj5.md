# G_ig_heyharut_DYDls6tzTj5 — fake

## Term

Fake is Harut's word for Instagram itself: the wins and lifestyles on display hide the struggles, health problems, and personal issues that every person has. It appears three times in this source, beside client wins, comparing ourselves to each other, and humility and empathy and kindness. The trap is comparison: seeing someone making a million a month and feeling behind, without seeing the parts of their life they never share. His advice is to remember that no one has it all, including himself and his own content, and to be kind to others and to yourself.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Have you all noticed that Instagram is fake as fuck?" — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DYDls6tzTj5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that every post is staged: fake here means curated — wins shown, struggles hidden — not that nothing on Instagram is real.
- Read as permission to share everything: Harut says people should not share their private struggles, and privacy is very important; the point is to stop comparing, not to stop keeping things private.
