# G_ig_heyharut_DaJkOZyh5gx — demonstrating proof

## Term

Demonstrating proof is the one thing Harut says decides whether a business gets clients: not just collecting proof, but showing it in the marketing. The phrase appears once in this source, beside most views in your niche, best client results in your niche, and scale your business to 100k month. You can lead your niche in views, in being the best person, and in client results, and still get no clients without this one thing. The difference is between having the results and letting buyers see them where they make the decision.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "And that one thing is not only collecting proof, but demonstrating proof in your marketing." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DaJkOZyh5gx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as collecting testimonials: demonstrating proof here is showing the proof inside the marketing, not filing it away after the client wins.
- Read as being the best: the source says you can be the best in your niche and still lose clients if the proof never reaches the buyer.
