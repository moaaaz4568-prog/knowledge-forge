# G_ig_heyharut_DYD2W_3z-2r — highlight funnel

## Term

A highlight funnel is Alex Hormozi's second-channel system as Harut describes it: many super highly targeted videos with very specific titles, each aimed at one exact situation. It appears once in this source, beside almost 100 times less subscribers, How to Double Your Sales Team as an Electrician, and 20 posts a day on YouTube. The small channel brings more sales for acquisition.com than the main channel because every video reaches the exact right people, even with far fewer views. Harut's takeaway is to stop making only generic content and to post way more, each piece specific to one avatar's pain.
