# G_ig_heyharut_DceVrnVz1ql — private community

## Term

A private community is what the fifteen thousand eight hundred buys access to, together with done-for-you YouTube title strategy ideas and everything else in the offer. The phrase appears once in this source, beside done for you YouTube title strategy ideas, fifteen thousand eight hundred, and have to pay money, and the whole clip is a two-sentence price announcement with no pitch beyond the access. The buyer pays money to get all of it, and the community is listed first among the things included. A private community here is the gated room sold as part of a bundle, not a free group.
