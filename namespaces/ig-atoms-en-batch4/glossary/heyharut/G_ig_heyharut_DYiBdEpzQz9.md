# G_ig_heyharut_DYiBdEpzQz9 — ROAS

## Term

ROAS is the result Harut tells coaches to record on their calls: the return on ad spend a client gets after working with them. It appears once in this source, beside Facebook ads, run better ads, and eighty percent of the time focusing on what you can do. His rule is to spend eighty percent of the time doing real work and twenty percent documenting it, because doing is what creates stories that stand out. He points to Alex Hormozi and Dan Martell as people whose content converts precisely because most of their time goes to doing things.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "For example, if you are a coach and you help people with Facebook ads, then actually make sure to get better results for people, run better ads, record coaching calls when people get ROAS after working with you." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DYiBdEpzQz9.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a vanity number: ROAS here is proof recorded on real coaching calls, not a screenshot of ad spend with no client result behind it.
- Read as a reason to spend more time editing: the source says the opposite — eighty percent doing, twenty percent documenting — and packaging only matters after the doing exists.
