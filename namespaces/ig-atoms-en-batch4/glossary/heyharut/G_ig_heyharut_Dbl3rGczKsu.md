# G_ig_heyharut_Dbl3rGczKsu — viral packaging

## Term

Viral packaging is the second piece of advice Harut gives the owner of a newly acquired call DM outreach tool: do not make content and then try to package it, but look for viral packaging, viral titles, and thumbnails in similar niches first. The phrase appears once in this source, beside avatar, get started, and one to three videos a week, and the order is content and leads first, then the funnel, with the avatar kept in mind throughout. He tells the owner to take inspiration from viral titles and thumbnails without copying them, and the third advice is simply to get started, since the first few videos are going to suck. Viral packaging here is the title and thumbnail that carry the video, studied before the video is made.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Go and look for viral packaging, viral titles and thumbnails in similar niches and take some inspiration from them, don't copy inspiration." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dbl3rGczKsu.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as copying viral videos: Harut says take inspiration and do not copy it, so the packaging is studied, not stolen.
- Read as a promise that packaging guarantees reach: the packaging comes after getting content and leads going, so it improves distribution, it does not replace the work.
