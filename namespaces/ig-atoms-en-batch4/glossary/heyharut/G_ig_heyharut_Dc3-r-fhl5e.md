# G_ig_heyharut_Dc3-r-fhl5e — learn from others

## Term

Learn from others is the habit Harut says separates people who grow from people who stay stuck: one group thinks they know too much and will not learn, and the other had one or two bad experiences and stopped learning entirely. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside don't stop learning, massive arbitrage, and learn from the wrong people, and Harut's own example is investing over $300, 000 into mentors and learning from the biggest brands and business owners. The danger he names is learning from the wrong people, whose marketing is so effective, so the habit needs picking the right people and then taking massive action. Learning from others here is an arbitrage on their experience, not a confession of ignorance.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And the second group is people that are open to learn from others, whether it's free with content or they invest in themselves, but they had one or two bad experiences and then they stop learning." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dc3-r-fhl5e.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to learn from anyone: Harut warns that the only bad move is learning from the wrong people, so the choice of teacher is the whole point.
- Read as a claim that coaches are useless: the cynical group he criticises decided all coaches are bad after two bad experiences, and Harut calls that a very bad thing to do.
