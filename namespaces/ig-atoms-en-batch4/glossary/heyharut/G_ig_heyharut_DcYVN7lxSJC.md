# G_ig_heyharut_DcYVN7lxSJC — content creation

## Term

Content creation is the number one skill Harut says separates people struggling to make money online from people printing millions of dollars, and he proves it by recommending three people who actually did it. The phrase appears once in this source, beside Caleb, Sam, and first principles thinking, and the three are Caleb with his free six-hour course, Sam with his YouTube and Instagram strategy, and Hormozi with first principles thinking behind a hundred-million-dollar launch. The viewer is told to follow all three and apply their advice for a year. Content creation here is the skill to learn from practitioners, not a talent.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Content creation is the number one skill that separates from people that are struggling to make money online to people that are printing millions of dollars, and I mean it literally." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcYVN7lxSJC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of the only three teachers: Harut recommends three people he trusts, so the list is a starting point, not the whole field.
- Read as a promise of a bigger brand in a year: he says the brand will grow if you apply their advice and execute, so the result depends on doing the work, not on watching.
