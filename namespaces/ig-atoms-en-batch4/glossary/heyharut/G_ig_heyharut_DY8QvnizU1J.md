# G_ig_heyharut_DY8QvnizU1J — qualified leads

## Term

Qualified leads are the constraint Harut names in this source: with a hundred times more of them, the offer would hardly matter. The phrase appears twice, beside way more qualified leads, 100 times more qualified leads, and over-optimize and improve your offer. His argument is that offers are overrated in 2026 because a good offer can be copied in a second, while a brand and distribution that keep bringing qualified leads cannot. The fix he prescribes is content and brand that build an audience which likes and trusts him, so they convert no matter what he sells.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But that said, what is your constraint most likely if you're watching this and if you want to scale to 100k a month is way more qualified leads." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DY8QvnizU1J.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any leads at all: qualified leads here are buyers ready enough to convert, and Harut's test is whether a hundred times more of them would make the offer irrelevant.
- Read as a reason to stop improving the offer: he still says to make the offer as good as possible and proven to work — the point is that the offer is not the constraint holding the business back.
