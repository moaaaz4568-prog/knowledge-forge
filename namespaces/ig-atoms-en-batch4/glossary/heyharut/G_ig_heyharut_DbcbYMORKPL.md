# G_ig_heyharut_DbcbYMORKPL — emotionally invested

## Term

Emotionally invested is Harut's test for whether content is real: the only thing that matters is whether you care about the video and its message more than you care about getting views. The phrase appears once in this source, beside being authentic, am I proud of what I am making, and do you emotionally care about it, and the proof he offers is a video that took only a few hours yet brought 10, 000 followers, 1. 7 million views, and hundreds of thousands of dollars. People can feel whether someone is expressing inner desires or just trying to get something out of the video, and that feeling is what being authentic means here. Emotional investment is the filter before making anything, not a style of editing.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You only need to care about not the systems, not the frequency, not the consistency, but whether you are emotionally invested into the content you make." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DbcbYMORKPL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule to post only emotional videos: the investment is in the message you care about, so a plain tutorial you believe in passes the test and a tearful one you do not fails it.
- Read as a claim that effort does not matter: Harut says stop caring how long editing takes, not that editing is worthless, since the video he cites still took a few hours.
