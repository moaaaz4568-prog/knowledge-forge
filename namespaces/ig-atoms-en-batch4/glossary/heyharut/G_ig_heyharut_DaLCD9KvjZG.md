# G_ig_heyharut_DaLCD9KvjZG — bottleneck

## Term

Bottleneck is the current block Harut asks a client to name every week: whatever stops the business right now, asked so Harut can give actionable steps on it. It appears twice in this source, beside doing 20K per month, more traffic than I can handle, and thousand opt-ins and people trying to book. The client here has the opposite of a shortage — around a thousand opt-ins a week and only room for about a hundred calls. Harut's first move is to name who to hire, and the client says the most useful part was the live call itself, on top of the recorded materials in the classroom.

## Live Example

> "every week you can come with specific questions, whatever is your current bottleneck, ask questions to Harut and get actionable steps on what to do next to grow from there or to tackle that bottleneck." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DaLCD9KvjZG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a missing tool: the bottleneck here is capacity — too much traffic for one person — not a piece of software the business lacks.
- Read as a one-time fix: Harut asks for the current bottleneck every week, because the block changes as the business grows.
