# G_ig_heyharut_DcAd8jrv9GO — VSL

## Term

VSL is the video Harut defines by its job: it should convince people why you are the best person to help them get where they want to get to, before they ever talk to you. The term appears three times in this source, beside best person, where they want to get to, and before they ever talk to you, and the whole source is a single question and its answer, with nothing else around it. The VSL is judged by whether it makes that case, so a video that only describes the product fails the definition. A VSL here is a pre-call convincer, not a sales video in general.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Your VSL should be something that convinces people why you are the best person to help them get where they want to get to before they ever talk to you." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcAd8jrv9GO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any video about the offer: the VSL must argue why you are the best person to help, so a feature list without that argument is not a VSL by this definition.
- Read as a replacement for the call: the VSL works before they ever talk to you, so the call still happens, and the video only earns it.
