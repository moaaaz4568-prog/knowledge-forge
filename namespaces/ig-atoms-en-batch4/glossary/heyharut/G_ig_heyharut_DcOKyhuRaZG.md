# G_ig_heyharut_DcOKyhuRaZG — human edge

## Term

The human edge is what Harut says to lean into instead of AI slop: the flaws, humor, weird things, and hobbies that make a person the true version of themselves on camera. The phrase appears once in this source, beside people follow people, true version of ourselves, and real human connection, and the argument runs through Hormozi, Gary Vee, Bartlett, Messi, and Ronaldo, whose personal followings dwarf their companies. If a creator can be on camera the same person their family sees, people crave that connection over perfect manufactured content. The human edge here is the uncopyable personal texture AI cannot fake, not a production trick.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "how about we focus on our human edge, the things that make us us, like to be the true version of ourselves in front of a camera." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcOKyhuRaZG.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on AI tools: Harut attacks AI slop, not AI itself, so the human edge is about what is posted, not which tools are used.
- Read as a promise that authenticity guarantees growth: he says it makes creators relatable, not that it replaces consistency or a message worth sharing.
