# G_ig_heyharut_DcQaG3IxibC — hiring a virtual assistant

## Term

Hiring a virtual assistant is the four-out-of-five move Harut rates for people who should delegate the tasks that are not making them the most money, so the saved time goes into the things that do. The phrase appears once in this source, beside hiring an agency, hiring an email copywriter, and buying books, and the clip rates five purchases in a row, from two out of five for an agency to five out of five for books about wealth. An agency makes sense only for people with no time who want the basics, and a copywriter only if writing is not your strength. Hiring a virtual assistant here is buying back time for money-making work, not hiring for status.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Hiring a virtual assistant. Four out of five." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcQaG3IxibC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ranking of all hires: the scores are Harut's opinions for one situation, so a four out of five is not a rule for every business.
- Read as a claim that books beat hiring: books score five out of five as a purchase, while the assistant scores four as a hire, so the two are not competing options.
