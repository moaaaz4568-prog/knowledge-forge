# G_ig_heyharut_DbKTHf2RodQ — community

## Term

Community is what Harut says entrepreneurs must build now instead of a brand, an audience, or a course: a group of people with the same values and goals who help each other and keep each other accountable. The word appears twelve times in this source, beside school, Vantage, and trust, with three more uses as communities, and the argument is that in a world of AI abundance the thing people will want is other people just like them. He points to Hormozi's Vantage community and to billionaires starting meetups in real life, and the test is whether the people are treated as real human beings rather than numbers. A community here is a place where members give to each other, not a list of followers to broadcast at.

## Live Example

> "So the thing that you need to build right now is not a brand, it's not audience, it's not a course or a group program or whatever, you need to build a community." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DbKTHf2RodQ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as starting a paid group: Harut says the community can be free or paid and can live anywhere, so the payment model is not the point.
- Read as growing an audience: the source contrasts a community with an audience, since members connect with each other rather than only watching Harut.
