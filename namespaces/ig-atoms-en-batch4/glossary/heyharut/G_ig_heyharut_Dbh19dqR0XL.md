# G_ig_heyharut_Dbh19dqR0XL — younger self

## Term

Younger self is who Harut tells the creator to make content for: picture the younger self, remember what he felt, what questions he had, what insecurities he had, and what was stopping him. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside B two B sales space, younger Daniel, and one of our biggest advice is start, and the exercise is to help people starting out in B2B sales by speaking to the person you were. The advice that follows is simply to start and post, because you learn more by doing than by learning more about YouTube. The younger self is a stand-in for the beginner audience, not nostalgia.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to relive your past: the younger self is a way to remember the beginner's questions, so the content serves today's beginners, not the creator's memories.
- Read as a claim that only beginners matter: the exercise picks the audience by picturing who you would help for free, and that audience happens to be starters.
