# G_ig_heyharut_DYA7DDiT2QU — outlast

## Term

Outlast means staying in business longer than everyone else, and Harut calls it the most important of the three ways to beat competitors, ahead of outsmarting and outworking them. It appears four times in this source, beside most people quit, the only guaranteed way for you to lose is to stop, and how do I make it more fun. The logic is that the market is big enough for many to succeed, so people usually lose by deciding to stop, not by being beaten. His one question for staying in the game is how to make the work more fun, like filming while walking in the sunlight instead of forcing lines in a dark room.

## Synonyms

- outlasting (variant form used in the source)

## Live Example

> "You can outsmart them, you can outwork them, and the most important one is you can outlast them." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DYA7DDiT2QU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as waiting passively: outlasting here means keeping going by making the work fun enough to stick to, not sitting still while competitors move.
- Read as a promise that no one can beat you: Harut only claims stopping guarantees losing, so outlasting removes the one sure way to fail rather than guaranteeing a win.
