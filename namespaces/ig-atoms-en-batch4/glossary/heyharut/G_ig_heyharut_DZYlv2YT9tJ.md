# G_ig_heyharut_DZYlv2YT9tJ — fallacy of marketing

## Term

Fallacy of marketing is Harut's name for the bias that kills more businesses than anything else: assuming the audience already knows what you do, your proof, and why you are better. The phrase appears once in this source, beside most people sometimes don't even know that they have a problem, assume that no one knows anything about you, and marketing will become so much more effective. Most people don't even know the problem exists, let alone what your business is, so the fix is to market as if nobody knows anything. Harut's challenge is simple: explain yourself to a stranger as if they know nothing, and watch the marketing work better.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "There is one bias that probably killed more businesses than anything else, and it is the fallacy of marketing." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DZYlv2YT9tJ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a dishonesty charge: the fallacy here is an assumption about what the audience knows, not a lie in the marketing itself.
- Read as advice to say more words: the fix is not longer copy — it is assuming no one knows anything about you and proving it from zero.
