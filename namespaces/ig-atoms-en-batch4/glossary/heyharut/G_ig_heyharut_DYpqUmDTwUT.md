# G_ig_heyharut_DYpqUmDTwUT — pre-sold

## Term

Pre-sold describes the buyer Harut wants on a sales call: someone the content has already nurtured so well that the call takes almost no selling. It appears once in this source, beside do calls only with qualified people, a really strong sales process, and repost your winners. It is the Best tier of his ladder, above judging content by views or by engagement, and above doing calls with everyone who has a pulse and a credit card. The companion move is reposting winners and recreating them from five different angles.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Best: Make content that nurtures people and before people jump on a call, they are already pre-sold, so you don't even have to try that hard." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DYpqUmDTwUT.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a closed deal: pre-sold here means the buyer arrives already convinced by the content, not that money has changed hands before the call.
- Read as skipping the call: the call still happens — it is just easy, because the nurturing did the work before anyone booked.
