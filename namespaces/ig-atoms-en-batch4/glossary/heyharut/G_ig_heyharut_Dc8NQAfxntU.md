# G_ig_heyharut_Dc8NQAfxntU — Hormozi Highlight

## Term

Hormozi Highlight is the second channel Harut presents as Hormozi's new meta funnel: a smaller channel of very specific clips that attracts serious buyers in the same exact situation as the person in the video. The name appears once in this source, beside second channel, more qualified leads, and optimize around trust, and the paradox is that the channel is 40 to 50 times smaller than the main one yet makes more sales from it. The specific titles filter for buyers, so fewer views turn into more qualified leads and more clients, and Harut's advice is to open a second channel or account and post specific clips for the ideal person there. Hormozi Highlight here is the specific-clips channel, not a second brand.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "So he's talking about his second channel called Hormozi Highlight, which only has about 100,000 subscribers at the time of recording this video, and it's 40 to 50 times is smaller than his main channel, and it's getting the tiny fraction of the views that he's getting on the main one." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dc8NQAfxntU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to chase views: the channel gets a tiny fraction of the main channel's views, and Harut says to optimise around trust instead, so views are the wrong target.
- Read as a claim that small channels always earn more: the sales come from specific titles attracting serious buyers, so a small channel without that filter would just be small.
