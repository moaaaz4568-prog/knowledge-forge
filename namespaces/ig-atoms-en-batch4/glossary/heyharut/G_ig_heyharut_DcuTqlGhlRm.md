# G_ig_heyharut_DcuTqlGhlRm — emotionally care

## Term

Emotionally care is the one question Harut says to ask before posting anything: do I emotionally care about this topic, because if you do not care, the audience will not care either. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside one question, strong emotion, and make content when you have something to say, and the filter accepts anger, upset, optimism, or inspiration, as long as the feeling is strong. Chasing viral topics you do not care about wastes the time, energy, and money spent scripting and editing. Emotionally care here is the test for whether a topic deserves a video, not a mood.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to only post angry videos: anger is one strong emotion among others, and optimism or inspiration pass the same filter, so the test is strength of feeling, not negativity.
- Read as a claim that passion guarantees views: caring carries over to the video, but the clip never promises that caring alone brings reach.
