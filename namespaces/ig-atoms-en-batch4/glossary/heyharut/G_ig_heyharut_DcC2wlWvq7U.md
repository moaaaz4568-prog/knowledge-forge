# G_ig_heyharut_DcC2wlWvq7U — client wins

## Term

Client wins is what Harut calls one of the dumbest mistakes of his life not to document: he did not document all his client wins, and now he collects case studies every single day because he is finally prioritising it. The phrase appears three times in this source, beside dumbest mistakes, case studies, and incentivize them, and the pro tip he adds is to incentivise the team to collect more proof, which produced way more proof. The wins are the raw material of the case studies, so documenting them is the habit that feeds the proof. Client wins here are the results to record, not trophies to admire.
