# G_ig_heyharut_DaSl3RoM1QQ — in the game

## Term

In the game is Harut's answer to Hormozi's challenge: he is no longer just in the comments, he is playing for real now. The phrase appears once in this source, beside My name is Harut, you challenged me to become a millionaire in six months, and I heard you're having a son. The clip is a reply to the challenge — Harut takes the dare, says just watch me, and cancels Hormozi's seat on the calls as a joke. It is a boast of entry, not a business lesson, and the millionaire deadline stays at six months.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a business strategy: in the game here is a boast that Harut has entered the challenge, not a method for growing anything.
- Read as Harut leaving the comments for good: the line is a punchline about the challenge — he is still talking to Hormozi in the same clip, not quitting the platform.
