# G_ig_heyharut_Dcl2X_5PUwA — first three seconds

## Term

The first three seconds are more important than the rest of the thirty seconds, because without them nobody even sees what follows, and that is why Harut paid over twenty-four thousand dollars to Hormozi for proven hooks. The phrase appears twice in this source, beside perfect hook, ideal avatar, and comment the word hooks, and the clip opens with a fake magician promising a new client in three seconds before admitting the joke. The problem he names is that creators try to be useful while viewers skip, so the opening must earn the watch first. The first three seconds here are the gate to the whole video, not an editing detail.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule that the rest of the video does not matter: the first seconds earn the watch, but the video still has to hold it, so the opening is the gate, not the whole video.
- Read as a claim that hooks must be bought: Harut paid Hormozi for proven hooks, but the lesson is about the opening seconds, not about paying for them.
