# G_ig_heyharut_DYpqL0Gsicu — cheering people

## Term

Cheering people is what Harut says the internet needs more of: celebrating a moment like Alex and Leila Hormozi expecting a baby instead of tearing successful people down. It appears once in this source, beside less human, millions of lives changed, and a new, very interesting and exciting chapter. His point is that very successful people are still human, and a happy moment reminds everyone of that. The wish he leaves is simple joy and happiness for both of them as their new chapter begins.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as flattery of the successful: cheering people here means treating a human moment as human, not praising business results.
- Read as news about Harut himself: the baby news belongs to Alex and Leila Hormozi, and Harut is only reacting to it as a viewer.
