# G_ig_heyharut_DcF6dF9hcaf — public commitment

## Term

Public commitment is the pressure device Harut prescribes for people stuck between ten to thirty K a month and one hundred K a month: make a public commitment, move cities, have stakes, make a bet with your friend, and do something that gets you out of your comfort zone. The phrase appears once in this source, beside don't have enough pressure, self-sabotaging, and the next milestone becomes inevitable, and the diagnosis is that they do not want it bad enough, or do not love the work enough to push as hard as they can. The trap is being too attached to the outcome while comfortable, so the brain keeps self-sabotaging, and the cure is to obsess about the input instead. A public commitment here is a stake that makes quitting costly, not a motivational poster.

## Live Example

> "So you need to make a public commitment, move cities, have stakes, make a bet with your friend, do something that will get you out of your comfort zone." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcF6dF9hcaf.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that wanting is enough: Harut says they probably do not want it bad enough, and the commitment is the device that forces the wanting into action.
- Read as advice to move cities for its own sake: moving is one example of a stake beside the bet and the commitment, so the point is the pressure, not the relocation.
