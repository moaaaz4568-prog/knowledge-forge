# G_ig_heyharut_DXyECMJTaGd — trust

## Term

Trust is the reason people buy in this source, and the Best tier of every ladder is the move that builds it. It appears twice, beside fixing the reason people don't trust you enough to buy and content that builds trust and pre-sells your offer. Harut contrasts it with surface fixes: changing the offer at random or selling at a mid-range price never repairs trust. The payoff he names is content that makes the offer feel already sold before any sales conversation.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a feeling alone: trust here is earned by fixing the specific reason people hesitate to buy, not by sounding confident about the offer.
- Read as a synonym for the offer: the source keeps them apart — the offer is what is sold, while trust is what makes people willing to buy it at any price.
