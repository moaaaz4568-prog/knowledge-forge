# G_ig_heyharut_DcYJEHEx94C — hooks

## Term

Hooks are the only difference between the same video flopping and the same video reaching millions, so Harut tells the viewer to test hooks, change them, and keep posting the winners over and over. The word appears three times in this source, beside same exact video, test hooks, and till it stops working, and the first post got almost no views while later posts of the same clip brought millions of views and tens of thousands of followers. He says to remake the winners, ignore the people complaining about reposts, and post until the video stops working. Hooks here are the variable that decides reach, not the video itself.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So just test hooks and don't stop posting." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcYJEHEx94C.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to repost anything forever: Harut says to post until it stops working, so the reposting ends when the winners stop winning.
- Read as a claim that the video itself does not matter: the hooks decide reach, but the video still has to be good enough to repost, so the clip is not disposable.
