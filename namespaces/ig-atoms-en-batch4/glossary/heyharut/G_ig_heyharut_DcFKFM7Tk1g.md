# G_ig_heyharut_DcFKFM7Tk1g — transformation

## Term

Transformation is what Harut says makes a good case study: if someone had an amazing result but was not struggling before, it is irrelevant, and if someone was struggling and still is, it also sucks. The word appears three times in this source, beside the bigger the pain before, the bigger the change after, and what makes a good case study, and the rule is that the bigger the transformation, the bigger the pain before and the bigger the change after, the better the case study. A result without a before-and-after is not a story, and a struggle without a breakthrough is not one either. Transformation here is the change from pain to result, and it is the whole measure of the case study.

## Live Example

> "What makes a good case study? The transformation." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcFKFM7Tk1g.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any big result: a result with no struggle before it is irrelevant by this rule, so size alone never makes a case study.
- Read as a sympathy story: a struggle with no breakthrough also sucks, so the pain only counts when it ends in a change.
