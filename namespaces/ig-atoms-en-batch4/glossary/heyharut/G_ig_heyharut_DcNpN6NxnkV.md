# G_ig_heyharut_DcNpN6NxnkV — playground

## Term

A playground is Harut's replacement for the funnel: a person finds you somewhere, checks your content, forgets you, then meets you again on a different platform, and that wandering is the real path to a client. The word appears once in this source, beside funnel, forgets about you, and different platform, and the clip promises the funnel that made over three hundred and seventy thousand dollars in a few months to anyone who comments VSL. The funnel image fails because the journey is scattered across platforms rather than a straight line down. A playground here is the messy, multi-platform wandering before someone buys, not a pipeline.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It's less of a funnel and more of a playground." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DcNpN6NxnkV.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that funnels do not exist: Harut still sells a funnel that made hundreds of thousands, so the playground describes the journey, not the absence of a funnel.
- Read as advice to abandon funnels: the point is that people wander across platforms before buying, so the funnel must follow them, not disappear.
