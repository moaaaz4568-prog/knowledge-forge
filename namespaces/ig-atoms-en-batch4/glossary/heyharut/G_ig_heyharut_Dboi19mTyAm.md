# G_ig_heyharut_Dboi19mTyAm — data

## Term

Data is what Harut says posting one hundred times more than you buys him: one hundred times more data about what worked, what did not, what thumbnails worked, and what captions worked. The word appears three times in this source, beside retention graphs, one hundred, and compounds, and the argument is that more posting means more retention graphs to analyse on Instagram and faster learning about hooks and audience loss. The learning compounds on top of itself, which is why some people make hundreds of millions of dollars while others struggle to make one hundred K a month. Data here is the record of what worked, not a dashboard.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If I post one hundred times more than you, I have one hundred times more data." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_Dboi19mTyAm.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that posting more guarantees learning: the data only helps if the posts are analysed, and Harut's point is the analysis of a hundred graphs, not the count alone.
- Read as a promise of hundreds of millions: the millions belong to the people who learn fastest from their data, so the data is the mechanism, not the money.
