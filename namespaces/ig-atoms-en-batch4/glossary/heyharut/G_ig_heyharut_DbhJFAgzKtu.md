# G_ig_heyharut_DbhJFAgzKtu — millionaire

## Term

Millionaire is the dare Harut shouts at Farouk in a live greeting: you better be a millionaire in the next like six months, man, before Farouk introduces himself and reports the numbers. The word appears once in this source, beside 210k, first $1, 000 a month, and $30, 000 in a day, and the greeting turns into a quick results report with days that made $30, 000. Harut's dare is the frame and Farouk's numbers are the reply, so the millionaire target sits beside real revenue rather than a wish. It is a challenge issued on a live, not a title Farouk already holds.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Fuck it, Farouk. You better be a millionaire in the next like six months, man." — sources/S_ig_heyharut_DbhJFAgzKtu.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Farouk already being a millionaire: the dare sets a six-month target, so the word is a goal, and the $30, 000 days are the progress toward it.
- Read as a random insult: the greeting is a dare between people who know each other on the lives, so the swearing is part of the cheer, not an attack.
