# G_igocr_fundedfounder_DSN6ODiDB8l — networking dinners

## Term

"networking dinners" appears once in this source, in the headline announcing that a16z organizes them for founders to pitch ideas and find cofounders. Founders connect, exchange ideas, and form partnerships over private dinners in San Francisco organized by Andreessen Horowitz, under the A16Z BUILD banner — a dinner series and community for world-class founders and operators figuring out their next big thing. The source frames the dinners as part of a16z's greater attempt to embed itself deeper into the founder ecosystem after launching Speedrun, its YC competitor, in 2023. The words most often collocated with it are: A16Z, founders, cofounders, San Francisco.
