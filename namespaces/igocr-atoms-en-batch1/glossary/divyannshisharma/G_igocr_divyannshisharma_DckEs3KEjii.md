# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_DckEs3KEjii — AI growth team

## Term

"AI growth team" is the three-bot crew this source builds with Grok Bot: Olivia owns content, Atlas owns lead gen, and Jim owns distribution and outreach. The phrase appears three times, beside Grok Bot, Olivia, Atlas, and Jim. The workflow runs content, then lead gen, then outreach: Olivia drafts posts and keeps the calendar moving, Atlas cleans and enriches lists and feeds qualified leads to Jim, and Jim finds the people to reach and starts conversations with them. The unlock the source claims is not the 200K+ followers but a team that keeps the growth engine moving without micromanagement — one message to the bot responsible, and it gets to work.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the team being three bots with fixed names: the slides themselves mix the outreach bot's name — the title card says Jim owns distribution and outreach, while later slides call the same role Scout — so the roles, not the names, are the stable part.
- Read as 200K+ followers proving the team works: the source presents the follower count as the result, but the workflow shown is content, lead gen, and outreach — the number is a claim about the account, not a measurement of the bots.
