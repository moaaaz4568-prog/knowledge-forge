# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_DdZMSdTksDE — Board of Advisors

## Term

"Board of Advisors" appears twice in this source, as the first of four Claude projects meant to multiply output. It is a personal board of advisors inside Claude Code, built in five steps: let Claude interview you, pick your board members, feed it their content, build an /ask-the-board skill, and ask your question. The result is advice that sounds like your own personal panel of mentors, every time you ask. The words most often collocated with it are: ask-the-board, mentors, board members, Claude Code.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as asking real people: the board is Claude pretending to be a panel of mentors built from content you feed it — the advice sounds like your own panel, but no real board member is consulted.
- Read as a single question answered once: it is a skill you build (/ask-the-board) and reuse — the interview, the member-picking, and the content-feeding are setup steps, not the question itself.
