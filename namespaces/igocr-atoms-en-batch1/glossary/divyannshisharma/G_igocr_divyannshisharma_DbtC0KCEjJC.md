# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_DbtC0KCEjJC — second brain

## Term

"Second brain" is what the source calls the store Granola builds from everything it records: every conversation, indexed and saved, connected to Claude and your other AI tools. The phrase appears once, beside Claude, conversations, AI tools, and memories. The payoff is shown as a question — asking what was decided with the client on Tuesday — answered from the call itself, with scope, timeline, fee, and next steps quoted back. Granola's difference from other note-takers is that it never joins the meeting as a visible bot; it records in the background, on calls and in person.
