# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_Dcban0XEqvO — Data Sharing Program

## Term

"Data Sharing Program" is the name of OpenAI's hidden program in this source: turn on two switches in Data controls, and free credits arrive every single day. The phrase appears once, beside free credits, Data controls, Sharing tab, and two switches. The switches share evaluation and fine-tuning data, and inputs and outputs, with OpenAI — up to 2.5 million free tokens a day for lighter models like GPT-5.1-Mini and GPT-5-Nano, 250 thousand for powerful models like GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.2, and over $50,000 of free credits over time. The catch is stated plainly: the data gets shared to improve OpenAI's models, so it is not for private or client work, and the account needs a positive balance — even $1 added — to activate.
