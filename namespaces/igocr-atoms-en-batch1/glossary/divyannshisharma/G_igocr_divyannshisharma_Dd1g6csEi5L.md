# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_Dd1g6csEi5L — your own machine

## Term

Here "your own machine" names where these ten GitHub repos run: on hardware you control instead of someone else's cloud. The phrase appears twice, beside host, server, drive, and network. The list runs from a private search engine and a translation engine with no per-character fee to a manga server no legal letter can reach, a personal Wayback Machine, an AirDrop for every device, and a Bitwarden server on a $5 box. The closing line is the pitch: most people will scroll past and keep paying for all of it, while the ones who bookmark it never go back.
