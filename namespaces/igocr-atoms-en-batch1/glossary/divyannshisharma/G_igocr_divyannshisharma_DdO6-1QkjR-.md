# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_DdO6-1QkjR- — Claude Skills

## Term

"Claude Skills" appears once in this source, in the slide asking what they are: skills are like firmware upgrades for Claude. By default Claude is smart but generic, giving average code, average design, and average output; installing a skill once — the carousel lists ten, from Frontend Design and Algorithmic Art to Context Optimization and the meta-skill Skill Creator — makes Claude work at a completely different level, with 3–4x faster iteration and fewer manual corrections. The pitch is one setup, a permanent upgrade, and agency-level output in a fraction of the time. The words most often collocated with it are: firmware upgrades, install, folders, 10.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as ten skills that replace judgment: the Frontend Design skill only picks an aesthetic before Claude writes code, and Systematic Debugging only follows a four-phase process — the skill is a recipe Claude follows, not a replacement for deciding what to build.
- Read as install once and never think again: Skill Creator is the meta-skill that teaches Claude to build its own new skills, and Context Optimization exists because Claude still gets slow and confused after 30–40 minutes when its context window fills up.
