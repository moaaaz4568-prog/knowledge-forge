# G_igocr_divyannshisharma_Dbvm3avEjJk — effort level

## Term

"Effort level" is the dial Fable 5 gives each task: Low or medium for quick questions, High as the default, Xhigh for the hardest problems, and Ultracode for full autonomous multi-step projects. The phrase appears three times, once as "effort levels" and twice as "effort settings", beside Xhigh, High, Ultracode, and Low or medium. The guide's point is matching, not maximising: using Xhigh for everything wastes money and time, and the level goes up only when the job earns it. The same tip sits beside four others — asking questions first, giving the why, keeping instructions short, and setting checkpoints — that together make up the prompting guide.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as always use the highest: the guide says the opposite — Xhigh for everything wastes money and time, High is the default, and Ultracode is only for big multi-step projects.
- Read as longer prompts working better: the source says over-explaining constrains Fable 5 and performs worse, so you give the goal and the context, then get out of the way.
