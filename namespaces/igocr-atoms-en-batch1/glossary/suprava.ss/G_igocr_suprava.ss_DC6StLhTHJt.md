# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DC6StLhTHJt — PEC Formula

## Term

The PEC Formula is one of three cold email formulas in this post — highlight a pain point, address a desire or fear of the prospect, then show how the solution handles both. The phrase appears twice, beside 'Pain point', 'Emotion', and 'Call to action'. The post spells it out as PEC equals pain point plus emotion plus call to action, and demonstrates it with a SaaS example that names a tough month, a cause, and a free 30-day trial. The formula is a structure for the email body, not a subject-line trick.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a template to copy word for word: the post gives the formula as a skeleton — pain, emotion, call to action — and the example fills it with the prospect's own tough month and cause.
- Read as the same thing as the PPC Formula: the PPC Formula in the same post offers a partial solution instead of addressing emotion, so swapping the two changes what the email promises.
