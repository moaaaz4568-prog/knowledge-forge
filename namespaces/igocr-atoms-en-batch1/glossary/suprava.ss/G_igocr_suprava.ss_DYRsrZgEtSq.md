# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DYRsrZgEtSq — leverage

## Term

Leverage here is Naval's word for the thing that makes growth compound — media, code, labor, and capital, ranked from high to low, with permissioned leverage on the old side and permissionless on the new. The word appears eight times, beside 'Naval', 'distribution', and 'attention'. Suprava's point is that lead generation is the last step, not the first: markets reward pre-existing belief, built by repeated exposure before contact, and cold outreach is only the final trigger. The five steps that follow are about attention — understand where it is created, build a distribution loop, watch what compounds, teach what you learn, and learn how buyers decide.
