# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DWlShR_DsUV — Pattern Interrupt

## Term

Pattern Interrupt here is why the first email worked — it happens with the prospect's data, not the sender's pitch, and the brain wasn't expecting it. The phrase appears once, beside 'their data', 'not your pitch', and 'Brain wasn’t expecting it'. The post compares five emails that got clients against five that hit spam, and names a device for each winner: pattern interrupt, information gap, sunk cost trigger, trigger-based personalization, and pain intercept. The interrupt works by opening with the prospect's own numbers — 3,000 visitors on the pricing page — instead of an introduction.
