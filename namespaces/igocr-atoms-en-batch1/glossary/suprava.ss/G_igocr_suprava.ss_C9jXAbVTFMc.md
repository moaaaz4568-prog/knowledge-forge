# G_igocr_suprava.ss_C9jXAbVTFMc — Lead magnet

## Term

A lead magnet in this source is the free asset Suprava gives away to collect contacts — Taskade is named for making lead magnets, and the same post pairs them with smart leads sent by email. The phrase appears twice, once as 'Lead magnet' and once as 'lead magnets', beside 'Use Taskade', 'smart leads', and 'Cold-email campaign'. In use, a lead magnet sits at the start of the flow: Taskade builds it, then instantly and smart leads carry it to inboxes, and Gamma ai repurposes the same material. The post treats the magnet as the entry point of a multichannel funnel, not as the product itself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a free product in itself: the magnet here is bait for contact details, built with Taskade and followed by an email sequence, not something the audience buys or keeps as the offer.
- Read as any downloadable file: a file counts as a lead magnet in this post only when it feeds the funnel — the post ties it to tracking visitors, verifying data, and sending cold emails, not to handing out content for its own sake.
