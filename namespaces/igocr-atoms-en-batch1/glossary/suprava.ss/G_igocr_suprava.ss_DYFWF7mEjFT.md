# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DYFWF7mEjFT — directories

## Term

Directories here are the free places to find clients — Clutch for agency clients, DesignRush for design agencies, Klaviyo for e-commerce businesses, Goodfirms for IT and software, Product Hunt for SaaS, and more through Zillow and Yelp. The word appears once, beside 'find clients', 'for free', and 'Clutch.co'. Each directory is matched to a client type: AgencyVista for marketing agencies, Semrush for SEO agencies, Avyo for lawyers, Hacker News for YC startups hiring, Yelp for local businesses. A directory in this source is a hunting ground sorted by who hangs out there, not a list of companies to cold-call blindly.
