# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DZ67Omfkeh1 — reddit-researcher

## Term

Reddit-researcher is the Claude skill that finds people complaining right now — tell Claude the niche and the problem, and it searches Reddit for posts where people vent about it, ranked by relevance. The name appears three times, beside 'Find people complaining', 'last 30 days', and 'ranked list'. It is step one of a seven-step playbook: find the complaint, craft a personal message with the LinkedIn DM or cold-email PAS skill, research the ICP, send one DM a day like a human, bring up the offer only after they name the problem, and automate the repeat part weekly. One thread is one lead, and sending stays manual because Reddit bans mass-DM accounts.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a lead list generator: the skill returns ranked threads of real complaints, and each thread still needs a personal message, an ICP pass, and a human send — the finding is step one of seven.
- Read as an automation tool: the playbook bans automation on the sending side — one DM per thread, one thread per day — so the skill automates research and writing, never the send.
