# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DWgbrexkvu7 — expected path

## Term

Expected path here is the label on the conventional route the entrepreneur is supposed to follow — college, a clear direction, a mentor — set against the real journey of failing, stopping telling people, and starting again. The phrase appears three times, beside 'Hates college', 'the rock bottom', and 'No clear direction'. The post's arc runs from hating college and feeling different, through the first sale and the failure that breaks the ego, to rock bottom and the quiet phase of showing up on a Sunday when nobody is watching. The expected path is what the journey is not.

- Read as a map the post recommends: the expected path is the route that fails in the story — the real journey leaves it, fails, and starts again without it.
