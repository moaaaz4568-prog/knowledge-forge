# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DG5LTZsMg5u — triggers

## Term

Triggers here are the buying signals Suprava watches for instead of personalising cold emails — hiring, community scraping, job changes, negative press, podcasts, a competitor releasing a feature, a new tech stack. The word appears five times, beside 'High intent', 'Personalisation', and 'Hiring'. Her test run proves the point: high-intent triggers gave 9 good leads from 637 emails, while AI personalisation gave 3 from 4,400. The tools that find them are Clay, ScrapingBee, and Trigify, and the example email turns a competitor's new feature into a subject line about stolen customers.
