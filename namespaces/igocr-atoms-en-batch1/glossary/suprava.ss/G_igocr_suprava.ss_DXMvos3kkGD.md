# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DXMvos3kkGD — viral loop

## Term

Viral loop here is the mechanism that makes a product spread on its own — every new user brings in more users, a chain reaction of exponential growth, and the ideal loop is as quick and frictionless as possible. The phrase appears once, beside 'chain reaction', 'exponential growth', and 'frictionless'. The post's examples are Facebook and PayPal, where every share or payment invites more people, and funny videos or memes, where a kitten is seen, felt warm about, and forwarded in seconds. The loop is the product's core functionality encouraging invites, not a marketing campaign around it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as going viral: a viral loop here is a built-in invite in the product's core function, not a video that happens to get millions of views.
