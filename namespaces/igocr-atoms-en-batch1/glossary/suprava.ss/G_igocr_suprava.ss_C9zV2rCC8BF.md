# G_igocr_suprava.ss_C9zV2rCC8BF — leads

## Term

Leads here are the people Suprava's tips are trying to win — quality leads from webinars, 27% more leads from a free community, and leads generated through copywriting and automation. The word appears three times, beside 'quality', 'Webinars', and 'generate'. The post measures everything in leads: webinars give 30% more chances of quality leads than any other method, a free community beats an email list by 27% more leads, and weak copywriting means no leads at all. A lead in this source is a prospect close enough to convert, not a name on a list.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as raw traffic numbers: the post counts leads as people who can convert — webinars and communities are ranked by the quality leads they give, not by views or followers.
- Read as a synonym for subscribers: an email list is the comparison point here, and the post says a free community gives 27% more leads than building an email list, so a subscriber who never converts is not a lead in this sense.
