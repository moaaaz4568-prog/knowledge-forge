# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DaqHIlAEpyv — Cold email templates

## Term

Cold email templates here are the five emails that got Suprava clients — 'you are trending', 'your competitors can't', 'Influencer push last month?', 'before your new AEs ramp', and 'saw your post about cold outreach'. The phrase appears once, beside 'got me clients', 'you are trending', and '3% to 27% reply rate'. Each template is shown in full: a subject, an observation about the prospect, an agent or engine already running for named companies, and a small ask. The results quoted are concrete: 42,000 paid users from one content push, ramp time cut from 90 to 28 days, and reply rates lifted from 3% to 27%.

- Read as templates to send as-is: every template has bracketed slots — [Co. 1], [Company], [Their Product] — and the results quoted belong to the sender's own clients, not to the reader.
- Read as five different strategies: the five share one shape — an observation, a working agent, a proof number, and a small ask — so the variation is in the trigger, not the structure.
