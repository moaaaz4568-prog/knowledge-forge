# G_igocr_suprava.ss_DIbjKYkC9jI — ice breakers

## Term

Ice breakers here are the one-sentence personalized openers GPT-4 writes for each cold email, built from the lead's own data so the email feels human. The phrase appears four times, beside 'personalized', 'GPT-4', and 'human'. The prompt Suprava gives is exact: start with 'I know you are tired of automated emails...', prove it with one very specific detail from the prospect's website, one sentence only, and delete all superfluous information. The ice breaker is one module of a larger agent — search, enrich, qualify with a RAG qualifier, then the breaker, then auto-add to Smartlead or LinkedIn campaigns.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as small talk at the top of an email: the ice breaker here must prove the email is not automated with a specific detail from the prospect's site — a generic compliment does not count.
- Read as the whole personalization: the breaker is only the opener; the post qualifies the lead first with a RAG agent and scores it high, medium, or low before any breaker is written.
