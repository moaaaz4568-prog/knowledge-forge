# G_igocr_geniuzss_DGUSd-5CtRU — solitude

## Term

"solitude" appears 7 times in this source, the positive pole of Renuka Gavrani's The Art of Being Alone, set against loneliness from the cover line onward: loneliness was my cage, solitude is my home. The book's two parts turn loneliness into solitude, then solitude into a growth period, and its sharpest definition is that loneliness is not having no people around — it occurs when you cannot find yourself inside you, so you can feel lonely while surrounded by people. Wayne W. Dyer's line quoted in the book closes the point: you cannot be lonely if you like the person you're alone with. The words most often collocated with it are: loneliness, growth, love, alone.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as loneliness with a nicer name: the book draws the line sharply — loneliness is not when you don't have people around, it occurs when you cannot find yourself inside you; solitude is liking the person you're alone with.
- Read as isolation from people: the book's solitude is a growth period — turning loneliness into solitude, then solitude into growth — not cutting people off; people leave and life goes on, but the work is facing your own mind.
