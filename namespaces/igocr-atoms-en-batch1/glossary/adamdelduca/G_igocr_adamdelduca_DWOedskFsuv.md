# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWOedskFsuv — YouTube Automation Timeline Roadmap

## Term

In this carousel, the "YouTube Automation Timeline Roadmap" maps income to time: month 1 pays $0 for selecting a niche, setting up the channel and planning content, months 2 to 6 pay $1K for uploading and identifying outliers, and months 6 to 24 pay $5K for optimizing videos and integrating offers. It appears once, as the title of the first image, and the post gives no step that earns before month 2. The roadmap's point is patience: the money comes in the third phase, after the uploading and the learning. A reader copying it would judge the plan at month 6, not month 1, when the income is still $0.
