# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWB0Hcnlo2b — in-demand niche

## Term

In this carousel, an "in-demand niche" is the niche a beginner should pick instead of a topic nobody watches, as step 2 of a six-step process already 60 days into 2026. It appears 2 times: once in the opening list "Select an in-demand niche and niche" and once in "Next, select an in-demand niche", where the test is videos already getting 100k to 1M views. The examples given are "Car secrets", "Business stories" and "Product warnings", so demand means proven views, not personal interest. A reader copying it would check that videos in the niche already reach six figures before committing to it.
