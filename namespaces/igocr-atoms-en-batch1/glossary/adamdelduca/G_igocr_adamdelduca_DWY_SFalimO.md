# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWY_SFalimO — 1st year of YouTube Automation

## Term

In this carousel, the "1st year of YouTube Automation" is a month-by-month earnings ladder: Jan-Apr at $0, May at $257, Jun at $592, Jul at $1,081, Aug at $2,125, Sept at $3,918, Oct at $4,593, Nov at $6,141 and Dec at $7,962. It appears once, in the heading "How much I earned my 1st year of YouTube Automation", and the post then explains how the author would do it again from scratch. The ladder's point is the slow start: four months at zero come before any money at all. A reader copying it would plan for a zero-income first third of the year rather than expecting money in month one.
