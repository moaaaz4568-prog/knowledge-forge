# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DV_PbMYlqtI — VPH

## Term

In this carousel, "VPH" is the metric for spotting video topics worth making, defined in the post itself as "Views Per Hour". It appears 2 times, both in step 2: "Find high VPH topics" and the definition "VPH = Views Per Hour". The step explains the use: VPH identifies high-interest topics, and the author finds them with "the VIDIQ trending topics features", so the number ranks topics by how fast they gather views. A reader following the step would open VIDIQ's trending topics, sort by VPH, and pick the fast-rising topics as the next videos to make.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for total views: VPH measures views per hour, so an old video with many views can have a low VPH while a new video with fewer views ranks higher.
- Read as a VIDIQ-only number: the post defines VPH as views per hour and says the author uses VIDIQ to find it, but the metric itself is just a rate, not a feature of one tool.
