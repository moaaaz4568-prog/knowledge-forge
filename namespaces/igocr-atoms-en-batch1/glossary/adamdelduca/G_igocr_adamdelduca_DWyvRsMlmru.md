# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWyvRsMlmru — Double down on winners

## Term

In this carousel, "Double down on winners" is the last step of making $6,000 a month from YouTube with no fluff: find a niche with "TubeLab", pick a unique format, identify outlier topics with "VIDIQ", produce with "VidRush", upload 20 to 30 videos, then double down on winners. It appears once, in that opening six-step list, and step 6 explains it: stop guessing, look at the winners, scale what has high CTR and high retention, and cut everything else. The step's point is selection after volume: the doubling only starts once 20 to 30 videos have produced data. A reader copying it would upload the volume first and only then double down on what already won.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of $6,000 a month: the figure is the plan's target, and the post shows no channel that reached it by doubling down.
- Read as doubling down from the start: the step comes after uploading 20 to 30 videos, so there is nothing to double down on before the volume exists.
