# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVvyl4PjbBd — faceless YouTube channel

## Term

In this carousel, "faceless YouTube channel" is the channel that earns without showing its creator, here a documentary channel making $18,000 a month at only 6 months old. It appears once, in the opening claim "This faceless YouTube channel is making $18,000/mo", and the rest of the post lists 5 niches that can make money fast, from "Religious History" to "US Buyer Warnings". The example channel "Lume" has 131K subscribers and 66 videos of original documentaries released weekly, which is the model the post holds up. A reader copying it would pick one of the five niches, stay off camera, and publish weekly documentaries rather than vlogs.
