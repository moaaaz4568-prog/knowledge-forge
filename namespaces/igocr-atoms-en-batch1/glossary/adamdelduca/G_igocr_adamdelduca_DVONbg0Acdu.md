In this post, "Does Your Niche Matter?" is the question a demand, supply and RPM table is meant to answer. It appears once, above a table that scores four niches: "Jets" with Mid demand, Low supply and $5.04 RPM; "Geography" with Mid demand, Mid supply and $3.05 RPM; "Nostalgia" with Mid demand, Low supply and $4.27 RPM; and "Survival" with Mid demand, Mid supply and $7.01 RPM. A reader answering the question from this table would say yes: the niche changes the RPM from $3.05 to $7.01, even when demand stays Mid.
