# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVQ_8BzgQ0M — MILLION SUBSCRIBERS

## Term

In this post, "MILLION SUBSCRIBERS" is the end of a five-year timeline that starts today. It appears once, at the bottom of a ladder that reads "TODAY", "IN A MONTH", "IN A YEAR", "1K VIEWS", "IN 5 YEARS" and then "MILLION SUBSCRIBERS". The ladder's steps are uneven on purpose: a month, then a year, then only a thousand views, then five years to a million subscribers, so the payoff is placed far in the future. A reader measuring their own channel against the image would expect the first year to bring just 1K views and treat the million subscribers as a five-year outcome, not a first-year one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a schedule that guarantees a million subscribers in five years: the image lists time markers and milestones with no method, no channel and no evidence behind any step.
- Read as a claim that 1K views takes a full year: the ladder prints "IN A YEAR" above "1K VIEWS" as a milestone marker, and the image gives no dates or growth rates to read as a timetable.
