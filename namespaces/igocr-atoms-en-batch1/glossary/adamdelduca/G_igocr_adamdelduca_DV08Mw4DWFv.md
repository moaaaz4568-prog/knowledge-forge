# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DV08Mw4DWFv — winning video topics

## Term

In this carousel, "winning video topics" names the step after niche selection: finding subjects that already attract views before filming anything. It appears once, in the slide where the author explains finding new, high "VPH" topics in the niche using "VIDIQ" and then approaching them with a "unique angle". The phrase sits between two other named steps — scanning for "$5,000/mo" niches with "TubeLab" and producing videos without editing through "Vidrush" — so it marks the research stage of a fixed sequence. In practice the post's reader would open VIDIQ, look for high-VPH topics inside the chosen niche, and only then decide what to make.
