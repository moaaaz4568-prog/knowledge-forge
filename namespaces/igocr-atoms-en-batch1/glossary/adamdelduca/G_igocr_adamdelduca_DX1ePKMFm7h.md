# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DX1ePKMFm7h — Under $50/Mo

## Term

In this carousel, "Under $50/Mo" is the budget tier of a YouTube Automation toolkit: "GenAIPro", "ChatGPT" and "Canva" sit under $50 a month, against a mid-tier of $50-100 a month with "TubeLab", "SubscribeAI" and "Pikzels", and a high-cost tier over $100 a month with "TubeGen", "VidRush" and "InVideo". It appears once, as the first tier label "Budget - Under $50/Mo", and the toolkit's point is price first: the tools are sorted by what they cost, not by what they do. A reader copying it would start on the budget tier and move up only when the channel can pay for the next one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that the budget tier is enough: the toolkit sorts tools by price, and gives no test showing the under-$50 tools match the high-cost tier.
- Read as exact prices: the tiers give price bands, not price tags, so a tool's real cost still needs checking.
