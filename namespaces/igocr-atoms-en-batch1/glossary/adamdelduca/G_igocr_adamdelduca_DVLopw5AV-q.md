In this post, the "YouTube Automation Gameplan for 2026" is a table of three niches with their RPM and their tools. It appears once, heading rows that give "Books" an RPM of $14 with "TubeBuddy" and "Videoscribe", "Cars" an RPM of $5 with "VIDIQ" and "Vidrush", and "History" an RPM of $10 with "VIDIQ" and "TubeGen". A reader picking from the table would weigh the RPM against the tool pair, since Books pays $14 but needs different tools from the $5 Cars row.
