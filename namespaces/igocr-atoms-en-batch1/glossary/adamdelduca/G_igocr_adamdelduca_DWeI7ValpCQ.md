# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWeI7ValpCQ — AMI Headline Analyzer

## Term

In this carousel, the "AMI Headline Analyzer" is the titling tool in a $5,000-a-month toolkit: "3. Title: AMI Headline Analyzer" in the opening list, and step 3 says "my student uses AMI Headline Analyzer" to test titles before use. It appears 2 times: once in the list and once in the titling step, where the test is to enter a title and aim for a score of 40% or higher. The tool's job is pre-testing: a title gets scored before it is published, so a weak title never reaches viewers. A reader copying the toolkit would run every title through the analyzer and keep only those scoring 40% or more.
