# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVn2cjgDWLU — YouTube Essentials

## Term

In this post, "YouTube Essentials" are the four things the author says a $10,000/mo channel needs — including coffee. It appears once, in the title "$10,000/mo YouTube Essentials", above four slides: "STARBUCKS COFFEE" to "Energize to ideation and produce videos", "TubeLab" to find high-profit niches, "VidRush" to create "entire videos in one single click", and "Studio" to "Analyze your video and channel analytics". Coffee is listed first and treated as an essential like the tools, so the post's joke is also its method: energy first, then niches, then production, then analytics. A reader copying the list would start with coffee, pick a niche in TubeLab, produce the video in VidRush, and check the result in Studio.
