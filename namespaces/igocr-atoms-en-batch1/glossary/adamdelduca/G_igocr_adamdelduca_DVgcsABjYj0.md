# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVgcsABjYj0 — AMI Headline Analyzer

## Term

In this carousel, the "AMI Headline Analyzer" is the tool for testing video titles before they are used. It appears 2 times: once in the "$3,000/mo YouTube channel toolkit" list as step 3, "Title: AMI Headline Analyzer", and once in the titling step itself. The step explains the use: a title "can make or break a video's success", the analyzer is free, and the author tells the reader to enter a title, "aim for a score of 40% or higher" and "keep re-iterating until you find a winner". A reader following the step would draft a title, run it through the analyzer, and rewrite it until the score passes 40%, before spending any time on the video itself.

- Read as a promise that a 40% score guarantees views: the post only says to aim for 40% or higher and keep re-iterating, and gives no views or results tied to any score.
