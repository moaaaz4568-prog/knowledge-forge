# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DW1GYfKFtu9 — 4 AI Apps

## Term

In this carousel, "4 AI Apps" are the four replacements for four YouTube jobs: "Scripts", "Voice", "Edits" and "Design", introduced by "I Replaced These 4 YouTube Tasks With 4 AI Apps". It appears once, in that opening line, and the post never names the four apps — it shows only the four jobs they replace. The swap is the whole point: each human task gets one app, with a "Swipe >>>" prompt to see more. A reader copying it would map each of their own four jobs to one app rather than hiring for them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a named stack of four apps: the post lists the four jobs, not the apps, so a reader cannot tell which app replaces scripts or voice from this post alone.
- Read as proof that AI replaces the work: the carousel claims the replacement in its headline and shows no before-and-after output for any of the four jobs.
