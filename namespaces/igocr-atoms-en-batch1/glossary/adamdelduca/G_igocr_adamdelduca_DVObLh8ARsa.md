# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVObLh8ARsa — YOUTUBE SHORTCUTS

## Term

In this post, "YOUTUBE SHORTCUTS" names the habit the author warns against: skipping the real work of growing a channel. It appears once, in the sentence "WHEN WE TAKE YOUTUBE SHORTCUTS... ...WE MISS OUT VIEWER GROWTH & ENGAGEMENT." The cost is named, not the shortcut: the post says what is lost — viewer growth and engagement — without saying which shortcuts it means. A creator reading it would take the warning as a rule to do the slow work, since the post offers no shortcut that keeps growth and engagement intact.
