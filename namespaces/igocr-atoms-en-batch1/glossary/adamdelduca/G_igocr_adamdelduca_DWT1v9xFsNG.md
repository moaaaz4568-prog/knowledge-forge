# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWT1v9xFsNG — $16,000 a month

## Term

In this carousel, "$16,000 a month" is what an AI YouTube Automation channel makes at only 3 months old: "This AI YouTube Automation channel is making $16,000 a month The crazy part? It's only 3 months old". It appears once, in that opening claim, and the post then lists 10 niches that can make $10,000 a month, from "Biblical Healing" to "Space". The figure's point is speed: three months from launch to sixteen thousand a month is the hook that sells the niche list. A reader copying it would pick one of the ten niches and expect the same speed only if their channel grows the same way.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of $16,000 in 3 months: the figure is the post's opening claim about one channel, and the ten niches that follow are suggestions, not that channel's earnings.
- Read as a list of niches that each pay $16,000: the niches are pitched at $10,000 a month, so the $16,000 belongs to the example channel, not to the list.
