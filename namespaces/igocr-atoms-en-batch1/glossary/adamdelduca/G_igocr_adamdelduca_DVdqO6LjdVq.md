# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVdqO6LjdVq — Monetization Checker

## Term

In this carousel, the "Monetization Checker" is a free Chrome extension from TubeLab that checks whether a video or channel can earn. It appears once, on TubeLab's $20/mo tool page, where it is described as a download that checks "adSense status and monetization potential for any video or channel on YouTube". It sits among TubeLab's other finders on the same page — the "YouTube Niche Finder", the "YouTube Outliers Finder", "YouTube Title Formulas", "AI Outlier Ideation" and the "Niche Analyzer" — as the check you run before committing to a niche. A creator would install the extension, open any video or channel, and read its AdSense status and monetization potential before deciding the niche is worth entering.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee that a checked channel is monetized: the extension only checks AdSense status and potential, and the post gives no example of a result it returns.
- Read as a TubeLab subscription feature: the post calls it a free Chrome extension download on a page that also lists TubeLab at $20/mo, and never says the checker itself costs anything.
