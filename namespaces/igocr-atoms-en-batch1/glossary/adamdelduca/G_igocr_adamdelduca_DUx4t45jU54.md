# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DUx4t45jU54 — YouTube Automation

## Term

Here "YouTube Automation" introduces a different question from the toolkit post: how much each niche can pay per month. It appears once, heading a list that ties "$1,000/mo" to "ANIME", "$5,000/mo" to "FOOTBALL", "$10,000/mo" to "INVESTING" and "$20,000/mo" to "ECONOMY". The niche, not the tool, is the variable this time — the same automation model is assumed throughout, and the income changes with the topic chosen. Someone using the post would read it as a niche menu: pick "ECONOMY" for the top figure, "ANIME" for the lowest, and expect the gap to come from the audience's value rather than from extra work.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as measured earnings for real channels in each niche: the post prints round income labels beside niche names and gives no channel, no timeframe and no evidence behind any figure.
- Read as advice that ECONOMY is the best niche for everyone: the "$20,000/mo" label sits beside "ECONOMY" only as the highest rung, and the post says nothing about competition, effort or who can enter that niche.
