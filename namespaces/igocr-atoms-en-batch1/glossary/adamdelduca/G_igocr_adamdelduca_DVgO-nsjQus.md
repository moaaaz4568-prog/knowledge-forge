## Term

In this post, the "YouTube Workflow" is the production choice priced per ten minutes of video. It appears once, heading a three-way price list under the "$10,000/mo" label: "UpWork" at "$75", "TubeGen" at "$20" and "VidRush" at "$25". The gap is the argument: hiring on UpWork costs roughly three times the AI tools, with TubeGen cheapest at $20 and VidRush in the middle at $25, all for the same ten minutes of video. A reader choosing a workflow from the post would compare the $75 human option against the $20 and $25 AI options and decide by budget, since the image gives no quality or speed figures to compare.
