# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWRDONQlkdG — YouTube Starter Kit

## Term

In this carousel, the "YouTube Starter Kit" is a seven-tool list for a $10,000-a-month channel: "Subscribr" for scripts, "NexLev" for niches, "VIDIQ" for topics, "VidRush" for editing, "Pikzels" for thumbnails, "Creator Hooks" for titles and "Studio" for performance. It appears once, in the heading "$10,000/mo YouTube YouTube Starter Kit", and each tool gets one line that says what it does. The list's point is completeness: every stage from niche to review has its named tool. A reader copying it would take the seven tools as a starting stack and replace any one of them only after testing it.
