# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVYuRggjYWW — Faceless channels

## Term

In this carousel, "Faceless channels" are channels that earn without their owner appearing on camera, built with AI tools. It appears once, in the opening claim that faceless channels "are making people rich" and "you can build them using AI", followed by "5 channels you can build to $10,000/mo". The five examples run from 5 to 1 — "Canadian Shopping" at $6.03 RPM, "Travel Destinations" at $7.93, "Quakes" at $6.03, "Military Updates" at $5.17 and "Naval History" at $7.76 — each paired with an AI tool, "Vidrush" or "TubeGen". A reader copying the post would pick one of the five niches, take its RPM as the earning rate, and build with the AI tool named beside it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that faceless channels make people rich: the post names five example channels with RPM figures and shows no earnings for any of them, so the opening claim is an assertion, not evidence.
- Read as a ranking where number 1 earns most: "Naval History" is listed first at $7.76 RPM, but "Travel Destinations" carries a higher $7.93 RPM, so the order is not sorted by RPM.
