# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DW3rJXhFojn — YouTube RPMs

## Term

In this carousel, "YouTube RPMs" are the per-niche earnings table headed "YouTube RPMs (based on niche)", ranking twelve niches from $1-2 to $10+. It appears once, as that table's heading, with "Pranks", "Welding" and "Cartoons" at $1-2, "Fitness", "Aviation" and "Biblical" at $3-5, "Travel", "Space" and "AI" at $6-9, and "Crypto", "Real Estate" and "Legal" at $10+. The table's point is that the niche sets the pay per thousand views, so the same views earn very different money. A reader using it would pick a niche from the top rows rather than the bottom ones if income is the goal.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as exact pay rates: the table gives wide bands like $1-2 and $10+, so it ranks niches against each other rather than quoting what any channel is paid.
- Read as a reason to pick only $10+ niches: the post ranks pay, not demand or competition, so a high-RPM niche with no audience still earns nothing.
