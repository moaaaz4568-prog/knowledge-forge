# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DVd378CDVvU — faceless YouTube channel

## Term

In this carousel, a "faceless YouTube channel" is again the model: a channel that earns with no presenter, this time "completely AI". It appears once, in the opening slide, which says the channel is making "$23K a month", is "completely AI", and introduces "10 niches that can make you $10,000/mo". The ten niches run from "Biblical" and "Motorcycles" through "Buyer Warnings", "Production Plants", "Firearms", "Nostalgia", "War Footage", "Collisions" and "Airplanes" to "Tragedies", with "Sleepy Time History" shown first as the example of the format. A reader would take the phrase as the same model with a bigger number attached: pick one of the ten niches, run it with AI, and stay off camera while it earns.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that any of the ten niches earns $10,000/mo: the post shows screenshots of existing channels in each niche and gives no earnings figure for any of them except the unnamed $23K channel.
- Read as a list of channels the author runs: the ten niches are illustrated with other people's channels, and the post does not say the author owns any of them.
