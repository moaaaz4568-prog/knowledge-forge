# G_igocr_adamdelduca_DWc2irtFp6m — per 1,000,000 views

## Term

In this carousel, "per 1,000,000 views" is the pay scale for 2026: "Tech" pays $6,000, "Crypto" $9,000, "Dividends" $12,000 and "Credit Cards" $17,000 per million views. It appears once, in the heading "How much YouTube pays you per 1,000,000 views in 2026", and the post explains the gap with purchasing power: viewers from high-income countries who are older have more money to spend. The scale's point is that the topic sets the pay, not the view count: credit cards pay nearly three times tech for the same million views. A reader using it would pick a topic from the top of the scale rather than chasing raw views.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as exact pay per million views: the four figures are the post's own scale, and the post shows no channel paid exactly those amounts.
- Read as proof that credit cards beat tech for everyone: the scale ranks pay per view, not demand, so a high-paying topic with no audience still earns nothing.
