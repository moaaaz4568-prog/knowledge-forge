## Term

"accountable" appears once in this source, a single-image post quoting Geoff Ketterer on the value of being held to account. The person who holds you accountable cares more than the one who says nothing and allows you to settle for mediocrity: accountability here is framed as care, not criticism, and silence as permission to stay mediocre. The post is dated 14 Mar 2023 and carries no advice beyond that contrast between the one who speaks up and the one who stays quiet. ## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as accountability meaning harsh criticism: the post frames the person who holds you accountable as the one who cares more, not as an adversary, and contrasts them with the silent bystander rather than with a supporter.
- Read as a rule that any silence is permission to settle: the post speaks of one person saying nothing while another settles for mediocrity, not of every quiet moment or every unspoken thought.
