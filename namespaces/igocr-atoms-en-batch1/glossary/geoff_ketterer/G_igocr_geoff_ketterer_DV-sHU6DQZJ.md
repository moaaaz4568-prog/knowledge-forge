# G_igocr_geoff_ketterer_DV-sHU6DQZJ — objections

## Term

"objections" appears 5 times in this source, a six-slide carousel arguing that objections are a marketing problem, not a sales problem. When someone gets on a call already convinced and already pre-sold, they do not have objections but questions — so handling the same objections on repeat means the marketing did not do its job, and the call is trying to fix a problem that should have been solved before the prospect booked. The remedy is a pre-education system that eliminates objections: trust-building content before the call, pre-call sequences that pre-sell the offer, and qualification that filters out tyre-kickers before they book. The words most often collocated with it are: marketing problem, questions, pre-education, tyre-kickers.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that objections never happen: Geoff says a pre-sold prospect has questions instead of objections, not that no prospect ever objects — the claim is about what good marketing removes, not about abolishing objections entirely.
- Read as an instruction to abandon all sales training: the post says to stop training for objections and start building a pre-education system, replacing one kind of preparation with another rather than ending preparation.
