# G_igocr_geoff_ketterer_DWWKYrvl7aD — pre-selling systems

## Term

"pre-selling systems" appears twice in this source, a ten-slide carousel telling Geoff's story from chef to sales-systems coach. He used to be a chef working 60-70 hours a week, fell into sales, and in 2018 started selling online as a remote closer, where he grew fascinated with marketing because the better the marketing, the easier the closes happened. The problem he kept seeing was everyone blaming sales for low revenue when marketing was the real issue — the warmer the lead, the easier the close — so he left consulting to start his own coaching company focused entirely on helping people build pre-selling systems. The words most often collocated with it are: close, marketing, warmer, lead.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a biography that proves the systems work for everyone: the carousel is Geoff's own story — chef, remote closer, consultant, then coach — and it offers his path as an example, not as evidence that the same path produces the same outcome for any reader.
- Read as a claim that sales skill does not matter: Geoff says marketing was the real issue behind low revenue, not that selling is irrelevant — his own story includes getting good at sales by treating it like an actual career.
