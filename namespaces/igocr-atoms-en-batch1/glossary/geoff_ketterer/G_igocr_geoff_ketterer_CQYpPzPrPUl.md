# G_igocr_geoff_ketterer_CQYpPzPrPUl — 7 Figure Club

## Term

"7 Figure Club" appears once in this source, in an ad for membership into the club for earning over $1,000,000 with ad clients. Training and coaching come with it, from Liam Ryan & Jay Muno of Assets For Life. The million is the entry bar — the club is the status the earnings buy into, and the training is how you get there. The words most often collocated with it are: membership, $1,000,000, ad clients, training.
