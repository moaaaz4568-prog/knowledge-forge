# G_igocr_geoff_ketterer_CaZQhFHLAOA — pitch

## Term

"pitch" appears 4 times in this source, a five-slide carousel on the correct way to pitch your product or service. The first stage of the pitch occurs right at the start of the call: gathering specific details about your prospect's challenges, which is vital to communicating effectively during the pitch. Those specifics let you take your product or service and tailor it in a way that sounds irresistible to your target market, and during the conversation you make notes of the types of words your prospect uses when describing their problem — then you tailor the pitch in their words, as the John example shows. The words most often collocated with it are: product, service, prospect, tailor.
