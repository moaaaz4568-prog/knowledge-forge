# G_igocr_geoff_ketterer_DWgGa5DjYoE — 5-step process

## Term

"5-step process" appears once in this source, a nine-slide carousel on growing an online coaching business to $20k a month in three months. The five steps are one solution for one problem in one niche, a blend of organic content and $5-a-day ads that builds trust fast, booking one to two calls a day from posts and stories without a big following, turning $1 into $5 with paid ads once $1k a month was available to spend, and tracking data to run the business by the numbers. The process is framed as what let the business start working for him and build a full-time career that allows travel and remote work. The words most often collocated with it are: $20k a month, one solution, $5/day ads, tracking data.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that the five steps produce $20k a month in three months: the figure is what Geoff says he grew his own business to, and the carousel presents the steps as what he did rather than as a guaranteed outcome.
- Read as a no-audience method: the process still relies on organic content and $5-a-day ads to build trust and turn strangers into followers, and on paid ads once $1k a month is available — it avoids a big following, not all audience-building.
