History of Modern AI
(Artificial
Intelligence)
<!-- image 1 -->
1943
1950
Neural Network Theory
McCulloch and Pitts published the first mathematical model of a neural network. This became the foundation for every modern AI system in use today.
<!-- image 2 -->
Turing Test
Alan Turing asked the question that started it all: can machines think? His Imitation Game became the first real benchmark for machine intelligence.
19
1966
Dartmouth & ELIZA
John McCarthy coined the term Artificial Intelligence at Dartmouth in 1956. A decade later ELIZA became the first chatbot to mimic human conversation.
<!-- image 3 -->
74
AI Winter
Early AI promised too much and delivered too little. Funding dried up, hype collapsed, and the field went quiet for nearly a decade.
1986
Backpropagation
Researchers revived neural networks with backpropagation. This let machines learn from data instead of hand-coded rules. The shift from symbolic AI to machine learning had officially begun.
<!-- image 4 -->
Blue
1997
IBM's Deep Blue beat world champion Garry Kasparov at chess. It was the first time a machine outplayed a human at something we thought required pure intelligence.

Big Tech went all in. Google, Facebook and Microsoft started hiring every AI researcher they could find. The modern AI race officially started here.

2012
AlexNet
AlexNet crushed the ImageNet competition using GPUs and deep neural networks. This kicked off the deep learning era and changed AI forever.
<!-- image 5 -->
GANs
Ian Goodfellow invented Generative Adversarial Networks. Two neural nets competing against each other could now create realistic images, voices and video. The era of generative AI officially started here.
2014
AlphaGo
DeepMind's AlphaGo beat world champion Lee Sedol at Go, a game with more possible moves than atoms in the universe. AI was no longer just memorizing patterns. It was learning strategy.
2016
Voice AI
Voice assistants like Siri, Alexa and Google Assistant exploded into millions of homes. AI quietly moved from research labs into everyday life.
<!-- image 6 -->
The Transformer
Google researchers published "Attention Is All You Need". This one paper introduced the transformer architecture and quietly became the engine behind every modern AI model. GPT, Claude and Gemini all run on this.
2017
GPT-3
OpenAI shipped a 175 billion parameter language model that could write essays, code and emails. The prompt engineering era began here. The first signs of what AI could really do.
2020
<!-- image 7 -->
2022
ChatGPT
Hit 100 million users in two months and became the fastest growing product in history. AI walked out of research labs and into everyday life.
GPT-4 & multimodal AI
AI started seeing images, hearing voices and reasoning across formats. Every major company scrambled to ship its own model. The chatbot era turned into the agent era almost overnight.
