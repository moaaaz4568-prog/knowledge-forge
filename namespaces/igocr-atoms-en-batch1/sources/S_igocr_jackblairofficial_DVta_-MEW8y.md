<!-- image 1 -->

1. "If nothing changes in 6 months, what does that cost you?"

Most reps try to sell the upside. Buffett never cared about upside.

He cared about downside. What does this cost me if I'm wrong.

Flip that on your buyer. Stop selling what they gain.

Make the cost of doing nothing so specific and so real that your offer becomes the cheaper option.

That's not pressure. That's just math.
<!-- image 2 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

2. "I've been seeing something across a lot of businesses like yours, tell me if this sounds familiar."

Buffett's entire edge is seeing what everyone else refuses to look at.

When you bring a buyer a perspective they haven't considered you stop being a rep and start being a resource.

Resources don't get ghosted. Resources get called back.

Lead with an insight before you ever mention your product. That one shift changes everything.
<!-- image 3 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
3. "Is the budget not there or is this just not the priority right now?"
Two completely different problems. Most reps never figure out which one they're in.
Budget not there means restructure the deal.
Not a priority means you haven't made the case yet.
One question. Saves you three weeks of chasing the wrong thing.

@jackblairofficial

Say the thing every other rep is too scared to
say.

When you ask this with zero desperation in
your voice the buyer almost always tells you
what's actually going on.
