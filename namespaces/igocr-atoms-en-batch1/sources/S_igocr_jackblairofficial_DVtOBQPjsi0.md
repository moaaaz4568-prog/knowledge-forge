Prompt 3 — The Pitch Sharpener

"Here is my current pitch: [paste it]. Rewrite it so it leads with the buyer's problem instead of my product. Cut anything that sounds like a feature. Make it under 90 seconds when spoken out loud."

Prompt 4 — The Objection Deepdive

"My buyers keep saying [specific objection]. Write me 5 different ways to respond depending on whether it's a price objection, a timing objection, or a trust objection. Make each response feel natural and confident, not rehearsed."

This prompt makes sure you have the right answer no matter which version shows up.

Prompt 5 — The Roleplay Setup

"Act as a skeptical [buyer] who is interested but keeps stalling. I'm going to try to close you. Push back the way a real buyer would. After 10 exchanges tell me exactly what I did wrong and what lost the deal."

They read the prompt. They think about running it. And then they never actually do it.

They get on a live call and freeze on the exact objection they already knew was coming.
