How to Build
AI Agent with Claude
in 10 Minutes
No coding. No complex setup. Just Claude + a few simple steps.

STEP 1
Go to Claude (claude.ai)
Open your browser and visit claude.ai.
Sign in to your account (or create one if you’re new).

STEP 2
Use this prompt
“I want to build an AI agent. Ask me the right questions new to this, so keep it simple one by one to set it up. I’m beginner-friendly.”

STEP 3
Add a skill (optional but powerful)
Go to the Skills section and enable useful skills like:
Web Search, File Reader, or Code Interpreter.
This helps your agent do more.

STEP 4
Connect your tools
Go to the Connectors section and add the tools you need.
Examples: Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Slack, or custom APIs.
Just click, authorize and connect.

STEP 5
Set up your agent
Give your agent a name, description and define its goal.
Example: “Help me manage my emails and summarize important messages daily.”

STEP 6
Configure settings
Adjust the behavior, response style and permissions.
You can choose how it should respond, what tools to use, and what it can access.

STEP 7
Test your agent
Try a few real tasks. Ask it something like:
“Search for the latest AI news and give me a 3-point summary.”

STEP 8
Make it live
If everything works, save your agent. You can now use it anytime in Claude or through the web app.

STEP 9
Improve over time
See what works, tweak the prompt, add more skills or tools, and make it even better.
The more you use it, the smarter it gets.

STEP 10
Enjoy your AI agent!
You just built your first AI agent with Claude in 10 minutes.
Now go build something amazing!
