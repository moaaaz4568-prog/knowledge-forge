Step 5: Auto-launch in Smartlead

Once approved, the system builds the campaign in Smartlead via API, uploads 5,000 verified leads, configures the schedule and follow-ups, and starts sending.

Zero clicks inside Smartlead.

Step 6: Monitor and kill

Every night at 7:50 PM, the system checks every active campaign and kills the ones that hit any of these:

► Cost-per-lead too high
► Bounce rate above 5%
► Zero replies after 5,000 sends
► Out of leads → uploads the next 5,000

Bad campaigns die before they damage sender reputation. Winners get more leads.
