Most people vastly underestimate how much more visible they need to be
If you’re not getting hundreds of thousands of views per month or more, you’re going to struggle to make good money and feel like you’re in a constant scramble to get more customers and clients.
You probably need way more people seeing your stuff than you realize. It’s a number that deludes yourself into thinking you can get away with playing small or hiding behind the scenes in your business.
It doesn’t matter if you’re a genius or the best in the world at what you do. If you’re invisible, you’re still broke.
<!-- image 2 -->
2
Organic marketing is your testing ground. Paid ads is how you amplify what’s working organically. You need both.

Don’t get stuck in “either or” thinking. It’s not organic OR paid. It’s organic AND paid. If you want to grow quickly, combine them.

Anyone saying “organic is the way” or “paid ads are the way” is trying to sell you something, so they’re heavily incentivized to make you believe that “their way” is “the way”
<!-- image 3 -->
3
There will always be more work to do. There is no destination. Put down your work for the day. It’ll be there for you tomorrow.

As an entrepreneur there’s this feeling of needing to get to the finish line. So you’ll be tempted to work more than you should.

But when you realize the finish line is an illusion, you’ll see that what matters isn’t working longer or harder. What matters is simply staying consistent and doing a little bit each day to move yourself forward.
<!-- image 4 -->
4
If you need more than 25 hours a week, you’re probably being extremely inefficient with your time and focusing on the wrong things.
5 hours of focused work per day (or even less) should be enough to get your work done.
If it’s not, it’s a sign that you either need to eliminate, automate, or delegate certain tasks.
