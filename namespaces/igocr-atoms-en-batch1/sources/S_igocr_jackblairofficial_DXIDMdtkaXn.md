This phrase is not empathy. It is precision. It sounds warm enough to disarm and firm enough to hold your ground.

The buyer feels heard. The deal stays alive. You never moved an inch.

This phrase validates without agreeing. It redirects the energy toward consequence, not combat. The buyer stops debating price and starts thinking about cost.

3. "I am not going to pretend the investment is small. It is not. Neither is what you are solving."

This phrase owns the number with no apology. It pairs the price with the problem and makes both feel equal. Now the buyer is not comparing you to cheap, they are comparing bearing cheap to the cost of standing still.

By the time they finish answering, they have already sold themselves.

Let the buyer close the deal. You just asked the right question.

It resets the dynamic and puts you back in control of the room.
What follows it lands ten times harder because you earned the right to say it.

Respect is a frame. Set it early and never apologise for it.

6. "You do not have to decide today. I just want to make sure you have everything you need if you do."

It removes pressure without removing momentum. The buyer relaxes. Their guard drops. The real objection surfaces. Now you are solving the actual problem instead of fighting a decoy.

Give them the exit. Most will not take it.
