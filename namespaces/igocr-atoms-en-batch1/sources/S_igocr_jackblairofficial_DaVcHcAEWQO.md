2. Let me tell you where this isn't a fit.

In Originals, Grant tells of a founder who pitched investors with a slide listing the top reasons not to invest. It worked.

Leading with the flaw disarms the skeptic. Name the weakness first and the buyer stops hunting for one and starts arguing your side.

Every offer has a real limit. Say yours out loud before they find it.

Grant's own rule is to argue like you are right but listen like you are wrong.

Most reps hear an objection and reach for the counterpunch. The fight makes the objection stronger. Conceding the chance makes them defend it out loud, where it usually falls apart.

The objection they explain to you is one you can actually work with.
