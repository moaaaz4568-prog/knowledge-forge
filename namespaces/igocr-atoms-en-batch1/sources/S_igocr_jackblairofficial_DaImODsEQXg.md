1. "How soon could we actually get started?"

They've quietly switched from if to when.
In their head they already own it, they're
just asking about delivery.

Most reps hear a question and start re-
selling. Don't. Answer it like it's already
decided, because to them it is.
<!-- image 2 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

2. "What does it normally run?"

That word "normally" is the tell. They're slotting themselves in with your other buyers, the ones who already paid.

They're not asking whether, they're asking where they land. Say the number flat. The flinch is what reopens the door you just closed.
<!-- image 3 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

3. "When I'm using this, does it also..."

Listen for the tense. The second they say
"when I" instead of "if I," they've moved in
and started decorating the place.

Don't drag them back to the decision by
overselling. Confirm the thing and let
them keep picturing it.
<!-- image 4 -->

@jackblairofficial
4. (they go quiet)
The most misread moment in sales.
Silence isn't doubt, it's math. They're
adding it up.
The rep who panics and fills the air talks
them right out of it. Sit in it. The quiet is
them closing themselves.
<!-- image 5 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

5. "Do you work with many people like me?"

Translation: give me permission. They're not vetting you, they're looking for cover that someone like them did this and didn't get burned.

Don't list features. Tell them about the person who was sitting exactly where they are.
<!-- image 6 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
6. "What happens after I say yes?"
They already said it in their head. Now
they're asking about the honeymoon, not
the wedding.
Walk them through day one like it's
happening. The more real the after feels,
the harder it is to walk back the before.

7. "Yeah, but what about..."

The objection isn't the wall. It's the last thing they need handled before they let themselves buy.

A silent prospect is gone. A prospect arguing with you is still in the room. Answer it calm and clean, then ask for the close. They were waiting for you to.
