3. "On a gut level, how does this actually sit with you?"

Ekman's deception work centered on hot spots. The exact moments where words and behavior stop matching. That mismatch is a flag, not a verdict.

6. "Compared to the start of this call, where are you now?"

Ekman's rule for reading anyone: never judge a single expression. Read the change from their baseline.

Every buyer starts the call somewhere. Guarded. Curious. Tired. This question measures the distance traveled, and their answer tells you exactly what moved them.

Read the change, not the moment.

7. "Before we book anything, what's the part you're still not sold on?"
