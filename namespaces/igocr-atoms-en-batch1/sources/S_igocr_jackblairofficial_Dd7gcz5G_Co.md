Michael Scott: the people pleaser.

You make every buyer feel like your best friend. They laugh, they open up, and they'd grab a beer with you tomorrow.

Where it breaks: you need them to like you more than you need the yes. So you never ask for the money, and a great call ends with no decision.

Be liked. Then ask anyway.

Dwight Schrute: the hunter.

You know every spec, every price and
every weak spot in the other guy's offer.
You never miss a follow up and never stop.

Where it breaks: the buyer feels hunted.
You win every argument, and they sign
with the rep who made them feel heard.

Push the deal, not the person.

Jim Halpert: the natural.

You build rapport in thirty seconds. The buyer relaxes, the call feels easy, and you make it look like you're barely trying.

Where it breaks: you are barely trying. You coast on charm, skip the hard question, and leave money on the table because the call felt good.

Talent opens the call. Effort closes it.
