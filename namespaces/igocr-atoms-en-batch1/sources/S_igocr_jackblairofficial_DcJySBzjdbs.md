THE $1 GROUP CONVINCED THEMSELVES
In 1959 Festinger and Carlsmith had students do a mind numbing peg turning task, then paid them $1 or $20 to tell the next student it was fascinating.
The $20 group knew exactly why they lied. The $1 group had no excuse, so their minds quietly decided the task really had been interesting. Belief grows where outside justification runs out.
People believe what they cannot otherwise explain doing.

EVERY PRESSURE TACTIC IS A $20 BILL
A yes bought with countdown pressure or a manager push comes with its own explanation attached: I only signed because. That explanation waits like a receipt.
When you feel the urge to force a close, know what you are actually buying: a signature today, and a ready made reason to cancel later.
Pressure closes deals and pre-writes their cancellations.

THE DISCOUNT THAT UNSOLD THE DEAL
A large unearned discount is the cleanest outside
justification there is. The buyer's story becomes the
price, not the product.
If you concede anything, attach it to something they
did: a scope cut, a case study, a reference call.
Never let cheap be the reason they signed.
A buyer who signed for the discount stays until the
discount ends.

SMALL REASONS, THEIR MOUTH

The effect runs in your favour when the reasons are
the buyer's own and the outside push stays light.

Before the close, have them state what this fixes, in
their words, and write exactly that into the recap.
You are banking their justification, not yours.

The reason they said out loud is the one that
survives.

LET THE YES COST THEM SOMETHING SMALL
Festinger's principle: choice and effort bind belief. A yes that required nothing binds nothing.
Give the buyer a real decision between two configurations and one small task before kickoff.
Chosen and worked for beats granted and pushed.
A yes with fingerprints on it holds.

AFTER THE SIGNATURE, FEED THE BELIEF
Post decision doubt is real. Every buyer wakes at
2am asking whether they were fooled and whatever
they find next decides the renewal.
Inside week one, send their own stated reasons
back, plus the first small win. Give the belief
something to eat.
The sale ends the doubt or the doubt ends the sale.
