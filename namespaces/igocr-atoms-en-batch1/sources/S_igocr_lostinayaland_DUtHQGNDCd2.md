No. 2
Sell your offers like your life depends on it

No one is coming to save you. So learn how to sell. “But I’m a creative”, well you can be a broke artist for however long you want, but that won’t pay for lifestyle you have saved on your Pinterest board. Package your creativity, storytelling, editing skills and start pitching. It’s like s*x, gets better the more you do it.

Put your marketing hat on
Why do the brands you so badly want to work with need
content? To sends you so badly want to sell their products. Do you know who their target
audience is? Do you know why that audience is buying that
product? If you don't take the time to understand that, and only
focus on posting project screengrabs on your IG stories, you're
missing the point. Because without understanding the business
behind the content, you're just another person
not someone brands trust to build their growth.

No. 4
Network strategically
Stop DM-ing ChatGPT-ed bullsh*t to the people you admire.
Networking isn’t about taking something for yourself, it’s
about giving back. And if you think you have nothing to offer,
you’re wrong, you can always offer a genuine perspective.
But what matters more is progress. Showing up day after
day, building your body of work, becoming someone worth
knowing. That’s what gets you into those rooms. Real
connections are built when people see you’re serious, not
when you ask them to make you serious.

No. 5
Your brand comes first
I see a lot of people start building their personal brand, only to abandon it the moment they land a client brand, busy. Your brand stays bound over time, brings better opportunities to you and gives you leverage.
