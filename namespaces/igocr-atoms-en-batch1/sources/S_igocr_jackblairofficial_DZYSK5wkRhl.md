JEFFREY GITOMER WAS A STRUGGLING SALESMAN UNTIL ONE IDEA CHANGED EVERYTHING: PEOPLE LOVE TO BUY BUT HATE TO BE SOLD. HERE ARE THE 7 PHRASES THAT MADE HIM MILLIONS:
<!-- image 1 -->

1. "People love to buy but hate to be sold."

This was Gitomer's whole turn. He stopped pushing and started helping people convince themselves. The pressure that felt like selling was the exact thing pushing buyers away. Take the push out and the buying instinct takes over.
<!-- image 2 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
2. "Suppose we could, did you know that, I think we can."
Gitomer led with these to remove a buyer's risk one brick at a time. Each phrase quietly dismantles a fear before it becomes a no. Clear the risks one by one and the objection never gets built.
<!-- image 3 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

3. "Can you see any other reason not to proceed?"

After clearing the risks, he asked this to flush out the last hidden objection. Either there is nothing left, or the real blocker finally shows itself. Surface the last no on purpose so it cannot ambush the close.
<!-- image 4 -->

@jackblairofficial

4. "Decision makers don't need to talk it over with anyone."

When a buyer stalled with I need to check with someone, Gitomer read it as proof he was not in front of the real decision maker. Find the person who can say yes alone, or you are rehearsing, not closing.
<!-- image 5 -->

5. "They have to like you, believe you, trust you, then they may buy."

Gitomer's ladder ran like to believe to trust to confidence, in that order. Skip a rung and the sale collapses. Earn the rungs in sequence, because trust is what they are actually buying.

6. "It's not who you know, it's who knows you."

Gitomer built demand by becoming known before he ever needed the sale, so buyers came to him warm. Make your name travel ahead of you and selling turns into receiving. Be known and the room is warm before you speak.

7. "Resilience is how you react to, respond to, and recover from what happens."

Citomer treated every no as data, not damage. The rep who recovers fast outsells the talented one who sulks. Lose the feeling about the no and just run the next call.
