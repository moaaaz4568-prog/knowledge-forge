2. "What do you want it to do for you?"

Herman taught reps to stop describing
the product and start asking what the
buyer wanted it to accomplish.

The answer hands you the exact words to
close with, in the buyer's own language.
You are no longer guessing what matters,
they just told you.

Let them define the win, then sell them
that win.

3. "Tell me more about that."

Four plain words that kept the buyer talking long after most reps would have jumped in to pitch.

The longer they talk, the more they reveal what it will actually take to close them. Herman knew the rep who listens longest usually wins.

The quietest closer in the room is often the richest.

4. "Sell the appointment, not the product."
Herman taught that you don't close the whole deal on the first contact, you close the next step.
The only job of the opening is to earn the meeting where the real selling happens. Try to do everything at once and you lose the chance to do anything.
Win the next yes, not the whole thing at once.

5. "The sale is made in your head before theirs."

Herman believed the close happened in the rep's mind before a single word was spoken.

Walk in expecting the sale and your voice, posture and questions all carry it. Walk in hoping and the buyer feels that too. Certainty is contagious, and so is doubt.

Decide they're buying before you pick up the phone.

6. “When you’ve made the sale, stop talking.”
Herman watched countless reps win the yes and then keep talking until they talked the buyer right back out of it.
The moment that they agree, your job is done. Confirm it and go. Every extra word after yes is a new chance for doubt to creep in.
Get the yes, shut up, and write it down.

7. “I’ll make it easy to say yes.”

Herman's whole philosophy came down to removing friction, not adding pressure.

When the next step is small, clear and obvious, saying yes feels like the easy choice instead of the risky one. He sold Carson an ashtray on live TV by making yes the simplest thing in the room.

Don't push them to yes. Clear the path to it.
