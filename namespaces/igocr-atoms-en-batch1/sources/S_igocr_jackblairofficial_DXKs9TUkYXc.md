Every call is the first call if you decide it is.

7. "I am going to ask you to make a decision today. Not because I need you to. Because you need you to."

The buyer feels the weight of their own indecision without feeling pushed.
That shift, from rep's urgency to buyer's urgency, is where every real close lives.

Close for their reason, not yours. That's the only close that sticks.
