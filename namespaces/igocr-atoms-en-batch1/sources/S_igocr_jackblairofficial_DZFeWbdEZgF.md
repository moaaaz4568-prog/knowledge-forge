Once a buyer agrees to something small, they act in line with it. Get them to say out loud what the problem is early, and the close becomes them staying true to their own words.

A small yes sets up the big one.

3. "People exactly like you are already doing this."
Cialdini proved we follow what others do. Social proof.
Buyers look to similar people to decide what is safe. Show the buyer that others in their exact situation already chose this, and the decision stops feeling risky and starts feeling normal.
Show the crowd and the fear drops.
