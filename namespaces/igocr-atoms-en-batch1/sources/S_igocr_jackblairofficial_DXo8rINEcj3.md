Tone: confident, fresh.

No fluff. Zero guesswork, credibly specific.

03. Fresh Eyes Frame

"Write me 3 cold outreach messages for [job title]
at [company type] in the [industry] space
that turn my fresh perspective as someone new to this space
into the reason the buyer should speak to me.

My offer is: [one sentence].
My background before this role was: [one sentence].

Each message must:
Position my outsider perspective as a genuine advantage,
reference one observation about their industry that someone inside it might
have stopped noticing,
and end with one question that makes that observation feel worth exploring.

Under 80 words each.

Tone: curious, observational, genuinely fresh.

Zero guesswork."

You stop apologising for your newness and start selling the perspective it
gives you.
