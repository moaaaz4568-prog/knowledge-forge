STEP #1
ASK YOURSELF
THESE QUESTIONS

Was this account originally for friends and family that you later turned into a business account?

Were you posting in a different niche or on different topics than the ones you’re focusing on now?

If yes to either, it’s better to start a new account—your current audience likely won’t engage with your new content.

If this is NOT the case, skip this step and follow the next ones.
<!-- image 3 -->
STEP #2
FIND THE FIRST 10
VIRAL VIDEOS IN YOUR NICHE
Go to Instagram search, find content with
100K+ views in your space. Save their hooks
and formats. This way you will learn what's
performing in your niche.
<!-- image 4 -->
STEP #3
RECREATE WITH
YOUR OWN VALUE
Hooks
Formats
Take those viral hooks and formats, but give
your own solution and expertise. You're not
copying - you're strategically adapting what
already works.
<!-- image 5 -->
STEP #4
BATCH AND
SCHEDULE
MON TUE WED THU FRI SAT SUN
Create 7 videos in one day using your
research. Schedule them across the week.
This consistent output trains the algorithm
on your content style and niche.
<!-- image 6 -->
STEP #5
POST ONCE
PER DAY

Now that you have strategic content ready,
post consistently. This will make you learn
fast what's working and what's not and it
will allow you to pivot towards the right
strategy for you.
