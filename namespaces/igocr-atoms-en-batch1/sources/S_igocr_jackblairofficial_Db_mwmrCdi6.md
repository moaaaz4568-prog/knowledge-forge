THE HONEST POST MORTEM
"Here is my last call with a buyer who has gone quiet. Tell me the exact moment their tone changed and what I said right before it."
You will almost always find the sentence that cost you the deal, and it is rarely the one you remember.
Run it on every deal that dies. The pattern shows up by the third one.

THE REAL REASON
"Give me the five most likely reasons this buyer
stopped replying, ranked by probability, based only
on what they actually said."
It forces you off the comfortable explanation, which
is almost always that they just got busy.
The top two are what your next message has to
answer.

THE ONE LINE RE OPEN
"Write me one sentence I can send this buyer that asks for nothing and still gives them a reason to reply."
Most follow up fails because it asks for a meeting. A message that asks for nothing gets answered.
Send it, then say nothing until they come back.

"From this call, list everyone the buyer mentioned who could influence the decision, and what each one likely cares about."

"What objection did this buyer raise softly that I treated as a comment instead of a concern?"
The real objection is almost always mentioned once, quietly, early, and then moved past by both people.

THE COST OF WAITING
"Write three specific things that get worse for this buyer if they do nothing for another quarter, using only facts they gave me."
Urgency you invent gets ignored. Urgency quoted back from their own words does not.
Pick the one they said with the most emotion behind it.

THE PIPELINE VERDICT
"Based on everything here, tell me whether this deal is still real, and what single piece of evidence would prove it either way."
Most reps carry dead deals for months because nobody ever made them decide.
Run it across the whole list once a month and forecast honestly.

NONE OF THIS IS ABOUT EMAIL.
It is about seeing what you missed.
A deal is almost never dead because the buyer changed their mind. It is dead because the buyer got said, or not said, on a call nobody else ever went back and reviewed. The reps who close consistently are not the ones with better follow up. They are the ones who find out what actually happened, then say the one thing that reopens it.
No product fulfillment. No cold calling, all from a laptop. 👉
