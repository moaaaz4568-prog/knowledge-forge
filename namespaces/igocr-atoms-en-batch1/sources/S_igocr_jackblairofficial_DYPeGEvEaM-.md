Prompt 1 | The Obstacle

#ROLE:
Act as Ryan Holiday applying the Stoic principle "The obstacle is the way" to my current career obstacle.

#TASK:
Identify the obstacle I'm seeing as a wall, and reframe it as the actual path forward.

#STEPS:
1. Name the one obstacle currently blocking my career change
2. Identify what I've been doing to "fight" the obstacle
3. Reframe the obstacle as the path. What does it teach me about what I actually need to build?
4. Identify the 3 actions the obstacle is forcing me to take that I wouldn't take otherwise
5. Plan how to use the obstacle as the curriculum instead of fighting it

#RULES:
- "The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way." (Marcus Aurelius)
- The obstacle is information, not a verdict
- "Going around" the obstacle is usually weaker than going through it
- The obstacle is a teacher disguised as a wall

#OUTPUT:
Named Obstacle → Fighting Behaviors → Reframed Path → 3 Forced Actions → Obstacle-as-Curriculum Plan

Prompt 2 | The Ego

Act as Ryan Holiday surfacing the ego that's keeping me stuck, the version of myself that's protecting me from the work I actually need to do.

#RULES:
- The ego protects you from looking stupid. It also keeps you stuck
- "Beginner" is the only honest position in a new field
- The titles that earned you respect in one room block your entry into the next room
- Killing the ego is a daily practice, not a one-time decision

Prompt 4 | Memento Mori

#ROLE:
Act as Ryan Holiday applying the Stoic practice of memento mori, remembering that I will die, to my career decisions.

#TASK:
Identify what I'd change about my career trajectory if I really internalized that my time is finite.

#STEPS:
1. Calculate how many productive years I likely have left (be honest)
2. Calculate how many of those years my current trajectory would consume
3. Identify what I'd do instead of 50 if I had 5 years guaranteed
4. Identify what I'd stop doing if I had 5 years guaranteed
5. Plan the 1-month action that would matter even if it were my last month

#RULES:
- "You could leave life right now. Let that determine what you do and say and think." (Marcus Aurelius)
- The calculation must be specific, not vague
- The action plan must be concrete (not "live more fully". It's clarifying and think.")
- Memento mori isn't morbid. It's clarifying

Years Left Estimate → Current Trajectory Cost → 5-Year Plan → 5-Year Stop List → 1-Month Action

Prompt 5 | Premeditatio Malorum

#ROLE:
Act as Ryan Holiday running premeditatio malorum (negative
visualization) on my career change so the fear stops controlling me.

#TASK:
Visualize the worst-case outcome of the career change in specific detail,
and identify whether I could survive it.

#STEPS:
1. Name the career change I'm considering (specifically)
2. Name the 3 worst things that could realistically happen if I made the
change
3. For each, identify what I'd actually do (concrete actions, timelines,
resources)
4. For each, identify what I'd have learned even if it didn't work
5. State the actual worst case in one sentence, and whether I could
survive it

#RULES:
- Worst cases must be realistic, not catastrophic fantasy
- "Could I survive it" requires answering with actual resources (savings,
network, skills, family)
- Most worst cases are recoverable in 12-18 months
- Premeditatio doesn't make the fear go away. It makes the fear
manageable

#OUTPUT:
Career Change → 3 Realistic Worst Cases → Recovery Actions → Lessons
Even If It Fails → 1-Sentence Worst Case + Survival Verdict
