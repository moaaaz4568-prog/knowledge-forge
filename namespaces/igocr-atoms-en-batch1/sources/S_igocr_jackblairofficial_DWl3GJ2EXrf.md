2. "Never discount the main offer. Add bonuses instead."

The most important pricing rule Hormozi teaches.

When you drop the price you tell the buyer the price was wrong from the start.

When you add a bonus you increase the value without touching the number.

One signals weakness.
The other signals confidence.

Never confuse the two.
