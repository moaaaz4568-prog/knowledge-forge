Harvey Specter: the certainty closer.

You walk in like the deal is already signed.
Buyers borrow your confidence, and it
carries the room.

Where it breaks: the buyer who needs to
feel heard before they feel led. To them,
your certainty sounds like you stopped
listening.

Be certain after the second question, not
before the first.

Jessica Pearson: the calm power.

You never raise your voice. You say less than anyone on the call, and people lean in to catch it.

Where it breaks: a nervous buyer reads your silence as judgment, goes quiet, and never calls back.

Calm works when it's warm. Let them see you're on their side of the table.

Donna Paulsen: the mind reader.

You know what the buyer wants before
they say it. You hear the pause, the budget
they won't mention, the spouse in the
background.

Where it breaks: you say it before they do.
A buyer who didn't get to say it doesn't
own it.

Know it early. Let them say it first.
