GPT
ChatGPT | Claude | Gemini | DeepSeek

This is the layer you already use every day. A **trained model** that takes your **prompt** and *turns it into a response*.

On its own, it has **no memory**, **no tools**, **no way to take action**. It just **predicts** and **generates**, one word at a time.

It stands for **Generative Pre-trained Transformer**, the *tech* that started the whole **AI** boom.
<!-- image 3 -->
LLMs
Parameters | Training | Tokens
Tokens
LLMs are the category GPT belongs to.
They are advanced AI systems trained on
massive datasets to understand, generate
& predict human-like language.
Based on transformer architectures,
this is the brain behind every AI tool you
use today. It's what understands your
question and writes back an answer.
<!-- image 4 -->
AI Agents
Tools | Memory | Planning

An AI Agent is an LLM with tools,
memory, and the ability to take action
on its own. Instead of just answering your
question, it goes out and does the work.

Give it a goal and it figures out the steps.
It books the meeting, writes the email,
pulls data from your CRM. This is where
AI starts doing actual work.
<!-- image 5 -->
Agentic Systems
Planning | Orchestration | Coordination

Multiple AI Agents working together
instead of one agent trying to do
everything alone.

One plans, another executes, another
checks the work. Each handles its own
piece and passes context to the next.
This is how a single AI helper becomes
an entire AI workforce.
<!-- image 6 -->
Agentic Infra
Servers | Scaling | Monitoring
The plumbing that keeps all those Agents running. Most people never see this layer, but everything else depends on it.
When your agent starts, it spins up compute, pulls from a database, and logs every step. All of that runs in the background, invisible to the user.
Without this layer, even the smartest AI system falls apart the moment something goes wrong.
