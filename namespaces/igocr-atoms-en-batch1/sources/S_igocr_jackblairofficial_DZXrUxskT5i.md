1. "You can have everything in life you want, if you will just help enough other people get what they want."

Ziglar spent his early years selling like every dollar was for him, and he stayed broke. The year he flipped it and made the customer's win the goal, his numbers exploded. Buyers feel which one you're chasing. Chase their outcome out loud and the sale follows on its own.
<!-- image 2 -->

@jackblairofficial
2. "People don't buy for logical reasons. They buy for emotional reasons."
Ziglar drilled that facts tell but emotion sells. He would let a buyer picture the safer family, the respect, the relief, then let logic justify what feeling already decided. Sell the feeling first, then hand them the reasons to defend it.

@jackblairofficial

3. "Every sale has five obstacles: no need, no money, no hurry, no desire, no trust."

He refused to wing objections. He named the only five that exist and built an answer for each before he ever walked in. Know the five no's cold and nothing a buyer says can catch you flat.

4. "If you go looking for a friend, you're going to find they're very scarce. If you go out to be a friend, you'll find them everywhere."

Ziglar opened by being useful, not by pitching. He gave before he asked and walked in as someone helping rather than someone selling. Be the one bringing value first and the guard comes down before the offer goes up.

5. "Timid salesmen have skinny kids."

His bluntest line, and it was about the courage to ask. The closer who never actually asks for the order goes home empty while a lesser rep who asks eats well. The ask is the job. Say the number, then go quiet.

6. "Expect the best, prepare for the worst, and capitalize on what comes."
Ziglar walked into every call assuming the yes, while quietly ready for the objection. That balance kept him neither arrogant nor afraid, just prepared for whatever the buyer did. Plan for the no so thoroughly that the yes feels routine.

7. "Your attitude, not your aptitude, will determine your altitude."
Ranked last, Ziglar did not get smarter first, he got his head right first. He decided he was a top closer and then sold like one. Fix the belief before the script and the script got finally starts working.
