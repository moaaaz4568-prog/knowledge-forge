2. "LET'S LOOK AT THE NUMBER TOGETHER.
NOT THE COST. THE NUMBER."
Frank Luntz tested estate tax against death tax and
watched support for repeal run toward 70 percent.

"Cost" is your buyer's death tax. Say "the number"
and the same figure stops sounding like a loss and
starts sounding like arithmetic.
The buyer isn't afraid of the amount. They're afraid
of the word.

3. "STOP CALLING THIS THE DEAL. FROM HERE ON IT'S YOUR PLAN."
School vouchers kept losing the room, so Luntz renamed them opportunity scholarships. Same policy, same dollar, and the resistance thinned. People weren't rejecting the thing. They were rejecting the label.
"The deal" is a label your buyer braces against. Hand them "your plan" and the thing on the table stops being yours to be theirs to protect.
Deals get negotiated. Plans get followed.

HERE. YOU'RE PICKING A START DATE."

7. "I'M NOT ASKING YOU FOR A DECISION. I'M ASKING FOR A GREEN LIGHT."
More than 2,000 surveys, focus groups, and dial sessions across two dozen countries showed Luntz the same recoil: people freeze at words that sound final. Decision is one of them.
