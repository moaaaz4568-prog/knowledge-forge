"I haven't had a real vacation in years"
Something most entrepreneurs would have to say if they were being 100% honest.
That was me for many years. Working basically 7 days a week.
Even if I wasn't working, I was still thinking about work.
This is the curse of the creator.
Here's what to do about it →
<!-- image 1 -->
Because our work is so intimately tied to who we are and what we love, it's hard to go even a day without being engaged in it.

You might say, "Well if I enjoy what I do, why does it matter?"

For one, most creators go through hidden struggles and certainly don't enjoy every aspect of what they do.

But even if you did, it's still not healthy to be in a place where you couldn't take time off if you needed it.

Because life happens...
<!-- image 2 -->
Like when my daughter was born early this year. Work certainly wasn't my priority during that period of time.

Or God-forbid you or someone you know ever gets sick, or there's an emergency of some kind that comes up.

A typical 9-5 job isn't a good place to be during those times, but most entrepreneurs aren't in a very good position either.

You should be able to pass the "3 week test" to be able to say you're in a good position...

without asking anyone permission, and
with little to no advanced notice.

least twice a year, either for an
emergency or simply an extended
vacation.

So ask yourself, "Does my business pass
the 3 week test?"

3. Automate or delegate as much as possible
