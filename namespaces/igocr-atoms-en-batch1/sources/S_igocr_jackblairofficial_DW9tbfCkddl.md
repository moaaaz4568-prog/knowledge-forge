This phrase stops the conversation cold.

Price is rarely the real objection. Fear is.
Politics is. Ego is.
This phrase opens the door to the actual
conversation you need to be having.

Wendy never treated the surface as the story.

2. "What would it mean for you personally if this works?"

Not for the company. For you personally. This shifts the conversation from logic to identity in one sentence.

Wendy knew that people don't make decisions with spreadsheets. They make them with the part of themselves that wants to be right, respected, and safe.

Find what the deal means to them and you find the close.
