5 high-paying niches
to make $10,000 as an agency owner
@atishayhyperke
<!-- image 1 -->
1. PE and VC Firms

Why: they have funded mandates to source deals, and most of them still do it through warm intros and LinkedIn. outbound is a wide open lane.

What you do
▶ run cold email campaigns that surface acquisition targets or LP intros for funds with active capital to deploy.

Rate
▶ $4,000 to $8,000 per month per client
<!-- image 2 -->
2. SMBs in Tier 1 Countries

Why: a $5k retainer is real money in India but pocket change for a US contractor doing $2M a year. the dollar arbitrage is the whole game.

What you do
▶ book sales meetings for SMB owners in the US, UK, Australia, and Canada who don't have an in-house sales team.

Rate
▶ $2,000 to $4,000 per month per client
<!-- image 3 -->
3. B2B SaaS Companies

Why: every SaaS founder is under pressure to grow pipeline and most can't afford a full SDR team. they pay agencies to do it for them.

What you do
▶ build outbound systems that book demos for SaaS companies between $1M and $20M ARR.

Rate
▶ $3,000 to $7,000 per month per client
<!-- image 4 -->
4. Marketing Agencies

Why: Marketing agencies sell high-ticket services, have strong margins, and happily pay $1,500-$2,000+ to acquire new clients. One client can generate recurring revenue for years.

What you do
▶ Run cold email campaigns that book qualified sales calls for agency owners looking to grow their client base.

Rate
▶ $2,000 to $5,000 per month per client
<!-- image 5 -->
Staffing Companies

Why: Staffing firms constantly need candidates and clients. Cold email works extremely well for outreach and placements are highly profitable.

What you do
- Run outbound campaigns to connect recruiters with hiring managers and source qualified candidates at scale.

Rate
- $2,000 to $4,000 per month per client
