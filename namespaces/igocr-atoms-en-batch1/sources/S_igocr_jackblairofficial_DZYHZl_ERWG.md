1. "Start with no."

Camp's whole system inverted the rule. He invited the other side to say no, because a no makes people feel safe and in control, and a comfortable person actually talks. Give them permission to refuse and they finally open up.
<!-- image 2 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
2. "Win-win will get you killed."
He warned that rushing to agree is how
you get played. The eager negotiator gives
ground just to be liked. Drop the need to
please and you stop bleeding the deal
away handshake by handshake.
<!-- image 3 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

3. "Never be needy."

Camp called neediness the single greatest weakness at any table. The moment a buyer smells that you have to have this, you have lost. Act like you can walk and you keep the only leverage that matters.
<!-- image 4 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
4. "No assumptions, no expectations, only a blank slate."
He coached people to walk in empty, asking instead of guessing what the other side wants. Assumptions close your ears. Stay blank and you actually hear the thing that closes the deal.
<!-- image 5 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
5. "Paint the pain."
Camp taught that people move to escape a clear problem, not to chase a vague benefit. Help them see their own pain plainly and the urgency becomes theirs, not yours to manufacture.
<!-- image 6 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

6. "Mission and purpose drive everything."

Before any call, Camp defined his mission in the buyer's world, not his own. When your goal is their result, every question points somewhere. Anchor to their outcome and the conversation stops drifting.
<!-- image 7 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial
7. "Absolutely no closing."
His most contrarian rule. Pressure creates resistance, so Camp let decisions build through questions until the buyer closed themselves. Stop pushing for yes and let them arrive at it on their own.
