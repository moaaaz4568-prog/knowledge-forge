Prompt #2
ENGINEER YOUR FIRST 10 VIDEOS

Role: Launch strategist who designs high-compounding YouTube starts from zero.
Task: Create a 10-video sequence that maximizes early growth instead of random uploads.
Steps:
1. Ask for niche, persona, format, and what I can realistically film in 30 days.
2. Define 3 buckets: search-based, algorithm-driven, identity-building videos.
3. Compare what I want to post vs what actually grows the channel.
4. Generate 10 specific video ideas ranked by growth impact.
5. Order them into a strategic launch sequence (not random uploads).
6. Give 30/60/90-day growth milestones.

Rules:
• No generic topics
• Every video must serve growth or identity
• Clear intent for each upload in sequence
• Must build subscriber clarity by video 7

Output:
3 Buckets → Idea Gap → 10 Video Plan → Upload Order → Milestones

Prompt #3
BUILD YOUR PUBLISHING SYSTEM

Role: Creator ops strategist designing sustainable YouTube workflows.
Task: Build a weekly content system that fits my real time, energy, and skill so I can stay consistent long-term.

1. Ask my weekly upload goal.
2. Map full workflow: research → script → film → edit → upload → engagement.
3. Compare desired output vs sustainable output batch, automate, or cut.
4. Identify what to batch, automate, or cut.
5. Design a realistic weekly schedule (day-by-day + cadence).
6. Add tools + body to between ambition and reality

Rules:
• Must fit real life, not ideal creator setups
• Sustainability > output speed
• Show clear gap between ambition and reality
• Include time estimates for each task
• Must survive 52-week consistency test

Output:
Workflow Map → Cadence Plan → Weekly Schedule → Burnout Signals
Cadence Gap → Optimization Signals
