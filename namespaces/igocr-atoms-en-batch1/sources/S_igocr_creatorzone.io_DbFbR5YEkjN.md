<!-- image 3 -->
B-ROLL MISTAKES TO
AVOID

✔ Cluttered / busy backgrounds
✔ Too much background noise
✔ Wearing white (creates exposure issues)
✔ Shaky handheld shots with no stabilizer
✔ Filming in harsh direct sunlight
✔ Too many distracting objects in frame
✔ Inconsistent lighting between clips
✔ Filming only one angle per activity
✔ Moving the camera too fast
✔ Bad audio even if it's just B-roll
<!-- image 4 -->
ORGANIZE YOUR BANK
✔ Create folders by activity type
✔ Label clips by mood (energetic / calm / focused)
✔ Back up everything to cloud
✔ Keep a "ready to use" folder
✔ Sort by vertical vs. horizontal
✔ Star your best clips immediately
✔ Review and restock every 2 weeks
