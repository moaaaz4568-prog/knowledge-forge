1. "Most reps are not bad at selling. They are bad at prospecting."

Weinberg's core finding from years of fixing sales teams.

He found struggling reps could usually close fine once they were in front of someone. The hole was upstream. They were not creating enough new conversations. The selling was not broken. The pipeline was empty.

Fix the prospecting and the closing problem often disappears.
<!-- image 2 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

2. "The phone is still the most powerful weapon you own."

Weinberg's answer to reps hiding behind email.

He calls the phone the most powerful tool in sales and watches reps avoid it anyway. Email is safe and slow. A real conversation moves a deal in minutes. The discomfort is the price of speed.

The weapon everyone is scared to pick up is the one that works fastest.

effective.
He calls out reps who just check in with no
agenda and no ask. It feels like activity. It
closes nothing. Every contact should
move the deal, not just keep you politely
on the radar.
Checking in is not selling. It is just being
polite at scale.

4. “Pick the accounts. Do not wait for them.”

Weinberg on what he calls the sales attack.

He has reps build a focused target list and go after it on purpose, instead of waiting for marketing or luck. Decide exactly who you want, then point your week at them.

A chosen target gets worked. A random one gets hoped for.
<!-- image 5 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

5. “Lead with the problems you solve, not what you do.”

Weinberg on the power statement.

Reps open by describing their company and their product. Buyers do not care yet. Weinberg has them open with the specific problems they fix for clients. Lead with the pain and the buyer leans in.

Nobody wakes up wanting your product. They want their problem gone.
<!-- image 6 -->
Jack Blair
@jackblairofficial

6. "Talk less. Ask more. Listen hardest."

Weinberg on the discovery call most reps rush.

He watches reps fill the call with their own pitch. The strong move is the opposite. Ask sharp questions, then go quiet and actually hear the answer. The buyer tells you how to sell them.

The rep doing most of the talking is usually learning the least.
<!-- image 7 -->
Jack Blair

7. "Block the time to prospect or it never happens."

Weinberg on protecting the work that feeds everything.

Prospecting is the first thing bumped when the day gets busy. He has reps block it on the calendar like a real meeting and defend it. Unprotected time gets eaten every single day.

If it is not on the calendar, it is not going to happen.
