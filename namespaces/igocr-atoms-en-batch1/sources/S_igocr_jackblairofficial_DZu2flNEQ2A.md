Ask what matters most, then sell to that.

4. "Does it make sense to at least look at the numbers?"
Verde used a low-commitment yes to move the deal forward.
A small, easy agreement keeps momentum without triggering resistance. "At least look" feels safe, and motion toward the close beats a hard ask that stalls.
Lower the bar of the next yes and keep moving.

Isolate the objection down to one solvable thing.
