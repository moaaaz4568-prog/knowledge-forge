4. "Let me tell you who else lives on this street."

Flagg never sells just a house, he sells the address and the world around it.

Flagg holds the line on price without ever getting emotional.
He pushes back on a low offer calmly, as a simple fact, not a fight. A composed, confident no often pulls a better number out of the other side on its own.
Hold firm with calm and the number usually moves.

6. "What's actually most important to you in this deal?"
Flagg always finds the one thing the other side cares about most.
Sometimes it is price, often it is timing, privacy, or pride. Once you know the real priority, you can give on what is cheap to you and win on what matters.
Find what they truly want and trade everything else for it.

When they are ready, paper it before the moment cools.
