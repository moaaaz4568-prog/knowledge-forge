The Investor Teardown
"You are a skeptical VC partner doing due diligence on my business. You've seen 200 pitches this year and you're looking for reasons to pass.
Review my business and give me: 1. The 3 biggest holes in my model that would make you say no 2. The question you'd ask in the partner meeting that I probably can't answer yet 3. The comparable company that tried this and failed, and why I might repeat their mistake
Here's my business overview: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS, REVENUE MODEL, TRACTION, AND MARKET]"

Prompt #4
The Single Point of Failure Scan

"Audit my business for single points of failure. These are the things where if one element breaks, the whole operation stalls.

Check across these categories: → People (key person dependency) → Tools and platforms (what if this tool goes down or changes pricing?) → Revenue (customer concentration, channel dependency) → Supply chain (single suppliers, single distribution channels) For each vulnerability you find, rate it: how likely is it to happen and how badly does it hurt if it does.

Here’s how my business operates: [DESCRIBE YOUR TEAM, TOOLS, REVENUE SOURCES, AND KEY DEPENDENCIES]"

Prompt #5
The Market Shift Scenario

"Generate 3 plausible market shifts that could happen in the next 18 months and would seriously damage my current business model.

For each scenario:
1. What changes (regulatory, technological, or consumer behavior)
2. How it specifically impacts my revenue, customers, or competitive position
3. The early warning signs I should be watching for right now
4. What I'd need to pivot or adjust if it happens Don't give me doomsday fantasies. Give me the realistic shifts that have a real chance of happening.
Here's my business and market: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS, INDUSTRY, AND CURRENT MARKET POSITION]"
