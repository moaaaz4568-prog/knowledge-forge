1. “Approach every customer with the idea of helping them solve a problem.”

Documented core principle.

He never thought of himself as selling. He thought of himself as a problem-solver. Every call was a chance to remove pain for the prospect.

Reframe the call before you make it.

2. "Are you ready to do this?"

Documented sharp-angle close.

When the prospect was leaning yes but hadn't committed, this question forced the decision. The prospect had to either say yes or surface the real objection.

Most chose yes.

4. “Just suppose...”
Documented hypothetical opener.
When a prospect resisted, he reframed the resistance as a hypothetical: “Just suppose you did move forward, what would change?” Removes the threat of commitment while still walking them to it. Hypotheticals lower defenses.

5. "Let me ask you this."

Documented gentle redirect.

When a prospect went off-track, he never interrupted. He let them finish, then redirected with this. Keeps control without breaking rapport.

Question your way back to the close.

Documented gap-analysis question.

He asked the prospect to define the gap.

Make them write the close for you.
