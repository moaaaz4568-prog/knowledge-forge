3. "Here's what people in your position usually miss."

Jim positioned himself as the one who could see what the buyer couldn't.

Buyers trust the person who knows something they don't. Naming a blind spot they recognize instantly makes you the expert worth listening to.

Show them what they're missing and you become the guide.

4. "This only matters if you're actually serious."

Jim qualified the buyer instead of begging for the sale.

The moment you make someone prove they belong, the dynamic flips. Now they're selling you on why they're a fit, and wanting something you might want it more.

Make them qualify and they start chasing the yes.

5. "Picture where you are a year from now if nothing changes."

Jim made the cost of staying still feel real and personal.

People move to escape a future they don't want more than to chase one they do. Painting the price of inaction turns "maybe later" into "I can't keep doing this."

Make standing still feel more expensive than moving.
