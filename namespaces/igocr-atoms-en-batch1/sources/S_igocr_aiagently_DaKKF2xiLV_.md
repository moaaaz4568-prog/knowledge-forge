Never let your workflows fail silently. Add a separate "Error Trigger" workflow that catches every failure across all your automations. When something breaks, it sends you a Slack message or email instantly with the workflow name and error. One workflow protects all.

Tip 4 – Retry
Retry On Fail
Not every failure is permanent. Enable "Retry On Fail" inside any node's settings to automatically retry failed steps up to 3 times. Perfect for flaky APIs, slow servers, or unstable connections. It fixes most errors before you even know they happened.

Tip 5 – Pin Data
Google Drive
create: folder
Stop triggering live APIs every time you tweak a node. Right-click any node and "Pin Data" to freeze its real output. Now you can edit and test everything downstream without wasting API credits, sending test emails, or waiting for slow triggers to fire again.

Human review
Request approval via services like Slack and Telegram before making tool calls
