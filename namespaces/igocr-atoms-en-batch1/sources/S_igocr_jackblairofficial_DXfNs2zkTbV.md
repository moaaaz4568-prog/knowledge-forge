1. "I am going to tell you something about where this market is heading that most people in your position have not considered yet."

Tommy Shelby Year One never opened with who he was. He opened with what he knew and what he knew was always worth more than what his current position suggested.

This phrase bypasses the credibility question entirely by leading with intelligence the buyer does not have. The buyer who was about to ask who you are more about what you just said. Intelligence delivered before credentials are requested is the fastest authority any unknown rep can establish.

Lead with what you know. The buyer who needs the information stops asking who you are to give it.

2. "I know you do not know me yet. By the end of this conversation I think that will have changed."

Tommy always named the dynamic before anyone else could use it against him.
This phrase converted the unknown rep's greatest vulnerability into the opening statement of a compelling pitch.

The buyer who was about to dismiss you on the basis of unfamiliarity suddenly has a reason to stay curious.
You have not defended your lack of reputation. You have made overcoming it the subject of the next twenty minutes.
That reframe alone is worth every second it buys you.

Name the vulnerability first. The buyer who hears you acknowledge it stops using it as a reason to disengage.

3. "I do not have a long track record in this room. What I have is a very clear read of what you need and exactly how to deliver it."

Tommy Shelby Year One never pretended to have the history he had not yet built.
He offered something more immediately valuable which was precision about the present situation.

This phrase converts the absence of a track record into a statement of focused current relevance.
The buyer stops evaluating your past and starts evaluating your read of their present.
A precise read of the current situation closes faster than a long history of unrelated wins.

Trade the track record for the precision. The buyer who needs the second does not care about the first.

4. "I am not here to impress you with what I have done. I am here to show you what I am going to do."

Tommy always spoke in futures because his past was not yet the asset his vision was.
This phrase pulls the entire conversation forward into the territory where you are strong rather than your own history.

The buyer who was about to ask for case studies suddenly finds themselves evaluating a plan instead.
Plans are harder to dismiss than credentials because they speak directly to the buyer's future rather than your past.
The rep who sells the past never reaches.
Sell the future when the past is thin. The buyer who can see the plan buys the plan.

5. "I want to be straight with you. I am building something here and I want you to be part of it from the beginning."

Tommy Shelby made the ground floor of something real.
This phrase converts the absence of an established brand into an invitation that feels more exclusive than any established name could offer.

The buyer who was evaluating you against better-known competitors suddenly feels the pull of being first.
Being first is always more exciting than being safe and excitement closes deals that safety comparisons cannot.

The unknown rep who makes the buyer feel like an early partner always beats the known rep who makes them feel like a transaction.

Make them feel first. The buyer who accepts that position almost never goes to the established alternative.

6. "I understand why you would choose the safer option. I am here to show you why the better one is worth the difference."

Tommy always acknowledged the case for the safer choice before making the case for himself. This phrase signals that you understand the buyer's hesitation completely and have come prepared to address it specifically.

The buyer who was leaning toward the safer choice stops feeling like they need to defend that instinct. You validated it. Now you can redirect from it. The buyer who feels understood stops defending their position and starts evaluating yours.

Validate the safe choice before you make the case against it. The buyer who feels heard opens to being redirected.

7. "Give me this deal and I will show you what I am capable of. You will not offer it to anyone else after."

This is Tommy Shelby Year One in one sentence.
Not arrogance. Total conviction delivered with complete calm by someone who already knows how this ends.

This phrase asks for the chance rather than assume the result and frames the ask as an investment the buyer will not regret. The buyer who hears this and feels the certainty behind it stops thinking about risk and starts thinking about what they might miss.

Conviction without track record is the rarest in sales. It is also the most memorable.

Ask for the chance with complete certainty behind the ask. The buyer who feels the certainty takes the bet.
