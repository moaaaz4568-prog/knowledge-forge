1. "I want to be clear about something before we go any further. This is what we are working with."

Cersei never let anyone else define the terms of a room she was standing in.
She set the parameters early and made everyone else negotiate within them.

This phrase reestablishes your position at the exact moment a buyer starts rewriting the deal.
It is not aggressive. It is definitive.
The buyer who was reaching for control suddenly finds the boundary they did not see coming.

Set the terms before they do. Every negotiation belongs to whoever frames it first.

2. "I understand what you are asking for. That is not where we are going to land on this."

Most reps soften a no until it sounds like a maybe. Cersei understands that a soft no invites a harder push and a harder push costs you the deal.

This phrase closes the door on a concession without anger, without apology, without a gap they can negotiate into. The buyer hears complete finality delivered with complete composure. And composure in the face of pressure is the most powerful signal of leverage available on any call.

Say no like you mean it. The room will adjust to the certainty.

3. "You have more options than you think you do. So do I."

This phrase is surgical.
It acknowledges the buyer's position without conceding to it and signals your own optionality in the same breath.

Cersei always made sure the other side knew she had moves they had not considered.
That awareness shifted every negotiation before a single concession was made.

The buyer who believes you have alternatives treats your offer with an entirely different level of respect.

Signal your options. You do not have to name them. The room just needs to know they exist.

4. "I am going to make you one offer. It is a strong one. I would encourage you to take it seriously."

Cersei Lannister never came to a negotiation with a range.
She came with a position and the absolute certainty that it was the right one.

This phrase converts your offer from a starting point into a statement.
The buyer stops treating it as an opening bid and starts treating it as what it is.
One clear, confident offer delivered without flinching closes faster than any negotiation dance ever will.

One offer. Full conviction. Let the room decide if it wants to be difficult.

5. "I think you already know this is the right decision. The question is whether you are willing to act on what you know."

This phrase removes the intellectual distance a buyer uses to avoid committing.
Cersei always made the cost of inaction personal rather than theoretical.

The buyer who has been hiding behind process and policy suddenly has nowhere left to stand.
They either act on what they know or they admit they are choosing to stay stuck.
Most buyers will not admit that out loud. The close follows the silence.

Make the cost of not deciding more uncomfortable than the decision itself.

6. "I respect that you have a process. My timeline does not move to accommodate it."

This phrase signals scarcity and self-respect in a single sentence without ultimatum or aggression.
The buyer who was planning to run out the clock suddenly understands that the clock is yours.
Your time has value. The moment the buyer feels that, the dynamic shifts permanently.
