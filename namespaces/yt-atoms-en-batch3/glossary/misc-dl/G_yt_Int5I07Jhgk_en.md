# G_yt_Int5I07Jhgk_en — contract

## Term

The speaker uses "contract" for the professional document a graphic designer makes for a client, the object of the whole tutorial. The word appears 17 times, with the plural "contracts" also used twice, next to "professional", "design", "text", and "Illustrator". He shows how to build it in Adobe Illustrator, keeping the text inside the margins with a text box, adding guides so the content stays away from the print edge, and finishing with a digital signature. Before the tutorial he demos PDFelement Pro on the same contracts, including editing text directly on the PDF.
