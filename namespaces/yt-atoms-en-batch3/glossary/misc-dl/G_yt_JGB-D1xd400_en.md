# G_yt_JGB-D1xd400_en — agent

## Term

In this source, agent is an AI program set up to do a job on its own, and the video shows a whole structure of them. The word agent appears 23 times, next to business, team, Claude, and dossier, as the speaker explains how to monetize the setup, find customers, and form an offer from it through the AI Operator Academy. His own example is a courier agent that monitors his mail for incoming responses he might miss, because procrastination kills all deals, and he calls it a luxury rather than a necessity. An agent here is therefore a working AI helper, and the skill behind it does one simple thing very well.
