# G_yt_IwYf6bKBrHc_en — appointment setting

## Term

The speaker uses "appointment setting" for the agency service of booking calls for clients, and his whole lesson is to stop selling it on its own. The phrase appears 23 times, next to "agency", "systems", "appointments", and "sell". His argument is that setting appointments does not solve the client's problem, because a booked appointment has not made any money yet, so the agency must solve more steps than just the booking. He illustrates it with his own history, starting an appointment setting agency in 2020 and later scaling an acquisition company that builds appointment infrastructures.
