# G_yt_IyrscafMX4A_en — avatar

## Term

John Benson uses "avatar" for the specific person a sales letter speaks to, the reader the copy must picture. The word appears 3 times, next to "talking to", "inclusion", and "emotional". In the dating VSL he breaks down, the copy speaks to its avatar with total inclusion: rich or old, young or poor, any color, any religion, the message applies to everyone. The avatar is one of the major reasons the VSL makes over 750,000 dollars a month, because the copy never talks past the person it is written for.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a profile picture: the "avatar" here is the target customer in the copywriter's mind, not an image, and the VSL's inclusion works because the copy addresses that person directly.
- Read as a claim that the VSL works for literally everyone: the inclusion is a copywriting move to make each viewer feel spoken to, so "avatar" names the intended reader, not a promise that the product fits every person.
