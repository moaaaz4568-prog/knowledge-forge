# G_yt_J9ocP1dFaQ8_en — team

## Term

In this source, team is the staff a small agency grows so its work no longer depends on the owners alone. The word team appears 25 times, next to clients, business, and agency, as two owners talk about being confronted by potential clients comparing them to other offers. About six years ago, they say, they got serious and started growing out a team and offering services that did not just depend on them, and they stay careful about which projects to take because they must do their own work plus help the team with its work. A team here is therefore the people who carry the agency's delivery, and the owners' rule is that the agency will never outgrow the person in charge.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sports squad, team misses the business sense: here it is the staff of a small agency, and growing it is how the owners stop depending on themselves alone.
- Read as everyone in the company, it is too wide: the speakers' team is the group whose work they must do alongside their own when they take on a project.
