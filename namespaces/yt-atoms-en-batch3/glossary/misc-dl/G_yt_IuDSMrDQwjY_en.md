# G_yt_IuDSMrDQwjY_en — growth infrastructure

## Term

The speaker uses "growth infrastructure" for the system a client sells and installs to grow a business, the product his partners build and release. The phrase appears 6 times, with the plural "growth infrastructures" also used 5 times, next to "selling", "SaaS", "email marketing", and "cold email". His clients sell different kinds of it: content growth infrastructures, SaaS growth infrastructures, email marketing ones, and cold email ones. The example he gives is an Ecom growth infrastructure that cleared 30K in a day after launch, which he presents as proof that the model works when the client comes with something real to sell.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a course or a coaching call: the "growth infrastructure" is the system the client sells to their own customers, and the weekly live offer breakdown call is only where the speaker reviews it, not the infrastructure itself.
- Read as a promise of 30K days: the 30K day belongs to one client who had just launched their Ecom growth infrastructure, so the phrase names the product type, not a revenue guarantee for anyone who builds one.
