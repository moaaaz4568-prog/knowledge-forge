# G_yt_JeNo0ywT5fA_en — speak

## Term

In this interview, speak is the act of speaking on stage at conferences, and the video is about how that wins clients years later. The word speak appears 18 times, next to agency, lot, and the gigs the speaker has done, as he explains that speaking at so many conferences over a few years built up until leads started coming from gigs he did two or three years ago. To speak here is therefore not to talk but to perform on a stage, and the performance is the marketing.
