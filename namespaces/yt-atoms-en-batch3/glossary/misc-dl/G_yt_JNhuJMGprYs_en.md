# G_yt_JNhuJMGprYs_en — pen tool

## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial, pen tool is the instrument for drawing and bending paths, and the video teaches its key combinations. The phrase pen tool appears 12 times, next to tool, path, and technique, as the speaker holds down the Alt or Option key while hovering over the pen tool path to click and drag the line. The command key lets the line be adjusted when it does not match up fully, and the option key stretches out the path to finish half of a shape. This pen tool technique, he says, is one to remember and to use in graphic design work where needed, because it will help a lot. A pen tool here is therefore not a pen but the path editor, and the tutorial is about controlling it with modifier keys.
