# G_yt_J5LjHs4E2-M_en — assessment

## Term

In this source, assessment is the small paid diagnostic that opens the door to a larger paid AI service, not a test a student sits. The word assessment appears 32 times, most often inside the speaker's 90-day protocol, where mini-assessments given to three to five contacts a day become the channel for paid assessments. The target is a business with over $5 million in annual revenue, and the reward for following the protocol is a first monthly advance contract for paid AI services. An assessment here is therefore a short, chargeable piece of work that proves the provider's ability before the concierge engagement begins.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a school exam or a graded test, assessment misleads: here it is a paid business diagnostic that leads to an AI services contract, with no students and no scores.
- Read as a free lead magnet, it misses the money: the speaker charges for assessments and treats them as the channel that produces paying clients.
