# G_yt_JNCtGMR7XGk_en — clients

## Term

In this source, clients are the goal of a documented 143-day journey to become a millionaire, and the speakers stake their appearance on winning them. The word clients appears 10 times, next to cold, day, and email, as they look back on the last few weeks as a game changer after two or three months of searching for a breakthrough. In the earlier videos they were going full Viking mode, not getting haircuts and not shaving until they got clients, and now that money is starting to come in the first thing is to delegate the right systems to the right people. Clients here are therefore not a metric but the prize the whole journey is measured against.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as anyone who replies, clients misses the challenge: here they are the prize of a 143-day journey, and the speakers refused haircuts until they got them.
- Read as a number to grow, it ignores the delegation: once money starts coming in, the speakers say the first thing is to delegate the right systems to the right people.
