# G_yt_JoR7Yn92CCY_en — team

## Term

In this interview, team is the group of people a boss builds and leads, and the conversation is about the attitudes that make that work. The word team appears 26 times, next to business, back, and the distributed model of working the speaker discusses. The crucial hurdle, he says, is the one a boss has to get over: recognising that when you help them succeed, they will automatically help you succeed, and the whole thing is about communication, about listening for how people are thinking. A team here is therefore not a headcount but a relationship, and the boss's job is to help the team succeed first.
