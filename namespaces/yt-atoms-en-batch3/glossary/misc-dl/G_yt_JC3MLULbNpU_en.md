# G_yt_JC3MLULbNpU_en — money

## Term

In this vlog, money is the thing the speaker works to be free of, not the thing he worships. The word money appears 9 times, next to month, business, and team, as he films from Tulum on a three-monthly vacation and explains that he works long hours seven days a week to make as much money as he can and to grow as much as he can. His definition of building a business is blunt: today they made sixteen thousand dollars, and that is what building a business is, because it frees you from slavery and means you do not have to be a slave to money. Money here is therefore both the fuel of the business and the master the speaker is trying to escape.
