# G_yt_JIrb0zPZ5bE_en — tracking

## Term

In this source, tracking is the system that shows where each person comes from and whether a video actually sells. The word tracking appears 8 times, next to link, data, guide, and case, as the speaker builds the beginning of the system: UTMs collect data on where each person comes from, and the bio and the lead magnets on Instagram are treated as distinct sources. The rule he gives is practical: whenever you want to create a lead magnet, add a row here and you will have UTM tracking to know if they are coming from that video, because views, likes, and comments will not tell you if the video is actually selling. Tracking here is therefore the measurement that replaces vanity numbers with proof of sales.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spying on people, tracking misses the purpose: here it is the system that shows which video or lead magnet actually sells, because views, likes, and comments will not tell you.
- Read as a single metric, it is too narrow: the speaker's tracking is a whole setup, from UTMs in every link to a row for each lead magnet, that collects where each person comes from.
