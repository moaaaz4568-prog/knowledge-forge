# G_yt_JG8HDKflz_Y_en — laziness

## Term

In this source, laziness is the feeling the speaker reframes as a signal about priorities rather than a defect. The word laziness appears 16 times, next to lazy, brain, and priorities, and the speaker is blunt about the stakes: give you slack because you are too lazy to do your job and they will fire you, and a partner will not put up with unemployment forever. But he insists laziness might not be your fault, and that the feeling might just be a lack of caring about the thing in front of you, because we all have the ability to tap into an endless reservoir of energy for the things we truly value. Laziness here is therefore a mismatch between the task and what a person cares about, not an empty tank.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a character flaw, laziness misses the speaker's argument: here it may not be the person's fault, and what matters is what they do about it.
- Read as having no energy at all, it contradicts the video: the speaker says we all can tap into an endless reservoir of energy, but only for the things we truly value and care about.
