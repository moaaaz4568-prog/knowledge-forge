# G_yt_KEs1gAX1ZYU_en — copywriter

## Term

In this short video, a copywriter is the person who earns money writing copy, and the channel promises three ways to do it. The word copywriter appears 7 times, next to copywriting, money, courses, and marketer, as the speaker welcomes anyone who is a copywriter or wants to be a professional one and earn more from copywriting. His pitch is that the free training beats most paid courses, even ones costing thousands of dollars, with deep dives and live breakdowns of multi-million dollar copy. A copywriter here is therefore a learner of a paid craft, not a job title on a resume, and the video's only promise is the three ways to earn as one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a course buyer, copywriter misses the work: here the person earns by writing copy, and the training is only the way in.
- Read as a marketer, it misses the craft: the speaker welcomes marketers looking for copy techniques, but the copywriter is the one who writes for a living.
