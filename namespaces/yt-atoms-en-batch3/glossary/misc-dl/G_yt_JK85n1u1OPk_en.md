# G_yt_JK85n1u1OPk_en — cold email

## Term

In this source, cold email is the outreach method the speaker has run at scale, and the video teaches how to get replies from it. The phrase cold email appears 6 times, next to call, company, scripts, and send, as he reports the numbers from 237 days of documenting his agency: over 300,000 cold emails sent and over 200 appointments booked. The mistake most people make, he says, is asking for the call first, while the email itself has to spark curiosity and feel easy to say yes to, with copy under 70 words. A cold email here is therefore a short, valuable first message, and the reply is earned by what the email gives, not by what it asks.

## Synonyms

- cold emails

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spam sent in bulk, cold email misses the craft: here it is a short, curious message under 70 words that must feel easy to say yes to, and 300,000 of them were sent with care.
- Read as a pitch, it reverses the order: the speaker's cold email gives a ton of value first, and only then does the reader get on the call.
