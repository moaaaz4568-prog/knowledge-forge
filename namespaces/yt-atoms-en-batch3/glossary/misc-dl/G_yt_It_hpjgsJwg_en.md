# G_yt_It_hpjgsJwg_en — brand

## Term

The speaker defines "brand" as what the audience associates you with when they think of you. The word appears 31 times, with the plural "brands" also used twice, next to "audience", "content", "intentional", and "journey". Her test is simple: if she wants to speak on stages to help young women build their businesses, she must be known as a woman who has built a business, not just her own. The brand journey framework is the tool for that: pairing yourself with one thing, consistently and intentionally, until the audience makes the association on its own.

## Synonyms

- brands (the plural form used in this source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a logo or a color scheme: the "brand" here is the association in the audience's mind, built by pairing yourself with one thing over time, not a visual identity.
- Read as a claim that any content builds a brand: she warns that content only counts when it is optimized off what already performed, and the brand comes from intentional repetition, not from posting a lot.
