# G_yt_K6gc84a2awY_en — money

## Term

In this short video, money is the extra revenue a marketing agency owner makes without extra work, and the pitch is that it is already sitting in the business. The word money appears 12 times, next to business, agency, revenue, and client, as the speaker opens by saying that if you want to make more revenue today with zero extra work, and you do not have a marketing agency, the fulfillment is crazy. His rule is about inputs and outputs: you reach out to 10,000 people and 10 close, that is your metric, and you just keep doing that and you keep making money. Money here is therefore not a salary but the yield of a metric, and the work is feeding the inputs.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as revenue, money misses the pocket: here it is the money in your pocket — a more successful client means more money regardless of whether they spend it with you.
- Read as extra work, it inverts the pitch: the video promises more revenue today with zero extra work, through inputs and outputs, not through more hours.
