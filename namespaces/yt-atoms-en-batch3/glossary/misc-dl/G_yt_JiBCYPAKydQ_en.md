# G_yt_JiBCYPAKydQ_en — business card

## Term

In this design tutorial, business card is the small printed card a designer must make effective, and the video teaches how to judge one. The phrase business card appears 13 times, next to card, design, and VAS, as the speaker explains that the choice of font is more important than ever when concerning business cards, because these designs are so small. A card must have a call to action that works, and he tests his design with VAS by 3M to measure the gaze sequence and the points of interest, while every effective business card has a theme throughout the entire design, often tied to the brand identity. A business card here is therefore not a contact slip but a designed object, and its small size is what makes every choice matter.
