# G_yt_K2j_lN6PdjU_en — content

## Term

In this interview, content is the writing a young viral creator makes for clients, and the conversation is about how he turned it into a business. The word content appears 33 times, next to twitter, writing, distribution, and viral, as the host opens with his formula for writing virals: make the benefit huge, make the perceived effort really low, make sure it is relevant to your audience, and make sure you have good reach distribution. The interviewee explains what he does for clients in two parts — writing and distribution — so he creates content for different types of people that he wants to take on and then distributes that content on their channels. Content here is therefore not a post but a service, and the business is built on saying yes to the right people.
