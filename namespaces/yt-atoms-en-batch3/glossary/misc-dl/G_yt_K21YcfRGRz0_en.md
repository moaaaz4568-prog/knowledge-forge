# G_yt_K21YcfRGRz0_en — clean

## Term

In this source, clean is the first of three descriptors the speaker gives to good packaging design, and the video is about why so few cleaning products have it. The word clean appears 7 times, next to balanced, fresh, designs, and products, as he says he came up with three main descriptors for these designs — clean, balanced, and fresh — and then turns to the first one. He is shocked by how few cleaning products actually have clean designs, while Method's brand soaps use a very simple iconic silhouette without excessive detail, and the design team has evolved that language further while staying true to the original. Clean here is therefore not about the soap but about the design, and the shock is that the products meant to clean are the ones that look the messiest.
