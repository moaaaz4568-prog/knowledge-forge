# G_yt_IgyFufhWoEA_en — webinar

## Term

The speaker presents the "webinar" as the first of five methods for selling expertise without depending on social media algorithms. The word appears 21 times, often as "free webinar", next to "trust", "course", and "content". His sequence is reach, then trust, then sale: a free webinar lets a specialist show in-depth knowledge so strangers become buyers of the course. He stresses that the topic must be specialized rather than general, because the audience judges the speaker's expertise from how deep the webinar goes.

## Synonyms

- webinars (the plural form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "Of course, your content is in-depth because you're a specialist with experience, and you chose a specialized webinar topic, not a general one." — sources/S_yt_IgyFufhWoEA_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that any webinar sells the course: the speaker insists the topic must be specialized and the content in-depth, because a general webinar gives the audience no reason to trust the speaker's expertise.
- Read as a free offer with no selling in it: the free webinar is the first step of a sequence that ends in a sale, so "free" describes the entry point, not a promise that the expertise itself costs nothing.
