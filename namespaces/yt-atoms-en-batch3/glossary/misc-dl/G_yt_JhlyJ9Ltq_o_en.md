# G_yt_JhlyJ9Ltq_o_en — agency

## Term

In this interview, agency is the business founders build and eventually sell, and the conversation is about how to sell it without breaking it. The word agency appears 19 times, next to years, owners, and the employees the sale must protect. The founders were adamant about an internal sale, because they wanted to make sure the culture stayed intact and everyone was protected, and the speaker notes that people do not think about selling to their employees because they assume the employees cannot afford it. His advice is that asset purchases are the way to go, because even a COO of the agency might not know the hidden things inside it. An agency here is therefore not a generic company but a founder's baby, and the sale is judged by what survives it.
