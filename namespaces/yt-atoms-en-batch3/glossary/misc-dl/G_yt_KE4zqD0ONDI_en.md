# G_yt_KE4zqD0ONDI_en — thumbnail

## Term

In this video, a thumbnail is the still image that decides whether a viewer clicks, and the speaker has reviewed thousands of them over five years. The word thumbnail appears 18 times, next to clicks, questions, emotions, and TV-friendly, as he opens by saying that 90 percent of the time he gives the same advice and then starts with making the thumbnail TV-friendly. His rule is that questions and emotions always trump relevance for clicks, and that a thumbnail whose key elements hide in the bottom half shows nothing at all on TV. A thumbnail here is therefore a question asked in one image, not a summary of the video, and the vast majority he sees could improve with just three small changes.
