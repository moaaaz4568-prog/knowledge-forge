# G_yt_IgHDmgC8CF4_en — fulfillment

## Term

Here "fulfillment" means the delivery model an agency sells after winning the client, and the speaker contrasts it with monthly retainers. The word appears 4 times, in "fulfillment model" and "fulfillment support", and next to "build and release", the name of the model his partner sells. The build and release model means building a growth system for a local business and handing it over, instead of charging every month for services. He says his partner left the retainer agency because fulfillment done this way, now with AI building the offers and ads, runs a six-figure-a-month business with a small team.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the product itself: fulfillment here is the delivery model after the sale, and the build and release model is valuable because of the system handed over, not because the word "fulfillment" describes a service package.
- Read as a promise that AI does the fulfillment: the interview says AI now builds the offers and ads inside the model, but the partner still sells, prices, and runs the business, so AI assists the fulfillment rather than replacing the operator.
