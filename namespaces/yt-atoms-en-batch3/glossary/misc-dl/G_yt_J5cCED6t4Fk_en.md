# G_yt_J5cCED6t4Fk_en — brands

## Term

In this source, brands are the paying clients of an influencer marketing agency, the side of the deal that writes the cheque. The word brands appears 29 times, alongside influencer, creators, and the agency model the speaker says to choose first when starting out. He built his own agency from zero to multiple six figures a year and worked with over 100 brands and creators, and he explains that brands pay 5, 10, or even 20K plus per campaign because the agency removes all their headaches. Brands here are therefore not a vague audience but the customers whose campaigns fund the whole business.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the agency's own identity, brands confuses the two sides: here the brands are the clients who pay, while the agency and its creators do the work.
- Read as any company with a logo, it is too broad: the speaker's brands are specifically the ones buying influencer campaigns at 5K to 20K plus.
