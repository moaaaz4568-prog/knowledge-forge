# G_yt_K0dI82q-mqE_en — clients

## Term

In this source, clients are the new customers an agency signs each week, and the video is about why that number goes up and down. The word clients appears 27 times, next to week, month, inputs, and calls, as the speaker explains that they get about three to four clients per week on average — not three or four every single week, but the average across the last 12 weeks distributed evenly. His diagnosis is that when people get one client a month, the issue is the amount of inputs going into the system, and you should measure against the inputs, not against the time frame. Clients here are therefore not a goal to wish for but the output of inputs, and the work is feeding the system.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a headcount, clients misses the rate: here it is three to four clients per week on average, and the issue is the amount of inputs going into the system.
- Read as a monthly target, it measures the wrong thing: the speaker says to measure against the inputs coming into the system, not against the time frame.
