# G_yt_K-XClfRQWek_en — saturated

## Term

In this source, saturated is the condition of the graphic design market, and the whole video is the question of whether it is too saturated to enter. The word saturated appears 10 times, next to design, market, competition, and skills, as the speaker opens by asking if graphic design is too saturated, with an impossible wall of competition to climb, and answers yes and no. His point is that the market is quite saturated but that does not mean it cannot be a lucrative means of income — you just need to know how to put yourself in a position where you are noticed. The designers he warns about are the ones who never understand their niche, slinging logos at fifty bucks a pop.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as too many designers, saturated misses the answer: the speaker says yes and no — the market is saturated but can still be lucrative if you position yourself to be noticed.
- Read as a verdict on design, it ignores the survival part: the video is about how to survive in a saturated market — niche, skills, positioning — not about quitting design.
