# G_yt_IzED6Q59DAY_en — color

## Term

The speaker uses "color" for the hues chosen in a graphic design and the feeling each one creates. The word appears 15 times, with the plural "colors" also used 12 times, next to "design", "theme", "contrast", and "monochrome". His example is a black theme complemented with white across the whole design, with small areas of red that carry the viewer's eye from one element to the next. He explains color groups such as monochrome and analog, and says each color we see evokes an emotional reaction, which is why the choice matters.
