# G_yt_JDnhpF4OfPU_en — contractors

## Term

In this source, contractors are the independent tradespeople whose relationships carry a reconstruction business. The word contractors appears 36 times, next to business, niche, and years, as the speaker follows Ron, who quit his job as an electrician a year and a half ago to start his own business without any experience. Ron wants to establish himself in the niche of reconstructions because there is a fantastic margin there, and the limiting factor is order flow: once he establishes a relationship with a contractor, all the orders are his. Contractors here are therefore not staff but the source of the work, and growing the network of them is the path to eventually hiring employees.

## Live Example

> "It seems to me that if you had more work, it would give you the opportunity to start expanding your network of contractors and eventually hire employees." — sources/S_yt_JDnhpF4OfPU_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as employees, contractors misleads: here they are independent tradespeople the speaker works with, and he hires employees only after the contractor network grows.
- Read as the customers, it reverses the flow: the contractors are the ones who bring the orders, and the speaker's business depends on keeping their relationship.
