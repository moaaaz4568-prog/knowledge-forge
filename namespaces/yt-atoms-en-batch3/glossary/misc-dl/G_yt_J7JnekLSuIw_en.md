# G_yt_J7JnekLSuIw_en — stroke

## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial, stroke is the outline weight of a shape, the property the speaker adjusts with the width tool. The word stroke appears 11 times, next to design, circle, blur, and effects, as he builds a grunge-style design with brushes and masks. After selecting the design he presses Shift and W for the width tool, which lets him increase or decrease the stroke weight anywhere around the shape, and the result, he says, will make a big difference to the final design solution. A stroke here is therefore a controllable edge thickness, not a brush mark, and changing it reshapes the whole look.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a brush stroke painted by hand, stroke misses the tool: here it is the vector outline of a selected shape, adjusted numerically with the width tool.
- Read as the design itself, it overreaches: the stroke is one property of the design, and the tutorial changes it alongside effects, masks, and brushes.
