# G_yt_JdZIQkfguZc_en — designer

## Term

In this source, designer is the working professional whose day the video follows, from a fast morning to the moment of disconnecting. The word designer appears 14 times, next to design, day, and workflow, as the speaker sets the scene: you are working on a design, the concepts have been sent to your clients, and it is not even lunchtime yet. The amateur designer, he says, might fall into a trap like the one seen earlier, while the pro designer holds a superpower: whenever the day or the working week comes to a close, they are actually able to disconnect themselves. A designer here is therefore not a job title but a person with a working day, and the difference between amateur and pro is what they do with it.
