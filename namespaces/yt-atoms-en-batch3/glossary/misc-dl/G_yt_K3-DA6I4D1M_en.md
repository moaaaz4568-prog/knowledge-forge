## Term

In this tutorial, text is the lettering you curve in Adobe Illustrator, and the video teaches one technique for curving it fully. The word text appears 14 times, next to curve, design, Illustrator, and gradient, as the host opens by saying that in today's video tutorial he is going to show one technique that will allow you to fully curve text in wonderful and weird ways. Text here is therefore not the words but the shape they take, and the difficulty is that Illustrator does not allow easy ways to create gradients on curved text.
