# G_yt_JErxMo-Ja84_en — process

## Term

In this short source, process is the creative path a designer explains to a client before they ever speak, from the first idea to the finished mockup. The word process appears 5 times, next to problem, creative, and recipe, as the speaker asks what problems were solved and what kind of innovative solutions were tailor built for this problem. The work sits somewhere between a wireframe and a mockup, and the designer describes each and every process as part of a creative formula, so that clients know what to expect before they call or email. A process here is therefore not only the making but the telling of it, and the formula is the story that wins the client.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fixed recipe to follow blindly, process misses the speaker's point: here it is the story a designer tells a client, from wireframe to mockup, before any call or email.
- Read as the work itself, it is too narrow: the process the speaker describes includes the talking about the work, not just the making of it.
