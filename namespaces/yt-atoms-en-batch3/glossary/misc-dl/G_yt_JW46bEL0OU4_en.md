# G_yt_JW46bEL0OU4_en — goal

## Term

In this source, goal is the outcome a person names and repeats until it filters every decision they make. The word goal appears 32 times, next to filter, achieve, and month, as the speaker traces a transformation from November last year to last month, generating 75 grand in revenue. The method is simple, he says: start with the goal, repeat it, and the goal becomes a filter, so that when friends say let us go out on Friday, the decision is already made by the goal. A goal here is therefore not a wish but a repeated sentence that does the deciding, and the filter it creates is what turns the goal into revenue.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a wish, goal misses the method: here it is a sentence you repeat to yourself until it becomes a filter for every decision.
- Read as a target you set once, it ignores the repetition: the speaker says people who have a goal but never repeat it to themselves never end up creating a filter.
