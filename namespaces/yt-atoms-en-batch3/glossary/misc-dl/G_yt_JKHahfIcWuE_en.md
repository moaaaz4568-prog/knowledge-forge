# G_yt_JKHahfIcWuE_en — business

## Term

In this source, business is the coaching company the speaker built to eight figures, and the video is the list of lessons it taught him. The word business appears 23 times, next to track, school, and engineering, as Fedex Felis introduces himself: he has built his business and personal brand, his team has well over eight figures, and together they have taken four businesses to over seven figures in a year. The backstory runs through engineering school, chosen because petroleum engineers make 100 grand, and through learning the hard way that what people tell you about making money is not how money is made. A business here is therefore the vehicle of those lessons, and the video's value is what the speaker learned by building it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any company, business is too wide: here it is specifically the coaching business the speaker built to eight figures, with a team and a personal brand behind it.
- Read as the money alone, it misses the lessons: the speaker's business is the school of his hard lessons, from engineering degrees chased for a 100-grand salary to businesses taken to seven figures in a year.
