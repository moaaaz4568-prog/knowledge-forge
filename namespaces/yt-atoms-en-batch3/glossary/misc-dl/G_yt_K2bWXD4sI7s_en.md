# G_yt_K2bWXD4sI7s_en — poster

## Term

In this source, poster is the Creed 2 movie poster the speaker analyzes piece by piece, and the video is a design critique of it. The word poster appears 7 times, next to movie, eyes, highlight, and film, as he says the poster was designed by a little company called Concept Arts, and they are going to talk about them a lot on this show. His method is to follow the eyeline: when you see somebody's eyes it gives you a little bit of pause, so you start at Creed and go right over, and the little highlight on his chin helps differentiate him from the background. A poster here is therefore not a photo of the cast but a constructed image, built from independent stills pulled from the film and touched up to look high resolution.
