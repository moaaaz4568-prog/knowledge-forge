# G_yt_JG63EVRXq0k_en — content

## Term

In this source, content is the free material a creator posts to reach people at a scale no other method can match. The word content appears 15 times, next to outreach, paid, traffic, and free, as the speaker compares three ways of reaching an audience. A video can get 100 views or a million views, while cold outreach reaches people one to one, and paid ads cost money and cost a lot of money to scale. To reach 12 million people with ads, or to knock on 12 million doors, is impossible, and that is literally the reason why creating content is the best way. Content here is therefore the scalable alternative to outreach and ads, and its advantage is reach without a per-person cost.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any post, content is too broad: here it is specifically the free videos and posts that reach people at scale, set against cold outreach and paid ads.
- Read as a marketing expense, it misses the comparison: the speaker's content costs nothing to post and can reach a million views, while paid ads cost a lot of money to scale.
