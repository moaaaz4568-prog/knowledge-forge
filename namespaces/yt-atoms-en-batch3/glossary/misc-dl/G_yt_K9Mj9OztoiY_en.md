# G_yt_K9Mj9OztoiY_en — business

## Term

In this long interview, business is The Right Fit, the company the founder built, and the conversation is about how it actually worked. The word business appears 60 times, next to advertising, agency, model, and platform, as she explains the old payment chain: the advertising agency would get paid by the client, then the advertising agency would pay the modeling agency, and then the modeling agency would pay the model, and that was probably a month for each one. She also describes the gift the team discovered in her character — going all in and figuring it out as she goes — and how she sincerely believed they would create a tech platform and have an engineer maintain it. Business here is therefore not an idea but a chain of people getting paid, and the platform was the bet on top of it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a startup, business misses the chain: here it is The Right Fit and the payment chain — advertising agency to modeling agency to model, a month for each one.
- Read as the platform, it skips the belief: the founder sincerely believed they would build a tech platform and have an engineer maintain it, and the business grew from that belief.
