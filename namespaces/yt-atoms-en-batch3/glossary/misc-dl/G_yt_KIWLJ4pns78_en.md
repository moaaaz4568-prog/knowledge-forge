# G_yt_KIWLJ4pns78_en — perspective

## Term

In this episode, perspective is the Chinese perspective, the compositing style the speaker promises to simplify for the viewer. The word perspective appears 12 times, next to shadow, car, angle, and lighting, as he opens by calling it a very strong episode titled The Chinese Perspective, which drives many people crazy and they want to know how it is done. His method is to photograph first: build a full mood board, shoot with the angles and lighting wanted, and then work on the design, instead of searching for stock photos or making them with AI. Perspective here is therefore a photographed way of seeing, not a filter, and the shadows and the car in the lesson exist to prove the angle works.
