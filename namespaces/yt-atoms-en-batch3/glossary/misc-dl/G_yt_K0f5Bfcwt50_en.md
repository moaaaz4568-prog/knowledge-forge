# G_yt_K0f5Bfcwt50_en — venture capital

## Term

In this source, venture capital is the world of startup investing the speaker is excited to learn about, and the video is an introduction to it. The phrase venture capital appears 21 times, next to startups, investors, business, and branding, as the speaker says he is excited about this release because venture capital is something he has wanted to learn a lot more about. The video covers how businesses should present themselves to venture capitalists, and the related idea of crowdfunding, where in a highly regulated way retail investors can invest in startups. Venture capital here is therefore not money in general but a specific way companies get funded, and the speaker is the student, not the expert.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as investment in general, venture capital is too wide: here it is the VC world the speaker wants to learn — how businesses present themselves to venture capitalists.
- Read as crowdfunding, it confuses the two: the crowdfunding concept is presented as a separate, regulated way for retail investors to invest in startups.
