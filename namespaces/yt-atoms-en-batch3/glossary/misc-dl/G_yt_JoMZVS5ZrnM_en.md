# G_yt_JoMZVS5ZrnM_en — call

## Term

In this short video, call is the conversation with a busy business owner that an outreach campaign is trying to win. The word call appears 12 times, next to trying, business, and sell, as the speaker explains the frustration: until you understand this, you will always be frustrated around why your acquisition, your appointment setting, your outreach, and your response rate are not working. He remembers his own start, when he could not understand why it was so hard to book meetings, sending hundreds of messages, and he admits he is not getting anyone on a call and always felt bad, but the truth is it is just how the world works. A call here is therefore not a phone call but the prize of the outreach, and the way to win it is an offer that makes it unreasonable to say no.
