# G_yt_JHGWFTxt1R4_en — crm

## Term

In this source, CRM is the tool an agency uses to track deals, revenue, and the cost of acquiring clients, and the conversation treats it as a partner rather than a database. The word CRM appears 36 times, next to agency, year, and doing, as the speakers explain that a proper CRM tracks how much revenue is expected to come in, the average size of deals, and how far a client is from their goal. The firm stays on board after implementation, becoming an accountability partner as much as a coach and a strategic partner, because a CRM on its own does not change habits. A CRM here is therefore a simple visual tool whose value comes from the coaching around it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a database alone, CRM misses the role: here it is an accountability partner as much as a tool, and the firm stays on board with clients after the implementation.
- Read as a complex system, it contradicts the speakers: they call it a very simple tool, designed in a very visual way, that tracks revenue, deal size, and cost per acquisition.
