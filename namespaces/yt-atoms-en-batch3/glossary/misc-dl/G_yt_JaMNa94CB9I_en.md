# G_yt_JaMNa94CB9I_en — client

## Term

In this source, client is the agency customer whose journey through the business must run on systems, not on the owner's time. The word client appears 48 times, next to agency, agencies, and place, as the speaker explains the pain of bleeding profit because the systems are not where they need to be. The fix is automation: when you do new client onboarding as an automated workflow, the client automatically goes in as a new project, and people can already access it without somebody having to go in and create that client. A client here is therefore not only revenue but the test of the agency's systems, and the business could not be exited while it still needed the owner to operate those contracts.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a buyer, client misses the systems: here the client is the person who moves through an automated onboarding workflow, and the agency's profit bleeds when the systems around the client fail.
- Read as a name in a project tool, it is too small: the speaker's client is the reason for the knowledge base, because having a system does not mean the person knows how to do the job.
