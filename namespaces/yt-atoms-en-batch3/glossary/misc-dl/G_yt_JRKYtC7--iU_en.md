# G_yt_JRKYtC7--iU_en — content

## Term

In this long interview with Dan Martell, content is the material a creator makes to reach and resonate with an audience, and the conversation returns to it again and again. The word content appears 51 times, next to audience, worth, and the SaaS businesses Martell founded, scaled, and exited. His test for content is resonance: it must speak to the audience so that people feel it was made for them, not recycled from a tweet or said in a different place. He started creating content and then immediately realized that if he was going to do it, he should go all in. Content here is therefore not a post but a commitment, and the difference between creators is whether they treat it that way.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any post, content is too broad: here it is the material a creator makes to resonate with an audience, and the speaker judges it by whether people feel it was made for them.
- Read as a marketing chore, it misses the commitment: the speaker says that if you are going to create content you should go all in, and that is the big difference between people who grow and people who do not.
