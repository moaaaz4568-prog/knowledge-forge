# G_yt_Ii0Un_7OxHc_en — podcast

## Term

Tracy DeForge uses "podcast" for a business show that its owner runs to win clients and grow revenue, not just to publish episodes. The word appears 38 times, with the plural "podcasts" also used 5 times, next to "audience", "show", and "content". Her advice is that the fastest way to grow a show is to appear on another podcast whose audience already listens to similar content. She runs Produce Your Podcast, an agency that serves business owners who use their podcasts to drive revenue, and she warns that a show must keep reaching new people or its audience stalls.

## Synonyms

- podcasts (the plural form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "If you and if ideally you have a podcast while you're doing that, then it would be to connect via that other podcast because there's no better way to expose and grow a show than to an audience that's already consuming podcast content that's similar to what you share." — sources/S_yt_Ii0Un_7OxHc_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a hobby show: DeForge's "podcast" is a business tool for winning clients and revenue, so advice about growing the show assumes the owner wants it to drive business, not just to reach more listeners.
- Read as a promise that guesting on other shows grows any podcast: she recommends shows whose audiences already consume similar content, so appearing on an unrelated podcast is not the growth move described here.
