# G_yt_Ik3SbD9xzM0_en — bio

## Term

The speaker uses "bio" for the short text at the top of an Instagram page, the first thing a visitor reads. The word appears 13 times, with the plural "bios" also used once, next to "page", "followers", "specific", and "problem". His rule is that the bio must name the specific problem you solve for a specific kind of person, as in his example of helping tech founders use AI to scale their business. The bio is one of five tweaks, alongside proof of concept, a personal touch, and a clear statement of who the page is for.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a slogan about yourself: the speaker insists the bio is not about you but about the value you provide, so a bio that only lists titles or praise misses the point of the tweak.
- Read as a trick that brings followers by itself: the bio is the first of five tweaks, and it works because visitors who land on the page can immediately see whose problem is solved, not because the words themselves attract people.
