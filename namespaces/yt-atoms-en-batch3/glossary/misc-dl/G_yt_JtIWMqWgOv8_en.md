# G_yt_JtIWMqWgOv8_en — sales

## Term

In this source, sales is the work the speaker hires people to do, and the video is about how he builds a sales team. The word sales appears 118 times, next to offer, team, and the companies he has scaled. His hiring rule is unusual: he typically hires people that do not know anything about sales, because they are going to be loyal to him, respect him, and work with him for a much lower rate. He was a sales agent himself, and his method is to go in and do the closing first, to see what kind of KPIs he is seeing, before letting the team work on the offers. Sales here is therefore not a skill to buy but a function to build, and the founder's own closing is how the team learns it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as selling, sales misses the hiring: here it is the job the speaker hires for, and he hires people who know nothing about sales because they will be loyal and work for a much lower rate.
- Read as a department, it ignores the founder: the speaker was a sales agent himself, and he closes first to see the KPIs before letting his team work on the offers.
