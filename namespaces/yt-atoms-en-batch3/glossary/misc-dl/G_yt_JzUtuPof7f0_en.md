# G_yt_JzUtuPof7f0_en — system

## Term

In this source, system is the client-acquisition infrastructure the speaker sells, and the video warns that it only pays off for the person who deserves it. The word system appears 8 times, next to potential, deserve, yield, and month, as he explains that you tap into the potential of the system only when you become the person who can get the best yield out of it. His example is his own numbers: the version of him making 30k a month could only pull 30k a month from the system, while the version doing 200 grand in July could pull much more. A system here is therefore not a shortcut but a multiplier of the person using it, and the work is becoming that person before expecting 50k a month in three to six months.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a tool you buy, system misses the condition: here it only yields for the person who deserves it, and beginners cannot tap into its potential yet.
- Read as a promise of 50k a month, it repeats the hype the speaker is fighting: he tells clients not to expect 50k a month in three to six months, because the system multiplies the person, not the purchase.
