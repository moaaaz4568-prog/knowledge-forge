# G_yt_J6_ZE0fRXDM_en — image

## Term

In this source, image is the picture a creator feeds into AI websites to get work done on it, the raw material of every demo. The word image appears 20 times, next to website, design, and the AI tools the speaker uses in his own work, which he says he cannot do without. His first demo starts on Pinterest, where he finds an image he likes and clicks on it, then uploads it to ShadGPT and asks for a precise profiling so he can reproduce the same zoom, perspective, colours, and lighting in ImageFX. An image here is therefore not a finished artwork but the input a tool reads, copies, and regenerates.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a finished illustration the speaker drew, image misleads: here it is a picture found on Pinterest and fed to an AI tool as an input to copy and rework.
- Read as any picture on screen, it is too loose: the speaker's images are the specific pictures he uploads to ShadGPT and ImageFX and asks the tools to profile and regenerate.
