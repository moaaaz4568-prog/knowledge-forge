# G_yt_JTi3nJAvzRs_en — price

## Term

In this pricing masterclass, price is the number a freelancer charges for a project, and the whole lesson is the psychology of choosing it. The word price appears 78 times, next to client, budget, and value, as the speaker warns against the common trap: taking the scope, going back, creating a proposal, throwing it into an email, and hoping it fits inside the client's budget. Picking the number has everything to do with the state of the business and the needs of the business, and the next rule is never to tell the client the amount of profit in the price, because it is none of their business. A price here is therefore not a cost plus a margin but a decision about the business, and the proposal is only the paper it is written on.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a number on an invoice, price misses the psychology: here it is the figure a freelancer chooses from the state of the business and the needs of the business, not from a rate card.
- Read as the cost of the work, it confuses price with profit: the speaker says never to tell the client the amount of profit in the price, because it is none of their business.
