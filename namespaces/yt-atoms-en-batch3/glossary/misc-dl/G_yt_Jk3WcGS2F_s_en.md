# G_yt_Jk3WcGS2F_s_en — money

## Term

In this source, money is what a creator earns by creating value, and the video is the story of how hard that work is. The word money appears 37 times, next to editing, life, and the 20K the speaker is now a little bit under. He worked hard for it while everyone around him called him unbalanced, and he feels literally exactly the same as he did in his early days. The rule he states is simple: we are going to create value to make money, and you are able to give the most value when you become extremely good at one thing and do it super efficiently for lots of people. Money here is therefore not the prize but the byproduct of value, and the work is becoming extremely good at one thing.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as cash in hand, money misses the value: here it is the result of creating value, and the speaker says you give the most value when you become extremely good at one thing.
- Read as the goal itself, it distorts the story: the speaker worked hard for under 20K and felt exactly the same as before, so the money did not change him.
