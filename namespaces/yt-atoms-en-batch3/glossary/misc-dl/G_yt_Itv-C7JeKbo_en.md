# G_yt_Itv-C7JeKbo_en — winner

## Term

In this cold-outbound video a "winner" is the angle or email variant that beats the others in testing. The word appears 12 times, next to "angle", "testing", "five", and "batches". The main principle is to find the winner fast: test five different angles, and if none wins, test another five in new batches until one does, then look for the next winner. The proof is Local Pipe, where after a thousand sends with five variations the team had a clear winner and booked 27 demos in 21 days.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a lucky guess: the "winner" is found by testing batches of five angles and measuring the sends, so it is the measured best of a set, not the first idea that felt right.
- Read as the end of testing: finding a winner only starts the search for the next winner, so the word names the current best variant, not a final answer.
