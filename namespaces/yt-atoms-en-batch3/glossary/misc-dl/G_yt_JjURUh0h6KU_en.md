# G_yt_JjURUh0h6KU_en — data

## Term

In this prospecting tutorial, data is the information about companies and people that a tool sells, and the video reviews the tool that provides it. The word data appears 18 times, next to search, companies, and platform, as the speaker explains why a database and a prospect list are everything after sending hundreds of thousands of cold emails. The tool in question is Prospect.io, and the review covers everything from data accuracy to how the credit system works, mobile numbers for cold calling, list building, monitoring, integrations, and costs. At Propel, he adds, you can filter out people you have already extracted, because they keep it integrated into the platform, and that is why he loves their credit system. Data here is therefore not a file but the product, and its accuracy is what the money buys.
