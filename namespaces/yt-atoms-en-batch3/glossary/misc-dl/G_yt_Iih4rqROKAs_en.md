# G_yt_Iih4rqROKAs_en — infrastructures

## Term

The speaker uses "infrastructures" for the growth systems his clients build and release inside a company, replacing one-off services. The word appears 7 times, with the singular "infrastructure" also used twice, next to "sell", "commoditized services", and "lead flow". An infrastructure is not the outcome itself but the inputs that create it: the lead flow, nurturing, CRM, and sales script that turn prospects into paying clients. His program helps agencies and coaches make that switch because selling one mastered service, he argues, no longer builds a six- or seven-figure business on its own.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a product the agency installs once and walks away from: the infrastructure is the inputs that create the outcome, lead flow, nurturing, CRM, and script, so "build and release" still means building something the client's business keeps running.
- Read as a claim that one service can never work: the speaker's objection is to selling a single commoditized service as the whole business, not to offering one service inside an infrastructure that creates the outcome.
