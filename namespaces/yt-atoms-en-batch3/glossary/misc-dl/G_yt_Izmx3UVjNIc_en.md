# G_yt_Izmx3UVjNIc_en — shorts

## Term

The speaker uses "shorts" for YouTube Shorts, the vertical short videos everyone tells creators to make. The word appears 30 times, next to "YouTube", "views", "subscribers", and "long-form". His argument is that shorts are great for consistently bringing in more views without extra effort, especially when a team can repost them to TikTok and Instagram Reels. But for a creator whose main focus is subscriber growth, especially toward the 1,000 subscriber mark for monetization, he says long-form videos are the better bet.
