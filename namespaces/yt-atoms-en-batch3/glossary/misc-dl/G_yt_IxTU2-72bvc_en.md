# G_yt_IxTU2-72bvc_en — fractional

## Term

The speaker uses "fractional" for executives a business hires part-time instead of employing full-time. The word appears 12 times, in "fractional executive" and "fractional leadership", next to "CMO", "agency", "revenue", and "culture". His comparison is with Uber changing cars: just as Uber changed transportation, fractional leadership changes the marketing space, because a full-time CMO costs six figures and a fractional one does not. The first thing to fix in such a business, he says, is often a toxic culture, and a fractional leader is brought in to fix it before chasing growth.
