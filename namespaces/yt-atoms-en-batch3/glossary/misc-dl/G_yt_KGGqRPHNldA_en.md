# G_yt_KGGqRPHNldA_en — agency

## Term

In this podcast, an agency is the business the whole show exists to help owners run better, and the guest brings more than twenty years of it. The word agency appears 34 times, next to client, business, coach, and pipeline, as Steve Goberman opens Agency Bytes, a podcast for agency owners, and introduces Mark Riggs, who shifted from a long agency career into coaching. The guest's ideas are practical: solve, don't sell, in every agency setting he has worked in, and watch over-servicing, because giving away work for free means working the last months of the year unpaid. An agency here is therefore a service business whose profit leaks through the pipeline, and the episode's lesson is to price the work the agency actually does.
