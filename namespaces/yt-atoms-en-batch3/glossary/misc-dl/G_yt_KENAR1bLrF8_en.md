# G_yt_KENAR1bLrF8_en — problem

## Term

In this video, the problem is the first of two things an agency beginner must get right, before the niche and before any solution. The word problem appears 30 times, next to niche, solution, ads, and offer, as Josh opens by saying there are two things to start with and most people start with the wrong one: you need the niche and then you need the problem. His charge is that most people go for the solution first, hoping and praying their niche needs Facebook ads, without understanding the problem they are actually trying to solve. A problem here is therefore the job a client will pay to remove, and the fastest way to $10,000 a month runs through naming it before building anything.
