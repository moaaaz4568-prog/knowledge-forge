# G_yt_IfjNqBWaw94_en — cold email

## Term

Daniel uses "cold email" for the outreach method his agency runs to book sales calls, not for email in general. The phrase appears 4 times, with the plural "cold emails" also used 4 times, and it sits next to "campaigns", "ICP", and "appointments". His claim is that cold email is not just a volume game: success starts with knowing exactly who the ideal client is and where those buyers already gather. He backs it with his own numbers, 16 calls booked in 14 days from hyper-targeted cold emails to one client.

## Synonyms

- cold emails (the plural form used in this source)

## Live Example

> "And basically, yeah, so this is how we booked 16 calls in 14 days with hyperargeted cold emails. All right, cold email is not just a volume game." — sources/S_yt_IfjNqBWaw94_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a volume game: Daniel's point is the opposite, that blasting more emails without knowing the ideal client profile makes the cold email useless, and targeting is what booked the 16 calls.
- Read as a guarantee of appointments: the 16 calls in 14 days came from one low-volume client account with hyper-targeted campaigns, so the numbers describe that account's result, not what any cold email campaign will produce.
