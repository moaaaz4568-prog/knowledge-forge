# G_yt_JF7jXBbY0Ko_en — hats

## Term

In this source, hats are the roles a business owner wears, and the whole lesson is choosing which ones to keep. The word hats appears 5 times, next to content, business, and job, and the speaker opens with it: you wear a ton of different hats in your coaching business, and the secret is knowing which hats to take off if you want to grow and which hats you must keep firmly on. His own example is one role he played for 12 hours last week, worth 1.32 million dollars because he decided to keep that hat on and not outsource it. Hats here are therefore the jobs inside the business, and growth means dropping the ones that do not pay and holding the ones that do.

## Live Example

> "And the secret is knowing which hats to take off if you want to grow and which hats you must keep firmly on." — sources/S_yt_JF7jXBbY0Ko_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as literal headwear, hats is nonsense: here each hat is a role the owner plays in the business, and the secret is knowing which roles to keep and which to take off.
- Read as all roles being equal, it misses the choice: the speaker kept one hat on for 12 hours a week because it was worth 1.32 million dollars, and outsourced the rest.
