# G_yt_JexJM85sp90_en — design

## Term

In this industrial design analysis, design is the quality of an object that makes us feel something, and the video teaches how to read it. The word design appears 15 times, next to performance, technical, and the HTC Vive headset the speaker examines. You can tell a lot about two objects just by looking at them, he says, how they will perform and whether you like them, and it is really important to articulate why we have certain emotional responses to design so that we can apply it to our own practice. The headset's silhouette has a lot of moving parts and places for adjustability, which make the design look more technical, while its curved forms give it refinement and elegance. Design here is therefore not decoration but the feeling an object creates, and the analysis is about naming that feeling.
