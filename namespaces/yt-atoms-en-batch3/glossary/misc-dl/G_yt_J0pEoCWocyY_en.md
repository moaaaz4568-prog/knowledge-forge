# G_yt_J0pEoCWocyY_en — brand

## Term

In this long interview "brand" is the identity a company builds in the market, and copy is judged by whether it serves that identity. The word appears 83 times, with the plural "brands" also used 29 times, next to "copy", "strategy", "culture", and "impact". The speakers discuss how a brand stays relevant as generations adopt new brands, using Nike staying relevant through athletes as the example. Their copy test is the moment of desire: a customer who wants to be more confident, more athletic, and in charge, and copy that hits that moment is what a strong brand sounds like.
