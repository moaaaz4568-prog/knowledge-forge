# G_yt_JUOpIEq2Cq8_en — marketing

## Term

In this source, marketing is the way a coach finds clients, and the video argues that most coaches do it badly. The word marketing appears 18 times, next to coaches, sales, and process, as the speaker lists the three things a coach wants: income, impact, and independence, the freedom to work your own hours, like teaching from the beach in Fiji. The problem is that a coach's marketing is either too passive or too patchy: they do a great job, get themselves out there a little, and then wait for the phone to ring, hoping people recognize their work. Marketing here is therefore not an advert but the system that brings clients in, and the coaching model itself is unscalable because it is built around trading time for money.
