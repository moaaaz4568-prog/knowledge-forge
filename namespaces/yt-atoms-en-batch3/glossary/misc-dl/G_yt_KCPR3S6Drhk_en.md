# G_yt_KCPR3S6Drhk_en — assessment

## Term

In this episode, an assessment is the paid middle rung of a simple ladder for selling AI services at $10,000 a month. The word assessment appears 32 times, next to concierge, diagnostic, ladder, and $999, as the speaker lays out the ladder: a free 15-minute AI diagnostic, then a paid assessment for $999, then an AI concierge service for $1,000 to $2,000 a month. The assessment's job is to prove value with one tool, so the natural next step is the concierge work that implements everything the assessment found. An assessment here is therefore a small paid proof, not a sales call, and the math of the whole model depends on it converting into the monthly service.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a free audit, assessment misses the price: here it is the $999 paid step, and the free step is the 15-minute diagnostic before it.
- Read as the finished service, it misses the ladder: the assessment only selects the tools, while the concierge implements them for $1,000 to $2,000 a month.
