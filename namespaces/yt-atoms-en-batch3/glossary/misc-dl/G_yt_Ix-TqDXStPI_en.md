# G_yt_Ix-TqDXStPI_en — foot in the door

## Term

Jason's "foot in the door" is the small first offer that gets a client to say yes before the bigger sale. The phrase appears 13 times, next to "converting", "clients", "discovery call", and "framework". The problem it solves is offering the wrong thing at the wrong time: a prospect who will not buy the full service may accept the small first step, and then the conversion can move faster. The speaker says the framework-to-close rate may dip because more people come in, but overall throughput goes up, which is the trade he accepts.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to push people in: the "foot in the door" here is a smaller genuine offer matched to the moment, so it is a sequencing choice about timing, not pressure on a prospect who said no.
- Read as a promise of faster closes for everyone: the speaker admits the close rate can fall even as throughput rises, so the technique trades selectivity for volume rather than improving every number.
