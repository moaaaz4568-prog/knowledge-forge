# G_yt_JxjWZT8On0k_en — business

## Term

In this source, business is the company the speaker builds and rebuilds, and the video tells the story of two of them. The word business appears 93 times, next to month, recurring, revenue, and launch, as he opens by saying that last month his business hit $100,000 in monthly recurring revenue for the first time. The year before, he had a business making an average of $135,000 per month, and he closed everything in February to build the business he has now. His rule is that every dollar earned last month is a dollar earned again next month, and the month after that, so a business here is not a launch but a recurring machine.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a startup, business misses the model: here it is both a startup-basis business and a monthly recurring business, and the speaker closed the first to build the second.
- Read as a launch, it misses the recurrence: the rule is that every dollar earned last month is earned again next month, so the business is the recurring machine, not the launch event.
