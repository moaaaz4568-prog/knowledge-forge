# G_yt_J82GLRs3skw_en — students

## Term

In this source, students are the buyers of an online coaching program called Time to Build, and the speaker measures his own credibility by their results. The word students appears 38 times, next to business, result, and access, and he opens with the proof he is proudest of: his students last year made a 127% return on their investment in 12 months. The program works, he says, because information alone is never implemented, so he provides access and continued support at an affordable rate, with the commitment baked in. Students here are therefore not a classroom but a client base whose outcomes are the product's advertisement.

- student

## Live Example

> "Because often times as an online coach or an online educator, you're essentially the surrogate for the confidence that your students lack." — sources/S_yt_J82GLRs3skw_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as school pupils, students misleads: here they are adult buyers of a coaching program, and their results are measured in return on investment, not grades.
- Read as a passive audience, it misses the relationship: the coach presents himself as the surrogate for the confidence his students lack, and the program works by continued access and support, not by lectures alone.
