# G_yt_KJ8n-xGuvVE_en — organic

## Term

In this question-and-answer session, organic means content that earns attention without paid ads, and the whole debate is when it stops being enough. The word organic appears 29 times, next to ads, paid, content, and appointment setting, as a new student asks whether he should build organic foundations until 100 thousand a month before running ads. The host's answer refuses a fixed rule: organic content is a signal that guides paid ads, and the right mix depends on whether the person can afford to run ads at all. Organic here is therefore a foundation and a test, not a ban on advertising, and the session's warning is that people who cannot afford ads hear the message very differently from those who can.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as free marketing, organic misses the signal: here organic content guides paid ads, so skipping it leaves the ads blind.
- Read as a rule for everyone, it misses the wallet: the advice to wait until 100 thousand a month fits people who cannot afford ads, not a universal law.
