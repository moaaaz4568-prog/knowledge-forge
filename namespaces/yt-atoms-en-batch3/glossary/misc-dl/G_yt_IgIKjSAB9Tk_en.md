# G_yt_IgIKjSAB9Tk_en — typography

## Term

For Satori Graphics, "typography" is the arrangement of type on a layout, and the video teaches two quick ways to improve it. The word appears 13 times, next to "hierarchy", "layout", "font", and "character styles". The first way is hierarchy: arranging type so the eye goes to the important information first, which instantly makes a business card look more appealing and professional. The second is choosing font combinations, two fonts with differences like thin and thick but enough similarity to sit together, with 20 combinations linked in the description.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule that any two fonts work together: the video requires the pair to share similarities as well as differences, so a random thin-plus-thick pairing is not what "improve your typography" means here.
- Read as a claim that hierarchy fixes any layout: hierarchy is only the first of two ways shown, and it works on the business card example by guiding the eye to the important information, not by rescuing a layout with nothing worth highlighting.
