# G_yt_JIajiyYHlsk_en — business partner

## Term

In this interview, business partner is the person a founder builds a company with, and the speaker's story is about how those bonds form and break. The phrase business partner appears 13 times, next to business, kind, and doing, as a creative Canadian from northern Ontario talks about his first business partner, whose name he will not use because he will not throw him under the bus. He says it is amazing how business partners come together because each knows how to do something the other does not and vice versa, and he counts the times he could have quit, when everybody would have understood. A business partner here is therefore a complementary ally, and the relationship is held together by what each one brings that the other lacks.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a co-owner on paper, business partner misses the human story: here partners come together because each knows how to do something the other does not, and the speaker will not name the one who wronged him.
- Read as a permanent bond, it ignores the breakups: the speaker counts the times he could have quit, and says a business partner can throw the other under the bus.
