# G_yt_JCX2Y0Nprhc_en — offer

## Term

In this long training, offer is the packaged service a business sells, and everything turns on building one with leverage. The word offer appears 130 times, next to month, clients, and leverage, as the speaker explains that working ten times harder for twice the results is negative leverage, the opposite of what an offer should do. His own example is raising the price: they started to charge quite a bit more for their offer, around four or 5K, and that helped, but the maths still showed they could not generate enough profit to acquire clients. An offer here is therefore not a discount but the unit of the business, and its price must cover the cost of finding the next client.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a discount or a special price, offer misses the meaning: here it is the whole packaged service a business sells, priced at 4K or 5K, not a markdown.
- Read as a promise to help, it is too soft: the speaker's offer is a leveraged product that must generate enough profit to acquire clients, and a weak one fails that maths test.
