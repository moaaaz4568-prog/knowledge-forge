# G_yt_KCgElROFRIg_en — team

## Term

In this interview, the team is the people an agency founder must grow so the business can run without him. The word team appears 28 times, next to agency, director, company, and decisions, as Warren Wensky, president and founder of the Montreal digital agency Plank, describes owning every aspect and having nobody left to push things to. His test for people is the director level: give some people in the team the chance to see if they like the feeling of running a company, because a director makes the senior decisions. A team here is therefore a succession plan, not a headcount, and the founder's measure of success is a company that keeps winning business when he is not in the room.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as staff to manage, team misses the handover: here the point is growing people who can run the company, not adding hands.
- Read as a hiring problem, it misses the dependence: the founder says the agency could not bring in new business on its own, so the team had to grow past needing its founders in the room.
