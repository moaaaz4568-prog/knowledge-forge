## Term

In this video, cold email is the lead-generation channel Matt Lucero built his agency on, and the video breaks down how it filled a record book launch. The phrase cold email appears 16 times, next to leads, agency, signups, and emails, as he introduces himself as the operator of a cold email lead generation agency that has generated thousands of sales meetings for clients. His proof is the numbers: over 5,000 signups for one book launch, third place in the affiliate challenge, and a sit-down interview as the reward. Cold email here is therefore a numbers game with setup time, not a trick message, and the video's lesson is that the accounts and the volume have to be built before the results come.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spam, cold email misses the operation: here it is accounts, setup time, and volume aimed at signups, not a single clever message.
- Read as a shortcut, it misses the wait: the speaker warns it takes a couple of weeks to set things up before the emails can do anything.
