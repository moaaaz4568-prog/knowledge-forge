# G_yt_J97JYAMh7Uc_en — class

## Term

In this source, class is a rank inside the medieval hierarchy, the layered pyramid the speaker walks through from the bottom to the top. The word class appears 25 times, alongside system, model, hierarchies, and medieval, as he places the nobles with their local political control below the pope, whose power could rival even exceed monarchs. He stresses that the church does not really fit within the pyramid, and that the whole five class model he is using as an example is a big misconception that does not apply everywhere in the world. Class here is therefore a historical social rank, not a modern income group, and the speaker uses it to correct a popular misunderstanding rather than to endorse the pyramid.
