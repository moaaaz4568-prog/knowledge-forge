# G_yt_J42QA_Nptxc_en — European

## Term

The guest uses "European" for the institutions of the European Union in Brussels, the world his agency works in. The word appears 10 times, in "European Union" and "European institutions", next to "Brussels", "bubble", "laws", and "agency". Brussels is described as the Washington DC of the European Union, where the laws are made that affect nearly half a billion people. He manages a digital communications agency inside that bubble, the ecosystem of the Commission, the Parliament, the Council, and thousands of interest groups that lobby them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a travel story about Belgium: the beer and the Brussels setting open the interview, but "European" here names the institutions and the market the guest's agency serves, not a tourist's impression.
- Read as a claim that EU work is only lobbying: the bubble includes thousands of interest groups, yet the guest's agency does digital communications for them, so "European" covers the whole ecosystem, not one tactic.
