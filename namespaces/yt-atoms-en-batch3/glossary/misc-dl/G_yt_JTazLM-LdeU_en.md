# G_yt_JTazLM-LdeU_en — level

## Term

In this design tutorial, level is the skill tier of a design, amateur or professional or master, and the video teaches the viewer to tell them apart. The word level appears 11 times, next to design, master, and amateur, as the speaker shows three designs and asks which is which and more importantly why. The first giveaway of the amateur level is a chaotic and almost random layout, with typography sprinkled all over the design, which distracts the viewer and makes the design confusing instead of leading the eye. A level here is therefore not a badge but a readable quality of the work, and the tutorial's job is to make the difference visible.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a difficulty setting, level misses the craft: here it is the skill tier of a design, amateur or professional or master, judged by composition, typography, and texture.
- Read as a rank to climb, it ignores the test: the speaker's level is decided by what the design does to the viewer's eye, and a chaotic layout gives the amateur away at once.
