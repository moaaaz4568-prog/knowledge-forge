# G_yt_KCPlS6y7aRk_en — niche

## Term

In this video, a niche is the editor's chosen corner of the market, and the speaker's framework is the niching onion for picking one that pays. The word niche appears 26 times, next to niching, client, editor, and videos, as he opens by saying that at the highest levels of editing every niche makes money, but how you niche and the one you choose decide how much you make. His warning is to pigeonhole yourself: hyper-target one niche while your portfolio is still short-form videos, and you end up struggling to get anyone outside it. A niche here is therefore a pricing decision as much as a creative one, and the most profitable niche is a client whose business makes far more than it pays the editor.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a passion choice, niche misses the money: here the test is whether the client's business earns far more than the editor is paid.
- Read as narrowing forever, it misses the trap: picking too narrow too early pigeonholes the editor, and the niching onion exists to avoid exactly that.
