# G_yt_IlnxDlPFKFs_en — customers

## Term

The speaker uses "customers" for the people a business already serves, and losing one is the problem the whole video is built around. The word appears 37 times, with the singular "customer" also used 21 times, next to "favorite", "churn rate", and "month". He opens with a favorite customer who texts that it is time for a break, and calls churn the silent killer: even a 3% churn rate, 3% of customers leaving every month, is not a big deal with 10 customers but compounds as the base grows. His remedy is to make sure it never happens again by catching the risk early, before the customer is already gone.
