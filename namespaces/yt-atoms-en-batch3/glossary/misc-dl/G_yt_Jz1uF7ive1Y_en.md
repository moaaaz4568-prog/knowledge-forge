# G_yt_Jz1uF7ive1Y_en — funnel

## Term

In this source, funnel is the two-stage system that turns attention into customers, and the video teaches exactly how to run it. The word funnel appears 22 times, next to retargeting, CPMs, ads, and organic, as the speaker says there are only two funnel stages to use: top of funnel and middle of funnel. Top of funnel is broad hooks that feed the algorithm, and the goal is to reach new eyeballs and attract the ideal customer into your world. A funnel here is therefore not a diagram but the path a stranger walks from a video to a booked call, and the friction in it is what keeps ad costs climbing.
