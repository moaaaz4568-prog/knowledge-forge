# G_yt_KBFAUTcnPYQ_en — close rate

## Term

In this source, close rate is the share of outbound prospects who actually close once they get on a call, and the speaker's whole problem is raising it. The phrase close rate appears 11 times, next to outbound, lead quality, calls, and clients, as he opens by saying he will talk about outbound lead quality and how to increase close rate on outbound. His diagnosis is that the rate is low because the people booking calls are undereducated, not because they lack money, so the fix is pre-education that builds trust before the call. A close rate here is therefore a measure of how well the calls were set up, and he quotes his own triaged calls at 33 percent and his personal rate at 35 percent.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales-skill score, close rate misses the setup: here a low rate blames unqualified, undereducated bookings, not the closer's pitch.
- Read as a promise, it misleads: the speaker's 33 and 35 percent figures describe his own triaged calls, not what a new caller should expect.
