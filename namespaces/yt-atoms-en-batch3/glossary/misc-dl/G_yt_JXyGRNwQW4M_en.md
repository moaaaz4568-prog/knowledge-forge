# G_yt_JXyGRNwQW4M_en — mentor

## Term

In this interview, mentor is the experienced person whose guidance turns a beginner's effort into closed deals. The word mentor appears 20 times, next to money, lot, and the cold outreach and negotiation the speaker learned. He is blunt about the debt: if he did not have Noah, he probably would not have made big deals or any deals at all, and the knowledge that lets him make 3K lets him make 10K, because his negotiation, his cold outreach, and his professionalism are all better. A mentor here is therefore not a teacher of theory but the person who makes the beginner's work pay, and the advice to a complete beginner is to build consistency and then come to a mentor.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a teacher, mentor misses the deals: here the mentor is the person whose guidance turns a beginner into someone who closes big deals, and the speaker credits Noah with the deals he made.
- Read as a guru to follow blindly, it ignores the choice: the speaker says to come to mentor me or whoever you choose, and the choice is the beginner's.
