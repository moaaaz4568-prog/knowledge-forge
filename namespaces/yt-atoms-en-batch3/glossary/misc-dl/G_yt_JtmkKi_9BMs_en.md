# G_yt_JtmkKi_9BMs_en — logo

## Term

In this logo design tutorial, logo is the mark a designer creates after researching the business it stands for. The word logo appears 12 times, next to client, research, and target, as the speaker explains the work before the drawing: for a sports brand, look into sport related books or magazines to gain insight into the language used within that niche, and the imagery relating back to it. The brief asks what the USP is, who the main competitors are, and what the client dislikes about the current logo, and finally for five to eight adjectives that best describe the business. A logo here is therefore not a picture but the answer to a brief, and the research is what makes the answer right.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a picture, logo misses the research: here it is the mark that must be designed after research into the niche, the competitors, and the language the niche uses.
- Read as the finished mark, it ignores the brief: the speaker's logo starts with questions, what is the USP, who are the competitors, and five to eight adjectives that best describe the business.
