# G_yt_IurXMXRques_en — shape

## Term

In this Illustrator tutorial a "shape" is the drawn object the instructor builds and edits on the artboard. The word appears 13 times, with the plural "shapes" also used 5 times, next to "stroke", "design", "click", and "Pathfinder". He shows how to increase a stroke by 20 points until the shape looks right, and how to align the top three shapes to the top height of a diamond design. The Pathfinder window is the tool he opens to convert a shape into a compound shape, which is the payoff of the lesson.

## Synonyms

- shapes (the plural form used in this source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any drawn mark: a "shape" here is the object being styled and aligned in Illustrator, so a brush stroke or a text box is not the shape the tutorial means.
- Read as a beginner tip only: the stroke-weight and Pathfinder steps are shown on a real design, so the lesson is about controlling shapes precisely, not about drawing a first shape.
