# G_yt_JRFMrCrSFP4_en — cold email

## Term

In this source, cold email is the outreach channel the speaker defends with numbers, proving it is not dead. The phrase cold email appears 9 times, next to emails, month, and reply, as he shows a snapshot from September to October where the positive reply rate dipped to about 15% from a typical 20%, and reports a 40% positive reply rate at scale when sending hundreds of thousands of emails per month. The system behind it is industrial: burner domains rotate in immediately when anything breaks, and a set is constantly doing warm-up. A cold email here is therefore not a hopeful message but a measured, maintained operation, and the reply rate is the proof it works.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a dead channel, cold email misses the evidence: here it works at scale, with a 40% positive reply rate across hundreds of thousands of emails a month.
- Read as a single campaign, it understates the system: the speaker's cold email runs on burner domains that rotate in when anything breaks, with a set constantly doing warm-up.
