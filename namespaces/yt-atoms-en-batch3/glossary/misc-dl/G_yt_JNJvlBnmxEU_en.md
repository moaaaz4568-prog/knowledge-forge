# G_yt_JNJvlBnmxEU_en — sequence

## Term

In this source, sequence is the automated outreach chain Apollo builds, from the first visit to the last follow-up. The word sequence appears 30 times, next to list, AI, and Apollo, as the speaker shows how the tool completely changes how you can build lists, research prospects, and create sequences. Apollo uses ChatGPT's AI web agent to scour the internet and check whether each company is B2B or B2C, and the workflow it builds starts when someone visits the website and adds that person to the sequence. A sequence here is therefore not a single email but the ordered steps a prospect moves through, and the AI does the checking before the sequence ever begins.
