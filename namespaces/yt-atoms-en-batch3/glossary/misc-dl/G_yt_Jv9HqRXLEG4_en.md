## Term

In this long interview, brand is the streetwear label the speaker built by studying Supreme, and the video returns again and again to what the brand is worth. The word brand appears 31 times, next to Supreme, luxury, equity, and discipline, as the speaker says Supreme was always a unicorn brand in streetwear and he modelled its founder.
