# G_yt_IprEaVWtgQo_en — AI

## Term

In this video "AI" means the artificial-intelligence solutions that small businesses know about but almost nobody is selling to them. The word appears 31 times, next to "businesses", "solutions", "gap", and "trust". The speaker's point is that the gap has grown: more businesses are aware AI exists, yet adoption among small businesses is still in the single digits. The trust problem is the heart of it: a cold lead who has no clue who you are must decide to trust you from zero, and the best roadmap or offer fails unless the seller makes it work by taking action.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that small businesses reject AI: the speaker says they are aware of it and the gap is a lack of providers, not a lack of interest, so "AI" here names an underserved market, not an unwanted technology.
- Read as a promise that an AI offer sells itself: the roadmap, the offer, and the lead generation strategy are all called useless without implementation, so AI is the product to deliver, not the reason a client buys.
