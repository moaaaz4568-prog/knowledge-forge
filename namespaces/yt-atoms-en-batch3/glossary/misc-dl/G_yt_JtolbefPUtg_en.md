# G_yt_JtolbefPUtg_en — money

## Term

In this source, money is the real money a creator makes on YouTube, and the video argues that views and virality do not produce it. The word money appears 19 times, next to views, viral, paycheck, and channel, as the speaker opens by saying that if you want to make real money on YouTube, you have probably been told the same thing he was. His claim is that the money the platform pays was never going to be your paycheck, and he made $106,000 in his first year of actually earning money without a single video going viral. The rule he teaches is to point the same audience at your own thing, because a single sign-up of his is worth more than everything a brand pays him for the 10 to 15 he sent to theirs.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as AdSense income, money misses the point: here it is the money from your own thing, and the platform's money was never going to be the paycheck.
- Read as views, it confuses the metric: the speaker made $106,000 in a year without a single viral video, so views are not the money.
