# G_yt_IpmtYxaODXk_en — story

## Term

The speaker uses "story" for the narrative a brand tells about itself, the first thing to decide before any logo or poster is designed. The word appears 12 times, next to "brand", "tell", "name", and "tequila". His example is Teremana tequila: the name relates to the rock's Polynesian roots, and "terra", or earth, fits a tequila brand because tequila is made from the earth. His instruction is to ask what story you want to tell, who it is told to, and how you will tell it, before drawing anything.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a slogan to print on the label: the "story" here is the brand's background and heritage that the name points to, not a tagline, and the Teremana example works because the name itself carries the story.
- Read as a claim that any story sells a product: the speaker ties the story to the product's own roots, the agave, the brick ovens, the distilling, so a story with no link to how the product is made is not the technique shown.
