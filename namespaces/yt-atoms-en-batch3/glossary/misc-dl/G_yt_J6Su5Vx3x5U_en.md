# G_yt_J6Su5Vx3x5U_en — value

## Term

In this long conversation, value is what a client is willing to pay for, and the whole discussion turns on measuring it before naming a price. The word value appears 82 times, next to client, risk, and business, as the speakers work through how a freelancer or firm should price its work. The speaker's guiding questions are direct: what do you want, what will we measure, how much value, what might we create, and what would you be willing to pay for this. Value here is therefore not a feeling about the work but the outcome the client can name and pay for, and the price follows from it rather than from hours spent.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the price itself, value confuses the two: here value is the outcome the client wants, and the price is only what that outcome is worth to them.
- Read as the quality of the work in the abstract, it misses the test: the speakers measure value by what the client will pay for, not by how hard the provider worked.
