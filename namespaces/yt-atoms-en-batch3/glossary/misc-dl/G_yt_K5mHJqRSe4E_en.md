# G_yt_K5mHJqRSe4E_en — clients

## Term

In this source, clients are the high-ticket customers the speaker signed in a single month, and the video debriefs that month. The word clients appears 18 times, next to month, signed, ticket, and business, as he opens by saying that last month he signed 45 high ticket clients at $30,000 each, while he traveled all around the world with his family for the whole month. The month included the Black Belt event run on Zoom from Arizona, and a hot air balloon mastermind at 3,000 feet in the air. Clients here are therefore not a funnel metric but the people in the room, and the debrief covers the good, the bad, and the horrendously awkward.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a headcount, clients misses the ticket: here they are 45 high ticket clients at $30,000 each, signed in one month while traveling the world.
- Read as a steady stream, it ignores the debrief: the video is a month debrief — the good, the bad, and the horrendously awkward — including the most disastrous webinar of his life.
