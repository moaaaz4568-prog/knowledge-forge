# G_yt_JRRD1U620wk_en — client

## Term

In this source, client is the person whose feedback and data feed the AI system the speaker is building for his business. The word client appears 23 times, next to AI, context, and system, as he explains how he has been looking since 2023 to figure out how to leverage AI to print money.
