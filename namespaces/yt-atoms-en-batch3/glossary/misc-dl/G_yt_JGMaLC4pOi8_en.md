# G_yt_JGMaLC4pOi8_en — team

## Term

In this source, team is the staff a coaching business is told to grow, and the speaker argues against growing it. The word team appears 9 times, next to business, month, and sales, and he opens by rejecting the advice: they say that to grow and scale a coaching business you have to grow a big team, but he did the big team thing, threw people at the problem instead of systemizing, and ended up with a team of 36 people while scaling himself out of sales, marketing, and delivery. His warning is financial too: if you are dumping tens of thousands of dollars a month in ad spend, you have to get that cash back or you go broke pretty fast. A team here is therefore a cost and a structure to question, not a badge of growth.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the measure of a business, team reverses the video's argument: here a big team is what the speaker warns against, and he scaled himself out of every part of the work with a team of 36.
- Read as the people doing the work, it is only half the story: the speaker's team is also the cost that ad spend must cover, and dumping tens of thousands a month without getting the cash back means going broke.
