# G_yt_Ij7AIB1Ae0c_en — offer

## Term

In this interview the "offer" is the packaged service Mateo sells, and crafting it is presented as the turning point of his agency. The word appears 11 times, with the plural "offers" also used 3 times, next to "killer", "predictable", and "clients". The speaker's claim is that a killer offer plus a predictable way of getting clients took Mateo from zero to 15K a month in six months while he studied and worked a job on the side. He contrasts that with tutorial hell, watching video after video without an offer anyone wants to buy.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any service an agency lists: the "offer" here is the specific packaged promise Mateo crafted to win clients, so a list of services with no clear promise is not the offer the video means.
- Read as the reason Mateo succeeded on its own: the interview pairs the offer with a predictable client-getting method and with Mateo doing the scary work of sending videos, DMs, and ads, so the offer alone did not produce the 15K months.
