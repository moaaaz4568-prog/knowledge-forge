# G_yt_JOCR2pL6kTI_en — customers

## Term

In this long conversation, customers are the local people a business attracts through community, recommendations, and trust rather than through hard selling. The word customers appears 45 times, next to business, local, and example, as the speakers work through how a local business wins work in its own city and state. One speaker's proof is simple: every time he publishes a post, he gets three customers almost every time, and sometimes more, which makes it one of the cheapest ways to get as many leads as possible. Customers here are therefore not strangers to be hunted but people already connected to the business by friends, and the sales approach is to make it easy for them to come.
