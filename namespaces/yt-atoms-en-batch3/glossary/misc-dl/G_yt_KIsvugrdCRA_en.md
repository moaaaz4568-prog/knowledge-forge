## Term

In this podcast, account management is the episode's subject, and the host opens by asking what the heck it actually is. The phrase account management appears 13 times, next to agency, client, culture, and satisfaction, as the host welcomes a guest who has done everything there is to do in agency work to dig into the topic. The conversation's tests are practical: understanding what the agency's capabilities and focus are before promising a client anything, and training people into what client satisfaction means.
