# G_yt_JoxgbXW96iM_en — business

## Term

In this source, business is one holding in a portfolio of businesses, and the video is about surviving economic seasons. The word business appears 46 times, next to businesses, kind, and the sales the speaker trains his staff to find. His rule is to think about a business like an investment portfolio: if all the money is in one stock, the reward can be high, but diversity creates a safety net, and most businesses are not negatively impacted by economic seasons. The other lesson is about the team: five or six years ago he wanted to adjust how the team was structured, and now he trains staff on how everybody can look for sales opportunities, even when they are not a salesman. A business here is therefore not a single company but one asset among many, and the portfolio is what protects it.
