# G_yt_JRQcqefI6io_en — emails

## Term

In this source, emails are the outreach messages sent to a named list of accounts, and the video is about the system that makes them work. The word emails appears 30 times, next to send, campaigns, and accounts, as the speaker walks through cold outreach: if you sent 100 emails, spent a month and your entire marketing budget to reach 100 accounts, the question is whether the system behind them is any good. The biggest mistake, he says, is setting up a great system, reaching out to a bunch of people, and then failing on the people side, because you need the right people, paid properly, to do the technical work. Emails here are therefore not a numbers game but the front of a system, and the system includes the team that handles what comes back.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spam, emails misses the system: here they are sent to 100 named accounts with a month and a whole marketing budget behind them, and the mistake is sending them without the right people.
- Read as the message alone, it ignores the work around it: the speaker's emails only work when the right people, paid properly, do the technical work that follows.
