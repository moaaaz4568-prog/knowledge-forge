# G_yt_J4nYlgG6YNw_en — service

## Term

In this episode, service is the work of selling expertise itself, set against product as the two things a company can build. The word service appears 43 times, framed by the episode's title question, Should I Build a Product and Service, and What Are the Steps?, and by the stages a person passes through from beginning to advanced success. The speaker's own illustration is a course called Brand Strategy Camp, created as the first product related to the service, which taught him to deliver the service itself. A service here is therefore not a task performed once but a repeatable offering of expertise that a company organizes, prices, and improves.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a one-off favour done for a client, service misses the episode's meaning: here it is a structured offering of expertise that a company sells again and again.
- Read as interchangeable with product, it collapses the episode's central distinction: the speaker treats product and service as two different things a business must choose between and sequence.
