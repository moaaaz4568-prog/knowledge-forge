# G_yt_JxK33bCi070_en — agency

## Term

In this source, agency is the marketing agency whose churn problem the video dissects, and the interview is about how it cut churn in half. The word agency appears 40 times, next to churn, client, dashboard, and margins, as the host opens with a 200% agency that reduced churn by 50% from one year to the next. The work was operational: intense focus throughout the agency to reduce client churn, retraining the sales team to head off churn earlier in the client life cycle, and one dashboard for financial reports, sales reports, and churn every day. An agency here is therefore not a brand but an operation, and the churn number is what the whole operation is managed against.
