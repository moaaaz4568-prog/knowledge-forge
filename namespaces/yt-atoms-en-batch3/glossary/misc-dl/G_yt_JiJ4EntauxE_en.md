# G_yt_JiJ4EntauxE_en — design

## Term

In this brand critique, design is the visual identity of a company, and the video compares how the big brands do it. The word design appears 22 times, next to text, font, and the companies the speaker looks at: Nike, FedEx, Microsoft, and IBM. One designer chose a slab serif to keep things traditional and retro, but also trendy and sporty, while Microsoft is usually fairly good when it comes to branding. The speaker's test is experience: it is really great being able to show how a design looks, but it is even more impressive if people can actually experience the design itself, and understand how it feels and how it behaves. Design here is therefore not a picture but a brand's behaviour, and the critique is about what the design does, not only what it shows.
