# G_yt_J_YmhVzOkEU_en — level

## Term

In this design critique, level is the tier a graphic design belongs to, amateur or pro or master, and the video shows real designs in each category. The word level appears 10 times, next to design, logo, and amateur, as the speaker asks the viewer to place their own work somewhere on the spectrum and then looks at business cards first. The correction comes early: when he says amateur in this video it does not necessarily mean a bad design, it is more of an entry level or beginner type of design. One card acts like a viewfinder on a camera, and another is missing the background information on the project, the clients, and the logic behind the logo. A level here is therefore a readable tier of craft, not a verdict on the designer.
