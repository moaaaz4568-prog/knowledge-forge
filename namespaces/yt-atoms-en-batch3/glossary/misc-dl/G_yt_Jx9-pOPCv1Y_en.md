# G_yt_Jx9-pOPCv1Y_en — job

## Term

In this short video, job is the creative project a designer or filmmaker takes on, and the lesson is that its price means nothing until you count what you keep. The word job appears 9 times, next to proposal, budget, profit, and expenses, as the speaker says you need to get paid first, and that is how you know the job is real, because otherwise it is just all talk. His test is simple: a million dollar job where you spend nine hundred ninety nine thousand dollars is really a one dollar job, while a hundred thousand dollar job where you spend fifty leaves fifty thousand dollars of gross profit. A job here is therefore judged by the profit after all expenses are paid, including paying yourself and your partner, not by the headline number.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the headline price, job misleads: a million dollar job where you spend $999,000 is really a one dollar job, and the test is what you keep after expenses.
- Read as any gig, it misses the reality check: here a job is only real when you get paid up front — 50 percent upfront is how you know it is not just talk.
