# G_yt_JJkknoS6v7A_en — instagram

## Term

In this source, Instagram is the platform the speaker recommends for reaching solar companies, judged by volume and replies. The word Instagram appears 26 times, next to Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter, as he compares the four platforms for finding gems, the good prospects hidden among the many. Twitter is not going to be useful for most of this work, and LinkedIn gems are very hard to find, while Instagram has influencers who target solar companies specifically and a lot of engagement with their content. Instagram here is therefore not a social feed but a prospecting channel, and the test is how much volume you can push through it and how many positive replies come back.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a place to post content, Instagram misses the use here: it is a hunting ground for solar companies and gems, and the speaker judges it by volume and positive replies.
- Read as the only platform that works, it overstates the claim: the speaker says any of the four platforms will work, and Instagram wins only on volume and reply rate for these niches.
