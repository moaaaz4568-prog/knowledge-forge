# G_yt_JCuKLSgXF6I_en — designer

## Term

In this source, designer is the career role the video addresses, a graphic designer deciding how to position herself in 2024. The word designer appears 15 times, next to design, skills, tools, and portfolio, and the speaker opens by telling any graphic designer, or anyone training to become one, that she absolutely needs to watch. The advice is practical: stay up to date with how designers use AI tools, keep like-minded designers in your inner circle, and make each project tell a story about the design process, the challenges faced, and the solutions implemented. A designer here is therefore not just a person who draws but a professional whose career decisions the video is trying to improve.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as anyone who makes things look nice, designer is too loose: here it is a career role with skills, a portfolio, and a workflow that AI tools are changing.
- Read as the design itself, it confuses the person with the product: the video addresses designers about their own decisions, while design is the work they produce.
