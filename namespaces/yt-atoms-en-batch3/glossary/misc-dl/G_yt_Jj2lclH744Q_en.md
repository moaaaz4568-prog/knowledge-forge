## Term

In this interview, email is the marketing service an agency sells by the batch, and the conversation is about pricing it. The word email appears 49 times, next to lead, magnet, and the meetings the speaker says anyone should be able to book. The numbers are concrete: sometimes they have to do 20 emails for a client in the first batch, which bounces to about four to five thousand dollars, depending on what the client can pay. The work pairs with a free lead magnet, and the speaker puts people on a wait list for 24 hours before delivering it, which makes the total number of people on the wait list public information. An email here is therefore not a message but a unit of work, and the magnet is what fills the list it is sent to.
