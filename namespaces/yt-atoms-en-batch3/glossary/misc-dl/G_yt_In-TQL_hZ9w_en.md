# G_yt_In-TQL_hZ9w_en — desire

## Term

Daniel treats "desire" as the force behind his vision on day 94 of becoming a millionaire, and he warns that it cuts both ways. The word appears 13 times, next to "vision", "fuel", "goal", and "actions". His rule is that thought energy put toward a vision creates more desire, and that desire can be used as fuel or can pull you further from the goal when actions and intentions do not align with it. The business context is concrete: three clients stuck in service delivery, and the choice to drop one to reduce risk while the vision stays the same.

## Live Example

> "and desire can be dangerous. Believe it or not, you can use desire as as fuel or you can use desire to actually get you further from the goal." — sources/S_yt_In-TQL_hZ9w_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pep talk that desire alone is enough: the speaker pairs desire with aligned actions and intentions, so desire without the work is exactly the case he says pulls you further from the goal.
- Read as a religious claim: Daniel mentions God putting him through enough before he can reap, but "desire" here is his own philosophy of wanting and working, not a doctrine about prayer.
