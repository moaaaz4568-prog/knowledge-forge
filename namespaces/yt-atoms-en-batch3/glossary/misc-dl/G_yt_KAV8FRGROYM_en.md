# G_yt_KAV8FRGROYM_en — typeface

## Term

In this video, a typeface is one of the free display faces the speaker walks through, judged by how clean and refined it looks in a real design. The word typeface appears 15 times, next to design, fonts, serif, and headings, as he opens with ten typefaces and fifty-six fonts, all free for commercial use. His test is practical: Copenhagen Grotesque Nova 2019 is an improved version of a 2015 typeface, a very clean sans serif solution with moderate ascenders and descenders that works on body text and main headings. A typeface here is therefore a working tool with a time and a place, not a specimen in a catalogue, and the video keeps score by which faces make a client's design smile.
