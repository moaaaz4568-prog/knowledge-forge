# G_yt_JJFacWGsFaE_en — graphic designer

## Term

In this source, graphic designer is an identity the speaker asks the viewer to look at honestly, not just a job. The phrase graphic designer appears 13 times, next to design, plan, and wall, as he asks whether a designer ever looks at who she is and what kind of designer she is. He tells the monk's story, a man who saw only the two bricks that spoiled his wall among hundreds of good ones, and warns against settling for work that is merely good enough because everything can be improved. A graphic designer here is therefore a person whose self-image shapes the work, and the video's question is what kind of designer you want to be.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a job title on a CV, graphic designer misses the video's question: here it is an identity the speaker asks the viewer to examine, not a label to wear.
- Read as the work produced, it confuses the person with the product: the designer's own view of himself decides the work, and the monk's wall shows how two bad bricks can ruin the whole picture in his mind.
