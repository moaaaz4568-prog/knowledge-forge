# G_yt_JLb-bchUo6w_en — stories

## Term

In this very short source, stories are the speaker's whole method, listed as a chain of moves in a single breath. The word stories appears 2 times, in a 23-word source that reads like slide titles: stories influence a person, meet resistance, make a connection and link it together, then teach and sell, and finally tell stories and link it to your business. There is no fuller transcript to draw on, so the entry takes the list at face value: a story is not told for its own sake but to move a person past resistance toward a sale. Stories here are therefore the selling tool itself, and the link to the business is the point of telling them.
