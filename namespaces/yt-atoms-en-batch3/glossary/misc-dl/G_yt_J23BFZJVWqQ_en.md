# G_yt_J23BFZJVWqQ_en — compound path

## Term

The instructor uses "compound path" for the Illustrator object made of several paths that act as one unit. The phrase appears 20 times, with the plural "compound paths" also used twice, next to "selection", "shapes", "gradient", and "text". His rule is that selecting any part of it with the selection tool selects the whole compound path, because its components act as one individual unit. He shows it in a non-destructive workflow: cutting two circles out of a square, and turning text into a compound path to place over shapes under a clipping mask.

## Synonyms

- compound paths (the plural form used in this source)

> "lines no matter how many components make up a compound path that you act as one individual units if you try to select separate paths that make up the compound path with the selection tool then the whole compound path will be selected however you" — sources/S_yt_J23BFZJVWqQ_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a group of shapes: a compound path is not grouped objects, and its parts act as one unit under the selection tool, so ungrouping will not separate it the way a group separates.
- Read as a destructive edit: the workflow is non-destructive on purpose, so the compound path and clipping mask let the instructor change things later, not cut them away for good.
