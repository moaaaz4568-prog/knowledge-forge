# G_yt_JJzBeC8noE4_en — concept

## Term

In this source, concept is the original creative idea that turns a repurposed design into something of its own. The word concept appears 10 times, next to design and designs, and the speaker states the goal plainly: to take designs like this one and not just repurpose them, but add a truly creative and original concept, because some of them frankly lack any creativity. The video then works through examples, including a playful test of which font to save, and points the viewer on to the next video for more learning in this direction. A concept here is therefore the fresh idea itself, and a design without one is only a copy.
