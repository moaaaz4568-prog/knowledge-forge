# G_yt_J7ZiJqMxaIY_en — sales funnel

## Term

In this source, sales funnel is the path that turns Instagram viewers into buyers of a group offer, and the speaker locates every problem inside it. The phrase sales funnel appears 9 times, alongside call, course, and the prices he quotes, from 2K to 5K. His argument is to raise the price and work with fewer, more qualified people: even if only half the people who come to the call buy, the speaker still makes the same amount of money while working with people who have a better chance of getting results. A sales funnel here is therefore not a diagram but a sequence of stages, and the middle of it, where people already know the seller, is where he says the problem actually sits.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any set of sales steps, sales funnel loses its shape here: the speaker's funnel is the specific path from Instagram videos to a sales call to a group offer.
- Read as the funnel doing the selling, it misleads: the funnel only moves people toward the call, and the call itself is where the speaker presents and closes.
