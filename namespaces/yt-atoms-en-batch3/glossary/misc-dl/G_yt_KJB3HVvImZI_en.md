# G_yt_KJB3HVvImZI_en — marketing

## Term

In this short source, marketing is the activity whose goal is the brand, the mental image created in people's minds, and content is its only way there. The word marketing appears 4 times, next to goal, content, message, and brand, as the speaker says there is no direct way to do marketing to reach the goal of marketing except through content. The message is not the advertising or the copy itself, the speaker warns, but what gets stuck in the mind of the person listening to the copy. Marketing here is therefore the building of a mental image, not the shouting of a claim, and the source's whole argument fits in one line about content carrying the message.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So, there's no other way in the world to reach the goal of marketing except by creating content, because that's the only way we have to send a message." — sources/S_yt_KJB3HVvImZI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advertising, marketing misses the goal: here the goal is the brand in the listener's mind, and advertising is only one way to carry a message.
- Read as the copy itself, it misses the listener: the message is what gets stuck in the mind of the person hearing the copy, not what the speaker said.
