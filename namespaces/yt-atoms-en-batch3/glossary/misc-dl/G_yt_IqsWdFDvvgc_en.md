# G_yt_IqsWdFDvvgc_en — webinar

## Term

The speaker calls the "webinar" a conversion event, and the whole tip is about planning it in reverse. The word appears 10 times, with the plural "webinars" also used 5 times, next to "sales", "conversion", "plan", and "deliver". His point is that the way you deliver a webinar and the way you plan it are completely reversed: you start from the result you want, the moment people say the event was amazing and ask for more, and plan backwards from there. The example is his own coaching program and offer, where 20, 30, or 40 percent of the audience takes action toward the next step.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a talk you give live: the "webinar" here is a planned conversion event, so the delivery is only the last part and the planning, done in reverse from the ending, is what makes it convert.
- Read as a promise that webinars sell by themselves: the speaker asks the listener directly whether they are using webinars to sell right now, because the event only converts when it is planned around the offer and the next step.
