# G_yt_InX6ZK-czec_en — culture

## Term

In this interview "culture" is how a business actually behaves, the habits and values that decide whether it grows or stalls. The word appears 18 times, next to "entrepreneurship", "family", "execution", and "leadership". The guest traces it from the family into the business: a family culture that bleeds into other areas of life, and a business that runs on culture and execution rather than on money alone. His sharpest statement is that what made the business go the way it did was culture and execution, with leaders watching the details that create the outcome.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a poster on the wall: the "culture" here is shown in actions, a family culture bleeding into business and leaders keeping their eye on the ball, not a written set of values.
- Read as a claim that culture replaces hard work: the guest pairs culture with execution every time, so culture names the way the work is done, not a substitute for doing it.
