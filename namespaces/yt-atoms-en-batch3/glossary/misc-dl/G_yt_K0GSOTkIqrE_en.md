# G_yt_K0GSOTkIqrE_en — ai

## Term

In this source, AI is the product an agency sells to businesses, and the video is about which businesses will never buy it. The word AI appears 37 times, next to agency, businesses, solutions, and pitch, as the speaker opens by saying that a lot of people getting into the AI agency space are wasting weeks if not months pitching businesses that simply will never buy AI. His test is practical: if a business already uses a free appointment booking system and it works fine, you will not convince them to pay $1,500 per month for an AI version that does the exact same thing. AI here is therefore not a technology to admire but a solution to sell to the right buyer, and the skill is walking away from the wrong ones before you even get on a call.
