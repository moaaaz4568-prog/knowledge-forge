# G_yt_tLZPE-reRJE_en — mouse wheel

## Term

The mouse wheel is the star of the first Illustrator tip in this video: hover any numerical value and scroll to adjust it, one digit at a time, or hold shift to move in increments of 10. The phrase appears three times, beside shift, stroke weight, and Adobe Illustrator, and the speaker says the trick works literally everywhere in the program. His warning is practical too: scrolling that hard wears the wheel out, and you will need a new mouse after wearing it out. One concrete reuse he shows is jacking up a group's stroke weight with the wheel in an instant, after applying the stroke to the whole group.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a zoom control: the mouse wheel here adjusts numbers, not the canvas zoom — hovering a value and scrolling changes the value itself, which is why the tip works in any panel.
- Read as a free trick: the speaker jokes that the cost is a worn-out wheel and a new mouse — the tip saves clicks, not money, and heavy use wears the hardware.
