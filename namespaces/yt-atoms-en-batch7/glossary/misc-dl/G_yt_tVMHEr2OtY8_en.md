# G_yt_tVMHEr2OtY8_en — hyper agent

## Term

Hyper Agent is the AI agent platform this episode is built around: Alex McDonald from its team shows how to sell AI services to local businesses, live on screen. The name appears thirty-three times, beside AI agents, local businesses, and speed to quote, and the core demo is finding brick-and-mortar businesses with great reviews and terrible websites, then building their whole digital presence automatically. One concrete build is a speed to quote system for a landscaper, done live for under $9. The offer in the video is 1,000 people getting $1,000 in free Hyper Agent credits — enough, the speaker says, to do everything shown a hundred times over.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a generic name for any AI agent: Hyper Agent is one specific platform in this episode — the demos, the credits, and the speed to quote build all run on it, not on agents in general.
- Read as a promise that the $9 build earns money by itself: the build is a demo of the method — the episode's point is selling the service to local businesses, and the money comes from the offer, not the tool alone.
