# G_yt_tQGQtHKsn2w_en — digital product

## Term

A digital product is what this video says Headspace really is: not just an app, but short content delivered daily behind a checkout page — the same type of product that made the speaker over 30 million. The phrase appears six times, with the plural digital products five times, beside app, Headspace, and subscription, and the reframe is the point: the app is packaging, and the content inside is the product. Headspace's version is short guided sessions every day — five minutes, ten minutes, press play and follow — sold for 10 bucks a month to millions of people. One concrete claim is the scale: the app made 348 million in a single year, and the speaker says he will rebuild a better version from scratch in seven minutes using AI, with no coding.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for app: the video insists the app is only packaging — the digital product is the content inside, and the same content sells as ebooks and courses without any app at all.
- Read as a promise to clone Headspace in seven minutes: the rebuild is a demo of the method, not a finished competing app — the video teaches the reframe, and the seven minutes cover the build, not the business.
