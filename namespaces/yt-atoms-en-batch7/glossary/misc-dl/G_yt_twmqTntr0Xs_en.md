# G_yt_twmqTntr0Xs_en — grid tool

## Term

The grid tool is the Illustrator tool this tutorial uses to build intricate circular designs, and the speaker calls it the most interesting grid tool in Illustrator: the polar grid tool, found under the line segment tool. The phrase appears four times, beside line segment tool, Pathfinder and rotate tool, and the method is exact: make five circumference sections and ten slices, so one slice is thirty-six degrees, then rotate the circle half a slice, eighteen degrees, to align one slice perfectly. His concrete trick is the isolation mode on a single slice: whatever is drawn inside that slice is copied around the entire circle, and the gridlines are deleted at the end to leave the finished design. A second way he shows uses the same grid locked in place, drawing a shape that meets the edge of a slice and copying it by thirty-six degrees with the rotate tool until the circle is full.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any grid in Illustrator: this grid tool is the polar grid tool under the line segment tool, not a layout grid, and its slices and degrees are what make the circular design work.
- Read as a single technique: the video teaches two ways to use the grid tool, and the second way locks the grid down and copies shapes by thirty-six degrees, so following only the first way misses half the tutorial.
