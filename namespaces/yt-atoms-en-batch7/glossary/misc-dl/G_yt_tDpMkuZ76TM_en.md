# G_yt_tDpMkuZ76TM_en — brand identity

## Term

A brand identity is the full system this free course teaches for turning design into a career: onboarding the client, strategy, mood boarding, and designing the identity for a real brand. The phrase appears four times, beside mood board, brand values, and color palette, and the speaker's complaint is that most people fail because they cannot justify their design decisions. Her framework is KPS: a brand identity is not just how you look, but how you show up, what you stand for, and how you make people feel. One concrete case runs through the video — a hair care brand — where three values (authenticity, confidence, and innovation) drive the mood boards, the color palette, and the word mark.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a logo alone: the speaker is explicit that a brand identity is not just how you look — the course covers strategy, values, tone of voice, color, and typography, and the logo is only one asset among them.
- Read as a promise of 10, 20, or 50 thousand per project: the speaker says the video does not promise those fees — its goal is getting a beginner to about 5K per month, not to top-tier branding rates.
