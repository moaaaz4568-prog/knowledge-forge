# G_yt_trP-r8BAxr4_en — hook builder

## Term

The hook builder is the worksheet this video uses to write an ad and figure out the right angle: the left-hand column says who you want, and the target market goes in there. The phrase appears once, beside big promise, raw ingredients, and without, and the method is four columns — who they are, what they want, what they do not want to do to get it, and the identity-based objection. The without column is the list of three to five things they are scared they will have to do: business coaches do not want a cold call, do not want to network, do not want to waste money on ads. One concrete piece of the worksheet is the big promise in little time — more clients now, thin thighs in 30 days, the four hour work week — and the columns together are the raw ingredients for the ad.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the ad itself: the hook builder is the worksheet, not the ad — the columns give you the raw ingredients, and the ad gets assembled from them in a second, which is a separate step.
- Read as a promise generator: the big promise is only one column's output — the worksheet also needs the who, the want, the without, and the identity objection, and the hook comes from the set, not the promise alone.
