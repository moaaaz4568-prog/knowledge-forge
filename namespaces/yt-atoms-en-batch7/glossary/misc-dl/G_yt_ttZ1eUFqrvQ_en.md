# G_yt_ttZ1eUFqrvQ_en — blend tool

## Term

The blend tool is the Illustrator tool this tutorial uses to turn separate circles into a three-dimensional minimal poster, and the speaker presents it as the single step that creates the 3D shape after the circles are drawn. The phrase appears twice, beside ellipse tool, direct selection tool and blend options, and the method is concrete: draw four circles in different sizes and bright bold colors, select them, then click them one by one with the blend tool so the blending happens in front of the viewer. His concrete follow-up is the direct selection tool, which lets the designer click and drag anchor points to rearrange the blended shape, and the curvature tool, which replaces straight lines with curved ones on the same 3D design. The tutorial's frame is a gray background with a rectangle locked down first, so the blend work sits on a stable canvas and the circles never move around while the designer works.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the tool that draws the circles: the circles are drawn with the ellipse tool, and the blend tool only blends shapes that already exist, so selecting nothing first produces no 3D shape.
- Read as a one-click finish: after blending, the speaker still rearranges anchor points with the direct selection tool and adds typography, so the blend is the middle of the poster, not the whole poster.
