# G_yt_tcxncBI64VI_en — conviction

## Term

Conviction is the belief in your own product or service that this video says decides what an editor can charge — more than workflow, more than volume, more than editing ability. The word appears thirteen times, beside belief, prices, and per video, and the speaker's own proof is his price ladder: his first commission in February 2021 was $5 for a video, and he raised it slowly — 50 bucks, then 100, 150, 200, 300, 400 — until he charged $1,000 to $2,000 per video. The argument is that raising prices is the most effective way to change how much money you make, and what stops most editors is not skill but the lack of this belief. One concrete result: he has had his 10K months, and the video's question is what stops other editors from charging what their work is worth.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as arrogance: conviction here is belief in the product or service, not in yourself as a person — the video defines it as belief in what you sell, and the test is whether you can name a price for it.
- Read as a substitute for skill: the speaker says he tried workflow and volume first — conviction is what changed his income, but only after four years of editing, and the video never says belief replaces the work.
