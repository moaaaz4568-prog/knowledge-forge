# G_yt_tHfFkXpJ0E0_en — anchor point

## Term

An anchor point is the control point on a shape's path that this minimal-logo tutorial cuts and aligns: the scissors tool clicks the middle right anchor point and the lower one to remove a 90-degree section of a circle. The phrase appears four times, beside scissors tool, smart guides, and selection tool, and the whole logo is built by operating on these points. The alignment check is visual: the lower anchor point must sit in line with the other one, and the magenta smart guides show when it does. One concrete move he teaches is rotating the shape by 315 degrees after the cut, then rounding the stroke with a round cap for the finished feel.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a decorative dot: an anchor point here is a working handle on the path — clicking and deleting at it changes the shape itself, so it is a construction tool, not a visual detail.
- Read as a step you can eyeball: the tutorial insists on the magenta smart guides to confirm the lower anchor point lines up — guessing the alignment leaves the logo uneven.
