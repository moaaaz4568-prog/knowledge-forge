# G_yt_tH39TRCzi_E_en — agency brand

## Term

An agency brand is the reputation of the agency itself, as opposed to its founder's personal brand — and Clare Hutchings says small agencies get the balance wrong. The phrase appears six times, beside founder's brand, scale, and equity, and her rule is size-based: agencies under 15 people massively over-index on the founder's brand, while over 30 people the agency brand is more established and scores higher. The reason to build it early is exit and scale: if you want to sell, or grow beyond about 30 people, equity in the agency brand is what carries value. One concrete case in the source is Chime's own data set project, which positioned the agency as the experts in the data and attracted three times revenue with eight times more in the pipeline.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a logo or a name: the agency brand here is the equity and reputation that lets an agency sell or scale past 30 people — not its visual identity, which the video never discusses.
- Read as advice to hide the founder: Hutchings never says to drop the founder's brand — small agencies lean on it naturally, and her point is to start building the agency brand alongside it before scale forces the issue.
