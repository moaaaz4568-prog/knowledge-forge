# G_yt_tPed0ZBIX94_en — sales model

## Term

A sales model is how Taki's coaching business sells, and this video is about changing it: after years of an events model, they streamlined down and are testing a zero event sales model for the next four months. The phrase appears three times, beside events model, marketing, and lab, and the change is framed as a lesson learned: the old model leaned on events, and the new test drops them for four months. The operating principle is to treat the business like a lab: do not keep doing things just because that is the way they have always been done. One concrete test he names is the zero-event experiment itself, with two keys he promises to explain — the model is being tested, not declared a success.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof that events do not sell: the video tests a zero-event model for four months — an experiment in progress, not a verdict that events never worked.
- Read as a new fixed model: the zero-event sales model is a four-month test inside a lab mindset, so treating it as the permanent model ignores the video's own framing.
