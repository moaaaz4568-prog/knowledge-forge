# G_yt_th0nnZQMxvc_en — bank statement

## Term

A bank statement is the basis of the loan program Jesse Cronin describes for self-employed borrowers: instead of tax returns, the lender looks at your actual dollars. The phrase appears nine times, beside North Point Bank, self-employed, and down payment, and the pitch is that tax returns go out the window — the program looks at what you actually earn, which matters for business owners bringing in 70, 80, 90 grand a month in receivables. The interviewer is learning the backend process live: Jesse works with North Point Bank, and if North Point cannot fund somebody, they work with a partner. One concrete number: with a 680 credit score, a bank statement loan runs more like 20 to 30% down — the score decides the down payment, not just the income.
