# G_yt_trzeMg6vwFQ_en — fuel source

## Term

A fuel source is what drives an entrepreneur — the inner energy that keeps them working — and this interview divides it into two kinds: dirty fuel, built on anger, revenge and the need to prove doubters wrong, and clean fuel, built on love, care and confidence from within. The phrase appears nine times, beside dirty fuel source, clean fuel source and default fuel source, and the speaker traces his own switch from one to the other to becoming a father, when family and its health became what really mattered. His concrete test is the entrepreneur who clings to anger as a warrior mentality and fears that without it there will be no fuel at all, while the interview argues that both kinds of fuel do the job and that clean fuel leaves the engine feeling more pristine. The point is not that anger never appears — the speaker says every entrepreneur starts from a grudge — but that a person can keep doing the same work on a different fuel source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a literal fuel like petrol: a fuel source here is an inner drive, not something poured into an engine, and the interview measures it by what the entrepreneur feels, not by any physical quantity.
- Read as proof that clean fuel always outperforms dirty fuel: the speaker says both kinds of fuel do the job and that many successful billionaires run on anger, so the interview argues for choosing the fuel, not for a universal performance ranking.
