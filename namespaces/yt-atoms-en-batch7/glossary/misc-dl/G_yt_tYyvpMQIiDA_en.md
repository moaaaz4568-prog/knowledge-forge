# G_yt_tYyvpMQIiDA_en — content marketing

## Term

Content marketing is the purpose of this podcast's whole argument: we live in a post-trust world, and the purpose of content marketing is to build trust. The phrase appears three times, beside trust, conversions, and revenue, and the claim is that trust in humanity has been falling since Covid and the 2020 era, so the work is to combat that. The show is Coffee in Conversion with Clay and Luke from Good Agency, talking about increasing revenue and ramping up conversions. One concrete aside sets the tone: for most healthy adults 400 milligrams of caffeine a day is considered safe — about eight cups of brewed coffee — and the hosts admit the podcast alone puts them over the limit.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for advertising: the podcast defines content marketing by its purpose — building trust in a post-trust world — not by selling, and the episode pairs it with conversions as the outcome, not the method.
- Read as a claim that trust is gone: the argument is that trust has fallen, not vanished — the work of content marketing is to combat the fall, and the hosts treat it as a rebuild, not a lost cause.
