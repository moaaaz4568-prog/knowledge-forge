# G_yt_teux3CIp5bU_en — gross margin

## Term

Gross margin is the financial metric this episode says agencies should care about most: how much money you made on the work you delivered. The phrase appears thirty-one times, beside financial metrics, project profit, and operating leverage, and the episode teaches five financial metrics successful agencies get right, drawn from Upsourced's own analysis of their client data. The guest is Ryan Watson from Upsourced Accounting, a former agency COO and CFO, and the building blocks he teaches are a project profitability analysis. One concrete framing: several guests on the show answered the same question the same way — they wished they had invested in their own financial literacy sooner — and the episode's hierarchy of financial needs tells you which metric to prioritize now.
