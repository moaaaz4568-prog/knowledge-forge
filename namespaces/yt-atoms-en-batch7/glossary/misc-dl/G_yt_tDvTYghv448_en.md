# G_yt_tDvTYghv448_en — co-founder

## Term

A co-founder is the partner the speaker says he will never build another business without: more than someone who complements your weaknesses, a co-founder is also your mental therapist through the hard parts. The word appears seven times, beside business, Fanbytes, and agency, and the claim is absolute — 100%, never again solo. His own history is the evidence: he built and exited two companies by 27, including Fanbytes, the middleman business connecting brands and influencers, and he credits the partnership with carrying the mental side. One concrete moment he recalls is turning to his co-founder when clients started asking for services and realizing they had become an agency — a pivot he says he followed from the market, not from a plan.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a hiring tip: the co-founder here is a partner in the business, not an employee — the speaker's point is about sharing the mental load of building, not about delegating tasks.
- Read as a claim that co-founders guarantee success: his rule is personal — he will never start another business without one — and it rests on his two exits, not on a comparison with solo founders.
