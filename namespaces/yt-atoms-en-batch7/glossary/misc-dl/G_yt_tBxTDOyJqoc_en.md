# G_yt_tBxTDOyJqoc_en — Uber movement

## Term

The Uber movement is the speaker's name for this moment in AI: tools are absurdly cheap now, just as Uber rides were $3 in 2015, because venture money is buying loyalty before prices rise. The phrase appears once, beside cheap AI, smart operators, and AI pricing, and the whole warning hangs on the parallel — those $3 rides are now 30 to 100, and people still pay because it became normal. His answer is not to quit the tools but to document everything outside them: prompts, decisions, workflows, and logic saved separately, so a price spike or a rule change means porting, not rebuilding. One concrete case he gives is an agency owner whose four-person team does the work of 20 and saves 700 to 800 thousand dollars in salary — real leverage today, and exactly the setup that breaks if the cheap ride ends.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a prediction that AI tools will fail: the warning is about price, not capability — the speaker trains his own custom GPT and agents, and his plan is to keep using the tools without depending on them.
- Read as advice to avoid AI entirely: the video explicitly says it is not anti-AI — the recommendation is to audit, document, and build AI-augmented IP, not to stop using the tools.
