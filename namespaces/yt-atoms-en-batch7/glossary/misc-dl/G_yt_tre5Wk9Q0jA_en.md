# G_yt_tre5Wk9Q0jA_en — visual identity

## Term

Visual identity is the field James Martin — Made by James — says he has always talked about: logo, visual identity, brand identity, and he has stuck there. The phrase appears seven times, beside logo design, brand identity, and design education, and the episode is a podcast for agency owners, Agency Bytes, where the host admires how James grew his following and his practice. James evolved from graphic designer into logo designer, visual identity designer, and brand designer, and now focuses on design education — a lot of it for free. One concrete detail: the stuff that is not free is a couple of bucks, nothing, and he puts it all out there in his content, from tips about Adobe to the rest of his design teaching.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for logo design: visual identity is the wider field — James lists graphic designer, logo designer, visual identity designer, and brand designer as separate steps, and the identity is the system, not the mark.
- Read as a claim that James only teaches logos: the education covers the whole space — his content runs from Adobe tips to brand identity, and the free teaching is the practice, not a funnel for the paid couple of bucks.
