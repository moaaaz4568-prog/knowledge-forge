# G_yt_tgemxESv8bQ_en — white label

## Term

White label is the model of Napit Koshell's agency, Page Traffic: a white label SEO agency whose clients are other agencies, not end businesses. The phrase appears twice, beside SEO, agency, and clients, and the speaker has been doing SEO for almost 24 years, with a backend office in India and a sales office in the US. The backstory is accidental like everyone else's: nobody he knows consciously went into the SEO business — he made a website in college just testing it out, people started calling asking if he did SEO, and he said yes and learned on the job. One concrete detail from the early days: back then you could put keywords the same color as the background and build as many backlinks as you wanted and get ranked — and the first ones to do that kept their position forever.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for SEO agency: white label is who the agency serves — other agencies resell its work, and the speaker's clients are white label clients, not the businesses whose sites get ranked.
- Read as a plan Napit set out with: he says nobody consciously went into SEO — the agency started with a college test website and a yes to the first caller, and the white label model grew out of that, not out of a plan.
