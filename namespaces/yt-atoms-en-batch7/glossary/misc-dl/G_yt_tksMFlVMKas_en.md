# G_yt_tksMFlVMKas_en — organic e-commerce

## Term

Organic e-commerce is the business Smith describes: dropshipping, but instead of spending a thousand dollars on advertising, he creates videos on social media using AI to promote products and sites. The phrase appears twice, beside dropshipping, traffic, and creative, and the whole basis of the model is getting the most traffic — how to get as many people as possible to watch the video further. The example product is a sleep mask with built-in music and headphones, sold on sleep problems on airplanes, travel anxiety, and panic attacks. One concrete number: the videos bring him almost $30,000 a week, and the creative strategy brings $20,000 to $30,000 every week — income he attributes to the creative, not the ad spend.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as free traffic: organic here means no ad spend, not no work — the videos are made with AI and the whole model depends on getting the most traffic, which is the work itself.
- Read as a claim about the product: the sleep mask is only the example — the video's subject is the creative strategy, and the same approach is meant to work for other products with other hooks.
