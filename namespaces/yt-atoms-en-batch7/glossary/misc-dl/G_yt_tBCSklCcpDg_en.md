# G_yt_tBCSklCcpDg_en — minimal logo

## Term

A minimal logo is a logo built on simplicity: the minimal logo design style strips a mark down to the fewest elements that still carry the brand. The phrase appears twelve times, with the fuller minimal logo design seven times, beside minimalism, negative space, and brand, and the video opens a series looking at logo design styles one by one. Minimalism here is a design approach that uses negative space in a big way, and the speaker insists less is always more — though he warns the style may look effortless while being anything but empty and boring. One concrete tip he gives is to be aware of negative space: it can produce interesting designs that fit the style, and he shows two of his own past designs as examples.
