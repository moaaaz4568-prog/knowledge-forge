# G_yt_tGpNqBL-U_E_en — middleman model

## Term

The middleman model is the business the speaker says he would rebuild from zero if he lost everything: sit between two sides that need each other, connect them, and take a cut. The phrase appears three times, beside influencer marketing, agency, and bridge, and the whole video is a month-by-month rebuild plan for it — starting a brand new influencer marketing agency with no team, no connections, and no money. His argument is that every business he tried before was built on hype, while the middleman model is built on solving real problems. One concrete picture he gives is the biggest companies in the world being middlemen — Uber among them — where the person in between always gets paid, and gets paid a lot.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a shortcut to a million in 12 months: the video frames it as a 12-month rebuild with month-by-month work and no team or money — a plan with clean systems and consistency, not a promise the million arrives.
- Read as another hype business: the speaker's whole point is the opposite — he tried freelancing, drop shipping, SMA, and crypto, lost more than he made, and picked the middleman model precisely because it is not built on hype.
