# G_yt_tpEYPp_NvO4_en — info product

## Term

An info product is what Gabriel sells: a 19-year-old running faceless YouTube channels, who went from 8,000 followers and zero dollars from Instagram to 44K followers and $20,000 a month with his info product business. The phrase appears four times, beside personal brand, viral, and content strategy, and the story is the gap he closed — one viral video, but no real strategy, no consistent sales, and no idea how to turn attention into revenue. The fix the video shows is a clear organic short form content strategy: the science of virality and scripting, turning Instagram into a consistent sales channel. One concrete result: by the end, Gabriel had a clear path from just posting content to running a profitable personal brand, making 15 to 20K a month in revenue on the info product business.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for content: the info product is the business — Gabriel had the content and the viral video already, and the product is what turns attention into revenue, not the content itself.
- Read as a claim that followers equal money: Gabriel had 8,000 followers and zero dollars — the video's point is that the product and the strategy do the selling, and followers alone paid nothing.
