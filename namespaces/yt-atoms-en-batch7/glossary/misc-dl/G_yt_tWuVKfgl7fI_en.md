# G_yt_tWuVKfgl7fI_en — case studies

## Term

Case studies are the trust tool in Daniel Diaz's lead generation process: after gathering attention and nurturing it by email, the agency leverages case studies to show competence. The phrase appears seven times, beside email campaigns, trust, and demo, and the rule is total — case studies are used for every single email campaign. If a client has no case studies, the team finds another way to get someone on a call, for example a demo. One concrete step in the process comes first: gather attention by some sort of marketing, organic or paid, then nurture it back and forth with qualifying questions, never sounding like a salesman or pushing the sale.

## Synonyms

- case study (the singular form used in this source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the first step: case studies come after attention and nurturing — the process gathers eyes first, and the case study is what turns a nurtured prospect toward a call.
- Read as something only the agency's own clients need: the rule covers every campaign, and when a client has none, the team substitutes a demo — the trust proof is required either way.
