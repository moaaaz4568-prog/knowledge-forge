# G_yt_tGdo3i9MBZk_en — open source

## Term

Open source here means free GitHub repositories you turn into a paid service business: Corey shows seven of them, and the trick is selling the output of the repo, not the repo itself. The phrase appears fifteen times, beside CRM, setup fee, and recurring revenue, and the process starts with the repository, then a paid assessment to identify pain points. The money model is concrete: a setup fee plus monthly recurring revenue — one example is a $1,000 setup fee, then monthly, with hosting and updates included. One concrete case is Twenty, an open source CRM with about 20.57 thousand GitHub stars, sold to companies that mostly do not even use a CRM.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as selling the repositories: Corey is explicit that you sell the output and the service around the repo, not the repo itself — the code stays free on GitHub.
- Read as a no-code promise for every repo: the video says you can do this even if you do not know how to code, but the offer still requires finding buyers, running assessments, and hosting the service — the repos alone earn nothing.
