# G_yt_tAVA8tFPXRY_en — organic acquisition funnel

## Term

The organic acquisition funnel is the system this video walks through for getting clients to 100K per month or more without paid ads: attract attention, then nurture and warm people until they come to you ready to pay. The phrase appears twice, beside attention, sales calls, and appointment setting, and the speaker contrasts it with a paid acquisition funnel that scales from 500K a month to a million. The point is not attention alone — anybody can send DMs, cold email, or tweets — but warming an audience over time so you always have people to pull from. One concrete picture he gives is a prospect arriving with 10 or 20 grand in hand saying they want his help, which is what the funnel is built to produce.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for getting attention: the funnel's work is the warming and nurturing after attention — the speaker says anybody can get attention, and few can warm an audience over time.
- Read as a free version of the paid funnel: the organic funnel is presented for reaching 100K a month, while the paid funnel is a separate system for scaling from 500K to a million — the video does not claim one replaces the other.
