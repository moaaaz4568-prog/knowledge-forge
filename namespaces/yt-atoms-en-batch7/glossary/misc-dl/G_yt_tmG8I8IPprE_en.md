# G_yt_tmG8I8IPprE_en — stylescape

## Term

A stylescape is a mood board on steroids: a wide, landscape layout of specific colors, font choices, textures, and imagery that shows the client the path you intend to take in the project. The word appears eight times, with the plural stylescapes three times, beside vision, mood boards, and brand identity, and the point is agreement before the real work — the client can agree, disagree, or add input on your vision before you spend countless hours on designs that get rejected. The speaker calls stylescapes a key part of his design process, and clients absolutely love them. One concrete use: stylescapes work for brand identity projects, website designs, video creation, and much more — and the video then makes one, starting with starscapes.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for mood board: a stylescape is a mood board on steroids — the difference is the driving seat: it shows the client the path you intend to take, and the client agrees or disagrees before the concrete designs exist.
- Read as a presentation trick: the stylescape is a risk control — without one, you spend countless hours on designs the client rejects, and the video's point is knowing where you stand on the client's vision first.
