# G_yt_tgDjT6G5VZo_en — brand strategy

## Term

Brand strategy is the space this designer moved into from design: products are made in factories, but brands are built in the hearts and minds of people, and every experience is a chance to nurture or influence that relationship. The phrase appears five times, beside brand strategist, impression management, and design, and the speaker starts with stories about how he got into brand strategy, to give context for the conversation. The work, as he frames it, is shaping the company — and people want to be brand strategists because they get to do something important. One concrete example from the video: the very first time they did brand strategy, it was for an apron — somebody called it a skirt, and he just went with it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for design: brand strategy is the relationship work around the product — the product is made in a factory, and the brand lives in people's hearts and minds, so the strategist's job is the relationship, not the artifact.
- Read as a job title you claim: the video's strategist is a facilitator — if clients say things you do not understand, you have to be brave enough to say so, and the role is about asking, not about having all the answers.
