# G_yt_t2LnB2wF2X0_en — kerning

## Term

Kerning is the space between each letter, and this video's first typography tip is that default kerning settings need tweaking to be more legible and appealing. The word appears four times, beside default font, logos, and headings, and the speaker shows the difference between the default settings and his edited kerning. His advice is hands-on: go in and change the kerning especially for logos or headings, where the spacing itself helps the words read well. One concrete contrast in the source is the before-and-after he points to — default spacing versus the edited spacing — rather than any numeric kerning value.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a font choice: kerning here is the spacing between letters of a chosen font, not the choice of typeface itself — the video treats typeface choice as a separate second tip.
- Read as a fixed numeric rule: the source gives no kerning value, only a before-and-after comparison, so no number in this video can be lifted as a standard setting.
