# G_yt_tCm9LM-W2HA_en — agency

## Term

An agency here is Justin Rashidi's company Cedex, which he says he started 100% by accident after a tutoring company that grew from SAT students acing their tests. The word appears six times, beside clients, hiring, and company, and the interview is the story of that accidental agency — from tutoring high-earning professionals' children to a business he could not sustain alone. Rashidi's sharpest point about agencies is hiring: an agency forces you to hire much faster than ecom or SaaS companies, and he says he learned the most from that pressure. One concrete marker is the scale he reached, maybe a $2 million agency, and the burnout that came with it: he had put everything into it and felt he could not give any more.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a how-to for starting an agency: Rashidi stresses his start was accidental and unplanned, so the interview tells how one agency grew, not a replicable plan for starting one.
- Read as a claim that agencies always burn founders out: the burnout here is Rashidi's own at around $2 million, tied to hiring pressure in his agency — not a general rule about agency size.
