# G_yt_tQ1SB9_ctYE_en — Chat GPT

## Term

Chat GPT is the invisible thing Abraham says started taking his agency's work: a client realized they could cut costs by feeding it basic information, and he lost part of an account to it. The phrase appears four times, beside cost, account, and alarm bell, and his reaction is the story — he had heard about it but did not know much, and it rang an alarm bell for him. The same tool later powers his own work: a low-budget, high-impact AI campaign that gave a client five minutes of FaceTime with 40,000 users. One concrete contrast in the source is the old agency cocoon — creatives speaking the same language, never learning to deal with other people — against a world where a client can feed a tool and cut the agency out.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that ChatGPT replaces agencies: Abraham's story is one client cutting costs on one part of one account — and he answers with his own AI campaign, so the video shows adaptation, not replacement.
- Read as a story about cost alone: the five-minutes-of-FaceTime campaign is valued for the interaction itself, not the savings — the client walked away happy because users got real time with them, not because it was cheap.
