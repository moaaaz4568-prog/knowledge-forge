# G_yt_tZrEG2l1aAc_en — client flow

## Term

Client flow is the engine of a video editor's career, in this video's terms: how many clients you are getting, and the system that keeps them coming. The phrase appears ten times, beside perceived value, social proof, and portfolio, and the speaker explains it again because the last video introduced it — getting clients is hard, with tens of thousands of editors competing, and no client flow means no editing career at all. The other half of the lesson is perceived value: objective value is how good your editing is, and perceived value is how valuable it seems — and the speaker says editors better than him make less money because his perceived value is higher. One concrete fact about the speaker: three years in, nineteen years old, working with the largest creators in the world, and not working a normal job.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for getting clients once: client flow is the ongoing engine — the video's point is a system that keeps clients coming, not a single hire.
- Read as a claim that editing skill decides income: the video separates objective value from perceived value — better editors can earn less, and the speaker credits his branding, portfolio, and how he talks to people.
