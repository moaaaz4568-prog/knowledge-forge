# G_yt_ttDaQwL5QwA_en — lead magic

## Term

Lead Magic is a data vendor the speaker uses to validate cold email addresses before they are loaded into a campaign, and he presents it as one of the two best premium sources on the market, alongside the Wizard guys, while still refusing to trust any vendor's email without his own validation. The name appears ten times, beside private database, Apollo and catch-all, and the speaker's workflow runs every scraped address through Lead Magic first to learn whether it is valid, then checks his own back-end database, and only then sends the survivors to a second validator. His concrete example is a Google scrape of accountants across the United States: the Apollo emails go through Lead Magic, the ones it cannot find error out, and a risky result means the address has not been personally validated in the last 30 days. The point of naming the vendor is the contrast with the common failure he opens with: buying garbage data from Apollo or ZoomInfo and trusting it straight into the sequencer, which he says kills campaigns before they start.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an endorsement that Lead Magic data can be trusted as-is: the speaker says he does not trust any email from any vendor, including Lead Magic, and runs a second validation before any address enters a campaign.
- Read as the whole lead process: Lead Magic covers only the validation step, while scraping, the private database of almost 50 million leads and enrichment are separate parts of the system.
