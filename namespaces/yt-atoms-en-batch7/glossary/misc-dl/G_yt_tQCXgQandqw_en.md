# G_yt_tQCXgQandqw_en — Continuously Rasterize

## Term

Continuously Rasterize is the After Effects switch this tutorial says to enable so Quran pages can be zoomed freely without losing any quality. The phrase appears three times, beside shape layer, Tint, and 3D switch, and the rule is simple: create a solid, import the page, and enable the switch before zooming. The same switch comes up again later in the build — enable the 3D switch, pre-compose, enable 3D again, then enable Continuously Rasterize before the camera and null object. One concrete reason it matters is the source material: the pages come as SVG files from the King Fahd Complex, chosen because they zoom without losing quality, and the switch is what keeps that quality inside After Effects.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a quality fix for any footage: Continuously Rasterize here is for vector pages that must stay sharp while zooming — the video never claims it improves raster images or video.
- Read as a one-time setup: the switch has to be enabled again after pre-composing, so enabling it once at import does not cover the later 3D layers.
