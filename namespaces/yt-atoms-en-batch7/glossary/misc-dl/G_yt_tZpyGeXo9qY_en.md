# G_yt_tZpyGeXo9qY_en — compensatory drive

## Term

A compensatory drive is the video's name for dark motivation: something unmet in your childhood that you spend your adult life compensating for. The phrase appears four times, beside validation, power and control, and unmet childhood need, and the first example is validation — you post on social media, buy a flashy car or a diamond watch, because the need was never met. The speaker's own example is the second one: power and control, which he calls his personal compensatory drive vacuum. One concrete claim opens the video: dark motivation is the cheat code to getting rich, because most people try to motivate themselves with what they will buy when they get rich — and once they buy it, they get unmotivated and complacent.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a motivational trick: a compensatory drive is a cause, not a technique — the video traces the drive to an unmet childhood need, and the car or watch is the compensation, not the motivation itself.
- Read as a claim that all rich people are compensating: the video names validation and power as two drives, not a universal diagnosis — the speaker says power is his personal one, and the examples are his own and his friends'.
