# G_yt_tgsIx69XN3k_en — two questions

## Term

Two questions are the whole selling strategy in this video: understand them, use them when needed, use them intelligently, and you get projects consistently while keeping your image as a true expert. The phrase appears four times, beside first step, expert, and projects, and the speaker has used the strategy for the last three years to secure projects he loves with clients he enjoys working with — without advertising, marketing, or even updating his website. The audience is experts and service providers in the creative field: life coaches, entrepreneurship consultants, visual identity designers, writers, and anyone selling expertise. One concrete starting point: the first step is generating opportunities and getting people to contact you, and the two questions do the rest.
