# G_yt_tRspjsSJIqY_en — pay for performance

## Term

Pay for performance is the pricing model Eric Siu discusses for agencies: the agency gets paid for results, and his rule is to focus on one niche because the model is really just affiliate marketing. The phrase appears four times, beside value based pricing, niche, and affiliate marketing, and the episode frames it as a when-and-where question — when and where it makes sense for most agencies. The context is pricing as a lever: raising prices, value based pricing, or pay for performance are different ways to change what an agency earns. One concrete warning in the source is that the model only works with focus: a single niche you understand well, not a spread of unrelated services.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guaranteed income model: pay for performance pays for results, so a niche you do not understand well leaves you unpaid — the video presents it as a focus bet, not a safe fee.
- Read as a synonym for value based pricing: the source lists them as different options — value based pricing and pay for performance sit side by side, and Eric treats them as separate levers.
