# G_yt_tCJ-PRRUy8M_en — real time

## Term

Real time is how this B2B system works on Alliance's visitors: as people browse, the system identifies them and segments them across 14 websites instead of waiting for a batch process. The phrase appears six times, beside AI, Clay, and captured URL, and the speaker's whole demo is the live flow — 40,000 web visitors identified since April, enriched with job titles and the URLs they visited. The point of doing it live is priority: when a potential business customer is spotted, the sales team can contact them first rather than treating every visitor the same. One concrete mechanism is the captured URL field, which tells the system whether a visitor is viewing an event so the right campaign can follow.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as instant sales: real time here means identification and prioritization as visitors browse — the system still routes them to campaigns and the sales team still has to contact them, so no sale happens in the moment itself.
- Read as a claim about all 14 websites at once: the demo walks through one Alliance website in detail, and the 40,000-visitor figure covers the whole system — the video does not show all 14 sites segmented live.
