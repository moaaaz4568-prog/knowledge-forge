# G_yt_tb0xPS8I_VI_en — micro niche

## Term

A micro niche is the channel strategy in this video's $5,000-in-seven-days plan: pick a channel that solves one very specific problem for one specific avatar, and sell a high ticket product to that avatar. The phrase appears six times, beside specific problem, specific avatar, and high ticket, and the channel is deliberately not built for views — views are great, but the goal is five grand in seven days. The example is a personal trainer helping diabetic moms over 30 lose weight — that is how specific the problem has to be. One concrete proof offered is a hair health brand that implemented the micro niche channel and still got views while selling to its narrow audience.
