# G_yt_t9V3z0z_e7I_en — clipping mask

## Term

A clipping mask is the Illustrator step that finishes this funky fresh typography design: it clips the abstract shapes to the letterforms so the shapes show only inside the text. The phrase appears three times, beside abstract shapes, Pathfinder, and compound shape, and the speaker uses it twice in the build — once to set it up and once to choose it from the right-click menu. The setup is specific: bring the text to the front of all layers, convert it in the Pathfinder window, then select the relevant layers and choose a clipping mask. One concrete payoff he points to is that the mask effects stay applied while you adjust and move the shapes around, because the text is a compound shape.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a filter or effect: a clipping mask here is a container that hides everything outside the text shape, not a visual filter — the shapes themselves are unchanged, only what shows is limited.
- Read as a one-click finish: the mask only works after the text is brought to the front and converted in Pathfinder, so skipping the setup leaves the mask with nothing to clip to.
