# G_yt_tHO33ZR9fGw_en — clone

## Term

A clone here is an AI copy of yourself: the tool builds it from a profile that tells the AI who you are, fed with your social media, Google Drive, courses, and files. The word appears twice, beside profile, social media, and courses, and the pitch is that the clone is a better product and a better experience than a static course. The data sources are the point: your processes, philosophy, and methods are already on your social media, so the clone learns them from what you have published. One concrete use he gives is selling the clone as a standalone product, or adding it to current courses so students can talk to it instead of only reading.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a copy of your voice alone: the clone here is built from a profile plus your social media, Drive files, and courses — the source presents it as your knowledge and methods, not a voice imitation.
- Read as a finished product you can buy: the video is a build demo with an invitation to DM for details — it never names a price, a launch date, or a working link, so no purchase claim can be drawn from it.
