# G_yt_t2jyq9Ak4K4_en — mindset shift

## Term

A mindset shift is the change in attitude Alan credits with breaking his five-month stall at 1K a month: the idea that it is actually okay to make money with editing. The phrase appears twice, beside free stuff, the business side, and 6K, and both mentions frame the same turning point rather than two different shifts. Before it Alan did not know what to work on — approve videos or do outreach — and after it he reached around 5–6K in the next six months. One concrete marker of the shift is how he talks about the work afterwards: editing becomes the business he started in the first place, not a hobby he feels guilty charging for.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a technique for editing faster: the shift here is about permission to charge for editing, not a workflow or tool — nothing in the source ties it to speed or software.
- Read as the reason he reached 10K: the shift moved Alan from 1K to around 5–6K; the later climb to 10K also involved joining the program for structure, so the shift alone does not explain the full rise.
