## Term

Accent era is the style name for the logo this tutorial builds in Illustrator: a mountain scene with two peaks, snow shapes, and a star, finished as a mock-up in Photoshop. The phrase appears twice, beside logo design, triangle, and mock-up, and both mentions frame the same project — the opening promise and the closing sign-off.
