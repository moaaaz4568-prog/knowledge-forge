# G_yt_tCbDfRoTAv4_en — vortex design

## Term

A vortex design is the spiral vector graphic this tutorial builds in Illustrator: a swirling pattern made from a single repeated section, finished as a clean vector. The phrase appears three times, beside pattern, pen tool, and clipping mask, and the whole video is the build — from one section of the pattern to the finished vector. The method is specific: draw one straight line with the pen tool holding shift, turn it into a brush, and preview the shape until the spiral looks right. One concrete finish he shows is creating a clipping mask over the result to get the vector vortex design, then cleaning it up and adding shadow effects into the middle.
