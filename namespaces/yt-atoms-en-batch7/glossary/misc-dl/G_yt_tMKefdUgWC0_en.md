# G_yt_tMKefdUgWC0_en — modern icon

## Term

A modern icon is the Illustrator project of this tutorial: a clean symbol built from basic shapes, demonstrated with a paper plane but meant to generalize to many different icons. The phrase appears seven times, with the fuller modern icon design throughout, beside paper plane, Pathfinder, and shades of blue. The build starts with the polygon tool — six sides for a hexagon base, resized holding alt and shift — then rounds the corners through effects and round corners. One concrete detail is the coloring: shades of blue with a very slight transition between two tones, lighter at the top left, plus a triangle cut into the paper plane with the Pathfinder.

- Read as a paper-plane tutorial only: the plane is the example, not the point — the speaker says you do not have to make a paper plane and the same methods create many different icons.
- Read as a one-shape job: the icon is built from a hexagon base, rounded corners, a triangle cut, and a back triangle joined in Pathfinder — skipping any of those steps leaves a different shape.
