# G_yt_tiClTJoNyNE_en — rectangle tool

## Term

The rectangle tool is the Illustrator tool this geometric pattern tutorial is built on: press M for it, and the pattern starts with a square. The phrase appears three times, beside shift, artboard, and stroke, and the first move is the beige background — made with the rectangle tool and locked down with Command or Control 2. The key move is holding down shift while clicking and dragging, which makes a perfect square on the artboard, and the thickness of the square can be changed later. One concrete later use: grab the rectangle tool with M again and drag a rectangle around the perimeter of the box, then select everything and duplicate from the top.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a drawing tool only: the rectangle tool here is a pattern tool — the tutorial builds a geometric pattern from squares, and the same tool makes the background, the square, and the perimeter box.
- Read as a claim that the square must be exact first time: the speaker says the thickness is entirely up to you and can be changed later — the perfect square comes from holding shift, not from getting the size right at once.
