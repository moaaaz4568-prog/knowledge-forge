# G_yt_tBHlge6mzyU_en — content strategy

## Term

A content strategy is the seven-step plan Dave Gela lays out for going full-time as a creator within 12 months: find your niche, know your target market, and build from a single worksheet. The phrase appears ten times, beside target market, unfair advantages, and value proposition, and Gela says he studied it inside and out for three years before packaging it. His unlock is that the strategy solved his own stuck problem — discovering his channel's true purpose — and he claims it works for every platform. One concrete step in the source is finding your niche by listing interests, strengths, unfair advantages, target market, edge, and value proposition, with his own target market being his younger self.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee of going full-time in a year: Gela's claim is that sticking to the steps and staying consistent can get you there — a conditional promise about his process, not a measured outcome for his viewers.
- Read as a substitute for making content: the strategy is a plan for what to make and for whom — Gela's own story is that it solved his channel's purpose problem, not that planning alone grows a channel without publishing.
