# G_yt_tbnJa0mucCY_en — coaching program

## Term

A coaching program is one of the income sources Jace says took him to $10,000 a month in 90 days — by accident. The phrase appears twice, with the plural coaching programs twice, beside online business, influencers, and six figures, and the story is that he moved into a coaching model helping influencers scale their programs, then landed one client he chased for over six months. That program scaled to big numbers — six figures a month — and they kept scaling it. One concrete detail is the price: he tells a customer he will take five thousand to launch their coaching program, with recruitment closing on August 15th.
