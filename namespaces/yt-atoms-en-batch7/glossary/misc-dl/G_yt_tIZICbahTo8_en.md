# G_yt_tIZICbahTo8_en — get rich fast

## Term

Get rich fast is the speaker's stated goal for this video, set against getting rich doing what you like: first build capital quickly, then do what you like doing. The phrase appears twice, beside CPM, seven figures, and Facebook ad agency, and the whole argument is the sequence — money first, passion second. His route is a growing market with few advertisers, like TikTok, where the cost to reach a thousand people is lower, and that cheap CPM is how you would get rich fast. One concrete next step he gives is to put a few six figures in the bank, then build the agency, hire people, and build infrastructure that makes success hard to avoid.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to chase any fast money: the route here is specific — a growing market with low CPM, like TikTok — not any scheme, and the speaker's own example is building a Facebook ad agency after the capital is banked.
- Read as a claim that passion does not matter: the video postpones doing what you like until after the capital is built — it never says the work you like is worthless, only that it comes second.
