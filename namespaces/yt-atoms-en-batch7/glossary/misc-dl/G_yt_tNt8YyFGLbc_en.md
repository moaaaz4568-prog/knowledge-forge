# G_yt_tNt8YyFGLbc_en — puppet warp tool

## Term

The puppet warp tool is the newest tool addition to Adobe Illustrator CC 2018 in this video: it bends vector artwork by placing pins on it and dragging them. The phrase appears five times, beside pins, polygons, and digital typography, and the speaker says it is great for digital illustrations as well as digital typography. The method is hands-on: place pins on the artwork, hold and click a pin, and move the cursor to rotate the design around that point. One concrete payoff is that adding multiple pins lets you manipulate a vector design in many different ways, which the speaker calls a very powerful tool indeed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a warp preset: the puppet warp tool does nothing until you place pins — the pins are the control, and without them there is no bend to apply.
- Read as a photo tool: the video presents it for vector artwork in Illustrator — digital illustration and typography — not for retouching photographs.
