# G_yt_tFHqaOOIXJ0_en — blending mode

## Term

A blending mode is the Photoshop setting this tips video demonstrates in four uses: it changes how a layer's pixels mix with the layers below it. The phrase appears twelve times, with the plural blending modes five times, beside overlay, layer, and Photoshop, and the video is a tour of four dope uses rather than a definition. The first use is hand lettering from Illustrator: choose overlay, keep the text white, and put it over a somewhat dark image so the light shines through the text. One concrete tip he repeats is to experiment — try other blending options in the same category, and adjust to whatever looks best, rather than treating overlay as the only answer.
