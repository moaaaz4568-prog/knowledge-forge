# G_yt_td_-2d8M5bM_en — accidental agency owner

## Term

An accidental agency owner is someone who never planned to run an agency and ended up doing it anyway — the interviewer's opening question to Sarah Seagull, and her answer is 100% yes. The phrase appears once, beside CEO and founder, broadcast journalist, and PR, and the backstory is a career change: she was a broadcast journalist in New York City and then a TV reporter in San Francisco, until a horrific story she covered for a good month made her realize she could not desensitize herself to it anymore. With small children at home, she switched gears into PR, worked at a number of different firms, and became CEO and founder of Seagull Communications. One concrete fact about the agency: it is a San Francisco headquartered lifestyle communications agency with a satellite office in New York City, focused on retail, hospitality, events, and food.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a failure to plan: accidental here is a career path, not a lack of competence — Seagull ran a San Francisco agency with a New York office after years as a journalist, and the accident is how she arrived, not how she works.
- Read as a claim that anyone can stumble into an agency: the backstory is specific — a TV reporter who hit a wall on a horrific story and moved into PR first — and the video never says the agency itself was an accident.
