# G_yt_t2G1N1pS_dY_en — pain points

## Term

Pain points are the prospect's frustrations that Jaiden Lee Lua's whole cold-email framework is built to find first: the video opens by insisting copy must address the potential customer's problem or it will not convert. The phrase appears twelve times, with the singular pain point seven times, beside demand mining, Reddit, and Hacker News. Lua's step one is demand mining on Reddit — feeding Claude a prompt that returns keywords, pain point phrases, subreddits, and lead magnet signal phrases — and step two defines the problem to solve from that research. One concrete case makes it tangible: for HVAC workers the mined pains include losing a bid to the cheapest offer and budgeting a compressor replacement, and the cheapest-option complaint is the one people actually repeat.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of complaints to quote back: Lua mines pain points to define the problem the copy will solve, not to paste the complaint itself into the email — the mined phrase becomes copy only after Claude links it to the offer.
- Read as proof that the cheapest option always wins: the HVAC example treats losing to the cheapest offer as one pain among several, and Lua picks the most common pains, not a universal rule about price.
