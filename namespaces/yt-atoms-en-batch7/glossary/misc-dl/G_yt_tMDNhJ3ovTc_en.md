# G_yt_tMDNhJ3ovTc_en — personal brand

## Term

A personal brand is what Tom Noski built by posting 1,350 pieces of content on Instagram over three years and eight months, reaching 416,000 followers there: an audience for the things you say and want to do. The phrase appears four times, beside content creator, avatar, and online business, and the video lists the five biggest mistakes that keep creators from building the brand they want. Mistake number one is targeting a niche instead of targeting an avatar — film making or personal finance are things, not niches, and orienting your brand around one confines it to a tiny box. One concrete alternative he gives is to let personal finance be one aspect of your brand if you are an expert at it, rather than attaching your entire brand to it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a niche label: Noski's point is that a niche is a thing like film making or personal finance, while a brand is built around an avatar — confusing the two is mistake number one, not a naming choice.
- Read as a follower-count goal: the 416,000 followers are presented as the result of three years of daily posting, not the brand itself — the video's subject is the mistakes that block the brand, not the count.
