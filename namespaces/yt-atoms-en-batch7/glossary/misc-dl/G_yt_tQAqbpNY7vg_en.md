# G_yt_tQAqbpNY7vg_en — culture

## Term

Culture is the concept Marcus Collins says marketers talk about abstractly without knowing what it means: his book tries to give it concreteness — a way to talk about it, apply it, and codify it, with case studies. The word appears thirty-nine times, beside brands, meanings, and company, and his test is simple: ask 100 people what the culture of their company is and watch them fold up like origami. The key idea is closeness: you have to be close to the culture to understand its meanings, even when everyone speaks the same language. One concrete frame he gives is brands as vessels of meaning — identifiable signifiers that conjure thoughts and feelings to make people more likely to move.
