# G_yt_tsGdLrdaJ2E_en — conversation workflow

## Term

A conversation workflow is the speaker's four-step way of turning a stranger into a booked sales call without pitching first: create context and familiarity, ask questions that surface inefficiencies in the prospect's business, educate and create curiosity, then offer a walk-through session. The phrase appears five times, beside four-step conversation workflow, Reptilian Brain and walk-through session, and the speaker presents it as the second of three things his successful clients do, after creating long-form content that builds belief and before adding conversion mechanisms. His concrete proof is the result he reports from it: 7,000 appointments generated in the last 10 months and almost four million dollars in the last 12 months, all from this exact workflow rather than from knocking on doors with an immediate pitch. The image he uses for the wrong way is a doctor who knocks on doors prescribing medicine without diagnosing anything, which is how he describes agencies that pitch short-form content in the first message.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a script for the first message: the workflow starts by creating context and familiarity and only offers a walk-through at step four, so a pitch sent in the first message is the opposite of what the speaker teaches.
- Read as a guarantee of appointments: the speaker reports 7,000 appointments from his own use of the workflow, not a promise that the same steps produce the same numbers for any agency.
