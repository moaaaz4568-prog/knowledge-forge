## Term

The phrase appears six times, beside boomers, assets, and technology, and the point is hope for the young — the video is a hybrid of debunking Doomer content and synthesizing a bright future for millennials and Gen Z in the decades ahead. The numbers keep moving: in 10 years you could have a life expectancy increased by 15 years, and in 15 years by 30 years, as technology lets people live longer. One concrete framing: the series talked to Chris about China and California first, and this third part brings it home to the USA as a whole — the question is the economic future of the United States, and the answer is short-term pain for something worth having.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fixed number: life expectancy here is a moving target — the video's point is that the number keeps rising with technology, so your generation should not expect the current figure to hold.
- Read as a claim that everyone lives to 88: the 88 is for boomers with real assets — the video splits the generations by wealth, and the average is 78, so the number depends on who you are talking about.
