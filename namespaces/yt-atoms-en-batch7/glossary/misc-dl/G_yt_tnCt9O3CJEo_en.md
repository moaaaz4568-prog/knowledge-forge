# G_yt_tnCt9O3CJEo_en — bite-sized agency briefs

## Term

Bite-sized agency briefs is a webinar series that packs a ton of important agency information on one topic from one expert into a 25-minute brief. The phrase appears three times, beside agency owners, expert, and episode 40, and the length is the point: who has the attention span for much more these days, and you can squeeze in a listen between meetings with time for a bathroom break or coffee refill. The host is Steve Gooberman from Agency Outside, where he coaches agency owners to build the agency of their dreams. One concrete episode: number 40, with Tracy Schubert Barrett, president and founder of Navigate the Journey and author of What If There's More — talking about launching a billion dollar media empire and the initial launch of HGTV.
