# G_yt_tvYDYtQhreo_en — word mark

## Term

A word mark is a clean custom typographic logo — the brand name set in type, with no symbol attached — and this video treats it as one piece of the logo suite inside the visual identity, alongside the icon, the color palette and the typography selections. The phrase appears thirty times, beside logo suite, brand presentation and clear space, and the speaker's deliverables make the word mark the first thing a client sees in the visual identity, before the brand assets and the presentation that explains every design decision. His concrete example is a haircare brand with four hairlines and four curls: the word mark gets a stacked variation with the second word underneath, the letters aligned to the same width by converting them to outlines, and a clear space measured from the width of the letter N. The rules he teaches for it are strict: never change its composition, add a stroke, rotate it, use a color outside the palette, outline it, add a drop shadow or use multiple colors at once.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole logo: the word mark is only the typographic part, and the speaker still has to complete the logo with an icon and the rest of the logo suite before the identity is done.
- Read as a font choice: the word mark is a custom typographic logo built from a chosen font like HF Jesco, but the font alone is not the word mark — the aligned, outlined lettering and its clear space are part of it.
