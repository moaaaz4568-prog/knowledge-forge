# G_yt_ta9kd7mlmqQ_en — mindset shift

## Term

A mindset shift is the single change that turned this creator's channel around: stop creating for yourself and start creating for your viewers. The phrase appears three times, beside mirror, stage, and reflection, and the idea is that YouTube is not a stage where you perform — it is a mirror where viewers look for content that reflects them, their interests, their problems, their aspirations. The speaker failed at YouTube until he understood this: he used to make videos he loved that nobody watched, barely reaching 200 views. One concrete result after the shift: his channel grew over 2,000 subscribers in 3 weeks, and his average view count grew by 10 times.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to get views: the shift is about who the video serves — viewers watch for themselves, not for the creator — and the views follow the service, not the other way round.
- Read as a claim that the creator does not matter: the speaker says viewers stay for who you are once you have earned their attention — the shift changes what you create for, not who you are.
