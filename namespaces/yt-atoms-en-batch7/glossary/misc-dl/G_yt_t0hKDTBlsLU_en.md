# G_yt_t0hKDTBlsLU_en — cold email

## Term

Cold email here means outbound mail sent to strangers to win business, not spam blasted at scale: Adam Robinson credits it with 42% of RB2B's revenue, 1.69 million ARR, so the term carries a revenue claim in this source. The phrase appears four times, with the plural cold emails twice, beside deliverability, infrastructure, and warm-ups. Robinson's use is operational — Taylor Hearn built the whole apparatus from scratch, domains, inboxes, warm-ups, and three-layer rotation systems, and wrote the copy in Robinson's voice. One concrete case shows the stakes: a typo in 34 domains burnt the infrastructure in a day, and the backup system saved the run.

## Synonyms

- cold emails (the plural form used in this source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for spam blasting: Robinson stresses the work was not just blasting — infrastructure, warm-ups, and copy in his voice — so treating cold email here as bulk spam misses the point of the testimonial.
- Read as a guarantee of the same 42% revenue share: the figure belongs to RB2B's run with Taylor Hearn from March to October 2024, not a promise that any cold email program returns the same share.
