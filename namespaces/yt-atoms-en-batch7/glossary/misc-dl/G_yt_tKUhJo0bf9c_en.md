# G_yt_tKUhJo0bf9c_en — vision

## Term

Vision is the first thing this video says to establish before launching a product: what the goal is, what you hope to achieve, and why the product matters beyond profit. The word appears ten times, beside design process, vision statement, and team, and the speaker calls it the step he cannot stress enough — without it, even the best team and designers do not matter. The vision has to be shared among everybody on the team, and it drives the details: a product designed to feel luxurious and comfortable, with plywood that bends to cradle the body, is judged against the vision statement. One concrete timeline in the source shows the stakes: discovery takes a month, the design process runs about 13 months to distribution, and the vision is what keeps that long process on track.

## Synonyms

- vision statement (the fuller form used in this source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a slogan on a slide: the vision here is a working test for every decision — the plywood, the comfort, the luxury feel are all judged against it — not a sentence written once and forgotten.
- Read as the designer's private idea: the video insists the vision must be shared among everybody on the team, so a vision only the designer holds fails the video's own standard.
