# G_yt_tf_H7B-reT8_en — positioning

## Term

Positioning is creating contrast — what makes you different — in this video's definition, and it matters because prospects ask it directly. The word appears four times, beside contrast, different, and better, and the story that proves it is a call a few years ago, where somebody asked somebody else on the call what the one reason is to do business with you and not somebody else — and he had no answer. The speaker says the same question tripped him up too, and after a lot of work with clients over the past two or three years, he arrived at something fairly useful. One concrete trap he names: answering what makes you different with I'm better, I care more, better, more, less — those are hard for people to see value in.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for being better: positioning is contrast, not superiority — answering I'm better fails, because better is hard for people to see value in, while a difference is something they can see.
- Read as a slogan you invent once: the video's positioning came from years of client work — it is an answer to the prospect's real question, not a line you write for yourself.
