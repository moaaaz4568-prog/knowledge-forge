# G_yt_tdlazTvFQws_en — back end

## Term

The back end is where the real money sits in this video's lead strategy: the upsells that follow a cheap first sale, sold to people who already bought from you. The phrase appears seven times, beside front end, leads, and high ticket, and the speaker's own number is $920,000 created in the back end from something sold cheap for $250 or less. The point is that leads do not need to come through the front end or be cold — good coaching businesses make most of their money from the back end because upsells are easier to sell and need less convincing. One concrete tactic: facilitate ways to get leads through the back end, because the leads you can get from the front end are already exhausted.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a synonym for the whole funnel: the back end is only the part after the first sale — the front end is where leads enter, and the video's point is that the money concentrates after, not that the front end disappears.
- Read as a claim that cheap products lose money: the $250 sale is the entry — it created $920,000 in the back end, so the cheap price is the door, and the upsell is the room behind it.
