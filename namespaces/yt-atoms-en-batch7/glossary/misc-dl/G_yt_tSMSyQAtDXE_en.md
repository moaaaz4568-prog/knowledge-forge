# G_yt_tSMSyQAtDXE_en — lifestyle business

## Term

A lifestyle business is one of the two paths the speaker lays out at $10,000 a month: keep the agency small and live off it, or build a business that grows beyond you. The phrase appears four times, beside build a business, equity, and 10 years, and the point is that the two are different things — getting to $10,000 a month is not super hard, and the real decision comes after. The speakers' own horizon is the opposite pole: they do not get excited about short-term money, and what excites them is where they could be ten years from now. One concrete image of the lifestyle pole is a youngster the speaker describes as living it, pictured sitting with the sea behind him.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a failure to grow: the video treats the lifestyle business as a legitimate choice — the decision is which of two different things you want, not a ranking where building always wins.
- Read as the speakers' own goal: they say short-term money does not excite them and their horizon is ten years out — the lifestyle business is the path they describe, not the one they chose.
