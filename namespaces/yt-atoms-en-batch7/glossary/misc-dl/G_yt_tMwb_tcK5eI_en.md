# G_yt_tMwb_tcK5eI_en — culture

## Term

Culture here is the agency's shared mission, vision, and values — the thing Ann Green says agencies neglect in themselves while selling it to clients. The word appears seven times, beside client service, growth oriented, and Mission Vision Values, and her observation is the shoemaker's-kids pattern: agencies do not revisit their own culture for five, six, seven, or ten years. Green's background is the credential for the point: 30 years in integrated marketing communications, president and CEO of an award-winning agency she helped build for more than two decades before it was acquired by GNS in 2018. One concrete practice she names is revisiting the culture itself — the mission, vision, and values — rather than letting it sit untouched while the agency sells the same work to clients.
