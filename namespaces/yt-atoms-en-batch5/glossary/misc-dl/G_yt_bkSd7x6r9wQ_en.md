# G_yt_bkSd7x6r9wQ_en — release valve

## Term

Sports team fandom is a constructive — or at least distracting — outlet for tribalism, the speaker says: a release valve for the tribalistic energy in society, because without sports that effort might go into ethnic rivalries. Release valve appears once, as the function of fandom: it lets the tribal energy out somewhere harmless. The same talk weighs the economics — expensive stadiums, the revenue of games — against the other power of subsidizing sports: its power as a social signal, and its ability to get a message out covertly, more effectively than direct marketing, which is why the military and the national anthem are part of it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as sports causing tribalism: the speaker says fandom releases tribalistic energy that already exists.
- Read as the stadium math being the argument: the revenue numbers are the setup; the release valve and the social signal are the point.
