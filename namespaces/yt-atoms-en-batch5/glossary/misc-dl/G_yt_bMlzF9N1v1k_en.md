# G_yt_bMlzF9N1v1k_en — global color

## Term

Select a global color option and press OK, the speaker says, and you will notice a white symbol in the bottom right corner of the box — that tells you it is now in fact a global color. Global color appears three times as the Illustrator setting that links a swatch across the artwork: duplicate the pink circle and the colour follows. The same lesson moves through grouped objects, envelope warp for editing objects and type at the same time, and vector illustration — and ends with a pro tip to start saving your work on a regular basis, at least once every few minutes.
