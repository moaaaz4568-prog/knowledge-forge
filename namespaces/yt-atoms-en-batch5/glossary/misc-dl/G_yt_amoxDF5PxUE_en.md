# G_yt_amoxDF5PxUE_en — values

## Term

Values here are the consultant's working material on social media: understand your values well, explain them to people who share them, and you will naturally build a relationship with them. The word appears six times, beside understand, explain, share, and relationship, in an answer to how a consultant, trainer, or mentor wins online. The mechanism has a second half — a common enemy: people who hate the same enemy turn to you because of that similarity. Winning, the speaker is careful to say, means customers, not fame; fame is a content creator's topic, and this video is about the other one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a list of nice words: the values here are meant to be stated plainly enough that strangers who share them recognise themselves and come closer.
- Read as targeting everyone: the speaker's values work by selecting — they build a relationship only with people who already share them, and the shared enemy does the same filtering.
