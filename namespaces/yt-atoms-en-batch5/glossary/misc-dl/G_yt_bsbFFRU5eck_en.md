# G_yt_bsbFFRU5eck_en — call-to-action button

## Term

The color here is chosen to fall in line with the design's message but used less, as the base color — white, after all, is still a color choice. Call-to-action button appears twice as the point of the whole layout: a small orange circle ushers the eye over to the right, and the designer wants the call-to-action button to be clicked, found below the typography layout in blue. The same eye reads the heading in white as intrigue, one line of text in white as contrast — every color choice serves the button.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the blue being decoration: it is the color of the button the whole layout is built to get clicked.
- Read as white being the absence of color: the speaker insists white is, after all, still a color choice.
