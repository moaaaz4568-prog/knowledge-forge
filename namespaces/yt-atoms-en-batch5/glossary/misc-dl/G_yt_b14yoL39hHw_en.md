# G_yt_b14yoL39hHw_en — sales letters

## Term

Sales letters are one of the two things the speaker says sales teams need to learn to build, beside great sales assets, before offering anything to everybody. The phrase appears once, beside sales teams, build, and great sales assets, at the end of a tour video that drifts from bedrooms and fridges into sales advice. The speaker's order is strict: learn to build the assets and the letters first, then offer it to everybody. The same stretch names the distraction to avoid — watching other people on yachts on Stripe, Discord, Slack, and Instagram while not being present with them.
