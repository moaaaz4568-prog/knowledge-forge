# G_yt_chdKuYME09o_en — empty-handed

## Term

Empty-handed is the state the speaker warns against when meeting senior people, and his rule is simple: never show up without something to give. Empty-handed appears once, next to Tik Tok, social media and algorithms, and the speaker's context is investors far more senior than him who knew little about the space he worked in. His example is the information itself: an article about how a competitor uses TikTok Shop, or a contact, or a relationship that adds value to the other person's life. The gift does not have to be physical, and the point is that the meeting starts with value flowing toward the other person.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as bringing a present: empty-handed here means arriving with nothing to offer, and the offering is information or a connection.
- Read as flattery: the speaker's rule is to add real value to the other person's world, not to impress them with praise.
