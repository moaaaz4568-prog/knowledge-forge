# G_yt_biZEA2jkBNo_en — sink your ship

## Term

The source is short — two atoms — and its advice is blunt: if the business owner isn't serious about the business, don't work with them, because they'll sink your ship. Sink your ship appears once, as the consequence of taking on an unserious owner. The other half of the advice is the way out of the marketing role: you can create the business for them if you leave marketing and become a business and sales person. The ship is the business; the sinking is what an unserious owner does to it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If the business owner isn't serious about the business, don't work with them because they'll sink your ship." — sources/S_yt_biZEA2jkBNo_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never take clients: the warning is about owners who aren't serious, not about clients in general.
- Read as business and sales person being a job title: it is the role you step into when you stop being only the marketer.
