# G_yt_cNzJkkfIhYw_en — positioning

## Term

Positioning in this source is not a branding exercise but a decision about where the speaker is leading his followers, and he states it as standing behind him while he takes the wheel and they go. Positioning appears three times, next to northstar, thesis and the future, and the speaker contrasts it with simply giving value. His own example is the Lexing Cash way: create twenty thousand a month and then twenty thousand a week, without sales calls, and that clarity is what makes marketing easier for him. When he says positioning was just non-existent, he means the offer never needed a separate position because the destination itself did the work.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I had a northstar, right? I had I didn't have to think of what do you want this offer to be like? What's my positioning? Positioning was just non-existent." — sources/S_yt_cNzJkkfIhYw_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a logo or a tagline: positioning here is the direction the speaker leads people toward, not a visual identity.
- Read as a fixed market slot: the speaker's positioning moves with his stated destination, so copying the words without the destination misses it.
