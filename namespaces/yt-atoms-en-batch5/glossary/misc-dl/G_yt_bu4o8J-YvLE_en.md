# G_yt_bu4o8J-YvLE_en — hill that you would die on

## Term

When a new member joins, the speaker takes them through a process that starts with their biggest issue — and warns that solving it will create other issues, because it is an up and down business. Hill that you would die on appears once, as the question that ends the process: of the top two or three things you want your company to stand for, if all of them had to die but one, what is the hill that you would die on? The answer has to survive the patterns the speaker keeps identifying, the crossroad of a path already invested in, and the temptation to cheat by handing over the ultimate answer.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So uh I always take people through this process where I come down and I say of these top two or three things you want your company to stand for, if all of them had to die but one, what is the hill that you would die on?" — sources/S_yt_bu4o8J-YvLE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a literal hill: it is the one value that survives when every other thing the company stands for has to die.
- Read as the biggest issue: the issue is the starting point; the hill is the value chosen after the process.
