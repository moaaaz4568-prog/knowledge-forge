# G_yt_arIqRz3D110_en — mentorship

## Term

Mentorship here is what made the speaker's entire career: he says it was actually made by mentors and mentorship and listening to people smarter than him. The word appears once, beside mentors, listening, and smarter than me, in a 260-episode interview with a PR veteran. It is presented as a practice, not a tribute — anytime he can listen to someone with better ideas, or who looks at things differently, he takes it. The same career produced an infomercial division that grew very profitable, and the same speaker warns that when a company's cash register is not ringing, the communications people are among the first cuts.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I'm, you know, I'm 260 or whatever episodes in, I'm always still surprised when somebody tells me that they listen to it. >> I think it's my entire career was actually made by mentors and mentorship and listening to people that are smarter than me." — sources/S_yt_arIqRz3D110_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as having had a mentor once: mentorship here is described as the ongoing habit of listening to smarter people, repeated across a whole career.
- Read as formal training: nothing in the source describes a programme or a course — the mentorship is listening and learning from people in the room.
