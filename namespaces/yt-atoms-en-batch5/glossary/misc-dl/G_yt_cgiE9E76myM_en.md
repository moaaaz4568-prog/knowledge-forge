# G_yt_cgiE9E76myM_en — ICP

## Term

ICP is the speaker's shorthand for the ideal customer profile, and the video insists it must be clarified before any content is made. ICP appears twelve times, next to organic views, back end and IG stories, and the speaker warns that brands without it make content that gets no views and wonder why. His example is the bio: specific keywords must call out the specific ICP, so the Instagram page works as a landing page for potential customers. Content that does not relate to the ICP or to what is being offered brings visitors who do not want to buy anything.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a target audience in general: an ICP here is a defined person with a demographic and a problem, not everyone who might watch.
- Read as a content step: the speaker puts the ICP before the content, so posting first and defining later is the mistake itself.
