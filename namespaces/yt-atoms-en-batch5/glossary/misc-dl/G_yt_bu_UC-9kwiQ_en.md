# G_yt_bu_UC-9kwiQ_en — negative space

## Term

The video walks through what is wrong with a business card design, and the speaker gives clues until the flaw shows itself: the lines of the logo symbol are too close together and too thin, and the whole thing is slightly too large. Negative space appears once, as the missing ingredient — there is not enough of it, so the design is cramped, and making the elements a bit smaller gives them more room to breathe around the edges and against the title. The same review treats size and spacing as the design itself: interest and intrigue come from the arrangement, and information only lands when it is displayed in importance.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the only flaw: the video finds more than one thing wrong, including the lines of the logo symbol.
