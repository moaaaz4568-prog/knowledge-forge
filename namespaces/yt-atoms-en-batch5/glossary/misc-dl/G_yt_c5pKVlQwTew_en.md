# G_yt_c5pKVlQwTew_en — influencer marketing

## Term

The speaker founded BCE at twenty-one, just before the lockdown, after starting to blog at sixteen or seventeen and posting for brands like a typical influencer — and at twenty-one she started doing it for other brands, seeing a niche. Influencer marketing appears three times as the reason a brand became what it is: he explained that one of the reasons The Iconic became the brand it is today is because of influencer marketing. The same talk builds the practice around it — a really cool PR strategy when a brand has no budget, especially during a launch, managing TikTok accounts, chatting on camera, doing pranks, and delegating the little things so the team of ten can collaborate.
