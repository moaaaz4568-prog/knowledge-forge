# G_yt_bjcSjk-ALbc_en — deliverability

## Term

Gmail updates were a big pain for the speaker, because every one of them broke something — and deliverability appears twenty-three times as the thing at stake. Turn open tracking off, he advises, since Gmail made it so tracking open rates flags you; send a few follow-ups, spaced a few days out, no more than four, with a relevant offer to the right person. When deliverability drops and leads fall, the two-step process is to find the problem domains and pause them — because a spammer might get a new email and try to send hundreds of thousands of emails in the first month, and taking the same behaviors gets the algorithm to catch you.
