# G_yt_co0Tjw08dVw_en — niche targeting

## Term

Niche targeting is what this entire video is about, and the speaker calls it the hardest thing on the planet even though getting views is easy. Niche targeting appears twelve times, next to targeting, algorithm and intentional views, and the speaker defines it as getting the right impressions from the people who will actually purchase. His example is the value product going viral: without niche targeting the views are the wrong people, and with it the same video makes hundreds of thousands of dollars. Targeting on its own is the speaker's shorthand for the same discipline when the niche part is already understood.
