# G_yt_b6JzNMRJyoo_en — coach

## Term

The speaker, a sales leader, lists three jobs for a leader and names coaching as the second: train people to be good, confident and effective, then coach them to perform under pressure. Coach appears six times, and the model he points to is the sideline coach — Belichick, Saban — walking up and down, recruiting good talent, putting players into the system, watching film, developing them into big-time players. Coaching here is not a pep talk; it is the daily work of making producers better after the training, under real pressure.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the same job as train: the speaker separates them — training builds skills and knowledge, coaching carries people through performance under pressure.
- Read as a sports metaphor only: the coaches named are the template for how a sales leader works with producers.
