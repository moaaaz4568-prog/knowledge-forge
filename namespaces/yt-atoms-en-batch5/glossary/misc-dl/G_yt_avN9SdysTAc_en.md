# G_yt_avN9SdysTAc_en — sales funnels

## Term

Sales funnels here are the three every coach should use if they want to build an audience, fill a webinar, and get clients. The phrase appears four times, with the singular sales funnel three more times, beside three, every coach, and build an audience, in a quick lesson on Facebook advertising. The funnels run on audience and targeting — the speaker calls that one of the main things to think about for successful Facebook ads. The automation is the point: people click the ad, reach the registration page, and find a webinar starting the next hour, and the speaker says that very often leaves him inundated with 10 to 20 or 30 sales call requests a day.

## Synonyms

- sales funnel (singular form used in the source).

## Live Example

> "And in this quick query, Takuki, I want to talk to you about the three sales funnels that every coach should use if you want to, uh, if you want to build an audience, if you want to fill a webinar, if you want to get clients." — sources/S_yt_avN9SdysTAc_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as one funnel: the speaker teaches three funnels with three different jobs — audience, webinar, clients — not a single path.
- Read as a manual process: the funnels are automated from ad click to registration to an hourly webinar, and the volume of calls is the result of that automation.
