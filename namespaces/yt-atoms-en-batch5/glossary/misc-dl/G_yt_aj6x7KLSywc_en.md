# G_yt_aj6x7KLSywc_en — positioning

## Term

Positioning here is the brand's clarified place — the point in a seven-day build when the research phase can be crossed out and design can start. The word appears twice, beside brand values, mission, vision, and tone, in a client job that ends with presenting the result in person. It is treated as the decision the research produces: once the positioning is clear, sketches force the choice of one specific idea. The speaker's warning shows what positioning protects: without proper instructions on how to use the brand, the work will be completely defaced within a month or two.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "So, I would say that the overall positioning is becoming much clearer, which means that we can officially cross out the research phase, as we now have everything we need to start the design." — sources/S_yt_aj6x7KLSywc_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the visual layout of the brand: positioning here is the strategic decision about what the brand stands for, made before any design work, not where elements sit on a page.
- Read as a finished deliverable: the positioning only becomes clear through the research phase, and the speaker crosses that phase out only when the picture is complete enough to design from.
