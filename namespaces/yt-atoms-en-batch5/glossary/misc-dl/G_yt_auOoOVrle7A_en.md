# G_yt_auOoOVrle7A_en — personal assistant

## Term

A personal assistant here is the hire Jay made when the coaching work became a grind: there were times when he was really burnt out, so he wanted to hire one, and went through a comprehensive training with Jeresa and Kenisha. The phrase appears once, beside burnt out, hire, and comprehensive training, in a Coffee with Coaches interview with an e-commerce master turned coach. The assistant is presented as the answer to burnout, not to growth — the scaling question comes separately, as three super tactical strategies for a thousand customers at two-plus thousand each. Jay's own frame for the whole conversation is staying grounded: being in the circle brings him down to earth when he asks when he last did something fun.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And there were times when I was really burnt out, so I wanted to hire a personal assistant , and I went through a comprehensive training with Jeresa, Kenisha." — sources/S_yt_auOoOVrle7A_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a luxury hire: the assistant here is hired because the work had become a grind and Jay was burnt out, not as a status symbol.
- Read as the scaling strategy: the assistant solved the burnout; the scaling is a separate question about tactics and tools, answered apart from the hire.
