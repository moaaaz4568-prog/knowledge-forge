# G_yt_c0hd54NOADE_en — graphic designer

## Term

When it comes to AI, the speaker feels the developers really do not care about the possible future where jobs are replaced with AI — and his example is his own trade. Graphic designer appears twice, as the role he measures against the entrepreneur: in this day and age, he says, it pays to be more of an entrepreneur than a graphic designer, though someone who uses graphic design skills to earn different kinds of money has different revenue streams. The same talk widens the gap: even if anyone could pick up the tools, it would only make the gap between low tier clients and higher tier clients even larger.

## Synonyms

- graphic designers

## Live Example

> "In my opinion, in this day and age, it pays to be more of like an entrepreneur than a graphic designer." — sources/S_yt_c0hd54NOADE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the job disappearing: the speaker's point is the gap between client tiers widening, not a date for replacement.
- Read as designer versus entrepreneur being a rivalry: he is comparing revenue streams, and the designer can hold several.
