# G_yt_bXkV4dG7xiA_en — design decisions

## Term

Only this distinction matters, the speaker says, when we discuss which design decisions were hers and which were not. Design decisions appears once, as the line between the artist's work and the company's: she wanted to share it online partly because she thought the company that hired the artist deserved recognition. The same talk draws the ethical edge — if you believe a product is detrimental to creative workers, helping to brand it may be crossing a line for you.
