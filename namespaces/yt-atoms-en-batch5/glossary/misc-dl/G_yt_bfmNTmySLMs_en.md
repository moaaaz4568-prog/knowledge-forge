# G_yt_bfmNTmySLMs_en — strategy session

## Term

If you're using a two-call model — triage and strategy session — the next step is to get them to book a second call. Strategy session appears twice as the second call in the model: the triage qualifies, the strategy session closes, and the speaker's document for the first sale carries a deadline to scale it up in a few weeks. The same talk is about creating demand — a sale a day or more — and the thing that makes the difference in closing the deal is always at the beginning of the process.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single call: the model is two calls, triage first, strategy session second.
- Read as the second call being the close itself: the speaker says the difference is made at the beginning of the process.
