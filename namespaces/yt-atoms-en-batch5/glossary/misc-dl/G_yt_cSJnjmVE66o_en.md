# G_yt_cSJnjmVE66o_en — sharpness

## Term

Sharpness is the image setting the speaker says ninety nine percent of people ignore when making AI avatar videos, and his whole lesson is to turn it down. Sharpness appears four times, next to Higgs Field, HitFilm and iPhone quality, and the speaker shows the same monk image before and after to prove the difference. His example is the default HitFilm setting of thirty: at thirty the face shows every pore, and at zero it looks like a real iPhone video. Lowering the sharpness is what makes the artificial image read as natural.
