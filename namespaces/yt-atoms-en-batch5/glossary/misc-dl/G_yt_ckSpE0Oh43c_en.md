# G_yt_ckSpE0Oh43c_en — portfolio review

## Term

A portfolio review is the event this video records, a live session where Chris Do and his guests look at real work in real time and score it. Portfolio review appears three times, next to consistency, specialize and VFX, and the session runs as a lightning round through many portfolios at once. His example is the spread itself: some pages are more yellow, some more warm, and some definitely cooler or green, which is a minor consistency issue across the board. The review is unrehearsed, and the hosts say so, because the value is in seeing the judgement happen live.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a critique service: a portfolio review here is a live public session, not private feedback.
