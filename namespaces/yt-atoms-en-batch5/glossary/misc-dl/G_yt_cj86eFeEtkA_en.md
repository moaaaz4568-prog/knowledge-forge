# G_yt_cj86eFeEtkA_en — design principle

## Term

A design principle is what this video tests, and the speaker turns it into a quiz that starts easy and gets progressively more difficult. Design principle appears seven times, next to contrast, hierarchy and repetition, and the questions ask the viewer to spot the principle at work on real designs. His example is the business card: the back looks so different from the front because of contrast, a simple design principle that creates an obvious difference. Design principles in the plural names the whole set the video teaches, from balance and negative space to proximity and repetition.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule to memorize: a design principle here is shown working on a real design, so the test is recognition, not recall.
- Read as one right answer: the speaker allows multiple correct answers, and the points go to the most obvious and main one.
