# G_yt_ancODnOEEok_en — blank page

## Term

A blank page is the hardest thing in the world for this speaker when it comes to creating content: not knowing what to write about or what to make a video about. The phrase appears twice, beside hardest thing, creating content, and knowing what to talk about, in a conversation about videos and topic choice. Her fix is a note, not inspiration — at the end of every coaching session she writes down what the topic was, so the week's or month's content already has its subjects. The page stays blank only when that note is missing; with it, the topic is decided before the writing starts.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "we've just been talking about videos and about how to know what to talk about and so the hardest thing in the world is to have like a blank page when it comes to creating content like not knowing what to write about or video" — sources/S_yt_ancODnOEEok_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as writer's block in general: the blank page here is specifically the moment of choosing a content topic, and the speaker's cure is a note taken after a coaching session.
- Read as a lack of ideas: the speaker has the ideas — they come out in coaching sessions — and the blank page is what happens when none of them were written down.
