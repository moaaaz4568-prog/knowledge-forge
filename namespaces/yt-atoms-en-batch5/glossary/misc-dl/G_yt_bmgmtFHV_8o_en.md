## Term

I want to create 100 million touchpoints with 100 million people, the speaker says — and the chances of you hitting on someone who believes you, trusts you, likes you, and buys are zero. Touchpoints appears five times as the volume strategy he is demolishing: a hundred million touches, a hundred million people, and a zero chance of finding the one who buys.
