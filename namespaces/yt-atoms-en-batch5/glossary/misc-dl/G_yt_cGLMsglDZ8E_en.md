# G_yt_cGLMsglDZ8E_en — CoWork

## Term

Stick around till the end, the speaker says, and he will show the most critical step everyone misses: building your global instructions, so that every CoWork session improves from that on out. CoWork appears twenty-seven times as the workspace being taught: you can see exactly where it is in the work, in the workflow, in the process, and track its progress, and the onboarding plugin asks if you want to include the quarterly context review. The same video draws the boundary: if you are using Co-work mainly for internal work processes with your own data, this should be a non-issue — and if you want a Google Doc, you say so, and if you want a spreadsheet, you say so.
