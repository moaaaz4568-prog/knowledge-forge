# G_yt_bEf7yK5OV3E_en — psychology

## Term

The advanced designers, the speaker says, understand how psychology is built and woven into the graphic design process and solution — and at the moment AI cannot take psychology into consideration and then build it into design solutions. Psychology appears seven times as the human edge: Adobe Illustrator is a mathematical genius, graphic design in the 1950s was all handmade, and AI can press a button and produce a design — but it can't get typography right, and it can't weave psychology in. The low tier designers, the ones working for five dollars, will be pushed out of the market easily with AI; the functional, intelligent designer survives on psychology.
