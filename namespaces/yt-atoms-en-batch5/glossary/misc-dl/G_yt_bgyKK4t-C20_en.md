# G_yt_bgyKK4t-C20_en — EBITDA margin

## Term

If you're thinking about selling, the speaker says, think through the different numbers: how much money you're making per client, the number of clients you have, the EBITDA margin — essentially the earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. EBITDA margin appears once, as the number that matters in a sale, beside the story of selling his own company after six and a half years building it, and the brokerage Merge, catered toward marketing and development agencies. The same talk warns against ClickFunnels 2.0's rough rollout and for building ethical tools to help clients grow.
