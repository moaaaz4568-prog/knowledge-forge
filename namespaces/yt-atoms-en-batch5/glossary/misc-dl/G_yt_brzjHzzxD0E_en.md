# G_yt_brzjHzzxD0E_en — normalizing

## Term

If you've been looking at a design for too long, the speaker says, your brain can do some weird gymnastics of normalizing everything — including problems or missteps. Normalizing appears once, as the trap: the longer you look, the more the flaws look normal. The same talk is about finishing: the urge early on to keep tweaking until it feels done, quote unquote done, even when you're not really improving anything, and the delivery — a clean collection page or a single asset link with permissions.
