# G_yt_b7dQXL-3jj8_en — domain expertise

## Term

On a sales call where the client engineer started asking very technical questions, the team realised the domain expertise was the number one thing the client needed to hear. Domain expertise appears twice, both times as the thing to prove: not by the partners answering themselves, but by bringing in the best person for the job. The speaker's rule is to be completely transparent and in the moment — say what you think — while letting the expert carry the technical proof.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as know everything yourself: the speaker's move is to bring in someone else who has the expertise.
- Read as a pitch line: the expertise is demonstrated by answering the client's questions on the call, not by claiming it.
