# G_yt_cnngZccBJ9I_en — collateral

## Term

Collateral is the stuff the speaker says a salesperson needs to be successful, and this episode is about making sure it is in place before selling. Collateral appears six times, next to foundational, coffee and sales misses, and the speaker lists it plainly: a website, great photo and video, customer testimonials and a good product. His example is the trust itself: all the collateral is really trying to do is build trust, by proving authority and showing the work. The coffee talk is the episode's running joke, and the collateral is the serious point underneath it.
