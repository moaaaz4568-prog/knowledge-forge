# G_yt_bdLExVseg3M_en — 30-day plan

## Term

Everything in the video — the queries, the logic, the plan — is what the speaker would do today if he were starting from scratch. The 30-day plan appears once, as the thing he gave: what he would do today if starting now with Claude as co-pilot in 2026. The plan sits beside his own story — banned from PayPal because he was underage, a store scaled to 2 thousand and banned — and the audience it is for: 10 to 15 people who actually know about your existence and trust you will reach out when the right opportunity arises.
