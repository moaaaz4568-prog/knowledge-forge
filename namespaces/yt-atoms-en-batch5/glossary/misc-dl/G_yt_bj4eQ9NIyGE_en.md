# G_yt_bj4eQ9NIyGE_en — micro magnet

## Term

If you just have a list of the 10 Instagram hooks that will double your engagement, the speaker says, that's a micro magnet. Micro magnet appears three times as the small free thing that pulls people in — against the sales call where the prospect knows the difference, knows it's not super valuable, and knows the only reason you want time on the phone or Zoom is to extract money from their wallet. The same talk name-checks Dean Jackson's nine-word email and the choice of when to show skin versus when to draw value, visuals to match, poses, moves — sexy, but strategic.
