# G_yt_c1GfYnl6jn4_en — swatch group

## Term

Choose a tint for the highlight, the speaker says, and then a third color vastly different from the first — and once you have the colors, make a swatch group, a global swatch group with all of them. Swatch group appears twice, as the container for the palette: the stroke is kept really low so the design is easier to see, and the fourth contrasting color waits until the end, when shadows and highlights are done and the complementary color can be added. The same video has used this method before on the channel, and the point of the technique is that you can really have fun with it.

## Synonyms

- global swatch group

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any group of colours: the swatch group here is the global group made from the three chosen colours.
- Read as the fourth colour being an afterthought: it is held back on purpose until the shadows and highlights are done.
