# G_yt_bXi5UZgZRdA_en — AI filters

## Term

A month ago the reply rates were 20%, even better than now, and the speaker traces the drop to AI filters: once a message gets enough spam messages or complaints, that copy starts not working anymore. AI filters appears once, as the new force in cold email — the data only started streaming in mid-September, when it started becoming perfect, and the response is to kill the dead copy and draft something to beef up the keyword. The filter is the reason a working message can stop working without anything changing in the message itself.
