# G_yt_amUpZsEl5Rs_en — retention

## Term

Retention here is the product metric the speaker uses to value a business: the range growing quite quickly has the highest retention, so when she looks at the business she looks at retention of product first. The word appears eight times, beside highest, product, business value, and growth, in a long interview that moves between business and personal life. It is presented as the exciting place to look — not follower counts or new launches, but whether customers stay with the products they already have. The contrast in the same conversation is work-life balance, which she reframes by saying she does not see her job as a job at all.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as follower growth: retention here is about customers staying with products, and the speaker values the business on it rather than on the 50,000 followers mentioned elsewhere.
- Read as a single product's sales: the speaker reads retention across product bases to judge the whole business, not one launch.
