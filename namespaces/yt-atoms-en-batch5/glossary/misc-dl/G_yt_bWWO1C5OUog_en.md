# G_yt_bWWO1C5OUog_en — misconception of success

## Term

The speaker's reality looks vastly different from the picture people carry, and she says people create a misconception of success: that the people who achieved it moved in a straight line, each step more successful than the last, until they got where they are. Misconception of success appears once, as the belief she is dismantling on a Friday the 14th of February, updating viewers after a flood in which someone home alone could very easily have drowned or been electrocuted. The question that hit her — who are you comparing yourself to, who made you think this would be easy — is the same point from the other side: the linear story is what makes the struggle feel like failure.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "and what my reality actually looks like is vastly different and I think people create this misconception of success that the people that have achieved it it was just this linear you know each step more successful than the last and they eventually get where" — sources/S_yt_bWWO1C5OUog_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as success being a lie: the misconception is about the path being linear, not about success being unreal.
- Read as the flood being a metaphor: it is the real event behind the update, and the reason the video exists that week.
