# G_yt_bJXcc_SxQF8_en — leaky bucket

## Term

A lot of people figure this out once and then go broke, the speaker says, because they don't realise that if it's a leaky bucket, you just make a lot of money and burn it just like how you made it. Leaky bucket appears once, as the simple mental model he has been looking for: the business is the hardest thing you can decide to participate in, and the issue may not be the thing you're selling — it may be the tiny hole letting ten grand a month of value out, because you're the bottleneck, serving every client, building all the assets, doing all the strategy. The fix he points to is selling a front-end product and then a recurring back-end offer, so the bucket stops leaking.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as stop selling: the leak is in the delivery, not the product; the fix is a recurring back-end, not fewer sales.
- Read as bottleneck meaning a staffing problem: here the bottleneck is the founder doing everything themselves.
