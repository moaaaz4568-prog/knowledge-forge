# G_yt_cP6sEVzYt_U_en — constraint

## Term

A constraint in this source is the single bottleneck that caps a business, and the speaker's whole method is to find it and fix it before doing anything else. Constraint appears twenty-two times, next to appointments, beliefs and the Silver Bullet, and the speaker treats fixing the wrong thing as the expensive mistake. His example is a business stuck under four appointments showed per week: the constraint is appointments, so the fix is appointments, not more sales calls. Constraints in the plural names the same blockers when he lists them across the business.

## Synonyms

- constraints

## Live Example

> "the infrastructure to be able to support more appointments more sales and more Service delivery anyways back on track so if you're not getting at least appointments or four appointments showed per week consistently your constraint is appointments and you need to fix that now" — sources/S_yt_cP6sEVzYt_U_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a general business problem: a constraint here is the one specific cap on growth, not any difficulty the business faces.
- Read as the fix itself: naming the constraint does nothing until the speaker's prescribed fix is applied to it.
