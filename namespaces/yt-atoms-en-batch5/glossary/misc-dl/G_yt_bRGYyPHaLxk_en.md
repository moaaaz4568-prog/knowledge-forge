# G_yt_bRGYyPHaLxk_en — VIX

## Term

The speaker's subject is volatility, and VIX appears seventeen times as the index it is measured against: the current month is so short-term that a long-term bet on volatility at the current VIX level is pretty hard to do. The mechanics around it are concrete — a stock rising to $150 by the time the option expires in three months makes the option worth $50, cash and collateral offset the riskiness of credit card borrowers, and you can buy or sell the underlying asset for delta hedging to get rid of the directional bias and capture the change in vega, or volatility. VIX here is the level the whole trade is priced off.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a stock: it is the volatility level the speaker bets on, not an asset to own.
- Read as hard to do meaning impossible: the difficulty is the mismatch between a short-term month and a long-term volatility bet.
