# G_yt_ch1RVxCA9K4_en — strategic narrative

## Term

Strategic narrative is the speaker's answer to the question of how an agency niches down, and he presents it as the story that flows down into recruiting, hiring, onboarding and reward. Strategic narrative appears thirty times, next to niche down, thought leadership and Gabby, and the conversation keeps returning to it as the thing agency leaders should take away. His example is the decision itself: the agency went down this route and the narrative shaped how the team was built, not just how the agency talked. Narrative on its own is the speaker's shorthand for the same story when the context is already clear.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a tagline: a strategic narrative here runs the whole agency from hiring to reward, not just the marketing copy.
- Read as a positioning trick: the speaker's narrative has to be lived inside the agency, so a story the team does not follow is not one.
