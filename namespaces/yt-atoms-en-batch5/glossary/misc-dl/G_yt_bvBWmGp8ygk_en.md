# G_yt_bvBWmGp8ygk_en — founder

## Term

On Shark Tank, people are constantly pitching ideas, and the speaker prices the money behind them: the cost of equity in a small company runs somewhere between twenty and forty percent, while the cost of debt might be six to ten. Founder appears eighty-four times as the person at the centre of those pitches — the one investors know, the one whose business has to survive a market shifting quicker than the business itself, and the one whose ego can be part of the problem when the wrong investor comes on. The same talk watches Australia punish failure like a black mark on a coffee table book, and still bets the idea could work anywhere, because it is a human nature thing.

## Synonyms

- founders

## Live Example

> "If you know someone and knows you as a founder, knows your business and then knows the sort of investors out there who who could really help." — sources/S_yt_bvBWmGp8ygk_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the person with the money: the founder is the one pitching, not the investor writing the cheque.
- Read as cost of equity being the topic: it is the price tag on the founder's money, not the founder.
