# G_yt_bh70BkV__mU_en — grain

## Term

The video is about grain — Ukraine is a significant portion of global grain production, and wheat exports from Ukraine and Russia combined are about 25 percent. Grain appears twenty-two times as the commodity whose supply is the story: the supply of food is stocked for months of inventory in advance, down to three and a half to four months of excess supply in some grains, and production came off 2.5 billion tons as commodity prices fell through the floor and farmers cut back. The bottom line the speaker draws is that a shortage in grain is an equal variable to what drives revolutions, alongside education and social and political sense.
