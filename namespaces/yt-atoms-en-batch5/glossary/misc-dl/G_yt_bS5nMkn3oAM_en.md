# G_yt_bS5nMkn3oAM_en — psychological trick

## Term

Place a design in an urban environment and you give off a very different feeling with it — this psychological trick, this technique, the speaker says, won't be relevant or useful in every single design or project. Psychological trick appears three times as the move of changing how a design feels by changing its context: the same trick plays on the human mind in marketing material for Jean-Paul Gaultier, so superficial it couldn't try harder, and Harvard University talks about how, when we see a quote, the mind responds. The next thing he shows, he promises, can be used anywhere — but the trick itself is situational.
