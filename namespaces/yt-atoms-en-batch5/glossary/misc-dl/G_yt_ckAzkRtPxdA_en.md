# G_yt_ckAzkRtPxdA_en — illustrator

## Term

Illustrator is the program this video teaches, and the speaker presents six problem hacks for it in a single Satori Graphics tutorial. Illustrator appears seventeen times, next to hacks, scale stroke and scissors tool, and the first hack is the stroke that will not stay the same size when a shape is resized. His example is the scale stroke option in preferences: uncheck or check it, and the stroke stays the same throughout while the shape changes. The pen tool, the smooth tool and the scissors tool are the other hacks, and each one solves a specific problem the speaker names.
