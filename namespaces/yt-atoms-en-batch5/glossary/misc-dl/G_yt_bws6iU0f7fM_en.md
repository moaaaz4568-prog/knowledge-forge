# G_yt_bws6iU0f7fM_en — gradient tool

## Term

The artist starts with the eyes and thinks in lines, building a base to run an image online, and the trick is that much of the artwork can be duplicated because it is symmetrical. Gradient tool appears twice, named in the passage about the dark areas around the design: it is important not to go so dark that the design gets lost in the backgrounds. The same video works across multiple lines at one time, and the design is really coming together as the shading builds.
