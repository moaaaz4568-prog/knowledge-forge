# G_yt_b59SnUtL81U_en — critical thinking

## Term

The speaker warns that people who lean on AI have dropped critical thinking and learning skills from the equation, which he calls ironic because the real advantage was never access to information. Critical thinking appears twice, paired with learning skills in the same breath. The point lands through the learning experience itself: the headache, the tension, the light bulb moment of understanding something deeply. His rule follows from that: when you are learning, never offload the step of thinking to AI — that step is the learning.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But a lot of people mistakenly also removed critical thinking and learning skills from the equation which is so ironic because the real advantage has never been getting access to information." — sources/S_yt_b59SnUtL81U_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as AI makes thinking unnecessary: the speaker says the opposite — offloading this step is exactly what leaves you behind.
- Read as a general attack on AI tools: the warning is about the learning process specifically, not about using AI at work.
