# G_yt_cNZ8ekskYzM_en — aida

## Term

Nobody has said what he is about to tell you, the speaker says — not that they haven't, he just hasn't heard it — but aida is all about making your prospect feel something, making them engaged. Aida appears ten times as the formula the tutorial breaks down in crazy detail: fill the first two sentences well and you have the attention and the interest going, then ramp it up with desire, and the action is simply to keep watching for the next ninety seconds. The same video shows the path millions of Americans are taking to become self-made millionaires, and the whole thing is a magic wand you can wave — presto — once the prospect feels something.
