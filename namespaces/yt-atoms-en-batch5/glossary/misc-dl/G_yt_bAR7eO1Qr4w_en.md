# G_yt_bAR7eO1Qr4w_en — visual movement

## Term

The speaker reads a vehicle's lines — converging and intertwining, less rigid and straight — and finds a bit more visual movement in them, just like lightning. Visual movement appears once, as the quality that keeps a vehicle from feeling static or unmoving. Against it he sets the other vehicle, dominated by very straight blocky proportions tied to the chassis and overall package, with the designers choosing to highlight the hood. Movement here is a design reading: the lines suggest speed even when the vehicle stands still.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "lines in this converge and intertwine a little bit more the lines are less rigid and straight so they have a bit more visual movement just like lightning generally speaking the last thing you want is for a vehicle to feel static or unmoving most" — sources/S_yt_bAR7eO1Qr4w_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the vehicle actually moving: the movement is in the lines, a visual suggestion of speed.
- Read as blocky being a failure: the other design is conservative and executed competently — a different choice, not a mistake.
