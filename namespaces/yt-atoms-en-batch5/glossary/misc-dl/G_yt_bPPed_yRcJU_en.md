# G_yt_bPPed_yRcJU_en — enforcer class

## Term

The job of the enforcer class, the speaker says, is really to hold down the vast majority of the population — the slave class, the peasants, the serfs, whatever you want to call them. Enforcer class appears three times as the layer that keeps the system in place for the old money and the upper tiers, who no longer need people to do the work and keep the economy running once AI and robotics reach a competent level. The speaker's scenario is that the relationship between the classes then changes drastically: if his goal were to maintain power and eliminate challenges while keeping a life of luxury, the best way would not be to continue the class conflict.
