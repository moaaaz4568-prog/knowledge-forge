# G_yt_bgStKKE6RUk_en — poetry

## Term

A lot of people did or they hated it, the speaker says, and he has no problem sitting right in the center of them — because it means that people care. Poetry appears four times as the subject: one of the good things about poetry is that poetry doesn't describe an experience — good poetry is an experience. The second quote comes from Ray Eames herself: a designer is like a good host, anticipating the needs of their guests — and anticipating is a word for him that was far more important than the rest.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a poetry reading: the poetry is the example; the point is that caring, even hating, proves the work matters.
- Read as the Eames quote being decoration: anticipating the guest's needs is the word the speaker carries away from it.
