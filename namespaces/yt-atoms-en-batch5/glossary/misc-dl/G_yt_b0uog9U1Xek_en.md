## Term

Vibe marketing is the speaker's named framework for using AI to create a connection with the end viewer, consumer, buyer, or prospect — whatever the audience is called. The phrase appears 11 times, beside term, framework, and connection, in a podcast where the host asks Pete to dig into the point of view behind it.
