# G_yt_at_StbviEQY_en — founder-led sales

## Term

Founder-led sales is the stage the speakers want agencies to get out of: the founder still doing the selling, and the question is why agency owners hesitate to install a sales process instead. The phrase appears once, beside agency owners, hesitate, install, and sales process, in a conversation about making sales methodical and agency-wide. The alternative is shown, not just described — a straightforward letter, hand signed by the new business director, that shows the prospect a real person. The speakers treat the shift as the whole endeavour: new business is not the founder's side job but something the agency does together.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "to get out of that founder-led sales what do you think are some of the main reasons agency owners hesitate to install that sales process whether they're looking to do that now just to make sure that they're being methodical about their own sales efforts" — sources/S_yt_at_StbviEQY_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the founder selling well: founder-led sales here is the problem to leave behind, and the hesitation to install a process is what keeps agencies stuck in it.
- Read as hiring a salesperson: the replacement described is a sales process the whole agency runs, with letters and a new business director, not one hire.
