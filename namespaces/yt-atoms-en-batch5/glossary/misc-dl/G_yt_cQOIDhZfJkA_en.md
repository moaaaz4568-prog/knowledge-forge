# G_yt_cQOIDhZfJkA_en — gestalt theory

## Term

Gestalt theory is the graphic design theory the speaker uses on pretty much all of his designs, and he defines it as the brain organizing a complex group of images on a subconscious level. Gestalt theory appears eight times, next to continuation, figure and ground and symmetry and order, and the speaker walks through its six parts on Adidas posters, the Amazon logo and the FedEx arrow. His example is the Adidas basketball poster: the eye follows the arch of the frame over to the player, which is continuation doing its work. Gestalt on its own is the speaker's shorthand for the same theory.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a single rule: gestalt theory here is a collection of six principles unified into one theory, not one trick.
- Read as conscious choice: the effects work on a subconscious level, so a viewer who cannot name the principle is still affected by it.
