# G_yt_bPdSdfNq5XA_en — Valley of informed pessimism

## Term

After the first excitement, you see the positives, then you slowly start getting informed and realise some things are harder than you expected — and you fall into the Valley of informed pessimism, where, the speaker says, successful people are. Valley of informed pessimism appears once, as the stage most people flee: they get distracted, chase the new opportunity, and start over, because they don't have clarity in their goal. The way through, in his framing, is to master focus in the short term, eliminate distraction, and learn how to be bored — to embrace boredom instead of searching for the next new thing.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "which you just see the positives of that and then you slowly start getting informed you realize that there are some things that are harder than you expected and you fall into that Valley of informed pessimism now there is literally where successful people are" — sources/S_yt_bPdSdfNq5XA_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the Valley being failure: the speaker places successful people literally there; it is a stage to pass through, not a trap.
- Read as embrace boredom meaning doing nothing: it is the discipline of staying with one goal instead of chasing the next opportunity.
