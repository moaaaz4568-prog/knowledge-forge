# G_yt_b9rWnX2cQj4_en — eds and meds

## Term

Boston became a big VC healthcare pipeline because of the talent coming out of its universities — Harvard and MIT based in greater Boston — and the speaker sums the engine up as education healthcare, or eds and meds. Eds and meds appears once, as shorthand for the two sectors that let a place do a much better job: the education and healthcare institutions that produce talent and attract investment. The phrase sits in a wider comparison of places — Switzerland's export value and low tax cantons, Boston's universities — and names the institutional base of a regional economy.

> "They just did a lot better of a job of it than other states which is the education healthcare or eds and meds. they can do a much better job at it because Boston became a big VC healthc care pipeline because of all the talent coming out of its universities and they have both Harvard and MIT based in greater Boston." — sources/S_yt_b9rWnX2cQj4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a policy slogan: here it is the speaker's shorthand for the education-and-healthcare economy of a city.
- Read as hospitals and schools in general: the point is their role as an economic engine feeding venture capital and talent.
