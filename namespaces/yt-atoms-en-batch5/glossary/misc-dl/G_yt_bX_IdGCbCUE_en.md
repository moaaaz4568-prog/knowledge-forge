# G_yt_bX_IdGCbCUE_en — personal brand

## Term

If you think you can't build a personal brand simply because your specific job situation is unique, the speaker says, you will never be able to build one. Personal brand appears eleven times as the thing to build regardless of the job: if he helps you do it, they can partner together and eventually sell the business and make a profit. The scale he points to is the team — with the right team you can make as much money as you want — and the biggest companies, even Jensen Huang with Nvidia saying they have made people trillions.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So if you think you can't build a personal brand simply because you know your specific job situation is unique, you will never be able to build one." — sources/S_yt_bX_IdGCbCUE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as unique situation being a barrier: the speaker treats it as the excuse that stops the brand from being built.
- Read as a solo project: the brand here is built to be partnered on and eventually sold.
