# G_yt_bDElLNtrQpQ_en — automation as a service

## Term

If you know a specific industry very well, the speaker says, you can offer automation as a service for that industry — build something that helps revolutionise it and make your billions there. Automation as a service appears six times as the business he is selling: a done-for-you offer, harder to turn profit than selling info because it is what you have already done, and not for everybody, because not everybody has that kind of personality. The offer is framed against figuring it out on your own — his business will just help you do it, with a link in the description and pinned comments.
