# G_yt_bNtIUWB6B-c_en — portfolio

## Term

A portfolio doesn't get you a job, the speaker says — it gets you the interview. Portfolio appears sixteen times as the document that opens the door: the first page has to grab his attention, the work has to be communicated in a style where you know what's happening without text overload, just a brief synopsis. When he sends the initial email through, he leads with three or four bullet points on why he wants the person interviewed — the unique kind of selling point of that person. The portfolio is the pitch for the interview, not the proof of the job.
