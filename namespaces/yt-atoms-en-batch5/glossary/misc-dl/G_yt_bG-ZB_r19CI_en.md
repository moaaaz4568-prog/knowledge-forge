# G_yt_bG-ZB_r19CI_en — correct perception

## Term

Two videos that haven't been particularly viral still have a high conversion rate, and the speaker says that is precisely why he can sell at high prices: every piece of content, every deliverable, every sequence of stories is executed at the exact moment. Correct perception appears once, as the condition for all of it — if you haven't yet formed a correct perception, you will have to start over. Acting on the wrong perception causes stagnation and later a decrease in revenue, because you are always acting at the wrong time.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And if you haven't yet formed a correct perception, I'm sorry to say you're going to have to start over." — sources/S_yt_bG-ZB_r19CI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as market research: here it is the seller's own read of timing and audience, formed before the content goes out.
- Read as high conversion contradicting low views: the speaker treats the conversion rate as the proof the perception is correct.
