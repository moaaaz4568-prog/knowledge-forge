# G_yt_cMJXiD0Cjt4_en — offer

## Term

When you learn what these are, the speaker says, you will have the ability to troubleshoot any single marketing or sales thing you ever do — any activity, any campaign — because if it did not work, it is only one of these three things. Offer appears fifty-one times as the first of the three: the NOM framework stands for niche, offer and message, and the test is whether people ask you what you do at a conference and understand it, or feel worried because they are spending money on ads that are not converting. The same talk warns against life hacks: there is no hack or quick trick for success, only tips, tricks and tactics that help along the way, and the three things are what you fix.

> "And that's exactly what we're talking about here with the NOM framework, which stands for niche offer and message." — sources/S_yt_cMJXiD0Cjt4_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a discount: the offer here is one of three things to troubleshoot, beside niche and message.
- Read as the three things being a guarantee: they are what you check when something did not work, not a promise it will.
