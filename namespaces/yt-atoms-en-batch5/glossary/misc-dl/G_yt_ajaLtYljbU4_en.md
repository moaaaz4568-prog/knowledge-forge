# G_yt_ajaLtYljbU4_en — nurture sequence

## Term

A nurture sequence here is an email sequence the speaker wonders whether to run through a GPT: he asks whether the sequence he built is a good GPT to run through, because it carries his email format, or whether it is only for nurture specifically. The phrase appears once, beside GPT, email format, and built, in a conversation about pitching with a lowered barrier and handling the main objection. It sits between two bigger problems the speaker names — fulfillment is a huge problem for the coaching industry, but getting clients first is always the bigger one. The sequence is treated as a tool for the follow-up after the pitch, not as the pitch itself.
