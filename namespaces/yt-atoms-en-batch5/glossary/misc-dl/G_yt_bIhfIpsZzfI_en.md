# G_yt_bIhfIpsZzfI_en — student loans

## Term

From 2008 to 2020, the speaker says, student loans were a big source of extra income for younger generations — you had increased auto loan debt and other forms of consumer debt as well. Student loans appears once, as the first and largest of the debts that supplemented a lack of income for people who were in college when 2008 happened. The same talk traces what followed: a spike in defaults, though not to the level of 2008, and the Dodd-Frank regulation, alongside a society so used to safety nets — regulatory or fiscal — that a clean downturn like the 1921 depression is barely remembered.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as extra income meaning free money: the speaker names loans as debt that supplemented income, with defaults following.
- Read as a story about students only: the loans are one part of a generational pattern of debt, alongside auto loans and consumer debt.
