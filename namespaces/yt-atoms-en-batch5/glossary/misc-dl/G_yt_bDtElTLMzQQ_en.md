# G_yt_bDtElTLMzQQ_en — build and release

## Term

When you are starting out, the speaker asks, do you do build and release, or do you approach it a different way — and his answer is that you can't do build and release, because it requires something he is about to name. Build and release appears nine times as the debated approach to launching, set against the agencies all chasing the same thing: reach, a million views. The same talk ranges from giveaway winners to growth specialists paid a base pay plus a profit share or rev share, and the through-line is choosing one approach that works and doing it.
