# G_yt_cWk4k3GHI5c_en — badge logo

## Term

A badge logo is the design the speaker builds in this Illustrator tutorial, a vintage looking emblem made from a star, circles and curved text. Badge logo appears three times, next to star tool, anchor points and stroke, and the speaker walks through the whole build from the star shape to the five pointed stars at the end. His example is the five star design: five stars are added because it is a five star design after all, and the stars are aligned along the same horizontal line. The badge is finished when everything is selected, aligned and grouped into one emblem.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any logo: a badge logo here is the specific circular emblem with curved text, not a logo in general.
- Read as a single tool result: the badge comes from the star tool, the ellipse tool and the type on a path tool together, not from one click.
