# G_yt_b0JBLDsDx3c_en — ebooks

## Term

Ebooks here are the cheap product the speaker warns against settling for: you can sell them for super cheap, but why settle for nine to fifty dollars when you could immediately sell your service for several hundred or several thousand. The word appears 14 times, with ebook five more times, beside call funnel, super cheap, and sell your service, in a video about turning videos into books. The making is mechanical — take the transcripts, put them in YouTube Scribe, and get them written in ebook format — and the speaker's own rule is timing: a book is something to do after you have a big audience, because you want those book sales to make money forever. The ebooks sit at the bottom of a ladder whose top is the service, and the products and services are what bridge the gap to the close.
