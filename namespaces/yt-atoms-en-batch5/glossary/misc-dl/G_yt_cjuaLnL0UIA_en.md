# G_yt_cjuaLnL0UIA_en — mask

## Term

Mask is the tool the speaker applies again and again in this textures tutorial, and its job is to keep a texture from filling the whole screen. Mask appears ten times, next to Tint effect, CC Lens and lighting, and the speaker creates the mask himself because it lets him do what he wants with the edges. His example is the third texture: a mask is applied because setting it to screen leaves unpleasant edges, and the mask is never left sharp because sharp is very ugly. The tutorial's goal is an aesthetic look that grabs attention without straining the eye, and the mask is what makes the textures sit together.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And we will apply a mask to it. Because if we set it to screen now, you will find there are still some unpleasant edges." — sources/S_yt_cjuaLnL0UIA_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as hiding something: a mask here shapes where a texture shows, it does not remove the texture.
- Read as a preset: the speaker builds each mask by hand for the texture in front of him, so no single mask fits every case.
