## Term

If people don't know what a geofence is, the speaker explains, it is putting a digital perimeter around a physical location and collecting information from the location where you're on the people's cell phones. Geofence appears three times, defined once and used as the targeting idea behind the agency's move toward connected television — making commercials and a lot of video, because the creativity involved and the results people get from television and video are unsurpassed.
