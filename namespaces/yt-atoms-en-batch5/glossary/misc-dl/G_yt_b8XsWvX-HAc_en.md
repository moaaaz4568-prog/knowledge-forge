# G_yt_b8XsWvX-HAc_en — booking funnel

## Term

The speaker's system is built to extract the most value from leads already spoken to — you cannot extract value from something you don't track or own — and the booking funnel appears five times as the mechanism. Once a client pays an invoice they get the onboarding booking funnel: the link for someone to book a call, the onboarding steps, the tracking of who has paid, who has received the proposal. The funnel runs on Go High Level, which he calls an amazing tool for anyone who wants to use it, with a free welcome course to start.
