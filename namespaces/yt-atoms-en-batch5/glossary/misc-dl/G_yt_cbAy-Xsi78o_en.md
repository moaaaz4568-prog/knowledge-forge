# G_yt_cbAy-Xsi78o_en — perfectionism

## Term

Perfectionism is what killed the speaker's YouTube channel, and he tells the story as a warning about letting a planned short break turn into an indefinite one. Perfectionism appears three times, next to analysis paralysis, board games and burnt out, and the speaker says it won when he could not decide whether to stick to his plan or ride the wave of his card game video. His example is the epic battle with perfectionism itself: while he efficiently procrastinated, the almighty algorithm blessed his inactive channel and the audience arrived for content he no longer wanted to make. The channel died not from failure but from doing nothing while the decision stayed open.
