# G_yt_bGYuTu7kobg_en — hook

## Term

Worse than a lesson, the speaker says, is when they give you the slides and just read the points — so the job is to grab their attention early and hook them, because you need to create a connection at the start of the webinar. Hook appears twice as the opening move: the early grab that makes the connection, so the speaker can find out exactly what is going on with the audience and tailor the content to give them exactly what they need. Interaction is not cool for its own sake here; it is how the hook turns into a tailored webinar.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a gimmick: the speaker's hook exists to create a connection that lets him tailor the content.
- Read as the whole webinar: the hook is the start; the tailoring is what follows.
