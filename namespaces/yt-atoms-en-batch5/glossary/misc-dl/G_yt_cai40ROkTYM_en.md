# G_yt_cai40ROkTYM_en — artificial intelligence

## Term

Artificial intelligence is the force the speaker says is on the rise for agencies, and the video follows it through creativity, graphic design foundations and client work. Artificial intelligence appears fifteen times, next to content, information and foundations, and the speaker contrasts thinking of it like Google with thinking of it as a creative partner. His example is the I Robot future he opens with: a potential reality of death and destruction at robotic hands, shown to make people feel what is at stake. AI is the same force in its short form, used whenever the speaker talks about the tools rather than the idea.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a tool that replaces designers: artificial intelligence here is presented as something agencies must learn to use in creativity, not as a replacement for it.
