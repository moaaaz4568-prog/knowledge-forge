# G_yt_bJlAOwr80kI_en — structured content

## Term

Content that comes out of your mouth spontaneously is ruined if you try to structure it, the speaker says — but structured content, worked at over long periods of time, trains your brain cells to become accustomed to it. Structured content appears three times, set against improvised content: the spontaneous kind must stay spontaneous, while the structured kind is a discipline built by repetition. The whole point, in his framing, is that the two kinds of content obey different rules, and structuring the wrong one destroys it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never structure content: the warning is about structuring spontaneous content; structured content is a separate, trained habit.
- Read as brain cells become accustomed being a biology claim: it is the speaker's way of describing a habit formed by long practice.
