# G_yt_brf1on-55Ik_en — the model

## Term

The whole source is a slide of short lines, and its argument is about the model: you, prospects, clients, business — you become overworked. The model appears once by that phrase, twice as the word: the job of the model is to set you free, not to trap you, and the right model allows you to scale. The other lines are the two sides of it — ignored and underpaid, a business that will be limited, against freedom, the right fit, getting clients results. The model is the fit between you and the business; the wrong one traps you in overwork.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the model being a template to copy: it is the fit of the business to you, and the right fit is what sets you free.
- Read as the slide lines being separate tips: they are the two outcomes of the same choice — the wrong model limits you, the right one scales.
