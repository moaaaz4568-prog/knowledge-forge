# G_yt_ahJCOIrpd84_en — flux mode

## Term

Flux mode is CG Dream's newly launched image-generation mode, presented as the tool's next level. The phrase appears once, beside aspect ratio, sliders, prompts, and CG Dream, in a walkthrough of the generation settings: how many images to make and how refined the outcomes should follow the prompt. The speaker treats it as the headline feature of the tour, the thing the rest of the settings serve. In use, it sits alongside filters and strength controls — the same panel where an image can be turned into an alien world or a 3D model restyled into 2D with an anime filter.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "have settings for aspect ratio how many images we want to generate and then sliders here to control how refined in accordance to our prompts the outcomes are so CG dream have recently launched the flux mode and this is their next level image generation" — sources/S_yt_ahJCOIrpd84_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a filter: flux mode is the generation mode itself, not one of the filters applied inside it — the filters are chosen separately after the mode.
- Read as a prompt: the mode is a setting of CG Dream, while the prompt is the text the user writes; the sliders decide how closely the outcome follows that text.
