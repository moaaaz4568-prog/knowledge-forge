# G_yt_bqpCAjcL2Yo_en — symbolic interaction

## Term

There is one aspect or theory in graphic design so important that if overlooked, the designer would likely create designs that simply just aren't good. Symbolic interaction appears twenty-one times as that theory: designers need to be aware of it because it exists in pretty much every aspect of graphic design. The proof is the advert — a static image of a mouse on a desk seems slow, boring and bland — against interactive designs like websites or apps, where intuitive symbols and gestures align the user's expectations and create a seamless and meaningful interaction.
