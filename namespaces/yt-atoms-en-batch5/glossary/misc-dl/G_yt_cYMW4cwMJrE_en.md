# G_yt_cYMW4cwMJrE_en — agency

## Term

Agency is the world this whole conversation lives in, and the speaker's point is that an agency owner eventually sheds competitiveness once he realizes he is not really competing with the people around him. Agency appears twenty-four times, next to deliverable, net 90 and True Value, and the host frames the series as bite sized agency briefs for owners building the agency of their dreams. His example is Second Melody, a branding agency that spent seventeen years in business and competed for one project with somebody the owner knew. Agencies in the plural names the same firms when the speakers talk about the industry as a whole.
