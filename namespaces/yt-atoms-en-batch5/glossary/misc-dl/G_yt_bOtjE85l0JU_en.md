# G_yt_bOtjE85l0JU_en — outlier

## Term

When one of your videos gets five times the average number of views or more, the speaker says, that's your outlier — and it's not a coincidence. Outlier appears twice as the signal in the data: a video that breaks the pattern tells you something worked, and the content system is built to find it and repeat it. The same talk adds AI tools to the content because there is a huge interest in AI right now, and keeps everything focused on Instagram, the main focus and what they are strongest at. The outlier is the clue the system is designed to catch.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as luck: the speaker says explicitly it is not a coincidence, it is a signal.
- Read as five times the average being a fixed rule: it is the speaker's threshold for spotting the signal, not a law of the platform.
