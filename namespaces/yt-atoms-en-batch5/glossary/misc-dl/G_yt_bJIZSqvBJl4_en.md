# G_yt_bJIZSqvBJl4_en — common sense

## Term

On a box design with two bad design errors, the speaker says the problem is just common sense: as a graphic designer it is important to apply common sense and then to realise how the viewer is going to actually see that work. Common sense appears three times as the missing ingredient — the girl on the box has already won the game, a letter is missing somewhere, and the end result is an insulting and funny design no business could ever actually use. Simple common sense, he concludes, plus mastering the important graphic design principles, can take you a long way.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as design needing no training: the speaker pairs common sense with mastering graphic design principles.
- Read as the errors being cleverness: the designs fail because nobody checked how a viewer would actually read them.
