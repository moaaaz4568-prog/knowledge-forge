# G_yt_bDbRsh_cB_A_en — camera confidence

## Term

Camera confidence is something that just takes practice, the speaker says — his mini camera confidence course sits inside the Rebels Business tribe. Camera confidence appears once, as the skill of being yourself on camera: you can smell it when someone is not quite being themselves or is putting on an act, because they can't maintain that for very long. The same talk is full of that honesty — admitting he didn't really know who he was at the beginning, that a bigger player with a bigger warehouse would always beat him, and that lockdown once locked him in his bedroom when the door handle fell off.
