# G_yt_ca4xbLavQgY_en — brand

## Term

Brand in this source is a living and breathing thing, and the speaker defines it as everyone's reaction to the brand rather than a logo or a color. Brand appears one hundred eighteen times, next to branding, concept and visual, and the conversation keeps returning to the brand as the thing clients actually experience. Her example is the feedback a brand gets: it is like a person, and the reactions it provokes are the brand itself. Brands in the plural names the same living things when the speakers compare one company's reactions with another's.

> "thing yes yeah like so many things come to mind it is consistency but also people have to understand that brands are a living and breathing thing and like you said it's everyone's reaction to that brand the feedback you get it's like a person" — sources/S_yt_ca4xbLavQgY_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a logo: brand here is the reactions and the feedback a company provokes, not its visual marks.
- Read as a slogan: the speaker's brand is built from what clients experience, so a claim without the experience behind it is empty.
