# G_yt_c19T1lhcU9k_en — logo

## Term

The speaker wants the logo to be as clear and visible as possible while still communicating the brand message, and a good logo is not overcrowded with useless design elements. Logo appears thirty times as the thing being judged against its brief: ask yourself throughout the entire logo design project whether your design communicates and represents the brief or whether you have lost your way, and keep it timeless and functional for ten, twenty or thirty years. The same video ends with a checklist to refer back to when designing a logo, saved as a PDF in the description box below, because the bare essentials are what present the company.
