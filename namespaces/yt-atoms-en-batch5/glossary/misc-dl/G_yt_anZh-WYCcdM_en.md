# G_yt_anZh-WYCcdM_en — typography tricks

## Term

Typography tricks are the Illustrator shortcuts this quick video teaches for saving time: keys on screen for Mac or Windows that increase or decrease the weight of the typography by two points either way. The phrase appears twice, beside Illustrator, shortcuts, and save time, in a short tutorial whose promise is a faster, easier, more productive workflow. One named trick is tracking — another shortcut that increases or decreases the spacing between letters. The video is deliberately short because the tricks themselves are small: a key press, two points, and the work moves faster.
