# G_yt_b1I_NLeTChU_en — long-term revenue

## Term

Long-term revenue here is the stick — the staying power — in a business like the client's: when you look at a business like yours, it is more about the stick in the long-term revenue. The phrase appears once, beside business like yours, stick, and look, in a call between an agency and a client with 20 years of working history behind it. The speaker is contrasting it with the short-term win: the results they had working together were great, but the judgment that matters is whether the revenue sticks. The client's own verdict is the recommendation — anybody considering working with the agency should absolutely give them a chance.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But then when you look in a business like yours, it's more about the stick in the long-term revenue." — sources/S_yt_b1I_NLeTChU_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as total revenue: long-term revenue here is specifically the revenue that sticks around, and the speaker uses the stick as the measure of the business.
- Read as a forecast: the phrase describes the durability of the revenue already being earned, judged from 20 years of working together, not a projection.
