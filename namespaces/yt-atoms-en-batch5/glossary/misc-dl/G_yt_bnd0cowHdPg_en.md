# G_yt_bnd0cowHdPg_en — whales

## Term

I built a business only talking to whales, the speaker says — up to a hundred thousand dollars per month — and he really enjoyed it, because he had more active clients than likes on a post, with two hundred thousand followers. Whales appears eight times as the client strategy: talk only to the big ones. The same video plays with the opposite idea — Cash Creator, which means nothing, because he made it up — and the point that ideas and identity are real because we believe in them, not because they're real. The persuasive move he names is the flashlight: include one, demonstrate a path to the goal, and you become far more persuasive than everybody else.

## Live Example

> "I built a business only talking to whales, up to a hundred thousand dollars per month, and I really enjoyed it, because I had more active clients than likes on a post, and I have, like, two hundred thousand followers." — sources/S_yt_bnd0cowHdPg_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as ignoring small clients out of snobbery: the whales are the strategy that produced $100K months with fewer clients than likes.
- Read as Cash Creator being a real term: the speaker says it means nothing, because he made it up.
