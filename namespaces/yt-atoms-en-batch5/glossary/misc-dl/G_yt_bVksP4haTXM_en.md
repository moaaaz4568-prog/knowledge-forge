# G_yt_bVksP4haTXM_en — digital product

## Term

With zero technical background — and you don't need any either — the speaker uses the best no-code app builder to make a digital product: click generate and the tool creates a fully formatted product with a title, table of contents and complete contents for each section. It also has a field for the brand name, a color scheme selector and the option to upload a logo. Digital product appears eleven times as the thing being built and sold: charge $47, get two sales a day, and that is almost $3,000 a month from one single AI digital product built in less than 5 minutes.

> "And when they click generate, the tool creates a fully formatted digital product with a title, table of contents, and complete contents for each section." — sources/S_yt_bVksP4haTXM_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the $3,000 being income in hand: it is the arithmetic of two $47 sales a day, not reported earnings.
- Read as no-code meaning no work: the product still needs a brand, a price and buyers; the builder only formats it.
