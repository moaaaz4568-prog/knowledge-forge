# G_yt_cpHtamvjwHY_en — business development

## Term

Business development is the guest's profession, and the episode follows Christian Bak from concert promotions through the 2008 recession into agency business development. Business development appears four times, next to newsletter, agency and pickle ball, and the host introduces the guest as the expert who helps agencies land six and seven figure opportunities predictably. His example is the pivot itself: after the recession dried up his events business, a sales consultant opened him up to the world of business development, and he went full steam ahead into it. A decade later he is still in the industry, which is the proof the episode offers for the career.

- Read as sales: business development here is the guest's whole career of landing opportunities for agencies, not cold calling alone.
