# G_yt_cGsCgJh2bsU_en — movie poster

## Term

Welcome to the one sheet, the show where they talk about movie poster design, hosted by Stuart Schuster — and the poster under review is a billing block, where all the information takes up a very specific amount of space. Movie poster appears twice as the object of the show: the format is the image itself, very dark, almost completely black background, with red at the very top to sandwich everything and anchor it together. The same episode argues about the details — one host thinks a pinned element is distracting and does not go, and some designer was heartbroken when they had to pin it on there — and the old VW ads follow the same format, information nicely tucked below the main image.

- Read as the black background being a mistake: the darkness is the format; the argument is about the pinned element.
