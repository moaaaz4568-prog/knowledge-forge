# G_yt_bSCxlUGatAI_en — properties panel

## Term

You can access more than half of Illustrator's functions and tools in this one panel alone, the speaker says — with a shape selected you go into the properties panel and reach transform and scaling options among other things, and with text selected the panel changes again. Properties panel appears four times as the working hub: select, move and edit design assets inside of groups, and assign a hotkey of shift and G because command G is grouping and G on its own is the gradient. The same hub records a scale up action at 110 of original scale — then the same for scaling down by 10, typing 90 over the original scale.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a menu: the panel changes with the selection, showing transform for shapes and text options for text.
- Read as the hotkeys being defaults: shift and G is a custom shortcut the speaker assigned himself.
