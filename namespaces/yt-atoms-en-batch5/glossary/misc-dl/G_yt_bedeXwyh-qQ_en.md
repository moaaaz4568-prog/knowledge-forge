# G_yt_bedeXwyh-qQ_en — typeface

## Term

The speaker tours typefaces for a retro illustrative poster: zyo, tall and narrow, matches the retro vibes — but only comes in uppercase letters, which he thinks works for headings. Typeface appears thirteen times as the unit of the tour: jupiter in upper and lower case, rubik with 10 different versions in the download, premesh with decorative motifs built in, totally free for commercial use, a typeface he would personally add into his arsenal. Every example of typography in the design is a typeface judged on the same terms — its style, its versions, its price.
