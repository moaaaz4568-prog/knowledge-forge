# G_yt_bD83ZNYs98Q_en — magic wand tool

## Term

The speaker pulls the tolerance really high on the magic wand tool so it selects all of his objects even though they are not the same full colour. Magic wand tool appears four times, always around the tolerance setting: high tolerance for a wide selection, low tolerance — as he advised before — because it narrows down the selection. The tool sits among his Illustrator habits: point type objects, the select same function for fill colour, shape and stroke weight, used often in his graphic design process.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as high tolerance being better: the speaker uses high tolerance for a broad sweep and low tolerance when the selection must be narrow.
- Read as a one-click fix: the tolerance has to be set deliberately for the objects in front of you.
