# G_yt_cLieMKqT1fc_en — the chosen

## Term

The host is honored to have Big James — the amazing and talented Abe — on today to chat about the chosen series, which is currently going into season four. The chosen appears twenty-seven times as the show the whole interview orbits: a line they always say on the chosen, get used to different, and the guest's path from a degree in linguistics with a minor in psychology in Spanish, and a Master's in dramatic arts from USC, to being in an environment with twelve other individuals all the time. The same interview turns practical at the end: faith-based media does not have Hollywood budgets, the work is a forever side hustle, and even when he is done with the chosen he has to find out where the money is coming from next.
