# G_yt_bridFGBYH5Y_en — deadlines

## Term

Tight deadlines give the speaker more of a sense of urgency in her work — and in a graphic design workflow, if you don't have tight deadlines from your client, or any deadlines at all, make some for yourself. Deadlines appears seven times as the tool: it can be a kind of rewiring of your work habits. The same talk is about the rest of the life around the work: a healthy mind is one you want for a graphic design project, as well as positive well-being and a positive life outside of work — only your projects become your life, and you have to know when to stop.
