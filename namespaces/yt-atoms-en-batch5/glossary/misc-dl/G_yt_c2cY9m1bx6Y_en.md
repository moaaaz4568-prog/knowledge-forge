# G_yt_c2cY9m1bx6Y_en — funnel

## Term

If you cannot answer each of them at once, without thinking, the speaker says, then you are not taking your business seriously enough — and this video will help you. Funnel appears thirty-three times as the thing being measured: the click-through rate over the last seven days from video viewing to the first step in your funnel, and the net conversion from that first step to the final conversion stage. The same video weighs the funnel against the workshop — he is not telling you not to do a workshop, because it was his first experience — and measures the total revenue that can be attributed to this week.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the video itself: the funnel is the path from video viewing to final conversion, not the video.
- Read as one number: the funnel is measured in stages — click-through rate, then net conversion, then revenue.
