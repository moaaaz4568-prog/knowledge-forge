# G_yt_c1AjlbD8T_A_en — advertisers

## Term

They study your behaviors, the speaker says, and they know which type of ad will best suit you, because it is a win-win for them. Advertisers appears sixteen times as the paying side of Zuckerberg's business model: he wants billions of users that he can get advertisers to pay money to reach, and that is all it is. The same talk turns the lens on the viewer — once you understand everything in life as a business, you start to see the game for what it is, a system of its own — and warns that if you are not going to spend thirty thousand on ads a month, you might as well not waste your money on ads.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the people watching ads: advertisers are the ones paying to reach the users, not the users.
- Read as a win-win meaning fair: the win-win is the platform's, and the speaker's point is that the game has its own system.
