# G_yt_c8Q-ykjAn3w_en — acquisition

## Term

One of the goals, the speaker says, is to have the opportunity to sell your agency — and the question he puts to the buyer is what you would change the next day after you bought the business, and to start doing that stuff now. Acquisition appears seven times as the deal being studied: acquisitions are really hard, most sophisticated buyers will see when a business is not integrated, and a financial acquirer may make decisions the seller does not understand. The same talk insists the opportunity matters even if you do not plan to sell, because something in your life is going to change and you want to have that opportunity — and the vision, the process and the revenue are the things to get right first.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as selling being the goal: the goal is the opportunity to sell, and the work is to start doing now what you would change after the sale.
- Read as the buyer understanding everything: the talk says a lot of buyers just don't understand, and that is why they make certain decisions.
