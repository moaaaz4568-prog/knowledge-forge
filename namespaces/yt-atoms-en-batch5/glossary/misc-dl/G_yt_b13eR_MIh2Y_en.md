# G_yt_b13eR_MIh2Y_en — high ticket

## Term

High ticket here is the starting point the speaker recommends: start off with high ticket because it allows somebody on a smaller scale to start making money while providing a transformation. The phrase appears four times, beside start off, smaller scale, and start making money, in a video about building a personal brand to seven figures. The audience matters as much as the price — the content must serve a financially qualified audience, delivering value to the public. The speaker's own path shows the scale: hundreds of thousands of followers and hundreds of millions of views, with a product that can eventually make millions a month beside the service.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And we can start off with high ticket because that allows somebody on a smaller scale to start making money and providing a transformation to the people they can help." — sources/S_yt_b13eR_MIh2Y_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as charging more for the same thing: high ticket here is the entry offer for a smaller-scale seller, chosen because it funds the transformation the buyer gets.
- Read as a mass-market play: the speaker ties high ticket to a financially qualified audience, not to the general public.
