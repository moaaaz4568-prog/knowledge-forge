# G_yt_bkZ_xujsENM_en — attribution

## Term

The whole problem with attribution, the speaker says, is that not every piece of content needs to bring you a customer — it's the compounding effect, the invisible comments and conversations that bring you more sales. Attribution appears three times as the mis-measurement: the work is helping creators build brands and brands engage like creators, learning from creators, defining brand strategy. The ads he admires prove the point — the Apple 1984 commercial that looked like a movie, Nike ads that look like movies — and Mr Beast, great at capturing and managing attention, building a massive empire on storytelling.
