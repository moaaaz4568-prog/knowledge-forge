# G_yt_cgBLrEGLFH8_en — value model

## Term

A value model is the ladder the speaker draws on his webinar to show a prospect where they are, where they could get to and how to climb. Value model appears eleven times, next to ladder, currency of value and strategy session, and the speaker credits the idea to his friend Matt Church, who learned it from Dan Kennedy. His example is the three key pieces every value model must have: a currency of value such as money or time or status or happiness, a location, and an aspiration. Without a value model the speaker says you are selling a solution to a problem instead of a much bigger possibility.
