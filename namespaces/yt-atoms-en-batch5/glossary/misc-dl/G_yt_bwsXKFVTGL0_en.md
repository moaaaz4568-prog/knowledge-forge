# G_yt_bwsXKFVTGL0_en — typographic logo

## Term

The video collects designs where the typography was so bad that a designer made it look like a naval officer took his own life — and the speaker's point is that some designers think they can make a huge change in the world. Typographic logo appears once, for Awesomeness: the designer's only question is why, since the designer connected the D with the p, took a little step back, looked at it, and saw what the speaker can see now. The same video wanders from pineapple on pizza to Michelangelo's statue of David, eating pizza one weird way, because the joke is that bad type turns up everywhere.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a good logo: the Awesomeness logo is the example of typography gone wrong, not a model to copy.
- Read as the D and the p being a detail: the connection is the whole joke — step back and you see it.
