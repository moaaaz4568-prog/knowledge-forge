# G_yt_cC5N4xIkhCU_en — skill

## Term

The speaker opens with the disclaimer that not everybody gets in, and then gets to skill number one: problem discovery and business analysis, by far the most important one on the list. Skill appears thirty-six times as the unit of the whole course — each skill a thing to build, from the tutorials people jump between, tool to tool, landing no clients after six months, to the automation process that serves the client. The same talk ties every skill to the client: the reason clients are not happy is that the expectation they have is higher than the results they got, and showing you care about the client experience is what makes you look professional.
