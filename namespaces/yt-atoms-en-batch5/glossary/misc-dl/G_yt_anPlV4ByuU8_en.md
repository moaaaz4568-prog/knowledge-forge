# G_yt_anPlV4ByuU8_en — click-through rate

## Term

Click-through rate is one of the four numbers the speaker reads together when judging a YouTube video after upload: impressions, click-through rate, retention, and traffic sources. The phrase appears 10 times, beside impressions, retention, and channel, in a video about finding where the bottleneck really is. It matters only in combination — if videos are not getting views from potential buyers, it hardly matters whether a landing page converts at 20% or 40%. The speaker's use is diagnostic rather than promotional: the rate tells him which channel's numbers to compare and what to fix next, not how good a single video is.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the whole verdict on a video: the speaker insists the rate is read together with impressions, retention, and traffic sources, never alone.
- Read as a landing-page metric: the rate here belongs to the YouTube video's own performance, and the landing page's conversion is the separate number it is contrasted with.
