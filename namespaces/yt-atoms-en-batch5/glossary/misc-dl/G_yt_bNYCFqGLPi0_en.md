# G_yt_bNYCFqGLPi0_en — expected value

## Term

If you have a bet with 50/50 odds where the potential win is five times greater than the loss, the speaker says, then the expected value is heavily tilted in your favor. Expected value appears three times as the arithmetic of a good bet — the same arithmetic he applies to his life: he dropped everything he didn't want to do and immersed himself fully in business, and he asks what he will regret not doing when younger, and what he will beat himself up for. The body is part of the same calculation: he becomes much more irritable when he doesn't get enough sleep, and taking care of it is the long bet.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So, if you have a bet with 50/50 odds, where the potential win is five times greater than the loss, then the expected value is heavily tilted in your favor." — sources/S_yt_bNYCFqGLPi0_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as gambling advice: the bet is the speaker's model for decisions, applied to business and health as much as to money.
- Read as tilted in your favor meaning a guarantee: expected value is a tilt over many bets, not a win on one.
