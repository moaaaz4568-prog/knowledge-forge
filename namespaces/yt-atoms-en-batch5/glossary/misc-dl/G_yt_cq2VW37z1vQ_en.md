# G_yt_cq2VW37z1vQ_en — Benson

## Term

Benson is the speaker's own AI copywriting app, and the video is a demonstration of it turning boring emails into emails that crush it. Benson appears fifteen times, next to negative hook, refiners and hook, and the speaker says it was trained only on the top performing emails, ads and sales letters. His example is the refiners themselves: copy a text, come back over to Benson, paste it in, save it, and modify it, which is the whole workflow in four moves. Chat and Claude are allowed for finishing touches, but the speaker says Benson is the tool for most of the work.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a chatbot: Benson here is a copywriting app trained on top performing copy, not a general chat tool.
- Read as a magic button: the speaker shows the copy paste click workflow, so the app still needs the user's emails and judgement.
