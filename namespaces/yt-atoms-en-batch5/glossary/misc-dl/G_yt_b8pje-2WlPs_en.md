# G_yt_b8pje-2WlPs_en — old money

## Term

The speaker wraps up an analysis of old and new money vacation destinations in North America, and old money appears twenty-five times as one half of the frame — the wealthy who go to summer on the East Coast, against the new money enclaves growing along the Atlantic coast in the southeast. The southeast, with its economy grown over the last 30 to 40 years, has developed old money and new money enclaves, but the speaker notes one place is still a ways from truly becoming a super upscale exclusive new money enclave — one to watch for a future place on the East Coast. Old money here marks established wealth with established summer places; new money marks the newer, still-forming enclaves.
