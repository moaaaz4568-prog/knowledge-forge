# G_yt_bhDlYPofdZE_en — what does this make possible

## Term

The whole source is a run of short leadership lines, and the one the speaker lands on is the reframe: instead of the things out of your control, say what does this make possible. What does this make possible appears once, as the replacement for the complaints around it — stop talking about your customers' problems and they stop paying attention; if your people aren't making mistakes they're not trying hard enough. The speaker is going off script, and the line is the script he reaches for: possibility instead of control.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as positive thinking: it is a management reframe for things out of your control, not a mood.
- Read as making mistakes meaning carelessness: the speaker treats mistakes as proof people are trying hard enough.
