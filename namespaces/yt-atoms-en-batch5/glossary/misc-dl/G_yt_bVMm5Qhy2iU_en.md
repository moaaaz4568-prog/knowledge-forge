# G_yt_bVMm5Qhy2iU_en — focused niche version

## Term

If he had to start completely over today — zero audience, zero money, zero team — and make his first $10,000 with Claude, the speaker would build the focused niche version, because nobody is building it yet while everyone chases the next shiny AI model. Focused niche version appears once, as the whole strategy: pick one narrow audience, here pre-med students 6 to 12 months out from their MCAT, a medical exam college students in the United States must take for medical school. He asks Claude for 10 viral script concepts targeting those students, because most products fail not from being bad but because nobody can find them and nobody understands what they do in the first 5 seconds.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as pick any niche: the version works because it is focused on one exam, one audience, one window before the test.
- Read as the $10,000 being a promise: it is the speaker's plan for starting over, with Claude as the tool, not a guaranteed result.
