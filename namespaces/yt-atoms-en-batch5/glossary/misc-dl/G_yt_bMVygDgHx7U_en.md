# G_yt_bMVygDgHx7U_en — Social Media Marketing World

## Term

When the speaker started planning the event in 2007, there were no pages and no ads — and the event became Social Media Marketing World, run by Michael Stelzner, who also owns Social Media Examiner and has a very unique way of getting people on stage. Social Media Marketing World appears three times as the proof of the story: you can do what he did, ten times faster, ten times cheaper, ten times easier, because he was doing it a hundred years ago, in dinosaur years. The same talk weighs becoming the actual CEO against leaving all this stuff behind, and looks back to an agency started in '99, when the internet was just getting popular.
