# G_yt_bVSiLqS9IUs_en — AI optimization

## Term

The speaker is building a separate application with Latido — a service to sell to clients — because AI optimization remains a mystery to many companies. AI optimization appears once, as the unnamed skill the whole business is built on: drafts are created, shown in an articles tab, approved, edited, and published directly to the website with one click. In his opinion it is one of the biggest business opportunities right now, precisely because no one knows how to go about it.
