# G_yt_cYSR71REvfA_en — job

## Term

A job in this source is the speaker's redefinition of employment as a single client relationship, and his whole argument is that one job is as fragile as a business with one client. Job appears twelve times, next to money, yolo and economy, and the speaker says he would rather hold ten jobs or a hundred jobs than depend on one payer. His example is eighteen people paying him a thousand or two thousand dollars a month: if one fires him he simply finds another, which is the safety a single job never gives. Jobs in the plural is the speaker's own remedy, a portfolio of payers instead of one employer.

## Live Example

> "build a business or if i'm going to get a job i'm at least going to get 10 jobs okay i'm going to get 100 jobs because at the end of the day people pay me i'm still literally their employee right because they pay" — sources/S_yt_cYSR71REvfA_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a career: a job here is a payer relationship, so losing one job is losing one client, not losing a career.
- Read as hustle for its own sake: the speaker's many jobs are a safety structure, not a boast about working more hours.
