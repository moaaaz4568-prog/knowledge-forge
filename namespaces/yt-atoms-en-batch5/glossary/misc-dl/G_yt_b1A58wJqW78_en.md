# G_yt_b1A58wJqW78_en — launch strategy

## Term

A launch strategy here is the thing the speaker is most known for: she says it is one of the things she is most known for, because she had to mirror it out. The phrase appears twice, beside most known for, mirror it out, and launching, in a video about taking 20 traders to work one-to-one from November 29th. The strategy works by calling out: she is constantly calling out the people she wants to go off of, so the right traders recognise themselves. The interest it generates is the proof — the reaction is that someone is going to be getting this thing, and the launch is built to produce exactly that feeling.
