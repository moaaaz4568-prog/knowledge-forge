# G_yt_bILUZjZq4zI_en — identity

## Term

Looking at a set of designs, the speaker asks why it is a problem to come up with a design concept this way: the designs are so similar they lose any kind of identity, easily forgotten due to their lack of uniqueness. Identity appears once, as what the copycat designs sacrifice — and the speaker contrasts them with a bad choice for a logo that he still likes for its originality and the thought behind its approach. A design in his top 10 earns its place the same way: by being thought about, not by resembling the rest.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as identity meaning the logo alone: here it is the design's distinctness, what keeps it from being forgotten.
- Read as similarity being safety: the speaker treats designs that look alike as the problem, not the solution.
