# G_yt_at73ztkiTmE_en — recurring revenue

## Term

Recurring revenue here is money that arrives every month until the customer leaves — and the speaker's warning is that every single wallet is going to die at some point, so all recurring revenue will get to zero. The phrase appears 12 times, beside wallet, die, and zero, in a video about business decisions and pricing. The same speaker refuses to think about things that will change, saying he will not pretend to know more than the OpenAI engineers about AI. His pricing advice follows the same caution: asking someone who gave you zero to give you one, two, or three thousand dollars a month is a big jump, so create some sort of inertia first.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as guaranteed income: recurring revenue here is explicitly temporary — every wallet dies, and the speaker's point is that it all reaches zero eventually.
- Read as a pricing trick: the speaker ties the revenue to real inertia and to giving value, warning that giving stuff away for free satisfies the customer's self-interest but not yours.
