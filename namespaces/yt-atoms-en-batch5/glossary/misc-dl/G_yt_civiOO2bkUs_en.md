# G_yt_civiOO2bkUs_en — cold email

## Term

Cold email is the speaker's whole subject, and he breaks down what makes it successful from infrastructure and leads to offer and framework. Cold email appears fifteen times, next to framework, pain points and competitor, and the speaker's central claim is that helping the prospect's competitor is the most compelling proof there is. His example is the email itself: if he got an email saying someone had helped his biggest competitor, he would want what they are doing, because the strategies work for them. Cold emails in the plural names the same messages when the speaker talks about the list and the scraping that shapes what they say.

## Synonyms

- cold emails

## Live Example

> "By no means am I going to say that you're going to get all these results because there's a lot that goes into making cold email successful from the infrastructure and leads and offer and all that stuff." — sources/S_yt_civiOO2bkUs_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as spam: a cold email here is a researched message built on infrastructure, leads and offer, not a bulk blast.
- Read as a numbers game: the speaker's cold email succeeds by naming the competitor and the pain point, so volume without relevance is the failure.
