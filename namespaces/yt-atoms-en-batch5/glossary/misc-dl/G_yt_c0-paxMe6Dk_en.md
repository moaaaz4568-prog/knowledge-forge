# G_yt_c0-paxMe6Dk_en — gradient

## Term

The speaker clicks and moves a shape around independently of everything else, and the shape's orientation can be changed — and the gradient will also change relative to the design orientation. Gradient appears nineteen times as the effect under review: add a gradient, press G for the gradient tool, draw a line to stretch it in any which way, and it still will not spread across every synced component of the design. The tip of the video is to experiment and take time to decrease the distance between each swatch, because the small issue listed on some areas is worth fixing on an actual project.

## Synonyms

- gradients

## Live Example

> "we can add a gradient but it wouldn't spread across every sync component of that design we just need to press G for the gradient tool where you can access in the toolbar and draw a line to stretch the gradient in any which way" — sources/S_yt_c0-paxMe6Dk_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a colour: a gradient is the blend you stretch with a line, not a single colour.
- Read as spreading everywhere: the gradient will not spread across every synced component — that is the limit the speaker names.
