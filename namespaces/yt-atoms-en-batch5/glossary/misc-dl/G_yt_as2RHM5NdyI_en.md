# G_yt_as2RHM5NdyI_en — AI process

## Term

An AI process here is one weekly addition to a business: stick with it for 12 weeks and implement one new AI process per week, and by week 12 the time saved starts to increase exponentially. The phrase appears twice, beside 12 weeks, per week, and time, in a complete guide for people who have never used AI before. The pattern is always the same — the AI prepares the message, a human approves it to make sure it sounds natural and accurate, and then it goes out in minutes instead of hours or days. The speaker's warning is about order: new to AI and automation, do not start with sales — gain experience in business administration first.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I promise , if you stick with it for 12 weeks and implement one new AI process per week, by week 12 the time will start to increase exponentially." — sources/S_yt_as2RHM5NdyI_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as full automation: the process here always keeps a human approving the message before it is sent, so the AI prepares and the person decides.
- Read as a one-week project: the promise is twelve weekly processes in a row, and the exponential time saving only starts around week 12.
