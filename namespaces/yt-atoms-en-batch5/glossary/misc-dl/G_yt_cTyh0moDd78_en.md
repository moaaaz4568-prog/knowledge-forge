# G_yt_cTyh0moDd78_en — time audit

## Term

A time audit is the speaker's prescribed check on where a week actually goes, and he presents it as the first step before any talk of tactics or pricing. Time audit appears four times, next to muscle memory, revolving pricing and leadership style, and the speaker guarantees that nobody in the room knows what they are doing in a week. His example is a week that looks pretty good from the outside while the hours quietly go nowhere, which is why the audit has to come before the plan. The audit is a measurement, not a schedule, and it exposes the gap between the week the speaker thinks he had and the week he really had.
