# G_yt_bGntuFu1xx8_en — right people on the bus

## Term

Get the right people on the bus and let them drive is amazing advice when you have amazing people and you can afford to get the right people, the speaker says — quoting the Good to Great idea that the question to ask yourself is who. Right people on the bus appears once, paired with the companion phrase the right people on the right seat, which he uses in the same breath. The advice sits among his other working habits: a zero click name he finds interesting, agents his company just launched, and self-learning loops that create drafts for him after meetings, which he then changes, especially when they are really bad.

## Synonyms

- right people on the right seat

## Live Example

> "Get the right people on the bus and let them drive is amazing advice when you have amazing people and you can afford to get the right people." — sources/S_yt_bGntuFu1xx8_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as hire anyone and step back: the advice only works, in the speaker's own condition, when you have amazing people and can afford them.
- Read as let them drive meaning abdication: the speaker still changes the drafts his loops produce; the driving is within his business.
