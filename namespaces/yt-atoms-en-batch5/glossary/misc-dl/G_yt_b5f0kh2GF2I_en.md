# G_yt_b5f0kh2GF2I_en — typography

## Term

The speaker walks through a showcase of outstanding typography from around the world — 182 creatives from 36 countries, 166 pieces of type design — and typography appears fourteen times as the thread tying the tour together. It is the craft itself: type to admire, type to edit in Illustrator, type to protect, with his much-loved fonts kept safe if his computer is damaged or stolen. The tour ends at Type Wolf, a resource that gives web designers and developers daily inspiration and pointers on typography. In this source the word covers both the finished pieces and the working practice of handling type.
