# G_yt_bV-TSMepYMg_en — digital typography

## Term

The speaker shows two ways to make digital typography, starting on paper and finishing in Illustrator — reboot Illustrator after the paper step and the drawing becomes digital type. Digital typography appears three times as the product of that process: a quick video on doing it very easily, with fixed preset settings or manual settings, and the ignore white option so the background does not rise. The same video is personal too — an unboxing video for the channel when the item arrives, and tour graphics for the channel if you have something physical, like a shirt.
