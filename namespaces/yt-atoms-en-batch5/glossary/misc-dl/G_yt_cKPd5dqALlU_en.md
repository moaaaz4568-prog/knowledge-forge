# G_yt_cKPd5dqALlU_en — personal brand

## Term

You can literally have never made a piece of content online and have a tremendous personal brand, the speaker says, because personal branding is not viewership. Personal brand appears twenty-seven times as the thing to build apart from views: it needs to be attached to Tom Noske, because for some reason you relate to him talking about this topic more than anyone else, and money makes the world go around, so platforms push people to your video. The same talk adds a catalyst: add it to something and it increases the temperature, raises the friction, raises the energy, raises the reactions, and those reactions create an output — and the periods of high effort always come with a downturn in the work, because they are too difficult to maintain.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as viewership: personal branding is explicitly not viewership — the brand exists without a single piece of content.
- Read as the catalyst being the brand: the catalyst raises temperature and friction; the brand is what it is attached to.
