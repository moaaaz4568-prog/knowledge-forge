# G_yt_b8zBcnk15e4_en — New California

## Term

The speaker says he is involved in New California, a plan to restructure the state by splitting it into two states — the only way, in his view, given a constitution that locks spending in. New California appears four times, always as the proposed new state that would take the workers while cutting management overhead: California has 16 to 17 layers of management, and the new state would cut that to six, ending with 61% of the employees but only about 38% of the state. The hardest challenge he names is on the federal level, in the Senate, where either party would oppose adding a state that might shift the balance.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a secession: the plan is to carve a new state out of California, not to leave the union.
- Read as a population split: the 15% and 85% shares are shares of the state, with about 60-plus percent of employees in the larger part and virtually all senior management in the smaller one.
