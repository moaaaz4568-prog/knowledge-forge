# G_yt_bhKlkg_2Wcc_en — positioning

## Term

This is my beginning, the speaker says, because when he works on positioning, the question is what aspect of the service and product he wants to master. Positioning appears seven times as the work itself: a brand 100 years old whose founder isn't there, so you work with the person in charge; a good work culture that people talk about, especially the new generation; the role of the person or company you're working with to tell you what they already have, so you arrive prepared with the data. The goal, in his words, is the problem in the current situation — and positioning is naming the aspect to master.
