# G_yt_c0tYmRlgsDo_en — deserve

## Term

On the call, the speaker tells him he is not ready: he is not committed to his vision, and in himself he does not have the belief that he even deserves the success. Deserve appears nine times as the claim at the centre of the rant — two thousand a month is not him, he is more than that, he was not born and did not win the race just to settle for it. The same talk refuses the car too: he could buy himself a car, but he is not yet at the level where he could buy a car for every person that he loves, and it is not even about the material.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "you're not ready you're not committed to your vision like in yourself you don't have the belief that you even deserve the success okay because a lot of people don't really understand" — sources/S_yt_c0tYmRlgsDo_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as arrogance: deserve here is the belief in your own success that the speaker says is missing.
- Read as wanting a car: the car is the example of what is not yet the level — buying one for every person he loves.
