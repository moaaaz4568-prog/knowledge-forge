# G_yt_at2IrCIwYXI_en — origin story

## Term

An origin story here is the story a creator's new audience has not heard yet — and the reason a channel keeps growing is that new people discover the content every single day. The phrase appears 23 times, with origin stories three more times, beside work with us, get on a call, and figure out, in a video about building an audience on YouTube. The speakers treat it as something earned, not written: they spent 10 years training and going to school, becoming doctors, so their first videos already carried credibility. On a call, figuring out a client's origin story is the first job — before any talk of strategy, the story itself has to be found.

## Synonyms

- origin stories (plural form used in the source).

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a biography to recite: the origin story here is a working asset that new viewers meet for the first time every day, and the speakers mine it from a decade of training, not from a script.
- Read as something you invent once: the speakers say there is only one origin story to tell, and it has to be earned — it cannot be swapped for a better one when you get bored.
