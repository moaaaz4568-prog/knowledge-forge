# G_yt_atKAufREaG0_en — persistence muscle

## Term

A persistence muscle is the one skill this video says matters more than editing, thumbnails, or titles: the trained ability to keep going, built by redefining failure. The phrase appears once, beside struggling, redefining failure, and building, in a video about why most creators quit. The speaker's number is the argument: if 90% of creators quit in their first year, staying for two years puts you ahead of 90% of your potential competition. His mentorship program, he says, works on exactly this — redefining failure and building the persistence muscle — for creators who feel stuck.
