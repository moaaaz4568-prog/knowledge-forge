# G_yt_caAYfs8Elj4_en — Epic email

## Term

Epic email is the name of the sales page the speaker dissects in this third part of his copywriting series, and he calls it one of the biggest sales pages of the year for him. Epic email appears four times, next to sub headline, Todd Brown and four emails, and the speaker shows how his coach kept his headline and repositioned the sub headline he was going to test. His example is the four emails idea: the page promises crazy sales even from a near dead list, and the number four is what Todd loved most. Epic email sales page names the same page in full when the speaker introduces the dissection.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an email campaign: Epic email here is the sales page itself, not the emails it sends.
- Read as a finished winner: the speaker is still letting data show whether the page works, so the dissection is a lesson, not a victory lap.
