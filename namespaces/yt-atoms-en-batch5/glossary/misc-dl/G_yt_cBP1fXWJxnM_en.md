# G_yt_cBP1fXWJxnM_en — adventure

## Term

Beyond the demands of life, there is a call none of us can ignore — the call back to the great outdoors, to the wild — and the video is a short poem for it. Adventure appears twice as the thing that reminds us we were built for more: for some it is a getaway day at a lake, for others the thrill of the open road, and the best conversations happen around a campfire. The same ad ties the feeling to a place and a seller: a love for land and country only instilled by the winding road and the roar of the engine, and the greatest lessons are not taught in a classroom.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trip: adventure here is the call back to the outdoors, not the destination — the lake, the road, the campfire are its forms.
- Read as the ad being the poem: the poem is the ad for Wood Cycle Country, and the adventure is what it sells.
