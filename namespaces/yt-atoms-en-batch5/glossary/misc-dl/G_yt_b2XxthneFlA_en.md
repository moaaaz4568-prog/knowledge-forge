# G_yt_b2XxthneFlA_en — free deliverable

## Term

A free deliverable is the bold giveaway the speaker dares the listener to offer: if you have the stones to give a free deliverable or a free trial, you are going to be way ahead of everyone else. The phrase appears once, beside stones, free trial, and way ahead, in a video about getting clients without hard selling. The speaker's easier path comes first — convince people to meet you or have a quick chat, which is a lot easier than trying to sell — and the deliverable is the bolder version of the same move. Getting booked on 10 podcasts in your niche is the parallel example: no one is talking about it, and the speaker says it gets clients for sure.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as free work for anyone: the deliverable is offered as a deliberate move to get ahead, and the speaker frames it as needing nerve — the stones to give it — not as a default.
- Read as the whole strategy: the deliverable is one bold option beside easier ones, like a quick chat or a podcast booking, and the video's point is the meeting, not the giveaway.
