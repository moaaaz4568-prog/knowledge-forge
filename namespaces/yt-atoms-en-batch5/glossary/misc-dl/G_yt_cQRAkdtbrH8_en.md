# G_yt_cQRAkdtbrH8_en — relevance line

## Term

A relevance line is the first line of the speaker's cold email framework, and its job is to keep the reader reading long enough to reach the offer. Relevance line appears six times, next to poke the bear question, offer and call to action, and the speaker calls it one of the most important lines in the email. His example names the prospect's company while researching DTC brands in the industry space online, which is what makes the email feel written for that reader. A good enough relevance line earns the offer a hearing, and a weak one ends the email at the first sentence.

> "So the first line should always have a relevance line. Okay? And this relevance line is honestly one of the most important lines because it keeps the reader reading your email." — sources/S_yt_cQRAkdtbrH8_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a greeting: a relevance line here proves the sender researched this prospect, it does not merely say hello.
- Read as the whole email: the relevance line only opens the door, and the offer and the call to action still have to do their own work.
