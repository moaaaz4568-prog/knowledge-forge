# G_yt_btuzOYltFEY_en — agency

## Term

The speaker runs a small agency and teaches its leaders to hold a strategic point of view in social media, especially in the beauty industry, where the work is more than just posting. Agency appears twenty-three times as the business being built: a coaching methodology of asking questions, an interview process that tests salespeople by barely talking, and the demand to keep a big company mindset when dealing with big companies. The same talk counts the hours — twenty a week against a hundred — and admits the systems only worked once the architect role was handed over.

- agencies

## Live Example

> "You're a small agency but if we're dealing with the big companies, you have to kind of still have a big company mindset." — sources/S_yt_btuzOYltFEY_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any company: the agency here is a small social-media agency with a team, a coaching methodology and clients to hire for.
- Read as the big company mindset being size: it is how a small agency deals with big companies, not becoming one.
