# G_yt_ai8Vjtkl_zg_en — gradient slider

## Term

The gradient slider is the Illustrator control for adding colour nodes along a gradient: the speaker heads back to the gradient window and clicks the slider to add a new colour, noting the spacing along it does not have to be precise. The phrase appears twice, beside gradient window, colour nodes, and spacing, in a comparison of hand-built Illustrator work with Midjourney output. It belongs to the manual side of that comparison — a small, deliberate adjustment inside a design the speaker is assembling piece by piece. The same design later gets a foil effect and typography the speaker changes countless times, so the slider is one precise step in a long fiddly process.
