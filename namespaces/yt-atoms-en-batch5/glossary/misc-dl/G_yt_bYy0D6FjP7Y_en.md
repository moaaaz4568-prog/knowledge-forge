# G_yt_bYy0D6FjP7Y_en — mindset

## Term

It was his mindset and his confidence, the speaker says, that changed once he joined the program — and once he joined the growth team, with people on all of his calls. Mindset appears three times as the thing that shifted before the results: months with no calls booked, then one or two calls a month, a prospect signing the contract to move forward next month because she watches all his content and understands his sales process and tactics. The payoff he promises is for the committed: if you're really committed with yourself, it's unreasonable not to sign your first client in the next 30 days.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as mindset alone signing clients: the mindset change sits beside a program, a growth team, content and a sales process.
- Read as the 30 days being a guarantee: it is the speaker's claim for the really committed, not a measured result.
