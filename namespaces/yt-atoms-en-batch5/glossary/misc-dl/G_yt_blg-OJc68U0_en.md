# G_yt_blg-OJc68U0_en — commodity market

## Term

AI automation is terrible, the speaker opens, and if you want to start an AI automation agency, his advice is just don't do it. Commodity market appears twice as the reason: there are four rules of the commodity market, which you won't hear about on YouTube, because a lot of these people want you to sell AI services. The automation he describes is more of a liability than a real benefit for the business owner, even if it saves a couple of hours a week — and his own offer runs on two 45-minute calls with the business owner every month, about an hour and a half a month.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as AI automation not working: the claim is that it is a commodity market, so an agency built on it can't win.
- Read as the two calls being the product: they are how little time his own model takes, against the hours the automation supposedly saves.
