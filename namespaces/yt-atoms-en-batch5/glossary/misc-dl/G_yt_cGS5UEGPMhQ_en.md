# G_yt_cGS5UEGPMhQ_en — lifestyle

## Term

If the business is going to impact your lifestyle anyway, the speaker says, why not set it up so that as your business gets bigger, your life gets better? Lifestyle appears twenty-four times as the thing the business is built around: twenty thousand, maybe ten clients, and the wish to have thirty, and the question of what number, hit this year, would make it a really successful year. The same video points to the Payday Playbook, a video that outlines the whole campaign start to finish, and to twelve months ahead of you regardless of what day it is right now — with money, fun, empire and freedom as the words around it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as luxury: lifestyle here is the life the business is set up to improve, not spending.
- Read as the Payday Playbook being the lifestyle: the Playbook is the campaign; the lifestyle is what the business is built around.
