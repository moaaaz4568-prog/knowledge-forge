# G_yt_alsBNAYeRV0_en — personal brand

## Term

A personal brand here is the way out of barely surviving: create one and you can reach millions of people with the suffering of the last decade behind the expertise. The phrase appears 11 times, beside millions of people, suffering, decade, and expertise, in a push to show the world what you understand. The speaker's test is undeniability — expertise so clear that showing it, giving feedback, and saying what to do and not to do is enough. The payoff he names is concrete: the value you provide to a company, a hospital, or an establishment is what gets you paid six figures, sometimes even less, and the brand is what makes that value visible.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as posting for fame: the brand here is a vehicle for expertise the speaker says is already undeniable, and its job is to reach the people who will pay for that value.
- Read as inventing a new persona: the speaker insists the brand shows what the person already understands from a decade of experience, not a character made up for the camera.
