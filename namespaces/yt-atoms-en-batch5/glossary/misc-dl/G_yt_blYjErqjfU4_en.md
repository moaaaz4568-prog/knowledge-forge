# G_yt_blYjErqjfU4_en — global swatches

## Term

The lesson starts with the eyedropper tool — press I on the keyboard, sample a color to base the combination around, or choose one from the color palette. Global swatches appears once, as the step that makes the color editable: the objects had two different color groups for contrast, and because these are global swatches, the speaker can go into the swatch menu with nothing selected and choose a color, and the design updates. The same video closes with the magic wand tools from before, selecting an object and adding the color from the swatch menu — a very quick and easy way to edit a design with preset color swatches.
