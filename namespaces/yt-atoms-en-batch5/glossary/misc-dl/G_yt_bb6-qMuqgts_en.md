# G_yt_bb6-qMuqgts_en — delivery margin

## Term

If the agency had a million dollars in gross income and spent 400k to earn it, the speaker says, then it would have a 60% delivery margin. Delivery margin appears twenty-one times as the number the whole talk is built to explain: how to stay on track ahead of time, understand not only whether you're going to be profitable but exactly where to focus in the business. The same talk weighs the weight of a website build against a $115-an-hour rate, and a software developer working the same project every week for months, very utilized and not feeling stressed. The margin is the lens; the examples are the weights on either side of it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If we had a million dollars in agency gross income and we spent 400k to earn that agency gross income, then we would have a 60% delivery margin." — sources/S_yt_bb6-qMuqgts_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as 60% being the agency's actual margin: it is a worked example on round numbers.
- Read as utilized meaning profitable: the developer can be fully utilized without the margin following.
