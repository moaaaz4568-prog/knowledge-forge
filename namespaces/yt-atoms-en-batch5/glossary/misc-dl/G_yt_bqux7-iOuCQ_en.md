# G_yt_bqux7-iOuCQ_en — rule of thirds

## Term

A good composition is to follow the rule of thirds, the speaker says — areas of interest should hit the major intersection points. Rule of thirds appears once, as the composition rule inside a lesson on light: a very small singular source of light, really intense and far away, like photographs taken outside in early afternoon during harsh intense lighting, and a rim of light behind the subject that makes the design feel more natural. The same lesson covers HDRI light pins in Keyshot — click where you want it to highlight the model — and comparing design details side by side for a packaging or design review.

- Read as the rule being about light: the light is the lesson's other half; the rule is about where interest lands.
