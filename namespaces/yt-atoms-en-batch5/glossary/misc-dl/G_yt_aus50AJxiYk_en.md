# G_yt_aus50AJxiYk_en — Black Friday promo

## Term

A Black Friday promo here is the speaker's own sale for the service he built — a service he calls his baby and refuses to discount carelessly. The phrase appears three times, beside launching, 24th till the 30th of November, and different, in an email the speaker sends about the promotion. His rule is blunt: there is no in between, either pay the price or do not pay at all, because cutting the price disrespects the value. The promo is deliberately different from all the other promos being done by everybody else, and its stated aim is a better Christmas for everyone — more clients, not cheaper ones.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a site-wide discount: this promo is for one named service, run on the speaker's own terms, and he explicitly refuses the middle ground of a small cut.
- Read as a plea for sales: the speaker frames the promo as confidence in the service's value, and the email is sent to people who already know the price.
