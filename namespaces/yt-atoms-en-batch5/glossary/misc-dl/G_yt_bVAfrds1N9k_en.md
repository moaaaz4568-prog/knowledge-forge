# G_yt_bVAfrds1N9k_en — curve tool

## Term

The Illustrator curve tool sits right next to the pen tool in the toolbar — press P on your keyboard — and the speaker shows how to get creative with it in the tutorial. Curve tool appears seven times, always as the easier way to draw curves: it might take some time getting used to, and adjusting the curve to what you want, but in his experience it is a lot easier than the pen tool. The same video closes on soft light as a blend mode, because it blends in well with the design, and asks whether you have ever used the Illustrator curve pencil.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a replacement for the pen tool: the speaker compares them and calls the curve tool easier for curves, not a universal substitute.
- Read as easier meaning instant: he warns it takes time getting used to and adjusting.
