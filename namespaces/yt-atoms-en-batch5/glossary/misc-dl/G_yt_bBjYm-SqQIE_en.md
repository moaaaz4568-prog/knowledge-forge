# G_yt_bBjYm-SqQIE_en — TAM

## Term

Before choosing a tier of cold email, the speaker asks whether the economics check out and whether you have a real TAM. TAM appears six times as the gate: if the economics check out and there is a real total addressable market, the next question is which tier of cold email makes sense for where you are. The offer is for companies with a proven product and paying customers who want to find out if cold email is actually a viable channel — and when you nail the offer, you can cover 100% of your market almost immediately because you haven't exhausted them with bad campaigns.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If the economics check out [music] and you have a real TAM, the next question is just what tier of cold email makes sense for where you are." — sources/S_yt_bBjYm-SqQIE_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as everyone who might buy: the speaker requires a real TAM, tested against economics, before picking a tier.
- Read as a permanent tier choice: winning campaigns stop working, and the speaker says you must plan for what happens when they do.
