# G_yt_bOYApeAr6DM_en — banner ads

## Term

Early in his career the speaker was designing and building little things — landing pages, micro sites, and of course banner ads, because everybody had to design and build banner ads back then. Banner ads appears seven times as the entry-level work that built his book of business: agencies kept calling to ask for landing pages and banner ads, his first client might have been Casio, and for a very long time almost all of his clients were ad agencies across the US. The work is very different now — no more banner ads and landing pages, but custom bespoke websites, web applications, mobile applications and large scale software platforms. The banner ad here is the small, repeatable job that started a studio.
