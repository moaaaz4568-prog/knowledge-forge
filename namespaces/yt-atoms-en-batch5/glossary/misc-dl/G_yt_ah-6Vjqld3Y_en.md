# G_yt_ah-6Vjqld3Y_en — AI audits

## Term

AI audits here are free, short working sessions the speaker offers to people already in his network — he goes in on a Thursday afternoon, sits with a laptop for an hour, and answers members' questions about AI. The phrase appears once, beside network, phone contacts, and free, and the audit is presented as the next step after meetups that position him as an AI expert in his town. Local owners are curious about AI and prefer hiring people they know and trust over strangers online, so the audit works as a trust visit rather than a sales pitch. A concrete use is the weekly slot itself: one hour, in person, questions answered on the spot.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "The next free AI audits for your network, because your client is likely to be in your phone contacts ." — sources/S_yt_ah-6Vjqld3Y_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a paid audit product: the audits in this source are explicitly free and aimed at the speaker's own contacts, not sold as a service.
- Read as a technical systems review: nothing here is audited in the software sense — the session is answering owners' questions face to face, not inspecting their tools.
