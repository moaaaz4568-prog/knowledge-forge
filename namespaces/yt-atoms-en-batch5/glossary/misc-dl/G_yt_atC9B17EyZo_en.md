# G_yt_atC9B17EyZo_en — value per minute

## Term

Value per minute is the measure the speaker wants creators to optimise for instead of views and going viral: how much value each minute — or each sentence, or each second in a short — actually carries. The phrase appears twice, beside views, going viral, and optimise your content, in a video that opens with the speaker realising, two minutes into sitting down, that this was not a good video. The unit changes with the format: per minute for a video, per sentence for a script, per second when you only have a minute. The shift is the whole point — stop chasing the viral outcome and make every minute worth watching.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And so what I want you to shift is instead of focusing around views and going viral, optimize your content around the value per minute or value per sentence or if you're making shorts, the value per second cuz we only got a minute." — sources/S_yt_atC9B17EyZo_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a target number: value per minute is a way of judging content, not a score to hit — the speaker never gives a number for how much value is enough.
- Read as a views strategy: the speaker explicitly contrasts it with focusing on views and going viral, and presents it as the replacement for that focus.
