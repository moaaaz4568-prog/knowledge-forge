# G_yt_b2FVTCeLg4c_en — podcast analytics

## Term

Podcast analytics here are the numbers the hosts pull up on screen to judge their own show: toggle to 90 days and the total views are just over 1,800, with an average duration time of 247. The phrase appears three times, beside 90 days, views, and average duration time, in a conversation between two hosts competing against themselves. The numbers are small and the speakers say so — a hundred people watching feels discouraging beside videos with millions all over social media. The analytics sit beside the business move the episode announces: launching revenue fractional executive services for their clients.
