# G_yt_cgKherxHH_s_en — niche

## Term

Niche is the market the speaker warns against choosing badly, and his whole training is that picking the wrong game to play in means losing before the work even starts. Niche appears twenty-four times, next to service, transformation and long term, and the speaker says people waste months hunting a blue ocean niche that has yet to be sold to. His example is the house flipping business: compare the price of starting it against the price of the second idea, and pick by the price, not by the excitement. Niches in the plural names the same markets when the speaker talks about choosing between them.

## Synonyms

- niches

## Live Example

> "they try to find a specific Niche a specific Market that has yet to be sold to they're like oh this is too this is too competitive there are so many people offering the same thing" — sources/S_yt_cgKherxHH_s_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a small audience: a niche here is the business and market chosen together, not just a demographic slice.
- Read as a safe bet: the speaker says building a sustainable business includes a bit of luck, so the niche improves the odds without removing them.
