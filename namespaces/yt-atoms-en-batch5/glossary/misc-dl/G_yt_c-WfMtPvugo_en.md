# G_yt_c-WfMtPvugo_en — cash flow

## Term

The speaker sold marketing services as part of his initial startup, and he knew the problems marketing agencies had back then: a lot of project work in the late 2000s and early 2010s, and trouble around cash flow. Cash flow appears three times as the agencies' old wound — the reason they were doing project work, and the problem that still shapes how they are hired and fired, since the speaker fired almost all the agencies he hired because they were not leading him. The same talk jumps to the present, where one founder went from under a million to well over thirty million and built a board of advisors of custom GBTs, one for finance, one for marketing, one for sales, one for acquisition, trained on data poured in.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Um, so along the way, because I had been selling some marketing services as part of my initial startup, I knew some of the problems that the marketing agencies had back then, specifically around cash flow, because they were doing a lot of project work in the late 2000s, early 2010s." — sources/S_yt_c-WfMtPvugo_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as having no money: cash flow is the timing of money in project work, not the absence of it.
- Read as the agencies being fired for price: they were fired because they were not leading, and looked to him for ideas.
