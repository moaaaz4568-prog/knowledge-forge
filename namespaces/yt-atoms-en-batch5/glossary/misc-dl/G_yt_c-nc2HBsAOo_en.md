# G_yt_c-nc2HBsAOo_en — AI agent

## Term

In late August, OpenAI said advertising was already generating a billion dollars a year, less than two hundred days after its launch, used by tens of thousands of companies — and the speaker's advice is to invest a small test budget while it is still early, because each advertising platform is getting more expensive as more companies get involved. AI agent appears twice, as the thing you hand your email, ad accounts or customer list to: give it the bare minimum access that will get the job done, and then check what it did. The same video keeps Claude beside OpenAI, and the warning is the same on both: access is the risk, and the check afterwards is the job.

## Synonyms

- AI agents

## Live Example

> "So, if you're going to give an AI agent your email, ad accounts, or customer list, you might want to give it the bare minimum access that will get the job done, and then check what it did." — sources/S_yt_c-nc2HBsAOo_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an ad platform: the AI agent is the tool you give access to your accounts, not the place the ads run.
- Read as trust the agent: the instruction is the bare minimum access, then check what it did.
