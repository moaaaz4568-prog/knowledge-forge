# G_yt_bXw_YfIeN3w_en — radical transparency

## Term

One of the company's values is radical transparency, the speaker says — a conclusion he came to about 120 days ago — and he will be radically transparent: there have been two times he has made over a million dollars. Radical transparency appears once, as the value that frames a long interview about how the business actually grew: most growth came from outbound and content over the last few years, then paid ads, more aggressive now; the mission is to enable the world's businesses to achieve massive scale through simple means. The transparency is the method of the talk itself — the numbers, the channels, the order they worked in.
