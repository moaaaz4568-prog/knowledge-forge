# G_yt_bQjD8t6IITw_en — class of one

## Term

The speaker finally remembers why he started the business — why he wanted to become rich, why he wanted to become wealthy, why he wanted to stay in a class of one. Class of one appears five times as the goal that got him back to being an entrepreneur again: a category of one, not a rank among competitors. The story around it is pure momentum — a private beach club you don't have to be 21 to drink at, five grand by Friday that didn't come but something more incredible happening instead, a sales call where he had to be on his game and felt, in that moment, that he was good.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a wealth number: the speaker ties it to remembering why he started, not to a dollar figure.
- Read as isolation: it is about standing alone as a category, in the middle of a story full of clients, calls and deals.
