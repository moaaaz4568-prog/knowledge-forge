# G_yt_cgsKl4_WkO4_en — sales process

## Term

A sales process is what this video reviews, and the speaker's version is designed to make prospects beg to work with the business. Sales process appears twelve times, next to VSL, mid ticket client and move with the market, and the speaker contrasts the average process with his own: the average one pitches a service, while his removes risk with the or you don't pay wave. His example is the ad to funnel to VSL sequence: the ad calls out the desired outcome, the VSL repeats that there is no risk, and the call never backtracks. The process works because every prospect has already bought a similar service that failed, so risk removal is the only pitch left.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales script: a sales process here is the whole system from ad to call, not the words on the call alone.
- Read as a guarantee of results: the or you don't pay wave removes risk for the prospect, it does not promise the outcome.
