# G_yt_bxgSIV8nSg8_en — systemology

## Term

A big part of systemology, the speaker says, is getting the business owner to start by focusing on the primary product or service and the core target audience they are looking to serve. Systemology appears twenty-one times as the operating discipline of the talk: duplicate first, find the very first gateway product to sell a person when they come into your world, and know what the operating departments look like even as a small or micro team. The same talk warns that many digital agency owners run successful agencies that still depend entirely on them — and the answer it keeps returning to is consistency in what you are already doing, not the big shiny home run.
