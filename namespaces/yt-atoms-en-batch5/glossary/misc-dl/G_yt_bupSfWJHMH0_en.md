# G_yt_bupSfWJHMH0_en — critical thinking

## Term

Many people seem to be aware of certain aspects of critical thought, the speaker admits, but he himself has not always thought critically when making decisions or coming to conclusions. Critical thinking appears forty-seven times as the practice the whole video teaches: knowing what is relevant and what is not in each case, so you can be resistant to manipulation, and questioning other people's beliefs because the emotional attachment isn't there. Since absolute truths are hard to come by, the next best thing is to attempt to move closer to the truth, one factor at a time — even the legs of a table, which only have to be equal length if the table stands on relatively flat ground.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as thinking hard: it is the disciplined check of relevance and factors, not effort or intelligence.
- Read as reaching the truth: the speaker says absolute truths are hard to come by; the aim is to move closer.
