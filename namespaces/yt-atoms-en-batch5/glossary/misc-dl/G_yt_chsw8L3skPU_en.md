# G_yt_chsw8L3skPU_en — builder

## Term

Builder is the identity the speaker claims for himself, and he states it against the two titles he rejects: businessman and founder. Builder appears three times, next to building, businessman and economic downturn, and the interview keeps returning to the joy he finds in putting things together. His example is the t-shirt company itself: he took a mechanical engineering degree and a consulting salary and went to make apparel with two best friends, because building is where he finds his energy. The biggest threat he names is an economic downturn, which is true for any business, and the builder is the person who keeps building through it.

## Live Example

> "I love building. Like I'm I'm a builder at heart. I think for me that is like where I find so much joy and energy." — sources/S_yt_chsw8L3skPU_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a job title: builder here is how the speaker describes himself first and foremost, not a role on an org chart.
- Read as a founder by another name: the speaker says he is not a founder, so the builder identity is a deliberate refusal of that label.
