# G_yt_amvY67pzZXk_en — story interrupt

## Term

A story interrupt is the mid-story break where the speaker shows a picture of someone and promises an unbelievable backstory — you are not going to believe how this guy got on a cover of a fitness magazine. The phrase appears four times, beside 20 minutes, picture, and fitness magazine, in a lesson on holding attention through a long story. It works by delaying the reveal: the answer to the opening question is held for about 20 minutes, and the interrupt keeps the listener inside that wait. The speaker's own example is personal: he connects the device to turning 35 and being 35 pounds overweight, the same age-and-weight coincidence the story turns on.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the story itself: the interrupt is a break inside the story that points at another story, not the main story being told.
- Read as a random picture: the picture is chosen to make the listener need the answer — a cover story that sounds impossible until it is explained.
