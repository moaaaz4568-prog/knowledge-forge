# G_yt_clcsEm-Y6DI_en — headlines

## Term

Headlines are the speaker's whole subject, and he teaches them as formulas that grab attention and convert. Headlines appears eleven times, next to formula, negative triggers and headline generator, and the speaker says teachers and gurus have said headlines are only for one thing: to grab attention. His example is the headline generator itself: five years as one of the leaders of the industry, and the tool shows positive and negative triggers so the viewer gets what the formulas are doing. Headline in the singular names a single one of these attention grabbers when the speaker talks about writing it.
