# G_yt_bjTe6yRmVpw_en — industrial mindset

## Term

Why are the big brands, the Fortune 500 companies in America, considered wrong? The speaker says they have people outside the industry who understand them and are asking why these companies think wrong. Industrial mindset appears once, as his answer: the big players abroad say the Fortune 50 companies in America haven't succeeded in marketing because they think with the industrial mindset of the last 50, 60, 70 years. They think branding is about presenting the brand in a polished image, and they look at the whole internet as if it's just something new, where you upload a few videos.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because they think with the industrial mindset of the last 50, 60, 70 years." — sources/S_yt_bjTe6yRmVpw_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the speaker's own judgment: he stresses he is not the one saying it; the people outside the industry are.
- Read as industrial meaning manufacturing: it is the mindset of the last half-century, applied to marketing.
