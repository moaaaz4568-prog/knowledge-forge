# G_yt_bKgEtNcZPng_en — foreign country

## Term

The speaker was travelling with his girlfriend when they were split up at the border — he was allowed through and she wasn't — and he was stuck alone in this country, in this foreign country in Southeast Asia, not knowing what to do, denied access back to where she was. Foreign country appears twice, both times as the place of the ordeal: stuck in an airport for 24 hours in Malaysia, then losing his passport in a foreign country and going through the whole process of getting it again, with visa situations on top. In the same two months he lost possessions including his camera, had a biopsy for potential cancer and another cancer scare. The phrase carries the helplessness of being stranded somewhere you cannot navigate.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a travel guide warning: the story is a personal run of disasters, not advice about a place.
- Read as the foreign country being the cause: the cause is the border split and the lost passport; the country is where he was stranded.
