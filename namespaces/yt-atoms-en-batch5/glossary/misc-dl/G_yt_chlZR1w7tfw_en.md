# G_yt_chlZR1w7tfw_en — Accidental entrepreneurship

## Term

Accidental entrepreneurship is the speaker's own label for how she became a business owner without ever planning to, and the video is built around that surprise. Accidental entrepreneurship appears once, next to accidental business owner, agency and pipeline, and the speaker's version is simple: she just wanted to be a designer, and suddenly she had to manage clients. Her example is the cost for delivery: she knows what it costs to deliver, so she prices for thirty percent profit, which is a business owner's calculation, not a designer's. Accidental business owner is the same label in the form the speaker uses when she names the person rather than the path.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a business plan: accidental entrepreneurship here means the business arrived before the plan, so the owner learns the business after starting it.
- Read as a complaint: the speaker treats the accident as the story of her career, and the pricing and pipeline work is how she runs it now.
