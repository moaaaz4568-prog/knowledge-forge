# G_yt_brK9DsinI8Y_en — color scheme

## Term

The design process starts in the color scheme explore option: from research on the brand Ripple, the speaker formulated the keywords energy, positive and change, then searched positive to find the palette. Color scheme appears twice as the starting point of the logo work. The rest is the tooling: quick access to the recolor tab at the top, a slider to increase or decrease brightness and saturation, and — back in the research phase — imagery from the brand or the client, from which the new feature can sample colors directly.
