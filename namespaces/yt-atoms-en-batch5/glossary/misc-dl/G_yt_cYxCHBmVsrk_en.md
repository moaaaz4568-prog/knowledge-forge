# G_yt_cYxCHBmVsrk_en — simplest possible route

## Term

The simplest possible route is the speaker's method for reaching a revenue goal: strip everything away until only the shortest mathematical path is left. Simplest possible route appears twice, next to LTV, bottleneck and the two things, and the speaker insists the point is not napkin math but finding the one thing to focus on. His example is a million dollar goal with a thousand dollar product: sell a thousand of those products and nothing else, nothing more, nothing less. Simplest possible path names the same stripped back route when the speaker repeats the question in different words.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a shortcut: the simplest possible route here is the most likely path because it removes complexity, not because it skips work.
- Read as the goal itself: the route is the way to the goal, and doubling the goal first is what forces the route to appear.
