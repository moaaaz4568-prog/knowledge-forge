# G_yt_aoOxj7rvMGU_en — sector

## Term

A sector is one of the three zones the speaker splits a design into: sector 1, then a subheading as sector 2, then body text as sector 3. The word appears eight times, with sectors once more, beside subheading, body text, and designs, in a set of graphic-design tips. The speaker's claim is practical — you will be surprised how much difference this can actually make visually speaking. The same three-part thinking carries into the next tip, where a healthy food product is shown with vibrant vegetables, a healthy person smiling, or simply the food itself, so the design resonates with its audience.
