# G_yt_b5StaIcxT0g_en — vanity metric

## Term

The speaker calls click to rate a vanity metric, one of the stats marketers watch on a regular basis that flatters without informing. Vanity metric appears five times, always attached to click rates that look impressive but mislead: very high rates from old company inboxes that are really spam traps or bot traps inflating the clicks, or rates that simply track the total number of recipients and the engagement level. The honest read, in his framing, is to ask what is behind the rate — the offer cadence, the brand's age, the list quality — before celebrating the number.
