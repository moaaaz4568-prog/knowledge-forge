# G_yt_cRqCrEx05Ro_en — typography

## Term

Typography is the speaker's main instrument in this before and after redesign video, and he uses it to fix cold, lifeless designs by giving them character and hierarchy. Typography appears fourteen times, next to color hierarchy, font psychology and sans-serif typefaces, and every one of the four redesigns gets a typography decision of its own. His example is the tea poster: warm, appealing type that evokes aromatic pleasure, with superior and exquisite set in a refined serif because the words themselves are high brow. Edgy typography that reads loud and angry is the failure case, used on a weight loss design where it fights the theme.
