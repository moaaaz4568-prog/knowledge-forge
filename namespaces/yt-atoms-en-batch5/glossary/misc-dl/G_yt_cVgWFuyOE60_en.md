# G_yt_cVgWFuyOE60_en — identity

## Term

Identity is the speaker's explanation for why people never get what they want out of life, and he argues that the old version of the self has to be replaced rather than improved. Identity appears eight times, next to belief systems, umbrella terms and input goals, and the speaker quotes the chains of identity as too light to be felt until they are too heavy to be broken. His example is a person forcing the old self to work harder while staying stuck in old habits, which is why the situation never changes. What you think defines what you do, and what you do defines what you get, so the identity has to change first.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Anything that you want out of life is through a constant constant journey and progression of not chasing a status milestone, not chasing a goal, but chasing an identity, an avatar, and a version of yourself that you want to become." — sources/S_yt_cVgWFuyOE60_en.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as self belief alone: identity here includes habits, beliefs and actions, not just what the person tells himself.
- Read as a goal: the speaker says to chase an identity and a version of the self, not a status milestone.
