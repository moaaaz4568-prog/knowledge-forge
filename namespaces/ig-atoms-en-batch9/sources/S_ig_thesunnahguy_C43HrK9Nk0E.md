Ramadan is the month of dua, and many people want to know the best way to make dua to Allah. And I'm going to teach you something called the sandwich method, where you structure your dua and say something before and after it, as a lot of people don't know this. And this is the best etiquette when calling upon Allah Azawajal. So you start off by praising Allah. So for example, you say, "Glory and praise is to You, O Allah. I'm ashamed of my sins, and You are the most merciful." So here you mention Allah's mercy as His attribute, then proceed with your dua and ask Allah without holding back. Be engrossed in your supplication and make it personal and intimate. And lastly, the Prophet ﷺ said that every dua is suspended between the heavens and the earth until you make salutations upon me. So end the dua by sending salutations upon the Prophet ﷺ. And it could be as simple as just saying, "Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad," "O Allah, send Your salutations upon Muhammad." So praise Allah and recognize Him as your Creator. Make your dua and then send salutations upon the Prophet ﷺ. And share this video so you can help others make dua as well.
