One day a man saw a young boy sitting sadly. He approached him and asked the boy, "Would you like to have ten million dollars?" The boy got excited and said, "Yes, why not?" The man then asked, "What would you do with it?" As the boy started listing him the things that he wanted to do, the man told him that it has a price for that. The boy got curious and asked him what the price was he has to pay. The man then told him that if he takes the ten million dollars, this would be his last day on earth and he would die tomorrow. The boy went silent for a moment and walked away. He understood that just waking up every day was worth more than ten million dollars. So live your life to fullest because money will not buy you time.
