In the last two years of posting content on Instagram, I've grown my page to more than 100,000, then lost 25,000 followers, then grew it back to 100,000. But here are the three lessons I wish someone told me before that. Lesson one: Pick your one thing. I tried teaching everything I knew, but it only made me look scattered. Experts are experts because they own one piece of knowledge deeply. So find the thing you're best at, the one thing you can talk about endlessly. That's your power. Lesson two: Revamp, don't just repost. Reposting is fine, but revamping is even better. Take your old content and sharpen it. Use a stronger hook, change the angle, tell the same story in a new way. It's how you stay fresh without starting from zero. Lesson three: Post more and fail more. I used to fear posting too much, but posting more was the fastest way to learn. Every post is feedback, every flop is data. The more you publish, the faster you build instincts for the game. Lesson four: Detach from views. Views are a dopamine trap. I learned that the hard way. Yes, they look good. Yes, they feed your ego. But they don't pay your bills. A million random views is worth less than 100 engaged people who trust you enough to buy. The real game isn't attention. It's turning attention into trust, and trust into business. Followers feed your ego, but systems, products, and connection, that's what builds your freedom.
