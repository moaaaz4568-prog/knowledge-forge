A full time VP of sales is going to cost you at least two hundred grand a year in salary. Then you add equity and commissions on top of that. You add ramp time because they're not going to be productive for at least ninety days probably. Then you add the risk that maybe they're the wrong hire and now you've just burned six months and one hundred thousand dollars trying to figure it out. Whereas a fractional CRO costs let's call it ten grand a month. So six months commitment that would be sixty k total on the low end. So you get immediate execution, no ramp time, no equity. If it's not working, you are out in six months instead of being stuck with a bad hire for a year or more. So you are saving at least forty thousand dollars right there. So if you are between a million and five million ARR, you need to build the foundation before you hire a full time leader. Fractional makes sense. Once you got a proven playbook, that's when you might consider hiring a full time VP, but not before that.
