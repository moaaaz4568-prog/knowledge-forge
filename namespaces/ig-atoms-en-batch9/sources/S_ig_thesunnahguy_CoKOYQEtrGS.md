My friend messaged me the other day asking me, "Yo, what time is Jummah prayer?" And I said 12:45. He said, "No, no, no, the prayer, not the khutbah." And I said, "You do know that if you miss the khutbah deliberately, it counts as you missing the Jummah prayer. Because you see, Jummah prayer is when the khutbah begins. And the Prophet ﷺ said that the angels stand at the door writing down the names of the attendees, and when the khutbah begins, they roll up their scrolls and go and sit and listen to the khutbah. Why am I saying this? Because the Prophet ﷺ said that if a person misses Jummah deliberately three times in a row, then Allah places a black seal on his heart. And wallahi, you never know, but this could be one of the reasons why you feel nothing in your heart for Islam and can't get on the deen properly. By Allah, these are small things I feel a lot of people need to know about. Because we are massively misinformed when it comes to things like this, which could be the means to determine our long-term guidance.
