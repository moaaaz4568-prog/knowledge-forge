There's a hadith of the Prophet SAW where he says, "Remember Allah during times of ease, and He will remember you during times of difficulty." You know why this is so powerful? Because those who remember Allah through all the good, they know that Allah will take care of them in times of difficulty. Allah promises to remember those who remember Him. So the next time things are going okay, don't make the mistake of forgetting Allah, because you're going to need Him in bad times.
