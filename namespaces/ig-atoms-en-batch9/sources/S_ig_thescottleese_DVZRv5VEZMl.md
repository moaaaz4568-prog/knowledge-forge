If you're selling X as a better piece of software, you're asking your prospect to make the wrong type of decision. If we are just better software, then the decision is, do I want to upgrade my software? This is not the type of crowd that cares about the latest and the greatest. That doesn't work with them. Instead, you should be selling the idea that their business can be more efficient. You should be selling the idea that their team can get more done in less time. And you should be selling the idea that in a commoditized market, X can help you stand out from your competitors. If you do that, the decision becomes, do I want to improve the way my business operates? Sell the hole, not the drill. See the difference?
