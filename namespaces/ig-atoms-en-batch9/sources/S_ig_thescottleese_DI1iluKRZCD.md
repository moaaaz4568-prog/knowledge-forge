Three principles that get your company from zero to $25 million. Number one, know your ICP and get smaller and smaller. I don't want to hear about your total addressable market being 900 trillion people and you could sell to every company under the sun. No, shrink it down. How many employees? 100 to 200 employees. You simplify the message and you have to come down to three or four things that appeal to everybody. The last thing is document everything.
