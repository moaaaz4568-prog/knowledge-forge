If you're doing most of the talking on a discovery call, you've got it backwards. The people who close the most don't talk about the product on that first call. They ask questions until the buyer says the problem out loud themselves. Here's why. If you tell someone their problem, they're impressed for a second. If you have them say it themselves, they actually believe it's urgent. You can't fake that by pitching harder. You get that by asking better questions and staying silent longer so it feels uncomfortable. The best discovery calls don't feel like a pitch at all. They feel like the other person talking themselves into it while you take notes. Try this on your next call and save this video.
