Founders spend six months building an ideal customer profile and then they ignore it the moment a warm lead comes in. Someone in the network makes an intro. The company does not fit the profile. The problem is slightly different. The budget is smaller, but it's a real conversation, so they take it. Two months later, they have a customer they can't case study, they can't reference sell, and they can't serve as well as the customers the ICP was built around. And they're wondering why the account is already showing churn signals. Your ICP is not a marketing document. It's a filter. A good filter saves you from six months of work that produces nothing repeatable. The right customer is not the next available one. It's the one where everything you have built actually fits.
