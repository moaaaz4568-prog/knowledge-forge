Most people teaching sales right now have never actually sold anything, and I don't mean they haven't sold in a while. I mean they built their entire sales reputation with content on how to sell instead of actually selling. I've been in this space long enough to know the difference immediately. Someone who learned sales from experience talks about the deals they lost, the quarter they missed, the moment they thought they closed someone but didn't, the pain of all of this stuff. But someone who learned sales from frameworks just talks about frameworks. And frameworks are fine when everything goes according to plan, but in 21 plus years of selling now, I've never ever seen a deal going according to plan. Not one. When the prospect disappears when they were supposed to sign, that's not in the framework because the only thing that prepares you for that exact moment is having been in that moment before.
