People think you need fifty sales skills. In reality, you only need five. It's called the five selling rules. You need one skill that fills your pipeline. Pick one channel, calls, referrals, whatever fits your world, and go so deep nobody outworks you there. Second, you need one skill that finds the truth, real discovery, the problem behind the problem. Most reps skip this and wonder why deals go cold. Third, you need one skill that builds trust. Do what you said, buy when you said it, every single time. Boring, and it closes more than charisma ever will. Fourth, you need one skill that moves deals forward. Never leave a call without knowing exactly what happens next, and when. A date on the calendar or a clean no. And lastly, you need one skill that compounds. Keeping in touch with every buyer who ever told you no, because half of them will be ready in a year, and they'll remember who stayed. So no, you don't need fifty skills. You need one from each of these five. Which one are you missing?
