The smartest rep that I ever hired was the worst closer I ever hired. This guy had an MBA from a top school, knew the product inside out, could explain every technical detail, but couldn't close a deal to save his freaking life. I sat in on one of his calls. Prospect asked a simple question, "How does this help me?" He launches into this like ten minute explanation, features, integrations, architecture, use cases. This prospect's eyes just like glazed over and they say, "Interesting, send me some info," and then they ghost him. Of course, I watched him do this more than I should have. I watched him do it over and over, every single call, same thing. That's the problem. Smart people think complexity equals credibility. They think that if they explain everything perfectly, the buyer will be impressed. But buyers don't want to be impressed. They want to be understood. They don't care about your fancy degree and your big words with all those syllables. So if you're the smartest person on your team and you're not closing, now you might know why.
