After working with many early-stage startups, here's something me and Christian wish someone would have told us on day one. Three things we had to learn the hard way. First, your ideal customer profile is almost always wrong. Not a little wrong, completely wrong. The founders who win are not the ones who got this right the first time. They're the ones who updated it based on real conversations with real buyers. Second, the message you think will land almost never does. The message that actually lands comes from listening to how your best customers describe the problem you solve. Not from a brainstorm, not from a brand sprint, from your customers' own words. Third, pipeline does not build itself. The founders who wait for inbound in the first 12 months run out of runway. You have to get the first 50 customers yourself. Founder-led, direct, uncomfortable, and absolutely necessary. We learned all of this by doing it wrong first. You don't have to. You can figure this out the hard way, or you can work with someone who has already done it. Or you can work with me and Christian.
