Here's three ways you can pray the Tahajjud. I'll start with the most virtuous. Number one, you sleep after Isha, then wake up a few hours before Fajr, pray two or more units of prayer, and then go back to sleep and wake up for Fajr later. This is the ideal way to do it. Number two, you go to sleep and wake up 15 to 20 minutes before Fajr and pray two or more units of prayer, then pray Fajr straight after that. And number three is that you pray Isha and before you pray your Witr, you pray two or more units of prayer before going to sleep. And although this is the least virtuous, I remember one time, Ustadh Abu Tameemah, he encouraged me to do this if I can't pray in the middle of the night as a way to simply show Allah that you're trying to seek closeness with Him. SubhanAllah, man, and never forget that the Tahajjud is a secret weapon, and if you really, truly want something bad enough, you will make sure that you pray the Tahajjud for it.
