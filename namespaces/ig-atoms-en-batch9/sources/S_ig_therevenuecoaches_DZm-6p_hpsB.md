74% of the fastest growing startups fail. Not the ones that stalled, the ones who were actually growing. They got momentum and stepped on the gas, hired aggressively, opened new markets, doubled their marketing budget. And it felt like exactly what you're supposed to feel when you're being successful and everything is working. You scale what is working. Except what was working was not the system. It was a specific set of circumstances that existed in one market, with one type of customer, at a particular moment. They scaled before they understood why it was working, so they replicated the activity without replicating the results. More salespeople calling the wrong customers, more ad spend targeting the wrong market, more headcount building features nobody asked for. The growth curve looked incredible right until the moment it fell off a cliff, and by then they had built an organization that needed revenue that was no longer coming in.
