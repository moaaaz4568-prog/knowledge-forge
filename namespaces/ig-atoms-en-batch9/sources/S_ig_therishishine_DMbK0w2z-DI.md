The reason why you always fail to hit your goals, no matter how hard you try, isn't because you are doing it wrong. It's because you are too busy focusing on the wrong things. I recently found this underrated article that explains it perfectly. Let's say I tell you to take a one-foot wide plank, put it on the floor, and walk across it. You can do that a thousand times without failing. But if I take that exact same plank, put it 1000 feet high in the sky, 99% of the people are going to fall. But not because the challenge got harder, but because they were too busy looking down, thinking, "What if I fall?" And the reality is most people wouldn't even try because they are too scared of falling from a thousand feet. But you are doing the same thing to yourself. You know exactly what step you need to take in order to move forward, but you are frozen, thinking about what could go wrong. You are too busy thinking, "What if I fail? What if I mess up?" The path isn't harder. You have just convinced yourself it is. It's not your skills, it's not your goals, it's your mindset. And the second you stop looking down is the moment you start moving forward.
