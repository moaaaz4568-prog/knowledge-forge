Matching every product feature your competitor has will not win you the deal. It never does. It's chasing feature parity instead of building a reason to switch. Three problems with the parity trap. You're always building last, reacting to someone else's roadmap instead of setting your own. Second, buyers can't tell you apart, so they default to whoever's cheapest or biggest. Third, your team burns cycles matching features nobody actually asks for. Differentiation isn't longer a feature list. It's a sharper answer to why you not them.
