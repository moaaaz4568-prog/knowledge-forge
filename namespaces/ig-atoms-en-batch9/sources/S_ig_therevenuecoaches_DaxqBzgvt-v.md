Your best customers are not referring anyone to you, and it's probably not because they're unhappy. It's because you never gave them a reason to. You deliver the product, you check in occasionally, and then you assume the good work would generate word of mouth on its own. Word of mouth does not happen automatically, even when the product is excellent. It happens when the customer has a moment, a prompt, a reason to think of you when someone they know mentions the problem you solve. The founders who build referral into their customer relationships from the beginning close more new business from existing customers than from any outbound motion they run. You already earned the trust. You are just leaving the referral on the table.
