Someone in our office hours last week asked if he should keep closing every deal himself or start handing it off now. He thought handing it off meant he was doing something wrong. Not true. We asked him one thing back: Could someone else using only what you've written down close the exact deal you close? He went quiet. If it's not written down, handing it off right now just breaks what's working. Founder-led sales isn't the problem everyone says it is.
