70% of startup sales hires fail within their first year, and almost every founder says the same thing afterwards: wrong person, bad attitude, just wasn't hungry enough. And we always ask them one question: what did you hand that person when they started? And there's always this pause. Because the honest answer is nothing. A laptop, a Salesforce login, a revenue target. No documented sales process for how deals were actually getting closed. No clear picture on which customers were a good fit. No explanation of what objections came up and what worked to handle them. The founder knew all of that. It was living in their head. They just never wrote any of it down. So the new hire spent six months trying to reverse engineer what the founder did instantly and never could. That is not a hiring problem. That is a documentation problem that gets blamed on one person.
