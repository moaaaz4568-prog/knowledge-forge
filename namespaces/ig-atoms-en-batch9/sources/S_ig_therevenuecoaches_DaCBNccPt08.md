You have 47 leads in your CRM. None of them are real. What you have is 47 people who clicked something, replied to something, or had a meeting that went well enough to end with a no. That's not a sales pipeline. That's a list of open loops that feels like progress because the number is big enough to believe in. Real sales pipeline has a next step. A date someone committed to, a problem they named specifically, a reason why now matters to them. If you cannot answer why a lead is going to close this quarter in one sentence, the lead is not real. It's a number that makes the dashboard look better than it is. Clean your sales pipeline before you add to it. The honest number is almost always smaller, and the honest number is the one you can actually build a plan around.
