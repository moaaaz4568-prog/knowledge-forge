Here's three things you can do after you finished your prayer. Number one, say Astaghfirullah, which means I seek your forgiveness O Allah. And we do this because in our prayer we often find ourselves losing concentration and so we're seeking forgiveness for all our shortcomings during the prayer. Number two, say Allāhumma anta salām wa minka al-salām, tabārakta yā dhī al-jalāl wa al-ikrām, which translates as, O Allah, you are peace and from you comes peace. Blessed are you, owner of majesty and honor. The Prophet ﷺ used to say this after the prayer, and what a beautiful way to show humility to our Creator. And number three, recite the Ayatul Kursi as the Prophet ﷺ said, whoever does so after every obligatory prayer, then the only thing between him and Jannah is death. And share this video so others can learn as well.
