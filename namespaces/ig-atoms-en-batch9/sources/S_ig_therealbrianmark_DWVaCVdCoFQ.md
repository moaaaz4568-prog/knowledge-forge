Never say these words in sales. They make you sound weak and that's why nobody's buying from you. First, I'm just checking in with you. I mean, can you get more desperate than that? Instead, say, I just had a minute to get back to you. This implies that you're a busy person. Second, I haven't heard from you. If you say I haven't heard from you, you sound like a desperate dork that's been chasing them. Instead, say, it's great to connect again. This seems a lot more natural. And the worst thing that you can say is, I think you should move forward today. When you tell a prospect what to do, they'll resist you. So instead, say, where do you think we should go from here? Your prospects like to feel like they're in control. Follow for more value.
