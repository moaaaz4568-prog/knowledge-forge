What can I say about this? SubhanAllah. Who else should everything be about? Who else deserves that our minds are constantly engaged in His remembrance? Who else deserves that we constantly remember Him? Is it not fair that the One who created me and you inside the wombs of our mothers deserves all our respect, praise, and is worthy of our remembrance? And is it SubhanAllah? Is He not the One who, when you were inside your mother's womb, you were a sperm drop and He created you? Is He not the One that gave you your nose so that you could breathe when your mouth was closed when you were an infant? Is He not the One that made the trees on this earth take in our carbon dioxide and give out our oxygen? Is He not the One that spins the earth? Is He not the One that created the sun that gives you daylight? Is He not the One that gives you the food that you have on your plate? Is He not the One that keeps you alive and gave you your internal organs so that you can filter the food that comes in? Is He not the One that gave you everything? Who else should it be about? Allahumma SubhanAllah, ya Amma Yusri Kun.
