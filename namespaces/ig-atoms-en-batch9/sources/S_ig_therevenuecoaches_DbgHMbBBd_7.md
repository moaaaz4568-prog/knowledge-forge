A sales deck that looks good and a sales deck that closes are two different documents. Most people only build the first one. A good looking deck walks them through a product. A deck that closes walks through the buyer's problem, what is costing them, and why you're the fastest way out of it. Do a quick test. Open your sales deck right now and count how many slides are about you versus about the buyers. Most sales decks are 80% you. Flip that and watch what happens to the questions after. Less "what does this do?" More "when can we start?" The sign gets your attention in the room is not what gets you the signature after. Go count your ratio before you send that deck out again.
