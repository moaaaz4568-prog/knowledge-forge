Do not enter a sales call and start by saying, "Hey, this is Scott with XYZ Company." Nobody cares about that, and nobody cares about you. We all unfortunately have met somebody who knows they have a problem but is not doing anything about it. It's because they don't understand why it's important to fix. Same thing in business. Gotta get people to admit they have a problem. Nobody cares about who you are, what you sell, or what your solution is unless they believe that they're struggling with it. You gotta take some time to educate them and make them understand why it's important to solve this problem. But that's not enough to get somebody to buy. Just like an addict, you can't walk up to them and say, "Hey Scott, I got this killer rehab facility down in Malibu. You ready to go?" They have to hit this little thing called rock bottom. Rock bottom in sales is urgency. We have to create urgency. We have to make somebody understand that right now is the most important time to try to solve XYZ problem and challenge. Right now, this is higher up on the priority list than anything else. That's creating urgency that makes people move. Once you've got somebody to admit they have a problem, then and only then is somebody open to hearing about your product, your solution. That's the rehab aspect of it. So find pain, build value, create urgency, and then and only then should you discuss your solution.
