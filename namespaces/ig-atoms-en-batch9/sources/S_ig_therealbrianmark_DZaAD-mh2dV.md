Nine numbers every business owner needs to know. Number one, revenue per owner hour. Figure out the total revenue divided by the amount of hours that the owner works. So if you're making 100k a month, you do 160 hours. The owner's time is worth $625 an hour. Then look at the owner's calendar and ask what can you outsource on this calendar that is not an income generating task that's less than $625 an hour. Do this in order to scale. Number two is churn rate. The total amount of clients you have at the start of the month divided by the total amount of clients that left your program that month gives you your churn rate. Ideally, your churn rate should be under 7%. Number three is conversion rate. Your conversion rate on your sales calls should be 35% or higher if you're trying to scale. Number four is a cash runway. Take your living expenses per month times that by six. You should have that much cash in the bank in case anything ever goes wrong in your business so you're not in a sticky situation. Number five is your ROAS. For every dollar that you spend on ads, you should be making at least $3 back. If you're not doing that, don't scale the campaign. Number six is your break-even point. You need to know the minimum amount of revenue that your business needs to generate before you cover all the costs without generating any profit. Ideally, your recurring revenue should be above that point. Number seven is your profit margins. Percentage of revenue left after all your expenses are paid. Ideally, that number should be higher than 60%. Number eight is ROI. Every dollar that you invest, what's the return on that investment? If you invest $15,000 into a mentor and he helps you collect $100,000 in cash this year, that's a good ROI. Nine is your capacity. Maximum number of clients that each coach on your team can handle before they get overwhelmed. Ideally, you want to be hiring at 80% capacity.
