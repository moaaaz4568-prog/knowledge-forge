Never say these five words in sales. Don't say hurry up, say don't miss out. You want to create urgency. Don't say discount, say limited time offer. Discount sounds like you're doing something for cheap. Limited time offer makes it feel exclusive. Don't say cheap, say affordable. Cheap screams low quality. Affordable seems like they're getting a deal. Don't say basic, say standard. If you say your basic program, it sounds like they're not getting a lot of value. Whereas when you say standard, it seems like that's what everyone does. Don't say different, say exceptional. Different doesn't have a positive word association with it, whereas exceptional does. The words that you choose matter. Follow for more value.
