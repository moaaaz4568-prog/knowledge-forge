Uthman ibn Affan, he would often stand by the graves, and Uthman, he would weep at the grave. And they would ask him, "Ya Uthman, when the hellfire is mentioned, when Jannah is mentioned, you don't cry, but ya Uthman, why is it that you cry at the graves?" And Uthman, there, his beard soaked with tears, he stands there and he says, "Al-qabr awwalu l-manazil al-akhir," that the grave is the first stage of the afterlife. By Allah, brothers and sisters, you could go outside, not look on the road properly, and get run over, and within seconds, you are in the presence of Al-Malik, you are in the presence of the King, you are in the presence of Allah, you are in the presence of Ar-Rahman, you are in the presence of Al-Jabbar, you are in the presence of the One who sees and hears everything on planet Earth, you are in the presence of the One who saw you when you were a baby, you are in the presence of the One who not a leaf does it fall except that He knows about it, you are in the presence of the One, whenever you sin in the darkness of and depth of your own room, He sees everything that you do.
