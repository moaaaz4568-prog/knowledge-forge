The best thing you can do after this Umrah trip is to go and to better your knowledge, your Deen, and then to put it into practice. إِلَى عَرَفَاتٍ سَنَنْضِغَدَا وَشَوْقٌ بِقَلْبِ الْمُحِبِّ بَدَا فَهَذَا مَقَامُ الدُّعَاءِ الَّذِي يُجَابُ فَمُدُّ لِرَبِّ الْيَدَا يُبَاهِي بِكُمْ رَبُّكُمْ فَسَعَدُوا وَيَنْزِلُ الحج عرفه. Hajj is Arafah. The ninth of Dhul Hijjah, that's the only day where you can come and stand here and make dua for your Hajj to be accepted. Remember what the Sahaba did. They didn't just sit and they didn't just seek knowledge and that was it. Wallahi, they got involved with their communities. Whether it's as simple as a youth night, standing at a dawah stall, handing out a leaflet, doing something. Throw yourselves into environment where Deen is present. And the best way you can do that is to be amongst other brothers who are serving Allah's religion. You yourself cannot do it, then at least support them in doing that. Because the Nabi said, those who are present convey to those who are not. لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبْ. Comment the word "future" below and I'll send you the link. Let this day of Arafah be the day you gave, the day you protected, the day you chose to leave your mark, not just for yourself, but for the children of this Ummah.
