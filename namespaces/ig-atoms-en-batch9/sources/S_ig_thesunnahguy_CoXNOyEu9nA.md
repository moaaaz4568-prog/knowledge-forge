If you're going through some type of hardship in your life, then there is such an amazing Hadith to help you get through your difficult phase and give you perspective like never before. On the Day of Judgment, Allah will take a person who never experienced any hardship in this world and lived a life of ease, comfort, and luxury. And He will simply dip him into the Hellfire, and the man will then be asked, "Did you ever experience any pleasures or ease in your life?" And just because of that dip in the Hellfire, he will scream out and say, "No, by Allah, I never experienced any ease or comfort." And then a man who lived a hard and difficult life full of trials and tests, experiencing little to no comfort or ease in this world, he will be taken and simply dipped into Paradise. And then he'll be asked if he had ever experienced any hardship. And he will say, "By Allah, I did not experience any hardship or difficulty in this world." And this here gives us perspective that whatever pain or hardship we go through in this world, it will all be worth it when you set that first foot into Paradise.
