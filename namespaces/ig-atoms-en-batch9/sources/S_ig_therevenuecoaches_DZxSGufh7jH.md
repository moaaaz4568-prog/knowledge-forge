Seventy-five percent of venture-backed startups fail, even after raising millions. The moment money hits the accounts, something shifts. The founder stops selling and starts managing. They hire a salesperson, a marketing person, they build out the team. Six months later, they're in a board meeting trying to explain why the pipeline is empty and revenue has not moved. Because the founder was the one closing the deals, not because they had a sales process, because they had relationships and conviction that nobody they just hired has yet. You cannot hire your way out of founder-led sales until you have a documented sales process, exactly what were you doing and what was working. Most founders never do that. They just hire and hope the new person figures it out. They never do.
