If you've been making dua and it's not being answered, just know that Allah answers your dua in three ways. The first is that He'll answer it immediately and gives you what you want. The second is that He delays it, or He may divert an evil that was about to afflict you. And lastly is that He'll withhold answering your dua in this world and will answer your dua on the Day of Judgment. And when you see what you get and the way He answers it on the Day of Judgment, you would wish that He had never answered a single one of your duas on the earth. And this is why you should never stop making dua, because the results are not always in this world. Allah will respond and He does respond, just in a different way. That's all.
