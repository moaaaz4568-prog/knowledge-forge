One of the best ways to start and end your day is to do the morning and evening remembrance, which are beautiful statements the Prophet ﷺ taught us to say so that our heart remembers Allah. But it's quite long and you may not be able to do all of them. So here's the four most basic ones that you can do. Say them three times each. بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّةِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ عَدَدَ خَلْقِهِ وَرِضَا نَفْسِهِ وَزِنَةَ عَرْشِهِ وَمِدَادَ كَلِمَاتِهِ رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبَّاً وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيًّا I'll put the translation in the comments. Say this after Fajr and between Asr and Isha, but make sure you say it with the present heart and that you understand what you're saying. And I can guarantee you that your life will never be the same.
