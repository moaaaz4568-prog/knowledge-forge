When you're posting on social media, a big mistake that everyone makes is, "I do real estate investing, so I'm only gonna post about real estate investing." You can't just be posting like, "Here's how I can get you these results," and "Here's how I can help you grow your business," and "Here are three tips to help you do this thing." Boring, unfollow. You should have connection, proof, and value. Connection is like, "This is me with my family," "This is me golfing," "This is me watching my favorite sports teams." Value is, "Here are three tips to help you get your next client." Proof is, "Look at all these clients that I'm working with that are getting incredible results." And then documentation is like, "Hey, I'm just going for a walk and I wanted to share something with you." I would highly recommend having one of your best client transformations or a case study of a client that you've worked with pinned to the top of your profile so people can instantly see that you know what you're talking about and that if they follow you, they're gonna get results.
