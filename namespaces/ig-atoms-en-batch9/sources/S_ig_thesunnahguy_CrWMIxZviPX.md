Here's four things you should do before going to sleep every night. Number one, make wudu because the Prophet Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam said that whoever makes wudu and goes to sleep, an angel will reside with him in his bed, and when he wakes up, the angel will ask Allah to forgive him. Number two, cup your hands, read the three quls and Ayatul Kursi and blow a dry spit into your hands three times and wipe over your body starting from the back down to your feet, as this was the Sunnah and is a means of protection during the night. Number three, dust your bed three times as this was done by the Prophet Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam. You can just dust it with your hands or with a cloth. And lastly, number four, remove all hatred in your heart for anyone, as one of the companions was promised Jannah because every night before bed, he would remove any ill feelings he had in his heart for others. Make this a habit and share this video so others can benefit as well.
