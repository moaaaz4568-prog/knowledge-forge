Christian and I didn't start the Revenue Coaches because we needed another job. We started it because we kept watching the same mistake happen: founders who were brilliant at building but terrible at revenue. I'd sit in rooms with founders who built something incredible and they had no idea how to sell it. We both lived on both sides, the growth wins and the ones that nearly broke us. We didn't want to be consultants who showed up after the damage is done. We wanted to be in it with founders before it's a crisis. Comment "growth" if that's you right now.
