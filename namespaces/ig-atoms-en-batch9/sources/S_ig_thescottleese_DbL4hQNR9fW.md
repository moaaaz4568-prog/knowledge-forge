Never say these five words in sales, part two. Number one, don't say "does that make sense?" It makes you look insecure. Say "what questions do you have?" instead. That makes you appear more confident and pulls out the real objections. Number two, don't say "just." I just wanted to follow up. I just had a quick question. Just makes you sound small. Number three, don't say "honestly." Honestly tells the buyer everything you said before might have just been a lie. Just say the thing. Number four, don't say "but." But cancels everything you said before it. Say "and" instead. Number five, don't say "no problem." No problem reminds the buyer there could have been one. Say "of course" instead. These look like small changes, but they're not. The buyer can hear every one of them. Words matter. Use the right ones and you'll feel the difference on your next call.
