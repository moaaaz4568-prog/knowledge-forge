Do you want to get married? Yes. Do you want good income? Yes. Do you want both? Yes. Say this dua which Musa alayhi salam said and Allah gave him a wife along with a secured income. Rabb I inni lima anzalta ilayya min khayrin fabil, which translates as, "My Lord, I am truly in need of whatever good You send down to me." The key here is to say this dua with humility and put your full trust and reliance on Allah, knowing that He is the only one who can help you. Now put the effort in and watch doors open up for you, in sha Allah.
