Two friends were walking in the desert and fell into an argument. One of them swears at the other, and he writes in the sand, "Today my friend swore." As they walk further along, they come across a snake, and the same friend that swore at the other saves his friend's life from the snake. And so he engraved in stone, "Today my friend saved my life." This is how close friends and relationships should be. The bad things your friend does, write it in the sand so it fades away with time. And the good things your friend does, engrave it in stone so that you never forget it. But oftentimes we lose relationships and fall out with people because we do the exact opposite. We forget all the good someone has done and we just remember the one bad thing they did. If you want to be someone who is able to hold valuable relationships long term, then learn to overlook the small mistakes and bad things someone does, because we're all human and we all make mistakes. And learn to amplify and make a big deal out of the good things a person does. I can guarantee you that if you implement this, you will live a much better fulfilling life with your friends, relatives and loved ones.
