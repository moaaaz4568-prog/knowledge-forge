A lot of people trying to get married get frustrated, upset and lose hope when you can't marry the person you wanted, even though you've been making so much dua. I have a message for you guys. Sometimes Allah delays responding to your dua just so He can hear your voice calling out to Him in sujood. And He knows that if He answers you right away, then you'll probably stop making dua. And secondly, He may be preventing you from being with someone who you may end up being miserable with. The reality is that you truly don't know someone until you wake up next to them every single day and the novelty wears off. Trust me, people change and it's best not to go against the plan of Allah if you've prayed your istikhara and it's not working out. Because as one of the Salaf said, that if something is decreed for you, you will find it even if it is between two mountains. And if something is not decreed for you, then you will never attain it even if it is right between your two hands.
