You cannot hire your way out of selling in year one of your early stage startup, not even with the best hire in the world. Three reasons founder-led sales isn't an option early on. First, you can't adapt the live pitch based on what you're hearing for the first time. A hire rep can't sell conviction, they don't have it yet. You build it, you have to sell it first. Second, every one of those calls is market research. A hire would never bring that back to you directly. The founders who wait for a sales hire to do this lose six months they didn't have. Get uncomfortable now or get irrelevant later. Those are the only two options for you as a founder. You've got to start selling.
