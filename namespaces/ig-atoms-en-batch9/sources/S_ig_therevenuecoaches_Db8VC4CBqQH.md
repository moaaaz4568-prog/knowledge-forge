One in three startups never close their first ten customers. Not their first hundred, their first ten. Three reasons why. First, they wait for the product to be perfect before they sell it. Perfect doesn't close customers, conversations do. Second, they sell to whoever will listen instead of who actually fits. Ten random customers teach you nothing. Ten ICP fit customers teach you everything. Third, they treat it like a numbers game instead of a research project. Your first ten customers aren't a revenue milestone. They're the foundation everything else gets built on. Get that foundation wrong and there's no next fifty customers.
