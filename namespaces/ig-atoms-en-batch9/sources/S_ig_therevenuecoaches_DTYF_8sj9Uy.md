Most salespeople try to get prospects excited way too early. In discovery, is not your job to get them all hyped up. It's to make them see clearly the cost of staying where they are. Not pressure, not shaming, just clarity. When a prospect finally says, "I'm done, I need help and I need it now," that's the shift. Now you move them into the hero's journey. From stuck to momentum, from frustration to possibility, that's where the excitement belongs. When you structure this way, any top salesperson on your team can run the same play because sales is not about pressure, it's emotional sequencing.
