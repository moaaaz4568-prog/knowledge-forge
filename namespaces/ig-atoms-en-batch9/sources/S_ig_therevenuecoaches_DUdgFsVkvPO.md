Here's a quick sales tip that will instantly change your calls. Stop accepting the first answer you get. When a prospect tells you, "We're just looking," or "Timing is not great," most sales rep will explain or move on, and that's exactly where the deal dies. Instead, repeat the last two, three words they said, then pause. "Timing not great?" Then pause. That silence is gonna force them to keep explaining, and that's where the real reason comes out. Money, trust, partner, bad experience. And once you have the real reason, now you know what to address next. Try this on your next sales call.
