There was once a woman and she asked her neighbor for some salt. This woman's daughter said to her, "Mother, we already have salt. Why are you asking for some salt?" And the woman she then said, "See, my daughter, we don't need salt. But you see, this neighbor of ours, they always ask us for things. They always want to borrow things from us. They always need things from us. They are poor people. And so, I wanted to ask for something that's very, very small and not burdensome, so that they don't feel bad when asking us, so that to them they think that we also need them as well. Because you see, I'll tell you, I'll share something with you, brothers and sisters. You see, sometimes in life, you may be someone who other people need help from. You may be someone in a superior position. You may have the upper hand in life. But you see, a lot of people don't have the upper hand. And you see, the Prophet ﷺ, he said in a hadith that none of you should make yourself lord over another. Meaning none of you should come across as if you are better than another person. Or even if that little bit of pride in your heart enters, and so you think these people need me. And you see, a lot of us, we do this with our family members. We do this with people in our day-to-day lives, where somebody needs us, and then all of a sudden we start giving them advice and we start, you know, looking down upon them. The attitude we should have is what the Prophet ﷺ said, that none of you should hold yourself superior to another. This is where humility comes into it. And you see, humility is an essential part of being a true believer and a true follower of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.
