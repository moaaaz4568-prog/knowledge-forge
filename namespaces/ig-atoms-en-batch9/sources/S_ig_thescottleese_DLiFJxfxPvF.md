If you are trying to figure out whether to stay or whether to take off and go look for something else as far as your role and your job goes, there is a simple framework that I use to help make this decision. Three steps. It's called learned, earned, and burned. Step one is learned. You got to ask yourself, have I learned everything that I can possibly learn in this role? Can I do it in my sleep? Is there really truly nothing else for me to pick up and glean and get better at? If the answer to that is yeah, there is nothing left, then we move to the next stage. The second step is earned. Have I maximized my comp plan? Have I hit my maximum OTE? Is there no more money for me to make in this role? I've hit the ceiling basically. If the answer to that one is next, then we move to the final phase three, which is burned. Have I been burned in any way, shape or form? Have I been mistreated? Have I been passed over? Have I been wronged? If all three of those criteria are met, it's probably time to go. If two of those three have been met, you might want to proactively start looking. If one of those three has been met, maybe passively start looking. If none of those three is met, probably best for you to stay put.
