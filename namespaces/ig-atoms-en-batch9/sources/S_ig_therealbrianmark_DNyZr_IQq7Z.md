Never ever ever send a DM on Instagram until you understand these four settings. First, open any chat, tap on the name at the top, then tap Privacy and Safety. Turn off Read Receipts. Now you can read messages without them knowing. Just below that, also turn off Typing Indicator. Now they won't see typing signal when you're typing. Second, while typing, write @silent before your message. When you send it, the message will go through, but the other person won't get a notification. Third, type /imagine, then write whatever image you want. Instagram will automatically generate an AI image and send it automatically. For this example, I typed /colda silva's bald head and got this. Fall for more value.
