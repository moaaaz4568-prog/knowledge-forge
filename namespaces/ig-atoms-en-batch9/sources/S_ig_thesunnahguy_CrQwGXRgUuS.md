These three tips will help you wake up for Fajr. Number one, sleep on time. It's simple, but a lot of people miss Fajr because you're just sleeping too late. Number two, drink water before you sleep and set your alarm away from your arms' reach. The water will make you want to go to the toilet, and having your alarm a little further away will make you have to get out of your bed a little to turn it off. Combine those two together, and you give yourself a much better chance of not sleeping through your alarm. And number three, remember this hadith whenever you feel like going back to sleep. The Prophet ﷺ said, "Whoever prays Fajr is under the protection of Allah." So if you want goodness in your life and you want things to go your way, you need Allah on your side and for Him to protect you, right? And the best way to do this is to start your day with Fajr, because how can you be comfortable going through life without Allah's protection?
