How short is this life where we are travelers here? As the Prophet ﷺ simply said, "كن في الدنيا كأنك غريب أو عابر سبيل." Be in this world as if you are a stranger or a traveler. No, hale Islam, who lived 950 years, he said, "My period and my time in this dunya was as if I came, I opened a door, I stood for a moment, and then I opened the door to the hereafter." That's the nature of this world, and on Yawm al-Qiyamah, when Allah calls mankind, and a caller will call and ask, "How long did all of you stay on earth?" We will start discussing amongst ourselves, and the conclusion will be a day or a portion of a day. This is the reality of the dunya, and remember that the gravestones that you see in the graveyard now, many of those people have been under the earth longer than they have walked above the earth. And such too will be our end as well. So let us attach our hearts to the home of the hereafter, rather than your whims and hopes and desires and putting everything into this dunya, which will ultimately come to an end.
