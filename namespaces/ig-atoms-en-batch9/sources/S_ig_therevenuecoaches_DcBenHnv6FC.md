Your price isn't too high; it's just not justified yet. Two different problems. It's that value hasn't been proven before the price gets mentioned. Three pricing mistakes we see every time from early stage founders. Pricing based on what feels fair to you instead of what the outcome is worth to the buyer. Second, leading with price before the buyer understands the transformation. Third, never testing a higher number because you personally would hesitate to pay for it. Your buyer isn't you. Price for their world, not yours.
