Sales is just like curing an addiction. To sell intelligently, you must help the prospect understand the following. You must get them to believe and admit that they have a problem. Then you must get them to admit and agree that this problem is important and worth solving. And then you have to get them to agree that the problem is worth solving right now, ASAP. And then and only then are they open to hearing about the solution to that problem. Super closely models how you would treat an addict. You need an addict to admit they have a problem, understand that fixing the problem is important, and that they need to do something about it right now before it's too late. And then and only then is somebody going to be open to treatment. You can't just walk up to someone with an addiction problem and loudly proclaim, hey, I found this killer rehab facility in Malibu called Passages. You ready to go? It's not going to get you very far. It doesn't work like that. You're not going to get far discussing the solution with somebody who isn't ready to admit they even have a problem in the first place. You'd never try to help an addict in a different sequence. It simply wouldn't work. So why shouldn't we follow the same methodology in sales? Find pain, build value, create urgency, and then and only then is somebody open to a solution.
