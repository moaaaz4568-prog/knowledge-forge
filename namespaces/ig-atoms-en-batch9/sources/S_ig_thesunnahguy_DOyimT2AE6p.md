An easy life doesn't mean Allah is happy with you. People think wealth, health, and ease equals Allah's love. But you can have the house, the car, the career, the family. And all of that could still be a test. And don't think just because you're going through hardship or difficulties that Allah hates you or isn't happy with you. Sometimes, hardship is the very thing that brings you back to Him. You know, I remember a quote from Ibn Taymiyyah where he said, "A blessing that takes you further from Allah is actually a calamity, and a hardship that brings you closer to Him is the greatest blessing of all." So don't be fooled by what looks like a blessing, and don't overlook the blessings hidden in your tests.
