You don't need a hobby where you're good. You need a hobby where you suck. If you don't have something that forces you to be bad at it, you'll burn out. I try to surf as much as possible every week if I can, and I'm still not that good at it. I still wipe out. I still get pounded by waves. I paddle for waves I don't catch. I look like an idiot and a kook half the time. I've been surfing for 20 something years, but that's exactly why it works. Because on the other hand, in sales, when you're always winning, you're always the expert, you're always in control, but in the ocean, I'm humbled every freaking time. This is exactly what most people get wrong about burnout. They think they need to relax, so they go play golf, they go to the spa, they go to nice dinners, they take vacations, but they're still winning. They're still good at it. They're still in control. So their brain never actually resets. When you're bad at something, you can't think about work. You can't optimize. You can't be in your head. You're just present, struggling and learning. That's the reset. So I encourage you to actually find something you're bad at. Stay bad at it. Keep working at it. Because burnout doesn't come from working hard. It comes from never turning off the part of your brain that needs to win.
