When someone says, "How much do you charge?" never ever ever give them the price. As soon as you give somebody the price immediately when they ask, they're gonna judge your program based on the cost instead of the value that you can provide for them. So instead, say this word for word: "Nick, I've got a bunch of programs for different budgets. Would you mind if I ask you a couple of questions to see which one would be the best fit for you?" Now you can ask a prospect's questions to get to know them and you can make a recommendation based on what they need. Follow for more value.
