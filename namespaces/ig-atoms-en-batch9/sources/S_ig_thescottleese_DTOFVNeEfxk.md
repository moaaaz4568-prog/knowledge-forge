A real sales manager is a coach, a trainer, a developer of people. Someone who gets in the trenches and who role plays, who diagnoses skill gaps, who teaches, who prepares reps so pressure becomes performance instead of panic. If you're a sales manager, your job is not to sit in a room all day auditing CRM notes, updating dashboards and pretending that busyness equals leadership.
