And you know something? All I see is gravestones in loving memory of, in loving memory of our beloved father, our beloved mother. I hear pain in these gravestones, and rightfully so. But Subhanallah, you know what's even more painful? You know what's even more painful is the fact that I come here and Wallahi it's a reminder to me. But yet I know many people bury their dead and nothing happens except that they just bury their dead and that's it, and they live a life of pain and misery because of their dead. And you know what, Subhanallah? Yes, you will be upset for the passing away of a loved one. But you know what's worse? What's worse is you as an individual do not change. This stage, this stage right in front of us, right here, my dear brothers and sisters, this stage right here is the first step in our journey to Allah, is our first step in meeting Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala and standing in front of Him. Why are we not taking heed? Why are we not reflecting upon our lives? We need to make a change, dear brothers and sisters. We need to make a change.
