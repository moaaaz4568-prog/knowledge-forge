Price objections are not about price. I'll give you an example. A founder asks a consultant, "So how much do you charge?" Then the consultant will give their rate, and the founder will say, "That's over our budget." That's where the deal dies. In most situations, when this happens, people will either give a discount or walk away, but that keeps the entire conversation stuck on price. The better move is this: What happens if this problem stays unsolved for the next six months, and what does it cost you in lost revenue or missed growth? When the anchor shifts from cost to consequence, price stops being the focus. People only bring up price for negotiation when they cannot see the value. It's your job to make that value undeniable.
