This founder just lost a half million dollar deal with one sentence, and you're probably making similar mistakes. I'm listening to this sales call. The prospect goes, "So what does your product do?" And the founder says, "We're an AI-powered platform that streamlines workflow automation for enterprise team." Deal's dead. Here's why. First, the prospect asked because they're trying to figure out if you understand their problem, and you just gave them a generic pitch. Second, there's no value there. Streamlines workflow automation, what the does that even mean? You didn't tell them what changed or what's gonna change. It's just buzzwords. Got zero urgency is the third thing. Nothing about this makes them want to act now. It just feels expensive. So the prospect says, "Interesting, send me some information," and then just ghosts you forever. You should have said, "Before I answer that, can I ask you what's broken right now that make you want to take this call?" And then you just shut up and listen, and then you tie your answer to their pain. Pain first, value second, urgency third, then solution. Answer things out of order and you've already lost the deal.
