What's the difference between B2B and B2C calls? In B2B, you're selling ROI. In B2C, you're selling emotion. In B2B, they need to see how much money you're saving them or how much money you're making them. In B2C, you're selling them a new identity of themselves, like how can they improve by using your product or services. Focus on this by aligning your sale to what matters to your buyer and your close rate will start improving, whether you sell into B2B, B2C or both.
