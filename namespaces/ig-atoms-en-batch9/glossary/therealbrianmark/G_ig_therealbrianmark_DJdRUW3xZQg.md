# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJdRUW3xZQg — participation trophies

## Term

Participation trophies are Brian's example of a fucked-up notion: in life there are winners and losers, the best business in town and the second best, the guy who gets the girl and the guy who never does. The phrase appears once, beside grade two, third place, and they didn't learn how to lose. His argument is that a kid who gets a trophy their entire life and then enters the real world, tries for a career, and gets third place with nothing for it will be distraught, because they never learned to lose young. The trophy stands for shielding kids from losing, and the lesson he wants taught early is how to lose.

## Synonyms

- participation trophy (the singular form used in the source)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim about child development research: the clip is Brian's opinion argued from examples, and it cites no study or expert on how kids learn to lose.
- Read as a claim that every child with a trophy fails: the scenario is one imagined kid reaching the real world, not a measured outcome for all children who received trophies.
