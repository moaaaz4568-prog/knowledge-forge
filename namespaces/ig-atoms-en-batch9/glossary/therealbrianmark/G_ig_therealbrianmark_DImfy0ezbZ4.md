# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DImfy0ezbZ4 — challenge

## Term

A challenge is a free, time-boxed group event a coach runs to collect buyers: members join, engage for 14 days, and a share of them convert into paying clients. The word appears 9 times, beside type form, sales call and social proof, and Brian Mark lays out the sequence as challenge, community group, five-question typeform, then sales calls booked before the challenge even starts. His concrete conversion claim is 15 to 25% of the people in the challenge, worth three to five thousand dollars, plus a batch of social proof for future marketing.
