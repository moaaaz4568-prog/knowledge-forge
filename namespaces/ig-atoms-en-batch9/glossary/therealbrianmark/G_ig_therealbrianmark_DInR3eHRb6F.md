# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DInR3eHRb6F — season

## Term

A season is a bounded stretch of intense work Brian Mark negotiates with his partner: 14 to 16 hour days for a while, with support, until the business is off the ground. The word appears once, beside health, wealth, relationships and self, and he contrasts the season with the two failures he sees everywhere: going all in on business and abandoning the relationship, or all in on the relationship while the business suffers. His concrete promise is time off together as soon as the business is off the ground, because success with no one to share it with is just rich and lonely.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I'm in a season right now where I'm literally gonna be working 14 to 16 hour days, and I need your support." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DInR3eHRb6F.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to disappear into work: the season is negotiated with the partner in advance, with an end and time off promised, not taken unilaterally.
- Read as a balance formula: health, wealth, relationships, and self are named as the four parts, and the source never says how much of each counts as balance.
