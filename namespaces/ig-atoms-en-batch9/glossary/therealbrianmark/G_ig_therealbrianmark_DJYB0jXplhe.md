# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJYB0jXplhe — dollar per follower

## Term

Dollar per follower is the efficiency test Brian uses for a boosted fitness ad: find a post that gets you under a dollar per follower, then run that one to the moon. The phrase appears once, beside boosting the post for 10 to 20 dollars a day, USA, Canada, and Australia, and age 25 plus. His ad recipe is specific, a seven to fifteen second video with a call-out aimed at the ideal client's pain points, social proof from best clients, only the after photo, never the before and after, and a simple follow CTA. Targeting stays broad on location and age, because the specificity lives in the video itself, calling out busy dads, busy moms, or vegans, so the algorithm learns exactly who to show it to.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee of cheap followers: under a dollar is the threshold for scaling a winning post, and the clip never says every boosted post will reach it.
- Read as the whole ad strategy: the dollar figure is the decision rule at the end, and the targeting, the video, and the DM script are separate steps that come before it.
