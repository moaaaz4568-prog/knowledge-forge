# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIlpSBbJKdH — offer

## Term

An offer is the priced package that turns the success math into something sellable: $1,000 for 12 weeks of online coaching. The word appears once, beside brick by brick, 40 clients and one new client per week, and Brian Mark presents the offer as the first step once the principle is understood. His concrete arithmetic is one new client a week reaching 40 clients in six months, which is $10,000 a month, and the warning is that most people overcomplicate the plan and quit when results do not come immediately.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you wanna make $10,000 a month, create an offer, let's say $1,000 for 12 weeks of online coaching." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIlpSBbJKdH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that one client a week is easy: the math needs forty clients in six months, and the source never shows where those weekly clients come from.
- Read as a complete plan: create an offer is only the first step, and the reel leaves out the content and sales work that must fill the six months.
