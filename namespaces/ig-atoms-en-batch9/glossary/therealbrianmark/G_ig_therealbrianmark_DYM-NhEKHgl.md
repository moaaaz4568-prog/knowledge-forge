# G_creator — creator

## Term

A creator is how Brian says a poster should view social media, as someone who posts with the intention of reaching an ideal client instead of collecting followers. The word appears twice, beside the little guys, my client John, and ideal client demographic. His numbers draw the line: the average influencer with a million followers makes fifteen thousand a month, the little guys from a thousand to a million make one to five thousand, and his client John makes ninety-three thousand a month on twenty-one thousand followers because every post is aimed at his ideal client. The lesson is the switch itself: stop posting random content and hoping to sign clients, and post like a creator instead.

## Live Example

> "21,000 followers, $93,000 a month because he doesn't view social media like an influencer, he views it like a creator." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYM-NhEKHgl.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a follower-count rule: John's ninety-three thousand comes from posting with intent, not from the twenty-one thousand followers themselves.
- Read as a claim that influencers earn nothing: Brian gives the average influencer fifteen thousand a month, so the gap he points to is the approach, not the label alone.
