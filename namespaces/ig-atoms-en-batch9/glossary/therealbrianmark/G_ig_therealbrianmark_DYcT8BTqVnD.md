# G_banger — banger

## Term

A banger is a piece of content that hits, and Brian uses the word for the run of hits he credits to June Yu, the number five coach on his list. The word appears three times, beside phenomenal content, banger after banger, and number five. The clip ranks ten content coaches from ten to one, and the banger line is the whole case for June Yu: phenomenal content, one hit after another, with no further detail. The top of the list, Devin Jota, gets a longer case — viral on every video and business-specific content — while the banger is left to speak for itself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a measured result: banger is Brian's praise for a run of strong posts, not a view count or a ranking score.
- Read as a review of June Yu's coaching: the clip rates content coaches, and the banger line praises his content, not his coaching.
