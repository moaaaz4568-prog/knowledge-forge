# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DImtPvozwlz — lead flow

## Term

Lead flow is the stream of new prospective clients arriving, and here Brian Mark calls it absolutely insane at one hundred and twenty eight k for the month. The phrase appears once, beside two hundred twelve thousand followers, two to three k a day and a brand new all black Audi, in a travel update where growth continued while the team was in Bali. His concrete picture is Kiara gaining two to three thousand followers a day while everything ran smoothly and the business kept growing.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So we're on one hundred and twenty eight k for the month. The lead flow is absolutely insane." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DImtPvozwlz.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a measure of business health on its own: an insane lead flow sits beside a monthly total, and the source never says how many of those leads became paying clients.
- Read as a stable situation: the update is a snapshot from Bali, and a lead flow that is insane this month can slow without the team noticing while traveling.
