# G_spouse_objection — spouse objection

## Term

The spouse objection is the prospect saying they need to talk to their spouse before deciding, and Brian treats it as a smokescreen for the real worry, usually the finances. The phrase appears twice, beside isolate the objection, smokescreen, and paid-in-full. His handling is to isolate it first, asking whether the spouse knows about the call and what concern she might have, then to roll into the financial objection with a monthly payment plan. When the prospect still insists on talking to their spouse, he books the follow-up call on the call and sends a short Loom video so the spouse can see the program instead of hearing it secondhand.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof the spouse is the real blocker: Brian says the line is a smokescreen for money or uncertainty 95% of the time, so taking it at face value misses his whole diagnosis.
- Read as permission to pressure the prospect: pushing hard on the spouse objection turns the prospect off, which is why he switches to booking a call when the spouse is home instead of arguing.
