# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJHtJlDR6o7 — three hundred and sixty five days

## Term

Three hundred and sixty five days is the length of Christian Guzman's daily-video formula that Brian copied to start his YouTube channel: a video every single day for a full year. The phrase appears three times, beside vlog, six million dollar a year company, and five hundred subscribers to twenty five hundred. The result disappointed him, a modest climb to 2,500 subscribers, but it taught him a lesson and moved him into his YouTube flow, now at thirteen point eight thousand. His challenge to the viewer is the comparison: if he could record every day while running a six-million-dollar company and a gym with fifty employees, the viewer has time to post.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of blowing up: Guzman blew up and Brian only reached 2,500 subscribers, so the same year of daily videos produced very different outcomes.
- Read as Brian's current routine: the year of daily vlogging is in his past, and he says he is now more into his YouTube flow instead.
