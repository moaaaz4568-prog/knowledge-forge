# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ7TByAScbW — steal like an artist

## Term

Steal like an artist is Brian's name for copying the pattern behind a viral video instead of inventing a format from scratch. The phrase appears twice, beside 33 million views, 15 million views, and the people who tell you not to steal like an artist. His demo runs through doubled food and drinks given to the next person, then a fitness version where oatmeal is called peasant food, showing the same pattern rebuilt in a new niche. The skill he is teaching is pattern recognition: spot what is currently going viral in the algorithm and rebuild it, rather than trying to recreate the wheel with no idea how.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to repost someone's video unchanged: Brian's examples change the niche, the food, and the script, so a straight re-upload is not the move he describes.
- Read as a claim that originality is worthless: his point is that the viral pattern is the starting material, and the creator still has to rebuild it for their own audience.
