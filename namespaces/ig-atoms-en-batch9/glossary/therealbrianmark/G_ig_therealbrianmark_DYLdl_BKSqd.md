# G_picnic — picnic

## Term

A picnic is the outing the clip builds toward, after a round of hide-and-seek directions about the fridge, the hallway, the fireplace, and the trunk of the car. The word appears three times, beside a real pot of eggs, Cleo, and what do you think. The search turns up a pot of eggs, and the invitation follows at once — a picnic for two, then the same question extended to Cleo. The clip is a playful invitation, not an instruction: the picnic is the point the whole search was leading to.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Are you like to go on a picnic with me? Yes, I would like to go on a picnic with you." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYLdl_BKSqd.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a set of hiding places: the fridge, hallway, and trunk are the search, while the picnic is the invitation the search leads to.
- Read as a real event: the clip is a staged invitation with a pot of eggs as the found object, not a record of a picnic that happened.
