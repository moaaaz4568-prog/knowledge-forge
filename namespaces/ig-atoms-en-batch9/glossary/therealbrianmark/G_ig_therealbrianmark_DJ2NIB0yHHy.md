# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ2NIB0yHHy — DM script

## Term

A DM script is the written set of messages a coach uses to convert followers into clients in the DMs, and Brian Mark offers his as the reward for commenting the word script. The phrase appears once, beside progressive overload, macronutrient and fifth grade reading level, and the reel teaches the script by showing four lines never to say, then the lines that open a sales conversation instead. His concrete replacement for a weak opener is a 10 to 15 minute call framed around the person's number one struggle and number one goal, with a game plan as the payoff.

> "If you want my DM script that you can use to convert clients in the DMs, come with the word script and I'll send it over." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ2NIB0yHHy.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a script that sells on its own: the four banned lines are replaced by questions and a call offer, and the script still needs a real conversation after the call is set.
- Read as a promise that every DM turns into a sales conversation: the opener either opens a sales conversation or reveals no interest, and the source never says how often each happens.
