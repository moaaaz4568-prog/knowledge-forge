# G_next_level — next level

## Term

The next level is the bigger outcome Brian says people claim to want while refusing the work and the pain that come with it. The phrase appears three times, beside incredible amount of overwhelm, tenacity, and breakthrough. His picture of it is overwhelm, stress, fear, and frustration, feeling brought to the knees over and over, with a breakthrough only for those with the tenacity and courage to stick it out. The clip's accusation is the gap: people want the outcomes of the next level, not the work associated with it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I want the next level so fucking bad. No, you don't. Because the next level comes with incredible amount of overwhelm, stress, fear, frustration." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYTBVvhKYAY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as motivation to push harder: Brian's point is that wanting the level and wanting its work are different things, and most people only want the first.
- Read as a promise of a breakthrough: the breakthrough comes only if you last through being brought to your knees again and again.
