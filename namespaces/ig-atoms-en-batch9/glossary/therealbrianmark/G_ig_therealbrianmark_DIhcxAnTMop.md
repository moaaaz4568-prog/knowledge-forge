# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIhcxAnTMop — social proof

## Term

Social proof here means opening a reel with client results so new viewers see proof before anything else. The phrase appears once, beside transformative content, story call to actions and speaking to one person, and Brian Mark puts it first in his four changes because views without clients mean the creator is over-leveraging awareness content. His concrete instruction is to place client results within the first 10 seconds of the video, as he does at the start of this reel.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Number one, start with social proof. Start putting more client results at the start of your reels just like this." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIhcxAnTMop.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick that works on cold viewers: the proof goes at the start because the viewer already needs a reason to trust, and results shown late may never be seen.
- Read as permission to fake results: the proof must be real client results, and borrowing someone else's would break the trust the reel is trying to build.
