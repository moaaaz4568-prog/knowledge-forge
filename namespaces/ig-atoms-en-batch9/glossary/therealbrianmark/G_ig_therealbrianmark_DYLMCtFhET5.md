# G_prompts — prompts

## Term

Prompts is what Brian calls the ready-made AI instructions he offers for each business job, from writing like a human to landing pages. The word appears once, beside writing like a human, marketing and sales, and content strategy. The clip lists six uses — writing, marketing and sales, content strategy, monetization, landing pages, productivity — each introduced the same way, with the actual prompts held back for anyone who comments the word prompt. The entry records the offer, not the prompts themselves, since the source never shows them.
