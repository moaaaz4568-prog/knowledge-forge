# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIpIVjpzHGr — record-breaking month

## Term

A record-breaking month is the best month a trainer has ever had, and here it is the interviewer's question that frames the advice. The phrase appears once, beside 8.9k last month, 14.2k and 3,000 followers growing to about 4,950, and the trainer's record is $14,200 after starting from 3,000 followers. His concrete advice for reaching 14.2k is not to give up, to stop doing what is not working, and to find somebody who has done it and can help do it faster.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a stable income level: a record month of $14,200 sits beside $8,900 last month, so the record is a peak, not the new normal.
- Read as advice that applies at any follower count: the trainer grew from 3,000 to about 4,950 followers, and the don't-give-up advice is tied to that small-account climb.
