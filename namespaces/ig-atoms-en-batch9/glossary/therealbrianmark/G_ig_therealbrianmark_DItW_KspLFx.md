# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DItW_KspLFx — marketing on lock

## Term

Marketing on lock means the skill is mastered and reliable: reaching new people, going viral, running ads, gaining followers, growing an audience. The phrase appears once, beside great marketers, ideal clients and $100,000 plus per month, and Brian Mark claims every client of his making that much is a great marketer. His concrete point is that every other skill can be hired, outsourced or delegated, but weak marketing cannot be made up for in any other area of the business.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "However, if you have marketing on lock and you know how to reach new people, you know how to go viral, you know how to turn on ads, you know how to get followers, you know how to grow your audience, every other skill in your business is something that you can either hire, outsource, or delegate." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DItW_KspLFx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that marketing skill alone earns $100,000 months: the clients are great marketers, and the source never says marketing was the only thing they did well.
- Read as a skill that cannot be learned: the point is that weak marketing cannot be covered by other strengths, not that marketing itself cannot be taught.
