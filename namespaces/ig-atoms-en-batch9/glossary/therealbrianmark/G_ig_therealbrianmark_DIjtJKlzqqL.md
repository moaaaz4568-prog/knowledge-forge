# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIjtJKlzqqL — online fitness business

## Term

An online fitness business is the coaching operation a trainer builds beside a day job and eventually quits that job for. The phrase appears twice, beside online coaching business, ten to fifty thousand dollars a month and six to ten hours a day, and Brian Mark says coaches at that income put in a minimum of six to ten hours daily. His concrete rule is to quit the day job only when the online business starts making more money than it does, so all time and energy can go into growing it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "However, when your online fitness business starts making more money than your day job, that's when you need to quit so you can fully commit all of your time and energy into growing it." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIjtJKlzqqL.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to quit a job now: the quit comes only when the online business already earns more than the day job, not when the trainer merely wants it to.
- Read as a claim that hours alone create the income: six to ten hours a day is the schedule of coaches already earning ten to fifty thousand, not a wage paid by the hour.
