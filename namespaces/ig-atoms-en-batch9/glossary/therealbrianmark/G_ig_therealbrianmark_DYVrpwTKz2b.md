# G_build_trust — build trust

## Term

Build trust is the work Brian puts above money, crypto, real estate, stocks, and gold, calling trust the most valuable resource on the planet. The phrase appears four times, beside keeping their secrets, keeping promises to yourself, and consistently delivering value. He splits the work four ways: with friends by keeping secrets and staying loyal, with yourself by keeping promises to yourself, with an audience by showing up as a consistent character, and with customers by delivering the service they paid for. His warning closes it: trust is very difficult to earn and very easy to break, so the whole point is becoming a person worthy of other people's trust.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You need to build trust with your friends by keeping their secrets and staying loyal to them." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYVrpwTKz2b.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a networking trick: Brian ties trust to keeping secrets, keeping promises, and delivering value, not to a technique for winning people over.
- Read as a claim that money does not matter: money is only the contrast — without trust there are no relationships, friends, income, or growth.
