# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJUWJhHtRGJ — hook

## Term

Hook is the opening line of a video, and this clip is a catalogue of five kinds: controversial, relatable call-out, time-based problem-solving, negative, and authority-based. The word appears six times, beside wash your bread before eating it, flashy jackets, and a decade plus of business lessons summarized in 60 seconds. Each type gets one demo line, from "Do not go to the movies until you know this" to the authority claim that packs a decade of lessons into a minute. Brian's rule for all five is the same: if the first three seconds don't captivate the audience, they won't pay attention, and he offers a ChatGPT prompt that generates hooks like these.
