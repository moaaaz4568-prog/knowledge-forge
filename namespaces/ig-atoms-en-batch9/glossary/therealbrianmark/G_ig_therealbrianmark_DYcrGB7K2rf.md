# G_recreate — recreate

## Term

Recreate is Brian's order to take what is already working and make it again with your own spin, instead of copying it. The word appears three times, beside outliers, formats, and best performing content. His sequence is fixed: post daily, batch write the script, use AI to find outliers and recreate them, recreate formats working on social media now, and recreate your best performing content in different formats. The line that frames it all is the warning at the start: don't plagiarize — recreation is the allowed version of copying.
