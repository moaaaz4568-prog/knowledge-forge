# G_violent_action — violent action

## Term

Violent action is Brian's description of how he actually works: he acts hard and fast, then figures out what he is doing later. The phrase appears once, beside I am not your mentor, I am not your coach, and nobody knows what the fuck we're doing. The clip's argument is that no coach, thought leader, or survivor of a hard experience owns the one true way, and that opinions get dressed up as fact or law. His own method is the opposite of waiting for certainty: take the action first, and let the understanding catch up afterward.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I think that half of the time I'm just taking violent action and then I figure out what I'm doing later." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJFd9JzSI04.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to be reckless: Brian pairs the action with figuring it out later, so the move includes learning from what happens, not ignoring the result.
- Read as a claim that planning is useless: his target is people who sell one way as the only way, not the idea of thinking before acting in general.
