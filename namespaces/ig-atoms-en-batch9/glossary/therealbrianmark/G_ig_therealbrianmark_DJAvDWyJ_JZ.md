# G_systems — systems

## Term

Systems are what the personal trainer credits for reaching 25 to 30 thousand a month: the repeatable way he runs ads and scripts his DMs. The word appears twice, beside proper ads, proper scripting in DMs, and major sauce for the training business. Brian notes the trainer used the same systems in his other businesses too, so the value is not tied to one offer. His advice to the audience follows the same logic: hire a mentor, because spending money beats wasting time learning it alone.

## Live Example

> "Definitely your systems. Your systems, how to do proper ads, how to do proper scripting in DMs." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJAvDWyJ_JZ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a software tool: no platform or product is named in the clip, so systems here means the trainer's way of working, not an app he bought.
- Read as a guarantee of 25 to 30K: the number is one trainer's result from using the systems, not a promise that copying them produces the same income.
