# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJSD4mYyg_7 — pull back the curtain

## Term

Pull back the curtain is what the best students will do on stage at the PT Domination All Access Event in Kelowna, sharing the secrets of their businesses in public. The phrase appears once, beside PT Dom Superstars, $50,000 to $250,000 per month, and August 14th and 15th. The event is pitched as the most valuable one for personal trainers in the market, with past guest speakers like Layne Norton, Phil Heath, and Tony Robbins' number one sales coach, and a last event that sold out over 250 tickets in less than 30 days. The curtain is the secrecy around how top students actually run their businesses, and the event sells the reveal.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "We're bringing some of our best students that are making $50, 000 to $250, 000 per month to come on stage and pull back the curtain and share their secrets in their business." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJSD4mYyg_7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a report of what happened: the event is upcoming, August 14th and 15th, so the curtain-pulling is a promise in an advertisement, not a summary of a past talk.
- Read as a typical student result: the $50,000 to $250,000 range describes the best students chosen to speak, not the average student in the program.
