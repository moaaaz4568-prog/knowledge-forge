# G_banned_words — banned words

## Term

Banned words are the fitness words Brian says will get an account restricted and shadowbanned if they appear in content, because Instagram's AI scans posts against community guidelines. The phrase appears twice, beside shadowbanned, my account is currently restricted, and comment the word safe. His claim is personal evidence: his own account is restricted because he used a banned word, and without knowing the list, a coach can trigger the restriction unknowingly and stop being shown to non-followers. The list itself is not in the clip; he offers it by DM to anyone who comments "safe", along with the alternatives to use instead.

## Synonyms

- banned word (the singular form used in the source)

## Live Example

> "There's a list of banned words as fitness coaches that if you use, I guarantee you your account will get restricted and you will get shadowbanned." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJaYFh6yzvU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as Instagram's published policy: the banned list is Brian's, offered by DM, and the clip quotes no Instagram document naming the words.
- Read as proof the AI works as described: the mechanism is Brian's explanation of his own restriction, and the clip gives no test that separates the word from other causes.
