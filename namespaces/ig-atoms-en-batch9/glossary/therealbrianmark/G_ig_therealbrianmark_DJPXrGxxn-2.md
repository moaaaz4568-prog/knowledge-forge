# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJPXrGxxn-2 — story poll

## Term

Story poll is the lead-capture device in Brian's Sunday routine: the single poll he posts after deleting all his stories, with the CTA built into the poll itself. The phrase appears once, beside best engagement on any poll, four thousand five hundred views, and seventy five to three hundred people for his VA to message. His rule is to leave it up a minimum of ten to twelve hours, posted around six p.m. Sunday, so people engage overnight and the VA has a full list on Monday morning. The key move is order: never the controversial poll first and then the CTA, because the CTA is the poll, and that is what keeps engagement super high.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any poll: the story poll is the specific single lead poll posted after clearing the stories, not the softer follow-up poll he posts the next day.
- Read as a guarantee of seventy-five to three hundred messages: the range is what his VA found after his best-engagement Sunday, not a promised yield for every poll.
