# G_social_media_money — social media money

## Term

Social media money is the income Brian says a single post can bring, set against the two hundred dollars a day he made bartending at an Irish pub. The phrase appears once, beside fucking crazy, side hustle, and one video a day. His contrast is a client reaching out from one post and paying more in a day than a month of bartending, and he extends the same money into speaking opportunities, friendships, and a bigger brand. The clip's push is to start posting today, one video a day talking to the camera, because each post grows the brand that brings the next opportunity.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I wish more people would talk about how fucking crazy social media money is." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYITWg-KbA7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that one post pays: Brian describes what a post caused once, not a rate any single post is guaranteed to earn.
- Read as a claim that bartending pays nothing: the two hundred dollars a day is his own past wage, used as the baseline his social media income now beats.
