# G_holding_momentum — holding momentum

## Term

Holding momentum is staying at the pace of a strong month instead of sliding backwards after it, and Brian talks about it as the thing he was worried about losing. The phrase appears once, beside crash and burn, wicked month, and twenty k so far for the month. His worry was having a big month and then going backwards, so holding momentum means carrying last month's pace into this one rather than starting over. The clip's number is the proof he offers: twenty thousand so far for the month, with a weekend still to go.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So we're kind of holding the holding momentum from from last month, which is great." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYFas41xAos.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that growth is guaranteed: Brian is describing holding a pace he already reached, not promising the next month will match it.
- Read as a strategy: the clip names the worry about crashing and burning but gives no method for holding momentum beyond the month's running total.
