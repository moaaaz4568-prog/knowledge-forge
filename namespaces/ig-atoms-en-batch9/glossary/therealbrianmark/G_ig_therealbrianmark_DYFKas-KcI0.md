# G_emotion — emotion

## Term

Emotion is what Brian says a business must sell instead of the thing itself, and the whole clip is built as a set of contrasts to prove it. The word appears once, beside Lamborghini sells status, Nike sells motivation, and Apple sells innovation. Each brand in his list sells a feeling rather than its product — status, motivation, innovation — and his rule for the viewer is to sell the feeling their business creates rather than the object it ships. The point lands in the closing line, where the business is told to sell an emotion, not a thing.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Your business needs to sell an emotion, not a thing." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYFKas-KcI0.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a slogan about feelings in general: Brian's point is a sales rule — price the feeling the product stands for — not a claim that feelings replace the product.
- Read as a list of brand facts: the Lamborghini, Nike, and Apple lines are examples of the same move, not a ranking of the brands themselves.
