# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIjEulRRmJO — cold DMs

## Term

Cold DMs are unsolicited sales messages sent to strangers, and Brian Mark treats them as the online version of knocking on a neighbor's door to pitch a fitness program. The phrase appears twice, beside door-to-door salesman, a thousand a day and a 1% response rate, and he blames dumbass internet marketers for teaching the tactic. His concrete image is walking up to a neighbor's doorstep and asking them to join a fitness program, which is how he says cold outreach actually feels to the recipient.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Holy, you're sending out cold DMs to clients." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIjEulRRmJO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on all outreach: the attack is on mass cold DMs taught as a numbers game, not on every first message to a stranger.
- Read as proof the tactic never works: a 1% response rate is the number he mocks, and the reel never shows what a better first message would look like.
