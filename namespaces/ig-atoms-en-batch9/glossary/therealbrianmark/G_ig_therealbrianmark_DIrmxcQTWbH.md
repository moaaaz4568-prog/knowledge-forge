# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIrmxcQTWbH — five-step framework

## Term

A five-step framework is Brian Mark's ordered fix for a boring creator stuck at 200 views: negative hook, personality, fast pacing, contrast, strong call to action. The phrase appears once, beside no hook, no format and no soap, and he opens by rejecting the algorithm as the cause, saying the creator is just boring. His concrete pacing rule is changing the scene every 1.5 to three seconds and cutting out dead space so the sentences roll into each other.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So here's my five-step framework to fix that and actually go viral." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIrmxcQTWbH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a diagnosis for every low-view video: the framework assumes the creator is boring, and a video can also fail from a bad topic or a mistargeted audience.
- Read as a guarantee of virality: the five steps fix the format, and the source never promises the fixed video will actually go viral.
