# G_routine — routine

## Term

Routine is what the trainer credits, alongside consistency, for going from zero to 20,000 a month online: showing up every day and staying in a routine. The word appears three times, beside get consistent, stick to the little things, and the little things get you the big things. Brian's question draws out the answer in two parts, consistency and the daily routine, and the advice to viewers repeats it as an instruction: get a routine, get consistent. The routine is not a schedule he details; it is the daily showing-up that makes the small things compound into the big ones.
