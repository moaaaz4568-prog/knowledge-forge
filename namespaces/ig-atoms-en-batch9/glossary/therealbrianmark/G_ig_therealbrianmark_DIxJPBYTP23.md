# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIxJPBYTP23 — $70,000 month

## Term

A $70,000 month is the revenue target Brian Mark's interviewee is chasing: at $58,000 on the 22nd of April, one more sale takes him to $68,000. The phrase appears once, beside 58,000, another sale and the 22nd of April, and Brian Mark answers that the target will be beaten, not just met. His concrete picture is a month already nearly closed with eight days left, where a single additional sale closes most of the gap to the goal.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So going for a $70,000 month this month." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIxJPBYTP23.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a closed month: the total is $58,000 on the 22nd of April with one more sale expected, so the $70,000 is a target, not a result.
- Read as a guarantee from one sale: the gap from $58,000 to $68,000 is one sale, and the last $2,000 to the target is still unaccounted for.
