# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIyhi2JJ78j — being yourself

## Term

Being yourself is the advice this trainer gives to a viewer with 300 followers: stop trying to manufacture something viral and say things that are valuable to the audience instead. The phrase appears once, beside 49,000 a month, 72.6 thousand followers and 15 years on and off, and the trainer says things started going off the moment he stopped trying to create virality. His concrete starting point is 300 followers, the same count the viewer has now, against 72,600 followers five years into running his own business.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to say whatever comes to mind: being yourself here means saying things valuable to the audience, not dropping the filter entirely.
- Read as a claim that authenticity alone grows an account: the trainer also ran his own business for five years, and the advice is one piece for a viewer with 300 followers.
