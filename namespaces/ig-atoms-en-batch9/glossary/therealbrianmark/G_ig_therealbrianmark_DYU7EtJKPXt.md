# G_gaps — gaps

## Term

Gaps are the holes Brian tells a business owner to look for in the discovery stage when closers suddenly start getting smoked with financial objections. The word appears twice, beside auditing the sales calls, price objection, and two to three of those calls. His diagnosis is that a sudden wave of price objections has nothing to do with the price: something is going wrong in discovery, and closers are not following the process as closely as assumed. The fix is an audit of two to three calls from prospects who raised a price objection, where he guarantees the gaps will show.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So if I were you, I would be auditing the sales calls of the prospects that quote unquote had a price objection and looking for holes and looking for gaps." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYU7EtJKPXt.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pricing problem: Brian says the sudden objections have nothing to do with price, so cutting the price treats the wrong cause.
- Read as a call for more training: the audit looks for gaps in the discovery process itself, not for a new script to hand the closers.
