# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIyxMixP98q — mindset

## Term

Mindset is the teachable attitude Brian Mark makes a pillar of his remote company culture: people who can regulate themselves without a manager watching. The word appears 3 times, beside remote teams, manufacture organic conversations and self-regulate, and he argues companies fail at remote work by hiring people who need heavy regulation and constant direction. His concrete contrast is his own 50-person remote team generating $10 million a year without babysitting, against leaders who never teach mindset and end up with highly unregulated people.
