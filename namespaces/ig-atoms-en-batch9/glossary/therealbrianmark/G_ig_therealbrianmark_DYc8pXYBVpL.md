# G_angles — angles

## Term

Angles are the different ways Brian's Claude code approaches one topic, so a creator never runs out of content ideas. The word appears twice, beside viral, hook, and polarize, the other three codes in his list of four. The four codes are viral, which rewrites a post to be shareable; hook, which perfects the opening line of each sentence; angles, which gives ten approaches to a topic; and polarize, which makes every sentence bold and opinionated to drive controversy. His use for angles is the repeat case: plug in a viral video and get different angles for the same viral concept.
