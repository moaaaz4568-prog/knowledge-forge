# G_project_best_b_mark — Project Best B Mark

## Term

Project Best B Mark is Brian's public commitment to become the best version of himself before his kid is born in the next 150 days, after shaving his face as a representation of a new version of him. The phrase appears twice, beside five commitments for the next 150 days, early wake-ups, and daily posts for accountability. The five commitments are waking at 4 AM five times a week and 6 AM twice, no work from 4 to 8 AM, thirty minutes of cardio daily, thirty minutes of mobility daily, and daily posts so the audience holds him accountable. The project starts from an admission: he put both businesses first for six months, and his health and relationships took a backseat.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a finished transformation: the project is announced at its start, with 150 days ahead, so the best version is the goal, not the current state.
- Read as a business program: the commitments are personal health and time rules, and the audience's role is accountability, not enrollment.
