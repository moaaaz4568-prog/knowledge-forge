# G_do_the_work — do the work

## Term

Do the work is the trainer's advice to anyone who wants his results: stop thinking you are owed something because you joined a program, and put the effort in. The phrase appears twice, beside just be fucking consistent, stop trying to be somebody else, and don't copy but get ideas. The interview sets it up with his numbers, a biggest month of 56 thousand and a next biggest of 42, growing from 2,400 followers to 32 thousand. The small things are the work he means: focus yourself, do the shit, stay consistent, and the results follow the repetition rather than the membership.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a workout instruction: the work here is the daily business grind of posting, improving, and staying consistent, not a training program.
- Read as a promise that effort alone guarantees 56 thousand months: the numbers are his past results, and the clip offers the habit, not a guaranteed outcome.
