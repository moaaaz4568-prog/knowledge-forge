# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ2owjDSgw1 — predictable lead funnel

## Term

A predictable lead funnel is a repeatable path from content to signed client, where doing the steps once more signs one more client. The phrase appears once, beside burnt out, overwhelmed and systematically scale, and Brian Mark says burnout comes from posting for clients and then drowning in serving them without such a funnel. His concrete offer is a free 60 minute masterclass teaching the system from a $2k month to a $5k month, then a $10k month.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It's because you don't have a predictable lead funnel where you know for a fact every time you do this, you're gonna sign a client." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ2owjDSgw1.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a cure for burnout by itself: the funnel makes signups predictable, and the overwhelm of serving too many clients is a delivery problem the funnel does not solve.
- Read as a free solution: the masterclass teaching the system is free, and the system itself still needs the content and ads that feed the funnel.
