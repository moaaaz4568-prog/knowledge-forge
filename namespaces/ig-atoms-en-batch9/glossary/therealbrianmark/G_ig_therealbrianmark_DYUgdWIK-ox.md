# G_going_viral_on_instagram — going viral on Instagram

## Term

Going viral on Instagram is the outcome Brian defines four ways over, as likes, shares, instant watching, and the algorithm pushing content to more people. The phrase appears four times, beside trigger the algorithm, high retention videos, and emotionally compelling content. Each definition adds a condition: people like and share, they watch and engage instantly, the content attracts attention quickly and keeps people engaged, and the videos are engineered for retention and emotion. His practical note closes the clip: after grade five you start losing people, so dumb the videos down.

## Synonyms

- go viral on Instagram (the base form Brian uses to open the clip)

## Live Example

> "Going viral on Instagram happens when lots of people like and share your content." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYUgdWIK-ox.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a luck event: Brian defines going viral as a process of engineering retention and emotion, not a random spike.
- Read as a promise that likes alone do it: likes and shares are the trigger, but the content must also hold attention long enough for the algorithm to distribute it.
