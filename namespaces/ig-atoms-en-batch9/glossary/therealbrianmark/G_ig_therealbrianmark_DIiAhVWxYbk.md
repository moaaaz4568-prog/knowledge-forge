# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIiAhVWxYbk — consistent

## Term

Consistent here describes showing up on Instagram every single day over an extended period until a small audience learns to trust the creator. The word appears twice, beside five times a week, less than ten thousand followers and ninety-nine percent of people, and Brian Mark calls daily consistency the differentiator because almost nobody else sustains it. His concrete picture is a creator with fewer than ten thousand followers who still stands out simply by posting every day.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because all you need to do is be consistent to stand out, because ninety nine of people aren't." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIiAhVWxYbk.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that posting daily earns trust on its own: the source pairs consistency with showing up, not with improving, so daily posts can still stay flat.
- Read as a rule for every creator: five times a week is Brian Mark's floor for personal brands, and a small audience under ten thousand followers is his example, not a universal law.
