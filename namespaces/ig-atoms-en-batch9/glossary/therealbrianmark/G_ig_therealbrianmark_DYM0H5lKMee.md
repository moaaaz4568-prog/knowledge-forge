# G_excellent — excellent

## Term

Excellent is the top grade in Brian's bad, good, excellent ladder, awarded to the best version of each content choice. The word appears six times, beside tripod, ring light, and mic, content pillars, and ideal client avatar. The pattern is fixed: the bad version is named, the good version improves it, and the excellent version adds the extra piece — a tripod, ring light, and mic for the iPhone, or education plus story plus social proof instead of education alone. The clip's hardest number is the posting ladder: once a week is bad, seven times is good, forty-two times is excellent.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Not posting talking head videos is bad. Only posting talking head videos is good, but mixing in trending formats such as carousels and split screens is excellent." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYM0H5lKMee.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fixed scoring rubric: excellent marks the best of the three versions Brian names for each choice, not a measured result.
- Read as a promise that forty-two posts guarantee growth: the number is the top of his posting ladder, and the clip gives no result tied to it.
