# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ10KJ6pR05 — b roll

## Term

B roll is the cutaway footage an editor lays over the speaker's words, and here it is a shot of a designer dog space's fire hydrant. The phrase appears once, beside fire hydrant, editing in post and the studio with the dog, and Brian Mark wants a really good shot of the hydrant so the edit can cut from the speaker to the hydrant and back. His concrete direction is to add the fire hydrant over what the speaker is saying, then return to him, so the object illustrates the words.

> "So it's like he's talking and then it cuts to like a b roll, the fire hydrant, then it can go back to him." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ10KJ6pR05.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a rule that cutaways always help: the fire hydrant works because it illustrates what the speaker is describing, and a random cutaway would only distract.
- Read as a claim about the whole edit: the direction covers one shot of the hydrant, and the rest of the video's edit is never described.
