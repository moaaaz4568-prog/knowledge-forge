# G_virality_predictor — virality predictor

## Term

A virality predictor is the Higgsfield AI tool Brian tests, which scores an uploaded video's likelihood of going viral on a heat map. The phrase appears once, beside viral heat map, 51 out of 100, and 50 out of 100. His test is three of his own videos: five hundred thousand views scored 51, a hundred and sixty thousand views scored 50, and sixteen thousand views scored higher than both. His verdict is that the tool is good but not there yet, and that humans who understand the art still have to interpret what the AI scores.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a verdict on the tool's accuracy: the scores ran backwards against the view counts, which is exactly why Brian says the AI is not 100% there.
- Read as a review of Higgsfield itself: the clip tests one predictor feature on three videos, not the platform as a whole.
