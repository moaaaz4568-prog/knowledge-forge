# G_30_day_content_calendar — 30-day content calendar

## Term

30-day content calendar is the output Brian's stolen ChatGPT prompt produces: a month of Reel ideas for a six-figure online fitness coach, sorted into five polarizing hot takes, five educational tips, five relatable struggles, five client wins, five calls to booked calls, and five connection pieces about his personal life. The phrase appears once, beside viral content strategist, busy moms who want to lose weight, and ideas designed to generate comments, DMs, or saves. The calendar is the deliverable the prompt is built around: every idea short-form ready, aimed at a defined audience, and sorted by the job each piece does.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a posting schedule alone: the calendar here is a content plan with categories and audience targeting, not just dates on which to post.
- Read as Brian's own calendar: the prompt is a template for the viewer to run, and the six categories are the prompt's structure, not a record of what Brian posted.
