# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIp1JgMxJv- — conviction

## Term

Conviction is the certainty with which a coach presents a new, higher price, and Brian Mark says it, not a lower price, is the answer to objections. The word appears once, beside objections, price point and close more sales, and his argument is that a prospect senses hesitation when the coach is not confident selling at the new price. His concrete instruction is to increase conviction in delivering the offer instead of lowering the price after a few more objections, because the close follows the certainty.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So instead of lowering your price because you're getting a few more objections now that you increased it, increase your conviction in delivering your offer, and I guarantee you you're gonna close more sales." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIp1JgMxJv-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a license to raise prices without changing anything: the conviction has to be in delivering the offer, and a higher price with the same hesitant presentation still meets objections.
- Read as proof objections mean the price is wrong: objections after a raise are treated here as a delivery problem, and the reel never tests whether the new price itself is too high.
