# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJb7yV2Rlmn — stopped dreaming

## Term

Stopped dreaming is Brian's diagnosis for jealousy: you only feel jealous of a friend's win because you stopped dreaming, pushing yourself to the next level, and doing the hard shit. The phrase appears four times, beside tinge of jealousy or anger, stopped pushing your comfort zones, and take a look in the fucking mirror. His chain is that stopping the dreaming, the imagining of how good the goal will feel, and the trusting that you will get there turns into doubt, and then a friend's success triggers you instead of exciting you. The cure he gives is a mirror check and getting back to work, not a conversation with the friend.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that all jealousy works this way: the clip explains jealousy toward a friend's success in this one frame, and it never addresses jealousy with other causes.
- Read as permission to ignore the feeling: Brian treats the jealousy as a signal to look in the mirror and resume the work, not as something to suppress or deny.
