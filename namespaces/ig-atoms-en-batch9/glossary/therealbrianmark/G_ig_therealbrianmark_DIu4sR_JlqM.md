# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIu4sR_JlqM — viral hugging

## Term

Viral hugging is Brian Mark's insult for creators who borrow someone else's virality instead of making their own ideas: hugging a famous person on the internet and riding their attention. The phrase appears once, beside come up with your own ideas, screen recording Ashton Hall and clout, and his charge is that reaction videos give the reactor no relevance of their own. His concrete target is people screen recording Ashton Hall and giving feedback on her content, which he says only proves the other person is the relevant one.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on reacting to other creators: the attack is on borrowing relevance from someone else's virality, not on every video that mentions another person.
- Read as proof the reactors gain nothing: the reel says their only relevance comes from the relevant person, and it never measures what their own audiences actually do.
