# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIhLSicz_da — quality

## Term

In this source quality is the standard a piece of content must meet before it earns a second daily post: one great piece beats a hundred average ones. The word appears 3 times, beside quantity, engagement and views, and Brian Mark ties it to doing proper ideation and writing scripts carefully instead of rushing. His concrete comparison is one great piece of content that gets 100,000 views against 100 pieces that get 1,000 views each, and the rule is less quantity and more quality until every piece clears the bar.

## Live Example

> "Less quantity, more quality, until you can make sure that every single piece of content that you put out is quality." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIhLSicz_da.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a call to post less: the rule is one great piece over two average ones, not a fixed schedule, so posting once a day can still fail if that one piece is average.
- Read as a views guarantee: a great piece can still underperform, since quality here is Brian Mark's judgment about ideation and scripting, not a measured score.
