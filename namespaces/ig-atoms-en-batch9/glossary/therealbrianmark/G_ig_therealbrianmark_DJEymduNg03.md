# G_stitch — stitch

## Term

Stitch is the feature Brian attacks: creators who stitch other people's videos and say "stitch incoming" just to ride someone else's virality. The word appears three times, beside dorks, relevance off of other people, and point blank, end of story. His charge is that the stitchers add nothing and get their reach secondhand, and he dares them to stitch him, confident the insult stands on its own. The stitch here is not a collaboration tool; it is the shortcut he calls out by name.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Stitch me, okay? You suck. That's why you're getting relevance off of other people." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJEymduNg03.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on all stitching: Brian's target is stitching for borrowed relevance, not every use of the feature, and the clip never offers a legitimate-use rule.
- Read as a debate about the videos themselves: the argument is about where the reach comes from, not about whether the stitched videos are good or bad.
