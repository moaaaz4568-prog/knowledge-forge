# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIooKx5pdlU — delivery

## Term

Delivery is how a coaching program is actually served to clients after signup, and here it is the reason six clients left in a month. The word appears 3 times, beside customer journey, onboarding call and revolving door business, and Brian Mark says a training plan plus a nutrition plan can feel awesome to the coach while clients still drop off. His concrete fix is to trace every canceled client's journey from signup, find the week they stopped complying, and build a new system so the pattern does not repeat.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that the program content was bad: the complaint is about the delivery experience, and a training plan plus a nutrition plan can be sound while the onboarding and check-ins fail.
