# G_matching_the_energy — matching the energy

## Term

Matching the energy is Brian's rule for DMs: the seller should mirror how much the prospect is investing in the conversation, and never out-talk them. The phrase appears once, beside ghosted in the DMs, DM script, and subcommunicates that you need the prospect. His test is to scroll the last ten DM conversations and ask who is talking more, because talking more signals neediness. The fix he gives is to condense what you want to say into fewer words, so you are always saying less than the prospect, illustrated by the date who texts too eagerly after one night out.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It's because you're not matching the energy of the prospect, and here's how to fix that in 30 seconds." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ74mYmygHN.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to ignore prospects: Brian wants shorter replies, not silence, and the point is to match their level of investment, not to withdraw from the conversation.
- Read as a script problem: he says the ghosting is not because the DM script sucks, so swapping scripts misses the diagnosis, which is about message volume and neediness.
