# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJJi4N_RxHy — parasocial relationship

## Term

Parasocial relationship is the bond between a creator and an audience member that feels like hanging out with a friend, because the same neurons fire while they watch a video. The phrase appears seven times, beside level one, level two, and level three, and the most important thing, depth. Brian's three levels are talking on reels, talking in stories where viewers see you in real time having dialogue, and going live. Talking on reels alone is not enough to develop it, he says, and depth is what matters: a thousand story views where people get to know your actual opinion are worth more than 50,000 reel views, because the connection is more authentic and more real.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a real friendship: the relationship is one-sided by definition, a viewer feeling close to a creator, not a mutual friendship with shared history.
- Read as a views game: Brian's whole point is depth over reach, so chasing bigger reel numbers at the cost of real dialogue works against the relationship he describes.
