# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIrrn25Tk4V — pink bunny

## Term

A pink bunny is the prize in this family's egg hunt: one of two special bunnies hidden for the children to find. The phrase appears twice, beside special bunny, jackpot and egg hunt, and the hunt's rule is that the two people who find the two special ones are the winners. The concrete scene is a noisy hunt with Haley called really close again and again, the pink bunny placed in one half or the other, and an accusation of cheating when someone tells her where it is.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a contest with clear rules: the hunt has two special bunnies and two winners, but the shouting and the cheating accusation show the rules were argued live.
- Read as a story about winning: the jackpot and the pink bunny are named as prizes, and the transcript never settles who actually found the pink bunny.
