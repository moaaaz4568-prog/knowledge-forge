# G_funnel_content — funnel content

## Term

Funnel content is the Instagram content Brian sorts into top, middle, and bottom of the funnel, each level aimed at a narrower audience. The phrase appears six times, beside widely relatable, highly targeted, and ideal client avatar. Top of funnel is broad and relatable, like low calorie snacks or a personal story about losing fifteen pounds through a divorce. Middle of funnel is targeted conversion and social proof for one ideal client avatar, like a busy mom over thirty or a forty-two-year-old executive who lost fifteen pounds. Bottom of funnel is whatever gets people into the DMs: the bio keyword, the application link, a story, or the three pinned posts.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as three separate strategies: the three levels are one funnel, and content only counts as bottom of funnel if it moves someone toward the DMs.
- Read as a claim that broad content converts: top of funnel content is for reach and connection, while the converting work sits in the middle and bottom.
