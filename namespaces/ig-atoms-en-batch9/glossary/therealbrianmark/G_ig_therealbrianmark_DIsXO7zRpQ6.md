# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIsXO7zRpQ6 — avatar

## Term

An avatar is the specific ideal client a coach speaks to in content, and here it is one of the skills that took a trainer from five or six thousand a month to thirty thousand. The word appears twice, beside hooks on point, go viral and systems and scale, and the trainer credits learning to read his content, talk to his avatar, and be a better coach. His concrete advice to a trainer stuck at $2,000 a month is to get a real mentor with real transformations and testimonials, because nine out of ten do not have them.

## Live Example

> "Learned how to go viral, get my hooks on point. Yeah. And talk to my avatar." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIsXO7zRpQ6.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that talking to an avatar is enough: the trainer also learned hooks, content reading, systems, and scale, and the avatar is one skill among several.
- Read as a fixed identity: an avatar is the ideal client the coach speaks to, and the reel never says how the coach finds out who that client is.
