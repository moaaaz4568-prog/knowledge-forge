# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIkZc2ZPSzz — morning routine

## Term

A morning routine is the fixed sequence Brian Mark credits for keeping a $1 million a month business owner consistent instead of up and down like a roller coaster. The phrase appears twice, beside wake up at the same time, no phone for the first hour and 20 minutes on your mind, and the routine has five parts: same wake time, no phone for the first hour, moving the body, journaling and meditation, and no other phone use until the routine is done. His concrete warning is that activating the work brain early on a negative message leaves the owner stressed for the whole day.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a routine anyone can copy exactly: the wake time can be 5 a.m. or 8 a.m. as long as it stays the same, so the fixed part is the sameness, not the hour.
- Read as a guarantee of a calm day: avoiding the phone only delays the first negative message, and the routine manages the morning, not the inbox.
