# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJK7dJwtwZr — 100 DMs a day

## Term

100 DMs a day is the habit Brian tells a caller to stop: sending a hundred cold DMs daily to strangers asking how to get more leads. The phrase appears twice, beside content is the number one thing, two hours a day creating content, and they're sending DMs to you. His replacement is to spend two hours a day making one piece of content that is actually good, so the video earns ten thousand, twenty thousand, fifty thousand, even a million views, and the strangers start messaging first. The number stands for the whole grind-it-out approach: volume without anything worth watching.

## Live Example

> "What if instead of sending 100 DMs a day to strangers, you spent two hours a day creating content and making a piece of content that was actually good?" — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJK7dJwtwZr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on DMs entirely: Brian's fix is to earn inbound DMs with good content, not to stop messaging people, and the clip never says outreach itself is wrong.
- Read as a promise of a million views: the view counts are an illustration of what good content can do, not a guarantee attached to two hours of work.
