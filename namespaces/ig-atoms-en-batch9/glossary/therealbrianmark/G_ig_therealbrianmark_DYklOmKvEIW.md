# G_dm_conversation — DM conversation

## Term

A DM conversation is the direct-message exchange Brian grades bad, good, and excellent, and he says how it is run is the difference between a thousand-a-month business and a hundred-thousand-a-month one. The phrase appears six times, beside validate, relate, lean in, and they're basically selling themselves. In the bad version the coach does all the talking in long paragraphs, pitches immediately, and gets one-word replies, ghosting, or blocking. In the good version the coach asks questions but follows a script like a robot, and in the excellent version the same questions are followed with validate, relate, lean in — so the prospect pours out deep emotions and pain points and basically sells themself.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a script to memorize: the excellent version uses the same questions as the good one, and the difference is validating and relating after each response, not new lines.
- Read as a promise of sales calls: the good version already books calls that mostly no-show, so a good DM conversation alone does not mean a held call.
