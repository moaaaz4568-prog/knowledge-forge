# G_transformation_story — transformation story

## Term

Transformation story is the content Brian says actually books sales calls: showing the old you versus the new you, so the audience can see themselves in the journey. The phrase appears once, beside before and after photos, an empty sales calendar, and posting more transformative content. His criticism is that most coaches only post photos where they look amazing, so viewers think they always had it together and cannot see themselves in the story. The fix is to show that you once struggled just like them, because when they see the path from where you started to where you are now, it inspires them to want their own transformation and book a call.

> "But one of the most effective ways to share a transformation story is show the old you versus the new you." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJO0rt4xfVv.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as before-and-after photos: Brian says most people overestimate that format, and the story he wants is the struggle and the change, not two pictures side by side.
- Read as a sympathy play: the story works because the viewer recognizes their own starting point in it, not because they feel sorry for the coach.
