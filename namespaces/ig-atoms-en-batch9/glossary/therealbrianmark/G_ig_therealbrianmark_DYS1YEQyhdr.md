# G_goat — goat

## Term

Goat is Brian's label for the best in a field, handed out twice in his ranked list of entrepreneurs: Grant Cardone for sales, Jeremy Haines for marketing. The word appears twice, beside eight, three, and original gangsters. Cardone gets an eight and the sales crown as one of the original gangsters, though past Brian's generation, while Haines gets a three with the claim that nobody else understands ads on his level. The ranking runs ten to one across ten names, and goat marks the two fields where Brian concedes someone else is the best.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Grant Cardone, eight. Again, Grant Cardone is the goat when it comes to sales." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYS1YEQyhdr.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ranking of the whole list: goat names the best in sales and the best in marketing, while the numbers rank the ten entrepreneurs overall.
- Read as Brian's own claim to the title: he hands goat to Cardone and Haines, and puts Gary Vaynerchuk at number one without the word.
