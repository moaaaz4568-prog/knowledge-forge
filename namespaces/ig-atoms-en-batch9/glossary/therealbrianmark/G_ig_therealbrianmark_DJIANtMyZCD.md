# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJIANtMyZCD — surround yourself

## Term

Surround yourself is Brian's closing advice to trainers who want to make 10K a month: get into an environment of like-minded, high-level, successful people, because you can only learn so much online. The phrase appears twice, beside didn't have the systems in place, didn't know what resonated, and trial and error wasting time. The trainer's early struggles were all gaps in structure and systems, and the community is what lets him see other people succeeding and believe he can do it too. The environment is the teacher the clip points to after the systems: seeing success up close makes it feel possible for yourself.

## Live Example

> "When you surround yourself with high-level individuals and successful people, you know you can do it for yourself as well." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJIANtMyZCD.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a substitute for systems: the trainer's first problem was missing systems and structure, and the environment is offered alongside them, not instead of them.
- Read as networking advice: the point is proximity to people already succeeding, not collecting contacts, and the clip ties it to belief, not to introductions.
