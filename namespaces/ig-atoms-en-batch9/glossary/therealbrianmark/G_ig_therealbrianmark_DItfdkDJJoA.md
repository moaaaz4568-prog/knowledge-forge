# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DItfdkDJJoA — 30-day content strategy

## Term

A 30-day content strategy is the month-long posting plan Brian Mark's ChatGPT prompt builds for a fitness coach: ten reels, five carousels, five value posts, five story prompts, and five client win breakdowns. The phrase appears once, beside seven-figure fitness business mentor, social media strategist and emotional trigger, and every post in the plan must carry a hook, personality, main point, CTA, and emotional trigger. His concrete audience for the plan is an online fitness coach who helps women over 40 lose 10 pounds.
