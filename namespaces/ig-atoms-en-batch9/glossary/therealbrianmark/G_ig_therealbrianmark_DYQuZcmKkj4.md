# G_new_group_of_friends — new group of friends

## Term

A new group of friends is the higher-level circle Brian says you can only join after becoming a person of value, not by asking to join. The phrase appears four times, beside old group of friends, lonely path, and same frequency. His argument is that old friends were old friends because you were like them, so arriving as the old version of yourself gets you rejected by people operating at a higher level. The work he prescribes is solitary first: develop the character traits, the consistency, and the discipline on a lonely path, then join people who operate on the same frequency.

## Live Example

> "When you moved on from your old group of friends, how do you move into the new group of friends?" — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYQuZcmKkj4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as permission to abandon old friends: Brian's point is about becoming someone a new group wants around, not about cutting people off.
- Read as a networking tactic: the lonely path and the discipline come before any approach, so the entry is about the change in the person, not the introduction.
