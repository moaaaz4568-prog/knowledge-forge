# G_custom_connectors — custom connectors

## Term

Custom connectors is the Claude settings entry Brian uses to plug an ad-building tool into Claude for free, instead of paying an agency fifteen hundred to three thousand dollars a month. The phrase appears once, beside open Claude, go to settings, and add the URL you see on screen. Once connected, Claude is told what is being sold, who it is for, and the budget, then builds the campaign, the ad sets, the targeting, and the copy, paused for review before anything goes live. His warning is attached to the same feature: under five thousand dollars a month, post organic content instead, and never spend on ads without understanding what is being sold.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "Open Claude, go to settings, hit connectors, scroll down to custom connectors, add the URL you see on screen right here." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYHw5AdNInt.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that the tool replaces skill: Claude builds the campaign, but Brian says the user still needs to understand the advertising game or the spend will be wasted.
- Read as advice for every budget: the free setup is gated to people making over five thousand dollars a month; below that he tells them to post organic content.
