# G_posting_schedule — posting schedule

## Term

A posting schedule is the weekly and daily quota Brian sets for a ten thousand dollar month: reels, photo posts, carousels, stories, calls to action, DMs, and collaborations. The phrase appears once, beside ten k month, reels one a day, and DMs fifty to one hundred per day. The numbers are the whole instruction: one reel a day, two to four photo posts a week, carousels twice a week, stories two to four times a day, two calls to action a week, fifty to a hundred DMs a day, and one collaboration a week. The schedule is the offer's core, with help growing an online coaching business held out to anyone who comments ten k.
