# G_train — train

## Term

Train is one of Brian's two verdicts for an employee, given when the fault can be fixed and the person is willing to fix it. The word appears five times, beside fire, top performer, and they're hungry. The pattern is conditional: train the great seller who misses small details but draw a hard line, train the honest mistake-maker unless mistakes become a pattern, train the slow worker who asks for feedback and implements it immediately. The opposite verdict, fire, falls on the constant complainer, the blame-shifter, and the employee whose work always needs double-checking.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Top performer, but nobody on the team likes them? Train. Too bad, he's a top performer." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYS3xBAhDO8.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a soft option: every train verdict in the clip carries a hard line or a firing condition if the person does not step up.
- Read as a verdict on the person: train and fire judge the behavior and the willingness to fix it, and the same employee can move from one verdict to the other.
