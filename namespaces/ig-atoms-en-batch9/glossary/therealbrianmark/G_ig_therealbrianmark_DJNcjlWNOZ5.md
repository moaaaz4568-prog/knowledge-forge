# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJNcjlWNOZ5 — pre-frame

## Term

Pre-frame is the opening Brian uses on sales calls to separate himself as an authority and set the tone for the prospect to buy. The phrase appears twice, beside closed over $30 million in sales, back-to-back meetings booked all day, and a game plan to achieve your goals. The first part lands thirty seconds in, after small talk: he says he is booked all day and asks if they can jump straight in, which signals he is in demand. Then he lays out the call word for word, a few questions to find the struggle, the goals, a game plan, and an invitation to work together only if they are a good fit, closing by asking if that sounds good.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to pressure the prospect: the pre-frame ends with an honest out, pointing them elsewhere if they are not a fit, so it frames a mutual decision, not a trap.
- Read as the whole sales script: Brian offers the full script separately for commenters, and the pre-frame is only the opening that sets the tone.
