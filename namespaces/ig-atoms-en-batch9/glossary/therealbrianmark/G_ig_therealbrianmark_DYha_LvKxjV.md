# G_grind_season — grind season

## Term

A grind season is the stretch Brian went into to get his gym reopened while still running his other company, with his wife's support for the first month and a half. The phrase appears once, beside sat me down, thirty pounds, and hold each other accountable. After forty-five days she sat him down and told him the truth: she loved him, but he had put on thirty pounds, needed a haircut, and she was not attracted to him anymore. His answer was the work itself — he lost thirty pounds, got the haircut, and the two of them hold each other accountable.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The gym just got shut down and we needed to get it reopened, and I was still running my other company, and so she understood that that was the grind season that I had to go into." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYha_LvKxjV.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complaint about his wife: the sit-down is presented as the truth he needed, and the clip credits it with the thirty pounds he then lost.
- Read as a fitness story: the grind season is the business push to reopen the gym, and the weight is what the push cost him, not the goal of it.
