# G_split_testing — split testing

## Term

Split testing is the experiment Brian ran on one video: film the same video with different hooks, post the versions, and keep the winner. The phrase appears once, beside good, bad, excellent, three different hooks, and 500,000 views. His own test changed only the order of the good, bad, excellent segment — content ideas first in the version that got seven hundred views, video editing first in the version that passed five hundred thousand. His instruction generalizes it: film three different hooks, post them all on TikTok, then move them to Instagram.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "This is called split testing. So instead of just posting the video that you were gonna post, film three different hooks for the videos, post them all on TikTok, move it over to Instagram." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYh3Kk-qA0g.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof the hook order caused the views: Brian changed the order and the views jumped, but the clip reports one test on one video.
- Read as a re-editing trick: split testing here means filming different hooks and posting the versions, not recutting the same video.
