# G_retention_graph — retention graph

## Term

Retention graph is the chart inside Instagram's Edits app that Brian calls the real sauce, because it shows exactly when people drop off a Reel. The phrase appears once, beside skip rate, like rate, and share rate, all of which he lists as less important. His use is diagnostic: the graph tells him why his content sucks so he can fix the next video, instead of guessing from likes. The clip walks through downloading Edits and scrolling to the analytics, but the graph is the feature the whole walkthrough exists to reach.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a vanity metric: the graph is not a score to brag about, it is a drop-off map, and Brian uses it to find the weak moment in a video.
- Read as the whole of the Edits app: auto captions, cutout tracking, and the music library are also in the clip, so the graph is one feature, not the app itself.
