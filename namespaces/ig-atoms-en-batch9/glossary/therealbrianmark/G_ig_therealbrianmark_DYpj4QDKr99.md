# G_banning_accounts — banning accounts

## Term

Banning accounts is what Brian says Instagram is doing again, and the clip gives two updates to keep a fitness coach from getting banned. The phrase appears twice, beside getting them unrestricted, May 2026 algorithm rollout, and results disclaimer. The context is his own ban a year ago — still unable to go live, with ninety-seven days left — and clients now blocked from sending DMs for thirty days. The two mistakes he names are talking about optimization supplements and claiming a specific client result without adding a disclaimer, and his closing point is that nobody owns the platform, so its rules have to be learned.
