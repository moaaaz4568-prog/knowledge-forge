# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJAl_VCz00j — hook

## Term

Hook is the opening of a Reel, and Brian's first viral tip is that it must trigger emotion rather than attention. The word appears once, beside scene changes, film vertical, and props that make a video visually stimulating. His five tips in ten seconds run from the hook through a scene change every one to three seconds, vertical filming, and props, ending with a follow. The hook here is not a clever line: it is the emotional trigger that decides whether anyone stays past the first seconds.
