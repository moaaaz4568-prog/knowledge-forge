# G_main_problem_at_this_stage — main problem at this stage

## Term

The main problem at this stage is the diagnosis Brian gives for each of three business stages, and each stage gets a different one. The phrase appears three times, beside sales calls in your calendar, churn rate, and you are the bottleneck. At the first stage the problem is too few sales calls to make ten thousand dollars, from an unclear niche, weak lead flow, and weak conversion. At the next it is churn and hiring the right people at the right time, and at the last it is the owner himself: the business depends too heavily on him for delivery, sales, and content, so it is capped by his hours.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Main problem at this stage is you don't have enough sales calls in your calendar to be able to produce ten thousand dollars in revenue and this comes from having an unclear niche inconsistent with your content not enough lead flow and not enough conversion skills." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DYXqAApKU65.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as one problem for every business: each stage in the clip has its own main problem, and the fix for one stage does not fit the others.
- Read as a complaint about leads: the first stage's problem includes lead flow, but it also names the niche and conversion skills beside it.
