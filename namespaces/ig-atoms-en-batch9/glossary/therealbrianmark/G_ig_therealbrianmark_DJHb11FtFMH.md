# G_step_back — step back

## Term

Step back is what being sick forced on Brian after ninety-six hours of the worst illness of his life: a massive step back from the business, down to opening his phone to post and going back to sleep. The phrase appears twice, beside back to back event weekends, momentum, and the universe giving him exactly what he needed. He had not taken a break in a long while, and his will would never have allowed the pause on its own. The step back turned out to be the best thing that happened to him, giving him present time with his grandma in Newfoundland while spreading his grandfather's ashes, and his closing advice is to pay attention when the worst thing may be the universe telling you something.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "It forced me to take a massive step back from the business to the point where all I did was open up my phone to post on Instagram and then went back to sleep." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJHb11FtFMH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a planned vacation: the step back was forced by illness, not chosen, and Brian says his will would not have allowed it otherwise.
- Read as a claim that sickness is good: he calls it the best thing that happened to him in hindsight, because of what it made room for, not because being sick is desirable.
