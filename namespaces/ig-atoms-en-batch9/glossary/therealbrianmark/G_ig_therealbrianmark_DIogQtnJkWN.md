# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIogQtnJkWN — masterclass

## Term

A masterclass is a free, hours-long live teaching session Brian Mark uses to give away methods and collect signups. The word appears 3 times, beside three million followers, content audit and the word game, and the session promises the hosts' secrets for growing plus live feedback on attendees' content. His concrete offer is a four hour masterclass with a content audit for a bunch of people, free to anyone who comments the word game for the link.
