# G_clear_lighting — clear lighting

## Term

Clear lighting is the filming condition Brian grades as good outdoors and excellent when the creator holds a random prop in it. The phrase appears twice, beside bad lighting, ninety seconds, and reels under sixty second. The clip is a ladder of filming and posting choices: bad lighting is not to be filmed in, reels over three minutes will not get pushed, ninety seconds is good, and under sixty seconds is most likely to go viral. Clear lighting sits inside that ladder as the one filming condition given two grades at once — good on its own, excellent with a prop.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a lighting tutorial: the clip names clear lighting as a grade in a ladder, and gives no setup for achieving it.
- Read as the reason reels go viral: clear lighting is graded for filming quality, while the viral claim belongs to reels under sixty seconds.
