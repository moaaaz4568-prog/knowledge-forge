# G_unlimited_content_ideas — unlimited content ideas

## Term

Unlimited content ideas are what Brian's three-step method promises: a feed of ideas drawn from real conversations instead of invented from nothing. The phrase appears once, beside check the DMs, check-ins, and question box on your story. Step one is the last one hundred DM conversations, listing the top twenty struggles of those people so you know who you are speaking to. Step two is client check-ins, writing down struggles in the clients' own words, so the content sounds like it speaks directly to them. Step three is a weekly question box asking the audience what they are struggling with, and the questions then become viral reels in three formats: split screen, talking head, and Q and A box.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as literally unlimited: the method produces ideas as long as conversations keep coming, and the clip never claims the supply has no end without new DMs, check-ins, or questions.
- Read as a guarantee of viral reels: the three formats are offered as the way to turn the questions into reels, and no result is promised for any single idea.
