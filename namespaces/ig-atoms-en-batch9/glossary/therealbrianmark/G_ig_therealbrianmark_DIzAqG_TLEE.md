# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DIzAqG_TLEE — financial qualifier

## Term

A financial qualifier is the question that checks whether a prospect actually has money to invest before a sales call is booked. The phrase appears once, beside calendly questions, red flags and pre-sold, and Brian Mark makes it step one of fixing calls that never close: change the Calendly questions. His concrete red-flag test is asking what the prospect does for work and how active they are in the DMs, and not booking the call at all if they say they have no job.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You're gonna ask them a financial qualifier to make sure that they actually have money to invest in your program." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DIzAqG_TLEE.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee that qualified prospects close: the qualifier checks money to invest, and a prospect with money can still refuse the program.
- Read as a rule to reject everyone without a job: the red-flag test is one signal before booking, and the source never says a jobless prospect can never buy.
