# G_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ1mkuYxbHu — resting on my laurels

## Term

Resting on my laurels is sitting on past accomplishments instead of pushing toward the next level, and Brian Mark calls it the attitude that leaves the future up to chance. The phrase appears once, beside I ain't shit, breaking point and every single moment is creating my future, and his claim is that ten years of accomplishments mean nothing once the work stops. His concrete picture is showing up every day as if already complete, then realizing that not working toward potential is the same as gambling the future.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So if I'm sitting, resting on my laurels, reflecting on yesterday, then my future is up to chance." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DJ1mkuYxbHu.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on ever reflecting: the complaint is about resting on yesterday instead of working toward potential, not about a single moment of reflection.
- Read as a claim that past success is worthless: the accomplishments are called irrelevant to the future, and the source never denies they happened or mattered.
