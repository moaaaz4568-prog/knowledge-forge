# G_tier — tier

## Term

Tier is the grade Brian gives each kind of content, from D to S, in a ranking of what actually converts clients. The word appears five times, beside pure informational content, day in the life, and client transformation stories. Pure informational content is D tier because anyone can get the same information from ChatGPT, educational content with context is B tier, day in the life is C tier as top-of-funnel material, and personal story content is A tier for standing out from AI slop. The top grade, S tier, goes to client transformation stories: real people, their career, their age, and the specific results they got in the program.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a quality score for videos: the tiers rank content by whether it converts clients, not by how well it is made.
- Read as a dismissal of informational content: D tier means informational content alone is dead, while the same information with context earns B tier.
