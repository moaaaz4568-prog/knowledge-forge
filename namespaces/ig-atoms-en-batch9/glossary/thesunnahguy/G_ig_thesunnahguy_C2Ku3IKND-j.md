# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C2Ku3IKND-j — morning and evening remembrance

## Term

The morning and evening remembrance in this source is one of the best ways to start and end your day: beautiful statements the Prophet taught us to say so that our heart remembers Allah. The phrase appears once, beside Fajr, Asr, Isha, and the present heart. It is quite long, the speaker admits, and you may not be able to do all of it — so here are the four most basic ones, to say three times each, given in Arabic with the translation in the comments. Say them after Fajr and between Asr and Isha, but make sure you say them with a present heart and that you understand what you are saying — and he guarantees that your life will never be the same.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "One of the best ways to start and end your day is to do the morning and evening remembrance, which are beautiful statements the Prophet ﷺ taught us to say so that our heart remembers Allah." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C2Ku3IKND-j.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a long list to get through: the speaker cuts the long remembrance down to four basic statements, said three times each, because doing all of it may not be possible.
- Read as words alone: the instruction is to say them with a present heart and with understanding — the remembrance is the heart remembering Allah, not the recitation by itself.
