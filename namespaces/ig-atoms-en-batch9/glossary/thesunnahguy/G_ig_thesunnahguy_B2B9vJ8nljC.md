# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B2B9vJ8nljC — character

## Term

Character in this source is what makes Islam visible to people who will never enter a masjid: thesunnahguy tells of a young man at the gym who kept his eyes low every time and never looked at the receptionist, until after four months she asked him why he never looked at her. The word appears twice, beside the gym, the receptionist, fitnah, and the young people. The man answered that he had to follow either his temptations or Allah, and he stopped coming because the conversation might turn into a fitnah — and the girl then looked up Islam and became a Muslim because of his character. The point is aimed at young people who meet America at the mall and on campus: neighbours will only ever see Islam through them, so if their character does not shine, America will never know what Islam is.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "She started looking up Islam after that because of the character of this young man, subhanAllah." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B2B9vJ8nljC.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as reputation or good manners in general: the character here is specifically the visible restraint — lowered eyes, refusing temptation — that led one woman to Islam, not a vague niceness.
- Read as preaching: the young man never lectured the receptionist; she came to Islam by watching how he behaved, so treating the passage as a sermon misses how the character works in the story.
