# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B4I_8hOp5-Z — the honor of Islam

## Term

The honor of Islam in this source is the greatest honor a sister can have, set against the attention girls chase with selfies: thesunnahguy tells girls never to feel shy to wear the hijab and the abaya, while girls out there take millions of selfies, put them on Snapchat, and then message him saying they are depressed, suicidal, and anxious, asking someone to sort their lives out. The phrase appears once, beside the hijab, the abaya, selfies, and Snapchat, and the word honor appears five times in the passage, culminating in the honor of la ilaha illallah, the honor of Islam, and the honor of Aisha. All the pictures and all the attention from guys, he says, should have made them the happiest people in the world — and it does not. The honor here is worn, not displayed.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Oh, why, his sisters, the greatest honor that you can ever have, the greatest honor that you can ever have, is the honor of لا إله إلا الله, is the honor of Islam, is the honor of عائشة رضي الله عنها." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B4I_8hOp5-Z.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as pride in being Muslim in the abstract: the honor here is tied to the concrete act of wearing the hijab and abaya without shyness, not to a feeling alone.
- Read as the selfies bringing happiness: the source says the girls getting all that attention are the ones messaging about depression and anxiety, so attention is the failed substitute for the honor.
