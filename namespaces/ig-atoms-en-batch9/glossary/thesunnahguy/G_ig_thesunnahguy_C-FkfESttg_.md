# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C-FkfESttg_ — willingness

## Term

Willingness in this source is the willingness to experience the opposite of what you want: working out will make you feel weak while it is actually making you strong, learning something new will make you feel stupid while it is actually making you smarter, and investing your money will make you feel broke while it is actually making you richer. The word appears once, beside the future that you want, the opposite, weak, and broke. The rule the passage states is direct — your ability to create the future that you want is directly determined by your willingness to experience its opposite — so the feeling is the price, not the proof that the thing is failing.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Your ability to create the future that you want is directly determined by your willingness to experience its opposite." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C-FkfESttg_.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as enduring hardship for its own sake: the willingness here is tied to a specific future you want, and the opposite feeling is the sign the work is happening, not the goal.
- Read as the feeling proving the result: feeling weak, stupid, or broke is the experience, while being strong, smarter, or richer is the actual effect — the two run opposite in this passage.
