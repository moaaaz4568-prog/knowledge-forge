# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C-7h55bNCLE — test

## Term

A test in this source is how Allah tries a person, in exactly three ways: He gives you what you want to test your gratitude, He delays giving you what you want and tests your patience, and He does not give you what you want and tests your faith. The test family appears six times — tests four times, test once, tested once — beside gratitude, patience, faith, and always remember. The closing line makes the three ways into a standing condition rather than three separate events: no matter what happens, always remember that you are being tested. Getting what you want is therefore as much a test as not getting it, only the thing being tested changes.

## Synonyms

- tests (the form the speaker uses most)
- being tested (the passive form in the closing line)

## Live Example

> "The first is that He gives you what you want to test your gratitude." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C-7h55bNCLE.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as punishment: all three ways — giving, delaying, and withholding — are tests, and the first of them is a gift, so the test is not a penalty.
- Read as a one-time trial: the speaker closes by saying always remember you are being tested, which makes it the ongoing state of life, not a single exam.
