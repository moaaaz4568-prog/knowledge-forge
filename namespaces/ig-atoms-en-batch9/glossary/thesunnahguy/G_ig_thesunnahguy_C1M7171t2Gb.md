# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C1M7171t2Gb — overshare

## Term

Overshare in this source is the habit the speaker learned to stop: one thing he learned throughout his life is never to overshare with everyone, because he used to share things with others, mentioning certain things thinking they would share the same excitement he did. The word appears twice, beside trust, vulnerability, the right people, and who to share what with. He trusts quite quickly and loves that element of vulnerability in himself, yet he has learned that to get anywhere you must share the right things with the right people and not with everyone — different people for different things, a relationship with certain brothers but not others, finances with certain people and not others. The most important thing, he says, is to learn and know who to share what with.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "One of the problems I used to have is I used to overshare with people." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C1M7171t2Gb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as never sharing anything: the lesson is to share the right things with the right people — the relationship and the finances both still get shared, only with certain people.
- Read as distrust: the speaker says he trusts quickly and loves his own vulnerability, so the oversharing is a habit of openness, not a lack of trust.
