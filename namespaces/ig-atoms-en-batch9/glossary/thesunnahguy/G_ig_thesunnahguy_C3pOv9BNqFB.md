# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C3pOv9BNqFB — envy

## Term

Here "envy" is the hidden resentment behind the praise that showing off online attracts. It occurs 2 times as "envy", with "envied" once more in the quoted saying that everyone who is blessed is envied. Its neighbours are "fire emoji", "burning", "flashing" and "blessed", and the speaker contrasts the cheerful comment with the fire burning inside the commenter. The concrete picture is an office worker on a Monday morning resenting a couple's holiday photos while leaving a fire emoji under them.

## Synonyms

- envied

## Live Example

> "They may throw a fire emoji in your comments, but the real fire is inside them because they're burning with envy." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C3pOv9BNqFB.md
> "And what's worse is that not only is it that people don't care, but they actually don't like it. And people envy you, not in a good way." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C3pOv9BNqFB.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as ordinary admiration: the speaker's envy is resentment from people who lack what is being shown off, not the friendly pleasure of seeing someone do well.
- When the fire emoji is read at face value: in this source the emoji is the mask for the opposite feeling, so taking the comment as proof that people care misses the point entirely.
