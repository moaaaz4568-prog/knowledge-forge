# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C4qCKIItNa3 — intention

## Term

Here "intention" is the single thing everything comes down to, underneath recognition, applause, popularity and status. It occurs once, at the end of a chain where climbing mountains, seeking knowledge, growing a Dawa page and reciting Qur'an are all judged by why they are done. Its neighbours are "recognition", "applause", "impact" and "positive change", and each example is reframed the same way: climb so you can see the world, become a scholar so you can save others, recite so a brother in the back row falls in love with the Book. The test he leaves is practical: let your actions inspire others, and let the motive be the lives touched rather than the praise collected.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Remember, it's not about recognition or the applause. It's about the impact you make, the lives you touch and the positive change you bring, which ultimately comes down to one thing, and that's your intention." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C4qCKIItNa3.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a ban on ambition: the speaker still urges climbing mountains, seeking knowledge and growing a page, and only the motive is corrected, not the deed.
- When the good deed is read as enough on its own: in this source the same recitation counts differently depending on whether it seeks applause or seeks to guide a listener.
