# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C5dwlB1tqxR — Shawwal challenge

## Term

Here "Shawwal challenge" is the same post-Ramadan programme, now aimed at the Eid test: staying on track once the month ends. It occurs once, beside "Eid", "deceive Allah", "training" and "accepted", and the speaker's sign of accepted Ramadan worship is becoming a different person once the month ends. The four parts repeat unchanged, six days of fasting, five daily prayers, one page of Quran a day and one continued habit, and the real test begins now that Ramadan is over. His advice to anyone who feels weak and does not want to sin on Eid is to join, and not to waste all the good deeds by doing something stupid.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And so we've come up with a challenge that will help you stay on track after Ramadan. It's called the Shawwal Challenge." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C5dwlB1tqxR.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a punishment for Eid: the challenge is presented as help for staying on track, not a penalty, and the speaker explicitly says he knows people want to have fun.
- When Eid is read as a break from worship: in this source the Lord worshipped inside Ramadan is the same Lord worshipped outside it, so Eid ends the month, not the worship.
