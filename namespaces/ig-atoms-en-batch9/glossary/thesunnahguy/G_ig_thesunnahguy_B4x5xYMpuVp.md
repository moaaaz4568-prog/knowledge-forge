# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B4x5xYMpuVp — content

## Term

Content in this source is what the dunya can never give the heart: the speaker says he has had most things in this dunya and it is a waste of time — you chase and chase and chase, and your hearts will never be content. The word appears twice, beside the tracksuit, the car, the deen, and the brotherhood. He walks with his head high in a thawb, guiding the brothers in the evenings, and says the only thing that can make a person content for the rest of his life is being upon the deen and doing what Muhammad was sent to do — spreading Islam. The brotherhood, he adds, will grow with or without you, because they are doing Allah's work.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You think that tracksuit or that car or whatever that you desire, you think that's gonna make you content for the rest of your life." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B4x5xYMpuVp.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as satisfaction with what you own: the tracksuit and the car are the examples of what does not content the heart, so owning more is the opposite of the term here.
- Read as giving up ambition: the contentment is tied to being upon the deen and spreading Islam — it replaces the chase, it does not replace the work.
