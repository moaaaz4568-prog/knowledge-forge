# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B4YM_DupH35 — remembrance

## Term

Remembrance in this source is the constant engagement of the mind with Allah, and the speaker asks who else could deserve it: the One who created us inside our mothers' wombs from a sperm drop, gave the infant a nose to breathe with when its mouth was closed, made the trees take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen, spins the earth, created the sun, and gives the food on the plate. The word appears twice, beside the wombs, the sperm drop, the nose, and the trees. He keeps the one who is alive, gave the internal organs that filter food, and gave everything — so the remembrance is not a ritual phrase but the mind staying on the one who did all of this. The passage ends by asking again, who else should it be about?

## Synonyms

- remember Him (the verb form the speaker uses for the same act)

## Live Example

> "Who else deserves that our minds are constantly engaged in His remembrance?" — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B4YM_DupH35.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as occasional mention of Allah: the remembrance here is constant engagement of the mind, not a passing reference.
- Read as gratitude for one blessing: the source lists the womb, the nose, the trees, the earth, the sun, the food, and the organs — the remembrance answers all of it together, not a single gift.
