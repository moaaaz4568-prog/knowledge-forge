# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C4irXqMNrbZ — disciplined

## Term

Here "disciplined" describes the Muslim life the elites supposedly fear, because a disciplined person is difficult to sell to and difficult to control. It occurs 2 times, beside "anti-consumerism", "ambitions", "sell" and "control", and the speaker ties it to following the money through the alcohol, gambling and pornography industries. His chain runs from discipline to resistance: discipline makes ambitions anti-consumerism, and that makes the person hard to profit from. The defence he names against the programming of children toward lust and desire is Islam itself, presented as the only stronghold left.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as personal self-control alone: in this source discipline is economic resistance, a life that cannot be sold to, not just willpower over habits.
- When the industry figures are read as the argument: the dollar amounts illustrate the motive, and the claim itself is that discipline blocks control, which the figures only support.
