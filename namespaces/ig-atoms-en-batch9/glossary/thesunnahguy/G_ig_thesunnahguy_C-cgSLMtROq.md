# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C-cgSLMtROq — forgotten

## Term

Forgotten in this source is what happens to you astonishingly fast after your funeral: in a few short hours the crying dies down and the family is busy making arrangements for food or drink, friends and relatives discuss current affairs over coffee, others call to say they could not make it, and the employer begins to search for your replacement. The word appears once, beside the funeral, the replacement, bereavement leave, and the comedy show. In a few days the kids go back to school or work because their bereavement leave has ended, and in a month the spouse watches a comedy or TV show, laughing as if you were never there. The question the forgetting forces is who you are living your life for: you spent your whole life concerned about what other people will think of you, when in reality nobody even cares — so live your life to please your Creator, because He is the one you are going back to.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You'll be forgotten at such an astonishing pace." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C-cgSLMtROq.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as people being cruel: the passage presents the forgetting as ordinary life resuming — food, coffee, school, work, a comedy show — not malice.
- Read as a reason to despair: the forgetting is the argument for living to please the Creator, not for giving up on life.
