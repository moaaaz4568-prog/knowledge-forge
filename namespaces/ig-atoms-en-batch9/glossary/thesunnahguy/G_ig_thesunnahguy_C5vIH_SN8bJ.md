# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C5vIH_SN8bJ — Salah

## Term

Here "Salah" is the prayer the speaker insists is for the worshipper, not for Allah, who needs nothing from anyone. It occurs 2 times, beside "swallow", "tonsils", "blessing" and "humble yourselves", and the proof he opens with is the body itself: the glands in the throat are submissive to Allah, and swallowing itself is a permission granted. His point is that a swollen tonsil that blocks swallowing shows how desperate a person becomes, and prayer is the same kind of gift, not a favour done to God. The warning follows: after the iman boost of Ramadan, ignoring Allah outside Ramadan forgets that the same God exists outside it as inside it.

## Live Example

> "And some of us, boys, let's come and pray Salah." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C5vIH_SN8bJ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a favour done to Allah: the speaker asks directly, for who are you praying, and answers that Allah does not need any of you.
- When the swallowing image is read as a separate topic: the sip of water is the proof for the prayer, that even swallowing is a granted permission, not an aside.
