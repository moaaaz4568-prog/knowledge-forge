# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C-AG-axtlJA — happiness lies within

## Term

Happiness lies within in this source is the answer to where happiness is, once people are ruled out: humans will never appreciate you, no matter what you do, and people will always forget what you have done — even your own kids, spouse, friends, and family. The phrase appears once, beside human nature, the test, the journey, and content. Your job is not to be bitter about it but to accept it as human nature, because seeking happiness in the acceptance or recognition of others leaves you severely disappointed. Life is a test and a journey to connect with Allah, and attachment to this world only adds to sadness — while a man shot under a tree in Gaza, his body eaten by dogs and foxes, is known by Allah, who will give him something far greater. Happiness is truly found in being content with whatever Allah gives you, knowing it is good for you and your hereafter, because the journey is between you and Allah, not between you and people.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You know, the answer is that happiness lies within." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C-AG-axtlJA.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as self-help optimism: the "within" here is contentment with what Allah gives and a journey toward Him, not confidence in yourself.
- Read as indifference to people: the source does not say people do not matter — it says happiness cannot be found in their appreciation, and bitterness about their forgetting is the error to avoid.
