# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C-bCXXstD28 — appreciate

## Term

Appreciate in this source is what we do too little of for the people who are good to us and valuable in our life: thesunnahguy cites the Prophet, who said that if you love someone, then you should tell them. The word appears twice, beside love, valuable, tag, and share. The action he asks for is concrete and public — tag someone you love and appreciate in the comments, and if you do not want to tag them, share the video with them to let them know that you love them. Appreciation here is not a feeling kept inside; it is telling the person, in the comments or through the video itself.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "One of the things we don't do enough is appreciate the people who are good to us and are valuable in our life." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C-bCXXstD28.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as silently valuing someone: the passage measures appreciation by telling — the Prophet's instruction is to say it, and the speaker's ask is a tag or a share.
- Read as appreciation for things: the people here are the ones good to us and valuable in our life, not possessions or achievements.
