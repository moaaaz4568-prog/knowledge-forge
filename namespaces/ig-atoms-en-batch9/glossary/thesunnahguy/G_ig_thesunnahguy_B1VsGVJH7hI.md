# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B1VsGVJH7hI — state of your heart

## Term

The state of your heart in this source is the inner condition that decides whether a person actually changes: thesunnahguy says what goes into the eyes and the ears affects it, and it in turn decides how someone changes. The phrase appears twice, beside eyes, ears, practice, and getting closer to Allah. He ties it to a practical cleanup — unfollowing pages that distract from Allah and following Islamic pages instead — so the heart here is not a feeling alone but the part of a person that what he watches and listens to shapes. A man who only says he wants to change, without cleaning up what enters his eyes and ears, stays the same in this picture.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "What goes into your eyes and what goes into your ears is gonna affect what is the state of your heart." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B1VsGVJH7hI.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a feeling that changes on its own: the source ties the heart's state to what enters the eyes and ears, so treating it as a mood that shifts without changing what one watches misses the point.
- Read as a slogan about wanting to change: saying "I want to change" does nothing here; the change follows the cleanup of follows and likes, not the wish.
