# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C4bPWSxt7n_ — repentance

## Term

Here "repentance" is the return to Allah the speaker insists is simple, not a long-winded complicated process. It occurs once as "repentance", with "repenting" once more in the thing stopping you from repenting. Its neighbours are "complicated", "drowning in sin", "feel sorry" and "never to do it again", and the whole procedure he gives is three moves from the heart: feel sorry, intend never to repeat it, and ask Allah to forgive you. His concrete form of it needs no complicated words or sophisticated dua, just closed eyes and a whispered plea for forgiveness.

## Synonyms

- repenting

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a ritual with fixed wording: the speaker's repentance is three heartfelt moves, and he explicitly says no complicated words or sophisticated dua are needed.
- When the simplicity is read as lightness: feeling sorry from the heart and intending never to repeat the sin are still required, so simple does not mean casual.
