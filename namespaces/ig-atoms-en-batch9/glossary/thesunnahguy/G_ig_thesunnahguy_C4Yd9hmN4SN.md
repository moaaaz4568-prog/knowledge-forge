# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C4Yd9hmN4SN — banter

## Term

Here "banter" is the mocking jokes between friends that the speaker blames when two best friends fall out and become enemies. It occurs once, beside "fall out", "bottled up", "cuss" and "mock", and his claim is that the hurt person stays silent because admitting hurt feelings feels weak. The pattern he describes starts at school in years nine, ten and eleven and is still running when the friends are twenty-four or twenty-five, grown men still cussing each other. His contrast is the Prophet, who never mocked anyone or called anyone a nickname they disliked, but told his companions how much he loved them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as harmless joking: in this source banter is the mocking that quietly wounds, and the damage is exactly that nobody names it as hurt.
- When it is read as a one-off argument: the speaker's point is the habit carried from school into adulthood, not a single joke gone wrong.
