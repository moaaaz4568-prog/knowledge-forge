# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B2J7K1Ip_6h — proud Muslim

## Term

A proud Muslim in this source is someone who shows his Islam openly even when the whole world is watching: thesunnahguy points to Khabib, who has over 13 million followers on Instagram and is worldwide known as a celebrity, yet still says Alhamdulillah and does sajood in front of everyone. The phrase appears once, beside Khabib, 13 million followers, sajood, and the pressure of being a Muslim. The message is for Muslims at school, college, university, and work who feel the pressure of being the only one there: they should take strength from Khabib's posture, be the standout person, and not care if people mock them, because they have Allah on their side.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And what he does is that he goes above and beyond to show the world that he is a proud Muslim." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B2J7K1Ip_6h.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as private faith kept to oneself: the proud Muslim here performs his Islam where everyone can see it — Alhamdulillah, sajood — so a hidden belief does not match the term.
- Read as pride in fame: the pride is in the religion, not in the 13 million followers; Khabib's celebrity is the test of it, not its source.
