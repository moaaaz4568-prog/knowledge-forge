# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C1nDM5CNeq5 — Umrah

## Term

Umrah in this source is the pilgrimage the speaker loves most in one particular form: asked which Umrah was his favorite, he says it was the one he performed in the month of Ramadan back in 2012, even though he did Umrah so many times and spent six years in Al Madina. The word appears eight times, beside Ramadan, Al Madina, Taraweeh, and the Sunnah Experience. He has not been back since, and Insha'Allah this year he will do it with the Sunnah Experience — and as the Messenger told us, Umrah in the month of Ramadan is equivalent to a Hajj. It is always a different experience, he says, and he still reminisces over Taraweeh on the roof: that was so amazing. The rest of the passage is an invitation — he hopes every single one joins, brothers and sisters alike, on a once-in-a-lifetime Umrah with Ustadh Abu Hafsah and the Sunnah Guy, open to families from the UK, Europe, America, Canada, and Australia, with limited spots, almost 50% already sold out, and the early bird ending on January the 15th.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And as the Messenger sallallahu alaihi wasallam told us, Umrah in the month of Ramadan is equivalent to a Hajj, and I honestly can't wait." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C1nDM5CNeq5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as any Umrah being the same: the speaker singles out the Ramadan 2012 Umrah above all his others, and the equivalence to Hajj is specific to Umrah in Ramadan.
- Read as a private memory only: the memory opens an invitation — the passage ends by asking the listener to join the Sunnah Experience journey, not by closing on nostalgia.
