# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C5WSdMuNoap — love

## Term

Here "love" is what people go out into the world seeking before they have found Allah, and the speaker reverses the order. It occurs once, beside "seeking", "find Allah", "standing" and "everything", and his claim is that finding Allah first is what lets a person keep standing when anyone else comes and goes. The two balances he sets are sharp: everything without Allah is really nothing, and Allah with nothing else is everything you need. The practical use is a sequence, find Allah first, and only then let other loves come and go.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Before you go out into the world seeking love, find Allah." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C5WSdMuNoap.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a ban on loving people: the speaker only reorders the search, find Allah first, and says nothing against loving others afterwards.
- When having Allah is read as having nothing: in this source having Allah with nothing else is having everything you need, not an empty life.
