# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C4nmf8LNuMp — last generation

## Term

Here "last generation" is the speaker's name for his own generation, the last to grow up with an innocent mother without social media and a father who never played video games or watched porn. It occurs 3 times, beside "innocent mother", "real man", "changed so dramatically" and "responsibility". His claim is that the world has never changed so dramatically since man set foot on earth, and that the challenges of the last twenty years are new to everyone. The duty he draws from it is to guide the next generation and fix the problems rather than become part of them.

> "We are the last generation who has an innocent mother that doesn't have social media and doesn't know how to use a smartphone." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C4nmf8LNuMp.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as nostalgia alone: the label carries a duty to guide children who have no clue what life was like before, not just a fond memory.
- When it is read as a claim about every family: the speaker describes his own generation's parents, and the point is the scale of change, not a survey of all households.
