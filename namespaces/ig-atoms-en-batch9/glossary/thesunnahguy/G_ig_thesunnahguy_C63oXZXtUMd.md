# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C63oXZXtUMd — soft

## Term

Here "soft" describes hearts made tender by the hardship and bloodshed of the Ummah, so that believers cry in sujood and feel the pain of others. It occurs 2 times, beside "hearts", "rust", "squeeze" and "revive", and the speaker's image is rust and dirt being squeezed off the heart by shared pain. His purpose is stated plainly: the difficulty exists so hearts become soft, and through that softness Allah will revive the hearts of people. The concrete sign he names is weeping in sujood, the softness showing as tears and felt pain rather than words.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "The difficulty and hardship that is happening in the Ummah right now is for a significant purpose for us, and that is for our hearts to become soft." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C63oXZXtUMd.md
> "So when you see bloodshed happening, when you see the Muslims suffering, know that Allah is uniting the Muslims and Allah is creating the hearts to be soft." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C63oXZXtUMd.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as weakness: in this source softness is the intended fruit of hardship, the state in which hearts can be revived, not a failure to endure.
- When the suffering is read as pointless: the speaker assigns it a purpose, softening hearts, and removing that purpose leaves only the pain.
