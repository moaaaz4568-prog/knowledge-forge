# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B1ZttoBHdrT — my bike

## Term

My bike in this source is the bicycle the speaker claims as his own in a street confrontation, and the whole exchange turns on proving it: he says he built it, has witnesses, and has the serial number at home. The phrase appears four times, beside stolen, the police, witnesses, and the serial number. After the proof he does not simply take it back — he offers to let the other man keep borrowing it for work as long as they exchange numbers and stay in touch, until he can arrange a bike for him. The bike here is at once stolen property, proof of ownership, and something he is willing to lend once the ownership is settled.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I know it's my bike. I built it, have witnesses, have the serial number at home." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B1ZttoBHdrT.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a simple theft report: the speaker ends by lending the bike back for work rather than reclaiming it on the spot, so the confrontation is about settling ownership, not just recovering stolen goods.
- Read as the speaker giving the bike away: he keeps the claim and asks for the man's number so he can get the bike back eventually — the loan is conditional and temporary.
