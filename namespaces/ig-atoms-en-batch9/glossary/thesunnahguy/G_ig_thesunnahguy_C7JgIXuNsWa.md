# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C7JgIXuNsWa — marriage

## Term

Here "marriage" is the relationship the speaker says is solved, ninety percent of it, by never telling other people about its problems. It occurs 4 times, beside "mothers", "toxic voice", "safeguard" and "divorce", and his warning is aimed especially at sisters who phone their mothers over a petty argument. The example is a little too much salt in the husband's food, an upset husband, and a mother lying awake thinking the marriage is ending while the couple have already kissed and made up. His rule is to solve problems together without involving other people, and to keep mothers out unless there is no solution and the marriage is about to end in divorce.

## Live Example

> "You know, if there's one thing I can tell you that will solve 90% of your marriage problem, it's that you never tell other people about your marriage issues and never involve other people." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C7JgIXuNsWa.md
> "Safeguard your marriage, and unless there's no solution and it's about to end in divorce, try and solve your problems together without involving other people." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C7JgIXuNsWa.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a ban on all advice: the speaker allows involving others when there is no solution and divorce is near, and only the everyday involving is forbidden.
- When the salt example is read as the real problem: the salt is deliberately petty, and the point is that a petty cause can grow into a toxic voice in the mother's head.
