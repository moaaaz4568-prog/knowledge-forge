# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C6bULsGNYhN — feminism

## Term

Here "feminism" is the narrative the speaker blames for destroying the family unit, set against his mother's happiness with her grandchildren. It occurs once, beside "independence", "narratives", "destroying" and "fulfillment", and his evidence is his mother's face: sheer happiness at a sleepover, breakfast cooked for grandchildren, ten human souls from one woman. His challenge is direct: do women buying into independence not see how much fulfillment comes from family, and how much grandmothers gain from grandchildren. His verdict is that degrees, travel and being a boss babe leave a mistake exposed at the end, when settling down finds no family around.

## Live Example

> "I see the sheer happiness on my mom's face and I'm thinking, how is the next generation of women promoting independence and buying into narratives such as feminism, which is destroying the family unit, thinking they're doing themselves any favors?" — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C6bULsGNYhN.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a claim about all working women: the speaker's target is the narrative that independence replaces family, and his evidence is his own mother's fulfilment, not a survey.
- When the mother's happiness is read as the argument: her joy is the illustration, and the claim itself is that family brings a fulfilment independence cannot replace.
