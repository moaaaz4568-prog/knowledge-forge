# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B3A7umDpzCy — honor

## Term

Honor in this source is what a person holds in the eyes of Allah, and it is set against the praise and disgrace of people: people will love you today and hate you tomorrow, praise you today and disgrace and shame you in front of others tomorrow. The word appears three times, beside praise, respect, disgrace, and the sight of Allah. If you are praised by everybody yet have no honor and respect in the eyes of Allah, you have nothing; and if people speak badly about you and disgrace your honor, yet you are worthy in the sight of Allah and close to Him, then what have you lost? The affair is with Him, not with people, so their opinions must not wear you down or make you sad and depressed.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You may be praised by everybody, but if you are somebody who does not have the honor and respect in the eyes of Allah, you have nothing." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B3A7umDpzCy.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as people's respect: the honor here is in the eyes of Allah, and the whole passage warns that attaching your heart to people's praise ends in sadness when they turn.
- Read as a call to ignore how people treat you entirely: the source does not deny the pain of disgrace — it weighs it against being accepted by Allah, and the acceptance is what makes the loss nothing.
