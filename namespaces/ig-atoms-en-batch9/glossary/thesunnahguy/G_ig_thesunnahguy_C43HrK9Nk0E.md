# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C43HrK9Nk0E — sandwich method

## Term

The "sandwich method" is the speaker's name for structuring a dua with praise before it and salutations after it. It occurs once, introduced as something he is going to teach, beside "praising Allah", "salutations upon the Prophet" and "etiquette". The layers are praise of Allah and mention of His mercy, then the request itself made personal and without holding back, then salutations upon the Prophet. His worked example opens with glory and praise to Allah and an admission of shame over sins, and closes with asking Allah to send salutations upon Muhammad.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And I'm going to teach you something called the sandwich method, where you structure your dua and say something before and after it, as a lot of people don't know this." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C43HrK9Nk0E.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a trick to get duas answered: the speaker presents it as the best etiquette when calling upon Allah, not a formula that guarantees a response.
- When the middle layer is dropped: a dua that is only praise and salutations with no personal request in between is not the sandwich he describes.
