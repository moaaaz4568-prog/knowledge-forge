# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C7WGVdYNTyx — peace

## Term

Here "peace" is the speaker's calm, which a tale-bearer disturbs by reporting what somebody else said about him. It occurs once, beside "disturb", "telling me", "what did you say" and "comfortable", and his reply is two questions rather than an answer: what did you say when they were talking about me, and why were they so comfortable sharing it with you. The point is that the reporter is part of the act, not a neutral messenger, and comfort with gossip is itself the charge. The practical use is a boundary: do not bring him other people's words, and expect to be asked what you said back.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Don't disturb my peace by telling me what somebody else said about me." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C7WGVdYNTyx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a refusal to hear criticism: the speaker's questions are aimed at the tale-bearer's own part in the talk, not at hiding from what was said.
- When the reporter is read as a friend helping: in this source the comfort of sharing gossip is the accusation, and bringing the words is itself the disturbance.
