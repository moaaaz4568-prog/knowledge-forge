# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C6EUNSTNGU5 — closure

## Term

Here "closure" is the answer to why you were hurt, which the speaker says is worth less than recovering from the hurt itself. It occurs once, beside "endless pursuit", "rationalize", "unfairness" and "pain", and his image is a snakebite victim chasing the snake to ask why instead of treating the poison. His lesson is that dwelling on the why does not change the fact of being hurt, and that centring life on what happens inside you beats centring it on what happens around you. The practical turn is to stop the endless pursuit, because the pursuit itself causes the most pain.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because often, it's the endless pursuit of closure that causes the most amount of pain." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C6EUNSTNGU5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a stage of healing you must reach: in this source the pursuit of closure is itself the wound, and healing starts by dropping it.
- When the snake image is read literally: the bite is the hurt and the chase is the demand for a reason, so the point is about recovery, not snakes.
