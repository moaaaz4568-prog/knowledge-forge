# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C6wCZ69NfhB — liberal

## Term

Here "liberal" describes the imams and activists in America the speaker says are waking up and drawing lines of disassociation. It occurs 4 times, beside "imams", "activists", "disassociation" and "7th of October", and the repeated line he quotes from them is that they will no longer be the same with you. His charge is that while brothers and sisters are dying, these voices focus only on what happened on the 7th of October with Hamas. The shift he reports is that lines are being drawn and sides chosen, and even the liberal voices are now insisting on a level of disassociation.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as the speaker's own label for all American imams: he reports a specific group waking up and drawing lines, not a judgement on every imam in America.
- When the quoted line is read as his words: "we will no longer be the same with you" is what he says the liberal imams are saying, and the speaker is reporting their shift.
