# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C1UkQgnN_5P — mankind

## Term

Mankind in this source is the measure of a way of living: the speaker sets up two islands — 100 women and 10 men on one, 100 trans women and 10 men on the other — and after 100 years one holds a thriving community of men, women, boys and girls while the other holds only the skeletons of 110 men. The word appears twice, beside the deserted island, the thriving community, and the critical question. In a world of confusion, he says, the question to ask yourself is whether mankind would continue to exist if everyone lived like this, and if the answer is no, then this way of living is not suitable for mankind. It really is that simple, he adds — and no one should fool you into thinking otherwise.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a prediction about one island: the islands are a test case for the whole of mankind, and the verdict is about whether a way of living suits mankind at all.
- Read as a complicated moral theory: the speaker insists it really is that simple — the continuation question alone decides suitability, and the warning is against being fooled into complicating it.
