# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C5bVWYRNiPw — Shawwal challenge

## Term

Here "Shawwal challenge" is the speaker's programme for carrying Ramadan momentum into the rest of the year. It occurs 4 times, beside "Ramadan challenge", "training month", "six days" and "11 and a half thousand", and the four parts are fasting the six days of Shawwal, praying all five daily prayers, reading one page of Quran per day, and continuing at least one Ramadan habit. His framing is that Ramadan was a training month for the whole year, not a thirty-day phase, and the challenge is the way to prove that to yourself. Joining is by commenting the word challenge, which brings the link into a community of eleven and a half thousand members taking part.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as a second Ramadan: the challenge is deliberately lighter, six fasts and a page a day, and its point is continuity, not repeating the month.
- When the numbers are read as the goal: eleven and a half thousand members is the community around the challenge, and the goal is proving to yourself that the habits continue.
