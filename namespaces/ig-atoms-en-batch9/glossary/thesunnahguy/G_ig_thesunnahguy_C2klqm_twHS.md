# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C2klqm_twHS — overshare

## Term

Overshare in this source is what not to do with the wrong people as your circle shrinks: as you get older, your circle gets smaller, so be mindful of who you share your dreams, goals, and vulnerabilities with. The word appears once, beside the circle, dreams, goals, and vulnerabilities, and the continuous form oversharing appears once more. Not everyone will share your excitement, so learn not to overshare things with the wrong people and be selective with who you open up to — you may be excited, but nine times out of ten, oversharing doesn't end well.

## Synonyms

- oversharing (the continuous form the speaker uses)

## Live Example

> "Learn not to overshare things with the wrong people." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C2klqm_twHS.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as keeping everything to yourself: the advice is to be selective about who you open up to, not to close the circle to nobody.
- Read as excitement being the problem: the excitement is natural — the failure is sharing it with the wrong people, nine times out of ten.
