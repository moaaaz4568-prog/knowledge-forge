# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C3Z5m83NLAM — reward

## Term

Reward in this source is what sharing an Islamic video earns you, again and again: the Prophet said that whoever guides to something good will have a reward like the one who does it, so whenever you share an Islamic video, if someone acts upon it or even shares it after you, they will be rewarded, but you will also get the same reward. The word appears five times, beside the video, praying, the grave, and circulate, and the form rewarded appears once more. Imagine if someone starts praying because you shared a video — every time they pray, you get the reward for it as well. And the best thing about sharing Islamic content is that it will stay online and circulate long after you have died, so you may be in your grave and still benefiting from something you shared many years ago. The speaker closes by telling the listener to go on, share this video and act upon what was learned today — because if someone watches this video because of you and learns about the reward of sharing videos, then you will get the reward for their actions and for everyone else who circulates the video after you pressed share.

## Synonyms

- rewarded (the form used for the person who acts on what was shared)

## Live Example

> "And therefore, whenever you share an Islamic video, if someone acts upon it or even shares it after you, they will be rewarded, but you will also get the same reward." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C3Z5m83NLAM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the sharer replacing the doer: the reward is like the doer's, not instead of it — the one who acts is rewarded, and the sharer gets the same reward alongside.
- Read as the reward ending at death: the passage says the content keeps circulating after you die, so the reward keeps coming while you are in your grave.
