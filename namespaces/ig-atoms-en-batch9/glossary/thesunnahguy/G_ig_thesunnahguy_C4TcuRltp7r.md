# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C4TcuRltp7r — burnout

## Term

Here "burnout" is the collapse of early-Ramadan motivation around day eight or nine, when Quran, sunnah prayers and donations all start slipping. It occurs 2 times, beside "motivation", "slacking", "consistent" and "habits", and the speaker traces it to willpower alone carrying the whole month. His two fixes are a habit-tracking app that shows how much Quran is read and how much is left, and automated donations signed up once so giving happens daily without choosing a charity each night. The aim of both is the same: keep the deeds streamlined so the worshipper stays consistent instead of fading.

## Live Example

> "I'm in the masjid every day, finishing the juz of Quran daily with ease, sunnah prayers on point, donating to different charities every day, and then comes day eight or nine and the evident Ramadan burnout begins." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C4TcuRltp7r.md
> "Hopefully this helps some of you make your habits and good deeds streamlined so you can remain consistent throughout Ramadan and avoid burnout." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C4TcuRltp7r.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as ordinary tiredness: this burnout is the loss of the whole early-Ramadan routine at once, not a single hard day.
- When the apps are read as the worship itself: the tracking app and the automated donations only remove friction, and neither replaces the Quran reading or the giving.
