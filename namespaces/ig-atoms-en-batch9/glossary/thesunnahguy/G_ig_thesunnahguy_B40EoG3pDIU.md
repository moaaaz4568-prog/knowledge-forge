# G_ig_thesunnahguy_B40EoG3pDIU — royal appointment

## Term

A royal appointment in this source is the meeting with the Lord that death brings, and thesunnahguy presents it as unavoidable: a day will come when you die, meet your Lord, face Him, and stand in front of Him, and you will have that appointment whether you like it or not. The phrase appears once, beside death, the grave, the Lord, and the world. The passage is built on a contrast — death is the one thing the human being is guaranteed, yet it is the last thing that occupies his mind, while he spends his life chasing a world he cannot take into the grave. The question the appointment forces is how much time you spend working for what will benefit you when you meet your Lord, against how much you spend on what will not.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "My dear brother, my dear sister, a day will come when you will die and you will meet your Lord and you will face Him and you will stand in front of Him and you will have that royal appointment whether you like it or not." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_B40EoG3pDIU.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a metaphor for any important meeting: the appointment here is specifically the standing before the Lord after death, not a worldly engagement.
- Read as something you can prepare for later: the source stresses that death is guaranteed and already promised, while people give it the least thought — delaying the preparation is exactly the failure it names.
