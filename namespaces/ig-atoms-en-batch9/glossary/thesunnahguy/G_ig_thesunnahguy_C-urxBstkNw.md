# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C-urxBstkNw — in your heart

## Term

In your heart in this source is where money does its harm: when there is money in your hand and not in your heart, it will not harm you, even if it is a lot — but when it is in your heart, it will harm you, even if there is nothing in your hands. The phrase appears twice, beside money, hand, harm, and a lot. The teaching turns on place, not amount: a lot of money in the hand is harmless, and no money at all in the hand is no protection once the money has moved into the heart. The heart, not the hand, is where money becomes dangerous.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And when it is in your heart, it will harm you, even if there is nothing in your hands." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C-urxBstkNw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a warning against wealth: the passage says a lot of money in the hand does not harm — the harm begins only when the money is in the heart.
- Read as a warning against poverty: having nothing in the hands is no protection either, so the amount in either direction is not the point.
