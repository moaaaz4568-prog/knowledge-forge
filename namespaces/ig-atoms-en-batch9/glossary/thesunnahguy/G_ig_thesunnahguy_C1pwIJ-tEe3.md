# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C1pwIJ-tEe3 — a hint of road

## Term

A hint of road in this source is what sisters say they want in a guy alongside deen: "I want a guy with deen but a hint of road" — and the speaker is still trying to figure out what it means, whether he walks a certain way or acts a certain way. The phrase appears twice, beside deen, the road life, the car, and the single mother. This road life, he says, is affecting a lot of people, and he has noticed that hijabis and sisters look up to a guy who has a little bit of road in him, while brothers start thinking they have to show themselves in a certain posture. But the reality he asks about is the end of that life: the road man may show you the car and that life, but how long will he stay on the road, and are you ready to accept a life of being a single mother? She may have the nicest of looks and he may be the most handsome of guys, but the moment he begins to disobey Allah or treats her a certain way, she will not even be focusing on the looks anymore — the main thing should be the deen, the number one priority, so she does not suffer later on.

## Synonyms

- road life (the speaker's name for the same street life)

## Live Example

> "You know, sisters say that I want a guy with deen but a hint of road, right?" — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C1pwIJ-tEe3.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a fashion or a walk: the "hint" is about a whole road life — the car, the posture, the disobedience — not a style detail.
- Read as deen plus a little edge being the ideal: the speaker's verdict is the opposite — the deen is the number one priority, and the road is what makes her suffer later.
