# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C5f6iFDt3z- — discreet

## Term

Here "discreet" is the way a marriage should be kept: private, unposted, lived in the moment rather than performed for Instagram. It occurs once, beside "private", "share things", "regret" and "best relationships", and the speaker's warning is that sharing is regretted every time it happens. The motive he questions is the wanting itself: why post about your marriage, why share your relationship, when the people watching may not be happy at all. His closing line is the rule: the best relationships are the ones nobody knows about.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But look, trust me, you will regret every time you share things about your marriage and your relationships. Keep it discreet and keep it private." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C5f6iFDt3z-.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as secrecy from everyone: the speaker's discreet means not posting the marriage publicly, not hiding a spouse from family or close people.
- When other couples' posts are read as proof of happiness: he warns that seeing everyone else do it does not mean they are happy, so imitation is not evidence.
