# G_ig_thesunnahguy_C5nf-lhtYlw — submission

## Term

Here "submission" is the exchange Islam offers: give up your free will for the submission God requires, and receive a reward no mind can comprehend. It occurs once, beside "free will", "reward", "paradise" and "neighborhood of your Lord", and the speaker describes the reward as a land no ear has heard, no eye has seen and no mind can ever comprehend. The message is framed as simple, live your life in accordance with the One who gave you life, and the exchange is the whole of it. His address closes on the intimacy of the promise: paradise, and the neighborhood of your Lord, my beloved brothers.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Exchange your free will for the submission that He requires from you." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C5nf-lhtYlw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- When it is read as giving up freedom for nothing: in this source the exchange is two-sided, submission now for a reward beyond comprehension, not a loss.
- When it is read as a complicated doctrine: the speaker calls Islam a simple message, and the submission is one act, living by the One who gave you life.
