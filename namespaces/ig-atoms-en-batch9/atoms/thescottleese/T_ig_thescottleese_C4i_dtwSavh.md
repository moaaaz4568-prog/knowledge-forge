"Figure out what habits you are trying to break and what new habits you are trying to make. And success and failure can become a habit. You hear all the time in sports about like this team is trying to learn how to win. You have to practice these things. One of the things that you need to do that we do a lousy job of, in my opinion, is rewarding ourselves for the things that we do right." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_C4i_dtwSavh.md

# T_C4i_dtwSavh_02

"We're very good, I think, as salespeople and leaders, at making these big huge goals for ourselves. We kind of suck at seeing progress. I know I do. I struggle with it even now. And one of the only ways for me to feel like I'm making progress is to create these micro goals, these little tiny baby steps that are really easy to attain." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_C4i_dtwSavh.md

# T_C4i_dtwSavh_03

"If I get too focused on the big outcome only, I struggle to see the progress, which discourages. If I get discouraged, I feel less motivated to keep going after it. And I end up starting to focus on the result too much. So breaking down these big goals we have into super small micro goals, I think, helps you form positive habits that are easy for you to latch onto and stick with." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_C4i_dtwSavh.md
