# T_DbGuK1ShWYV_01

"If you're selling to Germans, lead with the spec sheet, not the story. Emotional pitches feel like manipulation to them, and once they smell it, you've lost the room. Show the proof, then let the proof do the talking. If you're selling to Brits, never oversell. Enthusiasm sounds like a scam to them, and amazing makes them suspicious. Understate the upside and let them discover it themselves." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbGuK1ShWYV.md

You lose the warmth, and there's no warmth left to lose them with. If you're selling to Saudis, never come in with the clothes. The first three meetings are about whether you're someone they'd actually do business with. Patience is the entire pitch." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbGuK1ShWYV.md

Talk to them like a person, not a vendor, and they'll listen." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbGuK1ShWYV.md
