# T_DdCCK5xRnkK_01

"What level of selling are you on? Most people never get past level four. Level one is survival. You're just trying to get through the call. You read the script, you pray, and you hope they don't ask a question that isn't on the page." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DdCCK5xRnkK.md

# T_DdCCK5xRnkK_02

"Level two, when the buyer starts existing, you realize there's a real person on the other end with real problems, and you start asking about them instead of talking about you. Level three is being aware of the people around your buyer. They have a boss, a team, a budget owner. Now you're not selling to a person anymore, you're selling to a room that you can't see." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DdCCK5xRnkK.md

# T_DdCCK5xRnkK_03

"And level four is where you believe your solution is the right answer for everyone. So you push, you pitch hard, you think losing a deal means the buyer got it wrong. Most reps stay here their whole career. Level five is when you realize your product is not for everyone. You start disqualifying, you walk away from bad fits. Losing the wrong deal stops hurting." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DdCCK5xRnkK.md

# T_DdCCK5xRnkK_04

"Level six is understanding why buyers say no. You see why they pick a competitor, why they do nothing, why not now can be the smart move for them. Nothing feels personal anymore. But be careful, this level has a trap. Understand the buyer too well and you'll talk yourself out of pushing when you should. At level seven is when you add time." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DdCCK5xRnkK.md

# T_DdCCK5xRnkK_05

"Some buyers are a deal today, some are a deal next week, some are a deal next year. You stop forcing everyone into this quarter and start placing people where they actually belong. Level eight is when you can watch yourself sell while you're selling. You hear your own tone, you catch yourself rushing, you feel the desperation coming and you fix it live on the call." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DdCCK5xRnkK.md

# T_DdCCK5xRnkK_06

"It's like watching your own tape in real time. Level nine is one final step back. You're not working on the call anymore, you're working on the person making the call. You coach others because at this level you know the deal is just a mirror of whoever is running it. So those are the nine levels of selling. Let me know what level you've reached." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DdCCK5xRnkK.md
