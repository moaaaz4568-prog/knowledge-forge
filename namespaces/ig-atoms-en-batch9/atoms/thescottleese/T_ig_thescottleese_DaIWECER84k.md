# T_DaIWECER84k_01

"Three stupid things to do before a sales call that actually help a ton. They sound dumb, but do them anyways. Number one, call your own voicemail first. Dial yourself and leave a 30-second message, then play it back. It doesn't matter what you say. Most reps have no idea how they actually sound until they hear it back. Too fast, too nervous, too rehearsed. Fix it before they hear it, not after." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DaIWECER84k.md

# T_DaIWECER84k_02

"Number two, sit in silence for two minutes before you call. No phone, no music, no prep notes. Just sit there and do nothing. Make it boring on purpose, because your brain has been going 100 miles an hour all day, and the second they pick up, you need to actually hear what they're saying, not just wait for your turn to talk." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DaIWECER84k.md

# T_DaIWECER84k_03

"Silence before the call is the only thing that makes you actually listen on it. Number three, smile before they pick up. Even if you don't feel like it, even if the last call was terrible, just smile. 90% of reps think this is stupid until they try it, because buyers can't see you, but they can absolutely hear a smile. Your whole vocal tone changes and you don't even realize it's happening." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DaIWECER84k.md
