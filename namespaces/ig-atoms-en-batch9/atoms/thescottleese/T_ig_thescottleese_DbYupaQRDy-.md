# T_DbYupaQRDy-_01

"Ten out of ten unusual hobbies that'll make you a genius salesman, but they get progressively more niche. Watch your own sales calls every week. Write down where you lost them. Read the same book on selling three times a year. The book stays the same, you don't. Write down three things you're grateful for before every big call. It calms you down way better than any pep talk." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbYupaQRDy-.md

# T_DbYupaQRDy-_02

"Lose at something every day. Play a sport, play a video game, anything where you get beat. It keeps you humble. Once a year, go spend a few days in a small room with 20 people who are better than you. Not a conference, a real room. Surf. Not for the photos, for the wipeouts. You learn more about staying calm under pressure on a board than in any course." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbYupaQRDy-.md

# T_DbYupaQRDy-_03

"Find a mentor who tells you you suck every Friday. The internet will tell you you're great. That's how you stay average. Read the old stoics. Buyers haven't changed in 2000 years. Neither has the person who closes them. Sit in silence for 20 minutes a day. Most reps lose because they can't shut up. Practice it off the call first." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbYupaQRDy-.md

# T_DbYupaQRDy-_04

"Hang out with people who've already won and have nothing to sell you. Everything changes when nobody in the room needs the deal." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DbYupaQRDy-.md
