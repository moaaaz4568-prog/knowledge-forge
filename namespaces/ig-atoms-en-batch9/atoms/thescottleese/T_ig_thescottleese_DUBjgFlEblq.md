# T_DUBjgFlEblq_01

"Everything that you talk about complicates decisions. So the more features you discuss, the more pain points you go over, the more value props, the more ROI, the more report, the more, the more, the more. It actually has the opposite effect. It slows people down. It makes them feel a heaviness and the weight of the decision, and that elongates sales cycles." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DUBjgFlEblq.md

# T_DUBjgFlEblq_02

"So it's a mistake that many early sellers and early stage founders are constantly making where they feel like they have to run through all of the different things that their product can do and all the different ways that it might help you.
