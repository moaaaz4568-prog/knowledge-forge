# T_DaV2uPusuaU_01

"If you're selling B2B, the buyer wants to look smart. Give them the data they'll repeat to their team. If you're selling B2C, the buyer wants to feel smart. Give them the line they'll repeat to their friends. If you're selling B2B, objections come from logic, risk, budget, timing, fit. If you're selling B2C, objections come from doubt. Will I actually use it? Will I regret it? What if it's not me?" — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DaV2uPusuaU.md

# T_DaV2uPusuaU_02

"If you're selling B2B, your discovery is everything. Bad questions, bad deal. If you're selling B2C, your hook is everything. Bad first three seconds, no deal. If you're selling B2B, expect ghosting. Buyers go silent when they can't get internal alignment. If you're selling B2C, expect drop-off. Buyers go silent when their attention moves on. If you're selling B2B, the demo isn't the sale." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DaV2uPusuaU.md

# T_DaV2uPusuaU_03

"The follow-up plan is the sale. If you're selling B2C, the demo is the sale. If they don't feel it in the moment, you lost them. If you're selling B2B, prove you've solved this before. Logos, case studies, named clients. If you're selling B2C, prove someone like them got results. Faces, before and afters, real names." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DaV2uPusuaU.md
