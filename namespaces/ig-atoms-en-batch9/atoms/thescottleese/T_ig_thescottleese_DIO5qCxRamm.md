# T_DIO5qCxRamm_01

"This four-step framework has been the key to all of the sales growth that I've had. It's real simple. It just has four quick steps. The first one is you've got to get somebody to admit that they have a problem. You can't tell them that they have something busted. It just doesn't register the same way." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DIO5qCxRamm.md

# T_DIO5qCxRamm_02

"You've got to ask some questions and get them to admit to you and say, hey, this is a problem for me. I agree. The second thing is you have to get them to understand why they should care about fixing this problem. Loads of people know that they have problems and we do nothing about them at all because we don't think it's important enough." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DIO5qCxRamm.md

# T_DIO5qCxRamm_03

"So you've got to get somebody to understand why fixing this problem is in their best interest. The third thing is we've got to have some urgency. Urgency in recovery comes from somebody hitting rock bottom. So in some level you've got to get somebody to recognize, oh shoot, I'm at rock bottom here. This is the highest priority thing that I could be working on today." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DIO5qCxRamm.md

# T_DIO5qCxRamm_04

"If you're able to achieve those three things, then and only then is somebody ready to hear about your product, your solution. So you've got to find pain, you've got to build value, you've got to unearth this urgency, and then and only then should you be discussing your product or your solution." — sources/S_ig_thescottleese_DIO5qCxRamm.md
