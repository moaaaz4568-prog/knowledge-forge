"Never say these words in sales. They make you sound weak and that's why nobody's buying from you. First, I'm just checking in with you. I mean, can you get more desperate than that? Instead, say, I just had a minute to get back to you. This implies that you're a busy person. Second, I haven't heard from you." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DWVaCVdCoFQ.md

# T_DWVaCVdCoFQ_02

"If you say I haven't heard from you, you sound like a desperate dork that's been chasing them. Instead, say, it's great to connect again. This seems a lot more natural. So instead, say, where do you think we should go from here?" — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DWVaCVdCoFQ.md
