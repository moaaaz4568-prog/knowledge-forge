# T_DTte-_nkiT7_01

"These five words are killing your sales and you don't even know it. Instead of saying it's cheap, say it's our most affordable option. Because cheap screams low quality, when affordable seems like they're getting a deal. Instead of saying we are better, say here's how we're different. Better is subjective and invites argument, where different can't be negotiated." — sources/S_ig_therealbrianmark_DTte-_nkiT7.md
