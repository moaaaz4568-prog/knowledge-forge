"One of the worst things you can do is broadcast your sins. Because the Prophet ﷺ said, all of my Ummah will be excused except those who broadcast and make their sins known. And you see, previously if you were going to expose your sins, you'd meet up with the group of friends and boast about how you did something haram, right?" — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CsioVzlLWLK.md
