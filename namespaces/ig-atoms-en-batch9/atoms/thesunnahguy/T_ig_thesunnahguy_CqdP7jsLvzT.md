# T_CqdP7jsLvzT_01

"Here's three things you can do after you finished your prayer. Number one, say Astaghfirullah, which means I seek your forgiveness O Allah. And we do this because in our prayer we often find ourselves losing concentration and so we're seeking forgiveness for all our shortcomings during the prayer." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CqdP7jsLvzT.md

# T_CqdP7jsLvzT_02

"Number two, say Allāhumma anta salām wa minka al-salām, tabārakta yā dhī al-jalāl wa al-ikrām, which translates as, O Allah, you are peace and from you comes peace. Blessed are you, owner of majesty and honor. The Prophet ﷺ used to say this after the prayer, and what a beautiful way to show humility to our Creator." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CqdP7jsLvzT.md

# T_CqdP7jsLvzT_03

"And number three, recite the Ayatul Kursi as the Prophet ﷺ said, whoever does so after every obligatory prayer, then the only thing between him and Jannah is death. And share this video so others can learn as well." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CqdP7jsLvzT.md
