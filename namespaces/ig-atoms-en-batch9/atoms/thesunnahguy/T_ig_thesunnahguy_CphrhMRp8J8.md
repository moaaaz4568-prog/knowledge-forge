# T_CphrhMRp8J8_01

"A lot of people think that if you accidentally sleep through your alarm at Fajr, then you've missed Fajr, and that demotivates them and makes them feel like they're missing Salah, and they sometimes stop praying because of that as well. Well, let me tell you something. Did you know that the Prophet ﷺ also slept through Fajr accidentally one time?" — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CphrhMRp8J8.md

# T_CphrhMRp8J8_02

"Yeah, I mean, look, he woke up when the sun hit his face, then realized that he missed Fajr unintentionally and simply made wudu and prayed." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CphrhMRp8J8.md

# T_CphrhMRp8J8_03

"Because if you miss Salah unintentionally, like sleeping through Fajr, or you've like unconsciously lost track of time, then the moment you wake up or remember you've missed your Salah, you simply get up and pray, and that doesn't count as a missed prayer because you didn't do it on purpose. You made the intention to pray, you set your alarm, but you just slept through it." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CphrhMRp8J8.md

# T_CphrhMRp8J8_04

"And when that happens, you just get up and pray as soon as you wake up. Don't let things like this demotivate you. It's just a lack of knowledge, but alhamdulillah, now you know." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CphrhMRp8J8.md
