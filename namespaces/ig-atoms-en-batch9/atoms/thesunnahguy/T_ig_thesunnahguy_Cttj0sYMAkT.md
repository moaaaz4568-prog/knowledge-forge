"If you're struggling to practice your religion at work or school, then consider this. The language we use is very important in shaping your identity and confidence as a Muslim in the West. For example, instead of saying I can't, say I don't. What's the difference?" — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_Cttj0sYMAkT.md

# T_Cttj0sYMAkT_02

"Well, if you're in the workplace and say I can't shake hands, I can't listen to music, I can't go to the party, then the power of choice is not with you, and you can be persuaded or convinced because when you say I can't, then that could be interpreted as you may want to go, but you're not allowed to go. And so your stance is not strong." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_Cttj0sYMAkT.md

# T_Cttj0sYMAkT_03

You'll be seen as a person of principle who deserves respect and people will know your boundaries. A simple thing like this can really make a big difference in your interactions at work or school." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_Cttj0sYMAkT.md
