# T_CqNZaauvARL_01

"Because, wallahi, brothers and sisters, sometimes it is not ease that gets us to where we want to go; rather, it is through hardship. And the mark of the believer, what is it? It is hardship. Did Allah not love the Messenger? Did He not love the Prophet ﷺ? Yet he was born an orphan." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CqNZaauvARL.md

# T_CqNZaauvARL_02

"His mother passed away, his grandfather passed away, his uncle passed away, his wife passed away, and he buried six of his children. Think of a person who has gone through more hardship than that. Not to mention what happened with him and his people, what they called him, what they accused him of. And subhanallah, when Allah loves a believer, He blesses him with difficulty." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CqNZaauvARL.md

# T_CqNZaauvARL_03

"So remember that is your mark. Whatever pain and suffering that you are going through right now, this is from your Rabb to strengthen you and to build you and to uplift you and to elevate you and to purify you so that you have entry to Paradise. Allahu Akbar." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CqNZaauvARL.md
