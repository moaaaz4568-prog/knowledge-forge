# T_CrOdzmJsSJT_01

"If you're fed up with life and feel regretful over the years of sin, then say this one dua that was said by Adam alayhis salaam when he ate from the tree and disobeyed Allah. He didn't lose hope, but rather he humbled himself and found comfort in turning back to his Lord." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CrOdzmJsSJT.md

# T_CrOdzmJsSJT_02

"The dua is: رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ Which translates as: Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, then we will surely be doomed and lost." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CrOdzmJsSJT.md

# T_CrOdzmJsSJT_03

"Say this dua in a state of humility, focusing on the words, hoping to change and find comfort with Allah, and know that when you say this with a sincere remorseful heart, that Allah has forgiven you, and you've now turned a new leaf in your life." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CrOdzmJsSJT.md
