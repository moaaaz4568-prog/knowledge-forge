# T_C4qCKIItNa3_01

"Climb mountains not so the world can see you, but so you can see the world. Strive for success not for popularity, fame or status, but so your wealth, freedom and time becomes a means of helping others. Become a scholar or a person of knowledge not so you can be praised, but so you can save others from eternal torment." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_C4qCKIItNa3.md
