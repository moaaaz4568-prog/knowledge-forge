# T_Cpu9e23tWGJ_01

"If you're sexually active with your spouse and you're married, then this is the dua taught to us by the Prophet ﷺ to say before you have intercourse. Bismillahi Allahumma jinnibna sh-shaytana wajinnibna sh-shaytana ma razqatana. In the name of Allah, O Allah, keep the shaitan away from us and keep the shaitan away from what you have blessed us with." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_Cpu9e23tWGJ.md

# T_Cpu9e23tWGJ_02

"And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, whoever says this dua and the wife was pregnant from that night, the shaitan will never be able to harm the child. Some of you might be uncomfortable hearing this, but remember that the Prophet ﷺ told us that there is no shyness when it comes to religion." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_Cpu9e23tWGJ.md

# T_Cpu9e23tWGJ_03

"And he would speak about these subjects openly with his companions and he'd teach them how to be with your spouse in a halal way. And that's exactly what I'm doing here, because so many people don't know about this dua." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_Cpu9e23tWGJ.md
