# T_CF-PrnBhhBS_01

"The very first thing a habit comes from is a thought. And Ibn al Qayim said that when you get a thought, repel it, because if you don't repel the thought, it will become an idea. And that idea, if you don't repel it, it will become a desire and a passion. And that desire and a passion, if you don't fight against it and repel that, that will become then an action." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CF-PrnBhhBS.md

# T_CF-PrnBhhBS_02

"And the action, if you don't fight that, the action will then become a habit. And then if you don't fight the habit, the habit will control you and you no longer have power or control over the habit." — sources/S_ig_thesunnahguy_CF-PrnBhhBS.md
