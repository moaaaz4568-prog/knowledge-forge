# T_DcFcujSht5a_01

"Pre-revenue to product-market fit is not one milestone. It's four stages, and most founders skip two of them. Stage one: problem validation. Do real people have this problem badly enough to pay for a fix? Stage two: solution validation. Will they pay for your specific answer to it? Most founders think they're at stage four when they're really stuck at stage one or two. Stage three: repeatability." — sources/S_ig_therevenuecoaches_DcFcujSht5a.md

# T_DcFcujSht5a_02

"Can you get a second and third customer the same way you got your first? Stage four: product-market fit. Are customers pulling the product from you faster than you can ship it? Knowing your actual stage changes everything you should be doing right now. Guess your stage wrong and you'll spend money solving a problem you don't have, which will hurt your runway." — sources/S_ig_therevenuecoaches_DcFcujSht5a.md
