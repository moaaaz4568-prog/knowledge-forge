"Having a product does not mean you have a product market fit. Most founders confuse the two. Three signs you don't have it yet, even if you think you do. First, customers like it, but they don't reorder it, renew it, or refer it to others. Second, every sale takes a founder on the call to close. That's not a fit yet. That's persuasion, and persuasion doesn't scale to your business." — sources/S_ig_therevenuecoaches_DcHEOKnhbhb.md
