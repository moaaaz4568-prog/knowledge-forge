# G_ig_mattyr_ecom_CeqDitFj_Ay — donate

## Term

Donate is the action of this take-two video: Matt gives $5,000 to Ryan Trahan, a YouTuber his viewers might know, and watches to see whether he gets a shoutout. The word appears twice, beside $5,000, shoutouts for YouTube channels, and Feeding America, the charity he plugs at the end. The shoutout never comes, because Trahan only reads messages from channels with millions of viewers, but Matt calls it a good cause anyway. The donation is both the stunt and the punchline: five thousand dollars spent, no shoutout earned, and a nudge for viewers to give their own schmickles to Feeding America.
