# G_ig_mattyr_ecom_CeR8HAXJYfA — Bitcoin billionaire

## Term

A Bitcoin billionaire is the fortune Matt asks a genie in a box to predict for him: a great Gizzy Genie in a box, tell me, am I going to be one? The phrase appears once, beside Gizzy Genie, in a box, and tell me. The whole video is a single playful question, with the genie and the box as a joke frame for asking about his future wealth. There is no claim here, only a wish addressed to a toy fortune-teller.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a real prediction: the genie is a toy in a box, and the video asks the question without answering it.
- Read as a claim Matt will be a Bitcoin billionaire: he only asks whether he is going to be one, with no evidence offered either way.
