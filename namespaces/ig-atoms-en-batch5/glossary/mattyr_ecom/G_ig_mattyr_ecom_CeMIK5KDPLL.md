# G_ig_mattyr_ecom_CeMIK5KDPLL — Moved

## Term

Moved is the verb that carries Matt's whole life story in this video: born in Poland, moved to the US for a year, moved back to Poland for four years, then moved to Italy before returning to the US, where he has lived eleven years. The word appears three times, beside born in Poland, parents divorced, and stayed there for four years. Each move is dated by a stretch of his childhood, and the story lands on the present: he did the dropshipping thing and now he is making the schmickles. The moves are not travel anecdotes; they are the frame for an immigrant biography that ends in his current business.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a travel story: the moves are childhood relocations tied to his parents' divorce and his mother's choices, not trips Matt chose.
- Read as a claim that moving made him rich: the story ends with doing the dropshipping thing, and the moves are biography, not a business method.
