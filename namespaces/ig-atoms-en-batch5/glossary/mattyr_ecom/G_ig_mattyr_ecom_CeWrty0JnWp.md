# G_ig_mattyr_ecom_CeWrty0JnWp — high ticket

## Term

High ticket is the price tier behind Matt's boast of twenty-two thousand dollars from only eighteen orders: a few expensive sales rather than many cheap ones. The phrase appears once, beside twenty-two k, eighteen orders, and a really strong community that tells its friends to buy. His recipe is a unique product plus a community that spreads the word plus a problem the product actually solves. The point of the high ticket framing is leverage: with the right product, a handful of orders is worth more than a flood of small ones.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise of twenty-two thousand dollars from eighteen orders for anyone: the result depends on a unique product, a strong community, and a problem the product solves.
- Read as a claim that cheap ads do the work alone: he credits emotional appeal and organic-looking ads, not just the sub-thirty-cent clicks.
