# G_ig_mattyr_ecom_Cf2LIoEDgto — hard work

## Term

Hard work is the target of this rant: Matt opens by telling viewers to reject their job, then says hard work is bad for something the recording cuts off before naming. The phrase appears once, beside Fuck your job, paid to do, and office bitch, as the thing he warns against doing. He draws a line between doing only what you are paid to do and being a slave to the office, with hard work on the wrong side of it. The use is a provocation, not a program: a dare to stop overworking, left unfinished mid-sentence.
