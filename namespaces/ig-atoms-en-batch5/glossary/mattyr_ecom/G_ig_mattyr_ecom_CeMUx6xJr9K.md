# G_ig_mattyr_ecom_CeMUx6xJr9K — AliExpress

## Term

AliExpress is the supplier Matt warns newcomers against: gurus tell beginners it is the best and cheapest, then customers refuse to wait two months for a product while Amazon exists. The name appears three times, beside newcomers, cheapest and awesome, and a Facebook ban as the wake-up call that follows. He says the beginner is left out of luck without a better plan, and tells the gurus to stop recommending it. The point is speed and trust: a two-month wait loses the customer, and the ad account pays the price.
