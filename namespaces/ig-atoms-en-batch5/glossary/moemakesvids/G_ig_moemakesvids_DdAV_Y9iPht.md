# G_ig_moemakesvids_DdAV_Y9iPht — talking head

## Term

In this video a talking head is the raw, unedited footage of the speaker addressing the camera, the material the whole edit starts from. The phrase occurs three times: once for the raw talking head footage uploaded into ChatCut, and twice for the talking head video that ChatCut reads and reshapes.
