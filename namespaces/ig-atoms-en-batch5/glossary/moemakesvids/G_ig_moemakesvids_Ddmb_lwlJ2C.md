# G_ig_moemakesvids_Ddmb_lwlJ2C — Delta Airlines

## Term

Dallas Fort Worth International is where the service ends in this announcement: the flight reaches Atlanta first, then continues there. Delta Airlines occurs once, naming the carrier of flight 2998, and the welcome to ladies and gentlemen opens the sentence. Its neighbours are the flight number, Atlanta, and the phrase continuous service. The far city matters because it shows the flight is not only an Atlanta flight; it is one service covering both.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome aboard Delta Airlines flight 2998 bound for Atlanta, Georgia, with continuous service to Dallas Fort Worth International." — sources/S_ig_moemakesvids_Ddmb_lwlJ2C.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a stopover, continuous service means the same service goes on to Dallas; the announcement does not describe a change of plane.
- Taken as the destination, Dallas Fort Worth is the onward city, while Atlanta is where the flight is bound first.
