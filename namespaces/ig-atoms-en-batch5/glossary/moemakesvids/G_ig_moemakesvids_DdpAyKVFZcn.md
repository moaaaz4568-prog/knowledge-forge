# G_ig_moemakesvids_DdpAyKVFZcn — Delta Airlines

## Term

The airline is the operator in this announcement: Delta Airlines runs flight 2998, and the passengers are welcomed aboard its aircraft. The name occurs once, beside the flight number, with Atlanta and Dallas Fort Worth giving the route in the same sentence. Its neighbours are the greeting to ladies and gentlemen and the phrase continuous service. The announcement's job is identification, and the carrier's name is the first fact it gives.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome aboard Delta Airlines flight 2998 bound for Atlanta, Georgia, with continuous service to Dallas Fort Worth International." — sources/S_ig_moemakesvids_DdpAyKVFZcn.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as an advertisement, the announcement only welcomes passengers aboard; it makes no claim about the airline's service quality.
- Taken as a flight status, the single sentence says the flight is boarded, not that it has departed or arrived.
