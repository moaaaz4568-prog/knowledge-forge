# G_ig_moemakesvids_DdEE9nrireO — B-roll

## Term

Here B-roll means the supporting footage, product shots and cutaway clips, that ChatCut matches against the talking head material to complete a brand video. The term appears twice, once as B-roll beside the product shots and once as the plural B-rolls among the captions and pacing ChatCut arranges. Its neighbours are the brand script the speaker hands over, the talking head footage it must match, and the complete, well-structured brand video that is ready within minutes. The speaker never has to review and organise every piece of footage by hand, because the plugin selects the right clips and orders them itself.

## Synonyms

- B-rolls (the plural form, used once in this source)

## Live Example

> "ChatCut automatically selects the right clips and arranges the captions, B-rolls, and pacing." — sources/S_ig_moemakesvids_DdEE9nrireO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a claim that B-roll is the main footage, the usage here is the opposite: B-roll is the supporting material matched to the talking head shots, not the lead shot.
- Taken as a general editing rule, the two mentions describe only what ChatCut does with this one brand script and its filmed footage.
