# G_ig_moemakesvids_DeDn1z_DknG — Delta Airlines

## Term

Flight 2998 is the announcement's numbered service: one flight, two cities, and a single welcome aboard. Delta Airlines occurs once, as the carrier of that service, with Atlanta named first and Dallas Fort Worth named as the continuation. Its neighbours are the greeting to ladies and gentlemen, the flight number, and continuous service. The number is what a passenger would look for on a board; the airline's name is what tells them which board to check.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a flight number for a different route, 2998 belongs to this announcement's service only; the number says nothing about other flights.
- Taken as a timetable entry, the announcement gives no times; it only names the carrier, the flight, and the cities.
