# G_ig_moemakesvids_DdkuL7HgB2d — Delta Airlines

## Term

Atlanta is named first in this announcement: the flight is bound for Atlanta, Georgia, before its service continues to Dallas. Delta Airlines occurs once, as the carrier of flight 2998, with ladies and gentlemen welcomed aboard in the same sentence. Its neighbours are the flight number, Atlanta, and Dallas Fort Worth International. The order of the cities is the order of the journey, and the carrier's name is what ties the sentence together.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as the final stop, Atlanta is the first city named; the service continues to Dallas Fort Worth after it.
- Taken as a city pair, the announcement names one flight serving two cities in sequence, not a round trip.
