# G_ig_moemakesvids_DdJuycQAn3T — terminator

## Term

The verse opens with wet wipes and a warning, then hands the speaker his title: the terminator. The word occurs twice, in the claim that he is the terminator and in the claim that others need one, while terminate appears once as the verb that turns the title into a threat. Neighbouring words are the germinator, the queen, and the bugs told to move back. The figure works because it is both a name and a promise: say the name and the consequence follows in the same breath.
