# G_ig_moemakesvids_DdbcYXtCTQ7 — Delta Airlines

## Term

The route is the point of this announcement: the flight is bound for Atlanta, Georgia, and the service continues on to Dallas Fort Worth International. Delta Airlines occurs once, as the carrier of flight 2998, and the welcome to ladies and gentlemen frames the whole sentence. Its neighbours are the flight number, the two cities, and the phrase continuous service that links them. The name identifies the operator; the cities identify the journey, in order.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a nonstop flight, the wording says continuous service to Dallas, with Atlanta named first; it does not say the aircraft skips Atlanta.
- Taken as a route name, flight 2998 is this announcement's numbered service, not a permanent label for the Atlanta to Dallas run.
