# G_ig_moemakesvids_Ddr99flk6Ee — Delta Airlines

## Term

Bound for is the announcement's direction word: flight 2998 is bound for Atlanta, Georgia, and its service continues to Dallas. Delta Airlines occurs once, as the carrier, with the welcome to ladies and gentlemen opening the sentence. Its neighbours are the flight number, the two cities, and continuous service. Bound for says where the aircraft is headed first; continuous service says the journey does not stop there.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as the only destination, bound for Atlanta names the first stop; Dallas Fort Worth is the onward destination in the same sentence.
- Taken as a guarantee, the announcement states the planned route only; it promises no arrival time.
