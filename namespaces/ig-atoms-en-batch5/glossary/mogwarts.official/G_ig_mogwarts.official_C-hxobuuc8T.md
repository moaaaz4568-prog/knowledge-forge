# G_ig_mogwarts.official_C-hxobuuc8T — friends

## Term

Friends is the status this dialogue keeps circling: a couple of six months who are just friends, though he wants more. The word occurs twice, in the plain statement that they are friends and in the hedge that they are just friends, with the question of how long they have been together opening the exchange. Its neighbours are the six months, the newish relationship, and the request not to talk about it in front of everyone. The word does the awkward work of the scene: it names the relationship while admitting it might not stay that way.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a settled fact, the friends label is contested inside the dialogue itself; he wants more than friendship.
- Taken as a timeline, six months describes how long they have been together, not how long they have been only friends.
