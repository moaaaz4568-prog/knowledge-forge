# G_ig_mogwarts.official_DCeKLG3RG25 — chant

## Term

This source has no conceptual term, and chant is only the entry's type label for a repeated Portuguese dance hook. The line is a greeting and a game: playing in her wheels, with the word chota sounded three times as the hook. Its neighbours are the wheels she is played in and the greeting that opens the line. The source is in Portuguese, and the hook is the whole content: a short dance line built around one repeated word.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a term, chota is the hook word repeated three times, not a concept the source defines.
- Taken as a game with rules, the playing line is a lyric in a dance song, not a description of an actual game.
