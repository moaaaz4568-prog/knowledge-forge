# G_ig_mogwarts.official_C-PiJDVyn_t — dancing

## Term

Dancing is the image this short source leaves: a pretty little body dancing across the room like no one is watching, seen from across the room. The word occurs once, and its neighbours are the problems the speaker says he has had enough of for 24, the pretty little body, and the room itself. The source is a fragment of a song lyric: a complaint, then a vision across the room, with the dancing as the thing that catches the eye.
