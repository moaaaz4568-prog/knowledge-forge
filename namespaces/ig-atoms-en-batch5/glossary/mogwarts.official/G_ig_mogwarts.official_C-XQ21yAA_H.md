# G_ig_mogwarts.official_C-XQ21yAA_H — looks

## Term

Looks are the subject this source argues against: the speaker says looks just simply do not matter, and that people are shocked when he says so. The word occurs three times, each time to deny its importance, while being funny, being cool, and being fit are offered as the things that do matter, especially over time. Its neighbours are the shock of the listeners, the followers drawn by looks, and the question of where such a following can go. The argument is practical, not moral: a following built on looks has nowhere to go when looks fade.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Whenever I say that looks don't matter to me, a lot of people are shocked by that." — sources/S_ig_mogwarts.official_C-XQ21yAA_H.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Taken as a claim that looks never count, the source argues they do not matter over time, not that no one notices them.
- Taken as a rule for everyone, the argument is the speaker's own view in this clip, not a study of attraction.
