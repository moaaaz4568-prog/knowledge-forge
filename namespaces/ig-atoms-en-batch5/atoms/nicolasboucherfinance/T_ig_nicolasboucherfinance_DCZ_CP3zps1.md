# T_DCZ_CP3zps1_02

"On the other hand, budgeting for growth focuses on investing in new opportunities and expanding the business. It assumes a willingness to take on additional risk, prioritizes future potential over current stability, and often requires higher levels of funding. Regarding the advantages, budgeting for stability provides a sense of security and predictability." — sources/S_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_DCZ_CP3zps1.md
