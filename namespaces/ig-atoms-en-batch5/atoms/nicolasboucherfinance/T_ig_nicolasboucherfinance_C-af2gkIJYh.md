# T_C-af2gkIJYh_01

"What is EBITDA? EBITDA stands for Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. It's a metric showing a company's earnings before non-operational and non-cash expenses. Why is EBITDA important for businesses? It shows how much money businesses are generating from their operations. This is also useful for investors and lenders to check the profitability of a company." — sources/S_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_C-af2gkIJYh.md

# T_C-af2gkIJYh_02

"It's like a scorecard to know how much money a company is making. How is EBITDA calculated? Start with a company's revenue and subtract its cost of goods sold. Then, you subtract its operating expenses. In EBITDA, you don't take into consideration depreciation, taxes, and interest. But the net income is what remains as actual profit after depreciation, interest, and taxes are taken in account." — sources/S_ig_nicolasboucherfinance_C-af2gkIJYh.md

# T_C-af2gkIJYh_03
