Here's how to actually transform yourself and your life permanently. Now most of us have had those times where we felt more disciplined and on top of our shit, but that only lasted like a month and then it kind of fell apart. But the thing you need to make these changes last are systems. Now I know you're probably thinking, what the fuck is a system? A system is a set of rules or protocols that you follow when you don't have motivation. So for example, if you want to stay consistent in the gym and be in good shape for the rest of your life, you need a system to get yourself to show up when you're hungover and sleep deprived. And if that system is show up for two hours and have the best workout of my life, that's a fucking shit system because it's unrealistic. My system for staying consistent in the gym is simply show up and do three exercises minimum. That's the system that gets me to show up every day. And my system for when I miss a day is don't miss two days in a row. Notice how my systems exist specifically for the worst case scenario. That's how systems are supposed to work. Now if you struggle to stay consistent with your systems, that doesn't mean you're too lazy or too undisciplined. It just means your systems are too unrealistic. So don't compare your systems with other people because some people might love going to the gym and their system can be showing up for two hours every single day. Personally, that's not me. I don't really like going to the gym. The trick is to test and find systems that work for you.
