# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DUJqWoijO6w — practice in private

## Term

To "practice in private" is the speaker's secret to becoming world class at sales: drill the scripts so much you are "numb, bored out of your mind of hearing your own lines" — and then forget the script entirely so all your attention can be present with the prospect. The phrase occurs twice in the source, both times in the Tony Robbins line the video is built on: "we get rewarded in public for what you practice in private". Its neighbors are "war tracks", "pre-frames", "boring work", and "rewarded in public". Concretely, the private work is going over calls, putting in new war tracks, repeating lines to yourself — "the hard work that we do in silence" while everyone else talks about the glamour, the 50K and 100K — and its payoff is that on the call you can hear the prospect's beliefs and reframe them instead of reciting.

## Live Example

> "No one wants to talk about it. Everyone wants to talk about the glamour, the 50K, 100K, all of that, but no one wants to talk about the hard work that we do in silence because again, you'll get rewarded in public for what you practice in private." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DUJqWoijO6w.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as rehearsing lines before a call: the practice here ends in forgetting the script — the goal is presence, not recall — so drilling to recite word-for-word on the call inverts the point and leaves no attention for the prospect.
- Read as working hard in general: the private work is specific — calls reviewed, war tracks installed, lines repeated to yourself — and vague hard work with no script to drill produces none of the boredom the video says is the proof it worked.
