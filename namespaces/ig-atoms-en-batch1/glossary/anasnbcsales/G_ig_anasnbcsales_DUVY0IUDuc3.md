# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DUVY0IUDuc3 — money objection

## Term

A "money objection" is the casual price pushback that arrives early on an outbound call — "oh, I don't know, it depends, what do you have?" — and that the speaker says makes most salespeople crumble. The phrase occurs once in the source, and the video's whole lesson is to lean into it instead of dodging it: his closer Maddie answers "yeah, of course", frames a quick five-to-ten-minute chat to check alignment, and the objection is gone — "Objection gone. Moving the sale forward." Its neighbors are "lean into the objection", "customized proposal", "alignment", and "outbound call". Concretely, the rep then uses the same lean-in to ask for the next step — "build your customized proposal, just see if there's some alignment" — and to connect the dots on what the prospect is doing, instead of freezing at the first mention of money.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pricing problem: the objection here is casual and early, a reflex rather than a budget fact — treating it as a signal to discount or justify price answers a question nobody asked and stalls the call the lean-in was meant to advance.
- Read as a cue to pitch harder: the lean-in is agreement ("yeah, of course... not a big deal"), not a counter-argument — pushing the proposal at the moment of the objection repeats the crumbling the video is sick of watching, just louder.
