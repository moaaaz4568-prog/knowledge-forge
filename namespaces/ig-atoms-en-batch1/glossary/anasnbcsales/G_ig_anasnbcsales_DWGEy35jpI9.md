# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DWGEy35jpI9 — repeated exposure

## Term

"Repeated exposure" is how the speaker says you build the muscle for rejection: get slapped by it every single day until it stops registering. The phrase occurs twice in the source — once as the method ("the way you build that muscle is repeated exposure") and once as his own history ("That repeated exposure got me so numb to rejection"). Its neighbors are "tough skin", "cold calls", "numb to rejection", and "unshakable confidence". Concretely, knocking doors meant getting "slapped every single day", and now rejection "just feels like a normal part of the day" — which is why his fastest prescription for the resistance people feel before outbound calls is to "pick up the phone and do some fucking cold calls" and endure the volume.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "rejection doesn't hurt": the point is numbness through volume, not fearlessness — the exposure works because it is daily and repeated, so a single hard rejection taken as proof the muscle failed mistakes the dose for the cure.
- Read as a license to be reckless: the exposure is to rejection on the phones, in service of confidence, money, and girls — treating it as a general dare to seek pain anywhere turns a training method into a stunt with no skill attached.
