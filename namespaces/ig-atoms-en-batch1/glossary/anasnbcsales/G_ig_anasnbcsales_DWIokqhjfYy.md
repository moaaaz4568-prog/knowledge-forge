# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DWIokqhjfYy — faith

## Term

"Faith" is the speaker's name for believing in a future that hasn't happened yet — a positive, compelling, beautiful one — as opposed to fear, which believes in a negative one with the same mechanism. The word occurs twice in the source ("What is faith?" and "would you rather go towards faith"), and the choice matters because the belief drives the body: fear sends stress up, cortisol up, motivation down, "nervous system is shutting down", while faith demands surrender and positive visualization. Its neighbors are "fear", "positive visualization", "biochemical change", and "steak". Concretely, he proves visualization works with a steak — "Imagine a steak coming out sizzling... does your mouth start to salivate?" — and concludes "Visualize, experience biochemical change, emotion", so the future you picture literally moves your chemistry before it exists.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as religious faith: the faith here is a chosen direction for the imagination — a future, a movie of your life, money coming in, health returning — not a doctrine, so importing a theological reading loads the word with a meaning the video never assigns it.
- Read as wishful thinking: faith here is work — sitting with intention, creating the scenes, feeling the intrinsic drive — and treating it as waking up to everything being amazing skips the visualization the speaker says the change depends on.
