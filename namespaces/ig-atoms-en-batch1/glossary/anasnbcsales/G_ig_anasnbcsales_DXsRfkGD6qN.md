# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DXsRfkGD6qN — prosperous journey

## Term

A "prosperous journey" is the outcome the speaker promises as he closes the roofing-company sales call: a great, prosperous journey together, God willing. The phrase occurs once in this 20-word source, which is nothing but the call's sign-off — the promise, the agreement, and the goodbye. Its neighbors are "God willing", "Saturday", and "Bye bye". Concretely, the whole source is the closing frame of the demo call: the journey is the work ahead together, the "Saturday" is the next meeting, and the doubled "Bye bye" is the farewell — so the term here is the prospect-facing name for the future the call just sold, not a technique.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a sales technique: the phrase is a closing blessing, not a method — treating "prosperous journey" as a named framework invents a system out of a goodbye.
- Read as a guarantee of results: the journey is conditional on "God willing" and on the work the call set up — quoting it as a promised outcome turns a hopeful sign-off into a contract the speaker never signed.
