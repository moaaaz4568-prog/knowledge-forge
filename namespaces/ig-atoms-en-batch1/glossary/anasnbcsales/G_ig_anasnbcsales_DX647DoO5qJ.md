# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DX647DoO5qJ — sea of sameness

## Term

The "sea of sameness" is where the speaker says saturation is actually felt: not in the niche, but in everyone copying the same thing. The phrase occurs once in the source, as the diagnosis — "it's that you're in that sea of sameness" — and the argument is that niches don't saturate, offers do: when you say the same things, do the same things, and look the same way, the market feels saturated. Its neighbors are "saturated", "positioning the offer", "unique name", and "novelty". Concretely, the reason you cannot hit a saturation is that "all businesses always need new marketing", and the prospect's "I've literally heard it all" is not rudeness but the symptom — the fix is positioning: "Unique name, unique approach, unique guarantees. That is where the novelty comes".

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "the niche is fine, keep going": the sea of sameness is a verdict on the offer, not a pass for it — staying in the same copy, the same look, the same pitch keeps you in the sea, so the phrase licenses a repositioning, not persistence.
- Read as a branding slogan: the sameness is about the whole offer — name, approach, guarantees — not just the logo, so fixing the visuals while keeping the same words leaves the prospect hearing the same thing again.
