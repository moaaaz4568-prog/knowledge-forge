# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DU9IRBtkhhW — reset ritual

## Term

A "reset ritual" is the one-minute routine the top 1% closers the speaker coached run between sales calls so the previous call cannot poison the next one. The phrase occurs once in the source, as the habit that separates those closers — "they have a reset ritual between call to call to call to call, even if it's just one minute" — and the video then teaches three of the speaker's own: a ridiculous physical reset from his door-to-door days, eyes closed and bouncing with big breaths, and blasting a favorite song. Its neighbors are "physiological reset", "pre-locker prep game", "top 1% closers", and "one minute". Concretely, after a door slam and a threat he would turn on the porch, drop down, leap, and shout "hey" — something "so ridiculous that shift my physiology" that "you can't be mad, you can't be flustered, you can't be upset" — and then walk into the next call fresh.

## Live Example

> "What the top 1% closers that I've coached do very well is they have a reset ritual between call to call to call to call, even if it's just one minute." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DU9IRBtkhhW.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a pep talk: the ritual here is physical — a leap, a breath, a song — not a motivational sentence, and treating it as self-talk keeps the previous call's funk in the body the ritual is meant to clear.
- Read as a break between calls: the reset is not rest but a switch — "I reset my body, I get some oxygen, I close my eyes so I can give my brain a little bit of break, and boom, I'm back" — so stretching it into downtime misses that its job is to make the next call start at 100%.
