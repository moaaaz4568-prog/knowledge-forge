# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DXAkcsUj5xu — worst case scenario

## Term

The "worst case scenario" is how the speaker handles skepticism: guide the prospect through the worst case and show that even then, the deal still works. The phrase occurs once in the source, as the named move, and the role-play runs on pessimism — "if half of this was true, if a quarter of this was true", "let's just say for the first month be pessimistic" — until the prospect's own math lands at six thousand dollars a month and the answer is yes, it would still be worth it and they would profit. Its neighbors are "super pessimistic", "certainty", "courage", and "the only way through... is through it". Concretely, the certainty the prospect wants can never arrive before the process — "you could sleep on it, you could pray on it, you could think on it, you could nothing will ever give it to you" — because courage is acting despite the fear, and "the only thing that fuels the fear is time", so the close is to take time out of the equation and get in motion.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guarantee: the worst case here is the prospect's own pessimistic math, not a promise — the rep says "we'll definitely never ever hit something this low" while refusing to over-promise, so quoting the scenario as a floor turns a role-play into a warranty.
- Read as a way to skip the fear: the worst case is walked through precisely because the fear is real — certainty only comes from going through the process — so rushing past the scenario to the close leaves the prospect's nerve exactly where it was.
