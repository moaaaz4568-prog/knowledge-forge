# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DXR_DPDEQlb — different offer

## Term

A "different offer" is what the speaker says actually makes you great at sales: not more calls on your own offer, but closing calls on someone else's. The singular occurs once in the source — "jumping on a different offer" — and the method is to hit up a homie for three, four, five closing calls, take a 10-minute crash course on what they say, get on their calendar, and close deals outside your industry and outside your offer. Its neighbors are "outside context", "stay sharp and relevant", "level you the fuck up", and "crash course". Concretely, that outside context teaches how to deal with different types of prospects, how to position, how to sell, how to reframe — and four or five such calls level you up "way more than taking another 50 calls on your exact offer", which is why he and every one of his coaches close on different offers every single week.

## Synonyms

- different offers (the plural form the speaker uses for the weekly practice: "close on different offers every single week")

## Live Example

> "And that's why myself and every single one of my coaches today close on different offers every single week so we can stay sharp and relevant." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DXR_DPDEQlb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as quitting your offer: the speaker explicitly says "I'm not saying quit what you're doing" — the different offer is a training ground, not a replacement, so switching offers permanently mistakes the exercise for the career.
- Read as a shortcut past practice: the growth comes from being humbled by unfamiliar prospects, not from collecting offers — taking five calls on another offer and changing nothing about how you sell keeps the 50-call plateau, just on a new product.
