# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DU2fIZejhF0 — acknowledgement

## Term

"Acknowledgement" is the speaker's art of naming a person's efforts, successes, struggles, attempts, and intentions before ever making a request — the move that makes persuasion possible. The noun occurs twice in the source ("master the art of acknowledgement" and "acknowledgement first"), and its payoff is that the person feels "seen, heard, and validated", so later, when the speaker leads them toward a decision, "they feel understood" and commit, believe, and trust "way more". Its neighbors are "seen, heard, and validated", "master persuader", "empathy", and "good intention". Concretely, when a prospect lists everything they tried, the wrong move is to dig into the pain of what didn't work; the right move is "Wow, man, okay, so how much time did you spend on that?" — acknowledging the commitment before ever pitching the strategy.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as flattery or empty praise: acknowledgement here names specific costs — time spent, effort, energy, struggle — and flattery that names nothing earns no trust, while the real move makes the person feel the speaker actually saw the attempt.
- Read as a persuasion trick to deploy after the pitch: the video insists on order — "acknowledgement first" and "before we ever try to convince them" — so used as a closer after the ask, it is just a compliment attached to a request, not the art being taught.
