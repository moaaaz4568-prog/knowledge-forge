# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DWxgz7qDDUJ — doing you a service

## Term

"Doing you a service" is the speaker's reframe of cold calling: you are not intruding, you are doing the prospect a service by calling, because what you have is the golden goose — the thing so good no one else has. The phrase occurs once in the source in this form, and its general form "doing them a service" is the video's whole shift: "the big shift is you're not bothering them". Its neighbors are "golden goose", "not bothering them", "frame and status", and "dive deeper". Concretely, the rep keeps status while outbounding — "I have all the frame and status" — and opens with honest uncertainty, "I don't know if you're the right person for it, I just want to make sure you're the right person", before checking whether the thing can help and diving deeper.

## Synonyms

- doing them a service (the general form the speaker uses for the same move: "You're doing them a service")

## Live Example

> "I'm not intruding. I'm doing you a service by calling because you got the golden goose." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DWxgz7qDDUJ.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as arrogance: the service claim rests on the golden goose — a thing genuinely that good — so using the line to sell an ordinary offer turns a status reframe into entitlement the prospect can hear immediately.
- Read as a permission to push: the service is a call to check fit ("let me just see if thing is can help you"), not a license to pitch — steamrolling the prospect with the pitch after claiming to serve them proves the intrusion the line denies.
