# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DX7ERRNAgK6 — just dial

## Term

"Just dial" is the speaker's prescription for a rep who says the dialing isn't working: stop debating it and dial until your throat hurts. The phrase occurs twice in the source, doubled as the opening command — "Bro, just dial, just dial" — and the promise attached to it is a zero percent chance of not breaking through if you speak all day. Its neighbors are "zero percent chance", "rounding error", "your throat hurts", and "not even data". Concretely, the rep who speaks to 10 or 12 people "is not going to win", and the one who tells the speaker it didn't work after 30 people is "a rounding error" — 30 conversations is "not even data", so the volume has to come first and the verdict only after it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Bro, just dial, just dial. Speak till like your throat hurts so much at the end of the day, like all day." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DX7ERRNAgK6.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as mindless dialing: the dialing is the volume that makes the breakthrough possible, not the whole skill — dialing all day with no pitch to move the call wastes the throat the line asks you to spend.
- Read as "30 people is nothing, keep going forever": the point is that 30 is too small a sample to judge the method, not that volume has no limit — using the line to refuse ever judging the pitch turns a sample-size warning into an excuse to never improve.
