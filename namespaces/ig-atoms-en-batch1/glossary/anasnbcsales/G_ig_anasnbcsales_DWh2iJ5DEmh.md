# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DWh2iJ5DEmh — NBC vortex method

## Term

The "NBC vortex method" is the speaker's three-layer system for pushing a bunch of deals through within a 24-hour timeline, offered as the exact strategy behind a client about to hit 100K cash collected with zero ad spend, all off cold outbound. The phrase occurs once in the source, as the name of the method, and only the first two layers are described on camera: layer one is the low hanging fruit — everyone in the CRM who took a sales call and didn't buy, hit with the reoffer exactly as shown, "people will literally pay you by text without getting on a sales call" — and layer two is the colder leads, the rest of the CRM with no context. Its neighbors are "low hanging fruit", "sad panda", "Oprah", and "reoffer". Concretely, layer two reopens the conversation with playful re-engagement ("the sad panda, the Oprah, all of it") until the lead comes back laughing — "oh my god, I was out sick" — and is back "in the pocket", where the rep can push them over the edge and close.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a complete system: the video names three layers but only shows two — the third is never described on camera — so treating the clip as the end-to-end process mistakes a teaser (comment "vortex" for the full document) for the method itself.
- Read as a cold-outbound system: the vortex works the CRM — people already talked to — not fresh lists, so applying its reoffer and sad-panda moves to strangers who never took a call aims the method at people with no conversation to reopen.
