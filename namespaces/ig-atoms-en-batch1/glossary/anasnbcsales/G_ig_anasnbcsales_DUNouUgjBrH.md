# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DUNouUgjBrH — sense of belonging

## Term

A "sense of belonging" is what the speaker chased when his environment didn't inspire him: instead of changing his room, he stared obsessively at the environments he wanted — Dubai walking tours, Miami walking tours, stages he wanted to speak on — until his subconscious accepted them as familiar. The phrase occurs once in the source, as the feeling he wanted from places he had never been: "feel places that I wanted to be, where I wanted to get that sense of belonging". Its neighbors are "walking tour", "self-sabotaging inner critic", "subconscious", and "familiar". Concretely, he watched speakers and stages, then worked his way into those rooms — over 100 stage talks later — because seeing an environment again and again "penetrates the filters of our mind of what we see and believe is possible", so when action comes, the inner critic that asks "oh no, who are you?" stays silent.

## Live Example

> "And I would just look obsessively about things I wanted to experience, feel places that I wanted to be, where I wanted to get that sense of belonging." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DUNouUgjBrH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as wanting to fit in: the belonging here is claimed in advance, not requested — the speaker trains his subconscious to treat the stage and the penthouse as already his, so reading it as seeking acceptance from a room misses that the room is being used as fuel.
- Read as daydreaming about a lifestyle: the practice ends in action — "see it, feel it, believe it, and you put yourself in those situations in advance, then take the actions" — so watching walking tours with no move toward the rooms watched is the opposite of the rig the video teaches.
