# G_ig_anasnbcsales_DUDFs22DDb7 — window shopping mode

## Term

"Window shopping mode" is the state a prospect sits in when an old limiting belief — a past experience that burnt them — keeps them from ever entering a call intending to buy. The phrase occurs once in the source, and the speaker's diagnosis is exact: in this mode they "just gonna get on the call, listen to info", say "yeah, this sounds good", and ask for an email, because "they were never in the call thinking about making a buying decision". Its neighbors are "limiting belief", "defense mechanism", "reframe", and "window shopper". Concretely, the rep's job is to turn the window shopper into a buyer by reframing the old meaning — digging into the root cause until the prospect admits the past experience held "some positive and little wins here and there", not only burn and regret.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And the key thing is they now have this old belief and you can't because now right now they're in window shopping mode with that belief." — sources/S_ig_anasnbcsales_DUDFs22DDb7.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as casual browsing: the mode here is a defense mechanism, not curiosity — the prospect says "I don't like to make rash decisions" to protect themselves from being taken advantage of, so treating it as mild interest misses the fear underneath.
- Read as a permanent trait: the speaker treats the mode as a belief to reframe, not a personality — once the old experience is re-examined and re-empowered, the same person knows "exactly what you want", so labeling them a window shopper forever blocks the reframe.
