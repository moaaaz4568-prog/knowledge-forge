# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CScspHnnX77 — high production value

## Term

High production value is what the speaker says Kabbe Lame's videos do not have: they are not high production value, and that is his whole point about growing on social media. The phrase appears once, beside Kabbe Lame, points at things, and genius, in a source answering how to grow if you are not good on camera or lack good equipment. Most of the time Kabbe does not even speak, he just kind of points at things, yet what he has done is very clever and he is a genius for it. The speaker's concrete conclusion is that you do not need a lot external to create content that people will consume.
