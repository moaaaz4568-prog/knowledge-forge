# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLsGuAXHLKh — landing page builder

## Term

A landing page builder is what the speaker evaluates for marketers starting out without a budget, comparing three free options: Mailchimp, Carrd, and GroovePages. The singular appears once and the plural once, beside Mailchimp, Carrd, GroovePages, and opt-in form. Mailchimp's builder is limited — little design control, no background color change, a weird URL — Carrd gives more design power but no embedded opt-in form unless you pay, and GroovePages wins his vote: three free pages with total design control, email or SMS opt-in integration, and a URL that does not look bad.

## Synonyms

- landing page builders (the plural form the speaker uses for the free options)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a paid-tool roundup: all three builders he evaluates are free, and the budget limit is the whole premise of the comparison.
- Read as a Mailchimp endorsement: he says he loves Mailchimp as a free service, but its landing page builder is not his favorite.
