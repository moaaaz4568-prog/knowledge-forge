# G_ig_airesearcherguy_COvpYhNnrqT — Gen Zers

## Term

Gen Zers are the group the speaker says now uses TikTok more than Instagram in the US, a shift he calls kind of obvious and already known. The phrase appears once, beside 2019, 2020, and 2021 adoption, and Snapchat on top the whole time. His timeline: low TikTok adoption in 2019, TikTok and Instagram neck and neck in 2020, Instagram down and TikTok up in 2021 — while Snapchat stayed on top throughout. His punchline is that marketers are sleeping on Snapchat, the platform the numbers put first all along.
