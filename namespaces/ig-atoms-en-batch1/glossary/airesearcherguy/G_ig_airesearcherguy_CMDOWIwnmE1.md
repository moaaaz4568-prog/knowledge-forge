# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMDOWIwnmE1 — stock dimensions template

## Term

A stock dimensions template is Canva's new trick in this source: a template sized just for TikTok video, with a bunch of animated templates, for making all kinds of content without actually being in the video. The phrase appears once, beside Canva, TikTok video, animated templates, and opt-in form. The speaker's bet is that the template is enough to run a whole account on: make the content from the templates alone, with no face on camera at all.
