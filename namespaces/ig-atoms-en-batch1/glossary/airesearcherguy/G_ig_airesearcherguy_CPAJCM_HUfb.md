# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CPAJCM_HUfb — uncertainty

## Term

Uncertainty is the thing the speaker says hurts customers more than inconvenience: a bad experience can be recovered from, but leaving people unsure is unacceptable. The word appears twice, beside inconvenience, negative reviews, and customer experience, while the adjective form uncertain appears once, for a customer left without an answer. His concrete case is a lost order: acknowledge the loss, promise a replacement, and give a time to expect it, and the experience can turn positive. The failure mode is silence — no communication leaves the customer uncertain, and that uncertainty is what leads to negative reviews.

## Synonyms

- uncertain (the adjective form he uses for a customer left without an answer)

## Live Example

> "However, when people experience uncertainty, that is unacceptable." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CPAJCM_HUfb.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that inconvenience does not matter: he says it is easy to recover from, not that it is harmless, and the recovery still needs the replacement and the time.
- Read as advice to hide bad news: his fix is the opposite, to acknowledge the loss openly and name the replacement time.
