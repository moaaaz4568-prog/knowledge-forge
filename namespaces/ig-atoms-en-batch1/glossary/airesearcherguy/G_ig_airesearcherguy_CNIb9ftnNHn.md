# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CNIb9ftnNHn — product inserts

## Term

Product inserts are the physical cards the speaker says e-commerce sellers underuse: print marketing can outperform digital because the human mind forms a stronger emotional connection to something physical. The phrase appears once, beside the QR code, the NFC tag, and the funnel they feed. His complaint is that sellers focus on the code and the tag and ignore simple design elements — heavy card stock and raised lettering that improve the tactile experience. That touch, he says, creates a more memorable experience and a deeper emotional connection, and you can take that to the bank.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that print always beats digital: the neuroscience finding is about emotional connection to something physical, not a blanket win for print.
- Read as a call for fancier inserts: the fix he names is simple — heavy stock and raised lettering — not more technology in the card.
