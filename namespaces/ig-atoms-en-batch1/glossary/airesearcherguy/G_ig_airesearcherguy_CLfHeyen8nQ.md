# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLfHeyen8nQ — Frequently Bought Together

## Term

Frequently Bought Together is the speaker's single best place to find the next product for a brand: the Amazon section under a listing that shows what customers already buy alongside it. The phrase appears once, beside Bluetooth earbuds, armband, competitors, and expand. His example is a cell phone holder armband for runners, where the section points to Bluetooth earbuds — and the competitors' listings point the same way — so the logical next step is expanding into earbuds, following what both his customers and his competitors' customers already buy.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a guess about related products: the section reports what customers already frequently buy together, and the speaker treats that as evidence, not a hunch.
- Read as a reason to copy competitors: he checks competitors to confirm the same signal, but the decision follows his own customers' buying pattern.
