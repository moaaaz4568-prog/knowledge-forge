# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CN53xv5nWc4 — reverse reciprocity

## Term

Reverse reciprocity is the speaker's name for giving first and asking second: in the Brainfluence test a small business gave its lead magnet first and then asked for the opt-in, and claimed dramatically higher opt-in rates. The phrase appears once, beside reciprocity, the lead magnet, and a friend running rebates on Amazon. His own anecdote matches the pattern: the friend gave the rebate money first, before the purchase, and grew a huge fan base of loyal customers doing about a million dollars a month in sales. He closes by saying the idea is worth at least testing, not that it is proven.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But I want to talk to you about reverse reciprocity." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CN53xv5nWc4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a bribe by another name: a lead magnet is what most businesses use as a bribe to get the opt-in, and the reversal is the order — gift first, ask second — not the gift itself.
- Read as a proven growth tactic: both the test and the anecdote are claims he repeats, and his own verdict is only that it is worth testing.
