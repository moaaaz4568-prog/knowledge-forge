# G_ig_airesearcherguy_COBaSTDnuQ3 — cognitive dissonance

## Term

Cognitive dissonance is the research the speaker uses to question the easy path to purchase: people who stay in line for hours to get the new iPhone end up making more purchases and being more loyal to the brand. The phrase appears twice, beside cognitive dissonance resolution, conversions, and the path to purchase. His reading is that once somebody invests so much energy and time, they become even more committed to the purchase they struggled for. He adds the limit himself: there is a fine line, and a customer can only be inconvenienced so much before giving up — so he proposes a way to test it.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as advice to make buying hard: the finding is about commitment after effort, and he warns that too much inconvenience makes customers give up.
- Read as proof that easy checkout hurts sales: the experts' claim about easy paths stands, and his point is only that effort can also build commitment.
