# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMSOFXInMxY — 3D printed items

## Term

3D printed items are the speaker's hunt on Amazon: looking for them, he finds cosplay stuff with no sales numbers, which he reads as no money in it, and a whole category for 3D printed jewelry with no products at all. The phrase appears three times, beside cosplay, 3D printed jewelry, 3D printed lamp, and Amazon. The opportunity he sees is in the gap: one 3D printed lamp makes almost $10,000 in sales a month and a customized one almost $87,000, so a thriving market is missing where the category is empty.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a thriving market: the speaker says the cosplay items do not look like they make money, and the opportunity is the empty jewelry category, not the crowded one.
- Read as a product pick: the lamps are his evidence of demand, not items he tells viewers to print and sell.
