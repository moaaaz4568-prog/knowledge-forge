# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CN5y48inItZ — ownership prompts

## Term

Ownership prompts are the copywriting device the speaker recommends for making a buyer picture themselves owning the product, because behavioral science shows people assign a higher value to items they own. The phrase appears once, beside the ergonomic handle, slicing vegetables, and a knife the buyer is meant to imagine holding. His illustration is a single sentence: imagine how easy it will be to slice through vegetables because the ergonomic handle perfectly fits in your hand. If the person does imagine that, he says, they are probably going to buy the knife.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a visualization trick for any product: the prompt must make the buyer picture owning this product, and a generic daydream would not do the work.
- Read as a guarantee of purchase: he says the imagining buyer is probably going to buy, and the prompt only raises the likelihood.
