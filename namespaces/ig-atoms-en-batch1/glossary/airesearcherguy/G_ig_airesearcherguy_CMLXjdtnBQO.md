# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMLXjdtnBQO — learning hack

## Term

A learning hack is the speaker's speed trick: listen to audiobooks at 1.5x speed, and watch videos the same way with the Speed Video Chrome extension. The phrase appears once, beside audiobooks, 1.5x speed, Speed Video, and Chrome extension. The claim is simple and total: learn faster by playing everything faster, and the same multiplier works for video as for audio.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a study method: the hack is playback speed alone, and the speaker gives no note-taking, retention, or comprehension step with it.
- Read as a claim that faster is always better: he says learn faster at 1.5x, but the source gives no test of how much is actually retained at that speed.
