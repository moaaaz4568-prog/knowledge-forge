# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLsZy3cH1J5 — FBA courses

## Term

FBA courses are the question of this source: are Amazon FBA courses worth it, or are they all a scam? The phrase appears once, beside scam, Brandon Young, Seller Systems, and learning curve. His answer is neither extreme: he started with a course and learned more by doing, but the course introduced him to like-minded people without whom he would not have succeeded, and while scammers exist, talented people with courses exist too — his example is Brandon Young of Seller Systems, whose course he has never taken because he is sure it cannot be bad. The bottom line is that no course is needed to succeed, but the right one can cut months off the learning curve.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So a question that a lot of people ask is, are Amazon FBA courses worth it?" — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CLsZy3cH1J5.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a verdict that all courses are scams: the speaker says plainly they are not all scams, and names a competent course-maker as proof.
- Read as a claim that a course is required: he says you do not need one to succeed, and his own learning came mostly from doing.
