# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMALvsrnqjN — wealth

## Term

Wealth is, to the speaker, not riches but peace of mind: after most of his adult working life as a waiter, living hand to mouth and paycheck to paycheck, grinding with barely any energy left, his entrepreneurial endeavors pulled him and his family out of that. The word appears once, beside paycheck to paycheck, grind, food stamps, and entrepreneurship. What wealth feels like to him is concrete: being able to get whatever you need at the grocery store, or to buy his kids clothes at Target or the mall — which is why he advocates entrepreneurship for anyone feeling stuck and drained, by monetizing ideas or skills, or by acquiring the skills.

## Live Example

> "Now we are by no means rich, but it's amazing the amount of peace of mind and lifestyle change that comes with being able to get whatever you need at the grocery store, or when your kids need clothes, being able to just go to Target or go to the mall and get them. To me, that's what wealth feels like." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CMALvsrnqjN.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim to be rich: he says by no means rich, and the wealth he describes is the lifestyle change of covering needs, not a fortune.
- Read as a promise that entrepreneurship guarantees escape: he says anybody who wants to change their situation can do so, but his own story runs through years of waiting tables and grinding first.
