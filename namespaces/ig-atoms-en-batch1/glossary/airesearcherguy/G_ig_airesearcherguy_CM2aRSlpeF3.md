# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CM2aRSlpeF3 — print-on-demand

## Term

Print-on-demand is the speaker's correction to the easy-money videos: people say start your own business, it is so easy, and he says it is not easy, showing his own failures as proof. The phrase appears twice, beside Etsy, digital prints, Canva, and motivational quotes. He sold only a couple of digital prints from Canva designs, then a couple of funny pandemic designs for print-on-demand products, and it was not profitable; he has leaned more heavily into print-on-demand since, still not profitable yet, and stays determined because he personally knows people doing really well on Etsy.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof print-on-demand never works: he says he knows people doing really well on Etsy, and his point is that his own attempts have not worked yet.
- Read as an apology for his advice: he apologizes only if he ever made it seem easy, and the video is a correction of that impression, not a retraction of the model.
