# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CPAD1FZHvOY — repetition

## Term

Repetition is the speaker's rule for making a brand liked: repeat the brand or the message often enough and people grow fond of it. The noun appears once, in the opening line that repetition breeds fondness, while the verb repeat appears three times, beside memorable, trusted, and fondness. The chain he gives is that repeating makes something more memorable, being easier to recall makes it more trusted, and being recalled with fondness is the payoff. His concrete advice is to work the brand or the message into people's ears again and again, stopping short of annoying them, so that the repeated exposure itself builds the fondness.

## Synonyms

- repeat (the verb form the speaker uses when telling sellers to repeat the brand or the message)

## Live Example

> "So without being annoying, figure out a way to repeat your brand or repeat your message as often as possible, and it will make people more fond of you." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CPAD1FZHvOY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a call to spam the audience: he adds the qualifier without being annoying, so the repetition has to stay welcome or it stops working.
- Read as a claim that repetition alone sells: the payoff he names is fondness and trust from being recalled, not a purchase on its own.
