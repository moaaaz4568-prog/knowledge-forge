# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CRsBzS9nDEa — split test

## Term

To split test is the speaker's Prosper lesson for listing images: split test all of your images, the main image and the subsequent ones, and go with what converts the highest. The phrase appears five times, beside images, imagery, and managed experience in Amazon. His method is to use managed experience in Amazon, or a software, or ask an agency to do it, so the testing is a process, not a guess. The concrete rule is the repetition itself: every image gets tested, and the winner is the one with the highest conversion.
