# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CQZhZ7PnkQ5 — Bing ads

## Term

Bing ads are the search ads the speaker says digital marketers are sleeping on: Microsoft accounts for 26% of monthly searches, against Google's 61%. The phrase appears once, beside Microsoft, search ads, and the 40 to 45 billion searches a month he attributes to Microsoft. His arithmetic is that Google's 3.5 billion searches a day is roughly 105 billion a month, about 60%, which leaves Microsoft a huge share that marketers ignore. His concrete advice is the last line itself: if you are not running Bing ads, you probably should be.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that Bing beats Google: the speaker's numbers still put Google first, and his point is the size of the ignored share, not a win for Microsoft.
- Read as a guarantee of cheap clicks: he gives search volumes only, and says nothing about what Bing ads cost or how they convert.
