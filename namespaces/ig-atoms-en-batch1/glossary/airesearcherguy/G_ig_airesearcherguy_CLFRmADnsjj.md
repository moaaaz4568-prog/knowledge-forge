# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLFRmADnsjj — chatbot

## Term

A chatbot is the simple picker at the center of this Snapchat ad: swipe up from the 50% off Day Trader shirts ad, land on a page, click, and the chatbot asks which shirt you want, then hands over a code to use on Etsy. The word appears twice, beside Snapchat, Etsy, and the code. He deliberately kept it simple after a fancier plan failed: sending people to Etsy to add to cart first broke, because you cannot link out of the bot and let them come back, so the whole flow is now pick, copy the code, and buy.

- Read as a checkout inside the chatbot: the purchase still happens on Etsy, and the bot only picks the shirt and gives the code.
