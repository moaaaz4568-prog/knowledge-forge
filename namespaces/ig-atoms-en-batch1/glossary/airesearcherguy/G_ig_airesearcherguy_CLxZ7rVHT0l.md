# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLxZ7rVHT0l — Clubhouse

## Term

Clubhouse is the speaker's most recent example of a new platform being called a distraction: business owners post that it is a waste of time, and he says that thinking is wrong-headed. The name appears four times, beside distraction, ideal customer, newspaper, and social media. His analogy is an ad in a newspaper: finding out the same ad can run in another newspaper for free is not a reason to call the second newspaper a distraction, and every new platform is a new means to get the message in front of new people — you may skip it for lack of bandwidth, but not because it is a waste of time.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a Clubhouse endorsement: he does not say everyone must join it, only that skipping it for lack of bandwidth is different from calling it a distraction.
- Read as a claim that new platforms replace old ones: his newspaper analogy treats each platform as one more means to reach people, not a replacement.
