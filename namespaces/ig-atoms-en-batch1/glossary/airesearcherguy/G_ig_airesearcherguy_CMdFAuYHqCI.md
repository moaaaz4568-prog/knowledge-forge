# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMdFAuYHqCI — starving crowd

## Term

A starving crowd is the last restaurateur's answer to the question of what guarantees success, and the speaker calls it a powerful answer because it is the ingredient that ensures success for any industry, no matter what is being sold. The phrase appears twice, beside Ray Kroc, a million dollars, high-tech cooking equipment, and the best ingredients. His point is that demand comes before everything else: a million dollars, the best equipment, and the best-tasting food all lose to a crowd that is already hungry. The test he gives is simple — if you have a starving crowd and you can feed them, you will succeed.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that any hungry audience guarantees success: the speaker's test is feeding the crowd, and a crowd you cannot feed is not the answer he describes.
- Read as praise for Ray Kroc's business: the speaker is unsure the story is even true, and the crowd is the point, not the man who said it.
