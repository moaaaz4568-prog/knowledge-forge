# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CSz8ze_H5eM — preferential treatment

## Term

Preferential treatment is what the legislation would end, in the speaker's reading: Amazon showing itself preferential treatment on its own marketplace. The phrase appears once, beside break up Amazon, seller data, and superior brands, in a source about the government's attempt to impose legislation on Amazon. The legislation's core, as he puts it, is that Amazon cannot own the marketplace and compete on the marketplace at the same time, and it would also stop them skirting their own terms of service and using seller data to develop their own brands. His own position is uncertain: he doubts it would hurt sellers as much as Amazon's email claims, and he does not trust the government either, so he ends by asking what the reader thinks.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So this would put an end to them showing themselves preferential treatment, allowing them to skirt their own terms of service, or using seller data to then go and develop their own superior brands." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CSz8ze_H5eM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as the speaker endorsing the breakup: he says the government wants to break up Amazon, but he explicitly does not know if it would be good for sellers, and asks the audience instead.
- Read as a claim that Amazon's email is lying: he says he is not sure it would be as damaging as Amazon wants sellers to believe, which is doubt about the email's framing, not a verdict that it is false.
