# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CRE-rtonxpy — neuromarketing hack

## Term

A neuromarketing hack is the speaker's upside-and-downside offer: give potential customers both, and they become more likely to buy. The phrase appears once, beside upside, which appears three times, downside, which appears four times, and trust. His first function is trust: a realistic downside makes the prospect trust the seller more, and also downplays the impact of the downside itself. His second is optimism: research shows humans are usually pretty optimistic, so given good news and bad news they focus primarily on the good news, which is why the beneficial upside should come after the downside.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "If you give potential customers an upside and a downside, you'll increase the likelihood that they'll buy." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CRE-rtonxpy.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that any downside will do: he specifies a realistic downside, and the trust comes from the honesty of it, not from inventing a flaw.
- Read as a trick that hides the downside: the downside is stated openly first, and the effect depends on the buyer seeing it, not on burying it.
