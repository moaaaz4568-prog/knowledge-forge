# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CTFW0nKnBT3 — anthropomorphized

## Term

Anthropomorphized is the word the speaker uses for what his daughter did to her mother's hair clips: she had given them feelings, and was devastated because she killed one. The word appears once, beside hair clips, which appear twice, grieving, and the drain the clip fell down. The story is that the clip dropped down the exposed drain during a shower, and the girl was not afraid of trouble or upset about losing her mother's thing, but grieving the death of the clip itself. Her grief lasted the better part of 20 minutes, full on, for a hair clip she had made into a person.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "No, she was playing with the hair clips and had anthropomorphized them and was currently devastated because she killed her." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CTFW0nKnBT3.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a story about a lost hair clip: the loss is not the point, and the girl's upset is grief for a thing she had made alive, not fear of punishment.
- Read as a claim that children cannot tell real from pretend: the speaker's point is the depth of her imagination, which he says he had not appreciated until that night, not a judgment on her.
