# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CL7k3hvnu8t — product listing page

## Term

A product listing page is, in this source, the first place the speaker looks when a product is not selling: its copy may not be compelling buyers, even though the product itself is probably fine. The phrase appears once, beside traffic, product images, and optimization problems. His argument is diagnostic rather than blaming the product: wrong copy, wrong traffic, or images that do not fully convey the product are all fixable optimization problems and much more likely than the product simply being bad.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "There's so many more likely scenarios, like your product listing page just doesn't have the right copy." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CL7k3hvnu8t.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as proof the product is bad: the speaker's whole point is that a weak listing page is the more likely cause, and he lists it as a fixable problem.
- Read as a call to rewrite the product itself: his fixes are copy, traffic, and images — the page around the product, not a new product.
