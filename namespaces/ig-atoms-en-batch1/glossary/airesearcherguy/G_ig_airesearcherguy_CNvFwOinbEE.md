# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CNvFwOinbEE — bad profits

## Term

Bad profits are earned when a company takes advantage of customers or just does not do the right thing, usually a larger company with a monopoly and few options for its customers. The phrase appears three times, beside cell phone contract cancellation fees, a Google Pixel 4a, and a cracked screen protector. His example is his own repair: the power button stopped working under warranty, Google found a crack in the screen and refused the repair, charging ninety-eight dollars because the warranty does not cover physical damage. His charge is that Google trains employees to look for any minor physical damage, so anything of the sort voids the coverage — and that is the prime example of bad profits.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that all profit is bad: the bad kind is specifically profit from taking advantage of customers, and the example is a warranty refusal, not profit itself.
- Read as a complaint about the ninety-eight dollars: the money is the illustration, and the point is the trained search for damage that voids coverage.
