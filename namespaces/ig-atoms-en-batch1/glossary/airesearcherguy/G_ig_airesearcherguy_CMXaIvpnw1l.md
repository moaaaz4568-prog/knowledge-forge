# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMXaIvpnw1l — due diligence

## Term

Due diligence is one half of the consistent advice the speaker heard from seven and eight figure Amazon sellers and PPC agency owners in a Clubhouse room: do your due diligence and know math. The phrase appears twice, beside know math, seven and eight figure, newbies, and interview people. It sounds too simple, he admits, but every successful business decision reduces to those principles: know your numbers, know how much you stand to lose, ask questions, and interview people before deciding.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a checklist: the speaker pairs it with knowing math — the numbers and the loss — and the due diligence is the asking and interviewing, not a form to fill.
- Read as advice for big sellers only: the question came from newbies, and the room's answer was aimed at their mistakes, not at agency owners.
