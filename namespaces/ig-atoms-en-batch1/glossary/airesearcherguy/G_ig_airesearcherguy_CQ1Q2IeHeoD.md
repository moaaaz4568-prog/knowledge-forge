# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CQ1Q2IeHeoD — self-serve ad platform

## Term

A self-serve ad platform is what Hulu is launching: a way to go into Hulu and run ads the way a seller goes into Facebook and runs ads. The phrase appears once, beside Hulu, Prime TV, and Twitch through Amazon DSP. Before this, video ads while people watched TV were only a possibility for huge brands with huge budgets, and now sellers of all sizes can advertise on Prime TV, Twitch, and Hulu. His concrete point is the opening itself: the paid advertising space just got a lot more interesting because the TV screen is no longer reserved for the biggest budgets.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Hulu is launching their own self-serve ad platform." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CQ1Q2IeHeoD.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that Hulu ads are already live and proven: the speaker says Hulu is launching the platform, and he only asks sellers whether it sounds like something they will test.
- Read as a Facebook replacement: he presents Hulu as working just like Facebook ads, an additional place to run them, not a reason to leave Facebook.
