# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CN5TFpYHxO4 — contract manufacturer

## Term

A contract manufacturer is the company that actually makes a brand's supplements, and the speaker's point is that this information is private — you cannot find it. The phrase appears once, beside Alpha Brain, Onnit, and a testing lab. He is skeptical that Onnit imports its supplements, and his check of Import Yeti shows they import fitness equipment only, which confirms for him that the supplements are made in the US. His workaround is to send samples to a testing lab, have the ingredients analyzed, and then contract a supplement company to make something similar.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

> "So how do you find their contract manufacturer?" — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CN5TFpYHxO4.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a how-to for finding Onnit's manufacturer: his answer is that you cannot — the information is private — and the lab route makes a similar product, not the same one.
- Read as proof Onnit makes its supplements in the US: the import records show equipment only, which confirms his suspicion but is not a document from Onnit itself.
