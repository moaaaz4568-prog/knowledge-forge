# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLpuJmunY_m — personal brand

## Term

A personal brand is what the speaker has been building since January 2020, and his account of it is deliberately modest: a thousand followers on Instagram, a little over 11,000 on Newsclapper, 180 YouTube subscribers, 27 on Twitter, over 500 LinkedIn connections, and over 550 Facebook page likes, with TikTok his biggest following. The phrase appears three times, beside TikTok, consistent, content, and followers. His method is making videos about things he knows well and things he is just learning and passionate about — and his advice to anyone struggling is the same: be consistent and keep putting content out there.
