# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CQrs0g6H4pS — purchase frequency report

## Term

A purchase frequency report is the research the speaker ran for his keywords while negotiating with an Alibaba supplier: it tells him how often the keywords lead to purchases. The phrase appears once, beside Cerebro from Helium 10, search volume, and the sample units he got from the supplier. His update is a recap of a sourcing process: sample units in hand, images collected, the report run, and the listing still to be created on Amazon and Walmart. The concrete next step is the listing itself, and the report is what tells him which keywords the listing should be built around.

## When the Meaning Breaks
