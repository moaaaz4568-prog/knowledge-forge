# G_ig_airesearcherguy_COgC8Z_Hwni — self-perception theory

## Term

Self-perception theory is the psychological theory the speaker calls probably the most important for marketers: actions are not informed by attitudes, but the other way around — our attitudes are shaped by our actions. The phrase appears once, beside mirror neurons, changing the action, and the buy button. His point is that humans do not know themselves, so we look at our actions to figure out what kind of person we are. Marketing should therefore aim at changing the action, not the attitude: show the action yourself so people mimic it, or write copy aimed at clicking the buy button rather than at caring about the environment.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that attitudes do not matter: the theory says attitudes follow actions, and the marketer's job is to start with the action that shapes them.
- Read as a trick to make people buy: the examples are mimicry and copy aimed at the action, and the purchase is the action, not a deception.
