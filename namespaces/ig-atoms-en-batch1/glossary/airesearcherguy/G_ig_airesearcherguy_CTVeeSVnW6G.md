# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CTVeeSVnW6G — inventory forecasting algorithm

## Term

An inventory forecasting algorithm is what most of Amazon's success rests on, in the speaker's account: it tells Amazon where to send inventory so it can reach Prime users in two days. The phrase appears once, and the shorter form forecasting algorithm appears again for the history of the algorithm, beside FBA distribution and deep reinforcement learning models. The team's new direction is to make accuracy improvements translate directly into cost savings and lower costs for customers, designing a system that optimizes directly for savings as opposed to inventory levels. His concrete worry is the consequence: FBA distribution will be predicated on price instead of inventory opportunity, and he is curious how that will impact sellers.

## Synonyms

- forecasting algorithm (the shorter form he uses for the history of the algorithm)

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a leak of Amazon's plans: the speaker says the research team gave a glimpse into the future and he does not even think they meant to, but his source is a published article three weeks old, not a leak.
- Read as a promise of lower costs for sellers: the savings are for customers, and the speaker's own reaction is curiosity about how the price-based distribution will impact sellers, not relief.
