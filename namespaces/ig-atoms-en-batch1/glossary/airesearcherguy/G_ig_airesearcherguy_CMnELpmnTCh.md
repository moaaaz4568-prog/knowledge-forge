# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMnELpmnTCh — add to cart

## Term

Add to cart is the Amazon signal the speaker says holds so much weight in the ranking algorithm, because it follows the customer journey from the search query all the way to the purchase and signals the most buying intent. The phrase appears four times, beside ranking, scoring mechanisms, badges, and the keyword search. A separate algorithm tracks expected add to cart conversions, so if a product falls short of that expectation, any benefit from add to carts is nullified. His point is that the signal matters only when the product converts at the expected rate, not as a raw count of carts.

## Synonyms

- add to carts (the plural form he uses when the benefit from carts is nullified)

## Live Example

> "This is the reason why add to cart holds so much weight in the ranking algorithm." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CMnELpmnTCh.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a trick to inflate carts: the ranking benefit depends on expected conversions, and carts below expectation nullify it rather than help.
- Read as the only ranking signal: he describes scoring mechanisms and badges connected to the whole keyword journey, and add to cart is one signal inside it.
