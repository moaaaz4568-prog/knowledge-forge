# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CM43tQcnglg — lead times

## Term

Lead times are one of the things the speaker says matter more than price when talking to an Alibaba supplier: suppliers get emails all day asking for the best price, and most of those inquiries are not serious. The phrase appears once, beside quality, brand, BS internet inquiry pricing, and serious discussion. His order of importance is quality first — a cheap product that is a piece of crap is worthless — then lead times and whether the product can be branded, and showing you care about those is what gets a serious discussion and real pricing instead of the internet inquiry price.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Quality is way more important. It doesn't matter how cheap you can get the product if it's a piece of crap. Lead times are important." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CM43tQcnglg.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a ban on discussing price: he says price is not the most important thing to talk about first, not that it never matters.
- Read as a claim that cheap is always bad: his point is that quality, lead times, and branding decide whether the product is worth anything, and price only matters after those.
