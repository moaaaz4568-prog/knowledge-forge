# G_ig_airesearcherguy_COdbhginnCr — subject matter expert

## Term

A subject matter expert is the role the speaker announces he has taken: the Amazon subject matter expert for Canopy Management, after a friend made him an amazing offer when he left his previous company. The phrase appears once, beside the entrepreneur thing, Canopy Management, and cheers. He says he was completely prepared to go full time on his own, but the offer changed the plan. His pitch is that Canopy is doing great things for Amazon sellers and will do even more with his help — an update on him, not a lesson.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a job ad for Canopy: the announcement is about his own move, and no role, requirement, or opening is described.
- Read as a claim that he abandoned entrepreneurship: he says he was prepared to do it full time, and the offer is the reason the plan changed.
