# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CMhvwLHnlil — inspection service

## Term

An inspection service is what the speaker tells viewers to search for when their products are being manufactured overseas: he suggests Googling Chinese supplier inspection service or the place of manufacture, and says plenty of options pop up. The phrase appears once, beside Mavly, trust, and the quality the service offers. He recommends Mavly personally because he knows the owner and how he hires and trains his people, even though it costs a little more than the cheaper options. His argument is that inspections are all about trust, so paying more is worth it for the quality.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that any inspection service will do: he recommends one service by name because he trusts how its people are hired and trained, not because every option is equal.
- Read as a price comparison: the cheaper options exist, but his point is that trust outweighs saving money on the inspection.
