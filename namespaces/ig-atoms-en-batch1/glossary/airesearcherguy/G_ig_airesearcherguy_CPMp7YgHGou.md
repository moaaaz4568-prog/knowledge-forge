# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CPMp7YgHGou — availability heuristic

## Term

The availability heuristic is the reason the speaker gives for a logo mattering so much: it works through association, tying a brand to memories and emotions. The phrase appears once, beside association, symbolism, memorable, and positive emotions. A logo, in his account, acts as the symbolism that links the brand to what people already remember and feel. His concrete test is the feeling itself: a brand people can recall with positive emotions is a brand they remember, and the logo is the tie that does it.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And this is because of the availability heuristic and association." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CPMp7YgHGou.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that a logo alone builds a brand: the speaker's point is the association the logo carries, and without the memories and emotions behind it the symbol has nothing to tie to.
- Read as a design tip about making logos prettier: he never talks about how the logo looks, only about what it links the brand to.
