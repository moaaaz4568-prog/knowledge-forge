# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CPwVgEnHlRO — exclusive

## Term

Exclusive is the quality Frederick the Great manufactured around the potato: a royal garden nobody could enter, and guards told not to stop people sneaking in. The word appears once, beside royal garden, curiosity, and jealousy, for the exclusive new vegetable people stole and grew themselves. The mechanism is the ban itself: forbidding the vegetable made people curious and jealous enough to take it, which spread the crop the farmers had refused to grow. The speaker's coda is that Ralph Lauren is long speculated to have done the same thing to make his brand one of the most sought after in the 90s.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And sure enough, out of curiosity or jealousy of this exclusive new vegetable, people snuck in, stole potatoes, and started growing them themselves." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CPwVgEnHlRO.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that Frederick invented the potato: he only introduced it to Prussia, and the story is about how he got farmers to grow a crop they had rejected.
- Read as proof that Ralph Lauren copied the trick: the speaker says it is long been speculated, not established, and the Lauren line is a speculation, not the story's evidence.
