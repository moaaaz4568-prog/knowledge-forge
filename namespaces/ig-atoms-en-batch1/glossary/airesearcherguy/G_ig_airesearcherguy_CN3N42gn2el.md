# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CN3N42gn2el — want self

## Term

The want self is one of the two selves neuroscience has uncovered in everybody, the one seeking immediate gratification, set against the should self that looks for long-term benefit. The phrase appears twice, beside the should self, immediate gratification, and long-term thinking. His marketing rule follows the split: for a product offering immediate gratification, stress the immediacy — sign up now and get approved within seconds. For a long-term product like a supplement, focus the language on long-term thinking, perhaps with a twenty percent discount for a six-month supply.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a claim that people are only impulsive: the should self is the other half of the model, and long-term products need language aimed at it.
- Read as a personality test: the two selves describe how everyone decides, and the point is matching the message to the gratification, not sorting buyers.
