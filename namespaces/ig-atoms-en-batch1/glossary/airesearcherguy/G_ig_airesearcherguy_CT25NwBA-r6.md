# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CT25NwBA-r6 — legitimate hustles

## Term

Legitimate hustles are the speaker's five ways to make money without spending any, written for a friend who fell on hard times and asked for advice. The phrase appears once, and the short form hustle introduces each of the five items, beside Fiverr, affiliate marketing, and Craigslist. The five are offering services on Fiverr, affiliate marketing through ClickBank with free tools like GrooveFunnels, writing and self-publishing a book, becoming a content creator on TikTok, and picking up free stuff on Craigslist to resell on Facebook Marketplace. His concrete constraint runs through all five: none of them should cost money of your own, with free plans and free listings doing the work instead.

## Synonyms

- hustle (the short form he uses to introduce each of the five items)

## Live Example

> "So here is my list of five legitimate hustles that you can do to make money without having to spend any of your own." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CT25NwBA-r6.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a promise that any of the five pays fast: he presents them as ways he knows, and the book one he admits is a lot easier said than done.
- Read as a claim that affiliate marketing is free by nature: he notes a lot of affiliate marketers do spend money on funnel builders and email services, and his version only stays free by using the free plans.
