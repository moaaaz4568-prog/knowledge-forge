# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CQW2KkxHSMe — subconscious cues

## Term

Subconscious cues are the signals the speaker says give an inexperienced seller away: facial expressions, tones of voice, and body language, read instantaneously without consciously knowing it. The phrase appears three times, beside the subconscious system, passion, and impurities, in a source that opens by asking whether follow your passion is bad business advice. His concrete case is a seller promoting a product they do not fully believe in: their cues tip people off, while a seller with passion for the product sends enthusiastic cues that land positively. The water analogy is his setup: the brain evolved to not taste water so it could taste impurities, and the same hidden detection, he argues, reads the seller.

- Read as a claim that buyers consciously analyze sellers: the whole point is that the reading happens instantaneously and without consciously knowing it, so no buyer is doing it on purpose.
