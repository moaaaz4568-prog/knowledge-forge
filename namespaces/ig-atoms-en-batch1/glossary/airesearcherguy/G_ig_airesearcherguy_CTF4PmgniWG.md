# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CTF4PmgniWG — One Chip Challenge

## Term

The One Chip Challenge is Packy's product and campaign at once: the world's spiciest chip, wrapped up singly, put in a clever box, sold for $7 a chip. The phrase appears twice, beside Packy, the spiciest chip, and the thousands of hilarious videos buyers film of themselves eating it and sharing online. The sales the speaker quotes are about $200,000 a month, with $70,000 of that from the staple One Chip Challenge product, which means 10,000 people every month spend $7 buying a single tortilla chip. His own ending is the challenge itself: he takes it on camera, and the heat, he reports, hurts all the way up into his ear.
