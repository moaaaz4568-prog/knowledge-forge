# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CN5l52PH4cz — standard operating procedure

## Term

A standard operating procedure is the digital product the speaker sells: a procedure that walks people through setting up their own rebate campaigns so they can rank their products on Amazon. The phrase appears once, beside done for you services, expensive, and the link in the bio. He sells it because the done for you services are expensive and many people, like him, just want to figure it out on their own. His pitch is the contrast itself — show off what you sell, then tell everyone why you pick it.

## Live Example

> "I sell a digital product, a standard operating procedure that walks people through the process of setting up their own rebate campaigns so they can rank their products on Amazon." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CN5l52PH4cz.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a course about rebates in general: the product is a procedure for setting up your own campaigns, not a service that runs them for you.
- Read as a claim that done for you services are bad: he calls them expensive, and his reason for selling the procedure is that some people prefer doing it themselves.
