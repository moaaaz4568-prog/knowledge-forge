# G_ig_airesearcherguy_CLnCklfnzGx — super URL

## Term

A super URL is the speaker's trick URL for an Amazon listing: take the static listing URL and inject a fake QID, the Unix timestamp Amazon puts in the URL when you click from a search page, so Amazon supposedly thinks the visit came from a fresh search. The singular appears twice and the plural once, beside QID, two-step URL, and share URLs. Every click generates a new QID even though no search occurred — but he doubts the trick works now, saying Amazon is fully aware of all the URL tactics and is not fooled, which is why his personal favorite is the share URL.

## Synonyms

- super URLs (the plural form the speaker uses when introducing the topic)

> "First, let's go over what a super URL is." — sources/S_ig_airesearcherguy_CLnCklfnzGx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a working ranking hack: the speaker's own opinion is that Amazon is not fooled, and he prefers share URLs for exactly that reason.
- Read as an organic visit: the fake QID only mimics a fresh search, and the speaker contrasts it with the two-step URL, whose referring URL genuinely is Amazon.
