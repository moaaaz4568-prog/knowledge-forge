# G_ig_10feetstudios_C7M_KVCtp91 — self-made

## Term

Self-made is the badge the speaker says some people chase by insisting on making every mistake themselves — and the reason they stall. The word appears once, in "They want to make all the mistakes to be self-made", beside "mistakes" and "themselves". His counter is a quote he shares — learn from others' mistakes because you can't live long enough to make them all yourself — and his test is time: when he speaks to these people months later, they have made no real progress. The deeper claim is about time as a finite resource: you spend it whether you want to or not, so he would rather spend money to buy knowledge and skills than spend time acquiring them.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "never try things yourself": he objects to making every mistake firsthand, not to doing the work — his own fix is implementing a mentor's steps fast.
- Read as "money buys skill": the trade he describes is money for time — paying to skip the mistake-making, not to skip learning.
