# G_ig_10feetstudios_C6Pa6teNRom — tangible results

## Term

Tangible results is the third of three guesses the speaker makes about a content agency stuck at 10k a month, and it names the failure he treats as decisive: the agency cannot show clients real outcomes. The phrase appears once, in the sentence about not really being able to get tangible results for clients, next to "clients" and "honest". His proof for the diagnosis is personal — he says he knows because he has been through all of this himself — and the fix he points to is his own breakdown of scaling a content agency to 20k a month in profit.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as more views or likes: tangible here means client business outcomes, not engagement numbers.
- Read as a complaint about clients: the sentence blames the agency's delivery, not the clients' expectations.
