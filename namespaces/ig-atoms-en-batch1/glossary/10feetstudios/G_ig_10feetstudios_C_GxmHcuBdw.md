# G_ig_10feetstudios_C_GxmHcuBdw — client churn

## Term

Client churn is the long-term cost the speaker warns about when clients are not satisfied: losing more clients than you sign up is worse than not being able to sign clients at all. The phrase appears once, beside "long term" and "losing more clients", and it closes his list of three scaling blockers — booking fewer than 15 sales calls a month, being buried in service delivery with no team, and clients who are not satisfied because the agency cannot get them amazing results. The remedy he points to is an offer that not only gets the client's attention but converts that attention into more revenue for their business.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Because that will lead to client churn over the long term, and the only thing that is worse than not being able to sign up clients is losing more clients than you're signing up." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C_GxmHcuBdw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "churn means the agency is dead": he treats churn as a signal to fix the offer and results, not as a verdict.
- Read as a sales problem: churn here comes from delivery — unsatisfied clients — not from failing to sign new ones.
