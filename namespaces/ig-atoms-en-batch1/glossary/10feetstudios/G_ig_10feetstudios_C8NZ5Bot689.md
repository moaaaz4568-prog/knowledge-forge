# G_ig_10feetstudios_C8NZ5Bot689 — high ticket retainers

## Term

High ticket retainers are the pricing outcome of the speaker's first step for scaling past 20k a month: restructure the offer so the agency can charge above 2.5k a month per client while getting clients amazing results at the same time. The phrase appears once, beside "restructure your offer" and "amazing results". It sits in a three-step plan — the offer first, then the Loom outreach system to book 7 to 14 calls a week, then service delivery systems and a team — and his proof case is a client who closed a 3k-a-month agency he hated, then booked 21 calls in a week and added 7k in retainers.

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "charge more first": the retainer follows the offer restructure; the price is justified by results delivered at the same time.
- Read as a standalone price point: 2.5k is the floor he names for this step, tied to the offer that earns it.
