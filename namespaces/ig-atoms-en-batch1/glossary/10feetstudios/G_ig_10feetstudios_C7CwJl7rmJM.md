# G_ig_10feetstudios_C7CwJl7rmJM — scale fast

## Term

Scale fast is what the speaker says separates people who reach 20k a month in a few months from people who take years: knowledge and speed. The phrase appears once, in "all you need to scale fast", beside "knowledge" and "speed", and his own case is hiring mentors — someone who has done it before and is far more successful — then implementing what they say quickly. The two failure modes he names are mirror images: people who take long either lack knowledge and fear investing in themselves, or they have a mentor and still do not implement fast enough.

## Synonyms

- scale as fast (the form used in "what I did to scale as fast")

## Live Example

> "And that is really all you need to scale fast." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C7CwJl7rmJM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as rushing without knowledge: speed here is implementation speed after hiring someone who already knows the path, not skipping learning.
- Read as a promise of months for everyone: his timeline is his own case; the claim is about what the two ingredients do, not a guarantee.
