# G_ig_10feetstudios_DE0AsvQNFZx — true freedom

## Term

True freedom is what the speaker says he only understood after dropping out of university at 20 and making hundreds of thousands of dollars while traveling to eight countries: it is not more money, because more money means more to lose, more responsibility, and more stress. The phrase appears twice, beside "hundreds of thousands" and "eight countries", and his definition is waking up able to do whatever you want, yet still choosing to work because you love it — needing the challenge, the pressure, and the feeling of pushing yourself. The best part, he says, is doing that every day without worrying about health or next month's rent.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But after making hundreds of thousands of dollars and traveling to eight countries just in the last couple of months, I only now realized what true freedom really means." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DE0AsvQNFZx.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "money buys freedom": he argues the opposite — more money added stress, and freedom came from loving the work itself.
- Read as "do nothing all day": his freedom includes choosing to work daily; the point is the choice, not idleness.
