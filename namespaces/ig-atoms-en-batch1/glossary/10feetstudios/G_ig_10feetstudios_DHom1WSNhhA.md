# G_ig_10feetstudios_DHom1WSNhhA — close rate

## Term

Close rate is the result the speaker reports from 591 sales calls in the last year: a seventy seven percent close rate, which he credits to three lessons. The phrase appears once, beside "591 sales calls" and "seventy seven percent", and the lessons are to treat sales as helping people make the right decisions rather than pushing them to buy, to believe in what you are selling, and to learn objection handling instead of scheduling follow-ups. His test on a call is to listen to the prospect's problems and help them see why the offer solves them — and if it cannot help them, not to sell at all.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I took five hundred and ninety one sales calls in the last year, and here are three things I learned that helped me achieve a seventy seven percent close rate." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DHom1WSNhhA.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a technique for pushing harder: the first lesson is the opposite — stop thinking of sales as a way to just make more money.
- Read as "belief replaces the offer": he says if the offer isn't great, go and fix it — conviction without a good offer is not the lesson.
