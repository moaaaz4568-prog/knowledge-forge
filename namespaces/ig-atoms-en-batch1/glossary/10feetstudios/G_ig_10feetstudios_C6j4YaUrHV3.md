# G_ig_10feetstudios_C6j4YaUrHV3 — give before we ask

## Term

Give before we ask is the principle the speaker says makes his Loom strategy work: the first email already hands the prospect a personalized video with a strategy they can use without his help, and asks for nothing in return. The phrase appears once, next to "loom strategy" and "first email", and the mechanism is surprise — he sends the Loom without asking permission, which wows prospects enough to reply. From the reply he handles objections, books a call, and closes there, reporting over a five percent conversion rate on completely cold prospects selling high ticket.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "But the whole reason why my loom strategy works so well is because we give before we ask for anything in return." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C6j4YaUrHV3.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as giving away free work forever: the gift is one usable strategy in the first email; the close still happens on a call.
- Read as politeness: it is a conversion device — the wow is what produces replies, not courtesy.
