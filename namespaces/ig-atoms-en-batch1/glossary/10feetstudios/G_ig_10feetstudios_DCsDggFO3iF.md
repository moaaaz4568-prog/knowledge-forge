# G_ig_10feetstudios_DCsDggFO3iF — hybrid offer

## Term

A hybrid offer is the speaker's fix for an offer that produces no tangible results: keep producing content as usual, but help clients turn their attention into revenue with ads and appointment setters that fill their calendars. The phrase appears once, in "Create a hybrid offer", beside "tangible results" and "retainers". The economics he claims are a retainer above three thousand dollars a month, and the acquisition side is three-to-four-minute personalized videos embedded as GIFs, sharing a revenue strategy without a direct offer, so prospects try it, realize they cannot do it alone, and come back asking how the agency can help.

> "Here's how to fix it. Create a hybrid offer." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DCsDggFO3iF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "content plus ads" as a bundle: the hybrid part is the combination of content with revenue conversion — ads and setters that turn attention into cash.
- Read as a price increase alone: the higher retainer is justified by the client making more money, not by adding services to the invoice.
