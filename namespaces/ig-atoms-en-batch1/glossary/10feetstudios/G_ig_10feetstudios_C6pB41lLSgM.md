# G_ig_10feetstudios_C6pB41lLSgM — monk mode

## Term

Monk mode is the total-cutoff lifestyle the speaker quotes other people preaching — dial in, cut everything off — and then argues against: making ten, twenty, or thirty thousand a month means nothing if you have nobody to spend it with. The phrase appears once, beside "dial in" and "cut everything off", and his counter is relationships: maintain them with family and friends no matter what you are building. He says spending money on people he loves is far more fulfilling than spending it on himself, and losing those people while building makes the end result not worth it.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Well, what I am saying is you should still have a fucking life. Like I know that people are talking about monk mode and this and that, and yeah, you need to dial in and yeah, you need to cut everything off." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C6pB41lLSgM.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as an attack on focus: he concedes there are times to dial in and cut things off; the objection is to making cutoff the whole life.
- Read as money not mattering: he never says income is irrelevant — he says income without people to share it with is empty.
