# G_ig_10feetstudios_C9Im9JTNXAF — commoditized

## Term

Commoditized is the speaker's verdict on editing as a service: because editing is so easy to do, it is very hard to sell, and you cannot charge high prices for it. The word appears once, in "because it's very commoditized", beside "editing" and "hard to sell". His rule is that the harder a product or service is to build, the easier it is to sell — so agencies limit their offer to what they can already do (editing videos) instead of learning what the market needs, like paid ads or funnels, and then watch clients leave for someone cheaper after a couple of months.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And even when you do sell it, you can't really charge high prices because it's very commoditized." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C9Im9JTNXAF.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "editing has no value": he sells editing as one part of a larger system; the claim is that editing alone cannot carry a high price.
- Read as "learn ads and you are done": learning new skills is the means; the end is offering what the market needs and wants.
