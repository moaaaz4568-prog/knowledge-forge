# G_ig_10feetstudios_C9sZ-YjNmDS — under-promise and over-deliver

## Term

Under-promise and over-deliver is the speaker's first of three tips for keeping agency clients forever: set expectations with the client very clear at the start, and never over-promise just to be able to sell. The phrase appears once, beside "expectations" and "over-promise", and it sits between constant communication — daily reports, weekly check-in calls, Slack — and actually getting results, which he calls the thing clients stay for. He adds a sober note: some churn is normal, but if it runs above 10–20%, something is clearly wrong and scaling will be very hard.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "I'd rather under-promise and over-deliver than over-promise and under-deliver." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C9sZ-YjNmDS.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "promise little so anything impresses": the promise must still be clear and realistic — the point is honest expectations, not sandbagging.
- Read as a retention trick: it is listed first of three, and without real results the other two tips do not hold clients either.
