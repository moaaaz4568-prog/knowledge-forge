# G_ig_10feetstudios_C8psWQqNzpH — 10x my price

## Term

10x my price is the speaker's test for pricing: ask how you would have to change your service to justify charging ten times more, and do that instead of copying competitors and offering more for less. The phrase appears once, in "If I would 10x my price today how would I have to change my service to justify price?", beside "justify price" and "change my service". His premise is that decreasing price does not increase value — people pay a lot for something very valuable, which is why luxury brands exist, and will not pay even 50 bucks for something they think is useless. The question forces the agency to raise value rather than race to the bottom.

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So ask yourself this question. If I would 10x my price today how would I have to change my service to justify price?" — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C8psWQqNzpH.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "actually charge 10x tomorrow": the 10x is a thought experiment that reveals what the service lacks, not a pricing instruction.
- Read as "expensive means valuable": his point is that value must justify the price; a high price on bullshit still does not sell.
