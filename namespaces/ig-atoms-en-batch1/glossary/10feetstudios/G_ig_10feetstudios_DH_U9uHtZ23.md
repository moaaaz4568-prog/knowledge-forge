# G_ig_10feetstudios_DH_U9uHtZ23 — SOPs

## Term

SOPs are the documented systems the speaker created when he stopped selling his time: after hiring editors, assistants, and copywriters, he wrote standard operating procedures and stepped out of delivery. The abbreviation appears once, beside "hired help" and "stepped out of delivery", and the trigger was anxiety — signing a new client stressed him because it meant more work and more delivery, which told him he was not running an agency at all. The payoff he claims is an agency that feels like a business: happier clients, focus on growth, and space to think again.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "So I did the uncomfortable thing. I hired help, editors, assistants, copywriters, and I created SOPs and I ultimately stepped out of delivery." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DH_U9uHtZ23.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "SOPs alone scale you": the procedures came with hires and with stepping out of delivery — paperwork without the team changes nothing.
- Read as "systems replace the owner entirely": he stepped out of delivery, not out of the business — growth and thinking are still his job.
