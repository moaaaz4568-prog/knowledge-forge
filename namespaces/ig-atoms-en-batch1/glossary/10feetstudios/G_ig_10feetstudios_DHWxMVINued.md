# G_ig_10feetstudios_DHWxMVINued — being productive

## Term

Being productive is the speaker's opposite of being busy: it means determining the actual bottlenecks in the business and solving them, instead of filling the day with client work. The phrase appears twice, beside "busy" and "bottlenecks", and the examples he gives are learning to book more calls, optimizing the offer for better client results, and building workflow systems and hiring the right team. His image of the trap is working 10 hours a day — editing, scripting, servicing clients — without ever attempting to solve those problems, which leaves a ceiling the agency cannot go beyond.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Being productive, on the other hand, is determining the actual bottlenecks within your business and solving them." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DHWxMVINued.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "client work is wasted time": servicing clients is the busy work he names, but the point is balance — delivery without ever solving bottlenecks is what caps growth.
- Read as "work fewer hours": the distinction is what the hours go to, not how many — productive work here is bottleneck-solving, not rest.
