# G_ig_10feetstudios_DDAUM2LNVqD — use data, not emotions

## Term

Use data, not emotions is the speaker's rule for fixing outreach: when a method seems to fail, look at the numbers and see exactly what is going wrong instead of deciding emotionally that the method or the niche is bad. The phrase appears once, as the closing line, beside "fix your loom" and "call book rate". His diagnostic ladder is specific — few Loom views means change the email, views without replies means fix the Loom and give a reason to respond, replies without booked calls means fix appointment setting and its script — and the opening insult is deliberate: business and emotions don't mix, and he says he has seen people fail for this single reason.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Maybe there are a lot of people responding, but your call book rate is low. Well, then fix your appointment setting and see how you can change your script and that. In short, use data, not emotions." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DDAUM2LNVqD.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "feelings have no place in business": he admits everyone has emotions; the rule is about not making important decisions from them.
- Read as "data alone decides": the data shows where the break is; choosing the fix still requires judgment about the email, the Loom, or the script.
