# G_ig_10feetstudios_C6uOw8Cr2QK — follow every single thing

## Term

Follow every single thing is the speaker's rule for paid mentoring, drawn from spending over thirty thousand dollars on coaching: implement everything the mentor tells you to do, not just the parts you think will work. The phrase appears once, in the sentence about following every single thing that these people tell you to do, beside "mentoring and coaching" and "implement". His logic is blunt — cherry-picking the advice means you should not have hired the mentor at all — and the deeper point is that knowing the steps is not the blocker: he says people who know exactly what to do still fail to execute, which usually means something in their personal life needs fixing first.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Well, regardless, the biggest lesson that I learned from spending a shit ton of money on mentoring and coaching is that you need to follow every single thing that these people tell you to do." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C6uOw8Cr2QK.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "mentors solve personal problems": he explicitly says he would not expect a mentor to help with personal issues.
- Read as "more information fixes execution": his lesson is the opposite — information isn't everything, and execution fails for personal reasons, not knowledge gaps.
