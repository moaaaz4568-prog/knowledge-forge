# G_ig_10feetstudios_C6zYJH4r42A — client success manager

## Term

A client success manager is the fourth and final delegation level in the speaker's agency ladder: first you do everything yourself, then an editor, then a VA, then a client success manager. The phrase appears twice, both times as the last hire in that sequence, beside "editor" and "VA". The payoff he claims is concrete: with his service delivery mapped on a flowchart and those three hires in place, he works less than 30 minutes a week on service delivery in total — the manager is what takes the client-facing work off the owner entirely.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "And the fourth one is where you hire a client success manager." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_C6zYJH4r42A.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as a hire for big agencies only: he presents the four levels as the path from solo to 20k a month, not as enterprise staffing.
- Read as the first hire: the order matters — editor first, then VA, then the manager; skipping ahead leaves delivery on the owner.
