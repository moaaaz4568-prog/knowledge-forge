# G_ig_10feetstudios_DCm4BCNOQiY — content funnels

## Term

Content funnels are what the speaker says an agency must evolve into selling in 2025: not more cold DMs, better editing, or cheaper prices, but a system that grows a client's revenue through content, ads, and conversion mechanisms like setters. The phrase appears once, beside "grow and scale" and "revenue", and the reason is that the content space is crowded with agencies offering creation and editing, while clients care about return — how much money they put in versus what they get out. His line is absolute for the year: if you cannot deliver a clear ROI in 2025, you will not be able to scale.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "Evolve from a business who's only generating content for other businesses to a massively successful agency who helps other businesses grow and scale their revenue through content funnels." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DCm4BCNOQiY.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "editing is worthless": editing remains one part of the funnel; the change is that editing alone is no longer the offer.
- Read as "funnels replace content": the funnel runs on content — the shift is from selling content to selling the revenue the content produces.
