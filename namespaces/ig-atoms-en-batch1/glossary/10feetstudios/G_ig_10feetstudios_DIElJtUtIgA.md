# G_ig_10feetstudios_DIElJtUtIgA — paid ads

## Term

Paid ads are the skill the speaker says saved his agency when content alone stopped working: he was burned out, posting daily, getting likes and comments but no leads or bookings, and feeling like he was not helping anyone. The phrase appears twice, beside "inbound leads" and "appointments", and the turn was learning simple but effective ads and combining them with content — clients started getting inbound leads, calls booked, and sales made, stayed longer, and paid more. His warning is dated to 2025: an agency still only offering organic posts will get left behind.

## Live Example

> "And I did something that completely changed the game. I learned how to run simple but effective paid ads and actually started booking appointments for my clients." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DIElJtUtIgA.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "ads replace content": the result came from combining ads with his content services, not from dropping content.
- Read as "organic is dead": the claim is about agencies offering only organic posts — content still matters inside the combined service.
