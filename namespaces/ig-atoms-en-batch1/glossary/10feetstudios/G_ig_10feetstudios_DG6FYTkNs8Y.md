# G_ig_10feetstudios_DG6FYTkNs8Y — 400 days in a row

## Term

400 days in a row is how long the speaker says he pictured the same moment: pulling up his sales dashboard to $50,000 in sales in the last four weeks, imagining how it would feel, how others would react, and what he could buy. The phrase appears once, beside "$50,000" and "sacrifices", and the habit was nightly — the last thought before sleep every single night. His message to anyone wondering whether the sacrifices are worth it is to keep going, because achieving the goal and turning what was in your mind into reality is, in his words, one of the best feelings on the planet.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "and I have actually thought about this moment over 400 days in a row." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DG6FYTkNs8Y.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as visualization alone: the 400 days frame years of long nights and early mornings; the imagining accompanied the work, it did not replace it.
- Read as a promise of $50,000: the figure is the moment he dreamed about, not a result he guarantees to the viewer.
