# G_ig_10feetstudios_DC18eIFtDDy — two hands and a brain

## Term

Two hands and a brain is the speaker's answer to waiting for better conditions: you already have everything you need to scale your business right now. The phrase appears once, beside "everything you need" and "scale your business", and the waiting he dismisses is specific — the 20th YouTube video on client acquisition, better equipment, a better plan you will never really have. The method is to act and think while doing: pick a niche, build a solid offer that gets clients tangible results, get attention through Looms, then send Looms, mess up, improve, sign a client, and repeat day by day.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You have everything you need to scale your business right now. You have two hands and a brain, I hope, and that's pretty much all you need." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DC18eIFtDDy.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "planning is useless": he still requires a niche, an offer, and an attention channel before acting — the objection is to endless preparation, not to having a plan.
- Read as "talent doesn't matter": the brain in the phrase is doing the work — acting while thinking — not a claim that anyone succeeds without learning.
