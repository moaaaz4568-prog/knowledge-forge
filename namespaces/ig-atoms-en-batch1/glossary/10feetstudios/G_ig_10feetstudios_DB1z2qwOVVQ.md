# G_ig_10feetstudios_DB1z2qwOVVQ — ghosted

## Term

Ghosted is what the speaker says still happens in DMs when an agency struggles to book calls: prospects go silent and the agency waits for opportunities to magically pop up. The word appears once, in "you're still getting ghosted in your DMs", beside "waiting" and "opportunities". His diagnosis is that people will not give their time or attention without a good reason, so instead of asking them to book a call, the agency should offer something valuable first and show why it deserves attention. The system he points to took his agency from 4k to 20k a month in 96 days, and the offer is a breakdown video — comment "breakdown" to get it.

- Read as "DMs never work": the failure is asking for a call before giving value; the fix is a valuable first offer, not abandoning DMs.
- Read as a prospect problem: he frames ghosting as the agency's failure to earn attention, not rudeness from leads.
