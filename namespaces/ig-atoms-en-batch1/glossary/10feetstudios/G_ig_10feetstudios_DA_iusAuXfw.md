# G_ig_10feetstudios_DA_iusAuXfw — content funnel

## Term

A content funnel is what the speaker says an agency must build instead of selling content: it turns the views the agency gets for a client into cash and revenue for the client's business. The phrase appears once, beside "views" and "revenue", and it is the first of three steps out of below-five-figures — stop selling content, stop cold DMs and referrals and use value-packed personalized Looms to book three to eight qualified calls a week, and build a team for service delivery. His premise is blunt: nobody wants to pay for videos and views anymore; clients pay for money made.

## Synonyms

- No variant form of the term appears in this source.

## Live Example

> "You need to start making your clients actual money by building out their content funnel to turn the views that you're getting for them into cash and revenue for their business." — sources/S_ig_10feetstudios_DA_iusAuXfw.md

## When the Meaning Breaks

- Read as "content plus ads": the funnel is the conversion mechanism (views into revenue), not just adding a traffic source to the same videos.
- Read as "views don't matter": views are the top of the funnel here — the claim is that views alone are not the product.
