# G_ig_10feetstudios_C9f2W19Nw9m — capture attention

## Term

Capture attention is the first of three fundamentals the speaker says make clients money: capture attention, nurture it, and then convert it. The phrase appears once, beside "nurture it" and "convert it", and his point is that the specific funnel differs by niche — for info sellers, top-of-funnel ads capture followers, organic value content nurtures them, and DMs convert; for e-commerce, ads can build ManyChat lists and capture email to nurture and convert that way. The method changes, but the three steps are the same, and skipping them is why agencies give clients videos and nothing else.
