I think the biggest problem with scaling as a content agency owner is gonna be fulfillment, and there are two things you can set in place to solve this problem. The first thing is that you should start building a team very early on. I don't think that you should ever be stuck in service delivery as the CEO, not even if you're not making that much yet. What I would do is hire a VA first that takes care of all the admin tasks, and then make the VA hire your first editor based on your criteria, because that takes a lot of time. And then apart from hiring people, I would make sure to have a lot of systems and automations in place so your team can work as efficiently as possible. And then the second thing you can do is adjust your service delivery model. That means you move more service delivery aspects from done for you to done with you and implement a consulting aspect into your agency. So for example, you completely take care of the content side of things, you take care of the editing, the posting and all that stuff, but then you teach them, you show them how they can use that attention that they're getting to generate revenue for their business. You show them, for example, how to run ads as well. Maybe you help them script out their own appointment setting sequences instead of setting the appointments for them. Because video editing and content creation in general can be outsourced very well, systemized and automated. It doesn't take that much time. But then implementing a funnel for every single one of your clients will take a lot of time at scale. So you should move these aspects from done for you to done with you and create videos and a lot of resources showing them how they can do these things for themselves.
