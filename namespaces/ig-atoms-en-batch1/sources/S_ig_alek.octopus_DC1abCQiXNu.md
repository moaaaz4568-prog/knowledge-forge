What are three psychology tricks that can make customers buy instantly from you? Number one is the illusion of choice. Always give your clients two potential options to choose from, both of which are beneficial for you. The second one is odds in favor. Guarantee that they have winning results in all of the scenarios working with you. And finally, the rejection and retreat. Present a highly priced offer that can almost shock the customer, only to then retreat and present a more reasonable one, which will guarantee that they acquire it. This is how you use psychology to make more sales.
