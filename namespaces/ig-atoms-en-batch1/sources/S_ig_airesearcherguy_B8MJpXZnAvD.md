Now I want to talk about the endowment effect, and this is basically where people overvalue the things that they own. In many studies, people were asked how much they would buy something versus how much they would sell the same thing, and in almost every instance, the sell price was much higher, indicating that people overvalue things that they already own. So how you can apply this in your business is if you can find a way to guide your prospective customer in a journey where they picture themselves already owning your product or already experiencing your service, then that in their mind will create an instance where they've kind of felt that ownership, and just feeling that ownership will bring them closer to choosing to spend their money with you.
