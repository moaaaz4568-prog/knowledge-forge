These two or three websites replace most of the visual effects designers pay for, and almost nobody knows they exist. The first one is geometricart.com. Upload any image, pick an effect from the side panel, and it turns your photo into geometric line art. You can adjust the thickness, the colors, and it even works with videos where the effects react to the motion automatically. Then export it as an image, a video, or an embedded. The second one is playgroundtools.com. It has over fifty tiny tools for backgrounds, patterns, posters, and textures. Just click one and play with the settings on the left—change the colors, add motion, and export it when it looks right.
