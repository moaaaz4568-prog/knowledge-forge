What is the central limit theorem and why is it important in statistics? The central limit theorem is a statistical concept that applies to large samples of data. It states that if you take a large enough sample of data from any population, the distribution of the means of the samples will be approximately normal, even if the original population is not normally distributed. This means that we can make statistical inferences about a population based on a sample, even if we don't have data on the whole population. The central limit theorem is important because it is the foundation for many statistical methods, such as hypothesis testing and confidence intervals.
