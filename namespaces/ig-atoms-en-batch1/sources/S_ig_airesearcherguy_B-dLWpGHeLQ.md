Now I want to jump back into more on psychology. So during the Korean War, POWs actually cooperated with Chinese soldiers, and these are American POWs. Why? Because of a psychological principle known as consistency. So basically what the Chinese soldiers did was they had American soldiers write down essays copying pro-communist statements from like another book or something, and then had them read it out loud. And what would then happen is over time they were essentially brainwashed because when faced with a decision, when faced with thinking about these pro-communist statements, they would go back to when they wrote it down and when they said it out loud, and to remain consistent, their brain essentially believed that they thought those thoughts too. So this consistency principle works even when you're copying something, not even your own original ideas. And you can get the full explanation of this from the book Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion by Robert Cialdini.
