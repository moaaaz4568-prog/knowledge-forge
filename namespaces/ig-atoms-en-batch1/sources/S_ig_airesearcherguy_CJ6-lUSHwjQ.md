How Amazon's keyword search algorithm works. On many platforms, it is stressed that the most important aspect is SEO content. That's not so much the case on Amazon. See, on those other platforms, your search engine optimized content basically tells the platform, "This is what my product is. These are the keywords that are relevant." While SEO content is important on Amazon, it only serves as a guideline. Basically, you're telling Amazon, "This is what we think our product is, and these are the keywords that we believe are relevant." But because Amazon has so much purchase data, really, buyer behavior is what determines what your product is. See, Amazon tracks everything. All the listings that a buyer saw before they saw your listing, where they clicked out, what led to the purchase, whether it was your product that they purchased or another product, what products they added to cart, etc. Every search and every listing that a buyer goes through essentially creates a map of keyword relevance. This is what is referred to as associated relevance, and that will ultimately determine what keywords your listing is found relevant for and ends up being visible for.
