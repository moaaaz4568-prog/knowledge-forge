I run a 100k a month sales team, and the biggest question they always keep on asking me is, how do I actually dive deeper into the pain of the prospect? I think most salespeople, they don't realize that there's four levels of sales pain, and they mostly stay on level one and two. They would ask the surface level questions of, why do you want to lose weight? Why do you want to make money? And so the four levels of pain and how I define them is level one is just surface level basic desires. Level two is now tied up actually to feelings and emotions. And level three is where it gets really interesting. Level three is where you actually get into consequence and impact on their life. Hey, what would happen if six months from now nothing changes for you? And then the final level, level four, where only the one percent really of closers operate, is where it's legacy and identity tied. That's where it's like, well, what will happen if this continues for the rest of your life? And what happens if you never lose the weight and you actually become homeless and live under a bridge? And so this is why if you're staying on level one and two, you're never gonna close deals. You need to actually go to level four pain all the time. And most salespeople, they never get there.
