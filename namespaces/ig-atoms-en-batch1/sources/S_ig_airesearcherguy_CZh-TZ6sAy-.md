Why is Amazon still the number one e-commerce platform in America? It's not like a lot of people have brand loyalty to Amazon. In fact, most people speak negatively about the company these days, and yet they still rule the roost. This is because Amazon has worked so hard to reduce friction. Think about it. The platform remembers your login information and remembers your banking information and very rarely asks you to re-input it. Contrast that with shopping on another platform. You have to create a new account, verify that new account, re-enter all of your banking information, and hope that every time you visit, it doesn't ask you to log in again. It's that frictionless ease that makes Amazon so attractive. That's why I suggest if you have your own e-commerce store to engage in chatbot marketing. Conversational marketing will allow you to get information from a prospective customer through organic conversation. You can ask them their name. You can offer to send them something for free and ask for their address. You can ask them for their email address so that you can send a receipt or tracking information. All of that won't feel like a sign-up process, but then you can store that information so the next time they come to your site, you can have much less friction for them to get to a purchase.
