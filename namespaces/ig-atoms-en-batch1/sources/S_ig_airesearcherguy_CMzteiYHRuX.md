Quick neuromarketing tip for restaurant owners and food service providers. Whenever you introduce the concept of money, our brain switches from emotion to logic. However, most consumer purchases are done on an emotional level. So in many cases, introducing any reminder of money can be detrimental. In the book Brainfluence, the author talks about neuroscience research that was done on menu pricing. Menus that have one simple number, no currency symbol, no decimals, prompt customers to make more purchases. If you run a restaurant, take the currency symbol and decimal away. Have one small number. Less reminders of money, more emotional purchases.
