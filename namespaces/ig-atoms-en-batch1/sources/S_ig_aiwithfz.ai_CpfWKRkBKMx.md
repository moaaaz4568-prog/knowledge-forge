What are some common metrics used for the goodness of fit of a logistic regression model? Some common metrics used for assessing the goodness of fit of a logistic regression model include: Accuracy: Accuracy measures the proportion of correct predictions out of the total number of predictions. Precision: Precision measures the proportion of true positive predictions out of the total number of positive predictions. Recall: Recall measures the proportion of true positive predictions out of the total number of actual positives in the data. F1 score: The F1 score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, and provides a single score that balances the two metrics. Area under the curve: The AUC is a metric that measures the overall performance of a logistic regression model by calculating the area under the ROC curve. A higher AUC indicates a better performing model.
