Neuro marketing tip. This is called the foot in the door method. When a bunch of people were surveyed and asked which is better, only asking for one favor or asking for two, most people said only asking for one. They would be wrong. It turns out that if somebody has already given you a favor in the past, they are significantly more likely to give you another one. So if you're ever trying to sell anything, make a small ask, something very easy to say yes to. Ask somebody to sign up for a free trial or click the happy face on a review. When you come in for a bigger ask, you are much more likely to get a yes from them.
