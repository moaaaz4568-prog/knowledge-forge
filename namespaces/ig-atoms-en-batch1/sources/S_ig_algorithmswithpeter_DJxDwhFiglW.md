Let's understand decision trees in this video. A decision tree is like playing a game of twenty questions. It helps you make a decision by asking yes or no questions, step by step. Think of it like this. Imagine you're trying to decide what to wear. First question is it raining? If yes, then wear a raincoat. If no, then next question. Second question, is it cold outside? If yes, then wear a jacket. If no, then it's t-shirt time. Every question helps you go down a different path, just like the branches of a tree. That's why it's called a decision tree. It helps computers make choices too. Like is this fruit round? Is it red? Does it have seeds? Answering these leads the computer to say it's an apple. A decision tree asks questions one by one, and each answer helps it decide what to do next, just like you do every day without even realizing it. So next time you're stuck, think like a decision tree. Ask yourself questions, take each answer step by step, and bam, you got your answer. Making choices isn't so scary when you have a little decision tree in your brain helping out. And hey, now you know how computers do it too.
