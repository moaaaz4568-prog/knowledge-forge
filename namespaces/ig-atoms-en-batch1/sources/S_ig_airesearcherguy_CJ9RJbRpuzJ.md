What's your favorite car? Do you remember the day that you first saw it and then you decided that day that was your favorite car? Let's say for example it was a red Ferrari. You noticed that before red Ferrari was your favorite car, you never saw them anywhere, never noticed them, and then after, all of a sudden they were everywhere. One parked at the movies, one at that stoplight, one in your neighborhood. You didn't know anybody in your neighborhood drove a red Ferrari. The reason this is happening is because of something called the Baader-Meinhof effect. Basically, this is a frequency bias. It's not that something is increasing in occurrence, it's that you are increasing in observation of it. Essentially, what this means is that things that you find interesting or things you're focused on, you can see everywhere. So on some level, you have a choice.
