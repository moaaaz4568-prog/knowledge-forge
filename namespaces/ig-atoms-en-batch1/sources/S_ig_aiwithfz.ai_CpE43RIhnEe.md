What are the assumptions of linear regression? Linearity: There is a linear relationship between the independent variables and the dependent variable. Independence: The observations are independent of each other, meaning that the value of one observation does not affect the value of another observation. Homoscedasticity: The variance of the errors is constant across all levels of the independent variables. Normality: The errors are normally distributed with a mean of zero. No multicollinearity: There is no perfect correlation between the independent variables.
