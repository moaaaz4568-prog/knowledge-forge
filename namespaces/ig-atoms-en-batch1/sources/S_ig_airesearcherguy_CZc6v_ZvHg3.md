Let's talk about NFTs, specifically PFP (profile picture) generative art NFTs. I see a lot of people make comments about how these NFTs are lazy art, but I think this might be because they don't understand what it is. First of all, there's a misconception that generative NFT projects are created by clicking a button and the AI does all the work. That's not true at all. There are some programs out there that allow AI to create art. Those usually don't produce anything that sells for very much money on the marketplace. Generative projects use a simple Python program. Basically how it works is an artist still has to create the layers. They still have to draw the character, the clothes, the hat, the jewelry, all of that stuff. They also still have to create all of the variations, the different skin or fur color, and the different types of layers. What the program does though is it takes these 100 or 200 or so attributes and it shuffles them. And the artist or NFT creator has the ability to set how common or rare a trait is. And based on those parameters, the program will then spit out a whole collection, some of the pieces being super common and some of them being super rare. That's the reason why some Bored Apes are worth so much more than others, because there are some really rare ones. Generative art is cool. Using programs, software, and technology to create generative art projects is cool. Selling it on the blockchain is cool. And if you don't think so, too bad, it's happening, it's the future.
