Do you sell a physical product? Here's why I think you should be selling digital products too. First is the concept of the value ladder. Offer like a free ebook, offer a small product, and then upsell to a larger product, and then a monthly subscription. The benefits behind that are obvious. You get more people into the mouth of your funnel, and you increase average order value. However, the other reason is because when you sell a physical product, you give your customers something to feel and see. But imagine if you could affect more of their senses, more of their mind space. You give them something to read as well, something to think about. Maybe you give them a video or an audio file, something to hear. The more touch points like that you have with a customer, the stronger a bond your customer creates with your brand, and the more likely you'll get a referral. And that's how brands scale.
