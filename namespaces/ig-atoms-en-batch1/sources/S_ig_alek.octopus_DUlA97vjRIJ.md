The number one question I get asked the most is how do I get more clients? It's quite simple. The first thing that you are going to do is you will start telling people exactly what you have to offer. Because no matter how great your product or service is, if no one knows about it, no one will buy it. The second thing you want to do is you want to explain them why what you offer is different than X, Y, and Z that is on the market. This will establish you as a unique authority in your niche and be able to build your authority. And the third thing you want to do is you want to tell them how your unique solution fixes their unique problem. Don't just tell people that you help them lose weight. Tell them how you help pregnant women lose up to 20 pounds in the next three months. This is a specific solution to a specific problem, and that's how you get more clients.
