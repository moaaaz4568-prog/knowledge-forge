I used to close clients and immediately start worrying about when they would leave. Every month felt like a reason. I had anxiety worrying if they'd continue paying because I knew that they could bounce at any time. There was no commitment and no security for me. One day I decided I can't live like that anymore and I realized the problem was my offer and how I packaged it. So I stopped doing month-to-month contracts, I reworked my offer, built a clear outcome and positioned it as a six-month growth program, not just a content creation offer. And just like that, everything got easier. Things took it more seriously, retention went up, my cash flow got stable and I stopped chasing people every four weeks for their payments. So if you still have a week off or a selling month to month, you're leaving yourself open a churn, stress and inconsistent income.
