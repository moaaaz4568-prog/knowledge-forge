Show what works and show what doesn't works. Old ways versus new ways. This instantly creates clarity. You also have a quick value carousel. One small trick or hack that solves a very specific problem. Storytelling is another powerful one. Each slide builds a story, making people swipe till the end. Then there's transformation before and after with a clear explanation of what changed and how. Problem to solution carousel works really well. You highlight a pain point and immediately follow it with a fix. List-based carousels are simple but effective. Each slide works towards a new point. And finally, the types format.
