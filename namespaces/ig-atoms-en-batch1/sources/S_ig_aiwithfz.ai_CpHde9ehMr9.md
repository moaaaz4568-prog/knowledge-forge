What is the mean squared error MSE and how is it used to evaluate regression models? The lower the MSE, the better the performance of the regression model. MSE is calculated as the average of the squared differences between the predicted and actual values. It measures the quality of the predictions made by the model. MSE is sensitive to outliers and can be influenced by extreme values in the data. MSE is commonly used in conjunction with other measures such as R squared to evaluate the performance of regression models.
