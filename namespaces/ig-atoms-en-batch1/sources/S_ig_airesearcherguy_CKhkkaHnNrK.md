How Amazon's search algorithm works. Amazon's algorithm prioritizes search results based on what it thinks is the most relevant to the search terms used. And it determines this based on buyer behavior, but chief among those behaviors are add to carts, which signal purchase intent, and of course, purchases. So how you can help that process along if you have a listing that isn't ranking is with a rebate campaign. This is where you run an ad for a free product, which leads the buyer to a chatbot that asks them if they'd like to participate in a 100% rebate offer. And they say yes, your chatbot sends them to Amazon so they search for your product, find it, and buy it. This gives Amazon organic search, full priced purchase, relevance, higher ranking.
