Playing on the consistency principle, another thing that you can do is inconvenience your potential customer. See, if you ask your prospective customer to do you a favor or otherwise jump through a hoop and they do it, in order to remain consistent, they're more likely to buy from you or support your brand because in their head, if they went through that effort for you, then you must be someone that they like and trust.
