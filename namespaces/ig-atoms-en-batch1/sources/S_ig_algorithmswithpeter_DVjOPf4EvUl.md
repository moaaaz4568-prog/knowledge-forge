So Windows is like that one friend who says I won't tell anyone and immediately writes it down. Pretty much. Can we kill this creepy diary? Yes. Open Registry Editor, go to the Prefetch settings and disable EnablePrefetcher and enable Superfetch. Then restart the PC. And the spying stops? It stops new logs.
