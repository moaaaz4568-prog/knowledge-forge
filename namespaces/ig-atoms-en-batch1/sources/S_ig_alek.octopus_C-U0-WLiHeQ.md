What are three words to never say in sales? The first and most important thing is to stop saying "I am sorry." Instead, simply thanks people for understanding you. The second is "buy." Instead of saying "buy my product," you can say "acquire" or "reserve my spot," which directly has a correlation to the benefit that they obtain. And then finally, never say "trust me." Immediately, I don't trust you. Instead, just simply showcase proof of the work you've done and the results you have achieved for other clients.
