This is how I personally vet and select my suppliers from Alibaba.com. First off, I only work with a factory that's been a gold supplier for a minimum of three years. My logic behind that is if they paid for gold supplier status but they are a ripoff, they won't do it three years in a row. Next, I only work with suppliers that take trade assurance. I don't personally like to use trade assurance because sometimes it complicates the logistics process, but if they'll take it, that means that they're willing to allow the money to be held until they fulfill the order. Finally, I personally only like to work with suppliers that are listed as manufacturers, and I will go to their company website to make sure that the products that they manufacture are all within the same niche or basically use the same equipment, so I know that they're really manufacturing them. That way I know that I can make changes. Finally though, the biggest test is how they communicate back and forth. Do they respond to emails fast? Do they answer all of your questions? Ultimately, the final decision comes down to how comfortable you are with the communication that you've had.
