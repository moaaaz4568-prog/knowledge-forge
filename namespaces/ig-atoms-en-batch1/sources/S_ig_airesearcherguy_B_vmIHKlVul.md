The fifth principle of persuasion is liking. We prefer to do business and communicate with people that we like. There are three factors that make somebody like another person. We like people who are similar to us, we like people who compliment us, and we like people who cooperate with us. In a study done between two business schools, students dramatically increased the outcome of negotiations when they were coached first to find similarities with the person they were negotiating with and then compliment them. So how this plays out in your business, any opportunity you have to point out your similarities with people and then also paying genuine compliments will definitely increase your customer value. If you're online, then that means running contextual ads like geo-targeting and things like that, targeting individuals based on an attribute and then proclaiming that similarity, like hitting up everybody in Texas saying, hey, are you from Texas too? Check this out in an ad, for example. That's the kind of thing that gets people to like you, and then they'll want to do business with you.
