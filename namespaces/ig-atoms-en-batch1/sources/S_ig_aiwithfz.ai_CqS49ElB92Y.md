Purpose of feature importance in random forest and how is it calculated? Feature importance in random forest is a way of determining which features or variables are the most important in making accurate predictions. It is calculated based on how much each feature contributes to reducing the impurity of the decision tree nodes when making splits. In simpler terms, feature importance tells us which features have the most influence on the outcome of a prediction and thus should be given more weight in the model. We can optimize our model by focusing on those features and eliminating less important ones, which can improve model performance and reduce the risk of overfitting.
