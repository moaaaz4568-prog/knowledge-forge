Want to make your marketing better? Neuroscience has uncovered that basically everybody is made up of two selves: the want self and the should self. The want self is seeking immediate gratification. The should self looks for long-term benefit. So if you have a product that offers immediate gratification, make sure in your marketing you use language that stresses the immediacy. Sign up now and get approved within seconds. However, if you have a more long-term product, like a supplement, focus your language on long-term thinking. Maybe offer a 20% discount for a six-month supply.
