Let's go over a couple of real quick lessons in copywriting. When you're writing copy for your ad, focus on what the product allows you to do. Make sure that what you write is benefit-driven, emotion-evoking, and the most relevant driving force in your market. What I mean by that is, make sure you are illustrating the reason most people will buy this product. Don't pick a reason that a lot of people don't actually use as their buying decision. So in the book Made to Stick by Chip and Dan Heath, they talk about how abstract ideas don't really stick, but concrete ideas do. So you want to make your message highly relevant, make it concrete and tangible. And essentially what that means is don't paint abstract images for people. Don't talk about the joy of cooking. Talk about how they can crush more garlic with this awesome garlic crusher. Make it concrete so it's easier for them to picture themselves using your product.
