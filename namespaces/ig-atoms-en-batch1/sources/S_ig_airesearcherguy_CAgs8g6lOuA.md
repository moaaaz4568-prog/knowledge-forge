So a couple years ago, I was at a business conference and I got to hear both Tom Bilyeu of Quest Nutrition and Jesse Eiser of Marquis Jets speak. And they both spoke about this concept that they referred to as grit. In Tom's case, he talked about having grit when he worked 16-hour days grinding through his dream. When Jesse talked about it, he talked about how it took grit to follow David Goggins into freezing cold water in nothing but boxer shorts. And they're both inspirations. I have mad respect for them both, and they're both far more successful than I am. I mean no disrespect, but that is not what grit is. Everybody who has a side hustle works 80 hours a week. Working 16-hour days does not take grit. It takes heart. It's awesome to work through discomfort and jump into a frozen lake, but that also doesn't take grit. Grit is when you pour your heart, your soul, your time, your money, your energy, your all into something and then fail or get rejected over and over and over again until you feel like life has just smushed you as far into the dirt as you can go. And then you get up and you try again.
