Are you a picker or a chooser? In the book Barking Up the Wrong Tree, Eric Barker talks about how most people are pickers. What that means is that most people pick from the choices that are presented to them. When you think about it, that makes sense. Most people don't know there are other options. However, choosers can see if all the choices presented to them are bad and they don't pick any. At that point, they go and create their own options, which is probably a category most entrepreneurs fall in. So which one are you, a picker or a chooser?
