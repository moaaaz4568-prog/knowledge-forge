How to overcome cognitive biases. First thing to understand is that all humans have biases. Behavioral economists say we develop biases because our brain is lazy, but I don't think that's a very good description. Really, it's just tired. The brain spends an enormous amount of energy keeping our bodies moving, keeping our heart pumping, keeping our lungs breathing, and higher level cognitive function takes a lot of energy too. So in an effort to conserve energy, it'll bypass those functions when possible, and that's essentially how biases are created. How I've been overcoming that, and you can too, is like this. The next time you hear something and you have an immediate emotional reaction, maybe you immediately disagree or you immediately agree a lot, take a step back and ask yourself, why did I just react that way? Chances are that was a bias.
