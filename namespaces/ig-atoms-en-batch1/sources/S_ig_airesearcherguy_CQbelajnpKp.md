Why do people spend more money to buy name brands? Consciously, we like to think it's because we want to buy the best product. But that's impossible. It's not like we can test every product on the market. Realistically, it's because we humans are willing to pay a premium to avoid uncertainty. And a name brand product is less likely to be total crap. In the book Alchemy, the author tells a story. Imagine that you buy a television from an unknown brand. The unknown brand's name doesn't mean anything, so they can't suffer reputational damage. So if they get a bad batch of 20,000 crappy televisions, it actually makes sense for them to try and offload those to unsuspecting customers. Samsung, however, would suffer too much reputational damage and therefore couldn't do that. So if you want people to choose your brand, then you have to signal that you care about reputational damage and you'll provide quality to avoid it.
