In this book I'm listening to called Algorithms to Live By, I just learned about a concept called overfitting. And what happens when we overfit is we try to add too many data points in an effort to make our predictive models more effective, and we end up over-optimizing for the metrics and not for what really matters. So how that plays out in the real world, let's say in marketing, it's when you focus too much on all the little metrics like cost per click or cost per lead that you lose sight of what's important, which is the sale, and you end up optimizing really well for those little metrics, but you're still not profitable. This also plays out in professional sports a lot too. When you train so hard to make the points for the competition, your technique becomes ineffective in a real fight. So that's overfitting, and in business, in life in general, it can creep up on us, so always keep in mind the ultimate most important metric and pay attention to that. Let that guide all of the predictive models that you create.
