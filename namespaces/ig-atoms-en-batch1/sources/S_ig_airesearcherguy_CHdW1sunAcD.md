Did you know that Apple almost went out of business? Back in 1996, the media was convinced that they wouldn't survive. Check out a couple of these quotes. Fortune magazine wrote, "By the time you read this story, the quirky cult company will end its wild ride as an independent enterprise." Wired wrote, "Admit it, you're out of the hardware game." That year, sales were awful. The company really was on the brink. So how did they come back from the dead? Steve Jobs came back and basically asked his team, "We have so many products. Which one should I recommend to my friends?" And nobody could give a consistent answer. Due to the paradox of choice, Apple had too many offerings and so they didn't dominate any one category. So Jobs proceeded to cut down the number of products Apple offered by 70%. By focusing on just four products, within 10 more years, they'd be one of the most valuable and most talked about companies on the planet.
