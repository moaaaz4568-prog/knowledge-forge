Now I want to talk about the IKEA effect. The IKEA effect essentially is where when someone puts forth effort, when there's labor involved with the creation of something, they tend to overvalue it. This is the reason why it's called IKEA because if you buy something from IKEA, you'll actually value the thing that you built yourself more than you would ever value it already assembled. So how this can be used in your business is if you have the ability to allow your prospective customer to piece together maybe their own plan or their own features of a product, then they're much more likely to want that thing because of the effort it took to put it together. This is why the build-your-own-shoe or build-your-own-car websites work so well because people are very much so more attached to things that they spent time, energy, and effort into building themselves.
