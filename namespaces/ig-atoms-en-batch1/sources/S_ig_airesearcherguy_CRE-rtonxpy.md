Here's a neuromarketing hack. If you give potential customers an upside and a downside, you'll increase the likelihood that they'll buy. This serves two psychological functions. First, giving a realistic downside to your product will make the prospect trust you more, and they'll also downplay the impact of the downside. Second, on average, research shows that humans are usually pretty optimistic. So if you give them good news and bad news, they tend to focus primarily on the good news. So if you give your prospective buyers a downside and then really beneficial upside, they'll primarily focus on that beneficial upside.
