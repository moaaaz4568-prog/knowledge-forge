AAAEP stands for availability, association, action, emotion, and the preferences of others. And it follows a much more realistic pattern of the way people interact with brands. So that's availability, which is how easily your brand comes to a person's mind, which means you need to be everywhere that they are. Association are the concepts that come to people's minds when they think about your brand. So this means you have to be strategic with the imagery, the music, the shapes, the colors, the sounds that you use in your advertising. Action means that it's easier to get people to take an action to buy your product than it is to get them to change their mind. So rather than explain to people what you stand for as the reason why they should buy, give people specific steps to take to get your product. Emotion just means you got to get people to feel an emotion. And the preferences of others is social proof.
