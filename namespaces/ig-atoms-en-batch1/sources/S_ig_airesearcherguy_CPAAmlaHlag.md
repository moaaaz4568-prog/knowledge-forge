Copywriting tip: Ladder up to the emotion. The number one copywriting lesson everybody hears is not to focus on features, but instead benefits. So like if I'm selling a baby carrier, the feature is that it straps your baby to your chest so your hands can be free. The benefit is your hands are free so you can get stuff done while you're holding your baby. But you should take it a step further and focus on the emotion that that benefit will allow you to feel. Sticking with the same example, the emotion would be feel accomplished because you're actually getting stuff done while holding onto your baby. So just take it a step further than just benefit and ladder up to the emotion.
