You ever heard the phrase "so good it'll practically sell itself"? Pretty much anybody that sells a really high quality product may think that, and therefore think that marketing isn't necessary. But did you know that marketing can actually impact the experience that a customer has with a product? How is that even possible? Neuroscience research uncovered that when people blind tasted the exact same wine, those that thought it cost more actually genuinely enjoyed it more.
