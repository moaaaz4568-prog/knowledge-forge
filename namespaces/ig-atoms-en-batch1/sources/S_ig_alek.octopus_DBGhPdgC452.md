What are three words to never say in sales? The worst one that you can say is "trust me." Whenever you say this, you immediately lose all trust. Instead, simply say, "I'm certain about this," and showcase clients' results. The second one is the F-word, just following up. Don't say that ever. Instead, simply provide a valuable resource and find a creative way to connect with your client. And then finally, never say "low." Instead, say "funding." That's how you close more deals.
