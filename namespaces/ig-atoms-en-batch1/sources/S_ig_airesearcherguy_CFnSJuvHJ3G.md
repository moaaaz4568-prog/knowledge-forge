Did you know you have three brains? I'm kidding, sort of. There are three distinct parts of the brain, and understanding these parts of the brain will help you sell your stuff better. So first we have the reptilian brain, and this controls your autonomous functions. It tells your lungs to breathe, it tells your heart to beat, etc. Then you have the mammalian brain, or what I call the squirrel brain, which is the emotional center. It controls all of your emotions. And lastly, we have the primate brain, or the ape brain, and it controls logic. The reason it's important to understand this is because pretty much all consumer purchases happen when the squirrel brain is in the driver's seat. See, the ape brain always runs a cost-benefit analysis for everything. If he was in control every time we made a purchase, we'd never buy anything to make us happy, to make us feel good, or to make our lives more convenient. Literally, it would only be, do we absolutely need this? Engaging the ape brain is really good for like B2B purchases, but as consumers, the squirrel brain is the one that's in charge. This is the reason why it's so important for brands to connect emotionally, because it's the emotion that sells. This is also the reason why things like upsells work, because the squirrel brain attaches a good feeling to your product, and therefore will attach more of that good feeling to multiple units or to other products of the same type.
