Do you sell a product or service, and you feel like having as many options as possible is the best way to serve your customers? Well, turns out this might actually be a bad idea, and I'm gonna explain to you why. It's called the paradox of choice. This is a concept discussed at length in behavioral economics. But to illustrate, there was a study done by Sheena Iyengar and Mark Lepper of Columbia and Stanford Universities called the jam experiment. They went to a local supermarket and set up a table with samples of jam, and every hour they cycled through. One hour they offered 24 different options, the other hour they offered six. Then they studied the data to see which display led to the most sales. And it turns out that the display that only offered six options sold far more than the display with 24, about 10 times as much. And this is because when faced with too many choices, people become paralyzed and they don't make any choice at all. So if you have too many options for your customers, you may want to rethink that.
