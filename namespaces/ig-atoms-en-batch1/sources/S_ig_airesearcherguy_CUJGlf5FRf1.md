What's the difference between Alibaba and AliExpress? AliExpress is more of a wholesale marketplace. This is where you can buy things at discounted wholesale prices. You can usually order one item at a time rather than having to place a bulk order, but you're not dealing directly with the manufacturer, so you're not going to be negotiating customizations. You simply buy as is at wholesale prices. And that's the reason why dropshippers tend to use AliExpress because they can order one unit at a time and have it delivered to the person that ordered the product from their website. Alibaba is more of a B2B marketplace that puts you in touch with the factory directly. Here you can customize items, put your logo on them, or make tweaks to the design, but you also order them in bulk. Your minimum order quantities will typically be anywhere from a couple of hundred to a few thousand. And that's the reason I, as a brand owner, source my products using Alibaba because I order in bulk. That way I can send it into FBA and also get the items customized, put my brand on it, tweak the design, etc.
