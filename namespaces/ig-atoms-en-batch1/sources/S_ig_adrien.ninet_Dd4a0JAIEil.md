Something I've noticed with AI is that progress isn't linear. It's flat for months and then one model comes out and everything is ten times better. I've lived that twice this year. The first one was February, when a friend set me up on Claude Code. I'd never touched an automation tool before. Then one afternoon I watched the cursor move across my screen on its own and start doing my work while I just sat there. Within a week it was cleaning my inbox, organizing my Figma files, analyzing my call notes, all the boring stuff I thought was mine forever, just gone. I asked for a website in a very specific visual style and it just one-shot hits. The old models never got there, which means the hard part now is just knowing exactly what you want.
