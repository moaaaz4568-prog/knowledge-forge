In the book The Seven Secrets of Persuasion, the author introduces this concept called the leaky hose, which is basically a funnel building and optimization tool. So the first step is you map out every possible step a typical potential customer would take to get to purchase your product. For example, let's say it starts with a Google search. They search for these questions, they end up on your website, they book an appointment, they talk to a salesperson. That then informs you of all the places that you need to be visible to your potential customer. So if it starts with a Google search, you need search engine optimized content for them to find. If they end up on your website, you need an optimized landing page. The next step, look at leaks in the hose to see where people are dropping off. Rather than look at that and go, how can I optimize this? Ask the question, what is the potential customer doing instead? You may have all the copy, all the colors, everything optimized as much as it can be. But if you don't ask the question, what are they doing instead? You might miss out on a more efficient or new process that maybe a competitor is implementing. I just thought that was really cool, so hope that helps.
