If you're running your own messenger rebate campaigns to rank your products on Amazon, and your cost per rebate and cost per message are really high, here's how you lower your cost per acquisition. First, test different audiences against each other. The way I do this is I run a CBO campaign, and then I just add different ad sets with new audiences and see which one gets the lowest CPA. It's not always the obvious audience. It won't always be interests that are directly related to your product. It won't always be lookalike audiences from your buyers. Sometimes you gotta actually target people who have said that they like rebates. Sometimes you gotta change the demographics or the income levels. Just mess around with that. Once you find the most affordable audience and you're still not happy with the number, test the creative, specifically the media. Test the image or the video. Sometimes this can be the thing that makes all the difference. I've used professional level videos before and the ad performed poorly. Then I tested it with a stupid little image pop video that I made in Canva and my CPAs went down dramatically. So those are the things you want to test until your costs get to where you need them to be.
