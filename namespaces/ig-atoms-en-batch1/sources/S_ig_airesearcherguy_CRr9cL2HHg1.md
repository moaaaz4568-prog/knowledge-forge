Things I learned at Prosper Show, part one. Listing optimization. Make sure to optimize from the perspective of the browse page. Look at your listing from the search page, particularly on the mobile app. Is it obvious what you're selling? Is the primary benefit obvious? Is the main image clear? Pro tip, use a perfectly square or portrait-oriented image. Don't use landscape. This will make your product look very small.
