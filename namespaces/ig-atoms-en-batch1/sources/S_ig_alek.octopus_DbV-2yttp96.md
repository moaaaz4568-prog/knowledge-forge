Three phrases that make you sound weak in sales. Number one is "I'm sorry." Every time you're apologizing, you sound like a crybaby. Instead, say "Thank you for your patience." Number two, "I'm not so sure about this." Stop being unsure and start speaking with certainty. "I will check on this. I have the certainty that we'll find the solution for it." Much better. And number three is "I don't know if I can help you." If you don't know, then you might as well quit. Instead, say "We'll figure something out." That's how you don't sound weak and you sound strong.
