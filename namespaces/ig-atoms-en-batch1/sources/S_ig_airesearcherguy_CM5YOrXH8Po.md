Some advice for people who are using or experimenting with chatbots in their business. A colleague of mine referred to this as the 8,000 legged octopus. A single flow. Don't build this because it's too hard to scale. When you grow a team, it'll be impossible for them to fix anything or find problems with a flow this big. You want to chunk it out with smaller flows that lead into each other. So here's a flow, leads into that flow, then that flow leads into that flow, then that flow leads into that flow and makes it way more manageable.
