This is how the algorithm works on social media. When you first post content, it gets distributed to 20% of your followers and to a portion of people who are not your followers but who share similar interests to the content. Then, the algorithm measures two things: watch time and engagement. The more time a person spends watching your content, the more it gets pushed to people with similar interests. If the content doesn't grab the viewer's attention, it gets no boost. Since the platform likes earning money, they care more about the watch time because the longer someone spends on their platform, the more they get paid from ads. Also, if a person shares, saves, likes or comments on your post, the algorithm boosts the content, which in turn leads to more reach and engagement from a broader audience.
