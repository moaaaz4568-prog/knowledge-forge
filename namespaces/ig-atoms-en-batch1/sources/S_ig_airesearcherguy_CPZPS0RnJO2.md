Alright, today I want to talk about moonshots. No, not the crypto. Moonshots in business are basically these hairy, audacious goals to shoot for something that could be impossible. Here's the thing though. A lot of times when striving for a moonshot, businesses focus on making something physically better, like the fastest train go even faster. Problem is, is that's really expensive and sometimes impossible. Making the fastest train go 30% faster requires work on the engine or the track or the technology as a whole. But a psychological moonshot is way easier and a lot cheaper. Making a train 30% more enjoyable to ride, much easier process. So when you're looking at improvements to your business, don't only focus on the physical. See where you can implement psychological moonshots.
