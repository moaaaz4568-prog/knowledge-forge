There are eight creator communication archetypes, and knowing which ones you are can help you better determine what kind of content feels the most authentic to you and also what will get you the best results. So if you like leaving people with a clear framework of what to do and how to do it, you are a teacher. If you do better on camera without a script and your content feels like a FaceTime with a friend, you are a conversationalist. If you always turn to stories, memories, or metaphors to explain ideas, you are a storyteller. If you feel the need to call out the BS in your industry and explain what nobody else will say, you are a contrarian. If you get more excited about how you're gonna present the idea than the idea itself, you are an entertainer. If you feel more authentic when you're showing what's actually happening instead of this polished version of it, you are a documentarian. If you feel most confident when everything you say is backed up by facts, proof, or experience, you are an authority. If your whole approach is, I've been where you are and here's how I got out of it, you are a peer.
