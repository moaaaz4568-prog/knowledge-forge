If I need to close a deal, instead of saying, "Do you agree? Do you want to do this? Are you in favor of this?" I say, "Do you disagree? Is this a bad idea? Are you against this? Is this ridiculous?" Because you feel that yes is an obligation and you're going to be worried about digging yourself deeper in by saying anything after that. But having said no, you feel you have no obligation. You will lay the rest of the stuff out, not being worried about digging yourself into a hole. People feel safe and protected when they say no. They feel better. And so they're more likely to collaborate.
