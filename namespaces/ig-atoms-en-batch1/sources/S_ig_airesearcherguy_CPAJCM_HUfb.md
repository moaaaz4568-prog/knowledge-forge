Uncertainty sucks way more than inconvenience. When something bad happens and a customer is inconvenienced, this can lead to a negative customer experience, but it's also really easy to recover from. However, when people experience uncertainty, that is unacceptable. How this plays out in the e-commerce world, let's say a product somebody ordered gets lost. If you can acknowledge that you know it was lost and tell the customer you're going to replace it and give them a time to expect that replacement, most of the time you can turn that back into a positive customer experience. But if you don't communicate with them and leave it uncertain, that is the stuff that leads to negative reviews.
