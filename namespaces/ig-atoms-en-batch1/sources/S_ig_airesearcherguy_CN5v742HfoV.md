Do you survey your customers? It's a great way to gain valuable insights, except that most of the time self-reported survey data is not very reliable. And the reason is because as part of the human condition, we vastly underestimate the power subconscious forces have on our decision making. Unfortunately, most small businesses can't afford brain scanning technology or advanced heat maps. However, you can still conduct valuable surveys. You just got to ask the right questions. First, don't ask them what they think they would do in a situation. Ask them what they've done. How many products have you purchased from Amazon this month? How many of those products have you already left a review for? Second, if you do need to ask them what they think, don't ask them what they would do. Ask them what they think somebody else would do. People are surprisingly more accurate of their assessments of other people's actions than their own.
