Should you bother building a brand if you sell on Amazon? A lot of gurus say no because people aren't brand loyal, and self-reported surveys back that up. People tend to claim that they're not loyal to a specific brand. However, humans tend to shop with brands that they're familiar with. This is because of something called the availability heuristic. We as humans tend to be swayed more by the thing most readily available in our memory. So I say this is the reason why branding is important. The more you can get your brand in front of people, the more likely you'll end up being recognizable.
