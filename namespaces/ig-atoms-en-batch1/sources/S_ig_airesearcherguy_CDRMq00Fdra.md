I believe the most effective way to find a product to sell on Amazon is to find a market to serve. A market is just a group of people with similar interests, and therefore they'll have similar issues and problems that need solving. How serving a market is different from finding a niche is a niche might just be handheld kitchen products. Not everybody needs all the products you can hold in your hand in the kitchen, but a market might include people who need dog leashes for running and then also need something that attaches their cell phone to their arm.
