If you use ManyChat to build chatbot flows, here's a quick tip to keep you organized. All right, you want to avoid creating huge flows like this because it's very hard to remain organized and anytime you need to make a change, it's difficult. And anytime you need to duplicate a process for another product or another client, it becomes very cumbersome. So the best way to stay organized is to have each section of a flow be its own flow and then link to that flow. That way, overlapping flows can link to the same ones and it becomes a lot easier to fix problems or duplicate aspects of a big flow.
