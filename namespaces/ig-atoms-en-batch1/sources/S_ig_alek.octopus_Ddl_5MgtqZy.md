There are three sales psychology hacks that are gonna make you damn rich. Number one, cost of inaction versus cost of action. How much it's costing for your prospect to keep on not having the solution that you offer versus the investment that they can make in your services right now. Showcase the difference and find out how much they are losing. Number two, the paradox of choice. Do not show your prospects more than two options. Otherwise they're going to feel paralyzed and they're not going to buy from you. Instead, show them an A and a B and ask them, which one would you like to go with? Assume the close. And number three, self persuasion. Instead of telling them that you're the best, simply ask them, why do you think we would be the best choice to help you with this solution? Then hear in their own words what makes you the best person to work with.
