Someone once asked a group of successful restaurateurs, "If you had to start all over again, what is the one thing that you would need to guarantee your success?" One restaurateur said a million dollars. Another one said the absolute most high-tech cooking equipment. A third one said the best ingredients possible. If he had the best-tasting food, he was sure to succeed. The last restaurateur was supposedly Ray Kroc, though I'm not sure that's true, but he said a starving crowd. This is a powerful answer because it is the ingredient to ensure success for any industry, no matter what you're selling. If you have a starving crowd and you can feed them, you will succeed.
