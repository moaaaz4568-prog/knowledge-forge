The sixth and final principle of persuasion, according to Robert Cialdini, is consensus. Consensus basically states that humans prefer to do what most other humans are doing. In a study illustrating this, they went to a hotel. There were messages in the hotel bathroom talking about the benefits of reusing towels. Typically about 35% of people comply with that. However, they ran an experiment in a number of other rooms where the message actually talked about 75% of people tend to prefer to reuse their towels because of the benefits to the environment. Then they increased compliance dramatically. So in your business, social proof is king. If you can show other people how your business is preferred by their peers, you will win the sale more and more.
