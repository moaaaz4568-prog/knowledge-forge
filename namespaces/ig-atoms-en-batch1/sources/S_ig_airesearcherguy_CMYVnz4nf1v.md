My favorite copywriting lesson of all time is from Eugene Schwartz. He says your copy is basically a department store display window. If anybody ever notices it, it's like noticing a streak or a smudge on the window. That means that they're not seeing the product that it's made to show off. The job of your copy is to show off the beautiful product display. So if you ask somebody to review your copy, even if they compliment it, if the words that come out of their mouth aren't, hey, tell me more about this product, then you know you missed the mark.
